Real Numbers — Question 33 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q11 mark
Question 33 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
( √ 3 + 2)2 + ( √ 3− 2)2 is a/an (A) positive rational number (B) negative rational number (C) positive irrational number (D) negative irrational number
Answer
Answer as text
(A) positive rational number. (3 + 4 + 4 √ 3) + (3 + 4− 4 √ 3) = 14.
