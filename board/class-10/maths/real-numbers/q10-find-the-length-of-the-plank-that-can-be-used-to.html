Question as text
Find the length of the plank that can be used to measure the lengths 4 m 20 cm and 5 m 4 cm exactly, in the least time.
Answer
Answer as text
Required length = HCF(420, 504) cm. 420 = 2 2· 3· 5· 7, 504 = 2 3· 32· 7; HCF = 2 2· 3· 7 = 84. Plank length = 84 cm.
