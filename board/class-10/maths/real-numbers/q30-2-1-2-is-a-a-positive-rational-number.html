CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q21 mark
Question 30 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
( 1 + √ )2 − ( 1− √ )2 is: (A) a positive rational number. (B) a negative integer. (C) a positive irrational number. (D) a negative irrational number.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) a positive irrational number. The expression = 4 √ 3.
