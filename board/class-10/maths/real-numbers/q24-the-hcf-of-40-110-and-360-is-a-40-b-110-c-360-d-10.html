Real Numbers — Question 24 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/2/1Q181 mark
Question 24 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
The HCF of 40, 110 and 360 is : (A) 40 (B) 110 (C) 360 (D) 10
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 10. 40 = 2 3· 5, 110 = 2· 5· 11, 360 = 23· 32· 5; HCF = 2· 5.
