Real Numbers — Question 3 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q31 mark

Question as text
For any natural number n, 6n ends with the digit : (A) 0 (B) 6 (C) 3 (D) 2
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 6. Any power of 6 ends in 6.
