Real Numbers — Question 26 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q21 mark
Question 26 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
If x is the LCM of 4, 6, 8 and y is the LCM of 3, 5, 7 and p is the LCM of x and y, then which of the following is true ? (A) p = 35x (B) p = 4y (C) p = 8x (D) p = 16y
Answer
Answer as text
(A) p = 35x. x = 24, y = 105, p = LCM(24, 105) = 840 = 35× 24.
