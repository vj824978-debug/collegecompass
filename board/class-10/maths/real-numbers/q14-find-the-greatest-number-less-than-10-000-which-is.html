Find the greatest number less than 10,000 which is exactly divisible by 48, 60 and 65.

CBSE 2026QP 30/4/1Q313 marks
Question 14 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
Find the greatest number less than 10,000 which is exactly divisible by 48, 60 and 65.
Answer
Answer as text
48 = 24· 3, 60 = 2 2· 3· 5, 65 = 5· 13; LCM = 2 4· 3· 5· 13 = 3120. Multiples: 3120, 6240, 9360, 12480. Greatest below 10000 is 9360.
