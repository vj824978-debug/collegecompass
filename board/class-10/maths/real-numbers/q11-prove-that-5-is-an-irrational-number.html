Prove that √ 5 is an irrational number.

CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q283 marks
Question 11 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
Prove that √ 5 is an irrational number.
Answer
Answer as text
Suppose √ 5 = a b , a,b coprime integers, b̸= 0. Then a2 = 5b2, so 5 divides a2, hence 5 divides a; let a = 5c. Then 25c2 = 5b2⇒b2 = 5c2, so 5 divides b. Thus 5 is a common factor of a and b, a contradiction. Hence √ 5 is irrational.
