Prove that √ 2 is an irrational number.

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q263 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
(A) Prove that √ 2 is an irrational number. OR (B) Let x and y be two distinct prime numbers and p =x2y3, q =xy4, r =x5y2. Find the HCF and LCM of p, q and r. Further check if HCF(p,q,r )× LCM(p,q,r ) =p×q×r or not.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Suppose √ 2 = a b , a,b coprime integers, b̸= 0. Then a2 = 2b2, so 2 divides a2, hence 2 divides a; write a = 2c: 4c2 = 2b2⇒b2 = 2c2, so 2 divides b. Then 2 is a common factor of a,b — contradiction. Hence √ 2 is irrational. (B) HCF = xy2 (least powers), LCM = x5y4 (greatest powers). HCF ×LCM = x6y6, while pqr =x8y9. So HCF×LCM̸=p×q×r.
