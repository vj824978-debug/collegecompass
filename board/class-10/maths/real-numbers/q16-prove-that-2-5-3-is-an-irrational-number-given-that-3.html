Question 16 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
Prove that 2− 5 √ 3 is an irrational number given that √ 3 is irrational.
Answer
Answer as text
Suppose 2− 5 √ 3 =r is rational. Then √ 3 = 2−r 5 , which is rational (rationals are closed under subtraction and division by non-zero rationals). This contradicts that √ 3 is irrational. Hence 2− 5 √ 3 is irrational.
