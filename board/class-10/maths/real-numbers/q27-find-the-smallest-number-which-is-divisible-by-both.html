Find the smallest number which is divisible by both 644 and 462.

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q232 marks
Question 27 · Class 10 Mathematics · Real Numbers
Question as text
(a) Find the smallest number which is divisible by both 644 and 462. OR (b) Two numbers are in the ratio 4 : 5 and their HCF is 11. Find the LCM of these numbers.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 644 = 22× 7× 23, 462 = 2× 3× 7× 11. LCM = 2 2× 3× 7× 11× 23 = 21252. (b) Numbers 4× 11 = 44 and 5× 11 = 55; LCM = 2 2× 5× 11 = 220 (or 44× 55 11 = 220).
