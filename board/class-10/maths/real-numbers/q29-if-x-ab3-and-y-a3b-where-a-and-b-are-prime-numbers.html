Question as text
If x =ab3 and y =a3b, where a and b are prime numbers, then [HCF(x,y )− LCM(x,y )] is equal to: (A) 1−a3b3 (B) ab(1−ab) (C) ab−a4b4 (D) ab(1−ab)(1 +ab)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) ab(1−ab)(1 +ab). HCF = ab, LCM = a3b3; ab−a3b3 =ab(1−a2b2) =ab(1−ab)(1 +ab).
