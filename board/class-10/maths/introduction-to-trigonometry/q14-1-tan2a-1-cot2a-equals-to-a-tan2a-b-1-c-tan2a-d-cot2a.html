Introduction to Trigonometry — Question 14 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

CBSE 2026QP 30/4/1Q121 mark
Question 14 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
1 + tan2A 1 + cot2A equals to: (A) tan2A (B)−1 (C) − tan2A (D) cot2A
Answer
Answer as text
(A) tan2A. sec2A csc2A = sin2A cos2A = tan2A.
