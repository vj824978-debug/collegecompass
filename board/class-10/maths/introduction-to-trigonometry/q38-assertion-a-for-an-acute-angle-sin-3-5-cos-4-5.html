Assertion (A): For an acute angle θ, sinθ = 3 5⇒ cosθ =−4 5.

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q191 mark
Question 38 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
Assertion (A): For an acute angle θ, sinθ = 3 5⇒ cosθ =−4 5. Reason (R): For any value of θ, (0◦≤θ≤ 90◦) sin2θ + cos2θ = 1. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A is false (cos θ = +4 5 for acute θ); R is true.
