Introduction to Trigonometry — Question 19 (CBSE 2026, 2 marks)

CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q242 marks
Question 19 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
(a) Evaluate: sin3 60◦− tan 30◦ cos2 45◦ OR (b) For acute angles A and B and A + 2B and 2A + B are acute if tan(A+2B) = √ 3 and sin(2A+B) = 1√ 2, then find the measures of angles A and B.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) sin3 60◦ = 3 √ 8 , tan 30◦ = √ 3 ; numerator = 9 √ 3− 8 √ 24 = √ 24 ; divide by 1 2: √ 12 . (b) A + 2B = 60 ◦, 2A +B = 45 ◦. Adding: A +B = 35 ◦; subtracting: B−A = 15 ◦. So A = 10◦, B = 25◦.
