Introduction to Trigonometry — Question 17 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

Question 17 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
For an acute angle θ, if sinθ = 1 9, then value of 9 cosecθ + 1 9 cosecθ− 1 is (A) 0 (B) 80 81 (C) 1 (D) 82
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 82 80. cosec θ = 9: 81 + 1 81− 1.
