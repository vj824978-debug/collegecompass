(cotθ + tanθ) equals : (A) cosecθ secθ (B) sinθ secθ (C) cosθ tanθ (D) sinθ cosθ

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q91 mark
Question 30 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
(cotθ + tanθ) equals : (A) cosecθ secθ (B) sinθ secθ (C) cosθ tanθ (D) sinθ cosθ
Answer
Answer as text
(A) cosecθ secθ. cosθ sinθ + sinθ cosθ = 1 sinθ cosθ.
