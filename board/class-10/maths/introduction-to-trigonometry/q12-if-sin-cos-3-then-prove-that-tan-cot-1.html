Introduction to Trigonometry — Question 12 (CBSE 2026, 3 marks)

CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q263 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
(a) If sinθ + cosθ = √ 3, then prove that tan θ + cotθ = 1. OR (b) Prove that : (sin A + secA)2 + (cosA + cosecA)2 = (1 + secA cosecA)2
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Squaring: 1 + 2 sin θ cosθ = 3 ⇒ sinθ cosθ = 1. tan θ + cotθ = sin2θ + cos2θ sinθ cosθ = 1 1 = 1. (Note: the hypothesis cannot actually hold, since sin θ + cosθ≤ √ 2; the proof is formal.) (b) LHS = sin2A+cos 2A+2 sinA secA+2 cosA cosecA+sec 2A+cosec 2A = 1+2(tan A+cotA)+ sec2A cosec2A [since sec 2A + cosec2A = 1 sin2A cos2A] = 1 + 2 sinA cosA + sec2A cosec2A = (1 + secA cosecA)2 = RHS.
