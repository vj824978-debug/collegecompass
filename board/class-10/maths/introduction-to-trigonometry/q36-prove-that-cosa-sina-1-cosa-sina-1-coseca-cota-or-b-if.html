Prove that cosA + sinA− 1 cosA− sinA + 1 = cosecA− cotA OR (B) If cotθ + cosθ =p and cotθ− cosθ =q, prove…

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q293 marks
Question 36 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
(A) Prove that cosA + sinA− 1 cosA− sinA + 1 = cosecA− cotA OR (B) If cotθ + cosθ =p and cotθ− cosθ =q, prove that p2−q2 = 4√pq
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Divide numerator and denominator by sin A: LHS = cotA− cosecA + 1 cotA + cosecA− 1. Write 1 = cosec2A− cot2A in the numerator: (cosec A− cotA)(cosecA + cotA)− (cosecA− cotA) = (cosecA− cotA)(cosecA + cotA− 1). Cancel with the denominator: LHS = cosec A− cotA. (B) p2− q2 = ( p + q)(p− q) = 2 cot θ· 2 cosθ = 4 cot θ cosθ. pq = cot 2θ− cos2θ = cos2θ ( 1 sin2θ− 1 ) = cos2θ cot2θ, so 4√pq = 4 cotθ cosθ =p2−q2.
