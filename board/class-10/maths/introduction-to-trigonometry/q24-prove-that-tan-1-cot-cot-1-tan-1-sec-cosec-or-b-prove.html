Introduction to Trigonometry — Question 24 (CBSE 2025, 3 marks)

CBSE 2025QP 30/1/1Q273 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
(a) Prove that: tanθ 1− cotθ + cotθ 1− tanθ = 1 + secθ cosecθ OR (b) Prove that: sinA + cosA sinA− cosA + sinA− cosA sinA + cosA = 2 2 sin2A− 1
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Putt = tanθ: LHS = t2 t− 1− 1 t(t− 1) = t3− 1 t(t− 1) = t2 +t + 1 t = 1+t+ 1 t . And tanθ+cotθ = sin2θ + cos2θ sinθ cosθ = secθ cosecθ. So LHS = 1 + sec θ cosecθ. (b) LHS = (sinA + cosA)2 + (sinA− cosA)2 sin2A− cos2A = 2 sin2A− (1− sin2A) = 2 2 sin2A− 1.
