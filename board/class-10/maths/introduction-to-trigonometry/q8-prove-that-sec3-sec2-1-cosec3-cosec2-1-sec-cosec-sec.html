Prove that : sec3θ sec2θ− 1 + cosec3θ cosec2θ− 1 = secθ· cosecθ (secθ + cosecθ) OR (b) If secα cosecβ =p and…

Question 8 · Class 10 Mathematics · Introduction to Trigonometry
Question as text
(a) Prove that : sec3θ sec2θ− 1 + cosec3θ cosec2θ− 1 = secθ· cosecθ (secθ + cosecθ) OR (b) If secα cosecβ =p and tanα cosecβ =q, then prove that (p2−q2) sec2α =p2.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) LHS = sec3θ tan2θ + cosec3θ cot2θ = 1 cosθ sin2θ + 1 sinθ cos2θ = cosθ + sinθ sin2θ cos2θ = 1 sinθ cosθ ( 1 cosθ + 1 sinθ ) = secθ cosecθ(secθ + cosecθ) = RHS. (b) p2−q2 = sec2α− tan2α cosec2β = sin2β. (p2−q2) sec2α = sin2β sec2α = sec2α cosec2β =p2.
