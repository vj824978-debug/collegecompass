(a) Prove that if a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the same ratio. OR (b) As shown in the given figure, a girl of height 90 cm is walking away from the base of a lamp post at a speed of 1·2 m/s. If the lamp is 3 ·6 m above the ground, find the length of her shadow after 4 seconds.

Answer as text
