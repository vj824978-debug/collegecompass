D is the mid-point of side BC of △ABC.

CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q335 marks
Question 18 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
(a) D is the mid-point of side BC of △ABC. CE and BF intersect at O, a point on AD. AD is produced to G such that OD = DG. Prove that (i) OBGC is a parallelogram. (ii) EF ∥ BC (iii) △AEF∼△ABC OR (b) Through the mid-point Q of side CD of a parallelogram ABCD, the line AR is drawn which intersects BD at P and produced BC at R. Prove that (i) AQ = QR (ii) AP = 2PQ (iii) PR = 2AP
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Diagonals OG and BC of OBGC bisect each other at D (BD = DC, OD = DG), so OBGC is a parallelogram. (ii) Hence BG ∥ CE, i.e. EO ∥ BG; in△ABG, by BPT AE EB = AO OG. Also CG ∥ BF, i.e. OF ∥ GC; in△ACG, AF FC = AO OG. So AE EB = AF FC and by the converse of BPT, EF ∥ BC. (iii) EF ∥ BC⇒ ∠AEF = ∠ABC, ∠AFE = ∠ACB (corresponding); so △AEF ∼△ ABC (AA). (b) (i) In △ADQ and△RCQ: DQ = QC, ∠AQD = ∠RQC (vertically opposite), ∠ADQ = ∠RCQ (alternate, AD∥ BR). So△ADQ∼=△RCQ (ASA) and AQ = QR. (ii) AB∥ DQ⇒△APB∼△QPD (AA), so AP PQ = AB DQ = CD 2CD = 2, i.e. AP = 2PQ. (iii) PR = PQ + QR = PQ + AQ = PQ + (AP + PQ) = AP + 2PQ = AP + AP = 2AP.
