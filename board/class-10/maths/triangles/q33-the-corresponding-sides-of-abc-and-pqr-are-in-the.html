The corresponding sides of △ABC and△PQR are in the ratio 3 : 5.

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q335 marks
Question 33 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
(A) The corresponding sides of △ABC and△PQR are in the ratio 3 : 5. AD⊥BC and PS ⊥QR as shown in the following figures: (i) Prove that△ADC∼△PSR (ii) If AD = 4 cm, find the length of PS. (iii) Using (ii) find ar(△ABC) : ar(△PQR ) OR (B) State basic proportionality theorem. Use it to prove the following: If three parallel lines l, m, n are intersected by transversals q and s as shown in the adjoining figure, then AB BC = DE EF .
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(i)△ABC∼△PQR (sides proportional), so ∠C = ∠R; also ∠ADC = ∠PSR = 90◦. Hence △ADC∼△PSR (AA). (ii) AD PS = AC PR = 3 5⇒PS = 20 3 cm. (iii) ar(ABC) ar(PQR ) = 2BC·AD 2QR·PS = 3 5· 4 20/3 = 3 5· 3 5, i.e. 9 : 25 . (B) BPT: if a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, it divides the two sides in the same ratio. Join AF, meetingm at G. In△ACF ,BG∥CF : AB BC = AG GF . In△FAD ,GE∥AD: FG GA = FE ED , i.e. AG GF = DE EF . Hence AB BC = DE EF .
