Which of the following statements is false?

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q81 mark
Question 34 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
Which of the following statements is false? (A) Two right triangles are always similar. (B) Two squares are always similar. (C) Two equilateral triangles are always similar. (D) Two circles are always similar.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Two right triangles need not have their other angles equal.
