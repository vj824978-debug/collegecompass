Question 24 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
(a) In the given figure, D is a point on the side BC of △ABC such that ∠ADC = ∠BAC. Show that CA2 =CD·CB. OR (b) In the given figure, OA·OB =OC·OD. Show that ∠A = ∠C and ∠B = ∠D.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) In△ADC and△BAC: ∠ADC = ∠BAC (given), ∠C common. So△ADC∼△BAC (AA), hence CA CB = CD CA ⇒CA2 =CD·CB. (b) OA·OB = OC·OD⇒ OA OC = OD OB ; ∠AOD = ∠COB (vertically opposite). So △AOD∼ △COB (SAS similarity), hence ∠A = ∠C and ∠D = ∠B.
