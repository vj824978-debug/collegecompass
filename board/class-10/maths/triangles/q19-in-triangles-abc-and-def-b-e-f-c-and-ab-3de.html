Question as text
In triangles ABC and DEF , ∠B = ∠E, ∠F = ∠C and AB = 3DE. Then, the two triangles are: (A) congruent but not similar (B) congruent as well as similar (C) neither congruent nor similar (D) similar but not congruent
Answer
Answer as text
(D) similar but not congruent. AA similarity; AB = 3DE so sides are not equal.
