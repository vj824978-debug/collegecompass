State and prove Basic Proportionality Theorem.

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q345 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
(A) State and prove Basic Proportionality Theorem. OR (B) In the given figure, CM and RN are respectively the medians of△ABC and△PQR . If△ABC∼ △PQR , then prove that : (i)△AMC∼△PNR (ii)△CMB ∼△RNQ
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Statement: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio. Proof: In△ABC,DE∥BC with D on AB, E on AC. Join BE, CD; drawEN⊥AB,DM⊥AC. ar(ADE) ar(BDE) = 2AD·EN 2DB·EN = AD DB and ar(ADE) ar(DEC ) = AE EC . △BDE and△DEC are on the same base DE between the same parallels, so ar(BDE) =ar(DEC ). Hence AD DB = AE EC . (B) (i) △ABC ∼ △PQR ⇒ ∠A = ∠P and AB PQ = AC PR . As AM = 1 2AB, PN = 1 2PQ : AM PN = AC PR . By SAS, △AMC∼△PNR . (ii) ∠B = ∠Q and CB RQ = AB PQ = 2MB 2NQ = MB NQ . By SAS, △CMB ∼△RNQ.
