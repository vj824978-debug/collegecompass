If a line drawn parallel to one side of triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points divides…

CBSE 2025QP 30/6/1Q335 marks
Question 41 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
(a) If a line drawn parallel to one side of triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points divides the two sides in the same ratio, then it is parallel to third side. State and prove the converse of the above statement. OR (b) In the adjoining figure, △CAB is a right triangle, right angled at A and AD⊥BC. Prove that △ADB∼△CDA. Further, if BC = 10 cm and CD = 2 cm, find the length of AD.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The given statement is the converse of BPT, so its converse is the Basic Proportionality Theorem: if a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio. Proof: In△ABC, letDE∥BC meetAB at D,AC at E. Join BE, CD; draw EN⊥AB,DM⊥ AC. ar(ADE) ar(BDE) = 2AD·EN 2DB·EN = AD DB , ar(ADE) ar(DEC ) = 2AE·DM 2EC·DM = AE EC .△BDE and△DEC are on the same base DE between the same parallels DE and BC, so ar(BDE) =ar(DEC ). Hence AD DB = AE EC . (b) In △ADB and△CDA: ∠ADB = ∠CDA = 90 ◦; ∠ABD = 90 ◦− ∠C = ∠CAD. So △ADB∼△CDA (AA). Hence AD CD = DB DA⇒AD2 =CD·DB = 2× (10− 2) = 16, so AD = 4 cm.
