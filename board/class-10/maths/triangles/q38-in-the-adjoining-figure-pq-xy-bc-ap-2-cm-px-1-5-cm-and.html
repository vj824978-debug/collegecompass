CBSE 2025QP 30/6/1Q61 mark
Question 38 · Class 10 Mathematics · Triangles
Question as text
In the adjoining figure, PQ∥XY ∥BC, AP = 2 cm, PX = 1.5 cm and BX = 4 cm. If QY = 0.75 cm, then AQ +CY = (A) 6 cm (B) 4.5 cm (C) 3 cm (D) 5.25 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 3 cm. AP PX = AQ QY ⇒AQ = 2× 0.75 1.5 = 1; AX XB = AY YC ⇒ 3.5 4 = 1.75 CY ⇒CY = 2. Sum = 3 cm.
