Question as text
The diagonals of a quadrilateral ABCD intersect each other at the point O such that AO OC = BO OD . Show that quadrilateral ABCD is a trapezium.
Answer
Answer as text
Draw OE ∥ AB meeting AD at E. In △ABD, by BPT AE ED = BO OD = AO OC . So in △ADC, AE ED = AO OC⇒EO∥DC (converse of BPT). Hence AB∥EO∥DC, so ABCD is a trapezium.
