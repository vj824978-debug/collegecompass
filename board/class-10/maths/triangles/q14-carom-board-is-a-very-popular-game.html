Question as text
Carom board is a very popular game. The board is a square of side length 65 cm. It has circular pockets in each corner. Ansh strikes a disc, kept at position P with a striker. The disc, hits the boundary of the board at R and goes straight to pocket at corner C. It is given that PS = 9 cm, PQ = 35 cm, BR = x, ∠PRQ =α and ∠CRB =θ. Based on the above information, answer the following questions: (i) Using law of reflection i.e. ∠PRT = ∠CRT , prove that θ =α. [1] (ii) Prove that△PQR∼△CBR given that PQ is perpendicular to AB. [1] (iii) (a) Find the value of x using similarity of triangles. [2] OR (b) If Area△PQR Area△CBR = PQ 2 CB 2 , then find the value of x.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) TR⊥AB, so θ = 90◦− ∠CRT and α = 90◦− ∠PRT . Since ∠PRT = ∠CRT , θ =α. (ii) ∠PQR = ∠CBR = 90◦ and ∠PRQ =α =θ = ∠CRB, so△PQR∼△CBR (AA). (iii)(a) AQ = PS = 9, so QR = 65− 9−x = 56−x. PQ CB = QR BR ⇒ 35 65 = 56−x x ⇒ 100x = 3640⇒ x = 36.4 cm. (b) Ratio of areas = PQ 2 CB 2 = QR2 BR2⇒ 35 65 = 56−x x ⇒ x = 36.4 cm.
