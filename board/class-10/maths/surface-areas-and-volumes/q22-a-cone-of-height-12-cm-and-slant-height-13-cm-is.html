A cone of height 12 cm and slant height 13 cm is surmounted on a hemisphere having radius equal to that of cone. The entire height of the solid is (A) 17 cm (B) 18 cm (C) 22 cm (D) 23 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 17 cm. r = √ 132− 122 = 5; height = 12 + 5 = 17 cm.
