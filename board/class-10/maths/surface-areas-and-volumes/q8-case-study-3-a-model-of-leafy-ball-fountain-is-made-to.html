Question as text
Case Study – 3 A model of Leafy Ball Fountain is made to be kept on the tabletop. Water gently cascades down the ball into a decorative cylindrical pool where it is recycled. The diameter of spherical ball is 21 cm. Cylindrical pool – Outer diameter is 50 cm and inner diameter is 40 cm. Height of solid base is 14 cm. Height of water filled is 7 cm. Observe the figure and answer the following questions : (i) Determine the total height of the fountain. [1] (ii) Find the volume of the ball. [1] (iii) (a) If one-third of the ball is submerged in the water, find the volume of the water filled in the pool. [2] OR (iii) (b) Find the sum of the outer curved surface area of the cylindrical part and surface area of the ball. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Ball rests on the base: 14 + 21 = 35 cm. (ii) V = 4 3· 22 7 · (10.5)3 = 4851 cm 3. (iii)(a) Water region (inner radius 20 cm, height 7 cm) = 22 7 · 400· 7 = 8800 cm 3, less one-third of ball = 1617 cm 3: water = 7183 cm 3 (taking “one-third of the ball” as one-third of its volume). (iii)(b) Cylinder height = 14 + 7 = 21 cm, outer radius 25 cm: CSA = 2· 22 7 · 25· 21 = 3300 cm2; ball = 4· 22 7 · (10.5)2 = 1386 cm2; sum = 4686 cm 2.
