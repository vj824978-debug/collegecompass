Three tennis balls are just packed in a cylindrical jar. If radius of each ball is r, volume of air inside the jar is (A) 2πr3 (B) 3πr3 (C) 5πr3 (D) 4πr3
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 2πr3. Cylinder: πr2(6r) = 6πr3; balls: 3× 4 3πr3 = 4πr3; air = 2πr3.
