Surface Areas and Volumes — Question 18 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q121 mark
Question 18 · Class 10 Mathematics · Surface Areas and Volumes
Question as text
If the volumes of two cubes are in the ratio 8 : 125, then the ratio of their surface areas is : (A) 8 : 125 (B) 4 : 25 (C) 2 : 5 (D) 16 : 25
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 4 : 25. Sides 2 : 5, so surface areas 4 : 25.
