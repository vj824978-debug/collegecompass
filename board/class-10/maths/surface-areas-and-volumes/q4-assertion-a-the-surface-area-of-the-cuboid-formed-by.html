Assertion (A): The surface area of the cuboid formed by joining two cubes of sides 4 cm each, endto-end, is…

CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q191 mark
Question 4 · Class 10 Mathematics · Surface Areas and Volumes
Question as text
Assertion (A): The surface area of the cuboid formed by joining two cubes of sides 4 cm each, endto-end, is 160 cm 2. Reason (R): The surface area of a cuboid of dimensions l×b×h is (lb +bh +hl). (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C). Cuboid 8 × 4× 4: 2(32 + 16 + 32) = 160 cm 2, so A is true; R is false (surface area is 2(lb +bh +hl)).
