Surface Areas and Volumes — Question 12 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q71 mark
Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Surface Areas and Volumes
Question as text
A conical cavity of maximum volume is carved out from a wooden solid hemisphere of radius 10 cm. Curved surface area of the cavity carved out is (use π = 3.14) (A) 314 √ 2 cm2 (B) 314 cm 2 (C) 3140 3 cm2 (D) 3140 √ 2 cm2
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 314 √ 2 cm2. r =h = 10, l = 10 √ 2, CSA = 3.14× 10× 10 √ 2.
