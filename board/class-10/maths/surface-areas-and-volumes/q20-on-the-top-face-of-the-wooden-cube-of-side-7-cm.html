On the top face of the wooden cube of side 7 cm, hemispherical depressions of radius 0.35 cm are to be formed by taking out the wood. The maximum number of depressions that can be formed is: (A) 400 (B) 100 (C) 20 (D) 10
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 100. Diameter = 0.7 cm, so 7/0.7 = 10 along each edge; 10× 10 = 100.
