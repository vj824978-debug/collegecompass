A room is in the form of a cylinder surmounted by a hemispherical dome.

CBSE 2025QP 30/1/1Q303 marks
Question 16 · Class 10 Mathematics · Surface Areas and Volumes
Question as text
A room is in the form of a cylinder surmounted by a hemispherical dome. The base radius of the hemisphere is half of the height of the cylindrical part. If the room contains 1408 21 m3 of air, find the height of the cylindrical part. (Use π = 22 7 ).
Answer
Answer as text
Let radius r, height h = 2r. Volume = πr2(2r) + 2 3πr3 = 8 3πr3 = 1408 ⇒r3 = 1408× 3× 7 21× 8× 22 = 8⇒r = 2. Height of cylindrical part = 4 m.
