If a cone of greatest possible volume is hollowed out from a solid wooden cylinder, then the ratio of the volume of remaining wood to the volume of cone hollowed out is (A) 1 : 1 (B) 1 : 3 (C) 2 : 1 (D) 3 : 1
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 2 : 1. Cone = 1 3πr2h, remaining = πr2h− 1 3πr2h = 2 3πr2h.
