The perimeter of a right triangle is 60 cm and its hypotenuse is 25 cm.

CBSE 2025QP 30/1/1Q345 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
(a) The perimeter of a right triangle is 60 cm and its hypotenuse is 25 cm. Find the lengths of other two sides of the triangle. OR (b) A train travels a distance of 480 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 8 km/h less, then it would have taken 3 hours more to cover the same distance. Find the speed of the train.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Sides x and 35− x: x2 + (35− x)2 = 625⇒ x2− 35x + 300 = 0⇒ (x− 15)(x− 20) = 0. Sides are 15 cm and 20 cm. (b) Speed v: 480 v− 8− 480 v = 3⇒ 3840 = 3v(v− 8)⇒ v2− 8v− 1280 = 0⇒ (v− 40)(v + 32) = 0. Speed = 40 km/h.
