Question 19 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
(A) The sides of a right triangle are such that the longest side is 4 m more than the shortest side and the third side is 2 m less than the longest side. Find the length of each side of the triangle. Also, find the difference between the numerical values of the area and the perimeter of the given triangle. OR (B) Express the equation x− 2 x− 3 + x− 4 x− 5 = 10 3 ; ( x̸= 3, 5) as a quadratic equation in standard form. Hence, find the roots of the equation so formed.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Shortest = x, longest = x + 4, third = x + 2. x2 + (x + 2)2 = (x + 4)2⇒ x2− 4x− 12 = 0⇒ (x− 6)(x + 2) = 0⇒ x = 6. Sides: 6 m, 8 m, 10 m . Area = 1 2· 6· 8 = 24, perimeter = 24; difference = 0. (B) 3[(x− 2)(x− 5) + (x− 4)(x− 3)] = 10(x− 3)(x− 5)⇒ 6x2− 42x + 66 = 10x2− 80x + 150 ⇒ 2x2 − 19x + 42 = 0 (standard form)⇒ (x− 6)(2x− 7) = 0, roots x = 6, 7 2.
