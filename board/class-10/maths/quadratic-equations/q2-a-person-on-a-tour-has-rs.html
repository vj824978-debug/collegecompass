Question as text
(a) A person on a tour has Rs. 4,200 for expenses. If he extends his tour for 3 days, he has to cut down his daily expenses by Rs. 70. Find the original duration of the tour. OR (b) The area of a right-angled triangle is 600 cm 2. If the base of the triangle exceeds the altitude by 10 cm, find all the three dimensions of the triangle.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Let duration = x days: 4200 x − 4200 x + 3 = 70⇒ 12600 = 70x(x + 3)⇒ x2 + 3x− 180 = 0⇒ (x + 15)(x− 12) = 0. x = 12 (rejecting−15): 12 days. (b) Altitude h, base h + 10: 1 2 h(h + 10) = 600⇒ h2 + 10h− 1200 = 0⇒ (h + 40)(h− 30) = 0⇒ h = 30. Altitude 30 cm, base 40 cm, hypotenuse √ 302 + 402 = 50 cm.
