Quadratic Equations — Question 7 (CBSE 2026, 2 marks)

CBSE 2026QP 30/4/1Q212 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
Verify that roots of the quadratic equation (p− q)x2 +( q− r)x+( r− p) = 0 are equal when q + r = 2p.
Answer
Answer as text
D = ( q− r)2− 4(p− q)(r− p). With q + r = 2 p: p− q = r− q 2 and r− p = r− q 2 , so 4(p− q)(r− p) = (r− q)2. Hence D = (q− r)2− (q− r)2 = 0, so the roots are equal (both equal to 1).
