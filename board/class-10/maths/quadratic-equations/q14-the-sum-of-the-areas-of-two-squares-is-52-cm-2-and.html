The sum of the areas of two squares is 52 cm 2 and difference of their perimeters is 8 cm.

CBSE 2025QP 30/2/1Q335 marks
Question 14 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
(a) The sum of the areas of two squares is 52 cm 2 and difference of their perimeters is 8 cm. Find the lengths of the sides of the two squares. OR (b) The time taken by a person to travel an upward distance of 150 km was 2 1 2 hours more than the time taken in the downward return journey. If he returned at a speed of 10 km/h more than the speed while going up, find the speeds in each direction.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Sides x > y : 4 x−4y = 8⇒ x = y+2. ( y+2) 2+y2 = 52⇒ y2+2y−24 = 0⇒ (y+6)(y−4) = 0⇒ y = 4 (reject−6). Sides: 6 cm and 4 cm . (b) Upward speed x km/h: 150 x − 150 x + 10 = 5 2 ⇒ 1500 x(x + 10) = 5 2 ⇒ x2 + 10x− 600 = 0 ⇒ (x + 30)(x− 20) = 0⇒ x = 20. Upward 20 km/h, downward 30 km/h.
