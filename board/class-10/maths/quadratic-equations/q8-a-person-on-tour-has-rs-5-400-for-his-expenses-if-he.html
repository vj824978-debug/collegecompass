Question 8 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
(a) A person on tour has Rs. 5,400 for his expenses. If he extends his tour by 5 days, he has to cut down his daily expenses by Rs. 180. Find the original duration of the tour and daily expense. OR (b) The total cost of certain piece of cloth was Rs. 2,100. During special sale time, the shopkeeper offered 2 m extra cloth for free thus reducing the price of cloth per metre by Rs. 120. What was the original per metre price of cloth and its length?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Let duration = x days. 5400 x − 5400 x + 5 = 180 ⇒ 27000 x(x + 5) = 180 ⇒ x2 + 5x− 150 = 0 ⇒ (x + 15)(x− 10) = 0⇒ x = 10. Original duration 10 days; daily expense Rs. 540. (b) Let length = x m. 2100 x − 2100 x + 2 = 120 ⇒ 4200 x(x + 2) = 120 ⇒ x2 + 2x− 35 = 0 ⇒ (x + 7)(x− 5) = 0⇒ x = 5. Length 5 m; original price Rs. 420 per metre.
