A 2-digit number is seven times the sum of its digits and two (2) more than 5 times the product of its digits.

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q325 marks
Question 22 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
(a) A 2-digit number is seven times the sum of its digits and two (2) more than 5 times the product of its digits. Find the number. OR (b) Find the value(s) of p for which the quadratic equation given as ( p + 4)x2− (p + 1)x + 1 = 0 has real and equal roots. Also, find the roots of the equation(s) so obtained.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Let tens digit = a, units digit = b. 10 a + b = 7(a + b)⇒ 3a = 6b⇒ a = 2b. 10a + b = 5ab + 2⇒ 21b = 10b2 + 2⇒ 10b2− 21b + 2 = 0⇒ (b− 2)(10b− 1) = 0. b = 2 (b = 1 10 is not a digit), a = 4. The number is 42. (Check: 7 × 6 = 42, 5× 8 + 2 = 42.) (b) D = (p + 1)2− 4(p + 4) = p2− 2p− 15 = (p− 5)(p + 3) = 0⇒ p = 5 or p =−3. p = 5: 9 x2− 6x + 1 = 0⇒ (3x− 1)2 = 0, roots 1 3 , 1 3. p =−3: x2 + 2x + 1 = 0⇒ (x + 1)2 = 0, roots−1,−1.
