Quadratic Equations — Question 16 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q24(a)2 marks
Question 16 · Class 10 Mathematics · Quadratic Equations
Question as text
Find the value(s) of ‘ k’ so that the quadratic equation 4 x2 + kx + 1 = 0 has real and equal roots.
Answer
Answer as text
D = k2− 4(4)(1) = 0⇒ k2 = 16, so k = ±4.
