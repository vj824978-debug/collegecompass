Obtain the zeroes of the polynomial 7 x2 + 18x− 9.

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q273 marks
Question 23 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
Obtain the zeroes of the polynomial 7 x2 + 18x− 9. Hence, write a polynomial each of whose zeroes is twice the zeroes of given polynomial.
Answer
Answer as text
7x2 + 18x− 9 = 7x2 + 21x− 3x− 9 = (7x− 3)(x + 3); zeroes 3 7 and−3. New zeroes 6 7,−6: sum = −36 7 , product =−36 7 . Required polynomial: k ( x2 + 36 7 x− 36 ) , e.g. 7x2 + 36x− 36.
