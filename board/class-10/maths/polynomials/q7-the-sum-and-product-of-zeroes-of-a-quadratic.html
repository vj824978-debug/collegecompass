Polynomials — Question 7 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q141 mark
Question 7 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
The sum and product of zeroes of a quadratic polynomial p(x) are −1 3 and 2 respectively. The polynomial p(x) is : (A) 3x2−x + 6 (B) x2 + 1 3x− 2 (C) 3x2−x + 2 (D)−3x2−x− 6
Answer
Answer as text
(D)−3x2−x− 6. p(x) =k ( x2 + 1 3x + 2 ) ; k =−3 gives−3x2−x− 6 (sum =−−1 −3 =−1 3, product =−6 −3 = 2).
