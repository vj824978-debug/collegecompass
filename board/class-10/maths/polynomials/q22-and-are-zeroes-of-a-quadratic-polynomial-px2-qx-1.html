Polynomials — Question 22 (CBSE 2025, 3 marks)

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q303 marks
Question 22 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
α andβ are zeroes of a quadratic polynomial px2 +qx + 1. Form a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 2 α and 2 β .
Answer
Answer as text
α +β =−q p, αβ = 1 p. Sum = 2(α +β) αβ =−2q; product = 4 αβ = 4p. Required polynomial: k (x2 + 2qx + 4p), e.g. x2 + 2qx + 4p.
