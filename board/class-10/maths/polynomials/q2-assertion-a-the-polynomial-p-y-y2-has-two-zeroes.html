Assertion (A): The polynomial p(y) =y2 […] has two zeroes.

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q201 mark
Question 2 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
Assertion (A): The polynomial p(y) =y2 + 4y + 3 has two zeroes. Reason (R): A quadratic polynomial can have at most two zeroes. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B). y2 + 4y + 3 = (y + 1)(y + 3) has zeroes −1,−3, so A is true; R is true, but ‘at most two’ does not explain why this one has exactly two.
