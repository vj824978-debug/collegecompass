Question 11 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
If the zeroes of a polynomial p(x) are−3 and 8, then p(x) equals (A) x2 + 5x− 4 (B) ( x + 3)(−x + 8) (C) a(x2 + 5x− 24) (D) x2− 24
Answer
Answer as text
(B) (x + 3)(−x + 8). Its zeroes are −3 and 8. (Option (C) has zeroes 3 ,−8.)
