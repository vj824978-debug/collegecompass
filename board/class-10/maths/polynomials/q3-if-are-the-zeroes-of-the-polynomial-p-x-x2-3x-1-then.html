Polynomials — Question 3 (CBSE 2026, 2 marks)

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q212 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
If α,β are the zeroes of the polynomial p(x) =x2− 3x− 1, then find the value of 1 α + 1 β .
Answer
Answer as text
α +β = 3, αβ =−1. 1 α + 1 β = α +β αβ = 3 −1 =−3.
