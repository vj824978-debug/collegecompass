If p and q are zeroes of the polynomial p(y) = 21y2−y− 2, then find the value of (1 −p)· (1−q).

CBSE 2025QP 30/2/1Q212 marks
Question 17 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
If p and q are zeroes of the polynomial p(y) = 21y2−y− 2, then find the value of (1 −p)· (1−q).
Answer
Answer as text
p +q = 1 21, pq =− 2 21. (1−p)(1−q) = 1− (p +q) +pq = 1− 1 21− 2 21 = 18 21 = 6 7.
