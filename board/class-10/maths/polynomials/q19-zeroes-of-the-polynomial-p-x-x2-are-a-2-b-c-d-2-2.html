Polynomials — Question 19 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q171 mark
Question 19 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
Zeroes of the polynomial p(x) =x2− 3 √ 2x + 4 are : (A) 2, √ (B) 2 √ 2, √ (C) 4 √ 2, − √ (D) √ 2, 2
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 2 √ 2, √ 2. x2− 3 √ 2x + 4 = (x− 2 √ 2)(x− √ 2).
