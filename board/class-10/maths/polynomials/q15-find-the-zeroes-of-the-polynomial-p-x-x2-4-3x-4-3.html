Polynomials — Question 15 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 2025QP 30/1/1Q222 marks
Question 15 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
Find the zeroes of the polynomial p(x) =x2 + 4 3x− 4 3.
Answer
Answer as text
p(x) = 1 3(3x2 + 4x− 4) = 1 3(3x− 2)(x + 2). Zeroes: 2 3 and−2.
