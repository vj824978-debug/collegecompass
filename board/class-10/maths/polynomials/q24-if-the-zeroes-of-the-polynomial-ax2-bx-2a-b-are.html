Polynomials — Question 24 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/6/1Q41 mark
Question 24 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
If the zeroes of the polynomial ax2 +bx + 2a b are reciprocal of each other, then the value of b is (A) 2 (B) 1 2 (C)−2 (D) −1
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 2. Product of zeroes = 2a/b a = 2 b = 1⇒b = 2.
