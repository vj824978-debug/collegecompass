Find the zeroes of the polynomial p(x) = 3x2− 4x− 4.

CBSE 2025QP 30/6/1Q273 marks
Question 25 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
Find the zeroes of the polynomial p(x) = 3x2− 4x− 4. Hence, write a polynomial whose each of the zeroes is 2 more than zeroes of p(x).
Answer
Answer as text
3x2− 4x− 4 = (3x + 2)(x− 2), zeroes 2 and− 2 3. New zeroes: 4 and 4 3; sum = 16 3 , product = 16 3 . Polynomial: x2− 16 3x + 16 3 , i.e. k(3x2− 16x + 16), e.g. 3x2− 16x + 16.
