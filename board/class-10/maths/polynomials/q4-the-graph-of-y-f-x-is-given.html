Question as text
The graph of y =f(x) is given. The number of distinct zeroes of y =f(x) is : (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 2. The curve cuts the x-axis at A and touches it at one more point.
