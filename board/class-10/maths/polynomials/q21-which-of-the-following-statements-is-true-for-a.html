Which of the following statements is true for a polynomial p(x) of degree 3?

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q171 mark
Question 21 · Class 10 Mathematics · Polynomials
Question as text
Which of the following statements is true for a polynomial p(x) of degree 3? (A) p(x) has at most two distinct zeroes. (B) p(x) has at least two distinct zeroes. (C) p(x) has exactly three distinct zeroes. (D) p(x) has at most three distinct zeroes.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) p(x) has at most three distinct zeroes.
