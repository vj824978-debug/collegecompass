Case Study – 1 During a theatre drama, a backdrop of building arches was used. The shape of the curve shown below can be represented by the polynomial p(x) =−x2 + 2x + 8, where x is the length (in feet) on stage level. Based on the figure given above, answer the following questions : (i) Determine the height of the arch. [1] (ii) (a) Find zeroes of the polynomial p(x). Which points on the graph represent the zeroes? [2] OR (ii) (b) Find the span of the arch on the stage floor. [2] (iii) Write the coordinates of the point of intersection of the above curve with the y-axis. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Height = p(1) =−1 + 2 + 8 = 9 ft. (ii)(a) −x2 + 2x + 8 = 0⇒ (x− 4)(x + 2) = 0: zeroes 4, −2, represented by A(4 , 0) and B(−2, 0). (ii)(b) Span = AB = 4− (−2) = 6 ft. (iii) p(0) = 8: point ( 0, 8).
