Ifα andβ are the zeroes of polynomial 3x2 + 6x +k such that α +β +αβ =−2 3, then the value of k is: (A)−8 (B) 8 (C) −4 (D) 4
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 4. α +β =−2, αβ = k 3;−2 +k 3 =−2 3⇒k = 4.
