A peacock sitting on the top of a tree of height 10 m observes a snake moving on the ground. If the snake is 10 √ 3 m away from the base of the tree, then angle of depression of the snake from the eye of the peacock is (A) 30◦ (B) 45◦ (C) 60◦ (D) 90◦
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 30◦. tan θ = 10 √ 3 = 1√ 3.
