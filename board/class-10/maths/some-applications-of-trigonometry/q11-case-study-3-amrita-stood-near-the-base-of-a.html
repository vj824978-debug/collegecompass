Case Study – 3 Amrita stood near the base of a lighthouse, gazing up at its towering height. She measured the angle of elevation to the top and found it to be 60◦. Then, she climbed a nearby observation deck, 40 metres higher than her original position and noticed the angle of elevation to the top of lighthouse to be 45 ◦. Based on the above given information, answer the following questions: (i) If CD is h metres, find the distance BD in terms of ‘h’. [1] (ii) Find distance BC in terms of ‘h’. [1] (iii) (a) Find the height CE of the lighthouse [Use √ 3 = 1·73] [2] OR (iii) (b) Find distance AE, if AC = 100 m. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) tan 45◦ = CD BD⇒BD = h m. (ii) BC = √ h2 +h2 = h √ 2 m. (iii)(a) AE = BD = h, CE = h + 40; tan 60◦ = h + 40 h ⇒ √ 3h = h + 40⇒ h = 40√ 3− 1 = 20( √ 3 + 1) = 54.6. CE = 94.6 m. (iii)(b) In△AEC, AE =AC cos 60◦ = 100× 1 2 = 50 m.
