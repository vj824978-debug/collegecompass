Some Applications of Trigonometry — Question 15 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q101 mark
Question 15 · Class 10 Mathematics · Some Applications of Trigonometry
Question as text
A 30 m long rope is tightly stretched and tied from the top of pole to the ground. If the rope makes an angle of 60 ◦ with the ground, the height of the pole is: (A) 10 √ 3 m (B) 30 √ 3 m (C) 15 m (D) 15 √ 3 m
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 15 √ 3 m. Height = 30 sin 60◦ = 15 √ 3 m.
