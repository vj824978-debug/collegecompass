CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q384 marks
Question 9 · Class 10 Mathematics · Some Applications of Trigonometry
Question as text
Elevated water storage tanks are built to store and supply water to nearby colonies. In the diagram given above, AB is an elevated water tank and CD is a nearby multistorey building. The building is 54 metres away from the water tank. From a window (W) of the building, the angle of elevation of top of the tank is 45 ◦ and angle of depression of its foot is 30 ◦. (i) Write a relation between d (the height of window) and y. [1] (ii) Determine the value of h. [1] (iii) (a) Determine height of the water tank. [2] OR (iii) (b) Find the value of x and height of the window above ground level. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
XW = AC = 54 m. (i) In right△AXW , ∠XWA = 30◦: sin 30◦ = d y⇒ y = 2d. (ii) tan 45◦ = h 54⇒ h = 54 m. (iii)(a) d = 54 tan 30◦ = 18 √ 3 m; height of tank = h +d = 54 + 18 √ 3≈ 85.18 m. (iii)(b) x = 54 cos 45◦ = 54 √ 2≈ 76.37 m; height of window = d = 18 √ 3≈ 31.18 m.
