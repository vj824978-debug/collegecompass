Question as text
An observer 1.8 m tall stands away from a chimney at a distance of 38.2 m along the ground. The angle of elevation of top of chimney from the eyes of observer is 45 ◦. The height of chimney above the ground is (A) 38.2 m (B) 36.4 m (C) 40 m (D) (38 .2) √ 2 m
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 40 m. Height above eye level = 38 .2 tan 45◦ = 38.2 m; 38.2 + 1.8 = 40 m.
