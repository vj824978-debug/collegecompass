If the length of the shadow of a tower is √ 3 times that of its height, then altitude of the Sun is : (A) 45◦ (B) 30◦ (C) 60◦ (D) 15◦
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 30◦. tan θ = h√ 3h = 1√ 3.
