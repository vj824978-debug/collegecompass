Probability — Question 21 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q141 mark
Question 21 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
A die is thrown once. The probability of getting a number which is not a factor of 36, is : (A) 1 2 (B) 2 3 (C) 1 6 (D) 5
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 1 6. Only 5 is not a factor of 36.
