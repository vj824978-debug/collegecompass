Question as text
While shuffling a pack of 52 cards, one card was accidently dropped. Find the probability that the dropped card (i) is not a face card. (ii) is a black king.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Face cards = 12; P = 40 52 = 10 13. (ii) Black kings = 2; P = 2 52 = 1 26.
