Three unbiased coins are tossed simultaneously.

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q303 marks
Question 23 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Three unbiased coins are tossed simultaneously. Find the probability of getting : (a) exactly two tails (b) at least one head (c) at most two heads
Answer
Answer as text
8 equally likely outcomes. (a) HTT, THT, TTH: 3 8. (b) all except TTT: 7 8. (c) all except HHH: 8.
