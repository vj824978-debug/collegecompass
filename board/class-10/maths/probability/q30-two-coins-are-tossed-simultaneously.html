CBSE 2025QP 30/6/1Q81 mark
Question 30 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Two coins are tossed simultaneously. The probability of getting atleast one head is (A) 1 4 (B) 1 2 (C) 3 4 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 3 4. Outcomes HH, HT, TH, TT; 3 favourable.
