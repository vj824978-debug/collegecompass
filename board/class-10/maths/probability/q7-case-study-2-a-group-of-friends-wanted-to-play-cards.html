Case Study – 2 A group of friends wanted to play cards with two identical packs together. While shuffling the cards, three cards are dropped. Rest of the cards are shuffled and one card is drawn at random. Assuming that the dropped cards were a queen of hearts, a ten of spades and an ace of clubs, answer the following questions : (i) Find the probability that the drawn card is a face card. [1] (ii) Find the probability that the drawn card is either a king or a queen. [1] (iii) (a) Do you think that the probability of getting a queen was higher if none of the cards were dropped? Justify your answer. [2] OR (iii) (b) Find the probability that the drawn card is a jack. Compare it with the probability when none of the cards were dropped. In which case is the probability of getting a jack higher? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Remaining cards = 104− 3 = 101. (i) Face cards = 24 − 1 = 23: 23 101. (ii) Kings 8 + queens 7 = 15: 15 101. (iii)(a) Yes. Now P (queen) = 7 101≈ 0.069; with no card dropped 8 104 = 1 13≈ 0.077, which is higher. (iii)(b) P (jack) = 8 101; without dropping 8 104 = 1 13. Since 8 101 > 8 104, the probability of a jack is higher after the cards were dropped .
