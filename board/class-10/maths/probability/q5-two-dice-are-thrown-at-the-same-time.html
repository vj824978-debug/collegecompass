Two dice are thrown at the same time.

CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q313 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Two dice are thrown at the same time. Determine the probability that the (i) sum of the numbers on the two dice is 5, and (ii) difference of the numbers on the two dice is 3.
Answer
Answer as text
36 equally likely outcomes. (i) (1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1): P = 4 36 = 1 9. (ii) (1,4),(4,1),(2,5),(5,2),(3,6),(6,3): P = 6 36 = 1
