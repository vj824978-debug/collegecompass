CBSE 2026QP 30/4/1Q303 marks
Question 10 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
A bag contains 30 balls out of which ‘m’ number of balls are blue in colour. (i) Find the probability that a ball drawn at random from the bag is not blue. (ii) If 6 more blue balls are added in the bag, then the probability of drawing a blue ball will be 5 4times the probability of drawing a blue ball in the first case. Find the value of m.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) P (not blue) = 30− m 30 . (ii) m + 6 36 = 5 4· m 30 = m 24⇒ 24m + 144 = 36m⇒ m = 12.
