The number of red balls in a bag is 10 more than the number of black balls.

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q51 mark
Question 24 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
The number of red balls in a bag is 10 more than the number of black balls. If the probability of drawing a red ball at random from this bag is 3 5, then the total number of balls in the bag is: (A) 50 (B) 60 (C) 80 (D) 40
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 50. Black = b, red = b + 10: b + 10 2b + 10 = 3 5⇒ b = 20; total = 50.
