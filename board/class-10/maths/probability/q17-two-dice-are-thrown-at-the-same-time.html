Question 17 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Two dice are thrown at the same time. Determine the probability that the difference of the numbers on the two dice is 2.
Answer
Answer as text
Favourable: (1, 3), (3, 1), (2, 4), (4, 2), (3, 5), (5, 3), (4, 6), (6, 4) = 8 of 36 outcomes. P = 8 36 = 2 9.
