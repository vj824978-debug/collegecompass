CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q51 mark
Question 6 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Two dice are rolled together. The probability that the sum of the numbers obtained is divisible by 6, is : (A) 1 6 (B) 11 (C) 1 12 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
6. (A) 1 6. Sum 6: 5 outcomes; sum 12: 1 outcome; 6 36 = 1 6.
