Probability — Question 25 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q181 mark
Question 25 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
A pair of dice is thrown. The probability that sum of numbers appearing on top faces is at most 10 is: (A) 1 11 (B) 10 11 (C) 5 6 (D) 11
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 11 12. Sum 11 or 12 in 3 of 36 outcomes; 1 − 3 36 = 33 36 = 11 12.
