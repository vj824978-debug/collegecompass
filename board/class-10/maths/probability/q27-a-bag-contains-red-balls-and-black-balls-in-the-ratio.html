Question as text
A bag contains red balls and black balls in the ratio 3 : 7. A ball is drawn at random. The probability that ball so drawn is black in colour, is (A) 3 7 (B) 0.3 (C) 0.7 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 0.7. P = 7 3 + 7.
