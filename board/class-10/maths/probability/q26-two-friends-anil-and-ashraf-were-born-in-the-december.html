Question 26 · Class 10 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Two friends Anil and Ashraf were born in the December month in the year 2010. Find the probability that: (i) they share same date of birth. (ii) they have different dates of birth.
Answer
Answer as text
December has 31 days; fix Anil’s date, Ashraf’s date is any of 31 equally likely days. (i) P = 1 31. (ii) P = 1− 1 31 = 30 31.
