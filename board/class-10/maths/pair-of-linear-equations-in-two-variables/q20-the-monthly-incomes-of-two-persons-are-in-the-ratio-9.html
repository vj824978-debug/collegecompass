Question as text
The monthly incomes of two persons are in the ratio 9 : 7 and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio 4 : 3. If each saved Rs. 5,000, express the given situation algebraically as a system of linear equations in two variables. Hence, find their respective monthly incomes.
Answer
Answer as text
Incomes 9x, 7x; expenditures 4 y, 3y. Equations: 9 x− 4y = 5000, 7x− 3y = 5000. ×3 and×4: 27 x− 12y = 15000, 28x− 12y = 20000⇒ x = 5000, y = 10000. Monthly incomes: Rs. 45,000 and Rs. 35,000 .
