Aarush bought 2 pencils and 3 chocolates for Rs.

CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q325 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Mathematics · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
Question as text
Aarush bought 2 pencils and 3 chocolates for Rs. 11 and Tanish bought 1 pencil and 2 chocolates for Rs. 7 from the same shop. Represent this situation in the form of a pair of linear equations. Find the price of 1 pencil and 1 chocolate, graphically.
Answer
Answer as text
Let pencil = Rs. x, chocolate = Rs. y: 2 x + 3y = 11, x + 2y = 7. Points for 2x + 3y = 11: (1 , 3), (4, 1), (−2, 5); for x + 2y = 7: (1 , 3), (3, 2), (7, 0). Plot both lines; they intersect at (1 , 3). Pencil Rs. 1, chocolate Rs. 3.
