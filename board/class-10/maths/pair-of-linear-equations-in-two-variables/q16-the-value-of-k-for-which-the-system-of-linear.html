Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables — Question 16 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q31 mark
Question 16 · Class 10 Mathematics · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
Question as text
The value of ‘k’ for which the system of linear equations 6 x + y = 3k and 36x + 6y = 3 have infinitely many solutions is : (A) 6 (B) 1 6 (C) 1 2 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 1 6. 6 36 = 1 6 = 3k 3 ⇒ k = 1 6.
