Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables — Question 19 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q212 marks
Question 19 · Class 10 Mathematics · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
Question as text
Solve the following system of equations algebraically: 30x + 44y = 10; 40 x + 55y = 13
Answer
Answer as text
Multiply the first by 4 and the second by 3: 120 x + 176y = 40, 120 x + 165y = 39. Subtract: 11y = 1⇒ y = 1 11. Then 30 x + 4 = 10⇒ x = 1 5. x = 1 5 , y = 1 11.
