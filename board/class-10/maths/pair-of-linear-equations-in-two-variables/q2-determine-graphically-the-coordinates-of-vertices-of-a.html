Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables — Question 2 (CBSE 2026, 5 marks)

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q325 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Mathematics · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
Question as text
Determine graphically, the coordinates of vertices of a triangle whose equations are 2 x− 3y + 6 = 0; 2x + 3y− 18 = 0 and x = 0. Also, find the area of this triangle.
Answer
Answer as text
2x− 3y + 6 = 0: points (0 , 2), (3, 4), (−3, 0). 2 x + 3y− 18 = 0: points (0 , 6), (3, 4), (9, 0). x = 0 is the y-axis. Plotting, the lines meet at (3 , 4); they cut the y-axis at (0 , 2) and (0 , 6). Vertices: (0, 2), (0, 6), (3, 4). Area = 1 2× base 4× height 3 = 6 sq units.
