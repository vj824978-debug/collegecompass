Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
Question as text
Vijay invested certain amounts of money in two schemes A and B, which offer interest at the rate of 8% per annum and 9% per annum, respectively. He received Rs. 1,860 as the total annual interest. However, had he interchanged the amounts of investments in the two schemes, he would have received Rs. 20 more as annual interest. How much money did he invest in each scheme?
Answer
Answer as text
Let amounts be x (in A) and y (in B). 8x 100 + 9y 100 = 1860⇒ 8x + 9y = 186000; 9x 100 + 8y 100 = 1880⇒ 9x + 8y = 188000. Adding: 17( x + y) = 374000⇒ x + y = 22000. Subtracting: x− y = 2000. x = Rs. 12000 in scheme A, y = Rs. 10000 in scheme B. (Check: 960 + 900 = 1860.)
