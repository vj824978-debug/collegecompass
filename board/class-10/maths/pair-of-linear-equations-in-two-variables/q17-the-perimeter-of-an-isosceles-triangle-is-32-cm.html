The perimeter of an isosceles triangle is 32 cm.

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q325 marks
Question 17 · Class 10 Mathematics · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
Question as text
The perimeter of an isosceles triangle is 32 cm. If each equal side is 5 6th of the base, find the area of the triangle.
Answer
Answer as text
Let base = b, equal side = a. 2 a + b = 32 and a = 5 6 b⇒ 5 3 b + b = 32⇒ b = 12 cm, a = 10 cm. Height to base = √ 102− 62 = 8 cm (or Heron: s = 16, √ 16· 6· 6· 4 = 48). Area = 1 2× 12× 8 = 48 cm2.
