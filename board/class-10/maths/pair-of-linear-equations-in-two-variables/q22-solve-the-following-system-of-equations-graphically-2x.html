Solve the following system of equations graphically: 2x− y− 2 = 0 −4x + y + 4 = 0 Also, find the absolute difference between the ordinates of the points where given lines cut y-axis.
Answer
Answer as text
Line 1: y = 2x− 2, points (0 ,−2), (1, 0), (2, 2). Line 2: y = 4x− 4, points (0 ,−4), (1, 0), (2, 4). The lines intersect at (1 , 0): x = 1, y = 0. They cut the y-axis at (0 ,−2) and (0 ,−4); difference of ordinates = |− 2− (−4)| = 2.
