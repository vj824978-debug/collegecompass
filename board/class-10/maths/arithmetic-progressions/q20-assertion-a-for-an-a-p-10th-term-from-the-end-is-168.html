Arithmetic Progressions — Question 20 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q201 mark
Question 20 · Class 10 Mathematics · Arithmetic Progressions
Question as text
Assertion (A): For an A.P., 3, 6, 9, . . . ,198, 10th term from the end is 168. Reason (R): If ‘a’ and ‘l’ are the first and last term of an A.P. with common difference ‘ d’, then nth term from the end of the given A.P. is l− (n− 1)d. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 10th term from the end = 198 − 9× 3 = 171̸= 168, so A is false; R is true.
