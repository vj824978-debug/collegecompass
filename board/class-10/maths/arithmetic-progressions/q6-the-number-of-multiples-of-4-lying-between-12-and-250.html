Question as text
The number of multiples of 4 lying between 12 and 250 is : (A) 59 (B) 59.5 (C) 60 (D) 61
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 59. 16 , 20, . . . ,248: n = 248− 16 4 + 1 = 59.
