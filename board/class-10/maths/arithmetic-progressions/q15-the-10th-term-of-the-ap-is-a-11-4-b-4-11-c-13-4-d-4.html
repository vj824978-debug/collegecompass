Question as text
The 10th term of the AP 5 , 19 4 , 9 2 , 17 4 , . . . is : (A) 11 4 (B) 4 11 (C) 13 4 (D) 4
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 11 4 . d =− 1 4, a10 = 5 + 9 ( − 1 ) = 11 4 .
