In an A.P., 15th term exceeds the 8th term by 21.

CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q273 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Arithmetic Progressions
Question as text
(a) In an A.P., 15th term exceeds the 8th term by 21. If sum of first 10 terms is 55, then form the A.P. OR (b) The sum of first n terms of an A.P. is 2 n2 + 13n. Find its nth term and hence 10 th term.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) a15− a8 = 7d = 21⇒ d = 3. S10 = 5(2a + 27) = 55⇒ a =−8. A.P.: −8,−5,−2, 1, . . . (b) an = Sn− Sn−1 = 2 n2 + 13n− [2(n− 1)2 + 13(n− 1)] = 4n + 11. a10 = 51. (Check: a1 = S1 = 15.)
