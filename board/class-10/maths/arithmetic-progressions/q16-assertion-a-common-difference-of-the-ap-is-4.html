Arithmetic Progressions — Question 16 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/2/1Q191 mark
Question 16 · Class 10 Mathematics · Arithmetic Progressions
Question as text
Assertion (A): Common difference of the AP : 5 , 1, −3, −7, . . . is 4. Reason (R): Common difference of the AP : a1, a2, a3, . . . , an is obtained by d = an− an−1. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D). d = 1− 5 =−4, so (A) is false; (R) is true.
