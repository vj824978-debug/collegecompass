Question as text
Which of the following sequence is not an A.P.? (A) 2, 5 2 , 3, 7 2 , . . . (B)−1·2, −3·2, −5·2, −7·2, . . . (C) √ 2, √ 8, √ 18, . . . (D) 12, 32, 52, 72, . . .
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1, 9, 25, 49, . . .: differences 8 , 16, 24 are not equal. ((C) is √ 2, 2 √ 2, 3 √ 2, an A.P.)
