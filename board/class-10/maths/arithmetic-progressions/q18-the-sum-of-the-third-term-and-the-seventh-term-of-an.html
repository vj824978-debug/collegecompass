The sum of the third term and the seventh term of an AP is 6 and their product is 8.

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q335 marks
Question 18 · Class 10 Mathematics · Arithmetic Progressions
Question as text
(a) The sum of the third term and the seventh term of an AP is 6 and their product is 8. Find the sum of the first sixteen terms of the AP. OR (b) The minimum age of children eligible to participate in a painting competition is 8 years. It is observed that the age of the youngest boy was 8 years and the ages of the participants, when seated in order of age, have a common difference of 4 months. If the sum of the ages of all the participants is 168 years, find the age of the eldest participant in the painting competition.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (a + 2d) + (a + 6d) = 6⇒ a + 4d = 3. (3− 2d)(3 + 2d) = 8⇒ 9− 4d2 = 8⇒ d =±1 2. d = 1 2: a = 1, S16 = 8 ( 2 + 15 ) = 76. d =−1 2: a = 5, S16 = 8 ( 10− 15 ) = 20. (b) a = 8, d = 1 3 year. n [ 16 + n− 1 ] = 168⇒ n2 + 47n− 1008 = 0⇒ (n− 16)(n + 63) = 0⇒ n = 16. Eldest = a16 = 8 + 15× 1 3 = 13 years.
