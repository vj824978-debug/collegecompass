Assertion (A): The mean of first ‘ n’ natural numbers is n− 1 2 .

CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q201 mark
Question 4 · Class 10 Mathematics · Arithmetic Progressions
Question as text
Assertion (A): The mean of first ‘ n’ natural numbers is n− 1 2 . Reason (R): The sum of first ‘ n’ natural numbers is n(n + 1) 2 . (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D). Mean = n(n + 1)/2 n = n + 1 2 , so A is false; R is true.
