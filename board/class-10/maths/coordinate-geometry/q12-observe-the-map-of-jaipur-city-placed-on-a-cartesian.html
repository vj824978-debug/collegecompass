Observe the map of Jaipur city placed on a Cartesian plane.

CBSE 2026QP 30/4/1Q384 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Coordinate Geometry
Question as text
Observe the map of Jaipur city placed on a Cartesian plane. Taking Rambagh Palace as origin, the location of some places are given below: Point A: (−4, 2) Rajasthan High Court Point B: (4,−4) Birla Mandir Point C: (4, 3) Heera Bagh Point D: (−5,−2) Amar Jawan Jyoti Based on the above, answer the following questions: (i) Advocate Rehana stays at Heera Bagh. How much distance she has to cover daily to go to the court and coming back home? [1] (ii) There is a crossing on X-axis which divides AD in a certain ratio. Find the ratio. [1] (iii) (a) Is Birla Mandir equidistant from Heera Bagh and Amar Jawan Jyoti? Justify your answer. [2] OR (b) Using section formula, show that points A, O and B are not collinear.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) CA = √ 82 + 12 = √ 65 units; daily distance = 2 √ 65 units. (ii) Let the point divide AD in k : 1: y =−2k + 2 k + 1 = 0⇒k = 1. Ratio 1 : 1 (point (−4.5, 0)). (iii)(a) BC = √ 02 + 72 = 7, BD = √ 92 + 22 = √ 85. Since 7 ̸= √ 85, No. (b) If O divides AB in k : 1: x = 4k− 4 k + 1 = 0⇒k = 1; then y =−4k + 2 k + 1 =−2 2 =−1̸= 0. No single ratio gives O, so A, O, B are not collinear.
