Question as text
A point P (x, 7) divides a line segment joining the points A(−5, 4) and B(7, 9) in a certain ratio. Find the ratio and hence find the value of x.
Answer
Answer as text
Let ratio k : 1. 9k + 4 k + 1 = 7⇒k = 3 2, ratio 3 : 2. x = 3(7) + 2(−5) 5 = 11 5 .
