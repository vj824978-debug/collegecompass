Question as text
(a) If the mid-point of the line segment joining the pointsA(3, 4) andB(k, 6) isP (x,y ) andx+y−10 = 0, find the value of k. OR (b) Find the coordinates of the points which divide the line segment joining A(−2, 2) and B(2, 8) into four equal parts.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) P = ( 3 +k 2 , 5 ) . 3 +k 2 + 5− 10 = 0⇒ 3 +k = 10⇒ k = 7. (b) Points divide AB in ratios 1 : 3, 1 : 1, 3 : 1: ( −1, 7 ) , (0, 5), ( 1, 13 ) .
