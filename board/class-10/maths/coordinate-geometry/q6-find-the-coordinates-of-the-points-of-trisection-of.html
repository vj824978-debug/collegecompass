Find the coordinates of the points of trisection of the line segment joining the points A(−1, 4) and B(−3,−2).

Question as text
Find the coordinates of the points of trisection of the line segment joining the points A(−1, 4) and B(−3,−2).
Answer
Answer as text
P divides AB in 1 : 2: P = (−3− 2 3 ,−2 + 8 ) = ( −5 3, 2 ) . Q divides AB in 2 : 1: Q = (−6− 1 3 ,−4 + 4 ) = ( −7 3, 0 ) . Points: ( − 5 3, 2 ) and ( − 7 3, 0 ) .
