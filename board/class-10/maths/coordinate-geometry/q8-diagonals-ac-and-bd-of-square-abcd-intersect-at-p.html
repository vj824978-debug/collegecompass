Diagonals AC and BD of square ABCD intersect at P.

CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q232 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Mathematics · Coordinate Geometry
Question as text
(a) Diagonals AC and BD of square ABCD intersect at P. Coordinates of points B and D are (9 ,−2) and (1, 6) respectively. (i) Find the co-ordinates of point P. (ii) Find the length of the side of the square. OR (b) Find the coordinates of a point on the line x +y = 5 which is equidistant from (6, 4) and (5, 2).
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) P is the mid-point of BD: P = ( 9 + 1 2 ,−2 + 6 ) = ( 5, 2). (ii) BD = √ 82 + 82 = 8 √ 2; side = BD√ 2 = 8 units. (b) ( x− 6)2 + (y− 4)2 = (x− 5)2 + (y− 2)2⇒ 2x + 4y = 23; with x +y = 5: x =−3 2, y = 13 2 . Point ( − 3 2, 13 ) .
