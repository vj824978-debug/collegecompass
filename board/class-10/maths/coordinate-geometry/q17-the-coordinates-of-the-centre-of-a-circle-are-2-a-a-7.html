Question as text
The coordinates of the centre of a circle are (2 a, a− 7). Find the value(s) of ‘ a’ if the circle passes through the point (11,−9) and has diameter 10 √ 2 units.
Answer
Answer as text
Radius = 5 √ 2, so (2a− 11)2 + (a− 7 + 9)2 = 50 ⇒ 5a2− 40a + 75 = 0⇒a2− 8a + 15 = 0⇒ (a− 3)(a− 5) = 0. a = 3 or a = 5.
