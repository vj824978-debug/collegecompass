Find a relation between x and y such that P(x,y ) is equidistant from the points A(3 , 5) and B(7, 1).

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q293 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 Mathematics · Coordinate Geometry
Question as text
Find a relation between x and y such that P(x,y ) is equidistant from the points A(3 , 5) and B(7, 1). Hence, write the coordinates of the points on x-axis and y-axis which are equidistant from points A and B.
Answer
Answer as text
PA 2 =PB 2: (x− 3)2 + (y− 5)2 = (x− 7)2 + (y− 1)2⇒− 6x− 10y + 34 =−14x− 2y + 50⇒ 8x− 8y = 16, i.e. x− y = 2. On x-axis (y = 0): ( 2, 0). On y-axis (x = 0): ( 0,−2).
