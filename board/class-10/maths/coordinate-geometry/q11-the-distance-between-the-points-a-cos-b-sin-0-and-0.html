The distance between the points (a cosθ +b sinθ, 0) and (0, asinθ−b cosθ) is (A) √ a2 +b2 (B) a2−b2 (C) √ a2−b2 (D) a2 +b2
Answer
Answer as text
(A) √ a2 +b2. (a cosθ +b sinθ)2 + (a sinθ−b cosθ)2 =a2 +b2.
