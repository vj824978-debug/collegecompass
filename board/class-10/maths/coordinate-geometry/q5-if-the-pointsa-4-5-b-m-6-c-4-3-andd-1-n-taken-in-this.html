Question as text
If the pointsA(4, 5),B(m, 6),C(4, 3) andD(1,n ) taken in this order are the vertices of a parallelogram ABCD, then find the values of m and n.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagonals bisect each other: midpoint of AC = (4 , 4) = midpoint of BD = (m + 1 2 , 6 +n ) . ⇒ m = 7, n = 2.
