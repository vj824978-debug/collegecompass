Question as text
(a) A circle centered at (2 , 1) passes through the points A(5 , 6) and B(−3,K ). Find the value(s) of K. Hence find length of chord AB. OR (b) Prove that the point P dividing the line segment joining the points A( −1, 7) and B(4,−3) in the ratio 3 : 2, lies on the line x− 3y =−1. Also find length of PA and PB.

Answer as text
