Coordinate Geometry — Question 13 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q21 mark
Question 13 · Class 10 Mathematics · Coordinate Geometry
Question as text
The line segment joining the points P(−4,−2) and Q(10, 4) is divided by y-axis in the ratio (A) 2 : 5 (B) 1 : 2 (C) 2 : 1 (D) 5 : 2
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 2 : 5. Ratio = −x1 :x2 = 4 : 10 = 2 : 5.
