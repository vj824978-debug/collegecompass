Coordinate Geometry — Question 27 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q41 mark
Question 27 · Class 10 Mathematics · Coordinate Geometry
Question as text
The distance of a point A from x-axis is 3 units. Which of the following cannot be coordinates of the point A? (A) (1, 3) (B) ( −3,−3) (C) ( −3, 3) (D) (3 , 1)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) (3, 1). Its distance from the x-axis is|y| = 1, not 3.
