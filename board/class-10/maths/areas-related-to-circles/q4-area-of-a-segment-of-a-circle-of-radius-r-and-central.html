Question as text
Area of a segment of a circle of radius ‘ r’ and central angle 60◦ is : (A) πr2 2 − 1 2r2 (B) 2πr 4 − √ 4 r2 (C) πr2 6 − √ 4 r2 (D) 2πr 4 −r2 sin 60◦
Answer
Answer as text
(C) πr2 6 − √ 4 r2 (sector minus equilateral triangle).
