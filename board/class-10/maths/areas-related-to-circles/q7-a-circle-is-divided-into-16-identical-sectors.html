Question as text
A circle is divided into 16 identical sectors. If radius of the circle is 7 cm, area of each sector is (A) 77 4 cm2 (B) 77 cm 2 (C) 154 cm 2 (D) 77 8 cm2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 77 8 cm2. 1 16× 22 7 × 49 = 154 16 = 77 8 .
