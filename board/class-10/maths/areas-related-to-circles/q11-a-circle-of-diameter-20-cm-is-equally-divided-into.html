Question as text
A circle of diameter 20 cm is equally divided into five sectors. Find the area and perimeter of one of the sectors.
Answer
Answer as text
r = 10 cm, θ = 72◦. Area = 1 5πr2 = 1 5× 22 7 × 100 = 440 7 ≈ 62.86 cm 2. Arc = 1 5× 2× 22 7 × 10 = 88 7 cm; perimeter = 20 + 88 7 = 228 7 ≈ 32.57 cm.
