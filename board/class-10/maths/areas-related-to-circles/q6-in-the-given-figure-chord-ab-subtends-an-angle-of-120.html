Question as text
In the given figure, chord AB subtends an angle of 120 ◦ at the centre of the circle with radius 7 cm. Find (i) perimeter of major sector OACB, and (ii) area of the shaded segment, if area of△OAB = 21.2 cm2.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Major arc = 240 360· 2· 22 7 · 7 = 88 3 cm; perimeter = 88 3 + 14 = 130 3 ≈ 43.33 cm. (ii) Minor sector = 120 360· 22 7 · 49 = 154 3 ≈ 51.33 cm2; segment = 51.33− 21.2 = 30.13 cm 2 (exactly 452 15 ).
