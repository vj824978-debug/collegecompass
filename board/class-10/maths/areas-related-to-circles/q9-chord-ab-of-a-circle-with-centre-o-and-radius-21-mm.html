Question as text
Chord AB of a circle with centre O and radius 21 mm subtends an angle of 120 ◦ at the centre. Find the perimeters of the shaded region. (Use √ 3 = 1.73)
Answer
Answer as text
Arc AB = 120 360× 2× 22 7 × 21 = 44 mm. Chord AB = 2 × 21 sin 60◦ = 21 √ 3 = 21× 1.73 = 36.33 mm. Perimeter of shaded segment = 44 + 36.33 = 80.33 mm.
