A farmer has a circular piece of land.

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q364 marks
Question 21 · Class 10 Mathematics · Areas Related to Circles
Question as text
A farmer has a circular piece of land. He wishes to construct his house in the form of largest possible square within the land as shown below. The radius of circular piece of land is 35 m. Based on given information, answer the following questions: (i) Find the length of wire needed to fence the entire land. [1] (ii) Find the length of each side of the square land on which house will be constructed. [1] (iii)(a) The farmer wishes to grow grass on the shaded region around the house. Find the cost of growing the grass at the rate of Rs. 50 per square metre. [2] OR (iii)(b) Find the ratio of area of land on which house is built to remaining area of circular piece of land. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Circumference = 2× 22 7 × 35 = 220 m. (ii) Diagonal of square = diameter = 70 m, so side = 70√ 2 = 35 √ 2 m (≈ 49.5 m). (iii)(a) Area of land = 22 7× 352 = 3850 m2; area of square = (35 √ 2)2 = 2450 m2. Region around the house = 1400 m 2; cost = 1400× 50 = Rs. 70,000. (If only the two shaded segments drawn in the figure are taken, area = 700 m 2 and cost = Rs. 35,000.) (iii)(b) 2450 : 1400 = 7 : 4.
