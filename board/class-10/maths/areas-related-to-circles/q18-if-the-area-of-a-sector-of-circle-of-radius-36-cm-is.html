If the area of a sector of circle of radius 36 cm is 54 π cm2, then the length of the corresponding arc of the sector is : (A) 8π cm (B) 6 π cm (C) 4 π cm (D) 3 π cm
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 3π cm. Area = 1 2lr: 54π = 18l⇒l = 3π cm.
