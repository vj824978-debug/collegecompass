If a sector of a circle has an area of 40π sq. units and a central angle of 72◦, the radius of the circle is: (A) 200 units (B) 100 units (C) 20 units (D) 10 √ 2 units
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 10 √ 2 units. 72 360πr2 = 40π⇒r2 = 200.
