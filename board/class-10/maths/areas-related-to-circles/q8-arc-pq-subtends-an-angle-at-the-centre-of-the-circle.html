Arc PQ subtends an angle θ at the centre of the circle with radius 6.3 cm. If ⌢ PQ = 11 cm, then the value of θ is (A) 10◦ (B) 60◦ (C) 45◦ (D) 100◦
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 100◦. θ 360◦× 2× 22 7 × 6.3 = 11⇒θ = 11× 360◦ 39.6 = 100◦.
