The hour hand of a clock is 7 cm long.

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q151 mark
Question 2 · Class 10 Mathematics · Areas Related to Circles
Question as text
The hour hand of a clock is 7 cm long. The angle swept by it between 7:00 a.m. and 8:10 a.m. is : (A) (35 )◦ (B) (35 )◦ (C) 35◦ (D) 70◦
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 35◦. The hour hand turns 1 ◦ per minute; 70 min → 35◦.
