Prove that the parallelogram circumscribing a circle is a rhombus.

CBSE 2025QP 30/2/1Q263 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
(a) Prove that the parallelogram circumscribing a circle is a rhombus. OR (b) Prove that the angle between the two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle is supplementary to the angle subtended by the line-segment joining the points of contact at the centre.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Let ABCD touch the circle at P, Q, R, S on AB, BC, CD, DA. Tangents from an external point are equal: AP =AS, BP =BQ, CR =CQ, DR =DS. Adding, AB +CD =AD +BC. In a parallelogram AB = CD, AD = BC, so 2AB = 2BC, i.e. AB = BC; all sides equal, so ABCD is a rhombus. (b) Let PA, PB be tangents from P touching at A, B; O the centre. ∠OAP = ∠OBP = 90◦ (radius⊥ tangent). In quadrilateral OAPB, ∠APB + ∠AOB = 360◦− 90◦− 90◦ = 180◦. Hence they are supplementary.
