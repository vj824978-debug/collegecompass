CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q252 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
In the given figure, O is the centre of the circle. PQ and PR are tangents. Show that the quadrilateral PQOR is cyclic.
Answer
Answer as text
OR⊥PR and OQ⊥PQ (radius⊥ tangent), so ∠ORP = ∠OQP = 90◦. ∠ORP + ∠OQP = 180◦; opposite angles are supplementary, hence PQOR is cyclic.
