Question as text
In the adjoining figure, TS is a tangent to a circle with centre O. The value of 2 x◦ is (A) 22.5 (B) 45 (C) 67.5 (D) 90
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 45. ∠OST = 90◦⇒ 3x +x = 90⇒x = 22.5, so 2x = 45.
