If TP and TQ are two tangents to a circle with centre O from an external point T so that ∠POQ = 120◦, then ∠PTQ is equal to : (A) 60◦ (B) 70◦ (C) 80◦ (D) 90◦
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 60◦. ∠PTQ = 180◦− 120◦ = 60◦.
