Question 23 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
At point A on the diameter AB of a circle of radius 10 cm, tangent XAY is drawn to the circle. Find the length of the chord CD parallel to XY at a distance of 16 cm from A.
Answer
Answer as text
OA⊥XY and CD∥XY , so OP⊥CD and P bisects CD. AP = 16, OP = 16− 10 = 6 cm. CP = √ OC 2−OP 2 =√100− 36 = 8 cm, so CD = 2× 8 = 16 cm.
