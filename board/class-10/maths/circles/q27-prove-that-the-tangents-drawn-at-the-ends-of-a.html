Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are parallel.

CBSE 2025QP 30/3/1Q252 marks
Question 27 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are parallel.
Answer
Answer as text
Let AB be a diameter of a circle with centre O, and let l, m be the tangents at A and B. The radius is perpendicular to the tangent at the point of contact, so OA⊥ l and OB ⊥ m, i.e. ∠(l,AB ) = ∠(m,AB ) = 90◦. These are alternate (or co-interior summing to 180 ◦) angles made by the transversal AB, hence l∥m.
