In the given figure, O is the centre of the circle and BCD is tangent to it at C.

CBSE 2025QP 30/1/1Q263 marks
Question 21 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
(a) In the given figure, O is the centre of the circle and BCD is tangent to it at C. Prove that ∠BAC + ∠ACD = 90◦. OR (b) Prove that opposite sides of a quadrilateral circumscribing a circle subtend supplementary angles at the centre of the circle.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) OC⊥ BD (radius⊥ tangent), so ∠OCA + ∠ACD = 90◦. OA = OC (radii)⇒ ∠OCA = ∠OAC = ∠BAC. Hence ∠BAC + ∠ACD = 90◦. (b) Let ABCD circumscribe a circle with centre O, touching AB,BC,CD,DA at P,Q,R,S . △OAP ∼=△OAS (RHS), so ∠1 = ∠2 (angles at O); similarly the eight angles at O pair up: ∠1 = ∠2, ∠3 = ∠4, ∠5 = ∠6, ∠7 = ∠8 (taken in order around O). Their sum is 360 ◦, so 2(∠2 + ∠3 + ∠6 + ∠7) = 360◦, i.e. ∠AOB + ∠COD = 180◦; similarly ∠BOC + ∠AOD = 180◦.
