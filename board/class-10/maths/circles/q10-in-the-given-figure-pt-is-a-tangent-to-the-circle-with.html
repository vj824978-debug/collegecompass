Question 10 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
In the given figure, PT is a tangent to the circle with centre O and radius r. If ∠POT = 45◦, then the length of OP is : (A) r √ 2 (B) √ 2r (C) 2r (D) r2
Answer
Answer as text
(A) r √ 2. ∠OTP = 90◦, OP = r cos 45◦ =r √ 2.
