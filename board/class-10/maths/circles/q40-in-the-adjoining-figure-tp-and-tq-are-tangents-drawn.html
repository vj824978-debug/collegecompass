Question as text
In the adjoining figure, TP and TQ are tangents drawn to a circle with centre O. If ∠OPQ = 15◦ and ∠PTQ =θ, then find the value of sin 2θ.
Answer
Answer as text
∠OPT = 90◦⇒ ∠TPQ = 90◦−15◦ = 75◦. TP =TQ⇒ ∠TQP = 75◦, soθ = 180◦−150◦ = 30◦. sin 2θ = sin 60◦ = √ 2 .
