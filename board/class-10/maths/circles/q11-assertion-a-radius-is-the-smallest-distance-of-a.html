Assertion (A): Radius is the smallest distance of a tangent from the centre of the circle.

CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q191 mark
Question 11 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
Assertion (A): Radius is the smallest distance of a tangent from the centre of the circle. Reason (R): Radius is perpendicular to the tangent. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A). The radius to the point of contact is perpendicular to the tangent, and the perpendicular is the shortest distance from a point to a line; so R explains A.
