Two tangents PA and PB are drawn to a circle with centre O from an external point P.

CBSE 2026QP 30/3/1Q313 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
(a) Two tangents PA and PB are drawn to a circle with centre O from an external point P. Prove that ∠APB = 2∠OAB. OR (b) In the given figure, PA is the tangent to the circle with centre O such that OA = 10 cm, AB = 8 cm and AB⊥OP . Find the length of PB.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Let ∠APB = θ. PA = PB ⇒ ∠PAB = ∠PBA = 90◦− θ 2. OA⊥ PA ⇒ ∠OAB = 90◦− ∠PAB = θ 2. So ∠APB = 2 ∠OAB. (b) OB = √ 102− 82 = 6 cm. In right △OAP (∠OAP = 90◦) with altitude AB:△ABO∼△PBA , so AB2 =OB·PB⇒ 64 = 6PB⇒PB = 3 cm.
