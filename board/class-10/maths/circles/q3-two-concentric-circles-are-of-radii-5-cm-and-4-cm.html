Two concentric circles are of radii 5 cm and 4 cm.

CBSE 2026QP 30/1/1Q252 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
Two concentric circles are of radii 5 cm and 4 cm. Find the length of the chord of the larger circle which touches the smaller circle.
Answer
Answer as text
The radius to the point of contact is perpendicular to the chord and bisects it. Half-chord = √ 52− 42 = 3 cm, so chord = 6 cm.
