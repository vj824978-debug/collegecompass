Circles — Question 37 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/6/1Q91 mark
Question 37 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
In the adjoining figure, PA and PB are tangents to a circle with centre O such that ∠P = 90◦. If AB = 3 √ 2 cm, then the diameter of the circle is (A) 3 √ 2 cm (B) 6 √ 2 cm (C) 3 cm (D) 6 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 6 cm. OAPB is a square with diagonal AB = 3 √ 2, so side = r = 3 cm; diameter = 6 cm.
