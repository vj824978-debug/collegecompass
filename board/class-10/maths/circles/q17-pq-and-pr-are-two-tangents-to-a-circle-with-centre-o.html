PQ and PR are two tangents to a circle with centre O and radius 5 cm. AB is another tangent to the circle at C which lies on OP. If OP = 13 cm, then find the length AB and PA.
Answer
Answer as text
OQ⊥ PQ: PQ = √ 132− 52 = 12 cm. PC =OP−OC = 13− 5 = 8 cm, and AB ⊥ OP at C. LetPA =x; thenAC =AQ = 12−x (tangents from A). In right△ACP : x2 = 82 + (12−x)2⇒ 24x = 208⇒x = 26 3 . P A= 26 3 cm, AC = 10 3 cm; by symmetry BC =AC, so AB = 20 3 cm.
