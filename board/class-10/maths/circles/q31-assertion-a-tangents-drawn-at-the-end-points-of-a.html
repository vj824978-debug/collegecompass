Assertion (A): Tangents drawn at the end points of a diameter of a circle are always parallel to each other.

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q201 mark
Question 31 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
Assertion (A): Tangents drawn at the end points of a diameter of a circle are always parallel to each other. Reason (R): The lengths of tangents drawn to a circle from a point outside the circle are always equal. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true (each tangent is ⊥ to the diameter, hence parallel), but R does not explain A.
