Question as text
In the given figure, TP and TQ are tangents to a circle with centre M, touching another circle with centre N at A and B respectively. It is given that MQ = 13 cm, NB = 8 cm, BQ = 35 cm and TP = 80 cm. (i) Name the quadrilateral MQBN. [1] (ii) Is MN parallel to PA? Justify your answer. [1] (iii) Find length TB. [1] (iv) Find length MN. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) MQ⊥QB and NB⊥QB (radius⊥ tangent), so MQ∥NB with MQ̸=NB : MQBN is a trapezium (right trapezium). (ii) No. MP ⊥ PA and NA ⊥ PA , so MP ∥ NA. If also MN ∥ PA , PMNA would be a parallelogram (rectangle) giving MP =NA, but 13̸= 8. (iii) TQ =TP = 80 cm (tangents from T). TB =TQ−BQ = 80− 35 = 45 cm. (iv) Draw NL ⊥ MQ: NL = BQ = 35, ML = 13− 8 = 5. MN = √ 352 + 52 = √ 1250 = √ 2≈ 35.36 cm. (Note: the given data are not fully consistent with M, N, T being collinear; the answers follow the given values.)
