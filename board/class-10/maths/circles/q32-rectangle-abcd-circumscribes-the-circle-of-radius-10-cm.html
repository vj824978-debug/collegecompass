Rectangle ABCD circumscribes the circle of radius 10 cm.

CBSE 2025QP 30/4/1Q313 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
Rectangle ABCD circumscribes the circle of radius 10 cm. Prove that ABCD is a square. Hence, find the perimeter of ABCD.
Answer
Answer as text
Tangents from an external point are equal, so AB +CD =AD +BC. In a rectangle AB =CD, AD =BC, so 2AB = 2AD⇒AB =AD; a rectangle with equal adjacent sides is a square. Side = diameter = 20 cm; perimeter = 80 cm.
