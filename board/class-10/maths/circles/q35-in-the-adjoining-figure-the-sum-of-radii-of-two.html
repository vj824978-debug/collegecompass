In the adjoining figure, the sum of radii of two concentric circles is 16 cm.

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q141 mark
Question 35 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
In the adjoining figure, the sum of radii of two concentric circles is 16 cm. The length of chord AB which touches the inner circle at P is 16 cm. The difference of the radii of the given circles is (A) 8 cm (B) 4 cm (C) 2 cm (D) 3 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 4 cm. AP = 8, so R2−r2 = 64; (R +r)(R−r) = 64⇒R−r = 4.
