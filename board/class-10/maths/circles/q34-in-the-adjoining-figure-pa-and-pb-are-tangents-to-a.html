Question 34 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
In the adjoining figure, PA and PB are tangents to a circle with centre O. The measure of angle APB is (A) 210◦ (B) 150◦ (C) 105◦ (D) 30◦
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 30◦. ∠AOB = 360◦− 210◦ = 150◦, so ∠APB = 180◦− 150◦ = 30◦.
