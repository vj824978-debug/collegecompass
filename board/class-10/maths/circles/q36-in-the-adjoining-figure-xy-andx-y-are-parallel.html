In the adjoining figure, XY andX′Y′ are parallel tangents to a circle with centre O.

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q313 marks
Question 36 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
In the adjoining figure, XY andX′Y′ are parallel tangents to a circle with centre O. Another tangent AB touches the circle at C intersecting XY at A and X′Y′ at B. Prove that AB subtends right angle at the centre of the circle; or ∠AOB = 90◦.
Answer
Answer as text
Join OC. In△OPA and△OCA: OP =OC (radii),AP =AC (tangents from A), OA common, so they are congruent (SSS) and ∠OAP = ∠OAC, i.e. ∠OAC = 1 2 ∠PAB . Similarly ∠OBC = 2 ∠QBA. As XY ∥X′Y′, ∠PAB + ∠QBA = 180◦ (co-interior angles). So ∠OAB + ∠OBA = 90◦, and in △AOB, ∠AOB = 180◦− 90◦ = 90◦.
