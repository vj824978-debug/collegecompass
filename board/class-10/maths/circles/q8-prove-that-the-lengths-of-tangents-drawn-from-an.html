Prove that the lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q293 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
(a) Prove that the lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal. OR (b) Two tangents TP and TQ are drawn to a circle with centre O from an external point T. Prove that ∠PTQ = 2∠OPQ .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Let PQ, PR be tangents from P to circle with centre O. Join OQ, OR, OP. In right △OQP and△ORP (∠Q = ∠R = 90◦): OQ = OR (radii), OP common, so △OQP ∼=△ORP (RHS). Hence PQ =PR . (b) Let ∠PTQ = θ. TP = TQ⇒ ∠TPQ = ∠TQP = 90◦− θ 2. Since ∠OPT = 90◦, ∠OPQ = 90◦− ( 90◦− θ ) = θ 2. So ∠PTQ = 2∠OPQ .
