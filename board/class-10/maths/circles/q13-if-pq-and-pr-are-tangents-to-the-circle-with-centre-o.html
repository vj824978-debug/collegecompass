Circles — Question 13 (CBSE 2026, 1 mark)

CBSE 2026QP 30/4/1Q41 mark
Question 13 · Class 10 Mathematics · Circles
Question as text
If PQ and PR are tangents to the circle with centre O and radius 4 cm such that ∠QPR = 90◦, then the length OP is (A) 4 cm (B) 4 √ 2 cm (C) 8 cm (D) 2 √ 2 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 4 √ 2 cm. OQPR is a square of side 4 cm, so OP = 4 √ 2 cm.
