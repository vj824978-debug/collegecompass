Medical check-up was carried out for 35 students of a class and their weights were recorded as follows: Weight (in kg) 38–40 40–42 42–44 44–46 46–48 48–50 50–52 Number of Students 3 2 4 5 14 4 3 Find the difference between the mean weight and the median weight.
Answer
Answer as text
Class marks 39, 41, . . . , 51;∑fixi = 117 + 82 + 172 + 225 + 658 + 196 + 153 = 1603,N = 35. Mean = 1603 35 = 45.8 kg. Cumulative frequencies: 3, 5, 9, 14, 28, 32, 35. N 2 = 17.5, so the median class is 46–48 ( l = 46, cf = 14, f = 14, h = 2). Median = 46 + 17.5− 14 14 × 2 = 46.5 kg. Difference = 46.5− 45.8 = 0.7 kg.
