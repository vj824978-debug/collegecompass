CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q161 mark
Question 19 · Class 10 Mathematics · Statistics
Question as text
If x median + y mean = z mode; is the empirical relationship between mean, median and mode, then the value of x + y + z is (A) 6 (B) 3 (C) 2 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 2. 3 median− 2 mean = mode, so x = 3, y =−2, z = 1.
