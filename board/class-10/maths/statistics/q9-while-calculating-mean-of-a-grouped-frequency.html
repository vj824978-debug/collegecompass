CBSE 2026QP 30/5/1Q61 mark
Question 9 · Class 10 Mathematics · Statistics
Question as text
While calculating mean of a grouped frequency distribution, step deviation method was used(x− a h = u ) . It was found that ¯x = 64, h = 5 and a = 62.5. The value of ¯u is (A) 0.5 (B) 1.5 (C) 0.3 (D) 7.5
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 0.3. ¯ u = 64− 62.5 5 = 0.3.
