Statistics — Question 20 (CBSE 2025, 1 mark)

CBSE 2025QP 30/5/1Q171 mark
Question 20 · Class 10 Mathematics · Statistics
Question as text
Following data shows the marks obtained by 100 students in a class test: Marks obtained 20 29 28 33 42 38 43 25 Number of students 6 28 24 15 2 4 1 20 The median will be the average of which two observations? (A) 29 and 33 (B) 25 and 28 (C) 28 and 29 (D) 33 and 38
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 28 and 29. In ascending order the cumulative frequencies are 20:6, 25:26, 28:50, 29:78, . . . ; the 50th value is 28 and the 51st is 29.
