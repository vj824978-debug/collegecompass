An SBI health insurance agent found the following data for distribution of ages of 100 policy holders.

CBSE 2026QP 30/2/1Q355 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Mathematics · Statistics
Question as text
An SBI health insurance agent found the following data for distribution of ages of 100 policy holders. The health insurance policies are given to persons of age 15 years and onwards, but less than 60 years. Age (in yrs) Number of policy holders 15–20 2 20–25 4 25–30 18 30–35 21 35–40 33 40–45 11 45–50 3 50–55 6 55–60 2 Find the modal age and median age of the policy holders.
Answer
Answer as text
Mode: modal class 35–40; l = 35, f1 = 33, f0 = 21, f2 = 11, h = 5. Mode = 35 + 33− 21 66− 21− 11× 5 = 35 + 60 34≈ 36·76 years. Median: cf: 2, 6, 24, 45, 78, 89, 92, 98, 100; n 2 = 50, median class 35–40, cf = 45, f = 33. Median = 35 + 50− 45 33 × 5 = 35 + 25 33≈ 35·76 years.
