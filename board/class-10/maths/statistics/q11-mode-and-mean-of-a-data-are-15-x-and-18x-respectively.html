Mode and Mean of a data are 15 x and 18x, respectively. Then the median of the data is: (A) x (B) 11x (C) 17x (D) 34x
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 17x. 3 Median = Mode + 2 Mean = 15 x + 36x = 51x.
