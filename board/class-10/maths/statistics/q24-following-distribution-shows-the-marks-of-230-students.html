Following distribution shows the marks of 230 students in a particular subject. If the median marks are 46, then find the values of x and y. Marks Number of Students 10–20 12 20–30 30 30–40 x 40–50 65 50–60 y 60–70 25 70–80 18
Answer
Answer as text
150 + x + y = 230⇒ x + y = 80. Median 46 lies in 40–50: l = 40, cf = 42 + x, f = 65, h = 10, N 2 = 115. 46 = 40 + 115− (42 + x) 65 × 10⇒ 73− x = 39⇒ x = 34, y = 46.
