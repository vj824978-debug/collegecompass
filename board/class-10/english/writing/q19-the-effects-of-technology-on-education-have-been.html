CBSE 2025QP 2/3/1Q55 marks
Question 19 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) The effects of technology on education have been profound and multifaceted; influencing both teaching and learning in various ways. The data below shows the rising enrolment in online courses over the years. Analyze the data and write an analytical paragraph, in about 120 words, about how technology has transformed teaching and learning by shifting from classrooms to digital devices. Discuss benefits such as access to information and challenges like digital divide. S. No. Year Total number of enrolments (in millions) 1. 2017 26 2. 2018 43 3. 2019 59 4. 2020 76 5. 2021 143 6. 2022 189 OR (b) Read the given details about the pros and cons of artificial intelligence (AI). With the help of the given cues and your own ideas write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words on “AI – Benefits and Drawbacks”.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Topic sentence: The table depicts a steady and remarkable rise in enrolments in online courses from 26 million in 2017 to 189 million in 2022. Key trends: (1) enrolments rose steadily from 26 million (2017) to 43 million (2018), 59 million (2019) and 76 million (2020) — an increase of about 15–17 million every year; (2) the sharpest jump came in 2021, when enrolments nearly doubled from 76 to 143 million (the pandemic shift from classrooms to digital devices); (3) growth continued to 189 million in 2022 — about seven times the 2017 figure; (4) benefits: access to information and courses anytime, anywhere, self-paced and affordable learning, interactive tools for teachers; (5) challenges: the digital divide — lack of devices and internet in rural/poor households, screen fatigue, less personal interaction. Conclusion: Technology has transformed education into a flexible digital experience, but bridging the digital divide is essential so that its benefits reach every learner. (b) Topic sentence: Artificial Intelligence, the intelligence possessed by machines, has impacted our lives in many ways, bringing both benefits and drawbacks. Points: (1) AI is the ability of machines to think, learn and take decisions; (2) benefits: everyday tools like Google Maps guide routes and predict traffic; voice assistants, recommendations and chatbots save time; (3) knowledge is at our fingertips — instant information for students and professionals; (4) uses in health care, banking and industry improve speed and accuracy; (5) drawbacks: AI cannot wholly replicate humans — it lacks emotions, empathy and judgement; (6) misuse has led to an increase in cyber crime (fraud, data theft, deepfakes), along with privacy concerns and fear of job loss. Conclusion: AI is a powerful tool, but it must be used responsibly, with strong safeguards, to complement rather than replace human abilities.
