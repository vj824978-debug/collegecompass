Question as text
Note: All details presented in the questions are imaginary and created for assessment purpose. (A) You are Divya/Divij Kumar, Director, Nurturing India, an organization providing skill education to youth. You believe that today’s youth needs vocational and skill education that will encourage an independent and entrepreneurial lifestyle. Write a letter to the editor of a local daily expressing your views on the need and importance of skill education in creating employability and a self-employed independent generation. [5] OR (B) You are Nirmala / Nirmal, a resident of Jhunjhunu Colony. Recently there have been a series of accidents and petty crimes in your colony. One of the reasons is poor street lighting. Write a letter to the Commissioner of the Works department, drawing attention to the urgent need to install street lights to avoid further crimes and accidents. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Letter to the Editor – value points. Format: sender’s address (Divya/Divij Kumar, Director, Nurturing India, city); date; The Editor, name and address of the local daily; Subject: Need for skill education to create an employable, self-reliant generation; Sir/Madam; body; Yours faithfully, name and designation. Content: (1) introduce yourself/organisation and purpose of writing; (2) degrees alone do not guarantee jobs – a gap between academic learning and industry needs; (3) vocational/skill training (IT, trades, crafts, healthcare, agriculture, etc.) makes youth employable; (4) skills encourage entrepreneurship and self-employment, so youth become job creators rather than job seekers; (5) benefits – reduced unemployment, economic growth, confidence and independence; (6) suggestions – skill courses in schools/colleges, internships and apprenticeships, government–industry partnerships, awareness among parents; appeal to readers/authorities. OR (B) Letter to the Commissioner, Works Department – value points. Format: sender’s address (Nirmala/Nirmal, house no., Jhunjhunu Colony, city); date; The Commissioner, Works Department, address; Subject: Urgent need to install street lights in Jhunjhunu Colony; Sir/Madam; body; Yours faithfully/sincerely, name. Content: (1) introduce yourself as a resident and state the problem; (2) street lights missing or nonfunctional for a long time, roads dark after sunset; (3) recent series of accidents (vehicles, pedestrians, elderly and children) and petty crimes such as chain-snatching and thefts; (4) residents, especially women and senior citizens, feel unsafe going out after dark; (5) request: install new lights / repair existing ones urgently, regular maintenance, possibly solar/LED lights; (6) hope for prompt action; thank the authority.
