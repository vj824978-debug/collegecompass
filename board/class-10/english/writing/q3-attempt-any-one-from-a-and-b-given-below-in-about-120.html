CBSE 2026QP 2/3/1Q45 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
Attempt any one from (a) and (b) given below in about 120 words : [5] (a) You are Vaishali/Vishal, a student of Class X of PQS Public School, Lancer Road, Coldtown. You travel by the school bus every day. The seating capacity of the bus is 35 students but there is over-crowding and many students remain standing through most of the journey. Write a letter to the Principal, drawing attention to this inconvenience, the dangers, and requesting for a resolution to the situation. OR (b) You are Vaidehi/Vishal, a gym trainer. You want to volunteer an hour every weekend to provide free yoga classes to the residents of your colony in the community park. Write a letter to the President of the RWA, Purv Colony, Vrikshbagh, telling him about your plan and its advantages and requesting him to grant you permission to do so.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Format: Sender: Vaishali/Vishal, Class X, PQS Public School, Lancer Road, Coldtown; date; receiver: The Principal, PQS Public School, Lancer Road, Coldtown; Subject: Overcrowding in the school bus; salutation: Sir/Madam; closing: Yours obediently, name, class and section. Value points:  Introduce self; travel daily by school bus (route/number).  Bus meant for 35 students but carries many more; many students stand through most of the journey.  Inconvenience: discomfort, fatigue, heavy bags, pushing and quarrels, arriving tired for class.  Dangers: students may fall or be injured on sudden braking or turns; risk in an accident; violates safety norms.  Request: an additional bus or extra trip, fixed seat allotment, checking of numbers by the transport in-charge.  Hope for early action; thank the Principal. (b) Format: Sender: Vaidehi/Vishal, (house no.), Purv Colony, Vrikshbagh; date; receiver: The President, RWA, Purv Colony, Vrikshbagh; Subject: Permission to conduct free weekend yoga classes in the community park; salutation: Sir; closing: Yours sincerely/faithfully, name. Value points:  Introduce self as a gym trainer living in the colony (experience/qualification).  Plan: volunteer one hour every weekend (day, time) to give free yoga classes to residents of all ages in the community park.  Advantages: better fitness, flexibility and immunity; relief from stress and lifestyle diseases; healthy habits for children and the elderly; brings residents together and builds community spirit.  No cost to the RWA; residents to bring their own mats; will keep the park clean and not disturb others.  Request permission to use the park and help in informing residents (notice board/WhatsApp group).  Thank the President and hope for a positive reply.
