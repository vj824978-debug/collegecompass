CBSE 2025QP 2/4/1Q55 marks
Question 20 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(A) Today, many projects in India are being initiated and implemented with the aim of transforming India into a digitally empowered society and economy. Digital India Program IT – Jobs E – governance E – Kranti Electronic Delivery of Services Broadband for all – Urban and Rural Information for all. Write an analytical paragraph of 100-120 words based on the given information. OR (B) Read the given details of the different activities that interest children in the age group of 14-17 years.  dance, music, art  outdoor sports like cricket, hockey, basketball, soccer, tennis  computer games  net surfing  social media Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words about the suitability of the given activities. Focus on factors like interest, physical, mental and emotional development, personality development, and suitability.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Digital India Program. Topic sentence: The given information outlines the Digital India Program, which aims to transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy through five key pillars. Points to cover: IT for jobs – skilling youth and creating employment in the IT/IT-enabled sector, including smaller towns; e-governance – online government services, transparency, less paperwork and corruption; e-Kranti – electronic delivery of services such as education, health, banking and farmer services; broadband for all – connectivity in both urban and rural areas, bridging the digital divide; information for all – open access to information online and citizen participation. Note how the pillars are linked: connectivity enables services, which in turn create jobs and informed citizens. Conclusion: Taken together, these initiatives can make governance efficient and inclusive, though their success depends on digital literacy and reaching remote rural areas. (B) Activities of 14–17-year-olds. Topic sentence: The given details show a range of activities that interest teenagers aged 14–17, from creative and physical pursuits to screen-based ones, and they differ greatly in suitability. Points to cover: dance, music and art – develop creativity, emotional expression, confidence and personality: highly suitable; outdoor sports (cricket, hockey, basketball, soccer, tennis) – physical fitness, teamwork, discipline, leadership and stress relief: highly suitable; computer games – entertaining and may sharpen reflexes and problem-solving, but excess causes a sedentary lifestyle, addiction and isolation: suitable only in moderation; net surfing – useful for information and learning but risk of distraction and unsafe content: needs supervision; social media – helps connect and express, but can harm mental and emotional health (comparison, cyber-bullying, time-wasting): least suitable without limits. Conclusion: A balance favouring creative arts and outdoor sports, with screen-based activities used in moderation and under guidance, best supports the all-round development of adolescents.
