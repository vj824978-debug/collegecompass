Read the given details of a fun zone designed for kids in a mall :  A multi-level jungle themed play area…

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q55 marks
Question 15 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) Read the given details of a fun zone designed for kids in a mall :  A multi-level jungle themed play area with climbing walls, tunnels, and slides.  A ball pit and trampoline section for younger kids.  A gaming zone with arcade machines and interactive screens.  Safety padding on all surfaces and trained staff for monitoring of activities.  A dedicated toddler zone with soft toys, mini slides, and sensory play stations.  A small cafeteria for parents with a view of the play area. Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words on the suitability of this fun zone for kids in a mall. Focus on aspects such as safety, variety of activities, age – appropriateness, convenience for parents and overall experience. OR (b) Read the given details of a pre-school designed for young children :  Brightly coloured classrooms with themed decorations.  An outdoor grassy play area with slides, swings, and sand pits.  A sensory activity zone with toys for touch, sound, and visual stimulation.  Dedicated nap rooms with cozy beds and calming lights.  A library with picture books, story-telling corners, and interactive learning tools.  CCTV monitoring for safety and a nurse available for emergencies. Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words about the suitability of this pre-school for young children. Focus on aspects such as safety, learning opportunities, comfort, inclusivity and overall environment.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Fun zone. Topic sentence: The kids’ fun zone in the mall appears well suited to children of all ages, combining safety, variety and convenience. Points: safety – padding on all surfaces and trained staff monitoring activities; variety – multi-level jungle-themed play area with climbing walls, tunnels and slides, ball pit and trampolines, gaming zone with arcade machines and interactive screens; age-appropriateness – separate section for younger kids and a dedicated toddler zone with soft toys, mini slides and sensory stations, while older children enjoy climbing and gaming; convenience for parents – cafeteria with a view of the play area lets them relax while supervising. Conclusion: Overall, the fun zone offers a safe, engaging and family-friendly experience (a possible caution: screen-based gaming time may need limits). (b) Pre-school. Topic sentence: The pre-school seems highly suitable for young children, offering a safe, stimulating and nurturing environment. Points: environment – brightly coloured, themed classrooms that attract and engage children; physical development – outdoor grassy play area with slides, swings and sand pits; learning opportunities – sensory activity zone (touch, sound, visual stimulation), library with picture books, story-telling corners and interactive tools that build language and curiosity; comfort – nap rooms with cozy beds and calming lights; safety – CCTV monitoring and a nurse for emergencies; inclusivity – varied sensory and interactive activities cater to children with different needs and learning styles. Conclusion: Overall, the pre-school balances play, learning, comfort and safety, giving parents confidence and children a happy start to education.
