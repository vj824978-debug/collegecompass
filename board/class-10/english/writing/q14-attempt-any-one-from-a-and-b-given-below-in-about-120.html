Question as text
Attempt any one from (a) and (b) given below in about 120 words : [5] (a) Read the given details about sleep patterns and their effects.  restores energy  enhances immunity  promotes emotional well-being  benefits concentration and productivity  oversleep leads to fatigue, weight gain and irritability Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph on the importance of sleep in maintaining physical health, mental well-being, and academic performance. OR (b) Read the following details : Kharif and Rabi crops play a pivotal role in agriculture in India. Analyze the data given below and write an analytical paragraph by drawing a comparison between the two. Aspect Kharif crop Rabi crop Sowing season June to July October to November Harvesting season September to October March to April Major crops Rice, Maize, Cotton, Groundnut, Soybean Wheat, Barley, Soybean, Mustard, Peas, Gram Rainfall required High Low to moderate Regions dominated East and South India Northwest and Central India Irrigation dependency Less, as it is monsoon-based High, as it requires irrigation
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Topic sentence: Adequate sleep is a cornerstone of good health, influencing the body, the mind and academic performance alike.  Physical health: sleep restores energy and enhances immunity, helping the body repair itself and fight illness.  Mental well-being: it promotes emotional well-being, keeping mood stable and stress in check.  Academic performance: it benefits concentration and productivity, aiding learning and memory.  Caution: oversleeping is harmful too — it leads to fatigue, weight gain and irritability. Conclusion: Thus balanced, regular sleep — neither too little nor too much — is essential for a healthy body, a calm mind and success in studies. (b) Topic sentence: India’s two main cropping seasons, Kharif and Rabi, differ markedly in timing, crops, climate and regions.  Timing: Kharif is sown in June–July and harvested in September–October; Rabi is sown in October–November and harvested in March–April.  Crops: Kharif — rice, maize, cotton, groundnut, soybean; Rabi — wheat, barley, mustard, peas, gram (soybean appears in both lists).  Climate: Kharif needs high rainfall and warm, humid conditions; Rabi needs low to moderate rainfall and cool, dry weather.  Water: Kharif is monsoon-based and less dependent on irrigation; Rabi depends heavily on irrigation.  Regions: Kharif dominates East and South India; Rabi dominates Northwest and Central India. Conclusion: The two seasons complement each other, ensuring year-round cultivation and food security for the country.
