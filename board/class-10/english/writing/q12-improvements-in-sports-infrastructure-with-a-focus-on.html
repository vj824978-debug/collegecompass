Improvements in sports infrastructure with a focus on multi-purpose sustainable facilities as well as the…

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q55 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(A) Improvements in sports infrastructure with a focus on multi-purpose sustainable facilities as well as the integration of technology along with a grassroot centric approach is the key to bring Indian athletes on the world stage. Write an analytical paragraph on the above statement in about 120 words, expressing your views on how improved infrastructure can help Indian athletes win accolades for the nation. [5] OR (B) Read the following details about the characteristics of the Indian start-up ecosystem: 1. one of the most vibrant and largest in the world. 2. drives innovation across various fields. 3. brings unique solutions to major challenges both domestic and international. 4. has entered various fields - healthcare, e-commerce, agriculture. 5. supported by funding and government initiatives. In about 120 words analyse the future of startups in India considering factors that have supported its growth in the country. Focus on aspects of the need, benefits, and support needed for these startups. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Value points. Topic sentence: India’s success on the world sporting stage depends heavily on the quality and reach of its sports infrastructure. Points: (1) multi-purpose, sustainable facilities (stadiums, tracks, pools, indoor halls) allow many sports to be practised year-round at lower cost; (2) technology – performance analytics, video analysis, wearables, sports science, injury prevention and recovery – helps athletes train scientifically to international standards; (3) grassroot-centric approach – facilities in villages, small towns and schools to spot and nurture talent early; (4) access to quality coaching, equipment and competitions builds confidence and experience; (5) result: more medals and accolades at Olympics/Asian Games/Commonwealth Games. Conclusion: With sustained investment in modern, accessible and sustainable infrastructure, Indian athletes can turn potential into podium finishes and bring glory to the nation. OR (B) Value points. Topic sentence: India’s start-up ecosystem, one of the most vibrant and largest in the world, has a promising future. Points: (1) need – start-ups drive innovation and provide unique solutions to major domestic and international challenges; create jobs for a young population; (2) benefits – presence in healthcare, e-commerce and agriculture improves services, access and efficiency; boost to economic growth and self-reliance; (3) supporting factors – funding (investors, venture capital) and government initiatives; (4) support needed – easier access to capital, mentoring and incubation, simpler regulations, digital infrastructure and skill development, support in tier-2/3 cities. Conclusion: With continued funding, policy support and innovation, Indian start-ups are set to play a major role in the country’s growth and in solving global problems.
