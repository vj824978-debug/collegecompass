Question as text
(a) In the past few years, the teaching-learning process has taken a leap and gone beyond the four walls of the classroom. Today, it has become possible to learn not just by sitting in a physical classroom, but from anywhere. Given below is a chart depicting the differences between E-learning and Classroom learning. Write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words, assessing the benefits and drawbacks of both, by selecting features that support your analysis. E-learning vs. Classroom learning • accessible anytime anywhere • same place, same time • at your own pace • caters to the needs of the class • no travel costs, saves time • transportation cost and time consuming OR (b) Read the given details of a computer. ∗ electronic device that can store and process information ∗ makes life easy and simple ∗ physical description — monitor, keyboard, CPU and mouse ∗ computer technology used in all fields — education, medicine, commerce, entertainment Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words about the description and role of computers in today’s world.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) E-learning vs. classroom learning.  Topic sentence: The chart compares e-learning and classroom learning, two modes of education that have their own benefits and drawbacks.  E-learning: accessible anytime, anywhere; learners study at their own pace; no travel costs and saves time.  Its drawbacks (implied): less personal interaction and discipline; needs devices and internet.  Classroom learning: fixed place and time (less flexible) but caters to the needs of the whole class with direct teacher guidance and peer interaction.  Its drawbacks: transportation costs and time-consuming travel.  Conclusion: Each has its strengths; a blended approach combining the flexibility of e-learning with the interaction of the classroom serves learners best. (b) Computers in today’s world.  Topic sentence: The computer is an electronic device that can store and process information and has become indispensable in today’s world.  Physical description: consists of a monitor, keyboard, CPU and mouse.  It makes life easy and simple by doing complex tasks quickly and accurately.  Used in all fields: education (online classes, research), medicine (records, diagnosis), commerce (banking, online trade), entertainment (games, films, music).  Conclusion: Computers have transformed the way we live and work; used wisely, they will continue to drive progress.
