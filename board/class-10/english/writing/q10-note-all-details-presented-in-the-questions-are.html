Question as text
Note: All details presented in the questions are imaginary and created for assessment purpose. (a) Convenience, variety, discounts and laziness are some of the reasons for the growing popularity of ordering food online. You are Santa / Suhail student editor for the National Times. Compose a letter in about 120 words to the Editor of the newspaper highlighting the adverse effects of take-out food ordered online. Emphasize on the overall well-being of eating home-cooked meals. [5] OR (b) You visited your hometown after a period of four years and were disappointed at the condition of the roads. They were in a state of disrepair. The uneven surfaces, cracks and pot-holes were affecting daily commute and causing safety issues for the people. You are Sulekha/Salim. Write a letter to the Director of the Municipal Corporation in about 100-120 words drawing attention to the poor condition of roads and the inconvenience caused. Request him to look into the matter and take early action. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Letter to the Editor (format): sender’s address (Santa/Suhail, address, city); date; The Editor, The National Times, address; Subject: Adverse effects of online take-out food / Need to return to home-cooked meals; Sir/Madam; body; Yours faithfully, Santa/Suhail (Student Editor). Content points:  Ordering food online has become a trend — convenience, variety, discounts, laziness.  Take-out food is often high in oil, salt, sugar and preservatives; hygiene of preparation is unknown.  Leads to obesity, digestive problems, lifestyle diseases (diabetes, high blood pressure); expensive habit.  Plastic packaging adds to waste and pollution; sedentary lifestyle increases.  Home-cooked meals are fresh, nutritious, balanced, hygienic and economical; cooking and eating together strengthens family bonds and overall well-being.  Appeal: readers/parents/students should limit online orders to occasional treats and prefer home food; awareness drives in schools and media. (b) Letter to the Director, Municipal Corporation (format):sender’s address (Sulekha/Salim); date; The Director, Municipal Corporation, city; Subject: Poor condition of roads in . . . / Request for urgent repair; Sir/Madam; body; Yours faithfully/sincerely, Sulekha/Salim. Content points:  Introduce self; visited hometown after four years; disappointed to find roads in disrepair.  Describe the problem: uneven surfaces, cracks, pot-holes; waterlogging and dust.  Inconvenience: slow daily commute, traffic jams, damage to vehicles, difficulty for ambulances, school buses, elderly.  Safety issues: frequent accidents, especially for two-wheelers and at night.  Request: inspect the roads, repair/resurface them at the earliest, ensure quality and regular maintenance.  Close with hope of prompt action.
