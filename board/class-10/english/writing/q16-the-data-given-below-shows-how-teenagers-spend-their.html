The data given below shows how teenagers spend their daily screen time across different digital activities.

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q55 marks
Question 16 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(A) The data given below shows how teenagers spend their daily screen time across different digital activities. S. No. Digital Activities Percentage 1. Social media 35 2. Watching videos / streaming 30 3. Gaming 20 4. Online learning 10 5. Others (browsing, reading news, etc.) 5 Analyse the data provided and write a paragraph analyzing how teenagers allocate their daily screen time. Focus on the activity that occupies the largest proportion of time and on other major and minor activities in descending order, and on the balance between entertainment and educational screen use. Conclude with an observation about the overall trend. OR (B) Read the given details of a community library designed for your neighbourhood:  A well lit and ventilated reading lounge.  A digital section with computers, tablets and e-books.  A dedicated children’s corner with picture books and storytelling sessions.  A quiet study area with desk lamps and soundproof partitions for students.  Outdoor reading space under trees for senior citizens.  Book-sharing stands where people can donate and borrow books freely. Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph in about 120 words about the suitability of this library. Focus on factors such as accessibility, reading environment, suitability for different age groups, and community engagement.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Screen time of teenagers Topic sentence: The data on teenagers’ daily screen time shows that most of it goes to social media and entertainment, with only a small share for learning.  Social media takes the largest share, 35%.  Watching videos/streaming follows at 30%; together with social media this is 65%.  Gaming accounts for 20%.  Online learning gets only 10%; others (browsing, reading news) 5%.  Entertainment (social media + videos + gaming) = 85% versus 10% educational use: a heavy imbalance; entertainment is over eight times learning. Conclusion: Overall, teenagers’ screen use is dominated by entertainment; guided use and more time for learning are needed for a healthier balance. (B) Community library Topic sentence: The proposed community library is well suited to the neighbourhood as it caters to readers of every age and need.  Accessibility: free book-sharing stands; digital section with computers, tablets and e-books widens access.  Reading environment: well lit, ventilated lounge; quiet study area with desk lamps and soundproof partitions.  Age groups: children’s corner with picture books and storytelling; students’ study area; outdoor reading space under trees for senior citizens.  Community engagement: donating and borrowing books freely, storytelling sessions build a reading culture and bring residents together. Conclusion: With its inclusive facilities, the library can become a vibrant learning hub for the whole community.
