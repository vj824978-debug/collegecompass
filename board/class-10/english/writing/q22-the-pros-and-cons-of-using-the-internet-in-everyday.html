The Pros and Cons of using the internet in everyday life are numerous.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q55 marks
Question 22 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) The Pros and Cons of using the internet in everyday life are numerous. The internet is like a double-edged sword. Based on the information provided in the table below, write an analytical paragraph on the topic ‘Advantages and Disadvantages of the Internet’ in not less than 120 words. Pros and Cons of Internet Pros Cons (1) Connectivity (1) Data Security (2) Communication speed and versatile working (2) Social Isolation (3) Learning Re-defined (3) Addiction towards it (4) Transfer of Data (4) Plagiarism and copyright OR (b) Read the following details: Students today are becoming victims of cyber-crime and cyber bullying in schools, which is affecting the mental health of students. Write an analytical paragraph in120 words on the problems and causes of cyber-crimes. ⇒ Misuse of Information Technology with the intention to trouble others. ⇒ It comes in various forms – hacking, negative comments – spreading rumours Impact – spoils image, self-doubt, results in insecurities and complexes. Causes – desire of power, seeking entertainment, escapism, inadequate regulations.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Advantages and Disadvantages of the Internet – Topic sentence: The internet, like a double-edged sword, has transformed everyday life with remarkable benefits but also serious drawbacks. Points: Pros – (1) connectivity: people across the world stay connected instantly; (2) speed of communication and versatile working: emails, video calls, work from home, online banking/shopping; (3) learning re-defined: online classes, e-books, tutorials available anytime; (4) easy transfer and sharing of data. Cons – (1) data security: hacking, identity theft, privacy breaches; (2) social isolation: virtual contact replaces real interaction; (3) addiction: excessive screen time harms health and studies; (4) plagiarism and copyright violations through easy copying. Conclusion: The benefits clearly outweigh the risks when the internet is used wisely; safe, balanced and ethical use is the key. (b) Cyber-crime among students – Topic sentence: Cyber-crime and cyber bullying – the misuse of information technology to trouble others – are increasingly victimising school students and harming their mental health. Points: (1) forms: hacking of accounts, posting negative/abusive comments, spreading rumours online; (2) impact: spoils the victim’s image and reputation, causes self-doubt, insecurities and complexes, anxiety and poor performance; (3) causes: the bully’s desire for power, seeking entertainment, escapism, anonymity online and inadequate regulations/weak enforcement; (4) need for awareness, cyber-safety education, counselling, reporting mechanisms and stricter laws. Conclusion: Schools, parents and authorities must work together so that students can use the digital world safely and confidently.
