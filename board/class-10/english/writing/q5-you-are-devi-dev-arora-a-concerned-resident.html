You are Devi / Dev Arora a concerned resident.

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q45 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(A) You are Devi / Dev Arora a concerned resident. You are deeply concerned at the overflowing garbage dumps in the city. Besides being eyesores, these dumps create environmental and health risks. They produce a foul odour and attract stray animals and other scavengers. Write a letter to the Municipal Commissioner drawing attention to the situation. Suggest the need to identify systemic flaws in the collection system due to insufficient collection vehicles and irregular cleaning of dumps. OR (B) Living in multistoreyed complexes with limited open space for sporting activities, you feel that children today are forced to be indoors. This is leading to increased screen addiction, lack of concentration, and unhealthy eating habits and lifestyles. Write a letter to the editor on the need to build community centers with indoor sporting facilities for children. Highlight the benefits of creating a comfortable and safe environment, and its usefulness for residents. You are Shrushti / Sharan.
Answer
Answer as text
Format (both): sender’s address; date; receiver’s designation and address (The Municipal Commissioner, Municipal Corporation, . . . / The Editor, name of newspaper, . . . ); subject line; salutation (Sir/Madam); body in 3–4 paragraphs; complimentary close (Yours faithfully / Yours truly); name (Devi/Dev Arora; Shrushti/Sharan). (A) Letter to the Municipal Commissioner — Subject: Overflowing garbage dumps in the city.  Introduce self as a concerned resident; state the problem of overflowing garbage dumps in the locality/city.  Eyesores; foul odour; attract stray animals and scavengers; garbage scattered on roads.  Environmental and health risks: breeding of flies and mosquitoes, spread of diseases, contamination of soil and water.  Systemic flaws: too few collection vehicles, irregular and delayed clearing of dumps.  Suggestions: add collection vehicles, fix a regular pickup schedule, more covered bins, segregation at source, monitoring/complaint helpline, awareness among residents.  Request prompt action. (B) Letter to the Editor — Subject: Need for community centres with indoor sporting facilities for children.  Multistoreyed complexes leave little open space; children are forced to stay indoors.  Effects: screen addiction, lack of concentration, unhealthy eating habits, sedentary lifestyle.  Suggest community centres with indoor games (badminton, table tennis, chess, gymnasium, skating, etc.).  Benefits: safe and comfortable supervised environment, physical fitness, social skills, less screen time.  Usefulness to residents: space for all age groups, community bonding, events and meetings.  Appeal to authorities/builders/RWAs to act.
