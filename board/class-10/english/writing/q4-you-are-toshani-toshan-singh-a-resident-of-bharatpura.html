You are Toshani / Toshan Singh a resident of Bharatpura.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q45 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) You are Toshani / Toshan Singh a resident of Bharatpura. You observed that on weekend afternoon a group of senior residents teach children free of cost. You are delighted to see their spirit of giving back to the community. Write a letter to the editor expressing your views on the need of volunteerism and senior participation in community engagement. Mention the benefits of such activities and how it would even reduce loneliness. OR (b) You are Shereen / Shivaji. There has been a lot of construction going on in your colony since the past few months. However the contractors do not follow any protocols of timings, blocking pavements with building material and dumping the rubble in the back lanes and sewage pipes. Write a letter to the President of your RWA drawing attention to these issues. Suggest a set of protocols to be followed by the builders to reduce any inconvenience being caused to the neighbouring residents.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Letter to the Editor. Format: sender’s address (Bharatpura), date; The Editor, name/address of the newspaper; Subject: Need for volunteerism and senior citizens’ participation in community engagement; Sir/Madam; body; Yours faithfully, Toshani/Toshan Singh. Value points: (1) introduce the observation – senior residents teaching children free of cost on weekend afternoons, and appreciation of their spirit of giving back; (2) need for volunteerism – builds a caring, cohesive community, uses idle time and skills productively; (3) benefits to children – free guidance, values, life experience, tutoring for those who cannot afford it; (4) benefits to seniors – sense of purpose, respect, active mind and better health, reduces loneliness and isolation; (5) intergenerational bonding, transfer of knowledge and culture; (6) appeal to RWAs, schools and citizens to encourage and support such initiatives (spaces, recognition, more volunteers). (b) Letter to the President, RWA. Format: sender’s address, date; The President, Resident Welfare Association, (colony name); Subject: Inconvenience caused by unregulated construction in the colony; Sir/Madam; body; Yours sincerely/faithfully, Shereen/Shivaji. Value points (issues): construction going on for months; no fixed working hours – noise early morning/late night disturbs students, elderly and the sick; pavements blocked with building material, unsafe for pedestrians and children; rubble dumped in back lanes and sewage pipes – choked drains, overflow, unhygienic conditions, health risk. Suggested protocols: fixed working hours (e.g. 9 a.m.–6 p.m., no work on Sundays/holidays); material stored within the plot or a designated area, pavements kept clear; rubble removed regularly through authorised disposal, never into drains or lanes; covering material/sprinkling water to control dust; fines/penalties for violations and a monitoring committee; request for prompt action.
