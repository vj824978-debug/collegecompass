You are Soumya Hansal / Sumit Hansal living at F-32, Shanti Nagar, Vayugarh.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q45 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) You are Soumya Hansal / Sumit Hansal living at F-32, Shanti Nagar, Vayugarh. Seeing the inadequate healthcare infrastructure in your town, write a letter to the MLA of your area, in about 120 words, suggesting the need for improving existing healthcare facilities and creating more “Wellness Centres” including a specialized hospital for children and women. OR (b) You are Risha Khan / Zuhaan Khan, the sports captain of Highrise Public School, New Line, Rai Nagar. You believe that games and sports are an integral part of a student’s life. Write a letter to the editor of a national daily, in about 120 words, sharing your views on the importance of physical education and suggesting the need to establish a dedicated sports university, so that students can pursue and focus on their favourite sport.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Letter to the MLA. Format: sender’s address (F-32, Shanti Nagar, Vayugarh); date; receiver (The MLA, [Constituency], Vayugarh); Subject: Need to improve healthcare facilities and set up Wellness Centres in Vayugarh; salutation (Sir/Madam); body; complimentary close (Yours sincerely/faithfully), name (Soumya/Sumit Hansal). Value points:  Introduce self as a resident; draw attention to the inadequate healthcare infrastructure of the town.  Problems: overcrowded government hospital/dispensaries, shortage of doctors, beds, equipment and medicines; patients forced to travel to other cities.  Suggest upgrading existing hospitals and primary health centres (staff, diagnostics, ambulances, emergency care).  Suggest opening more Wellness Centres in each locality for check-ups, vaccination, preventive care and awareness.  Stress the need for a specialised hospital for women and children (maternity care, paediatrics, neonatal care).  Request prompt action; express hope and thank the MLA. (b) Letter to the editor. Format: sender’s address (Highrise Public School, New Line, Rai Nagar); date; receiver (The Editor, [name of a national daily], [city]); Subject: Importance of physical education and need for a dedicated sports university; salutation (Sir/Madam); request to publish the views in the newspaper; complimentary close (Yours sincerely), name and designation (Risha/Zuhaan Khan, Sports Captain). Value points:  Games and sports are an integral part of a student’s life, not merely an extra activity.  Benefits of physical education: physical fitness, mental health and stress relief, discipline, teamwork, leadership, sportsmanship.  Present situation: sports are neglected in favour of academics; lack of facilities and trained coaches.  A dedicated sports university would offer degrees, professional coaching, sports science and infrastructure, letting students specialise in their favourite sport.  This would produce national and international level players and open careers in sports.  Appeal to the government/authorities to take up the proposal.
