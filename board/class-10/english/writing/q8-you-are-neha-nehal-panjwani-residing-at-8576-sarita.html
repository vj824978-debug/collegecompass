You are Neha/Nehal Panjwani, residing at 8576, Sarita Apartments, Hanger Street.

CBSE 2025QP 2/3/1Q45 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) You are Neha/Nehal Panjwani, residing at 8576, Sarita Apartments, Hanger Street. As a responsible citizen, you believe in promoting environmentally responsible practices. Write a letter to the Director, Alternate Energy Commission, in about 120 words, sharing your views about ‘Reducing the Carbon Footprint’ by encouraging the use of renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind power and to provide subsidies to families who want to avail the facility. OR (b) As Shobita/Shobhit Das, a responsible citizen, residing in 14-A, GVR Colony, Hoshiyarbad, you have noticed that a road crossing in your area does not have a traffic signal which leads to chaos and traffic jams during peak hours. It is also an accident-prone area. Despite writing numerous letters to the Traffic Commissioner, no action has yet been taken. Write a letter to the editor of a national daily, in about 120 words, to draw the attention of concerned authorities to address the pressing issue affecting the commuters.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Format: Sender: 8576, Sarita Apartments, Hanger Street; date; receiver: The Director, Alternate Energy Commission, (address); Subject: Reducing the carbon footprint through renewable energy and subsidies; Respected Sir/Madam; body; Yours sincerely, Neha/Nehal Panjwani. Content points: (1) introduce yourself and the purpose — concern about rising carbon emissions and climate change; (2) dependence on fossil fuels (coal-based electricity, diesel) is the major source of the carbon footprint; (3) renewable sources such as solar rooftop panels and wind power are clean, inexhaustible and cut electricity bills in the long run; (4) the high initial cost of installation keeps many families away, so subsidies, easy loans and simple application procedures are needed; (5) suggest awareness campaigns and incentives for housing societies; (6) conclude with a request for prompt action and hope for a greener future. (b) Format: Sender: 14-A, GVR Colony, Hoshiyarbad; date; receiver: The Editor, (name of a national daily), (address); Subject: Need for a traffic signal at the road crossing in GVR Colony, Hoshiyarbad; Sir/Madam; body; Yours sincerely/truly, Shobita/Shobhit Das. Content points: (1) request space in the newspaper to highlight the problem; (2) the crossing (name/location) has no traffic signal — chaos and long traffic jams during peak hours; (3) it is accident-prone: risk to pedestrians, school children and the elderly; accidents/near-misses; (4) numerous letters to the Traffic Commissioner have brought no action; (5) suggestions: install traffic lights at the earliest, post traffic police during peak hours, zebra crossings and speed breakers; (6) hope that publication will draw the attention of the concerned authorities.
