CBSE 2025QP 2/2/1Q45 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
(a) As Sakshi/Saksham Bedi of 864, Solar Apartments, Lajpat Street, Dipak Nagar, you feel that the lack of proper street lighting in several areas of your neighbourhood is a threat to safety. Residents, especially those who work late shifts or return home after dark, feel vulnerable due to poorly lit streets. Write a letter to the District Magistrate of your city area, in about 120 words, stating the problem and requesting him to take necessary action for installation of streetlights in those areas. OR (b) You are Dharti Pradhan/Balal Goyal, the head girl/boy of Pinnacle School, Ustahpuri. You live in a culturally rich city with many historical monuments. You have observed that visitors have etched graffiti on these monuments and littered the area around. You are extremely disturbed at this irresponsible behaviour of the visitors. Write a letter to the editor of a national daily, in about 120 words, suggesting measures to stop such vandalism and creating awareness about the importance of these monuments.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Format: Sender: 864, Solar Apartments, Lajpat Street, Dipak Nagar; date; receiver: The District Magistrate, (city); Subject: Request for installation of streetlights in poorly lit areas of Dipak Nagar; Sir/Madam; complimentary close “Yours faithfully”, Sakshi/Saksham Bedi. Content points: (1) introduce self as a resident and state the purpose; (2) several streets/lanes (name them) lack streetlights or have non-functional ones; (3) residents, especially late-shift workers, women, children and the elderly, feel unsafe after dark — risk of theft, chain-snatching, harassment and accidents; (4) incidents/complaints already reported, fear among residents; (5) request immediate survey and installation/repair of streetlights, regular maintenance, possibly solar/LED lights; (6) hope for prompt action, thanks. (b) Format: Sender: Pinnacle School, Ustahpuri; date; receiver: The Editor, (name of national daily); Subject: Vandalism of historical monuments — need for urgent measures; Sir/Madam; “Yours faithfully”, Dharti Pradhan/Balal Goyal, Head Girl/Boy. Content points: (1) request to publish the letter to draw attention to the issue; (2) the city’s rich heritage — monuments are a symbol of history and culture and attract tourists; (3) problem: visitors etching graffiti, littering the premises, defacing walls — irreversible damage and a bad image; (4) measures: CCTV surveillance and guards, heavy fines/penalties, adequate dustbins, signboards, guided tours; (5) awareness: campaigns, school heritage clubs/walks, social-media drives, involving local communities; (6) appeal to citizens to treat monuments as national pride and preserve them for future generations.
