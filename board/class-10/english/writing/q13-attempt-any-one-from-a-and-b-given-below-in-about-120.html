Question 13 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
Attempt any one from (a) and (b) given below in about 120 words : [5] (a) Read the given details of the school activities that help children in their personality development.  student councils, clubs  activities in the Morning Assembly  celebration of important days  organization of various competitions  sports and academic activities Analyse the details and write an analytical paragraph about the impact of school activities on students. Focus on factors like fosters leadership, responsibility, communication skills, discipline, daily motivation, and team building. OR (b) Read the following details about fashion trends. In today’s world, fashion is not just about clothes, it is driven by images and impressions. Fashion trends provide a powerful reflection of society’s evolution, and creativity. Write an analytical paragraph about how the factors mentioned below have shaped the fashion trends in recent years with key causes, effects and future implications.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Impact of school activities. Topic sentence: School activities beyond the classroom play a vital role in shaping the all-round personality of students. Points to analyse: student councils and clubs foster leadership and a sense of responsibility; Morning Assembly activities (speeches, news reading, thought for the day) build communication skills, confidence and daily motivation; celebration of important days instils values, cultural awareness and patriotism; competitions encourage healthy competition, creativity and resilience in handling success and failure; sports and academic activities develop discipline, fitness, team building and cooperation. Link each activity to the trait it develops. Conclusion: Thus, a balanced mix of such activities turns students into confident, disciplined and responsible individuals ready for life’s challenges. (b) Factors shaping fashion trends. Topic sentence: In recent years fashion has evolved from a mere choice of clothing into a reflection of identity, image and social change. Points to analyse (causes → effects): creative expression — fashion as a means of showing individuality; social media influence and celebrity endorsements — trends spread instantly and shape aspirations, but also create pressure and fast-changing fads; blend of traditional and modern styles — fusion/Indo-western wear keeps heritage alive; commercialization of festivals — festive collections and sales boost demand; customized fashion choices — personalised, made-to-order clothing; rising e-commerce — wider access and variety even in small towns, convenient shopping. Mention effects such as fast fashion, overconsumption and environmental waste. Conclusion/future implications: Fashion will grow more digital, personalised and diverse; the need is to balance trends with sustainability and mindful consumption.
