CBSE 2026QP 2/2/1Q45 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 English · Writing
Question as text
Attempt any one from (a) and (b) given below in about 120 words : [5] (a) You are Sanjana/Sanjit of Avenue Apartments, XYZ Colony, Sakshamgarh. Recently, you read in the newspaper about adulteration in food and dairy products consumed by us daily. Write a letter to the editor of a national daily expressing your views on food adulteration. Highlight the health risks of eating such foods and suggest measures to tackle the issue. OR (b) Going for a walk in your residential colony offers a convenient way to integrate physical activity into your daily routine. Write a letter to the Director, Municipal Board, requesting maintenance of the colony parks, installation of lighting poles, and repair of walking tracks to facilitate walkers and create a safe community space. You are Rita/Raghav Mishra, President RWA, Senatown.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Letter to the Editor. Format: sender’s address (Avenue Apartments, XYZ Colony, Sakshamgarh); date; The Editor, (name of a national daily), city; Subject: Growing menace of food adulteration; Sir/Madam; body; Yours sincerely/truly, Sanjana/Sanjit. Value points: (1) Purpose: to draw attention, through the newspaper, to the recent reports of adulteration in daily food and dairy products. (2) Examples: milk diluted with water/detergent/urea, sweets with synthetic colours, spices mixed with brick powder or sawdust, artificially ripened fruits. (3) Health risks: food poisoning, stomach and liver disorders, allergies, kidney damage, even cancer; children and the elderly most vulnerable. (4) Causes: greed for profit, weak checks, low consumer awareness. (5) Measures: regular sampling and testing by food-safety authorities, strict penalties and cancellation of licences, awareness campaigns, simple home tests, consumers buying certified (FSSAI/AGMARK) products and reporting suspected adulteration. (6) Conclusion: appeal to authorities and citizens to act together. (b) Letter to the Director, Municipal Board. Format: sender’s address (Rita/Raghav Mishra, President RWA, Senatown); date; The Director, Municipal Board, (city); Subject: Request for maintenance of colony parks, lighting and walking tracks; Sir/Madam; body; Yours faithfully, Rita/Raghav Mishra, President, RWA, Senatown. Value points: (1) Introduce self and purpose on behalf of the residents. (2) Parks neglected: overgrown grass, broken benches, garbage, damaged swings. (3) No/insufficient street lights — unsafe for walkers in the early morning and evening, especially women, children and senior citizens; risk of theft and accidents. (4) Walking tracks broken and uneven — falls and injuries; residents avoid walking. (5) Requests: regular cleaning and upkeep of parks, installation of lighting poles, repair/levelling of tracks, perhaps benches and dustbins. (6) Benefits: healthy lifestyle, safe community space; request prompt action and offer RWA cooperation.
