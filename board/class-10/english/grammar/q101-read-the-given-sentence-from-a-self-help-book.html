Read the given sentence from a self-help book.

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q3(ii)1 mark
Question 101 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Read the given sentence from a self-help book. Identify the error and supply the correction in the given format: To identifying your goal is the biggest step in your success story.
Answer
Answer as text
Error: To identifying Correction: Identifying (gerund as subject; “To identify” is also acceptable).
