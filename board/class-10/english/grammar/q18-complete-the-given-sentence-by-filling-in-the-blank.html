Question 18 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Complete the given sentence by filling in the blank with the correct option. After she her homework, Anaisha finally relaxed and watched a movie with her friends. (A) finished (B) had finished (C) have finished (D) will finish
Answer
Answer as text
(B) had finished (past perfect for the earlier of two past actions)
