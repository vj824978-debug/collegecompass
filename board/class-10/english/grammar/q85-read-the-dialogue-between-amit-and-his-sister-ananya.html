Read the dialogue between Amit and his sister Ananya, regarding her travel to the railway station.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q3(iv)1 mark
Question 85 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Read the dialogue between Amit and his sister Ananya, regarding her travel to the railway station. Amit: Are you sure you can manage to go to the railway station on your own? Ananya: Oh yes! I have already booked a cab. Select the correct option to report the above dialogue. Amit asked Ananya to go to the railway station on her own. Ananya replied in the affirmative and said that she had already booked a cab. (A) if she is sure she can manage (B) if she was sure she could manage (C) if she surely can manage (D) if she was sure to managing
Answer
Answer as text
(B) if she was sure she could manage
