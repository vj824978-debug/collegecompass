CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q3(x)1 mark
Question 15 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Fill in the blank by choosing the correct option to complete the notice on a school notice board. We are pleased to announce that our Annual Sports Day on 30th April, 2025 at the school playground from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. (A) is held (B) will be held (C) had been held (D) to be held
Answer
Answer as text
(B) will be held (future event, passive voice).
