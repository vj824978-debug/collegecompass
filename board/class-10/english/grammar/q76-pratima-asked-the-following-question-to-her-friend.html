CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q3(iii)1 mark
Question 76 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Pratima asked the following question to her friend, Shifa – Do you like the painting, I gifted you yesterday. Report Pratima’s question correctly.
Answer
Answer as text
Pratima asked Shifa if (whether) she liked the painting she had gifted her the previous day (the day before).
