Identify the error and supply correction for the following pre-flight announcement before take-off.

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q3(vii)1 mark
Question 25 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Identify the error and supply correction for the following pre-flight announcement before take-off. Ladies and gentlemen, welcome onboard flight 487 flying from Hong Kong to San Francisco. We are currently third in line for take-off and were expected to be in the air in approximately seven minutes time. Use the given format for your response.
Answer
Answer as text
error: were correction: are (present tense, consistent with “We are currently third in line”).
