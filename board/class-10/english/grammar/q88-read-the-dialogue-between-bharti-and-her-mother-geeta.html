Read the dialogue between Bharti and her mother, Geeta, regarding Geeta’s birthday plans.

CBSE 2025QP 2/2/1Q3(iv)1 mark
Question 88 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Read the dialogue between Bharti and her mother, Geeta, regarding Geeta’s birthday plans. Bharti: Do you want to go to a hill station on your birthday? Geeta: No, I want to stay at home and call my friends over. Select the correct option that reports the above dialogue. Bharti asked Geeta if she wanted to go to a hill station on her birthday. Geeta replied in the negative and said that . (A) she wanted to stay home and call her friends for celebrating. (B) I wanted to stay home and call my friends to celebrate. (C) she wanted to stay at home and call her friends over. (D) she wants to stay home by calling her friends to celebrate.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) she wanted to stay at home and call her friends over.
