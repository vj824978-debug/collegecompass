Identify the error and supply correction for the given sentence from a preface of a book.

CBSE 2025QP 2/2/1Q3(vi)1 mark
Question 50 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Identify the error and supply correction for the given sentence from a preface of a book. Writing this book have been a journey of exploration and discovery. Use the given format for your response.
Answer
Answer as text
Error: have Correction: has (the gerund subject ‘Writing this book’ is singular).
