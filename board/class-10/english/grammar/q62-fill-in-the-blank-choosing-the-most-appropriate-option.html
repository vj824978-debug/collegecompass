CBSE 2025QP 2/4/1Q3(ix)1 mark
Question 62 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Fill in the blank choosing the most appropriate option: The teacher distributed a piece of paper to student. (A) few (B) all (C) many (D) each
Answer
Answer as text
(D) each (‘each’ is used with a singular countable noun).
