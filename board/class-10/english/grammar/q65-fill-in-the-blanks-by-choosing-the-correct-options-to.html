Fill in the blanks by choosing the correct options to conclude the report.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q3(xii)1 mark
Question 65 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Fill in the blanks by choosing the correct options to conclude the report. The report concludes that learners have knowledge of astrophysics. (A) very few / some (B) some / every (C) every / no (D) no / a lot of
Answer
Answer as text
(A) very few / some – The report concludes that very few learners have some knowledge of astrophysics.
