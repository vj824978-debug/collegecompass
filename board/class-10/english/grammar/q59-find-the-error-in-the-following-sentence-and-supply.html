Question as text
Find the error in the following sentence and supply the correction: I was disappointed to see that in the library there was less books on the subject than expected.
Answer
Answer as text
Error: less Correction: fewer (books is countable). The verb must also agree: “there were fewer books on the subject than expected” (error: was less → were fewer ).
