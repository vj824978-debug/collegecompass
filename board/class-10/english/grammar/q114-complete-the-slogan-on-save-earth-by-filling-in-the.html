CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q3(xi)1 mark
Question 114 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Complete the slogan on “Save Earth”, by filling in the blank with the correct option. Lets join hands, cultivate conservation, and a greener tomorrow for generations to come. (A) ensuring (B) ensure (C) ensures (D) ensured
Answer
Answer as text
(B) ensure (base form after ‘let’s’, parallel to ‘join’ and ‘cultivate’).
