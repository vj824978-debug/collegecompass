Report the dialogue between a customer and a worker at a grocery store.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q3(ix)1 mark
Question 77 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Report the dialogue between a customer and a worker at a grocery store. Customer : Did you pack all the items ? Worker : Yes sir, I did. The customer asked the worker .
Answer
Answer as text
. . . if (whether) he had packed all the items. The worker respectfully replied in the affirmative (that he had).
