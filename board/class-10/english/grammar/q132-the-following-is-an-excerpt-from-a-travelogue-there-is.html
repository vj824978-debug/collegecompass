The following is an excerpt from a travelogue: There is an error in the statement. Select the option that identifies the error and supplies the correction. Kanyakumari is popular for its beautiful beaches, unique Vivekananda Rock Memorial and stunning sunrise and sunset view. Error Correction (A) is are (B) for of (C) and also (D) view views
Answer
Answer as text
(D) view → views (sunrise and sunset views – plural).
