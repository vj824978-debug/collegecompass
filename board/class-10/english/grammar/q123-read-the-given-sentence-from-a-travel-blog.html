CBSE 2025QP 2/3/1Q3(ii)1 mark
Question 123 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Read the given sentence from a travel blog. Identify the error and supply the correction in the sentence. Spend a night under the stars in a traditional camp, where you can experience the warmth of our hospitable and savour the delicious local cuisine. Use the given format for your response.
Answer
Answer as text
Error: hospitable Correction: hospitality (a noun is needed after ‘our’)
