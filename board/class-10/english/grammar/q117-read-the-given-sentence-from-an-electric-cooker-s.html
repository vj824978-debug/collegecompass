Read the given sentence from an electric cooker’s instruction manual.

Question 117 · Class 10 English · Grammar
Question as text
Read the given sentence from an electric cooker’s instruction manual. Identify the error and supply the correction in the sentence. For avoid damaging the appliance, do not immerse the electric cooker in water or any other liquid. Use the given format for your response.
Answer
Answer as text
Error: For Correction: To (“To avoid damaging the appliance, . . . ”).
