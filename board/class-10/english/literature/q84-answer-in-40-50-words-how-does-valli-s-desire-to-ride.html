Answer in 40–50 words : How does Valli’s desire to ride the bus reflect her aspirations and challenges in context of her daily life ? (Madam Rides the Bus)
Answer
Answer as text
Eight-year-old Valli watched the bus from her doorway, as it was her chief pastime, and longed to ride it to town. Her desire shows her curiosity and wish to see the world beyond her village. To achieve it she faced challenges: she had to save every coin, resisting the temptation of peppermints, toys and balloons, and secretly gather information about fares and timings. She planned the trip carefully and went during her mother’s afternoon nap, showing determination and independence.
