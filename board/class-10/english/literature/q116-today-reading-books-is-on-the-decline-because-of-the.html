Today reading books is on the decline because of the prevalence of electronic media.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q9(ii)The Book That Saved the Earth3 marks
Question 116 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Today reading books is on the decline because of the prevalence of electronic media. Contrarily, earth is saved by a ‘Book’ in the chapter “The Book That Saved the Earth”. Elaborate.
Answer
Answer as text
In the play, the Martians planning to invade Earth find a book of Mother Goose nursery rhymes. Think-Tank misinterprets rhymes like ‘Humpty Dumpty’ and ‘Hey Diddle Diddle’, concludes that earthlings are highly advanced and dangerous, and abandons the invasion. So a simple book saved the Earth, showing the lasting value of books even in an age of electronic media.
