Answer in about 100-120 words: Explore the theme of loss as it is depicted in both the “Ball Poem” and “The Sermon at Benaras”, highlighting the inevitable nature of this aspect in life?
Answer
Answer as text
 Both texts present loss as an inevitable part of human life that everyone must learn to accept.  The Ball Poem: a boy loses his ball, which bounces into the harbour; it is his first experience of loss and he is grief-stricken.  The poet does not console him or offer money for another ball – money is external; the boy must learn the “epistemology of loss”, i.e. how to bear loss and stand up again.  The Sermon at Benaras: Kisa Gotami loses her only son and goes from house to house seeking medicine to bring him back.  The Buddha asks her for mustard seed from a house where no one has died; she finds none and realises that death is common to all and her grief was selfish.  The Buddha teaches that the wise do not grieve; lamentation brings only pain; peace comes from accepting the law of nature.  Conclusion: loss teaches maturity, acceptance and inner strength.
