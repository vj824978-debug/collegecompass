CBSE 2025QP 2/2/1Q10(a)A Letter to God / The Sermon at Benares6 marks
Question 145 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer the following question in 100–120 words. Analyse and evaluate the role of faith in ‘A Letter to God’ and ‘The Sermon at Benares’.
Answer
Answer as text
V alue points: (1) Lencho has unshakeable faith in God: after hail destroys his crop he writes to God for 100 pesos, believing God sees everything. (2) His faith moves the postmaster, who collects money to keep it alive; Lencho is not surprised, but his blind faith makes him suspect the post-office employees of stealing 30 pesos — an irony. (3) In ‘The Sermon at Benares’, Kisa Gotami’s faith in the Buddha leads her to seek a cure for her dead son. (4) The Buddha uses her faith to make her search for a mustard seed from a house where no one has died, leading her to realise that death is common to all. (5) Her faith thus brings enlightenment and acceptance; she overcomes selfish grief and attains peace. (6) Evaluation: faith gives hope and strength in adversity; Lencho’s is simple and blind, while Kisa Gotami’s leads to wisdom and self-realisation.
