Answer in about 100-120 words: Evaluate Griffin’s character as a brilliant scientist and a lawless person. (Footprints without Feet)
Answer
Answer as text
 Griffin was a brilliant scientist who discovered a rare drug that made the human body invisible – a remarkable feat of research.  But he misused his genius; he was lawless, selfish and lacked moral values.  When his landlord tried to evict him, he set fire to the landlord’s house out of revenge.  In London he took shelter in a big store, stole clothes and food, and later robbed a theatrical company’s shop of clothes, bandages and a false nose after knocking the shopkeeper down.  At Iping he was rude and secretive at the inn, and stole money from the clergyman’s desk.  When confronted, he threw off his clothes, attacked the constable and villagers and escaped.  Conclusion: intelligence without ethics turns a genius into a menace; science must be used responsibly.
