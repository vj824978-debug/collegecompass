Answer the following question in 100–120 words : Analyze, how the theme of appearance versus reality is developed in ‘The Necklace’ through Matilda’s life choices and their consequences. [6]
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Matilda, married to a petty clerk, is discontented and dreams of wealth and luxury; she values appearance over reality.  Invited to a ball, she is unhappy until her husband gives her his savings for a dress, and she borrows a diamond necklace from her rich friend Mme Forestier to look wealthy.  At the ball she is admired, but the brilliance is borrowed and temporary; she loses the necklace.  Instead of telling the truth, she and her husband borrow heavily and buy a replacement costing thirty-six thousand francs.  They spend ten years in poverty and hard labour to repay the debt; Matilda loses her beauty and becomes a coarse, worn woman.  The irony: Mme Forestier reveals the original necklace was imitation, worth at most five hundred francs.  Conclusion: the pursuit of false appearances and lack of honesty ruin her life; reality was simpler than the show she valued.
