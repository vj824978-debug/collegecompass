CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q11(b)Bholi6 marks
Question 176 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
It is truly said that a good teacher is the light that helps one cross even the darkest of paths through honing their inherent qualities which enable them to shine bright and strong in life. In what ways, does Sulekha’s teacher epitomize this saying? How did she become the pillar of support and strength for Sulekha?
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Sulekha (Bholi) was neglected – pock-marked, slow-witted, stammered; called Bholi; had no confidence.  On her first day the teacher spoke to her kindly, unlike others; her affectionate voice gave Bholi a feeling of being cared for.  The teacher encouraged her to speak her name without fear and promised that she would soon speak like others.  She gave her a picture book and told her she would become the most learned person in the village, so none could laugh at her.  Under the teacher’s guidance Bholi gained education, a voice and self-respect.  As a result Bholi refused to marry the greedy, old Bishamber who demanded dowry, and chose to serve her parents and teach in the same school.  The teacher was thus the light that transformed Bholi into the confident, self-reliant Sulekha.
