Answer in about 40-50 words: Bholi’s heart was brimming with “fresh optimism and a renewed existence.” What significance does the expression “a new hope and a new life” hold for Bholi?
Answer
Answer as text
Neglected at home because of her pock-marked face and stammer, Bholi had no confidence. The teacher’s kind and encouraging words gave her a new hope: she began to learn and to believe in herself. Education gave her a new life – she became confident, refused to marry the greedy old Bishamber who demanded dowry, and chose to look after her parents and teach in the same school.
