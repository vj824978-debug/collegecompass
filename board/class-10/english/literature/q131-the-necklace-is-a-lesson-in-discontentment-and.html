‘The Necklace’ is a lesson in discontentment and unreasonable materialism.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q9(ii)The Necklace3 marks
Question 131 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
‘The Necklace’ is a lesson in discontentment and unreasonable materialism. In light of the preceding statement, discuss Matilda’s greatest failing.
Answer
Answer as text
Matilda’s greatest failing was her discontentment and false pride. Unhappy with her modest life, she longed for luxury; she insisted on a costly dress and borrowed a diamond necklace to look rich at the ball. When she lost it, pride stopped her from telling Mme Forestier the truth; she bought a replacement on huge loans and spent ten years in hardship – only to learn the necklace was fake.
