Explain why time is probably one of the most valuable possessions that one intensely regrets losing.

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q8(v)The Ball Poem3 marks
Question 49 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Explain why time is probably one of the most valuable possessions that one intensely regrets losing. (The Ball Poem)
Answer
Answer as text
The poem teaches that some things, once lost, can never be bought back. Money is external and can replace a ball, but not the memories and moments attached to it. Like the ball that is gone forever, time that has passed cannot be recovered; this irreversible loss is why people regret it intensely, and learning to accept it is part of growing up.
