Compare the two stories, ‘Letter to God’ and ‘The Black Aeroplane’ from ‘Stories About Flying’.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q10(a)A Letter to God; The Black Aeroplane6 marks
Question 139 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Compare the two stories, ‘Letter to God’ and ‘The Black Aeroplane’ from ‘Stories About Flying’. Examine how both these stories illustrate that help can come in unexpected forms.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  A Letter to God: Lencho’s crop is destroyed by a hailstorm; with deep faith he writes to God asking for a hundred pesos.  Help comes unexpectedly from the postmaster and post-office employees, who collect money (about seventy pesos) and send it signed ‘God’.  Lencho’s unshakeable faith makes him believe God sent it and suspect the post-office employees of being ‘a bunch of crooks’ – irony of the story.  The Black Aeroplane: the pilot, flying his old Dakota home to England, enters huge black storm clouds; his instruments fail and fuel runs low.  A mysterious black aeroplane without lights appears and guides him safely to a runway; the control tower says no other plane was flying.  In both stories help arrives from an unexpected, unseen source – human kindness in one, a mysterious (perhaps supernatural) guide in the other.  Common themes: faith/hope in crisis, goodness of the unknown helper; difference – Lencho never learns the truth, while the pilot is left wondering who saved him.
