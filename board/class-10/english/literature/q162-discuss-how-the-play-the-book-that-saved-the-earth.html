CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q11(b)The Book That Saved the Earth6 marks
Question 162 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Discuss how the play “The Book that Saved the Earth” depicts how misunderstanding of cultural differences and lack of awareness about other races cause confusion and conflict.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  The Martians, led by Think-Tank, plan to invade Earth and send an expedition (Captain Omega and his crew) who land in a library in Centerville.  They have no idea what books are: they think they are sandwiches, communication devices, etc., and try to ‘eat’ or ‘hear’ them.  Using a translator, they read ‘Mother Goose’ nursery rhymes and take them literally as messages about Earthlings.  ‘Humpty Dumpty’ makes Think-Tank think Earthlings plan to destroy him; ‘Hey diddle diddle’ (the cow jumped over the moon) makes them think Earthlings are far more advanced than Martians.  Think-Tank’s arrogance and ignorance of human culture turn innocent rhymes into imagined threats, and he cancels the invasion and flees – so the book ‘saved the Earth’.  Message: lack of awareness about other cultures breeds suspicion, fear and conflict; knowledge and understanding lead to peace. Humour arises from the literal misreading.
