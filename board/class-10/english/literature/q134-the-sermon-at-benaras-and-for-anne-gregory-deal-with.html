“The Sermon at Benaras” and “For Anne Gregory”, deal with the themes of ‘love’ and ‘life’.

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q10(ii)The Sermon at Benaras; For Anne Gregory6 marks
Question 134 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
“The Sermon at Benaras” and “For Anne Gregory”, deal with the themes of ‘love’ and ‘life’. Discuss.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points: (1) Sermon at Benaras: Kisa Gotami’s love for her dead son makes her seek medicine to revive him. (2) The Buddha asks for mustard seed from a house where no one has died; she learns that death is common to all and life is transient. (3) Lesson: grief and selfish attachment bring suffering; the wise accept death and gain peace. (4) For Anne Gregory: the young man may love Anne only for her “yellow hair” – outward beauty. (5) Only God can love a person for herself alone and not for her looks; true love goes beyond appearance. (6) Both show that life and beauty are temporary, and real love/understanding looks beyond the self and outward appearance to inner truth.
