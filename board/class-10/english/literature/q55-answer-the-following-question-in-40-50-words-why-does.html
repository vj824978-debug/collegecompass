Answer the following question in 40–50 words : Why does Mandela mention that he was prevented from fulfilling his obligations as a son, a brother, a father, and a husband ? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Mandela says every man has twin obligations — to his family and to his people and country. In apartheid South Africa a man of colour who tried to fulfil his duty to his people was torn from his family and home, forced into secrecy and rebellion, and punished. Because he chose to serve his people’s struggle for freedom (and spent long years in prison), he could not fulfil his duties as a son, brother, father and husband; he mentions this to show the cruel price of oppression.
