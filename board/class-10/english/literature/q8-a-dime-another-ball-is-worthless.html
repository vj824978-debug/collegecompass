A dime, another ball, is worthless.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q7(b)The Ball Poem5 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow : A dime, another ball, is worthless. Now He senses first responsibility In a world of possessions. People will take Balls, balls will be lost always, little boy. And no one buys a ball back. Money is external He is learning, well behind his desperate eyes, The epistemology of loss, how to stand up Knowing what every man must one day know And most know many days, how to stand up. (The Ball Poem) (i) Comment on the portrayal of the character of the boy in the given extract. [2] (ii) Complete the following suitably : [1] “Money is external” implies that the boy (A) values material possessions. (B) learns the money cannot restore what is lost. (C) believes money is the solution to all problems. (D) is indifferent to material wealth. (iii) What does the poet mean by the phrase, ‘epistemology of loss’ ? [1] (iv) What kind of atmosphere is created through these lines ? Select the correct option. [1] 1. reflection 2. intrigue 3. acceptance 4. anger 5. fear (A) 1, 4 and 5 (B) Only 2 (C) 1 and 3 (D) 4 and 5
Answer
Answer as text
(i) The boy is grief-stricken and desperate at losing his ball, which held memories for him. Yet he is shown as growing up: he senses his first responsibility in a world of possessions and is learning, on his own, to bear loss and to stand up again. (ii) (B) learns the money cannot restore what is lost. (iii) The knowledge or understanding of the nature of loss – learning what it means to lose something and how to cope with it. (iv) (C) 1 and 3 – reflection and acceptance.
