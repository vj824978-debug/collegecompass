Question as text
Imagine you are Bholi and write a diary entry, explaining how you dare to take such a bold step of refusing to marry Bishamber. (Bholi) [6]
Answer
Answer as text
Value points (diary format: date, day, time; Dear Diary; signature — Bholi/Sulekha):  Recalls her childhood: a fall from the cot, smallpox scars, stammering; neglected and called “Bholi”, the simpleton.  Was sent to school only because no one would marry her; the kind teacher encouraged her and promised that education would remove her fears and earn her respect.  Today her marriage was fixed with Bishamber, an old, lame man with grown-up children, chosen because her parents feared no one else would marry her.  At the wedding Bishamber saw her pock-marked face and demanded a dowry of five thousand rupees; her father humbled himself and paid.  She felt disgusted by his greed and her father’s humiliation; the confidence and self-respect her teacher gave her made her speak boldly and refuse to marry a greedy, mean and contemptible man.  Resolves to look after her parents in their old age and to teach in the same school; feels proud that education has made her bold and independent.
