Question as text
Answer the following question in 40–50 words. How does “The Ball Poem” by John Berryman explore the theme of loss and its profound impact on an individual’s emotional state and perception of the world? (The Ball Poem)
Answer
Answer as text
The boy loses his ball, which bounces into the water; he stands rigid and grieving, recalling the happy days spent with it. The poet does not console him with money or a new ball, because the boy must learn the “epistemology of loss” — that loss is inevitable in life. The experience makes him realise that nothing is permanent and he must learn to bear loss and move on, a first step towards maturity.
