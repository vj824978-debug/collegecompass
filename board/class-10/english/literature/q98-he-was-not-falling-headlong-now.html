“He was not falling headlong now.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q8(iv)Two Stories about Flying3 marks
Question 98 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
“He was not falling headlong now. He was soaring gradually downwards and outwards, he was no longer afraid.” Describe the young seagull’s feelings, emotions and experiences, just before this moment.
Answer
Answer as text
Alone on the ledge, the young seagull was afraid to fly though his siblings had flown. He was hungry and lonely, and felt rejected when his family scorned him. When his mother brought fish close and then flew away, maddened by hunger he dived at it; he fell outwards and downwards with a scream of terror, but then his wings spread out, the wind caught them and he began to fly.
