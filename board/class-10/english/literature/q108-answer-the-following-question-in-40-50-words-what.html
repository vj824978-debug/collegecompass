Answer the following question in 40–50 words : What makes Bholi believe that the place where she was taken was better than her home ? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
At home Bholi was neglected and mocked for her stammer and pock-marked face. At school she found a large room with beautiful, colourful pictures of horses, goats, a parrot and a cow, and many girls like her. Above all, the teacher spoke to her softly and kindly, encouraged her and promised she would learn and stop stammering. This affection and hope made her feel that school was better than her home.
