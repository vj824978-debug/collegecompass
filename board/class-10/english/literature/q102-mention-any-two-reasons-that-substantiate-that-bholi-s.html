Question as text
Mention any two reasons that substantiate that Bholi’s mother was a negative presence in her life. (Bholi)
Answer
Answer as text
(1) She neglected Bholi, felt no love for her and was mainly worried about marrying her off; she agreed to send her to school only to get rid of her. (2) She was greedy and selfish – she was ready to marry Bholi to the old, lame Bishamber and gave in to his demand for dowry without considering Bholi’s feelings.
