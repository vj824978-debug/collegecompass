How does modern India recognize the tradition of courage and bravery in Coorgi culture?

Question as text
How does modern India recognize the tradition of courage and bravery in Coorgi culture? (Glimpses of India) [3]
Answer
Answer as text
The Coorgis are known for their martial traditions and hospitality. The Coorg Regiment is one of the most decorated in the Indian Army, and the first Chief of the Indian Army, General Cariappa, was a Coorgi. Coorgis are the only people in India permitted to carry firearms without a licence — a recognition of their courage and bravery.
