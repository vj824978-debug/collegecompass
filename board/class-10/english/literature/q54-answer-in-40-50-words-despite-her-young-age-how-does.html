Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] Despite her young age, how does Valli manage to gather the subtle details about the bus journey ? (Madam Rides the Bus)
Answer
Answer as text
Valli listened carefully to the conversations of neighbours and people who regularly used the bus, and asked a few discreet questions. In this way she learnt that the town was six miles away, that the fare was thirty paise one way and that the trip took forty-five minutes. She then planned her ride and saved her coins for the fare.
