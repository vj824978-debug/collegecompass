Illustrate Lencho’s unwavering faith in the Divine.

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q8(i)A Letter to God3 marks
Question 90 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Illustrate Lencho’s unwavering faith in the Divine. (A Letter to God) [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Even after the hailstorm destroyed his entire crop, Lencho’s one hope was help from God, “whose eyes see everything, even what is deep in one’s conscience”. He wrote a letter to God asking for a hundred pesos. When he received only seventy, he did not doubt God at all; he believed God had sent the full amount and the post office employees had taken the rest, calling them “a bunch of crooks”. This shows his unshakeable faith.
