Why does Horace Danby consider himself an honest man despite being a thief?

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q9(ii)A Question of Trust3 marks
Question 113 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Why does Horace Danby consider himself an honest man despite being a thief? How does this selfperception affect his decision in the story? (A Question of Trust)
Answer
Answer as text
Horace was a respectable locksmith who robbed only one safe a year, carefully planned, to buy rare and costly books, and otherwise lived honestly; so he thought of himself as good and honest. Believing others were like him, he trusted the young woman who posed as the lady of the house and opened the safe for her without gloves. She turned out to be a thief; Horace was caught because of his fingerprints — his misplaced trust led to his downfall.
