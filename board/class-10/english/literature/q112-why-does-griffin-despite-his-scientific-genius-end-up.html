Why does Griffin, despite his scientific genius, end up leading a destructive life?

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q9(i)Footprints Without Feet3 marks
Question 112 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Why does Griffin, despite his scientific genius, end up leading a destructive life? (Footprints Without Feet)
Answer
Answer as text
Griffin was a brilliant scientist who discovered how to make himself invisible, but he was a lawless, selfish and bad-tempered man. Instead of using his discovery for good, he used invisibility to escape and to steal — he set fire to his landlord’s house, robbed a shop and a clergyman’s house, and attacked people. Without moral values his genius led only to crime, loneliness and destruction.
