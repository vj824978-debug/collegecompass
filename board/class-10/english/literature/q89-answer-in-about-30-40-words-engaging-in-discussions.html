Question as text
Answer in about 30-40 words: Engaging in discussions and disputes rarely leads to problem resolution. Support the statement with evidence from the text. (The Proposal)
Answer
Answer as text
Lomov came to propose to Natalya but, instead of proposing, quarrelled with her over the ownership of the Oxen Meadows and later over whose dog – Guess or Squeezer – was better. Chubukov joined the dispute, insults flew, Lomov had palpitations and fainted, and the proposal was nearly lost. Even after the engagement they went on quarrelling; the disputes solved nothing and only created chaos.
