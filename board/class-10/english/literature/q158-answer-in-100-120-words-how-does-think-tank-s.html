Answer in 100 – 120 words : [6] How does Think-Tank’s arrogance and ignorance contribute to the theme of wit over power in ‘The Book that Saved the Earth’ ?
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Think-Tank, the Martian ruler, was arrogant and conceited — he considered himself the most intelligent being in the universe and insulted his subordinates.  He was also ignorant: he mistook the books found on Earth for sandwiches.  Planning to invade Earth, he took a book of Mother Goose nursery rhymes as a secret code.  He misread the rhymes literally — ‘Hey Diddle Diddle’ made him think Earthlings had trained animals to fly to the moon; ‘Humpty Dumpty’ looked like a threat to his own egg-shaped head.  Out of fear he called off the invasion and fled, so a simple children’s book ‘saved the Earth’.  Noodle, his humble assistant, showed common sense and real wit, in contrast to Think-Tank’s empty pride.  Theme: power without wisdom collapses; arrogance and ignorance are defeated by wit, knowledge and humility.
