Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] How does Matilda face ‘the horrible life of necessity’ ? (The Necklace)
Answer
Answer as text
Matilda took her part heroically. They dismissed their servant and moved to a small attic under the roof. She did all the heavy housework herself — washing dishes, linen and clothes, carrying water up the stairs — and bargained at the grocer’s and butcher’s to save every sou. After ten years of such toil the debt was paid, but she had grown old and coarse.
