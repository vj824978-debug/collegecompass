What vision did Ebright have in raising a flock of butterflies ?

Question 111 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
What vision did Ebright have in raising a flock of butterflies ? (The Making of a Scientist)
Answer
Answer as text
After his mother gave him the book ‘The Travels of Monarch X’, Ebright began raising butterflies to tag them for Dr Frederick Urquhart’s study of monarch migration. Raising them from eggs became a stepping stone to genuine research: he went on to study them for science fair projects, including the viceroy mimicry experiment and the gold spots on the monarch pupa, which led to his later discoveries.
