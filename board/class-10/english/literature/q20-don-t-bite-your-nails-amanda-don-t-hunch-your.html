Don’t bite your nails, Amanda! Don’t hunch your shoulders, Amanda! Stop that slouching and sit up straight,…

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q7(b)Amanda!5 marks
Question 20 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow: Don’t bite your nails, Amanda! Don’t hunch your shoulders, Amanda! Stop that slouching and sit up straight, Amanda! (“There is a languid, emerald sea, where the sole inhabitant is me a mermaid, drifting blissfully.”) (Amanda) (i) What image does the above extract create about Amanda’s mother? [1] (ii) Why is the second verse written in brackets? [1] (iii) Amanda calls herself a mermaid because [1] (A) Mermaid is Amanda’s favourite fantasy character. (B) Amanda loves watching a mermaid drifting in water. (C) Amanda longs to be free like a mermaid which sails effortlessly in the sea. (D) Amanda wants to sail with a mermaid in the deep-sea water. (iv) Amanda wants to be the sole inhabitant in the sea. Elucidate. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) She appears to be a strict, nagging and over-critical mother who constantly instructs and corrects Amanda. (ii) The bracketed verse shows Amanda’s inner thoughts/daydreams — she is not listening to the nagging but escaping into her imaginary world. (iii) (C) Amanda longs to be free like a mermaid which sails effortlessly in the sea. (iv) Amanda is tired of constant nagging and restrictions. She wants solitude and freedom, a world where no one can order or correct her; being the only inhabitant of a calm, languid sea, drifting blissfully like a mermaid, symbolises her wish to be free, peaceful and alone.
