Question as text
Answer the following question in 40–50 words. The transition from the apartheid system in South Africa to a new era of equality reflects broader historical trends in the struggle for human rights and social justice. Elaborate. (Nelson Mandela – Long Walk to Freedom)
Answer
Answer as text
Under apartheid, black people were denied basic rights and freedom by a white minority. The long, sacrificial struggle of Mandela and thousands of others ended in 1994 with the first democratic, non-racial government. Like freedom struggles across the world, it shows that oppression cannot last forever and that courage and perseverance win equality, dignity and justice for all.
