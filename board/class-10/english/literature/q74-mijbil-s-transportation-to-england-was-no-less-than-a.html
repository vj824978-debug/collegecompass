Mijbil’s transportation to England was no less than a nightmare for the author.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q8(v)Mijbil the Otter3 marks
Question 74 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Mijbil’s transportation to England was no less than a nightmare for the author. Justify the statement using incidents from the lesson.
Answer
Answer as text
The airline did not allow pets, so Maxwell had to pack Mij in a box. Mij tore the lining and escaped, his face and paws bleeding. Mij was then let out in the aeroplane and ran wild under the seats; a woman passenger screamed, and Maxwell had to crawl to catch him, ending up covered in fish and water. The journey was full of tension and embarrassment.
