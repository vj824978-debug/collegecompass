Question 95 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
“Not from weeping or from grieving will anyone obtain peace of mind; on the contrary his pain will be greater and his body will suffer.” Validate this preaching of Buddha with reference to the story ‘A Sermon at Benaras’.
Answer
Answer as text
Kisa Gotami, grieving for her dead son, went from house to house seeking medicine to revive him, but her grief only increased her suffering. When the Buddha asked her for mustard seed from a house where no one had died, she realised death is common to all. Giving up selfish grief, she found peace – proving that lamenting brings only more pain, while acceptance brings peace of mind.
