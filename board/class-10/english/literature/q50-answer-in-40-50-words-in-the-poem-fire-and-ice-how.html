Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] In the poem ‘Fire and Ice’, how does the intense human emotion symbolised by fire, lead to the destruction of the world ?
Answer
Answer as text
Fire symbolises desire — greed, lust, avarice and uncontrolled passion. When desire goes unchecked, people want more and more; this leads to conflict, exploitation, violence and wars that can consume the world just as fire does. The poet, having ‘tasted of desire’, holds with those who believe the world will end in fire.
