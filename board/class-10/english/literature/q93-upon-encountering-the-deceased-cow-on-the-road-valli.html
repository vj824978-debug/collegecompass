Question as text
Upon encountering the deceased cow on the road, Valli was emotional. Comment with reference to ‘Madam Rides the Bus’. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
On the way out Valli had laughed at a young cow running in front of the bus. On the return journey she saw the same cow lying dead by the roadside, run over by a vehicle. She was deeply shaken; her joy vanished and the image haunted her. It showed her sensitivity and was her first encounter with death — she realised how fragile and uncertain life is. She did not want to look out of the window again.
