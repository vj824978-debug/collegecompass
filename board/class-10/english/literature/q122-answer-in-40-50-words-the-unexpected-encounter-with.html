Answer in 40–50 words : The unexpected encounter with the voice in the doorway affects Horace’s burglary attempt. Elaborate. (A Question of Trust)
Answer
Answer as text
As Horace was opening the safe, a young woman’s voice from the doorway startled him. She pretended to be the lady of the house and claimed she would call the police but did not. Disarmed by her charm, Horace believed her and, at her request, opened the safe and handed over the jewels without his gloves on. Thus the encounter turned his “perfect” burglary into his own downfall — his fingerprints led to his arrest.
