Question as text
Answer the following question in 100–120 words. Anil’s acceptance and forgiveness of the thief demonstrates empathy and generosity. Elaborate. (The Thief’s Story)
Answer
Answer as text
V alue points: (1) Anil, a struggling writer, trusts Hari Singh (the thief) and gives him work and food though he cannot pay him. (2) He teaches him to write his name and promises to teach him to write whole sentences and to add figures — he wants to help him improve. (3) When Hari steals his money (600 rupees) and then returns and replaces it, Anil realises the theft — the notes are still wet from the rain. (4) Yet he does not scold, shame or punish him; instead he hands him a fifty-rupee note and says they will start writing sentences the next day. (5) This silent forgiveness shows his empathy, large-heartedness and faith in human goodness. (6) His kindness transforms the thief, who decides to give up stealing and become an educated, honest man.
