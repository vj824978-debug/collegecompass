CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q7(a)For Anne Gregory5 marks
Question 11 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the following extract and answer the questions that follow. “I heard an old religious man But yesternight declare That he had found a text to prove That only God, my dear, Could love you for yourself alone And not your yellow hair.” (For Anne Gregory) (i) Complete the sentence appropriately: [1] The speaker addresses the person he is speaking to as “my dear” because . (ii) The poet mentions “yellow hair”. What is its significance in the context of the statement? Answer in about 40 words. [2] (iii) Fill in the blank with the correct word from the brackets. [1] The poet suggests that God’s love in comparison to that of a human being is (selfish/selfless). (iv) As per the above extract, what attributes does God not love a person for? Select the correct option. [1] 1. Wealth 2. Physical appearance 3. Intelligence 4. Inner qualities 5. Youth (A) 1 and 4 (B) 3, 4, and 5 (C) 2 and 4 (D) Only 4
Answer
Answer as text
(i) . . . he is fond of the young girl Anne (he is an older, affectionate well-wisher speaking to her lovingly). (ii) ‘Yellow hair’ stands for outward physical beauty. Young men are attracted to Anne only for her beautiful hair; the speaker says only God can love her for her true self (inner qualities), not for her external appearance. It shows that human love is superficial. (iii) selfless (iv) Only ‘physical appearance’ (2) is named in the extract as something God does not love a person for (“not your yellow hair”). Option (C) 2 and 4 is the only option containing it and is the key usually given. (Note: the item is flawed, since God does love a person for inner qualities.)
