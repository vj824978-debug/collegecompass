Answer in 40–50 words : How do Anil’s initial reaction to Hari Singh’s cooking and his subsequent actions reflect Anil’s evolving relationship with Hari Singh ? (The Thief’s Story)
Answer
Answer as text
The first meal Hari cooked was so terrible that Anil gave it to a stray dog and told him to go, but Hari’s flattering smile made him stay. Anil then taught him to cook, to write his name and promised to teach him to write whole sentences and add figures. He also trusted him with the daily shopping and money. This shows Anil’s growing trust, kindness and readiness to mentor Hari.
