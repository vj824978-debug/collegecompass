Question as text
Answer the following question in 40–50 words. What role does each of Belinda’s pets play in the narrative? Select any two of her pets. (The Tale of Custard the Dragon)
Answer
Answer as text
Belinda had a kitten Ink, a mouse Blink, a little yellow dog Mustard and a dragon Custard. Ink and Blink (and Mustard) boast of being brave and tease Custard as a coward; but when the pirate comes, Ink and Blink hide and Mustard runs away, while Custard fights and gobbles up the pirate. The pets thus create humour and the contrast between boastful and true bravery. (any two pets with roles)
