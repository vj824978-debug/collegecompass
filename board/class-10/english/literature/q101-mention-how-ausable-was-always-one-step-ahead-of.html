Question 101 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Mention how Ausable was always one step ahead of everyone. (The Midnight Visitor)
Answer
Answer as text
Ausable stays calm when Max threatens him with a pistol. Using his presence of mind, he invents a story about a balcony below the window and pretends that the knocking at the door is the police. Max, panicked, jumps out of the window and falls, while Ausable keeps the report safe. His wit and quick thinking keep him ahead.
