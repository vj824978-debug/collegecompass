Question as text
In ‘Mijbil the Otter’, how does the narrator’s patience contrast with the reactions of fellow passengers and local people? What does this reveal about human understanding of the unfamiliar?
Answer
Answer as text
Maxwell patiently cared for Mij — he cleaned up after Mij tore open the box on the plane, kept him calm and close, and later learnt his habits. The passengers, however, panicked (a woman shrieked that it was a rat), and Londoners made wild guesses about what animal it was (a baby seal, squirrel, walrus, hippo, beaver, bear cub, etc.). This shows that people often react to the unfamiliar with fear and ignorance, while patience brings understanding.
