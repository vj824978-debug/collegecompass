Question as text
What was Chubukov’s reaction when he heard about Lomov’s proposal for his daughter’s hand? (The Proposal)
Answer
Answer as text
At first Chubukov suspected Lomov had come to borrow money. On hearing of the proposal he was overjoyed: he embraced and kissed Lomov, said he had long wished for it and was very happy, and at once went to call Natalya. His excessive joy shows he was eager to marry off his daughter to a rich neighbour.
