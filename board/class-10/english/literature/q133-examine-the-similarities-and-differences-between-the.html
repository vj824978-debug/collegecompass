Question as text
Examine the similarities and differences between the tiger in “A Tiger in the Zoo” and the dragon in “The Tale of Custard the Dragon”.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points: (1) Similarities: both are powerful creatures shown in captivity/confinement – the tiger in a concrete cage, Custard in a little wagon/cage; both are not allowed to show their true strength. (2) Both have a hidden fierceness: the tiger’s latent anger and power; Custard has sharp teeth, spikes and claws and finally fights. (3) Differences: the tiger longs for freedom and the jungle; he is angry and helpless, ignoring visitors and staring at the stars. (4) Custard seems content in the house; he is called a coward and teased but shows great courage when the pirate attacks. (5) Tone: Leslie Norris’s poem is serious, criticising captivity of wild animals; Ogden Nash’s poem is humorous, a mock-heroic ballad. (6) Message: one pleads for animals’ natural freedom; the other shows that real courage is shown in action, not boasting.
