Question as text
Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] Mention the details that Anne provides about her family in her diary. (From the Diary of Anne Frank)
Answer
Answer as text
Anne writes that her father, the most adorable father she had ever seen, married her mother when he was thirty-six and she twenty-five. Her sister Margot was born in Frankfurt in 1926 and Anne on 12 June 1929. As Jews, the family emigrated to Holland in 1933, where her father became Managing Director of the Dutch Opekta Company. She also mentions her grandmother, who died in January 1942 and whom she still missed.
