The journeys of Anne Frank and Valli teach us the vital lesson that optimism and self-assurance empower women…

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q10(b)From the Diary of Anne Frank; Madam Rides the Bus6 marks
Question 154 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
The journeys of Anne Frank and Valli teach us the vital lesson that optimism and self-assurance empower women to rise above circumstances and overcome obstacles. Examine the similarities.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Both are young girls with curiosity, confidence and independent thinking.  Anne, lonely despite family and friends, makes her diary (“Kitty”) her true friend – she writes candidly and optimistically about her life.  Anne faces her teacher Mr Keesing’s punishment with wit – writes humorous essays (“Quack, Quack, Quack”) and wins him over; turns a problem into success.  Valli, an eight-year-old village girl, longs to ride the bus to town; she plans carefully, saves sixty paise secretly and learns everything about the route and fare.  Valli travels alone with self-assurance, refuses to be treated as a child, pays her own fare and stands up to the teasing conductor and elderly woman.  Both learn and mature from experience (Valli’s encounter with the dead cow brings awareness of death).  Conclusion: their optimism, determination and self-belief help them overcome limits – an inspiring lesson for girls.
