Question 117 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Discuss the character development of Griffin, as he grapples with the repercussions of his actions and attempts to navigate a world, where his once extraordinary abilities have become a burden.
Answer
Answer as text
Griffin, a brilliant but lawless scientist, became invisible and first enjoyed his power. Soon invisibility became a burden: he was homeless, cold and hungry, had to wrap himself in bandages and could not go back to being visible. Instead of using his gift well he turned to theft and violence, becoming more desperate and isolated — a warning about misused science.
