Answer in about 30-40 words: In the poem “Dust of Snow”, the ‘hemlock tree’ and the ‘crow’ hold specific meanings. Elaborate on how these symbols are utilized within the context of the poem.
Answer
Answer as text
Traditionally the crow is a symbol of ill omen and gloom, and the hemlock is a poisonous tree associated with sorrow and death. The poet, already in a sad mood, uses these negative symbols to show that even ordinary or unpleasant things can bring joy: when the crow shook dust of snow from the hemlock on him, his mood changed and he saved part of a day he had rued.
