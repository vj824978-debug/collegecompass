Question as text
Answer the following question in 100–120 words : Anil’s love, trust and compassion influenced Hari Singh’s inner conflict and played a crucial role in transforming him. Elucidate. [6]
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Hari Singh, a fifteen-year-old thief, befriends Anil, a struggling writer, intending to rob him.  Anil gives him work, food and shelter, and teaches him to write his name, add numbers and write full sentences — treating him with trust and kindness.  Hari steals Anil’s money (six hundred rupees) and goes to the station, but cannot board the train; he is torn by guilt.  He realises that Anil trusted him completely and that education (which Anil was giving him) could make him a “big man”, far more valuable than stolen money.  He returns and puts the damp notes back under the mattress.  Anil knows the truth (the notes are still wet) but says nothing; he forgives silently and gives Hari a fifty-rupee note, promising regular payment and continued lessons.  Conclusion: Anil’s love, trust and compassion awaken Hari’s conscience and transform him from a thief into an honest person.
