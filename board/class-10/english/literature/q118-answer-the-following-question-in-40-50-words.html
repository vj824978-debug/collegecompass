Answer the following question in 40–50 words. How did Ausable get rid of Max? (The Midnight Visitor)
Answer
Answer as text
When Max held him at gunpoint for the report, Ausable calmly invented a story that a balcony below his window was used by intruders and complained that the management had not blocked it. When a knock came (it was the waiter with drinks), Ausable pretended it was the police. Panicked, Max jumped out of the window towards the non-existent balcony and fell to the ground, so Ausable was rid of him with his presence of mind.
