Mme Loisel was a great success.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q6(b)The Necklace5 marks
Question 30 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow : Mme Loisel was a great success. She was the prettiest of all – elegant, gracious, smiling, and full of joy. All the men noticed her, asked her name and wanted to be presented. She danced with enthusiasm, intoxicated with pleasure, thinking of nothing but all this admiration, this victory so complete and sweet to her heart. (The Necklace) (i) How is “success” perceived by Matilda ? [1] (ii) Fill in the blank with the correct word from the bracket. [2] ‘All the men noticed her, asked her name and wanted to be presented’ indicates that the men wanted to be formally (introduced / withholden) to her. (iii) Which of the following best describe Matilda’s state of mind in the context ? [1] (A) overwhelmed (B) anxious (C) confused (D) sad (iv) Explain the phrase “this victory so complete and sweet to her heart”. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) For Matilda, success means being admired and noticed – being the prettiest and most elegant woman at the ball, the centre of everyone’s attention. (ii) introduced. (iii) (A) overwhelmed (with joy and pleasure). (iv) It refers to the complete triumph of her beauty and charm at the ball; her long-cherished desire to be admired and envied was fulfilled, which gave her deep satisfaction.
