Self-doubt and fear often act as barriers to success.

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q10(a)His First Flight; Madam Rides the Bus6 marks
Question 151 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Self-doubt and fear often act as barriers to success. On the other hand, curiosity leads to learning new things. Justify the statement citing instances from ‘His First Flight’ and ‘Madam Rides the Bus’. [6]
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  His First Flight : the young seagull was afraid of flying; the great expanse of sea frightened him and he felt his wings would never support him.  Because of his fear and self-doubt he remained alone on the ledge while his brothers and sister flew; he was taunted, called a coward and left hungry.  Only when hunger drove him to dive at the fish his mother held did he overcome fear; then he flew, floated and joined his family — fear had been the only barrier.  Madam Rides the Bus : Valli, an eight-year-old, was intensely curious about the bus and the world beyond her street.  She listened to conversations, planned carefully, saved sixty paise and took the bus to the town alone and returned safely.  Her curiosity taught her about the countryside, the town, and even about life and death (the dead cow) — she learnt new things through her own effort.  Conclusion: fear holds one back, while curiosity backed by courage and planning leads to learning and success.
