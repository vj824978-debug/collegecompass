Answer in about 100-120 words: Analyse the similarities and differences between the young seagull from “Two stories about flying” and Lencho from “A Letter to God”, and provide a rationale for the significant role faith plays in challenging and adverse situations in life.
Answer
Answer as text
 Similarities: both face adversity – the seagull is left alone, hungry and afraid on the ledge; Lencho’s crop is destroyed by a hailstorm; both are helped out of their crisis by others (the mother seagull, the postmaster and post-office employees).  Differences: the young seagull lacked faith in himself and his wings; fear held him back until hunger and his mother’s trick with the fish made him dive and fly.  Lencho, in contrast, had unshakable faith in God – he wrote to God for a hundred pesos and was not surprised when money came.  But Lencho lacked faith in people: he called the post-office employees “a bunch of crooks”, not knowing they had helped him.  Role of faith: faith in oneself (the seagull) gives courage to overcome fear; faith in God (Lencho) gives hope and strength to bear loss.  Conclusion: faith keeps hope alive in adverse situations and helps people act and survive, but it should be balanced with trust in oneself and in others.
