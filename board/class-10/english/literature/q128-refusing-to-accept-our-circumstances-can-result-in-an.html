Refusing to accept our circumstances can result in an unhappy life.

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q9(ii)The Necklace3 marks
Question 128 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Refusing to accept our circumstances can result in an unhappy life. Discuss in brief with reference to the story ‘The Necklace’. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Matilda Loisel was born in a family of clerks and married a clerk, but she was always unhappy and dreamt of luxury she could not have. To look rich at a ball she borrowed a diamond necklace and lost it. To replace it the Loisels borrowed 36,000 francs and spent ten years in hard labour and poverty — only to learn the necklace was fake. Her refusal to accept her circumstances ruined her life.
