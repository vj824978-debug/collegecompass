Answer in 100 – 120 words : [6] How do Lencho in ‘A Letter to God’ and Kisa Gotami in ‘The Sermon at Benares’ respond to their experiences of suffering and loss ? How did they overcome their respective losses ?
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Lencho’s loss: a hailstorm destroyed his ripe corn crop; his family faced hunger.  His response: despair, but unshaken faith in God; he wrote a letter to God asking for a hundred pesos.  The postmaster, moved by his faith, collected seventy pesos and sent it; Lencho, sure God could not err, blamed the post-office employees as ‘crooks’ — his faith (though naive) carried him through.  Kisa Gotami’s loss: her only son died; mad with grief, she went from house to house seeking medicine to revive him.  The Buddha asked for mustard seed from a house where no one had lost a child, husband, parent or friend; she found none.  She realised that death is common to all and that she had been selfish in her grief; she accepted mortality and overcame grief through wisdom.  Comparison: Lencho overcame his loss through faith (and others’ compassion); Kisa Gotami through understanding the truth of life — both show the human ability to cope with suffering.
