Question as text
The story, ‘Mijbil the Otter’, has it all – emotions, humour, certain surprising incidents and the freshness of a new and unique type of story. Explain how Maxwell has highlighted these in his story.
Answer
Answer as text
Emotions: Maxwell’s deep bond with Mij and his distress when Mij escaped from the box on the plane. Humour: Mij playing with ping-pong balls, juggling marbles, the air hostess’s cooperation and passengers’ reactions, Londoners guessing Mij to be a baby seal, a beaver or even a “hippo”. Surprises: Mij tearing the box lining, getting loose on the flight, Mij learning to open the bath tap. Keeping an otter as a pet made it fresh and unique.
