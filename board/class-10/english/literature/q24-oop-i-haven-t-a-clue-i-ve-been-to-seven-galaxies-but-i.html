OOP: I haven’t a clue. I’ve been to seven galaxies, but I’ve never seen anything like this. Maybe they’re…

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q6(B)The Book that Saved the Earth5 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions: OOP: I haven’t a clue. I’ve been to seven galaxies, but I’ve never seen anything like this. Maybe they’re hats. (He opens a book and puts it on his head.) Say, maybe this is a haberdashery! OMEGA: (bowing low) Perhaps the Great and Mighty Think-Tank will give us the benefit of his thought on the matter. THINK-TANK: Elementary, my dear Omega. Hold one of the items up so that I may view it closely. (Omega holds a book on the palm of her hand.) Yes, yes, I understand now. Since Earth creatures are always eating, the place in which you find yourselves is undoubtedly a crude refreshment stand. OMEGA: (to Iota and Oop) He says we’re in a refreshment stand. OOP: Well, the Earthlings certainly have a strange diet. (The Book that Saved the Earth) I. “Elementary, my dear Omega.” What is Think-Tank’s tone in the sentence? [1] (A) patronising (B) admonishing (C) ironic (D) sarcastic II. Why does Omega call Think-Tank great and mighty? [2] III. Fill in the blank with the correct word from the bracket: After viewing the book closely, Think-Tank concludes that Earth creatures (enjoy/dislike) eating food. [1] IV. Why does Oop open the book and put it on his head? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
I. (A) patronising. II. Think-Tank is the vain, self-important ruler of Mars who insists on being addressed with grand titles. Omega, his subordinate, flatters him out of fear and respect, as she must humour his vanity. III. enjoy (he believes Earth creatures are always eating). IV. The Martians had never seen books; Oop has no idea what they are and guesses they might be hats, so he puts one on his head.
