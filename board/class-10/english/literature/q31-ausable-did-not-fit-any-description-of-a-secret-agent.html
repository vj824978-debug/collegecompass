Ausable did not fit any description of a secret agent Fowler had ever read.

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q6AThe Midnight Visitor5 marks
Question 31 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow: Ausable did not fit any description of a secret agent Fowler had ever read. Following him down the musty corridor of the gloomy French hotel where Ausable had a room, Fowler felt let down. It was a small room, on the sixth and top floor and scarcely a setting for a romantic adventure. Ausable was, for one thing, fat, very fat. And then there was his accent. Though he spoke French and German passably, he had never altogether lost the American accent he had brought to Paris from Boston twenty years ago. “You are disappointed,” Ausable said wheezily over his shoulder. (The Midnight Visitor) (i) Which of the following describes how Fowler felt on seeing Ausable for the first time? [1] (A) excited and happy (B) indifferent and disgusted (C) surprised and disappointed (D) envious and in awe (ii) How did the hotel’s atmosphere influence Fowler’s mood? [2] (iii) Fill in the blank with the correct word from the bracket: [1] Ausable’s reaction on seeing how Fowler felt shows that he was . (observant/ embarrassed) (iv) In what way does Ausable defy the typical spy image? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) (C) surprised and disappointed. (ii) The musty corridor, the gloomy French hotel and the small room on the sixth floor were dull and ordinary, “scarcely a setting for a romantic adventure”. Fowler, who expected glamour and excitement, felt let down and his disappointment deepened. (iii) observant. (iv) Unlike the smart, dashing spy of fiction, Ausable was very fat, wheezy, spoke with an American accent and lived in a small, shabby room in a gloomy hotel.
