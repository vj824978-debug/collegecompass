Question as text
Answer the following question in 100–120 words : Both, Rajvir in ‘Tea from Assam’ and Valli in ‘Madam Rides the Bus’ are motivated by a strong desire to experience the world beyond their everyday life. How do their respective journeys reflect their curiosity, enthusiasm for discovering new things and shape their understanding of the world around them ? [6]
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Rajvir travels by train with his friend Pranjol to Pranjol’s home in Assam; he is excited to see the tea gardens and gazes out of the window at the greenery.  He has prepared by reading about tea: he tells Pranjol the legends of its discovery (the Chinese emperor and the Indian legend of Bodhidharma) and about tea’s history — showing curiosity and eagerness to learn.  At the tea estate he watches the plucking season and the tea-pluckers, turning bookish knowledge into first-hand understanding.  Valli, an eight-year-old village girl, longs to ride the bus to town; she plans carefully, saves her coins (sixty paise for the round trip) and gathers information about the route and fare.  On the journey she is thrilled by the sights, behaves confidently with the conductor and the passengers and refuses to be treated as a child.  On the way back she sees a dead cow that she had admired earlier and is shaken — she encounters the reality of death and loses interest in the view.  Conclusion: both journeys satisfy their curiosity; Rajvir’s deepens his knowledge, while Valli’s teaches her independence and a sobering truth about life.
