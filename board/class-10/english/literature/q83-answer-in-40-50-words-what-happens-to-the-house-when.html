Answer in 40–50 words : What happens to the house when the trees move out of it ? Comment. (The Trees)
Answer
Answer as text
As the trees move out, the house is left empty and quiet. The roots work loose from the cracks of the veranda floor, the leaves strain toward the glass, and the small twigs stiff with exertion; the long-cramped boughs shuffle under the roof. The whole night the poet hears the sounds of the trees escaping, the glass breaking. By morning the trees have gone out into the empty forest, and the house is left bare and silent. It shows nature reclaiming its freedom from human confinement.
