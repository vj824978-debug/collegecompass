Examine the role of Nature in the poems ‘The Dust of Snow’ and ‘Fog’.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q10(a)Dust of Snow; Fog6 marks
Question 153 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Examine the role of Nature in the poems ‘The Dust of Snow’ and ‘Fog’.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  In both poems nature is not merely background; it actively affects or behaves like a living being.  Dust of Snow (Robert Frost): a crow shakes down dust of snow from a hemlock tree onto the poet.  Crow and hemlock, usually symbols of gloom and death, here bring about a positive change – nature as healer.  The small incident changes the poet’s mood; he saves part of a day he had “rued”.  Fog (Carl Sandburg): the fog is personified as a cat – comes silently “on little cat feet”, sits looking over harbour and city “on silent haunches” and moves on.  Nature shown as quiet, mysterious and transient, governed by its own rhythm.  Conclusion: both poets show that nature, even in small or ordinary moments, influences human feelings and deserves observation.
