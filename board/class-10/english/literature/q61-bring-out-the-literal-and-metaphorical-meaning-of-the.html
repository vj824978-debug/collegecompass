Question as text
Bring out the literal and metaphorical meaning of the word “plunge” with reference to the young seagull who failed to muster up the courage to the plunge. (Stories About Flying)
Answer
Answer as text
Literally, ‘plunge’ means to dive or jump down from the ledge into the air (over the sea) and fly. Metaphorically, it means taking a bold, risky first step into the unknown and overcoming one’s fear. The young seagull was afraid that his wings would not support him; only when hunger drove him to dive at the fish his mother held did he take the plunge and discover he could fly.
