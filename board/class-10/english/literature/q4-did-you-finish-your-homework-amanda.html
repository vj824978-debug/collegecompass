Did you finish your homework, Amanda?

CBSE 2026QP 2/2/1Q7(b)Amanda!5 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the following extract and answer the questions that follow : [5] Did you finish your homework, Amanda? Did you tidy your room, Amanda? I thought I told you to clean your shoes, Amanda! (I am an orphan, roaming the street. I pattern soft dust with my hushed, bare feet. The silence is golden, the freedom is sweet.) (Amanda!) (i) Complete the sentence appropriately : [1] Amanda wants to be an orphan because . (ii) What does the image of Amanda “roaming barefoot in the dusty street” signify ? [2] (iii) Amanda’s tone while imagining herself as an orphan is : [1] (A) excited (B) fearful (C) angry (D) peaceful (iv) What does the speaker do on the dust ? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) . . . she wants freedom from the constant nagging and instructions of her parent; an orphan, she imagines, has no one to order her about. (ii) It signifies freedom, carefree living and peace — a life without rules, restrictions and nagging, where she can do as she pleases and enjoy silence and solitude. (iii) (D) peaceful. (iv) She makes patterns on the soft dust with her hushed, bare feet.
