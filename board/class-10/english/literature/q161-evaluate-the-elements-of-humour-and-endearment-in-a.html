Evaluate the elements of humour and endearment in “A Triumph of Surgery”.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q11(a)A Triumph of Surgery6 marks
Question 161 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Evaluate the elements of humour and endearment in “A Triumph of Surgery”.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Tricki, Mrs Pumphrey’s pampered Pekinese, is grossly overfed on cream cakes, chocolates, malt and cod-liver oil – the over-indulgence itself is comic.  Humour in the narrator Herriot’s tone and comic descriptions of the fat, lethargic, wheezing dog; and the ‘operation’ is merely strict diet and exercise.  Herriot takes Tricki to his surgery and keeps him with the other dogs; Tricki recovers by eating and playing with them.  Comic irony: Mrs Pumphrey sends eggs, wine and brandy ‘to build up’ Tricki, which the vet and his partners enjoy themselves.  Endearment: Mrs Pumphrey’s genuine love and fussing over Tricki; Herriot’s real fondness for the dog.  The reunion: Tricki leaps into her arms and she tearfully calls it ‘a triumph of surgery’ – humorous because there was no surgery at all.  Message: excessive love and pampering can harm; proper care, discipline and exercise are true affection.
