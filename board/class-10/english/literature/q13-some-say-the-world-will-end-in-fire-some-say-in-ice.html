Some say the world will end in fire Some say in ice.

CBSE 2025QP 2/2/1Q7(a)Fire and Ice5 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the following extract and answer the questions that follow: Some say the world will end in fire Some say in ice. From what I’ve tasted of desire I hold with those who favour fire. (Fire and Ice) (i) The speaker offers evidence to support his preference for one theory over the other. Mention the evidence. [1] (ii) What is the prediction of the poet about how the world will come to an end? Answer in about 40 words. [2] (iii) “Fire” and “Ice” are used in the poem as . [1] (A) similes (B) metaphors (C) repetition (D) oxymorons (iv) Comment on the overall mood of the extract. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) His own experience of desire — “From what I’ve tasted of desire”. (ii) The poet agrees with those who believe the world will end in fire. Fire stands for desire — greed, lust, avarice — which, once tasted, keeps growing and consumes people; such unchecked desire, he predicts, will ultimately destroy the world. (iii) (B) metaphors (iv) Serious, reflective/contemplative and somewhat grim, as the poet thoughtfully weighs how the world will end.
