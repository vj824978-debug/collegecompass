Question 141 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
How does Lencho’s faith in God, as shown in ‘A Letter to God’ contrast with the skepticism of Kisa Gotami in ‘Sermon at Benares’. Support your answer with details from the two texts.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Lencho: after the hailstorm destroyed his crop, he had complete, unquestioning faith that God would help; he wrote to God asking for a hundred pesos.  The postmaster and employees collected seventy pesos; Lencho, sure God could not err, accused the post office employees of stealing the rest — blind faith in God, distrust of humans.  Kisa Gotami: overwhelmed by the death of her only son, she could not accept it and went from house to house seeking medicine — doubting and resisting the reality of death.  The Buddha asked her for mustard seed from a house where no one had died; she found none.  She realised death is common to all and that grief must give way to understanding; her doubt turned into wisdom and acceptance.  Contrast: Lencho’s faith is innocent and unchanging; Kisa moves from denial and scepticism to enlightenment through experience.
