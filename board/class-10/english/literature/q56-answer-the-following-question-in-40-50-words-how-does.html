Answer the following question in 40–50 words : How does the line ‘people will take balls, balls will be lost always’, justify that material losses are necessary for emotional growth and to understand the harsh realities of life ? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
The line shows that loss is inevitable and universal. The poet does not buy the boy another ball, as money is external and cannot buy back what is lost. By bearing the loss himself, the boy learns the “epistemology of loss” — that possessions and moments pass and must be let go. This experience of grief teaches him to stand up and accept loss, helping him grow emotionally and understand life’s harsh realities.
