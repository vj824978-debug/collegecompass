Question as text
Answer the following question in 40–50 words : Ausable displayed a remarkable presence of mind combined with a good sense of humour to handle Max. Justify. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
When Max held him at gunpoint for the missile papers, Ausable stayed calm. He invented a story about a balcony below the window and complained casually that it was a nuisance through which someone had entered. When a knock came, he said it was the police he had asked to guard him. Panicked, Max ran to the window to escape onto the non-existent balcony and fell; the visitor was only the waiter with the drink Ausable had ordered. His cool wit outwitted Max without any fight.
