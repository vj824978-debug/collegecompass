Question as text
Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] Why does Hari Singh find it pleasant to work for Anil, though he was a thief ? (The Thief’s Story)
Answer
Answer as text
Anil was kind, simple and trusting. He gave Hari food and shelter, did not mind the bad food he cooked, and let him keep a small profit (about a rupee a day) on the shopping. He even taught him to write his name and promised to teach him to write whole sentences and add figures. Anil’s easy-going trust made the work pleasant.
