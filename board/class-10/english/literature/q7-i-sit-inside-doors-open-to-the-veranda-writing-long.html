CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q7(a)The Trees5 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow : I sit inside, doors open to the veranda writing long letters in which I scarcely mention the departure of the forest from the house. The night is fresh, the whole moon shines in a sky still open the smell of leaves and lichen still reaches like a voice into the rooms. (Trees) (i) Comment on the portrayal of the narrator in the given extract. [2] (ii) Complete the following suitably : [1] The trees’ movement from the house to the forest, reflect the broader theme of . (iii) What has the poet hardly mentioned in her letter ? [1] (iv) What kind of atmosphere is created through the given extract ? Select the correct option(s) : [1] 1. nostalgia 2. serenity 3. detachment 4. loss 5. joy (A) 1 and 4 (B) Only 2 (C) 1, 2 and 3 (D) 2 and 5
Answer
Answer as text
(i) The narrator sits calmly inside writing long letters, scarcely mentioning the departure of the forest; she appears detached or unconcerned, preoccupied with her own world, yet sensitive to the fresh night, the moonlight and the smell of leaves reaching into the rooms. (ii) . . . nature breaking free from human confinement and reclaiming its rightful place (the conflict between man and nature). (iii) The departure of the forest (the trees) from the house. (iv) (C) 1, 2 and 3 – nostalgia, serenity and detachment.
