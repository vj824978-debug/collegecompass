Though to distinguish beasts of prey A novice might nonplus, The crocodile you always may Tell from the Hyena…

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q7(B)How to Tell Wild Animals5 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions: Though to distinguish beasts of prey A novice might nonplus, The crocodile you always may Tell from the Hyena thus: Hyenas come with merry smiles But if they weep they’re crocodiles. (How to tell Wild Animals) I. What is the most likely reason the poet uses the word ‘nonplus’ instead of confused? [1] II. Explain the irony in the last two lines of the stanza. [2] III. Fill in the blank with one word: The word ‘novice’ signifies a/an person. [1] IV. What kind of atmosphere is created through these lines? [1] Select the correct option: 1. humorous 2. mysterious 3. aggressive 4. confusing 5. fearful Options: (A) 1 and 4 (B) Only 5 (C) 1, 2 and 3 (D) 4 and 5
Answer
Answer as text
I. To maintain the rhyme scheme – ‘nonplus’ rhymes with ‘thus’ – and to add a humorous, slightly formal effect. II. A smile normally shows friendliness, but the hyena’s “merry smile” comes before it attacks; tears normally show sorrow, but the crocodile’s tears are false (“crocodile tears”). The irony is that these signs of emotion are deceptive and one would recognise the animal only when it is too late. III. inexperienced. IV. (A) 1 and 4 (humorous and confusing).
