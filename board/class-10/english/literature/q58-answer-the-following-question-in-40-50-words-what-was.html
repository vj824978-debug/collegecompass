Answer the following question in 40–50 words : What was Chubukov’s reaction when he discovered the actual purpose behind Lomov’s visit ? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
At first Chubukov was suspicious and thought Lomov had come to borrow money. When he learnt that Lomov had come to ask for his daughter Natalya’s hand, he was overjoyed: he embraced and kissed Lomov, said he had long hoped for this, called him his darling and rushed off to call Natalya, claiming she was in love with him — showing his eagerness to marry her off to a wealthy neighbour.
