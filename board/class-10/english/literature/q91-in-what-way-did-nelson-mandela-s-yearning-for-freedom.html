In what way did Nelson Mandela’s yearning for freedom alter the course of his life?

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q8(ii)Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom3 marks
Question 91 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
In what way did Nelson Mandela’s yearning for freedom alter the course of his life? (Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom) [3]
Answer
Answer as text
As a boy Mandela wanted only personal freedoms — to run in the fields, stay out at night. As a young man he realised that he and all black people were not truly free. His hunger for his own freedom became the greater hunger for the freedom of his people. This turned a law-abiding attorney into a “criminal”, a fighter against apartheid; he gave up family life and spent about thirty years in prison, and finally became the first black President of South Africa.
