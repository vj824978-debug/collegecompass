Evaluate how “The Necklace” critiques the obsession with appearances and social status.

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q11(ii)The Necklace6 marks
Question 156 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Evaluate how “The Necklace” critiques the obsession with appearances and social status.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points: (1) Matilda Loisel, from a clerk’s family, is unhappy with her modest life and dreams of luxury and high society. (2) For the ministerial ball she insists on a new dress and borrows a diamond necklace from Mme Forestier to look rich. (3) She enjoys the ball but loses the necklace; to keep up appearances and avoid shame, they replace it with one costing 36,000 francs. (4) Ten years of hardship – debts, hard labour, poverty – age her. (5) Irony: the necklace was fake, worth only 500 francs; her vanity and false pride ruined their lives. (6) The story critiques materialism and the desire for social status; honesty and contentment matter more than appearances.
