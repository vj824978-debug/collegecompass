What is the contribution of the extended metaphor in the poem “Fog”?

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q8(iii)Fog3 marks
Question 47 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
What is the contribution of the extended metaphor in the poem “Fog”?
Answer
Answer as text
Carl Sandburg compares the fog to a cat throughout the poem. Like a cat, the fog comes silently “on little cat feet”, sits looking over the harbour and city “on silent haunches” and then moves on. The metaphor makes the fog vivid and alive, conveying its silent, gentle, mysterious arrival and departure.
