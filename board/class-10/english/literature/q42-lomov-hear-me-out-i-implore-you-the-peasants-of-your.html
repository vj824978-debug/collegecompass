CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q6(b)The Proposal5 marks
Question 42 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow: Lomov : Hear me out, I implore you! The peasants of your father’s grandfather, as I have already had the honour of explaining to you, used to bake bricks for my aunt’s grandmother. Now my aunt’s grandmother, wishing to make them a present. Natalya : I can’t make head or tail of all this about aunts and grandfathers and grandmothers. The Meadows are ours, that’s all. Lomov : Mine Natalya : Ours! You can go proving it for two days on end. (i) What is Lomov pleading for? [1] (A) to be heard (B) bricks for his aunt’s grandmother (C) documents of the Meadows (D) for two days time to prove himself (ii) What effect does the following sentence have on Natalya? Why? [2] ‘The peasants of your father’s grandfather . . . . . . used to bake bricks for my aunt’s grandmother.’ (iii) Fill in the blank with the correct word from the bracket: [1] The use of the phrase ‘can’t make head or tail’ adds to a sense of (confusion/realization) to the extract. (iv) What is Lomov trying to prove? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) (A) to be heard (ii) It confuses and irritates Natalya. Lomov’s long, tangled explanation about grandfathers, aunts and grandmothers makes no sense to her; she is convinced the Meadows belong to her family and refuses to accept his claim, so she becomes more stubborn and angry. (iii) confusion (iv) He is trying to prove that the Oxen Meadows belong to him (that they were only lent to her family’s peasants to bake bricks).
