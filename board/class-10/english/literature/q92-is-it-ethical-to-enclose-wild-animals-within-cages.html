Is it ethical to enclose wild animals within cages?

CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q8(iii)A Tiger in the Zoo3 marks
Question 92 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Is it ethical to enclose wild animals within cages? Discuss with reference to the poem, ‘A Tiger in the Zoo’. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
No. The poem shows that a caged tiger is unhappy and helpless. In the wild it would hide in long grass, hunt deer and terrorise villages; in the cage it stalks the few steps of its cage in quiet rage, ignores visitors and stares at the brilliant stars with brilliant eyes, longing for freedom. Confinement takes away its natural life and dignity, so caging wild animals is cruel and unethical; they should live in their natural habitat.
