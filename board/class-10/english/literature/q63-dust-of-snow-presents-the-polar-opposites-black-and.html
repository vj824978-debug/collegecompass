“Dust of Snow” presents the polar opposites black and white in a colour pallet.

CBSE 2026QP 2/4/1Q8(iv)Dust of Snow3 marks
Question 63 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
“Dust of Snow” presents the polar opposites black and white in a colour pallet. Justify, that ‘black’ and ‘white’ signify something deeper than the superficial meaning in the poem.
Answer
Answer as text
The black crow and the poisonous hemlock tree symbolise sorrow, negativity, ill omen and the poet’s gloomy mood. The white snow symbolises joy, hope, purity and renewal. When the crow shakes the dust of snow on the poet, the white overcomes the black: a small, ordinary act of nature lifts his depression and saves the rest of a day he had ‘rued’.
