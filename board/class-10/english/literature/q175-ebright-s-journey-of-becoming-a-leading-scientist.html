Ebright’s journey of becoming a leading scientist conveys a strong message to us.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q11(a)The Making of a Scientist6 marks
Question 175 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Ebright’s journey of becoming a leading scientist conveys a strong message to us. His inquisitiveness and competency drove him to become a great scientist. In what manner does Richard Ebright’s perseverance, dedication and inquisitiveness inspire you to pursue your dreams?
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  As a child Ebright collected butterflies, coins, fossils and stars – boundless curiosity.  His mother encouraged him; the book The Travels of Monarch X opened the world of science and he tagged butterflies for Dr Urquhart.  He learnt from failure: his first science-fair project on frog tissues lost, so he decided to do real experiments.  Persevered with years of projects – viceroy butterfly mimicry, the spots on monarch pupa (gold spots producing a hormone), cell research.  Discovered how cells read their DNA blueprint; published in a scientific journal while still young.  Also a debater, public speaker and canoeist – an all-rounder with a competitive spirit to do his best, not just to win prizes.  Inspiration: curiosity, hard work, learning from setbacks and the right guidance help anyone achieve their dreams.
