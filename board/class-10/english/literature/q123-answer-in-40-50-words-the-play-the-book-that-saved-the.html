Answer in 40–50 words : The play, ‘The Book that Saved the Earth’, uses humour and misunderstandings between the Martians and Earthlings to explore the themes of perception and cultural differences. Comment. (The Book that Saved the Earth)
Answer
Answer as text
The Martians misread everything on Earth: they think a library is a storage place for “sandwiches”, and Think-Tank, the proud “intelligent” leader, interprets a book of Mother Goose rhymes as a coded plan to attack Mars — Humpty Dumpty as a high-ranking officer and “Hey Diddle Diddle” as Earthlings’ power to reach the moon. These misunderstandings create humour and show how a different culture’s perception, coloured by arrogance and ignorance, can lead to absurd conclusions.
