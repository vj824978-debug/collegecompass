Question 109 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Why was Fowler interested in meeting Ausable ? (The Midnight Visitor)
Answer
Answer as text
Fowler was a romantic-minded young writer who wanted to see a real secret agent and experience the thrill and mystery of spy work – sinister men, dangerous encounters, beautiful women and secret messages – possibly as material for his writing. He was disappointed to find Ausable fat, untidy and ordinary.
