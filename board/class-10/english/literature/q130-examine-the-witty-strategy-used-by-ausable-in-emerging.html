Examine the witty strategy used by Ausable in emerging as a super spy.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q9(i)The Midnight Visitor3 marks
Question 130 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Examine the witty strategy used by Ausable in emerging as a super spy.
Answer
Answer as text
Though he did not look like a spy, Ausable used presence of mind. When Max held him at gunpoint for the papers, Ausable calmly invented a story of a “balcony” below the window and complained that police would arrive soon. When the waiter knocked, Max, fearing police, jumped onto the non-existent balcony and fell. Ausable’s quick, cool wit, not physical force, made him a real spy.
