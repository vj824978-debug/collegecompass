“I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society in which all persons live together in harmony…

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q6BNelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom5 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow: “I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society in which all persons live together in harmony and with equal opportunities. It is an ideal which I hope to live for and to achieve. But if needs be, it is an ideal for which I am prepared to die”. (Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom) (i) What is the main ideal Mandela is talking about? [1] (A) economic prosperity (B) representative and free society (C) absolute power (D) finishing racism (ii) How does the statement reflect Mandela’s commitment to justice? (Answer in about 40 words) [2] (iii) Fill in the blank with the correct word from the bracket: [1] Nelson’s vision was not simply to reverse the oppression but to end it completely as he wishes to (fight / appease) all forms of domination in which all persons live with equal opportunities. (iv) The phrase “prepared to die” shows Mandela’s: [1] (A) anxiety (B) desperation (C) passion (D) uprightness
Answer
Answer as text
(i) (B) representative and free society (a democratic and free society). (ii) Mandela wants a society where all people live in harmony with equal opportunities. He not only hopes to live for this ideal but is ready to die for it, showing that his commitment to justice and equality is total and beyond personal safety. (iii) fight. (iv) (C) passion (his deep, wholehearted commitment to the ideal).
