Question as text
Answer the following question in 100–120 words : How does the method of the seagull’s mother to teach her child to be independent contrast with Amanda’s mother’s constant nagging ? Examine how this affects the emotional responses of the seagull and Amanda. [6] (‘Two Stories about Flying’ and ‘Amanda!’)
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Seagull’s mother (with the family) uses tough love with a purpose: after taunting and scolding fail, she refuses to feed him and then holds a piece of fish just out of his reach, luring him off the ledge.  The method gives hunger a goal; when he dives at the food he falls, then spreads his wings and flies — her method builds courage and self-reliance.  The seagull’s response: fear and loneliness give way to the thrill and joy of flying; his family praises him and he gains confidence and independence.  Amanda’s mother (an adult voice) nags constantly about posture, nails, chocolate, homework, cleaning her shoes and sulking — instructions without understanding.  Amanda’s response: she withdraws into a fantasy world, imagining herself a mermaid, an orphan or Rapunzel, longing for freedom and silence; she becomes moody and resentful.  Contrast: guided encouragement leads to growth and confidence; constant correction suppresses a child and drives her away.  Conclusion: children need freedom, patience and encouragement, not incessant nagging.
