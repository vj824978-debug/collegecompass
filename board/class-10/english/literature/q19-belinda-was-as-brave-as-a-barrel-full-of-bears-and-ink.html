CBSE 2025QP 2/5/1Q7(a)The Tale of Custard the Dragon5 marks
Question 19 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow: Belinda was as brave as a barrel full of bears, And Ink and Blink chased lions down the stairs, Mustard was as brave as a tiger in a rage, But Custard cried for a nice safe cage. Belinda tickled him, she tickled him unmerciful, Ink, Blink and Mustard, they rudely called him Percival (Tale of Custard the Dragon) (i) What poetic device is used in the first sentence? [1] (ii) What was ironical about Custard being referred to as Percival? [2] (iii) Complete the following suitably: [1] The word ‘unmerciful’ in the given context implies that Belinda is . (iv) What image of Custard is created in the above lines? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Simile (“as brave as a barrel full of bears”); also alliteration (brave, barrel, bears) and hyperbole. (ii) “Percival” is the name of a brave knight (of King Arthur’s Round Table). Custard was mocked by this name because he was considered a coward — the irony is that the timid dragon is called by a brave knight’s name; and later it is Custard who proves truly brave by killing the pirate. (iii) teasing/mischievous — she teases Custard relentlessly, without mercy (her “bravery” is shown in mocking him). (iv) Custard appears as a cowardly, timid dragon who wants a nice safe cage and is teased and mocked by everyone.
