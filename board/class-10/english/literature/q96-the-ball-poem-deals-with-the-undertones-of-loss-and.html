‘The Ball Poem’ deals with the undertones of loss and responsibility.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q8(ii)The Ball Poem3 marks
Question 96 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
‘The Ball Poem’ deals with the undertones of loss and responsibility. How is the poem very relatable to our lives and what important message/s do we learn from it?
Answer
Answer as text
Like the boy who loses his ball, we all lose things and people we love. The poem teaches that loss is inevitable and money cannot buy back what is lost. We must learn to bear loss, accept it with courage and move on – taking responsibility for our own lives; this is how we grow up and learn the “epistemology of loss”.
