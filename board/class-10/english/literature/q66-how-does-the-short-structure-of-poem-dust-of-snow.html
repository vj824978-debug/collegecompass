How does the short structure of poem ‘Dust of Snow’ enhance its message about emotional renewal?

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q8(ii)Dust of Snow3 marks
Question 66 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
How does the short structure of poem ‘Dust of Snow’ enhance its message about emotional renewal? Support your answer briefly.
Answer
Answer as text
The poem has just two short stanzas of four lines, written as a single sentence. This brevity mirrors the sudden, brief moment when a crow shook down the dust of snow from a hemlock tree on the poet. That small moment changed his mood and saved part of a day he had rued; the compact form shows how a tiny incident can bring about a big emotional change.
