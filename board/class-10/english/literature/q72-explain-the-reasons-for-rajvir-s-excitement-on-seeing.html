Explain the reasons for Rajvir’s excitement on seeing the plantation and Pranjol’s lack of enthusiasm.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q8(iii)Glimpses of India: Tea from Assam3 marks
Question 72 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Explain the reasons for Rajvir’s excitement on seeing the plantation and Pranjol’s lack of enthusiasm.
Answer
Answer as text
Rajvir was seeing a tea garden for the first time; he had read a lot about tea and was fascinated by the sea of green bushes and the pluckers, so he was excited. Pranjol had grown up on a tea estate in Assam, where his father was a manager, so the sight was ordinary and familiar to him.
