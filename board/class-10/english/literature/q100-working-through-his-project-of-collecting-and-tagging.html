Question as text
Working through his project of collecting and tagging butterflies, Ebright developed a scientific temperament. Elaborate. (Making of a Scientist)
Answer
Answer as text
Tagging butterflies for Dr Urquhart’s migration study and raising them made Ebright observe closely and keep records. Science fair failures taught him the value of real experiments; he went on to study viceroy-monarch mimicry, the gold spots on the pupa and a hormone, and cell theory. Curiosity, patience, careful observation, and a will to win for the right reasons built his scientific temperament.
