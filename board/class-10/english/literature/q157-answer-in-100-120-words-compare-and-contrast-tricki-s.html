Answer in 100 – 120 words : Compare and contrast Tricki’s condition before and after his stay at the surgery.

CBSE 2026QP 2/2/1Q11(a)A Triumph of Surgery6 marks
Question 157 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer in 100 – 120 words : [6] Compare and contrast Tricki’s condition before and after his stay at the surgery. How does this contrast help in understanding the role of proper care, discipline and environment in ensuring Tricki’s well-being ? (A Triumph of Surgery)
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Before: Mrs Pumphrey’s over-indulgence — cream cakes, chocolates, extra snacks, malt, codliver oil and Horlicks, and no exercise — made Tricki hugely fat, ‘like a bloated sausage’.  He became listless, lethargic, refused food, vomited, had bloodshot eyes and a lolling tongue; he could hardly walk.  Herriot took him to the surgery for observation; for two days he was given only water, then the normal diet he shared with the other dogs.  There he played and ran with the big dogs, competed for food and became active.  After about two weeks he was transformed into a lean, hard-muscled, lively dog that leapt into his mistress’s arms.  Lesson: love must be sensible; proper diet, exercise, discipline and a healthy environment, not pampering, ensure well-being. Mrs Pumphrey called it ‘a triumph of surgery’, though it was really the result of proper care.
