Analyse how Amanda in the poem ‘Amanda’ and the dragon in the poem ‘The Tale of Custard the Dragon’ yearn for…

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q10BAmanda; The Tale of Custard the Dragon6 marks
Question 142 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Analyse how Amanda in the poem ‘Amanda’ and the dragon in the poem ‘The Tale of Custard the Dragon’ yearn for acceptance and a life free from criticism.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Amanda is constantly nagged: don’t bite your nails, don’t hunch your shoulders, sit up straight, finish your homework, don’t eat chocolate (acne), don’t sulk.  She escapes into fantasies — a mermaid in a languid emerald sea, an orphan roaming free, Rapunzel in a tower — longing for freedom, peace and silence.  Her silence and sulking show a child wanting to be accepted as she is, free from criticism.  Custard is mocked as a coward by Belinda, Ink, Blink and Mustard; he cries for a nice safe cage.  When the pirate comes, the others flee and Custard bravely fights and eats the pirate.  Even then the others claim they would have been braver; Custard meekly agrees — his courage is not truly acknowledged.  Both want to be accepted and appreciated for who they are rather than judged.
