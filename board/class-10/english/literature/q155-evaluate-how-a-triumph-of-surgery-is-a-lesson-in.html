Evaluate how “A Triumph of Surgery” is a lesson in thoughtful parenting.

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q11(i)A Triumph of Surgery6 marks
Question 155 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Evaluate how “A Triumph of Surgery” is a lesson in thoughtful parenting.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points: (1) Mrs Pumphrey, wealthy and loving, overindulges Tricki with rich food, cakes, cream and sweets. (2) Lack of exercise and overfeeding make him obese and ill – lethargic, vomiting, bloodshot eyes. (3) Mr Herriot realises the real cause is overindulgence, not disease, and takes him to his surgery. (4) There Tricki gets a disciplined diet (no food for two days, then water), exercise and play with other dogs, and recovers. (5) Lesson: love must be balanced with discipline; excessive pampering harms children/pets; proper nutrition, activity and social interaction are essential. (6) Thoughtful parenting means caring for real well-being, not just giving in to every wish.
