In ‘A Triumph of Surgery’, how does Tricki’s condition reflect the consequences of pampering pets excessively?

CBSE 2026QP 2/5/1Q9(iii)A Triumph of Surgery3 marks
Question 114 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
In ‘A Triumph of Surgery’, how does Tricki’s condition reflect the consequences of pampering pets excessively? What does this suggest about human attachment?
Answer
Answer as text
Mrs Pumphrey overfed Tricki with cream cakes, chocolates, malt and cod-liver oil and let him go without exercise, so he became hugely fat, listless, refused food and vomited. Only when Herriot took him away and gave him a plain diet and exercise did he recover. It suggests that blind, excessive attachment can harm those we love; true care needs good sense and discipline.
