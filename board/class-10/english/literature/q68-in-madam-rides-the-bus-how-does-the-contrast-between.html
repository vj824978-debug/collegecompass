Question as text
In ‘Madam Rides the Bus’, how does the contrast between Valli’s boldness and her emotional response to the dead cow shape your view of her maturity?
Answer
Answer as text
Valli is bold and independent: she saves sixty paise, travels alone to town and firmly handles the conductor and the elderly woman, insisting she is a grown-up. But on the return journey, the sight of the dead cow she had admired earlier shocks and saddens her, and she loses all interest in the scenery. This shows that she is sensitive and that her first real encounter with death makes her grow up emotionally — true maturity combines courage with empathy.
