Answer in 40–50 words : The portrayal of the baker’s attire and role in the village emphasize the cultural and social significance of bread-making within the community. Elaborate. (Glimpses of India – A Baker from Goa)
Answer
Answer as text
The baker (pader) wore a peculiar dress called the kabai, a single-piece long frock reaching down to the knees; later a shirt and trousers shorter than full length. His arrival with the jingling bamboo was eagerly awaited. Bread was essential at marriages (sweet bread bol), engagements, feasts and Christmas (cakes and bolinhas), and the lady of the house had to prepare bread sandwiches for her daughter’s engagement. Thus the baker was an important member of Goan village life.
