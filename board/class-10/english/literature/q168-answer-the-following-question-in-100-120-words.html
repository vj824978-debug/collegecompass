Question 168 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer the following question in 100–120 words. Trace the transformation of Richard Ebright from a young man with potential to a respected and accomplished scientist. (The Making of a Scientist)
Answer
Answer as text
V alue points: (1) As a child in Reading, Pennsylvania, Ebright collected butterflies and other insects; his mother encouraged his curiosity with books, telescopes and microscopes. (2) The book ‘The Travels of Monarch X’ and tagging monarchs for Dr Urquhart opened the world of science to him. (3) A failure at a science fair taught him that real experiments, not just neat displays, win; he then did projects on viceroy butterflies, the hormone of the pupa’s gold spots, etc., winning prizes. (4) His research led to a new theory of how cells read their DNA; as a college student he and his room-mate worked out this theory, which was published in a scientific journal. (5) He was a well-rounded person — debater, public speaker, canoeist, photographer — with a first-rate mind, curiosity and a will to win for the right reasons. (6) Through hard work, persistence and guidance, he grew into a respected scientist and educator, showing that curiosity and dedication make a scientist.
