Question as text
Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] The teacher built up Bholi’s confidence on her first day at school. Discuss. (Bholi)
Answer
Answer as text
The teacher spoke to the frightened, stammering Bholi softly and affectionately, patted her, and encouraged her to tell her name without fear. She assured her that if she came to school regularly her stammer would go and she would become the most learned person in the village, so no one would laugh at her. She gave her a picture book, filling Bholi with new hope and confidence.
