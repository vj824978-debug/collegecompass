“Mijbil the Otter” sensitises human beings especially city people to wildlife.

CBSE 2026QP 2/1/1Q8(ii)Mijbil the Otter3 marks
Question 46 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
“Mijbil the Otter” sensitises human beings especially city people to wildlife. What emotions are conveyed through the association of Mijbil and Maxwell?
Answer
Answer as text
Their bond conveys love, affection, trust and companionship. Maxwell cares for Mij like a pet child – letting him splash in the bath, playing with him and protecting him on the journey; he feels anxiety and guilt when Mij is distressed in the box. Mij in turn becomes attached to Maxwell and follows him like a dog. It shows that wild animals respond to kindness and patience.
