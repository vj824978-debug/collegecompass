Question as text
Lencho’s last letter to God depicts astute faith on one level and irony on the other. Bring about the element of faith and irony in the situation. (A Letter to God)
Answer
Answer as text
Faith: Lencho does not doubt for a moment that God sent the money, so he writes again asking for the rest. Irony: the money was actually collected by the kind postmaster and employees, yet Lencho calls them “a bunch of crooks” and asks God not to send money through the post office. The very people who helped him are suspected of stealing.
