Question 88 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer in about 30-40 words: In what manner did Lencho’s cornfield suffer damage? Did he hold any expectations of receiving assistance? (A Letter to God)
Answer
Answer as text
A strong wind came and very large hailstones fell for an hour, covering the field white as if with salt; the corn and flowers were completely destroyed, leaving not a leaf on the trees. Yes, Lencho had firm faith that God, whose eyes see everything, would help him; so he wrote a letter to God asking for a hundred pesos.
