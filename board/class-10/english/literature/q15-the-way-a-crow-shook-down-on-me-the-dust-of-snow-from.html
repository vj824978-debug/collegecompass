The way a crow Shook down on me The dust of snow From a hemlock tree Has given my heart A change of mood…

CBSE 2025QP 2/3/1Q7(a)Dust of Snow5 marks
Question 15 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the following extract and answer the questions that follow : The way a crow Shook down on me The dust of snow From a hemlock tree Has given my heart A change of mood (Dust of Snow) (i) Fill the blank to complete the sentence. [1] Shaking down of dust of snow gave the speaker a change of heart by . (ii) How does the interaction between the natural elements (crow, snow, hemlock tree) contribute to the speaker’s emotional transformation in the poem ? Answer in about 40 words. [2] (iii) ‘Dust of Snow’ is referred to as : [1] (A) mist (B) dust of branches (C) snow residue (D) snowflakes (iv) The crow and the hemlock tree represent . [1] 1. sorrow 2. elation 3. hope 4. anxiety 5. enthusiasm (A) 1 only (B) 2, 3 and 5 (C) 4 only (D) 1, 4 and 5
Answer
Answer as text
(i) . . . lifting his gloomy mood and making him feel fresh and cheerful, so that he could save the rest of the day (which he had regretted/considered wasted). (ii) The crow and the hemlock tree are usually seen as symbols of gloom and sorrow, and the poet was in a depressed mood. Yet when the crow shook down the dust of snow from the hemlock onto him, this small natural act refreshed him, changed his mood and saved part of a day he had held in rue — showing that ordinary moments in nature can uplift us. (iii) (D) snowflakes (fine particles of snow) (iv) (A) 1 only
