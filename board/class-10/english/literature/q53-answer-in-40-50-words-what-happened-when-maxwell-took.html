Answer in 40 – 50 words : [3] What happened when Maxwell took Mijbil to the bathroom ? (Mijbil the Otter)
Answer
Answer as text
On the second day Maxwell took Mijbil to the bathroom. Mij went wild with joy in the water: for half an hour he plunged and rolled in it, shot up and down the length of the bath underwater and made enough slosh and splash for a hippo. Within a day or two he had learnt to turn on the tap by himself.
