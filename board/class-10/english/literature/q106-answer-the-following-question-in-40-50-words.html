Answer the following question in 40–50 words : Considering the emotional attachment of Mrs. Pumphrey with Tricki, state the impact of the word ‘convalescing’ on her. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Mrs. Pumphrey doted on Tricki and was anxious when he was taken to the surgery. When Herriot told her that Tricki was “convalescing”, the word reassured and comforted her, giving her hope that he was recovering. Wanting to help him regain strength, she began sending fresh eggs (two dozen at a time) and bottles of wine and brandy every day, showing her deep, excessive affection.
