Question as text
Answer in 40–50 words : The young seagull’s fear of flying and reluctance to leave his ledge contribute to the development of the theme of independence and courage in the story. Justify. (Two Stories about Flying)
Answer
Answer as text
The young seagull was afraid to fly; his brothers and sister had flown, but he stayed alone on the ledge, and his family scolded and ignored him. Hunger finally drove him: when his mother flew towards him with a piece of fish and stopped short, he dived at it, fell into space and spread his wings. His first flight shows that fear can only be overcome by courage and self-effort, which brings independence.
