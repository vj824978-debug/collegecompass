CBSE 2026QP 2/3/1Q7(a)A Tiger in the Zoo5 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the following extract and answer the questions : But he’s locked in a concrete cell, His strength behind bars, Stalking the length of his cage, Ignoring visitors. (A Tiger in the Zoo) (i) Complete the sentence suitably : [1] The tiger ignores the visitors because . (ii) The above lines juxtapose the tiger’s natural instincts with its caged existence. Justify. [2] (iii) What mood is created by the poet in the above lines ? [1] (A) sympathetic and thoughtful (B) angry and violent (C) cheerful and happy (D) exciting and fun (iv) Fill in the blank with the correct option from the brackets. [1] The length of his cage highlights the (vast space/limited space/free space) in which the tiger is confined.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) he is angry, frustrated and helpless in captivity; he longs for freedom and has no interest in the people staring at him. (ii) By nature the tiger is powerful and a stalker of prey in the jungle, lurking in long grass and hunting freely. Here his strength is “behind bars” and he is “locked in a concrete cell”; his stalking is reduced to pacing the few steps of his cage — the wild instinct survives, but captivity robs it of purpose. (iii) (A) sympathetic and thoughtful (iv) limited space
