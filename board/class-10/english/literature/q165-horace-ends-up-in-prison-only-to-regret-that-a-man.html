Question 165 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Horace ends up in prison only to regret that a man should not trust anyone blindly without finding out the truth. Do you think his own foolishness was responsible for his arrest? Give a reasoned answer. (A Question of Trust)
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Yes, his own carelessness and over-confidence led to his arrest.  Horace Danby, a respectable locksmith, stole once a year; he planned to rob the safe at Shotover Grange very carefully.  He was caught off guard by a young lady who pretended to be the lady of the house; he believed her completely without checking.  Flattered and scared, he opened the safe for her; he even took off his gloves, leaving his fingerprints.  The woman was actually a thief who took the jewels; Horace was arrested because his fingerprints were on the safe.  He was foolish in trusting a stranger blindly and in being careless; ironically, he who stole from others was outwitted by another thief.  Conclusion: his own folly, not just bad luck, put him in prison.
