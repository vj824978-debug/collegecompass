“Paper has more patience than people.

CBSE 2025QP 2/4/1Q6(A)From the Diary of Anne Frank5 marks
Question 39 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions that follow: “Paper has more patience than people. I thought of this saying on one of those days when I was feeling a little depressed and was sitting at home with my chin in my hands, bored and listless, wondering whether to stay in or go out. I finally stayed where I was, brooding: Yes, paper does have more patience, and since I’m not planning to let anyone else read this stiff backed notebook grandly referred to as a ‘diary’, unless I should ever find a real friend, it probably won’t make a bit of difference.” (From the Diary of Anne Frank) I. In light of the excerpt, what can we infer about the speaker’s emotional state? [1] II. The narrator enhances the narrative technique of the extract by using: [2] (A) descriptive language (B) literary allusions (C) humour (D) reflective III. Fill in the blank with the correct word from the bracket: [1] The narrator doesn’t plan to let anyone read her diary as she doesn’t have any real . (friend / incidents) IV. Why does the narrator say that ‘paper has more patience than people’? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
I. She is feeling low – a little depressed, bored, listless and lonely, as she has no real friend to confide in. II. (D) reflective (she broods over a saying and introspects on her feelings). III. friend. IV. Paper listens to everything without losing interest, judging or interrupting, whereas people lack such patience; she can pour out her innermost feelings in the diary as she has no real friend.
