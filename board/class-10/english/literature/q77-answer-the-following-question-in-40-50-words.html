Question 77 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer the following question in 40–50 words. How does the description of the baker’s lifestyle in the past evoke a sense of nostalgia? (Glimpses of India – A Baker from Goa)
Answer
Answer as text
The author fondly recalls the Portuguese days when the baker (pader) was a friend and guide of the children: the jingling thud of his bamboo staff in the morning, the children running to meet him for bread-bangles, his kabai (knee-length frock) and the monthly accounts written on the wall. Bread and cakes were essential at marriages and feasts. These warm memories of a fading tradition evoke nostalgia.
