Amanda and Valli both represent a longing for freedom.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q10(a)Amanda / Madam Rides the Bus6 marks
Question 143 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Amanda and Valli both represent a longing for freedom. Examine the above observation with reference to the two texts. (Amanda and Madam Rides the Bus)
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  Both are young girls who long to be free and to experience the world on their own terms.  Amanda is constantly nagged by her mother/elder with instructions (don’t bite your nails, don’t hunch, finish your homework, don’t eat chocolate) and escapes into daydreams.  She imagines being a mermaid in a languid sea, an orphan roaming the streets, and Rapunzel in a tower — symbols of freedom, solitude and peace.  Valli, an eight-year-old, longs to go beyond her village; she secretly saves sixty paise and plans her bus ride to town.  She shows independence and confidence: buys her own ticket, refuses help, and enjoys the ride on her own.  Difference: Amanda’s freedom stays in her imagination, while Valli actively achieves hers (and learns about life and death through the dead cow).  Conclusion: Both texts show a child’s natural desire for freedom and independence and the need for adults to respect it.
