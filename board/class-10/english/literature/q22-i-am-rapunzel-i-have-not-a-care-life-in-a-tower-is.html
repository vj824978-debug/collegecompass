Question 22 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Read the given extract and answer the questions. (I am Rapunzel, I have not a care; life in a tower is tranquil and rare; I’ll certainly never let down my bright hair!) Stop that sulking at once, Amanda! You’re always so moody, Amanda! Anyone would think that I nagged at you, Amanda! (Amanda) (i) Complete the following suitably: [1] Amanda sulked and became moody because . (ii) What does the girl constantly yearn for? What do you pattern about Amanda from the poem? [2] (iii) What kind of picture is painted by the poet through the line, “life in a tower is tranquil and rare”? [1] (iv) The tone of the speaker in the exclamation “Stop that sulking at once, Amanda!” is [1] 1. uncertain 2. aggressive 3. dominating 4. meek 5. moody (A) 1, 2, 4 (B) 2 and 3 (C) 2, 4 and 5 (D) 4 and 5
Answer
Answer as text
(i) . . . she was constantly nagged, scolded and instructed by her mother/the speaker. (ii) She yearns for freedom, solitude and peace – to be a mermaid, an orphan, or Rapunzel in a tower, away from constant instructions. Amanda emerges as a sensitive, imaginative child who escapes into a dream world and silently ignores the nagging. (iii) A picture of a peaceful, solitary, unusual life, free from interference and nagging – which Amanda longs for. (iv) (B) 2 and 3 (aggressive and dominating)
