CBSE 2026QP 2/2/1Q10(b)The Black Aeroplane; The Tale of Custard the Dragon6 marks
Question 136 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer in 100 – 120 words : [6] How does the contrast between the pilot’s fear in ‘The Black Aeroplane’ and Custard’s initial cowardice in ‘The Tale of Custard the Dragon’ highlight that courage can be found in the most unexpected characters when circumstances demand it ? Discuss with reference to the two.
Answer
Answer as text
Value points:  The pilot, flying his old Dakota from Paris to England, met huge black storm clouds; low on fuel, he decided to take the risk and fly into the storm.  Inside the storm his compass and radio failed; he was afraid and lost, yet he kept calm and followed the mysterious black aeroplane, landing safely — courage shown despite fear.  Custard was called a ‘cowardly dragon’ and cried for a nice safe cage; Belinda, Ink, Blink and Mustard teased him for his cowardice.  When the pirate attacked, the boastful ‘brave’ ones fled or hid, but Custard jumped at the pirate and gobbled him up.  Contrast: the pilot is an adult facing real danger, Custard a comic, timid pet — yet both rise to the occasion.  Theme: true courage is not the absence of fear but acting when the situation demands; it may lie in the least expected person, while outward bravado can be hollow.
