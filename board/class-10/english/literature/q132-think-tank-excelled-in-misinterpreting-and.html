Think-tank excelled in misinterpreting and misrepresenting things.

CBSE 2025QP 2/6/1Q9(iii)The Book that Saved the Earth3 marks
Question 132 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Think-tank excelled in misinterpreting and misrepresenting things. Do you agree? Justify giving appropriate reasons.
Answer
Answer as text
Yes. Think-Tank, the arrogant Martian leader, thought himself a genius but drew absurd conclusions: he took books to be sandwiches/communication devices and the nursery rhymes book (Mother Goose) as a plan of Earth’s attack – “Humpty Dumpty” as a threat, a cow jumping over the moon as an attack. Fearing a highly advanced Earth, he called off the invasion, so his misinterpretation saved the Earth.
