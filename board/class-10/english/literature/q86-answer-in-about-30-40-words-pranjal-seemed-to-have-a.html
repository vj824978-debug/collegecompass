Answer in about 30-40 words: Pranjal seemed to have a different level of enthusiasm compared to Rajvir when they both saw the tree plantation. Justify. (Glimpses of India)
Answer
Answer as text
Pranjal had been born and brought up in Assam, where his father managed a tea garden, so the tea plantations were nothing new to him and he showed little excitement. Rajvir, a newcomer from Delhi, was thrilled: he gazed out of the train at the sea of tea bushes, called it a magnificent view and eagerly shared the legends and history of tea he had read about.
