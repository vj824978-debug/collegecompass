Answer the following question in 40–50 words : The religious man’s perspective in ‘For Anne Gregory’ reinforces the concept of divine love. Illustrate. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Anne wants to be loved for herself alone and not for her yellow hair. The poet recalls an old religious man who found a text proving that only God could love her for herself alone and not her yellow hair. This shows that human love is drawn by outward beauty, whereas divine love is pure and unconditional, seeing the inner self beyond appearances.
