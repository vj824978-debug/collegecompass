‘Dust of Snow’, by Robert Frost is a poem about nature and simplicity.

CBSE 2025QP 2/1/1Q8(ii)Dust of Snow3 marks
Question 71 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
‘Dust of Snow’, by Robert Frost is a poem about nature and simplicity. Elaborate.
Answer
Answer as text
The poem describes a simple, everyday moment in nature: a crow shakes off a dust of snow from a hemlock tree onto the poet. This small incident changes his sad mood and saves part of a day he had regretted. It shows how simple things in nature can heal and uplift us.
