Answer in about 100–120 words : Discuss how Bholi evolves through the story.

CBSE 2025QP 2/3/1Q11(a)Bholi6 marks
Question 169 · Class 10 English · Literature
Question as text
Answer in about 100–120 words : Discuss how Bholi evolves through the story. What are the qualities that contribute to her transformation from a timid girl to a confident young woman ? (Bholi)
Answer
Answer as text
Value points: (1) Sulekha (“Bholi”) fell off a cot as a baby and was slow-witted; smallpox left her face pock-marked; she stammered and was neglected and mocked, so she became shy and timid. (2) She was sent to the village school only because no one would marry her; she was frightened at first. (3) The kind teacher’s loving words and encouragement gave her hope and confidence; she wanted to learn and studied hard. (4) Education made her self-confident and aware of her rights and dignity. (5) At her wedding, when the old, lame groom Bishamber demanded a dowry of five thousand rupees, Bholi spoke clearly without stammering, refused to marry the greedy man and threw away the garland. (6) She decided to look after her parents and teach in the same school — showing courage, self-respect and determination. (7) Conclusion: her transformation shows the power of education, a caring teacher and inner courage.
