Question as text
“Democracy is a better form of government compared with any other alternative.” Examine the statement.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Democracy promotes equality among citizens, enhances the dignity of the individual and produces an accountable, responsive and legitimate government. (ii) It improves the quality of decision-making through discussion, provides a method to resolve conflicts peacefully and allows room to correct mistakes.
