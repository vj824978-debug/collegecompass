Differentiate between democracy and dictatorship.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q252 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Outcomes of Democracy
Question as text
Differentiate between democracy and dictatorship.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Democracy is accountable, responsive and legitimate — it is elected by the people and answerable to them; a dictatorship is not elected and is not accountable to the people. (ii) In a democracy decisions follow norms, procedures, deliberation and transparency and citizens’ rights and dignity are protected; a dictatorship takes quick decisions without consultation and often suppresses citizens’ rights.
