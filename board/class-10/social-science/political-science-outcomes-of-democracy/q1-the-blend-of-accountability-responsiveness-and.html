“The blend of accountability, responsiveness and legitimacy forms the foundation of a democratic government.”…

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q275 marks
Question 1 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Outcomes of Democracy
Question as text
(A) “The blend of accountability, responsiveness and legitimacy forms the foundation of a democratic government.” Examine the statement. OR (B) “Dignity and freedom of the citizens are the essence of democracy.” Examine the statement.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) Democracy ensures that decisions are taken according to norms and procedures, so citizens can find out whether decisions were taken correctly (transparency). (ii) Citizens have the right and means to examine the process of decision-making (e.g. Right to Information), making the government accountable. (iii) Regular, free and fair elections and open public debate on major policies and legislation make the government responsive to people’s needs. (iv) Democratic government is legitimate — it is the people’s own government, chosen by them; hence people everywhere support democracy. (v) Though democracies may be slower and not always fully responsive, accountability, responsiveness and legitimacy together make democracy better than its alternatives. (B) (i) Democracy is far superior to other forms of government in promoting dignity and freedom of the individual; every individual wants respect from fellow beings. (ii) Long struggles by women have gained them some respect and equal treatment; democracy recognises this in principle and gives legal and moral backing to their claims. (iii) Democracy has strengthened the claims of disadvantaged and discriminated castes to equal status and opportunity; caste inequalities and atrocities no longer have legal or moral basis. (iv) Citizens are free to express views, form associations and protest, which protects their dignity. (v) Democracy constantly examines its own practices; the fact that people complain is itself a sign that democracy has made them conscious of their dignity and rights.
