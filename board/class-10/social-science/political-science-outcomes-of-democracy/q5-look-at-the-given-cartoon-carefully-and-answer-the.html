Look at the given cartoon carefully and answer the question that follow : Which of the following concepts is best illustrated in the cartoon ? (A) Inclusive economic growth amongst nations (B) Stagnation of economic development amongst nations (C) Uneven distribution of wealth amongst nations (D) Balanced economic growth amongst nations
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Uneven distribution of wealth amongst nations. The gains of growth have gone mostly to the rich (huge upper body), while the middle and poor (thin body and legs) gained little.
