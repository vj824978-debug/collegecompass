Question as text
Match Column I with Column II and choose the correct option : [1] Column I (List) Column II (Subject) a. Union list i. Police b. State list ii. Computer software c. Concurrent list iii. Adoption d. Residuary subjects iv. Defence Options : (A) a-i, b-ii, c-iii, d-iv (B) a-ii, b-iii, c-iv, d-i (C) a-iv, b-i, c-iii, d-ii (D) a-iv, b-i, c-ii, d-iii
Answer
Answer as text
(C) a-iv, b-i, c-iii, d-ii. Defence – Union List; Police – State List; Adoption – Concurrent List; Computer software – residuary subject.
