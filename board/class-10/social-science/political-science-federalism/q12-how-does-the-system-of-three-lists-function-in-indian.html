How does the ‘system of three lists’ function in Indian federalism ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q263 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Federalism
Question as text
How does the ‘system of three lists’ function in Indian federalism ? Explain with examples.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Union List: subjects of national importance such as defence, foreign affairs, banking, communications and currency; only the Union Government can make laws on them. (ii) State List: subjects of State and local importance such as police, trade, commerce, agriculture and irrigation; State Governments alone make laws on them. (iii) Concurrent List: subjects of common interest such as education, forest, trade unions, marriage, adoption and succession; both can make laws, but if they conflict the Union law prevails. Residuary subjects (e.g. computer software) go to the Union Government.
