Which one of the following countries is an example of a ‘coming together federation’ ?

Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Federalism
Question as text
Which one of the following countries is an example of a ‘coming together federation’ ? [1] (A) India (B) Belgium (C) Spain (D) Australia
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Australia (like the USA and Switzerland), formed by independent states coming together.
