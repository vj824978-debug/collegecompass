“The creation of linguistic states was the first and a major test for democratic politics in India.” Explain the statement. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) In 1947 the boundaries of old provinces were changed; many new states were created in 1956 (and later) so that people speaking the same language lived in the same state. (ii) Many national leaders feared it would lead to disintegration of the country, and the Central Government resisted linguistic states for some time. (iii) Experience has shown that it made the country more united and administration easier, since it was based on language and also recognised differences of culture, ethnicity and geography (e.g. Nagaland, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand); it strengthened federalism and democracy.
