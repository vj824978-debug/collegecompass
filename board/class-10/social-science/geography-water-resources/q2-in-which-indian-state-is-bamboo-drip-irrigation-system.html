In which Indian state is ‘Bamboo drip irrigation’ system used ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q111 mark
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Water Resources
Question as text
In which Indian state is ‘Bamboo drip irrigation’ system used ? [1] (A) Himachal Pradesh (B) Kerala (C) Rajasthan (D) Meghalaya
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Meghalaya. A 200-year-old system of tapping stream and spring water through bamboo pipes is practised there.
