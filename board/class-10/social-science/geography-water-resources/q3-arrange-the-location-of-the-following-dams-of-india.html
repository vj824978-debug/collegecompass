Arrange the location of the following dams of India from North to South and choose the correct option : I. Pravara II. Salal III. Koyna IV. Gandhi Sagar (A) II, IV, III, I (B) II, IV, I, III (C) III, II, IV, I (D) III, IV, II, I
Answer
Answer as text
(B) II, IV, I, III. Salal (Chenab, Jammu & Kashmir) → Gandhi Sagar (Chambal, Madhya Pradesh) → Pravara (Ahmednagar, Maharashtra) → Koyna (Satara, Maharashtra).
