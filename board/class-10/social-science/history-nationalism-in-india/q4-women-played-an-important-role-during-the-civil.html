CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q85 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Nationalism in India
Question as text
(a) “Women played an important role during the Civil Disobedience Movement.” Support the statement with appropriate arguments. [5] OR (b) “The revival of Indian folklore strengthened nationalism in India.” Support the statement with appropriate arguments. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Thousands of women came out of their homes to listen to Gandhiji during the Salt March. (ii) They participated in protest marches and manufactured salt. (iii) They picketed foreign cloth and liquor shops. (iv) Many went to jail; in urban areas they came from high-caste families, in rural areas from rich peasant households. (v) Participation in national service was seen as a sacred duty; this gave women a public role, though Gandhiji felt their duty was mainly at home and Congress was reluctant to give them positions of authority. (b) (i) In the late 19th century nationalists began recording folk tales sung by bards and toured villages to gather folk songs and legends. (ii) These tales were seen as giving a true picture of traditional culture that had been corrupted by outside forces. (iii) Preserving this folk tradition was essential to discover one’s national identity and restore a sense of pride in one’s past. (iv) In Bengal, Rabindranath Tagore led the movement for folk revival, collecting ballads, nursery rhymes and myths. (v) In Madras, Natesa Sastri published a massive four-volume collection of Tamil folk tales, The Folklore of Southern India ; such revival created a sense of collective belonging.
