Between which two leaders was the Poona Pact signed in 1932 ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q41 mark
Question 3 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Nationalism in India
Question as text
Between which two leaders was the Poona Pact signed in 1932 ? [1] (A) Bhimrao Ambedkar and Mahatma Gandhi (B) Bhimrao Ambedkar and Jawaharlal Nehru (C) Mahatma Gandhi and Lord Irwin (D) Mahatma Gandhi and M.A. Jinnah
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Bhimrao Ambedkar and Mahatma Gandhi. The Poona Pact (September 1932) gave the Depressed Classes reserved seats in provincial and central legislative councils, voted in by the general electorate.
