Question as text
Arrange the following events in chronological order and choose the correct option: I. Second Round Table Conference II. Formation of Depressed Class Association III. Lahore Session of Indian National Congress IV. Formation of Awadh Kissan Sabha (A) I, II, IV, III (B) I, II, III, IV (C) IV, III, II, I (D) IV, III, I, II
Answer
Answer as text
(C) IV, III, II, I. Awadh Kisan Sabha (October 1920) → Lahore Session (December 1929) → Depressed Classes Association (1930) → Second Round Table Conference (1931).
