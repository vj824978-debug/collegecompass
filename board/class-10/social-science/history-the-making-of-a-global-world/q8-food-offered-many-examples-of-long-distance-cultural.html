“Food offered many examples of long distance cultural exchange during pre-modern times.” Explain the…

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q52 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Social Science · History: The Making of a Global World
Question as text
(a) “Food offered many examples of long distance cultural exchange during pre-modern times.” Explain the statement with any two examples. OR (b) “Trade and cultural exchange went hand-in-hand during pre-modern times.” Explain the statement with any two examples.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Traders and travellers carried new crops and food items to distant lands; noodles are believed to have travelled west from China to become spaghetti in Italy, probably through Arab traders. (ii) Many common foods of Europe and Asia — potatoes, soya, groundnuts, maize, tomatoes, chillies, sweet potatoes — came from America after Columbus reached it; the potato became the staple food of the European poor. (b) (i) The silk routes linked Asia with Europe and northern Africa; besides silk, pottery, textiles and spices, they carried knowledge and ideas. (ii) Christian missionaries, Muslim preachers and Buddhist teachers travelled along these routes; Buddhism spread from eastern India in several directions through the silk routes.
