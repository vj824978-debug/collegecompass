Which of the following deadly disease spread in America through Portugal and Spain in mid-sixteenth century ? [1] (A) Yellow fever (B) Smallpox (C) Malaria (D) Cholera
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Smallpox. Carried by the Spanish and Portuguese conquerors, it wiped out large sections of the native American population, who had no immunity.
