Assertion (A) and Reason (R).

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q21 mark
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · History: The Making of a Global World
Question as text
Two statements are given below. They are Assertion (A) and Reason (R). Read both the statements and choose the correct option. Assertion (A): Silver from Peru and Mexico enhanced Europe’s wealth in the sixteenth century. Reason (R): Europe used this silver to finance its trade with Asia. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). Silver from the mines of Peru and Mexico both enhanced Europe’s wealth and financed its trade with Asia; using the silver for trade does not explain why Europe’s wealth grew.
