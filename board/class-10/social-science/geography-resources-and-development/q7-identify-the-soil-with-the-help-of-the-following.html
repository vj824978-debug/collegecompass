Identify the soil with the help of the following informations given in the box and choose the correct option : • It is made up of extremely fine material. • It is rich in soil nutrients. • It is ideal for growing cotton. (A) Alluvial Soil (B) Black Soil (C) Laterite Soil (D) Arid Soil
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Black Soil (regur soil), made of extremely fine clayey material, rich in nutrients and ideal for cotton.
