Explain any three main factors responsible for land degradation.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q153 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Resources and Development
Question as text
Explain any three main factors responsible for land degradation. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Deforestation due to mining (e.g. Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha), leaving deep scars after mining is abandoned. (ii) Overgrazing (Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra). (iii) Over-irrigation causing waterlogging, salinity and alkalinity (Punjab, Haryana, western U.P.); also mineral processing (grinding of limestone for cement, calcite and soapstone for ceramics) and industrial effluents.
