Which of the following is an example of public sector ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q321 mark
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Sectors of the Indian Economy
Question as text
Which of the following is an example of public sector ? [1] (A) Birla Cement (B) Tata Iron and Steel Company (C) Reliance Industries Limited (D) Indian Railways
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Indian Railways – owned and run by the government.
