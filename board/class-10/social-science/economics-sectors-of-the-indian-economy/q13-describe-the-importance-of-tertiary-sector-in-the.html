Describe the importance of tertiary sector in the economy.

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q385 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Sectors of the Indian Economy
Question as text
(a) Describe the importance of tertiary sector in the economy. OR (b) Describe the features of organized sector of the economy.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) It provides basic services such as hospitals, educational institutions, post and telegraph, police stations, courts, banks and insurance, needed by all people. (ii) It supports the primary and secondary sectors: growth of agriculture and industry increases demand for transport, trade, storage and communication. (iii) As income rises, people demand more services such as eating out, tourism, shopping, private hospitals and schools, and professional training. (iv) New services based on information and communication technology have become important and are growing rapidly. (v) The tertiary sector has become the largest producing sector in India in terms of GDP and is a growing source of employment. OR (b) (i) Enterprises or places of work are registered by the government and follow its rules and regulations (e.g. Factories Act, Minimum Wages Act, Payment of Gratuity Act, Shops and Establishments Act). (ii) Employment terms are regular, so workers have assured work and job security. (iii) Workers work fixed hours and are paid for overtime by the employer. (iv) They get paid leave, payment during holidays, provident fund and gratuity. (v) They get medical benefits, safe working environment and pension after retirement.
