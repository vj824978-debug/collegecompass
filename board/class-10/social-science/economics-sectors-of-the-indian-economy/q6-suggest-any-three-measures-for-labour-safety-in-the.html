Suggest any three measures for labour safety in the unorganised sector.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q363 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Sectors of the Indian Economy
Question as text
Suggest any three measures for labour safety in the unorganised sector. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Government should fix minimum wages and working hours and ensure they are implemented. (ii) Provide safety equipment, safe working conditions and medical facilities at the workplace; provide social security such as health benefits, provident fund and paid leave. (iii) Enforce laws through regular inspection and register workers/units; support small workers with cheap credit and help in marketing so they can protect themselves.
