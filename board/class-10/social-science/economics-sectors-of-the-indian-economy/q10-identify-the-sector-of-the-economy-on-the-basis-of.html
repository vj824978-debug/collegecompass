Identify the sector of the economy on the basis of ownership with the help of following information:  The government owns most of the assets.  Delivery of services in the hands of government.  Fair price shops is an example of it. (A) Private Sector (B) Public Sector (C) Joint Sector (D) Cooperative Sector
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Public Sector — the government owns most of the assets and provides the services.
