Choose the correct characteristic of private sector of the economy.

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q331 mark
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Sectors of the Indian Economy
Question as text
Choose the correct characteristic of private sector of the economy. (A) Controlled by the government (B) Individually controlled (C) Work for public welfare (D) Work for the poor
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Individually controlled – ownership of assets and delivery of services is in the hands of private individuals or companies, mainly to earn profit.
