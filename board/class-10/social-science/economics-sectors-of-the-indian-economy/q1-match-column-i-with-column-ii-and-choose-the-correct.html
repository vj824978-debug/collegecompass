Match Column - I with Column - II and choose the correct option: Column - I (Sectors on the basis of ownership) Column - II (Examples) a. Public Sector (i) Amul Dairy b. Private Sector (ii) Maruti Suzuki c. Joint Sector (iii) Oil and Natural Gas Corporation d. Cooperative Sector (iv) Airtel Limited Options: a b c d (A) (ii) (iv) (iii) (i) (B) (iii) (ii) (i) (iv) (C) (iii) (iv) (ii) (i) (D) (i) (iii) (iv) (ii)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) a–(iii), b–(iv), c–(ii), d–(i). ONGC is public; Airtel is private; Maruti Suzuki (originally Maruti Udyog, a government–Suzuki venture) is joint; Amul is cooperative.
