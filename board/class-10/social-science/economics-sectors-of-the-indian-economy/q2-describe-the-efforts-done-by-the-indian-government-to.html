Describe the efforts done by the Indian government to increase employment in rural areas.

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q385 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Sectors of the Indian Economy
Question as text
(A) Describe the efforts done by the Indian government to increase employment in rural areas. OR (B) Describe the rising importance of Tertiary Sector in India.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) MGNREGA 2005 guarantees 100 days of employment a year to rural households (unemployment allowance if work is not given). (ii) Cheap credit from banks to small farmers to build irrigation (wells, dams, canals) and improve farming, creating more work. (iii) Investment in roads, transport and storage (e.g. cold storage) in rural areas employs many people and helps farmers sell produce. (iv) Promoting agro-processing and small-scale/cottage industries (e.g. dal mills, honey collection, vegetable and fruit processing). (v) Developing tourism, regional crafts, IT and services, and expanding schools and health centres in villages to create jobs. (B) (i) Basic services such as hospitals, schools, post and telegraph, police, courts, banks and transport are needed in every country and are mostly provided by the government. (ii) Development of the primary and secondary sectors increases demand for services like transport, trade and storage. (iii) As incomes rise, people demand more services — eating out, tourism, shopping, private hospitals and schools, professional training. (iv) New services based on information and communication technology (IT, call centres, software) have grown rapidly. (v) As a result the tertiary sector has become the largest producing sector in India’s GDP (though it has not created proportionate employment).
