Question as text
Identify the place where the secret underground society named ‘Young Europe’ was founded by Mazzini. (A) Berne (B) Brussels (C) Marseilles (D) Vienna
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Berne. Mazzini founded Young Italy in Marseilles and then Young Europe in Berne.
