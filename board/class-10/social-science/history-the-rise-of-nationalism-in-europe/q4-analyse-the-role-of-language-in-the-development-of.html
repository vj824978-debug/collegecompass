Analyse the role of language in the development of nationalism in Poland during the nineteenth century.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q73 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · History: The Rise of Nationalism in Europe
Question as text
(a) Analyse the role of language in the development of nationalism in Poland during the nineteenth century. [3] OR (b) Analyse the main provisions of the Treaty of Vienna of 1815. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) After Poland was partitioned by Russia, Prussia and Austria, Polish became a symbol of the struggle against Russian dominance; Polish was forced out of schools and Russian imposed. (ii) The Catholic Church used Polish for Church gatherings and religious instruction, keeping national feeling alive (after the failed armed rebellion of 1831). (iii) Many priests and bishops were jailed or exiled for refusing to preach in Russian; using Polish came to be seen as a symbol of the struggle against Russian dominance. Music (e.g. polonaise, mazurka) also kept nationalism alive. (b) (i) The Bourbon dynasty was restored to power in France and France lost territories annexed under Napoleon. (ii) A series of states were set up on France’s boundaries: the Kingdom of the Netherlands (incl. Belgium) in the north, and Genoa added to Piedmont in the south. (iii) Prussia was given important new territories on its western frontiers; Austria got control of northern Italy. (iv) The German confederation of 39 states set up by Napoleon was left untouched; in the east, Russia was given part of Poland and Saxony to Prussia. (Aim: undo Napoleon’s changes and restore conservative monarchies.)
