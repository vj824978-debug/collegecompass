Describe the process of unification of Germany.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q75 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · History: The Rise of Nationalism in Europe
Question as text
(a) Describe the process of unification of Germany. OR (b) Describe the process of unification of Italy.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) In 1848 middle-class Germans tried to unite the German Confederation into a nationstate under an elected parliament (Frankfurt Parliament), but the attempt was crushed by the monarchy and the military, supported by the Junkers of Prussia. (ii) Thereafter Prussia took over the leadership of the movement for national unification. (iii) Its chief minister Otto von Bismarck carried out the process with the help of the Prussian army and bureaucracy. (iv) Three wars over seven years — with Austria, Denmark and France — ended in Prussian victory and completed unification. (v) In January 1871 the Prussian king, William I, was proclaimed German Emperor in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles; the new state emphasised modernising currency, banking, legal and judicial systems. (b) (i) Italy was divided into seven states; only Sardinia-Piedmont was ruled by an Italian princely house. (ii) Giuseppe Mazzini formed Young Italy to spread the goal of a unitary Italian republic, but the revolutionary uprisings of 1831 and 1848 failed. (iii) Sardinia-Piedmont under King Victor Emmanuel II then took up unification through war; Chief Minister Cavour formed a diplomatic alliance with France and defeated the Austrian forces in 1859. (iv) In 1860 Giuseppe Garibaldi and his armed volunteers (Red Shirts) marched into South Italy and the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies and drove out the Spanish rulers. (v) In 1861 Victor Emmanuel II was proclaimed king of united Italy.
