“Energy saved is energy produced.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments.

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q175 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Minerals and Energy Resources
Question as text
(A) “Energy saved is energy produced.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments. OR (B) “Electricity has a wide range of applications in today’s world that its per capita consumption is considered as an index of development.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) Energy is a basic requirement for economic development in every sector, but most of it comes from exhaustible fossil fuels (coal, petroleum, natural gas). (ii) India depends heavily on imported petroleum; rising prices and limited reserves threaten energy security, so every unit saved reduces this pressure. (iii) Judicious use: using public transport instead of individual vehicles, switching off electricity when not in use, using power-saving devices. (iv) Using non-conventional sources (solar, wind, biogas) and sustainable development of energy resources. (v) Each unit not wasted is available for other uses without new production, saving fuel, money and the environment — hence “energy saved is energy produced”. (B) (i) Electricity is needed in industry to run machines, so manufacturing depends on it. (ii) In agriculture it runs tube-wells and pumps for irrigation and supports agro-processing. (iii) Households use it for lighting, cooking, heating, cooling and appliances — a measure of living standards. (iv) It runs transport (electric trains, metro), communication, IT, hospitals, schools and other services. (v) Since almost every economic and social activity uses electricity, higher per capita consumption reflects higher economic development; hence it is used as an index of development (it is generated mainly as hydel and thermal power).
