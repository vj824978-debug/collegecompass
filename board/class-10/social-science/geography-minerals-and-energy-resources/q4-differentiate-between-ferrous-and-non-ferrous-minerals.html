Differentiate between ferrous and non-ferrous minerals.

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q142 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Minerals and Energy Resources
Question as text
(a) Differentiate between ferrous and non-ferrous minerals. [2] OR (b) Differentiate between conventional and non-conventional sources of energy. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 1. Ferrous minerals contain iron, e.g. iron ore, manganese, nickel, cobalt; they account for about three-fourths of the total value of metallic minerals produced and provide a strong base for metallurgical industries. 2. Non-ferrous minerals do not contain iron, e.g. copper, bauxite, lead, zinc, gold; India’s reserves and production of these are not very satisfactory. (b) 1. Conventional sources: firewood, cattle dung cake, coal, petroleum, natural gas and electricity (hydel and thermal); mostly exhaustible (non-renewable) and polluting, in use for a long time. 2. Non-conventional sources: solar, wind, tidal, geothermal energy, biogas and atomic energy; mostly renewable, eco-friendly and newer in use.
