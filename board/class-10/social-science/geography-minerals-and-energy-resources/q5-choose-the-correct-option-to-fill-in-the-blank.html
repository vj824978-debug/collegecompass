Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: Minerals ↓ ↓ ↓ Ferrous Non-Ferrous —?— ↓ ↓ ↓ Cobalt Lead Sulphur (A) Non-metallic (B) Metallic (C) Precious (D) Energy
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Non-metallic. Sulphur is a non-metallic mineral (like mica, salt, potash, granite, limestone).
