Read the following case and choose the correct option : Megha has taken a loan of Rs. 5 lakhs from bank to purchase a house. The annual rate on the loan is 12% and loan is to be repaid in 5 years in monthly instalments. The 12% interest charged by the bank to Megha is related to which of the following ? (A) Collateral (B) Cost of Credit (C) Duration of Loan (D) Documentation
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Cost of Credit – the interest rate is the cost the borrower pays for the loan.
