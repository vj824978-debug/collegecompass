Explain the role of Reserve Bank in Indian economy.

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q363 marks
Question 11 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Money and Credit
Question as text
Explain the role of Reserve Bank in Indian economy.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) The RBI issues currency notes on behalf of the Central Government. (ii) It supervises the functioning of formal sources of loans; it sees that banks maintain the minimum cash balance out of the deposits they receive. (iii) It sees that banks give loans not just to profit-making businesses and traders but also to small cultivators, small-scale industries and small borrowers; banks must periodically submit information on how much they lend, to whom and at what interest rate.
