Explain the role of Reserve Bank of India in the Indian economy.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q363 marks
Question 9 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Money and Credit
Question as text
Explain the role of Reserve Bank of India in the Indian economy.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) The RBI issues currency notes on behalf of the Central Government; no one else can issue currency. (ii) It supervises the functioning of formal sources of loans; e.g. it sees that banks maintain a minimum cash balance out of the deposits they receive. (iii) It ensures that banks give loans not only to profit-making businesses and traders but also to small cultivators, small-scale industries and small borrowers; banks must periodically report how much they lend, to whom and at what interest rate.
