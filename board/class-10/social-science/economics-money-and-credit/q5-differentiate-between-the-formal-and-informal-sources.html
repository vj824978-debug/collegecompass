Differentiate between the formal and informal sources of credit.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q385 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Money and Credit
Question as text
(a) Differentiate between the formal and informal sources of credit. [5] OR (b) Explain the functioning of ‘Self-Help Groups’. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Formal: banks and cooperatives; Informal: moneylenders, traders, employers, relatives and friends. (ii) Formal lenders are supervised by the RBI; informal lenders have no organisation to supervise them. (iii) Formal sources charge a low rate of interest; informal lenders charge very high interest. (iv) Formal loans need collateral and documentation; informal lenders may lend without collateral but use unfair means to recover loans. (v) Formal credit benefits the development of the country; high-cost informal credit can trap the borrower in a debt trap and leaves little income. (b) (i) An SHG has 15–20 members, usually women, from one neighbourhood, who meet and save regularly (Rs. 25 to Rs. 100 or more per person). (ii) Members can take small loans from the group itself at an interest lower than the moneylender’s. (iii) After a year or two, if the group is regular in saving, it becomes eligible for a bank loan, sanctioned in the name of the group, to create self-employment. (iv) The group takes most decisions – savings, loan purpose, amount, interest, repayment; it is responsible for repayment, and non-repayment is followed up seriously. (v) Thus borrowers overcome lack of collateral, get timely credit at reasonable rates, and the groups help women become financially self-reliant and discuss social issues (e.g. Grameen Bank model).
