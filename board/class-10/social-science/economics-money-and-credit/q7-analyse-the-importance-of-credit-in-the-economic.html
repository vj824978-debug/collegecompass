Analyse the importance of credit in the economic development of the country.

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q385 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Money and Credit
Question as text
(a) Analyse the importance of credit in the economic development of the country. [5] OR (b) Analyse the importance of ‘Self-Help Groups’ in the rural development of the country. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 1. Credit provides working capital for production – e.g. farmers buy seeds, fertilisers and pesticides, and manufacturers buy raw materials, before receiving payment. 2. It helps people start and expand businesses and small-scale industries, increasing output and employment. 3. Timely credit helps people complete production on time and increase their earnings, making them better off. 4. Cheap and affordable credit (from formal sources) helps the poor raise incomes and is crucial for the country’s development; it lowers dependence on costly informal lenders. 5. Formal credit must therefore be expanded, especially to rural households, so that the benefits are shared more equally; high-cost credit can push borrowers into a debt trap. (b) 1. SHGs organise rural poor, especially women, in groups of 15–20 members who pool their savings regularly. 2. Members can get small loans from the group at reasonable interest, without collateral, to meet their needs. 3. After one or two years of regular saving, the group becomes eligible for bank loans, sanctioned in the group’s name, to create self-employment opportunities. 4. Loans are used for releasing mortgaged land, working capital (seeds, fertilisers, raw materials), housing and assets such as sewing machines, handlooms and cattle; the group ensures repayment, reducing default. 5. SHGs free borrowers from informal lenders and the lack of collateral, make women financially self-reliant, and provide a platform to discuss social issues such as health, nutrition and domestic violence.
