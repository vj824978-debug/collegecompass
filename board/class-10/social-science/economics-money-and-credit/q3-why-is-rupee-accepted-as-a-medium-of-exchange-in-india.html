Why is Rupee accepted as a medium of exchange in India ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q311 mark
Question 3 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Money and Credit
Question as text
Why is Rupee accepted as a medium of exchange in India ? [1] (A) Because it is issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the State Government. (B) Because it is issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Central Government. (C) Because it is issued by the State Bank of India on behalf of the Central Government. (D) Because it is issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the World Bank.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Because it is issued by the RBI on behalf of the Central Government; it is authorised by the government, and the law legalises its use as a medium of payment.
