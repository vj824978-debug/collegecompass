Question as text
In which of the following regions did the “Beej-Bachao Andolan” of farmers begin? (A) Kumaon (B) Tehri (C) Almora (D) Chamoli
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Tehri. The Beej Bachao Andolan in Tehri showed that diversified crops can be grown economically without synthetic chemicals.
