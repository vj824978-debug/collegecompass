Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched?

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q151 mark
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Forest and Wildlife Resources
Question as text
Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched? Column – A (National Parks) Column – B (States) (A) Periyar – Madhya Pradesh (B) Manas – Assam (C) Bandhavgarh – Rajasthan (D) Corbett – Uttar Pradesh
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Manas – Assam. (Periyar is in Kerala, Bandhavgarh in Madhya Pradesh, Corbett in Uttarakhand.)
