Describe the role of communities in the conservation of forests in India.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q142 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Forest and Wildlife Resources
Question as text
(a) Describe the role of communities in the conservation of forests in India. [2] OR (b) Describe any two steps taken by the Government of India to protect forests and wildlife. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Local communities protect habitats themselves, e.g. villagers of Sariska (Rajasthan) fought mining under the Wildlife Protection Act; 5 villages of Alwar declared 1,200 ha as Bhairodev Dakav ‘Sonchuri’ with their own rules banning hunting. (ii) Movements such as Chipko (Himalayas), Beej Bachao Andolan (Tehri) and Navdanya, and Joint Forest Management (Odisha, 1988) involve communities in protecting and regenerating degraded forests; sacred groves are also preserved. (b) (i) The Indian Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972: an all-India list of protected species, ban on hunting, legal protection to habitats and restricted trade in wildlife. (ii) Setting up national parks and wildlife sanctuaries, and special projects such as Project Tiger (1973) to protect endangered species.
