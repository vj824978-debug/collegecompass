Why is sustainable development essential?

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q373 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Development
Question as text
Why is sustainable development essential? Explain with examples.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Many resources are limited/non-renewable; e.g. crude oil reserves will last only a limited number of years, so present use must not exhaust them. (ii) Overuse of renewable resources harms future generations; e.g. groundwater is being overused in many parts of India (about one-third of the country), threatening future supply. (iii) Environmental degradation does not respect boundaries and affects everyone; development must meet present needs without compromising the needs of future generations.
