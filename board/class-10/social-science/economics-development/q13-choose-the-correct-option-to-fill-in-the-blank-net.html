Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: Net attendance ratio is expressed as a of total children in the age group of 6 – 10 years attending school. (A) Ratio (B) Percentage (C) Fraction (D) Index
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Percentage. Net attendance ratio is the number of children of the age group attending school as a percentage of total children in that age group.
