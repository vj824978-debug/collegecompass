Study the given data carefully and answer the question that follows: Monthly income of citizens in Rs. Country I II III IV V AY A 9,500 10,500 9,800 1,000 10,200 B 500 500 500 500 4,800 Identify the average monthly income of citizens in ‘country A’. (A) 8,200 (B) 9,000 (C) 1,000 (D) 9,020
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 8,200. Average = (9 ,500 + 10,500 + 9,800 + 1,000 + 10,200)/5 = 41,000/5 = Rs. 8,200.
