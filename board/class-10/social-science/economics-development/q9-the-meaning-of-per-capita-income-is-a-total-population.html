The meaning of per capita income is : (A) Total population of the country ÷ Total income of the country (B)…

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q301 mark
Question 9 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Development
Question as text
The meaning of per capita income is : (A) Total population of the country ÷ Total income of the country (B) Total population of the country ÷ Total wealth of the country (C) Total population of the country × Total income of the country (D) Total population of the country × Total wealth of the country
Answer
Answer as text
(A) is the intended answer. Strictly, per capita income = Total income of the country ÷ Total population of the country; option (A) as printed has the two terms reversed, so no option is exactly correct.
