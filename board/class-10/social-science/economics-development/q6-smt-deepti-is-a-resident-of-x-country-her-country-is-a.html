Smt. Deepti is a resident of X country. Her country is a developed country. Which of the following would be a characteristic of her country ? [1] (A) High Infant Mortality Rate (B) High Per Capita Income (C) Low Literacy Rate (D) Low Net Attendance Ratio
Answer
Answer as text
(B) High Per Capita Income – the World Bank classifies countries as rich/developed mainly by high per capita income.
