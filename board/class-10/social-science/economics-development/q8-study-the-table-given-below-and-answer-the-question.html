Study the table given below and answer the question that follows : State Infant Mortality Rate per 1000 live births (2018) Literacy Rate % 2017–18 Net Attendance Ratio (per 100 persons) secondary stage (aged 14 and 15 years) 2017–18 P 32 62 43 Q 7 94 43 R 30 82 61 S 20 83 78 Identify the State whose Infant Mortality Rate is almost three times that of State ‘Q’. (A) Q (B) P (C) S (D) R
Answer
Answer as text
(C) S. IMR of Q is 7; 3 × 7 = 21, and S has an IMR of 20.
