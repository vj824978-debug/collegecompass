“Different persons can have different developmental goals.” Explain the statement with examples.

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q373 marks
Question 18 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Development
Question as text
“Different persons can have different developmental goals.” Explain the statement with examples.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) People have different life situations, so their notions of development differ, e.g. landless rural labourers want more days of work and better wages, while prosperous farmers want assured high family income through higher support prices for crops. (ii) Urban unemployed youth want more jobs, while a girl from a rich urban family may want as much freedom as her brother. (iii) Goals may even conflict: an industrialist may want more electricity and dams, but these may submerge land and disrupt the lives of displaced people such as tribals. Besides income, people also seek equal treatment, freedom, security and respect.
