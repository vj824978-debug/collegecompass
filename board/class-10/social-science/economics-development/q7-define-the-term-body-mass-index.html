Define the term ‘Body Mass Index’.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q373 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Development
Question as text
Define the term ‘Body Mass Index’. Explain the method of its calculation. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) BMI is a measure used to find out whether adults are undernourished or overnourished (an indicator of nutritional status). (ii) Calculation: take the weight of the person in kg and the height in metres; divide the weight by the square of the height: BMI = weight (kg) (height (m))2 . (iii) If BMI is less than 18.5 the person is undernourished; if it is more than 25 the person is overweight.
