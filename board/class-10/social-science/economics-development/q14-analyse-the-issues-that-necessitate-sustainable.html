Analyse the issues that necessitate sustainable development for the future.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q373 marks
Question 14 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Development
Question as text
Analyse the issues that necessitate sustainable development for the future.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Overuse of resources such as groundwater (falling water tables in many areas) threatens their availability for future generations. (ii) Non-renewable resources like crude oil are limited and will be exhausted if used at the present rate. (iii) Environmental degradation does not respect state or national boundaries and affects all; so development must meet present needs without compromising those of future generations.
