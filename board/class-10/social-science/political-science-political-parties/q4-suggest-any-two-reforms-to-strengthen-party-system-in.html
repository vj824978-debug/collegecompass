Suggest any two reforms to strengthen ‘party system’ in India.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q232 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Political Parties
Question as text
Suggest any two reforms to strengthen ‘party system’ in India. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) A law to regulate the internal affairs of parties: compulsory record of members, independent constitutional authority as judge in disputes, open elections to the highest posts. (ii) Mandatory minimum share of tickets (about one-third) to women and quotas in decisionmaking bodies; also state funding of elections (fuel, paper, telephone etc.) to reduce the role of money; and the anti-defection law and affidavits on candidates’ assets and criminal cases.
