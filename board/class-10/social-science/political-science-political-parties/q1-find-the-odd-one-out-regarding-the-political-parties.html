Find the odd one out regarding the Political Parties of India: (A) Indian National Congress and Communist Party of India (M) (B) Rashtriya Janta Dal and Lok Jan Shakti Party (C) Indian National Lok Dal and Jan Nayak Janta Party (D) Rashtriya Lok Dal and Jan Satta Dal
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Indian National Congress and Communist Party of India (M) — both are recognised national parties; the others are pairs of state/regional parties.
