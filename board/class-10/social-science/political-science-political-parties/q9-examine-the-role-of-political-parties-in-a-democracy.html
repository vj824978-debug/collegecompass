Examine the role of political parties in a democracy.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q275 marks
Question 9 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Political Parties
Question as text
(a) Examine the role of political parties in a democracy. OR (b) Examine the need of the political parties in a democratic government.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Parties contest elections and put up candidates. (ii) They put forward different policies and programmes for voters to choose from. (iii) They play a decisive role in making laws for the country. (iv) Parties form and run governments; those that lose play the role of opposition, criticising the government and mobilising opposition. (v) They shape public opinion and provide people access to government machinery and welfare schemes. (b) (i) Without parties every candidate would be independent and no one could make promises about major policy changes. (ii) No one would be responsible for how the country is run; the government’s utility would be uncertain. (iii) Large societies need representative democracy, and parties gather different views on issues and present them to the government. (iv) Parties bring representatives together so that a responsible government can be formed and policies decided collectively. (v) They provide mechanisms to support or restrain the government, make policies, justify or oppose them — thus every representative government needs political parties.
