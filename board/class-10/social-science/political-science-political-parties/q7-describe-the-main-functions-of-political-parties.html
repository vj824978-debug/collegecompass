Describe the main functions of political parties.

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q265 marks
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Political Parties
Question as text
(a) Describe the main functions of political parties. [5] OR (b) Describe the main challenges before political parties in India. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 1. Parties contest elections and put forward candidates. 2. Parties put forward different policies and programmes, and voters choose from them. 3. Parties play a decisive role in making laws for the country (through their members in the legislature). 4. Parties form and run governments; the parties that lose play the role of opposition, criticising the government and mobilising opposition. 5. Parties shape public opinion, and provide people access to government machinery and welfare schemes. (b) 1. Lack of internal democracy: power is concentrated in one or a few top leaders; parties do not keep membership registers or hold regular organisational meetings and internal elections. 2. Dynastic succession: top positions are usually held by members of one family, unfair to other members. 3. Growing role of money and muscle power: parties nominate rich candidates and criminals who can win elections. 4. Lack of meaningful choice: ideological differences among parties have reduced, so voters do not get real alternatives. 5. Ordinary workers lacking connections have little chance of rising to top positions, which hurts the democratic character of parties.
