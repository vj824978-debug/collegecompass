Read the following statements about the functions of political parties and choose the correct option : [1] I. Political parties build public opinion. II. Political parties play the role of the opposition. III. Political parties present their programmes to the public. IV. Political parties make foreign policy. Options : (A) Only I, II and III are correct. (B) Only II, III and IV are correct. (C) Only I, III and IV are correct. (D) Only I, II and IV are correct.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Only I, II and III are correct. Parties form and run governments, but foreign policy is made by the government, not by parties as such.
