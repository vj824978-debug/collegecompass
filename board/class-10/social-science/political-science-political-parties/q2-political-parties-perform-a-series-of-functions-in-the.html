“Political parties perform a series of functions in the democracy.” Explain the statement with examples.

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q263 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Political Parties
Question as text
“Political parties perform a series of functions in the democracy.” Explain the statement with examples.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Parties contest elections and put forward candidates (in India, chosen by party leaders). (ii) They put forward different policies and programmes for voters to choose from, and play a decisive role in making laws in the legislature. (iii) They form and run governments; the parties that lose play the role of opposition, criticising the government and mobilising opposition; they also shape public opinion and give people access to government machinery and welfare schemes.
