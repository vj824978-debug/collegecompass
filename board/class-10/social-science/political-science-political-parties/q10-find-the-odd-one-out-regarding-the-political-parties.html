Find the odd one out regarding the Political Parties of India : (A) Bharatiya Janata Party & Aam Aadmi Party…

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q221 mark
Question 10 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Political Parties
Question as text
Find the odd one out regarding the Political Parties of India : (A) Bharatiya Janata Party & Aam Aadmi Party (B) Indian National Lok Dal & Jananayak Janata Party (C) National’s People’s Party & United Democratic Party (D) Rashtriya Janata Dal & Loktantrik Samajwadi Party
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Bharatiya Janata Party & Aam Aadmi Party – both are national parties, while each of the other pairs consists of regional (State) parties of one State (Haryana, Meghalaya, Bihar).
