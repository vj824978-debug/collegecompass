Suggest two ways to improve womens’ participation in sports leadership roles.

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q242 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Gender, Religion and Caste
Question as text
Suggest two ways to improve womens’ participation in sports leadership roles.
Answer
Answer as text
Any two: (i) Reserve a share of positions for women in sports federations, associations and selection committees, as done for local bodies. (ii) Provide leadership training, mentorship, equal pay and facilities to women athletes, and campaign against gender stereotypes so that women are encouraged to take up coaching and administrative posts.
