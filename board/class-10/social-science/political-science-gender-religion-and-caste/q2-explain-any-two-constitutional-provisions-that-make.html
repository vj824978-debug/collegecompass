Explain any two Constitutional provisions that make India a secular nation.

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q252 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Gender, Religion and Caste
Question as text
Explain any two Constitutional provisions that make India a secular nation.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) There is no official religion for the Indian state; unlike Buddhism in Sri Lanka or Islam in Pakistan, the Constitution gives no special status to any religion. (ii) The Constitution gives all individuals and communities freedom to profess, practise and propagate any religion, and prohibits discrimination on grounds of religion (the state may also intervene in religious matters to ensure equality, e.g. banning untouchability).
