Suggest measures to increase the political representation of women in India.

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q242 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Political Science: Gender, Religion and Caste
Question as text
Suggest measures to increase the political representation of women in India.
Answer
Answer as text
1. Make it legally binding to have a fair proportion of women in elected bodies – e.g. one-third of seats in local government bodies (panchayats and municipalities) are reserved for women, and the Constitution (106th Amendment) Act, 2023 provides for one-third reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies. 2. Political parties should give more tickets to women candidates; spread education and awareness and empower women so that they participate actively in politics.
