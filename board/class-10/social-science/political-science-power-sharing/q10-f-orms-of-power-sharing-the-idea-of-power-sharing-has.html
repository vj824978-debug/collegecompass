Question as text
Read the given source carefully and answer the questions that follow : F orms of Power-sharing The idea of power-sharing has emerged in opposition to the notions of undivided political power. For a long time, it was believed that all power of a government must reside in one person or group of persons located at one place. It was felt that if the power to decide is dispersed, it would not be possible to take quick decisions and to enforce them. But these notions have changed with the emergence of democracy. One basic principle of democracy is that people are the source of all political power. In a democracy, people rule themselves through institutions of self-government. In a good democratic government, due respect is given to diverse groups and views that exist in a society. Everyone has a voice in the shaping of public policies. Therefore, it follows that in a democracy, political power should be distributed among as many citizens as possible. (28.1) How does power sharing promote stability of political order ? [1] (28.2) How are pressure groups part of the power sharing mechanism ? [1] (28.3) How does power sharing contribute to the spirit of democracy ? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(28.1) Power sharing reduces the possibility of conflict between social groups; since social conflict often leads to violence and political instability, sharing power ensures the stability of political order (prudential reason). (28.2) Interest/pressure groups of traders, industrialists, farmers, workers, etc. get a share in governmental power by participating in governmental committees or by bringing influence on the decision-making process. (28.3) (i) People are the source of all political power, and power sharing lets them rule themselves through institutions of self-government; everyone has a voice in shaping public policies. (ii) It gives due respect to diverse groups and views, and citizens affected by policies are consulted, so the government becomes legitimate (moral reason).
