Explain the reasons why most of the Jute industries in India are located in West Bengal.

CBSE 2026QP 32/2/1Q165 marks
Question 1 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Manufacturing Industries
Question as text
(a) Explain the reasons why most of the Jute industries in India are located in West Bengal. [5] OR (b) Explain the measures to reduce industrial pollution. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Proximity of the jute-producing areas (Ganga–Brahmaputra delta). (ii) Inexpensive water transport. (iii) Good network of railways, roadways and waterways to move raw material to the mills. (iv) Abundant water for processing raw jute. (v) Cheap labour from West Bengal and adjoining states (Bihar, Odisha, U.P.); and Kolkata as a large urban centre provides banking, insurance and port facilities for export of jute goods. (b) (i) Minimising the use of water by reusing and recycling it in two or more successive stages. (ii) Harvesting rainwater to meet water requirements. (iii) Treating hot water and effluents before releasing them into rivers and ponds (primary, secondary and tertiary treatment). (iv) Reducing particulate matter by fitting smoke stacks with electrostatic precipitators, fabric filters, scrubbers and inertial separators; using oil or gas instead of coal. (v) Redesigning machinery to be energyefficient and reduce noise; using silencers and noise-absorbing material, and earplugs/earphones for workers.
