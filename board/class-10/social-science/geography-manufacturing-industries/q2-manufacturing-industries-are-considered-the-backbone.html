“Manufacturing industries are considered the backbone of the economic development of the country.” Justify…

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q165 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Manufacturing Industries
Question as text
(a) “Manufacturing industries are considered the backbone of the economic development of the country.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments. [5] OR (b) “Agriculture and industry move hand-in-hand.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 1. Manufacturing industries help in modernising agriculture. 2. They reduce heavy dependence of people on agriculture by providing jobs in secondary and tertiary sectors. 3. Industrial development helps eradicate unemployment and poverty. 4. Export of manufactured goods expands trade and commerce and brings in much-needed foreign exchange. 5. Transforming raw materials into finished goods of higher value brings prosperity; industries set up in tribal and backward areas help reduce regional disparities. (b) 1. Agro-based industries (cotton, jute, sugar, edible oil) depend on agriculture for their raw materials. 2. Industries sell their products such as irrigation pumps, fertilisers, insecticides, pesticides, PVC pipes, machines and tools to farmers. 3. Agro-industries have given a major boost to agriculture by raising its productivity. 4. Development of manufacturing has made the production processes of agriculture very efficient. 5. Industries create jobs and reduce dependence on agriculture, while agriculture provides a market for industrial goods; hence the two are interdependent and move hand-in-hand.
