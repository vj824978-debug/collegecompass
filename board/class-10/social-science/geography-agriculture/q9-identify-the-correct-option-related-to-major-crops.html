Identify the correct option related to major crops grown in the ‘Kharif’ cropping season in India : (A) Paddy, Peas, Mustard (B) Paddy, Mustard, Gram (C) Paddy, Jowar, Maize (D) Paddy, Maize, Gram
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Paddy, Jowar, Maize. Peas, mustard and gram are rabi crops.
