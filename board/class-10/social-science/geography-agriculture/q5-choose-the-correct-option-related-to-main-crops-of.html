Choose the correct option related to main crops of Kharif cropping season : (A) Urad, Gram, Groundnut, Mustard (B) Wheat, Peas, Soyabean, Bajra (C) Maize, Mustard, Gram, Jowar (D) Paddy, Maize, Groundnut, Soyabean
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Paddy, Maize, Groundnut, Soyabean. Gram, mustard, wheat and peas are rabi crops.
