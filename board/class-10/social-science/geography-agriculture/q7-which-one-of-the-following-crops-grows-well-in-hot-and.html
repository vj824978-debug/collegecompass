Which one of the following crops grows well in hot and humid climate?

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q151 mark
Question 7 · Class 10 Social Science · Geography: Agriculture
Question as text
Which one of the following crops grows well in hot and humid climate? (A) Sugarcane (B) Peas (C) Wheat (D) Gram
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Sugarcane. It grows well in hot and humid climate with 21 ° C to 27 ° C temperature and 75–100 cm rainfall; peas, wheat and gram are rabi crops of the cool season.
