Q9 (History). Two places ‘A’ and ‘B’ have been marked on the given political outline map of India. Identify them with the help of the following information and write their correct names on the lines drawn near them: A. The place where Gandhiji broke the Salt Law. [1] B. The place where the session of Indian National Congress was held. [1] Q19 (Geography). On the same outline map of India given for Q. No. 9, locate and label any three of the following with suitable symbols: (i) The major dam constructed on the Chenab river [1] (ii) Nuclear power plant in Maharashtra [1] (iii) Software Technology Park in Tamil Nadu [1] (iv) International Airport in Punjab [1]
Answer
Answer as text
Q9: A — Dandi (Gujarat coast, where Gandhiji broke the Salt Law in April 1930). B — Calcutta (Kolkata), West Bengal (special session of the INC, September 1920). Q19 (any three): (i) Salal Dam — on the Chenab, Jammu and Kashmir. (ii) Tarapur — coast of Maharashtra, north of Mumbai. (iii) Chennai — north-east coast of Tamil Nadu. (iv) Amritsar (Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport) — north-west Punjab, near the Pakistan border.
