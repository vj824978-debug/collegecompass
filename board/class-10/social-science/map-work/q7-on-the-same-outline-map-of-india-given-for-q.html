Question as text
On the same outline map of India given for Q. No. 9, locate and label any three of the following with suitable symbols : [3 × 1 = 3] (i) The dam constructed on Satluj river (ii) A nuclear power plant located in Gujarat (iii) A software technology park located in Uttar Pradesh (iv) An International Airport located in Maharashtra
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Bhakra Nangal Dam – on the Satluj, Himachal Pradesh–Punjab border. (ii) Kakrapara Nuclear Power Plant – near Surat, south Gujarat. (iii) Noida Software Technology Park – western Uttar Pradesh, near Delhi. (iv) Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport – Mumbai, Maharashtra coast.
