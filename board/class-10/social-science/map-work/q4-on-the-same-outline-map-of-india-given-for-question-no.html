Question 4 · Class 10 Social Science · Map Work
Question as text
On the same outline map of India given for question no. 10, locate and label anythree of the following with appropriate symbols : [3 × 1 = 3] (18.1) The largest dam built on Narmada river in Gujarat (18.2) International airport located in Punjab (18.3) Software Technology Park located in Karnataka (18.4) Major sea port located in Odisha
Answer
Answer as text
(18.1) Sardar Sarovar Dam – on the Narmada in Gujarat (near Kevadia, Narmada district). (18.2) Amritsar (Raja Sansi / Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport) – north-western Punjab. (18.3) Bengaluru (or Mysuru) – southern Karnataka. (18.4) Paradip – Odisha coast near the Mahanadi delta.
