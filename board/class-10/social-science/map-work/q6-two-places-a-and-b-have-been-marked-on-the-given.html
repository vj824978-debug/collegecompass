Two places ‘A’ and ‘B’ have been marked on the given political outline map of India.

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q92 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Social Science · Map Work
Question as text
Two places ‘A’ and ‘B’ have been marked on the given political outline map of India. Identify them with the help of the following informations and write their correct names on the lines drawn near them. (a) The place where Gandhiji launched satyagraha, for the Indigo planters. [1] (b) The place where the session of Indian National Congress was held. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A – Champaran (Bihar). (b) B – Nagpur (Maharashtra), venue of the INC session of December 1920.
