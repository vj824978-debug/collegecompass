Question as text
Q10 (History). Two places ‘A’ and ‘B’ have been marked on the given political outline map of India. Identify them with the help of the following information and write their correct names on the lines drawn near them : [2] A. The place where the session of the Indian National Congress was held in September 1920. B. The place where Mahatma Gandhi led the Peasant Movement against Indigo Cultivation. Q18 (Geography). On the same outline map of India given for question no. 10, locate and label any three of the following with suitable symbols : [3 × 1 = 3] (18.1) A major dam built on the Mahanadi river (18.2) A Software Technology Park located in Uttar Pradesh (18.3) A major sea port located in Tamil Nadu (18.4) Eastern-most terminal station of East-West Corridor of India
Answer
Answer as text
Q10: A – Calcutta (Kolkata), West Bengal (special Congress session, September 1920). B – Champaran, Bihar (Indigo Satyagraha, 1917). Q18 (any three): (18.1) Hirakud Dam – on the Mahanadi near Sambalpur, Odisha. (18.2) Noida – western U.P., adjoining Delhi. (18.3) Chennai on the Coromandel coast, northern Tamil Nadu (or Tuticorin/Thoothukudi, southern Tamil Nadu). (18.4) Silchar – southern Assam (East- West Corridor runs Porbandar to Silchar).
