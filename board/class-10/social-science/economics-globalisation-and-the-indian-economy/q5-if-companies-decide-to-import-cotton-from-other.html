If companies decide to import cotton from other countries, what will be its impact on domestic cotton cultivation ? Choose the most appropriate option : I. Less profitable II. More profitable III. The price of domestic cotton will decrease IV. The price of domestic cotton will increase (A) Only I and IV are correct. (B) Only I and III are correct. (C) Only II and III are correct. (D) Only II and IV are correct.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Only I and III are correct. Cheaper imports reduce demand for domestic cotton, so its price falls and cultivation becomes less profitable.
