Which of the following is the main purpose of a trade barrier in global trade ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q321 mark
Question 9 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Globalisation and the Indian Economy
Question as text
Which of the following is the main purpose of a trade barrier in global trade ? Choose the correct option. (A) To promote the growth of foreign companies in a country (B) To control the production of domestic industries (C) To regulate foreign trade into the country (D) To stop foreign trade between countries
Answer
Answer as text
(C) To regulate foreign trade into the country – governments use trade barriers such as import tax to increase or decrease foreign trade and decide what goods, and how much, come in.
