“New technology has helped to connect the world.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q353 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Globalisation and the Indian Economy
Question as text
“New technology has helped to connect the world.” Justify the statement with suitable arguments.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Improvements in transportation technology (e.g. containers, faster and cheaper transport) have made delivery of goods across long distances possible at lower costs. (ii) Information and communication technology — telecommunication, computers, internet, satellite communication, e-mail, voice-mail and video conferencing — lets people contact each other instantly anywhere in the world at low cost. (iii) It enables services to be produced and delivered across countries, e.g. call centres and services outsourced to India, connecting markets and production globally.
