How does the interlinking of production across the world promote globalisation ?

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q363 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Social Science · Economics: Globalisation and the Indian Economy
Question as text
How does the interlinking of production across the world promote globalisation ? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
1. MNCs set up production in different countries – in partnership with local companies, through buying them up or through contracts with small producers – spreading production across countries. 2. Different stages/parts of production are carried out in different countries and then combined (e.g. Ford Motors in India makes car parts for its plants elsewhere), so production in widely dispersed locations gets interlinked. 3. This increases foreign investment, trade and movement of goods, services, technology and people, leading to rapid integration of markets and economies of different countries, which is globalisation.
