“Print culture created the favourable conditions for the French Revolution.” Explain the statement with…

CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q63 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Print Culture and the Modern World
Question as text
(a) “Print culture created the favourable conditions for the French Revolution.” Explain the statement with examples. OR (b) “Throughout the nineteenth century, there were a series of innovations in printing technology.” Explain the statement with examples.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Print popularised the ideas of Enlightenment thinkers such as Voltaire and Rousseau, who criticised tradition, superstition and despotism and argued for the rule of reason. (ii) Print created a new culture of dialogue and debate; all values, norms and institutions were re-evaluated and the public became aware of the power of reason and the need to question existing ideas. (iii) By the 1780s there was a large literature that mocked the royalty and criticised their morality, raising questions about the social order and producing hostile sentiments against the monarchy. OR (b) (i) By the mid-nineteenth century, Richard M. Hoe of New York perfected the power-driven cylindrical press, which could print 8,000 sheets per hour; it was useful for printing newspapers. (ii) In the late nineteenth century the offset press was developed, which could print up to six colours at a time. (iii) From the turn of the twentieth century electrically operated presses accelerated printing; methods of feeding paper improved, plates became better, and automatic paper reels and photoelectric controls of the colour register were introduced.
