Analyse Gutenberg’s contribution to printing in Europe.

CBSE 2026QP 32/3/1Q73 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Print Culture and the Modern World
Question as text
(a) Analyse Gutenberg’s contribution to printing in Europe. [3] OR (b) “Printing is the ultimate gift of God and the greatest one.” Analyse the statement. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 1. Gutenberg, son of a merchant, grew up on a large agricultural estate and had seen wine and olive presses; he also learnt to polish stones, became a master goldsmith and made lead moulds for trinkets. 2. Using this knowledge, he adapted the existing olive/wine press to design the printing press, and his moulds were used to cast metal types for the letters of the alphabet. 3. By 1448 he perfected the system; the first book he printed was the Bible (about 180 copies in three years) – much faster than hand copying, beginning the print revolution in Europe. (b) 1. The statement was made by Martin Luther, who was deeply grateful to print. 2. In 1517 he wrote the Ninety-Five Theses criticising the Roman Catholic Church; these were printed in vast numbers and read widely, leading to division in the Church and the Protestant Reformation. 3. Luther’s translation of the New Testament sold 5,000 copies within a few weeks and a second edition appeared within three months – print spread new ideas quickly to a large audience and encouraged debate.
