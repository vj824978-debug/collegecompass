CBSE 2026QP 32/5/1Q41 mark
Question 12 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Print Culture and the Modern World
Question as text
Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched ? Column A (W riter) Column B (Contribution) (A) Bal Gangadhar Tilak Gulamgiri (B) Jyotiba Phule Amar Jiban (C) Raja Rammohan Roy Sambad Kaumudi (D) Tarabai Shinde Istri Dharam Vichar
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Raja Rammohan Roy – Sambad Kaumudi. (Gulamgiri was written by Jyotiba Phule, Amar Jiban by Rashsundari Debi; Tarabai Shinde wrote Stripurush Tulana.)
