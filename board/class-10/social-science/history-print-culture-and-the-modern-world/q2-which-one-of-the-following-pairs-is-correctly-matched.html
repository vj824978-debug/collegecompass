Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched?

CBSE 2026QP 32/1/1Q41 mark
Question 2 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Print Culture and the Modern World
Question as text
Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched? Column - A (W riter) Column - B (Contribution) (A) Sudarshan Chakra – Anandmath (B) Kashi Baba – Sacchi Kavitayen (C) Bal Gangadhar Tilak – Kesari (D) Pandita Ramabai – Amar Jiban
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Bal Gangadhar Tilak – Kesari. (Anandmath was by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay; Sacchi Kavitayen by Sudarshan Chakra; Kashi Baba wrote Chhote Aur Bade Ka Sawal; Amar Jiban was the autobiography of Rashsundari Debi.)
