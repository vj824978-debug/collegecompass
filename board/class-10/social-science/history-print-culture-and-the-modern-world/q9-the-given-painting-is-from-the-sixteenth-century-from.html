Question as text
The given painting is from the sixteenth century from Mughal India. Identify the work shown in this painting from the following options: (A) Minting of coins (B) Metal crafting (C) Pottery making (D) Book making
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Book making. The painting shows the stages of producing a hand-written manuscript: writing, illustrating and binding.
