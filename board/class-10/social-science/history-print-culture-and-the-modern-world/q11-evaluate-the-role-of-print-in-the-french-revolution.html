Evaluate the role of print in the French Revolution.

CBSE 2026QP 32/4/1Q63 marks
Question 11 · Class 10 Social Science · History: Print Culture and the Modern World
Question as text
(a) Evaluate the role of print in the French Revolution. OR (b) Evaluate the role of Buddhist Missionaries in printing culture.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Print popularised the ideas of Enlightenment thinkers like Voltaire and Rousseau, who attacked tradition, superstition and despotism and argued for reason. (ii) Print created a new culture of dialogue and debate; all values, norms and institutions were re-evaluated and questioned. (iii) By the 1780s literature mocking the royalty and criticising its morality spread, raising questions about the existing social order and turning people against the monarchy. (b) (i) Buddhist missionaries from China introduced hand-printing technology into Japan around AD 768–770. (ii) The oldest Japanese book, printed in AD 868, is the Buddhist Diamond Sutra, containing six sheets of text and woodcut illustrations. (iii) Printing helped spread Buddhist texts and teachings widely, laying the foundation of a rich print culture in Japan.
