Question as text
Arrange the following historical events in chronological order and choose the correct option : [1] I. Publication of Bengal Gazette II. Publication of Diamond Sutra III. Publication of Bible by Johann Gutenberg IV. Return of Marco Polo to Italy from China Options : (A) I, II, III, IV (B) IV, III, II, I (C) II, IV, I, III (D) II, IV, III, I
Answer
Answer as text
(D) II, IV, III, I. Diamond Sutra (868) → Marco Polo returns (1295) → Gutenberg’s Bible (1450s) → Bengal Gazette (1780).
