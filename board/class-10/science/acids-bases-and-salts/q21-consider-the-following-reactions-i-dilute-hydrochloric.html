Question 21 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
Consider the following reactions : (i) Dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with sodium hydroxide. (ii) Magnesium oxide reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid. (iii) Carbon dioxide reacts with sodium hydroxide. It is found that in each case : (A) Salt and water is formed. (B) Neutral salts are formed. (C) Hydrogen gas is formed. (D) Acidic salts are formed.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Salt and water is formed. HCl + NaOH−−→NaCl + H2O; MgO + 2 HCl−−→MgCl2 + H2O; CO2 + 2 NaOH−−→Na2CO3 + H2O.
