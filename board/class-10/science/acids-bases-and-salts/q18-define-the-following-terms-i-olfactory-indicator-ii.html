Question as text
Define the following terms : (i) Olfactory indicator [1] (ii) Water of crystallization [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Substances whose odour changes in acidic or basic media, e.g. onion, vanilla, clove oil. (ii) The fixed number of water molecules present in one formula unit of a salt, e.g. CuSO 4· 5 H2O has 5 water molecules.
