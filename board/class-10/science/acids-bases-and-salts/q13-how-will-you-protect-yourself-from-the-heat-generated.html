How will you protect yourself from the heat generated while diluting a concentrated acid?

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q231 mark
Question 13 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
How will you protect yourself from the heat generated while diluting a concentrated acid? (A) By adding acid to water with constant stirring. (B) By adding water to acid with constant stirring. (C) By adding water to acid followed by base. (D) By adding base to acid with constant stirring.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) By adding acid to water slowly with constant stirring, so the heat is absorbed by the large amount of water.
