Common salt is an important raw material for various chemicals of daily use.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q283 marks
Question 29 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
(a) Common salt is an important raw material for various chemicals of daily use. State in brief the method of preparation of (i) Sodium hydroxide, and (ii) Sodium hydrogen carbonate from common salt. Write balanced chemical equations of the reactions that occur. OR (b) Design an experimental set-up to demonstrate that “Alcohol and glucose contain hydrogen but are not categorised as acids”. Also give the reason to justify this fact.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Chlor-alkali process: electricity is passed through brine (aqueous NaCl); Cl 2 at anode, H2 at cathode, NaOH formed near cathode: 2 NaCl(aq) + 2 H 2O(l)−−→2 NaOH(aq) + Cl2(g) + H2(g). (ii) Solvay process: brine is reacted with ammonia and carbon dioxide; sparingly soluble NaHCO3 separates: NaCl + H2O + CO2 + NH3−−→NH4Cl + NaHCO3. OR (b) Fix two nails/graphite rods in a cork in a beaker and connect them through a bulb, switch and 6 V battery. Pour in, one at a time, dilute HCl, dilute H 2SO4, glucose solution and alcohol solution, and switch on. The bulb glows with the acids but not with glucose or alcohol. Reason: acids ionise in water to give H + ions which conduct current; glucose and alcohol contain hydrogen but do not ionise to give H + ions, so they are not acids.
