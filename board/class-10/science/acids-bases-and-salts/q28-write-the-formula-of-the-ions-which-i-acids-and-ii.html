Write the formula of the ions which (i) acids, and (ii) bases generate in water solutions.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q222 marks
Question 28 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
(a) Write the formula of the ions which (i) acids, and (ii) bases generate in water solutions. (b) Dry HCl gas does not change the colour of dry litmus paper. Why ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Acids give H + ions (as H 3O+); (ii) bases give OH – ions. (b) Acidic behaviour needs H + ions, which HCl produces only in the presence of water. Dry HCl gas does not dissociate, so no H + ions are formed and the dry litmus does not change.
