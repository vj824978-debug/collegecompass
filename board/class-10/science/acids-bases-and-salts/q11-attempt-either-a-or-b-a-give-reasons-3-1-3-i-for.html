CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q273 marks

Question as text
Attempt either (a) or (b): (a) Give reasons: [3×1=3] (i) For dilution of an acid, acid is added into water and not water into acid. (ii) Dry HCl gas does not turn blue litmus red whereas dilute hydrochloric acid does. (iii) Blue colour of Copper(II) sulphate crystals turns white on heating. OR (b) Write the preparation of the following compounds with balanced chemical equation: [3×1=3] (i) NaOH by chlor-alkali process (ii) Baking Soda (iii) Plaster of Paris
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Mixing an acid with water is highly exothermic. Adding water to concentrated acid may generate so much heat that the mixture splashes out and causes burns, and the glass container may break; adding acid slowly to water (with stirring) lets the heat dissipate. (ii) Acidic behaviour is due to H + (H3O+) ions, which are produced only in the presence of water. Dry HCl gas does not ionise, so it does not change blue litmus; in dilute HCl, HCl + H2O−−→H3O+ + Cl– . (iii) On heating, copper sulphate crystals lose their water of crystallisation: CuSO 4· 5 H2O heat−−→ CuSO4 + 5 H2O; anhydrous CuSO 4 is white. OR (b) (i) Electricity is passed through brine (aq. NaCl): 2 NaCl(aq) + 2 H2O(l)−−→2 NaOH(aq) + Cl2(g) + H2(g); Cl2 at anode, H 2 at cathode, NaOH near the cathode. (ii) Baking soda from sodium chloride: NaCl + H 2O + CO2 + NH3−−→NH4Cl + NaHCO3. (iii) Gypsum is heated at 373 K: CaSO 4· 2 H2O 373 K−−−→CaSO4· 1 2 H2O + 3 2 H2O.
