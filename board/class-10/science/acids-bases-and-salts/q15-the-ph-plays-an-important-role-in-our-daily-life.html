The pH plays an important role in our daily life.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q284 marks
Question 15 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
The pH plays an important role in our daily life. Strength of acids and bases depends on the number of H+ ions and OH – ions produced by these respectively. pH scale helps in predicting the strength of acids and bases by measuring hydrogen ion concentration in a solution. Answer the following: (a) What type of indicator is used to predict how strong an acid or base is? (b) Three solutions A, B and C showed pH as 3, 5 and 7. Which solution has the highest concentration of H+ ions? (c) What are strong acids and weak acids? OR (c) (i) Under what condition is rainwater called acid rain? (ii) In the following acids, identify weak acids: Nitric acid, hydrochloric acid, acetic acid, sulphuric acid, formic acid. [1+1+2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Universal indicator (a mixture of indicators that shows different colours at different pH). (b) Solution A (pH 3) – the lower the pH, the higher the H + ion concentration. (c) Strong acids ionise almost completely in water giving a large number of H + ions, e.g. HCl, H2SO4, HNO 3. Weak acids ionise only partially giving fewer H + ions, e.g. acetic acid, formic acid. OR (c) (i) When the pH of rainwater falls below 5.6. (ii) Weak acids: acetic acid and formic acid.
