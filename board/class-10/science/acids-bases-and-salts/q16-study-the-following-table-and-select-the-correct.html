Study the following table and select the correct option : Salt Acid used Base used Nature of Salt (A) NaCl HCl NaOH Basic (B) Na 2CO3 H2CO3 NaOH Neutral (C) Na 2SO4 H2SO4 NaOH Acidic (D) CH 3COONa CH 3COOH NaOH Basic
Answer
Answer as text
(D) CH 3COONa, from weak acid and strong base, is basic. (NaCl and Na 2SO4 are neutral; Na2CO3 is basic.)
