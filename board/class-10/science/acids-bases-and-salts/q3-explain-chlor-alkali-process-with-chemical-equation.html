Explain chlor-alkali process with chemical equation.

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q273 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
(a) Explain chlor-alkali process with chemical equation. Name the products formed at anode and cathode. OR (b) Write the preparation of following compounds with balanced chemical equation : (i) Baking soda (ii) Bleaching powder (iii) Plaster of Paris
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Electricity is passed through brine (aqueous NaCl); it decomposes to give sodium hydroxide, chlorine and hydrogen (chlor for chlorine, alkali for NaOH): 2 NaCl(aq) + 2 H2O(l)−−→2 NaOH(aq) + Cl2(g) + H2(g) Anode: chlorine gas; cathode: hydrogen gas; NaOH solution is formed near the cathode. OR (b)(i) NaCl + H2O + CO2 + NH3−−→NH4Cl + NaHCO3 (ii) Chlorine is passed over dry slaked lime: Ca(OH) 2 + Cl2−−→CaOCl2 + H2O (iii) Gypsum is heated at 373 K: CaSO 4· 2 H2O 373 K−−−→CaSO4· 1 2 H2O + 3 2 H2O
