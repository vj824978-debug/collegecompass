To a small amount of copper oxide in a beaker, when we add dilute hydrochloric acid slowly with stirring, the change in the colour of the solution is due to the formation of: (A) Copper(II) oxide, which is black in colour. (B) Copper(I) chloride, which is blue in colour. (C) Copper(II) chloride, which is blue-green in colour. (D) Copper(I) oxide, which is black in colour.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Copper(II) chloride, which is blue-green in colour: CuO + 2 HCl −−→CuCl2 + H2O.
