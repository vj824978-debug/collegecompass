An aqueous solution turns red litmus blue.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q191 mark
Question 12 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
An aqueous solution turns red litmus blue. Excess addition of which of the following solution would reverse the change? (A) Sodium hydroxide (B) Hydrochloric acid (C) Baking soda (D) Slaked lime
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Hydrochloric acid. The solution is basic; excess acid neutralises it and makes it acidic, turning blue litmus red again.
