Which of the given option represents a family of salts ?

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q21 mark
Question 26 · Class 10 Science · Acids, Bases and Salts
Question as text
Which of the given option represents a family of salts ? (A) NaCl, Na 2SO4, CaSO4 (B) K2SO4, Na2SO4, CaSO4 (C) NaNO3, CaCO3, Na2CO3 (D) MgSO4, CuSO4, MgCl2
Answer
Answer as text
(B) K2SO4, Na2SO4, CaSO4 – all have the same anion (sulphate), so they belong to the sulphate family.
