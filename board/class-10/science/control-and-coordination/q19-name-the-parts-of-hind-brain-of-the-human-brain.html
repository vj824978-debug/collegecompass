Name the parts of hind-brain of the human brain.

CBSE 2025QP 31/2/1Q232 marks
Question 19 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
Name the parts of hind-brain of the human brain. Which part of the hind-brain controls involuntary actions such as blood pressure and salivation ?
Answer
Answer as text
Hind-brain: pons, medulla (medulla oblongata) and cerebellum. Medulla controls involuntary actions such as blood pressure, salivation and vomiting.
