Question as text
(A) Observe the given figure and identify the labelled parts P, Q, R and S : OR (B) Which of the plant hormones are responsible for the following processes ? (i) Promote cell division (ii) Inhibition of growth (iii) Detection of light (iv) Wilting of leaves
Answer
Answer as text
(A) P – receptor (in the skin of the hand); Q – sensory neuron; R – relay neuron in the spinal cord; S – effector (muscle in the arm). OR (B) (i) Cytokinins; (ii) Abscisic acid; (iii) Auxin (light is sensed at the shoot tip, auxin causes bending towards light); (iv) Abscisic acid.
