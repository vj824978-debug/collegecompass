Question as text
The plant hormone whose concentration stimulates the cells to grow longer on the side of the shoot which is away from light is : (A) Cytokinins (B) Gibberellins (C) Adrenaline (D) Auxins
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Auxins – auxin diffuses to the shaded side and makes those cells elongate, so the shoot bends towards light.
