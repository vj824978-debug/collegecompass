Question 18 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
Observe the given figures A and B. When chhui-mui (sensitive) plant is touched, its leaves fold. This is due to : (A) Hormonal effect (B) Thermal effect (C) Change in amount of water in cells (D) Electromagnetic effect
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Change in amount of water in cells. Cells change shape by changing their water content, so the leaves fold.
