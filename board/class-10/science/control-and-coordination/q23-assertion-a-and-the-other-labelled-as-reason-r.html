Assertion (A) and the other labelled as Reason (R).

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q181 mark
Question 23 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
For Questions number 17 to 20, two statements are given – one labelled as Assertion (A) and the other labelled as Reason (R). Select the correct answer to these questions from the codes (A), (B), (C) and (D) as given below. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true. Assertion (A) : In our actions of writing or talking, our nervous system communicates with the muscles. Reason (R) : Cranial nerves and spinal nerves form the peripheral nervous system.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) – both true, but R (composition of the PNS) does not explain A (nervous control of voluntary muscles).
