How is brain protected in our body ?

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q222 marks
Question 31 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
(a) How is brain protected in our body ? (b) A doctor finds in one of his patients that he is not maintaining a proper posture and balance of his body. State the region of brain and also the part of brain which is responsible for it.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The brain sits inside a bony box, the cranium (skull), and is surrounded by a fluid-filled balloon (cerebrospinal fluid between membranes) which acts as a shock absorber. (b) Region: hind-brain; part: cerebellum (maintains posture and balance of the body).
