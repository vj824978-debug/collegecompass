CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q384 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
The growth movements of plant parts in which the direction of the stimulus determines the direction of the response is known as tropic movements or tropism. Plants also have non-directional movements which may not be growth dependent. (I) Name the movement which causes ‘X’ and ‘Y’ to grow downwards and upwards respectively. (Refer above figure) [1] (II) Write the name of a hormone that plays a major role in (i) falling of leaves (ii) rapid cell division [1] (III) (a) Leaves of the sensitive plant move very quickly in response to ‘touch’. How is this stimulus of touch communicated and explain how the movement takes place. [2] OR (III) (b) Name the plant hormone which is synthesized at the shoot tip. How does this hormone helps the plant to bend towards light ? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(I) Geotropism (gravitropism): roots X are positively geotropic (grow down, towards gravity) and shoot Y is negatively geotropic (grows up, away from gravity). (II)(i) Abscisic acid; (ii) cytokinin. (III)(a) The plant has no nervous tissue; the touch stimulus is communicated from cell to cell by electrical-chemical means. The cells then change shape by changing the amount of water in them (they swell or shrink), which causes the leaves to fold and droop. This movement is not due to growth. OR (III)(b) Auxin. When light falls from one side, auxin diffuses towards the shady side of the shoot. Auxin stimulates the cells on the shady side to elongate more, so the shoot bends towards the light (phototropism).
