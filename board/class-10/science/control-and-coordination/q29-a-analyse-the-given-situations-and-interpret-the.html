Question 29 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
(A) (a) Analyse the given situations and interpret the possible reason for each : (i) Iodine deficiency in diet increases the possibility of a disease of swollen neck in a person. (ii) Some people in population may have very short heights (dwarfs). (iii) Thick facial hairs develop in boys at the age of 10-12 years. (b) Explain two reasons which necessitate the need of chemical communication in multicellular organisms. OR (B) (a) Differentiate between voluntary and involuntary action. (b) Define reflex action. With the help of a flow diagram, show the correct sequence of path of Nerve impulse from place of its origin.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (a) (i) Iodine is needed by the thyroid gland to make thyroxin; its deficiency causes goitre (swollen neck). (ii) Deficiency of growth hormone (from the pituitary) during childhood causes dwarfism. (iii) At puberty the testes secrete testosterone, which brings about secondary sexual changes such as growth of facial hair. (b) (1) Electrical impulses reach only those cells that are connected by nervous tissue, not every cell of the body. (2) After generating an impulse a cell needs time to reset, so it cannot create impulses continuously. Hence chemical (hormonal) communication, which diffuses to all cells and acts steadily over long periods, is needed (plants also have no nervous system). OR (B) (a) Voluntary actions are under conscious control/our will (e.g. writing, walking) and are controlled by the fore-brain (cerebrum). Involuntary actions occur without our will/thinking (e.g. heartbeat, salivation, breathing) and are controlled by the mid-brain, hind-brain (medulla) or spinal cord. (b) Reflex action: a sudden, automatic, involuntary response to a stimulus, without conscious thought, controlled by the spinal cord. Flow: Stimulus → Receptor (sense organ) → Sensory neuron → Relay neuron in spinal cord → Motor neuron → Effector (muscle/gland) → Response.
