Walking in a straight line and riding a bicycle are activities which are possible due to coordination of which part of the brain? (Part of the Brain) (Name of the area of the Brain) (A) Fore-Brain Cerebrum (B) Mid-Brain Cerebellum (C) Hind-Brain Cerebellum (D) Hind-Brain Medulla
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Hind-Brain – Cerebellum. The cerebellum (part of the hind-brain) maintains posture and balance and coordinates precise voluntary movements such as walking in a straight line and riding a bicycle.
