A person while climbing up a rocky hill comes into a panic state and fear.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q384 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
A person while climbing up a rocky hill comes into a panic state and fear. His body starts reacting in a “fight-or-flight” condition to adjust to the dangerous and stressful situation. Based on the above facts, answer the questions that follow. (a) (i) Name the hormone secreted in the blood of the person in this situation. [2] OR (a) (ii) Name the source gland of the hormone secreted in this condition. [2] (b) State any two responses in the body of the person as a result of the secretion of this hormone. [1] (c) How does the action of the chemical signal in terms of hormones differ from the electrical impulses via nerve cells ? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Adrenaline. OR (ii) Adrenal gland. (b) Any two: heart beats faster, supplying more oxygen to muscles; blood supply to digestive system and skin is reduced (diverted to skeletal muscles); breathing rate increases (diaphragm and rib muscles contract more). (c) Hormones travel through blood and can reach every cell of the body, acting slowly but with a persistent, widespread effect; nerve impulses are fast but reach only cells connected by nerve tissue and are short-lived, and nerve cells need time to reset before the next impulse.
