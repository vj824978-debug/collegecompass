Human brain has various parts or regions that help in different actions, responses and coordination. From the following, identify the part responsible for precision of voluntary actions : (A) Cerebrum (B) Cerebellum (C) Medulla (D) Pons
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Cerebellum — it controls precision of voluntary actions and maintains posture and balance.
