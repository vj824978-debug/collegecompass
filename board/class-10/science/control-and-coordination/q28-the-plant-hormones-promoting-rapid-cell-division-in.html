The plant hormones promoting rapid cell division in seeds and wilting of leaves respectively are (A) Auxins and Abscisic acid (B) Cytokinins and Abscisic acid (C) Gibberellins and Auxins (D) Abscisic acid and Gibberellins
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Cytokinins (cell division, high in fruits and seeds) and Abscisic acid (inhibits growth, causes wilting of leaves).
