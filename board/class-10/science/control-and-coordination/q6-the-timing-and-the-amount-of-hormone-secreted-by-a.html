“The timing and the amount of hormone secreted by a gland are regulated in the human body.” Justify this…

CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q102 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Science · Control and Coordination
Question as text
(a) “The timing and the amount of hormone secreted by a gland are regulated in the human body.” Justify this statement with the help of an example. OR (b) Electrical impulse is an excellent means to transmit information in the animal body, but there are some limitations to the use of electrical impulses. State any two limitations.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) By feedback mechanisms. Example: when blood sugar level rises, cells of the pancreas detect it and secrete more insulin; as the sugar level falls, insulin secretion is reduced. Thus both timing and amount are controlled. OR (b) (i) Electrical impulses reach only those cells that are connected by nervous tissue, not every cell of the body. (ii) After generating and transmitting an impulse, a cell takes some time to reset before it can generate a new impulse, so cells cannot create and transmit impulses continuously.
