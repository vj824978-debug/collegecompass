Question as text
(A) Varun is diabetic. His latest ultrasound reports showed a blockage in his pancreatic duct. How will this affect the dual functioning of the pancreas? OR (B) Sunil unconsciously touched the hot iron rod and immediately withdrew his hand. Which type of action is involved? Also define this action. Draw a flow chart to show the path followed for this action.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) The pancreas is both exocrine and endocrine. Exocrine: pancreatic juice (trypsin, lipase, amylase) reaches the small intestine through the duct; the blockage stops it, so digestion of proteins, fats and starch is impaired. Endocrine: insulin from the islets of Langerhans is poured directly into the blood (no duct), so the duct blockage does not affect hormone secretion; his diabetes is due to deficient insulin, not the duct. (B) Reflex action: a sudden, involuntary and automatic response to a stimulus, controlled by the spinal cord without the thinking part of the brain. Flow chart: Stimulus (heat) → Receptor (skin of hand) → Sensory neuron → Spinal cord (relay neuron) → Motor neuron → Effector (muscles of arm) → Response (hand withdrawn).
