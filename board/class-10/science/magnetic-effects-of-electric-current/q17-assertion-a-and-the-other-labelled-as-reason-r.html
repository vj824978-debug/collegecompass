Question 17 · Class 10 Science · Magnetic Effects of Electric Current
Question as text
For Questions number 17 to 20, two statements are given – one labelled as Assertion (A) and the other labelled as Reason (R). Select the correct answer to these questions from the codes (A), (B), (C) and (D) as given below. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true. Assertion (A) : Magnetic field lines around a bar magnet never intersect each other. Reason (R) : Magnetic field produced by a bar magnet is a quantity that has both magnitude and direction.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) – both true and R explains A: the field has a unique direction at each point; if two lines crossed, the compass would point in two directions at one point, which is impossible.
