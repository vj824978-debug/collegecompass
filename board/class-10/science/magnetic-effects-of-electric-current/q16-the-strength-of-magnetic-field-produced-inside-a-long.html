Question as text
The strength of magnetic field produced inside a long straight current carrying solenoid does not depend upon : (A) number of turns in the solenoid (B) direction of current flowing through the solenoid (C) material of the core filled inside the solenoid (D) radius of the coil of the solenoid
Answer
Answer as text
(B) direction of current – reversing the current only reverses the direction of the field, not its strength. (Strength depends on number of turns, current and core material.)
