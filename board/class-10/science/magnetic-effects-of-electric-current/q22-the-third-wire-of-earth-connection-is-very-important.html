Question as text
(a) “The third wire of earth connection is very important in domestic electric appliances.” Justify this statement. (b) List two precautions to be taken to avoid the overloading of domestic electric circuits.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The earth wire connects the metallic body of the appliance to the earth. It provides a lowresistance path for any leakage current, keeping the body at the potential of the earth, so the user does not get a severe electric shock. (b) (i) Do not connect too many appliances to a single socket. (ii) Do not switch on many high-power appliances at the same time; use a fuse/MCB of proper rating in the circuit.
