From the following options choose the one which gives negative magnification greater than one: (A) Concave mirror; Nature of image – real (B) Concave mirror; Nature of image – virtual (C) Convex mirror; Nature of image – real (D) Convex mirror; Nature of image – virtual
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Concave mirror, real image – real images are inverted ( m < 0); with the object between F and C the image is magnified (|m| > 1).
