A ray of light after reflection from a concave mirror becomes parallel to its principal axis.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q363 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
(a) A ray of light after reflection from a concave mirror becomes parallel to its principal axis. Draw a labelled ray diagram to show this. (b) (i) Differentiate between the virtual images formed by a convex mirror and concave mirror. (ii) “The value of magnification ‘m’ for a mirror is−2.” Calculate the height of object if the height of image is 20 cm.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: concave mirror with P, F, C on the principal axis; an incident ray passing through the principal focus F (or directed towards it) strikes the mirror and reflects parallel to the principal axis. (b) (i) Concave mirror: virtual image is erect and enlarged (object between P and F). Convex mirror: virtual image is erect and diminished (for all object positions). (ii) m = h′/h. Negative m means the image is real and inverted, so h′ =−20 cm: h = h′/m = (−20)/(−2) = 10 cm. Object height = 10 cm.
