Out of the two lenses, one concave and the other convex, state which one will diverge a parallel beam of…

CBSE 2025QP 31/2/1Q252 marks
Question 20 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
(a) Out of the two lenses, one concave and the other convex, state which one will diverge a parallel beam of light falling on it. Draw a ray diagram to show the principal focus of the lens. OR (b) A ray of light after refraction from a convex lens emerges parallel to its principal axis. (i) Draw a labelled ray diagram to show it. (ii) In this case, the incident ray before refraction from the lens passes through a point on its principal axis. Name the point.
Answer
Answer as text
20. (a) Concave lens. Diagram: rays parallel to the principal axis diverge after refraction; their backward extensions (dotted) meet at a point F on the principal axis on the same side as the incident light: principal focus F, optical centre O. OR (b) (i) Diagram: convex lens with principal axis, O, F1, F2; a ray from the left passing through F1 strikes the lens and emerges parallel to the principal axis. (ii) The principal focus (F1) of the lens.
