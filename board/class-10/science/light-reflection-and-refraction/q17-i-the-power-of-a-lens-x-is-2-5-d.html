(i) The power of a lens ‘X’ is −2· 5 D.

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q365 marks
Question 17 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
(a) (i) The power of a lens ‘X’ is −2· 5 D. Name the lens and determine its focal length in cm. For which eye defect of vision will an optician prescribe this type of lens as a corrective lens ? (ii) “The value of magnification ‘ m’ for a lens is −2.” Using new Cartesian Sign Convention and considering that an object is placed at a distance of 20 cm from the optical centre of this lens, state : (I) the nature of the image formed; (II) size of the image compared to the size of the object; (III) position of the image, and (IV) sign of the height of the image. (iii) The numerical values of the focal lengths of two lenses A and B are 10 cm and 20 cm respectively. Which one of the two will show higher degree of convergence/divergence ? Give reason to justify your answer. OR (b) (i) Draw a ray diagram to show the refraction of a ray of light through a rectangular glass slab when it falls obliquely from air into glass. (ii) State Snell’s law of refraction of light. (iii) Differentiate between the virtual images formed by a convex lens and a concave lens on the basis of : (I) object distance, and (II) magnification.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Negative power, so X is a concave (diverging) lens. f = 1 P = 1 −2.5 m =−0.4 m =−40 cm. Prescribed for myopia (near-sightedness). (ii) (I) m negative: real and inverted (the lens is convex). (II) |m| = 2: image is twice the size of the object (magnified). (III) m = v u, u =−20 cm, so v = (−2)(−20) = +40 cm: 40 cm on the other side of the lens. (IV) Height of the image is negative. (iii) Lens A: power P = 1 f , so the lens with the shorter focal length (10 cm, P = 10 D vs 5 D for B) has the greater power, i.e. higher degree of convergence/divergence. OR (b) (i) Diagram: ray AO incident obliquely on the upper face, bends towards the normal inside the slab (OO ′), bends away from the normal on emerging at the lower face; emergent ray O′B parallel to the incident ray but laterally displaced. Labels: incident ray, refracted ray, emergent ray, normals, angles i, r, e, lateral displacement. (ii) The ratio of sine of angle of incidence to sine of angle of refraction is constant for a given pair of media and a given colour of light: sin i sin r = constant (= n21); (the incident ray, refracted ray and normal lie in one plane). (iii) (I) Convex lens forms a virtual image only when the object is between F and the optical centre O; a concave lens forms a virtual image for every position of the object. (II) Convex lens: virtual image is enlarged ( m > 1); concave lens: virtual image is diminished (0 < m < 1), both positive (erect).
