Question as text
Study the figure in which the path of a ray of light going from Medium 1 to Medium 2 is shown. (a) Out of the two Media – Medium 1 and Medium 2, in which is the speed of light more ? (b) State reason of bending of the refracted ray away from the normal. (c) Express refractive index of Medium 2 with respect to Medium 1 in terms of speed of light in two media.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Medium 2 (the ray bends away from the normal, so medium 2 is optically rarer). (b) Light goes from an optically denser medium to a rarer medium, where its speed increases; hence it bends away from the normal. (c) n21 = speed of light in medium 1 speed of light in medium 2 = v1 v2 .
