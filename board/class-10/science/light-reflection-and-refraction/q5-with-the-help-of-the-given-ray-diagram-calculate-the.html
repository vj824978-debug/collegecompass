CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q332 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
With the help of the given ray diagram, calculate the refractive index of Medium 2 with respect to Medium 1. (Given that √ 3 = 1.732)
Answer
Answer as text
Angle of incidence i = 90◦− 30◦ = 60◦ (angle with the normal ON); angle of refraction r = 30◦. n21 = sin i sin r = sin 60◦ sin 30◦ = √ 3/2 1/2 = √ 3 = 1.732.
