Question as text
An object is placed at a distance of 60 cm from a concave lens of focal length 30 cm. Use lens formula to find the position of the image formed in this case.
Answer
Answer as text
u =−60 cm, f =−30 cm. 1 v = 1 f + 1 u =− 1 30− 1 60 =− 3 60, so v =−20 cm. The image is 20 cm from the lens on the same side as the object (virtual, erect).
