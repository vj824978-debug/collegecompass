To get an image of magnification−1 on a screen using a lens of focal length 20 cm, the object distance must be: (A) Less than 20 cm (B) 30 cm (C) 40 cm (D) 80 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 40 cm — m =−1 (real, same size) when the object is at 2 F , i.e. 2 f = 40 cm.
