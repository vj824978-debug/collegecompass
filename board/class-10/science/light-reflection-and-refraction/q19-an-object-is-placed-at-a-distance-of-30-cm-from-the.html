An object is placed at a distance of 30 cm from the pole of a concave mirror. If its real and inverted image is formed at 60 cm in front of the mirror, the focal length of the mirror is : (A)−15 cm (B) −20 cm (C) +20 cm (D) +15 cm
Answer
Answer as text
(B)−20 cm. u =−30 cm, v =−60 cm; 1 f = 1 v + 1 u =− 1 60− 1 30 =− 1
