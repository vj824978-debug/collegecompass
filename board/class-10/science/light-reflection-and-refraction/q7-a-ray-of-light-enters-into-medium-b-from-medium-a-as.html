Question as text
A ray of light enters into medium B from medium A as shown in the given figure: (a) Which one of these two media is optically denser than the other medium? Justify your answer. (b) If the speed of light in medium A is VA and in medium B is VB, then what is the refractive index of medium B with respect to medium A?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Medium A is optically denser. NN′ is the normal; the angle of refraction in B is greater than the angle of incidence in A, i.e. the ray bends away from the normal on going from A to B, which happens when light passes from a denser to a rarer medium. (b) nBA = speed of light in A speed of light in B = VA VB (which is less than 1 here, since VB > V A).
