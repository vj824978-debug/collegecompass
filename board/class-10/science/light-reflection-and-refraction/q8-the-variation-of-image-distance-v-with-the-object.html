Question as text
The variation of image distance ( v) with the object distance ( u) for a convex lens is given in the following observation table. Analyse it and answer the questions given below: S.No. Object distance ( u) cm Image distance ( v) cm 1 −150 +30 2 −75 +37 .5 3 −50 +50 4 −37.5 +75 5 −30 +150 6 −15 +37 .5 (a) Without calculation, find out the focal length of the given convex lens. Justify your answer. [1] (b) Which one of the observations given in the table is not correct and why? [1] (c) (i) Find out the value of magnification for u =−30 cm. Write the nature of the image formed. Give reason for your answer. [2] OR (c) (ii) Analyse the data given in the table above and draw the labelled ray diagram for u =−30 cm and v = 150 cm. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) f = 25 cm. In observation 3, |u| =|v| = 50 cm; object and image distances are equal only when the object is at 2 F , so 2 f = 50 cm. (b) Observation 6 ( u =−15 cm, v = +37.5 cm). Here the object is between the optical centre and F (15 < 25 cm), so the image must be virtual, on the same side as the object, i.e. v should be negative ( v =−37.5 cm), not positive. (c) (i) m = v u = +150 −30 =−5. The image is real, inverted and magnified (5 times) – m is negative (real, inverted) and|m| > 1 (enlarged); the object lies between F and 2F (25 cm < 30 cm < 50 cm), so the image is formed beyond 2 F on the other side. OR (c) (ii) Since f = 25 cm, the object at 30 cm lies between F1 and 2 F1. Diagram: object AB between F1 and 2F1; one ray parallel to the principal axis refracted through F2, another through the optical centre O undeviated; they meet beyond 2 F2 (at 150 cm) forming a real, inverted, enlarged image A′B′; label F1, 2F1, O, F2, 2F2, AB, A′B′.
