Relate the speed of light in the given medium with its optical density.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q332 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
(a) Relate the speed of light in the given medium with its optical density. (b) Using the information given in the table below, arrange the medium A, B and C in the ascending order of their optical density. Medium Speed of light (m/s) A 2 .25× 108 B 2 × 108 C 2 .08× 108
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The greater the optical density of a medium, the smaller the speed of light in it ( n = c/v). (b) Speed: A > C > B, so optical density in ascending order: A < C < B.
