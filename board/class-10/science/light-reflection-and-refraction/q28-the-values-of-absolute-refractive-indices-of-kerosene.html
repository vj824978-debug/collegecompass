The values of absolute refractive indices of kerosene and water are 1.44 and 1.33 respectively.

CBSE 2025QP 31/5/1Q242 marks
Question 28 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
The values of absolute refractive indices of kerosene and water are 1.44 and 1.33 respectively. Compare the two media on the basis of their (a) optical density (b) mass density (c) relative speed of propagation of light. What do you infer on the basis of above comparisons ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Kerosene ( n = 1.44) is optically denser than water ( n = 1.33). (b) Kerosene has lower mass density than water (it floats on water). (c) Light travels slower in kerosene: vwater/vkerosene = 1.44/1.33≈ 1.08. Inference: optical density is not the same as mass density; an optically denser medium need not have greater mass density.
