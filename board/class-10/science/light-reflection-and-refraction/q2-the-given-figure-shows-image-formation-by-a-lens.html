CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q332 marks
Question 2 · Class 10 Science · Light - Reflection and Refraction
Question as text
The given figure shows image formation by a lens. Analyse the figure and answer the following questions : (a) What is the type of lens used for image formation in the given ray diagram ? (b) If the real image is formed at a distance of 30 cm from the lens and the size of image is twice the size of the object, then where was the object placed ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Convex (converging) lens — it forms a virtual, erect, magnified image on the same side as the object (object between F and O). (b) Real image is inverted: m = v u =−2, v = +30 cm, so u = v m = 30 −2 =−15 cm. The object was placed 15 cm in front of the lens (between F and 2F ; here f = 10 cm).
