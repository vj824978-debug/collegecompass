Absolute refractive index of water and glass is 4 3 and 3 2 respectively. If the speed of light in glass is 2× 108 m/s, the speed of light in water is : (A) 9 4 m/s (B) 7 3 m/s (C) 16 9 m/s (D) 9 8 m/s
Answer
Answer as text
(A) – vw = vg ng nw = 2× 108× 3/2 4/3 = 9 4× 108 m/s. (The options as printed omit the factor 10 8; (A) is the intended answer.)
