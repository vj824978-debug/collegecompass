In a bisexual flower the male gametes are present in the: (A) anther (B) ovary (C) stigma (D) filament

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q81 mark

Question as text
In a bisexual flower the male gametes are present in the: (A) anther (B) ovary (C) stigma (D) filament
Answer
Answer as text
(A) anther — its pollen grains contain the male gametes.
