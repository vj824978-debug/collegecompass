In human beings, the implantation of fertilised egg takes place in which part of female reproductive system ?

Question as text
In human beings, the implantation of fertilised egg takes place in which part of female reproductive system ? (A) Oviduct (B) Cervix (C) Uterus (D) Vagina
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Uterus – the embryo gets implanted in the thickened lining of the uterus.
