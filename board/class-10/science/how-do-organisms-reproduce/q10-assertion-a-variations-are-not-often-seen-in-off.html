Assertion (A): Variations are not often seen in off-springs produced by asexual reproduction.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q81 mark
Question 10 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
Assertion (A): Variations are not often seen in off-springs produced by asexual reproduction. Reason (R): DNA molecule generated by replication is not similar to the original DNA. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A is true, but R is false. DNA copying produces copies that are similar (though not absolutely identical) to the original, so asexually produced offspring show very little variation.
