Question 20 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
(a) Write one function each of the following, in a seed : (i) Seed coat (ii) Cotyledon (iii) Radicle (iv) Plumule OR (b) Write the main steps to culture yeast in the laboratory.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Seed coat: protects the embryo. (ii) Cotyledon: stores food for the embryo. (iii) Radicle: develops into the root. (iv) Plumule: develops into the shoot. OR (b) Dissolve about 10 g sugar in 100 mL water; take 20 mL in a test tube and add a pinch of yeast granules; plug with cotton and keep in a warm place; after 1–2 hours put a drop of the culture on a slide, cover with a coverslip and observe under a microscope (budding is seen).
