Question as text
Give reasons : (a) The male reproductive organ responsible for formation of germ cells is located outside the abdominal cavity. (b) The roles of the glands, present along the path of the vas-deferens, are very significant.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Testes lie in the scrotum outside the abdominal cavity because sperm formation requires a temperature lower than the normal body temperature. (b) The seminal vesicles and prostate gland add their secretions to the sperms; this fluid makes their transport easier and also provides nutrition to the sperms.
