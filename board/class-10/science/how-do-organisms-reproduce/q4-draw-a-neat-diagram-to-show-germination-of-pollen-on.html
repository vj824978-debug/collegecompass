CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q122 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
Draw a neat diagram to show germination of pollen on the female reproductive part of the flower. Name and label only the following parts : (a) The part that receives the pollen grain. (b) The structure that carries the male germ cell to reach the female germ cell.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: pistil (stigma, style, ovary with ovule) with a pollen grain on the stigma sending a pollen tube down through the style into the ovule. Labels: (a) Stigma; (b) Pollen tube.
