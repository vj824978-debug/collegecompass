Question 11 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
Give reasons for the following: (a) Adrenal gland is known as the stress gland of the body. (b) Prenatal sex determination is banned in India. (c) Vegetative propagation is advantageous in some plants.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) It secretes adrenaline in emergency/stress situations; adrenaline increases heart rate, breathing rate and blood supply to muscles, preparing the body to deal with stress (fight or flight). (b) It is misused for selective female foeticide, which has led to a declining female–male child sex ratio; hence it is prohibited by law. (c) Plants that have lost the capacity to produce seeds (e.g. banana, rose, orange) can be propagated; plants bear flowers and fruits earlier; all new plants are genetically similar to the parent, so desirable characters are preserved.
