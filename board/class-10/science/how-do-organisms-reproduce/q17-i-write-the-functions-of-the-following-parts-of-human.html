Question 17 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
(a) (i) Write the functions of the following parts of human female reproductive system : (I) Ovary (II) Fallopian tube (III) Uterus (ii) State briefly two contraceptive methods used by human males. OR (b) (i) Differentiate between self-pollination and cross-pollination. (ii) Identify A, B and C in the diagram given below and write one function of each.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) (I) Ovary: produces female gametes (eggs/ova) and hormones (oestrogen, progesterone). (II) Fallopian tube (oviduct): carries the egg from the ovary to the uterus; site of fertilisation. (III) Uterus: implantation of the zygote and development of the embryo/foetus, nourished through the placenta. (ii) Mechanical barrier – condom covering the penis prevents sperms from reaching the egg (also protects against STDs); surgical method – vasectomy, the vas deferens is blocked/cut so sperms cannot be transferred. OR (b) (i) Self-pollination: transfer of pollen from anther to stigma of the same flower (or another flower of the same plant). Cross-pollination: transfer of pollen from the anther of one flower to the stigma of a flower on another plant of the same species, through agents like insects, wind, water. (ii) A – stigma: receives pollen grains (sticky surface). B – pollen tube: grows from the pollen grain through the style to the ovule and carries the male germ cell to the female germ cell. C – female germ cell (egg) in the ovule: fuses with the male germ cell to form the zygote.
