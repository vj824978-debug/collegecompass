CBSE 2025QP 31/2/1Q384 marks
Question 21 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
When a girl is born, the ovaries already contain thousands of immature eggs. On reaching puberty, some of these start maturing. One matured egg is released every month by one of the ovaries. The two oviducts unite into an elastic bag-like structure known as uterus. (a) Write the site of fertilization in human female. [1] (b) How does the uterus prepare itself to receive and nurture the growing embryo ? Explain. [1] (c) (i) What happens when the egg is not fertilized ? [2] OR (c) (ii) How does the developing embryo get nutrition from the mother’s blood ? Explain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Oviduct (fallopian tube). (b) Every month the lining of the uterus thickens and becomes richly supplied with blood to nourish the embryo if fertilisation occurs. (c) (i) The egg lives for about one day. The thick, spongy uterine lining is not needed, so it slowly breaks down and comes out through the vagina as blood and mucus – menstruation, occurring roughly every month and lasting about 2–8 days. OR (c) (ii) Through the placenta, a disc-like special tissue embedded in the uterine wall. It has villi on the embryo’s side and blood spaces on the mother’s side surrounding the villi, giving a large surface area for glucose and oxygen to pass from mother to embryo; wastes pass from the embryo to the mother’s blood.
