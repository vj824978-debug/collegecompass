CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q165 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
(a) Given below are certain situations. Analyse each and describe its possible impact : (i) A population of bacteria living in temperate waters whose temperature increased by global warming. (ii) The sperm encounters the egg when it reaches the oviduct in human females. (iii) Self pollination does not occur in a flower that contains only pistil. (iv) Egg does not get fertilised in a human female. (v) When the seed is placed under appropriate condition of water and air in the soil ? OR (b) Given below are certain situations. Analyse and describe what would happen when : (i) Spores are liberated from blob-like structures of the bread mould? (ii) Leaves of bryophyllum fall on wet soil ? (iii) A pollen from different species land on the stigma of totally unrelated species ? (iv) Copper-T is placed in the uterus of a human female ? (v) Spirogyra breaks into smaller fragments upon maturation ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Most bacteria would die; only the variants able to tolerate heat would survive and multiply — variation helps the species survive. (ii) Fertilisation takes place; a zygote is formed which divides and gets implanted in the uterine wall to develop into an embryo. (iii) The flower is unisexual (female); it needs cross-pollination by agents (wind, water, insects) bringing pollen from another flower. (iv) The thickened uterine lining is not needed; it breaks down and is shed with blood and mucus as menstruation (about every month). (v) The seed germinates: the embryo grows, the radicle forms the root and the plumule the shoot, giving a seedling. OR (b)(i) Spores, on landing on moist food (bread), germinate and grow into newRhizopus (bread mould) hyphae. (ii) Buds in the notches along the leaf margin develop into new plants (vegetative propagation). (iii) Fertilisation does not occur; the pollen tube does not grow/reach the ovule since the pollen is incompatible. (iv) It prevents implantation (pregnancy) — a contraceptive method; it does not protect against STDs and may cause irritation of the uterus. (v) Each fragment grows into a new individual (fragmentation).
