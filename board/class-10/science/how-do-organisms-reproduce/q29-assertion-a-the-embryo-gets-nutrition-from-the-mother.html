Question as text
Assertion (A) : The embryo gets nutrition from the mother’s blood with the help of a special tissue called placenta. Reason (R) : Placenta is a disc which is embedded in the uterine wall. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true, but R (structure/location of placenta) does not explain how nutrition passes; that is due to villi giving a large surface for exchange of glucose and oxygen.
