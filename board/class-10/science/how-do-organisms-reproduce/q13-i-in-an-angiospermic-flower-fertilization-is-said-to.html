CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q165 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
(A) (i) In an angiospermic flower, fertilization is said to take place when male gametes carried in the pollen tube fuse with the female gamete, present in the embryo sac. This results in lot of changes in different parts of the flower. State the changes taking place in the following structures of flower post fertilization : (a) Zygote (b) Ovule (c) Ovary (d) Sepals (ii) Define germination. OR (B) (i) Sugarcane does not produce seeds so name the process through which it will be able to reproduce. (ii) List any two advantages of this method. (iii) Besides sugarcane, give two more examples of plants that reproduce by this method. (iv) Why regeneration is not possible in all the types of animals ?
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) (a) Zygote divides repeatedly to form the embryo. (b) Ovule develops a tough coat and becomes the seed. (c) Ovary grows rapidly and ripens to form the fruit. (d) Sepals shrivel and fall off (may persist in some). (ii) Germination: the process by which the embryo in a seed develops into a seedling under suitable conditions (water, air, temperature). OR (B) (i) Vegetative propagation (by stem cuttings). (ii) Plants that do not produce viable seeds can be grown; plants bear flowers and fruits earlier than those from seeds; all plants are genetically similar to the parent, so desirable characters are preserved. (iii) Rose, grapes, banana, orange, potato, money plant (any two). (iv) Regeneration needs specialised cells that can divide and differentiate into all cell types; complex animals have highly specialised cells and organ systems and lack such cells, so they cannot regenerate the whole body.
