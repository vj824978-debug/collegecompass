Question as text
Draw labelled diagrams to show different stages of budding in Hydra.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: sequence of 4–5 Hydra figures – parent Hydra with a small outgrowth (bud) on the side; the bud enlarging and developing tentacles and mouth; the young Hydra detaching from the parent. Labels: parent Hydra, bud, tentacles, new (daughter) Hydra.
