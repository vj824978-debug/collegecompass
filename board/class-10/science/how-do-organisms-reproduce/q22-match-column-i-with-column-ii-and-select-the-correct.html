Question 22 · Class 10 Science · How do Organisms Reproduce?
Question as text
Match Column-I with Column-II and select the correct option from the choices provided. Column-I Column-II a. Site of fertilisation of egg with the sperm (i) Vagina b. Site of implantation of embryo (ii) Uterus c. Site of entry of sperm into the female reproductive tract (iii) Oviduct d. Site through which the waste materials generated by the developing embryo are removed (iv) Placenta (v) Cervix (A) a-(ii), b-(i), c-(v), d-(iv) (B) a-(iii), b-(i), c-(v), d-(iv) (C) a-(iv), b-(ii), c-(iii), d-(i) (D) a-(iii), b-(ii), c-(i), d-(iv)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) a-(iii), b-(ii), c-(i), d-(iv) – fertilisation in oviduct, implantation in uterus, sperms enter through vagina, wastes removed via placenta.
