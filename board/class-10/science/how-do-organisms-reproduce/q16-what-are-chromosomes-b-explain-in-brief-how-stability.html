Question as text
(a) What are chromosomes ? (b) Explain in brief how stability of DNA content of a species is ensured in sexually reproducing organisms ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Chromosomes are thread-like structures in the nucleus that carry hereditary information in the form of DNA (genes). (b) Germ cells (gametes) are formed by a special type of division in which the chromosome number (DNA content) is halved. When male and female gametes fuse at fertilisation, the zygote restores the original number, so the DNA content of the species stays the same from generation to generation.
