Question as text
The gene combination of purple flowered pea plants is denoted as (WW) and that of white flowered pea plants as (ww), when these two plants are crossed F 1 generation is obtained. (a) List two observations made by Mendel in F 1 generation plants. (b) Give the (i) percentage of white flowered plants and (ii) ratio of the gene combinations WW, Ww and ww in F 2 generation. (c) Write one difference between dominant and recessive trait.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) All F 1 plants had purple flowers; no white-flowered (or intermediate) plants appeared, i.e. only one parental trait was expressed (all were Ww). (b)(i) 25% white-flowered plants. (ii) WW : Ww : ww = 1 : 2 : 1. (c) A dominant trait is expressed even when only one copy of its gene is present (Ww); a recessive trait is expressed only when both copies are recessive (ww).
