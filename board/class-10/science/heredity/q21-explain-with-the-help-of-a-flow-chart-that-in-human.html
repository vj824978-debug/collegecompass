Question as text
Explain with the help of a flow chart that in human beings father is responsible for the sex (male or female) of the child.
Answer
Answer as text
Father (XY) produces two kinds of sperms: X or Y; mother (XX) produces only X eggs. Egg (X) + sperm (X) → XX→ girl; Egg (X) + sperm (Y) → XY→ boy. Since the child’s sex depends on whether the sperm carries X or Y, the father decides the sex of the child.
