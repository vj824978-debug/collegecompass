Question as text
A tall pea plant with round seeds (TTRR) is crossed with a short pea plant with wrinkled seeds (ttrr). The F1 generation will be (A) 25% tall with round seeds (B) 50% tall with wrinkled seeds (C) 75% tall with wrinkled seeds (D) 100% tall with round seeds
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 100% tall with round seeds. All F 1 plants are TtRr; tall and round are dominant.
