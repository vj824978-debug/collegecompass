Question as text
When a pure-tall pea plant is crossed with a pure-dwarf pea plant, the percentage of tall pea plants in F1 and F2 generation pea plants will be respectively: (A) 100%; 25% (B) 100%; 50% (C) 100%; 75% (D) 100%; 100%
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 100%; 75% — F 1 all Tt (tall); F 2 TT : Tt : tt = 1 : 2 : 1, so 3/4 tall.
