Assertion (A): In reptiles, the temperature at which the fertilized eggs are kept decides the sex of the offsprings. Reason (R): Sex is not genetically determined in some animals. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both true; in some reptiles sex is not genetically determined, so the incubation temperature decides it.
