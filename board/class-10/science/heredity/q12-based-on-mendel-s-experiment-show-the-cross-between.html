Question as text
Based on Mendel’s experiment show the cross between round, yellow seeds of pea plant (RRYY) with wrinkled and green seeds of pea plant (rryy) upto F 2 generation. Write the number of seeds/plants with following genetic (Genotype) makeup in F 2 generation : (i) RRYY (ii) rryy
Answer
Answer as text
Parents: RRYY× rryy; gametes RY and ry. F 1: RrYy (all round, yellow). F 1 selfed: RrYy × RrYy; gametes RY, Ry, rY, ry from each parent → 4× 4 checkerboard of 16 combinations. F 2 phenotypes: round yellow 9 : round green 3 : wrinkled yellow 3 : wrinkled green 1. (i) RRYY: 1 out of 16; (ii) rryy: 1 out of 16.
