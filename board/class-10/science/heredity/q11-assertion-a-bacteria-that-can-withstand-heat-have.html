Assertion (A) : Bacteria that can withstand heat have better chances of survival in a heat wave.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q81 mark
Question 11 · Class 10 Science · Heredity
Question as text
Assertion (A) : Bacteria that can withstand heat have better chances of survival in a heat wave. Reason (R) : Accumulation of variations in a species increases the chances of its survival in changing environment. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. The heat-resistance variation helps those bacteria survive the heat wave.
