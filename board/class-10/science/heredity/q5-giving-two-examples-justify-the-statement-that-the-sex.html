CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q143 marks
Question 5 · Class 10 Science · Heredity
Question as text
(a) Giving two examples, justify the statement that the sex of the offspring in all animals is not always genetically determined. (b) Differentiate between the sex chromosomes of male and female human beings.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) In some reptiles the temperature at which the fertilised eggs are kept determines whether the young will be male or female. In some animals like snails, individuals can change sex. So sex is not always genetically determined. (b) Human female: a perfect pair of sex chromosomes, both X (XX). Human male: a mismatched pair – one normal-sized X and one short Y (XY). Hence all ova carry X, while half the sperms carry X and half Y.
