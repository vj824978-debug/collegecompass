“Proteins control the expression of various characters.” Explain this statement by taking an example of…

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q232 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Science · Heredity
Question as text
(a) “Proteins control the expression of various characters.” Explain this statement by taking an example of “tallness” as a characteristic in plants. OR (b) Explain the mechanism of inheritance used by sexually reproducing organisms to ensure the stability of DNA of the species.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A gene (section of DNA) provides information to make a protein, often an enzyme. Tallness depends on the amount of a plant growth hormone, whose quantity depends on how efficiently the enzyme for making it works. If the gene gives an efficient enzyme, more hormone is made and the plant is tall; if the gene is altered and the enzyme is less efficient, less hormone is made and the plant is short. OR (b) Germ cells (gametes) are formed by a special division in which the chromosome number (and DNA content) is halved, so each gamete carries only one set of chromosomes (one copy of each gene). When male and female gametes fuse at fertilisation, the normal number of chromosomes is restored in the zygote. Thus the amount of DNA stays constant from generation to generation.
