Question 2 · Class 10 Science · Heredity
Question as text
Mendel took garden pea plants with different characteristics, such as height to study the inheritance pattern of factors (genes). He crossed tall pea plant with short pea plant and obtained all the tall plants in the F 1 generation. Answer the following questions : (a) Why only tall pea plants were observed in F 1 progeny ? [1] (b) By which method did Mendel obtain F 2 progeny ? [1] (c) (i) Write one difference between dominant and recessive trait. [2] OR (c) (ii) Write two observations made by Mendel about F 1 progeny. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Tallness (T) is dominant over shortness (t); the F 1 plants (Tt) carry both factors but only the dominant trait is expressed. (b) By self-pollination (selfing) of the F 1 tall plants. (c)(i) A dominant trait is expressed even when only one copy of its factor is present (TT or Tt), whereas a recessive trait is expressed only when both copies are recessive (tt); the recessive trait remains hidden in the F 1. OR (c)(ii) (1) All F 1 plants were tall; no plants of medium or intermediate height were seen. (2) Only one of the parental traits appeared; the short trait was not seen in F 1 but was not lost (it reappeared in F 2).
