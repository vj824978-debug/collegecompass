Observe the given ray diagram showing refraction through a prism and choose the correct option for colour of rays 1 and 2: Colour of ray 1 Colour of ray 2 (A) Blue Red (B) Green Yellow (C) Red Violet (D) Violet Red
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Red, Violet. Red light bends (deviates) the least and violet the most, so ray 1 (least deviated) is red and ray 2 (most deviated) is violet.
