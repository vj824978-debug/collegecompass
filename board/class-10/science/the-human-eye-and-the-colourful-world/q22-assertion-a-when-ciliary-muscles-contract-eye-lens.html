Assertion (A): When ciliary muscles contract, eye lens becomes thin.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q201 mark
Question 22 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
Assertion (A): When ciliary muscles contract, eye lens becomes thin. Reason (R): Ciliary muscles control the power of the eye lens. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A is false — when ciliary muscles contract the lens becomes thicker (focal length decreases); R is true.
