Draw the ray diagram for refraction of light through a glass prism and mark angle of refraction and angle of…

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q353 marks
Question 15 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
(a) Draw the ray diagram for refraction of light through a glass prism and mark angle of refraction and angle of deviation. (b) When the path of a light ray refracted through a glass prism is reversed how will the angle of deviation change ? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: triangular glass prism with incident ray PE, refracted ray EF bending towards the normal, emergent ray FS bending away from the normal; mark angle of incidence ∠i, angle of refraction ∠r, angle of emergence ∠e and angle of deviation ∠D between the extended incident ray and the emergent ray; angle of prism A. (b) The angle of deviation remains the same. By the principle of reversibility of light the ray retraces its path: the old emergent ray becomes the incident ray, so the angles of incidence and emergence just interchange and the deviation is unchanged.
