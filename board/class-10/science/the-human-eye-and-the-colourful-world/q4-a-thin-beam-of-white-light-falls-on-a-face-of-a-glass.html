Question as text
A thin beam of white light falls on a face of a glass prism and a spectrum is obtained on a screen. Which of the following options is the correct sequence of colours from top to bottom on the screen? (A) Red, Green, Yellow, Violet, Blue (B) Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet (C) Violet, Blue, Green, Yellow, Red (D) Violet, Red, Green, Yellow, Blue
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet – red bends least (top), violet bends most (bottom).
