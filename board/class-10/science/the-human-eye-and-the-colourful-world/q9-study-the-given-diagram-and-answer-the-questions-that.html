Question as text
Study the given diagram and answer the questions that follow: (a) Write the name of the eye defect shown in the diagram. Where is the image formed in this eye defect with respect to the retina of the eye? (b) List two causes of this eye defect. (c) With the help of a diagram, show how this eye defect of vision is corrected.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Hypermetropia (far-sightedness). Light from the near point N ′ converges behind the retina, i.e. the image is formed beyond the retina. (b) Causes: (i) the focal length of the eye lens is too long; (ii) the eyeball has become too small. (c) Corrected by using a convex (converging) lens of suitable power. Diagram: a convex lens placed in front of the eye; rays from an object at N (25 cm) are made to appear to come from N ′ and are then focused on the retina.
