The students in a class took a thick sheet of cardboard and made a small hole in its centre.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q394 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
The students in a class took a thick sheet of cardboard and made a small hole in its centre. Sunlight was allowed to fall on this small hole and they obtained a narrow beam of white light. A glass prism was taken and this white light was allowed to fall on one of its faces. The prism was turned slowly until the light that comes out of the opposite face of the prism appeared on the nearby screen. They studied this beautiful band of light and concluded that it is a spectrum of white light. (i) Give any one more instance in which this type of spectrum is observed. [1] (ii) What happens to white light in the above case? [1] (iii) (A) List two conditions necessary to observe a rainbow. [2] OR (iii) (B) Draw a ray diagram to show the formation of a rainbow. Mark on it, points (a), (b) and (c) as given below: [2] (a) Where dispersion of light occurs. (b) Where light gets reflected internally. (c) Where final refraction occurs.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Rainbow in the sky after a rain shower (also: sunlight through a fountain/water spray). (ii) Dispersion: white light splits into its seven component colours (VIBGYOR), since different colours bend through different angles in the prism (violet the most, red the least). (iii)(A) The sun must be behind the observer; water droplets (e.g. after rain) must be present in the atmosphere in front of the observer. (iii)(B) Diagram: sunlight entering a spherical raindrop — (a) at the first surface it is refracted and dispersed; (b) at the back surface the light is internally reflected; (c) at the front surface it is refracted again as it comes out, the red and violet rays reaching the observer’s eye.
