In the given figure the angle of incidence and the angle of deviation respectively are : (A) 1 and 5 (B) 7 and 6 (C) 7 and 4 (D) 1 and 6
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1 and 6. Angle 1 is between the incident ray and the normal; angle 6 is between the produced incident ray and the produced emergent ray.
