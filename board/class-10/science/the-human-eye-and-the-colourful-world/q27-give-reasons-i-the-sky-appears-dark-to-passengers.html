Give reasons : (i) The sky appears dark to passengers flying at very high altitude.

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q252 marks
Question 27 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
(a) Give reasons : (i) The sky appears dark to passengers flying at very high altitude. (ii) ‘Danger’ signal lights are red in colour. OR (b) What is a rainbow ? “We see a rainbow in the sky only after the rainfall.” Why ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) At very high altitude there is almost no atmosphere (air molecules, fine particles) to scatter sunlight, so no scattered light reaches the eye and the sky looks dark. (ii) Red light has the longest wavelength and is least scattered by fog or smoke, so it can be seen in the same colour from a large distance. OR (b) A rainbow is a natural spectrum appearing in the sky after a rain shower, in the direction opposite to the sun. It forms by dispersion of sunlight by tiny water droplets in the atmosphere, which act like small prisms (refraction and dispersion, internal reflection, refraction again). Such droplets are present in the air only just after rainfall, so we see a rainbow only then.
