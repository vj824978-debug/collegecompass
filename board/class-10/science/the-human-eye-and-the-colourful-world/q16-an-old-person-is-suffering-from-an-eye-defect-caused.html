An old person is suffering from an eye defect caused by weakening of ciliary muscles and diminishing flexibility of the eye lens. If the defect of vision is ‘a’ which can be corrected by lens ‘b’, then ‘a’ and ‘b’ respectively are : (A) hypermetropia and convex lens (B) presbyopia and bifocal lens (C) myopia and concave lens (D) myopia and bifocal lens
Answer
Answer as text
(B) presbyopia and bifocal lens.
