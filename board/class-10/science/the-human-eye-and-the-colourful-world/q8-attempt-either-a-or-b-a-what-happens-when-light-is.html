Attempt either (a) or (b): (a) What happens when light is scattered from (i) very fine particles and (ii) very large size particles of medium? Justify your answer in each case. OR (b) With the help of a ray diagram, explain any phenomenon of formation of spectrum in nature.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Very fine particles scatter mainly blue light (shorter wavelengths), because the scattering by small particles is more for shorter wavelengths; e.g. blue colour of the clear sky. (ii) Very large particles scatter light of all wavelengths nearly equally, so the scattered light appears white; e.g. clouds appear white. OR (b) Rainbow: a natural spectrum seen in the sky opposite the sun after a rain shower. Tiny water droplets act like small prisms: sunlight is refracted and dispersed on entering a droplet, internally reflected at the back of the droplet, and refracted again on coming out, so the different colours emerge separately and reach the observer (red on top, violet at bottom). Diagram: sunlight entering a raindrop, splitting into colours, internal reflection, and red and violet rays emerging towards the observer.
