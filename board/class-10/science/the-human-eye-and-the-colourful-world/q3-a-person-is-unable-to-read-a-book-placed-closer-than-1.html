A person is unable to read a book placed closer than 1 meter from his eyes.

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q353 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
A person is unable to read a book placed closer than 1 meter from his eyes. Identify the defect of vision in his eyes. Draw the ray diagrams to show the defect of vision and its correction.
Answer
Answer as text
Defect: hypermetropia (far-sightedness); his near point has receded to 1 m (beyond 25 cm). Cause: focal length of eye lens too long or eyeball too short. Diagram 1 (defect): rays from an object at N (25 cm) converge behind the retina; the near point N′ (1 m) is shown, rays from N ′ focus on the retina. Diagram 2 (correction): a convex lens placed in front of the eye makes rays from N (25 cm) appear to come from N ′, so the image is formed on the retina.
