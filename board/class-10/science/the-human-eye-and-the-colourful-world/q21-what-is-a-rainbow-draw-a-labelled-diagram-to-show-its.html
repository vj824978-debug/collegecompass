What is a rainbow ? Draw a labelled diagram to show its formation.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q313 marks
Question 21 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
What is a rainbow ? Draw a labelled diagram to show its formation.
Answer
Answer as text
A rainbow is a natural spectrum of sunlight appearing in the sky (opposite the Sun) after a rain shower, caused by dispersion of sunlight by tiny water droplets acting as small prisms. Light is refracted and dispersed on entering a droplet, internally reflected, and refracted again on leaving. Diagram: sunlight entering a raindrop; labels – sunlight, raindrop, refraction and dispersion, internal reflection, refraction at exit, red and violet rays emerging towards the observer.
