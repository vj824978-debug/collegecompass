Explain the phenomenon of rainbow formation in the sky.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q363 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
Explain the phenomenon of rainbow formation in the sky. Draw the labelled ray diagram in support of your explanation.
Answer
Answer as text
A rainbow is a natural spectrum seen in the sky opposite the Sun after a shower. Tiny water droplets act like small prisms: sunlight is refracted and dispersed on entering a droplet, then internally reflected at the back surface, and refracted again on coming out. Because different colours bend by different amounts, the light separates into VIBGYOR, with red on the outer side and violet on the inner side. Diagram: a spherical raindrop with sunlight (white light) entering, dispersion at the first surface, internal reflection at the back, refraction at exit; labels – sunlight, raindrop, refraction and dispersion, internal reflection, red and violet emergent rays, observer.
