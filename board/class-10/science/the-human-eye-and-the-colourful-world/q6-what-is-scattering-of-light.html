What is scattering of light?

CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q363 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
What is scattering of light? How is scattering of light different from reflection of light? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
Scattering: when light falls on very small particles (molecules, fine dust, colloidal particles), it is sent off (spread) in all directions; the colour of scattered light depends on particle size – very fine particles scatter mainly shorter wavelengths (blue), e.g. blue sky, Tyndall effect. Difference: in reflection, light falling on a (large, smooth) surface bounces back in a definite direction obeying the laws of reflection (∠i = ∠r), and all colours are reflected alike. In scattering, light is redirected in all directions by tiny particles, it follows no such law, and the extent of scattering depends on the wavelength of light and the size of the particles.
