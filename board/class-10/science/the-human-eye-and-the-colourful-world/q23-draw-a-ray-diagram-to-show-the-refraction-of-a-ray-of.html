Draw a ray diagram to show the refraction of a ray of light passing through an equilateral glass prism.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q252 marks
Question 23 · Class 10 Science · The Human Eye and the Colourful World
Question as text
(A) Draw a ray diagram to show the refraction of a ray of light passing through an equilateral glass prism. Mark the angle through which the emergent ray bends from the direction of the incident ray and also name it. OR (B) Name the type of lenses required by the persons for the correction of their defect of vision called presbyopia. Write the structure of the lenses commonly used for the correction of this defect giving reason for such designs.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Diagram: triangular prism; incident ray, refracted ray bending towards the normal at the first face and emergent ray bending away from the normal at the second face (towards the base); incident ray produced forward and emergent ray produced backward meet; the angle between them marked ∠D — the angle of deviation . (B) Convex lenses (for near vision); often bi-focal lenses are needed. A bifocal lens has a concave lens in the upper portion (for distant vision) and a convex lens in the lower portion (for near vision). Reason: such persons often suffer from both myopia and hypermetropia.
