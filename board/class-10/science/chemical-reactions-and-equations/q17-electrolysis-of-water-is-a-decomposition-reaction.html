Electrolysis of water is a decomposition reaction.

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q11 mark
Question 17 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
Electrolysis of water is a decomposition reaction. The mass ratio ( MH : MO) of hydrogen and oxygen gases liberated at the electrodes during electrolysis of water is : (A) 8 : 1 (B) 2 : 1 (C) 1 : 2 (D) 1 : 8
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1 : 8. 2 H 2O−−→2 H2 + O2: 4 g of H 2 to 32 g of O 2, i.e. 1 : 8 by mass (volume ratio is 2 : 1).
