Question as text
Account for the following: (a) White silver chloride turns grey when exposed to sunlight. (b) Lead (II) nitrate on heating releases brown fumes. (c) Bags of oil and fat containing food items are flushed with nitrogen gas.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Silver chloride decomposes in sunlight (photolytic decomposition) to grey silver: 2 AgCl (s) sunlight −−−−−→2 Ag(s) + Cl2(g). (b) On heating it decomposes and gives brown nitrogen dioxide gas: 2 Pb(NO 3)2(s) heat−−→2 PbO (s) + 4 NO2(g) + O2(g). (c) To prevent rancidity: nitrogen is unreactive and displaces oxygen, so the oils and fats are not oxidised and the food does not develop a bad smell and taste.
