The gases evolved on heating lead (II) nitrate crystals are : (A) NO and O 2 (B) N2 and NO2 (C) NO2 and H2 (D) NO2 and O2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) NO2 and O2: 2 Pb(NO3)2 ∆−−→2 PbO + 4 NO2 + O2.
