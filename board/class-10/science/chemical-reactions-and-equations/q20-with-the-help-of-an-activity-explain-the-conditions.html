With the help of an activity, explain the conditions under which iron articles get rusted.

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q28(a)3 marks
Question 20 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
With the help of an activity, explain the conditions under which iron articles get rusted.
Answer
Answer as text
Take three test tubes with clean iron nails. A: nails in ordinary tap water, cork it. B: nails in boiled distilled water with a layer of oil on top (no air), cork it. C: nails with anhydrous calcium chloride (dry air, no moisture), cork it. After a few days only the nails in A rust. Conclusion: iron rusts only when both air (oxygen) and water (moisture) are present.
