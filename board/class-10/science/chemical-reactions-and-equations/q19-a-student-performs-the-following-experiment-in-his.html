Question as text
A student performs the following experiment in his school laboratory. List two observations to justify that in this experiment a chemical change has taken place.
Answer
Answer as text
Zn + H2SO4 −−→ZnSO4 + H2↑ . Observations: (i) bubbles of a gas (hydrogen) are evolved around the zinc granules (effervescence); (ii) the flask becomes warm (change in temperature); the zinc granules also gradually get smaller.
