Question 21 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
The correct balanced chemical equation showing exothermic reaction in which natural gas burns in air is : (A) CH4 + O2−−→CO2 + 2 H2O (B) CH4 + 2 O2−−→2 CO2 + 2 H2O + Energy (C) CH4 + 2 O2−−→CO2 + 2 H2O (D) CH4 + 2 O2−−→CO2 + 2 H2O + Energy
Answer
Answer as text
(D) CH4 +2 O2−−→CO2 +2 H2O + Energy. It is balanced and shows heat released (exothermic); (B) is unbalanced.
