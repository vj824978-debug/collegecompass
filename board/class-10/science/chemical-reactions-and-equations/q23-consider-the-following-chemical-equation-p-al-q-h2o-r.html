Consider the following chemical equation : p Al + q H2O−→ r Al2O3 + s H2 To balance this chemical equation, the values of ‘p’, ‘q’, ‘r’ and ‘s’ must be respectively : (A) 3, 2, 2, 1 (B) 2, 3, 3, 1 (C) 2, 3, 1, 3 (D) 3, 1, 2, 2
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 2, 3, 1, 3; balanced equation: 2 Al + 3 H 2O−−→Al2O3 + 3 H2.
