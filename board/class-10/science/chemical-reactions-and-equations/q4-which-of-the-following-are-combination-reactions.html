Which of the following are combination reactions?

CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q171 mark
Question 4 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
Which of the following are combination reactions? (i) 2 KClO3−−→2 KCl + 3 O2 (ii) MgO + H2O−−→Mg(OH)2 (iii) 4 Al + 3 O2−−→2 Al2O3 (iv) Zn + FeSO4−−→ZnSO4 + Fe Options: (A) (i) and (iii) (B) (iii) and (iv) (C) (ii) and (iv) (D) (ii) and (iii)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) (ii) and (iii) – two reactants combine to form a single product. (i) is decomposition, (iv) is displacement.
