Which of the following is an example of a displacement reaction?

CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q171 mark
Question 8 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
Which of the following is an example of a displacement reaction? (A) The combustion of natural gas (B) The reaction of a reactive metal with dilute acid (C) The electrolysis of water (D) The reaction of quick lime with water
Answer
Answer as text
(B) A reactive metal displaces hydrogen from a dilute acid, e.g. Zn + H 2SO4−−→ZnSO4 + H2.
