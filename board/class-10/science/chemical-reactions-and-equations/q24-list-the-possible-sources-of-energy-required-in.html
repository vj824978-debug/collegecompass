List the possible sources of energy required in decomposition reactions.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q212 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
(a) List the possible sources of energy required in decomposition reactions. Illustrate any one with a suitable example. OR (b) What is observed when hydrated ferrous sulphate crystals are heated in a dry boiling tube ? Give balanced chemical equation(s) of the reactions(s) that occur(s).
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Heat (thermal decomposition), light (photolytic decomposition) and electricity (electrolytic decomposition). Example (heat): CaCO 3 heat−−→CaO + CO2; (light): 2 AgCl sunlight −−−−−→2 Ag + Cl2; (electricity): 2 H2O electricity −−−−−−→2 H2 + O2. OR (b) Green crystals first lose water of crystallisation and turn white (anhydrous); on further heating a reddish-brown solid (Fe 2O3) remains and gases with a smell of burning sulphur are given off. FeSO 4· 7 H2O heat−−→FeSO4 + 7 H2O; 2 FeSO 4 heat−−→Fe2O3 + SO2 + SO3.
