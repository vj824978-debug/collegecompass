Select from the following a statement which is not true about burning of magnesium ribbon in air : (A) It burns with a dazzling white flame. (B) A white powder is formed on burning. (C) It is an endothermic reaction. (D) It is an example of a combination reaction.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) It is an endothermic reaction. Burning of Mg is exothermic: 2 Mg + O 2−−→2 MgO + heat and light.
