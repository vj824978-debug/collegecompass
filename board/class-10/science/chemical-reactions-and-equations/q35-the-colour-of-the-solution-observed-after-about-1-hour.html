The colour of the solution observed after about 1 hour of placing iron nails in copper sulphate solution is (A) Blue (B) Pale green (C) Yellow (D) Reddish brown
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Pale green, due to formation of FeSO 4: Fe + CuSO4−−→FeSO4 + Cu.
