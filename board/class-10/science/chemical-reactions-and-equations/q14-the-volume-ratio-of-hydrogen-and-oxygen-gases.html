The volume ratio of hydrogen and oxygen gases liberated during electrolysis of water is : (A) 1 : 2 (B) 2 : 1 (C) 1 : 3 (D) 4 : 1
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 2 : 1, since 2 H 2O−−→2 H2 + O2.
