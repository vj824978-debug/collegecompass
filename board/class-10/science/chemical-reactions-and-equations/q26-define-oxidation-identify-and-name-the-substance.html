Define oxidation. Identify and name the substance oxidised in the following reaction: CuO + H2−−→Cu + H2O
Answer
Answer as text
Oxidation: gain of oxygen (or loss of hydrogen) by a substance during a reaction. Substance oxidised: hydrogen (H 2), which gains oxygen to form water. (CuO loses oxygen, i.e. is reduced to Cu.)
