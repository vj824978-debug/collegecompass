What happens when (i) lead (II) nitrate is heated ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q263 marks
Question 16 · Class 10 Science · Chemical Reactions and Equations
Question as text
What happens when (i) lead (II) nitrate is heated ? (ii) iron (II) sulphate is heated ? (iii) silver chloride is exposed to sunlight ? Write balanced chemical equations of the reactions involved in support of your answer.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) It decomposes giving yellow lead oxide, brown fumes of nitrogen dioxide and oxygen: 2 Pb(NO3)2 heat−−→2 PbO + 4 NO2 + O2. (ii) Green crystals lose water, then decompose to ferric oxide with SO2 and SO3 (smell of burning sulphur): 2 FeSO4 heat−−→Fe2O3 + SO2 + SO3. (iii) White silver chloride turns grey as it decomposes to silver and chlorine: 2 AgCl sunlight −−−−−→2 Ag+ Cl2.
