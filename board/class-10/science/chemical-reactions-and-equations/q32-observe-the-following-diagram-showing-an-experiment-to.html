Question as text
Observe the following diagram showing an experiment to determine the conditions under which a metal ‘M’ corrodes. List your observations in each of the three cases A, B and C with reason, if the metal ‘M’ is generally protected against corrosion by the method of galvanisation.
Answer
Answer as text
M is iron (iron nails), which rusts in the presence of both air and moisture. A: nails get rusted — they are exposed to both air and water (moisture). B: no rusting — the layer of oil keeps air (and moisture) from reaching the nails (NCERT set-up: boiled water covered with oil, so no dissolved air). C: no rusting — the air is dry (anhydrous calcium chloride absorbs moisture), so there is no water. Conclusion: both air and moisture are needed for iron to corrode.
