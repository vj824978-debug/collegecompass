Select from the following a hydrocarbon having one C–C bond and one C ≡C bond: (A) Benzene (B) Cyclohexane (C) Butyne (D) Propyne
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Propyne, CH3 –C –––CH: one C–C single bond and one C≡C triple bond (butyne has two C–C bonds).
