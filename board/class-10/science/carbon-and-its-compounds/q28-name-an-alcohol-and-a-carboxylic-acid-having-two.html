Name an alcohol and a carboxylic acid having two carbon atoms in their structures.

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q345 marks
Question 28 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
(a) Name an alcohol and a carboxylic acid having two carbon atoms in their structures. Draw their structures and state how this alcohol can be converted into a carboxylic acid. What happens when these two compounds react in the presence of an acid ? Write chemical equations for the reactions involved in the two cases mentioned above. OR (b) What are soaps ? Write the structure of a soap molecule. Explain the cleansing action of a soap. Why are soaps not considered suitable for washing clothes in a region where water is hard ? How is this problem overcomed ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Ethanol, CH 3CH2OH, and ethanoic acid, CH 3COOH. Structures: ethanol H C H H C H H O H ethanoic acid H C H H C O O H Conversion: ethanol is oxidised by alkaline potassium permanganate (or acidified potassium dichromate) on heating: CH3CH2OH alk · KMnO4 + heat−−−−−−−−−−−−→CH3COOH With an acid catalyst (conc. H 2SO4), they react to form a sweet-smelling ester, ethyl ethanoate (esterification): CH3COOH + CH3CH2OH acid−−→CH3COOCH2CH3 + H2O OR (b) Soaps are sodium or potassium salts of long-chain carboxylic acids (fatty acids), e.g. sodium stearate C17H35COO– Na+. Structure: a long hydrocarbon tail (hydrophobic, dissolves in oil) attached to an ionic –COO – Na+ head (hydrophilic, dissolves in water). Cleansing action: in water, soap molecules form micelles: the tails point inward towards the oily dirt and the ionic heads face the water. The oily dirt is trapped in the centre of the micelle, which stays dispersed in water and is rinsed away. Hard water contains calcium and magnesium salts, which react with soap to form an insoluble precipitate (scum); much soap is wasted and clothes are not cleaned well. Remedy: use detergents (sulphonate or ammonium salts of long-chain carboxylic acids), whose charged ends do not form insoluble precipitates with Ca 2+ and Mg2+ ions.
