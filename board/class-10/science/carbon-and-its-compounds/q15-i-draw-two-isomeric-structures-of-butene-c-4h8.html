(i) Draw two isomeric structures of Butene (C 4H8).

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q345 marks
Question 15 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
(a) (i) Draw two isomeric structures of Butene (C 4H8). (ii) Name the following compounds : (iii) Write the chemical equations for the following reactions. Mention one essential condition each for these reactions to take place. (I) Ethanol undergoes complete oxidation (II) Propene undergoes hydrogenation (III) Ethanoic acid reacts with ethanol OR (b) (i) A carbon compound X is a good solvent. On reaction with sodium, X forms two products Y and Z. Z is used to convert vegetable oil into vegetable ghee. Identify and name X, Y and Z. Also write the equation of reaction of X with sodium to justify your answer. (ii) Write chemical equation to show what happens when ethanol : (I) burns in oxygen/air. (II) is heated at 443 K in excess conc. H 2SO4. (III) reacts with acidified potassium dichromate.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) But-1-ene CH2 ––CH–CH 2 –CH 3 and but-2-ene CH3 –CH ––CH–CH 3 (also 2-methylpropene (CH3)2C––CH2). (ii) (I) Chloropropane (1-chloropropane); (II) Butanone (butan-2-one). (iii) (I) C2H5OH + 3 O2−−→2 CO2 + 3 H2O + heat and light; condition: combustion in sufficient oxygen/air (ignition). [Or oxidation to acid: CH 3CH2OH alk · KMnO4 / acidified K2Cr2O7 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−−−−−−−→heat CH3COOH.] (II) CH3 –CH ––CH2 + H2 Ni−−→CH3 –CH 2 –CH 3; condition: nickel (or palladium) catalyst. (III) CH3COOH + C2H5OH conc · H2SO4 −−−−−−−−→CH3COOC2H5 + H2O; condition: warming with a few drops of conc. H 2SO4 (acid catalyst). OR (b) (i) X = ethanol (C 2H5OH), Y = sodium ethoxide (C 2H5ONa), Z = hydrogen (H 2, used in hydrogenation of oils with Ni catalyst). 2 Na + 2 CH 3CH2OH−−→2 CH3CH2ONa + H2↑ . (ii) (I) C 2H5OH + 3 O2−−→2 CO2 + 3 H2O + heat and light. (II) CH 3 –CH 2OH hot conc· H2SO4 −−−−−−−−−−→443 K CH2 ––CH2 + H2O (dehydration to ethene). (III) CH 3CH2OH acidified K2Cr2O7 −−−−−−−−−−−→CH3COOH (oxidation to ethanoic acid; orange solution turns green).
