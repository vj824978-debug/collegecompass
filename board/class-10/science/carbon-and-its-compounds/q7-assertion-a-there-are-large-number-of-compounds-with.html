Assertion (A): There are large number of compounds with many carbon atoms linked to each other.

CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q241 mark
Question 7 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
Assertion (A): There are large number of compounds with many carbon atoms linked to each other. Reason (R): Carbon-Carbon bond is very strong and stable. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. Carbon shows catenation because the C–C bond is very strong and stable, giving a large number of compounds with long chains of carbon atoms.
