(i) Give reasons for the following : (I) Covalent compounds are poor conductor of electricity.

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q295 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
(a) (i) Give reasons for the following : [3] (I) Covalent compounds are poor conductor of electricity. (II) Soap does not form lather in hard water. (III) Carbon shows catenation but silicon does not. (ii) Write chemical equations for the following : [2] (I) Oxidation of ethanol by acidified K 2Cr2O7. (II) Hydrogenation of ethene. OR (b) Mohan heated ethanol with a compound ‘X’ in the presence of a few drops of conc. H 2SO4 and observed a sweet smelling compound ‘Y’ is formed. When ‘Y’ is treated with sodium hydroxide it gives back ethanol and a compound ‘Z’. [5] (i) Identify ‘X’, ‘Y’ and ‘Z’. (ii) Write the role of conc. H 2SO4 in the reaction. (iii) Write the chemical equations involved and name the reactions.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) (I) Bonding is by sharing of electrons, so no ions are formed; hence they do not conduct electricity. (II) Calcium and magnesium salts in hard water react with soap to form an insoluble scum (precipitate), so soap is wasted and lather does not form. (III) The C–C bond is very strong and stable because carbon atoms are small; silicon atoms are larger, so Si–Si bonds are weak and silicon forms chains of only up to 7–8 atoms, which are reactive. (ii) (I) CH 3CH2OH acidified K 2Cr2O7 −−−−−−−−−−−→heat CH3COOH (ethanol to ethanoic acid) (II) CH2 ––CH2 + H2 Ni catalyst −−−−−−→CH3 –CH 3 OR (b)(i) X = ethanoic acid (CH 3COOH); Y = ethyl ethanoate (CH 3COOC2H5), an ester; Z = sodium ethanoate (CH 3COONa). (ii) Conc. H 2SO4 acts as a catalyst (and dehydrating agent, removing the water formed). (iii) CH3COOH + C2H5OH conc. H 2SO4 −−−−−−−−→CH3COOC2H5 + H2O — esterification. CH3COOC2H5 + NaOH−−→C2H5OH + CH3COONa — saponification (alkaline hydrolysis of ester).
