Which of the following will not undergo addition reaction ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q211 mark
Question 13 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
Which of the following will not undergo addition reaction ? (A) C4H8 (B) C2H2 (C) C3H8 (D) C2H4
Answer
Answer as text
(C) C3H8 (propane) is saturated, so it does not undergo addition.
