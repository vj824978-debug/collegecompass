Assertion (A): Carbon compounds can form chains, branched and ring structures.

CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q241 mark
Question 4 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
Assertion (A): Carbon compounds can form chains, branched and ring structures. Reason (R): Carbon exhibits the property of catenation. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both true and R explains A: catenation (ability to link with other carbon atoms) gives chains, branches and rings.
