Attempt either (a) or (b): (a) (i) What is homologous series? Which two of the following organic compounds belong to the same homologous series? C2H4, C4H10, C3H4, C4H8 (ii) Write the product(s) in the following reactions: (I) CH3 –CH 2 –OH + CH3COOH Acid−−−→ (II) C2H5OH + O2 Burning −−−−−→ (iii) Why does micelle formation take place when soap is added to water? [2+2+1=5] OR (b) (i) Name the following compounds: (I) (II) (ii) Name the reaction which is commonly used in the conversion of vegetable oils to fats. (iii) Why is the melting point of C 4H10 higher than C 3H8? (iv) What happens when a piece of sodium is dropped into ethanol? (v) Write the products in the following reaction: [5×1=5] CH3COOC2H5 + NaOH−−→
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) A homologous series is a series of compounds in which the same functional group substitutes for hydrogen in a carbon chain; successive members differ by a –CH 2 – unit (14 u), have similar chemical properties and show a gradation in physical properties. C 2H4 and C 4H8 (alkenes, CnH2n) belong to the same series. (ii) (I) Ethyl ethanoate (ester) and water: CH 3CH2OH + CH3COOH acid−−→CH3COOCH2CH3 + H2O (esterification). (II) Carbon dioxide and water: C 2H5OH + 3 O2−−→2 CO2 + 3 H2O (+ heat and light). (iii) A soap molecule has an ionic (hydrophilic) end that dissolves in water and a long hydrocarbon (hydrophobic) tail that does not. In water the molecules arrange themselves in clusters with the tails inside, away from water, and the ionic ends on the surface facing water – this cluster is a micelle. OR (b) (i) (I) Propanone (acetone); (II) Propyne. (ii) Hydrogenation (addition of hydrogen in presence of nickel/palladium catalyst). (iii) Both belong to the same homologous series (alkanes); melting point increases with increase in molecular mass. C 4H10 has a higher molecular mass (58 u) than C 3H8 (44 u) and larger size, so stronger intermolecular forces. (iv) Sodium reacts to form sodium ethoxide and hydrogen gas is evolved (brisk effervescence): 2 Na + 2 CH3CH2OH−−→2 CH3CH2O– Na+ + H2. (v) Sodium ethanoate and ethanol (saponification): CH 3COOC2H5 + NaOH−−→CH3COONa + C2H5OH.
