Given below are the structures of some hydrocarbons. Select the two structures which are related to each other from the given options : (A) (i) and (iv) (B) (ii) and (iv) (C) (ii) and (iii) (D) (i) and (iii)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (ii) and (iii) – both have formula C 4H10 (butane and isobutane/2-methylpropane); they are structural isomers.
