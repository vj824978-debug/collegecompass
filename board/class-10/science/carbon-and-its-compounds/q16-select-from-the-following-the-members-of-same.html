(a) Select from the following the members of same homologous series : (b) What happens to (i) the melting point, and (ii) the solubility of compounds as the molecular mass of the compounds in a homologous series increases ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (1) and (4): both are aldehydes (–CHO), propanal C 2H5CHO and butanal C 3H7CHO, differing by a –CH 2 – unit. ((2) is a ketone, (3) a carboxylic acid.) (b) (i) Melting point increases. (ii) Solubility in a particular solvent shows a gradation; solubility in water decreases.
