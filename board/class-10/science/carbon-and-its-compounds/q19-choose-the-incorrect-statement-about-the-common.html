Choose the incorrect statement about the common reaction used in hydrogenation of vegetable oils.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q51 mark
Question 19 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
Choose the incorrect statement about the common reaction used in hydrogenation of vegetable oils. (A) It is an addition reaction. (B) It takes place in the presence of nickel or palladium catalyst. (C) The product contains only single bonds between carbon atoms. (D) It is an addition reaction which occurs in the presence of an acid catalyst.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) – hydrogenation uses a nickel/palladium catalyst, not an acid catalyst.
