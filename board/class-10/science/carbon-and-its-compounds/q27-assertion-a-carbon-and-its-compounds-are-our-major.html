Assertion (A) : Carbon and its compounds are our major sources of fuels.

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q171 mark
Question 27 · Class 10 Science · Carbon and its Compounds
Question as text
Assertion (A) : Carbon and its compounds are our major sources of fuels. Reason (R) : Most of the carbon compounds on burning release a large amount of heat and light. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A). Both true; carbon compounds are used as fuels because they release a lot of heat and light on burning.
