CBSE 2025QP 31/5/1Q232 marks
Question 44 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
(A) How is lymph formed ? State its important function. OR (B) (a) Identify ‘X’ in the figure of human nephron shown below. What role does it play in the process of urine formation ? (b) Why some substances are selectively reabsorbed from the initial filtrate of urine, as it flows along the tabular part of nephron ?
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Some plasma, proteins and blood cells escape through pores in the walls of capillaries into the intercellular spaces to form tissue fluid, i.e. lymph. Functions: it carries digested and absorbed fat from the intestine and drains excess fluid from the intercellular spaces back into the blood. OR (B) (a) X is Bowman’s capsule. It collects the filtrate (water, glucose, amino acids, salts, urea) filtered from the blood in the glomerulus — the first step of urine formation. (b) The initial filtrate contains useful substances such as glucose, amino acids, salts and a large amount of water; these are selectively reabsorbed (according to the body’s need) so that they are not lost from the body.
