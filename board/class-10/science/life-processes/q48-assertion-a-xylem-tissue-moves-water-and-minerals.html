Assertion (A) : Xylem tissue moves water and minerals obtained from the soil by the roots.

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q181 mark
Question 48 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Assertion (A) : Xylem tissue moves water and minerals obtained from the soil by the roots. Reason (R) : Xylem tissue is found only in the roots of a plant. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C). Xylem is present in roots, stems and leaves (continuous channel), so R is false.
