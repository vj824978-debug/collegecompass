“Transpiration in plants is a necessary evil.” Comment on the statement giving two reasons.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q102 marks
Question 18 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
“Transpiration in plants is a necessary evil.” Comment on the statement giving two reasons.
Answer
Answer as text
Necessary: (i) transpiration creates a suction pull that helps absorb and move water and dissolved minerals upward from roots to leaves through xylem; (ii) evaporation cools the leaves (temperature regulation). Evil: a very large part of the water absorbed by the plant is lost as vapour through stomata, which can cause wilting when water is scarce. Hence it is a necessary evil.
