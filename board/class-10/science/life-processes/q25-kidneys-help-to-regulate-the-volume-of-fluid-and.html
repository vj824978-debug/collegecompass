Kidneys help to regulate the volume of fluid and various metabolites waste products in the body.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q154 marks
Question 25 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Kidneys help to regulate the volume of fluid and various metabolites waste products in the body. Normally, kidney filter about 180 L of fluid daily but the volume actually excreted out is only a litre or two a day. Patients with kidney failures can be saved by dialysis and kidney transplant. (a) Write the structure and function of Bowman’s capsule. [1] (b) Although kidneys filter a large amount (about 180 L daily) of fluids, still the excretion from the body is only about a litre or two. Why ? [1] (c) What is excretion ? Why is it necessary for any living organism ? [2] OR (c) State two similarities between lungs and kidneys. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Bowman’s capsule is the cup-shaped upper end of a nephron tubule enclosing the glomerulus (cluster of capillaries). Function: collects the filtrate filtered from blood in the glomerulus. (b) Most of the filtrate (glucose, amino acids, salts and a major amount of water) is selectively reabsorbed by the tubules as it passes along, so only a litre or two is excreted as urine. (c) Excretion is the biological process of removing harmful nitrogenous metabolic wastes from the body. It is necessary because accumulation of these wastes (e.g. urea) is toxic and they must be removed to maintain the body’s internal balance. OR (c) (1) Both are excretory organs – lungs remove CO 2, kidneys remove nitrogenous wastes. (2) Both have very large surface area of thin-walled units richly supplied with blood capillaries (alveoli / nephrons) for exchange. (Also: both are paired organs.)
