Question 20 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Identify the part that controls the closing and opening of the stomatal pore in leaves of plants. Select the correct option. (A) Stomata (B) Epidermal cells (C) Guard cells (D) Chloroplasts
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Guard cells. They swell (take in water) to open the pore and shrink to close it.
