Besides minimising the loss of blood, why is it essential to plug any leak in a blood vessel ?

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q232 marks
Question 28 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
(a) Besides minimising the loss of blood, why is it essential to plug any leak in a blood vessel ? Name the component of blood which helps in this process and state how this component perform this function. OR (b) (i) The transport system in plants is relatively slower than in animals. Give reasons. (ii) State the role of phloem in the transport of materials in plants.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A leak causes loss of pressure in the circulatory system, which would reduce the efficiency of pumping/transport (and lets germs in). Platelets help: they circulate in the blood and plug leaks by helping the blood to clot at the injury. OR (b) (i) Plants do not move; a large proportion of their body is dead cells, so they have low energy needs and can use a relatively slow transport system. (ii) Phloem carries soluble products of photosynthesis (sucrose), amino acids etc. from leaves to other parts of the plant (translocation), using energy from ATP.
