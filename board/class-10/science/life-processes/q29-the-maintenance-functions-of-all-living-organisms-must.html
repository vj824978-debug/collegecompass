The maintenance functions of all living organisms must go on even when they are not doing anything particular.

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q384 marks
Question 29 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
The maintenance functions of all living organisms must go on even when they are not doing anything particular. Even when we are just sitting in a class or even asleep, this maintenance job has to go on. These maintenance processes require energy to prevent damage and break-down of cells and tissues, which is obtained by the individual organism from the food prepared by the autotrophs, called producers. (a) Name and define the process by which green plants prepare food. [1] (b) Write chemical equation involved in the above process. [1] (c) (i) State in proper sequence the events that occur in synthesis of food by desert plants. [2] OR (c) (ii) Explain giving reasons what happens to the rate at which the green plants will prepare food [2] (I) during cloudy weather, and (II) when stomata get blocked due to dust.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Photosynthesis: the process by which green plants make carbohydrates (glucose) from carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll, releasing oxygen. (b) 6 CO2 + 12 H2O sunlight − −−−−−− →chlorophyll C6H12O6 + 6 O2 + 6 H2O. (c) (i) Desert plants open their stomata at night and take in CO 2, which is stored as an intermediate (acid); during the day stomata stay closed (to save water), chlorophyll absorbs light energy, which is converted to chemical energy and splits water into hydrogen and oxygen; the stored CO2 is then reduced to carbohydrate. OR (c) (ii) (I) Rate decreases: less sunlight reaches the leaves, so less light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll. (II) Rate decreases: blocked stomata reduce the intake of CO 2 (gas exchange), the raw material for photosynthesis.
