Name the blood vessel that brings (i) oxygenated blood (ii) deoxygenated blood, to the human heart.

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q293 marks
Question 49 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Name the blood vessel that brings (i) oxygenated blood (ii) deoxygenated blood, to the human heart. Also name that chamber of the heart which receives deoxygenated blood and state how deoxygenated blood from this chamber is sent to lungs for oxygenation.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Pulmonary vein (from the lungs to the left atrium). (ii) Vena cava (superior and inferior, from the body). Chamber: right atrium. When the right atrium contracts, blood passes into the right ventricle, which relaxes to receive it; the right ventricle then contracts and pumps the blood into the pulmonary artery, which carries it to the lungs. Valves prevent backflow.
