Pancreas secretes pancreatic juice which contain certain enzyme that helps in digestion of food. Choose the correct option from the following : (A) Trypsin digests emulsified fats and lipase digests proteins. (B) Trypsin digests proteins and lipase digests emulsified fats. (C) Trypsin and lipase both digests fats. (D) Trypsin digests proteins and lipase digests carbohydrates.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Trypsin digests proteins and lipase digests emulsified fats.
