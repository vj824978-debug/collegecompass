Write the name and one function of respiratory pigment found in human beings.

CBSE 2025QP 31/5/1Q293 marks
Question 45 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
(a) Write the name and one function of respiratory pigment found in human beings. (b) Why do lungs always contain a residual volume of air ? (c) Why is ATP known as energy currency of the living beings ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Haemoglobin (in RBCs); it has a high affinity for oxygen and carries oxygen from the lungs to the tissues. (b) The residual air ensures that there is sufficient time for oxygen to be absorbed and carbon dioxide to be released, so gas exchange goes on continuously (and the alveoli do not collapse). (c) Energy released during respiration is stored as ATP; breaking its terminal phosphate bond releases energy that is used for all cellular activities (muscle contraction, protein synthesis, nerve impulses). Like currency it can be “spent” anywhere in the cell.
