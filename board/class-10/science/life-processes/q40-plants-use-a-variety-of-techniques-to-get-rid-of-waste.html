“Plants use a variety of techniques to get rid of waste material.” Justify this statement giving any four ways.
Answer
Answer as text
(1) Oxygen (from photosynthesis) and CO 2 (from respiration) diffuse out through stomata. (2) Excess water is removed by transpiration. (3) Many wastes are stored in leaves and bark, which fall off; some are stored in cell vacuoles. (4) Wastes like resins and gums are stored in old xylem; some wastes are excreted into the soil around the roots.
