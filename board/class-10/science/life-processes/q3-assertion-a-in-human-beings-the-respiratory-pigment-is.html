Assertion (A) : In human beings, the respiratory pigment is haemoglobin present in red blood cells.

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q91 mark
Question 3 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Assertion (A) : In human beings, the respiratory pigment is haemoglobin present in red blood cells. Reason (R) : Haemoglobin has a very high affinity for carbon dioxide. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A is true but R is false — haemoglobin has a very high affinity for oxygen; most CO 2 is carried dissolved in plasma.
