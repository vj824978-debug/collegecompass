Which structure in a leaf is mainly responsible for gaseous exchange ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q11 mark
Question 1 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Which structure in a leaf is mainly responsible for gaseous exchange ? (A) Xylem (B) Stomata (C) Phloem (D) Cuticle
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Stomata — tiny pores in the epidermis of the leaf through which O 2 and CO2 are exchanged.
