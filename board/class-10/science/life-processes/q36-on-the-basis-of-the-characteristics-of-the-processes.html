On the basis of the characteristics of the processes given in the brackets in each case, differentiate between the following : (a) Products of breakdown of pyruvate in aerobic and anaerobic respiration in human beings (product(s) of the processes) (b) Respiration and photosynthesis in plants (gas released) (c) Respiration in terrestrial animals and fishes (organs involved)
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Aerobic (in mitochondria): pyruvate → CO2 + water + much energy. Anaerobic (in muscle cells, lack of oxygen): pyruvate → lactic acid + less energy. (b) Respiration releases carbon dioxide; photosynthesis releases oxygen. (c) Terrestrial animals breathe atmospheric air using lungs (or tracheae/skin); fishes use gills to take dissolved oxygen from water.
