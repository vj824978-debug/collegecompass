Assertion (A) : The peristaltic movements occur all along the gut.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q91 mark
Question 22 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Assertion (A) : The peristaltic movements occur all along the gut. Reason (R) : The lining of the gut has muscles that contract rhythmically to push the food in regulated manner. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
