The essential element taken up from the soil by the plants to synthesize proteins is: (A) Phosphorus (B) Nitrogen (C) Iron (D) Magnesium
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Nitrogen — taken up as nitrates/nitrites and used to make proteins.
