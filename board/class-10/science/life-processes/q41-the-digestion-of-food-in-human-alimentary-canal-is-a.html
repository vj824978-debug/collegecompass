The digestion of food in human alimentary canal is a complex process.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q293 marks
Question 41 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
The digestion of food in human alimentary canal is a complex process. State the enzyme/salt present in the following and mention their function in the process of digestion: (i) Saliva (ii) Bile Juice (iii) Pancreatic Juice
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Saliva: salivary amylase — breaks down starch into simple sugar (maltose). (ii) Bile juice: bile salts (no enzyme) — emulsify fats (break large fat globules into small ones); bile also makes the medium alkaline for pancreatic enzymes. (iii) Pancreatic juice: trypsin — digests proteins; lipase — breaks down emulsified fats into fatty acids and glycerol; (pancreatic amylase — starch to sugar).
