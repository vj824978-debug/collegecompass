(a) Draw a labelled diagram showing open and closed stomatal pore.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q165 marks
Question 19 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
(A) (a) Draw a labelled diagram showing open and closed stomatal pore. (b) List the main events that occur during the process of photo-synthesis. (c) Write a balanced chemical equation showing photo-synthesis in plants. OR (B) (a) Draw a well-labelled diagram of nephron and label atleast 4 of its parts. (b) Write the role of capillary cluster inside Bowman’s capsule. (c) What is meant by selective reabsorption?
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (a) Diagram: (i) open stomatal pore – turgid, swollen guard cells with the pore between them; (ii) closed stomatal pore – flaccid, shrunken guard cells. Labels: guard cells, stomatal pore, chloroplast, nucleus, epidermal cells. (b) (i) Absorption of light energy by chlorophyll; (ii) conversion of light energy to chemical energy and splitting of water molecules into hydrogen and oxygen; (iii) reduction of carbon dioxide to carbohydrates. (c) 6 CO2 + 12 H2O sunlight − −−−−−− →chlorophyll C6H12O6 + 6 O2 + 6 H2O (B) (a) Diagram: nephron, with labels Bowman’s capsule, glomerulus, renal artery branch (afferent arteriole), renal vein branch, tubule (proximal tubule, loop of Henle, distal tubule) and collecting duct. (b) The capillary cluster (glomerulus) filters the blood under pressure: water, glucose, amino acids, salts and urea pass into Bowman’s capsule as the initial filtrate, while blood cells and proteins remain in the blood. (c) As the filtrate flows along the tubule, useful substances such as glucose, amino acids, salts and a major amount of water are taken back into the surrounding capillaries according to the body’s needs; only the wastes and excess water remain to form urine. This is selective reabsorption.
