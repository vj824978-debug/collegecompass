CBSE 2025QP 31/2/1Q355 marks
Question 33 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
(a) (i) “The length of the small intestine in various animals depends on the food they eat.” Justify the statement. (ii) Discuss the role of the pancreas and bile juice in the digestion of food in human beings. (iii) How is the small intestine designed to absorb digested food ? OR (b) (i) State the role of rings of cartilage present in the throat. (ii) Discuss the role of the ribs and diaphragm when air is taken in during the breathing cycle. (iii) Why do we get muscle cramps during heavy exercise ? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Herbivores eating grass need a longer small intestine to allow the cellulose to be digested; carnivores (e.g. tiger) eat meat, which is easier to digest, so they have a shorter small intestine. (ii) Pancreas secretes pancreatic juice: trypsin digests proteins, lipase breaks down emulsified fats, amylase breaks starch. Bile (from the liver) makes the food coming from the stomach alkaline so pancreatic enzymes can act, and its salts emulsify fats into small globules. (iii) Its inner lining has numerous finger-like projections called villi which greatly increase the surface area for absorption; villi are richly supplied with blood vessels that carry the absorbed food to all cells; its great length also gives more time and area. OR (b) (i) Rings of cartilage in the throat (trachea) ensure that the air passage does not collapse. (ii) When we breathe in, the ribs are lifted (move up and out) and the diaphragm flattens (contracts); the chest cavity becomes larger, pressure falls and air is sucked into the lungs, filling the alveoli. (iii) During heavy exercise the oxygen supply to muscles falls short, so muscle cells respire anaerobically: glucose → pyruvate→ lactic acid + energy. Accumulation of lactic acid in muscles causes cramps.
