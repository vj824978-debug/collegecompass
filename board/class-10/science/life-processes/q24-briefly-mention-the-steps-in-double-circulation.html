Briefly mention the steps in double-circulation through human heart.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q122 marks
Question 24 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Briefly mention the steps in double-circulation through human heart.
Answer
Answer as text
(1) Deoxygenated blood from the body enters the right atrium → right ventricle→ pumped to the lungs through the pulmonary artery for oxygenation. (2) Oxygenated blood returns from the lungs by pulmonary veins to the left atrium→ left ventricle→ pumped to the body through the aorta. Thus blood passes through the heart twice in one complete cycle (pulmonary + systemic circulation).
