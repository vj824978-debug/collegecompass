CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q133 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Complete the pathways given below, showing the breakdown of glucose. Identify ‘A’, ‘B’, ‘C’, ‘D’, ‘E’ and ‘F’.
Answer
Answer as text
A – glycolysis in the cytoplasm (glucose, 6C → pyruvate, 3C); B – ethanol and C – carbon dioxide (anaerobic respiration in yeast); D – lactic acid (lack of oxygen in muscle cells); E – carbon dioxide and F – water (aerobic respiration in mitochondria). (B/C and E/F may be in either order.)
