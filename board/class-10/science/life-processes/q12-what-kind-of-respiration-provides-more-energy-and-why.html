What kind of respiration provides more energy and why?

CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q112 marks
Question 12 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
What kind of respiration provides more energy and why?
Answer
Answer as text
Aerobic respiration provides more energy. In aerobic respiration (in mitochondria, in presence of oxygen) glucose is broken down completely into CO2 and H2O, releasing much more energy (about 38 ATP per glucose), whereas in anaerobic respiration glucose is broken down only partially (to ethanol + CO 2 or lactic acid), releasing little energy (2 ATP).
