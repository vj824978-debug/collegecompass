Select TRUE statements about lymph from the following: A.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q61 mark
Question 38 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
Select TRUE statements about lymph from the following: A. Lymph vessels carry lymph through the body and finally open into larger arteries. B. Lymph contains some amount of plasma, proteins and blood cells. C. Lymph contains some amount of plasma, proteins and red blood cells. D. Lymph vessels carry lymph through the body and finally open into larger veins. The true statements are: (A) A and B (B) B and D (C) A and C (D) C and D
Answer
Answer as text
(B) B and D — lymph has plasma, proteins and blood cells (mainly lymphocytes, no RBCs) and lymph vessels drain into larger veins.
