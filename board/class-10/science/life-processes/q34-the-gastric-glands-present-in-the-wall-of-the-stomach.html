The gastric glands present in the wall of the stomach release : (A) Mucus and Trypsin (B) Pepsin and Trypsin…

Question 34 · Class 10 Science · Life Processes
Question as text
The gastric glands present in the wall of the stomach release : (A) Mucus and Trypsin (B) Pepsin and Trypsin (C) Mucus and Pepsin (D) Pepsin and Salivary amylase
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Mucus and Pepsin (along with HCl). Trypsin comes from the pancreas; salivary amylase from salivary glands.
