Which of the following is a poor conductor of electricity ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q211 mark

Question as text
Which of the following is a poor conductor of electricity ? (A) Pb (B) Cu (C) Ag (D) Al
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Pb — lead is a comparatively poor conductor of heat and electricity; Ag, Cu and Al are the best conductors.
