The most common method of extraction of metals from their oxide ores is : (A) Reduction with carbon (B) Reduction with hydrogen (C) Reduction with aluminium (D) Electrolytic reduction
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Reduction with carbon (coke is cheap and widely used, e.g. ZnO + C −−→Zn + CO).
