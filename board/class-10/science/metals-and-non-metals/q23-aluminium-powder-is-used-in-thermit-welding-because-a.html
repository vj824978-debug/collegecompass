Aluminium powder is used in thermit welding because : (A) Its reaction with iron is highly exothermic.

CBSE 2025QP 31/2/1Q41 mark
Question 23 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
Aluminium powder is used in thermit welding because : (A) Its reaction with iron is highly exothermic. (B) When it is heated with iron (III) oxide, molten iron is obtained. (C) When it is heated with iron (III) oxide, molten aluminium oxide is obtained to join railway tracks. (D) Its melting point is low as compared to iron and a molten alloy of iron and aluminium is formed on heating which is used to join railway tracks.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) When it is heated with iron (III) oxide, molten iron is obtained: Fe 2O3 + 2 Al−−→2 Fe + Al2O3 + heat; the molten iron joins the tracks.
