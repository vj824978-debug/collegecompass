Show the formation of magnesium chloride by electron transfer.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q222 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
(A) Show the formation of magnesium chloride by electron transfer. Write the name of the cation and anion present in the compound formed. (Atomic Number of Mg = 12, Cl = 17) OR (B) How is zinc extracted from its ore? Name the processes involved in the extraction and write chemical equations for the reactions that occur during these processes.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Mg (2,8,2) loses 2 electrons: Mg −−→Mg2+ + 2 e– ; each Cl (2,8,7) gains 1 electron: Cl + e –−−→Cl – . Mg + 2 Cl−−→Mg2+ (Cl – )2, i.e. MgCl 2. Cation: magnesium ion Mg 2+; anion: chloride ion Cl – . (B) Zinc ore (ZnS or ZnCO 3) is first converted into zinc oxide and then reduced with carbon. Roasting (sulphide ore, heated strongly in air): 2 ZnS + 3 O 2 heat−−→2 ZnO + 2 SO2 Calcination (carbonate ore, heated in limited air): ZnCO 3 heat−−→ZnO + CO2 Reduction with carbon (coke): ZnO + C −−→Zn + CO
