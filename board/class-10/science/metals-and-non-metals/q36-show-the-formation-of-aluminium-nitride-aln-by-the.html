Show the formation of Aluminium Nitride (AlN) by the transfer of electrons.

CBSE 2025QP 31/5/1Q28(B)3 marks
Question 36 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
(a) Show the formation of Aluminium Nitride (AlN) by the transfer of electrons. [At. no. of Al = 13; At. no. of N = 7] (b) “Ionic compounds are solids and are generally brittle and break into pieces when pressure is applied.” Give reason to justify the statement.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Al (2,8,3) loses 3 electrons: Al−−→Al3+ + 3 e– ; N (2,5) gains 3 electrons: N + 3 e–−−→N3 –. The ions Al 3+ and N3 – are held by electrostatic attraction, forming Al 3+N3 – (AlN). (Diagram: 3 electron dots/crosses transferred from Al to N, each ion attaining an octet.) (b) Strong electrostatic forces between oppositely charged ions make them solids. On applying pressure, layers of ions shift so that like-charged ions come face to face and repel each other, so the crystal breaks into pieces — they are brittle.
