What are amphoteric oxides ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q252 marks
Question 3 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
(a) What are amphoteric oxides ? [1] (b) Categorise the following based on their nature : ZnO, Na2O, CO2 [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Metal oxides which react with both acids and bases to give salt and water, e.g. Al 2O3, ZnO. (b) ZnO — amphoteric oxide; Na 2O — basic oxide; CO 2 — acidic oxide.
