The products formed when Aluminium and Magnesium are burnt in the presence of air respectively are : (A) Al3O4 and MgO2 (B) Al2O3 and MgO (C) Al 3O4 and MgO (D) Al 2O3 and MgO2
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Al2O3 and MgO. 4 Al + 3 O2−−→2 Al2O3; 2 Mg + O2−−→2 MgO.
