The reaction of calcium with cold water is: (A) Less violent, but the heat evolved is sufficient for the hydrogen to catch fire. (B) Very violent and exothermic and hydrogen catches fire. (C) Exothermic, but the heat evolved is not sufficient for the hydrogen to catch fire. (D) Slow and endothermic.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Ca + 2 H 2O−−→Ca(OH)2 + H2; the reaction is less violent and the heat evolved is not enough for hydrogen to catch fire (calcium starts floating as hydrogen bubbles stick to it).
