Which gas is evolved when magnesium metal reacts with very dilute HNO 3 ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q231 mark
Question 15 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
Which gas is evolved when magnesium metal reacts with very dilute HNO 3 ? (A) Oxygen (B) Nitrogen (C) Hydrogen (D) Nitrogen Dioxide
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Hydrogen: Mg + 2 HNO3−−→Mg(NO3)2 + H2. (Mg and Mn give H 2 with very dilute nitric acid.)
