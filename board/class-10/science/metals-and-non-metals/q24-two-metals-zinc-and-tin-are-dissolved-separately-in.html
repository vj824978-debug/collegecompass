Two metals zinc and tin are dissolved separately in definite proportions in molten copper (the primary metal) to obtain two different alloys respectively known as : (A) Bronze and Brass (B) Brass and Solder (C) Brass and Bronze (D) Solder and Bronze
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Brass and Bronze. Brass = Cu + Zn; bronze = Cu + Sn.
