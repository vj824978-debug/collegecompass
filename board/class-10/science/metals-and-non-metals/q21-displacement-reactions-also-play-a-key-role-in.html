“Displacement reactions also play a key role in extracting metals in the middle of the reactivity series.”…

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q273 marks
Question 21 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
(a) “Displacement reactions also play a key role in extracting metals in the middle of the reactivity series.” Justify this statement with two examples. (b) Why can metals high up in the reactivity series not be obtained by reduction of their oxides by carbon ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A more reactive metal (e.g. Al) displaces a less reactive metal from its oxide (highly exothermic): Fe 2O3 + 2 Al−−→2 Fe + Al2O3 + heat (thermit reaction, used to join railway tracks); 3 MnO2 + 4 Al−−→3 Mn + 2 Al2O3 + heat. (b) These metals (Na, Mg, Ca, Al etc.) have more affinity for oxygen than carbon has, so carbon cannot reduce their oxides; they are obtained by electrolytic reduction.
