CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q252 marks

Question as text
Show the formation of the following ionic compounds by transfer of electrons between the combining atoms: [1+1=2] (i) Sodium oxide (Na 2O) (ii) Magnesium chloride (MgCl 2) (Given: Atomic number: Na = 11, Mg = 12, O = 8, Cl = 17)
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Na (2,8,1) loses 1 electron; O (2,6) gains 2 electrons. Two Na atoms transfer one electron each to one O atom: 2 Na −−→2 Na+ + 2 e– ; O + 2 e–−−→O2 –; giving (Na +)2O2 – (Na+ 2,8; O2 – 2,8). (ii) Mg (2,8,2) loses 2 electrons, one to each of two Cl atoms (2,8,7): Mg −−→Mg2+ + 2 e– ; 2 Cl + 2 e–−−→2 Cl– ; Mg 2+(Cl–) 2 (Mg2+ 2,8; Cl – 2,8,8). (Electron-dot diagrams showing the transfer of electrons are expected.)
