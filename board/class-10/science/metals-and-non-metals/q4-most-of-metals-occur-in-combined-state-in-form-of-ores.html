Most of metals occur in combined state in form of ores.

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q284 marks
Question 4 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
Read the following passage and answer the questions given below : Most of metals occur in combined state in form of ores. Carbonate ores are converted into oxides by calcination and sulphide ores by roasting. Oxides are reduced with suitable reducing agent like carbon to get free metal. Highly reactive metals like – Al, Mg are also used as reducing agents to obtain metal from their oxides. Most reactive metals are obtained by electrolytic reduction of their molten ores. Alloying is a very good method of improving the properties of a metal. We can get desired properties by this method. The electrical conductivity and melting point of an alloy is less than that of pure metals. (a) Why carbonate or sulphide ores are converted to oxides before extraction of metal from it ? [1] (b) Write a reaction in which Aluminium is used as a reducing agent to obtain metal from its oxide. [1] (c) (i) How is copper obtained from its ore (Cu 2S) ? Give equations of the reactions. [2] OR (c) (ii) (I) Why highly reactive metals cannot be obtained from their oxides by using carbon as a reducing agent ? [1] (II) Why solder, an alloy of lead and tin, is used for welding electrical wires together ? [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) It is easier to obtain a metal from its oxide (by reduction) than from its carbonate or sulphide. (b) Fe2O3 + 2 Al−−→2 Fe + Al2O3 + heat (thermit reaction) [or 3 MnO 2 + 4 Al−−→3 Mn + 2 Al2O3 + heat]. (c)(i) Cu2S is heated in air; part of it is converted to Cu2O, which then reacts with the remaining Cu2S (self-reduction): 2 Cu2S + 3 O2 heat−−→2 Cu2O + 2 SO2; 2 Cu 2O + Cu2S heat−−→6 Cu + SO2 OR (c)(ii)(I) These metals have more affinity for oxygen than carbon, so carbon cannot reduce their oxides. (II) Solder has a low melting point, so it melts easily to join the wires without damaging them.
