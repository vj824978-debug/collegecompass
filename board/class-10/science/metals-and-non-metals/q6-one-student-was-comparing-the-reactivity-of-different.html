One student was comparing the reactivity of different metals for his science project.

CBSE 2026QP 31/2/1Q252 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
Read the following passage and answer the questions that follow: One student was comparing the reactivity of different metals for his science project. He added iron filings in four test tubes A, B, C and D containing aqueous solutions of ZnSO 4, CuSO 4, FeSO4 and Al2(SO4)3. (i) In which of the test tubes will he observe the reaction to be the most vigorous and why? (ii) Write a balanced equation for the reaction involved. [1+1=2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Test tube B (CuSO 4). Iron is more reactive than copper, so it displaces copper; it is less reactive than Zn and Al, and cannot displace itself, so no reaction occurs in A, C and D. (Blue solution turns pale green; reddish-brown copper deposits.) (ii) Fe(s) + CuSO4(aq)−−→FeSO4(aq) + Cu(s)
