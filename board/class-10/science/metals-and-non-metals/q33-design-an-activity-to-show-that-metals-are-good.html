Design an activity to show that metals are good conductors of heat and have high melting points.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q283 marks
Question 33 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
Design an activity to show that metals are good conductors of heat and have high melting points.
Answer
Answer as text
Take an aluminium or copper wire, clamp it on a stand and fix a pin to its free end with wax. Heat the wire near the clamped end with a burner. After some time the wax melts and the pin falls — heat is conducted along the metal, so metals are good conductors of heat. The wire itself does not melt even though it is held in the flame (while the wax melts at a low temperature) — metals have high melting points.
