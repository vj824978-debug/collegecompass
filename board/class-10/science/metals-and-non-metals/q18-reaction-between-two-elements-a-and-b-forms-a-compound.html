Reaction between two elements A and B, forms a compound C.

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q51 mark
Question 18 · Class 10 Science · Metals and Non-metals
Question as text
Reaction between two elements A and B, forms a compound C. A loses electrons and B gains electrons. Which one of the following properties will not be shown by compound C ? (A) It has high melting point. (B) It is highly soluble in water. (C) It has weak electrostatic forces of attraction between its oppositely charged ions. (D) It conducts electricity in its molten state or aqueous solution.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) C is an ionic compound; its oppositely charged ions are held by strong electrostatic forces, which is why it has a high melting point.
