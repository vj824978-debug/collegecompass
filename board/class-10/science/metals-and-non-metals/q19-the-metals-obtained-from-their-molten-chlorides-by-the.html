The metals obtained from their molten chlorides by the process of electrolytic reduction are : (A) Gold and silver (B) Calcium and magnesium (C) Aluminium and silver (D) Sodium and iron
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Calcium and magnesium – highly reactive metals (top of the reactivity series) are obtained by electrolysis of their molten chlorides.
