Name a device which is used to: (i) Maintain a constant potential difference in a circuit.

CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q363 marks
Question 6 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
(a) Name a device which is used to: (i) Maintain a constant potential difference in a circuit. (ii) Change the electric current in an electric circuit. (b) When the potential difference between the terminals of an electric heater is 110 V, a current of 5 A flows through it. What will be the value of current flowing through it when the potential difference is increased to 220 V?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) A cell or battery. (ii) A rheostat (variable resistance). (b) R = V I = 110 V 5 A = 22 Ω. At 220 V: I = V R = 220 22 = 10 A.
