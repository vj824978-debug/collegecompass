Question 15 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
(a) Write the relationship between resistivity and resistance of a cylindrical conductor of length l and area of cross-section A. Hence derive the SI unit of resistivity. (b) Why are alloys used in electrical heating devices ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) R =ρ l A, so ρ = RA l ; unit = Ω m2 m = Ω m (ohm metre). (b) Alloys have higher resistivity than their constituent metals and do not oxidise (burn) readily at high temperatures.
