The resistance of a wire of 0.01 cm radius is 7 ohms.

Question 29 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
The resistance of a wire of 0.01 cm radius is 7 ohms. If the resistivity of the material of the wire is 44× 10−6 ohm meter, calculate the length of the wire.
Answer
Answer as text
r = 0.01 cm = 1× 10−4 m; A =πr2 = 22 7 × 10−8 m2. R =ρ l A⇒l = RA ρ = 7× 22 7 × 10−8 44× 10−6 = 22× 10−8 44× 10−6 = 5× 10−3 m = 0.5 cm.
