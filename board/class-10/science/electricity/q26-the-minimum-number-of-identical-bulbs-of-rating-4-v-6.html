The minimum number of identical bulbs of rating 4 V; 6 W, that can work safely with desired brightness, when connected in series with a 240 V mains supply is: (A) 20 (B) 40 (C) 60 (D) 80
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 60 — each bulb needs 4 V, so n = 240/4 = 60.
