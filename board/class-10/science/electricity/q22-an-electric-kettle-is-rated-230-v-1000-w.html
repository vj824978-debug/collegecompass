An electric kettle is rated 230 V; 1000 W.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q262 marks
Question 22 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
An electric kettle is rated 230 V; 1000 W. Calculate the resistance of its heating element when in operation.
Answer
Answer as text
P = V 2 R ⇒R = V 2 P = (230)2 1000 = 52900 1000 = 52.9 Ω.
