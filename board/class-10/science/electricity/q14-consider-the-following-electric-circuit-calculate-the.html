Consider the following electric circuit : Calculate the values of the following : (a) The total resistance of the circuit (b) The total current drawn from the source (c) Potential difference across the parallel combination of 10 Ω and 15 Ω resistors
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 10 Ω∥ 15 Ω: 10× 15 25 = 6 Ω; 60 Ω∥ 40 Ω: 60× 40 100 = 24 Ω. TotalR = 6 + 24 = 30 Ω. (b) I = V R = 15 30 = 0.5 A. (c) V =IR = 0.5× 6 = 3 V.
