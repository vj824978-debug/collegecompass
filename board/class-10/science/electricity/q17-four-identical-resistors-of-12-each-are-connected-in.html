Question as text
Four identical resistors of 12 Ω each are connected in series to form a square ABCD as shown in the figure. The resistance of the network between the two points 1 and 2 is : (A) 48 Ω (B) 36 Ω (C) 9 Ω (D) 6 Ω
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 9 Ω. Between A and B: 12 Ω (AB) in parallel with 36 Ω (A–C–D–B); 12× 36 48 = 9 Ω.
