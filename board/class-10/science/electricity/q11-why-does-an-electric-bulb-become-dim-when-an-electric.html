Why does an electric bulb become dim when an electric heater in parallel circuit is switched ON ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q373 marks
Question 11 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
(a) Why does an electric bulb become dim when an electric heater in parallel circuit is switched ON ? (b) How to connect three resistors each of resistance 8 Ω, so that the equivalent resistance of the combination is 12 Ω ? Draw diagram of the combination and justify your answer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The heater has low resistance and draws a large current, so the total current in the supply line increases; the potential drop across the supply wires increases and the voltage across the bulb falls, so it glows dim. (b) Connect two 8 Ω resistors in parallel and this combination in series with the third. Parallel: Rp = 1 8 + 1 8, Rp = 4 Ω; total R = 4 + 8 = 12 Ω. Diagram: one 8 Ω resistor in series with a parallel pair of 8 Ω resistors.
