Assertion (A): Nichrome is an alloy which is commonly used in electrical heating devices such as electric irons, toasters, etc. Reason (R): The resistivity of nichrome is high and its resistance decreases with increase in temperature. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A true, R false. Nichrome has high resistivity, but its resistance increases with temperature (and it does not oxidise readily at high temperature).
