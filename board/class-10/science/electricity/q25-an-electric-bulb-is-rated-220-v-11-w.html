Question as text
An electric bulb is rated 220 V; 11 W. The resistance of its filament when it glows with a power supply of 220 V is: (A) 4400 Ω (B) 440 Ω (C) 400 Ω (D) 20 Ω
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 4400 Ω — R = V 2 P = 2202 11 = 4400 Ω.
