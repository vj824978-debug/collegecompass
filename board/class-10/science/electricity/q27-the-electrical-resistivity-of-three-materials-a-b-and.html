The electrical resistivity of three materials A, B and C at 20 ° C is given below: Material Resistivity (Ω m) A 10 17 B 44 × 10−6 C 1 .62× 10−8 (i) Classify these materials as conductor, alloy and insulator. (ii) Give one example of each of these materials and state one use of each material in the design of an electrical appliance say an electric stove or an electric iron.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) A (10 17 Ω m) — insulator; B (44 × 10−6 Ω m) — alloy; C (1.62× 10−8 Ω m) — conductor. (ii) A: ebonite (or rubber/plastic) — insulation/handle of the iron or stove, cover of connecting wires. B: manganin (alloy, like nichrome) — heating element, as alloys have high resistivity and do not oxidise (burn) readily at high temperature. C: copper — connecting wires/cord that carry current to the appliance, due to low resistivity.
