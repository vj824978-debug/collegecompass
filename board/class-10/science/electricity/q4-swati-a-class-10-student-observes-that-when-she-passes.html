Read the following passage and answer the questions that follow: Swati, a class 10 student, observes that when she passes close to the refrigerator in her kitchen, she feels the heat, although the things kept inside the refrigerator are cool. (a) Describe the cause of heating in the above-mentioned case. [1] (b) A current I flows through a resistor of resistance R when the potential difference across it is V . Applying Ohm’s law, write the formula for amount of heat produced by the resistor in time t. [1] (c) (i) Write any two practical applications of heating effect of electric current. [2] OR (c) (ii) Define the commercial unit of electric energy and express it in Joules (J). [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Heating effect of electric current: part of the electrical energy supplied to the refrigerator’s compressor/motor and wiring is dissipated as heat (Joule heating); the heat removed from inside is also released through the coils at the back. (b) H =VIt ; with V =IR, H =I 2Rt ( = V 2t R ) . (c)(i) Any two: electric iron, electric heater/room heater, toaster, oven, kettle, electric bulb filament, electric fuse. OR (c)(ii) The commercial unit is the kilowatt hour (kWh): the energy consumed by an appliance of power 1 kW used for 1 hour. 1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3.6× 106 J.
