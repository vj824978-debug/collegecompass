Question 24 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
A piece of wire of resistance ‘R’ is cut lengthwise into three identical parts. These parts are then connected in parallel. If the equivalent resistance of this combination is R′, then the value of R/R′ is: (A) 1/9 (B) 1/3 (C) 3 (D) 9
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 9 — each part has R/3; three in parallel: R′ = R/3 3 = R 9 , so R/R′ = 9.
