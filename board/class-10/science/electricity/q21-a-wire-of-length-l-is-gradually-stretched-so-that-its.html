A wire of length ‘l’ is gradually stretched so that its length increases to 3 l. If its original resistance is R, then its new resistance will be : (A) 3R (B) 6R (C) 9R (D) 27R
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 9R – volume constant, so A′ =A/3; R′ =ρ 3l A/3 = 9R.
