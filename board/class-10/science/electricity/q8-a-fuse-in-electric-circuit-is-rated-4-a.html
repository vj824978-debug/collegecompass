A fuse in electric circuit is rated 4 A.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q33(A)2 marks
Question 8 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
A fuse in electric circuit is rated 4 A. Can it be used with an electric heater of rating 2 kW, 200 V? Explain your answer.
Answer
Answer as text
No. Current drawn by heater I = P V = 2000 W 200 V = 10 A, which is more than 4 A; the fuse would melt (blow) and break the circuit as soon as the heater is switched on. A fuse of about 10–11 A rating is needed.
