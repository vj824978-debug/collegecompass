Question 2 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
(a) (i) Due to change in length and area of cross-section of a conductor, resistance of conductor changes while resistivity does not change. Why ? (ii) Conductors of electric toasters and electric iron are made of an alloy rather than a pure metal. Why ? (iii) Define the S.I. unit of electric current. OR (b) (i) How many bulbs of resistance 8 Ω each should be connected in parallel combination to draw a current of 2 A from a battery of 4 V ? (ii) Name the device used for measuring electric current. How is it connected in a circuit ? (iii) State Joule’s law of heating.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Resistivity is a characteristic property of the material; it depends only on the nature of the material (and temperature), not on its dimensions, whereas R =ρ l A depends on length and area. (ii) Alloys have higher resistivity than pure metals (produce more heat) and do not oxidise (burn) readily at high temperatures. (iii) Ampere: one ampere is the current when one coulomb of charge flows through a cross-section of a conductor in one second (1 A = 1 C s −1). OR (b)(i) Required R = V I = 4 2 = 2 Ω. For n equal resistors in parallel, R = 8 n = 2, so n = 4 bulbs. (ii) Ammeter; it is connected in series in the circuit (positive terminal towards the positive of the battery). (iii) The heat produced in a resistor is directly proportional to the square of the current, to the resistance and to the time for which current flows: H =I 2Rt.
