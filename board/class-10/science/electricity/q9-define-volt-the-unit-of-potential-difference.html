Define volt, the unit of potential difference.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q342 marks
Question 9 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
(a) Define volt, the unit of potential difference. (b) Calculate the work done required to move an electron between two points A and B located at a potential difference of 100 V in an electric field.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The potential difference between two points is 1 volt when 1 joule of work is done to move a charge of 1 coulomb from one point to the other: 1 V = 1 J C −1. (b) W =QV = (1.6× 10−19 C)(100 V) = 1.6× 10−17 J.
