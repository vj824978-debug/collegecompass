The correct way to connect an ammeter and a voltmeter in an electric circuit is: (A) Ammeter in parallel and voltmeter in series (B) Ammeter and voltmeter both in parallel (C) Ammeter in series and voltmeter in parallel (D) Ammeter and voltmeter both in series
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Ammeter in series and voltmeter in parallel.
