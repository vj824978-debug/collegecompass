A wire of resistance R is cut into three equal parts.

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q252 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 Science · Electricity
Question as text
(a) A wire of resistance R is cut into three equal parts. If these three parts are then joined in parallel, calculate the total resistance of the combination so formed. OR (b) Define electric power. When do we say that the power consumed in an electric circuit is 1 watt ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Each part has resistance R/3. In parallel: 1 Rp = 3 R + 3 R + 3 R = 9 R, so Rp = R 9 . OR (b) Electric power is the rate at which electric energy is consumed (dissipated) in a circuit, P = VI . Power is 1 W when a current of 1 A flows through a device operating at a potential difference of 1 V (1 W = 1 V × 1 A).
