Which of the following groups do not constitute a food chain ?

CBSE 2025QP 31/1/1Q151 mark
Question 17 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
Which of the following groups do not constitute a food chain ? (i) Wolf, rabbit, grass, lion (ii) Plankton, man, grasshopper, fish (iii) Hawk, grass, snake, grasshopper, frog (iv) Grass, snake, wolf, tiger (A) (i) and (iv) (B) (i) and (iii) (C) (ii) and (iii) (D) (ii) and (iv)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) (ii) and (iv). (i) grass → rabbit→ wolf→ lion and (iii) grass → grasshopper→ frog→ snake→ hawk are food chains; (ii) and (iv) cannot be arranged into feeding links (grasshopper does not eat plankton, snake does not eat grass).
