Which of the following is a non-biodegradable pollutant ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q61 mark
Question 14 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
Which of the following is a non-biodegradable pollutant ? (A) Paper (B) DDT (C) Wood (D) Vegetable peel
Answer
Answer as text
(B) DDT – it is not broken down by decomposers.
