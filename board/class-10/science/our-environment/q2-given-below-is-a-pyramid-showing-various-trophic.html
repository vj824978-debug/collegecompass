Given below is a pyramid showing various trophic levels in an ecosystem : (a) From the organisms listed below, identify which one is to be placed at which trophic level ? Deer, Grass, Lion, Snake, Rabbit (b) Discuss the reason why primary consumers will have more energy as compared to secondary consumers ? (c) Why is the base of the pyramid broad ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Producers: Grass; (ii) Primary consumers: Deer, Rabbit; (iii) Secondary consumers: Snake; (iv) Tertiary consumers: Lion. (b) Only about 10% of the energy of one trophic level is transferred to the next (10 per cent law); the rest is used in life processes or lost as heat. So secondary consumers receive only about one-tenth of the energy of primary consumers. (c) Producers form the base; they trap solar energy and hold the maximum energy (and numbers/biomass), which decreases at each higher level, so the base is broadest.
