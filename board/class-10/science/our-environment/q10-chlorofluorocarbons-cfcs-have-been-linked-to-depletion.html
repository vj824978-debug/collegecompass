Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) have been linked to depletion of ozone layer.

CBSE 2026QP 31/3/1Q102 marks
Question 10 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
(i) Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) have been linked to depletion of ozone layer. In 1987, the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) succeeded in making an agreement. State this agreement. (ii) What is the role of ozone in our atmosphere?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) In 1987 UNEP forged an agreement (Montreal Protocol) to freeze CFC production at 1986 levels; it is now mandatory for manufacturing companies to make CFC-free refrigerators. (ii) Ozone in the upper atmosphere shields the surface of the earth from harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation of the sun, which can cause skin cancer in humans and harm other organisms.
