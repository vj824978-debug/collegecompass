Explain why food chains generally consist of three or four steps.

CBSE 2026QP 31/4/1Q112 marks
Question 13 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
Explain why food chains generally consist of three or four steps.
Answer
Answer as text
Only about 10% of the energy at one trophic level is passed on to the next (10% law); the rest is used for life processes or lost as heat. So the energy available decreases sharply at each step, and after three or four trophic levels too little energy is left to support another level of organisms.
