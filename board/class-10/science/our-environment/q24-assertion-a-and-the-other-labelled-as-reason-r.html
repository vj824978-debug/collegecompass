Assertion (A) and the other labelled as Reason (R).

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q201 mark
Question 24 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
For Questions number 17 to 20, two statements are given – one labelled as Assertion (A) and the other labelled as Reason (R). Select the correct answer to these questions from the codes (A), (B), (C) and (D) as given below. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true. Assertion (A) : Use of jute bags for shopping reduces pollution. Reason (R) : Jute is biodegradable and its bag may be reused as and when needed.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) – both true and R correctly explains A.
