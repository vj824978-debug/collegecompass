In accumulation of toxic substances at higher trophic levels of an ecosystem through the food chain, which of the following organisms is affected the most? (A) Small aquatic plants (B) Human beings (C) Small fish (D) Large fish
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Human beings. By biological magnification, the concentration of non-degradable chemicals is maximum at the highest trophic level, which humans occupy in this food chain.
