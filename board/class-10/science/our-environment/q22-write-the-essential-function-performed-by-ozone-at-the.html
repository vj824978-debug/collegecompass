Write the essential function performed by ozone at the higher levels of the atmosphere.

CBSE 2025QP 31/2/1Q333 marks
Question 22 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
Write the essential function performed by ozone at the higher levels of the atmosphere. How is it formed in the upper atmosphere ? Write the name of the group of chemicals mainly responsible for the depletion of ozone layer.
Answer
Answer as text
Ozone shields the earth’s surface from harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation of the sun. Formation: UV radiation splits O 2 into free oxygen atoms, which combine with O2: O 2 UV−−→O + O; O + O2−−→O3. Depletion is mainly due to chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs).
