“In a food chain energy flow is unidirectional.” Give two reasons for the given statement.

CBSE 2025QP 31/3/1Q333 marks
Question 25 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
(a) “In a food chain energy flow is unidirectional.” Give two reasons for the given statement. (b) If 10,000 J energy is available at the producer level, how much energy will be available to the secondary consumers ? Give reason to justify your answer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Energy captured by autotrophs does not revert back to the Sun / energy transferred to herbivores does not come back to autotrophs. (ii) At each trophic level energy is lost as heat, so it is not available to previous levels; it moves progressively only to the next level. (b) 100 J. By the 10% law only about 10% of energy passes to the next level: producers 10,000 J → primary consumers 1000 J → secondary consumers 100 J.
