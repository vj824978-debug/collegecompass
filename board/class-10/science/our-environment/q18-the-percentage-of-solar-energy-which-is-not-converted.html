The percentage of solar energy which is not converted into food energy by the leaves of green plants in a terrestrial ecosystem is about : (A) 1% (B) 10% (C) 90% (D) 99%
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 99% – green plants convert only about 1% of the sunlight falling on their leaves into food energy.
