Question as text
The incorrect statement about ozone is (A) It is a deadly poisonous gas. (B) It shields the surface of the earth from UV radiation from sun. (C) It is used as a refrigerant and in fire-extinguishers. (D) It is formed by combining oxygen molecule with free oxygen atom.
Answer
Answer as text
(C). CFCs (not ozone) are used as refrigerants and in fire-extinguishers.
