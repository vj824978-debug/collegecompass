In the food chains given below.

CBSE 2025QP 31/4/1Q151 mark
Question 26 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
In the food chains given below. Select the most efficient food chain in terms of energy: (A) Grass→ Grasshopper→ Frog→ Snake (B) Plants→ Deer→ Lion (C) Plants→ Man (D) Phytoplankton→ Zooplankton→ Small Fish→ Big Fish
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Plants→ Man — shortest chain; only about 10% energy passes to each next level, so fewer levels means least loss.
