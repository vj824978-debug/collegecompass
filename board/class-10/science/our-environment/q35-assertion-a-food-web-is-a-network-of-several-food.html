Assertion (A) : Food web is a network of several food chains operating in an ecosystem.

CBSE 2025QP 31/6/1Q201 mark
Question 35 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
Assertion (A) : Food web is a network of several food chains operating in an ecosystem. Reason (R) : Food web decreases the stability of an ecosystem. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C). A food web provides alternative food paths and so increases the stability of an ecosystem; R is false.
