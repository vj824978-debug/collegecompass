Which of the following group is not ‘biodegradable’ ?

CBSE 2026QP 31/1/1Q71 mark
Question 1 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
Which of the following group is not ‘biodegradable’ ? (A) Vegetable peels, dead leaves, paper (B) Cow dung, leather bag, water (C) Polythene bag, rubber band, ball pen (D) Paper, fruits, bones
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Polythene bag, rubber band, ball pen — these plastics cannot be broken down by microorganisms.
