Mention any one harmful effect of using plastic bags on the environment.

CBSE 2026QP 31/5/1Q143 marks
Question 16 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
(a) Mention any one harmful effect of using plastic bags on the environment. Suggest better alternatives to the usage of plastic bags. (b) Paddy fields require a large amount of water. The pesticides and chemical fertilizers used are washed down into the soil or waterbodies. How do these chemicals reach our bodies ? What is this phenomenon known as ?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Plastic is non-biodegradable – it persists, clogs drains and soil, and harms animals that swallow it. Alternatives: cloth/jute bags, paper bags. (b) The chemicals are absorbed from soil/water by plants and aquatic organisms, and pass along the food chain to us. Being non-degradable they get accumulated at each trophic level, so humans (top level) have the highest concentration. Phenomenon: biological magnification.
