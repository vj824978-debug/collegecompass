Question as text
Assertion (A): There are generally a greater number of individuals at the lower trophic levels of an ecosystem. Reason (R): The greatest number is of the producers. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both statements are true, but R does not explain A; A is explained by the loss of energy (only about 10% transferred) at each trophic level.
