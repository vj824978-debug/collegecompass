Consider the following food chain : Grass→ Grasshopper→ Frog→ Snake→ Eagle If the amount of energy available at third trophic level is 50 kJ, the available energy at the producer level was : (A) 0.5 kJ (B) 5 kJ (C) 500 kJ (D) 5000 kJ
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 5000 kJ. Frog is at the 3rd trophic level; by the 10% law, producer energy = 50×10×10 = 5000 kJ.
