Why are the organisms of first trophic level important in any food chain ?

CBSE 2025QP 31/5/1Q262 marks
Question 32 · Class 10 Science · Our Environment
Question as text
(a) Why are the organisms of first trophic level important in any food chain ? (b) Justify the following statement : ‘The flow of energy in an ecosystem is unidirectional.’
Answer
Answer as text
(a) First trophic level organisms are producers (green plants); they trap solar energy and convert it into chemical energy of food, on which all other trophic levels depend. (b) Energy captured by autotrophs does not go back to the sun, and energy passed to herbivores does not return to autotrophs; it moves progressively to higher levels and only about 10% is passed on, the rest being lost as heat. Hence it cannot flow backwards.
