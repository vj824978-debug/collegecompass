The derivative of x2x w.r.t. x is: (A) x2x−1 (B) 2 x2x logx (C) 2 x2x(1 + logx) (D) 2 x2x(1− logx)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) log y = 2x logx⇒y′ = 2x2x(1 + logx).
