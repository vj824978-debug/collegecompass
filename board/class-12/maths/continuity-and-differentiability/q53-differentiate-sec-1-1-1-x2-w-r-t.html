Continuity and Differentiability — Question 53 (CBSE 2023, 3 marks)

CBSE 20233 marks
Question 53 · Class 12 Mathematics · Continuity and Differentiability
Question as text
(a) Differentiate sec−1 1√ 1−x2 w.r.t. sin−1 2x √ 1−x2 . OR (b) If y = tanx + secx, then prove that d2y dx2 = cosx (1− sinx)2 .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) With x = sinθ: u = sec−1(secθ) = sin−1x, v = sin−1(sin 2θ) = 2 sin−1x (for 0 <x< 1√ 2); du dv = 1 2. (b) Proof: y′ = secx(secx + tanx), y′′ = secx(secx + tanx)2 = (1+sinx)2 cos3x = 1+sinx cosx(1−sinx) = cosx (1−sinx)2 .
