Question as text
(a) Show that the function f(x) = cosx −x + π , x ̸= π 1, x = π is continuous at x = π 2 . OR (b) Find whether the function f(x) = ( x− 1, x< 2 2x− 3, x ≥ 2 at x = 2 is differentiable or not.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Put t = π 2−x: lim cosx π 2−x = limt→0 sint t = 1 =f(π 2 ), hence continuous. (b) Continuous at 2 (f(2−) = 1 =f(2)) but LHD = 1, RHD = 2; not differentiable at x = 2.
