Question as text
(a) If (x2 +y2)2 =xy, find dy dx. OR (b) If x =a(2θ− sin 2θ) and y =a(1− cos 2θ), find dy dx when θ = π 3 .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) dy dx = y− 4x(x2 +y2) 4y(x2 +y2)−x = y− 4x3− 4xy2 4x2y + 4y3−x. (b) dy dx = 2a sin 2θ 4a sin2θ = cotθ; at θ = π 3 : 1√ 3.
