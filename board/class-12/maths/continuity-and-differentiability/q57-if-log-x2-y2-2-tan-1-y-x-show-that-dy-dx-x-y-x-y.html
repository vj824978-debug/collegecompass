Continuity and Differentiability — Question 57 (CBSE 2019, 4 marks)

CBSE 20194 marks
Question 57 · Class 12 Mathematics · Continuity and Differentiability
Question as text
(a) If log(x2 +y2) = 2 tan−1 y x , show that dy dx = x +y x−y . OR (b) If xy−yx =ab, find dy dx.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Proof: differentiate: 2(x +yy′) x2 +y2 = 2(xy′−y) x2 +y2 ⇒x +y = (x−y)y′, so dy dx = x +y x−y . (b) u =xy, v =yx, u′ =v′: dy dx = yx logy−yxy−1 xy logx−xyx−1 .
