Sports car racing is a form of motorsport which uses sports car prototypes. The competition is held on special tracks designed in various shapes. The equation of one such track is given as follows: f(x) = ( x4− 4x2 + 4, 0≤x< 3 x2 + 40, x ≥ 3 Based on given information, answer the following questions: (i) Find f′(x) for 0 <x< 3. 1 (ii) Find f′(4). 1 (iii) (a) Test for continuity of f(x) at x = 3. 2 OR (iii) (b) Test for differentiability of f(x) at x = 3. 2
Answer
Answer as text
(i) f′(x) = 4x3− 8x. (ii) f′(x) = 2x for x> 3, f′(4) = 8. (iii)(a) LHL = 81− 36 + 4 = 49, RHL =f(3) = 9 + 40 = 49: continuous at x = 3. (iii)(b) LHD = 4(27)− 24 = 84, RHD = 2(3) = 6; not differentiable at x = 3.
