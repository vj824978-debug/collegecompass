If f(x) =−2x8, then the correct statement is: (A) f′ 1 =f′ −1 (B) f′ 1 =−f′ −1 (C) −f′ 1 =f −1 (D) f 1 =−f −1
Answer
Answer as text
(B) f′(x) =−16x7 is odd, so f′( 1 2) =−f′(− 1 2) (=− 1 8).
