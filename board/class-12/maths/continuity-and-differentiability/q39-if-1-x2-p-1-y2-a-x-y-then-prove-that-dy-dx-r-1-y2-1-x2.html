Continuity and Differentiability — Question 39 (CBSE 2025, 5 marks)

Question 39 · Class 12 Mathematics · Continuity and Differentiability
Question as text
(a) If √ 1−x2 + p 1−y2 =a(x−y), then prove that dy dx = r 1−y2 1−x2 . OR (b) If x =a cosθ + log tanθ and y = sinθ, then find d2y dx2 at θ = π 4 .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Proof: put x = sinA, y = sinB; cosA + cosB =a(sinA− sinB)⇒ cot A−B 2 =a, so sin−1x− sin−1y = 2 cot−1a; differentiate. (b) dx dθ = a cos2θ sinθ , dy dx = tanθ a , d2y dx2 = sinθ a2 cos4θ; at θ = π 4 : 2 √ a2 .
