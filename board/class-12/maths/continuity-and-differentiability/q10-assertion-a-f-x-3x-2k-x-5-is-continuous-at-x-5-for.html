Continuity and Differentiability — Question 10 (CBSE 2025)

CBSE 202565/1/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Mathematics · Continuity and Differentiability
Question as text
Assertion (A): f(x) = ( 3x− 8, x ≤ 5 2k, x> 5 is continuous at x = 5 for k = 5 2. Reason (R): For a function f to be continuous at x =a, lim x→a− f(x) = lim x→a+ f(x) =f(a). (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and the Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) f(5) = 7 = 2k⇒k = 7 2̸= 5 2, so A is false; R is the definition of continuity (true). (Publisher’s copy prints “ x< 5” in the second piece and “x =n” in R: misprints, corrected to x> 5 and x =a.)
