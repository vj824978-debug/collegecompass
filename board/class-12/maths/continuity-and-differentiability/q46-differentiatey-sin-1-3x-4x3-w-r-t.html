Question as text
(a) Differentiatey = sin−1(3x− 4x3) w.r.t. x, if x∈ −1 2, 1 . OR (b) Differentiatey = cos−1 1−x2 1 +x2 with respect to x, when x∈ (0, 1).
Answer
Answer as text
(a) x = sinθ, θ∈ [−π 6, π 6 ]: y = 3 sin−1x, dy dx = 3√ 1−x2 . (b) x = tanθ, θ∈ (0, π 4 ): y = 2 tan−1x, dy dx = 2 1 +x2 .
