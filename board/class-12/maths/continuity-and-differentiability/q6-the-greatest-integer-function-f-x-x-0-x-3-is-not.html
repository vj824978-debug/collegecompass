Question as text
The greatest integer function, f(x) = [x], 0 <x< 3 is not differentiable at how many points? (A) At only one point (B) At only two points (C) At no point (D) At three points
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Jumps at x = 1 and x = 2 in (0, 3).
