Question as text
(a) For a positive constant ‘a’, differentiateat+ 1 t with respect to t + 1 t a , where t is a non-zero real number. OR (b) Find dy dx if yx +xy +xx =ab, where a and b are constants.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) du dt =at+ 1 t loga 1− 1 t2 , dv dt =a t + 1 t a−1 1− 1 t2 ; du dv = at+ 1 t loga a t + 1 t a−1 (t̸=±1). (b) dy dx =−yx logy +yxy−1 +xx(1 + logx) xyx−1 +xy logx .
