Question as text
The slope of the curve y =−x3 + 3x2 + 8x− 20 is maximum at: (A) (1 ,−10) (B) (1 , 10) (C) (10 , 1) (D) ( −10, 1)
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Slope m =−3x2 + 6x + 8,m′ = 0 at x = 1, m′′ =−6< 0; y(1) =−1 + 3 + 8− 20 =−10.
