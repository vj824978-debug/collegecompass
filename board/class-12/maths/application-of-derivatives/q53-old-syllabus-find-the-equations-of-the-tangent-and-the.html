Application of Derivatives — Question 53 (CBSE 2018, 4 marks)

CBSE 20184 marks
Question 53 · Class 12 Mathematics · Application of Derivatives
Question as text
(a) [old syllabus] Find the equations of the tangent and the normal to the curve 16 x2 + 9y2 = 145 at the point (x1,y 1), where x1 = 2 and y1> 0. OR (b) Find the intervals in which the function f(x) = x4 4 −x3− 5x2 + 24x + 12 is (a) strictly increasing, (b) strictly decreasing.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Point (2, 3), slope− 32 27: tangent 32x + 27y = 145; normal 27x− 32y + 42 = 0. (b) f′(x) = (x + 3)(x− 2)(x− 4): strictly increasing on (−3, 2)∪ (4,∞), strictly decreasing on (−∞,−3)∪ (2, 4).
