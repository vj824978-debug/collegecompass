For the curve y = 5x− 2x3, if x increases at the rate of 2 units/s, then how fast is the slope of the curve changing when x = 2?
Answer
Answer as text
Slope m = dy dx = 5− 6x2; dm dt =−12xdx dt =−12(2)(2) =−48; slope decreases at 48 units/s.
