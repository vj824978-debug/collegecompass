Question as text
A rectangular visiting card is to contain 24 sq. cm of printed matter. The margins at the top and bottom of the card are to be 1 cm and the margins on the left and right are to be 1 1 2 cm as shown below: On the basis of the above information, answer the following questions: (i) Write the expression for the area of the visiting card in terms of x. (ii) Obtain the dimensions of the card of minimum area.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) xy = 24, card (x + 3)× (y + 2): A(x) = (x + 3) 24 x + 2 = 2x + 72 x + 30. (ii) A′ = 2− 72 x2 = 0⇒x = 6, A′′> 0; card 9 cm × 6 cm (printed area 6× 4), minimum area 54 cm 2.
