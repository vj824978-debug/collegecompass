Question 48 · Class 12 Mathematics · Application of Derivatives
Question as text
(a) If f(x) =x4− 10, then find the approximate value of f(2.1). OR (b) Find the slope of the tangent to the curve y = 2 sin2(3x) at x = π 6 .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) f(2.1)≈f(2) + 0.1f′(2) = 6 + 0.1(32) = 9.2. (b) y′ = 6 sin 6x; at x = π 6 slope = 6 sinπ = 0.
