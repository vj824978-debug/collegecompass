Question as text
The area of the circle is increasing at a uniform rate of 2 cm 2/s. How fast is the circumference of the circle increasing when the radius r = 5 cm?
Answer
Answer as text
2πrdr dt = 2⇒ dr dt = 1 5π; dC dt = 2πdr dt = 2 5 cm/s.
