CBSE 2023
Question 26 · Class 12 Mathematics · Integrals
Question as text
Assertion (A): Z 8 √10−x√x +√10−xdx = 3. Reason (R): Z b a f(x)dx = Z b a f(a +b−x)dx. (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). (B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). (C) (A) is true and (R) is false. (D) (A) is false, but (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) By (R), I = R 8 √x√10−x+√xdx; 2I = R 8 2 dx = 6⇒I = 3.
