Question 28 · Class 12 Mathematics · Application of Integrals
Question as text
(a) Prove that the curves y2 = 4x andx2 = 4y divide the area of the square bounded by sides x = 0, x = 4, y = 4 and y = 0 into three equal parts. OR (b) Using integration, find the area of the triangle whose vertices are (2 , 3), (3, 5) and (4, 4).
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Proof: curves meet at (0 , 0), (4, 4); the three parts have areasR 4 x2 4dx = 16 3 , R 4 2√x− x2 dx = 16 3 , R 4 y2 4dy = 16 3 (square area 16). (b) Sides y = 2x− 1, y = 8−x, y = x+4 2 ; area = R 3 2 (2x− 1)dx + R 4 3 (8−x)dx− R 4 x+4 2 dx = 4 + 9 2− 7 = 3 2 sq. units.
