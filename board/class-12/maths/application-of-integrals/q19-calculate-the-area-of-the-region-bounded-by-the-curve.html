Question 19 · Class 12 Mathematics · Application of Integrals
Question as text
Calculate the area of the region bounded by the curve x2 9 + y2 4 = 1 and the x-axis using integration.
Answer
Answer as text
Region above the x-axis (upper half of the ellipse): R 3 √ 9−x2dx = 4 3· 9π 4 = 3π sq. units (the lower half likewise has area 3π).
