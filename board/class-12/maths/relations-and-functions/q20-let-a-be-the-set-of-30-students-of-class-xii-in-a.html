Let A be the set of 30 students of class XII in a school.

CBSE 202565/5/14 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Mathematics · Relations and Functions
Question as text
Let A be the set of 30 students of class XII in a school. Let f :A→ N, N is a set of natural numbers such that function f(x) = Roll Number of student x. On the basis of the given information, answer the following: (i) Is f a bijective function? 1 (ii) Give reasons to support your answer to (i). 1 (iii) (a) Let R be a relation defined by the teacher to plan the seating arrangement of students in pairs, where R ={(x,y ) : x,y are Roll Numbers of students such that y = 3x}. List the elements of R. Is the relation R reflexive, symmetric and transitive? Justify your answer. 2 OR (iii) (b) Let R be a relation defined by R ={(x,y ) : x,y are Roll Numbers of students such that y =x3}. List the elements of R. Is R a function? Justify your answer. 2
Answer
Answer as text
(i) No. (ii) f is one-one (distinct students have distinct roll numbers) but not onto, since only 30 natural numbers are images (range ̸= N). (iii)(a) Taking roll numbers 1 to 30: R = {(1, 3), (2, 6), (3, 9), (4, 12), (5, 15), (6, 18), (7, 21), (8, 24), (9, 27), (10, 30)}; not reflexive ((1, 1) /∈R), not symmetric ((1, 3)∈R, (3, 1) /∈R), not transitive ((1, 3), (3, 9)∈R but (1, 9) /∈R). (iii)(b) R ={(1, 1), (2, 8), (3, 27)};R is not a function on the set of roll numbers, since roll numbers 4,..., 30 have no image (it is a function only on the domain{1, 2, 3}).
