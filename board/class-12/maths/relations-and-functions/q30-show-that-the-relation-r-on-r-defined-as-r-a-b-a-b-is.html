Question as text
(a) Show that the relation R on R defined as R ={(a,b ) : a≤b} is reflexive and transitive but not symmetric. OR (b) Prove that the function f : N→ N, defined by f(x) = x2 +x + 1 is one-one but not onto. Find inverse of f : N→S, where S is range of f.

Answer as text
(a) Proof: a≤a (reflexive);a≤b, b≤c⇒a≤c (transitive); 1≤ 2 but 2̸≤ 1 (not symmetric). (b) Proof: f(x1) =f(x2)⇒ (x1−x2)(x1 +x2 + 1) = 0⇒x1 =x2 (one-one); f(x)≥ 3, so 1∈ N has no pre-image (not onto). f−1 :S→ N, f−1(y) =−1 +√4y− 3 2 .
