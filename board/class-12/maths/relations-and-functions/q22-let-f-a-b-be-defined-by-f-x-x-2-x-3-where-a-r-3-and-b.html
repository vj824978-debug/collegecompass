Relations and Functions — Question 22 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 202565/7/12 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Mathematics · Relations and Functions
Question as text
Let f : A→ B be defined by f(x) = x− 2 x− 3, where A = R−{ 3} and B = R−{ 1}. Discuss the bijectivity of the function.
Answer
Answer as text
f(x1) =f(x2)⇒x1 =x2 (one-one); for y∈B, x = 3y− 2 y− 1 ∈A with f(x) =y (onto). Hence f is bijective.
