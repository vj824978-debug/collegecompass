CBSE 202565/1/14 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Mathematics · Relations and Functions
Question as text
A class-room teacher is keen to assess the learning of her students the concept of “relations” taught to them. She writes the following five relations each defined on the set A ={1, 2, 3}: R1 ={(2, 3), (3, 2)} R2 ={(1, 2), (1, 3), (3, 2)} R3 ={(1, 2), (2, 1), (1, 1)} R4 ={(1, 1), (1, 2), (3, 3), (2, 2)} R5 ={(1, 1), (1, 2), (3, 3), (2, 2), (2, 1), (2, 3), (3, 2)} The students are asked to answer the following questions about the above relations: (i) Identify the relation which is reflexive, transitive but not symmetric. 1 (ii) Identify the relation which is reflexive and symmetric but not transitive. 1 (iii) (a) Identify the relations which are symmetric but neither reflexive nor transitive. 2 OR (iii) (b) What pairs should be added to the relation R2 to make it an equivalence relation? 2
Answer
Answer as text
(i) R4. (ii) R5 (reflexive, symmetric; (1, 2), (2, 3)∈R5 but (1, 3) /∈R5). (iii)(a) R1 and R3. (iii)(b) Add (1, 1), (2, 2), (3, 3), (2, 1), (3, 1), (2, 3) (giving A×A).
