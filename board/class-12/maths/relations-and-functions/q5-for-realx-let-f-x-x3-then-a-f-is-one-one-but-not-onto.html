Question 5 · Class 12 Mathematics · Relations and Functions
Question as text
For realx, let f(x) =x3 + 5x + 1. Then: (A) f is one-one but not onto on R (B) f is onto on R but not one-one (C) f is one-one and onto on R (D) f is neither one-one nor onto on R
Answer
Answer as text
(C) f′(x) = 3x2 + 5> 0: strictly increasing (one-one); odd-degree polynomial, continuous, f→±∞ , so onto R.
