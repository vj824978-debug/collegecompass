Question as text
(a) The probability of hitting the target by a trained sniper is three times the probability of not hitting the target on a stormy day due to high wind speed. The sniper fired two shots on the target on a stormy day when wind speed was very high. Find the probability that (i) target is hit (ii) atleast one shot misses the target. OR (b) Mother, Father and Son line up at random for a family picture. Let events E: Son on one end and F : Father in the middle. Find P (E/F ).
Answer
Answer as text
(a) p = 3(1−p)⇒p = 3 4. (i) 1− 1 2 = 15 16 (at least one hit). (ii) 1− 3 2 = 7 16. (b) F ={MFS,SFM }, E∩F =F ; P (E/F ) = 2/6 2/6 = 1.
