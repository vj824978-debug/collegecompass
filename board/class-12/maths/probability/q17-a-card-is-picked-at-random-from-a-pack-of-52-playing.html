Question as text
A card is picked at random from a pack of 52 playing cards. Given that the picked card is a queen, the probability of this card to be a card of spade is (A) 1 3 (B) 4 (C) 1 4 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(C) P (spade| queen) = 1/52 4/52 = 1 4.
