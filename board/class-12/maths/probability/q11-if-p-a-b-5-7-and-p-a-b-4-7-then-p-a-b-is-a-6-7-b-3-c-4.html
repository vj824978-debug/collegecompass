Question 11 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
If P (A) = 1 7, P (B) = 5 7 and P (A∩B) = 4 7, then P (A|B) is: (A) 6 7 (B) 3 (C) 4 5 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(D) P (A|B) = P (B)−P (A∩B) P (B) = 1/7 5/7 = 1 5. Paper defect: data inconsistent,P (A∩B) = 4 7 >P (A) = 1 7; (D) is the intended answer.
