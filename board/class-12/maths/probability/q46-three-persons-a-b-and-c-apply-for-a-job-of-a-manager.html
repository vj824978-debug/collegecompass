CBSE 2022T erm II4 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
Three persons A, B and C apply for a job of a manager in a private company. Chances of their selection are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 4. The probability that A, B and C can introduce changes to increase the profits of a company are 0.8, 0.5 and 0.3 respectively. If increase in the profit does not take place, find the probability that it is due to the appointment of A.
Answer
Answer as text
P (E1) = 1 7,P (E2) = 2 7,P (E3) = 4 7; P (no increase|Ei) = 0.2, 0.5, 0.7. P (E1| no increase) = 0.2/7 (0.2+1+2.8)/7 = 1 20.
