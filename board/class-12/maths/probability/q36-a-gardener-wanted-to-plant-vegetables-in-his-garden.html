A gardener wanted to plant vegetables in his garden.

CBSE 202565/5/14 marks
Question 36 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
A gardener wanted to plant vegetables in his garden. Hence he bought 10 seeds of brinjal plant, 12 seeds of cabbage plant and 8 seeds of radish plant. The shopkeeper assured him of germination probabilities of brinjal, cabbage and radish to be 25%, 35% and 40% respectively. But before he could plant the seeds, they got mixed up in the bag and he had to sow them randomly. Based upon the above information, answer the following questions: (i) Calculate the probability of a randomly chosen seed to germinate. 2 (ii) What is the probability that it is a cabbage seed, given that the chosen seed germinates? 2
Answer
Answer as text
(i) P (G) = 10 30(0.25) + 12 30(0.35) + 8 30(0.40) = 9.9 30 = 0.33. (ii) Bayes: 4.2/30 9.9/30 = 14 33.
