Probability — Question 45 (CBSE 2022, 2 marks)

CBSE 2022T erm II2 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
(a) EventsA and B are such that P (A) = 1 2, P (B) = 7 12 and P ( ¯A∪ ¯B) = 1 4. Find whether the events A and B are independent or not. OR (b) A box B1 contains 1 white ball and 3 red balls. Another box B2 contains 2 white balls and 3 red balls. If one ball is drawn at random from each of the boxes B1 andB2, then find the probability that the two balls drawn are of the same colour.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Not independent: P (A∩B) = 1−P ( ¯A∪ ¯B) = 3 4̸=P (A)P (B) = 7 24. see note: the data are inconsistent ( P (A∩B) = 3 4 >P (A) = 1 2), but the intended answer is “not independent”. (b) 1 4· 2 5 + 3 4· 3 5 = 11 20.
