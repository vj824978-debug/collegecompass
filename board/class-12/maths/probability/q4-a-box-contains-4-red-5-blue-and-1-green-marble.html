Question 4 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
A box contains 4 red, 5 blue and 1 green marble. A child randomly takes out a marble from the box, notes down the colour and puts it back in the box. If the activity is repeated 3 times, what is the probability that at least one marble is red? (A) 27 125 (B) 8 (C) 2 125 (D) 98
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1 − 3 3 = 98 125.
