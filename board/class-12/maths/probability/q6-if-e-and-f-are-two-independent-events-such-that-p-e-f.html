If E and F are two independent events such that P (E) = 2 3, P (F ) = 3 7, then P (E/F ) is equal to: (A) 1 6 (B) 1 (C) 2 3 (D) 7
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Independence: P (E/F ) =P (E) = 2 3.
