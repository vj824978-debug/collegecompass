[old syllabus] (a) A die with number 1 to 6 is biased such that P (2) = 3 10 and probability of other numbers is equal. Find the mean of the number of times number 2 appears on the dice, if the dice is thrown twice. OR (b) Two dice are thrown. Defined are the following two events A and B: A ={(x,y ) :x +y = 9}, B ={(x,y ) :x̸= 3}, where (x,y ) denote a point in the sample space. Check if events A and B are independent or mutually exclusive.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) X = number of 2s, X∼B 2, 3 : P (0) = 49 100, P (1) = 42 100, P (2) = 9 100; mean = 3 5. (b) P (A) = 4 36 = 1 9, P (B) = 30 36 = 5 6, P (A∩B) = 3 36 = 1 12̸=P (A)P (B) = 5 54: not independent; A∩B̸=ϕ: not mutually exclusive.
