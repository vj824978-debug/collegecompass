[old syllabus] (a) 10 identical blocks are marked with ‘0’ on two of them, ‘1’ on three of them, ‘2’ on four of them and ‘3’ on one of them and put in a box. If X denotes the number written on the block, then write the probability distribution of X and calculate its mean. OR (b) In a village of 8000 people, 3000 go out of the village to work and 4000 are women. It is noted that 30% of women go out of the village to work. What is the probability that a randomly chosen individual is either a woman or a person working outside the village?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) X: 0, 1, 2, 3 with P : 2 10, 3 10, 4 10, 1 10; mean = 0+3+8+3 10 = 1.4. (b) P (W∪O) = 4000+3000−1200 8000 = 29 40.
