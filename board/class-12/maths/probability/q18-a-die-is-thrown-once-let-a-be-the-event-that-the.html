A die is thrown once. Let A be the event that the number obtained is greater than 3. Let B be the event that the number obtained is less than 5. Then P (A∪B) is (A) 2 5 (B) 3 (C) 0 (D) 1
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A∪B ={1,..., 6}, so P = 1.
