In answering a question on a multiple choice test, a student either knows the answer or guesses.

CBSE 20235 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
(a) In answering a question on a multiple choice test, a student either knows the answer or guesses. Let 3 5 be the probability that he knows the answer and 2 5 be the probability that he guesses. Assuming that a student who guesses at the answer will be correct with probability 1 3, what is the probability that the student knows the answer, given that he answered it correctly? OR (b) [old syllabus] A box contains 10 tickets, 2 of which carry a prize of Rs. 8 each, 5 of which carry a prize of Rs. 4 each, and remaining 3 carry a prize of Rs. 2 each. If one ticket is drawn at random, find the mean value of the prize.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 5·1 5·1+ 2 5· 1 = 9 11. (b) Mean = 8 · 2 10 + 4· 5 10 + 2· 3 10 = 21 5 = Rs. 4.20.
