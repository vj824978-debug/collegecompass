Assertion (A): Two coins are tossed simultaneously. The probability of getting two heads, if it is known that at least one head comes up, is 1 3. Reason (R): Let E and F be two events with a random experiment, then P (F/E ) = P (E∩F ) P (E) . (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). (B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). (C) (A) is true and (R) is false. (D) (A) is false, but (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) P (HH| at least one H) = 1/4 3/4 = 1 3, computed by the formula in (R).
