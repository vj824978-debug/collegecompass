Question as text
A box has 4 green, 8 blue and 3 red pens. A student picks up a pen at random, checks its colour and replaces it in the box. He repeats this process 3 times. The probability that at least one pen picked was red is: (A) 124 125 (B) 1 (C) 61 125 (D) 64
Answer
Answer as text
(C) P (red) = 1 5; 1− 4 3 = 61 125.
