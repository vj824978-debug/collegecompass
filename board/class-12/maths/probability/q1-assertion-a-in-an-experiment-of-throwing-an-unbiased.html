Assertion (A): In an experiment of throwing an unbiased die, the probability of getting a prime number given that number appearing on the die being odd is 2 3. Reason (R): For any two eventsA and B, P (A|B) = P (A∪B) P (B) (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and the Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true and Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false and Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) P (prime|odd) = |{3,5}| |{1,3,5}| = 2 3 (A true); R should be P (A∩B) P (B) (false).
