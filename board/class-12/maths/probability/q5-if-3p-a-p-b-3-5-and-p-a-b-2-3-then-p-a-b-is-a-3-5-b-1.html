Question as text
If 3P (A) =P (B) = 3 5 and P (A|B) = 2 3, then P (A∪B) is: (A) 3 5 (B) 1 (C) 2 15 (D) 2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) P (B) = 3 5, P (A) = 1 5, P (A∩B) = 2 3· 3 5 = 2 5; P (A∪B) = 1 5 + 3 5− 2 5 = 2 5. (Paper defect: the data are inconsistent since P (A∩B) = 2 5 >P (A) = 1 5; (D) is the intended formula answer.)
