CBSE 2023
Question 14 · Class 12 Mathematics · Probability
Question as text
If for any two events A and B, P (A) = 4 5 and P (A∩B) = 7 10, then P (B/A) is equal to: (A) 1 10 (B) 1 (C) 7 8 (D) 17
Answer
Answer as text
(C) P (B/A) = 7/10 4/5 = 7 8.
