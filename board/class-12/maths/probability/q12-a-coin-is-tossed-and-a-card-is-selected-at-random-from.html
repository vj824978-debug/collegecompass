A coin is tossed and a card is selected at random from a well shuffled pack of 52 playing cards. The probability of getting head on the coin and a face card from the pack is: (A) 2 13 (B) 3 (C) 19 26 (D) 3
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 1 2· 12 52 = 3 26.
