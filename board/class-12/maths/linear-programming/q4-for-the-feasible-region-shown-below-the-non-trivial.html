Question as text
For the feasible region shown below, the non-trivial constraints of the linear programming problem are (A) x +y≤ 5, x+ 3y≤ 9 (B) x +y≤ 5, x+ 3y≥ 9 (C) x +y≥ 5, x+ 3y≤ 9 (D) x +y≥ 5, 3x +y≤ 9
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Shaded region lies above x +y = 5 and below x + 3y = 9 (between (3, 2), (5, 0), (9, 0)).
