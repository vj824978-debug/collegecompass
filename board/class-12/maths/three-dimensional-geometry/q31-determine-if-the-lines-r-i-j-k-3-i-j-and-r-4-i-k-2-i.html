Question as text
Determine if the lines ⃗ r= (ˆi + ˆj− ˆk) +λ(3ˆi− ˆj) and ⃗ r= (4ˆi− ˆk) +µ(2ˆi + 3ˆk) intersect with each other.
Answer
Answer as text
General points (1 + 3λ, 1−λ,−1) and (4 + 2µ, 0,−1 + 3µ): z⇒µ = 0, y⇒λ = 1, x: 4 = 4 consistent. Yes, the lines intersect, at (4 , 0,−1).
