If a line makes angles of 3π 4 ,π 3 andθ with the positive directions of x,y andz-axis respectively, then θ is (A) −π 3 only (B) π 3 only (C) π 6 (D) ±π
Answer
Answer as text
see note cos2θ = 1− 1 2− 1 4 = 1 4, cosθ =± 1 2, so θ = π 3 or 2π 3 . No option is exactly right: (B) gives only one valid value, (D) −π 3 is not a direction angle. Intended answer most likely (B).
