Case Study. Two motorcycles A and B are running at the speed more than the allowed speed on the roads represented by the lines ⃗ r=λ(ˆi + 2ˆj− ˆk) and ⃗ r= (3ˆi + 3ˆj) +µ(2ˆi + ˆj + ˆk) respectively. Based on the above information, answer the following questions: (a) Find the shortest distance between the given lines. (2) (b) Find the point at which the motorcycles may collide. (2)
Answer
Answer as text
(a)⃗b1×⃗b2 = 3ˆi− 3ˆj− 3ˆk, (⃗ a2−⃗ a1)· (⃗b1×⃗b2) = (3ˆi + 3ˆj)· (3ˆi− 3ˆj− 3ˆk) = 0; shortest distance = 0 (lines intersect). (b) λ = 1, µ =−1: collision point (1, 2,−1).
