Question as text
Find the equation of the plane passing through the line of intersection of the planes ⃗ r· (ˆi +ˆj + ˆk) = 10 and⃗ r· (2ˆi + 3ˆj− ˆk) + 4 = 0 and passing through (−2, 3, 1).
Answer
Answer as text
(x +y +z− 10) +λ(2x + 3y−z + 4) = 0; through (−2, 3, 1): −8 + 8λ = 0⇒λ = 1. Plane 3x + 4y− 6 = 0, i.e. ⃗ r· (3ˆi + 4ˆj) = 6.
