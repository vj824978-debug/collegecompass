Three Dimensional Geometry — Question 20 (CBSE 2026, 5 marks)

CBSE 202665/3/15 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Mathematics · Three Dimensional Geometry
Question as text
(a) Represent the equations of lines l1 and l2 in vector form and check whether they are intersecting or not. l1 : x + 3 −3 = y− 1 1 = z− 5 5 l2 : x + 1 −1 = 2−y −2 = z− 5 OR (b) Opposite sides of a square are along the lines: ⃗ r= ˆi + 2ˆj− 4ˆk +λ(2ˆi + 3ˆj + 6ˆk) ⃗ r= 3ˆi + 3ˆj− 5ˆk +µ(2ˆi + 3ˆj + 6ˆk) Find the area of the square if direction ratios of other pair of opposite sides of the square are given by⟨−3, 6,p⟩. Also, find the value of p.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) l1: ⃗ r= (−3ˆi + ˆj + 5ˆk) +λ(−3ˆi + ˆj + 5ˆk); l2: ⃗ r= (−ˆi + 2ˆj + 5ˆk) +µ(−ˆi + 2ˆj + 5ˆk); (⃗ a2−⃗ a1)· (⃗b1×⃗b2) = (2, 1, 0)· (−5, 10,−5) = 0, so they intersect (at the origin, λ =µ =−1). (b) Side = distance between parallel lines = |(2ˆi+ˆj−ˆk)×(2ˆi+3ˆj+6ˆk)| 7 = √ 7 ; area = 293 49 sq units; −6 + 18 + 6p = 0⇒p =−2.
