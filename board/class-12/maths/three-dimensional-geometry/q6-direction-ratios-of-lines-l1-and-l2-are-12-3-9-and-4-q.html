Question as text
Direction ratios of lines l1 and l2 are⟨12,−3, 9⟩ and⟨4,q,−p⟩ respectively. The values of p and q for whichl1 and l2 are parallel are respectively: (A) −1, 3 (B) 3 , 1 (C) −3,−1 (D) −1,−3
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 12 4 = −3 q = 9 −p = 3⇒q =−1, p =−3.
