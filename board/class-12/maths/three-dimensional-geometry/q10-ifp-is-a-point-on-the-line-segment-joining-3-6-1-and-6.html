IfP is a point on the line segment joining (3, 6,−1) and (6, 2,−2) and y-coordinate of P is 4, then its z-coordinate is: (A) −3 2 (B) 0 (C) 1 (D) 3
Answer
Answer as text
(A) y = 6− 4t = 4⇒t = 1 2; z =−1− 1 2 =− 3 2.
