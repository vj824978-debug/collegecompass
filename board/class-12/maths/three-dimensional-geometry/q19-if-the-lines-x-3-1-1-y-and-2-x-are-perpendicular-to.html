Three Dimensional Geometry — Question 19 (CBSE 2026, 2 marks)

CBSE 202665/3/12 marks
Question 19 · Class 12 Mathematics · Three Dimensional Geometry
Question as text
(a) If the lines x− 3 1 = 1−y 1 = z + 2 p and 2−x 3 = y + 1 5 = z + 56 2p are perpendicular to each other, then find the value(s) of p. OR (b) Find the vector equation of a line passing through the origin and perpendicular to both the lines ⃗ r= 2ˆi− ˆj + 2ˆk +λ(3ˆi + 4ˆj + 2ˆk) and ⃗ r=µ(ˆi− ˆj + ˆk).
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Direction ratios⟨1,−1,p⟩ and⟨−3, 5, 2p⟩; −3− 5 + 2p2 = 0⇒p =±2. (b) (3ˆi + 4ˆj + 2ˆk)× (ˆi− ˆj + ˆk) = 6ˆi− ˆj− 7ˆk;⃗ r=λ(6ˆi− ˆj− 7ˆk).
