Three Dimensional Geometry — Question 46 (CBSE 2019, 4 marks)

CBSE 20194 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Mathematics · Three Dimensional Geometry
Question as text
Find the value of λ, so that the lines 1−x 3 = 7y− 14 λ = z− 3 2 and 7− 7x 3λ = y− 5 1 = 6−z 5 are at right angles. Also, find whether the lines are intersecting or not.
Answer
Answer as text
Directions −3, λ 7, 2 , − 3λ 7 , 1,−5 : 10λ 7 − 10 = 0⇒λ = 7. Lines through (1, 2, 3), (1, 5, 6) with directions (−3, 1, 2), (−3, 1,−5): (⃗b1×⃗b2)· (⃗ a2−⃗ a1) = (−7,−21, 0)· (0, 3, 3) =−63̸= 0, so the lines do not intersect (skew).
