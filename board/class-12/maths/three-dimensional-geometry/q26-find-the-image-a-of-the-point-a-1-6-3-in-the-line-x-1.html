Three Dimensional Geometry — Question 26 (CBSE 2025, 5 marks)

CBSE 202565/1/15 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Mathematics · Three Dimensional Geometry
Question as text
(a) Find the image A′ of the point A(1, 6, 3) in the line x 1 = y− 1 2 = z− 2 3 . Also, find the equation of the line joining A and A′. OR (b) Find a point P on the line x + 5 1 = y + 3 4 = z− 6 −9 such that its distance from point Q(2, 4,−1) is 7 units. Also, find the equation of line joining P and Q.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Foot of perpendicular (1, 3, 5) (λ = 1), A′ = (1, 0, 7); line AA′: x− 1 0 = y− 6 −3 = z− 3 2 . (b) P = (λ− 5, 4λ− 3, 6− 9λ), PQ 2 = 98λ2− 196λ + 147 = 49⇒λ = 1, P (−4, 1,−3); line PQ : x− 2 6 = y− 4 3 = z + 1 2 .
