Fill in the blank: (a) The integrating factor of the differential equation xdy dx + 2y =x2 is . OR (b) The degree of the differential equation 1 + dy dx 2 =x is .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) dy dx + 2 xy =x; I.F. = e2 logx =x2. (b) Degree = 2.
