Question 30 · Class 12 Mathematics · Differential Equations
Question as text
(a) Solve the differential equation: x2ydx− (x3 +y3)dy = 0. OR (b) Solve the differential equation (1 + x2)dy dx + 2xy− 4x2 = 0 subject to initial condition y(0) = 0.
Answer
Answer as text
