Question as text
Assertion (A): One of the particular solutions of the differential equationdy dx =ex+y can beex+e−y = −2. Reason (R): ex +e−y =C is the general solution of the differential equation dy dx =ex+y. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) e−ydy =exdx⇒ex +e−y =C (R true); ex +e−y > 0, so it can never equal−2 (A false).
