The order and degree of the differential equation d2y dx2 2 + dy dx 2 =x sin dy dx are: (A) order 2, degree 2 (B) order 2, degree 1 (C) order 2, degree not defined (D) order 1, degree not defined
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Highest order 2; equation is not a polynomial in derivatives (sin dy dx), so degree not defined.
