The order and degree of the following differential equation are, respectively: −d4y dx4 + 2edy/dx +y2 = 0 (A) −4, 1 (B) 4, not defined (C) 1 , 1 (D) 4 , 1
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Highest derivative d4y dx4 : order 4; edy/dx is not a polynomial in derivatives, so degree not defined.
