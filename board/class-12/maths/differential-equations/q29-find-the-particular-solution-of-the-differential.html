Question 29 · Class 12 Mathematics · Differential Equations
Question as text
Find the particular solution of the differential equation h x sin2 y x −y i dx +xdy = 0 given that y = π 4 , when x = 1.
Answer
Answer as text
Homogeneous: dy dx = y x− sin2 y x; y =vx: csc2vdv =−dx x ⇒ cotv = log|x| +C; x = 1, v = π 4 : C = 1. Solution: cot y x = log|x| + 1.
