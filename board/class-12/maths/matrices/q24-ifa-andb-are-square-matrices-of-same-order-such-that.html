Question as text
IfA andB are square matrices of same order such that AB =A andBA =B, then A2 +B2 is equal to: (A) A +B (B) BA (C) 2( A +B) (D) 2 BA
Answer
Answer as text
(A) A2 = (AB)A =A(BA) =AB =A; similarly B2 =B; sum =A +B.
