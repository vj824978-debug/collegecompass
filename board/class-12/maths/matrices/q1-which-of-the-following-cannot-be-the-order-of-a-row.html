Question as text
Which of the following cannot be the order of a row-matrix? (A) 2 × 1 (B) 1 × 2 (C) 1 × 1 (D) 1 ×n
Answer
Answer as text
(A) A row matrix has exactly one row (1 ×n); 2× 1 is a column matrix.
