Question as text
Assertion (A): IfA andB are two square matrices such that AB andBA are defined, then it is not necessary that AB =BA. Reason (R): Product of two diagonal matrices of same order is commutative. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; diagonal-matrix commutativity does not explain why general AB̸=BA.
