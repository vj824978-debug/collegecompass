Question 31 · Class 12 Mathematics · Matrices
Question as text
What is the total number of possible matrices of order 3 × 3 with each entry as √ 2 or √ 3? (A) 9 (B) 512 (C) 615 (D) 64
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 9 entries, 2 choices each: 2 9 = 512.
