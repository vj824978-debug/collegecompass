Matrices — Question 43 (CBSE 2023, 3 marks)

CBSE 20233 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Mathematics · Matrices
Question as text
If A = 1 2 3 3 −2 1 4 2 1 , then show that A3− 23A− 40I =O.
Answer
Answer as text
Proof: A2 = 19 4 8 1 12 8 14 6 15 , A3 = 63 46 69 69 −6 23 92 46 63 = 23A + 40I, hence A3− 23A− 40I =O.
