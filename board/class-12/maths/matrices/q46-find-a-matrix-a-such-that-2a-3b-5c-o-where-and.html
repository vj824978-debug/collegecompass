Matrices — Question 46 (CBSE 2019, 2 marks)

CBSE 20192 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Mathematics · Matrices
Question as text
Find a matrix A such that 2A− 3B + 5C =O, where B = −2 2 0 3 1 4 and C = 2 0 −2 7 1 6 .
Answer
Answer as text
A = 1 2(3B− 5C) = −8 3 5 −13 −1 −9 .
