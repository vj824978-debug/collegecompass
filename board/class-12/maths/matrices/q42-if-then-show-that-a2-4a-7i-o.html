Matrices — Question 42 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 202565/7/12 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Mathematics · Matrices
Question as text
If A = 2 3 −1 2 , then show that A2− 4A + 7I =O.
Answer
Answer as text
Proof: A2 = 1 12 −4 1 , 4A = 8 12 −4 8 , A2− 4A =−7I, so A2− 4A + 7I =O.
