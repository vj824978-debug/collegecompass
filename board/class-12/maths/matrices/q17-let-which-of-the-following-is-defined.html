CBSE 202565/1/1
Question 17 · Class 12 Mathematics · Matrices
Question as text
Let A = 1 −2 −1 0 4 −1 −3 2 1 , B = −2 −5 −7 , C = 9 8 7 , which of the following is defined? (A) Only AB (B) Only AC (C) Only BA (D) All AB, AC and BA
Answer
Answer as text
(A) A3×3B3×1 defined; AC (3× 3, 1× 3) and BA (3× 1, 3× 3) not defined.
