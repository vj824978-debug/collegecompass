Determinants — Question 34 (CBSE 2023, 4 marks)

CBSE 20234 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Mathematics · Determinants
Question as text
Case Study. Gautam buys 5 pens, 3 bags and 1 instrument box and pays a sum of Rs. 160. From the same shop, Vikram buys 2 pens, 1 bag and 3 instrument boxes and pays a sum of Rs. 190. Also Ankur buys 1 pen, 2 bags and 4 instrument boxes and pays a sum of Rs. 250. Based on the above information, answer the following questions: (i) Convert the given above situation into a matrix equation of the form AX =B. (ii) Find|A|. (iii) (a) Find A−1. OR (iii) (b) Determine P =A2− 5A.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) 5 3 1 2 1 3 1 2 4 x y z = (x,y,z = cost of pen, bag, instrument box). (ii)|A| =−22. (iii)(a) A−1 = 1 2 10 −8 5 −19 13 −3 7 1 (gives x = 10,y = 20,z = 50). (iii)(b) P =A2− 5A = 7 5 13 5 8 2 8 3 3 .
