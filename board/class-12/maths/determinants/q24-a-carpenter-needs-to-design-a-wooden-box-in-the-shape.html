Question as text
A carpenter needs to design a wooden box in the shape of a cuboid such that the sum of its length and breadth is 3 cm more than its height. Twice of its length, thrice of its breadth and its height add up to 10 cm. Its breadth added to 7 times its height is 1 cm less than 3 times its length. On the basis of the above information, answer the following questions: (i) Write the equations representing the various dimensions and express them as the matrix equation AX =B. 1 (ii) Find if A−1 exists. Justify your answer. 1 (iii) (a) Find A−1. 2 OR (iii) (b) Find A2 + 7I. 2
Answer
Answer as text
(i) l +b−h = 3, 2l + 3b +h = 10, 3l−b− 7h = 1 (i.e. −3l +b + 7h =−1): A = 1 1 −1 2 3 1 −3 1 7 , X = l b h , B = −1 (equivalent sign conventions accepted). (ii) |A| =−8̸= 0, so A−1 exists. (iii)(a) A−1 =−1 20 −8 4 −17 4 −3 11 −4 1 (giving l = 3, b = 1, h = 1 cm). (iii)(b) A2 + 7I = 13 3 −7 5 19 8 −22 7 60 .
