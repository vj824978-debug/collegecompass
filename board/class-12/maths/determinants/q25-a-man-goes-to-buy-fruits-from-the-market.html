Question as text
A man goes to buy fruits from the market. The shopkeeper informs him that 4 apples, 3 oranges and 2 bananas cost Rs. 60; 2 apples, 4 oranges and 6 bananas cost Rs. 90; whereas 6 apples, 2 oranges and 3 bananas cost Rs. 70. Using matrix method, find the cost of one fruit of each kind.
Answer
Answer as text
4a + 3o + 2b = 60, 2a + 4o + 6b = 90, 6a + 2o + 3b = 70;|A| = 50, X = 1 50(adjA)B gives apple Rs. 5, orange Rs. 8, banana Rs. 8.
