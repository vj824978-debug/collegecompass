Determinants — Question 11 (CBSE 2025)

CBSE 202565/4/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Mathematics · Determinants
Question as text
If M and N are square matrices of order 3 such that det( M) = m and MN = mI, then det(N) is equal to: (A) −1 (B) 1 (C) −m2 (D) m2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) |M||N| =m3|I|⇒ m|N| =m3⇒|N| =m2.
