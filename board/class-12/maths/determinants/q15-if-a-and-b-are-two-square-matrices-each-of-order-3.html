If A and B are two square matrices each of order 3 with |A| = 3 and|B| = 5, then|2AB| is: (A) 30 (B) 120 (C) 15 (D) 225
Answer
Answer as text
(B) |2AB| = 23|A||B| = 8· 15 = 120.
