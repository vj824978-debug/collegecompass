If the position vectors of three points A, B and C are 3ˆi + ˆj, 5ˆi + 6ˆj− 3ˆk and 4ˆj respectively, then show that they form an isosceles triangle.
Answer
Answer as text
Proof: ⃗AB = 2ˆi + 5ˆj− 3ˆk, ⃗BC =−5ˆi− 2ˆj + 3ˆk, ⃗CA = 3ˆi− 3ˆj; |AB| =|BC| = √ 38,|CA| = √ 18 (non-collinear), so isosceles.
