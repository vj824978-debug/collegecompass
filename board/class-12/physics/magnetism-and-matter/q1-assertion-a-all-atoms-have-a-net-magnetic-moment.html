Assertion (A): All atoms have a net magnetic moment.

CBSE 202655/1/1
Question 1 · Class 12 Physics · Magnetism and Matter
Question as text
Assertion (A): All atoms have a net magnetic moment. Reason (R): A current loop does not always behave as a magnetic dipole. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A false: atoms of diamagnetic substances have zero net magnetic moment. R false: a current loop always behaves as a magnetic dipole (m =IA).
