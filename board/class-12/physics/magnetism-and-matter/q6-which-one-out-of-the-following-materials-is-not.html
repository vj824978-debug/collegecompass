Which one out of the following materials is not paramagnetic?

CBSE 202555/4/1
Question 6 · Class 12 Physics · Magnetism and Matter
Question as text
Which one out of the following materials is not paramagnetic? (A) Aluminium (B) Sodium Chloride (C) Calcium (D) Copper Chloride
Answer
Answer as text
(B) NaCl is diamagnetic; Al, Ca, CuCl 2 are paramagnetic.
