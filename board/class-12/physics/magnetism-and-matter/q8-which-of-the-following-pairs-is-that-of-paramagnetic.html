Which of the following pairs is that of paramagnetic materials?

CBSE 202455/5/1
Question 8 · Class 12 Physics · Magnetism and Matter
Question as text
Which of the following pairs is that of paramagnetic materials? (A) Copper and Aluminium (B) Sodium and Calcium (C) Lead and Iron (D) Nickel and Cobalt
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Na and Ca are paramagnetic; Cu, Pb are diamagnetic, Fe, Ni, Co ferromagnetic.
