State Gauss’s law for magnetism.

CBSE 201955/1/13 marks
Question 10 · Class 12 Physics · Magnetism and Matter
Question as text
(a) State Gauss’s law for magnetism. Explain its significance. (b) Write the four important properties of the magnetic field lines due to a bar magnet. OR Write three points of differences between para-, dia- and ferro- magnetic materials, giving one example for each.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero, H ⃗B·d⃗S = 0; isolated magnetic poles (monopoles) do not exist — field lines form closed loops. (b) Field lines form continuous closed loops (S→N inside magnet); tangent gives direction of ⃗B; density of lines gives strength of field; no two lines intersect. OR: Dia: −1≤χ< 0, 0≤µr < 1, weakly repelled (move to weaker field), e.g. Bi, Cu. Para: small positive χ, µr slightly> 1, weakly attracted, e.g. Al, Na. Ferro: χ≫ 1, µr≫ 1, strongly attracted, e.g. Fe, Ni, Co.
