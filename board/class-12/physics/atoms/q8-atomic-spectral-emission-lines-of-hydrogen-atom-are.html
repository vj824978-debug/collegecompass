CBSE 202555/2/1
Question 8 · Class 12 Physics · Atoms
Question as text
Atomic spectral emission lines of hydrogen atom are incident on a zinc surface. The lines which can emit photoelectrons from the surface are members of (A) Balmer series (B) Paschen series (C) Lyman series (D) Neither Balmer, nor Paschen nor Lyman series
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Zinc work function ≈ 4.3 eV; only Lyman lines (10.2–13.6 eV, UV) exceed it; Balmer max 3.4 eV.
