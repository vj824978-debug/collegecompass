Assertion (A): The Balmer series in hydrogen atom spectrum is formed when the electron jumps from higher energy state to the ground state. Reason (R): In Bohr’s model of hydrogen atom, the electron can jump between successive orbits only. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Balmer lines end at n = 2, not the ground state; electron can jump between any allowed orbits, not only successive ones. Both false.
