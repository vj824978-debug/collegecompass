(i) Differentiate between ‘distance of closest approach’ and ‘impact parameter’.

CBSE 202355/5/13 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Physics · Atoms
Question as text
(a) (i) Differentiate between ‘distance of closest approach’ and ‘impact parameter’. (ii) Determine the distance of closest approach when an alpha particle of kinetic energy 3.95 MeV approaches a nucleus of Z = 79, stops and reverses its directions. OR (b) (i) State three postulates of Bohr’s theory of hydrogen atom. (ii) Find the angular momentum of an electron revolving in the second orbit in Bohr’s hydrogen atom.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Distance of closest approach: minimum distance from the nucleus reached by a head-on α-particle when all its KE becomes PE. Impact parameter: perpendicular distance of the initial velocity line of the α-particle from the centre of the nucleus. (ii) r0 = 1 4πε0 (Ze)(2e) K = 9× 109× 79× 2× (1.6× 10−19)2 3.95× 106× 1.6× 10−19 = 5.76× 10−14 m. OR (b)(i) Electrons revolve in certain stable (stationary) circular orbits without radiating; only orbits with L =nh/2π are allowed; a photon hν =Ei−Ef is emitted/absorbed in a transition between orbits. (ii) L = 2h 2π = h π≈ 2.11× 10−34 J s.
