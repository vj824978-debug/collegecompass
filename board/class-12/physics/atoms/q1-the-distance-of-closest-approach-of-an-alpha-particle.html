The ‘distance of closest approach’ of an alpha-particle is ‘d’ when it moves with a velocity v head-on towards the target nucleus. If the velocity of alpha particle is halved, the new ‘distance of closest approach’ will be (A) d 2 (B) 2 d (C) d 4 (D) 4 d
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1 2mv2 = k(2e)(Ze) d ⇒d∝ 1/v2; halving v gives 4d.
