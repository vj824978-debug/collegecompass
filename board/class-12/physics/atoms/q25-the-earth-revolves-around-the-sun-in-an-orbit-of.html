The earth revolves around the sun in an orbit of radius 1.5× 1011 m with orbital speed 30 km/s.

CBSE 202455/5/12 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Physics · Atoms
Question as text
The earth revolves around the sun in an orbit of radius 1.5× 1011 m with orbital speed 30 km/s. Find the quantum number that characterises its revolution using Bohr’s model in this case (mass of earth = 6.0× 1024 kg).
Answer
Answer as text
mvr = nh 2π⇒n = 2πmvr h = 2π× 6× 1024× 3× 104× 1.5× 1011 6.63× 10−34 ≈ 2.6× 1074.
