Assertion (A): In Bohr model of hydrogen atom, the angular momentum of an electron in n th orbit is proportional to the square root of its orbit radius r n. Reason (R): According to Bohr model, electron can jump to its nearest orbits only. (A) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (B) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (C) If Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false. (D) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) L = nh 2π , rn∝n2⇒L∝√rn (A true); electron can jump between any orbits (R false).
