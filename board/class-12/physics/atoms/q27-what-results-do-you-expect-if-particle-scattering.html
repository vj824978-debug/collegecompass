CBSE 2022T erm II55/5/12 marks
Question 27 · Class 12 Physics · Atoms
Question as text
(a) What results do you expect if α-particle scattering experiment is repeated using a thin sheet of hydrogen in place of a gold foil? Explain. (Hydrogen is a solid at temperature below 14 K) OR (b) Why it is the frequency and not the intensity of light source that determines whether emission of photoelectrons will occur or not? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The hydrogen nucleus (proton) is lighter than the α-particle, so α-particles are only slightly deflected (maximum deflection ≈ sin−1(1/4)≈ 14.5◦); no large-angle scattering or rebound occurs, so the presence of a massive nucleus cannot be inferred. OR: (b) Light is absorbed in photons of energy hν, one photon per electron. Emission needs hν≥ϕ0, i.e. ν≥ν0; intensity only changes the number of photons, not the energy of each, so below ν0 no emission occurs however intense the light.
