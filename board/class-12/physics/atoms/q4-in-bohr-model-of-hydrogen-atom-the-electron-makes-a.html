Question as text
In Bohr model of hydrogen atom, the electron makes a transition from n = 5 to n = 1 state. As a result, a photon of wavelength λ is emitted in the process. The wavelength of the photon emitted when an electron makes a transition from energy level n = 5 to n = 2 will be (A) 8 7λ (B) 24 7 λ (C) 16 7 λ (D) 32 7 λ
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1 /λ =R(1− 1/25) = 24R/25; 1/λ′ =R(1/4− 1/25) = 21R/100; λ′/λ = 96/21 = 32/7.
