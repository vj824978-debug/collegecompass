Calculate the de Broglie wavelength associated with the electron revolving in the first excited state of hydrogen atom. The ground state energy of hydrogen atom is −13.6 eV.
Answer
Answer as text
E2 =−13.6/4 =−3.4 eV,K = 3.4 eV= 5.44× 10−19 J. λ = h√ 2mK = 6.63× 10−34 √ 2× 9.1× 10−31× 5.44× 10−19≈ 6.7× 10−10 m ≈ 0.67 nm (check: λ = 2πr2/2 =π(4× 0.53 ˚A)≈ 6.65 ˚A).
