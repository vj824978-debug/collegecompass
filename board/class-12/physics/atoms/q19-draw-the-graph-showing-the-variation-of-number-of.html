Question as text
Draw the graph showing the variation of number of scattered alpha particles ( N) detected with scattering angle (θ) in Geiger-Marsden experiment. Infer two conclusions from the graph.
Answer
Answer as text
Graph: N (log scale) vs θ: very large at small θ, falling steeply with increasing θ (roughly N∝ 1/ sin4(θ/2)), a very small but non-zero number at θ> 90◦, even near 180◦. Conclusions: (1) most of the atom is empty space (most α particles pass nearly undeflected); (2) the positive charge and almost all the mass are concentrated in a tiny central nucleus (few particles deflected through large angles/rebound).
