State Bohr’s second postulate and mention its significance.

CBSE 202655/2/13 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Physics · Atoms
Question as text
(a) State Bohr’s second postulate and mention its significance. (b) Prove that, in Bohr model of hydrogen atom as principal quantum number n becomes large, the energy levels get closer and closer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Electron revolves only in orbits where mvr =nh/(2π); significance: quantisation of angular momentum, hence discrete stationary orbits/energies. (b) En =−13.6/n2 eV; En+1−En = 13.6 2n + 1 n2(n + 1)2≈ 27.2 n3 → 0 as n increases, so levels get closer.
