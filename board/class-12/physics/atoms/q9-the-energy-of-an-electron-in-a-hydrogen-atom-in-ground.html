Question as text
The energy of an electron in a hydrogen atom in ground state is −13.6 eV. Its energy in an orbit corresponding to quantum number n is −0.544 eV. The value of n is (A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 5
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 13.6 n2 = 0.544⇒n2 = 25, n = 5.
