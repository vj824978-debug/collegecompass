Paschen series in spectrum of hydrogen atom lies in: (A) infrared region (B) ultraviolet region (C) visible region (D) partly in ultraviolet region and partly in visible region
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Paschen series: transitions to n = 3; λ from 820 nm to 1875 nm, infrared.
