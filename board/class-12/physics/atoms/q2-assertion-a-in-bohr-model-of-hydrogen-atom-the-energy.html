Assertion (A): In Bohr model of hydrogen atom, the energy levels are discrete and quantised.

CBSE 202655/1/1
Question 2 · Class 12 Physics · Atoms
Question as text
Assertion (A): In Bohr model of hydrogen atom, the energy levels are discrete and quantised. Reason (R): In a hydrogen atom, the electrostatic force on the electron provides the necessary centripetal force to it to revolve around the nucleus. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; discreteness of energy levels comes from quantisation of angular momentum (mvr =nh/2π), not from the Coulomb force providing the centripetal force.
