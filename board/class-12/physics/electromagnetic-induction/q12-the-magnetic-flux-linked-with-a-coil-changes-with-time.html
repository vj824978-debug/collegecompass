Electromagnetic Induction — Question 12 (CBSE 2025)

CBSE 202555/5/1
Question 12 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
The magnetic flux linked with a coil changes with time t as ϕ = (8t2 + 5t + 7), where t is in seconds and ϕ is in Wb. The value of emf induced in the coil at t = 4 s is : (A) 32 V (B) 37 V (C) 64 V (D) 69 V
Answer
Answer as text
(D) |e| =dϕ/dt = 16t + 5 = 69 V att = 4 s.
