Show that the energy required to build up the current I in a coil of inductance L is 1 2LI2.

CBSE 202555/1/13 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
(a) Show that the energy required to build up the current I in a coil of inductance L is 1 2LI2. (b) Considering the case of magnetic field produced by air-filled current carrying solenoid, show that the magnetic energy density of a magnetic field B is B2 2µ0 .
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Derivation: back emf ε =LdI/dt ; work dW =εIdt =LIdI ; W = RI 0 LIdI = 1 2LI2. (b) Solenoid: L =µ0n2Al, B =µ0nI; U = 1 2LI2 = 1 2µ0n2Al B µ0n 2 = B2 2µ0 Al; energy per unit volume uB = B2 2µ0 .
