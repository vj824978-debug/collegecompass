(i) Define self-inductance of a coil.

CBSE 202555/5/15 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
(a) (i) Define self-inductance of a coil. Derive the expression for the energy required to build up a current I in a coil of self-inductance L. (ii) The currents passing through two inductors of self-inductances 10 mH and 20 mH increase with time at the same rate. Draw graphs showing the variation of : (I) the magnitude of emf induced with the rate of change of current in each inductor. (II) the energy stored in each inductor with the current flowing through it. OR (b) (i) Define the term mutual inductance. Deduce the expression for the mutual inductance of two long coaxial solenoids of the same length having different radii and different number of turns. (ii) The current through an inductor is uniformly increased from zero to 2 A in 40 s. An emf of 5 mV is induced during this period. Find the flux linked with the inductor at t = 10 s.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Self-inductance: L =NΦ/I (flux linkage per unit current), or emf induced per unit rate of change of current. Derivation: work against back emf dW =εIdt =LIdI ; W = RI 0 LIdI = 1 2LI2. (ii)(I) |ε| =L (dI/dt): two straight lines through the origin, the 20 mH line having twice the slope. (II) U = 1 2LI2: two parabolas from the origin, the 20 mH curve steeper (twice U at each I). (b)(i) Mutual inductance: flux linked with one coil per unit current in the other, M =N2Φ2/I1 (or emf induced in one per unit rate of change of current in the other). Derivation: inner solenoid ( r1, n1) and outer (r2, n2), length l; current I2 in outer gives B =µ0n2I2; N1Φ1 =n1lπr 2 1µ0n2I2⇒M =µ0n1n2πr2 1l (M12 =M21). (ii) dI/dt = 2/40 = 0.05 A/s;L =ε/(dI/dt) = 5× 10−3/0.05 = 0.1 H. At t = 10 s,I = 0.5 A, Φ =LI = 0.05 Wb.
