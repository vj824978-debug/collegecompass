(i) Define mutual inductance of a pair of coils.

CBSE 202655/2/13 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
(a) (i) Define mutual inductance of a pair of coils. Write its SI unit. (ii) A long solenoid of radius R and length L has n turns per unit length. A circular loop of radius r (<R ) is placed inside at the centre of the solenoid such that its axis coincides with the axis of the solenoid. Obtain the mutual inductance of the solenoid and the loop. OR (b) Two long straight parallel conductors A and B carrying steady currents Ia and Ib in the same direction are separated by a distance d. Deduce the expressions for the force acting on length L of conductor B due to conductor A and show it in figure. Write the expression for the force acting on length L of conductor A due to conductor B and show that it follows Newton’s third law.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Mutual inductance M: flux linked with one coil per unit current in the other, N2Φ2 =MI 1 (or emf induced per unit rate of change of current, ε2 =−MdI 1/dt); SI unit henry (H). (ii) B =µ0nI inside solenoid; flux through loop = µ0nIπr 2, so M =µ0nπr2. (b) Field of A at B: Ba =µ0Ia/(2πd); force on length L of B: Fba =IbLBa = µ0IaIbL 2πd , directed towards A (attractive). Similarly Fab = µ0IaIbL 2πd towards B; ⃗Fab =−⃗Fba (Newton’s third law). Figure: two parallel wires, currents same direction, forces pointing towards each other.
