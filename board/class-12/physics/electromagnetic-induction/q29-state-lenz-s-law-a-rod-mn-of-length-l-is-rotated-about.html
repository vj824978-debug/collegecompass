CBSE 202555/6/13 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
(a) State Lenz’s law. A rod MN of length L is rotated about an axis passing through its end M perpendicular to its length, with a constant angular velocity ω in a uniform magnetic field ⃗B parallel to the axis. Obtain an expression for emf induced between its ends. OR (b) Define ‘self-inductance’ of a coil. Derive an expression for self-inductance of a long solenoid of cross-sectional area A and length l, having n turns per unit length.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Lenz’s law: the polarity of induced emf is such that it tends to produce a current which opposes the change in magnetic flux that produced it. Derivation: element dr at distance r moves with v =ωr, dε =Bωrdr ; ε = RL 0 Bωrdr = 1 2BωL2. (b) Self-inductance: flux linked with a coil per unit current through it (NΦ =LI; or emf induced per unit rate of change of current). Derivation: B =µ0nI, total linkage NΦ = (nl)(µ0nI)A, so L =µ0n2Al.
