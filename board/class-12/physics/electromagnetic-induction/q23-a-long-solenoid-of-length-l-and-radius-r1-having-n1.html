Question as text
A long solenoid of length L and radius r1 having N1 turns is surrounded symmetrically by a coil of radius r2 (> r1) having N2 turns (N2 ≪ N1) around its mid-point. Derive an expression for the mutual inductance of solenoid and coil. Is M12 =M21 valid in this case?
Answer
Answer as text
Derivation: field of solenoid B =µ0N1I1/L exists only inside radius r1; flux linkage with coil N2Φ =N2µ0 N1 L I1πr2 1; M21 = µ0N1N2πr2 L . Yes, M12 =M21 (reciprocity) is valid, though M12 is hard to calculate directly because the coil’s field through the solenoid is non-uniform.
