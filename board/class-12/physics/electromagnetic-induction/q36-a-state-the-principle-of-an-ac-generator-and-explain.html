CBSE 20185 marks
Question 36 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
(a) (a) State the principle of an ac generator and explain its working with the help of a labelled diagram. Obtain the expression for the emf induced in a coil having N turns each of cross-sectional area A, rotating with a constant angular speed ‘ ω’ in a magnetic field ⃗B, directed perpendicular to the axis of rotation. [old syllabus] (b) An aeroplane is flying horizontally from west to east with a velocity of 900 km/hour. Calculate the potential difference developed between the ends of its wings having a span of 20 m. The horizontal component of the Earth’s magnetic field is 5 × 10−4 T and the angle of dip is 30 ◦. OR (b) A device X is connected across an ac source of voltage V = V0 sinωt. The current through X is given as I =I0 sin ωt + π . (a) Identify the device X and write the expression for its reactance. (b) Draw graphs showing variation of voltage and current with time over one cycle of ac, for X. (c) How does the reactance of the device X vary with frequency of the ac? Show this variation graphically. (d) Draw the phasor diagram for the device X.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Principle: electromagnetic induction. Diagram: coil (armature) rotating between poles N, S; slip rings, carbon brushes, external circuit. Derivation: Φ = NBA cosωt, e =−dΦ dt ⇒e =NBAω sinωt. (b) v = 250 m/s;BV =BH tan 30◦ = 2.89× 10−4 T; e =BVlv = 2.89× 10−4× 20× 250≈ 1.44 V. OR: (a) Current leads voltage by π/2: capacitor; XC = 1 ωC = 1 2πνC . (b) Diagram: V =V0 sinωt and I =I0 cosωt over 0 to 2π, current peaking a quarter cycle earlier. (c) XC∝ 1 ν ; graph: XC vs ν a rectangular hyperbola. (d) Diagram: phasor I0 ahead of phasor V0 by 90◦ (anticlockwise rotation).
