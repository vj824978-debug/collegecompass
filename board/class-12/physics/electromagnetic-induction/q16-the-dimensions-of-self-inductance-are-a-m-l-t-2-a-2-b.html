Question 16 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
The dimensions of ‘self-inductance’ are: (A) [M L T −2 A−2] (B) [M L 2 T−1 A−1] (C) [M L −1 T−2 A−2] (D) [M L 2 T−2 A−2]
Answer
Answer as text
(D) L =ε/(dI/dt): [ML 2T−3A−1]· [T A−1] = [ML2T−2A−2].
