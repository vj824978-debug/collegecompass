Two coils C 1 and C 2 are placed close to each other. The magnetic flux ϕ2 linked with the coil C 2 varies with the current I 1 flowing in coil C 1, as shown in the figure. Find (i) the mutual inductance of the arrangement, and (ii) the rate of change of current dI1 dt that will induce an emf of 100 V in coil C 2.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) M = ϕ2 I1 = 10 Wb 4 A = 2.5 H (slope of graph). (ii) ε =MdI1 dt ⇒ dI1 dt = 100 2.5 = 40 A/s.
