A square loop of side 50 cm is placed in a uniform magnetic field of 3.0 T acting perpendicular to the plane of the loop. If the loop is rotated through an angle of 90 ◦ in 0.3 s, the value of emf induced in the loop would be: (A) 0.25 V (B) 0.50 V (C) 0.75 V (D) 1.0 V
Answer
Answer as text
none ∆Φ =BA = 3.0× 0.25 = 0.75 Wb;ε = 0.75/0.3 = 2.5 V, not among options. The setter probably intended (C), which is ∆Φ in Wb.
