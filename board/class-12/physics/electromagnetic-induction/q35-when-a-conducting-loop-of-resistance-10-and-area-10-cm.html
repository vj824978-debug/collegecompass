When a conducting loop of resistance 10 Ω and area 10 cm 2 is removed from external magnetic field acting normally, the variation of induced current in the loop with time is as shown in the figure. Find the (a) total charges passed through the loop. (b) change in magnetic flux through the loop. (c) magnitude of the magnetic field applied.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) q = area under I–t graph = 1 2× 0.4× 1.0 = 0.2 C. (b) q = ∆ϕ R ⇒ ∆ϕ = 0.2× 10 = 2 Wb. (c) B = ∆ϕ A = 2 10× 10−4 = 2× 103 T (unphysically large, but follows from the given data).
