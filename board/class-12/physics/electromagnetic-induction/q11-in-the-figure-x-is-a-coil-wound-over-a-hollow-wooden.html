Question as text
In the figure X is a coil wound over a hollow wooden pipe. A permanent magnet is pushed at a constant speed v from the right into the pipe and it comes out at the left end of the pipe. During the entry and the exit of the magnet, the current in the wire YZ will be from (A) Y to Z and then Y to Z (B) Z to Y and then Y to Z (C) Y to Z and then Z to Y (D) Z to Y and then Z to Y
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Flux through coil increases on entry and decreases on exit, so current reverses. Winding (front strands slope down to the right): entry needs induced field towards right (right end N), giving current Z to Y in YZ; exit: Y to Z.
