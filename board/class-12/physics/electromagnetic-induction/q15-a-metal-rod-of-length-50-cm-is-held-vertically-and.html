A metal rod of length 50 cm is held vertically and moved with a velocity of 10 m/s towards east. The horizontal component of the Earth’s magnetic field at the place is 0.4 G. The emf induced across the ends of the rod is: (A) 0.1 mV (B) 0.2 mV (C) 0.8 mV (D) 1.6 mV
Answer
Answer as text
(B) ε =BHlv = 0.4× 10−4× 0.5× 10 = 2× 10−4 V = 0.2 mV.
