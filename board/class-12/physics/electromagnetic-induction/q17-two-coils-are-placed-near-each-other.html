Question 17 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
Two coils are placed near each other. When the current in one coil is changed at the rate of 5 A/s, an emf of 2 mV is induced in the other. The mutual inductance of the two coils is (A) 0.4 mH (B) 2.5 mH (C) 10 mH (D) 2.5 H
Answer
Answer as text
(A) M = ε dI/dt = 2× 10−3 5 = 0.4 mH.
