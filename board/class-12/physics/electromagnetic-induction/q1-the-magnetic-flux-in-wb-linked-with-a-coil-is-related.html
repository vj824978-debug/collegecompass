The magnetic flux ϕ (in Wb) linked with a coil is related to time t (in s) as ϕ = 5At2 +Bt− 2C The SI units of A and B are respectively (A) Wb s 2, Wb s (B) Wb s −1, Wb (C) Wb s−2, Wb s−1 (D) Wb s−1, Wb s−2
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Each term in Wb: [ A] = Wb s−2, [B] = Wb s−1.
