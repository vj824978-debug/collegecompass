Assertion (A): Induced emf produced in a coil will be more when the magnetic flux linked with the coil is…

CBSE 202655/5/1
Question 7 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Induction
Question as text
Assertion (A): Induced emf produced in a coil will be more when the magnetic flux linked with the coil is more. Reason (R): Induced emf produced is directly proportional to the magnetic flux. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Induced emf depends on the rate of change of flux (ε =−dΦ/dt), not on the flux itself; both statements are false.
