Assertion (A): During formation of a nucleus, the mass defect produced is the source of the binding energy of the nucleus. Reason (R): For all nuclei, the value of binding energy per nucleon increases with mass number. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Mass defect ×c2 = binding energy (A true); BE/nucleon rises then falls after A≈ 56 (R false).
