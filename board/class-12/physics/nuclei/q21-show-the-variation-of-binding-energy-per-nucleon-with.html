Show the variation of binding energy per nucleon with mass number.

CBSE 202555/2/13 marks
Question 21 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
(a) Show the variation of binding energy per nucleon with mass number. Write the significance of the binding energy curve. (b) Two nuclei with lower binding energy per nucleon form a nuclei with more binding energy per nucleon. (i) What type of nuclear reaction is it? (ii) Whether the total mass of nuclei increases, decreases or remains unchanged? (iii) Does the process require energy or produce energy?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Curve of Ebn vs A: rises steeply for light nuclei, broad maximum ≈ 8.75 MeV near A≈ 56 (Fe), nearly constant (≈ 8 MeV) for A = 30–170, falls slowly to≈ 7.6 MeV for A≈ 238. Significance: nuclear force is short-range/saturating; middle-mass nuclei most stable; energy released by fusion of light nuclei and fission of heavy nuclei. (b)(i) Nuclear fusion. (ii) Total mass decreases (mass defect). (iii) Produces (releases) energy.
