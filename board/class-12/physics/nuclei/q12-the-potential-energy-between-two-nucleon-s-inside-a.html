The potential energy between two nucleon’s inside a nucleus is minimum at a distance of about (A) 0.8 fm (B) 1.6 fm (C) 2.0 fm (D) 2.8 fm
Answer
Answer as text
(A) The nucleon–nucleon potential energy has its minimum at r0≈ 0.8 fm (repulsive below it).
