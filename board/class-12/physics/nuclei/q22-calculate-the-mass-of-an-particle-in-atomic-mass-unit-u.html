Calculate the mass of an α-particle in atomic mass unit (u).

CBSE 202555/4/12 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
Calculate the mass of an α-particle in atomic mass unit (u). Given, Mass of a normal helium atom = 4 .002603 u Mass of carbon atom = 1.9926× 10−26 kg
Answer
Answer as text
1 u = 1.9926× 10−26/12 = 1.6605× 10−27 kg; me = 9.1× 10−31/1.6605× 10−27 = 5.48× 10−4 u. mα =mHe− 2me = 4.002603− 0.001096≈ 4.001507 u (electron binding energy neglected).
