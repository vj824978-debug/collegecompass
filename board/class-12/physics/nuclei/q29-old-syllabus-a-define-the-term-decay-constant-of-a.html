Question 29 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
[old syllabus] (a) Define the term decay constant of a radioactive substance. (b) The half-life of 238 92 U undergoing decay is 4.5× 109 years. Calculate the activity of a 10 g sample of 238 92 U.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) λ in dN dt =−λN: the fraction of nuclei decaying per unit time (reciprocal of time in which N falls to 1/e of its value). (b) N = 10 238× 6.023× 1023 = 2.53× 1022; λ = 0.693 4.5× 109× 3.15× 107 s = 4.9× 10−18 s−1; A =λN≈ 1.2× 105 Bq (≈ 3.3µCi).
