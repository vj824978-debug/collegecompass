Question as text
A heavy nucleus P of mass number 240 and binding energy 7.6 MeV per nucleon splits in to two nuclei Q and R of mass numbers 110 and 130 and binding energy per nucleon 8.5 MeV and 8.4 MeV, respectively. Calculate the energy released in the fission.
Answer
Answer as text
Q = (110× 8.5 + 130× 8.4)− 240× 7.6 = (935 + 1092)− 1824 = 203 MeV.
