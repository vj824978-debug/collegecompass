Define ‘Mass defect’ and ‘Binding energy’ of a nucleus.

CBSE 202555/1/13 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
(a) Define ‘Mass defect’ and ‘Binding energy’ of a nucleus. Describe ‘Fission process’ on the basis of binding energy per nucleon. (b) A deuteron contains a proton and a neutron and has a mass of 2.013553 u. Calculate the mass defect for it in u and its energy equivalence in MeV. ( mp = 1.007277 u, mn = 1.008665 u, 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c2)
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Mass defect: ∆M = [Zmp + (A−Z)mn]−M; binding energy Eb = ∆Mc 2 (energy needed to separate the nucleus into its nucleons). Fission: a heavy nucleus (A≈ 240, BE/A≈ 7.6 MeV) splits into two medium nuclei (BE/A≈ 8.5 MeV); the gain of≈ 0.9 MeV per nucleon releases≈ 200 MeV per fission. (b) ∆m = 1.007277 + 1.008665− 2.013553 = 0.002389 u; E = 0.002389× 931.5≈ 2.225 MeV.
