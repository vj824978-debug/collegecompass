Assertion (A): Energy is released when heavy nuclei undergo fission or light nuclei undergo fusion.

CBSE 202655/5/1
Question 6 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
Assertion (A): Energy is released when heavy nuclei undergo fission or light nuclei undergo fusion. Reason (R): For heavy nuclei, binding energy per nucleon increases with increasing Z while for light nuclei, it decreases with increasing Z. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A true. R is false: for heavy nuclei BE/nucleon decreases with increasing mass number, and for light nuclei it increases, which is why fission and fusion release energy.
