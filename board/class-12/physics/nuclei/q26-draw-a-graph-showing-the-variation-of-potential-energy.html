Draw a graph showing the variation of potential energy of a pair of nucleons as a function of their separation. Indicate the region in which the nuclear force is (a) attractive and (b) repulsive.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: PE vs r — large positive for small r, falls to a minimum (about −100 MeV) atr0≈ 0.8 fm, then rises towards zero by a few fm. (a) Attractive for r>r 0 (slope positive); (b) repulsive for r<r 0.
