When a neutron collides with 235 92 U, the nucleus gives 140 54 Xe and 94 38Sr as fission products and two neutrons are ejected. Calculate the mass defect and the energy released (in MeV) in the process. Given : m 235 92 U = 235.04393 u, m 140 54 Xe = 139.92164 u m 94 38Sr = 93.91536 u, 1 0n = 1.00866 u 1 u = 931 MeV/c2
Answer
Answer as text
0n + 235 92 U→ 140 54 Xe + 94 38Sr + 21 0n. ∆m = (235.04393+1.00866)−(139.92164+93.91536+2.01732) = 0.19827 u. Q = 0.19827× 931≈ 184.6 MeV.
