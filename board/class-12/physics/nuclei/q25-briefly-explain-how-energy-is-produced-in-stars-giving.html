Briefly explain how energy is produced in stars, giving two examples of the nuclear reactions involved.

CBSE 202555/7/12 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
Briefly explain how energy is produced in stars, giving two examples of the nuclear reactions involved.
Answer
Answer as text
Energy in stars comes from thermonuclear fusion of hydrogen into helium at very high temperature (∼ 107 K), the mass defect being released as energy. Proton–proton cycle: 1H+1 1H→2 1H+e+ +ν + 0.42 MeV; 2 1H+1 1H→3 2He+γ + 5.49 MeV; 2He+3 2He→4 2He+2 1 1H+12.86 MeV; net 4 1 1H+2e−→4 2He+2ν + 6γ + 26.7 MeV (CNO cycle also acceptable).
