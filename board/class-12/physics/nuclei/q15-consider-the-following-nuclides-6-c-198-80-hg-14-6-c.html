Question 15 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
(a) Consider the following nuclides: 6 C, 198 80 Hg, 14 6 C, 197 79 Au Group them into isotopes and isotones. (b) How does the size of a nucleus depend on its mass number A? Hence prove that the density of nucleus is a constant, independent of A, for all nuclei.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Isotopes: 12 6 C, 14 6 C (same Z). Isotones: 198 80 Hg, 197 79 Au (both N = 118). (b) R =R0A1/3; density ρ = mA 3πR3 0A = 3m 4πR3 , independent of A (≈ 2.3× 1017 kg m−3).
