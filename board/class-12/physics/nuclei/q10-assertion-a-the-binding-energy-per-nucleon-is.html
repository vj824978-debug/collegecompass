Question as text
Assertion (A): The binding energy per nucleon is practically constant for mass number in the range (30<A< 170). Reason (R): Nuclear forces between the nucleons for mass numbers in the range (30 <A< 170) are not short-range. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A is true; R is false: nuclear forces are short-range (saturation), which is why B/A is nearly constant.
