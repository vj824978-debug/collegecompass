Differentiate between nuclear fission and nuclear fusion.

CBSE 202655/3/13 marks
Question 17 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
Differentiate between nuclear fission and nuclear fusion. Give one example for each with nuclear reaction.
Answer
Answer as text
Fission: a heavy nucleus splits into two medium nuclei (by neutron absorption), e.g. 235 92 U +1 0 n→141 56 Ba +92 36 Kr + 31 0n+≈ 200 MeV. Fusion: light nuclei combine into a heavier one (needs very high temperature), e.g. 1 H +2 1 H→3 2 He +1 0 n + 3.27 MeV. Fusion releases more energy per nucleon; fission is controllable (reactors).
