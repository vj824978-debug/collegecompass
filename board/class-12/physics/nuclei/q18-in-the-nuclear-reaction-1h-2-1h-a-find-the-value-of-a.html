Nuclei — Question 18 (CBSE 2026, 3 marks)

CBSE 202655/4/13 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
In the nuclear reaction: 1H + 2 1H→ A 2 X + 1 0n (a) Find the value of A. (b) Calculate the amount of energy released in the reaction. Given: m 2 1H = 2.014102 u, m A 2 X = 3.016049 u, m 1 0n = 1.008665 u, 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c2
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Mass number: 2 + 2 = A + 1⇒A = 3 (X = 3 2He). (b) ∆m = 2(2.014102)− 3.016049− 1.008665 = 0.003490 u; Q = 0.003490× 931.5≈ 3.25 MeV released.
