Question 32 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
(a) Explain the processes of nuclear fission and nuclear fusion by using the plot of binding energy per nucleon (BE/A) versus the mass number A. [old syllabus] (b) A radioactive isotope has a half-life of 10 years. How long will it take for the activity to reduce to 3.125%?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: BE/A rises steeply for light nuclei, peaks (≈ 8.75 MeV) near A≈ 56, falls slowly to≈ 7.6 MeV atA≈ 238. Fission: a heavy nucleus (A∼ 240) splits into two middle-mass nuclei of higher BE/A (≈ 8.5 MeV), releasing≈ 0.9 MeV per nucleon. Fusion: very light nuclei ( A< 10) combine into a nucleus of higher BE/A, releasing energy. (b) 3.125% = 1 32 = 1 5 ⇒t = 5T1/2 = 50 years.
