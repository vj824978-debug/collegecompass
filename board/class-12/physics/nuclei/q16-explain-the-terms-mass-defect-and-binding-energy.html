Explain the terms mass defect and binding energy. How are they related?
Answer
Answer as text
Mass defect: ∆M = [Zmp + (A−Z)mn]−Mnucleus. Binding energy: energy needed to separate a nucleus into its constituent nucleons (energy released when it is formed). Relation: Eb = ∆Mc 2.
