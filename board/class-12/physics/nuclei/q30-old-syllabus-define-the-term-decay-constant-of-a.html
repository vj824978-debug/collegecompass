[old syllabus] Define the term ‘decay constant’ of a radioactive sample. The rate of disintegration of a given radioactive nucleus is 10000 disintegrations/s and 5000 disintegrations/s after 20 hr and 30 hr respectively from start. Calculate the half life and initial number of nuclei at t = 0.
Answer
Answer as text
Decay constant: λ =−dN/dt N , the rate of decay per nucleus (λ = ln 2/T1/2 = 1/τ). Rate halves in 30− 20 = 10 h⇒T1/2 = 10 h. R0 =R20× 22 = 40000 s−1. λ = 0.693 36000 = 1.925× 10−5 s−1; N0 = R0 λ = 40000 1.925× 10−5≈ 2.08× 109 nuclei.
