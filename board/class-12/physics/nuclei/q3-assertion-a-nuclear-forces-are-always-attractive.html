Assertion (A): Nuclear forces are always attractive.

CBSE 202655/3/1
Question 3 · Class 12 Physics · Nuclei
Question as text
Assertion (A): Nuclear forces are always attractive. Reason (R): The nuclear force between protons and neutrons in a nucleus is a weak force. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Nuclear force is repulsive below ∼ 0.8 fm (A false); it is the strongest force, not weak (R false).
