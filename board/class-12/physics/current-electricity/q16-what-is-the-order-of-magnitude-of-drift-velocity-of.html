What is the order of magnitude of drift velocity of electrons in a conductor?

CBSE 202655/1/12 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
What is the order of magnitude of drift velocity of electrons in a conductor? Deduce the relation between the current flowing through a conductor and drift velocity of electrons in it.
Answer
Answer as text
vd∼ 10−4 m s−1 (fraction of a mm per second). Derivation: charge crossing area A in time ∆t is neAvd∆t, so I =neAvd (n = free-electron density).
