The resistances in the two arms of the metre bridge are R = 5 Ω andS respectively. When the resistance S is shunted with an equal resistance, the new balance length found to be 1 .5l1 where l1 is the initial balancing length. Calculate the value of S.
Answer
Answer as text
The figure labels both the 20 Ω and 40 Ω currents I1 (misprint); take I20 (A→B) and I40 (D→C); current I20 +I40 flows C→F→E through 10 Ω. Loop ABCDA: 80− 20I20 + 40I40 = 0. Loop DCFED: −40I40− 10(I20 +I40) + 40 = 0. Solving: I40 = 0, I20 = 4 A. Current through 40 Ω = 0; current through 20 Ω = 4 A (from A to B); (4 A through 10 Ω from F to E). OR: End error: error due to the zero/100 cm ends of the wire not coinciding with the ends of the scale and contact resistance at the copper strips; removed by interchanging R and S and averaging. 5 S = l1 100−l1 , S/2 = 1.5l1 100− 1.5l1 ⇒l1 = 100 3 cm, S = 10 Ω.
