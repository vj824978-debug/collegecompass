Current Electricity — Question 23 (CBSE 2026, 2 marks)

CBSE 202655/5/12 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
(a) An electric iron rated 2.2 kW, 220 V is operated at 110 V supply. Find: (i) its resistance, and (ii) heat produced by it in 10 minutes. OR (b) A current of 4.0 A flows through a wire of length 1 m and cross-sectional area 1.0 mm 2, when potential difference of 2 V is applied across its ends. Calculate the resistivity of the material of the wire.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) R =V 2/P = 2202/2200 = 22 Ω. (ii) At 110 V: P = 1102/22 = 550 W;H = 550× 600 = 3.3× 105 J. (b) R =V/I = 2/4 = 0.5 Ω; ρ =RA/l = 0.5× 1.0× 10−6/1 = 5× 10−7 Ω m.
