Question as text
The figure shows the voltage (V) versus the current (I) graphs for a wire at two temperatures T1 and T2. One can conclude that: (A) T2 = 2T1 (B) T1>T 2 (C) T1 =T2/3 (D) T1<T 2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Slope of I–V graph = 1 /R; larger slope for T1 so R1<R 2; resistance of a wire rises with temperature, so T1<T 2.
