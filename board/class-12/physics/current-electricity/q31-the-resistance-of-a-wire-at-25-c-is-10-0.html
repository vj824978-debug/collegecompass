CBSE 202555/5/12 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
The resistance of a wire at 25◦C is 10.0 Ω. When heated to 125◦C, its resistance becomes 10.5 Ω. Find (i) the temperature coefficient of resistance of the wire, and (ii) the resistance of the wire at 425 ◦C.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) α = R2−R1 R1(T2−T1) = 0.5 10× 100 = 5× 10−4◦C−1. (ii) R425 = 10[1 + 5× 10−4× 400] = 12.0 Ω.
