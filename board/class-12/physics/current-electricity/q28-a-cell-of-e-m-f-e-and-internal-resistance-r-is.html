Question as text
(a) A cell of e.m.f. E and internal resistance r is connected with a variable external resistance R and a voltmeter showing potential drop V across R. Obtain the relationship between V , E, R and r. (b) Draw the shape of the graph showing the variation of terminal voltage V of the cell as a function of current I drawn from it. How one can determine the e.m.f. of the cell and its internal resistance from this graph?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) I = E R +r, V =IR =E−Ir⇒V = ER R +r (or r = E V − 1 R). (b) Straight line with negative slope ( V =E−Ir): V -intercept (I = 0) gives E; magnitude of slope gives r (current-axis intercept = E/r).
