An electron moves around the nucleus in a circular orbit of radius r and makes n revolutions per second. The value of equivalent current in the orbit is: (A) e n (B) ne (C) ne r (D) e nr
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Charge e passes a point n times per second: I =ne.
