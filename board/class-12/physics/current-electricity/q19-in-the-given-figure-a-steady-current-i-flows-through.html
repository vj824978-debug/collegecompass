Question 19 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
(a) In the given figure, a steady current I flows through the circuit when points A and C are connected by a wire of negligible resistance. Find the potential difference between points B and C. OR (b) A battery of emf 21 V and internal resistance 3 Ω is connected to a resistor. If the current in the circuit is 3 A, find: (i) the resistance of the resistor. (ii) the terminal voltage of the battery.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) E1 (+ at A) and E2 (+ at C) oppose: I = (6− 4)/(1 + 3) = 0.5 A, flowing C→B→A.E2 is charged: VC−VB =E2 +Ir = 4 + 1.5 = 5.5 V, i.e. VB−VC =−5.5 V (check: VA−VB = 6− 0.5 = 5.5 V,VA =VC). (b)(i) R =E/I−r = 7− 3 = 4 Ω; (ii)V =E−Ir = 21− 9 = 12 V.
