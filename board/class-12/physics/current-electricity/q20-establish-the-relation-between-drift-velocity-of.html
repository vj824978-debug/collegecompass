Establish the relation between drift velocity of electrons (vd) and electric current (I) in a conductor.

CBSE 202655/3/13 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
(a) Establish the relation between drift velocity of electrons (vd) and electric current (I) in a conductor. (b) How isvd affected when the length of the conductor is doubled, keeping the voltage applied across the conductor constant?
Answer
Answer as text
Derivation: (a) charge crossing area A in time ∆ t is neAvd∆t, so I =neAvd. (b) vd = eEτ m = eVτ mL : doubling L at constant V halves vd.
