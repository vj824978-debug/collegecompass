CBSE 202555/7/12 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
A cell of emf E and internal resistance r is connected across a resistor of variable resistance R. Show graphically the variation of (a) the terminal voltage across the cell, (b) the current supplied by the cell, with R as it is increased from 0 to the maximum value.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) V = ER R +r: starts at 0 for R = 0 and rises, approaching E asymptotically. (b) I = E R +r: starts at E/r for R = 0 and decreases towards 0 (hyperbola-like). Graphs must show these intercepts/asymptotes.
