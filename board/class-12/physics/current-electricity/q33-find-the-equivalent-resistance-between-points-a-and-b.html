Question as text
Find the equivalent resistance between points A and B for the network shown in the figure.
Answer
Answer as text
Balanced Wheatstone bridge (5/10 = 15/30), so the vertical 5 + 10 Ω arm carries no current. Remaining: (5 + 10) = 15 Ω, (15 + 30) = 45 Ω and (25 + 20) = 45 Ω in parallel = 9 Ω;RAB = 1 + 9 = 10 Ω.
