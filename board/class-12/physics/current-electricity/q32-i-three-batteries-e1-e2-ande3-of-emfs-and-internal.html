The points A and B of the arrangement are connected in a circuit. Find the value of the effective resistance offered by it to the circuit. OR (b) (i) Current I (= 1 A) is passing through a copper rod (n = 8.5×1028 m−3) of varying cross-sections as shown in the figure. The areas of cross-section at points A and B along its length are 1 .0× 10−7 m2 and 2.0× 10−7 m2 respectively. Calculate : (I) the ratio of electric fields at points A and B. (II) the drift velocity of free electrons at point B. (ii) Two point chargesq1 (= 16µC) andq2 (= 1µC) are placed at points⃗ r1 = (3 m)ˆi and⃗ r2 = (4 m)ˆj.
