Differentiate between electrical resistance and resistivity of a conductor.

CBSE 202055/5/13 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Physics · Current Electricity
Question as text
(a) Differentiate between electrical resistance and resistivity of a conductor. (b) Two metallic rods, each of length L, area of cross-section A 1 and A2, having resistivity ρ1 and ρ2 are connected in parallel across a d.c. battery. Obtain the expression for the effective resistivity of this combination.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) R =ρL/A depends on material, temperature and dimensions (unit Ω); resistivityρ depends only on material and temperature (unit Ω m). (b) R1 = ρ1L A1 , R2 = ρ2L A2 ; equivalent rod of length L, area A1 +A2: A1 +A2 ρL = A1 ρ1L + A2 ρ2L⇒ρ = ρ1ρ2(A1 +A2) ρ1A2 +ρ2A1 .
