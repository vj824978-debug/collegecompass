Three cells A, B and C of emfs 2 V, 3 V and 5 V respectively are connected in parallel to each other. Their internal resistances are 5 Ω, 5 Ω and 1 Ω respectively. Calculate the currents flowing through the cells A, B and C.
Answer
Answer as text
Positive terminals joined: common terminal voltage V = PEi/riP 1/ri = 0.4 + 0.6 + 5 0.2 + 0.2 + 1 = 30 7 ≈ 4.29 V.I = (E−V )/r: A: −16 35≈− 0.46 A, B:− 9 35≈− 0.26 A (A and B are charged, current entering their positive terminals); C: 5 7≈ 0.71 A (discharging). Check: 16/35 + 9/35 = 5/7.
