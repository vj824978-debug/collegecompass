Question as text
Fill in the blank with appropriate answer. A copper wire of non-uniform area of cross-section is connected to a d.c. battery. The physical quantity which remains constant along the wire is .
Answer
Answer as text
Electric current (charge conservation in steady state); current density and drift speed vary.
