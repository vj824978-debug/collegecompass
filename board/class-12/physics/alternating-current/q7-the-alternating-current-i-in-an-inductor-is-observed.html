The alternating current I in an inductor is observed to vary with time t as shown in the graph for a cycle. Which one of the following graphs is the correct representation of wave form of voltage V with time t? (A) (B) (C) (D)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) V =LdI/dt : constant positive slope for 0 <t<T/ 2, constant negative after; square wave.
