Question as text
An ac voltage is given as v = 14 sin(314t) V. The average and the effective value of the voltage (in V) over a cycle are respectively: (A) 14 and 7 (B) 10 and 14 (C) 0 and 10 (D) 10 and 0
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Average of a sine over a full cycle = 0; Vrms = 14/ √ 2≈ 10 V.
