Discuss the behaviour of an inductor connected to (i) a dc source, and (ii) a high frequency ac source.

CBSE 202655/3/13 marks
Question 19 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
(a) Discuss the behaviour of an inductor connected to (i) a dc source, and (ii) a high frequency ac source. (b) What is the phase relation between current and voltage in an ideal inductor connected to an ac source? Draw a phasor diagram for the circuit.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) dc: after the brief growth of current, XL = 0; the inductor acts as a plain conductor (only its resistance limits current). (ii) high-frequency ac: XL =ωL very large, so it nearly blocks the current. (b) Current lags voltage by π/2. Phasor diagram: Vm phasor and Im phasor 90◦ behind it (clockwise), both rotating anticlockwise at ω.
