Question 13 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
An alternating current is given by I = I0 cos(100π)t. The least time the current takes to decrease from its maximum value to zero will be: (A) 1 s (B) 1 s (C) 1 s (D) 1 s
Answer
Answer as text
(A) ω = 100π⇒T = 1 50 s; maximum to zero takes T/4 = 1 200 s.
