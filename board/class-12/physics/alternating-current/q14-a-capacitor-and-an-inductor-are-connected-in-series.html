A capacitor and an inductor are connected in series across an ac source of voltage of variable frequency. The frequency is increased continuously. The nature of the circuit before and after the resonance will be: (A) inductive only (B) capacitive only (C) capacitive and inductive respectively (D) inductive and capacitive respectively
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Below resonance XC >X L (capacitive); above resonance XL>X C (inductive).
