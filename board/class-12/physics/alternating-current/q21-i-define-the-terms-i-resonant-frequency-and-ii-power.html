(i) Define the terms (I) resonant frequency, and (II) power factor of a series LCR circuit.

CBSE 202655/4/15 marks
Question 21 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
(a) (i) Define the terms (I) resonant frequency, and (II) power factor of a series LCR circuit. For what value of the power factor will the power dissipated in the circuit be maximum? (ii) An inductor of 5 π H, a capacitor of 50 π µF and a resistor of 400 Ω are connected in series across an ac voltage v = 140 sin(100πt) V. Calculate: (I) impedance of the circuit, and (II) rms value of current that flows in the circuit. (Take √ 2 = 1.4) OR (b) (i) Draw a labelled diagram of a step-up transformer. Obtain the ratio of secondary voltage to primary voltage in terms of number of turns in the two coils. (ii) The number of turns in the primary and the secondary coil of an ideal transformer are 100 and 5000 respectively. If 3.3 kW power is supplied to the transformer at 220 V, find (I) current in the primary coil, and (II) output voltage.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) (I) Resonant frequency: frequency at which XL =XC, so impedance is minimum (Z =R) and current is maximum: ω0 = 1/ √ LC, ν0 = 1/(2π √ LC). (II) Power factor cos ϕ =R/Z, where ϕ is phase difference between v and i; P =VrmsIrms cosϕ. Power is maximum for cosϕ = 1 (resonance/purely resistive). (ii) XL = 100π× 5/π = 500 Ω; XC = 1/(100π× 50× 10−6/π) = 200 Ω; (I)Z = √ 4002 + 3002 = 500 Ω. (II) Vrms = 140/1.4 = 100 V;Irms = 100/500 = 0.2 A. (b)(i) Diagram: soft-iron laminated core, primary with fewer turns ( Np) connected to ac input, secondary with more turns ( Ns) to output. Same flux linkage per turn: εs =−Nsdϕ/dt, εp =−Npdϕ/dt⇒Vs/Vp =Ns/Np (> 1 for step-up). (ii) (I) Ip =P/Vp = 3300/220 = 15 A. (II) Vs =VpNs/Np = 220× 50 = 11000 V (11 kV).
