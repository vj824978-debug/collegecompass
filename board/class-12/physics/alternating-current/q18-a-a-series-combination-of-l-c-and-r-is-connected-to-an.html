CBSE 202655/2/15 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
(A) (a) A series combination of L, C and R is connected to an a.c. source. Using a phasor diagram, derive an expression for the impedance of the circuit and phase difference between V and I. (b) Under what conditions the (i) impedance of the circuit is minimum? (ii) Wattless current flows in the circuit? OR (B) (i) With the help of a labelled diagram, explain the principle, construction and working of an a.c. generator. (ii) Deduce an expression for the induced emf in the coil of the generator. (iii) IfT is the time period of the rotation of the coil, at what values of T in a cycle, the emf generator is maximum?
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(a) Derivation: phasors VR along I, VL leads by 90◦, VC lags by 90◦; V 2 =V 2 R + (VL−VC)2, so Z = p R2 + (XL−XC)2, tanϕ = XL−XC R . (b)(i) XL =XC, i.e. ω = 1/ √ LC (resonance), Zmin =R. (ii) R = 0 (purely inductive/capacitive), ϕ = 90◦, power factor cosϕ = 0. (B)(i) Diagram: armature coil rotating in uniform field of magnet, slip rings, brushes, external load. Principle: electromagnetic induction (changing flux through rotating coil). (ii) Φ = NBA cosωt, ε =−dΦ/dt =NBAω sinωt, ε0 =NBAω . (iii) Taking coil plane ⊥ ⃗B at t = 0: emf is maximum at t =T/4 and 3T/4 (coil plane parallel to ⃗B).
