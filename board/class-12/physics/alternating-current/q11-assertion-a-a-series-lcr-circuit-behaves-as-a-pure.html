Assertion (A): A series LCR circuit behaves as a pure resistive circuit at resonance.

CBSE 202555/4/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
Assertion (A): A series LCR circuit behaves as a pure resistive circuit at resonance. Reason (R): At resonance, XL =XC givesω = 1√ LC . (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; A holds because XL =XC makesZ =R (current in phase with voltage); R only gives the resonant frequency, not why the circuit is resistive.
