An alternating voltage of 220 V is applied across a device X.

CBSE 202355/5/13 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
An alternating voltage of 220 V is applied across a device X. A current of 0.22 A flows in the circuit and it lags behind the applied voltage in phase by π/2 radian. When the same voltage is applied across another device Y, the current in the circuit remains the same and it is in phase with the applied voltage. (i) Name the devices X and Y and, (ii) Calculate the current flowing in the circuit when the same voltage is applied across the series combination of X and Y.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) X: pure inductor (current lags by π/2); Y: resistor (in phase). (ii) XL =R = 220 0.22 = 1000 Ω;Z = q R2 +X2 L = 1000 √ 2 Ω; I = 220 1000 √ 2≈ 0.156 A, lagging the voltage by 45◦.
