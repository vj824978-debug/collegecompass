CBSE 202555/4/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
An ammeter connected in series in an ac circuit reads 10 A. The maximum value of current at any instant in the circuit is: (A) 10 √ 2 A (B) 10√ 2 A (C) 10 π A (D) 10√ 2π A
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Ammeter reads rms: I0 = √ 2Irms = 10 √ 2 A.
