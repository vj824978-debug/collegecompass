Assertion (A): In an ideal step-down transformer, the electrical energy is not lost.

CBSE 202555/2/1
Question 9 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
Assertion (A): In an ideal step-down transformer, the electrical energy is not lost. Reason (R): In a step-down transformer, voltage decreases but the current increases. (A) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (B) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (C) If Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false. (D) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true ( VpIp =VsIs in ideal transformer), but R (V down, I up) is a consequence, not the explanation, of no energy loss.
