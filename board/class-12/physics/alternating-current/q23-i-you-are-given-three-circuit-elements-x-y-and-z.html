(i) You are given three circuit elements X, Y and Z.

CBSE 202455/5/15 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
(a) (i) You are given three circuit elements X, Y and Z. They are connected one by one across a given ac source. It is found that V and I are in phase for element X. V leads I by π for element Y while I leads V by π for element Z. Identify elements X, Y and Z. (ii) Establish the expression for impedance of circuit when elements X, Y and Z are connected in series to an ac source. Show the variation of current in the circuit with the frequency of the applied ac source. (iii) In a series LCR circuit, obtain the conditions under which (i) impedance is minimum and (ii) wattless current flows in the circuit. OR (b) (i) Describe the construction and working of a transformer and hence obtain the relation for vs vp in terms of number of turns of primary and secondary. (ii) Discuss four main causes of energy loss in a real transformer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) X: resistor; Y: inductor; Z: capacitor (intended). Strictly, a phase of π/4 means Y is an R–L combination with XL =R and Z an R–C combination with XC =R; a pure L or C gives π/2. (ii) Derivation: phasors VR =IR along I, VL =IXL and VC =IXC at ±90◦; V 2 =V 2 R + (VL−VC)2⇒Z = p R2 + (XL−XC)2. Graph: I vs ω rises to a sharp peak Imax =V/R at ω0 = 1/ √ LC and falls on either side. (iii) (i) Z minimum (=R) when XL =XC, i.e. ω = 1/ √ LC. (ii) Wattless current when cosϕ = 0, i.e. R = 0 (purely reactive circuit, ϕ =π/2). OR: (b)(i) Primary and secondary coils wound on a laminated soft-iron core; ac in primary gives changing flux linked with secondary (mutual induction). Same flux per turn: εp =−Npdϕ/dt, εs =−Nsdϕ/dt ⇒ vs vp = Ns Np . (ii) Flux leakage; Joule (copper) loss in windings; eddy-current loss in core; hysteresis loss in core.
