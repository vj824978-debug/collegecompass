Which of the following statements is not true for electric energy in ac form compared to that in dc form?

CBSE 202655/5/1
Question 4 · Class 12 Physics · Alternating Current
Question as text
Which of the following statements is not true for electric energy in ac form compared to that in dc form? (A) Production of ac is economical. (B) ac can be easily and efficiently converted from one voltage to the other. (C) ac can be transmitted economically over long distances. (D) ac is less dangerous.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) ac is more dangerous than dc (peak voltage √ 2Vrms, it attracts/grips); so (D) is not true.
