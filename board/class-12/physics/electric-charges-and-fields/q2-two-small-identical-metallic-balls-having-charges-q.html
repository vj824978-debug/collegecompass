Two small identical metallic balls having charges q and−2q are kept far at a separation r. They are brought in contact and then separated at distance r 2. Compared to the initial force F , they will now: (A) attract with a force F (B) repel with a force F (C) repel with a force F (D) attract with a force F
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Initially F =k 2q2/r2 (attractive). After contact each has −q/2: F′ =k(q2/4)/(r/2)2 =kq2/r2 =F/2, repulsive.
