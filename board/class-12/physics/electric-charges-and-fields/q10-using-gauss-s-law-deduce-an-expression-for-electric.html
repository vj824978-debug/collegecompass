(a) Using Gauss’s law, deduce an expression for electric field at a point due to a uniformly charged infinite plane thin sheet. (b) Two large thin plane sheets, each having surface charge density σ, are held close and parallel to each other in air. What is the net electric field at a point (i) inside and (ii) outside, the sheets? OR (a) Obtain the condition of balance of a Wheatstone bridge. (b) Find net resistance of the network of resistors connected between A and B, as shown in figure.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Derivation: Gaussian cylinder (pillbox) of end-area A through the sheet; flux 2EA =σA/ε0⇒E = σ 2ε0 , normal to the sheet (away for σ >0). (b) Both sheets with + σ: (i) inside (between) E = 0 (fields cancel); (ii) outside E = σ ε0 , directed away from the sheets. OR: (a) Derivation: Kirchhoff’s laws on the bridge with galvanometer currentIg = 0 give I1P =I2R and I1Q =I2S, so balance condition P Q = R S . (b) Between M and N: MO = ON = MP = PN = R, so the bridge M-O-P-N is balanced and arm OP carries no current; two 2 R paths in parallel give R. RAB = 2R +R + 3R = 6R.
