CBSE 202555/2/1
Question 5 · Class 12 Physics · Electric Charges and Fields
Question as text
Which one of the following statements is correct? Electric field due to static charges is (A) conservative and field lines do not form closed loops. (B) conservative and field lines form closed loops. (C) non-conservative and field lines do not form closed loops. (D) non-conservative and field lines form closed loops.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Electrostatic field is conservative ( H ⃗E·d⃗l = 0); lines start/end on charges, no closed loops.
