Question as text
Two charges−q each are placed at the vertices A and B of an equilateral triangle ABC. If M is the mid-point of AB, the net electric field at C will point along (A) CA (B) CB (C) MC (D) CM
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Fields at C point towards A and B (negative charges), equal magnitudes; resultant along the bisector from C towards M, i.e. along CM.
