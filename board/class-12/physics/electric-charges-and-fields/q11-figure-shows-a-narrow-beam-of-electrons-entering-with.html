Figure shows a narrow beam of electrons entering with a velocity of 3×107 m/s, symmetrically through the space between two parallel horizontal plates P 1P′ 1 and P2P′ 2 kept 2 cm apart. If each plate is 3 cm long, calculate the potential difference V applied between the plates so that the beam just strikes the end P′ 2.
Answer
Answer as text
Deflection y = 1 cm overL = 3 cm: y = 1 eV md L v 2 ⇒V = 2ydmv2 eL2 = 2(0.01)(0.02)(9.1× 10−31)(9× 1014) (1.6× 10−19)(9× 10−4) ≈ 2.28× 103 V (≈ 2275 V).
