The electric flux through a closed Gaussian surface depends upon: (A) Net charge enclosed and permittivity of the medium. (B) Net charge enclosed, permittivity of the medium and the size of the Gaussian surface. (C) Net charge enclosed only. (D) Permittivity of the medium only.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Gauss’s law: ϕ =qenc/ε, independent of the size or shape of the surface.
