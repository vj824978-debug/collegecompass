Draw the pattern of electric field lines, when a point charge−Q is kept near an uncharged conducting plate.

CBSE 201955/1/11 mark
Question 19 · Class 12 Physics · Electric Charges and Fields
Question as text
Draw the pattern of electric field lines, when a point charge−Q is kept near an uncharged conducting plate.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: field lines start from the plate (induced + charges on its near surface,− on far surface), meet the plate surface normally, and curve to end radially on−Q; arrows pointing towards−Q.
