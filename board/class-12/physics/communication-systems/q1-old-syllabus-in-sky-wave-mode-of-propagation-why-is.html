[old syllabus] In sky wave mode of propagation why is the frequency range of transmitting signals restricted to less than 30 MHz? OR On what factors does the range of coverage in ground wave propagation depend?
Answer
Answer as text
Waves above about 30 MHz are not reflected back by the ionosphere; they penetrate it and escape, so sky wave propagation is limited to frequencies below∼30 MHz. OR: Range of ground wave depends on the transmitted power and the frequency of the signal (attenuation by the ground increases with frequency); also on the nature (conductivity) of the earth’s surface.
