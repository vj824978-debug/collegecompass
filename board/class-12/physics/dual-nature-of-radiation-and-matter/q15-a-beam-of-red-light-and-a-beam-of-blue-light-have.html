Question 15 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
A beam of red light and a beam of blue light have equal intensities. Which of the following statements is true? (A) The blue beam has more number of photons than the red beam. (B) The red beam has more number of photons than the blue beam. (C) Wavelength of red light is lesser than wavelength of blue light. (D) The blue light beam has lesser energy per photon than that in the red light beam.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Equal intensity, Ephoton =hc/λ smaller for red, so the red beam has more photons.
