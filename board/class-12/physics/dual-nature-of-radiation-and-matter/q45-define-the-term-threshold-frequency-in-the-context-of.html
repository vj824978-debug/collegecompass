Define the term “threshold frequency” in the context of photoelectric emission.

CBSE 201955/1/11 mark
Question 45 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
Define the term “threshold frequency” in the context of photoelectric emission. OR Define the term “Intensity” in photon picture of electromagnetic radiation.
Answer
Answer as text
Threshold frequency: the minimum frequency of incident radiation below which no photoelectrons are emitted from a given metal surface, however intense the light (ν0 =ϕ0/h). OR: Intensity = energy carried by photons crossing unit area normal to the beam per unit time, I =nhν (n = number of photons per unit area per second).
