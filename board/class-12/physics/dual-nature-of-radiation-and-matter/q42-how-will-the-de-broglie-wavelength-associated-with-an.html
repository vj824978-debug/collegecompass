CBSE 202355/5/12 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
(a) How will the De Broglie wavelength associated with an electron be affected when the (i) velocity of the electron decreases? and (ii) accelerating potential is increased? Justify your answer. OR (b) How would the stopping potential for a given photosensitive surface change if (i) the frequency of the incident radiation were increased? and (ii) the intensity of the incident radiation were decreased? Justify your answer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) λ =h/mv: λ increases as v decreases. (ii) λ =h/ √ 2meV : λ decreases as V increases. OR (b)(i) eV0 =hν−ϕ0: V0 increases (linearly) with ν. (ii) No change: V0 is independent of intensity (intensity changes only the photocurrent).
