The work function of a material is 2.21 eV. Which of the following cannot produce photoelectrons from it? (A) Red light (B) Blue light (C) Violet light (D) Green light
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Threshold λ0 = 1240/2.21≈ 561 nm; red light (λ> 620 nm, E <2 eV) cannot eject electrons.
