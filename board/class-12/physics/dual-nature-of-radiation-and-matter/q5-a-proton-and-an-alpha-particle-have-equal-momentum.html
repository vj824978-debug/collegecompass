Question as text
A proton and an alpha particle have equal momentum. The ratio of their kinetic energies ( Ep/Ea) and de Broglie wavelengths associated with them ( λp/λa) respectively are: (A) 2, 1 (B) 1, 2 (C) 4, 1 (D) 1, 4
Answer
Answer as text
(C) E =p2/2m⇒Ep/Ea =ma/mp = 4; λ =h/p equal, ratio 1.
