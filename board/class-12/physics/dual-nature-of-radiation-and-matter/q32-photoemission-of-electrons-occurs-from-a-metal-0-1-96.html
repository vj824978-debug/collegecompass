Photoemission of electrons occurs from a metal ( ϕ0 = 1.96 eV) when light of frequency 6.4× 1014 Hz is incident on it. Calculate: (a) Energy of a photon in the incident light, (b) The maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons, and (c) The stopping potential.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) E =hν = 6.63× 10−34× 6.4× 1014 = 4.24× 10−19 J≈ 2.65 eV. (b) Kmax = 2.65− 1.96 = 0.69 eV≈ 1.1× 10−19 J. (c) V0 =Kmax/e≈ 0.69 V.
