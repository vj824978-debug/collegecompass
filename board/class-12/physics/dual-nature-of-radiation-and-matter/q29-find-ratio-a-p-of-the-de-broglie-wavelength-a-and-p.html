Question as text
Find ratio ( λa/λp) of the de Broglie wavelength λa and λp associated respectively with an alpha particle and a proton, (i) if they are moving with the same kinetic energy. (ii) just after they are accelerated through the same potential difference.
Answer
Answer as text
λ =h/ √ 2mK. (i) Same K: λa/λp = p mp/mα = 1/2. (ii) K =qV : λa/λp = p mpe/(4mp· 2e) = 1/(2 √ 2).
