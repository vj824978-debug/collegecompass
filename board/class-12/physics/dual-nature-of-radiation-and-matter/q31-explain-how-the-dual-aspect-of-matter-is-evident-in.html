Explain how the dual aspect of matter is evident in the de Broglie relation.

CBSE 202655/4/13 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
(a) Explain how the dual aspect of matter is evident in the de Broglie relation. (b) Radiation of wavelength λ is incident on a photosensitive surface. Find the de Broglie wavelength of electrons emitted from the surface. Assume that the work function of the surface is negligible.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) λ =h/p: λ is a wave property, momentum p a particle property; Planck’s constant links the two, so the relation expresses the wave–particle duality of matter. (b) K =hc/λ (work function negligible); p = √ 2mK = p 2mhc/λ; λe = h p = r hλ 2mc.
