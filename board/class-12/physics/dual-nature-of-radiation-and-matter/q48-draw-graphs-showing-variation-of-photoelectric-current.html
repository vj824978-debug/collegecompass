Question as text
Draw graphs showing variation of photoelectric current with applied voltage for two incident radiations of equal frequency and different intensities. Mark the graph for the radiation of higher intensity.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: photocurrent vs anode voltage, two curves starting from the same stopping potential−V0 on the negative voltage axis and saturating at different levels; the curve with the higher saturation current is for the higher intensity.
