In a photoelectric experiment, the emitter plate is irradiated with radiation of 200 nm. The photocurrent becomes zero when the collector plate potential is −0.80 V. Calculate the work function (in eV) of the emitter. [h = 6.63× 10−34 J s,c = 3× 108 m/s, e = 1.6× 10−19 C]
Answer
Answer as text
E =hc/λ = 6.63× 10−34× 3× 108 200× 10−9 = 9.945× 10−19 J≈ 6.22 eV; Kmax =eV0 = 0.80 eV;ϕ0 = 6.22− 0.80≈ 5.42 eV.
