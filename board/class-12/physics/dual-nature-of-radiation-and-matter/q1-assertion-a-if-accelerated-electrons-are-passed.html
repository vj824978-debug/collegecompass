Question as text
Assertion (A): If accelerated electrons are passed through a narrow slit, a diffraction pattern is observed. Reason (R): Electrons behave as both particles and waves. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Diffraction of electrons shows their wave nature (de Broglie); dual nature explains A.
