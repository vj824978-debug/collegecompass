If photons of frequency ν are incident on the surfaces of metals A and B of threshold frequencies ν and ν 3 respectively. The ratio of the maximum kinetic energy of electrons emitted from A to that from B is: (A) 2 : 3 (B) 3 : 4 (C) 1 : 3 (D) √ 3 : √
Answer
Answer as text
(B) KA =h(ν−ν/2) =hν/2, KB =h(ν−ν/3) = 2hν/3; KA :KB = 3 : 4.
