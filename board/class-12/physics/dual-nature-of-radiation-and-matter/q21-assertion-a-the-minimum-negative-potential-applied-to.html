CBSE 202555/7/1
Question 21 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
Assertion (A): The minimum negative potential applied to the anode in a photoelectric experiment at which photoelectric current becomes zero, is called cut-off voltage. Reason (R): The threshold frequency for a metal is the minimum frequency of incident radiation below which emission of photoelectrons does not take place. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both statements are correct definitions (stopping/cut-off potential; threshold frequency), but R does not explain A.
