Explain the following observations using Einstein’s photoelectric equation: (a) Photoelectric emission does not occur from a surface when the frequency of the light incident on it is less than a certain minimum value. (b) It is the frequency, and not the intensity of the incident light which affects the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons. (c) The cut-off voltage ( V0) versus frequency (ν) of the incident light curve is a straight line with a slope h e .
Answer
Answer as text
Kmax =hν−ϕ0. (a) Emission needs Kmax≥ 0, i.e. ν≥ν0 =ϕ0/h (threshold frequency). (b) Kmax depends on ν (photon energy) and not on intensity, which only changes the number of photons (and hence photocurrent). (c) eV0 =hν−ϕ0⇒V0 = h eν− ϕ0 e : straight line with slope h/e (same for all metals).
