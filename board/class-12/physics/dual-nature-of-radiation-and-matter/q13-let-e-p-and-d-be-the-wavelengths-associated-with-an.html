Letλe,λp andλd be the wavelengths associated with an electron, a proton and a deuteron, all moving with the same speed. Then the correct relation between them is (A) λd>λ p>λ e (B) λe>λ p>λ d (C) λp>λ e>λ d (D) λe =λp =λd
Answer
Answer as text
(B) λ =h/(mv); me<m p<m d, so λe>λ p>λ d.
