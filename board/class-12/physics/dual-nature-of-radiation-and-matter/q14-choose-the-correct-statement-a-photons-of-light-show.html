Question as text
Choose the correct statement: (A) Photons of light show diffraction whereas electrons do not show diffraction. (B) Electrons have momentum whereas photons do not have momentum. (C) Photons of light and electrons both exhibit dual nature. (D) All electromagnetic radiations do not have photons.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Both light and electrons show wave–particle duality.
