Question as text
A laser beam of frequency 3.0×1014 Hz produces average power of 9 mW. Find (i) the energy of photon of the beam, and (ii) the number of photons emitted per second on an average by the source.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) E =hν = 6.63× 10−34× 3× 1014 = 1.989× 10−19 J (≈ 1.24 eV). (ii) N =P/E = 9× 10−3 1.989× 10−19≈ 4.5× 1016 photons per second.
