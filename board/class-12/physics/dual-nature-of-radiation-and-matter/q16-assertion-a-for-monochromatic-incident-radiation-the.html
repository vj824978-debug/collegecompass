Assertion (A): For monochromatic incident radiation, the emitted photoelectrons from a given metal have speed ranging from zero to a certain maximum value. Reason (R): Each metal has a definite work function. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; spread of speeds is because electrons come from different levels/depths and lose different energies; the work function only fixes Kmax =hν−ϕ0.
