The stopping potentialV0 measured in a photoelectric experiment for a metal surface is plotted against frequency ν of the incident radiation. Let m be the slope of the straight line so obtained. Then the value of charge of an electron is given by (h is the Planck’s constant.) (A) mh (B) m h (C) h m (D) 1 mh
Answer
Answer as text
(C) eV0 =hν−ϕ0⇒V0 = h eν−ϕ0 e ; slope m =h/e, so e =h/m.
