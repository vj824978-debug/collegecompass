Calculate the energy and momentum of a photon in a monochromatic beam of wavelength 331.5 nm.

CBSE 2022T erm II55/5/13 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
(a) Calculate the energy and momentum of a photon in a monochromatic beam of wavelength 331.5 nm. (b) How fast should a hydrogen atom travel in order to have the same momentum as that of the photon in part (a)?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) E = hc λ = 6.63× 10−34× 3× 108 331.5× 10−9 = 6.0× 10−19 J (≈ 3.75 eV); p = h λ = 2.0× 10−27 kg m/s. (b) v = p mH ≈ 2.0× 10−27 1.67× 10−27≈ 1.2 m/s.
