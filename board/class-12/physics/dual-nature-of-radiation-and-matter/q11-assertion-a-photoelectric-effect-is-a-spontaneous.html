Assertion (A): Photoelectric effect is a spontaneous phenomenon.

CBSE 202655/5/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
Assertion (A): Photoelectric effect is a spontaneous phenomenon. Reason (R): According to the wave picture of radiation, an electron would take hours/days to absorb sufficient energy to overcome the work function and come out from a metal surface. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Photoemission is instantaneous (A true); R is a true statement of the wave-theory prediction, but it does not explain A (it is contrary to it; the photon picture explains A).
