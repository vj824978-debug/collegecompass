Write Einstein’s photoelectric equation.

CBSE 202455/5/13 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
Question as text
(a) Write Einstein’s photoelectric equation. How did Millikan prove the validity of this equation? (b) Explain the existence of threshold frequency of incident radiation for photoelectric emission from a given surface.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Kmax =eV0 =hν−ϕ0. Millikan measured V0 vs ν for several metals: straight lines of the same slope h/e, intercept giving ϕ0; h so obtained agreed with Planck’s value. (b) One photon is absorbed by one electron; emission needs hν≥ϕ0, so for ν <ν0 =ϕ0/h no electron is emitted however intense the light.
