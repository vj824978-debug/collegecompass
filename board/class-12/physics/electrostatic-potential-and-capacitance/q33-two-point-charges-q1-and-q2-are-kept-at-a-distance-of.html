Two point charges q1 and q2 are kept at a distance of r12 in air.

CBSE 202055/5/13 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Physics · Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance
Question as text
(a) Two point charges q1 and q2 are kept at a distance of r12 in air. Deduce the expression for the electrostatic potential energy of the system. (b) If an external electric field (E) is applied on the system, write the expression for total energy of this system.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Derivation: bring q1 (no work), then q2 against field of q1: W =q2· q1 4πε0r12 ⇒U = q1q2 4πε0r12 . (b) U =q1V (⃗ r1) +q2V (⃗ r2) + q1q2 4πε0r12 , V = potential of the external field.
