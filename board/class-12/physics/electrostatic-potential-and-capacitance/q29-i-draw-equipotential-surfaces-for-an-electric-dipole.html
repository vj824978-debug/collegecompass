(i) Draw equipotential surfaces for an electric dipole.

CBSE 202455/5/15 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Physics · Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance
Question as text
(a) (i) Draw equipotential surfaces for an electric dipole. (ii) Two point charges q1 and q2 are located at ⃗ r1 and⃗ r2 respectively in an external electric field ⃗E. Obtain an expression for the potential energy of the system. (iii) The dipole moment of a molecule is 10−30 Cm. It is placed in an electric field ⃗E of 105 V/m such that its axis is along the electric field. The direction of ⃗E is suddenly changed by 60◦ at an instant. Find the change in the potential energy of the dipole, at that instant. OR (b) (i) A thin spherical shell of radius R has a uniform surface charge density σ. Using Gauss’ law, deduce an expression for electric field (i) outside and (ii) inside the shell. (ii) Two long straight thin wires AB and CD have linear charge densities 10 µC/m and −20µC/m, respectively. They are kept parallel to each other at a distance 1 m. Find magnitude and direction of the net electric field at a point midway between them.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Diagram: closed curves around each charge, distorted, crossing the field lines at right angles; the perpendicular bisector plane of the dipole is the V = 0 equipotential. (ii) Derivation: bring q1 from infinity: q1V (⃗ r1); then q2: q2V (⃗ r2) + q1q2 4πε0r12 ⇒U =q1V (⃗ r1) +q2V (⃗ r2) + q1q2 4πε0|⃗ r1−⃗ r2|. (iii) U =−pE cosθ: ∆U =pE(cos 0◦− cos 60◦) = 10−30× 105× 1 2 = 5× 10−26 J (increase). OR: (b)(i) Spherical Gaussian surface of radius r: E· 4πr2 =q/ε0, q = 4πR2σ⇒E = σR2 ε0r2 (radial) for r>R ; no enclosed charge⇒E = 0 for r<R . (ii) r = 0.5 m. E1 = 2kλ1/r = 2× 9× 109× 10−5/0.5 = 3.6× 105 N/C (away from AB, towards CD); E2 = 7.2× 105 N/C (towards CD). Net 1.08× 106 N/C, directed from AB towards CD.
