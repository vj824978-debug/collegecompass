Two parallel plate capacitors X and Y are connected in series to a 6 V battery. They have the same plate area and same plate separation but capacitor X has air between its plates, whereas capacitor Y contains a material of dielectric constant 4. (a) Calculate the capacitances of X and Y, if the equivalent capacitance of the combination of X and Y is 4µF. (b) Calculate the potential difference across the plates of X and Y.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) CY = 4CX; series: CX· 4CX 5CX = 4CX 5 = 4µF⇒CX = 5µF, CY = 20µF. (b) Q = 4µF× 6 V = 24µC; VX = 24/5 = 4.8 V, VY = 24/20 = 1.2 V.
