(a) Two small solid metal balls A and B of radii R and 2R having charge densities 2 σ and 3σ respectively are kept far apart. Find the charge densities on A and B after they are connected by a conducting wire. OR (b) Two infinitely long straight wires ‘1’ and ‘2’ are placed d distance apart, parallel to each other, as shown in the figure. They are uniformly charged having charge densities λ and−λ 2 respectively. Locate the position of the point from wire ‘1’ at which the net electric field is zero and identify the region in which it lies.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) qA = 2σ· 4πR2 = 8πR2σ, qB = 3σ· 16πR2 = 48πR2σ; total 56πR2σ. Equal potentials⇒q∝ radius: q′ A = 56 3πR2σ, q′ B = 112 3 πR2σ. σ′ A = 14σ 3 , σ′ B = 7σ 3 (ratio σ′ A/σ′ B = 2). (b) Fields cancel only outside the wires, nearer the weaker charge: region C. At distance x from wire 1: λ 2πε0x = λ/2 2πε0(x−d)⇒x = 2d, i.e. at 2d from wire 1 (distance d to the right of wire 2), in region C.
