Fill in the blank with appropriate answer. A point charge is placed at the centre of a hollow conducting sphere of internal radius ‘ r’ and outer radius ‘2r’. The ratio of the surface charge density of the inner surface to that of the outer surface will be .
Answer
Answer as text
Inner surface carries−q, outer +q: σin σout = q/4πr2 q/4π(2r)2 = 4 : 1 in magnitude (opposite signs).
