Draw the equipotential surfaces corresponding to a uniform electric field in the Z-direction.

CBSE 201955/1/13 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Physics · Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance
Question as text
(a) Draw the equipotential surfaces corresponding to a uniform electric field in the Z-direction. (b) Derive an expression for the electric potential at any point along the axial line of an electric dipole.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: equidistant planes parallel to the X–Y plane (perpendicular to the Z-axis), equal ∆V steps, field lines along +Z. (b) Derivation: at distance r from centre on axis, V = 1 4πε0 q r−a− q r +a ⇒V = p 4πε0(r2−a2) (p = 2qa;≈ p 4πε0r2 for r≫a).
