Two horizontal plates, separated by 1 cm, are arranged one above the other. A particle of mass 5 mg and charge 2 nC is released in air between the plates. The potential difference that should be applied to the plates so that the particle remains suspended between them, is: (A) 250 V (B) 200 V (C) 100 V (D) 50 V
Answer
Answer as text
(A) qV/d =mg⇒V = mgd q = 5× 10−6× 10× 0.01 2× 10−9 = 250 V.
