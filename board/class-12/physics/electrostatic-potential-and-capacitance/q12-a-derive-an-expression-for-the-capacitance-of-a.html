Question as text
(A) (a) Derive an expression for the capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor of plate area A and plate separation d with air present between the plates. (b) Two air-filled capacitors of capacitances C1 and C2 are connected in parallel with a dc battery. After the capacitors are fully charged, a slab of dielectric constant K is inserted between the plates of each capacitor. How will the (i) charge on each capacitor and (ii) energy stored in the capacitor affected after the slab is introduced. OR (B) (a) An electric field ⃗E is established across the ends of a cylindrical conductor of length L and area of cross-section A. Discuss how electrons attain an average velocity, independent of time. Hence, obtain a relation between current in the conductor and this ‘average velocity’ of electrons. (b) (i) This ‘average velocity’ is found be few mm/s for currents in range of a few amperes. How then is current established almost the instant a circuit is closed? (ii) Two copper wires having their radii in the ratio of 3 : 2 are connected in series across a battery. Find the ratio of the drift velocities of the electrons in the wires.
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(a) Derivation: σ =Q/A, field between plates E =σ/ε0, V =Ed =Qd/(ε0A), so C =Q/V =ε0A/d. (b) Battery connected, V constant: C→KC , so (i) charge on each becomes K times (KC1V , KC2V ); (ii) energy 1 2CV 2 becomes K times. (If the battery were disconnected: charge unchanged, energy becomes 1 /K times.) (B)(a) Derivation: electron acceleration ⃗ a=−e⃗E/m; collisions randomise velocity, average timeτ between collisions, so ⃗ vd =−e⃗Eτ/m (constant in time). Charge crossing area in time ∆ t: neAvd∆t, so I =neAvd. (b)(i) Electric field is set up throughout the circuit almost at the speed of light; free electrons everywhere start drifting simultaneously, so current is established instantly. (ii) Same I, vd∝ 1/r2: v1 :v2 = (2/3)2 = 4 : 9.
