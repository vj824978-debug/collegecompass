CBSE 202455/5/13 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) Name the parts of the electromagnetic spectrum which are (i) also known as ‘heat waves’ and (ii) absorbed by ozone layer in the atmosphere. (b) Write briefly one method each, of the production and detection of these radiations.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Infrared; (ii) ultraviolet. (b) IR: produced by hot bodies / molecular vibrations; detected by thermopiles, bolometers, IR photographic film. UV: produced by special lamps (e.g. mercury vapour) and very hot bodies like the sun; detected by photocells and photographic film.
