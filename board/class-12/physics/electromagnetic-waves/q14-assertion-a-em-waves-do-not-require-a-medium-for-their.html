Assertion (A): EM waves do not require a medium for their propagation.

CBSE 202555/7/1
Question 14 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Assertion (A): EM waves do not require a medium for their propagation. Reason (R): EM waves are transverse waves. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true: EM waves are self-sustaining oscillating ⃗E and ⃗B fields, needing no medium; transverse nature is true but does not explain it.
