CBSE 202655/3/13 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) Depict the variation of electric field ( ⃗E) and magnetic field ( ⃗B) with respect to the direction of propagation of an electromagnetic wave. Write their two important characteristics. (b) Show that 1√ε0µ0 gives the velocity of an electromagnetic wave in free space.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: sinusoidal ⃗E (along y) and ⃗B (along z) in phase, propagation along x. Characteristics: ⃗E, ⃗B and direction of propagation mutually perpendicular (transverse wave); E and B oscillate in phase with E0/B0 =c (also: no medium needed). (b) Derivation: from Faraday’s law E0 =cB0 and from Ampere–Maxwell law B0 =µ0ε0cE0; eliminating gives c = 1/√µ0ε0 = 3× 108 m s−1.
