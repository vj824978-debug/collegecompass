Question as text
The amplitude of electric field in an electromagnetic wave in free space is 1000 V m−1. The amplitude of the magnetic field in this electromagnetic wave is: (A) 3 .0× 10−3 T (B) 3 .33× 10−8 T (C) 3 .0× 1011 T (D) 3 .33× 10−6 T
Answer
Answer as text
(D) B0 =E0/c = 1000/(3× 108) = 3.33× 10−6 T.
