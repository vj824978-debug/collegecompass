Which one of the following electromagnetic radiation has the least wavelength?

CBSE 202355/5/1
Question 16 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Which one of the following electromagnetic radiation has the least wavelength? (A) Gamma rays (B) Microwaves (C) Visible light (D) X-rays
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Gamma rays ( λ ≲ 10−12 m) lie at the short-wavelength end of the EM spectrum.
