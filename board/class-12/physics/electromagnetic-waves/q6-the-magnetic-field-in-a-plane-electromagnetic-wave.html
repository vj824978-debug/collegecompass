The magnetic field in a plane electromagnetic wave travelling in glass ( n = 1.5) is given by By = (2× 10−7 T) sin(αx + 1.5× 1011t) where x is in metres and t is in seconds. The value of α is: (A) 0 .5× 103 m−1 (B) 6 .0× 102 m−1 (C) 7 .5× 102 m−1 (D) 1 .5× 103 m−1
Answer
Answer as text
(C) v =c/n = 2× 108 m/s; α =k =ω/v = 1.5× 1011/2× 108 = 7.5× 102 m−1.
