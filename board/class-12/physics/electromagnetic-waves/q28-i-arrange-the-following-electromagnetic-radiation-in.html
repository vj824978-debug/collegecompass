CBSE 2022T erm II55/5/13 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) (i) Arrange the following electromagnetic radiation in the ascending order of their frequencies: X-rays, microwaves, gamma rays, radio waves (ii) Write two uses of any two of these radiation. OR (b) With the help of a ray diagram explain the working of a reflecting telescope. Mention two advantages of a reflecting telescope over a refracting telescope.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Radio waves < microwaves< X-rays< gamma rays. (ii) e.g. Microwaves: radar, microwave ovens. X-rays: medical diagnosis (fractures), detecting flaws in materials. (Radio: broadcasting, mobile communication;γ: cancer treatment, food sterilisation.) OR: (b) Diagram (Cassegrain): parallel rays reflect from large concave primary mirror towards a small convex secondary mirror, which sends them through a hole in the primary to form the image viewed with an eyepiece. Advantages: no chromatic aberration; paraboloid mirror free of spherical aberration; large mirrors are lighter and easier to support, giving greater light-gathering power and resolution.
