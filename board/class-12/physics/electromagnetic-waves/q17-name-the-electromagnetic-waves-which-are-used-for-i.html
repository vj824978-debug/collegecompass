Name the electromagnetic waves which are used for (i) detection of fractures in bones (ii) physiotherapy (iii) radar systems Also write their wavelength range.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) X-rays,∼ 1 nm to 10−3 nm; (ii) infrared,∼ 1 mm to 700 nm; (iii) microwaves,∼ 0.1 m to 1 mm.
