State any three characteristics of electromagnetic waves.

CBSE 202555/4/13 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) State any three characteristics of electromagnetic waves. (b) Briefly explain how and where the displacement current exists during the charging of a capacitor.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Any three: transverse, with ⃗E⊥ ⃗B⊥ direction of propagation; travel in vacuum (no medium) with c = 1/√µ0ε0; E0/B0 =c; carry energy and momentum (exert radiation pressure); produced by accelerated charges; not deflected by electric/magnetic fields. (b) While charging, conduction current flows in the wires but not across the gap; the electric field (flux) between the plates changes, giving displacement current id =ε0dΦE/dt in the gap between the plates, equal to the conduction current, so current is continuous.
