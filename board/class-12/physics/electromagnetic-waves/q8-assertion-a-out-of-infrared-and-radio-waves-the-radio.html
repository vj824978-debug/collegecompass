Assertion (A): Out of Infrared and radio waves, the radio waves show more diffraction effect.

CBSE 202555/2/1
Question 8 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Assertion (A): Out of Infrared and radio waves, the radio waves show more diffraction effect. Reason (R): Radio waves have greater frequency than infrared waves. (A) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (B) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (C) If Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false. (D) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Radio waves have longer wavelength, so diffract more (A true); but they have lower frequency than infrared (R false).
