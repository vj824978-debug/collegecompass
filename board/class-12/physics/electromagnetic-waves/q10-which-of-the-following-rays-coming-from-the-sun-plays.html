CBSE 202555/5/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Which of the following rays coming from the Sun plays an important role in maintaining the Earth’s warmth? (A) Infrared rays (B) γ rays (C) UV rays (D) Visible light rays
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Infrared (heat) radiation maintains Earth’s warmth (greenhouse effect).
