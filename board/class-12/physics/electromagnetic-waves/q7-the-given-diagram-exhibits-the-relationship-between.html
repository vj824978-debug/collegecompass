The given diagram exhibits the relationship between the wavelength of the electromagnetic waves and the energy of photon associated with them. The three points P, Q and R marked on the diagram may correspond respectively to: (A) X-rays, microwaves, UV radiation (B) X-rays, UV radiation, microwaves (C) UV radiation, microwaves, X-rays (D) Microwaves, UV radiation, X-rays
Answer
Answer as text
(A) E =hc/λ: P (highest energy) X-rays, Q (lowest) microwaves, R (intermediate) UV.
