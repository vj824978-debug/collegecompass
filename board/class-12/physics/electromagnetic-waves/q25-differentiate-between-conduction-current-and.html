CBSE 202555/7/13 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) Differentiate between ‘conduction current’ and ‘displacement current’, giving one similarity and one dissimilarity between them. (b) Explain the existence of electromagnetic waves in free space, using the concept of displacement current.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Conduction current: due to flow of charges in a conductor; displacement current Id =ε0 dΦE dt : due to changing electric field (e.g. between capacitor plates), no flow of charge. Similarity: both produce magnetic field (and are equal in a circuit with a capacitor; same unit). (b) A time-varying ⃗E field gives displacement current, which produces a time-varying ⃗B field (Ampere–Maxwell law), and a time-varying ⃗B produces ⃗E (Faraday’s law); mutually sustaining, perpendicular ⃗E and ⃗B fields propagate in free space as EM waves with speed c = 1/√µ0ε0.
