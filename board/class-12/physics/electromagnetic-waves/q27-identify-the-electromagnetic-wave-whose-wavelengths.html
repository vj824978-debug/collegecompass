Identify the electromagnetic wave whose wavelengths range is from about (a) 10−12 m to about 10−8 m.

CBSE 202355/5/12 marks
Question 27 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Identify the electromagnetic wave whose wavelengths range is from about (a) 10−12 m to about 10−8 m. (b) 10−3 m to about 10−1 m. Write one use of each.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) X-rays — medical diagnosis (radiography) / treatment of cancer. (b) Microwaves — radar systems / microwave ovens / aircraft navigation.
