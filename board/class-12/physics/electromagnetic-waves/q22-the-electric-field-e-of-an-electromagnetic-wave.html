The electric field ⃗E of an electromagnetic wave propagating in north direction is oscillating in up and down…

CBSE 202555/2/13 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) The electric field ⃗E of an electromagnetic wave propagating in north direction is oscillating in up and down direction. Describe the direction of magnetic field ⃗B of the wave. (b) Are the wave length of radio waves and microwaves longer or shorter than those detectable by human eyes? (c) Write main use of each of the following in human life: (i) Infrared waves (ii) Gamma rays
Answer
Answer as text
(a) ⃗B is horizontal, perpendicular to both ⃗E and propagation: oscillates along east–west (east when ⃗E is up, since ⃗E× ⃗B is along north). (b) Longer. (c)(i) Infrared: physiotherapy/heating, night vision, remote controls, keeping earth warm (greenhouse); (ii) Gamma rays: treatment of cancer (radiotherapy), sterilisation of medical equipment/food.
