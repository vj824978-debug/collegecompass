Question as text
What are infrared waves? Why are these waves referred to as ‘heat waves’? Give any two uses of these waves.
Answer
Answer as text
Infrared: EM waves of wavelength about 1 mm to 700 nm (between microwaves and visible light), produced by hot bodies and molecular vibrations. Called heat waves because they are readily absorbed by water molecules in most materials, increasing their thermal (vibrational) motion, i.e. they heat the substances. Uses (any two): physical therapy/heating, remote controls, night-vision devices/IR photography, greenhouse warming, IR detectors in earth satellites (military/crop monitoring).
