Why are infra-red waves often called heat waves?

CBSE 20182 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) Why are infra-red waves often called heat waves? Explain. (b) What do you understand by the statement, “Electromagnetic waves transport momentum”?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Infrared rays are readily absorbed by water molecules (and most substances), increasing their thermal motion, i.e. they heat whatever absorbs them. (b) An EM wave carrying energy U also carries momentum p =U/c; on absorption/reflection it transfers this momentum and exerts radiation pressure on the surface.
