Identify the part of electromagnetic spectrum used in (i) radar and (ii) eye surgery.

CBSE 201955/1/13 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
(a) Identify the part of electromagnetic spectrum used in (i) radar and (ii) eye surgery. Write their frequency range. (b) Prove that the average energy density of the oscillating electric field is equal to that of the oscillating magnetic field.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Radar: microwaves, ∼ 109–1011 Hz (GHz range; CBSE scheme: ∼ 1010–1012 Hz). (ii) Eye surgery (LASIK): ultraviolet, ∼ 1015–1017 Hz. (b) uE = 1 2ε0E2; with E =cB and c2 = 1 µ0ε0 : uE = 1 2ε0c2B2 = B2 2µ0 =uB (same for averages,⟨E2⟩ =c2⟨B2⟩).
