Name the electromagnetic wave used (i) in radar, (ii) in eye surgery and (iii) as diagnostic tool in medicine.

CBSE 202555/6/13 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Name the electromagnetic wave used (i) in radar, (ii) in eye surgery and (iii) as diagnostic tool in medicine. Write their wavelength range also.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Radar: microwaves,≈ 0.1 m to 1 mm. (ii) Eye surgery (LASIK): ultraviolet,≈ 400 nm to 1 nm. (iii) Diagnostic tool: X-rays, ≈ 10 nm to 10−3 nm.
