Gamma rays and Radio waves travel with the same velocity in free space.

CBSE 202055/5/12 marks
Question 30 · Class 12 Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
Question as text
Gamma rays and Radio waves travel with the same velocity in free space. Distinguish between them in terms of origin and the main application.
Answer
Answer as text
Origin: γ-rays – nuclear transitions/radioactive decay of nuclei; radio waves – accelerated motion of charges in conducting wires (oscillating circuits/antennas). Application: γ-rays – medicine (destroying cancer cells), sterilisation; radio waves – radio/TV communication (broadcasting, cellular phones).
