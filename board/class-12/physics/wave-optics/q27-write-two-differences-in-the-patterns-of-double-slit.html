CBSE 202555/7/12 marks
Question 27 · Class 12 Physics · Wave Optics
Question as text
Write two differences in the patterns of double-slit interference experiment and single-slit diffraction experiment. Light waves from two pinholes illuminated by two sodium lamps do not produce interference patterns. Explain why.
Answer
Answer as text
Differences: (1) interference fringes are (ideally) of equal width while in diffraction the central maximum is twice as wide as the others; (2) interference maxima have equal intensity while diffraction maxima fall off rapidly in intensity (also: interference is superposition of waves from two coherent sources, diffraction of wavelets from different parts of a single wavefront). Two independent sodium lamps are not coherent: their phase difference changes rapidly and randomly (∼ 10−10 s), so the intensity pattern averages out; no sustained interference pattern.
