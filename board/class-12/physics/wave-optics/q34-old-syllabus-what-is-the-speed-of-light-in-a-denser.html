[old syllabus] What is the speed of light in a denser medium of polarising angle 30 ◦?
Answer
Answer as text
For light going from denser to rarer (air): tan ip = 1 µ⇒µ = √ 3; v = c µ = 3× 108 √ 3 = √ 3× 108≈ 1.73× 108 m/s.
