(a) If one of two identical slits producing interference in Young’s experiment is covered with glass, so that the light intensity passing through it is reduced to 50%, find the ratio of the maximum and minimum intensity of the fringe in the interference pattern. (b) What kind of fringes do you expect to observe if white light is used instead of monochromatic light?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) a2 a1 = 1√ 2; Imax Imin = √ 2 + 1√ 2− 1 !2 = 17 + 12 √ 2≈ 34. (b) Central fringe white; on either side a few coloured fringes (violet/blue nearest the centre, red farther), then uniform white illumination with no clear fringes.
