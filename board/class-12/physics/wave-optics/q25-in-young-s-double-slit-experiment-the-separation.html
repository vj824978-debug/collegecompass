In Young’s double slit experiment, the separation between the two slits is 1.0 mm and the screen is 1.0 m away from the slits. A beam of light consisting of two wavelengths 500 nm and 600 nm is used to obtain interference fringes. Calculate : (a) the distance between the first maxima for the two wavelengths. (b) the least distance from the central maximum, where the bright fringes due to both the wavelengths coincide.
Answer
Answer as text
β =λD/d: β1 = 0.50 mm,β2 = 0.60 mm. (a) ∆y = 0.60− 0.50 = 0.10 mm. (b) n1× 500 =n2× 600, least: n1 = 6, n2 = 5; y = 3000× 10−9× 1 10−3 = 3 mm.
