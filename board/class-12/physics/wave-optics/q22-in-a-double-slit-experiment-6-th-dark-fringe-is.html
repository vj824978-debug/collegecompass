In a double-slit experiment, 6 th dark fringe is observed at a certain point of the screen.

CBSE 202555/2/12 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Physics · Wave Optics
Question as text
In a double-slit experiment, 6 th dark fringe is observed at a certain point of the screen. A transparent sheet of thickness t and refractive index n is now introduced in the path of one of the two interfering waves to increase its phase by 2 π(n− 1)t/λ. The pattern is shifted and 8 th bright fringe is observed at the same point. Find the relation for thickness t in terms of n and λ.
Answer
Answer as text
At the point, 6th dark fringe: path difference = 11λ 2 ; after the sheet, 8th bright: 8λ. Extra path (n− 1)t = 8λ− 5.5λ = 2.5λ⇒t = 5λ 2(n− 1).
