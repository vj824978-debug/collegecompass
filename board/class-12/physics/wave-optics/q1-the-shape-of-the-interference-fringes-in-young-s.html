The shape of the interference fringes in Young’s double-slit experiment, when the distance between the slit and the screen is very large as compared to the slit-separation, is nearly (A) straight (B) parabolic (C) circular (D) hyperbolic
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Fringes are hyperbolic in general but for D≫d they are nearly straight lines.
