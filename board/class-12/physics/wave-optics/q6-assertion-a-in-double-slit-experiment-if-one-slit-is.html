Question as text
Assertion (A): In double slit experiment if one slit is closed, diffraction pattern due to the other slit will appear on the screen. Reason (R): For interference, at least two waves are required. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) With one slit, only one wave: no interference, so only the single-slit diffraction pattern remains; R explains A.
