Explain why diffraction of sound is more common in daily experience than that of light.

CBSE 202655/3/12 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Physics · Wave Optics
Question as text
Explain why diffraction of sound is more common in daily experience than that of light.
Answer
Answer as text
Diffraction is appreciable when the obstacle/aperture size is comparable to the wavelength. Sound wavelengths (cm to m) are comparable to everyday objects (doors, walls); light (∼ 5× 10−7 m) is far smaller, so its diffraction is rarely noticed.
