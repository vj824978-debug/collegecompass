(a) Find the intensity at a point on the screen in Young’s double slit experiment, at which the interfering waves of intensity I0 each, have a path difference of (i) λ 3 , and (ii) λ 2 . OR (b) A point source of light in air is kept at a distance of 12 cm in front of a convex spherical surface of glass of refractive index 1.5 and radius of curvature 30 cm. Find the nature and position of the image formed.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) I = 4I0 cos2(ϕ/2), ϕ = 2π λ ∆x. (i) ϕ = 2π/3: I = 4I0 cos2 60◦ =I0. (ii) ϕ =π: I = 0. (b) n2 v − n1 u = n2−n1 R with u =−12 cm,R = +30 cm: 1.5 v + 1 12 = 0.5 30 ⇒v =−22.5 cm. Virtual (and erect) image, 22.5 cm from the surface on the same side as the object.
