Define the term wavefront.

CBSE 201955/1/13 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Physics · Wave Optics
Question as text
Define the term wavefront. Using Huygen’s wave theory, verify the law of reflection. OR Define the term, “refractive index” of a medium. Verify Snell’s law of refraction when a plane wavefront is propagating from a denser to a rarer medium.
Answer
Answer as text
Wavefront: locus of points oscillating in the same phase (surface of constant phase). Diagram: plane wavefront AB incident at angle i on surface MN; while B reaches C (BC =vt), secondary wavelet from A has radius AE =vt; tangent CE is the reflected wavefront. △EAC∼=△BCA (common AC, AE =BC, right angles)⇒i =r. OR: Refractive index n21 = v1 v2 = sini sinr (ratio of speed of light in medium 1 to that in medium 2; n =c/v absolute). Denser→rarer (v2>v 1): BC =v1t, AE =v2t; sini = v1t AC , sinr = v2t AC⇒ sini sinr = v1 v2 =n21 (Snell’s law), with r>i .
