Question as text
A Young’s double-slit experimental set up is kept in a medium of refractive index 4 . Which maximum in this case will coincide with the 6 th maximum obtained if the medium is replaced by air? (A) 4 th (B) 6 th (C) 8 th (D) 10 th
Answer
Answer as text
(C) yn = nλD µd ; nλD (4/3)d = 6λD d ⇒n = 8.
