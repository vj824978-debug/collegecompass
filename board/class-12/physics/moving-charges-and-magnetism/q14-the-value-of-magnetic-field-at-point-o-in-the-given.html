The value of magnetic field at point O in the given figure is: (A) µ0I 2πR (B) µ0I πR (C) µ0I 4R (D) µ0I R
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Straight parts are in line with O (no field); semicircle: B = µ0I 4R .
