Assertion (A): The energy of a charged particle moving in a magnetic field does not change.

CBSE 202455/5/1
Question 20 · Class 12 Physics · Moving Charges and Magnetism
Question as text
Assertion (A): The energy of a charged particle moving in a magnetic field does not change. Reason (R): It is because the work done by the magnetic force on the charge moving in a magnetic field is zero. (A) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is correct explanation of Assertion (A). (B) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (C) If Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false. (D) If both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) ⃗F =q⃗ v× ⃗B⊥⃗ v, so W = 0 and KE stays constant; R explains A.
