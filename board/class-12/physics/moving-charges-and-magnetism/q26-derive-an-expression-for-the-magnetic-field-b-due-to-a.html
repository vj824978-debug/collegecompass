Derive an expression for the magnetic field ⃗B, due to a circular coil of N turns, each of radius r carrying current I, at a distance ‘x’ from the centre along its axis.
Answer
Answer as text
Derivation: Biot–Savart, dB = µ0 4π Idl r2 +x2 ; perpendicular components cancel, axial components add (× sinϕ =r/ √ r2 +x2); integrate over 2πr and multiply by N: B = µ0NIr 2 2(r2 +x2)3/2 along the axis (right-hand rule).
