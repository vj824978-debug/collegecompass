Question as text
An electron of mass m and charge −e is revolving anticlockwise around the nucleus of an atom. (a) Obtain the expression for the magnetic dipole moment ( µ) of the atom. (b) If ⃗L is the angular momentum of electron, show that ⃗ µ=− e 2m ⃗L.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) I = e T = ev 2πr ; µ =Iπr 2 = evr 2 . (b) L =mvr⇒µ = e 2mL; electron is negative, so the current is opposite to its motion and ⃗ µis opposite to ⃗L: ⃗ µ=− e 2m ⃗L.
