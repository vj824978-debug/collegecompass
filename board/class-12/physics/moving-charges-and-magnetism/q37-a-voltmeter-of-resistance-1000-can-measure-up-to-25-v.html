Question as text
A voltmeter of resistance 1000 Ω can measure up to 25 V. How will you convert it so that it can read up to 250 V?
Answer
Answer as text
Ig = 25/1000 = 0.025 A. Required total resistance = 250/0.025 = 104 Ω; connect 9000 Ω in series with the voltmeter.
