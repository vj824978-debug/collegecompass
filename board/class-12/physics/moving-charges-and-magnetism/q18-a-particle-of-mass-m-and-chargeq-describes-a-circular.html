Moving Charges and Magnetism — Question 18 (CBSE 2024)

CBSE 202455/5/1
Question 18 · Class 12 Physics · Moving Charges and Magnetism
Question as text
A particle of mass m and chargeq describes a circular path of radius R in a magnetic field. If its mass and charge were 2m and q 2 respectively, the radius of its path would be (A) R 4 (B) R (C) 2 R (D) 4 R
Answer
Answer as text
(D) R = mv qB ∝ m q (same v, B): 2m q/2 = 4m q ⇒ 4R.
