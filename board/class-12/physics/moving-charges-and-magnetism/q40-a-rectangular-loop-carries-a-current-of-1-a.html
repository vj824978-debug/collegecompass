A rectangular loop carries a current of 1 A.

Question as text
A rectangular loop carries a current of 1 A. A straight long wire carrying 2 A current is kept near the loop in the same plane as shown in the figure. Find: (i) the torque acting on the loop, and (ii) the magnitude and direction of the net force on the loop.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Torque = 0: the forces on the loop all lie in its plane and are collinear in pairs (⃗ m∥ ⃗B). (ii) Near side (parallel currents, 1 cm): F1 = µ0I1I2l 2πd1 = 2× 10−7× 2× 1× 0.05 0.01 = 2× 10−6 N attraction; far side (antiparallel, 2 cm): F2 = 1× 10−6 N repulsion; forces on the horizontal sides cancel. Net F = 1× 10−6 N towards the wire (attractive, perpendicular to the wire in the plane).
