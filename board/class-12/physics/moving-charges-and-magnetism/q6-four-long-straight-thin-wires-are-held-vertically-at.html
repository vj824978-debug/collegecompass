Four long straight thin wires are held vertically at the corners A, B, C and D of a square of side ‘ a’, kept on a table and carry equal current ‘I’. The wire at A carries current in upward direction whereas the current in the remaining wires flows in downward direction. The net magnetic field at the centre of the square will have the magnitude: (A) µ0I πa and directed along OC (B) µ0I πa √ 2 and directed along OD (C) µ0I √ πa and directed along OB (D) 2µ0I πa and directed along OA
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Each wire gives B0 = µ0I 2π(a/ √ 2) = µ0I√ 2πa . B and D (same current direction, opposite corners) cancel; A (up) and C (down) add: B = 2B0 = √ 2µ0I πa , along OB (right-hand rule).
