A galvanometer of resistance 27 Ω is converted into an ammeter of range (0 – 10 mA) using a resistance of 3 Ω. The galvanometer will show full scale deflection for a current of about – (A) 10 mA (B) 100 mA (C) 1 mA (D) 3 mA
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Shunt: Ig× 27 = (10−Ig)× 3⇒Ig = 1 mA.
