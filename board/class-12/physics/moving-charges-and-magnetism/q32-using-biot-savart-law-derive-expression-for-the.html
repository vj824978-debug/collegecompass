Using Biot-Savart law, derive expression for the magnetic field ( ⃗B) due to a circular current carrying loop at a point on its axis and hence at its centre.
Answer
Answer as text
Derivation: dB = µ0 4π Idl (x2 +R2); perpendicular components cancel, axial components add (×R/ √ x2 +R2), integrate dl over 2πR: B = µ0IR2 2(x2 +R2)3/2 along the axis. At centre ( x = 0): B = µ0I 2R .
