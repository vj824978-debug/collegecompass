Question 33 · Class 12 Physics · Moving Charges and Magnetism
Question as text
(a) (i) A proton moving with velocity ⃗V in a non-uniform magnetic field traces a path as shown in the figure. The path followed by the proton is always in the plane of the paper. What is the direction of the magnetic field in the region near points P, Q and R? What can you say about relative magnitude of magnetic fields at these points? (ii) A current carrying circular loop of area A produces a magnetic field B at its centre. Show that the magnetic moment of the loop is 2BA µ0 r A π . OR (b) (i) Derive an expression for the torque acting on a rectangular current loop suspended in a uniform magnetic field. (ii) A charged particle is moving in a circular path with velocity ⃗V in a uniform magnetic field ⃗B. It is made to pass through a sheet of lead and as a consequence, it looses one half of its kinetic energy without change in its direction. How will (1) the radius of its path (2) its time period of revolution change?
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Forceq⃗V× ⃗B points towards the centre of curvature. Near P the path bends upward: ⃗B perpendicular to the paper, into the page; at Q (point of inflection, zero curvature) B = 0; near R the path bends downward: ⃗B out of the page. Since r =mV/(qB), B∝ curvature: BR >B P >B Q = 0. (ii) B = µ0I 2r , A =πr2⇒r = p A/π, I = 2Br µ0 ; m =IA = 2BA µ0 r A π . (b)(i) Derivation: forces on the two sides of length a (perpendicular to B) are IaB, equal and opposite, separated by b sinθ: τ =IabB sinθ =NIAB sinθ, i.e. ⃗ τ= ⃗ m× ⃗B (⃗ m=NI ⃗A); forces on the other two sides cancel and are collinear. (ii) (1) r = mV qB = √ 2mK qB ; K→K/2 gives r′ =r/ √ 2. (2) T = 2πm qB is independent of speed: unchanged.
