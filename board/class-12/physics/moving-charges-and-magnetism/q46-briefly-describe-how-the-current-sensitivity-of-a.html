Briefly describe how the current sensitivity of a moving coil galvanometer can be increased.

CBSE 202355/5/13 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Physics · Moving Charges and Magnetism
Question as text
(a) Briefly describe how the current sensitivity of a moving coil galvanometer can be increased. (b) A galvanometer shows full scale deflection for current I g. A resistance R 1 is required to convert it into a voltmeter of range (0 – V) and a resistance R 2 to convert it into a voltmeter of range (0 – 2V). Find the resistance of the galvanometer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Current sensitivity ϕ I = NAB k : increase it by increasing N, A, or B (strong magnet, soft-iron core) or using a suspension of small torsion constantk (e.g. phosphor bronze). (b) V =Ig(G +R1), 2V =Ig(G +R2)⇒ 2G + 2R1 =G +R2⇒G =R2− 2R1.
