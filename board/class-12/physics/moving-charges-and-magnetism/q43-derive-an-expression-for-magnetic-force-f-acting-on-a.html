Question as text
Derive an expression for magnetic force ⃗F acting on a straight conductor of length L carrying current I in an external magnetic field ⃗B. Is it valid when the conductor is in zig-zag form? Justify.
Answer
Answer as text
Derivation: force on each carrier q⃗ vd× ⃗B; number of carriers nAL; ⃗F =nALq⃗ vd× ⃗B, with I =nqAvd⇒ ⃗F =I⃗L× ⃗B. Yes, in a uniform field: ⃗F =IPd⃗l× ⃗B =I⃗L× ⃗B, where ⃗L is the straight vector joining the two ends of the zig-zag wire.
