A long straight wire is held vertically and carries a steady current in upward direction. The shape of magnetic field lines produced by the current-carrying wire are: (A) horizontal straight lines directed radially out from the wire. (B) straight lines parallel to the current-carrying wire. (C) concentric horizonal circles around the wire. (D) coaxial helixes around the wire.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Field lines of a straight current are concentric circles in planes perpendicular (horizontal) to the wire.
