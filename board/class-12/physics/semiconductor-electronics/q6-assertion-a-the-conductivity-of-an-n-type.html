Question as text
Assertion (A): The conductivity of an n-type semiconductor is higher than that of a p-type semiconductor at a given temperature. Reason (R): The electrons being in the conduction band in n-type semiconductor are more mobile than the holes in the valence band in p-type semiconductor. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Electron mobility exceeds hole mobility, so for comparable doping n-type has higher conductivity; R explains A.
