[old syllabus] What is a solar cell?

CBSE 202055/5/13 marks
Question 53 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
[old syllabus] What is a solar cell? Draw V-I characteristics. Explain the three processes involved in the work. OR Draw the circuit diagram of a full-wave rectifier. Explain its working showing its inputs and output waveforms.
Answer
Answer as text
A p-n junction that converts light energy into electrical energy (emf without bias). Diagram: V-I curve in the fourth quadrant, cutting the axes at Voc and Isc. Processes: generation of e–h pairs by photons with hν >Eg; separation of electrons and holes by the junction field; collection at the front/back contacts (p side +, n side −). OR: Diagram: centre-tapped transformer, two diodes D1, D2 to the ends A, B, load RL between common cathode and centre tap. In one half cycle D1 conducts, in the other D2, current through RL always in the same direction. Waveforms: sinusoidal input; output = full-wave pulsating d.c. (both halves positive).
