Write two points of difference between intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors.

CBSE 202655/2/12 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
Write two points of difference between intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors.
Answer
Answer as text
Intrinsic: pure, ne =nh =ni, low conductivity, depends only on temperature. Extrinsic: doped, ne̸=nh, higher conductivity controllable by doping (any two).
