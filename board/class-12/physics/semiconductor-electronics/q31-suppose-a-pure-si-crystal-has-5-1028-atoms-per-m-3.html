Question as text
Suppose a pure Si crystal has 5 × 1028 atoms per m 3. It is doped with 5 × 1022 atoms per m 3 of Arsenic. Calculate the majority and minority carrier concentration in the doped silicon. (Given: ni = 1.5× 1016 m−3)
Answer
Answer as text
Majority (electrons): ne≈ND = 5× 1022 m−3. Minority (holes): nh =n2 i/ne = (1.5× 1016)2/5× 1022 = 4.5× 109 m−3.
