Explain the characteristics of a p-n junction diode that makes it suitable for its use as a rectifier.

CBSE 202455/5/13 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
(a) Explain the characteristics of a p-n junction diode that makes it suitable for its use as a rectifier. (b) With the help of a circuit diagram, explain the working of a full wave rectifier.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Low resistance and large current in forward bias, very high resistance and negligible current in reverse bias: it conducts in one direction only. (b) Diagram: centre-tapped transformer secondary, diodes D1, D2 from the ends A, B, load RL between the joined diode outputs and centre tap. In one half cycle D1 conducts (D2 reverse biased), in the other D2 conducts; current through RL is in the same direction in both halves (pulsating dc for both half cycles).
