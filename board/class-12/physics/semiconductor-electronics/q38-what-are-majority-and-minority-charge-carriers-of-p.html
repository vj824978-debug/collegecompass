What are majority and minority charge carriers of p-type and n-type semiconductors?

CBSE 202555/4/13 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
(a) What are majority and minority charge carriers of p-type and n-type semiconductors? (b) Explain briefly the formation of diffusion current and drift current in a p-n junction diode.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) p-type: majority holes, minority electrons; n-type: majority electrons, minority holes. (b) Diffusion current: due to concentration gradient holes diffuse p→n and electrons n→p, leaving immobile ions (depletion layer) and setting up a barrier field from n to p. Drift current: this field drives minority carriers (electrons p→n, holes n→p) across the junction, opposite to the diffusion current; at equilibrium the two are equal and net current is zero.
