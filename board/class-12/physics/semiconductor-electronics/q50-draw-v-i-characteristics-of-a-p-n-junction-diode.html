Draw V-I characteristics of a p-n Junction diode.

CBSE 2022T erm II55/5/13 marks
Question 50 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
(i) Draw V-I characteristics of a p-n Junction diode. (ii) Differentiate between the threshold voltage and the breakdown voltage for a diode. (iii) Write the property of a junction diode which makes it suitable for rectification of ac voltages.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Diagram: forward bias (V in volts, I in mA) — negligible current up to threshold/knee voltage, then steep rise; reverse bias (V in volts, I in µA) — tiny nearly constant saturation current until breakdown voltage, then sharp rise. (ii) Threshold voltage: forward voltage (small, ∼0.7 V Si) beyond which current rises sharply. Breakdown voltage: (large) reverse voltage at which reverse current increases suddenly. (iii) Unidirectional conduction: low resistance in forward bias, very high resistance in reverse bias.
