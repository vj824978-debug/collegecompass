How are charge carriers created in an intrinsic semiconductor?

CBSE 202655/3/12 marks
Question 27 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
How are charge carriers created in an intrinsic semiconductor? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
Thermal energy breaks some covalent bonds; the freed electron becomes a conduction electron and leaves a vacancy (hole) with effective + e charge; electron–hole pairs are generated, so ne =nh =ni.
