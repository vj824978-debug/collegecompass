Briefly explain the two important processes that occur during the formation of a p-n junction.

CBSE 202655/1/13 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
Briefly explain the two important processes that occur during the formation of a p-n junction.
Answer
Answer as text
Diffusion: majority carriers diffuse across the junction (holes p →n, electrons n→p) due to concentration gradient, leaving immobile ions that form the depletion layer and give diffusion current. Drift: the resulting barrier field drives minority carriers across the junction (drift current, opposite to diffusion current); at equilibrium the two are equal, giving the barrier potential.
