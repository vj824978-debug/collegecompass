A pure Si crystal having 5× 1028 atoms m−3 is dopped with 1 ppm concentration of antimony. If the concentration of holes in the doped crystal is found to be 4 .5× 109 m−3, the concentration (in m−3) of intrinsic charge carriers in Si crystal is about (A) 1 .2× 1015 (B) 1 .5× 1016 (C) 3 .0× 1015 (D) 2 .0× 1016
Answer
Answer as text
(B) ne = 5× 1022; n2 i =nenh = 5× 1022× 4.5× 109 = 2.25× 1032⇒ni = 1.5× 1016 m−3.
