Draw the circuit arrangement for studying V-I characteristics of ap-n junction diode in (i) forward biasing…

CBSE 202355/5/15 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
(a) Draw the circuit arrangement for studying V-I characteristics of ap-n junction diode in (i) forward biasing and (ii) reverse biasing. Draw the typical V-I characteristics of a silicon diode. Describe briefly the following terms: (i) minority carrier injection in forward biasing and (ii) breakdown voltage in reverse biasing. OR (b) Name two important processes involved in the formation of a p-n junction diode. With the help of a circuit diagram, explain the working of junction diode as a full wave rectifier. Draw its input and output waveforms. State the characteristic property of a junction diode that makes it suitable for rectification.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: battery + rheostat, diode with milliammeter in series and voltmeter across (forward); reversed diode with microammeter (reverse). Characteristic: negligible current up to threshold ≈ 0.7 V, then steep exponential rise; small nearly constant reverse current ( µA) until sudden rise at breakdown. (i) Minority carrier injection: under forward bias electrons diffuse from n to p and holes from p to n, where they become minority carriers, raising the minority concentration near the junction. (ii) Breakdown voltage: the reverse bias at which reverse current increases sharply (Zener/avalanche breakdown). OR (b) Diffusion and drift. Diagram: centre-tapped transformer, diodes D1, D2 at the ends, load between joined cathodes and centre tap. In each half cycle one diode is forward biased, so current flows through the load in the same direction. Waveforms: sinusoidal input; output = all positive half-cycles (pulsating dc, frequency doubled). Property: diode conducts only in forward bias (low forward, very high reverse resistance).
