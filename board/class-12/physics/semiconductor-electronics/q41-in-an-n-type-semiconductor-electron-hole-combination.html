CBSE 202555/6/12 marks

Question as text
In an n-type semiconductor electron-hole combination is a continuous process at room temperature. Yet the electron concentration is always greater than the hole concentration in it. Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
Donor atoms supply a large number of free electrons ( ne≈ND) independent of temperature-driven pair generation; recombination removes equal numbers of electrons and holes, and in equilibrium nenh =n2 i , so with ne≫ni the hole concentration is suppressed (nh≪ni). Thermal generation continuously replenishes pairs, so electrons remain the majority carriers.
