Assertion (A): The impurities in p-type Si are not pentavalent atoms.

CBSE 202555/5/1
Question 14 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
Assertion (A): The impurities in p-type Si are not pentavalent atoms. Reason (R): The hole density in valance band in p-type semiconductor is almost equal to the acceptor density. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Acceptors in p-type Si are trivalent (A true); nh≈NA is true but does not explain why the dopant is not pentavalent.
