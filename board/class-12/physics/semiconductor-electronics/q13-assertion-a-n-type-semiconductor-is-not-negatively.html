Assertion (A): n-type semiconductor is not negatively charged.

CBSE 202555/4/1
Question 13 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
Assertion (A): n-type semiconductor is not negatively charged. Reason (R): Neutral pentavalent impurity atom doped in intrinsic semiconductor (neutral) donates its fifth unpaired electron to the crystal lattice and becomes a positive donor. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) The donor ion is positive and its released electron is in the lattice, so the crystal stays neutral; R explains A.
