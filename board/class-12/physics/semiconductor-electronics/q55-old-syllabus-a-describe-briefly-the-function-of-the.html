CBSE 201955/1/13 marks
Question 55 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
[old syllabus] (a) Describe briefly the function of the three segments of n-p-n transistor. (b) Draw the circuit arrangement for studying the output characteristics of n-p-n transistor in CE configuration. Explain how the output characteristics are obtained. OR Draw the circuit diagram of a full wave rectifier and explain its working. As so, give the input and output waveform.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Emitter: heavily doped, supplies majority carriers; base: very thin, lightly doped, passes most carriers to collector; collector: moderately doped, larger, collects carriers from emitter. (b) Diagram: n-p-n transistor with VBB, rheostat, microammeter (IB) and voltmeter (VBE) in base circuit; VCC , rheostat, milliammeter (IC) and voltmeter (VCE ) in collector circuit; emitter common. Keep IB fixed, vary VCE and record IC; plot IC vs VCE for different IB. OR: Diagram: centre-tap transformer secondary ends to two diodes D1, D2, joined cathodes to load RL returning to the centre tap. In each half cycle one diode is forward biased; current through RL flows in the same direction in both halves. Waveforms: sinusoidal input; output = unidirectional pulses in every half cycle (full-wave rectified).
