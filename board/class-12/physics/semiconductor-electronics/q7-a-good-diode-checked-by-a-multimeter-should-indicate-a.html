A good diode checked by a multimeter should indicate: (A) high resistance in reverse bias and a low resistance in forward bias (B) high resistance in both forward bias and reverse bias (C) low resistance in both reverse bias and forward bias (D) high resistance in forward bias and low resistance in reverse bias
Answer
Answer as text
(A) A p-n junction conducts in forward bias (low R) and blocks in reverse bias (high R).
