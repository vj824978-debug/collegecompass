Draw energy band diagrams of n-type and p-type semiconductors at temperature T >0 K.

CBSE 202555/7/12 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
Draw energy band diagrams of n-type and p-type semiconductors at temperature T >0 K. Show the donor/acceptor energy levels with the order of difference of their energies from the bands.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagrams with EC, EV and gap Eg: n-type: donor level ED just below conduction band, EC−ED∼ 0.01 eV (Ge) / 0.05 eV (Si), electrons in CB shown more than holes in VB. p-type: acceptor level EA just above valence band, EA−EV∼ 0.01–0.05 eV, holes in VB more than electrons in CB.
