With the help of a circuit diagram, explain briefly how a p-n junction diode works as a half-wave rectifier.

CBSE 2022T erm II55/5/12 marks
Question 48 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
With the help of a circuit diagram, explain briefly how a p-n junction diode works as a half-wave rectifier.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: ac source → transformer primary; secondary in series with diode D and load RL; output across RL. Positive half cycle: diode forward biased, conducts, output across RL. Negative half cycle: diode reverse biased, no current, no output. Output: pulsating dc for alternate half cycles only.
