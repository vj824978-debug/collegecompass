Explain the following, giving reasons: (a) A doped semiconductor is electrically neutral.

CBSE 202455/5/13 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
Explain the following, giving reasons: (a) A doped semiconductor is electrically neutral. (b) In a p-n junction under equilibrium, there is no net current. (c) In a diode, the reverse current is practically not dependent on the applied voltage.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Dopant and host atoms are neutral; every extra free carrier is balanced by an equal fixed ionised impurity charge. (b) Diffusion current of majority carriers is exactly balanced by the opposite drift current due to the barrier field. (c) Reverse current is due to minority carriers, whose number is fixed by temperature; even a small reverse voltage sweeps all of them across, so increasing voltage does not increase it (below breakdown).
