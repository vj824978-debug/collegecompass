Question as text
With the help of circuit diagrams, briefly explain the forward biasing and the reverse biasing of a p-n junction diode.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagrams: forward bias, p-side to + terminal and n-side to − terminal (through a resistor); reverse bias, p-side to − and n-side to +. Forward: applied V opposes built-in potential, barrier falls to V0−V , depletion layer narrows, majority-carrier diffusion gives a large current (mA). Reverse: barrier rises to V0 +V , depletion layer widens, only minority-carrier drift gives a tiny current (µA) until breakdown.
