With the help of a circuit diagram, explain the working of a full wave rectifier.

CBSE 202655/4/13 marks
Question 30 · Class 12 Physics · Semiconductor Electronics
Question as text
With the help of a circuit diagram, explain the working of a full wave rectifier. Depict the input and output waveforms.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: centre-tap transformer; diodes D 1, D2 from the two ends of the secondary joined at one end of load RL; load returned to the centre tap. Working: in one half cycle end A is positive, D 1 forward biased conducts, D2 reverse biased; in the next half B is positive, D 2 conducts; in both halves current flows through RL in the same direction. Waveforms: input – full sine wave; output – unidirectional pulsating dc with both half cycles (all humps on one side, frequency 2ν).
