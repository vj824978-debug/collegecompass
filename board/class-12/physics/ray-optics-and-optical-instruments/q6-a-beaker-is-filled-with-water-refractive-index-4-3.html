A beaker is filled with water (refractive index 4 3) upto a height H. A coin is placed at its bottom. The depth of the coin, when viewed along the near normal direction, will be (A) H 4 (B) 3H (C) H (D) 4H
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Apparent depth = H/µ = 3H/4.
