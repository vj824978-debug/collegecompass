A ray of light in air is incident at angle ∠i on a face of an equilateral glass prism and is refracted through the prism. As ∠i is varied, it is observed that the ray undergoes minimum deviation, when the ∠i is three-fourth of the angle of the prism. Calculate the speed of light in the prism.
Answer
Answer as text
A = 60◦, i = 45◦; at minimum deviation r =A/2 = 30◦; n = sin 45◦/ sin 30◦ = √ 2; v =c/ √ 2≈ 2.12× 108 m s−1.
