Two thin lenses of focal length f1 and f2 are placed in contact with each other coaxially.

CBSE 202655/5/12 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
Two thin lenses of focal length f1 and f2 are placed in contact with each other coaxially. Prove that the focal length f of the combination is given by f = f1f2 f1 +f2 .
Answer
Answer as text
Derivation: lens 1 forms image at v1: 1 v1 − 1 u = 1 f1 ; it acts as object for lens 2: 1 v− 1 v1 = 1 f2 . Adding: 1 v− 1 u = 1 f1 + 1 f2 = 1 f , so f = f1f2 f1 +f2 .
