Question as text
A right angled isosceles glass prism ABC is kept in contact with an equilateral triangular prism DBC as shown in the figure. Both prisms are made of the same glass of refractive index 1.6. Trace the path of the ray MN incident normally on face AB as it passes through the combination.
Answer
Answer as text
sinic = 1/1.6, ic≈ 38.7◦. Ray enters AB undeviated, meets AC at 45◦>i c: total internal reflection, travels down perpendicular to BC and passes into DBC undeviated (same glass). It meets CD at 60 ◦>i c: TIR again; reflected ray strikes BD normally and emerges undeviated, perpendicular to BD. Diagram must show these two TIRs and normal exit through BD.
