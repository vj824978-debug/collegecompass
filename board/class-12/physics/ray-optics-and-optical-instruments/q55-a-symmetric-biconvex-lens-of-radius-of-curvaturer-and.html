A symmetric biconvex lens of radius of curvatureR and made of glass of refractive index 1.5, is placed on a layer of liquid placed on top of a plane mirror as shown in the figure. An optical needle with its tip on the principal axis of the lens is moved along the axis until its real, inverted image coincides with the needle itself. The distance of the needle from the lens is measured to be x. On removing the liquid layer and repeating the experiment, the distance is found to be y. Obtain the expression for the refractive index of the liquid in terms of x and y.
Answer
Answer as text
Image coincides with object when the object is at the focus of the system. Lens alone: 1 y = (1.5− 1) 2 R⇒y =R. Liquid forms a plano-concave lens: fl =−µ− 1 R . Combination: 1 x = 1 y− µ− 1 y ⇒µ = 2− y x = 2x−y x .
