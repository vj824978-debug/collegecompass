Question as text
A ray of light passes through a prism of refractive index √ 2 as shown in the figure. Find: (i) The angle of incidence ( ∠r2) at face AC. (ii) The angle of minimum deviation for this prism.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Ray grazes face AC, so r2 = critical angle: sin r2 = 1/ √ 2⇒r2 = 45◦. (ii) At minimum deviation r =A/2 = 30◦: sini = √ 2 sin 30◦ = 1/ √ 2⇒i = 45◦; δm = 2i−A = 30◦.
