Assertion (A): A ray of light is incident normally on the face of a prism.

CBSE 202555/7/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
Assertion (A): A ray of light is incident normally on the face of a prism. The emergent ray will graze along the opposite face of the prism when the critical angle at glass-air interface is equal to the angle of the prism. Reason (R): The refractive index of a prism depends on angle of the prism. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Normal incidence: ray meets second face at angle A; grazes if A =ic (A true). Refractive index is a property of the material, not of the prism angle (R false).
