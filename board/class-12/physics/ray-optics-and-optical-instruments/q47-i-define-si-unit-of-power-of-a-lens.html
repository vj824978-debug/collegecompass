(i) Define SI unit of power of a lens.

CBSE 2022T erm II55/5/13 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
(a) (i) Define SI unit of power of a lens. (ii) A plano-convex lens is made of glass of refractive index 1.5. The radius of curvature of the convex surface is 25 cm. (iii) Calculate the focal length of the lens. (iv) If an object is placed 50 cm in front of the lens, find the nature and position of the image formed. OR (b) A slit of width 0.6 mm is illuminated by a beam of light consisting of two wavelengths 600 nm and 480 nm. The diffraction pattern is observed on a screen 1.0 m from the slit. Find: (i) The distance of the second bright fringe from the central maximum pertaining to light of 600 nm. (ii) The least distance from the central maximum at which bright fringes due to both the wavelengths coincide.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) 1 dioptre = power of a lens of focal length 1 m. (iii) 1 f = (1.5− 1) 1 25− 1 ∞ ⇒f = 50 cm. (iv) u =−50 cm =−f: object at focus, so the image is at infinity (real, inverted, highly magnified). OR: (b) Single-slit reading: secondary maxima at y = (2n + 1)λD/2a. (i) y2 = 5λD/2a = 5× 600× 10−9× 1 2× 0.6× 10−3 = 2.5 mm. (ii) 5(2n + 1) = 4(2m + 1) has no integer solution, so secondary maxima never coincide exactly. The question evidently intends double-slit fringes (d = 0.6 mm): (i) y = 2λD/d = 2 mm; (ii)n× 600 = (n + 1)× 480⇒n = 4, y = 4λ1D/d = 4 mm.
