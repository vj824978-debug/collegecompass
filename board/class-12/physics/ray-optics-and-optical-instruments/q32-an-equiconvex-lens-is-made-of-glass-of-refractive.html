Question as text
An equiconvex lens is made of glass of refractive index 1.55. If the focal length of the lens is 15.0 cm, calculate the radius of curvature of its surfaces.
Answer
Answer as text
f = (µ− 1) 2 R⇒R = 2(µ− 1)f = 2× 0.55× 15 = 16.5 cm.
