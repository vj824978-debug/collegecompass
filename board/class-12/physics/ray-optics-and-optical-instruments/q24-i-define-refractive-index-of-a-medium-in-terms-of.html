(i) Define refractive index of a medium in terms of speed of light.

CBSE 202655/4/15 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
(a) (i) Define refractive index of a medium in terms of speed of light. (ii) Derive the relation for the refractive index (µ) of a prism in terms of angle of minimum derivation (δm) and angle of prism (A). (iii) A ray of light QP is incident normally on the face BC of a triangular prism ABC of refractive index 1.5 kept in air, as shown in the figure. Trace the path of the ray as it passes through the prism and give relevant explanation. OR (b) (i) What is the difference between a ray and a wavefront? (ii) A plane wave is incident on a reflecting surface. Using Huygens principle, show how it is reflected from the surface. Hence, verify the law of reflection. (iii) Depict refraction of a plane wave by a convex lens.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) µ =c/v: ratio of speed of light in vacuum to its speed in the medium. (ii) Derivation: r1 +r2 =A, δ =i +e−A; at minimum deviation i =e, r1 =r2 =r =A/2, i = (A +δm)/2, so µ = sin A+δm sin A . (iii) Ray enters BC normally, undeviated. Taking the prism as equilateral, it meets face AB at i = 60◦; critical angle ic = sin−1(1/1.5)≈ 41.8◦< 60◦, so total internal reflection occurs at AB. The reflected ray then strikes face AC normally (i = 0◦) and emerges undeviated, perpendicular to AC. (b)(i) Wavefront: locus of points oscillating in the same phase (a surface); ray: line along the direction of energy propagation, always normal to the wavefront. (ii) Diagram: plane wavefront AB incident at angle i on mirror MN; while the disturbance from B reaches C (distance vτ ), secondary wavelet from A spreads to radius AE =vτ ; tangent CE is the reflected wavefront. Triangles EAC and BAC are congruent (AE =BC, common AC, right angles)⇒i =r. (iii) Diagram: plane wavefront incident on a convex lens emerges as a spherical wavefront converging to the focus F (centre portion delayed more as the lens is thickest there).
