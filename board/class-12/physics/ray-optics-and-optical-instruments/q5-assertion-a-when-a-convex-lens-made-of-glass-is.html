Assertion (A): When a convex lens made of glass is immersed in water, its converging power increases.

CBSE 202655/4/1
Question 5 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
Assertion (A): When a convex lens made of glass is immersed in water, its converging power increases. Reason (R): The focal length of a lens depends only on the radii of curvature of its two faces. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are false.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1 f = ng nw − 1 1 R1 − 1 R2 : focal length increases in water, so power decreases (A false); f also depends on relative refractive index (R false).
