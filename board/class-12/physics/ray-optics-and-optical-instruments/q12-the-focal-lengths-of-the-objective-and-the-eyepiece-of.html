Question 12 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
The focal lengths of the objective and the eyepiece of a compound microscope are 1 cm and 2 cm respectively. If the tube length of the microscope is 10 cm, the magnification obtained by the microscope for most suitable viewing by relaxed eye is (A) 250 (B) 200 (C) 150 (D) 125
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Relaxed eye (image at infinity): m = L fo · D fe = 10 1 × 25 2 = 125.
