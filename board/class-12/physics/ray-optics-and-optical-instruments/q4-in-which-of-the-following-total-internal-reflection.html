In which of the following, total internal reflection does not occur?

CBSE 202655/3/1
Question 4 · Class 12 Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
Question as text
In which of the following, total internal reflection does not occur? (A) Twinkling of stars (B) Brilliance of diamonds (C) Optical fibre (D) Reflecting prism
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Twinkling of stars is due to atmospheric refraction, not TIR.
