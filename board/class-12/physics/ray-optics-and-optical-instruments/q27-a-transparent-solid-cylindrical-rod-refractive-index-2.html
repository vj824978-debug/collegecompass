A transparent solid cylindrical rod (refractive index 2√ 3) is kept in air. A ray of light incident on its face travels along the surface of the rod, as shown in figure. Calculate the angle θ.
Answer
Answer as text
Ray grazes the curved surface: angle there = C, sinC = √ 3/2, C = 60◦; refraction angle at the face r = 90◦− 60◦ = 30◦. sinθ = 2√ 3 sin 30◦ = 1√ 3; θ = sin−1(1/ √ 3)≈ 35.3◦.
