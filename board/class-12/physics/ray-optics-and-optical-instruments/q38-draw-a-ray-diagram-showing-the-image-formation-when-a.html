Draw a ray diagram showing the image formation when a concave mirror produces a real, inverted and magnified image of an object and hence obtain the mirror formula.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: object between F and C of a concave mirror, image beyond C, real, inverted, magnified (two standard rays). Derivation: similar triangles B′A′ BA = B′F FP and B′A′ BA = B′P BP give v−f f = v u (sign convention), i.e. v + 1 u = 1 f .
