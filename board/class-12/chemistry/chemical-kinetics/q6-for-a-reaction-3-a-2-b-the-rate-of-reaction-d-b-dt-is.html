For a reaction 3 A−−→2 B, the rate of reaction +d[B] dt is equal to: (A) −3 d[A] dt (B) −2 d[A] dt (C) −1 d[A] dt (D) +2 d[A] dt
Answer
Answer as text
(B) − 1 d[A] dt = 1 d[B] dt ⇒ d[B] dt =− 2 d[A] dt .
