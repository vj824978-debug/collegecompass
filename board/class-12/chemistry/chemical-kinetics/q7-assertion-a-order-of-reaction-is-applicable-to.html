Assertion (A): Order of reaction is applicable to elementary as well as complex reactions.

CBSE 202656/3/1
Question 7 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
Assertion (A): Order of reaction is applicable to elementary as well as complex reactions. Reason (R): Order of a reaction is an experimental quantity. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Order is found experimentally from the rate law, so it can be determined for any reaction, elementary or complex (unlike molecularity, a theoretical concept for elementary steps).
