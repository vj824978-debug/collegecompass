Chemical Kinetics — Question 41 (CBSE 2025, 3 marks)

CBSE 202556/5/13 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
For the reaction A + B −−→Products, the following initial rates were obtained at various initial concentrations of reactants: Sl. No. [A]/mol L −1 [B]/mol L−1 Initial rate/mol L−1 s−1 1 0.1 0.1 0.05 2 0.2 0.1 0.10 3 0.1 0.2 0.05 Determine the order of the reaction with respect to A and B and overall order of the reaction.
Answer
Answer as text
Expt 1→2: [A] doubled, rate doubled: order w.r.t. A = 1. Expt 1 →3: [B] doubled, rate unchanged: order w.r.t. B = 0. Overall order = 1; rate =k[A].
