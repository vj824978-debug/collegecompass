Chemical Kinetics — Question 34 (CBSE 2025, 3 marks)

CBSE 202556/1/13 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
A certain reaction is 50% complete in 20 minutes at 300 K and the same reaction is 50% complete in 5 minutes at 350 K. Calculate the activation energy if it is a first order reaction. [R = 8.314 J K−1 mol−1; log 4 = 0.602]
Answer
Answer as text
First order: k∝ 1/t1/2, so k350/k300 = 20/5 = 4. log 4 = Ea 2.303R· 350−300 300×350; Ea = 0.602×2.303×8.314×105000 50 ≈ 2.42× 104 J mol−1 (≈ 24.2 kJ mol−1).
