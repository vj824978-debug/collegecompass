Question as text
(a) For the reaction 2 N2O5(g)−−→4 NO2(g) + O2(g) at 318 K Calculate the rate of reaction if rate of disappearance of N 2O5(g) is 1.4× 10−3 m s−1. (b) For a first order reaction derive the relationship t99% = 2t90%
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Rate =− 1 d[N2O5] dt = 1 2× 1.4× 10−3 = 7× 10−4 M s−1 (unit printed as m s−1 in the paper; read as mol L−1 s−1). (b) Proof: t = 2.303 k log [R]0 [R] ; t99% = 2.303 k log 100 1 = 2×2.303 k , t90% = 2.303 k log 100 10 = 2.303 k ; hence t99% = 2t90%.
