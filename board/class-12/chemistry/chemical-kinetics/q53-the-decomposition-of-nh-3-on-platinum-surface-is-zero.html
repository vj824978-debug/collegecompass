CBSE 20193 marks
Question 53 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
The decomposition of NH 3 on platinum surface is zero order reaction. If rate constant k is 4 × 10−3 M s−1, how long will it take to reduce the initial concentration of NH 3 from 0.1 M to 0.064 M?
Answer
Answer as text
Zero order: t = [R]0− [R] k = 0.1− 0.064 4× 10−3 = 0.036 0.004 = 9 s.
