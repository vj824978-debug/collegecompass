Assertion (A): Hydrolysis of an ester follows first order kinetics.

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 17 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
Assertion (A): Hydrolysis of an ester follows first order kinetics. Reason (R): The concentration of water does not get altered much during the reaction. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Water is in large excess, its concentration is nearly constant: pseudo first order.
