(a) Define activation energy. (b) Write the unit of rate constant for (i) zero order reaction, (ii) second order reaction.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Activation energy: the minimum extra energy (over the average energy of reactants) that reacting molecules must absorb to form the activated complex, Ea =Ethreshold−Eaverage. (b)(i) mol L−1 s−1; (ii) L mol−1 s−1.
