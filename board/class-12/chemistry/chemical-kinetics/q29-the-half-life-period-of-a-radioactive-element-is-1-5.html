Question 29 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
The half-life period of a radioactive element is 1 · 5× 1010 years. Calculate the time in which the activity of the element is reduced to 75% of its original value. [Given: log 2 = 0· 30, log 3 = 0· 48, log 4 = 0· 60]
Answer
Answer as text
Radioactive decay is first order: t = 2.303 k log 100 75 , k = 0.693 t1/2 ; log 4 3 = 0.60− 0.48 = 0.12. t =t1/2× log(4/3) log 2 = 1.5× 1010× 0.12 0.30 = 6.0× 109 years.
