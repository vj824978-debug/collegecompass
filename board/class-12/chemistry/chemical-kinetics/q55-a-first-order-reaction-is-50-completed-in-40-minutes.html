CBSE 20183 marks
Question 55 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
A first order reaction is 50% completed in 40 minutes at 300 K and in 20 minutes at 320 K. Calculate the activation energy of the reaction. (Given: log 2 = 0.3010, log 4 = 0.6021, R = 8.314 J K−1 mol−1)
Answer
Answer as text
First order: k∝ 1/t1/2, so k2/k1 = 40/20 = 2. log 2 = Ea 2.303× 8.314· 320− 300 320× 300⇒Ea = 0.3010× 19.147× 4800≈ 2.766× 104 J mol−1 = 27.66 kJ mol−1.
