(i) A first order reaction is 25% complete in 40 minutes.

CBSE 20205 marks
Question 51 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
(a) (i) A first order reaction is 25% complete in 40 minutes. Calculate the value of rate constant. In what time will the reaction be 80% completed? (ii) Define order of reaction. Write the condition under which a bimolecular reaction follows first order kinetics. 3+2 OR (b) (i) A first order reaction is 50% completed in 30 minutes at 300 K and in 10 minutes at 320 K. Calculate activation energy (Ea) for the reaction. ( R = 8.314 J K−1 mol−1) (ii) Write the two conditions for collisions to be effective collisions. (iii) How order of reaction and molecularity differ towards a complex reaction? 3+1+1 [Given : log 2 = 0.3010, log 3 = 0.4771, log 4 = 0.6021, log 5 = 0.6991]
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) k = 2.303 40 log 100 75 = 2.303× 0.1250 40 = 7.20× 10−3 min−1; t80% = 2.303 k log 5 = 40× 0.6990 0.1250≈ 224 min. (ii) Order = sum of the powers of the concentration terms in the experimentally determined rate law. A bimolecular reaction becomes pseudo-first order when one reactant is in large excess (e.g. acid hydrolysis of ester in excess water). (b)(i) k∝ 1/t1/2: k320/k300 = 30/10 = 3; log 3 = Ea 2.303R· 20 300× 320⇒ Ea = 0.4771× 2.303× 8.314× 96000 20 ≈ 4.38× 104 J mol−1 (43.8 kJ mol−1). (ii) Colliding molecules must have energy ≥ threshold energy, and proper orientation. (iii) Molecularity applies only to elementary steps and has no meaning for the overall complex reaction; the order of a complex reaction is found experimentally and is governed by the slowest (rate-determining) step; it may be zero or fractional.
