Question 18 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
The activation energy (Ea) of a reaction can be determined from the slope of which of the following plots? (A) ln k vs. T (B) lnk T vs. T (C) ln k vs. 1 T (D) T lnk vs. 1 T
Answer
Answer as text
(C) ln k = lnA− Ea RT : slope of ln k vs 1/T is−Ea/R.
