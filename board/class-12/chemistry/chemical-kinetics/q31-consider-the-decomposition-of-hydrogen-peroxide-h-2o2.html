Consider the decomposition of hydrogen peroxide (H 2O2) as per the equation given below : 2 H2O2 I – −−−−−−−−−−→Alkaline medium 2 H2O + O2 The mechanism for this reaction was found to be : Step I : H 2O2 + I–−−→H2O + IO– (slow) Step II : H 2O2 + IO–−−→H2O + I– + O2 (fast) (a) Write the rate law expression. (b) Determine the order of reaction with respect to H 2O2, I – and overall order of reaction. (c) What is the molecularity of the reaction in Step II?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Rate = k[H2O2][I−] (from slow step). (b) First order in H 2O2, first order in I – ; overall order 2. (c) Step II is bimolecular (molecularity 2).
