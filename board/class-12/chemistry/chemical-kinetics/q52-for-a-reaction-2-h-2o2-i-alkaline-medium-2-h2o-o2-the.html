Chemical Kinetics — Question 52 (CBSE 2019, 2 marks)

CBSE 20192 marks
Question 52 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
For a reaction, 2 H 2O2 I – − −−−−−−−−− →alkaline medium 2 H2O + O2 the proposed mechanism is given below: (1) H2O2 + I–−−→H2O + IO– (slow) (2) H2O2 + IO–−−→H2O + I– + O2 (fast) (i) Write rate law of the reaction. (ii) Write overall order of reaction. (iii) Out of step (1) and (2), which one is rate determining step?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Rate = k[H2O2][I−] (from slow step). (ii) Overall order = 2. (iii) Step (1), the slow step.
