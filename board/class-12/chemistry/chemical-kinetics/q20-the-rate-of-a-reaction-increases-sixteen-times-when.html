Question as text
The rate of a reaction increases sixteen times when the concentration of the reactant increases four times. The order of the reaction is (A) 2.5 (B) 2.0 (C) 1.5 (D) 0.5
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 16 = 4 n⇒n = 2.
