Assertion (A): The molecularity of the given reaction is 2 2 HI−−→H2 + I2 Reason (R): Two molecules of the reactants are involved in simultaneous collision between them. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Elementary bimolecular step: two HI molecules collide simultaneously, so molecularity 2; R explains A.
