Chemical Kinetics — Question 43 (CBSE 2025, 3 marks)

CBSE 202556/6/13 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemical Kinetics
Question as text
The rate of a reaction : A + B −−→product is given below as a function of different initial concentrations of A and B. Experiment [A] / mol L−1 [B] / mol L−1 Initial Rate/mol L−1 min−1 1 0.01 0.01 5× 10−3 2 0.02 0.01 1× 10−2 3 0.01 0.02 5× 10−3 Calculate the order of the reaction with respect to A and B. Determine the rate constant of the reaction.
Answer
Answer as text
Exp 1→2: [A] doubled, rate doubled: order 1 in A. Exp 1 →3: [B] doubled, rate unchanged: order 0 in B. Rate = k[A]; k = 5× 10−3/0.01 = 0.5 min−1; overall order 1.
