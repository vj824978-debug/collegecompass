Assertion (A): All naturally occurring α-amino acids except glycine are optically active.

CBSE 202556/1/1
Question 13 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Assertion (A): All naturally occurring α-amino acids except glycine are optically active. Reason (R): Most naturally occurring amino acids have L-configuration. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false and Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; optical activity is due to the chiral α-carbon, not to the L-configuration.
