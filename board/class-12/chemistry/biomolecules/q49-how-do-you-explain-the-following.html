How do you explain the following?

CBSE 202556/6/12 marks
Question 49 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
How do you explain the following? (a) Presence of an aldehydic group in glucose. (b) Presence of five –OH groups in glucose.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Glucose with bromine water gives gluconic acid HOCH2(CHOH)4COOH (–CHO oxidised to –COOH with same carbons). (b) Acetylation with acetic anhydride gives glucose pentaacetate, showing five –OH groups.
