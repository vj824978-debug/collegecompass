Differentiate between following: (i) Amylose and Amylopectin (ii) Globular protein and Fibrous protein (iii) Nucleotide and Nucleoside
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Amylose: water-soluble, unbranched chain of α-D-glucose (α-1,4 links), 15–20% of starch; amylopectin: water-insoluble, branched ( α-1,4 and α-1,6 links), 80–85%. (ii) Globular: chains coiled into spherical shape, water-soluble (insulin, albumins); fibrous: chains parallel, held by H- and disulphide bonds, fibre-like, insoluble (keratin, myosin). (iii) Nucleoside = base + pentose sugar; nucleotide = nucleoside + phosphate group (at 5′).
