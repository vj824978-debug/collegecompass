CBSE 202656/5/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
The polypeptide chain in a protein has amino acids linked with each other in a specific sequence. This specific sequence of amino acids is called : (A) Primary structure of protein (B) Secondary structure of protein (C) Tertiary structure of protein (D) Quaternary structure of protein
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Sequence of amino acids = primary structure.
