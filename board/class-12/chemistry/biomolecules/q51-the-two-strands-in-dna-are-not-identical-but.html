The two strands in DNA are not identical but complementary.

CBSE 202556/7/12 marks
Question 51 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
The two strands in DNA are not identical but complementary. Explain. What products would be formed when DNA is hydrolysed?
Answer
Answer as text
Bases pair specifically by H-bonds, A with T and G with C, so the sequence of one strand fixes the other: strands are complementary, not identical. Hydrolysis gives 2-deoxy-D-ribose, phosphoric acid and the bases adenine, guanine, cytosine, thymine.
