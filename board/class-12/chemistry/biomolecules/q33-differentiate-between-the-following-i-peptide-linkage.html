Differentiate between the following: (i) Peptide linkage and Glycosidic linkage (ii) Essential amino acids and Non-essential amino acids
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Peptide linkage: –CO–NH– amide bond between –COOH of one amino acid and –NH 2 of another (in proteins); glycosidic linkage: C–O–C ether linkage between two monosaccharide units (in carbohydrates). (ii) Essential amino acids cannot be synthesised by the body and must be supplied in diet (e.g. valine, leucine, lysine); non-essential amino acids are synthesised in the body (e.g. glycine, alanine).
