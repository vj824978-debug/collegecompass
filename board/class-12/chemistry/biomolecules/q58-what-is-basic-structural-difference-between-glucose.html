What is basic structural difference between glucose and fructose?

CBSE 20191 mark
Question 58 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
(a) What is basic structural difference between glucose and fructose? OR (b) Write products obtained after hydrolysis of lactose.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Glucose is an aldohexose (–CHO at C1); fructose is a ketohexose (C=O at C2) [cyclic forms: pyranose vs furanose]. (b) β-D-galactose + β-D-glucose.
