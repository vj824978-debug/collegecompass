How do you explain the following?

CBSE 202656/3/12 marks
Question 36 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
How do you explain the following? [1+1=2] (a) Presence of a straight chain in glucose (b) Presence of five –OH groups in glucose which are attached to different carbon atoms
Answer
Answer as text
(a) On prolonged heating with HI glucose gives n-hexane, showing all six C atoms in a straight chain. (b) Acetylation with acetic anhydride gives glucose pentaacetate, confirming five –OH groups; it is stable, so they are on different C atoms.
