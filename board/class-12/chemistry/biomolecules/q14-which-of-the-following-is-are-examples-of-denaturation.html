Which of the following is/are examples of denaturation of protein?

CBSE 202556/2/1
Question 14 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Which of the following is/are examples of denaturation of protein? (A) Coagulation of egg white (B) Curdling of milk (C) Clotting of blood (D) Both (A) and (B)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Both are NCERT examples of denaturation (heat/acid change 2◦/3◦ structure); blood clotting is enzymatic.
