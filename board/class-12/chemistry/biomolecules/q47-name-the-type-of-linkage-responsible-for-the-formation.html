Name the type of linkage responsible for the formation of proteins from α-amino acids.

CBSE 202556/5/13 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
(a) Name the type of linkage responsible for the formation of proteins from α-amino acids. 1 (b) Write any two differences between DNA and RNA. 2
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Peptide linkage (–CO–NH–). (b) Any two: DNA has 2-deoxyribose, RNA has ribose; DNA has thymine, RNA has uracil; DNA is double-stranded helix, RNA single-stranded; DNA replicates and carries genetic information, RNA directs protein synthesis.
