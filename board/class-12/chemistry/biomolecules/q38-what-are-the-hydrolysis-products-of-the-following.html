What are the hydrolysis products of the following?

CBSE 202656/4/12 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
What are the hydrolysis products of the following? (a) Lactose (b) DNA containing Thymine
Answer
Answer as text
(a) β-D-galactose + β-D-glucose. (b) 2-Deoxy-D-ribose, phosphoric acid and the bases adenine, guanine, cytosine, thymine.
