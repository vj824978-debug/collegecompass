Define the following terms: (a) Glycosidic linkage (b) Invert sugar (c) Oligosaccharides
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The C–O–C (oxide) linkage joining two monosaccharide units through an oxygen atom. (b) Sucrose is dextrorotatory but its hydrolysis gives an equimolar mixture of D-(+)-glucose and D-(−)-fructose that is laevorotatory; the change in sign of rotation is inversion, and the product is invert sugar. (c) Carbohydrates that yield 2–10 monosaccharide units on hydrolysis (e.g. sucrose, maltose, raffinose).
