Glucose on reaction with Br 2 water gives (A) Saccharic acid (B) Hexanoic acid (C) Gluconic acid (D) Salicylic acid
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Bromine water (mild oxidant) oxidises –CHO to –COOH: gluconic acid.
