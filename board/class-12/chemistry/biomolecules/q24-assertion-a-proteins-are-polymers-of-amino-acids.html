Assertion (A): Proteins are polymers of α-amino acids connected by a peptide bond.

CBSE 2023
Question 24 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Assertion (A): Proteins are polymers of α-amino acids connected by a peptide bond. Reason (R): A tetrapeptide contains 4 amino acids linked by 4 peptide bonds. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A tetrapeptide has 4 amino acids but only 3 peptide bonds; R false.
