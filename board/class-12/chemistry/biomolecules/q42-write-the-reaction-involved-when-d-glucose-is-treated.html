Write the reaction involved when D-glucose is treated with the following reagents: (a) HCN (b) Br 2 water
Answer
Answer as text
(a) HOCH2(CHOH)4CHO + HCN−−→HOCH2(CHOH)4CH(OH)CN (glucose cyanohydrin). (b) HOCH2(CHOH)4CHO Br2 water−−−−−−→HOCH2(CHOH)4COOH (gluconic acid).
