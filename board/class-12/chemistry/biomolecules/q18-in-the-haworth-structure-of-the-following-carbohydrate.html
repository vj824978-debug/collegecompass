Question as text
In the Haworth structure of the following carbohydrate, various carbon atoms have been numbered. The anomeric carbon is numbered as : (A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 5
Answer
Answer as text
(A) C-1 (attached to ring O and bearing OH) is the anomeric carbon.
