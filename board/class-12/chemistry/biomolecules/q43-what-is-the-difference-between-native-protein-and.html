What is the difference between native protein and denatured protein?

CBSE 202556/1/13 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
(a) What is the difference between native protein and denatured protein? (b) Which one of the following is a disaccharide? Glucose, Lactose, Amylose, Fructose (c) Which vitamin is responsible for the coagulation of blood? [1+1+1=3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Native protein: protein in its natural 3D (2 ◦/3◦) structure with biological activity; denatured: 2◦/3◦ structure destroyed (heat, pH) and activity lost, 1◦ structure intact. (b) Lactose. (c) Vitamin K.
