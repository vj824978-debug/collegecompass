Biomolecules — Question 52 (CBSE 2025, 3 marks)

CBSE 202556/7/13 marks
Question 52 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Write the reaction of D-Glucose with the following: 3 × 1 = 3 (a) HCN (b) Br 2 water (c) (CH 3CO)2O
Answer
Answer as text
(a) CHO(CHOH)4CH2OH + HCN−−→CH(OH)(CN)(CHOH)4CH2OH (cyanohydrin). (b) CHO(CHOH)4CH2OH Br2 water−−−−−−→COOH(CHOH)4CH2OH (gluconic acid). (c) CHO(CHOH)4CH2OH (CH3CO)2O −−−−−−−→CHO(CHOCOCH3)4CH2OCOCH3 (glucose pentaacetate).
