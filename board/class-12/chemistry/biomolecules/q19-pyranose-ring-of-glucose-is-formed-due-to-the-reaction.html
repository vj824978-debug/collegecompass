Pyranose ring of glucose is formed due to the reaction between : (A) C1 and C3 (B) C1 and C5 (C) C1 and C4 (D) C1 and C2
Answer
Answer as text
(B) C5–OH adds to the C1 aldehyde to form a six-membered hemiacetal ring.
