Assertion (A): D(+)-Glucose is dextrorotatory in nature.

CBSE 202656/5/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Assertion (A): D(+)-Glucose is dextrorotatory in nature. Reason (R): (+) represents dextrorotatory nature and D represents the configuration. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both true; (+) denotes dextrorotation, D the configuration, which explains the name.
