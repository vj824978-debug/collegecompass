Write any two fat soluble vitamins.

CBSE 202656/5/12 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
(a) Write any two fat soluble vitamins. (b) How will you confirm the presence of five –OH groups in a glucose molecule, which are attached to different carbon atoms?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Any two of vitamins A, D, E, K. (b) Acetylation with acetic anhydride gives glucose pentaacetate, showing five –OH groups; as glucose is stable, they are on different carbon atoms.
