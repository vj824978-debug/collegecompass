Question as text
Give reasons for any 3 of the following observations: (a) Penta-acetate of glucose does not react with hydroxylamine. (b) Amino acids behave like salts. (c) Water soluble vitamins must be taken regularly in diet. (d) The two strands in DNA are complimentary to each other.
Answer
Answer as text
Any three: (a) In the pentaacetate the C-1 OH is acetylated, so no free –CHO (open chain) can form; hence no reaction with NH 2OH. (b) They exist as dipolar zwitter ions (H 3N+ –CHR–COO – ) with strong electrostatic attractions: high m.p., water soluble. (c) They are readily excreted in urine and cannot be stored (except B 12), so must be supplied regularly. (d) Bases pair specifically by H-bonds (A with T, G with C), so the sequence of one strand fixes the other.
