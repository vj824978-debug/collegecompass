Assertion (A): Vitamin K can be stored in our body.

CBSE 202556/7/1
Question 21 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Assertion (A): Vitamin K can be stored in our body. Reason (R): Vitamin K is a water soluble vitamin. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Vitamin K is fat-soluble, hence stored in liver/adipose tissue; R is false.
