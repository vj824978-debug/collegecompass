What is meant by essential amino acids?

CBSE 202556/4/12 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
What is meant by essential amino acids? Why are amino acids amphoteric in nature?
Answer
Answer as text
Essential amino acids cannot be synthesised in the body and must be obtained through diet (e.g. valine, leucine, lysine). Amphoteric: exist as zwitterions H3N+ –CHR–COO – , with acidic (–NH 3+/–COOH) and basic (–COO–/–NH 2) groups, so they react with both acids and bases.
