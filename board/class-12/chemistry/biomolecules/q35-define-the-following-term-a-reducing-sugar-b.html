Question as text
(A) Define the following term: (a) Reducing sugar (b) Differentiate between the following: (i) Fibrous proteins and globular proteins (ii) Nucleotide and nucleoside OR (B) Write the reaction of glucose with the following: (a) H2N–OH (b) (CH3CO)2O (c) conc. HNO 3
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(a) Reducing sugars: carbohydrates that reduce Fehling’s solution and Tollens’ reagent (free aldehydic/ketonic group), e.g. all monosaccharides, maltose, lactose. (b)(i) Fibrous: polypeptide chains parallel, held by H- and disulphide bonds, fibre-like, insoluble in water (keratin, myosin); globular: chains coiled into spherical shape, soluble in water (insulin, albumin). (ii) Nucleoside = base + pentose sugar; nucleotide = base + sugar + phosphate group (phosphate ester of a nucleoside). (B)(a) HOCH2(CHOH)4CHO + NH2OH−−→HOCH2(CHOH)4CH––NOH + H2O (glucose oxime). (b) (CH3CO)2O acetylates all five OH: glucose pentaacetate CHO(CHOCOCH3)4CH2OCOCH3. (c) Conc. HNO 3 oxidises both ends: saccharic (glucaric) acid HOOC(CHOH) 4COOH.
