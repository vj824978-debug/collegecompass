Question 55 · Class 12 Chemistry · Biomolecules
Question as text
Give the reaction of glucose with hydrogen cyanide. Presence of which group is confirmed by this reaction?
Answer
Answer as text
CHO(CHOH)4CH2OH + HCN−−→HOCH2(CHOH)4CH(OH)CN (glucose cyanohydrin); confirms the presence of a carbonyl (C=O) group.
