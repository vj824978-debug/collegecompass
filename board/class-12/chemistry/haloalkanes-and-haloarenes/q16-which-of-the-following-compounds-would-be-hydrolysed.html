Which of the following compounds would be hydrolysed by aqueous KOH most easily?

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 16 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Which of the following compounds would be hydrolysed by aqueous KOH most easily? (A) CH 2 ––CH–Br (B) CH 3 –CH 2 –Br (C) CH 3 –CH(Br)–CH 3 (D) CH 2 ––CH–CH 2 –Br
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Allyl bromide gives a resonance-stabilised allylic carbocation; vinyl bromide is least reactive.
