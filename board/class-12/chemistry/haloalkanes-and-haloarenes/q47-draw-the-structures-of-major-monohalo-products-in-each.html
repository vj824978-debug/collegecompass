Draw the structures of major monohalo products in each of the following reactions : (a) (b)
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Markovnikov addition: 1-iodo-1-methylcyclohexane (I and CH 3 on the same ring carbon). (b) Allylic substitution: 3-bromocyclohexene.
