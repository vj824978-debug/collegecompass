Out of chlorobenzene and benzyl chloride, which one gets easily hydrolysed by aqueous NaOH and why?

CBSE 20181 mark

Question as text
Out of chlorobenzene and benzyl chloride, which one gets easily hydrolysed by aqueous NaOH and why?
Answer
Answer as text
Benzyl chloride: it ionises to the resonance-stabilised benzyl carbocation (fast SN1; also good SN2). In chlorobenzene the C–Cl bond has partial double-bond character (resonance, sp2 C), so it is not hydrolysed under these conditions.
