Identify A, B, C, D, E and F in the following:
Answer
Answer as text
Starting halide (CH 3)2CHCH2Br. A = (CH 3)2C––CH2 (2-methylpropene); B = (CH 3)3CBr (Markovnikov); C = (CH3)3C–C(CH 3)3 (2,2,3,3-tetramethylbutane, Wurtz); D = (CH3)2CHCH2MgBr; E = (CH 3)2CHCH3 (2-methylpropane); F = (CH3)2CHCH2OC2H5 (1-ethoxy-2-methylpropane, Williamson).
