Write any two differences between S N1 and S N2 reactions.

CBSE 202556/6/13 marks

Question as text
Write any two differences between S N1 and S N2 reactions. Which of the following compounds would undergo SN1 reaction faster and why?
Answer
Answer as text
SN1: two steps via carbocation, rate = k[RX] (first order), racemisation, favoured for 3◦ halides; SN2: one step via transition state, rate =k[RX][Nu−] (second order), inversion, favoured for 1◦. Benzyl chloride C6H5CH2Cl reacts faster by S N1: the benzylic carbocation is resonance-stabilised, while cyclohexylmethyl cation is an unstable 1 ◦ carbocation.
