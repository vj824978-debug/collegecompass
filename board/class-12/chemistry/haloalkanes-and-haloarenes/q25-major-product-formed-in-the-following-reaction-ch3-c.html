Major product formed in the following reaction CH3 –C(CH 3)2 –Br + NaOCH3−−→ (A) CH 3 –C(CH 3)2 –ONa (B) CH 3 –C(CH 3)2 –OCH 3 (C) CH 3 –C(CH 3)2 –O–C(CH 3)2 –CH 3 (D) CH 3 –C(CH 3)––CH2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 3 ◦ halide with strong base CH 3O– undergoes E2 elimination: 2-methylpropene.
