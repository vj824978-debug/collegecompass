Assertion (A): Inversion of configuration is observed in S N2 reaction.

CBSE 2024
Question 20 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Assertion (A): Inversion of configuration is observed in S N2 reaction. Reason (R): The reaction proceeds with the formation of carbocation. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) S N2 is concerted (backside attack via a transition state, no carbocation), giving inversion.
