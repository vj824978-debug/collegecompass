Assertion (A): The boiling points of alkyl halides decrease in the order RI > RBr > RCl> RF.

CBSE 202556/5/1
Question 15 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Assertion (A): The boiling points of alkyl halides decrease in the order RI > RBr > RCl> RF. Reason (R): The van der Waals forces of attraction decrease in the order RI > RBr > RCl> RF. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Larger, more polarisable halogen gives stronger van der Waals forces and higher b.p.
