Out of 2-Bromobutane, 1-Bromobutane, 2-Bromopropane and 1-Bromopropane, the molecule which is chiral in nature is : (A) 2-Bromobutane (B) 1-Bromobutane (C) 2-Bromopropane (D) 1-Bromopropane
Answer
Answer as text
(A) C-2 of 2-bromobutane carries H, Br, CH 3, C2H5: four different groups.
