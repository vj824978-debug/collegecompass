Why are haloarenes less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction?

CBSE 202656/1/12 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Why are haloarenes less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction? Give two reasons.
Answer
Answer as text
Any two: (i) resonance gives the C–X bond partial double-bond character, harder to break; (ii) sp2 carbon makes the C–X bond shorter and stronger; (iii) phenyl cation is unstable (S N1 not possible); (iv) electron-rich ring repels the incoming nucleophile; back-side attack blocked.
