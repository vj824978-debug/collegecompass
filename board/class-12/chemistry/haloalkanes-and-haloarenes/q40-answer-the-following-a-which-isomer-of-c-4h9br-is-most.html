Answer the following : (a) Which isomer of C 4H9Br is most reactive towards S N1 reaction?

CBSE 202656/4/13 marks
Question 40 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Answer the following : (a) Which isomer of C 4H9Br is most reactive towards S N1 reaction? (b) Predict the alkene that would be formed by dehydrohalogenation of 1-Bromo-1-methylcyclohexane. (c) Although chlorine shows strong −I effect, yet it is ortho- and para-directing in electrophilic aromatic substitution reactions. Why?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 2-Bromo-2-methylpropane, (CH3)3CBr (forms the stable 3◦ carbocation). (b) 1-Methylcyclohexene (Saytzeff product; methylenecyclohexane is minor). (c) −I effect deactivates the ring, but +R (resonance) donation of Cl lone pairs raises electron density at ortho and para positions and stabilises the o/p σ-complexes.
