Write the major product in the following reactions: (b) Define Racemic mixture.

CBSE 202656/2/13 marks
Question 36 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
(a) Write the major product in the following reactions: (b) Define Racemic mixture. (c) p-dichlorobenzene has a higher melting point as compared to its ortho and meta isomers. Why?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Allylic substitution at high temperature: 3-bromocyclohexene (+ HBr). (At ordinary temperature Br 2 would add to give trans-1,2-dibromocyclohexane.) (b) Racemic mixture: an equimolar mixture of the two enantiomers; it is optically inactive (external compensation). (c) The symmetrical para isomer packs better in the crystal lattice, so stronger intermolecular forces and higher m.p.
