Question as text
(a) Identify the chiral molecule in the following pair: (b) Write the structure of the product when chlorobenzene is treated with methyl chloride in the presence of sodium metal and dry ether. (c) Write the structure of the alkene formed by dehydrohalogenation of 1-bromo-1-methylcyclohexane with alcoholic KOH.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) is chiral: in 3-methylbutan-2-ol C-2 carries H, OH, CH 3 and CH(CH3)2. (ii) 2,4-dimethylpentan-3-ol has two identical isopropyl groups on C-3, so it is achiral. (b) Wurtz–Fittig: C 6H5Cl + CH3Cl + 2 Na dry ether −−−−−→C6H5CH3 + 2 NaCl; product toluene. (c) 1-Methylcyclohexene (major, Saytzeff; trisubstituted alkene), with methylenecyclohexane as minor product.
