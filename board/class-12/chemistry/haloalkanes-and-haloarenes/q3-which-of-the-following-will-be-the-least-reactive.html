Which of the following will be the least reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction?

CBSE 202656/3/1
Question 3 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Which of the following will be the least reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction? (A) (B) (C) CH 3 –Cl (D)
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Aryl halide (4-chlorotoluene): C–Cl has partial double bond character by resonance, sp2 carbon; least reactive.
