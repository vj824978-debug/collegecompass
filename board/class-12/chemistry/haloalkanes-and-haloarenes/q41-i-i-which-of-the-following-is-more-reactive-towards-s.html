(i) (I) Which of the following is more reactive towards S N1 reaction : 2-Bromo-2-methylbutane or…

Question as text
(a) (i) (I) Which of the following is more reactive towards S N1 reaction : 2-Bromo-2-methylbutane or 1-Bromopentane (II) What type of halide is present in the following compound : CH3 –CH(CH 3)–C(Cl) ––CH2 (III) Why is chloroform stored in dark coloured bottles? (ii) Define the following terms : (I) Ambident Nucleophiles (II) Racemic mixture OR (b) (i) Answer the following : (I) Which isomer of C 4H9Br is most reactive towards S N1 reaction? (II) Predict the alkene that would be formed by dehydrohalogenation of 1-Bromo-1-methylcyclohexane. (III) Although chlorine shows strong −I effect, yet it is ortho/para-directing in electrophilic aromatic substitution reactions. Why? (ii) Write the major product in the following reactions : (I) (II)
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i)(I) 2-Bromo-2-methylbutane (tertiary carbocation). (II) Vinylic halide (Cl on C ––C carbon). (III) In light and air it is oxidised to poisonous phosgene: 2 CHCl 3 + O2−−→2 COCl2 + 2 HCl. (ii)(I) Nucleophiles with two nucleophilic sites, e.g. CN – (C or N), NO 2 – (N or O). (II) Equimolar mixture of a pair of enantiomers; optically inactive. (b)(i)(I) 2-Bromo-2-methylpropane, (CH3)3CBr (tertiary). (II) 1-Methylcyclohexene (Saytzeff product; methylenecyclohexane minor). (III)−I deactivates the ring, but +R of Cl increases electron density at o- and p-positions and stabilises the o/p arenium ions. (ii)(I) Biphenyl, C6H5 –C 6H5 (Fittig reaction) + 2NaCl. (II) Benzylic (free-radical) bromination: p-O 2N–C 6H4 –C(CH 3)2Br (2-bromo-2-(4-nitrophenyl)propane) + HBr.
