Question as text
Give reasons for the following: [3 ×1=3] (a) Although chlorine is an electron-withdrawing group, yet it is ortho-, para-directing in electrophilic aromatic substitution reactions. (b) Aryl halides cannot be prepared by reacting phenol with concentrated halogen acids or phosphorus halides. (c) Chloroform is stored in closed dark coloured bottles.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Cl withdraws electrons by −I effect (deactivates the ring) but donates its lone pair by +R resonance, which increases electron density at o- and p-positions and stabilises the corresponding arenium ions; so it is o,p-directing. (b) The C–O bond in phenol has partial double bond character (resonance, sp2 C) and is very difficult to cleave. (c) In light and air CHCl 3 is oxidised to the poisonous gas phosgene: 2 CHCl 3 + O2 light − −− →2 COCl2 + 2 HCl; closed dark bottles exclude air and light.
