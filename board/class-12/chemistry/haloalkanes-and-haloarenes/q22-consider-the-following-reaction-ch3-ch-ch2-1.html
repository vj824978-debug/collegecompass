Haloalkanes and Haloarenes — Question 22 (CBSE 2022)

CBSE 2022T erm I
Question 22 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Consider the following reaction CH3 –CH ––CH2 1. HBr−−−−−−−→2. aq· KOH The major end product is (A) CH 3 –CH(OH)–CH 3 (B) CH 3 –CH(Br)–CH 3 (C) CH 3 –CH 2 –CH 2 –OH (D) CH 3 –CH 2 –CH 2 –Br
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Markovnikov addition gives 2-bromopropane; aq. KOH substitutes OH: propan-2-ol.
