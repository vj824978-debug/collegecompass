Auto-oxidation of chloroform in air and light produces a poisonous gas known as (A) Phosphine (B) Mustard gas (C) Phosgene (D) Tear gas
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 2 CHCl 3 + O2 light − −− →2 COCl2 + 2 HCl; COCl2 is phosgene.
