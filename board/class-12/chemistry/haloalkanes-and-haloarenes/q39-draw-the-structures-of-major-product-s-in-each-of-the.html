Draw the structures of major product(s) in each of the following reactions : (a) (b) CH3CH––C(CH3)2 + HBr−−→
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 2,4-Dinitrophenol (–Cl replaced by –OH; activated by o/p –NO 2). (b) Markovnikov addition: 2-bromo-2-methylbutane, CH3CH2C(Br)(CH3)2.
