Assertion (A): Chlorobenzene is less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction.

CBSE 2022T erm I
Question 31 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Assertion (A): Chlorobenzene is less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction. Reason (R): Nitro group in chlorobenzene increases its reactivity towards nucleophilic substitution reaction. (A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. (C) A is true but R is false. (D) A is false but R is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true: C–Cl has partial double-bond character (A); o/p-NO2 activates the ring (R), but R does not explain A.
