Why are haloarenes less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction?

CBSE 202556/7/12 marks
Question 49 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Why are haloarenes less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction? How does the presence of nitro (–NO 2) group at ortho- and para-positions in haloarenes increase the reactivity towards nucleophilic substitution reaction?
Answer
Answer as text
Resonance gives the C–X bond partial double-bond character; C is sp2 (shorter, stronger bond); phenyl cation is unstable; electron-rich ring repels the nucleophile. An o-/p-NO 2 group withdraws electron density and delocalises the negative charge of the carbanion (Meisenheimer) intermediate onto its own oxygens, stabilising it and speeding attack (m-NO2 cannot).
