Question as text
(a) Define the following: 2 (i) Enantiomers (ii) Racemic mixture (b) Why is chlorobenzene resistant to nucleophilic substitution reaction? 1
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Stereoisomers that are non-superimposable mirror images of each other. (ii) Equimolar mixture of the two enantiomers; optically inactive (zero rotation, external compensation). (b) Resonance gives the C–Cl bond partial double-bond character; C is sp2 (shorter, stronger bond); phenyl cation is unstable; the electron-rich ring repels the approaching nucleophile.
