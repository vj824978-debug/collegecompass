Question as text
Polyhalogen compounds have wide application in industries and agriculture. DDT is also a very important polyhalogen compound. It is a : (A) greenhouse gas (B) fertilizer (C) biodegradable insecticide (D) non-biodegradable insecticide
Answer
Answer as text
(D) DDT is a persistent, non-biodegradable insecticide.
