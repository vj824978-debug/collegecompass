Why is boiling point of o-dichlorobenzene higher than p-dichlorobenzene but melting point of para isomer is higher than ortho isomer?
Answer
Answer as text
m.p.: the symmetrical p-isomer packs better in the crystal lattice, so stronger intermolecular forces in the solid and higher m.p. b.p.: the o-isomer is polar (dipole moment) while the p-isomer has zero dipole moment, so stronger dipole–dipole attraction in the liquid gives o-isomer the higher b.p.
