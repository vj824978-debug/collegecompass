In the following pair of halogen compounds, which compound undergoes S N1 reaction faster and why?

CBSE 202556/2/12 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
(a) In the following pair of halogen compounds, which compound undergoes S N1 reaction faster and why? 1 (b) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their reactivity towards SN2 displacement: 2–Bromo–2–methylbutane, 1–Bromopentane, 2–Bromopentane.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 2-Chloro-2-methylpropane (tert-butyl chloride, first structure): it forms a more stable 3◦ carbocation than 3-chloropentane (2◦). (b) 2-Bromo-2-methylbutane< 2-bromopentane < 1-bromopentane (steric hindrance at the reacting carbon: 3 ◦>2◦>1◦).
