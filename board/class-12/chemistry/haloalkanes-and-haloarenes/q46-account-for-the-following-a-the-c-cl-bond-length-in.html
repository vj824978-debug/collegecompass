Account for the following: (a) The C–Cl bond length in chlorobenzene is shorter than that in methyl chloride.

CBSE 202556/5/13 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Account for the following: (a) The C–Cl bond length in chlorobenzene is shorter than that in methyl chloride. (b) Grignard reagents should be prepared under anhydrous conditions. (c) In case of optically active alkyl halides, S N1 reactions are accompanied by racemisation.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Resonance gives C–Cl partial double-bond character and the C is sp2 (more s character). (b) Grignard reagents react with water (RMgX + H2O−−→RH + Mg(OH)X). (c) S N1 goes through a planar carbocation; the nucleophile attacks from both faces giving a racemic mixture.
