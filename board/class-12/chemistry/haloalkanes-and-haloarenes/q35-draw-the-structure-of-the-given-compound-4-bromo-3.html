(a) Draw the structure of the given compound: 4–Bromo–3–methylpent–2–ene (b) What happens when chloroethane is treated with aqueous potassium hydroxide?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) CH3 –CH ––C(CH3)–CH(Br)–CH 3. (b) Nucleophilic substitution gives ethanol: C 2H5Cl + KOH(aq)−−→C2H5OH + KCl.
