Compound ‘X’ with molecular formula C4H9Br reacts with aqueous KOH to give an alcohol.

CBSE 202656/1/13 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Chemistry · Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
Question as text
Compound ‘X’ with molecular formula C4H9Br reacts with aqueous KOH to give an alcohol. The rate of this reaction depends only on the concentration of the compound ‘X’. When an optically active isomer ‘Y’ of the compound ‘X’ was treated with aqueous KOH solution, the rate of reaction was found to be dependent on concentration of compound ‘Y’ and aqueous KOH both. (a) Write down the structural formula of both ‘X’ and ‘Y’. (b) Out of ‘X’ and ‘Y’, which one will undergo racemisation and why? (c) Out of ‘X’ and ‘Y’, which one will form product with inversion of configuration and why?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) X: (CH 3)3C–Br (2-bromo-2-methylpropane, S N1); Y: CH3CH2CH(Br)CH3 (2-bromobutane, chiral, SN2). (b) X: reacts by S N1 through a planar carbocation that is attacked equally from both faces, giving a racemic product. (c) Y: S N2, the nucleophile attacks from the side opposite the leaving group (back-side attack), giving inversion (Walden inversion).
