(a) Give reasons: (i) H3PO3 undergoes disproportionation reaction but H 3PO4 does not.

CBSE 20185 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 26 · Class 12 Chemistry · The p-Block Elements
Question as text
(a) (a) Give reasons: (i) H3PO3 undergoes disproportionation reaction but H 3PO4 does not. (ii) When Cl 2 reacts with excess of F 2, ClF3 is formed and not FCl 3. (iii) Dioxygen is a gas while sulphur is a solid at room temperature. (b) Draw the structures of the following: (i) XeF4 (ii) HClO3 OR (b) (a) When concentrated sulphuric acid was added to an unknown salt present in a test tube, a brown gas (A) was evolved. This gas intensified when copper turnings were added to this test tube. On cooling, the gas (A) changed into a colourless solid (B). (i) Identify (A) and (B). (ii) Write the structures of (A) and (B). (iii) Why does gas (A) change to solid on cooling? (b) Arrange the following in the decreasing order of their reducing character: HF, HCl, HBr, HI (c) Complete the following reaction: XeF4 + SbF5−−→
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (a)(i) In H 3PO3 P is +3 (intermediate), so it can be both oxidised and reduced: 4 H 3PO3−−→3 H3PO4 + PH3; in H 3PO4 P is in its highest state +5, so it cannot. (ii) Cl is larger and has vacant d orbitals, so it can expand its octet as central atom; F is small, highly electronegative and has no d orbitals, so FCl 3 cannot form. (iii) O forms pπ–pπ double bonds giving small discrete O 2 molecules with weak van der Waals forces (gas); S prefers single bonds and catenates into S 8 rings with stronger van der Waals forces (solid). (b) XeF 4: square planar, Xe with 4 F and 2 lone pairs ( sp3d2). HClO 3: pyramidal Cl bearing –OH, two ––O and one lone pair. (b) (a) Salt is a nitrate. (i) A = NO 2 (brown), B = N 2O4 (colourless). (ii) NO2: angular (bent, ≈ 134◦), odd electron on N; N 2O4: planar O2N–NO 2 with N–N bond. (iii) NO 2 has an odd electron and dimerises on cooling: 2 NO 2−−⇀↽−−N2O4. (b) HI > HBr > HCl > HF. (c) XeF 4 + SbF5−−→[XeF3]+[SbF6]– .
