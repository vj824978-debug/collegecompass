Draw the structures of the following: (i) H2S2O8 (ii) XeF6
Answer
Answer as text
(i) H2S2O8: HO–SO 2 –O–O–SO 2 –OH, each S tetrahedral with two S=O, one S–OH and the peroxo O–O bridge. (ii) XeF 6: 6 bond pairs + 1 lone pair on Xe (sp3d3), distorted octahedral.
