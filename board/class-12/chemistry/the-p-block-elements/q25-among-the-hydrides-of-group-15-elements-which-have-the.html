Among the hydrides of Group-15 elements, which have the (a) lowest boiling point?

Question as text
Among the hydrides of Group-15 elements, which have the (a) lowest boiling point? (b) maximum basic character? (c) highest bond angle? (d) maximum reducing character?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) PH3 (no H-bonding, smallest of the heavier hydrides; NH 3 is H-bonded). (b) NH 3. (c) NH 3 (107.8◦). (d) BiH 3.
