Assertion (A): H 2S is less acidic than H 2Te.

CBSE 2022T erm IOut of current syllabus
Question 14 · Class 12 Chemistry · The p-Block Elements
Question as text
Assertion (A): H 2S is less acidic than H 2Te. Reason (R): H–S bond has more ∆ bondH◦ than H–Te bond. (A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. (C) A is true but R is false. (D) A is false but R is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Acidity increases down the group because H–E bond enthalpy decreases; R explains A.
