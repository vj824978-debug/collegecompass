Sulphuric acid is used to prepare more volatile acids from their corresponding salts due to its (A) strong acidic nature (B) low volatility (C) strong affinity for water (D) ability to act as a dehydrating agent
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Low volatility of H 2SO4, e.g. 2 MX + H2SO4−−→2 HX + M2SO4.
