Question as text
(a) Name the method of refining which is (i) used to obtain semiconductor of high purity, (ii) used to obtain low boiling metal. (b) Write chemical reactions taking place in the extraction of copper from Cu 2S.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Zone refining; (ii) distillation. (b) 2 Cu 2S + 3 O2−−→2 Cu2O + 2 SO2; 2 Cu2O + Cu2S−−→6 Cu + SO2 (self-reduction; blister copper).
