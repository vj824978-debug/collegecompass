First ionization enthalpy of Cr is lower than that of Zn.

CBSE 202656/2/12 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
(a) First ionization enthalpy of Cr is lower than that of Zn. Why? (b) Mention any one property of the transition elements which makes them good catalysts.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Cr (3d5 4s1) loses the single 4s electron easily (gives stable 3 d5); Zn (3d10 4s2) has a stable fully filled configuration and higher effective nuclear charge, so removing a paired 4s electron needs more energy. (b) Any one: variable oxidation states (form unstable intermediates), ability to form complexes, large surface area / adsorption of reactants.
