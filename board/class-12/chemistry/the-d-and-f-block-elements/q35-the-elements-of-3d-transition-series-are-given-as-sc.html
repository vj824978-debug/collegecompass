Question as text
The elements of 3d transition series are given as: Sc, Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn Answer the following: (a) Copper has exceptionally positive E◦ M2+/M value, why? (b) Which element is a strong reducing agent in +2 oxidation state and why? (c) Zn2+ salts are colourless. Why?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) High enthalpy of atomisation and high ∆ iH1 + ∆iH2 of Cu are not compensated by its hydration enthalpy, so E◦ Cu2+/Cu = +0.34 V. (b) Cr: Cr2+ (d4)→ Cr3+ (d3, stable half-filled t3 2g level). (c) Zn 2+ is 3d10: no unpaired electron / no vacant d orbital, so no d–d transition.
