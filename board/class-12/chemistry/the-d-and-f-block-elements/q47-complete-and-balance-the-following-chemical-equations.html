Complete and balance the following chemical equations: (a) Fe2+ + MnO4 – + H+−−→ (b) MnO4 – + H2O + I–−−→
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 5 Fe2+ + MnO4 – + 8 H+−−→5 Fe3+ + Mn2+ + 4 H2O. (b) Neutral/faintly alkaline: 2 MnO4 – + H2O + I–−−→2 MnO2 + 2 OH– + IO3 – .
