Assertion (A): Zinc, cadmium and mercury are not considered as transition elements.

CBSE 202656/2/1
Question 4 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Assertion (A): Zinc, cadmium and mercury are not considered as transition elements. Reason (R): These elements have completely filled orbitals in their ground state as well as in their common oxidation states. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Zn, Cd, Hg are ( n− 1)d10ns2 and their ions M 2+ ared10: no partly filled d orbitals in atom or common ion, which is why they are not transition elements.
