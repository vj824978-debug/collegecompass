Give the reasons for following: (i) Transition elements and their compounds act as catalysts.

CBSE 20193 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Give the reasons for following: (i) Transition elements and their compounds act as catalysts. (ii) E◦ value for (Mn2+/Mn) is negative whereas for (Cu 2+/Cu) is positive. (iii) Actinoids show irregularities in their electronic configuration.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Variable oxidation states (easy electron transfer, unstable intermediates) and ability to adsorb reactants on surface/form complexes. (ii) Mn 2+ is 3d5 (stable half-filled), so Mn is easily oxidised: E◦ negative; for Cu the high ∆ aH◦ (and ionisation enthalpies) is not compensated by the hydration enthalpy of Cu 2+: E◦ positive (+0.34 V). (iii) 5f, 6d and 7s orbitals have comparable energies, so electrons occupy them irregularly.
