Assertion (A): Highest oxidation state of Mn is +7 in first series of transition elements.

CBSE 202656/5/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Assertion (A): Highest oxidation state of Mn is +7 in first series of transition elements. Reason (R): Transition metals exhibit variable oxidation states. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; +7 for Mn is due to using all 3d and 4s electrons (3d54s2), not explained merely by variability.
