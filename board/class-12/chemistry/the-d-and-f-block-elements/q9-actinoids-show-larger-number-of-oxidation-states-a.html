Actinoids show larger number of oxidation states : (A) because they are radioactive in nature (B) because they have large atomic numbers (C) because they have large atomic masses (D) due to comparable energies of 5f, 6d and 7s orbitals
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 5f, 6d, 7s have comparable energies, so variable numbers of electrons can be lost.
