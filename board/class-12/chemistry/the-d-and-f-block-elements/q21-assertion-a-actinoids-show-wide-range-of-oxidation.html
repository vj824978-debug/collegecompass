Assertion (A): Actinoids show wide range of oxidation states.

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 21 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Assertion (A): Actinoids show wide range of oxidation states. Reason (R): Actinoids are radioactive in nature. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true, but the range of oxidation states is due to comparable energies of 5f, 6d and 7s orbitals, not radioactivity.
