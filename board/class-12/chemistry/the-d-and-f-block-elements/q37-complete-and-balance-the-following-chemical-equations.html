Complete and balance the following chemical equations: (a) MnO4 – + C2O42– + H+−−→ (b) KMnO4 Heat−−−→513 K (c) Cr2O72– + H2S + H+−−→
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 2 MnO4 – + 5 C2O42– + 16 H+−−→2 Mn2+ + 10 CO2 + 8 H2O. (b) 2 KMnO4 513 K−−−→K2MnO4 + MnO2 + O2. (c) Cr 2O72– + 3 H2S + 8 H+−−→2 Cr3+ + 3 S + 7 H2O.
