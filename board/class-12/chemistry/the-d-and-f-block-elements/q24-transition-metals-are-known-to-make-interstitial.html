Question as text
Transition metals are known to make interstitial compounds. Formation of interstitial compounds makes the transition metal (A) more hard (B) more soft (C) more ductile (D) more metallic
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Small atoms (H, C, N) in the lattice voids make the metal harder (and raise m.p.).
