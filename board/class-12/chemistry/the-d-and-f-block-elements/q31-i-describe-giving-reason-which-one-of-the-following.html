Question 31 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
(a) (i) Describe giving reason which one of the following pairs has the property indicated: (I) Fe or Cu – higher melting point (II) Ti3+ or Sc3+ – coloured in aqueous solution (III) Cr or Zn – higher third ionisation enthalpy (ii) Write the ionic equations for the oxidizing action of MnO 4 – in acidic medium with: (I) Fe2+ ion (II) I – ion [3+2=5] OR (b) (i) A black-brown coloured solid (A) when fused with KOH in the presence of air, produces a dark green coloured compound (B) which on electrolytic oxidation in alkaline medium gives a dark purple coloured compound (C). Identify (A), (B) and (C). Write the reactions involved. (ii) What happens when an acidic solution of the green compound (B) is allowed to stand for some time? Also write the equation involved. What is this type of reaction called? [3+2=5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) (I) Fe: more unpaired d electrons, stronger metallic bonding (Cu has 3d10). (II) Ti 3+ (3d1) is coloured due to d–d transition; Sc 3+ (3d0) is colourless. (III) Zn: third electron removed from stable completely filled 3d10 of Zn2+; for Cr, Cr 2+ (3d4)→ Cr3+ (3d3, stable t3 2g) is easier. (ii) MnO4 – + 5 Fe2+ + 8 H+−−→Mn2+ + 5 Fe3+ + 4 H2O; 2 MnO4 – + 10 I– + 16 H+−−→2 Mn2+ + 5 I2 + 8 H2O. (b)(i) A = MnO 2, B = K 2MnO4 (potassium manganate), C = KMnO 4. 2 MnO2 + 4 KOH + O2−−→2 K2MnO4 + 2 H2O; MnO42– electrolytic oxidation −−−−−−−−−−−−→alkaline MnO4 – + e– . (ii) The green manganate disproportionates into purple permanganate and brown MnO2: 3 MnO42– + 4 H+−−→2 MnO4 – + MnO2 + 2 H2O; disproportionation reaction.
