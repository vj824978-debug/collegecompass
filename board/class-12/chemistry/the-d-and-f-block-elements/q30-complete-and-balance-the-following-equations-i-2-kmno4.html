Question as text
(a) Complete and balance the following equations: (i) 2 KMnO4 513 K−−−→ (ii) Na2Cr2O7 + 2 KCl−−→ (b) Why is it difficult to separate lanthanoid elements in pure state?
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) 2 KMnO4 513 K−−−→K2MnO4 + MnO2 + O2; (ii) Na 2Cr2O7 + 2 KCl−−→K2Cr2O7 + 2 NaCl. (b) Due to lanthanoid contraction, their ionic radii are very similar, so their chemical properties are almost identical and separation is difficult.
