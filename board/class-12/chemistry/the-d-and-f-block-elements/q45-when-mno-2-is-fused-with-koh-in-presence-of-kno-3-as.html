When MnO 2 is fused with KOH in presence of KNO 3 as an oxidizing agent, it gives a dark green compound (A). Compound (A) disproportionates in acidic solution to give purple compound (B). An alkaline solution of compound (B) oxidises KI to compound (C) whereas an acidified solution of compound (B) oxidises KI to (D). Identify (A), (B), (C) and (D).
Answer
Answer as text
A = K 2MnO4 (manganate); B = KMnO 4 (3 MnO42– + 4 H+−−→2 MnO4 – + MnO2 + 2 H2O); C = KIO 3 (2 MnO4 – + H2O + I–−−→2 MnO2 + 2 OH– + IO3 – ); D = I 2 (2 MnO4 – + 10 I– + 16 H+−−→2 Mn2+ + 5 I2 + 8 H2O).
