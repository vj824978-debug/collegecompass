Question 40 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
(a) (i) Account for the following : (I) Orange colour of Cr 2O72– ion changes to yellow when treated with an alkali. (II) Zn, Cd and Hg are non-transition elements. (III) E◦ value for Mn3+/Mn2+ couple is highly positive (+1.57 V) as compared to Cr 3+/Cr2+. (ii) What happens when : (I) Manganate ion undergoes disproportionation reaction in acidic medium? (II) KMnO4 is heated? OR (b) Answer the following questions : (i) What is ‘Misch metal’? Give its one use. (ii) Write the formula of an oxoanion of chromium in which it shows the oxidation state equal to its group number. (iii) Why does Vanadium pentoxide (V 2O5) act as a catalyst? (iv) Why do transition elements have high enthalpies of atomisation? (v) How do you prepare Na 2Cr2O7 from Na2CrO4?
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i)(I) Cr2O72– + 2 OH–−−→2 CrO42– + H2O; orange dichromate becomes yellow chromate. (II) They have completely filled d10 orbitals in the ground state and in their common oxidation state (+2). (III) Mn2+ is 3d5 (stable half-filled), so Mn 3+ is readily reduced; Cr 3+ (t3 2g) is very stable, so E◦ Cr3+/Cr2+ is negative (−0.41 V). (ii)(I) 3 MnO42– + 4 H+−−→2 MnO4 – + MnO2 + 2 H2O. (II) 2 KMnO4 513 K−−−→K2MnO4 + MnO2 + O2. (b)(i) Alloy of∼95% lanthanoid metal and∼5% iron with traces of S, C, Ca, Al; used in Mg-based alloys for bullets, shells and lighter flints. (ii) CrO42– or Cr2O72– (Cr +6, group 6). (iii) V can change oxidation state (+5/+4) and provides a surface, forming unstable intermediates of lower activation energy. (iv) Large number of unpaired electrons give strong interatomic metallic bonding. (v) Acidify: 2 Na 2CrO4 + 2 H+−−→Na2Cr2O7 + 2 Na+ + H2O (with H 2SO4).
