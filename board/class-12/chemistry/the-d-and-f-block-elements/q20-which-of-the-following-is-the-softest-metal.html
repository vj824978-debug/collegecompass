Which of the following is the softest metal?

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 20 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Which of the following is the softest metal? (A) Zn (B) Sc (C) Cu (D) Fe
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Zn (3 d104s2) has no unpaired d-electrons, weak metallic bonding: softest of these.
