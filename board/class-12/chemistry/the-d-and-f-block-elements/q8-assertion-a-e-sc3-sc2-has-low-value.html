Assertion (A): E◦ Sc3+/Sc2+ has low value.

CBSE 202656/4/1
Question 8 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Assertion (A): E◦ Sc3+/Sc2+ has low value. Reason (R): Because of the stability of Sc 3+ ion which has a noble gas configuration. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Sc 3+ has the stable [Ar] configuration, so its reduction to Sc2+ is unfavourable; R explains A.
