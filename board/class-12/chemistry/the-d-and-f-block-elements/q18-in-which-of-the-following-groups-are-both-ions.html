In which of the following groups are both ions coloured in aqueous solution?

CBSE 202556/5/1
Question 18 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
In which of the following groups are both ions coloured in aqueous solution? I. Cu+ II. Ti4+ III. Co 2+ IV. Fe2+ [Atomic number: Cu = 29, Ti = 22, Co = 27, Fe = 26] (A) I and II (B) II and III (C) III and IV (D) I and IV
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Co 2+ (3d7) and Fe2+ (3d6) have unpaired d electrons; Cu + (3d10) and Ti 4+ (3d0) are colourless.
