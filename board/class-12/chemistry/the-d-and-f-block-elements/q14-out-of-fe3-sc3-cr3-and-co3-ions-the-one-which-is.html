Out of Fe3+, Sc3+, Cr3+ and Co3+ ions, the one which is colourless in aqueous solution is : [Atomic number : Fe = 26, Sc = 21, Cr = 24, Co = 27] (A) Sc 3+ (B) Fe 3+ (C) Cr 3+ (D) Co 3+
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Sc 3+ is 3d0: no d–d transition, colourless.
