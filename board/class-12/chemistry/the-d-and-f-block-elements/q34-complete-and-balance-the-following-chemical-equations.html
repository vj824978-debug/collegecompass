The d- and f-Block Elements — Question 34 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 202556/1/12 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Complete and balance the following chemical equations: (a) 2 MnO4 – (aq) + 10 I– (aq) + 16 H+(aq)−−→ (b) Cr2O72– (aq) + 6 Fe2+(aq) + 14 H+(aq)−−→
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 2 MnO4 – + 10 I– + 16 H+−−→2 Mn2+ + 5 I2 + 8 H2O. (b) Cr2O72– + 6 Fe2+ + 14 H+−−→2 Cr3+ + 6 Fe3+ + 7 H2O.
