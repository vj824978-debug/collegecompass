Give reasons: (a) E◦ value for Mn3+/Mn2+ couple is much more positive than that for Fe 3+/Fe2+.

CBSE 20183 marks
Question 48 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
Give reasons: (a) E◦ value for Mn3+/Mn2+ couple is much more positive than that for Fe 3+/Fe2+. (b) Iron has higher enthalpy of atomization than that of copper. (c) Sc3+ is colourless in aqueous solution whereas Ti 3+ is coloured.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Mn2+ is 3d5 (extra-stable half-filled), so Mn 3+ (3d4) is readily reduced; the third ionisation enthalpy of Mn is very high. Fe 3+ (3d5) is itself the stable state, so Fe 3+/Fe2+ is less positive. (b) Fe (3d64s2) has more unpaired electrons than Cu (3 d104s1), giving stronger metallic bonding (more interatomic interaction), hence higher ∆aH. (c) Sc3+ is 3d0: no unpaired d electron, no d–d transition, colourless; Ti3+ is 3d1: d–d transition absorbs visible light, coloured (purple).
