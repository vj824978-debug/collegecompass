Why is chemistry of actinoids complicated as compared to lanthanoids?

CBSE 20235 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Chemistry · The d- and f-Block Elements
Question as text
(a) Why is chemistry of actinoids complicated as compared to lanthanoids? (b) Complete the following reaction and justify that it is a disproportionation reaction: 3 MnO42– + 4 H+−−→ + + 2 H2O. (c) The given graph shows the trends in melting points of transition metals: Explain the reason why Cr has highest melting point and manganese (Mn) a lower melting point.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Actinoids show a wide range of oxidation states, are radioactive (many with short half-lives and available only in tiny amounts), making their study difficult. (b) 3 MnO42– + 4 H+−−→2 MnO4 – + MnO2 + 2 H2O. Mn in MnO 42– (+6) is simultaneously oxidised to +7 (MnO 4 – ) and reduced to +4 (MnO2): disproportionation. (c) Cr (3d54s1) has the maximum number (6) of unpaired electrons available for metallic bonding, giving strong interatomic bonding and high m.p. Mn (3d54s2) has a stable half-filled 3d5 configuration; its d electrons are held tightly and take little part in metallic bonding, so its m.p. is low.
