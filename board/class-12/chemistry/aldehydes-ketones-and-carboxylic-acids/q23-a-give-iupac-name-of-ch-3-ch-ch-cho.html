(a) Give IUPAC name of CH 3 –CH ––CH–CHO.

CBSE 202556/2/15 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(A) (a) Give IUPAC name of CH 3 –CH ––CH–CHO. 1 (b) Give a simple chemical test to distinguish between propanal and propanone. 1 (c) How will you convert the following: 3 (i) Toluene to benzoic acid (ii) Ethanol to propan–2–ol (iii) Propanal to 2–hydroxy propanoic acid OR (B) Complete each synthesis by giving missing starting material, reagent or products: (a) (b) (c) (d) (e)
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(a) But-2-enal. (b) Tollens’ reagent: propanal gives a silver mirror, propanone does not (or iodoform test: propanone positive). (c)(i) Toluene KMnO4/KOH, ∆ −−−−−−−−−−−→potassium benzoate H3O+ −−−−→benzoic acid. (ii) CH3CH2OH PCC−−−→CH3CHO CH3MgBr −−−−−−→H3O+ −−−−→CH3CH(OH)CH3. (iii) CH3CH2CHO K2Cr2O7/H+ −−−−−−−−→CH3CH2COOH Cl2/red P (HVZ) −−−−−−−−−−→ CH3CHClCOOH aq· NaOH − −−−−−− → then H3O+ CH3CH(OH)COOH. (The commonly expected HCN/hydrolysis route gives CH 3CH2CH(OH)COOH, 2-hydroxybutanoic acid; see note.) (B)(a) Cyclopentanone oxime (C 5H8 ––NOH) + H 2O. (b) ? = cyclohexylidenecyclohexane (C6H10 ––C6H10). (c) Cyclohexane-1,2-dicarbonyl dichloride (+ SO 2 + HCl); the cyclic anhydride is also accepted by some keys. (d) Cyanohydrin: 2-[cyano(hydroxy)methyl]cyclohexane-1-carboxylic acid (–CH(OH)CN at the former CHO). (e) CH 3COCl, anhydrous AlCl3 (Friedel–Crafts acylation).
