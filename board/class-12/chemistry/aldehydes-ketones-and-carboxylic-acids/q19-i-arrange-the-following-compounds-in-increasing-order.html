(iii) CH 3CH––N–NH 2 + H2O (acetaldehyde hydrazone). (b) 2-Methylbenzaldehyde (o-tolualdehyde), o-CH 3C6H4CHO. With 2,4-DNP: o-CH3C6H4CH––N–NH–C 6H3(NO2)2 + H2O (2,4-DNP derivative). Cannizzaro: 2 o-CH3C6H4CHO conc· NaOH−−−−−−−→o-CH3C6H4CH2OH + o-CH3C6H4COONa. Vigorous oxidation gives phthalic acid.
