How do you convert the following?

CBSE 20182 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(a) How do you convert the following? (a) Ethanal to Propanone (b) Toluene to Benzoic acid OR (b) Account for the following: (a) Aromatic carboxylic acids do not undergo Friedel-Crafts reaction. (b) pKa value of 4-nitrobenzoic acid is lower than that of benzoic acid.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (a) CH3CHO (i) CH3MgBr, dry ether −−−−−−−−−−−−−−→ (ii) H3O+ CH3CH(OH)CH3 CrO3 −−−→CH3COCH3 (or PCC / K 2Cr2O7/H+). (b) C 6H5CH3 KMnO4/KOH, ∆ −−−−−−−−−−→ then H 3O+ C6H5COOH. (b) (a) –COOH is strongly deactivating (electron-withdrawing) and the Lewis acid AlCl 3 gets bonded (complexed) to the carboxyl group. (b) –NO 2 (−I,−R) withdraws electrons and stabilises the carboxylate anion, so 4-nitrobenzoic acid is the stronger acid (lower p Ka).
