Question 35 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(a) (i) Write the products formed when benzaldehyde reacts with the following reagents : (1) CH3CHO in presence of dilute NaOH (2) (3) Conc. NaOH (ii) Distinguish between following: (1) CH3 –CH ––CH–CO–CH 3 and CH3 –CH 2 –CO–CH ––CH2 (2) Benzaldehyde and Benzoic acid 3+(1+1) OR (b) (i) Write the final products in the following : (1) (2) (3) CH2 ––CH–CH 2 –CN (a) DIBAL–H −−−−−−−−→ (b) H3O+ (ii) Arrange the following in the increasing order of their reactivity towards nucleophilic addition reaction: CH3COCH3, HCHO, CH 3CHO, (iii) Draw the structure of 2, 4 DNP derivative of acetaldehyde. 3+1+1
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) (1) C 6H5CH––CHCHO (cinnamaldehyde, cross aldol); (2) C6H5CH––N–NH–C 6H5 (benzaldehyde phenylhydrazone); (3) Cannizzaro: C 6H5CH2OH + C6H5COONa. (ii) (1) CH 3CH––CHCOCH3 is a methyl ketone: gives yellow iodoform with I 2/NaOH; CH3CH2COCH––CH2 does not. (2) Benzaldehyde gives silver mirror with Tollens’ reagent; benzoic acid gives brisk effervescence (CO 2) with NaHCO3. (b)(i) (1) CH 3CH2CH3 (Clemmensen); (2) C 6H6 (+ Na2CO3, decarboxylation); (3) CH 2 ––CH–CH 2 –CHO (but-3-enal). (ii) C6H5COCH3 < CH3COCH3 < CH3CHO < HCHO. (iii) CH3CH––N–NH–C 6H3(NO2)2 (acetaldehyde 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazone; NO2 at 2,4 positions of the ring).
