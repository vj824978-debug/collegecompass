Question 10 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
Give reasons for the following: (a) Carboxylic acids have higher boiling point than alcohols of comparable molecular masses. (b) Alpha (α) hydrogens of aldehydes and ketones are acidic in nature. (c) Nucleophilic addition of ammonia and its derivatives does not occur with carbonyl group in strongly acidic medium.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) More extensive intermolecular H-bonding; carboxylic acids exist as H-bonded dimers even in the vapour phase. (b) Strong electron-withdrawing carbonyl group, and the conjugate base (enolate) is resonance-stabilised. (c) In strongly acidic medium NH 3/its derivatives are protonated (NH4+, RNH3+) and lose the lone pair, so they are no longer nucleophiles (optimum pH ≈ 3.5).
