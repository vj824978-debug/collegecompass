Question as text
Would you expect benzaldehyde to be more reactive or less reactive in nucleophilic addition reactions than propanal? Justify your answer.
Answer
Answer as text
Less reactive: the phenyl ring’s + R (resonance) effect delocalises the π electrons and reduces the electrophilicity (partial positive charge) of the carbonyl carbon.
