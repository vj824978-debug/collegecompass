Draw the structures of major product(s) in each of the following reactions: (a) (b) (c)
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Tollens’ oxidises only –CHO: 4-oxocyclohexane-1-carboxylic acid (as its ammonium salt, + Ag mirror); ring C=O unaffected. (b) Haloform reaction of the methyl ketone: CH 3CH––C(CH3)COO– Na+ (sodium 2-methylbut-2-enoate) + CHI3. (c) HCN adds to –CHO only: cyanohydrino-HOOC–C 6H4 –CH(OH)CN (2-[cyano(hydroxy)methyl]benzoic acid).
