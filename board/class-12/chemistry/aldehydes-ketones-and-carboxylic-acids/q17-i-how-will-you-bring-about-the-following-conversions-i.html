CBSE 202656/4/15 marks
Question 17 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(a) (i) How will you bring about the following conversions : (I) Bromobenzene to 1-phenylethanol (II) Benzene to m-nitroacetophenone (ii) An organic compound with the molecular formula C 8H8O forms 2,4-DNP derivative, reduces Tollens’ reagent and undergoes Cannizzaro reaction. On vigorous oxidation with acidic or alkaline KMnO4 it gives Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid. Identify the compound and write the products when it undergoes Cannizzaro reaction. (iii) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their acid strength : C6H5COOH, O2N–CH 2 –COOH, CF 3 –COOH, HCOOH OR (b) (i) Write the products formed when benzaldehyde reacts with the following reagents : (I) 2,4-Dinitrophenylhydrazine (II) Acetophenone in the presence of dilute NaOH followed by heating (ii) Give reasons for the following : (I) Benzoic acid does not undergo Friedel-Crafts reaction. (II) Carboxylic acids have higher boiling point than alcohols of comparable molecular mass. (iii) Write simple chemical test to distinguish between propanal and propanone.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) (I) C 6H5Br Mg −−−−−→dry ether C6H5MgBr CH3CHO−−−−−−→ H3O+ C6H5CH(OH)CH3. (II) C6H6 CH3COCl−−−−−−−−→anhyd· AlCl3 C6H5COCH3 conc· HNO3 −−−−−−−−→conc· H2SO4 m-nitroacetophenone (–COCH 3 is meta-directing). (ii) 2-Methylbenzaldehyde (o-tolualdehyde); Cannizzaro: 2 o-CH3C6H4CHO conc· NaOH−−−−−−−→ o-methylbenzyl alcohol + sodium o-methylbenzoate. (iii) C 6H5COOH < HCOOH < O2N–CH 2 –COOH < CF3 –COOH. (b) (i) (I) Benzaldehyde 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazone, C6H5CH––N–NH–C 6H3(NO2)2. (II) Claisen–Schmidt: C6H5CH––CH–CO–C 6H5 (benzalacetophenone). (ii) (I) –COOH deactivates the ring, and the catalyst AlCl 3 binds to the carboxyl group. (II) More extensive intermolecular H-bonding (acids exist as H-bonded dimers). (iii) Tollens’ test: propanal gives a silver mirror, propanone does not (or iodoform test: propanone gives yellow CHI 3).
