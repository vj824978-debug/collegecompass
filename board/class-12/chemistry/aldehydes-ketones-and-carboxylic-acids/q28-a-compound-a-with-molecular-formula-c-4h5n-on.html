A compound (A) with molecular formula C 4H5N on reduction with DIBAL-H followed by hydrolysis, gives a compound (B). Compound (B) gives positive Tollens’ test but does not give iodoform test. Compound (B) can also be obtained when ethanal is treated with dilute NaOH followed by heating. Identify (A) and (B). Write the reactions of (A) with DIBAL-H followed by hydrolysis.
Answer
Answer as text
A = but-2-enenitrile CH 3CH––CHCN; B = but-2-enal (crotonaldehyde) CH3CH––CHCHO. CH 3CH––CHCN 1. DIBAL–H−−−−−−−−→2. H2O CH3CH––CHCHO; also 2 CH3CHO dil· NaOH−−−−−−→CH3CH(OH)CH2CHO ∆−−−−→–H 2O CH3CH––CHCHO.
