How can acetaldehyde be prepared from acetyl chloride?

CBSE 202556/5/12 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(a) How can acetaldehyde be prepared from acetyl chloride? 1 (b) Propanal is more reactive than propanone towards nucleophilic addition reaction. Give reason. 1
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Rosenmund reduction: CH 3COCl + H2 Pd–BaSO 4 −−−−−−−→CH3CHO + HCl. (b) Propanone has two alkyl groups: greater +I effect lowers the electrophilicity of the carbonyl carbon and greater steric hindrance to nucleophile.
