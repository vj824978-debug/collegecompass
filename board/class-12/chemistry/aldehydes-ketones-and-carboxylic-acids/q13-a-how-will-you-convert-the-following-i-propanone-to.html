(a) How will you convert the following: (i) Propanone to Propene (ii) Benzoic acid to Benzaldehyde (iii)…

Question 13 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(A) (a) How will you convert the following: (i) Propanone to Propene (ii) Benzoic acid to Benzaldehyde (iii) Benzene to m-Nitroacetophenone (b) (i) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their reactivity towards HCN: (ii) Identify the compounds, which would undergo Aldol condensation: HCHO, C6H5CHO, CH3CHO, OR (B) (a) Give chemical tests to distinguish between the following pairs of compounds: (i) Acetophenone and Benzophenone (ii) Propanal and Propanone (iii) Pentan–2–one and Pentan–3–one (b) (i) Which of the following acids is stronger and why? CH2FCOOH, CH3COOH (ii) Arrange the following compounds in the increasing order of their boiling points: CH3OCH3, CH3CH2CH3, CH3CH2OH, CH3CHO
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(a)(i) Propanone NaBH4 −−−−→propan-2-ol conc. H 2SO4, ∆ −−−−−−−−−−→propene. (ii) Benzoic acid SOCl2 −−−−→benzoyl chloride H2, Pd−BaSO4 − −−−−−−−−− →benzaldehyde. (iii) Benzene CH3COCl/anhyd. AlCl 3 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−−→acetophenone conc. HNO 3+H2SO4 −−−−−−−−−−−−−→ m-nitroacetophenone (COCH3 is meta-directing). (b)(i) ((CH3)3C)2C––O < (CH3)2C––O < CH3CHO (steric and +I effects). (ii) CH3CHO and cyclohexanone (have α-H); HCHO and C 6H5CHO do not. (B)(a)(i) Iodoform test: acetophenone (C 6H5COCH3) gives yellow CHI3, benzophenone does not. (ii) Tollens’ test: propanal gives silver mirror, propanone does not (or iodoform: propanone positive). (iii) Iodoform test: pentan-2-one (methyl ketone) gives yellow CHI 3, pentan-3-one does not. (b)(i) CH 2FCOOH is stronger: −I effect of F stabilises the carboxylate anion. (ii) CH 3CH2CH3 < CH3OCH3 < CH3CHO < CH3CH2OH.
