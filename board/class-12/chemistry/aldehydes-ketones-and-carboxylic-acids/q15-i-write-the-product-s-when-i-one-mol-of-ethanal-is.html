Question 15 · Class 12 Chemistry · Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
Question as text
(a) (i) Write the product(s) when: (I) One mol of ethanal is treated with 1 mol of CH 3OH in the presence of dry HCl gas. (II) Benzaldehyde is treated with conc. NaOH. (III) Ethanoic acid is heated in the presence of P 2O5. (ii) Write a simple chemical test to distinguish between Ethanal and Propanal. (iii) Write the name of the reagent to transform Allyl alcohol to Propenal. [3+1+1=5] OR (b) (i) Draw the structure of the semicarbazone of acetone. (ii) Why are α-hydrogen atoms of aldehydes and ketones acidic in nature? (iii) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their reactivity towards HCN: CH3COCH3, CH3CHO, (CH3)3C–CO–CH 3 (iv) Write the reaction involved in Etard reaction. (v) Write the product when reacts with Zn(Hg)/conc. HCl. [5 ×1=5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) (I) Hemiacetal CH 3CH(OH)OCH3 (1-methoxyethanol); with excess CH3OH the acetal CH 3CH(OCH3)2. (II) Cannizzaro: C6H5CH2OH + C6H5COONa. (III) Ethanoic anhydride (CH 3CO)2O (+ H2O absorbed by P 2O5). (ii) Iodoform test (NaOH + I2, warm): ethanal gives yellow precipitate of CHI3; propanal does not. (iii) PCC (pyridinium chlorochromate). (b)(i) (CH3)2C––N–NH–CO–NH 2. (ii) Strong electron-withdrawing effect of the C=O group, and the conjugate base (enolate) is resonance stabilised. (iii) (CH 3)3C–CO–CH 3 < CH3COCH3 < CH3CHO (steric and +I effects). (iv) C6H5CH3 CrO2Cl2 −−−−−→CS2 C6H5CH(OCrOHCl2)2 H3O+ −−−−→C6H5CHO (chromyl chloride oxidation of toluene to benzaldehyde). (v) Clemmensen reduction: cyclohexanecarbaldehyde→ methylcyclohexane C6H11CH3.
