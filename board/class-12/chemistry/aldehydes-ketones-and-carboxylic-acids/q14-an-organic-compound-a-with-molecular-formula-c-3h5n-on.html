Question as text
An organic compound (A) with molecular formula C 3H5N on reaction with C 6H5MgBr followed by hydrolysis, gives a compound (B). Compound (B) forms an orange-red precipitate with 2,4-DNP reagent and does not give iodoform test. It neither reduces Tollens’ or Fehling’s reagent nor does it decolourise bromine water. On drastic oxidation with chromic acid it gives a carboxylic acid (C) having molecular formula C 7H6O2. Identify the compounds (A), (B) and (C). Write the reactions of compound (A) with C 6H5MgBr followed by hydrolysis to give compound (B).
Answer
Answer as text
A = CH 3CH2CN (propanenitrile); B = C 6H5COCH2CH3 (propiophenone, 1-phenylpropan-1-one: ketone, gives 2,4-DNP test, not a methyl ketone so no iodoform); C = C 6H5COOH (benzoic acid). CH3CH2C–––N + C6H5MgBr−−→CH3CH2C(C6H5)––NMgBr H3O+ −−−−→C6H5COCH2CH3 + NH3 + Mg(OH)Br.
