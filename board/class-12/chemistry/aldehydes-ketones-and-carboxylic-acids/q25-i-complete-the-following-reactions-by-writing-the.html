Question as text
(a) (i) Complete the following reactions by writing the structure of the main products : (I) (1) (II) (CH3)2Cd + 2 CH3COCl−−→ (1) (III) (1) (ii) Give simple chemical test to distinguish between the following pairs of compounds : (I) Ethyl benzoate and benzoic acid (1) (II) Propanal and propanone (1) OR (b) (i) Complete each synthesis by giving missing starting material, reagent or products : (I) (1) (II) (1) (III) (1) (ii) Carry out the following conversions : (I) Benzaldehyde to Benzophenone (1) (II) Benzaldehyde to 3-phenyl propanol (1)
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i)(I) Cyclohexanone semicarbazone, C 6H10 ––N–NH–CO–NH 2. (II) 2 CH3COCH3 + CdCl2 (propanone). (III) C 6H5CHO (benzaldehyde, Rosenmund reduction). (ii)(I) NaHCO 3: benzoic acid gives brisk effervescence of CO2, ethyl benzoate does not. (II) Tollens’ reagent: propanal gives silver mirror, propanone does not (or iodoform test: propanone gives yellow CHI3). (b)(i)(I) C6H5COOH (benzoic acid; side-chain oxidation). (II) (i) B 2H6, (ii) H2O2/OH– (gives cyclohexylmethanol), (iii) PCC. (III) Only CHO is oxidised: 4-oxocyclohexane-1-carboxylate (4-oxocyclohexanecarboxylic acid after acidification) + Ag mirror. (ii)(I) C6H5CHO C6H5MgBr −−−−−−−→ H3O+ (C6H5)2CHOH PCC or CrO3 −−−−−−−−→(C6H5)2CO. (II) C6H5CHO + CH3CHO dil· NaOH−−−−−−→∆ C6H5CH––CHCHO H2/Ni − −−− →C6H5CH2CH2CH2OH.
