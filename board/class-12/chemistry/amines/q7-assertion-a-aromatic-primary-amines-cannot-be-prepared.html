Assertion (A): Aromatic primary amines cannot be prepared by Gabriel phthalimide synthesis.

CBSE 202656/3/1
Question 7 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
Assertion (A): Aromatic primary amines cannot be prepared by Gabriel phthalimide synthesis. Reason (R): Gabriel phthalimide synthesis is used for the preparation of primary aliphatic amines. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both true; A holds because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with the phthalimide anion, which R does not state.
