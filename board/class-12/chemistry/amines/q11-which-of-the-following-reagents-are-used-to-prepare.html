Which of the following reagents are used to prepare primary amines by Hoffmann bromamide degradation reaction?

CBSE 202656/5/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
Which of the following reagents are used to prepare primary amines by Hoffmann bromamide degradation reaction? (i) R–CO–NH 2 (ii) NaOH (iii) Br 2 (iv) CHCl3 (A) (i), (ii) and (iv) (B) (i) and (iii) (C) (i), (ii) and (iii) (D) (i), (iii) and (iv)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) RCONH 2 + Br2 + 4 NaOH−−→RNH2 + Na2CO3 + 2 NaBr + 2 H2O.
