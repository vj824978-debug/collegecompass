Which of the following would not be a good choice for reducing nitrobenzene to aniline?

CBSE 2023
Question 27 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
Which of the following would not be a good choice for reducing nitrobenzene to aniline? (A) LiAlH 4 (B) H 2/Ni (C) Fe and HCl (D) Sn and HCl
Answer
Answer as text
(A) LiAlH 4 reduces nitroarenes to azo compounds (azobenzene), not aniline.
