Question as text
A compound ‘X’ with molecular formula C 3H9N reacts with C 6H5SO2Cl to give a solid, insoluble in alkali. Identify ‘X’ and give the IUPAC name of the product. Write the reaction involved.
Answer
Answer as text
Alkali-insoluble sulphonamide⇒ 2◦ amine: X = CH 3NHCH2CH3 (N-methylethanamine). C 6H5SO2Cl + CH3NHC2H5−−→C6H5SO2N(CH3)C2H5 + HCl; product N-ethyl-N-methylbenzenesulphonamide.
