CH3CONH2 on reaction with NaOH and Br 2 in alcoholic medium gives (A) CH 3CH2NH2 (B) CH 3CH2Br (C) CH 3NH2 (D) CH 3COONa
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Hofmann bromamide degradation: CH 3CONH2 + Br2 + 4 NaOH−−→CH3NH2 + Na2CO3 + 2 NaBr + 2 H2O.
