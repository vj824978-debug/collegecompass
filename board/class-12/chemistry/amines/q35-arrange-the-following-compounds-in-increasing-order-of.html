Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their boiling point: (CH3)2NH, CH3CH2NH2, CH3CH2OH.

CBSE 202556/1/13 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
(a) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their boiling point: (CH3)2NH, CH3CH2NH2, CH3CH2OH. (b) Give plausible explanation for each of the following: (i) Aromatic primary amines cannot be prepared by Gabriel Phthalimide synthesis. (ii) Amides are less basic than amines. [1+2=3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (CH3)2NH < CH3CH2NH2 < CH3CH2OH (H-bonding increases). (b)(i) Aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with the phthalimide anion. (ii) In amides the N lone pair is delocalised onto the C=O group by resonance (and C=O is electron withdrawing), so it is less available for protonation.
