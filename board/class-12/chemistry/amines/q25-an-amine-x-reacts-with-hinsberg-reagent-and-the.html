Question as text
An amine ‘X’ reacts with Hinsberg reagent and the product obtained is soluble in alkali. The amine ‘X’ is: (A) CH 3 –NH 2 (B) (CH 3)2NH (C) (CH 3)3N (D)
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Primary amine gives N-alkylsulphonamide with acidic N–H, soluble in alkali.
