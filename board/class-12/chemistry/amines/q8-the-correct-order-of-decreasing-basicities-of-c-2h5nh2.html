Question as text
The correct order of decreasing basicities of C 2H5NH2, (C2H5)2NH and (C2H5)3N in aqueous solution is : (A) (C 2H5)3N > (C2H5)2NH > C2H5NH2 (B) (C 2H5)2NH > C2H5NH2 > (C2H5)3N (C) C 2H5NH2 > (C2H5)2NH > (C2H5)3N (D) (C 2H5)2NH > (C2H5)3N > C2H5NH2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Ethyl series in water: 2 ◦> 3◦> 1◦ (inductive, solvation and steric effects).
