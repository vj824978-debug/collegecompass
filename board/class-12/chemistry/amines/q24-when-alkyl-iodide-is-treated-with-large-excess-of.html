When alkyl iodide is treated with large excess of ammonia, the major product obtained is: (A) Tertiary amine (B) Quaternary ammonium salt (C) Secondary amine (D) Primary amine
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Large excess of NH 3 minimises further alkylation: RNH 2 major.
