Illustrate Sandmeyer’s reaction with an equation.

CBSE 20233 marks
Question 44 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
(a) Illustrate Sandmeyer’s reaction with an equation. (b) Explain, why (CH 3)2NH is more basic than (CH 3)3N in aqueous solution.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) C6H5N2+Cl– CuCl/HCl −−−−−−→C6H5Cl + N2 (with CuBr/HBr: C 6H5Br; CuCN/KCN: C6H5CN). (b) In water basicity depends on +I effect, H-bond solvation of the ammonium ion and steric hindrance; (CH3)2NH2+ has two N–H for H-bonding with water while (CH 3)3NH+ has only one and is more crowded, so the 2 ◦ amine’s cation is better stabilised.
