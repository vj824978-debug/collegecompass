Assertion (A): Diazonium salts of aromatic amines are more stable than those of aliphatic amines.

CBSE 202656/4/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
Assertion (A): Diazonium salts of aromatic amines are more stable than those of aliphatic amines. Reason (R): Diazonium salts of aliphatic amines undergo resonance. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A true (arenediazonium ion is resonance-stabilised); R false (aliphatic diazonium ions have no resonance).
