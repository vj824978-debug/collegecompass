Arrange the following in increasing order of boiling points.

CBSE 20191 mark
Question 46 · Class 12 Chemistry · Amines
Question as text
Arrange the following in increasing order of boiling points. (CH3)3N, C2H5OH, C2H5NH2
Answer
Answer as text
(CH3)3N < C2H5NH2 < C2H5OH (no H-bonding in 3◦ amine; O–H··· O stronger than N–H··· N).
