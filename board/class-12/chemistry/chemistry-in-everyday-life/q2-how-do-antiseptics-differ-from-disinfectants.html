How do antiseptics differ from disinfectants?

CBSE 20202 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 2 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemistry in Everyday Life
Question as text
How do antiseptics differ from disinfectants? Name a substance which can be used as a disinfectant as well as an antiseptic.
Answer
Answer as text
Antiseptics are applied to living tissues (wounds, skin) to kill/prevent growth of microbes; disinfectants are applied to inanimate objects (floors, drains) and are harmful to living tissue. Phenol: 0.2% solution is an antiseptic, 1% is a disinfectant (also SO 2 in very low concentration).
