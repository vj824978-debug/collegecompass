(i) What type of drug is used in sleeping pills?

CBSE 20193 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 3 · Class 12 Chemistry · Chemistry in Everyday Life
Question as text
(a) (i) What type of drug is used in sleeping pills? (ii) What type of detergents are used in toothpastes? (iii) Why the use of alitame as artificial sweetener is not recommended? OR (b) Define the following terms with suitable example in each: (i) Broad-spectrum antibiotics (ii) Disinfectants (iii) Cationic detergents
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Tranquilizers (hypnotic barbiturates, e.g. veronal). (ii) Anionic detergents (e.g. sodium lauryl sulphate). (iii) Alitame is very high-potency (about 2000 times sucrose), so sweetness of food is difficult to control. (b) (i) Antibiotics effective against a wide range of Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria, e.g. chloramphenicol, ampicillin. (ii) Chemicals that kill microorganisms but are applied to inanimate objects (floors, instruments), e.g. 1% phenol, 0.2–0.4 ppm Cl 2. (iii) Quaternary ammonium salts of amines with acetates/chlorides/bromides (cationic head), e.g. cetyltrimethylammonium bromide.
