What is the role of activated charcoal in gas mask?

CBSE 20193 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 4 · Class 12 Chemistry · Surface Chemistry
Question as text
(i) What is the role of activated charcoal in gas mask? (ii) A colloidal sol is prepared by the given method in figure. What is the charge on hydrated ferric oxide colloidal particles formed in the test tube? How is the sol represented? (iii) How does chemisorption vary with temperature?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) It adsorbs poisonous gases, purifying the air. (ii) FeCl 3 added to excess NaOH: particles adsorb OH – , so negatively charged sol, Fe2O3· xH2O/OH– . (iii) Chemisorption first increases with temperature (activation energy needed), then decreases.
