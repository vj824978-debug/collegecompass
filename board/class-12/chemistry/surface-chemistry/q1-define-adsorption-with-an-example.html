Define adsorption with an example.

CBSE 20202 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 1 · Class 12 Chemistry · Surface Chemistry
Question as text
(a) Define adsorption with an example. What is the role of adsorption in heterogeneous catalysis? OR (b) Define Brownian movement. What is the cause of Brownian movement in colloidal particles? How is it responsible for the stability of colloidal sol?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Adsorption: accumulation of a substance at a surface rather than in the bulk (e.g. gases on charcoal, dyes on charcoal). Reactants are adsorbed on the catalyst surface, their concentration increases, bonds weaken and Ea is lowered; products desorb, freeing the surface (e.g. Fe in Haber process). (b) Brownian movement: continuous zig-zag motion of colloidal particles; caused by unequal bombardment by molecules of the dispersion medium; it keeps particles stirred and opposes settling, so it stabilises the sol.
