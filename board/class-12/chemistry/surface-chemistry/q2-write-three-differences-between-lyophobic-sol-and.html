Write three differences between lyophobic sol and lyophilic sol.

CBSE 20203 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 2 · Class 12 Chemistry · Surface Chemistry
Question as text
(a) Write three differences between lyophobic sol and lyophilic sol. OR (b) Define the following terms: (i) Protective colloid (ii) Zeta potential (iii) Emulsifying agent
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Lyophobic vs lyophilic: little vs strong affinity for the medium; prepared by special methods vs by direct mixing; irreversible vs reversible; easily coagulated by small amounts of electrolyte (less stable) vs quite stable; little solvation vs highly solvated; viscosity similar to medium vs higher. (b) (i) Protective colloid: lyophilic colloid (gelatin, gum arabic) that protects a lyophobic sol from coagulation by electrolytes. (ii) Zeta (electrokinetic) potential: potential difference between the fixed layer and the diffuse layer of opposite charge around a colloidal particle. (iii) Emulsifying agent: substance that stabilises an emulsion by forming an interfacial film (soaps/detergents, proteins, gums).
