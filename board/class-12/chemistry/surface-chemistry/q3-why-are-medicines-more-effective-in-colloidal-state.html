Why are medicines more effective in colloidal state?

CBSE 20191 markOut of current syllabus
Question 3 · Class 12 Chemistry · Surface Chemistry
Question as text
(a) Why are medicines more effective in colloidal state? OR (b) What is the difference between an emulsion and a gel?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Large surface area of colloidal particles, so they are easily absorbed/assimilated. (b) Emulsion: liquid dispersed in liquid; gel: liquid dispersed in solid.
