Standard electrode potential for Sn 4+/Sn2+ couple is +0 .15 V and that for the Cr 3+/Cr couple is −0.74 V.

CBSE 202556/1/1
Question 5 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Standard electrode potential for Sn 4+/Sn2+ couple is +0 .15 V and that for the Cr 3+/Cr couple is −0.74 V. The two couples in their standard states are connected to make a cell. The cell potential will be (A) +1 .19 V (B) +0 .89 V (C) +0 .18 V (D) +1 .83 V
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Cr anode, Sn 4+/Sn2+ cathode: E◦ cell = 0.15− (−0.74) = +0.89 V.
