Define fuel cell. Give two advantages of fuel cell over ordinary cell.

CBSE 202556/4/12 marks
Question 36 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Define fuel cell. Give two advantages of fuel cell over ordinary cell.
Answer
Answer as text
Fuel cell: a galvanic cell that converts the energy of combustion of fuels (H2, CH4, CH3OH) directly into electrical energy, reactants being supplied continuously. Advantages (any two): high efficiency (∼70%); pollution free; continuous supply of energy (does not run down).
