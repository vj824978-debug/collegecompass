In an electrochemical cell, the following reaction takes place : 2 Cu+(aq) + Zn(s)−−→2 Cu(s) + Zn2+(aq) E◦ cell = 1·28 V As the reaction progresses, what will happen to the overall voltage of the cell? (A) Voltage will remain constant. (B) It will decrease as [Zn 2+] increases. (C) It will increase as [Cu +] increases. (D) It will increase as [Zn 2+] increases.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) E =E◦− 0.059 2 log [Zn2+] [Cu+]2 : as [Zn 2+] rises (and [Cu +] falls), E decreases.
