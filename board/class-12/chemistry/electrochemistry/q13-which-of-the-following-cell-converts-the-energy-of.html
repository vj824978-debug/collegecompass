Which of the following cell converts the energy of combustion of fuel into electrical energy?

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 13 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Which of the following cell converts the energy of combustion of fuel into electrical energy? (A) Mercury cell (B) Fuel cell (C) Dry cell (D) Lead storage cell
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Fuel cell (e.g. H 2–O2) converts the energy of combustion directly into electrical energy.
