Question as text
(a) (i) What should be the signs (positive/negative) for E◦ Cell and ∆G◦ for a spontaneous redox reaction occurring under standard conditions? (ii) State Faraday’s first law of electrolysis. OR (b) Calculate the emf of the following cell at 298 K: Fe(s)| Fe2+ (0.01 M)|| H+ (1 M)| H2(g) (1 bar), Pt(s) GivenE◦ Cell = 0.44 V.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) E◦ cell positive, ∆G◦ negative (∆G◦ =−nFE◦ cell). (ii) The amount of chemical reaction (mass of substance deposited or liberated) at an electrode is directly proportional to the quantity of electricity passed: w =ZIt . (b) Fe + 2 H+−−→Fe2+ + H2, n = 2; E = 0.44− 0.0591 2 log [Fe2+] [H+]2 = 0.44− 0.02955 log(0.01) = 0.44 + 0.059≈ 0.50 V (0.499 V).
