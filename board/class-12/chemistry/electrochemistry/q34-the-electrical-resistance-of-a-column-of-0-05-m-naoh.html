The electrical resistance of a column of 0.05 M NaOH solution of area 0.8 cm 2 and length 40 cm is 5× 103 ohm. Calculate its resistivity, conductivity and molar conductivity.
Answer
Answer as text
ρ =RA/l = 5000× 0.8/40 = 100 Ω cm;κ = 1/ρ = 0.01 S cm−1; Λm = 1000κ/M = 1000× 0.01/0.05 = 200 S cm2 mol−1.
