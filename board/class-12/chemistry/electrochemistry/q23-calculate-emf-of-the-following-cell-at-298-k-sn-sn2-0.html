Calculate emf of the following cell at 298 K: Sn| Sn2+ (0.001 M)∥ H+ (0.01 M)| H2(g) (1 bar)| Pt(s) Given: E◦ Sn2+/Sn =−0.14 V, E◦ H+/H2 = 0.00 V [log 10 = 1]
Answer
Answer as text
Sn + 2 H+−−→Sn2+ + H2, n = 2, E◦ cell = 0.14 V; E = 0.14− 0.059 2 log [Sn2+] [H+]2 = 0.14− 0.0295 log 10−3 10−4 = 0.14− 0.0295≈ 0.11 V (0.1105 V).
