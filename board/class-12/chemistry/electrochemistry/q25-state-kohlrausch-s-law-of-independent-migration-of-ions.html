State Kohlrausch’s law of independent migration of ions.

CBSE 202656/3/13 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
State Kohlrausch’s law of independent migration of ions. With the help of a curve, explain why it is not easy to determine Λ◦ m for weak electrolytes by extrapolating the concentration – molar conductivity curve, as it is for strong electrolytes.
Answer
Answer as text
Kohlrausch: limiting molar conductivity of an electrolyte is the sum of the individual contributions of its anion and cation, Λ◦ m =ν+λ◦ + +ν−λ◦ −. Curve: strong electrolyte Λ m vs√c is a nearly straight line (Λm = Λ◦ m−A√c), extrapolated to c = 0. For a weak electrolyte (e.g. CH3COOH) Λm rises very steeply at low concentration because α increases sharply on dilution; the curve is not linear, so it cannot be extrapolated to zero concentration.
