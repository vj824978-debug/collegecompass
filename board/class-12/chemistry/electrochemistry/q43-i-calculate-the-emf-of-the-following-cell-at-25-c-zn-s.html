Question as text
(a) (i) Calculate the emf of the following cell at 25 ◦C: Zn(s)| Zn2+ (0.1 M)|| H+ (0.01 M)| H2(g) (1 bar), Pt(s) [Given: E◦ Zn2+/Zn =−0.76 V,E◦ 2 H+/H2 = 0.00 V, log 10 = 1] 3 (ii) State Faraday’s second law of electrolysis. How much electricity is required in terms of Faraday for the reduction of 1 mol of Cr 2O72– to Cr3+? 2 OR (b) Answer the following questions: (i) The conductivity of 0.20 M solution of KCl is 2.48× 10−2 S cm−1. Calculate its molar conductivity and degree of dissociation (α). [Given: λ◦ (K+) = 73.5 S cm2 mol−1, λ◦ (Cl−) = 76.5 S cm2 mol−1] (ii) Calculate ∆rG◦ of the following cell: Mg(s) + Cu2+(aq)−−→Mg2+(aq) + Cu(s) [Given: E◦ Mg2+/Mg =−2.37 V,E◦ Cu2+/Cu = +0.34 V, 1 F = 96500 C mol−1] (iii) What type of cell is mercury cell? Why is it more advantageous than dry cell? 2+2+1=5
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Zn + 2 H+−−→Zn2+ + H2, n = 2, E◦ = 0.76 V; E = 0.76− 0.059 2 log 0.1 (0.01)2 = 0.76− 0.0295× 3 = 0.6715≈ 0.67 V. (ii) When the same quantity of electricity passes through different electrolytes, masses of substances liberated are proportional to their chemical equivalent weights. Cr 2O72– + 14 H+ + 6 e–−−→2 Cr3+ + 7 H2O: 6 F. (b)(i) Λm = 1000κ M = 1000× 2.48× 10−2 0.20 = 124 S cm2 mol−1; Λ◦ m = 73.5 + 76.5 = 150; α = 124/150 = 0.827. (ii) E◦ cell = 0.34− (−2.37) = 2.71 V; ∆rG◦ =−nFE◦ =−2× 96500× 2.71 = −523030 J mol−1≈− 523.03 kJ mol−1. (iii) Primary cell; it gives a constant voltage (≈ 1.35 V) throughout its life because the overall reaction (Zn(Hg) + HgO−−→ZnO + Hg) involves no ions whose concentration changes.
