Question as text
On electrolysis of very dilute aqueous solution of NaCl using platinum electrodes: (A) H 2 gas is evolved at anode. (B) Na is produced at cathode. (C) O 2 gas is evolved at anode. (D) H 2 gas is evolved at cathode.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) or (D) very dilute NaCl: at anode 2 H 2O−−→O2 + 4 H+ + 4 e– (O2, not Cl 2); at cathode H 2 is also evolved, so (D) is also true. Accept (C) or (D).
