Match the type of cell given in Column I with their use given in Column II.

CBSE 202556/5/1
Question 11 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Match the type of cell given in Column I with their use given in Column II. Column I Column II i. Lead storage cell a. Wall clock ii. Mercury cell b. Apollo Space Programme iii. Dry cell c. Wrist watch iv. Fuel cell d. Inverter (A) i-a, ii-b, iii-c, iv-d (B) i-d, ii-c, iii-a, iv-b (C) i-c, ii-d, iii-b, iv-a (D) i-b, ii-a, iii-d, iv-c
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Lead storage–inverter, mercury cell–wrist watch, dry cell–wall clock, fuel cell–Apollo programme.
