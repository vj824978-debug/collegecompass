Question as text
What amount of electric charge is required for the reduction of 1 mole of MnO 4 – into Mn2+? (A) 1F (B) 5F (C) 4F (D) 6F
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Mn: +7 → +2, 5 electrons per ion, so 5 F .
