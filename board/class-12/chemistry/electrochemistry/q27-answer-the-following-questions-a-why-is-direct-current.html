Question as text
Answer the following questions : (a) Why is direct current (DC) not used to measure the resistance of an ionic solution? (b) Why are the products of electrolysis different for the electrolysis of aqueous solution of AgNO 3 with silver electrodes and electrolysis of aqueous solution of AgNO 3 with platinum electrodes? (c) Why are magnesium blocks fixed to the iron pipelines carrying water?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) DC electrolyses the solution, changing its composition (and polarises the electrodes), so AC is used. (b) Ag electrodes are attackable: anode Ag −−→Ag+ + e– , cathode Ag+ + e–−−→Ag. Pt is inert: cathode Ag + + e–−−→Ag, but at the anode water is oxidised, 2 H2O−−→O2 + 4 H+ + 4 e– (NO3 – not oxidised). (c) Mg is more electropositive and corrodes preferentially (sacrificial anode), giving cathodic protection to iron.
