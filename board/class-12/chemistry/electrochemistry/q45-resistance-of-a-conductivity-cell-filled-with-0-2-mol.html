Resistance of a conductivity cell filled with 0.2 mol L−1 KCl solution is 200 Ω. If the resistance of the same cell when filled with 0.05 mol L−1 KCl solution is 620, calculate the conductivity and molar conductivity of 0.05 mol L−1 KCl solution. The conductivity of 0.2 mol L−1 KCl solution is 0.0248 S cm−1.
Answer
Answer as text
Cell constant G∗ =κR = 0.0248× 200 = 4.96 cm−1; κ(0.05M) = 4.96/620 = 8.0× 10−3 S cm−1; Λm = 1000κ c = 1000× 0.008 0.05 = 160 S cm2 mol−1.
