Electrochemistry — Question 38 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 202556/5/12 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Concentration of KCl Conductivity at Molar Conductivity at solution in mol/L 298.15 K in S cm −1 298.15 K in S cm2 mol−1 1.000 0.1113 111.3 0.100 0.0129 129.0 0.010 0.00141 141.0 Based on the data given above, give plausible reason for the variation of conductivity and molar conductivity with concentration.
Answer
Answer as text
Conductivityκ decreases on dilution because the number of ions per unit volume decreases. Molar conductivity Λ m =κ/c increases on dilution because the volume containing one mole of electrolyte increases and interionic attractions decrease (ions move more freely).
