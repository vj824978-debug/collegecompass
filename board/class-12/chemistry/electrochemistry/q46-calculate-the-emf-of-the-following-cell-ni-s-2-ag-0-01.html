Electrochemistry — Question 46 (CBSE 2024, 3 marks)

CBSE 20243 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Calculate the emf of the following cell: Ni(s) + 2 Ag+ (0.01 M)−−→Ni2+ (0.1 M) + 2 Ag(s) Given that E◦ cell = 1.05 V, log 10 = 1
Answer
Answer as text
n = 2; E = 1.05− 0.059 2 log [Ni2+] [Ag+]2 = 1.05− 0.0295 log 0.1 (0.01)2 = 1.05− 0.0295× 3≈ 0.96 V (0.9615 V).
