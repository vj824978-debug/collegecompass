Give reasons for the following : (a) The pH of aqueous NaCl increases when it is electrolysed.

CBSE 202556/6/13 marks
Question 40 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Give reasons for the following : (a) The pH of aqueous NaCl increases when it is electrolysed. (b) Unlike dry cell, mercury cell has a constant cell potential through its lifetime. (c) Conductivity of solution decreases with dilution.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) At cathode 2 H2O + 2 e–−−→H2 + 2 OH– , at anode Cl 2 is evolved; NaOH accumulates, so pH rises. (b) Overall reaction Zn(Hg) + HgO−−→ZnO + Hg involves no ions in solution whose concentration changes, so Ecell (≈ 1.35 V) stays constant. (c) On dilution the number of ions per unit volume decreases.
