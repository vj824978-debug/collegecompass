Calculate the cell voltage of the voltaic cell which is set up by joining following half-cells at 25 ◦C : Al/Al3+ (0.001 M) and Ni/Ni2+ (0.1 M) Given : E◦ Ni2+/Ni =−0.25 V, E◦ Al3+/Al =−1.66 V
Answer
Answer as text
2 Al + 3 Ni2+−−→2 Al3+ + 3 Ni,n = 6;E◦ cell =−0.25− (−1.66) = 1.41 V. E = 1.41− 0.059 6 log (10−3)2 (10−1)3 = 1.41+ 0.059× 3 6 = 1.41+0.0295≈ 1.44 V.
