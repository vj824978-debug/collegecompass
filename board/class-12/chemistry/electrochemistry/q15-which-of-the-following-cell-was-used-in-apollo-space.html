Which of the following cell was used in Apollo space programme?

CBSE 2024
Question 15 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Which of the following cell was used in Apollo space programme? (A) Mercury cell (B) H 2–O2 fuel cell (C) Dry cell (D) Ni-Cd cell
Answer
Answer as text
(B) H 2–O2 fuel cells powered the Apollo missions (and gave drinking water).
