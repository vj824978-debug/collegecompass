Question as text
(a) State the following: (i) Kohlrausch law of independent migration of ions and (ii) Faraday’s first law of electrolysis. (b) Using E◦ values of X and Y given below, predict which is better for coating the surface of iron to prevent corrosion and why? GivenE◦ X2+/X =−2.36 V, E◦ Y2+/Y =−0.14 V, E◦ Fe2+/Fe =−0.44 V
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Limiting molar conductivity of an electrolyte is the sum of the limiting ionic conductivities of its cation and anion: Λ◦ m =ν+λ◦ + +ν−λ◦ −. (ii) Mass of substance deposited/liberated at an electrode is proportional to the quantity of electricity passed: w =ZIt . (b) X (E◦ =−2.36 V, more negative than Fe) is better: it is more easily oxidised, so it corrodes sacrificially and protects iron even if the coating breaks; Y (−0.14 V) is less reactive than Fe, so a scratch would make Fe the anode and accelerate its rusting.
