Dilution affects both conductivity as well as molar conductivity.

CBSE 2024
Question 16 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Dilution affects both conductivity as well as molar conductivity. Effect of dilution on both is as follows: (A) both increase with dilution. (B) both decrease with dilution. (C) conductivity increases whereas molar conductivity decreases on dilution. (D) conductivity decreases whereas molar conductivity increases on dilution.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Ions per unit volume fall, so κ decreases; Λm =κ/c rises (more dissociation/mobility).
