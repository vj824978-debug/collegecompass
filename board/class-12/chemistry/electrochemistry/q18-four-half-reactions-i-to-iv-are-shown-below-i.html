Electrochemistry — Question 18 (CBSE 2023)

CBSE 2023
Question 18 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
Four half reactions I to IV are shown below: I. 2 Cl–−−→Cl2 + 2 e– II. 4 OH–−−→O2 + 2 H2O + 2 e– III. Na + + e–−−→Na IV. 2 H+ + 2 e–−−→H2 Which two of these reactions are most likely to occur when concentrated brine is electrolysed? (A) I and III (B) I and IV (C) II and III (D) II and IV
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Concentrated brine: Cl – is discharged at the anode (I, overpotential of O2); water/H+ reduced to H 2 at cathode (IV), not Na +.
