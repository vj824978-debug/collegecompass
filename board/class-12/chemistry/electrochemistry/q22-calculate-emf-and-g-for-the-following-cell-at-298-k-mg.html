Electrochemistry — Question 22 (CBSE 2026, 5 marks)

CBSE 202656/1/15 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
(a) Calculate emf and ∆G for the following cell at 298 K: Mg(s)| Mg2+(0.01 M)|| Ag+(0.001 M)| Ag(s) Given: E◦ Mg2+/Mg =−2.37 V, E◦ Ag+/Ag = +0.80 V [1 F = 96500 C mol−1, log 10 = 1] OR (b) For the reaction: 2 AgCl(s) + H2(g) (0.4 atm) −−→2 Ag(s) + 2 H+ (0.1 M) + 2 Cl– (0.2 M) Calculate emf of the cell at 25 ◦C. Given: ∆G◦ =−43500 J mol−1 [log 10 = 1, 1 F = 96500 C mol−1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Mg + 2 Ag+−−→Mg2+ + 2 Ag,n = 2; E◦ cell = 0.80− (−2.37) = 3.17 V. E = 3.17− 0.059 2 log 0.01 (0.001)2 = 3.17− 0.0295× 4 = 3.052 V. ∆G =−nFE =−2× 96500× 3.052≈− 5.89× 105 J mol−1 (−589 kJ mol−1). (b) n = 2; E◦ =−∆G◦/nF = 43500/(2× 96500) = 0.225 V. Q = [H+]2[Cl−]2 pH2 = (0.1)2(0.2)2 0.4 = 10−3. E = 0.225− 0.059 2 log 10−3 = 0.225 + 0.0885≈ 0.314 V.
