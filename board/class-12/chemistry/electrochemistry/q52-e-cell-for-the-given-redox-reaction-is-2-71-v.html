E◦ cell for the given redox reaction is 2.71 V.

CBSE 20195 marks
Question 52 · Class 12 Chemistry · Electrochemistry
Question as text
(a) E◦ cell for the given redox reaction is 2.71 V. Mg + Cu2+ (0.01 M)−−→Mg2+ (0.001 M) + Cu Calculate Ecell for the reaction. Write the direction of flow of current when an external opposite potential applied is (i) Less than 2.71 V (ii) Greater than 2.71 V OR (b) (a) A steady current of 2 amperes was passed through two electrolytic cells X and Y connected in series containing electrolytes FeSO4 and ZnSO4 until 2.8 g of Fe deposited at the cathode of cell X. How long did the current flow? Calculate the mass of Zn deposited at the cathode of cell Y. (Molar mass: Fe = 56 g mol−1, Zn = 65.3 g mol−1, 1 F = 96500 C mol−1) (b) In the plot of molar conductivity (Λ m) vs. square root of concentration ( C1/2), following curve obtained for two electrolytes A and B: Answer the following: (i) Predict the nature of electrolytes A and B. (ii) What happens on extrapolation of Λ m to concentration approaching zero for electrolytes A and B?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) n = 2; Ecell = 2.71− 0.059 2 log [Mg2+] [Cu2+] = 2.71− 0.0295 log 0.001 0.01 = 2.71 + 0.0295 = 2.7395≈ 2.74 V. (i) External potential < 2.71 V: cell acts as galvanic cell; electrons flow Mg → Cu, current flows from Cu to Mg (cathode to anode) in the external circuit. (ii) External potential > 2.71 V: cell reaction reverses (electrolytic cell); current flows from Mg to Cu, i.e. opposite direction (Cu dissolves, Mg deposits). [Strictly the switch-over is at Ecell = 2.74 V for these concentrations.] (b) (a) n(Fe) = 2.8/56 = 0.05 mol;Q = 0.05× 2× 96500 = 9650 C; t = 9650/2 = 4825 s (≈ 80.4 min). Zn: 0.05 mol×65.3 = 3.265 g (≈ 3.27 g). (b) (i) A (straight line) = strong electrolyte; B (steeply rising curve) = weak electrolyte. (ii) For A, extrapolating the line to C→ 0 gives the intercept Λ◦ m; for B, Λm rises steeply at low concentration and cannot be extrapolated, so Λ◦ m is not obtained graphically (use Kohlrausch’s law instead).
