Assertion (A): p-nitrophenol is more acidic than phenol.

CBSE 202656/5/1
Question 10 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
Assertion (A): p-nitrophenol is more acidic than phenol. Reason (R): Nitro group is an electron-withdrawing group, it stabilises phenoxide ion by dispersal of negative charge. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) –NO 2 (−I,−R) disperses the negative charge of the phenoxide ion.
