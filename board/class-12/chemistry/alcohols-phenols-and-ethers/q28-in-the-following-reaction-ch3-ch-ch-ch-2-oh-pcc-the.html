In the following reaction CH3 –CH ––CH–CH 2 –OH PCC−−−→ the product formed is: (A) CH 3 –CHO and CH 3CH2OH (B) CH 3 –CH ––CH–COOH (C) CH 3 –CH ––CH–CHO (D) CH 3 –CH 2 –CH 2 –CHO
Answer
Answer as text
(C) PCC oxidises a 1 ◦ alcohol only to the aldehyde and leaves C=C intact: but-2-enal.
