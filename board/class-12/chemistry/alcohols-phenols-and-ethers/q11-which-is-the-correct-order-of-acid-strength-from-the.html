Question 11 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
Which is the correct order of acid strength from the following? (A) C 6H5OH > H2O > ROH (B) C 6H5OH > ROH> H2O (C) ROH > C6H5OH > H2O (D) H 2O > C6H5OH > ROH
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Phenoxide is resonance stabilised; RO – is destabilised by +I of R, so ROH is weaker than water.
