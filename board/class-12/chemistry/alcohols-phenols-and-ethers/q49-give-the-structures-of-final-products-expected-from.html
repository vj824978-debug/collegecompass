Question 49 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
(a) Give the structures of final products expected from the following reactions : (i) Hydroboration of propene followed by oxidation with H 2O2 in alkaline medium. (ii) Dehydration of (CH 3)3C–OH by heating it with 20% H 3PO4 at 358 K. (iii) Heating of with HI. OR (b) How can you convert the following? (i) Phenol to o-hydroxybenzaldehyde (ii) Methanal to ethanol (iii) Phenol to phenyl ethanoate
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) CH3CH2CH2OH (propan-1-ol, anti-Markovnikov). (ii) (CH3)2C––CH2 (2-methylpropene). (iii) C 6H5CH2I + C6H5OH (benzyl iodide + phenol; aryl–O bond not cleaved). (b)(i) Reimer–Tiemann: phenol + CHCl 3/aq. NaOH, then H +→ salicylaldehyde. (ii) HCHO + CH3MgBr−−→CH3CH2OMgBr H3O+ −−−−→CH3CH2OH. (iii) C 6H5OH + CH3COCl pyridine −−−−−→CH3COOC6H5 + HCl (or acetic anhydride).
