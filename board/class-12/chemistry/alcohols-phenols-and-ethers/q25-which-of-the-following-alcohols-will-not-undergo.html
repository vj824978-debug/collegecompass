Which of the following alcohols will not undergo oxidation?

CBSE 2023
Question 25 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
Which of the following alcohols will not undergo oxidation? (A) Butanol (B) Butan-2-ol (C) 2-Methylbutan-2-ol (D) 3-Methylbuta-2-ol
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 2-Methylbutan-2-ol is a 3 ◦ alcohol (no H on the carbinol carbon).
