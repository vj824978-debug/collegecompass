An organic compound ‘A’, molecular formula C 2H6O oxidises with CrO 3 to form a compound ‘B’.

CBSE 202556/2/15 marks
Question 39 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
(A) An organic compound ‘A’, molecular formula C 2H6O oxidises with CrO 3 to form a compound ‘B’. Compound ‘B’ on warming with iodine and aqueous solution of NaOH gives a yellow precipitate of compound ‘C’. When compound ‘A’ is heated with conc. H 2SO4 at 413 K gives a compound ‘D’, which on reaction with excess HI gives compound ‘E’. Identify compounds ‘A’, ‘B’, ‘C’, ‘D’ and ‘E’ and write chemical equations involved. OR (B) (a) Write chemical equations of the following reactions: 3 (i) Phenol is treated with conc. HNO 3 (ii) Propene is treated with B 2H6 followed by oxidation by H2O2/OH– . (iii) Sodium t-butoxide is treated with CH 3Cl. (b) Give a simple chemical test to distinguish between butan–1–ol and butan–2–ol. 1 (c) Arrange the following in increasing order of acid strength: 1 phenol, ethanol, water
Answer
Answer as text
(A) A = ethanol CH 3CH2OH; B = ethanal CH 3CHO; C = iodoform CHI3; D = ethoxyethane C 2H5OC2H5; E = iodoethane C 2H5I. CH3CH2OH CrO3 −−−→CH3CHO; CH3CHO + 3 I2 + 4 NaOH−−→CHI3↓ + HCOONa + 3 NaI + 3 H2O; 2 C2H5OH conc· H2SO4 −−−−−−−−→413 K C2H5OC2H5 + H2O; C2H5OC2H5 + 2 HI ∆−−→2 C2H5I + H2O. (B)(a)(i) Phenol + conc. HNO 3→ 2,4,6-trinitrophenol (picric acid) + 3 H2O. (ii) 3 CH 3CH––CH2 + 2 B2H6−−→(CH3CH2CH2)3B 3 H2O2 −−−−→OH – 3 CH3CH2CH2OH + B(OH)3 (propan-1-ol). (iii) (CH 3)3CONa + CH3Cl−−→(CH3)3C–O–CH 3 + NaCl (2-methoxy-2-methylpropane). (b) Iodoform test: butan-2-ol (CH3CH(OH)– ) gives a yellow CHI 3 precipitate with I 2/NaOH, butan-1-ol does not (or Lucas test: turbidity in about 5 min only for butan-2-ol). (c) Ethanol < water< phenol.
