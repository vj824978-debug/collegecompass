At low temperature, phenol reacts with Br 2 in CS2 to form: (A) 2,4,6-tribromophenol (B) p-bromophenol (C) o-and p-bromophenol (D) 2,4-dibromophenol
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Low-polarity solvent at low temperature gives monobromophenols (o- and p-, p major).
