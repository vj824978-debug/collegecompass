Question as text
(a) Arrange the following compounds in the increasing order of their acidic strength: 1 3,5-dinitrophenol, 4-methylphenol, phenol, 2,4,6-trinitrophenol (b) What happens when: (write equations) 2 (i) Phenol is distilled with Zn dust? (ii) Anisole is treated with HBr?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 4-methylphenol < phenol < 3,5-dinitrophenol < 2,4,6-trinitrophenol. (b) (i) C 6H5OH + Zn ∆−−→C6H6 + ZnO. (ii) C 6H5OCH3 + HBr−−→C6H5OH + CH3Br.
