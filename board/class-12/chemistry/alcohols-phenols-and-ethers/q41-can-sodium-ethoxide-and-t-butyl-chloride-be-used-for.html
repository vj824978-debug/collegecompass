Can sodium ethoxide and t-butyl chloride be used for the preparation of t-butyl ethyl ether?

CBSE 202556/4/13 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
(a) Can sodium ethoxide and t-butyl chloride be used for the preparation of t-butyl ethyl ether? Give suitable explanation. Justify your answer by suggesting the appropriate starting material required for preparation of t-butyl ethyl ether. (2) (b) Give the IUPAC name of above mentioned ether. (1)
Answer
Answer as text
(a) No: C 2H5O– is a strong base and t-butyl chloride (3◦) undergoes elimination to 2-methylpropene. Use sodium tert-butoxide + ethyl chloride (1◦, SN2): (CH 3)3CONa + C2H5Cl−−→(CH3)3C–O–C 2H5 + NaCl. (b) 2-Ethoxy-2-methylpropane.
