CBSE 20232 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
For the pair phenol and cyclohexanol, answer the following (a) Why is phenol more acidic than cyclohexanol? (b) Give one chemical test to distinguish between the two.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Phenoxide ion is stabilised by resonance (negative charge delocalised over the ring) and the O is on an sp2 carbon; cyclohexoxide has no such stabilisation. (b) Neutral FeCl 3: phenol gives violet colour, cyclohexanol does not (or bromine water: phenol gives white ppt of 2,4,6-tribromophenol).
