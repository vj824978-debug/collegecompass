Question as text
The reaction R–OH + Na −−→RO– Na+ + 1 2 H2(g) suggests that alcohols are: (A) Acidic (B) Basic (C) Neutral (D) Amphoteric
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Alcohols donate a proton to Na, forming alkoxide and H 2.
