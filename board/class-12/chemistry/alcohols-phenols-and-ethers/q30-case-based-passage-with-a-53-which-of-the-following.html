(Case-based, passage with A-53.) Which of the following group increases the acidic character of phenol? (A) CH 3O– (B) CH 3 – (C) NO 2 – (D) All of these
Answer
Answer as text
(C) –NO 2 is electron withdrawing (−I,−R), stabilising phenoxide; CH3O– and CH 3– are electron donating.
