Question as text
Which of the following acids reacts with acetic anhydride to form a compound Aspirin? (A) Benzoic acid (B) Salicylic acid (C) Phthalic acid (D) Acetic acid
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Acetylation of salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid) with (CH3CO)2O gives acetylsalicylic acid.
