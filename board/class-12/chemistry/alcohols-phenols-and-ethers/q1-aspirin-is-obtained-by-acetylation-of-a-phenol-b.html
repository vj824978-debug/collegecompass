Aspirin is obtained by acetylation of (A) Phenol (B) Salicylaldehyde (C) 2-Hydroxybenzoic acid (D) Benzoic acid
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Acetylation of salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid) with acetic anhydride gives aspirin.
