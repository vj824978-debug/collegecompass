Write the reaction involved in the following: (a) Reimer-Tiemann reaction (b) Kolbe’s reaction (c) Friedal-Crafts acylation of anisole
Answer
Answer as text
(a) C6H5OH + CHCl3 + aq· NaOH (340 K)→ intermediate benzal chloride/phenoxide, then H+→ salicylaldehyde (2-hydroxybenzaldehyde). (b) C6H5ONa + CO2 (400 K, 4–7 atm)→ sodium salicylate H+ −−→salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid). (c) Anisole + CH 3COCl anhyd· AlCl3 −−−−−−−−→4-methoxyacetophenone (major) + 2-methoxyacetophenone (minor) + HCl.
