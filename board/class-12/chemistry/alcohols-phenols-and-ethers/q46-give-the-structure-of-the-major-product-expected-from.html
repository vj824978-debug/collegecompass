Answer as text
(a) CH3CHO + CH3MgBr−−→(CH3)2CH–OMgBr H3O+ −−−−→CH3CH(OH)CH3 (propan-2-ol). (b) CH 3CH2CH––CH2 dil· H2SO4 −−−−−−−→CH3CH2CH(OH)CH3 (butan-2-ol, Markovnikov). (c) 2,4,6-tribromophenol (white ppt) + 3HBr.
