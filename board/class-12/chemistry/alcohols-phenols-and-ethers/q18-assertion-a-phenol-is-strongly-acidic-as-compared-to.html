Assertion (A): Phenol is strongly acidic as compared to ethanol.

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 18 · Class 12 Chemistry · Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
Question as text
Assertion (A): Phenol is strongly acidic as compared to ethanol. Reason (R): Phenoxide ion is more stable than ethoxide ion. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Phenoxide is resonance-stabilised (charge delocalised into ring); ethoxide is not.
