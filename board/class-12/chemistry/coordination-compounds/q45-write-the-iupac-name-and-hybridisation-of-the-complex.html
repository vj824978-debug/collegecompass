Write the IUPAC name and hybridisation of the complex [Fe(CN) 6]3– .

CBSE 20202 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) Write the IUPAC name and hybridisation of the complex [Fe(CN) 6]3– . (Given : Atomic number of Fe = 26) (b) What is the difference between an ambidentate ligand and a chelating ligand? 1+1
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Hexacyanidoferrate(III) ion; Fe 3+ 3d5, strong-field CN – pairs electrons (1 unpaired), inner-orbital d2sp3, octahedral. (b) Ambidentate: unidentate ligand with two different donor atoms, binds through either one (NO2 – /ONO– , SCN – /NCS– ). Chelating: di-/polydentate ligand binding through two or more donor atoms simultaneously, forming a ring (en, C 2O42– , EDTA).
