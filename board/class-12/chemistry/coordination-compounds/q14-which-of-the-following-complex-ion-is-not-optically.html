Which of the following complex ion is not optically active?

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 14 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
Which of the following complex ion is not optically active? (A) [Co(ox) 3]3– (B) cis-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ (C) trans-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ (D) [Co(en) 3]3+
Answer
Answer as text
(C) trans-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ has a plane of symmetry, so it is achiral.
