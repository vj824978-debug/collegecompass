Question as text
Out of [CoF 6]3– and [Co(en)3]3+, which one complex is (i) Paramagnetic, (ii) More stable, (iii) Inner orbital complex and (iv) High spin complex (Atomic number of Co = 27)
Answer
Answer as text
Co3+ 3d6. [CoF 6]3– : weak field F – , sp3d2, 4 unpaired e−. [Co(en) 3]3+: strong field chelate, d 2sp3, 0 unpaired. (i) Paramagnetic: [CoF 6]3– ; (ii) more stable: [Co(en) 3]3+ (chelate effect, strong field); (iii) inner orbital: [Co(en)3]3+; (iv) high spin: [CoF 6]3– .
