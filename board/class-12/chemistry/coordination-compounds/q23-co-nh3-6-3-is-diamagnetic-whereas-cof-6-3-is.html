[Co(NH3)6]3+ is diamagnetic whereas [CoF 6]3– is paramagnetic.

CBSE 202656/2/13 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) [Co(NH3)6]3+ is diamagnetic whereas [CoF 6]3– is paramagnetic. Justify the statement. [Atomic number of Co = 27] (b) Write the electronic configuration for d4 ion if ∆ 0>P on the basis of crystal field theory.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Co3+ is 3d6. NH 3 (strong field) pairs electrons: t6 2ge0 g, d2sp3 inner-orbital complex, no unpaired electron ⇒ diamagnetic. F – (weak field): t4 2ge2 g, sp3d2 outer-orbital complex, 4 unpaired electrons ⇒ paramagnetic. (b) ∆ 0>P (strong field, low spin): t4 2ge0 g.
