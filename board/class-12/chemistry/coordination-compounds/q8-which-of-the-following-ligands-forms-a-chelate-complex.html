Which of the following ligands forms a chelate complex?

CBSE 202656/5/1
Question 8 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
Which of the following ligands forms a chelate complex? (A) Ammonia (B) Water (C) NO 2 – (D) Oxalate ion
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Oxalate is a bidentate ligand forming a five-membered chelate ring.
