Question 44 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) On the basic of crystal field theory write the electronic configuration for d5 ion with a strong field ligand for which ∆ 0>P . (b) [Ni(CO)4] has tetrahedral geometry while [Ni(CN)4]2– has square planar yet both exhibit diamagnetism. Explain. [Atomic number : Ni = 28]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) t5 2ge0 g (low spin, one unpaired electron). (b) [Ni(CO)4]: Ni(0) 3d84s2; strong-field CO pushes the 4s electrons into 3d, giving 3d10; sp3 hybridisation⇒ tetrahedral, no unpaired electrons, diamagnetic. [Ni(CN) 4]2– : Ni 2+ 3d8; strong-field CN – pairs the two unpaired electrons, freeing one 3d orbital; dsp2⇒ square planar, diamagnetic.
