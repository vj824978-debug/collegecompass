Question as text
PtCl4· 2 KCl doesn’t give precipitate of AgCl with AgNO3 solution. Write the structural formula and IUPAC name of the complex.
Answer
Answer as text
No free Cl – : complex is K 2[PtCl6] (octahedral [PtCl 6]2– , all 6 Cl bonded to Pt); IUPAC name: potassium hexachloridoplatinate(IV).
