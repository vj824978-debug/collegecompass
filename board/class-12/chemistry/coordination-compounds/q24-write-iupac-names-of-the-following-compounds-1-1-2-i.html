Write IUPAC names of the following compounds: [1+1=2] (i) [PtCl2(en)2]SO4 (ii) (NH4)2[CoF4] OR (b) Define the…

CBSE 202656/3/12 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) Write IUPAC names of the following compounds: [1+1=2] (i) [PtCl2(en)2]SO4 (ii) (NH4)2[CoF4] OR (b) Define the following terms with a suitable example in each case: [1+1=2] (i) Ambidentate ligand (ii) Double salt
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Dichloridobis(ethane-1,2-diamine)platinum(IV) sulphate; (ii) Ammonium tetrafluoridocobaltate(II). (b)(i) Ambidentate ligand: a ligand that can bind through two different donor atoms, e.g. NO 2 – (nitrito-N) / ONO – (nitrito-O), or SCN– /NCS– . (ii) Double salt: an addition compound stable in the solid state that dissociates completely into its simple ions in water, e.g. Mohr’s salt FeSO4· (NH4)2SO4· 6 H2O or carnallite KCl· MgCl2· 6 H2O.
