Define coordination number.

CBSE 202656/2/12 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) Define coordination number. (b) Indicate the type of isomerism exhibited by the following complex: [Co(en)3]Cl3
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Coordination number: number of ligand donor atoms directly bonded (by σ bonds) to the central metal atom/ion. (b) Optical isomerism (d and l enantiomers of the octahedral tris-chelate).
