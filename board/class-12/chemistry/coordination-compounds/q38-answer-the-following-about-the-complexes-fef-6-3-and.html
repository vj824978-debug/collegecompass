CBSE 202556/6/13 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) Answer the following about the complexes [FeF 6]3– and [Fe(CN)6]4– : (i) Write the hybridization involved in each case. (ii) Which of them is the outer orbital complex and which one is the inner orbital complex? (iii) Compare their magnetic behaviour. [Atomic number : Fe = 26] OR (b) (i) What happens to the colour of complex [Ti(H 2O)6]3+ when heated gradually? (ii) Write the electronic configuration for d5 ion if ∆o<P . (iii) Write the hybridization and magnetic behaviour of the complex [Ni(CO) 4]. [Atomic number : Ni = 28]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) [FeF6]3– : Fe3+ 3d5, weak-field F – , sp3d2, outer orbital, 5 unpaired e−, paramagnetic. [Fe(CN) 6]4– : Fe2+ 3d6, strong-field CN – ,d2sp3, inner orbital, no unpaired e−, diamagnetic. (b)(i) On heating it loses water ligands; with no ligand there is no crystal-field splitting, so the violet colour fades and it becomes colourless. (ii) t3 2ge2 g (high spin). (iii) Ni(0) 3 d10 (4s electrons paired into 3d): sp3, tetrahedral, diamagnetic.
