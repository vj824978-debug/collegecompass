Coordination Compounds — Question 18 (CBSE 2020)

CBSE 2020
Question 18 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
The oxidation state of Ni in [Ni(CO) 4] is (A) 0 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4
Answer
Answer as text
(A) CO is a neutral ligand; complex is neutral, so Ni is 0.
