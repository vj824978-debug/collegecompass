The diamagnetic species is: (A) [Ni(CN) 4]2– (B) [NiCl 4]2– (C) [Fe(CN) 6]3– (D) [CoF 6]3– [At. No. Co = 27, Fe = 26, Ni = 28]
Answer
Answer as text
(A) [Ni(CN) 4]2– : Ni 2+ d8, strong-field CN – , dsp2, all paired. (B) 2, (C) 1, (D) 4 unpaired electrons.
