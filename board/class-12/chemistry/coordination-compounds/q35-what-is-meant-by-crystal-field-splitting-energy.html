What is meant by crystal field splitting energy?

CBSE 202556/5/13 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) What is meant by crystal field splitting energy? For ad4 ion, write the configuration if (i) ∆o<P , and (ii) ∆o>P . (b) Explain why in tetrahedral coordination entities, low spin configurations are rarely observed.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) CFSE (∆o): energy difference between the eg and t2g sets after splitting of degenerate d orbitals by the ligand field. (i) ∆ o<P : t3 2ge1 g (high spin). (ii) ∆ o>P : t4 2ge0 g (low spin). (b) ∆t = 4 9∆o is small, smaller than the pairing energy, so electrons occupy higher orbitals rather than pair.
