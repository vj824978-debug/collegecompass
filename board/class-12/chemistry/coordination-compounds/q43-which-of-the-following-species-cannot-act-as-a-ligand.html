Which of the following species cannot act as a ligand?

CBSE 20232 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) Which of the following species cannot act as a ligand? Give reason. OH– , NH4+, CH3NH2, H2O (b) The complex [Co(NH 3)5(NO2)]Cl2 is red in colour. Give IUPAC name of its linkage isomer.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) NH4+: N has no lone pair left (all used in four N–H bonds), so it cannot donate an electron pair. (b) Linkage isomer [Co(NH 3)5(ONO)]Cl2: pentaamminenitrito-O-cobalt(III) chloride (the given one is pentaamminenitrito-N-cobalt(III) chloride).
