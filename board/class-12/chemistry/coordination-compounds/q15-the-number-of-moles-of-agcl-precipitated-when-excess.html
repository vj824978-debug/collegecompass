The number of moles of AgCl precipitated when excess AgNO 3 solution is mixed with one mole of [Co(NH3)3Cl3] is: (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3
Answer
Answer as text
(A) All three Cl are in the coordination sphere; non-ionisable, no free Cl – .
