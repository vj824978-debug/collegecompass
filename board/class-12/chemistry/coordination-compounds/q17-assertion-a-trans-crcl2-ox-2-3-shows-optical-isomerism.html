Assertion (A): Trans [CrCl2(ox)2]3– shows optical isomerism.

CBSE 2023
Question 17 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
Assertion (A): Trans [CrCl2(ox)2]3– shows optical isomerism. Reason (R): Optical isomerism is common in octahedral complexes involving bidentate ligands. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) The trans isomer has a mirror plane, so it is optically inactive (A false); R is a true statement (cis isomer is optically active).
