Write the IUPAC name of the complex [Pt(en) 2Cl2]2+.

CBSE 202556/7/12 marks
Question 39 · Class 12 Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
Question as text
(a) Write the IUPAC name of the complex [Pt(en) 2Cl2]2+. Draw the structure of geometrical isomer of this complex which is optically inactive. OR (b) (i) Write the formula of the following coordination compound: Pentaamminecarbonatocobalt(III)chloride (ii) Write the IUPAC name of the linkage isomer of the complex [Co(NH 3)5(NO2)]Cl2. 1+1=2
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Dichloridobis(ethane-1,2-diamine)platinum(IV) ion. The trans isomer (two Cl opposite each other, the two en rings in the equatorial plane) has a mirror plane and is optically inactive. (b)(i) [Co(NH3)5(CO3)]Cl. (ii) [Co(NH 3)5(ONO)]Cl2: pentaamminenitrito-O-cobalt(III) chloride.
