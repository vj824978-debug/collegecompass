Question as text
Give reasons for the following: (a) Measurement of osmotic pressure method is preferred for the determination of molar masses of macromolecules such as proteins and polymers. (b) Aquatic animals are more comfortable in cold water than in warm water. (c) Elevation of boiling point of 1 M KCl solution is nearly double than that of 1 M sugar solution.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Osmotic pressure is large and measurable even for very dilute solutions, is measured at room temperature (proteins/biomolecules are unstable at high temperature; polymers are poorly soluble) and uses molarity, so it suits macromolecules of high molar mass. (b) Solubility of O 2 in water decreases with rise in temperature (Henry’s KH increases), so cold water holds more dissolved oxygen. (c) KCl−−→K+ + Cl– , i≈ 2; sugar does not dissociate, i = 1; ∆Tb =iKbm is therefore nearly double.
