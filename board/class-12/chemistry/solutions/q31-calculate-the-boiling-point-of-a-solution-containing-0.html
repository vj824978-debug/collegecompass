Question as text
Calculate the boiling point of a solution containing 0.61 g of benzoic acid (Molar mass = 122 g mol−1) in 5 g of CS2 in which it dimerises to the extent of 88%. The boiling point and Kb of CS2 are 46.2◦C and 2.3 K kg mol−1 respectively.
Answer
Answer as text
n = 0.61/122 = 0.005 mol;m = 0.005/0.005 kg = 1 mol kg−1. Dimerisation α = 0.88: i = 1−α/2 = 0.56. ∆Tb = 0.56× 2.3× 1 = 1.288 K. Boiling point = 46.2 + 1.288≈ 47.49◦C.
