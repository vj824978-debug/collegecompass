In the following diagram point, ‘X’ represents (A) Boiling point of solution (B) Freezing point of solvent (C) Boiling point of solvent (D) Freezing point of solution
Answer
Answer as text
(D) X is where the steep solid-solvent vapour-pressure curve meets the lower (solution) curve, i.e. the freezing point of the solution (NCERT Fig. for ∆Tf).
