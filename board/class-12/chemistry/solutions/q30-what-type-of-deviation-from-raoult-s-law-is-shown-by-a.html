What type of deviation from Raoult’s law is shown by a mixture of phenol and aniline?

CBSE 202656/1/12 marks
Question 30 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
What type of deviation from Raoult’s law is shown by a mixture of phenol and aniline? Give reason. What will happen to the boiling point of the solution on mixing phenol and aniline?
Answer
Answer as text
Negative deviation: intermolecular H-bonding between phenolic –OH and –NH 2 of aniline is stronger than the phenol–phenol and aniline–aniline attractions, so vapour pressure falls (∆ mixH <0, ∆mixV <0). Boiling point of the mixture increases (forms a maximum-boiling azeotrope).
