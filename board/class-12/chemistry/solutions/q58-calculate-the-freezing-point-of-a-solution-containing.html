Question 58 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
Calculate the freezing point of a solution containing 60 g of glucose (Molar mass = 180 g mol −1) in 250 g of water. (Kf of water = 1.86 K kg mol−1)
Answer
Answer as text
m = 60/180 0.250 = 1.333 mol kg−1; ∆Tf = 1.86× 1.333 = 2.48 K; Tf = 273.15− 2.48 = 270.67 K (−2.48◦C).
