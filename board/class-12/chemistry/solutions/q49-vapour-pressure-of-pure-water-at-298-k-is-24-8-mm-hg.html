Question as text
Vapour pressure of pure water at 298 K is 24.8 mm Hg. Calculate the lowering in vapour pressure of an aqueous solution which freezes at −0.3◦C. (Kf of water = 1.86 K kg mol−1)
Answer
Answer as text
m = ∆Tf/Kf = 0.3/1.86 = 0.161 mol kg−1; xsolute = 0.161 0.161 + 55.5≈ 2.9× 10−3; ∆p =p◦x = 24.8× 2.9× 10−3≈ 0.072 mm Hg.
