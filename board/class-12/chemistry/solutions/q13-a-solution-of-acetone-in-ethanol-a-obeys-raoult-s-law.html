A solution of acetone in ethanol : (A) obeys Raoult’s law.

CBSE 202556/6/1
Question 13 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
A solution of acetone in ethanol : (A) obeys Raoult’s law. (B) forms an ideal solution. (C) shows a positive deviation from Raoult’s law. (D) shows a negative deviation from Raoult’s law.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Ethanol’s H-bonds are broken by acetone; weaker A–B interactions, so positive deviation.
