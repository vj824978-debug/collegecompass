CBSE 20245 marks
Question 52 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
(a) (i) Ishan’s automobile radiator is filled with 1.0 kg of water. How many grams of ethylene glycol (Molar mass = 62 g mol−1) must Ishan add to get the freezing point of the solution lowered to−2.8◦C. Kf for water is 1.86 K kg mol−1. 3 (ii) What type of deviation from Raoult’s law is shown by ethanol and acetone mixture? Give reason. OR (b) (i) Boiling point of water at 750 mm Hg pressure is 99.68 ◦C. How much sucrose (Molar mass = 342 g mol−1) is to be added to 500 g of water such that it boils at 100 ◦C? (Kb for water = 0.52 K kg mol−1). 3 (ii) State Henry’s law and write its any one application. 2
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) ∆Tf =Kf w/62 1.0 ⇒ 2.8 = 1.86× w 62⇒w≈ 93.3 g ethylene glycol. (ii) Positive deviation: acetone breaks some ethanol–ethanol H-bonds, so ethanol–acetone attractions are weaker than in the pure liquids; p> Raoult value, ∆mixH >0. (b)(i) ∆Tb = 100− 99.68 = 0.32 K;m = 0.32/0.52 = 0.615 mol kg−1; for 0.5 kg: 0.3077 mol×342≈ 105.2 g sucrose. (ii) Henry’s law: partial pressure of a gas over a solution is proportional to its mole fraction in solution, p =KHx (solubility∝ pressure). Application: soft-drink bottles sealed under high CO 2 pressure (or scuba tanks diluted with He to avoid bends).
