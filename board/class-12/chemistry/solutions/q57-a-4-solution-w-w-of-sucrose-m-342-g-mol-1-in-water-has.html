Solutions — Question 57 (CBSE 2019, 3 marks)

CBSE 20193 marks
Question 57 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
A 4% solution w/w of sucrose (M = 342 g mol−1) in water has a freezing point of 271.15 K. Calculate the freezing point of 5% glucose ( M = 180 g mol−1) in water. (Given: Freezing point of pure water = 273 .15 K)
Answer
Answer as text
Sucrose: m = 4/342 0.096 = 0.1218 mol kg−1, ∆Tf = 2.00 K ⇒Kf = 16.42 K kg mol−1 (as implied by the data). Glucose: m = 5/180 0.095 = 0.2924 mol kg−1, ∆Tf = 16.42× 0.2924 = 4.80 K; Tf = 273.15− 4.80 = 268.35 K.
