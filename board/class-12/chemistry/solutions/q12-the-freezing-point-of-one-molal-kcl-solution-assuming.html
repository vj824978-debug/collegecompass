The freezing point of one molal KCl solution, assuming KCl to be completely dissociated in water, is : (Kf for water = 1.86 K kg mol−1) (A) −3.72◦C (B) +3 .72◦C (C) −1.86◦C (D) +2 .72◦C
Answer
Answer as text
(A) i = 2: ∆Tf =iKfm = 2× 1.86× 1 = 3.72 K, so Tf =−3.72◦C.
