State Raoult’s law for a solution containing volatile components.

CBSE 20202 marks
Question 54 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
State Raoult’s law for a solution containing volatile components. What is the similarity between Raoult’s law and Henry’s law?
Answer
Answer as text
Raoult’s law: in a solution of volatile liquids the partial vapour pressure of each component is directly proportional to its mole fraction, pA =p◦ AxA. Similarity: Henry’s law p =KHx has the same form; Raoult’s law is the special case of Henry’s law with KH =p◦.
