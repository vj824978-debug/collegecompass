An aqueous solution of NaOH was made and its molar mass from the measurement of osmotic pressure at 27◦C was found to be 25 g mol−1. Calculate the percentage dissociation of NaOH in this solution. [Atomic mass : Na = 23 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u]
Answer
Answer as text
Mcalc(NaOH) = 40; i = 40/25 = 1.6 = 1 +α (2 ions)⇒α = 0.6; dissociation = 60%.
