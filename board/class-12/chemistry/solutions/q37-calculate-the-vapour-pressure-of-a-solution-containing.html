Question as text
Calculate the vapour pressure of a solution containing 61 g of benzoic acid (molar mass = 122 g mol−1) dissolved in 500 g of benzene when the vapour pressure of pure benzene at this temperature of experiment is 66 torr. Assume complete dimerization of benzoic acid in benzene.
Answer
Answer as text
n(benzoic acid) = 61/122 = 0.5 mol; complete dimerisation, i = 1 2, effective 0.25 mol. n(C6H6) = 500/78 = 6.41 mol. xsolute = 0.25/6.66 = 0.0375; p = 66× (1− 0.0375)≈ 63.5 torr (dilute approximationn2/n1 gives≈ 63.4 torr).
