1.00 molal aqueous solution of trichloroacetic acid is heated to its boiling point.

CBSE 202656/5/12 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
(a) 1.00 molal aqueous solution of trichloroacetic acid is heated to its boiling point. Boiling point of this solution was found to be 100.18 ◦C. Calculate the van’t Hoff factor for trichloroacetic acid. (Given : Kb for water = 0.512 K kg mol−1) OR (b) State Henry’s law. Calculate the mole fraction of CO 2 in water at 298 K under 760 mm Hg. (Given : KH for CO2 in H2O at 298 K = 1.25× 106 mm Hg)
Answer
Answer as text
(a) ∆Tb = 0.18 K =iKbm⇒i = 0.18 0.512× 1.00≈ 0.35 (data as printed; see note). (b) Henry’s law: the partial pressure of a gas in the vapour phase is proportional to its mole fraction in the solution, p =KHx. xCO2 = 760 1.25× 106 = 6.08× 10−4.
