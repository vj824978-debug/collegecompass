Question 55 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
A 0.01 m aqueous solution of AlCl 3 freezes at−0.068◦C. Calculate the percentage of dissociation. [Given : Kf for Water = 1.68 K kg mol−1]
Answer
Answer as text
i = ∆Tf Kfm = 0.068 1.86× 0.01 = 3.66 (using Kf = 1.86; the printed 1.68 gives i = 4.05> 4, impossible). AlCl 3−−→Al3+ + 3 Cl– : i = 1 + 3α⇒α = 0.885, i.e. about 88.5% dissociated.
