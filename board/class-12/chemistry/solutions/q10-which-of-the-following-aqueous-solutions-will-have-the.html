Question as text
Which of the following aqueous solutions will have the highest freezing point? (A) 1.0 M KCl (B) 1.0 M Na 2SO4 (C) 1.0 M Glucose (D) 1.0 M AlCl 3
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Glucose is a non-electrolyte ( i = 1), so the smallest ∆Tf and highest freezing point (KCl i = 2, Na2SO4 i = 3, AlCl3 i = 4).
