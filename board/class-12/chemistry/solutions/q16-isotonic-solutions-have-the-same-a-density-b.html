Isotonic solutions have the same (A) density (B) refractive index (C) osmotic pressure (D) volume

Question 16 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
Isotonic solutions have the same (A) density (B) refractive index (C) osmotic pressure (D) volume
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Isotonic solutions have equal osmotic pressure (equal molar concentration of particles) at a given temperature.
