The boiling point of an azeotropic mixture of water and ethanol is less than that of pure water and ethanol.

CBSE 202656/3/1
Question 3 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
The boiling point of an azeotropic mixture of water and ethanol is less than that of pure water and ethanol. The mixture shows: (A) positive deviation from Raoult’s Law. (B) negative deviation from Raoult’s Law. (C) no deviation from Raoult’s Law. (D) that the solution is an ideal solution.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Minimum-boiling azeotrope (higher vapour pressure than either component)⇒ positive deviation.
