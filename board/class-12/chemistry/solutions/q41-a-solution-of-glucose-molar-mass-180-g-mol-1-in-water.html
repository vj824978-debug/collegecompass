Question as text
A solution of glucose (molar mass = 180 g mol−1) in water has a boiling point of 100.20 ◦C. Calculate the freezing point of the same solution. Molal constants for water Kf andKb are 1.86 K kg mol−1 and 0.512 K kg mol−1 respectively.
Answer
Answer as text
m = ∆Tb/Kb = 0.20/0.512 = 0.3906 mol kg−1; ∆Tf = 1.86× 0.3906 = 0.727 K; freezing point≈− 0.73◦C (272.42 K).
