An azeotropic solution of two liquids has a boiling point lower than either of the two when it (A) shows a positive deviation from Raoult’s law. (B) shows a negative deviation from Raoult’s law. (C) shows no deviation from Raoult’s law. (D) is saturated.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Minimum-boiling azeotropes arise from large positive deviation (higher vapour pressure).
