What is meant by positive deviation from Raoult’s law?

CBSE 202556/7/12 marks
Question 50 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
What is meant by positive deviation from Raoult’s law? Give an example. What type of azeotrope is formed by positive deviation?
Answer
Answer as text
Vapour pressure of solution is higher than predicted by Raoult’s law (pA>p◦ AxA), because A–B attractions are weaker than A–A and B–B (∆mixH >0, ∆mixV >0). Example: ethanol + acetone (or ethanol + water, CS2 + acetone). Forms a minimum boiling azeotrope (e.g. 95% ethanol–water).
