Question as text
Which of the following solutions will have the highest boiling point in water? (A) 1% KCl (B) 1% glucose (C) 1% urea (D) 1% CaCl 2
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Per 100 g: i×n = 3/111 = 0.0270 (CaCl2) > 2/74.5 = 0.0268 (KCl)≫ urea 1/60 = 0.0167 > glucose 1/180; largest ∆Tb.
