Solutions — Question 46 (CBSE 2025, 2 marks)

CBSE 202556/5/12 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
(a) Calculate the elevation of boiling point of a solution when 3 g of CaCl2 (Molar mass = 111 g mol−1) was dissolved in 260 g of water, assuming that CaCl 2 undergoes complete dissociation. ( Kb for water = 0.52 K kg mol−1) OR (b) Liquids ‘X’ and ‘Y’ form an ideal solution. The vapour pressure of pure ‘X’ and pure ‘Y’ are 120 mm Hg and 160 mm Hg respectively. Calculate the vapour pressure of the solution containing equal moles of ‘X’ and ‘Y’.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) m = 3/111 0.260 = 0.104 mol kg−1; i = 3; ∆Tb =iKbm = 3× 0.52× 0.104≈ 0.162 K. (b) xX =xY = 0.5; p = 0.5× 120 + 0.5× 160 = 140 mm Hg.
