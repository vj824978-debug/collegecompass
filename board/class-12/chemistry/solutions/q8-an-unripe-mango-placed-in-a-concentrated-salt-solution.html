An unripe mango placed in a concentrated salt solution to prepare pickle, shrivels because (A) it gains water due to osmosis (B) it loses water due to reverse osmosis (C) it gains water due to reverse osmosis (D) it loses water due to osmosis
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Salt solution is hypertonic; water leaves the mango cells by osmosis (exosmosis).
