The value of Henry’s constant KH is : (A) greater for gases with higher solubility (B) greater for gases with lower solubility (C) constant for all gases (D) not related to the solubility of gases
Answer
Answer as text
(B) p =KHx: at a given pressure, larger KH means smaller mole fraction x, i.e. lower solubility.
