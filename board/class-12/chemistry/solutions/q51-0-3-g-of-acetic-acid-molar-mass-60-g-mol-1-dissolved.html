0.3 g of acetic acid (Molar mass = 60 g mol −1) dissolved in 30 g of benzene shows a depression in freezing point equal to 0.45◦C. Calculate the percentage association of acid if it forms a dimer in the solution. (Given: Kf for benzene = 5.12 K kg mol−1)
Answer
Answer as text
m = 0.3/60 0.030 = 0.1667 mol kg−1; ∆Tf(calc) = 5.12× 0.1667 = 0.853 K; i = 0.45/0.853 = 0.527; for dimerisation i = 1−α/2⇒α = 2(1− 0.527) = 0.945; association≈ 94.5%.
