CBSE 202556/2/13 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
At 25◦C the saturated vapour pressure of water is 24 mm Hg. Find the saturated vapour pressure of a 5% aqueous solution of urea at the same temperature. (Molar mass of urea = 60 g mol −1)
Answer
Answer as text
5 g urea in 95 g water: n2 = 5/60 = 0.0833, n1 = 95/18 = 5.278; x2 = 0.0833/5.361 = 0.0155; p = 24(1− 0.0155)≈ 23.6 mm Hg.
