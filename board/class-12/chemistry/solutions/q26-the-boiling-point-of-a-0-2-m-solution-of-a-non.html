The boiling point of a 0.2 m solution of a non-electrolyte in water is ( Kb for water = 0.52 K kg mol−1) (A) 100 ◦C (B) 100.52 ◦C (C) 100.104 ◦C (D) 100.26 ◦C
Answer
Answer as text
(C) ∆ Tb =Kbm = 0.52× 0.2 = 0.104 K;Tb = 100.104◦C.
