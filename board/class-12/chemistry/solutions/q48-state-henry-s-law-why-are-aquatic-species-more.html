State Henry’s law. Why are aquatic species more comfortable in cold water as compared to warm water?

CBSE 202556/6/12 marks
Question 48 · Class 12 Chemistry · Solutions
Question as text
State Henry’s law. Why are aquatic species more comfortable in cold water as compared to warm water?
Answer
Answer as text
Henry’s law: the partial pressure of a gas in the vapour phase is proportional to its mole fraction in the solution, p =KHx (solubility∝ pressure). Solubility of gases decreases with rise in temperature ( KH increases), so cold water holds more dissolved O 2.
