An element ‘X’ (At. mass = 40 g mol−1) having f.c.c. structure, has unit cell edge length of 400 pm. Calculate the density of ‘X’ and the number of unit cells in 4 g of ‘X’. ( NA = 6.022× 1023 mol−1)
Answer
Answer as text
fcc, Z = 4; a = 400 pm = 4× 10−8 cm, a3 = 6.4× 10−23 cm3. ρ = ZM a3NA = 4× 40 6.4× 10−23× 6.022× 1023≈ 4.15 g cm−3. Atoms in 4 g = 4 40× 6.022× 1023 = 6.022× 1022; unit cells = 6.022× 1022/4 = 1.506× 1022.
