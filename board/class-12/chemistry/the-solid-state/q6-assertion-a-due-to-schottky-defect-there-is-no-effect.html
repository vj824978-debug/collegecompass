Question 6 · Class 12 Chemistry · The Solid State
Question as text
Assertion (A): Due to schottky defect, there is no effect on the density of a solid. Reason (R): Equal number of cations and anions are missing from their normal sites in Schottky defect. (A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. (C) A is true but R is false. (D) A is false but R is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Schottky defect removes ions, so density decreases (A false); R is true.
