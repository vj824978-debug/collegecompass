Question as text
Match the following: I II (i) Stoichiometric defects (a) Crystalline solids (ii) Long range order (b) F-centres (iii) . . . ABC ABC ABC. . . (c) Schottky and Frenkel defects (iv) Number of atoms per unit cell = 2 (d) fcc structure (v) Metal excess defect due to anionic vacancies Which of the following is the best matched options? (A) (i)-(d), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(b), (iv)-(c) (B) (i)-(c), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(d), (v)-(b) (C) (i)-(c), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(d), (iv)-(b) (D) (i)-(a), (ii)-(b), (v)-(c), (iv)-(d)
Answer
Answer as text
(B) (i) stoichiometric defects – Schottky & Frenkel; (ii) long-range order – crystalline; (iii) ABC ABC – fcc (ccp); (v) anionic vacancies – F-centres. ( Z = 2 is bcc, unmatched.)
