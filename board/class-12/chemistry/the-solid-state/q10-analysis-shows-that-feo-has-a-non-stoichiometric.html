Question as text
Analysis shows that FeO has a non-stoichiometric composition with formula Fe 0.95O. Give reason.
Answer
Answer as text
Metal-deficiency defect: some Fe 2+ ions are missing and electrical neutrality is maintained by oxidation of two Fe 2+ to Fe3+ for each vacancy, so the crystal has less than one Fe per O.
