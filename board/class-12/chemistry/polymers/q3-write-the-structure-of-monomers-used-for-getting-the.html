Question as text
(a) Write the structure of monomers used for getting the following polymers: (i) Nylon-6,6 (ii) Glyptal (iii) Buna-S OR (b) (i) Is a homopolymer or copolymer? Give reason. (ii) Write the monomers of the following polymer: (iii) What is the role of sulphur in vulcanization of rubber?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) H 2N(CH2)6NH2 (hexamethylenediamine) + HOOC(CH2)4COOH (adipic acid). (ii) HOCH 2CH2OH (ethylene glycol) + phthalic acid o-C6H4(COOH)2. (iii) CH 2 ––CH–CH ––CH2 (1,3-butadiene) + C6H5CH––CH2 (styrene). (b) (i) Homopolymer (polypropene): only one monomer, propene CH2 ––CH–CH 3. (ii) Melamine (2,4,6-triamino-1,3,5-triazine) and formaldehyde HCHO. (iii) Sulphur forms –S–S– cross-links at reactive allylic sites, making rubber harder, stronger, more elastic and less sticky.
