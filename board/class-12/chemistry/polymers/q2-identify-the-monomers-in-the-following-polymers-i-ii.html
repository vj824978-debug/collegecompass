Identify the monomers in the following polymers: (i) (ii)
Answer
Answer as text
(i) As drawn (two C ––O groups on adjacent ring carbons): Glyptal — ethylene glycol HOCH2CH2OH + phthalic acid (benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid). Note: if Terylene (Dacron) was intended, the acid is terephthalic acid (benzene-1,4-dicarboxylic acid). (ii) Polyacrylonitrile (orlon): acrylonitrile CH 2 ––CH–CN.
