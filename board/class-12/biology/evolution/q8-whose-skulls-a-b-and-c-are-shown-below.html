Whose skulls ‘A’, ‘B’ and ‘C’ are shown below?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q243 marks
Question 8 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
(a) Whose skulls ‘A’, ‘B’ and ‘C’ are shown below? Which of the two are more similar to each other? [2] (b) Name the (i) ape like (ii) man like primates that existed 1.5 million years ago. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A: adult modern human skull; B: baby chimpanzee skull; C: adult chimpanzee skull. A and B are more similar to each other (the baby chimpanzee skull resembles the adult human skull more than the adult chimpanzee skull). (b) (i) Ape-like: Australopithecines. (ii) Man-like: Homo erectus (about 1.5 mya).
