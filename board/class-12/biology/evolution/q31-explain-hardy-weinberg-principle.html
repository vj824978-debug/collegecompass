Explain Hardy-Weinberg principle?

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIIQ183 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
Explain Hardy-Weinberg principle? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
The Hardy–Weinberg principle states that allele frequencies in a population are stable and constant from generation to generation (genetic equilibrium) unless disturbed by factors such as mutation, gene flow, genetic drift, genetic recombination or natural selection. If p and q are the frequencies of alleles A and a, then p + q = 1, and the genotype frequencies are AA = p2, Aa = 2 pq, aa = q2, so p2 + 2pq + q2 = 1 (binomial expansion of ( p + q)2). When the measured frequency differs from the expected one, the difference indicates the extent of evolutionary change.
