Mention one example each from plants and animals exhibiting divergent evolution.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ31 mark
Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
Mention one example each from plants and animals exhibiting divergent evolution. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
Plants: thorn of Bougainvillea and tendril of Cucurbita. Animals: forelimbs of whale, bat, cheetah and human (also vertebrate hearts or brains).
