At which stage during evolution did human use hides to protect their bodies and buried their dead?

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q11 mark
Question 11 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
At which stage during evolution did human use hides to protect their bodies and buried their dead? [1] (A) Homo habilis (B) Neanderthal man (C) Java man (D) Homo erectus
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Neanderthal man. Neanderthals (1400 cc brain) lived in the Near East and Central Asia between 1,00,000–40,000 years ago, used hides to protect their bodies and buried their dead.
