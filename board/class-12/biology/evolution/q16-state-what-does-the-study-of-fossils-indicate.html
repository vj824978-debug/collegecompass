State what does the study of Fossils indicate.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q163 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
(a) State what does the study of Fossils indicate. (b) Rearrange the following group of plants according to their evolution from Palaeozoic to Cenozoic periods: Rhynia; Arborescent Lycopods; Conifers; Dicotyledon. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Fossils (remains of hard parts of organisms preserved in rocks) indicate that: different-aged sedimentary rock layers contain different life forms, so life forms have changed over time; certain life forms were restricted to particular geological periods and many became extinct (e.g. dinosaurs); fossils trace evolutionary history and connecting links (e.g. horse evolution, Archaeopteryx). Fossils are thus evidence of evolution. (Any two points.) (b) [Part (b) is out of the current syllabus.] Rhynia → Arborescent Lycopods → Conifers → Dicotyledons.
