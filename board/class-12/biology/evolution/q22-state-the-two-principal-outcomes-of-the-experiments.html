State the two principal outcomes of the experiments conducted by Louis Pasteur on origin of life.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ21 mark
Question 22 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
State the two principal outcomes of the experiments conducted by Louis Pasteur on origin of life. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Life comes only from pre-existing life (biogenesis). (ii) Spontaneous generation was disproved: in pre-sterilised (closed) flasks no life arose from killed yeast, whereas in flasks open to air new organisms arose.
