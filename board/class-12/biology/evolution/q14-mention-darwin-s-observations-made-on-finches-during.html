CBSE 2023Delhi Set 3Q273 marks
Question 14 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
Mention Darwin’s observations made on finches during his visit to Galapagos Islands. Write the explanation given by Darwin on his observations. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Observations: Darwin saw many varieties of small black birds (Darwin’s finches) on the Galapagos Islands, living on the same islands but differing in beak shape and size and in feeding habits (seed-eating, insectivorous, vegetarian). Explanation: all the varieties evolved on the islands themselves from an original seed-eating ancestral finch; its beak became altered in different ways as the birds adapted to different food sources. Evolution of different species in a given area starting from a point and radiating to other habitats is called adaptive radiation.
