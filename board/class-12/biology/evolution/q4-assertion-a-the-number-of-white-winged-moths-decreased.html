Assertion (A): The number of white winged moths decreased after industrialisation in England.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q141 mark
Question 4 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
Assertion (A): The number of white winged moths decreased after industrialisation in England. Reason (R): Effects of industrialisation were more marked in rural areas of England. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. Industrial pollution (soot, smoke) affected urban/industrial areas most, darkening tree trunks so that white moths were easily picked off by predators.
