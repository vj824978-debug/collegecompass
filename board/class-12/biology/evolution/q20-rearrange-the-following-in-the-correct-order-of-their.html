CBSE 2020Delhi Set 3Q183 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
(a) Rearrange the following in the correct order of their appearance on Earth between two million years and 40,000 years back. Neanderthals, Australopithecus, Homo erectus and Homo habilis. (b) Which one of the above (i) had the largest brain size (ii) ate fruits [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Australopithecus → Homo habilis → Homo erectus → Neanderthals. (b) (i) Neanderthals (brain about 1400 cc). (ii) Australopithecus (ate fruits).
