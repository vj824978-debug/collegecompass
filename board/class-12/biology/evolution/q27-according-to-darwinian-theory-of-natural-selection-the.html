Question 27 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
According to Darwinian theory of natural selection the rate of appearance of new forms is linked to the life-cycle or the life-span of an organism. Explain with the help of an example. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Organisms with a short life cycle evolve faster because more generations pass in a given time. Example: a bacterial colony (A) growing in a medium has built-in variation in the ability to use a food component. A change in medium composition lets only the part of the population (B) able to survive the new conditions grow. In a short time this variant outgrows the others and appears as a new species, within days. The same change in a fish or fowl would take millions of years, as their life spans are in years.
