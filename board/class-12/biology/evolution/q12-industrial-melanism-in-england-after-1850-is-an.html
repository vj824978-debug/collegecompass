Industrial melanism in England after 1850 is an excellent example of Natural selection.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q243 marks
Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
Industrial melanism in England after 1850 is an excellent example of Natural selection. Explain how? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
In England, before industrialisation (1850s) white winged moths ( Biston betularia) outnumbered dark (melanised) moths, because white lichens on tree trunks camouflaged the white moths; dark moths were easily spotted by predators (birds). After industrialisation (1920) smoke and soot killed the lichens and darkened tree trunks; dark winged moths were now camouflaged, while white moths were picked off, so dark moths became more numerous. Thus the environment (predation) selected the better-adapted variant in each period: individuals with a useful variation survived and reproduced more. This change in population composition is natural selection in action. (In unpolluted rural areas white moths remained common.)
