Question 30 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
Answer the following questions based on the experiment conducted by S. L. Miller in 1953: (a) Name the gases present in the closed flask. (b) Why was the flask fitted with electrodes? (c) Write the observation he made. (d) State the significance of the observation made by him.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Methane (CH 4), ammonia (NH 3), hydrogen (H 2) and water vapour. (b) To create an electric discharge (spark), providing energy like lightning on the primitive Earth. (c) Amino acids were formed. (d) Organic molecules can form abiotically from inorganic ones; this supports chemical evolution, i.e. life could have arisen from non-living organic molecules (abiogenesis).
