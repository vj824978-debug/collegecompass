Natural selection operates in different ways in nature.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q335 marks
Question 6 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
(a) Natural selection operates in different ways in nature. (i) Identify the type of natural selection depicted in the graph above. [1] (ii) In England after industrialisation, the population of dark winged moths were more favoured than white winged moth. Explain. [2] (iii) Anthropogenic action can enhance the rate of evolution. Explain with the help of an example. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Directional selection. (ii) Before industrialisation (around 1850) tree trunks were covered by whitish lichens, so white winged moths were camouflaged and survived, while dark ones were picked off by predators. After industrialisation (around 1920) smoke and soot killed the lichens and darkened the trunks; now dark (melanised) moths were camouflaged and survived, whereas white moths were easily seen and eaten by predators. Hence the dark form was selected and its proportion rose (industrial melanism). (iii) Human activities create new, strong selection pressures, so resistant variants spread within a few generations. Examples: excessive use of herbicides and pesticides has selected resistant weeds and pests; overuse of antibiotics/drugs has selected resistant bacteria/eukaryotic cells; industrial pollution led to industrial melanism in moths.
