Differentiate between analogous and homologous structures.

CBSE 2018Q203 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Biology · Evolution
Question as text
(a) Differentiate between analogous and homologous structures. (b) Select and write analogous structures from the list given below: (i) Wings of butterfly and birds (ii) Vertebrate hearts (iii) Tendrils of bougainvillea and cucurbita (iv) Tubers of sweet potato and potato [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Analogous structures: anatomically different (different origin) but perform similar functions; result of convergent evolution. Homologous structures: anatomically similar (same basic structure and origin) but perform different functions; result of divergent evolution. (b) Analogous: (i) wings of butterfly and birds; (iv) tubers of sweet potato and potato.
