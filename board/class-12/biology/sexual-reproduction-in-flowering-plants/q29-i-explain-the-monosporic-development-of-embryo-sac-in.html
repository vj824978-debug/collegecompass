(i) Explain the monosporic development of embryo sac in the ovule of an angiosperm.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q315 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) (i) Explain the monosporic development of embryo sac in the ovule of an angiosperm. (ii) Draw a diagram of the mature embryo sac of an angiospermic ovule and label any four parts in it. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Of the four megaspores formed by meiosis of the MMC, only one is functional (the other three degenerate); the embryo sac develops from this single megaspore – monosporic development. Its nucleus divides mitotically: 2 nuclei move to opposite poles (2-nucleate), then two more freenuclear divisions give 4-nucleate and 8-nucleate stages. Cell walls then form: 6 nuclei become cells – egg apparatus (egg + 2 synergids) at the micropylar end and 3 antipodals at the chalazal end; the remaining 2 polar nuclei lie in the large central cell. Result: 7-celled, 8-nucleate embryo sac. (ii) Diagram: mature embryo sac. Labels (any four): filiform apparatus, synergids, egg cell, central cell, polar nuclei, antipodals.
