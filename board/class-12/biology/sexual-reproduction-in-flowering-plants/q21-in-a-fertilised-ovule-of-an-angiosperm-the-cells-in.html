Question as text
In a fertilised ovule of an angiosperm, the cells in which n, 2n and 3n conditions respectively occur are: (A) antipodal, zygote and endosperm (B) zygote, nucellus and endosperm (C) endosperm, nucellus and zygote (D) antipodals, synergids and integuments
Answer
Answer as text
(A) antipodal (n), zygote (2n) and endosperm (3n).
