Question as text
Draw a well labelled diagram of sectional view of male gametophyte/microspore of an angiosperm and write the functions of any two parts labelled. (Any four labels). [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: sectional view of a 2-celled pollen grain. Labels (any four): exine, intine, germ pore, vegetative cell, vegetative nucleus, generative cell. Functions (any two): exine (sporopollenin) protects the pollen against high temperature, acids, alkalis and enzymes; germ pore – site through which the pollen tube emerges; intine – forms the pollen tube; vegetative cell – stores food and forms the pollen tube; generative cell – divides to form two male gametes.
