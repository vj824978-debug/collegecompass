State why plant breeders are interested in artificial hybridisation programme.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 3Q253 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
State why plant breeders are interested in artificial hybridisation programme. How do they carry out this process? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Breeders use artificial hybridisation to combine desirable characters (high yield, disease/pest resistance, quality) of different parents into one superior variety, using only the desired pollen. Steps: (1) Emasculation – if the female parent has bisexual flowers, anthers are removed from the flower bud with forceps before they dehisce. (2) Bagging – the emasculated flower is covered with a butter-paper bag to prevent contamination by unwanted pollen. (3) When the stigma becomes receptive, pollen from the desired male parent is dusted on it and the flower is rebagged so the fruit develops. If the female parent is unisexual, emasculation is not needed; buds are simply bagged.
