Question 9 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B): (A) (i) Write two crucial changes, the seed undergoes while reaching maturity that enable them to be in a viable state until the onset of favourable conditions. (ii) Name the oldest viable seed excavated from Arctic Tundra as per the records. OR (B) (i) Pea flower produce assured seed sets. Give reason. (ii) In case of Polyembryony, an embryo ‘P’ develops from a synergid and the embryo ‘Q’ develops from the nucellus. State the ploidy of embryo ‘P’ and ‘Q’.
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(i) (1) Dehydration – water content falls to about 10–15% of seed mass, so metabolic activity slows down; (2) the embryo enters dormancy (state of inactivity). (ii) Lupinus arcticus (Arctic lupine), about 10,000 years old. (B)(i) Pea flowers are self-pollinated before the flowers open (anthers and stigma lie close together, enclosed by the keel petals), so pollination does not depend on pollinators and seed set is assured. (Source key: pea flowers are cleistogamous.) (ii) P (from synergid) – haploid (n); Q (from nucellus) – diploid (2n).
