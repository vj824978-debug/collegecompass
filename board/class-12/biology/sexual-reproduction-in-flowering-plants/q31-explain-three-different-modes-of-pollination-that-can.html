Explain three different modes of pollination that can occur in a chasmogamous flower.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q133 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Explain three different modes of pollination that can occur in a chasmogamous flower. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(1) Autogamy: transfer of pollen from anther to stigma of the same flower; needs synchrony of pollen release and stigma receptivity and anther–stigma proximity. (2) Geitonogamy: transfer to the stigma of another flower of the same plant; functionally cross-pollination by an agent, genetically like autogamy. (3) Xenogamy: transfer to the stigma of a flower on a different plant; brings genetically different pollen.
