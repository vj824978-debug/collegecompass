Question 18 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(b) (i) With the help of labelled diagram only, show the different stages of embryo development in a dicot plant. [4] (ii) Endosperm development precedes embryo development. Justify. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(b)(i) Diagram: sequence zygote → proembryo→ globular embryo→ heart-shaped embryo→ mature embryo. Labels: zygote, suspensor, globular embryo, heart-shaped embryo, cotyledons, plumule, hypocotyl, radicle. (ii) The primary endosperm cell divides before the zygote, forming endosperm whose cells store reserve food; this food is needed to nourish the developing embryo.
