Describe the process of double fertilisation in angiosperms.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 3Q265 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) Describe the process of double fertilisation in angiosperms. (b) Trace the development of polyploidal cell that is formed after double fertilisation in a nonalbuminous seed and albuminous seed. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The pollen tube enters a synergid and releases two male gametes. One fuses with the egg (syngamy) to form the diploid zygote; the other fuses with the two polar nuclei (triple fusion) to form the triploid PEN. Two fusions in one embryo sac = double fertilisation. (b) The PEN becomes the primary endosperm cell (3n), which divides repeatedly (usually freenuclear first, then cellular) to form triploid endosperm tissue storing food. In non-albuminous seeds (pea, groundnut, beans) the endosperm is completely consumed by the developing embryo, food being stored in the cotyledons. In albuminous seeds (wheat, maize, castor, barley) part of the endosperm persists in the mature seed and is used during germination.
