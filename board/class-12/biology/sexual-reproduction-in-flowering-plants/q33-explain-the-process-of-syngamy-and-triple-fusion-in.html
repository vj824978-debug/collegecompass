Explain the process of syngamy and triple fusion in angiosperms.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 2Q275 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) Explain the process of syngamy and triple fusion in angiosperms. (b) Trace the development of the product of syngamy upto its mature stage in a dicot plant. (c) Draw and label three important parts of a mature dicot embryo. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The pollen tube enters a synergid and releases two male gametes. Syngamy: one male gamete fuses with the egg to form the diploid zygote. Triple fusion: the other fuses with the two polar nuclei in the central cell to form the triploid primary endosperm nucleus (PEN). (b) The zygote (at the micropylar end) divides to form the proembryo, then the globular, heartshaped and finally the mature embryo, consisting of an embryonal axis and two cotyledons; epicotyl ends in plumule and hypocotyl ends in radicle (with root cap). (c) Diagram: mature dicot embryo with labels plumule, cotyledons, radicle (also hypocotyl, root cap).
