Question 28 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
One of the major approaches of crop improvement programme is Artificial Hybridisation. Explain the steps involved in making sure that only the desired pollen grain pollinate the stigma of a bisexual flower by a plant breeder. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(1) Emasculation: anthers are removed from the bisexual flower bud with forceps before they dehisce. (2) Bagging: the emasculated flower is covered with a butter-paper bag to keep out unwanted pollen. (3) When the stigma becomes receptive, pollen from the desired male parent is dusted on it and the flower is rebagged until the fruit develops.
