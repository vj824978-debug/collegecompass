CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIQ92 marks
Question 50 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
“For a common man both mango and strawberry are fruits, but not for a biology students”. Justify. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Mango is a true fruit – it develops from the ovary only. Strawberry is a false fruit – the thalamus also contributes to fruit formation.
