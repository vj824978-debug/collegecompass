When and where do tapetum and synergids develop in flowering plants?

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ133 marks
Question 48 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
When and where do tapetum and synergids develop in flowering plants? Mention their functions. [3] OR Where are the following structures present in a male gametophyte of an angiosperm? Mention the function of each one of them. (a) Germ pore (b) Sporopollenin (c) Generative cell
Answer
Answer as text
Tapetum develops during the development of the microsporangium (anther), as its innermost wall layer; it nourishes the developing pollen grains. Synergids develop in the embryo sac (ovule) during female gametophyte formation, at the micropylar end; their filiform apparatus guides the pollen tube into the synergid. OR (a) Germ pore – in the exine of the pollen grain; site through which the pollen tube emerges. (b) Sporopollenin – in the exine; protects the pollen from high temperature, strong acids, alkalis and enzymes. (c) Generative cell – inside the pollen grain (within the vegetative cell); divides to form two male gametes.
