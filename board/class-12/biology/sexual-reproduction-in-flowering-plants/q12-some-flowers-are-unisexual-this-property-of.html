Some flowers are unisexual, this property of unisexuality of flowers prevents which kind of pollination?

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 3Q11 mark
Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Some flowers are unisexual, this property of unisexuality of flowers prevents which kind of pollination? (A) Both Autogamy and Geitonogamy (B) Both Geitonogamy and Xenogamy (C) Geitonogamy but not Xenogamy (D) Autogamy but not Geitonogamy
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both Autogamy and Geitonogamy (as in dioecious plants such as papaya, where only xenogamy is possible). Note: per NCERT, unisexual flowers on a monoecious plant (castor, maize) prevent autogamy but not geitonogamy, so (D) is also arguable.
