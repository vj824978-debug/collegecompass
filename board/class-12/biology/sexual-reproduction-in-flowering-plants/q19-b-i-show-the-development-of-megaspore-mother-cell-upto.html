Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(b) (i) Show the development of megaspore mother cell upto the formation of mature embryo sac in flowering plants with the help of labelled diagrams only. (ii) How does geitonogamy differ from xenogamy? (iii) Name the type of flowers that are invariably autogamous. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(b)(i) Diagrams: ovule (nucellus) with large megaspore mother cell → dyad→ linear tetrad of megaspores (three degenerating, one functional) → 2-nucleate→ 4-nucleate→ 8-nucleate embryo sac → mature embryo sac. Labels: MMC, nucellus, integuments, micropyle, chalaza, functional and degenerating megaspores, filiform apparatus, synergids, egg, central cell with polar nuclei, antipodals. (ii) Geitonogamy: pollen goes to another flower of the same plant (genetically like selfpollination). Xenogamy: pollen goes to a flower of a different plant of the same species (genetically different pollen). (iii) Cleistogamous flowers.
