Endosperm is completely consumed by the developing embryo in which of the following?

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 3Q61 mark
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Endosperm is completely consumed by the developing embryo in which of the following? (A) Maize and Castor (B) Castor and Groundnut (C) Maize and Pea (D) Pea and Groundnut
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Pea and Groundnut – these are non-albuminous (ex-albuminous) seeds; maize and castor retain endosperm.
