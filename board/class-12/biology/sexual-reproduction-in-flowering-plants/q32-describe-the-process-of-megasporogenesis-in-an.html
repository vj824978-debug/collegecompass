Describe the process of megasporogenesis, in an angiosperm.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q255 marks
Question 32 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) Describe the process of megasporogenesis, in an angiosperm. (b) Draw a diagram of a mature embryo sac of angiosperm, label its any six parts. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Megasporogenesis is the formation of megaspores from the megaspore mother cell. A single MMC (large cell with dense cytoplasm and prominent nucleus) differentiates in the micropylar region of the nucellus; it undergoes meiosis to form four haploid megaspores; three degenerate and one remains functional, which develops into the embryo sac (monosporic development). (b) Diagram: mature embryo sac. Labels (any six): filiform apparatus, synergids, egg cell, central cell, polar nuclei, antipodals, micropylar end, chalazal end.
