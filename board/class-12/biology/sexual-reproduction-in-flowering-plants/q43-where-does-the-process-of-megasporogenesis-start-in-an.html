Where does the process of megasporogenesis start in an angiosperm?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIQ255 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Where does the process of megasporogenesis start in an angiosperm? Describe the process upto the formation of embryo sac. [1 + 4]
Answer
Answer as text
Megasporogenesis starts in the ovule, in the micropylar region of the nucellus, where a single megaspore mother cell differentiates. Process: the MMC undergoes meiosis to form four haploid megaspores; three degenerate and one is functional. Its nucleus divides mitotically (free-nuclear) to form 2-nucleate, 4-nucleate and then 8-nucleate embryo sac. Cell walls then form: egg apparatus (egg + 2 synergids) at micropylar end, 3 antipodals at chalazal end, and 2 polar nuclei in the large central cell – a 7-celled, 8-nucleate embryo sac.
