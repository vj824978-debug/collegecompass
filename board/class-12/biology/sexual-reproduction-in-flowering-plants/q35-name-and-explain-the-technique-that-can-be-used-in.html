Question as text
Name and explain the technique that can be used in developing improved crop varieties in plants bearing female flowers only. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Artificial hybridisation using bagging (no emasculation needed). Female flower buds are covered with a butter-paper bag before they open; when the stigma becomes receptive, pollen from the desired male parent is dusted on it and the flower is rebagged until the fruit develops.
