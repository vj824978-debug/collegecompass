A bilobed dithecous anther has 200 microspore mother cells per microsporangium.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q223 marks
Question 10 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) A bilobed dithecous anther has 200 microspore mother cells per microsporangium. How many male gametophytes can be produced by this anther? (b) Write the composition of intine and exine layers of a pollen grains.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A bilobed dithecous anther has 4 microsporangia: 4 × 200 = 800 microspore mother cells; each forms 4 microspores by meiosis: 800 × 4 = 3200 pollen grains (male gametophytes). (b) Intine: thin, continuous inner wall of cellulose and pectin. Exine: hard outer wall of sporopollenin, the most resistant organic material known (withstands high temperature, strong acids/alkalis, enzymes).
