Explain double fertilization in an angiosperm.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q143 marks
Question 36 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Explain double fertilization in an angiosperm. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
The pollen tube enters a synergid and releases two male gametes. One male gamete fuses with the egg cell – syngamy – forming the diploid zygote (which forms the embryo). The other fuses with the two polar nuclei in the central cell – triple fusion – forming the triploid primary endosperm nucleus (which forms the endosperm). As two fusions occur in an embryo sac, it is called double fertilisation.
