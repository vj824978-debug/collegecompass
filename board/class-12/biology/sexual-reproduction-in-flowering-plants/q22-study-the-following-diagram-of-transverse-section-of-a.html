Study the following diagram of Transverse Section of a young anther of an angiosperm: [1] Select the option where parts ‘A’, ‘B’ and ‘C’ are correctly identified. (A) A-Connective, B-Endothecium, C-Pollen grain. (B) A-Endothecium, B-Connective, C-Pollen grain. (C) A-Pollen grain, B-Connective, C-Endothecium. (D) A-Endothecium, B-Pollen grain, C-Connective.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) A – Connective, B – Endothecium, C – Pollen grain.
