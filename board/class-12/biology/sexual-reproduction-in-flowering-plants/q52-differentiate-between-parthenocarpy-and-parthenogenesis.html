Differentiate between Parthenocarpy and Parthenogenesis.

CBSE 2018Q133 marks
Question 52 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Differentiate between Parthenocarpy and Parthenogenesis. Give one example of each. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Parthenocarpy: development of fruit without fertilisation (seedless fruit), e.g. banana (also grapes; induced by growth hormones). Parthenogenesis: development of a new individual from an unfertilised egg/female gamete, e.g. drones (male honeybees) (also rotifers, turkey).
