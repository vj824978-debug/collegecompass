How does a bisexual flowering plant ensures cross pollination?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIIQ133 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
How does a bisexual flowering plant ensures cross pollination? Explain. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Outbreeding devices: (1) pollen release and stigma receptivity are not synchronised – pollen is released before the stigma is receptive or vice versa; (2) anther and stigma are placed at different positions so pollen cannot reach the stigma of the same flower; (3) self-incompatibility – a genetic mechanism that prevents self-pollen from germinating on the stigma or fertilising the ovules. (Production of unisexual flowers is another device.)
