“Farmers prefer apomictic seeds to hybrid seeds.” Justify giving two reasons.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q172 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) “Farmers prefer apomictic seeds to hybrid seeds.” Justify giving two reasons. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (1) Apomictic seeds of a hybrid do not show segregation of characters, so the farmer can resow seeds harvested from the hybrid year after year without losing hybrid vigour. (2) The farmer need not buy expensive hybrid seed every year, so cost is reduced.
