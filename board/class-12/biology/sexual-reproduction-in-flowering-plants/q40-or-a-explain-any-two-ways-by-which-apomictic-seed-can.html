Question as text
OR (a) Explain any two ways by which apomictic seed can develop. (b) List one advantage and one disadvantage of a apomictic crop. (c) Why do farmers find production of hybrid seeds costly? [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (1) A diploid egg cell is formed without reduction division and develops into an embryo without fertilisation. (2) Some diploid nucellar cells surrounding the embryo sac divide, protrude into the embryo sac and develop into embryos. (b) Advantage: hybrid characters do not segregate, so apomictic hybrid seed can be used year after year. Disadvantage: no genetic variation (offspring are clones), so reduced adaptability to changing environment. (c) Hybrid seed must be produced every year by controlled crossing (emasculation, bagging, pollination), which is labour-intensive; and seed from hybrids segregates, so farmers must buy fresh hybrid seed every year.
