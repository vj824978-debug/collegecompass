Question as text
If the cells in the leaves of a maize plant contain 10 chromosomes each, write the number of chromosomes in its endosperm and zygote. Name and explain the process by which an endosperm and a zygote are formed in maize. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Leaf cells are 2n = 10, so n = 5. Endosperm (3n) = 15 chromosomes; zygote (2n) = 10 chromosomes. (Source key: endosperm 30, zygote 20 – incorrect, since leaf cells are already diploid.) Process: double fertilisation. Of the two male gametes released into a synergid, one fuses with the egg (syngamy) to form the diploid zygote; the other fuses with the two polar nuclei of the central cell (triple fusion) to form the triploid primary endosperm nucleus, which develops into the endosperm.
