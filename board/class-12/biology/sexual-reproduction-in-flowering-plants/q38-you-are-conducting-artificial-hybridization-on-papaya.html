Question 38 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
You are conducting artificial hybridization on papaya and potato. Which one of them would require the step of emasculation and why? However for both you will use the process of bagging. Justify giving one reason. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Potato needs emasculation because it bears bisexual flowers (its own anthers must be removed to prevent self-pollination); papaya has unisexual flowers (dioecious). Bagging is needed in both to protect the stigma from contamination by unwanted pollen.
