Express the process of pollination in Vallisneria.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIQ62 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
Express the process of pollination in Vallisneria. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Vallisneria is water pollinated (hydrophily): female flowers reach the water surface on long stalks; male flowers or pollen grains are released on the water surface; they are carried passively by water currents; some reach the female flowers and their stigma, achieving pollination.
