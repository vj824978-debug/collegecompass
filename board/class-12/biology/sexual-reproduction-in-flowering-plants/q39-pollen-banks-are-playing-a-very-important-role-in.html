Question as text
Pollen banks are playing a very important role in promoting plant breeding programme the world over. How are pollens preserved in the pollen banks? Explain. How are such banks benefitting our farmer? Write any two ways. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Pollen grains are preserved by cryopreservation in liquid nitrogen (−196 ◦C), where they remain viable for years. Benefits (any two): pollen of desired/different genetic strains is available for crop-breeding programmes; it can be stored for a long time and used when needed; conserves genetic diversity of crop varieties.
