(b) (i) Why does endosperm development precede embryo development in angiosperm seeds?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 2Q315 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(b) (i) Why does endosperm development precede embryo development in angiosperm seeds? State the role of endosperm in mature albuminous seeds. [2] (ii) Draw a labelled diagram showing the different embryonic stages of a dicot plant, upto a fully mature embryo. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(b)(i) Endosperm develops first because its cells accumulate the reserve food needed to nourish the developing embryo. In mature albuminous seeds (e.g. wheat, maize, castor) the endosperm persists and serves as the food store used during seed germination. (ii) Diagram: zygote → proembryo→ globular embryo→ heart-shaped embryo→ mature embryo. Labels: zygote, suspensor, cotyledons, plumule, hypocotyl, radicle.
