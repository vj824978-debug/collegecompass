A phenomenon where a male insect mistakenly identified the patterns of a orchid flower as the female insect partner and tries to copulate and thereby pollinates the flower is said to be: [1] (A) Pseudocopulation (B) Pseudoparthenocarpy (C) Pseudopollination (D) Pseudofertilisation
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Pseudocopulation (e.g. Ophrys, where a petal resembles the female bee).
