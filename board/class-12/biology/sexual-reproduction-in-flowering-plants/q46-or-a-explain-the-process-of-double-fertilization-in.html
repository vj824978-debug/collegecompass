CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIIQ255 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
OR (a) Explain the process of double fertilization in angiosperms. (b) Why does the development of endosperm precedes that of embryo? (c) List the parts of a typical dicot embryo.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) One male gamete fuses with the egg (syngamy) forming the diploid zygote; the other fuses with the two polar nuclei (triple fusion) forming the triploid PEN; the two fusions together are double fertilisation. (b) Endosperm cells store reserve food that is used to nourish the developing embryo, so endosperm must form first. (c) Embryonal axis with plumule (epicotyl) and radicle (hypocotyl, root cap), and two cotyledons.
