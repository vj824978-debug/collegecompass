(i) State the objective of adopting artificial hybridisation programme in plants.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 3Q335 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
(a) (i) State the objective of adopting artificial hybridisation programme in plants. [5] (ii) Describe the steps followed in this technique.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) Objective: to combine desirable characters of two different parents into one plant and so produce new, superior varieties (higher yield, disease resistance, better quality), by ensuring that only the desired pollen pollinates the stigma. (ii) Steps: select parents with desired traits; emasculation – remove anthers of the bisexual flower bud of the female parent with forceps before dehiscence; bagging – cover the emasculated flower with a butter-paper bag; when the stigma becomes receptive, dust it with pollen collected from the desired male parent; rebag the flower and allow the fruit to develop. (Unisexual female flowers need no emasculation.)
