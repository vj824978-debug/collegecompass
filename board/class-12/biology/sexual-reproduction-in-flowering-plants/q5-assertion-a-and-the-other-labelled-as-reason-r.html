Question 5 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
For Question numbers 13 and 14, two statements are given – one labelled as Assertion (A) and the other labelled as Reason (R). Answer these questions by selecting the appropriate option given below: (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). (B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). (C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. (D) (A) is false, but (R) is true. Assertion (A): Perisperm is a diploid tissue. Reason (R): Perisperm is the remains of nucellus which surrounds the embryo in certain seeds.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). Perisperm is diploid because it is nucellar (maternal 2n) tissue; R only states that it is persistent nucellus in some seeds (e.g. black pepper, beet). (Some keys accept (A), arguing that nucellar origin explains the diploidy.)
