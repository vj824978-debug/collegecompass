CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q62 marks
Question 30 · Class 12 Biology · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
Question as text
State two advantages of an apomictic seed to a farmer. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(1) The hybrid character does not segregate in apomictic seeds, so the farmer can use the harvested seed to raise the crop year after year. (2) He need not buy costly hybrid seed every season.
