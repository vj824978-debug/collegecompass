Biodiversity hotspots cover less than 2% of Earth’s land area.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q212 marks
Question 11 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) Biodiversity hotspots cover less than 2% of Earth’s land area. Strict protection of these areas can reduce the rate of ongoing extinctions. Explain. (b) Name any two hotspots in India. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Hotspots are regions of very high species richness and high endemism; though they cover less than 2% of Earth’s land area, they harbour an extremely high number of species, so their strict protection can reduce ongoing mass extinctions by almost 30%. (b) Any two: Western Ghats and Sri Lanka; Indo-Burma; Himalaya.
