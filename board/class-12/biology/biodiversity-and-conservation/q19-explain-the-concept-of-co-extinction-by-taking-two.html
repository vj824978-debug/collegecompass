Explain the concept of “co-extinction” by taking two examples.

CBSE 2022Term II Outside Delhi Set 1Q113 marks
Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) Explain the concept of “co-extinction” by taking two examples. [3] OR (b) “Forests provide intangible benefits to us.” Explain by taking three different areas, how. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Co-extinction: when a species becomes extinct, the plant and animal species associated with it in an obligatory way also become extinct. Examples: (i) when a host fish species becomes extinct, its unique assemblage of parasites also meets the same fate; (ii) in a coevolved plant– pollinator mutualism (e.g. fig and its wasp), extinction of one leads to extinction of the other. OR (b) Any three intangible benefits: (i) climate/atmosphere – forests regulate temperature, rainfall and humidity and maintain O 2/CO2 balance (Amazon produces about 20% of atmospheric oxygen); (ii) soil and water – forests check soil erosion and floods and maintain the water cycle; (iii) pollination and wildlife – forests house pollinators and provide shelter, food and breeding sites to wildlife; (iv) aesthetic/recreational – pleasure of walking through forests, birdwatching, ecotourism.
