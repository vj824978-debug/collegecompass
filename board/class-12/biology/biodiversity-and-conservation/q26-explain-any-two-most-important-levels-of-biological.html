Question as text
Explain any two most important levels of biological organisation showing biodiversity with the help of an example each. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Any two: (i) Genetic diversity – variation in genes within a species over its distributional range, e.g. Rauwolfia vomitoria in different Himalayan ranges differs in potency/concentration of reserpine; India has over 50,000 strains of rice and 1,000 varieties of mango. (ii) Species diversity – diversity at the species level, e.g. the Western Ghats have greater amphibian species diversity than the Eastern Ghats. (iii) Ecological diversity – diversity at the ecosystem level, e.g. India with deserts, rain forests, mangroves, coral reefs, wetlands, estuaries and alpine meadows has greater ecosystem diversity than Norway.
