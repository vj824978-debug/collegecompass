Explain any three ways other than zoological parks, botanical gardens and wildlife safaries, by which threatened species of plants and animals are being conserved ‘ex situ’. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Any three: (i) Cryopreservation – gametes of threatened species preserved in viable and fertile condition for long periods in liquid nitrogen at −196 ◦C; (ii) in vitro fertilisation – eggs fertilised outside the body and the embryos used for breeding; (iii) tissue culture – plants propagated from small tissue explants (micropropagation); (iv) seed banks – seeds of commercially important plants stored for long periods.
