Assertion (A): Communities that comprise more species tend to be more stable.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q161 mark
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
Assertion (A): Communities that comprise more species tend to be more stable. [1] Reason (R): A higher number of species results in less year to year variation in total biomass. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). (Tilman found that plots with more species show less year-to-year variation in total biomass, which is what makes such communities more stable. Note: the solved paper’s key gives (B); (A) is the reading that follows NCERT.)
