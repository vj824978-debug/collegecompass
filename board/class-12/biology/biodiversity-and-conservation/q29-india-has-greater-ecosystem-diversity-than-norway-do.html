“India has greater ecosystem diversity than Norway.” Do you agree with the statement?

CBSE 2018Q213 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) “India has greater ecosystem diversity than Norway.” Do you agree with the statement? Give reasons in support of your answer. (b) Write the difference between genetic biodiversity and species biodiversity that exists at all the levels of biological organisation. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Yes. India, a tropical country, has many ecosystems (deserts, rain forests, mangroves, coral reefs, wetlands, estuaries, alpine meadows); tropical regions are less seasonal, more constant and predictable, promoting niche specialisation and greater diversity. Norway (temperate, far from the equator) is more seasonal and less predictable, so it has fewer ecosystems and lower diversity. (b) Genetic diversity is variation in genes within a single species over its distributional range (e.g. 50,000 strains of rice in India; Rauwolfia vomitoria varying in reserpine content). Species diversity is the variety (number) of different species in a region (e.g. amphibian species richer in Western Ghats than Eastern Ghats).
