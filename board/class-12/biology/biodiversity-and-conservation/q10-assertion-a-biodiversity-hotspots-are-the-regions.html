Question 10 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
Assertion (A): ‘Biodiversity hotspots’ are the regions which possess high levels of species richness, high degree of endemism. [1] Reason (R): Total number of biodiversity hotspots in the world is 22 with two of these hotspots found in India. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. (Hotspots are regions of high species richness and high endemism, but there are 34 hotspots in the world, three of which – Western Ghats and Sri Lanka, Indo- Burma, Himalaya – cover India’s biodiversity regions.) (Source key: (A).)
