Question as text
(a) Indiscriminate human activities such as alien species invasion, fragmentation and habitat loss have accelerated the loss of biodiversity. Justify by taking one example for each. (b) State the importance of the following: (i) IUCN Red data list (ii) Hot spots in conservation of biodiversity
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Alien species invasion: introduced species out-compete and eliminate native species, e.g. Nile perch introduced into Lake Victoria (East Africa) caused extinction of more than 200 species of cichlid fish. Fragmentation: large habitats broken into small pieces badly affect mammals and birds needing large territories and migratory animals, leading to population decline. Habitat loss: destruction of habitats eliminates the species living there, e.g. tropical rain forests once covering 14% of Earth’s land surface now cover only 6%; the Amazon rain forest is being cut for soyabean cultivation and cattle grasslands. (b) (i) IUCN Red Data List: documents species and categorises them by threat of extinction (e.g. extinct, endangered, vulnerable); it identifies species needing urgent protection and guides conservation planning. (ii) Hotspots: regions with very high species richness and high endemism; though they cover less than 2% of Earth’s land, strict protection of these areas can reduce ongoing mass extinctions by almost 30%.
