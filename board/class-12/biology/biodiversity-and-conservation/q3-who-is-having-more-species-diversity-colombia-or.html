Who is having more species diversity – Colombia or Greenland and why?

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 3Q273 marks
Question 3 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(A) Who is having more species diversity – Colombia or Greenland and why? (B) Explain the concept proposed by Paul Ehrlich.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Colombia (near the equator, tropical) has far more species (e.g. about 1400 bird species) than Greenland (high latitude, about 56 bird species), because tropics: (i) are less seasonal, more constant and predictable, promoting niche specialisation; (ii) remained undisturbed by glaciations for millions of years, allowing long evolutionary time for speciation; (iii) receive more solar energy, giving higher productivity. (B) Paul Ehrlich’s rivet popper hypothesis : an ecosystem is like an aeroplane and its species are the rivets. Losing a few rivets (species) may not affect flight (ecosystem function) at first, but as more are lost the ecosystem becomes weak and dangerous; loss of rivets on key parts (key species driving major ecosystem functions) is more serious. Hence every species matters for ecosystem stability.
