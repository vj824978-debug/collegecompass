List six advantages of “ex-situ” approach to conservation of biodiversity.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ233 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
List six advantages of “ex-situ” approach to conservation of biodiversity. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Any six: (i) endangered/threatened species can be protected and bred in captivity; (ii) gametes of threatened species can be preserved in viable, fertile condition for long periods by cryopreservation; (iii) eggs can be fertilised in vitro; (iv) plants can be propagated by tissue culture; (v) seeds of commercially important plants (genetic strains) can be kept for long periods in seed banks; (vi) reduces biodiversity loss / extinct-in-wild species can be reintroduced; (vii) large numbers of species can be conserved; (viii) economic, educational and aesthetic value.
