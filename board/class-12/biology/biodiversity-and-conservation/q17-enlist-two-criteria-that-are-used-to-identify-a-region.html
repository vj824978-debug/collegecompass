Enlist two criteria that are used to identify a region for maximum protection as ‘Biodiversity hotspots’.

CBSE 2022Term II Delhi Set 1Q123 marks
Question 17 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) Enlist two criteria that are used to identify a region for maximum protection as ‘Biodiversity hotspots’. (b) Name any two “hotspot” regions in our country. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) High level of species richness; (ii) high degree of endemism (species found nowhere else). (b) Any two: Western Ghats and Sri Lanka; Indo-Burma; Himalaya.
