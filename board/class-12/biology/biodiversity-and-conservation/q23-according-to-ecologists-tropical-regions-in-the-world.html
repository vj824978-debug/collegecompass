According to ecologists, tropical regions in the world account of greater biological diversity.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q265 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) According to ecologists, tropical regions in the world account of greater biological diversity. Justify. (b) Why are habitat loss and alien species invasion considered as the causes of biodiversity loss? Explain with the help of an example of each [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Tropics have greater biodiversity because: (i) they remained undisturbed by glaciations for millions of years, giving long evolutionary time for speciation; (ii) they are less seasonal, relatively constant and predictable, promoting niche specialisation; (iii) more solar energy is available, giving higher productivity. (b) Habitat loss destroys the places where species live, so species lose shelter and food and decline or become extinct; fragmentation especially harms animals needing large territories and migratory animals. Example: tropical rain forests reduced from 14% to 6% of land cover; Amazon forest cleared for soyabean and cattle grassland. Alien species invasion : introduced species become invasive, out-compete native species for resources and cause their decline or extinction. Example: Nile perch introduced into Lake Victoria led to extinction of more than 200 cichlid fish species (or Parthenium, Lantana, water hyacinth; African catfish).
