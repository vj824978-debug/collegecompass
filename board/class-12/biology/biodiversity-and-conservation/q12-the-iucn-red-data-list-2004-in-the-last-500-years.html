The IUCN Red Data List (2004) in the last 500 years documents the extinction of nearly 784 species including: [1] (A) 330 invertebrates (B) 338 invertebrates (C) 359 invertebrates (D) 362 invertebrates
Answer
Answer as text
(C) 359 invertebrates. (The 784 extinctions include 338 vertebrates, 359 invertebrates and 87 plants.)
