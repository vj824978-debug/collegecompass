Analyse the effects of ‘Alien species invasion’ on the biodiversity of a given area.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ243 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
Analyse the effects of ‘Alien species invasion’ on the biodiversity of a given area. Provide two examples.
Answer
Answer as text
Introduced alien species often become invasive: they out-compete native species for resources, spread and increase in number, causing decline or extinction of indigenous species. Examples (any two): Nile perch introduced into Lake Victoria caused extinction of more than 200 species of cichlid fish; invasive weeds carrot grass (Parthenium), Lantana and water hyacinth (Eichhornia) threaten native species; illegal introduction of African catfish ( Clarias gariepinus ) threatens indigenous catfishes.
