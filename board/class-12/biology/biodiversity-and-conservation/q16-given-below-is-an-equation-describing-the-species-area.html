CBSE 2022Term II Delhi Set 1Q103 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) Given below is an equation describing the Species-Area relationship between species richness and area for a wide variety of taxa as angiosperm plants, birds, bats etc. S = CAz [3] (i) Give a graphical representation of the given equation showing Species-Area relationship. (ii) What does ‘S’ represent in the given equation? (iii) What is the value of ‘Z’ (regression coefficient) for frugivorous birds and mammals in the tropical forests of different continents? OR (b) Given below is a ‘pie chart’ representing the global biodiversity: proportionate number of species of major taxa. (i) Identify (X) and (Y) in the given ‘pie chart’. (ii) “Extinction of species across taxa are not random.” Which group amongst the vertebrates is more vulnerable to extinction. (iii) Give one example each of recent extinctions of species in Russia, Mauritius and Australia. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Diagram: graph of species richness (S, y-axis) vs area (A, x-axis) – a curve rising and levelling off labelled S = CAZ, and a straight line on log–log scale labelled logS = log C+Z log A. (ii) S = species richness. (iii) Z = 1.15. OR (b) (i) X – Fishes; Y – Amphibians. (ii) Amphibians are more vulnerable to extinction. (iii) Russia – Steller’s sea cow; Mauritius – Dodo; Australia – Thylacine.
