Tropical regions harbour more species than the temperate regions.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q233 marks
Question 8 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
(a) Tropical regions harbour more species than the temperate regions. How have biologists tried to explain this in their own ways? Explain. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Three hypotheses: (i) Speciation is a function of time: temperate regions were subjected to frequent glaciations in the past, while tropical latitudes remained undisturbed for millions of years, giving long evolutionary time for species diversification. (ii) Tropical environments are less seasonal, relatively more constant and predictable, which promotes niche specialisation and greater species diversity. (iii) More solar energy is available in the tropics, contributing to higher productivity and indirectly to greater diversity.
