Question as text
The great German naturalist and geographer Alexander Von Humboldt observed that within a region species richness increased with increasing explored area, but only upto a limit. (a) For the above situation, construct a graph and write an equation. (b) Also write various values of Z (regression coefficient).
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Graph: species richness (S, y-axis) against area (A, x-axis) rising steeply and then levelling off (rectangular hyperbola); on log–log scale it is a straight line. Equation: S = CAZ, i.e. log S = log C+Z log A (S = species richness, A = area, C = Y-intercept, Z = slope/regression coefficient). (b) Z generally lies between 0.1 and 0.2, regardless of taxonomic group or region; for very large areas such as entire continents the slope is steeper, 0.6 to 1.2; for frugivorous birds and mammals in tropical forests of different continents Z = 1.15.
