Question as text
(b) The graphs (X) and (Y) given below depict the diurnal variations in the solar radiations in the month of the (Summer) and in December (Winters): (i) Which of the two graphs depicts tropical region and temperate regions respectively? (ii) Which of the two regions (X) or (Y) will show high biological diversity and why? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(Out of syllabus; no answer printed in source.) (i) Graphs (Y) depict the tropical region (similar solar radiation in June and December, little seasonal variation); graphs (X) depict the temperate region (large difference between summer and winter). (ii) Region (Y), the tropical region: it is less seasonal, relatively constant and predictable, which promotes niche specialisation; it also receives more solar energy, giving higher productivity – hence higher biological diversity.
