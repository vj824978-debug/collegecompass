CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q283 marks
Question 9 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
The world is facing accelerated rates of species extinction largely due to human activities. Explain any three human activities responsible for accelerated rates of species extinction. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Any three: (i) Habitat loss and fragmentation – deforestation, mining, urbanisation destroy habitats (e.g. rain forest cover reduced from 14% to 6%; Amazon cleared for soyabean/cattle); fragmentation harms animals needing large territories and migratory animals. (ii) Over-exploitation – overhunting and overharvesting, e.g. Steller’s sea cow and passenger pigeon became extinct; many marine fish are over-harvested. (iii) Alien species invasions – e.g. Nile perch in Lake Victoria caused extinction of over 200 cichlid species; African catfish ( Clarias gariepinus ) threatens native catfishes. (iv) Co-extinctions – e.g. when a host fish becomes extinct its unique parasites also become extinct.
