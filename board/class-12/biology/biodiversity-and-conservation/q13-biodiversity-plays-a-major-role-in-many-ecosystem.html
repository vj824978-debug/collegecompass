Question 13 · Class 12 Biology · Biodiversity and Conservation
Question as text
“Biodiversity plays a major role in many ecosystem services that nature provides.” [3] (a) Describe any two broadly utilitarian arguments to justify the given statement. (b) State one ethical reason of conserving biodiversity.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Broadly utilitarian arguments (any two): (i) the Amazon forest is estimated to produce through photosynthesis about 20% of the total oxygen in Earth’s atmosphere; (ii) pollination by bees, bumblebees, birds and bats, without which plants would not give fruits or seeds; (iii) aesthetic pleasures such as walking through forests, watching flowers in bloom or birdsong; (iv) other services like flood and erosion control and climate regulation. (b) Ethical argument: every species has an intrinsic value even if it is of no current economic value to us; we have a moral duty to care for their well-being and pass on our biological legacy in good order to future generations.
