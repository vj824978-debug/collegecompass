What is “population” according to you as a biology student?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ275 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
(a) What is “population” according to you as a biology student? (b) “The size of a population for any species is not a static parameter.” Justify the statement with specific reference to fluctuations in the population density of a region in a given period of time. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A population is a group of individuals of the same species living in a well-defined geographical area at a given time, sharing or competing for similar resources and potentially interbreeding. (b) Population density changes over time with food availability, predation pressure and weather; it fluctuates due to four basic processes: – Natality (B): number of births in a given period – adds to density. – Mortality (D): number of deaths in that period – decreases density. – Immigration (I): individuals of the same species entering the habitat from elsewhere – adds to density. – Emigration (E): individuals leaving the habitat – decreases density. Hence Nt+1 = Nt + [(B + I)− (D + E)]; density increases if ( B + I) > (D + E) and decreases otherwise, so population size is not static.
