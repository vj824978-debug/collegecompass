Question as text
(a) (i) Write the observations made at the end of Connell’s field experiment on barnacles on the rocky sea coasts of Scotland. (ii) Name any two categories of organisms that in general are adversely affected by competition. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) The larger, competitively superior barnacle Balanus dominated the intertidal area and excluded the smaller barnacle Chthamalus from that zone (competitive exclusion). (ii) Herbivores and plants (they are adversely affected by competition more than carnivores).
