Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Mammals are capable of maintaining homeostasis. But, very few smaller animals can survive in polar regions. Give reasons. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(Not answered in source – out of syllabus.) Small animals have a large surface area relative to their volume, so they lose body heat very fast in cold surroundings. They would have to spend a great deal of energy generating body heat through metabolism to maintain homeostasis, which is not possible in polar conditions; hence very few small animals live there.
