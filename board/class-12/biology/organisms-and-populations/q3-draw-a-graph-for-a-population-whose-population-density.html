Draw a graph for a population whose population density has reached the carrying capacity.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q283 marks
Question 3 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
(a) Draw a graph for a population whose population density has reached the carrying capacity. (b) Out of the two population growth curves, which one is considered a more realistic for most populations? Why? (c) Draw a growth curve where resources are not limiting for the growth of a population and give its equation.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Graph: population density (N) on y-axis vs time (t) on x-axis; a sigmoid (S-shaped) curve – lag phase, acceleration, deceleration, then levelling off (asymptote) at the dashed line labelled carrying capacity (K). Equation: dN dt = rN ( K− N K ) . (b) The logistic (Verhulst–Pearl, S-shaped) curve is more realistic, because resources in nature are finite; competition for limited resources makes growth slow down and level off at the carrying capacity K. No population can grow exponentially for long. (c) Graph: J-shaped (exponential) curve – N rises slowly at first and then ever more steeply with time, with no plateau. Equation: dN dt = rN (integrated form Nt = N0ert), where r is the intrinsic rate of natural increase.
