Interaction between clown fish living among the stinging tentacles of sea anemone is an example of– [1] (A) Amensalism (B) Parasitism (C) Mutualism (D) Commensalism
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Commensalism – the clown fish gets protection from predators among the stinging tentacles, while the sea anemone is neither benefited nor harmed.
