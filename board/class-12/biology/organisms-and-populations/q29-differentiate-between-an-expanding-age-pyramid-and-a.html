Differentiate between an ‘Expanding age pyramid’ and a ‘Stable age pyramid’.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ233 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Differentiate between an ‘Expanding age pyramid’ and a ‘Stable age pyramid’. Substantiate your answer with diagrams. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Expanding age pyramid: pre-reproductive individuals are far more numerous than reproductive ones (and post-reproductive are few); the population is growing. Stable age pyramid: pre-reproductive and reproductive individuals are about equal in number; population size remains stable. Diagrams: Expanding – triangular pyramid with a very broad base; Stable – bell-shaped pyramid with base and middle of nearly equal width. Labels: pre-reproductive (base), reproductive (middle), post-reproductive (top).
