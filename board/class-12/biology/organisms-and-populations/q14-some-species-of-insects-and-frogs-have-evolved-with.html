Question as text
“Some species of insects and frogs have evolved with various specific features that help them from being detected.” [2] (a) Justify the statement giving reasons. (b) Mention any two such features.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) These are anti-predator defences: by blending with their surroundings (camouflage) insects and frogs avoid being detected by predators, which increases their survival. (b) (i) Cryptic colouration (camouflage); (ii) being poisonous / distasteful to predators (e.g. warning colours).
