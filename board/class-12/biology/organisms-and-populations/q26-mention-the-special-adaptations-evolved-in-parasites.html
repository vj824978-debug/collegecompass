Mention the special adaptations evolved in parasites and why?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIQ233 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Mention the special adaptations evolved in parasites and why? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Any three, with reasons: – Loss of unnecessary sense organs (e.g. eyes) – they live inside the host and do not interact with the external environment. – Adhesive organs/suckers/hooks – to cling to the host. – Loss of digestive system – they absorb digested food directly from the host. – High reproductive capacity – to increase chances of survival/infecting new hosts. – Complex life cycle with one or more intermediate hosts – to facilitate parasitisation of the primary host. – Loss of chlorophyll and leaves ( Cuscuta) – it derives nutrition from the host plant.
