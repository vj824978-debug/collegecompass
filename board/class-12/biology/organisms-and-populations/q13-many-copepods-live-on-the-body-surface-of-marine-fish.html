Many copepods live on the body surface of marine fish.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 3Q31 mark
Question 13 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Many copepods live on the body surface of marine fish. This relationship is an example: [1] (A) Commensalism (B) Parasitism (C) Amensalism (D) Mutualism
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Parasitism – the copepods are ectoparasites that live on the fish’s body surface and feed on it.
