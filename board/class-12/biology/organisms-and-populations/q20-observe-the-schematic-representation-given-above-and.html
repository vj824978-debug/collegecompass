CBSE 2022Term II Outside Delhi Set 1Q62 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
(a) Observe the schematic representation given above and answer the following questions: [2] (i) Identify A and B. (ii) Calculate the growth rate of bacteria in a curd sample, where 1 million bacteria increased to two million, within a period of one hour. OR (b) Identify the type of pyramid given above. Write the identifying feature on the basis of which you identified it.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) A – Mortality (deaths, decrease the population); B – Natality (births, add to the population). (ii) Growth rate = increase in number / initial population = (2 − 1) million / 1 million = 1 per hour (100% per hour). OR (b) Expanding (growing) age pyramid – the base is broad: the pre-reproductive age group is the largest and post-reproductive individuals are few.
