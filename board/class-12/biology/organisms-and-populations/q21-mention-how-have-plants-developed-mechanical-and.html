Question as text
Mention how have plants developed mechanical and chemical defence against herbivores to protect themselves with the help of one example of each. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Mechanical (morphological) defence: thorns/spines, e.g. Acacia, cactus ( Opuntia). Chemical defence: production of poisonous chemicals, e.g. Calotropis produces cardiac glycosides; other plants produce nicotine, caffeine, quinine, opium (any one example each).
