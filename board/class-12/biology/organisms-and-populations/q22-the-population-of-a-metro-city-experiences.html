Question as text
“The population of a metro city experiences fluctuations in it’s population density over a period of time.” (a) When does the population in a metro city tend to increase? (b) When does the population in metro city tend to decline? (c) If ‘N’ is the population density at the time ‘ t’, write the population density at the time ‘ t + 1’. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Population increases when natality (B) and immigration (I) are more than mortality and emigration, i.e. ( B + I) > (D + E). (b) Population declines when mortality (D) and emigration (E) are more than natality and immigration, i.e. ( D + E) > (B + I). (c) Nt+1 = Nt + [(B + I)− (D + E)], where B = natality, I = immigration, D = mortality, E = emigration.
