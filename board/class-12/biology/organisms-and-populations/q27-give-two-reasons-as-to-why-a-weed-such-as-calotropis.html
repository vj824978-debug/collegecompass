Give two reasons as to why a weed such as Calotropis flourishes in abandoned fields.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ51 mark
Question 27 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Give two reasons as to why a weed such as Calotropis flourishes in abandoned fields. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
Any two: (i) it produces highly poisonous cardiac glycosides, so it is not grazed by animals; (ii) it has xerophytic adaptations (thick hairy leaves and stems) to survive dry conditions; (iii) light hairy seeds help in wide dispersal.
