Abingdon tortoise in Galapagos islands became extinct within a decade on introduction of goats in the island.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 2Q202 marks
Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Abingdon tortoise in Galapagos islands became extinct within a decade on introduction of goats in the island. Explain giving reason. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Goats introduced on the island had a much greater browsing efficiency than the tortoises. This is interspecific competition (interference competition): the goats depleted / interfered with the tortoise’s food supply, so the tortoise was out-competed and went extinct within a decade.
