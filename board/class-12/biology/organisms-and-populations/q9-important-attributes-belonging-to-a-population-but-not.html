Question as text
Important attributes belonging to a population but not to an individual are: [1] (i) Birth rate and death rate (ii) Male and female (iii) Birth and death (iv) Sex-ratio Select the correct option from the given options: (A) (i) only (B) (ii) only (C) (ii) and (iii) (D) (i) and (iv)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) (i) and (iv) – birth rate, death rate and sex ratio are population attributes; an individual is born, dies and is male or female.
