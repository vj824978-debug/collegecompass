(b) Explain Brood parasitism with the help of a suitable example.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q202 marks
Question 6 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
(b) Explain Brood parasitism with the help of a suitable example. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(b) In brood parasitism a parasitic bird lays its eggs in the nest of its host bird and lets the host incubate and raise its young. Example: the cuckoo (koel) lays eggs in the crow’s nest. Over evolution the parasite’s eggs have come to resemble the host’s eggs in size and colour, reducing the chance of the host detecting and ejecting them.
