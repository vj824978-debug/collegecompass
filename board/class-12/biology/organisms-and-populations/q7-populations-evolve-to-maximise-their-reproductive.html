Populations evolve to maximise their reproductive fitness in the habitat in which they live.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q294 marks
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Organisms and Populations
Question as text
Populations evolve to maximise their reproductive fitness in the habitat in which they live. Ecologists suggest, the life history of organisms has evolved in relations to the constraints imposed by the biotic and abiotic components of the habitat in which they live. This gets reflected in the population growth pattern of all organisms including humans. Study the population growth curves shown in the given graph and answer the questions that follow: (a) Identify the growth curves ‘A’ and ‘B’. [1] (b) Mention what does the dotted line in the graph indicate and state its importance also. [1] OR (b) Growth curve ‘B’ shows a different pattern from that of growth curve ‘A’. Justify giving one reason. [1] (c) (i) Which one of the two curves is more “realistic” and why? (ii) Which one of the two curves is relevant in present days with respect to human population in our country and why? [1+1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A – exponential (J-shaped) growth; B – logistic (S-shaped / sigmoid) growth. (b) The dotted line is the carrying capacity (K). Importance: it is the maximum number of individuals the habitat’s limited resources can support; it determines where population growth levels off. OR (b) B differs because resources are limited: as N approaches K, competition for resources lowers the growth rate, so the curve levels off at K, whereas A assumes unlimited resources and keeps rising. (c) (i) Curve B (logistic) is more realistic, because resources in nature are always finite and no population can sustain exponential growth for long. (ii) Curve B (logistic) – growth of our country’s population is increasingly being restricted by limited resources (land, water, food) and population-control measures, so growth will slow and level off.
