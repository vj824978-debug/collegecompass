How is the normal human body temperature of 37 ° C maintained during (i) Summer, and (ii) Winter? Explain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(Not answered in source – out of syllabus.) (i) Summer: we sweat profusely; evaporation of sweat cools the body (aided by dilation of skin blood vessels, which loses heat). (ii) Winter: we shiver – a form of exercise that produces heat and raises body temperature (skin blood vessels constrict to reduce heat loss).
