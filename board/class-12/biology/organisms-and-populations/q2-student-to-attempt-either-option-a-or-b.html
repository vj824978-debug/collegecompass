Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (A) Identify the type of pyramid given below and write two identifying features of such a pyramid:
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Age pyramid of an expanding (growing) population. Features: (i) very broad base – the pre-reproductive age group is the largest; (ii) narrow apex – post-reproductive individuals are very few compared with pre-reproductive and reproductive groups (high birth rate, population increasing).
