Explain one application of each one of the following: (A) Amniocentesis (B) Lactational amenorrhea (C) ZIFT…

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ255 marks
Question 17 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
(a) Explain one application of each one of the following: (A) Amniocentesis (B) Lactational amenorrhea (C) ZIFT (b) Prepare a poster for the school programme depicting the objectives of: “Reproductive and Child Health Care Programme”. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (A) Amniocentesis: prenatal detection of chromosomal/genetic disorders of the foetus (e.g. Down’s syndrome) by analysing fetal cells in amniotic fluid. (B) Lactational amenorrhea: a natural contraceptive method – no ovulation during intense lactation (up to about six months after parturition), so chances of conception are almost nil. (C) ZIFT: helps infertile couples have children – a zygote/early embryo (up to 8 blastomeres) formed by IVF is transferred into the fallopian tube. (b) Poster on RCH objectives (any relevant slogans/sketch), e.g.: creating awareness about reproduction-related aspects; providing facilities and support for building a reproductively healthy society; preventing STDs and HIV/AIDS; special health services, camps and adolescent clinics; slogans such as “Hum Do Hamare Do”, “Beti Bachao Beti Padhao”, “Sex determination is punishable”.
