Question as text
(b) Mention one advantage and one disadvantage of amniocentesis. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Advantage: detects genetic/chromosomal disorders of the foetus (e.g. Down’s syndrome, haemophilia) early in pregnancy. Disadvantage: it is misused for sex determination, leading to female foeticide (also, slight risk of miscarriage/infection).
