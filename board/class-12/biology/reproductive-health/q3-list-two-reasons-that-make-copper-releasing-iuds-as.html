List two reasons that make copper releasing IUDs as effective contraceptives.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q233 marks
Question 3 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
(a) List two reasons that make copper releasing IUDs as effective contraceptives. (b) Explain how the intake of oral contraceptive pills prevent pregnancy in humans.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Cu-releasing IUDs (i) increase phagocytosis of sperms within the uterus, and (ii) the released Cu ions suppress sperm motility and the fertilising capacity of sperms. (b) The pills contain small doses of progestogens or progestogen–estrogen combinations. They inhibit ovulation and implantation, and alter the quality of cervical mucus so that it prevents/retards the entry of sperms.
