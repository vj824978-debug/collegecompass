Study the table given below: Contraceptive / Contraceptive Method Mode of Action a.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q31 mark
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
Study the table given below: [1] Contraceptive / Contraceptive Method Mode of Action a. The pill I. Prevent sperm reaching cervix b. Condom II. Prevent implantation c. Vasectomy III. Inhibits ovulation d. Copper–T IV. Semen contains no sperm Select the option where contraceptive/contraceptive method are correctly matched with their mode of action. (A) a – III, b – II, c – I, d – IV (B) a – II, b – III, c – I, d – IV (C) a – II, b – I, c – IV, d – II (D) a – IV, b – III, c – II, d – I
Answer
Answer as text
(C) The correct match is a – III (pill inhibits ovulation), b – I (condom prevents sperm reaching cervix), c – IV (vasectomy: semen contains no sperm), d – II (Copper–T prevents implantation). Option (C) is the intended answer; its printed “a – II” is a misprint for “a – III”.
