Intensely lactating mothers generally do not conceive.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q182 marks
Question 8 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
(a) Intensely lactating mothers generally do not conceive. Why? (b) Our government has intentionally imposed strict conditions for MTP. Why? [2Ö 1=2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Lactational amenorrhea: during intense breastfeeding (up to about six months after parturition) ovulation and hence the menstrual cycle do not occur (high prolactin suppresses gonadotropins), so chances of conception are almost nil. (b) To prevent misuse of MTP, mainly illegal female foeticide after sex determination, and to prevent unsafe abortions by unqualified persons that endanger the mother’s life.
