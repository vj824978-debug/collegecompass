Assertion (A): Saheli is the World’s first non-steroidal oral contraceptive pill.

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 1Q131 mark
Question 1 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
Assertion (A): Saheli is the World’s first non-steroidal oral contraceptive pill. Reason (R): It has been developed by National Institute of Immunology, New Delhi. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. Saheli (centchroman) is the world’s first non-steroidal, once-aweek oral contraceptive pill, but it was developed by the Central Drug Research Institute (CDRI), Lucknow, not by NII, New Delhi.
