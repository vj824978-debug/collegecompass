Give one reason to justify statutory ban on amniocentesis.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ11 mark
Question 18 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
Give one reason to justify statutory ban on amniocentesis. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
To check/prevent female foeticide – amniocentesis is misused to determine the sex of the foetus followed by abortion of normal female foetuses.
