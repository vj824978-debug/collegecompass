CBSE 2025Delhi Set 1Q263 marks
Question 2 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
Shyam and Radha are expecting their first child with Radha being in her second month of pregnancy with no complications. Shyam’s family has a history of cystic fibrosis while Radha’s family has a history of Down’s syndrome, leading to a concern that the baby may have one of these conditions. (a) Suggest and explain a way of testing if their baby is at risk for any genetic disorders. (b) In case of presence of one or both of the abnormalities and posing a risk to the mother’s health, mention one possible option for them to consider. Is that option safe for Radha at the current gestational age? Justify. (c) Under what conditions is the process mentioned in (b) illegal?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Amniocentesis: a little amniotic fluid is drawn with a fine needle (usually around 15–20 weeks); the fetal cells in it are cultured and examined (karyotyping for chromosomal disorders like Down’s syndrome; DNA/biochemical tests for gene disorders like cystic fibrosis). (b) Medical termination of pregnancy (MTP). Yes, it is relatively safe now: Radha is in her second month, i.e. the first trimester (up to 12 weeks), when MTP is safest and can be done on the opinion of one registered medical practitioner. (c) MTP is illegal when used for sex selection/female foeticide (after illegal sex determination), when done by unqualified persons or at unregistered facilities, or when done beyond the legally permitted limit (after 24 weeks) without medical grounds.
