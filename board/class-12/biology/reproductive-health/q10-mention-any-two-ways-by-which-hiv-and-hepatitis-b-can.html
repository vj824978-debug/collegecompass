Mention any two ways by which HIV and Hepatitis–B can be transmitted to a healthy person.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 3Q192 marks
Question 10 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
(a) Mention any two ways by which HIV and Hepatitis–B can be transmitted to a healthy person. (b) Why is an early detection of these diseases essential? [2Ö 1=2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Any two: unprotected sexual contact with an infected person; sharing of injection needles/syringes; transfusion of infected blood; from infected mother to foetus/child. (b) Early detection allows proper and timely treatment, so the disease can be checked/cured before complications (e.g. liver damage, AIDS) develop, and further spread to others can be prevented.
