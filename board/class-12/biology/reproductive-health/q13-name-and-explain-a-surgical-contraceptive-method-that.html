Name and explain a surgical contraceptive method that can be adopted by the male partner of a couple.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q223 marks
Question 13 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
Name and explain a surgical contraceptive method that can be adopted by the male partner of a couple. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Vasectomy (sterilisation of the male). A small part of each vas deferens is removed or tied up through a small incision on the scrotum. This blocks the transport of sperms, so the semen contains no sperms and fertilisation (fusion of sperm with ovum) cannot occur. It is a highly effective, terminal (permanent) method; reversibility is very poor.
