What is artificial insemination in ART?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 2Q172 marks
Question 9 · Class 12 Biology · Reproductive Health
Question as text
What is artificial insemination in ART? Under what conditions is the person medically advised to go for it? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Artificial insemination (AI) is the technique in which semen collected from the husband or a healthy donor is artificially introduced into the vagina or into the uterus (IUI – intra-uterine insemination) of the female. It is advised when the male partner is unable to inseminate the female or has very low sperm counts in the ejaculate.
