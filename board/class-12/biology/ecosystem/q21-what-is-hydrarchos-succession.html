What is hydrarchos succession?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ275 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 21 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
(a) What is hydrarchos succession? (b) Compare the pioneer species and climax communities of hydrarchos and xerarch succession respectively. (c) List the factors upon which the type of invading pioneer species depend in secondary hydrarchos succession. Why is the rate of this succession faster than that of primary succession?[1 + 2 + 2]
Answer
Answer as text
(Out of syllabus; no official answer.) (a) Hydrarch succession is succession that starts in water (wet areas) and progresses towards a mesic (moderate) condition. (b) Hydrarch: pioneers are phytoplankton, followed by rooted submerged and floating plants, reed swamp, marsh meadow, shrubs; climax is forest. Xerarch: pioneers are lichens on bare rock, followed by mosses, herbs, shrubs; climax is also forest (both end in mesic conditions). (c) The invading species depend on the condition of the soil, availability of water, the environment, and the seeds or other propagules present. Secondary succession is faster because soil (sediment) is already present, so the slow process of soil formation is not needed.
