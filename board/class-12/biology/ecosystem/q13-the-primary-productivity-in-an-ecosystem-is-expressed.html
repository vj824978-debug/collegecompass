The primary productivity in an ecosystem is expressed as: (A) g m−2 yr−1 (B) g m−2 yr (C) k cal m−2 yr−1 (D)…

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q71 mark
Question 13 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
The primary productivity in an ecosystem is expressed as: [1] (A) g m−2 yr−1 (B) g m−2 yr (C) k cal m−2 yr−1 (D) kcal m−2
Answer
Answer as text
(A) g m−2 yr−1 (official key). Productivity is a rate of biomass production per unit area per unit time; (NCERT also allows the energy unit kcal m −2 yr−1, option (C)).
