Mention four significant services that a healthy forest ecosystem provide.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ122 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Mention four significant services that a healthy forest ecosystem provide. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Any four: purification of air and water; mitigation of droughts and floods; nutrient cycling; generation of fertile soil; wildlife habitat; maintaining biodiversity; pollination of flowering plants; storage of carbon; aesthetic, cultural and spiritual values; food, timber and medicinal products.
