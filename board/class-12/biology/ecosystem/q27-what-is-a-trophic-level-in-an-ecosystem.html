What is a trophic level in an ecosystem?

CBSE 2018Q265 marks
Question 27 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
(a) What is a trophic level in an ecosystem? What is ‘standing crop’ with reference to it? (b) Explain the role of the ‘first trophic level’ in an ecosystem. (c) How is the detritus food chain connected with the grazing food chain in a natural ecosystem? [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A trophic level is the specific place an organism occupies in a food chain according to its mode of nutrition (producer, primary consumer, etc.). Standing crop is the mass of living material (biomass) present at each trophic level at a particular time, measured per unit area. (b) The first trophic level consists of producers (autotrophs, green plants) that trap solar energy by photosynthesis and convert it into chemical energy of food; this is the base on which all other trophic levels depend for energy. (c) Some organisms of the detritus food chain are prey to animals of the grazing food chain (e.g. earthworms eaten by birds), and the dead remains of grazing food chain organisms form detritus that is decomposed by detritus food chain organisms.
