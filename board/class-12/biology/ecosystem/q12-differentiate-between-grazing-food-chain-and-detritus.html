Differentiate between grazing food chain and detritus food chain.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q202 marks
Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
(a) Differentiate between grazing food chain and detritus food chain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Grazing food chain: begins with living green plants (producers); energy comes from the sun via photosynthesis; e.g. Grass→ Goat→ Man. Detritus food chain: begins with dead organic matter (detritus); made of decomposers/detritivores (fungi, bacteria, earthworm); energy comes from detritus; e.g. Leaf litter → Earthworm→ Bird. In terrestrial ecosystems most energy flows through the DFC.
