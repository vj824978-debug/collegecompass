Describe the carbon cycle in nature.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIIQ243 marksOut of current syllabus
Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Describe the carbon cycle in nature. How does deforestation affect this cycle? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(Out of syllabus; no official answer.) Carbon (about 49% of the dry weight of organisms) cycles between the atmosphere/ocean and living organisms. Producers fix atmospheric CO 2 in photosynthesis into organic matter, which passes along food chains. CO 2 returns to the atmosphere by respiration of plants and animals and by decomposers acting on dead organic matter; some carbon is stored as fossil fuels and sediments and returns by burning of fuels, forest fires and volcanic activity. The oceans hold a large reservoir of dissolved carbon. Deforestation reduces the photosynthetic removal of CO 2 and releases stored carbon by burning/decay of wood, so atmospheric CO2 rises, adding to the greenhouse effect and global warming.
