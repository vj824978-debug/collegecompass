Construct a pyramid of biomass in sea with phytoplankton and fishes.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 3Q273 marks
Question 10 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
(a) Construct a pyramid of biomass in sea with phytoplankton and fishes. Explain giving reasons about the characteristic of the constructed pyramid. (b) In which condition will the pyramid remain always upright?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Diagram: inverted pyramid of biomass with phytoplankton (producers) at the small base and fishes at the broad top. It is inverted because the standing crop (biomass) of phytoplankton at any time is small, as they reproduce fast and are consumed rapidly, while the biomass of fishes accumulates and far exceeds it. (b) The pyramid of energy always remains upright, because energy flow is unidirectional and at each transfer a large part is lost as heat (only about 10% passes to the next level).
