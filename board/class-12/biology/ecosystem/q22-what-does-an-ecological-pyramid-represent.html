What does an ecological pyramid represent?

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ275 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
(a) What does an ecological pyramid represent? State any two limitations that these pyramids have. (b) Describe an inverted pyramid of biomass with the help of an example. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) An ecological pyramid represents the relationship between producers and consumers of successive trophic levels in terms of number, biomass or energy, with producers at the base. Limitations (any two): it does not take into account the same species belonging to two or more trophic levels; it assumes a simple food chain and does not accommodate a food web; saprophytes/decomposers are not given any place in it. (b) In the sea (or a pond), the pyramid of biomass is inverted. Diagram: Phytoplankton (producers) at the small base, then zooplankton, small fishes and large fishes upwards, each level broader. The biomass of fishes far exceeds the small standing crop of phytoplankton, which reproduce rapidly and are eaten quickly.
