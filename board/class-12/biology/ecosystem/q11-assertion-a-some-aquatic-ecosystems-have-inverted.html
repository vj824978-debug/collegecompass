Assertion (A): Some aquatic ecosystems have inverted biomass pyramids.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q161 mark
Question 11 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Assertion (A): Some aquatic ecosystems have inverted biomass pyramids. [1] Reason (R): More energy is required by the organisms occupying higher trophic levels. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. Aquatic biomass pyramids are inverted because fishes outweigh the small standing crop of phytoplankton; energy available actually decreases at higher trophic levels.
