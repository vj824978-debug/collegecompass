CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q212 marks
Question 5 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (B) (i) Construct an ideal pyramid of energy when 10,00,000 Joules of sunlight is available. (ii) Mention the energy obtained by the fourth level of this pyramid.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) (i) Diagram: an upright pyramid of energy. Taking about 1% of sunlight (10,00,000 J) fixed by producers and the 10% law between trophic levels: Producers 10,000 J → Primary consumers 1,000 J→ Secondary consumers 100 J → Tertiary consumers 10 J (each level labelled with its energy). (ii) The fourth level (tertiary consumers) receives 10 J. (If the 10% law is applied directly to the sunlight, the fourth level gets 100 J; examiners generally accept either working if shown.)
