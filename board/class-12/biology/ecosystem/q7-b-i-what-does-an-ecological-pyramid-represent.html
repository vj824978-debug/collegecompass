(b) (i) What does an ecological pyramid represent?

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q233 marks
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
(b) (i) What does an ecological pyramid represent? (ii) The ecological pyramids may have an ‘upright’ or an ‘inverted’ shape. Justify with the help of suitable examples. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(b) (i) An ecological pyramid is a graphic representation of the relationship between organisms at successive trophic levels, in terms of number, biomass or energy, with producers at the base. (ii) Upright: in most terrestrial ecosystems number, biomass and energy decrease from producers upward, e.g. grassland: grasses > herbivores > carnivores. The pyramid of energy is always upright. Inverted: the pyramid of biomass in the sea is inverted, since the biomass of fishes exceeds that of phytoplankton; the pyramid of numbers in a tree ecosystem (one tree supporting many insects) is also inverted.
