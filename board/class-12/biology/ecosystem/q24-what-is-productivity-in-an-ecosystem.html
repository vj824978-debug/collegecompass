What is productivity in an ecosystem?

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIQ243 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
What is productivity in an ecosystem? Explain the relationship between gross primary productivity and net primary productivity in an ecosystem. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Productivity is the rate of biomass production (expressed as g m −2 yr−1 or kcal m−2 yr−1). Gross primary productivity (GPP) is the rate of production of organic matter during photosynthesis. A part of GPP is used by plants in respiration (R); the rest is net primary productivity: GPP − R = NPP. NPP is the biomass available for consumption by heterotrophs (herbivores and decomposers).
