Name and explain the processes earthworm and bacteria carry on detritus.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q122 marks
Question 15 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Name and explain the processes earthworm and bacteria carry on detritus. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Any two: Fragmentation – detritivores such as earthworm break detritus into smaller particles. Catabolism – bacterial and fungal enzymes degrade detritus into simpler inorganic substances. Humification / Mineralisation – microbes form humus and then degrade it to release inorganic nutrients.
