Which one of the following is not a characteristic feature of “humus” that is formed during decomposition of…

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 2Q91 mark
Question 8 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Which one of the following is not a characteristic feature of “humus” that is formed during decomposition of detritus? [1] (A) amorphous, colloidal, dark coloured substance (B) amorphous, colloidal, light coloured substance (C) substance resistant to microbial action (D) colloidal substance
Answer
Answer as text
(B) amorphous, colloidal, light coloured substance. Humus is dark coloured, amorphous and colloidal, and is highly resistant to microbial action. (Source key: (A).)
