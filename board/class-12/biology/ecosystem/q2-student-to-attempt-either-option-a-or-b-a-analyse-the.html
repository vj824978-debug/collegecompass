Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B): (A) Analyse the following ecosystems and discuss, which will be more productive in terms of primary productivity: A young forest, a natural old forest, a shallow polluted lake. OR (B) Differentiate between Net primary productivity and Gross primary productivity in an ecosystem.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) The young forest is the most productive: its actively growing trees fix more carbon than they lose in respiration, so biomass accumulates fast (high NPP). The natural old forest is a mature, stable community where most of the GPP is used up in respiration, so its net productivity is moderate. The shallow polluted lake has the lowest useful productivity, as pollution reduces light penetration and dissolved oxygen and harms the producers. OR (B) Gross primary productivity (GPP) is the total rate of production of organic matter by producers during photosynthesis. Net primary productivity (NPP) is what remains after the producers’ respiratory losses (R): NPP = GPP − R. NPP is the biomass actually available to heterotrophs (herbivores and decomposers).
