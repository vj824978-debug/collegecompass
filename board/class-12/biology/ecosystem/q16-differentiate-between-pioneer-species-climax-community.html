Differentiate between “Pioneer-species”; “Climax-community” and “Seres”. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(Out of syllabus; no official answer.) Pioneer species: the first species to colonise a bare area (e.g. lichens on rock, phytoplankton in water). Climax community : the final, stable community in near equilibrium with the environment, reached at the end of succession. Seres: the entire sequence of transitional communities that successively replace one another from pioneer to climax (each stage is a seral stage).
