What makes humus a reservoir of nutrients?

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 2Q92 marks
Question 17 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
What makes humus a reservoir of nutrients? Name and write about the process humus undergoes that enriches the soil. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Humus is dark, amorphous and colloidal and is highly resistant to microbial action, so it decomposes very slowly and holds nutrients, acting as a reservoir. It undergoes mineralisation: microbes degrade humus and release inorganic nutrients (N, P, etc.) into the soil.
