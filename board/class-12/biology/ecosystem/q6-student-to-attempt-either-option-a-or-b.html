Question 6 · Class 12 Biology · Ecosystem
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (A) Construct a pyramid of biomass starting with phytoplankton, label its three trophic levels. Is the pyramid upright or inverted? Justify your answer. OR (B) Draw a pyramid of number where a large population of insects feed upon a very big tree. The insects in turn, are eaten by small birds which in turn are fed upon by big birds.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Diagram: an inverted pyramid of biomass with phytoplankton (producers) at the narrow base, zooplankton (primary consumers) above, and fishes (secondary consumers) at the broad top. It is inverted because the standing crop of phytoplankton at any time is small (they are eaten rapidly and reproduce fast), while the longer-lived consumers, especially fishes, accumulate a larger biomass. OR (B) Diagram: pyramid of number with levels labelled Tree (producer, 1) → Insects (primary consumers, very many)→ Small birds (secondary consumers, fewer)→ Big birds (tertiary consumers, fewest). The base (one tree) is narrow and the insect level is widest, so the pyramid is inverted at the base (spindle shaped).
