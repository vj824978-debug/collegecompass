State the principle involved in separation of DNA fragments using gel electrophoresis.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 2Q172 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) State the principle involved in separation of DNA fragments using gel electrophoresis. (b) How are DNA fragments visualized once they are separated by gel electrophoresis?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) DNA fragments are negatively charged and, under an electric field, move through the agarose gel matrix towards the anode; the gel acts as a sieve, so fragments separate according to size – the smaller the fragment, the farther it moves. (b) The gel is stained with ethidium bromide and exposed to UV light; the DNA appears as bright orange bands.
