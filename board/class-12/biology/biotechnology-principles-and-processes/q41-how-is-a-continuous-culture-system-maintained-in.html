How is a continuous culture system maintained in bioreactors and why?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ102 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
How is a continuous culture system maintained in bioreactors and why?
Answer
Answer as text
The used medium is drained out from one side of the bioreactor while fresh medium is added from the other side. This keeps the cells in their physiologically most active log/exponential phase, producing a larger biomass and higher yields of the desired protein.
