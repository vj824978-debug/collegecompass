Why must a cell be made ‘competent’ in biotechnology experiments?

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 2Q243 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) Why must a cell be made ‘competent’ in biotechnology experiments? How does calcium ion help in doing so? (b) State the role of “biolistic gun” in biotechnology experiments.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) DNA is a hydrophilic molecule and cannot pass through cell membranes, so the host cell must be made competent to take up DNA. Treating bacterial cells with a specific concentration of divalent calcium ions increases the efficiency with which DNA enters the bacterium through pores in its cell wall (followed by incubation on ice, heat shock at 42 ◦C and back on ice). (b) The biolistic (gene) gun introduces alien DNA directly into plant cells: cells are bombarded with high-velocity micro-particles of gold or tungsten coated with DNA. It is a vector-less method of gene transfer.
