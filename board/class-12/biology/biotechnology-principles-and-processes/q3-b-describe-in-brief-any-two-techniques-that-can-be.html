Question as text
(B) Describe in brief any two techniques that can be utilised to transfer recombinant DNA into the host cell directly without using any vector.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Any two: Microinjection: recombinant DNA is injected directly into the nucleus of an animal cell with a fine micro-needle. Biolistics (gene gun): plant cells are bombarded with high-velocity micro-particles of gold or tungsten coated with DNA. Electroporation: short electric pulses make temporary pores in the cell membrane through which DNA enters.
