How does EcoRI specifically act on DNA molecule?

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q92 marks
Question 39 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
How does EcoRI specifically act on DNA molecule? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
EcoRI inspects the DNA and binds only where it finds the palindromic sequence 5′-GAATTC-3′. It cuts each strand between G and A, a little away from the centre of the palindrome, leaving single-stranded overhangs (sticky ends) that facilitate the action of DNA ligase.
