Differentiate between Recombinant DNA and cDNA.

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 3Q283 marks
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(A) Differentiate between Recombinant DNA and cDNA. (B) Explain the method to increase the competency of the bacterial cell membrane to take up recombinant DNA.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Recombinant DNA: formed by joining DNA from two different sources (gene of interest + vector) using restriction enzymes and ligase. cDNA: single/double-stranded DNA synthesised from an mRNA template by reverse transcriptase; it carries only expressed sequences and no introns. (B) Bacterial cells are treated with a specific concentration of a divalent cation such as calcium (CaCl2), which increases the efficiency with which DNA enters through pores in the cell wall. The cells are incubated with recombinant DNA on ice, given a brief heat shock at 42 ◦C, and then placed back on ice, so the DNA is taken up.
