Given below is the restriction site of a restriction endonuclease Pst-I and the cleavage sites on a DNA molecule. 5′ C — T — G — C — A↓ G 3′ 3′ G — A — C — G — T↓ C 5′ ↑ Choose the option that gives the correct resultant fragments by the action of the enzyme Pst-I. (A) 5′ C – T – G C-A -G 3 ′ 3′– G – A – C – G – T C 5 ′ (B) 5′ C – T G – C – A – G 3 ′ 3′ G – A – G – C T – C 5 ′ (C) 5′ C – T – G – C A – G 3 ′ 3′ G – A – C – G T – C 5 ′ (D) 5′ C – T – G – C – A 3 ′ G 3′ G A – C – G – T – C 5 ′
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Pst-I cuts between A and G on the upper strand and between G and A on the lower strand, giving 5′-C-T-G-C-A (3′ overhang) + G-3′ on one strand and 3′-G + A-C-G-T-C-5′ on the other.
