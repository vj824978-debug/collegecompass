All cloning vectors do have a ‘selectable marker’.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q102 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
All cloning vectors do have a ‘selectable marker’. Describe its role in recombinant DNA-technology.
Answer
Answer as text
A selectable marker (e.g. antibiotic-resistance genes for ampicillin, tetracycline, kanamycin) helps identify and eliminate non-transformants and selectively permits the growth of transformants (cells that have taken up the vector/recombinant DNA).
