Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) Given below is the stepwise schematic representation of the process of electrophoresis. Identify the ‘alphabets’ representing (i) Anode end (ii) smallest/lightest DNA strand in the matrix (iii) Agarose gel (b) What is elution? State the importance of elution in this process.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Anode end – S; (ii) smallest/lightest DNA strand – R; (iii) agarose gel – T. (b) Elution is cutting out the separated DNA bands from the agarose gel and extracting the DNA from the gel piece. It yields purified DNA fragments that are used to construct recombinant DNA by joining them with cloning vectors.
