CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q315 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) (i) Draw a schematic diagram of the cloning vector pBR 322 and label (1) Bam HI site (2) gene for ampicillin resistance (3) ‘ori’ (4) ‘rop’ gene. (ii) State the role of ‘rop’ gene. (iii) A cloning vector does not have a selectable marker. How will it affect the process of cloning? (iv) Why is insertional inactivation preferred over the use of selectable markers in cloning vectors?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Diagram: circular pBR322 plasmid labelled with (1) BamHI site (within tet R), (2) ampR gene, (3) ori, (4) rop gene; other sites such as EcoRI, HindIII, SalI, PstI, PvuI, PvuII may also be shown. (ii) rop codes for the proteins involved in replication of the plasmid; it controls (keeps low) the copy number of pBR322. (iii) Without a selectable marker, transformants cannot be identified and selected from nontransformants (which are the majority), so cloning cannot be carried out efficiently. (iv) In insertional inactivation, the foreign DNA is inserted within a marker gene (e.g.lacZ coding for β-galactosidase), inactivating it; recombinants are then identified directly by a colour reaction (white vs blue colonies on a chromogenic substrate). This avoids the cumbersome simultaneous plating on two antibiotic media.
