Which one of the following enzymes a fungal cell should be treated with to get the DNA along with other macro molecules released from it? (A) Isozymes (B) Cellulose (C) Ribonuclease (D) Chitinase
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Chitinase – it digests chitin of the fungal cell wall, releasing DNA and other macromolecules.
