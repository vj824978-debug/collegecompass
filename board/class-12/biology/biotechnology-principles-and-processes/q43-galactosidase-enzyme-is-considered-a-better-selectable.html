β-galactosidase enzyme is considered a better selectable marker.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIQ102 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
β-galactosidase enzyme is considered a better selectable marker. Justify the statement.
Answer
Answer as text
The lacZ gene for β-galactosidase contains the cloning site. Insertion of foreign DNA inactivates it (insertional inactivation): on a chromogenic substrate, recombinant colonies give no colour (white), while non-recombinants produce blue colonies. Recombinants are thus identified in a single step, avoiding the cumbersome simultaneous plating on two antibiotic plates.
