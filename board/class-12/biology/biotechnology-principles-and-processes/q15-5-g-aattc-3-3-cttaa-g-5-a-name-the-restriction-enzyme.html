5′ – G↓AATTC – 3′ 3′ – CTTAA↑ G – 5′ (a) Name the restriction enzyme that recognises the given specific sequence of bases. What are such sequence of bases referred to as? [1] (b) What are the arrows in the given figure indicating? Write the result obtained thereafter. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) EcoRI; such sequences are called palindromic nucleotide sequences. (b) The arrows mark the cleavage sites, between G and A on each strand. Result: two fragments with sticky ends: 5′-G AATTC-3 ′ 3′-CTTAA G-5 ′
