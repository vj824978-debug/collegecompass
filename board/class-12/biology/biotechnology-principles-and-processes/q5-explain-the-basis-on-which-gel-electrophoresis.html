Explain the basis on which gel electrophoresis technique works.

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 1Q273 marks
Question 5 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
Explain the basis on which gel electrophoresis technique works. Write any two ways the products obtained through this technique can be utilised.
Answer
Answer as text
Basis: DNA fragments are negatively charged, so in an electric field they move through an agarose gel towards the anode; the gel matrix acts as a sieve, so fragments separate by size, smaller ones moving farther. Uses (any two): separated bands are eluted (cut out and extracted) and used to make recombinant DNA by joining with cloning vectors; checking the progress of restriction digestion; DNA fingerprinting; detecting genetic disorders/mutations.
