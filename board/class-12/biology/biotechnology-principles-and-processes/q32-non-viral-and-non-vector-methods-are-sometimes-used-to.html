Question as text
Non viral and non vector methods are sometimes used to transfer genes or alien DNA into a plant cell. Explain one such method used in genetic engineering.
Answer
Answer as text
Biolistics (gene gun) method: plant cells are bombarded with high-velocity micro-particles of gold or tungsten coated with the alien DNA; the particles penetrate the cell wall and membrane and carry the DNA into the cell, where it can integrate into the genome. (Microinjection or electroporation may also be described.)
