What is an origin of replication in a chromosome?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIIQ92 marks
Question 44 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
What is an origin of replication in a chromosome? State its function.
Answer
Answer as text
It is the sequence (point on DNA) from where replication starts. Any piece of DNA linked to it can be made to replicate in the host cell; it also controls the copy number of the linked DNA.
