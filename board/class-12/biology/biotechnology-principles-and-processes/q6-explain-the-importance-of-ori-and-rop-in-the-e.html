Question 6 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) Explain the importance of ‘ori’ and ‘rop’ in the E. coli cloning vector shown in the above given diagram. (b) Differentiate between exonucleases and endonucleases.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) ori: sequence where replication starts; any DNA linked to it can replicate in the host cell, and it controls the copy number of the linked DNA. rop: codes for the proteins involved in replication of the plasmid (Rop protein regulates/limits the plasmid copy number). (b) Exonucleases remove nucleotides one by one from the free ends (5′ or 3′) of a DNA strand. Endonucleases make cuts at specific positions within the DNA strand and do not need free ends (e.g. restriction endonucleases such as EcoRI).
