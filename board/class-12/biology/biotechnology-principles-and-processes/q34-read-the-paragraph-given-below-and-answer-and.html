CBSE 2022Term II Outside Delhi Set 1Q135 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) Read the paragraph given below and answer and questions that follow: Enzyme Taq polymerase, is extracted from a eubacterial microorganism Thermus aquaticus from Yellowstone National Park in Montana, USA and isolated by Chienet al. (1976). Taq polymerase successfully replaced the DNA polymerase from E.coli that was being used in PCR earlier and this shift revolutionised the PCR technique. (i) Taq polymerase after its discovery replaced E.coli DNA polymerase in PCR technique. Explain giving reasons why was the need felt for the change? (ii) What is a primer and its importance in PCR? (iii) Write the importance of PCR as a diagnostic tool.
Answer
Answer as text
(a)(i) In PCR the DNA is repeatedly heated to about 94 ◦C to denature it; E. coli DNA polymerase is denatured at this temperature and had to be added afresh after every cycle. Taq polymerase is thermostable, remains active through repeated heating, so the process could be automated. (ii) A primer is a small, chemically synthesised oligonucleotide complementary to a region of the template DNA. DNA polymerase can only extend an existing primer, so primers start DNA synthesis and determine the region to be amplified. (iii) PCR detects very low amounts of DNA/RNA of pathogens (e.g. HIV in suspected AIDS patients) early, before symptoms appear, and detects mutations in genes in suspected cancer patients and other genetic disorders.
