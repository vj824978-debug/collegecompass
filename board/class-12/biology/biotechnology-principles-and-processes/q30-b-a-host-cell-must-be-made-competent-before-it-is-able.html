(b) A host cell must be made competent, before it is able to receive an rDNA. Justify.
Answer
Answer as text
(b) DNA is a hydrophilic molecule and cannot pass through the cell membrane on its own. The host cell is therefore made competent (e.g. by treatment with divalent calcium ions, followed by incubation on ice and heat shock at 42 ◦C) so that the rDNA can enter the cell; only then can it be maintained, replicated and expressed in the host.
