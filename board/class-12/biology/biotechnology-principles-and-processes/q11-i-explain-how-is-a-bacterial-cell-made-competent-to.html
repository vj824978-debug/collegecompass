(i) Explain how is a bacterial cell made ‘competent’ to take up recombinant DNA from the medium.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q325 marks
Question 11 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(A) (i) Explain how is a bacterial cell made ‘competent’ to take up recombinant DNA from the medium. (ii) Explain the steps of amplification of gene of interest using PCR technique.
Answer
Answer as text
(A)(i) DNA is hydrophilic and cannot pass through the cell membrane, so the cell is made competent: cells are treated with a specific concentration of divalent cations (calcium), then incubated with recombinant DNA on ice, given a heat shock at 42 ◦C and placed back on ice; the DNA enters the cell. (ii) PCR steps (repeated for many cycles): Denaturation: the double-stranded DNA is heated to a high temperature (about 94 ◦C) to separate its strands. Annealing: at a lower temperature, two sets of primers (short chemically synthesised oligonucleotides) bind to the complementary regions of the single strands. Extension: thermostable Taq DNA polymerase (from Thermus aquaticus ) extends the primers using deoxynucleotides (about 72◦C). Repeating the cycle about 30 times amplifies the gene of interest up to about a billion copies.
