Write the palindromic nucleotide sequence for following sequence of DNA segment: 5′− GAATTC− 3′ (b) Name the…

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 1Q223 marks
Question 4 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) Write the palindromic nucleotide sequence for following sequence of DNA segment: 5′− GAATTC− 3′ (b) Name the restriction endonuclease that recognizes this sequence. (c) How are sticky ends produced? Mention their role.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) 5′–GAATTC–3′ / 3′–CTTAAG–5′ (reads the same on both strands in the 5 ′→3′ direction). (b) EcoRI. (c) The restriction enzyme cuts each strand a little away from the centre of the palindrome but between the same two bases (G and A) on both strands, leaving short single-stranded overhangs (sticky ends). Role: sticky ends pair by hydrogen bonds with complementary ends cut by the same enzyme; DNA ligase then seals them, joining foreign DNA to the vector to form recombinant DNA.
