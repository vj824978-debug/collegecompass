Explain the roles of the following with the help of an example each in recombinant DNA technology: (a) Restriction Enzymes (b) Plasmids
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Restriction enzymes recognise specific palindromic nucleotide sequences and cut DNA at specific sites, producing fragments with sticky ends that can be joined to vector DNA; e.g. EcoRI, HindII, BamHI. (b) Plasmids act as vectors: they carry the alien/foreign gene (gene of interest) into the host and replicate autonomously there, making many copies; e.g. pBR322, Ti plasmid of Agrobacterium tumefaciens.
