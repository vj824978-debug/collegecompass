How are DNA fragments visualised during gel-electrophoresis?

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ102 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
How are DNA fragments visualised during gel-electrophoresis? What is elution?
Answer
Answer as text
The separated DNA fragments are stained with ethidium bromide and exposed to UV light; they appear as bright orange bands. Elution is cutting out the separated bands from the agarose gel and extracting the DNA from the gel piece.
