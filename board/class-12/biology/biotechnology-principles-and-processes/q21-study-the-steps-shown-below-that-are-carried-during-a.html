Question as text
Study the steps shown below, that are carried during a specific technique: (a) Identify the steps ‘A’ and ‘D’ in the diagram. (b) What does ‘B’ represent? (c) Write what is ‘C’? Name its source organism. (d) Mention the use of this technique in molecular diagnostics.
Answer
Answer as text
The technique is PCR. (a) A – Denaturation; D – Extension. (b) B – Primers (short oligonucleotides annealed to the complementary regions of the template). (c) C – Taq polymerase, a thermostable DNA polymerase; source: Thermus aquaticus . (d) PCR detects very low amounts of DNA/RNA of pathogens (e.g. HIV in suspected AIDS patients) before symptoms appear, and detects mutations in genes in suspected cancer patients and other genetic disorders.
