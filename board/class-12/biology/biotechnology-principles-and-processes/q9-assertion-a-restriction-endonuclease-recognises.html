Assertion (A): Restriction endonuclease recognises sequence in DNA and cuts them.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q161 mark
Question 9 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
Assertion (A): Restriction endonuclease recognises sequence in DNA and cuts them. Reason (R): Palindromic sequence has two unique recognition sites PstI and PvuI recognised by restriction endonuclease. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. Restriction endonucleases recognise and cut specific palindromic sequences; PstI and PvuI are restriction enzymes with their own recognition sites in pBR322, not two sites on a single palindrome.
