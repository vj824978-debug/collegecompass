Assertion (A): Plasmids are autonomously replicating circular extra–chromosomal DNA.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q141 mark
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
Assertion (A): Plasmids are autonomously replicating circular extra–chromosomal DNA. Reason (R): Plasmids are usually present in Eukaryotic cells. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. Plasmids are autonomously replicating circular extrachromosomal DNA found mainly in bacteria (prokaryotes), not usually in eukaryotic cells.
