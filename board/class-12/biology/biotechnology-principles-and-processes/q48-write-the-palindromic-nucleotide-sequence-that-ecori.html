Question as text
Write the palindromic nucleotide sequence that EcoRI reads, and indicate the site of its action.
Answer
Answer as text
5′-G↓AATTC-3′ 3′-CTTAA↑G-5′ EcoRI cuts each strand between G and A, leaving AATT sticky ends.
