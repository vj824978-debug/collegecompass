Describe the formation of recombinant DNA by the action of EcoRI.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ213 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
Describe the formation of recombinant DNA by the action of EcoRI. OR Describe the process of amplification of “gene of interest” using PCR technique.
Answer
Answer as text
EcoRI recognises its palindrome 5′-GAATTC-3′ on both the vector DNA and the foreign DNA and cuts both strands between G and A, a little away from the centre of the palindrome. This leaves single-stranded sticky ends (AATT overhangs) on both DNAs. Being cut by the same enzyme, the sticky ends of vector and foreign DNA are complementary; they pair by hydrogen bonds and DNA ligase joins them, forming recombinant DNA. OR Denaturation: the DNA is heated so its two strands separate, each acting as a template. Annealing: two sets of primers bind to the complementary regions of the templates. Extension: Taq polymerase (thermostable, from Thermus aquaticus ) extends the primers using deoxynucleotides. Repeating the cycle (about 30 times) amplifies the gene of interest about a billion times.
