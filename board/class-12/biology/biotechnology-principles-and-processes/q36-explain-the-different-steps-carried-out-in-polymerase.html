Question 36 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
(a) Explain the different steps carried out in Polymerase Chain Reaction, and the specific roles of the enzymes used. (b) Mention application of PCR in the field of (i) Biotechnology (ii) Diagnostics
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Denaturation: the double-stranded DNA is heated (about 94 ◦C) to separate its strands. Annealing: two sets of primers bind to complementary regions of the single strands at a lower temperature. Extension: the enzyme Taq polymerase – a thermostable DNA polymerase from Thermus aquaticus – extends the primers using deoxynucleotides, copying the region between them; it stays active at high temperatures so it works through repeated cycles. About 30 cycles amplify the DNA about a billion times. (b)(i) Biotechnology: amplifies the gene of interest, which can then be ligated with a vector for cloning (to make rDNA). (ii) Diagnostics: early detection of very low amounts of pathogens (e.g. HIV in suspected AIDS patients), detection of mutations in suspected cancer patients and other genetic disorders.
