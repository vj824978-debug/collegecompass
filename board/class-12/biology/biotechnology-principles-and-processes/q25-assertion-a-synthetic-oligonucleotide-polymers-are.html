Assertion (A): Synthetic oligonucleotide polymers are used during Annealing in a PCR.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q141 mark
Question 25 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
Question as text
Assertion (A): Synthetic oligonucleotide polymers are used during Annealing in a PCR. Reason (R): The primers bind to the double stranded DNA at their complementary regions. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). The synthetic oligonucleotide primers anneal to the complementary regions of the (denatured) template DNA.
