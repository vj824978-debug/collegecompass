Question as text
The number of autosomes present in a human secondary spermatocyte (A) 44 (B) 22 (C) 23 (D) 46
Answer
Answer as text
(B) 22. The secondary spermatocyte is haploid (23 chromosomes = 22 autosomes + X or Y).
