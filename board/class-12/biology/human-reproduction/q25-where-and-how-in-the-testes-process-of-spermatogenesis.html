Where and how in the testes process of spermatogenesis occur in humans?

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q255 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(a) Where and how in the testes process of spermatogenesis occur in humans? (b) Draw diagram of human sperm and label four parts. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) In the seminiferous tubules of the testes, whose wall has spermatogonia and Sertoli cells; begins at puberty. Spermatogonia (2n) multiply by mitosis; some become primary spermatocytes that undergo meiosis I to give two haploid secondary spermatocytes; meiosis II gives four haploid spermatids; spermatids change into spermatozoa by spermiogenesis (heads embedded in Sertoli cells, which nourish them) and are released by spermiation. (b) Diagram: human sperm; labels (any four): acrosome, nucleus, head, neck, middle piece (mitochondria), tail, plasma membrane.
