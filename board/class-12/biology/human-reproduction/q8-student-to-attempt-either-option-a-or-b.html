Question 8 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (B) The reproductive cycle in the female primates is called menstrual cycle. The first menstruation begins at puberty. Answer the following questions: (i) Name the four phases of menstrual cycle in a proper sequence. (ii) How long does the menstrual phase last in a menstrual cycle? (iii) When and why hormones estrogen and progesterone reach their peak levels respectively, in the menstrual cycle? (iv) Give the significance of LH surge.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Menstrual phase → follicular (proliferative) phase → ovulatory phase → luteal (secretory) phase. (ii) About 3–5 days. (iii) Estrogen peaks in the late follicular phase, just before ovulation (around day 12–14), because the growing follicles and the mature Graafian follicle secrete increasing amounts of it. Progesterone peaks in the mid-luteal phase (around day 21–23), because the corpus luteum formed after ovulation secretes large amounts of it to maintain the endometrium. (iv) The LH surge at mid-cycle (about day 14) induces rupture of the Graafian follicle and release of the secondary oocyte (ovulation); LH also causes formation of the corpus luteum from the ruptured follicle.
