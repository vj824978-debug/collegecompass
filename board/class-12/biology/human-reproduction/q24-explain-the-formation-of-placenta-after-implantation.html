Explain the formation of placenta after implantation in a human female.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q133 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Explain the formation of placenta after implantation in a human female. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
After implantation the trophoblast forms finger-like projections called chorionic villi, which are surrounded by uterine tissue and maternal blood. The chorionic villi and uterine tissue become interdigitated and jointly form the placenta, a structural and functional unit between the developing foetus and the mother. It is connected to the foetus by the umbilical cord, which transports substances to and from the embryo.
