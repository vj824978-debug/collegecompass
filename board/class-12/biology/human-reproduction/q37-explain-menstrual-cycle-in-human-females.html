Explain menstrual cycle in human females.

CBSE 2018Q245 marks
Question 37 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(a) Explain menstrual cycle in human females. (b) How can the scientific understanding of the menstrual cycle of human females help as a contraceptive measure?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Menstrual phase (days 1–5): breakdown of the endometrium and its blood vessels gives menstrual flow, when the ovum is not fertilised. Follicular (proliferative) phase: under FSH and LH primary follicles grow into a mature Graafian follicle; estrogen from the follicles makes the endometrium regenerate by proliferation. Ovulatory phase: the mid-cycle LH surge (about day 14) ruptures the Graafian follicle and releases the secondary oocyte. Luteal (secretory) phase: the ruptured follicle forms the corpus luteum, which secretes large amounts of progesterone to maintain the endometrium; without fertilisation it degenerates and the next cycle begins. (b) Ovulation occurs around mid-cycle (day 14), so the chance of fertilisation is highest during days 10–17. Couples can avoid coitus during this fertile period (periodic abstinence/rhythm method) to prevent conception.
