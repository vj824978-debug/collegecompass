Assertion (A): Corpus luteum secretes the hormone progesterone.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q131 mark
Question 7 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Assertion (A): Corpus luteum secretes the hormone progesterone. Reason (R): Hormone progesterone is essential for maintenance of the endometrium. (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). (B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). (C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. (D) (A) is false, but (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). The corpus luteum secretes large amounts of progesterone, which is needed to maintain the endometrium. (Some keys argue (B), since R states the role of progesterone rather than why the corpus luteum secretes it; the source’s answer (A) is kept.)
