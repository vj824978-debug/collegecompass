Construct a flow chart exhibiting sequential events of oogenesis.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ143 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Construct a flow chart exhibiting sequential events of oogenesis. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Flow chart (foetal life) oogonia (2n) multiply by mitosis → primary oocytes (2n) begin meiosis I, arrested at prophase I → primary follicle (primary oocyte with granulosa cells) → secondary follicle (more granulosa layers, theca) → tertiary follicle (fluid-filled antrum); primary oocyte completes meiosis I→ secondary oocyte (n) + first polar body → Graafian follicle→ ovulation of secondary oocyte → after sperm entry meiosis II → ovum (n) + second polar body.
