CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q243 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Study the transverse section of human ovary given below and answer the questions that follow: [3] (a) Name the hormone that helps in the growth of A → B→ C. (b) Name the hormone secreted by A and B. (c) State the role of the hormone produced by D.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) FSH (follicle stimulating hormone), aided by LH. (b) Estrogen. (c) D is the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone; progesterone maintains the endometrium, needed for implantation of the embryo and maintenance of pregnancy.
