Explain the process of fertilization in human.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIQ255 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(a) Explain the process of fertilization in human. (b) Name the embryonic stage that gets implanted in human females. Explain the process of implantation.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) The sperm contacts the zona pellucida of the ovum (in the ampullary region), inducing membrane changes that block other sperms; acrosomal enzymes help the sperm enter the ovum cytoplasm; this induces completion of meiosis II of the secondary oocyte, forming the second polar body and the haploid ovum (ootid); the haploid nuclei of sperm and ovum fuse to form a diploid zygote. (b) Blastocyst. Its cells are arranged into an outer trophoblast and an inner cell mass; the trophoblast attaches to the endometrium, uterine cells divide rapidly and cover the blastocyst, so it becomes embedded in the endometrium (implantation); the inner cell mass then forms the embryo.
