Given below is the schematic representation of spermatogenesis in human males: Choose the option that shows the correct labelling of ‘I’, ‘II’, ‘III’ and ‘IV’ in the given diagram. I II III IV (A) spermatozoa spermatid sec. spermatocyte spermatogonia (B) spermatid spermatogonia sec. spermatocyte spermatozoa (C) spermatogonia sec. spermatocyte spermatozoa spermatid (D) spermatogonia sec. spermatocyte spermatid spermatozoa
Answer
Answer as text
(D) I – spermatogonia, II – sec. spermatocyte, III – spermatid, IV – spermatozoa.
