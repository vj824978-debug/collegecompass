Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (A) (i) Give a schematic representation of oogenesis in human females. (ii) Mention the number of chromosomes at each stage. Correlate the life phases of the individual with the stages of the process.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Schematic: Oogonium (2n) mitosis−−−−→oogonia→ primary oocyte (2n), enters meiosis I and is arrested at prophase I (in primary follicle) → [puberty] completes meiosis I in tertiary follicle → secondary oocyte (n) + first polar body (n) → secondary oocyte released at ovulation from Graafian follicle (arrested at metaphase II) → on sperm entry completes meiosis II → ovum (n) + second polar body (n). (ii) Oogonium 46 and primary oocyte 46 – formed during foetal life (primary oocytes stay arrested from birth till puberty); secondary oocyte 23 and first polar body 23 – formed from puberty onwards, one each cycle until menopause; ovum 23 and second polar body 23 – formed only after fertilisation in the fallopian tube.
