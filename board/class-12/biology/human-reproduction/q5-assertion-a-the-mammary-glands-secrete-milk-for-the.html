Assertion (A): The mammary glands secrete milk for the nourishment of the young ones.

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 3Q131 mark
Question 5 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Assertion (A): The mammary glands secrete milk for the nourishment of the young ones. Reason (R): These are modified sweat glands. (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). (B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A). (C) (A) is true, but (R) is false. (D) (A) is false, but (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). Mammary glands secrete milk to nourish the young; they are modified sweat glands, i.e. glands specialised for secretion. (Some keys argue (B), since the origin of the glands does not by itself explain the function; the source’s answer (A) is kept.)
