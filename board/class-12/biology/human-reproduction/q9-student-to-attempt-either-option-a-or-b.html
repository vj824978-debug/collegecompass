Question 9 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (i) Identify ‘P’ and ‘Q’ labelled in the diagram. (ii) Specify the source of the hormone ‘P’ and ‘Q’ marked in the diagram. OR (i) Identify ‘P’ and ‘Q’ labelled in the above diagram. (ii) Write down the role of hormone ‘P’ in both males and females.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) P – estrogen; Q – progesterone. (ii) Estrogen: growing ovarian follicles (Graafian follicle); progesterone: corpus luteum of the ovary. OR (B) (i) P – LH (luteinising hormone); Q – FSH (follicle stimulating hormone). (ii) LH in females: LH surge induces ovulation and formation (maintenance) of the corpus luteum. In males: LH acts on Leydig cells and stimulates synthesis and secretion of androgens (testosterone).
