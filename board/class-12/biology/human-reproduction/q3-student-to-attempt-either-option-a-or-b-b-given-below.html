Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B): (B) Given below is the diagram of a human ovum surrounded by a few sperms. Observe the diagram and answer the questions that follow: (i) Compare the fate of sperms ‘P’, ‘Q’ and ‘R’ shown in the diagram. (ii) Write the role of Zona pellucida in this process. (iii) Analyse the changes occurring in the ovum after the entry of sperm. (iv) How acrosome and middle piece of a human sperm are able to play an important role in human fertilization?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) P has passed through the corona radiata and is in contact with the zona pellucida/ovum membrane, so P will fertilise the ovum. Q (among the corona radiata cells) and R (outside the corona radiata) have not reached the zona pellucida; once P enters, entry of other sperms is blocked, so Q and R fail to fertilise and degenerate. (ii) The zona pellucida is the layer the sperm binds to; contact of a sperm with it induces changes in the membrane/zona that block the entry of additional sperms, preventing polyspermy. (iii) Changes blocking other sperms; the secondary oocyte completes meiosis II, forming the haploid ovum (ootid) and the second polar body; the haploid nuclei of sperm and ovum fuse to form a diploid zygote. (iv) Acrosome: cap over the head filled with enzymes (e.g. hyaluronidase, acrosin) that help the sperm penetrate the corona radiata and zona pellucida and enter the ovum. Middle piece: has numerous mitochondria that supply energy for tail movement, i.e. sperm motility needed to reach the ovum.
