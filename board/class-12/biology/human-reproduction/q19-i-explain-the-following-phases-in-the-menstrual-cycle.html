CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 2Q315 marks
Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(a) (i) Explain the following phases in the menstrual cycle of a human female: (1) Menstruation (2) Follicular phase (3) Luteal phase [3] (ii) ‘A proper understanding of the menstrual cycle can help immensely in family planning.’ Do you agree with the statement? Provide reasons for your answer. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) (1) Menstruation: days 1–5 (3–5 days); breakdown of the endometrial lining and its blood vessels forms the menstrual flow; occurs only if the released ovum is not fertilised. (2) Follicular phase: primary follicles grow into a mature Graafian follicle under FSH and LH; follicles secrete estrogen and the endometrium regenerates through proliferation; ends with the LH surge and ovulation (about day 14). (3) Luteal phase: the ruptured Graafian follicle becomes the corpus luteum, which secretes large amounts of progesterone to maintain the endometrium for implantation; without fertilisation the corpus luteum degenerates and menstruation starts. (ii) Yes. Ovulation occurs around day 14, so days 10–17 are the fertile period. Avoiding coitus in this period (periodic abstinence/natural method) prevents conception, while timing coitus in it improves the chance of conception.
