(i) Describe the events of spermatogenesis with the help of a schematic diagram.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 2Q325 marks
Question 15 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(a) (i) Describe the events of spermatogenesis with the help of a schematic diagram. [5] (ii) Explain the role of hormones in spermatogenesis.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) In seminiferous tubules from puberty: spermatogonium (2n = 46) multiplies by mitosis → some differentiate into primary spermatocytes (2n)→ meiosis I→ two secondary spermatocytes (n = 23) → meiosis II → four haploid spermatids → spermiogenesis→ spermatozoa; sperm heads embedded in Sertoli cells are released from the tubule by spermiation. (Schematic diagram showing these stages with chromosome numbers.) (ii) At puberty GnRH from the hypothalamus increases and acts on the anterior pituitary to secrete LH and FSH. LH acts on Leydig cells and stimulates synthesis and secretion of androgens, which stimulate spermatogenesis. FSH acts on Sertoli cells and stimulates secretion of factors that help in spermiogenesis.
