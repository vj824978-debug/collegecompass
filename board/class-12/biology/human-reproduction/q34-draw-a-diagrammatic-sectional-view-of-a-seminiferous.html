Draw a diagrammatic sectional view of a seminiferous tubule (enlarged) in humans and label its parts.
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: enlarged sectional view of a seminiferous tubule. Labels: spermatogonium, primary spermatocyte, secondary spermatocyte, spermatid, spermatozoa, Sertoli cell (also basement membrane/interstitial cells).
