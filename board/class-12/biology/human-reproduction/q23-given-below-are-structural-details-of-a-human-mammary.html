Question 23 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Given below are structural details of a human mammary gland: [1] (i) The glandular tissue in the breast has 15-20 clusters of cells called alveoli. (ii) The milk is stored in the lumen of alveoli. (iii) The alveoli join to form the mammary ducts. (iv) Mammary ampulla is connected to lactiferous ducts. Choose the option that gives the correct detail of human mammary gland. (A) (i) and (ii) (B) (ii) and (iii) (C) (ii) and (iv) (D) (i) and (iii)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) (ii) and (iv). The alveoli secrete milk, which is stored in their lumen; mammary ampulla is connected to the lactiferous duct. (i) is wrong (the 15–20 units are mammary lobes containing alveoli) and (iii) is wrong (alveoli open into mammary tubules, which join to form mammary ducts).
