CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 3Q335 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(b) (i) Describe the development of placenta during pregnancy in a human female. [2] (ii) Explain its role. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) After implantation the trophoblast develops finger-like projections, the chorionic villi, surrounded by uterine tissue and maternal blood. The chorionic villi and uterine tissue interdigitate to form the placenta, a structural and functional unit between foetus and mother. (ii) Supplies oxygen and nutrients to the embryo; removes carbon dioxide and excretory wastes; is connected to the embryo by the umbilical cord, which carries substances to and from it; acts as an endocrine tissue producing hCG, hPL, estrogens and progestogens that maintain pregnancy.
