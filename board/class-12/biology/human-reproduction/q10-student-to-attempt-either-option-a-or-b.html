CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 2Q315 marks
Question 10 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (A) (i) Draw a diagrammatic sectional view of human seminiferous tubule (enlarged) and label the following: (a) cell that undergoes spermiogenesis (b) cell that nourish male gametes (c) cell which undergoes meiosis I and meiosis II. (ii) State what is seminal plasma. Mention two constituents of seminal plasma. How is it different from semen? OR (B) (i) Mention the event that induces the completion of the meiotic division of the secondary oocyte in humans. (ii) Trace the journey of the zygote until its implantation inside the uterus.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) Diagram: enlarged sectional view of a seminiferous tubule showing spermatogonium, primary spermatocyte, secondary spermatocyte, spermatid, spermatozoa and Sertoli cell (interstitial cells outside). Labels asked: (a) spermatid; (b) Sertoli cell; (c) primary spermatocyte undergoes meiosis I and secondary spermatocyte undergoes meiosis II. (Source key: spermatogonium for (c).) (ii) Seminal plasma is the fluid formed by the secretions of the male accessory glands (seminal vesicles, prostate, bulbourethral glands). Constituents: fructose, calcium, certain enzymes (any two). Semen = seminal plasma + sperms; seminal plasma lacks sperms. OR (B) (i) Entry of the sperm into the secondary oocyte (fertilisation) induces completion of its meiosis II, forming the ovum and the second polar body. (ii) Zygote formed in the ampullary region of the fallopian tube → mitotic divisions (cleavage) as it moves through the isthmus towards the uterus, forming 2, 4, 8, 16-celled blastomeres → morula (8–16 blastomeres) → morula becomes a blastocyst (outer trophoblast, inner cell mass) on reaching the uterus → trophoblast attaches to the endometrium, uterine cells divide rapidly and cover the blastocyst, so it becomes embedded in the endometrium (implantation).
