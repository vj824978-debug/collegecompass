Where does fertilization occur in the oviduct of a human female?

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q275 marks
Question 29 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Where does fertilization occur in the oviduct of a human female? Describe the process of fertilization. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
Site: ampullary region (ampullary–isthmic junction) of the fallopian tube. Process: the sperm comes in contact with the zona pellucida of the ovum, inducing changes in the membrane that block the entry of other sperms; secretions (enzymes) of the acrosome help the sperm enter the cytoplasm of the ovum; this induces completion of meiosis II of the secondary oocyte, forming the second polar body and a haploid ovum (ootid); the haploid nuclei of sperm and ovum fuse to form a diploid zygote.
