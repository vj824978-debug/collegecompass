Name the gonadotropins in human.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 2Q275 marks
Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Name the gonadotropins in human. Explain their role in human male and female, respectively. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
Gonadotropins: LH (luteinising hormone) and FSH (follicle stimulating hormone), secreted by the anterior pituitary under GnRH. Male: LH acts on Leydig cells and stimulates synthesis and secretion of androgens, which stimulate spermatogenesis; FSH acts on Sertoli cells and stimulates secretion of factors that help spermiogenesis. Female: FSH stimulates growth of ovarian follicles into Graafian follicles and their estrogen secretion; LH (with FSH) also supports follicle growth; the mid-cycle LH surge induces ovulation, and LH causes formation and maintenance of the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone.
