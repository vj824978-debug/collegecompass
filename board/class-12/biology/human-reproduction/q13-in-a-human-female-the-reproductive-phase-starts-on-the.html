Question 13 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
In a human female, the reproductive phase starts on the onset of puberty and ceases around middle age of the female. Study the graph given below regarding menstrual cycle and answer the questions that follow: (a) Name the hormones and their source organ, which are responsible for menstrual cycle at puberty. [1] (b) For successful pregnancy, at what phase of the menstrual cycle an early embryo (upto 3 blastomeres) should be Implanted in the Uterus (IUT) of a human female who has opted for Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)? Support your answer with a reason. [1] (c) Name the hormone and its source organ responsible for the events occurring during proliferative phase of menstrual cycle. Explain the event. [2] OR (c) In a normal human female, why does menstruation only occurs if the released ovum is not fertilised? Explain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) FSH and LH from the anterior pituitary (under GnRH from the hypothalamus); estrogen and progesterone from the ovary (growing follicles and corpus luteum respectively). (b) Luteal (secretory) phase; progesterone from the corpus luteum keeps the endometrium thick, glandular and vascular, i.e. receptive for implantation. (c) Estrogen, secreted by the growing ovarian follicles (ovary). During the proliferative phase the primary follicles grow into a mature Graafian follicle and, under estrogen, the endometrium regenerates through proliferation (thickens, with glands and blood vessels). OR (c) After ovulation the corpus luteum secretes progesterone, which maintains the endometrium. If the ovum is fertilised, the corpus luteum is maintained (hCG), progesterone stays high and the endometrium is retained, so there is no menstruation. If it is not fertilised, the corpus luteum degenerates, progesterone falls, the endometrium breaks down and menstruation follows.
