CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q315 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
(b) (i) Explain the formation of placenta after the implantation in a human female. (ii) Draw a diagram showing human foetus within the uterus and label any four parts in it.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) After implantation the trophoblast forms finger-like chorionic villi surrounded by uterine tissue and maternal blood; villi and uterine tissue interdigitate to form the placenta, the structural and functional unit between foetus and mother, connected to the foetus by the umbilical cord. (ii) Diagram: human foetus within the uterus. Labels (any four): placenta, umbilical cord, chorionic villi, amniotic cavity (amniotic fluid), yolk sac, uterine wall/endometrium, myometrium, cervix with plug of mucus.
