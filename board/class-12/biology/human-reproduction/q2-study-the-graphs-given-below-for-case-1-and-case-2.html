Question 2 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Study the graphs given below for Case 1 and Case 2 showing different levels of certain hormones and answer the question that follows: (a) Which hormone is responsible for the peak observed in Case 1 and Case 2? Write one function of that hormone. (b) Write changes that take place in the ovary and uterus during follicular phase. Student to attempt either sub-part (c) or (d): (c) Name the hormone Q of Case 2. Write one function of hormone Q. OR (d) Which structure in the ovary will remain functional in Case 2? How is it formed?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) LH (luteinising hormone) of the anterior pituitary; its mid-cycle surge induces rupture of the Graafian follicle and release of the ovum (ovulation); it also converts the ruptured follicle into the corpus luteum. (b) Ovary: under FSH and LH, primary follicles grow into a fully mature Graafian follicle, and the growing follicles secrete increasing amounts of estrogen. Uterus: under estrogen the endometrium regenerates through proliferation (becomes thick and vascular). (c) Q is progesterone (secreted by the corpus luteum). Function: maintains the endometrium, which is essential for implantation and maintenance of pregnancy. OR (d) The corpus luteum remains functional (Case 2 is a pregnancy, shown by rising hCG). It is formed after ovulation from the remains of the ruptured Graafian follicle, under the influence of LH.
