Draw a diagram of a mature human sperm.

CBSE 2018Q113 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Biology · Human Reproduction
Question as text
Draw a diagram of a mature human sperm. Label any three parts and write their functions. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Diagram: mature human sperm. Labels with functions (any three): acrosome – enzymes that help fertilisation (penetration) of ovum; nucleus – contains chromosomal (genetic) material; middle piece – mitochondria, the energy source for swimming; tail – movement of sperm; plasma membrane – envelope of the sperm.
