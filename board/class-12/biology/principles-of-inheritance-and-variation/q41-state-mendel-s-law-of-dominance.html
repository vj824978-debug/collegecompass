State Mendel’s law of dominance.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q143 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
State Mendel’s law of dominance. How did he deduce the law? Explain with the help of a suitable example. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Law of dominance: characters are controlled by discrete units (factors) that occur in pairs; in a dissimilar pair one factor (dominant) is expressed and the other (recessive) is masked. Mendel deduced it from monohybrid crosses: round (RR) × wrinkled (rr) seeds → F1 all round (Rr), only one parental trait appearing; on selfing F 1, the F 2 had round and wrinkled seeds in 3 : 1 ratio (1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr). The recessive trait, hidden in F 1, reappeared unchanged in F 2.
