CBSE 2019Delhi Set IIIQ21 mark
Question 54 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Write the number of chromosomes body cells of honey bee workers and drone have. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
Workers: 32 (diploid); drones: 16 (haploid).
