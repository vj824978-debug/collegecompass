Question as text
By using Punnett square depict the genotypes and phenotypes of test crosses (where green pod colour (G) is dominant over yellow pod colour (g)) in Garden pea with unknown genotype. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Test cross: plant of unknown genotype (green pod) × recessive yellow (gg). Case I: GG× gg→ gametes G / g→ all Gg, green pods⇒ unknown plant is homozygous (GG). Case II: Gg× gg: G g g Gg (green) gg (yellow) 1 green : 1 yellow ⇒ unknown plant is heterozygous (Gg).
