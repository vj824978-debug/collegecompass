Question as text
Study the cross given below: Identify the abnormalities ‘1’ and ‘2’ in the offsprings of a cross done between a couple and distinguish between them.
Answer
Answer as text
Offspring 1: (22 + XY) sperm + (22 + X) ovum → 44 + XXY = Klinefelter’s syndrome . Offspring 2: (22 + O) sperm + (22 + X) ovum → 44 + XO = T urner’s syndrome. Difference: Klinefelter’s – male, extra X (47 chromosomes); masculine with feminine features (gynaecomastia), sterile. Turner’s – female, one X missing (45 chromosomes); sterile with rudimentary ovaries, short stature, lack of secondary sexual characters.
