CBSE 2018Q255 marks
Question 61 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(a) Write the scientific name of the organism Thomas Hunt Morgan and his colleagues worked with for their experiments. Explain the correlation between linkage and recombination with respect to genes as studied by them. (b) How did Sturtevant explain gene mapping while working with Morgan? [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Drosophila melanogaster. Genes located close together on the same chromosome are linked and do not segregate independently (F 2 ratio deviated from 9 : 3 : 3 : 1). Tightly linked genes show few recombinants (high parental frequency, e.g. white–yellow 1.3%); loosely linked genes (farther apart) show more recombinants (e.g. white–miniature 37.2%). Thus recombination frequency depends on the distance between linked genes. (b) Sturtevant used the frequency of recombination between gene pairs on the same chromosome as a measure of the distance between them and ‘mapped’ their positions on the chromosome (genetic/linkage map).
