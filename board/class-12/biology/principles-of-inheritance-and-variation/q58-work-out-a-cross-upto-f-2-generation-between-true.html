Question 58 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(a) Work out a cross upto F 2-generation between true breeding tall pea plants bearing violet flowers and dwarf pea plants bearing white flowers. (b) Explain Mendel’s laws of segregation and independent assortment on the basis of the cross. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Tall violet TTVV × dwarf white ttvv→ F1 TtVv (tall violet). F 1 selfed; gametes TV, Tv, tV, tv: TV Tv tV tv TV TTVV TTVv TtVV TtVv Tv TTVv TTvv TtVv Ttvv tV TtVV TtVv ttVV ttVv tv TtVv Ttvv ttVv ttvv F2: 9 tall violet : 3 tall white : 3 dwarf violet : 1 dwarf white. (b) Segregation: alleles of each pair (T/t, V/v) separate during gamete formation so each gamete gets only one of them; each trait separately gives 3 : 1 in F 2. Independent assortment: segregation of T/t is independent of V/v, giving four types of gametes in equal proportion and new combinations (tall white, dwarf violet) in a 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio.
