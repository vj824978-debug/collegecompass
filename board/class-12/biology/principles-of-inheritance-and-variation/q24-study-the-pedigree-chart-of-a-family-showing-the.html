CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q91 mark
Question 24 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Study the pedigree chart of a family showing the inheritance pattern of a certain disorder. Select the option that correctly identifies the nature of the trait depicted in the pedigree chart. [1] (A) Dominant X-linked (B) Recessive X-linked (C) Autosomal dominant (D) Autosomal recessive
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Autosomal recessive. Unaffected parents have affected children, so the trait is recessive; an affected daughter is born to an unaffected father, which rules out X-linked recessive inheritance. (Source key: (B) Recessive X-linked.)
