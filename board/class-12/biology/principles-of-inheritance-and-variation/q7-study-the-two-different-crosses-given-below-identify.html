Study the two different crosses given below: Identify the abnormalities 1 and 2 in the offsprings of given crosses and distinguish between them.
Answer
Answer as text
Cross I: (23 + X) ovum fertilised by (22 + X) or (22 + Y) sperm → 45 + XX / 45 + XY (47 chromosomes) = autosomal trisomy, e.g. Down’s syndrome (trisomy 21). Cross II: (22 + XX) ovum + (22 + Y) sperm → 44 + XXY = Klinefelter’s syndrome . Difference: Down’s – extra autosome 21, both sexes; short stature, small round head, furrowed tongue, palm crease, mental retardation. Klinefelter’s – extra sex chromosome X, males only; gynaecomastia, sterility.
