Name the Scientists and write how did they explain Mendel’s laws after the chromosomes were discovered.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 2Q62 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Name the Scientists and write how did they explain Mendel’s laws after the chromosomes were discovered. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri (chromosomal theory of inheritance). They noted that the behaviour of chromosomes parallels that of Mendel’s factors: chromosomes occur in pairs like genes; homologous chromosomes separate during meiosis so each gamete gets one of each pair (segregation); different pairs segregate independently (independent assortment); fertilisation restores the pairs. Genes are therefore located on chromosomes.
