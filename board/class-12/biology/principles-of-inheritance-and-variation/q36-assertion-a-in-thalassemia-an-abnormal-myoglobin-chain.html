Assertion (A): In Thalassemia an abnormal myoglobin chain is synthesized due to a gene defect.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q161 mark
Question 36 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Assertion (A): In Thalassemia an abnormal myoglobin chain is synthesized due to a gene defect. [1] Reason (R): α-Thalassemia is controlled by genes HBA1 and HBA2 on chromosome 16. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A is false, R is true. In thalassaemia fewer globin chains of haemoglobin are made (a quantitative defect), not an abnormal myoglobin; α-thalassaemia is controlled by HBA1 and HBA2 on chromosome 16.
