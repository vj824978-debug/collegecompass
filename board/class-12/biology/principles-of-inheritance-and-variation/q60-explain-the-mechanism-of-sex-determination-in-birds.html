Explain the mechanism of ‘sex determination’ in birds.

Question 60 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Explain the mechanism of ‘sex determination’ in birds. How does it differ from that of human beings?
Answer
Answer as text
(Out of syllabus; no source answer.) Birds have ZW–ZZ (female heterogamety): females are ZW and produce two kinds of eggs (Z and W); males are ZZ and produce one kind of sperm (Z). The egg decides the sex: Z egg + Z sperm → ZZ male; W egg + Z sperm → ZW female. In humans it is XX–XY (male heterogamety): the male (XY) produces two kinds of sperm, the female (XX) one kind of egg, so the sperm (father) decides the sex.
