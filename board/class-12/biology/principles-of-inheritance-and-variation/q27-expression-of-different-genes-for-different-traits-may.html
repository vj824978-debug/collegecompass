Expression of different genes for different traits may show dominance, incomplete dominance or codominance.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 2Q263 marks
Question 27 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Expression of different genes for different traits may show dominance, incomplete dominance or codominance. Write about expression of such genes with the help of one example each. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Dominance: in a heterozygote one allele is fully expressed and masks the other; e.g. pea flower colour – Vv is violet like VV. Incomplete dominance: the heterozygote is intermediate; e.g. snapdragon – RR red × rr white gives Rr pink. Co-dominance: both alleles are fully expressed together in the heterozygote; e.g. ABO blood group – I AI B has both antigen A and B (group AB).
