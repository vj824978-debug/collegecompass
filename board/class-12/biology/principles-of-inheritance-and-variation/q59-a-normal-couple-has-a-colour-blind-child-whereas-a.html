CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIIQ255 marks
Question 59 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
A normal couple has a colour blind child, whereas a child suffering from thalassemia is born to normal parents. Compare the pattern of inheritance of these two traits in the said cases. State the reasons how is it possible. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
Colour blindness – X-linked recessive. The normal mother is a carrier (XX c), father normal (XY): XX c× XY→ XX (normal daughter), XX c (carrier daughter), XY (normal son), X cY (colour-blind son). So the colour-blind child is a son (50% of sons), getting the allele from the mother; criss-cross inheritance, no father-to-son transmission. Thalassaemia – autosomal recessive. Both normal parents are carriers (Hb AHbT× HbAHbT)→ 1 normal : 2 carriers : 1 Hb THbT thalassaemic (25%); a son or daughter can be affected. Comparison: colour blindness needs only the mother to be a carrier and mainly affects sons; thalassaemia needs both parents to be carriers and affects both sexes equally.
