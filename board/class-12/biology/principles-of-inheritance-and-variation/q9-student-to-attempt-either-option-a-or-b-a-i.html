Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B): (A) (i) Haemophilia and red green colourblindness are usually observed in men. Why? (ii) Perform a cross(es) where haemophilic daughter(s) and haemophilic son(s) are produced in same ratio.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) Both are X-linked recessive disorders. A male (XY) has only one X, so a single recessive allele (received from his mother) is expressed; a female needs the allele on both X chromosomes, so she is usually only a carrier. (ii) Carrier female X HXh× haemophilic male X hY: Xh Y XH XHXh (carrier daughter) X HY (normal son) Xh XhXh (haemophilic daughter) X hY (haemophilic son) Haemophilic daughters : haemophilic sons = 1 : 1 (25% each).
