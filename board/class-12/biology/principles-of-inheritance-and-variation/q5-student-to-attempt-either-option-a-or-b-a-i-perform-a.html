Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B): (A) (i) Perform a cross between two sickle cell carriers. What ratio is obtained between carrier, disease free and diseased individuals in F 1 progeny? Name the nitrogenous base substituted, in the haemoglobin molecule in this disease. (ii) Explain the difference in inheritance pattern of flower colour in garden pea plant and snapdragon plant with the help of monohybrid crosses.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) (i) Hb AHbS× HbAHbS→ gametes Hb A, Hb S from each → 1 Hb AHbA (disease free) : 2 HbAHbS (carrier) : 1 Hb SHbS (diseased). Ratio carrier : disease free : diseased = 2 : 1 : 1. Base substitution: in the 6th codon of the β-globin gene, adenine is replaced (GAG → GUG in mRNA; A by T in DNA), so glutamic acid is replaced by valine. (ii) Garden pea – complete dominance: violet (VV) × white (vv) → F1 all violet (Vv); F 2 3 violet : 1 white (genotypes 1 VV : 2 Vv : 1 vv). Snapdragon – incomplete dominance: red (RR) × white (rr)→ F1 all pink (Rr); F 2 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white, phenotypic ratio = genotypic ratio.
