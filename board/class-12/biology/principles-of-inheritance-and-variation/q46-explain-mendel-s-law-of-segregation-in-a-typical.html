Explain Mendel’s “Law of segregation” in a typical monohybrid cross with the help of a suitable example.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 3Q193 marks
Question 46 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Explain Mendel’s “Law of segregation” in a typical monohybrid cross with the help of a suitable example. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Law of segregation: the two alleles of a character do not blend; they separate during gamete formation so each gamete gets only one of the two. Example: tall (TT) × dwarf (tt)→ gametes T and t → F1 Tt (tall). F 1 produces T and t gametes in equal proportion; selfing gives F 2 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt, i.e. 3 tall : 1 dwarf. The dwarf trait reappears unchanged, showing the alleles segregated.
