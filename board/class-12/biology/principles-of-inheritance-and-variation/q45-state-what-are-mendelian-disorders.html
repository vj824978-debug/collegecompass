State what are Mendelian disorders.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 3Q112 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
State what are Mendelian disorders. Both Thalassemia and colour blindness categorised as Mendelian disorders. Justify. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Mendelian disorders are caused by alteration or mutation in a single gene and are transmitted according to Mendel’s principles (traceable by pedigree analysis). Thalassaemia: autosomal recessive single-gene defect (HBA1/HBA2 or HBB) reducing globin synthesis. Colour blindness: X-linked recessive single-gene defect affecting red/green cones. Since each is due to one gene and follows Mendelian inheritance, both are Mendelian disorders.
