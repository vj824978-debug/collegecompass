Differentiate between the pattern of inheritance in humans of the blood diseases, haemophilia and thalassemia.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q153 marks
Question 48 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Differentiate between the pattern of inheritance in humans of the blood diseases, haemophilia and thalassemia. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Haemophilia: X-linked (sex-linked) recessive; gene on X chromosome; mostly males affected (one allele enough); passed from carrier mother to sons, never father to son; affected females are rare (mother carrier, father haemophilic). Thalassaemia: autosomal recessive (chromosome 16 or 11); males and females equally affected; disease only in homozygous recessive; two carrier (heterozygous) parents have a 25% chance of an affected child.
