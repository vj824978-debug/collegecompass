Question 34 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
With reference to flower colour two independent crosses were made, one between true breeding garden pea plants and another between true breeding Antirrhinum plants. Write the phenotypes of their F1–progeny. Justify your answer giving reason. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Garden pea: true-breeding violet (VV) × white (vv) → F1 all violet (Vv), because the violet allele is completely dominant over white. Antirrhinum: red (RR) × white (rr) → F1 all pink (Rr), because R is incompletely dominant over r and the heterozygote shows an intermediate phenotype.
