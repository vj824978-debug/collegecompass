Question 29 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(a) Work out a dihybrid cross upto F 2 generation between pea plants bearing violet coloured axial flowers and white coloured terminal flowers using Punnet’s square. Give their F 2 phenotypic ratio. State the Mendel’s law of inheritance that was derived from such a cross. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Violet axial VVAA × white terminal vvaa → F1 VvAa (violet axial); F 1 selfed, each gives gametes VA, Va, vA, va: VA Va vA va VA VVAA VVAa VvAA VvAa Va VVAa VVaa VvAa Vvaa vA VvAA VvAa vvAA vvAa va VvAa Vvaa vvAa vvaa F2 phenotypes: 9 violet axial : 3 violet terminal : 3 white axial : 1 white terminal. Law of Independent Assortment: when two pairs of traits are combined in a hybrid, segregation of one pair of characters is independent of the other pair.
