CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ153 marks
Question 52 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Compare in any three ways the chromosomal theory of inheritance as proposed by Sutton and Boveri with that of experimental results on pea plant presented by Mendel. [3] OR (a) Explain linkage and recombination as put forth by T.H. Morgan based on his observations with Drosophila melanogaster crossing experiment. (b) Write the basis on which Alfred Sturtevant explained gene mapping. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Any three parallels: (1) chromosomes occur in pairs – Mendel’s factors occur in pairs; (2) homologous chromosomes segregate at gamete formation so only one of each pair goes to a gamete – factors segregate and a gamete gets one of each pair; (3) one pair of chromosomes segregates independently of another – one pair of factors assorts independently of another. OR (a) Linkage: physical association of two or more genes on a chromosome so they do not segregate independently; in Morgan’s dihybrid crosses the F 2 ratio deviated from 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. Recombination: generation of non-parental gene combinations; tightly linked genes show low recombination (white–yellow 1.3%), loosely linked genes show high recombination (white–miniature 37.2%). (b) Sturtevant used the recombination frequency between gene pairs on the same chromosome as a measure of the distance between them and so mapped their positions on the chromosome.
