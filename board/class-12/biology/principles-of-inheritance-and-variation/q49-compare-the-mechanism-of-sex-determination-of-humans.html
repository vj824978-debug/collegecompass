Question as text
(a) Compare the mechanism of sex determination of humans with that of honey bees, with respect to chromosome number. (b) How is the gamete formation comparable in the above two cases? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Humans: XX–XY type; female 44 + XX, male 44 + XY (both 46, diploid); sex decided by the sperm (X or Y). Honey bee: haplodiploid; females (from fertilised eggs) are diploid with 32 chromosomes, males (drones, from unfertilised eggs) are haploid with 16 chromosomes; sex decided by fertilisation or not. (b) Humans: both sexes form gametes by meiosis; male makes two kinds of sperm (22 + X, 22 + Y), female one kind of egg (22 + X). Honey bee: females form eggs by meiosis (16 chromosomes), males form sperm by mitosis (16 chromosomes), so all sperms are alike.
