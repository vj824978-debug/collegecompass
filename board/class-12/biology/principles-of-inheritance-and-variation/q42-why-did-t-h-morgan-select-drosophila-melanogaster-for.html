CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q265 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(a) Why did T.H. Morgan select Drosophila melanogaster for his experiments? (b) How did he disprove Mendelian dihybrid F 2 phenotypic ratio of 9 : 3 : 3 : 1? Explain giving reasons. [2+3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Any four: completes its life cycle in about two weeks; a single mating gives a large number of progeny; can be grown easily on simple synthetic medium in the lab; males and females are clearly distinguishable; many easily visible hereditary variations. (b) Morgan made dihybrid crosses in Drosophila for X-linked genes (e.g. yellow body, white eye × brown body, red eye) and intercrossed the F 1. The two genes did not assort independently: parental combinations were far more frequent than recombinants, so the F2 ratio deviated significantly from 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. Reason – the genes are on the same chromosome (linkage); recombinants arise only by crossing over. Tightly linked genes (white, yellow) gave only 1.3% recombinants; loosely linked genes (white, miniature wing) gave 37.2%.
