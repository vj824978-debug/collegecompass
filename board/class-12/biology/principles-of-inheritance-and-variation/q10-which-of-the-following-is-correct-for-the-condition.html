Which of the following is correct for the condition when plant YyRr is back crossed with the double recessive parent? (A) 9:3:3:1 ratio of phenotypes only (B) 9:3:3:1 ratio of genotypes only (C) 1:1:1:1 ratio of phenotypes only (D) 1:1:1:1 ratio of phenotypes and genotypes
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 1:1:1:1 ratio of phenotypes and genotypes (YyRr × yyrr→ YyRr : Yyrr : yyRr : yyrr).
