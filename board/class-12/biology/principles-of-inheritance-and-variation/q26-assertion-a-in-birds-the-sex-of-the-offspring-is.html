Assertion (A): In birds, the sex of the offspring is determined by males.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q151 mark
Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Assertion (A): In birds, the sex of the offspring is determined by males. [1] Reason (R): Males are homogametic while females are heterogametic. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A is false, R is true. In birds males are ZZ (homogametic) and females ZW (heterogametic), so the female decides the sex of the offspring.
