Explain the cause responsible in a human to have sex chromosomes as ‘XXY’ instead of ‘XX’ or ‘XY’.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q72 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(a) Explain the cause responsible in a human to have sex chromosomes as ‘XXY’ instead of ‘XX’ or ‘XY’. (b) List any two ways such individuals are different from the normal being. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Non-disjunction of sex chromosomes during meiosis gives an XX egg (or XY sperm); its fertilisation by a Y sperm (or X egg) gives 44 + XXY – Klinefelter’s syndrome (extra X in a male). (b) Any two: gynaecomastia (breast development); sterility; overall masculine body with some feminine features.
