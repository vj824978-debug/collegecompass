Study the pedigree analysis given below: Answer the following questions: (A) Write genotypes of c and d. (B) Identify whether the trait is (i) Sex-linked or autosomal (ii) Dominant or recessive
Answer
Answer as text
(A) c: aa (affected). d: AA or Aa (unaffected daughter of normal parents who have an affected child). (B) (i) Autosomal – an unaffected father has an affected daughter, which is not possible for X-linked recessive inheritance. (ii) Recessive – unaffected parents have affected children (trait skips a generation).
