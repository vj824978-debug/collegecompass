Question as text
A pea plant with purple flowers, when crossed with a plant with white, produced 50 plants with only purple flowers. On selfing these plants produced 482 plants with purple flower and 162 with white flowers. Explain the pattern of inheritance with the help of Punnet square. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Purple (PP)× white (pp)→ F1 all purple (Pp): purple is dominant. F 1 selfed, Pp× Pp: P p P PP (purple) Pp (purple) p Pp (purple) pp (white) F2: 3 purple : 1 white (482 : 162 ≈ 3 : 1); genotypes 1 PP : 2 Pp : 1 pp. The recessive trait hidden in F 1 reappears in F 2 because the alleles segregate during gamete formation (law of dominance and law of segregation).
