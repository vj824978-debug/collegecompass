Assertion (A): ‘XX – XY’ type of sex-determination mechanism is an example of male heterogamety.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 2Q131 mark
Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Assertion (A): ‘XX – XY’ type of sex-determination mechanism is an example of male heterogamety. Reason (R): In birds male heterogamety is observed as males produce two different types of gametes. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A is true, R is false. XX–XY is male heterogamety, but in birds the female (ZW) is heterogametic and males (ZZ) are homogametic.
