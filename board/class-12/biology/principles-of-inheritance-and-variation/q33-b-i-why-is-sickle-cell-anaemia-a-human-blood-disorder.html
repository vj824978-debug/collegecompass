(b) (i) Why is sickle cell anaemia, a human blood disorder so named?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q325 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(b) (i) Why is sickle cell anaemia, a human blood disorder so named? [1] (ii) Explain the genetic basis that results in the expression of this disorder. [2] (iii) Work out a cross to explain how normal parents may have a sickle– cell anaemic child. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(b) (i) Under low oxygen tension the RBCs of affected persons change from biconcave discs to elongated sickle-shaped cells. (ii) Autosomal recessive trait controlled by one pair of alleles Hb A and Hb S; only Hb SHbS homozygotes show the disease, Hb AHbS are carriers. A single base substitution (GAG → GUG) in theβ-globin gene replaces glutamic acid by valine at the 6th position of the β chain; the mutant haemoglobin polymerises at low O 2 and sickles the RBCs. (iii) Carrier parents Hb AHbS × HbAHbS → gametes Hb A, Hb S → 1 Hb AHbA (normal) : 2 HbAHbS (carrier) : 1 Hb SHbS (sickle-cell anaemic). So normal (carrier) parents have a 25% chance of an affected child.
