Question as text
You are asked to find the genotypes of a tall pea plant growing in your school garden. Name the cross and explain how would you confirm the genotypes. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Use a test cross : cross the tall plant (TT or Tt) with a dwarf recessive plant (tt). If TT× tt→ all Tt, tall: the plant is homozygous TT. If Tt× tt→ 1 Tt (tall) : 1 tt (dwarf): the plant is heterozygous Tt.
