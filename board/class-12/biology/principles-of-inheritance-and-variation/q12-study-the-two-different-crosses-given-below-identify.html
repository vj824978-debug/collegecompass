Study the two different crosses given below: Identify the abnormalities ‘1’ and ‘2’ in the offsprings of given crosses and distinguish between them.
Answer
Answer as text
Cross I: (22 + O) ovum + (22 + X) sperm → 44 + XO = T urner’s syndrome. Cross II: (23 + X) ovum + (22 + Y) or (22 + X) sperm → 45 + XY / 45 + XX = autosomal trisomy (e.g. Down’s syndrome). Difference: Turner’s – monosomy of sex chromosome (45 chromosomes), only females; sterile, rudimentary ovaries, short stature. Down’s – extra autosome 21 (47 chromosomes), both sexes; mental retardation, furrowed tongue, palm crease.
