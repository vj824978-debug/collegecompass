Question 23 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
A person with trisomy of 21 st chromosome shows [1] (i) Furrowed tongue (ii) Characteristic palm crease (iii) Rudimentary ovaries (iv) Gynaecomastia Select the correct option from the choices given below: (A) (ii) and (iv) (B) (i), (ii) and (iv) (C) (ii) and (iii) (D) (i) and (ii)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) (i) and (ii) – furrowed tongue and palm crease are features of Down’s syndrome.
