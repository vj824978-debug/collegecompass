CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ255 marks
Question 57 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Mendel crossed a homozygous pea plant having yellow and round seeds with another pea plant bearing green and wrinkled seeds. He found that in some of the F 2 population new combination of parental characters were observed. How will you explain the appearance of a new combination of parental characters in F 2 offsprings? Support your answer with the help of Punnett square. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
P: yellow round (YYRR) × green wrinkled (yyrr) → F1 YyRr (yellow round). F 1 forms four kinds of gametes YR, Yr, yR, yr in equal proportion: YR Yr yR yr YR YYRR YYRr YyRR YyRr Yr YYRr YYrr YyRr Yyrr yR YyRR YyRr yyRR yyRr yr YyRr Yyrr yyRr yyrr F2: 9 yellow round : 3 yellow wrinkled : 3 green round : 1 green wrinkled. Yellow wrinkled and green round are new combinations. Explanation – law of independent assortment: when two pairs of traits are combined in a hybrid, segregation of one pair is independent of the other, so alleles of the two genes combine randomly in gametes.
