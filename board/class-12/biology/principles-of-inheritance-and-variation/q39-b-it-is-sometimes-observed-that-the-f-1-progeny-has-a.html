(b) It is sometimes observed that the F 1 progeny has a phenotype that does not resemble either of the two parents and has intermediate phenotype. Explain by taking a suitable example and working out the cross upto F 2 progeny. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(b) This is incomplete dominance , e.g. flower colour in snapdragon ( Antirrhinum). P: red (RR)× white (rr); gametes R and r; F 1: all Rr, pink (neither parent, intermediate). F1 selfed: Rr × Rr: R r R RR (red) Rr (pink) r Rr (pink) rr (white) F2: phenotypic ratio 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white = genotypic ratio 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr. R is not completely dominant over r, so the heterozygote is intermediate.
