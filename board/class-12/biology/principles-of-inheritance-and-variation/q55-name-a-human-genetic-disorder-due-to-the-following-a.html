Question as text
Name a human genetic disorder due to the following: (a) An additional X-chromosome in a male (b) Deletion of one X-chromosome in a female [1] OR State what does aneuploidy lead to.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Klinefelter’s syndrome. (b) Turner’s syndrome. OR Aneuploidy (gain or loss of a chromosome) leads to individuals with abnormal chromosome number and genetic disorders such as Down’s, Turner’s or Klinefelter’s syndrome.
