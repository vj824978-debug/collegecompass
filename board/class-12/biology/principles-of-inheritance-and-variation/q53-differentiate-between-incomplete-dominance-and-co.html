Differentiate between incomplete dominance and co-dominance.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ265 marks
Question 53 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Differentiate between incomplete dominance and co-dominance. Substantiate your answer with one example of each. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
Incomplete dominance: F 1 resembles neither parent but is intermediate; e.g. snapdragon ( Antirrhinum) red RR× white rr→ F1 pink Rr; F 2 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white. Co-dominance: both alleles express themselves fully in F 1, which resembles both parents; e.g. ABO blood groups: I AI A (A)×I BI B (B)→ gametesI A,I B→ F1 I AI B (group AB) with both A and B antigens on RBCs.
