Identify the category of genetic disorder depicted in the pedigree chart given below: [1] (A) X–Linked recessive (B) X–Linked dominant (C) Autosomal recessive (D) Autosomal dominant
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Autosomal recessive – unaffected parents have affected sons and an affected daughter.
