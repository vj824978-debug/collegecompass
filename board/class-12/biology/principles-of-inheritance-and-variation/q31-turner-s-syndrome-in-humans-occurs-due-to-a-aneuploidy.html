Turner’s syndrome in humans occurs due to (A) Aneuploidy (B) Euploidy (C) Polyploidy (D) Autosomal abnormality

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q71 mark
Question 31 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
Turner’s syndrome in humans occurs due to [1] (A) Aneuploidy (B) Euploidy (C) Polyploidy (D) Autosomal abnormality
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Aneuploidy (44 + XO, monosomy of X).
