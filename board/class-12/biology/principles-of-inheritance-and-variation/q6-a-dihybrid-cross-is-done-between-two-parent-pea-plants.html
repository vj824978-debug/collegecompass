A Dihybrid cross is done between two parent pea plants (pure line) who differ in two pairs of contrasting traits: Seed colour and seed shape. In the F 2 generation the number of phenotypes and genotypes will be: (A) phenotypes = 4; genotypes = 16 (B) phenotypes = 9; genotypes = 14 (C) phenotypes = 4; genotypes = 8 (D) phenotypes = 4; genotypes = 9
Answer
Answer as text
(D) phenotypes = 4; genotypes = 9 (9 : 3 : 3 : 1 phenotypic ratio; 3 × 3 = 9 genotypes).
