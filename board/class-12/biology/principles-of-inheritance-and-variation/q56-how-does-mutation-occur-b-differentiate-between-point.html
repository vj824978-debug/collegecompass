How does mutation occur? (b) Differentiate between point mutation and frame shift mutation.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ163 marks
Question 56 · Class 12 Biology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
Question as text
(a) How does mutation occur? (b) Differentiate between point mutation and frame shift mutation. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Mutation is a change in DNA sequence; it occurs by loss (deletion), gain (insertion/duplication) or change of position of DNA segments or chromosomes, or by change of a base pair – during replication or recombination, or due to mutagens (UV, chemicals). (b) Point mutation: change in a single base pair of DNA (e.g. sickle-cell anaemia). Frame-shift mutation: insertion or deletion of one or two bases, which shifts the reading frame from that point onwards.
