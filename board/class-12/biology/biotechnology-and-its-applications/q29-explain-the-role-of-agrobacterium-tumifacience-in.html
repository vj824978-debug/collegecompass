Explain the role of Agrobacterium tumifacience in developing resistance in tobacco plant against nematode Meloidegyne incognitia. Name the processes responsible for this. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(No answer printed in the source; out of syllabus.) Agrobacterium tumefaciens was used as a vector to introduce nematode-specific genes into the tobacco plant, in such a way that both sense and anti-sense RNA were produced in host cells. These complementary RNAs formed dsRNA, which silenced the specific mRNA of the nematode, so the parasite could not survive in the transgenic plant. Processes: genetic transformation using Agrobacterium (Ti plasmid) vector, and RNA interference (RNAi, gene silencing).
