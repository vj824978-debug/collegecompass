Name (i) a GM cereal crop having enhanced nutritional value, (ii) the nutrient it is rich in.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 3Q172 marks
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(a) Name (i) a GM cereal crop having enhanced nutritional value, (ii) the nutrient it is rich in. [2] (b) State any two benefits of Genetically modified crops.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Golden rice. (ii) Vitamin A (rich in β-carotene). (b) Any two: more tolerance to abiotic stresses (cold, drought, salt, heat); pest resistance, reducing use of chemical pesticides; reduced post-harvest losses; more efficient use of minerals (prevents early exhaustion of soil fertility); enhanced nutritional value.
