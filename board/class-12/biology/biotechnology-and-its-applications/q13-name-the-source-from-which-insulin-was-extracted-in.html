Name the source from which insulin was extracted in earlier times.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q192 marks
Question 13 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(a) Name the source from which insulin was extracted in earlier times. Why is this insulin no more in use by the diabetic patients? [2×1=2] (b) Why does the insulin synthesised in the human body undergo processing whereas the insulin produced by Eli Lilly company does not need to undergo any processing? Explain. [2×1=2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) From the pancreas of slaughtered cattle and pigs. It is not used now because, being a foreign protein, it caused allergy or other reactions in some patients. (b) In the body insulin is made as pro-insulin with an extra C-peptide, which must be removed to form mature insulin. Eli Lilly made chains A and B separately in E. coli and joined them by disulphide bonds, so no C-peptide is present and no processing is needed.
