What are cry-proteins? With the help of a suitable example, explain how it acts as a biological pesticide.

CBSE 2022Term II Outside Delhi Set 1Q93 marks
Question 25 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
What are cry-proteins? With the help of a suitable example, explain how it acts as a biological pesticide. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Cry proteins are insecticidal proteins (Bt toxins) produced as crystals by Bacillus thuringiensis , coded by cry genes. Action: they exist as inactive protoxins; in the alkaline gut of the insect they are solubilised and activated, bind to midgut epithelial cells and create pores, causing swelling, lysis and death of the insect. Example: Bt cotton carries cryIAc and cryIIAb, which control cotton bollworms ( cryIAb in Bt corn controls corn borer).
