Question 4 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(a) The insulin synthesised in our body is different from that synthesised by Eli Lilly company using recombinant DNA technology. Differentiate between them. (b) Why the insulin extracted from an animal source is not in use these days?
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Insulin in our body: made in the β-cells of the pancreas as a single-chain pro-insulin (with an extra C-peptide), which must be processed (C-peptide removed) to give mature insulin. Eli Lilly’s insulin: two DNA sequences for chains A and B were put into plasmids of E. coli ; chains A and B were produced separately, extracted and joined by disulphide bonds. It is formed without a C-peptide, so no processing is needed. (b) Insulin from slaughtered cattle and pigs is a foreign protein; some patients develop allergy or other reactions to it. (Supply from animals is also limited.)
