Why does the insecticidal protein produced by Bacillus thuringiensis not kill the bacterium, but kills the cotton bollworm? Explain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
The Bt toxin exists inside the bacterium as an inactive protoxin, so it does not harm the bacterium. In the alkaline gut of the bollworm the crystals are solubilised and the toxin is activated; it binds to midgut epithelial cells and creates pores, causing swelling and lysis, and the insect dies.
