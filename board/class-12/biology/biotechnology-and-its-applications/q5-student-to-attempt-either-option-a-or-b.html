Question 5 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
Student to attempt either option – (A) or (B). (B) (i) What are transgenic animals? (ii) Why are these animals being produced? Explain any four reasons.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) (i) Transgenic animals are animals whose DNA has been manipulated to possess and express an extra (foreign) gene. (ii) Any four reasons:  Normal physiology and development – to study how genes are regulated and how they affect normal body functions and development (e.g. insulin-like growth factor).  Study of disease – as models of human diseases (cancer, cystic fibrosis, rheumatoid arthritis, Alzheimer’s) to find new treatments.  Biological products – to produce useful proteins (e.g. α-1-antitrypsin for emphysema; humanprotein-enriched milk from cow Rosie).  Vaccine safety – transgenic mice are used to test vaccines (e.g. polio vaccine) before use in humans.  Chemical safety (toxicity) testing – animals carrying genes that make them more sensitive to toxic substances give results in less time.
