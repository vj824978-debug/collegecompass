CBSE 2022Term II Delhi Set 1Q113 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(a) How does a gene therapy involving direct modification of the cells, in order to achieve a therapeutic goal is used in the treatment of ADA deficiency? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Gene therapy corrects a gene defect by delivering a normal gene into the patient’s cells. ADA deficiency is caused by deletion of the gene for adenosine deaminase, needed for the immune system to work. Steps: lymphocytes from the patient’s blood are grown in culture outside the body; a functional ADA cDNA is introduced into them using a retroviral vector; these lymphocytes are returned to the patient. (Source key: lymphocytes taken from and returned to the bone marrow; NCERT: from the patient’s blood.) As these cells are not immortal, periodic infusions are needed. A permanent cure would be to introduce the gene isolated from ADA-producing bone marrow cells into cells at early embryonic stages.
