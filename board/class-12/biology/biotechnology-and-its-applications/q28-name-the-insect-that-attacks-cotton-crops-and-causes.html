Name the insect that attacks cotton crops and causes lot damage to the crop.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q275 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(a) Name the insect that attacks cotton crops and causes lot damage to the crop. How has Bt cotton plants overcome this problem and saved the crop? Explain. (b) Write the role of gene Cry IAb. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Cotton bollworm (larvae of lepidopterans). Bt cotton carries Bt toxin ( cry) genes ( cryIAc, cryIIAb) isolated from Bacillus thuringiensis , so the plant itself makes the insecticidal protein. The protein is an inactive protoxin; when the bollworm eats the plant, the alkaline pH of its gut solubilises and activates it. The active toxin binds to midgut epithelial cells and creates pores, causing swelling and lysis; the insect dies, so the crop is protected without chemical pesticides. (b) The protein coded by cryIAb controls corn borer.
