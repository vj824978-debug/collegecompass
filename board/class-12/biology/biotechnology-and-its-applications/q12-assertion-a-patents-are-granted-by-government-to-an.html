Assertion (A): Patents are granted by government to an inventor.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q151 mark
Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
Assertion (A): Patents are granted by government to an inventor. [1] Reason (R): Patents prevent others from commercial use of an invention. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). A patent is the right granted by a government to an inventor to prevent others from commercial use of the invention.
