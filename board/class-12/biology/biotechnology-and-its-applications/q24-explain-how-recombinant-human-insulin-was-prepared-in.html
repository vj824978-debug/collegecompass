Explain how recombinant human insulin was prepared in 1983 by Eli Lily an American company.

CBSE 2022Term II Delhi Set 2Q103 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
Explain how recombinant human insulin was prepared in 1983 by Eli Lily an American company. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Eli Lilly prepared two DNA sequences corresponding to chains A and B of human insulin and introduced them into plasmids of E. coli to produce the insulin chains. Chains A and B were produced separately, extracted, and combined by creating disulphide bonds to form mature human insulin (no pro-insulin/C-peptide step).
