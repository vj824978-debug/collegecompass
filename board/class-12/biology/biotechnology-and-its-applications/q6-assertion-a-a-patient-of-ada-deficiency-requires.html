Question as text
Assertion (A): A patient of ADA-deficiency requires periodic or repeated infusion of geneticallyengineered lymphocytes. Reason (R): Lymphocytes are not immortal, but have life span. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A). The engineered lymphocytes are not immortal, so infusions must be repeated.
