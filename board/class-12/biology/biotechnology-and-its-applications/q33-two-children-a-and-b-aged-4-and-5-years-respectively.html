CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ223 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
Two children, A and B aged 4 and 5 years respectively visited a hospital with a similar genetic disorder. The girl A was provided enzyme replacement therapy and was advised to revisit periodically for further treatment. The girl, B was, however, given a therapy that did not require revisit for further treatment. (a) Name the ailments the two girls were suffering from? (b) Why did the treatment provided to girl A required repeated visits? (c) How was the girl B cured permanently? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Adenosine deaminase (ADA) deficiency. (b) In enzyme replacement therapy, functional ADA is given by injection; the enzyme acts only for a limited time and the therapy is not completely curative, so repeated visits are needed. (c) As per the CBSE scheme, there is no permanent cure at the age of five; a permanent cure is possible only if the gene isolated from ADA-producing bone marrow cells is introduced into cells at early embryonic stages (the mark for (c) was given to part (b)). Alternatively, girl B may have received bone marrow transplantation, which cures ADA deficiency in some children.
