Why GMOs are so called? List the different ways in which GMO plants have benefitted and have become useful to humans. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Plants, bacteria, fungi and animals whose genes have been altered by manipulation are called genetically modified organisms (GMOs). Benefits of GM plants: more tolerance to abiotic stresses (cold, drought, salt, heat); pest resistance, reducing reliance on chemical pesticides; reduced post-harvest losses; increased efficiency of mineral use (prevents early exhaustion of soil fertility); enhanced nutritional value, e.g. Golden rice rich in vitamin A; tailor-made plants supplying starches, fuels and pharmaceuticals to industry.
