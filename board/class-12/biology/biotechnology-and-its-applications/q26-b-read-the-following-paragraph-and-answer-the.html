Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(b) Read the following paragraph and answer the questions that follow: Biotechnology revolves around the “gene of interest”, with an objective to open various avenues for human welfare in health, medicine, pharma, agriculture etc. using different techniques, tools and processes. One of the breakthroughs of biotechnology in medicine is the gene therapy. (i) Name the human disease for which the gene therapy was used for the first time. (ii) Explain the steps of gene therapy carried to cure the disease using the lymphocytes of the patient. Why is this therapy not a permanent cure of the disease? (iii) Write the possible permanent cure of the disease by the gene therapy that is in progress. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(b) (i) Adenosine deaminase (ADA) deficiency (first clinical gene therapy, 1990, to a 4-year-old girl). (ii) Lymphocytes from the patient’s blood are grown in culture outside the body; a functional ADA cDNA is introduced into them using a retroviral vector; these lymphocytes are returned to the patient. (Source key: lymphocytes taken from and returned to the bone marrow; NCERT: from the patient’s blood.) It is not permanent because these cells are not immortal, so periodic infusions of engineered lymphocytes are needed. (iii) Introduce the gene isolated from ADA-producing bone marrow cells into cells at early embryonic stages.
