A corn farmer has perennial problem of corn-borer infestation in his crop.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ112 marks
Question 34 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
A corn farmer has perennial problem of corn-borer infestation in his crop. Being environmentally conscious he does not want to spray insecticides. Suggest solution based on your knowledge of biotechnology. Write the steps to be carried out to achieve it. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Grow Bt corn. Steps: isolate the Bt toxin gene cryIAb from Bacillus thuringiensis ; introduce it into corn (e.g. with an Agrobacterium/Ti plasmid vector) to raise transgenic corn plants; the corn produces the Bt protoxin; when the corn borer feeds, the toxin is activated in its alkaline gut and kills it.
