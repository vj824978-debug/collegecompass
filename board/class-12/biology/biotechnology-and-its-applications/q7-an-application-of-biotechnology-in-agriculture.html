Question as text
An application of biotechnology in agriculture involves the production of pest resistant plants, using “cry” gene from a bacterium, Bacillus thuringiensis . (a) Proteins coded by which specific Bt. toxin gene control corn borer? (b) How does Bt. toxin produced by the bacterium kill the insect? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) cryIAb. (b) The bacterium makes Bt toxin as an inactive protoxin (crystal protein), so it is not harmed. When an insect eats it, the alkaline pH of the insect gut solubilises the crystals and converts the protoxin into active toxin. The active toxin binds to the surface of midgut epithelial cells and creates pores, causing cell swelling and lysis; the insect dies.
