“Cotton bollworms enjoy feeding on cotton plants, but get killed when feed on Bt cotton plant”.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q213 marks
Question 31 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
“Cotton bollworms enjoy feeding on cotton plants, but get killed when feed on Bt cotton plant”. Justify the statement. [3] OR (a) Mention the cause of ADA deficiency in humans. (b) How is gene therapy carried out to treat the patients suffering from this disease? (c) State the probability of a permanent cure of this disease. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Bt cotton carries cry genes (cryIAc, cryIIAb) from Bacillus thuringiensis and makes the Bt toxin protein as an inactive protoxin. When a bollworm feeds on the plant, the alkaline pH of its gut activates the toxin; the toxin binds to midgut epithelial cells and creates pores, causing swelling, lysis and death. Ordinary cotton has no toxin, so bollworms feed on it freely. OR (a) Deletion of (or mutation in) the gene for adenosine deaminase; without ADA the immune system cannot function. (b) Lymphocytes from the patient’s blood are grown in culture; a functional ADA cDNA is introduced using a retroviral vector; the lymphocytes are returned to the patient. (Source key: lymphocytes taken from and returned to the bone marrow; NCERT: from the patient’s blood.) Periodic infusions are needed as they are not immortal. (c) A permanent cure is possible if the gene isolated from ADA-producing bone marrow cells is introduced into cells at early embryonic stages. (Bone marrow transplantation and enzyme replacement therapy are not completely curative.)
