Why does a patient of ADA-deficiency require repeated infusions of genetically engineered lymphocytes?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 2Q182 marks
Question 15 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
Why does a patient of ADA-deficiency require repeated infusions of genetically engineered lymphocytes? Suggest a possible permanent remedy. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
The lymphocytes given functional ADA cDNA (by retroviral vector) are not immortal, so ADA production falls as they die and infusion must be repeated periodically. Permanent remedy: introduce the gene isolated from ADA-producing bone marrow cells into cells at early embryonic stages.
