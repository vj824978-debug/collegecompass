Question as text
Why is the genetically engineered insulin by American company Eli Lily preferred to the one produced by conventional methods? Explain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Conventional insulin was extracted from the pancreas of slaughtered cattle and pigs; being a foreign protein it caused allergy or other reactions in some patients. Eli Lilly’s insulin (chains A and B made in E. coli and joined by disulphide bonds) is human insulin, so it causes no such reactions (and can be made in large amounts).
