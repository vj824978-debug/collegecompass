Question as text
A child is born with ADA - deficiency (a) Suggest and explain a procedure for possible life-long (permanent) cure. (b) Name any other possible treatment for this disease. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Gene therapy: isolate the functional ADA gene from bone marrow cells producing ADA and introduce it into cells at early embryonic stages, so all cells derived from them carry the gene – a possible permanent cure. (Gene therapy with the child’s lymphocytes – grown in culture, given functional ADA cDNA by a retroviral vector and returned – needs periodic repetition.) (Source key: lymphocytes grown in culture, given ADA cDNA and “returned to the patient’s body at early embryonic stage”.) (b) Bone marrow transplantation; enzyme replacement therapy (injection of functional ADA).
