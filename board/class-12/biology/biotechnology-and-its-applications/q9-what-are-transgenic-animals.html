What are transgenic animals?

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q243 marks
Question 9 · Class 12 Biology · Biotechnology and its Applications
Question as text
(a) What are transgenic animals? (b) Name the transgenic animal having the largest number amongst all the existing transgenic animals. (c) State any three reasons for which these types of animals are being produced. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Animals whose DNA has been manipulated to possess and express an extra (foreign) gene. (b) Mice (over 95% of all existing transgenic animals). (c) Any three: study of normal physiology and development; study of disease; production of biological products; vaccine safety testing; chemical safety (toxicity) testing.
