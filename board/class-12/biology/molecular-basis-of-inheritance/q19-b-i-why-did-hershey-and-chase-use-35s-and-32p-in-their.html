(b) (i) Why did Hershey and Chase use ‘ 35S’ and ‘ 32P’ in their experiment?

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q335 marks
Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(b) (i) Why did Hershey and Chase use ‘ 35S’ and ‘ 32P’ in their experiment? Explain. [1] (ii) State the importance of (1) blending and (2) centrifugation in their experiment. [2] (iii) Write the conclusion they arrived at the end of their experiment. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Proteins contain sulphur but no phosphorus; DNA contains phosphorus but no sulphur. So 35S labelled the viral protein coat and 32P labelled the viral DNA, allowing them to track which one enters the bacterium. (ii) (1) Blending: removed the viral protein coats (ghosts) still attached to the bacterial surface after infection. (2) Centrifugation: separated the lighter viral particles/coats (supernatant) from the heavier bacterial cells (pellet). (iii) Bacteria infected with 32P phages were radioactive while those infected with 35S phages were not (radioactivity in supernatant). So DNA, not protein, enters the bacterium; DNA is the genetic material.
