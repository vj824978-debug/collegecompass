(B) (i) Where do transcription and translation occur in bacteria and eukaryotes respectively?

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 2Q335 marks
Question 5 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(B) (i) Where do transcription and translation occur in bacteria and eukaryotes respectively? (ii) Draw a labelled schematic sketch of replication fork of DNA. (iii) A DNA segment has a total of 1000 nucleotides, out of which 240 of them are Adenine containing nucleotides. How many pyrimidine bases this segment possesses?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Bacteria: both transcription and translation in the cytoplasm (coupled; translation can begin before transcription ends). Eukaryotes: transcription in the nucleus, translation in the cytoplasm (on ribosomes). (ii) Diagram: replication fork. Labels: parental (template) strands with 3 ′/5′ ends, leading strand (continuous, 5′→3′), lagging strand with Okazaki fragments (discontinuous), DNA polymerase, DNA ligase, direction of fork movement. (iii) A = T = 240, so A + T = 480 and G + C = 520, C = 260. Pyrimidines = T + C = 240 + 260 = 500.
