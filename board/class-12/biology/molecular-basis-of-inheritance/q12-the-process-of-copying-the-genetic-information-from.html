The process of copying the genetic information from one strand of DNA into RNA is termed as transcription.

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q304 marks
Question 12 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
The process of copying the genetic information from one strand of DNA into RNA is termed as transcription. The principle of complementarity of bases governs the process of transcription, also except that uracil comes in place of thymine. Study the complete transcription unit given below and answer the following questions: (a) Name the main enzyme involved in the process of transcription. (b) Identify coding strand and template strand of DNA in the transcription unit. Attempt either sub-part (c) or (d): (c) Identify (C) and (D) in the diagram, mention their significance in the process of transcription. OR (d) Describe the location of (C) and (D) in the transcription unit.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) DNA-dependent RNA polymerase. (b) Strand A (3′→5′, upper) is the template strand; strand B (5 ′→3′, lower) is the coding strand. (c) C – promoter: binding site for RNA polymerase, defines the start of transcription and the template strand. D – terminator: marks the end of transcription; RNA polymerase and the transcript are released here. OR (d) Promoter (C) lies upstream, towards the 5 ′ end of the coding strand (start of the unit); terminator (D) lies downstream, towards the 3 ′ end of the coding strand (end of the unit).
