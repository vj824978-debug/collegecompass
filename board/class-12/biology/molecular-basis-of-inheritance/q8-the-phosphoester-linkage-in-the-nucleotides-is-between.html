CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q61 mark
Question 8 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
The phosphoester linkage in the nucleotides is between (A) phosphate group and OH of 3 ′C of a nucleoside. (B) phosphate group and OH of 5 ′C of a nucleoside. (C) phosphate group and H of 3 ′C of a nucleoside. (D) phosphate group and H of 5 ′C of a nucleoside.
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Phosphate group and OH of 5′C of a nucleoside – forms a nucleotide by phosphoester linkage.
