(b) (i) Explain the process of amino-acylation of tRNA and its role in the process of translation.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 2Q325 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(b) (i) Explain the process of amino-acylation of tRNA and its role in the process of translation. (ii) How does initiation of the translation process occur in prokaryotes? Explain. [5] (iii) Where are the untranslated regions located on m-RNA and why?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Aminoacylation (charging) of tRNA: in the presence of ATP, each amino acid is activated and attached to its cognate tRNA by aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase. Role: charged tRNAs carry the specific amino acid to the ribosome and their anticodon reads the codon; the energy makes peptide bond formation favourable. (ii) Initiation: the small ribosomal subunit (30S) binds the mRNA at the 5 ′ region; the start codon AUG is recognised by the initiator tRNA carrying (formyl-)methionine (anticodon UAC) with initiation factors; the large subunit (50S) then joins to form the 70S initiation complex, with the initiator tRNA in the P site and the A site free for the next aminoacyl-tRNA. (iii) UTRs lie at the 5 ′ end (before the start codon) and at the 3 ′ end (after the stop codon); they are not translated but are required for efficient translation.
