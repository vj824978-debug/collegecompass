Explain the processing of heterogeneous nuclear RNA (hnRNA) into a fully functional mRNA in eukaryotes.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q273 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Explain the processing of heterogeneous nuclear RNA (hnRNA) into a fully functional mRNA in eukaryotes. Where does this processing occur in the cell? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
hnRNA (made by RNA polymerase II) contains exons and introns. Processing: – Splicing: introns are removed and exons joined in a defined order. – Capping: methyl guanosine triphosphate (cap) is added to the 5 ′ end. – Tailing: 200–300 adenylate residues (poly-A tail) are added at the 3 ′ end, templateindependently. Site: in the nucleus; the mature mRNA then moves to the cytoplasm for translation.
