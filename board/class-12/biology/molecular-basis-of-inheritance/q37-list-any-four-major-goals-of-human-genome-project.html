List any four major goals of Human Genome project.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q265 marks
Question 37 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(a) List any four major goals of Human Genome project. (b) Write any four ways the knowledge from HGP is of significance for humans. (c) Explain BAC and mention its importance. [2+2+1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Any four: identify all the approx. 20,000–25,000 genes; determine the sequence of the 3 billion base pairs; store the information in databases; improve tools for data analysis; transfer related technologies to other sectors; address ethical, legal and social issues (ELSI). (b) Any four: better understanding of human biology and genetics; diagnosis, treatment and prevention of genetic disorders (e.g. cancer, Alzheimer’s); personalised medicine; understanding human evolution and diversity; applications in biotechnology, health care, agriculture, energy and environment. (c) BAC: bacterial artificial chromosome – a cloning vector based on the F-plasmid of E. coli, carrying an ori and selectable markers, that can hold large DNA inserts. Importance: used in HGP to clone large fragments of human DNA in bacterial hosts for sequencing.
