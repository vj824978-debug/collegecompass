CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q101 mark
Question 14 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Match the following genes of the lac operon listed in column ‘A’ with their respective products listed in column ‘B’: [1] A B Gene Products a. ‘i’ gene (i) β–galactosidase b. ‘z’ gene (ii) lac permease c. ‘a’ gene (iii) repressor d. ‘y’ gene (iv) transacetylase Select the correct option: a b c d (A) (i) (iii) (ii) (iv) (B) (iii) (i) (ii) (iv) (C) (iii) (i) (iv) (ii) (D) (iii) (iv) (i) (ii)
Answer
Answer as text
(C) a–(iii), b–(i), c–(iv), d–(ii): i–repressor, z–β-galactosidase, a–transacetylase, y–permease.
