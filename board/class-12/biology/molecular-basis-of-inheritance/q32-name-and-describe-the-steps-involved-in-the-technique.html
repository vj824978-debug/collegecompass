Question as text
(a) Name and describe the steps involved in the technique widely used in forensics that serves as the basis of paternity testing in case of disputes.
Answer
Answer as text
DNA fingerprinting (based on VNTRs/satellite DNA, which differ between individuals and are inherited). Steps: 1. Isolate DNA (from child, mother and alleged father). 2. Amplify by PCR if needed. 3. Digest DNA with restriction endonucleases. 4. Separate the fragments by gel electrophoresis. 5. Blot onto nitrocellulose/nylon membrane (Southern blotting). 6. Hybridise with a labelled VNTR probe. 7. Detect by autoradiography; compare band patterns – each band of the child must be shared with the mother or the father.
