Name the enzyme that transcribes hn-RNA in eukaryotes.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIQ163 marks
Question 43 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Name the enzyme that transcribes hn-RNA in eukaryotes. Explain the steps that the hn-RNA undergoes before it is processed into m-RNA. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Enzyme: RNA polymerase II. Steps: (1) Splicing – introns removed, exons joined. (2) Capping – methyl guanosine triphosphate added at the 5 ′ end. (3) Tailing – adenylate residues (200–300) added at the 3 ′ end, template-independently.
