(b) Explain the process of transcription in prokaryotes.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 3Q335 marks
Question 23 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(b) Explain the process of transcription in prokaryotes. How is it different from transcription in eukaryotes? [5]
Answer
Answer as text
Prokaryotic transcription (single DNA-dependent RNA polymerase): – Initiation: RNA polymerase binds the promoter with the help of the initiation factor σ; DNA unwinds locally. – Elongation: using the template (antisense, 3 ′→5′) strand and ribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates, RNA is synthesised 5 ′→3′ by complementary base pairing; only a short stretch of RNA stays bound to the enzyme. – Termination: at the terminator, the termination factor ρ (rho) binds; RNA polymerase and the RNA are released. – mRNA needs no processing; transcription and translation occur together in the cytoplasm. Differences in eukaryotes: (1) three RNA polymerases in the nucleus – I (rRNAs 28S, 18S, 5.8S), II (hnRNA, precursor of mRNA), III (tRNA, 5S rRNA, snRNAs); (2) the primary transcript (hnRNA) contains introns and is processed by splicing, capping (5 ′) and tailing (3 ′ poly-A) before export; (3) transcription is in the nucleus, translation in the cytoplasm (not coupled).
