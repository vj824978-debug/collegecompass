Name the type of DNA that forms the basis of DNA fingerprinting and mention two features of this DNA.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q255 marks
Question 38 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(a) Name the type of DNA that forms the basis of DNA fingerprinting and mention two features of this DNA. (b) Write the steps carried out in the process of DNA fingerprinting technique and mention its application. [5] OR Explain the role of different genes in a lac operon, when in a ‘Switched On’ state. [5]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Repetitive DNA / satellite DNA (VNTRs – variable number tandem repeats). Features: short sequences repeated in tandem many times; highly polymorphic (copy number differs between individuals); inherited from parents; do not code for proteins. (b) Steps: isolate DNA; amplify by PCR if needed; digest with restriction endonucleases; separate fragments by gel electrophoresis; blot onto nitrocellulose/nylon membrane (Southern blotting); hybridise with labelled VNTR probe; detect by autoradiography – the band pattern is the DNA fingerprint. Applications (any): forensic identification of criminals, paternity disputes, studying population/genetic diversity and evolution. OR Lac operon switched ON (lactose present): – i (regulator) gene: codes for repressor; lactose (inducer) binds and inactivates it, so it cannot bind the operator. – Operator is free; RNA polymerase binds the promoter and transcribes the structural genes. – z gene: β-galactosidase – hydrolyses lactose into glucose and galactose. – y gene: permease – increases permeability of the cell to lactose. – a gene: transacetylase.
