How many base pairs will be there in 20 nucleosomes in a DNA double helix?

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 2Q21 mark
Question 20 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
How many base pairs will be there in 20 nucleosomes in a DNA double helix? [1] (A) 4000 (B) 40 (C) 20 (D) 2000
Answer
Answer as text
(A) 4000. One nucleosome contains about 200 bp of DNA; 20 × 200 = 4000 bp.
