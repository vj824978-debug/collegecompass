Question 4 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(B) Explain with the help of well-labelled diagrams how lac operon operates in E. coli : (i) In presence of an inducer. (ii) In absence of an inducer.
Answer
Answer as text
Lac operon = regulator gene i + promoter (p) + operator (o) + structural genes z, y, a. The i gene always produces the repressor. (i) Inducer (lactose/allolactose) present – operon ON: the inducer binds the repressor and inactivates it, so the repressor cannot bind the operator; RNA polymerase binds the promoter and transcribes z, y, a as one polycistronic mRNA; enzymes formed: β-galactosidase (lactose→ glucose + galactose), permease (lactose entry into cell), transacetylase. (ii) Inducer absent – operon OFF: the active repressor binds the operator, blocks RNA polymerase, and the structural genes are not transcribed (negative regulation). Diagrams: two labelled sketches – labels: regulator gene i, promoter, operator, z, y, a, repressor mRNA, repressor, RNA polymerase; (i) also inducer bound to inactive repressor, transcription, lac mRNA, β-galactosidase, permease, transacetylase; (ii) repressor bound to operator, “no transcription”.
