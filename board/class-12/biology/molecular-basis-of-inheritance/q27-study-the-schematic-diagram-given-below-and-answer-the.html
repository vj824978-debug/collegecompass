Question 27 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(a) Study the schematic diagram given below and answer the questions that follow: (i) Identify the polarity from ‘X’ to ‘ X’ in the mRNA segment shown. Mention how many more amino acids can be added to the polypeptide that is being translated and why. [1½ ] (ii) Write the initiating codon for translation, its anticodon and the amino acid it codes for. [1½ ] (iii) Explain the charging of an adaptor molecule. Why this molecule needs to be charged? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Polarity X to X is 5 ′→3′. Three more amino acids (Asn, Phe, Tyr) can be added in the segment shown, because three more codons ( AAU, UUC, UAU) follow in the reading frame after GUU (Val) and no stop codon is present in it. (Source key: no more amino acids, as a UAA stop codon is present – but UAA here is not in the reading frame.) (ii) Initiation codon AUG; anticodon UAC; codes for methionine. (iii) Charging (aminoacylation) of tRNA: in the presence of ATP, an amino acid is activated and linked to its own (cognate) tRNA by aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase. Needed because only charged tRNAs carry activated amino acids to the ribosome, and the energy of charging makes peptide bond formation energetically favourable.
