Question 30 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Given below is a sequence of bases in mRNA of a bacterial cell. Identify the amino acid that would be incorporated at codon position 3 and codon position 5 during the process of its translation. [1] 3′ AUCAGGUUUGUGAUGGUACGA 5′ (A) Phenylalanine, Methionine (B) Cysteine, Glycine (C) Alanine, Proline (D) Serine, Valine
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Phenylalanine, Methionine (official key). Taking the triplets as written from the 3 ′ end, codon 3 is UUU (Phe) and codon 5 is AUG (Met). (Note: read strictly 5 ′→3′ the codons would be GUA (Val) and UUU (Phe); the key accepts (A).)
