Expand VNTR and describe its role in DNA fingerprinting.

CBSE 2018Q123 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(a) Expand VNTR and describe its role in DNA fingerprinting. (b) List any two applications of DNA fingerprinting technique. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) VNTR – Variable Number of Tandem Repeats. These satellite DNA sequences are highly polymorphic (repeat number differs between individuals), so they are used as the probe; the band pattern obtained after hybridisation forms the individual’s DNA fingerprint. (b) Any two: forensic/criminal identification; paternity and maternity testing; determining population and genetic diversity; studying evolutionary biology.
