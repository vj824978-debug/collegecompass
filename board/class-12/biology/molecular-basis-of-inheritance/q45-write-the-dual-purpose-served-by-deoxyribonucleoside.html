Write the dual purpose served by Deoxyribonucleoside triphosphates in polymerisation.

CBSE 2018Q11 mark
Question 45 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Write the dual purpose served by Deoxyribonucleoside triphosphates in polymerisation. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
dNTPs act as substrates (building blocks) and also provide energy for polymerisation (from their two terminal phosphates).
