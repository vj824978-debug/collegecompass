CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ173 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
“Use of heavy isotope of nitrogen by Meselson and Stahl demonstrated semi conservative mode of replication of a DNA molecule.” Explain how did they arrive at this conclusion. [3] OR Explain the mechanism of translation that occurs in the ribosomes in a prokaryote. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Meselson and Stahl grew E. coli in a medium with 15NH4Cl for many generations, so all DNA became heavy (15N/15N). Cells were transferred to 14NH4Cl medium; DNA extracted at intervals was separated by CsCl density-gradient centrifugation. After 1 generation (20 min) all DNA had hybrid (intermediate) density (15N/14N); after 2 generations (40 min) DNA was half hybrid and half light (14N/14N). This pattern shows that each daughter DNA has one old and one new strand – semi-conservative replication. (A correctly labelled diagram of the experiment is also accepted.) OR Translation in prokaryotes: (1) Charging of tRNA by aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase. (2) Initiation: small ribosomal subunit binds the mRNA; the start codon AUG is recognised by initiator tRNA (methionine); large subunit joins. (3) Elongation: aminoacyl-tRNAs bind the next codon by codon–anticodon pairing; peptide bonds form (catalysed by rRNA); the ribosome moves codon to codon along mRNA (5 ′→3′). (4) Termination: a release factor binds the stop codon and the polypeptide is released; UTRs aid efficient translation.
