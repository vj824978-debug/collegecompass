Given below is a heterogeneous RNA formed during Eukaryotic transcription: How many introns and exons respectively are present in the hnRNA? (A) 7, 7 (B) 8, 7 (C) 8, 8 (D) 7, 8
Answer
Answer as text
(D) 7, 8. The 7 loops are introns; the 8 segments between and flanking them are exons.
