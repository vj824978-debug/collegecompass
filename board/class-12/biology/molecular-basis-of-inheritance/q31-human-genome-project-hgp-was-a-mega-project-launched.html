Question as text
Human Genome Project (HGP) was a mega project launched in the year 1990 with some important goals. (a) Enlist any four prime goals of HGP. (b) Name any one common non-human animal model organism which has also been sequenced thereafter. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Any four: identify all the approx. 20,000–25,000 human genes; determine the sequence of the 3 billion base pairs of human DNA; store this information in databases; improve tools for data analysis; transfer related technologies to other sectors (e.g. industry); address the ethical, legal and social issues (ELSI). (b) Any one: Drosophila, Caenorhabditis elegans, mouse, honey bee.
