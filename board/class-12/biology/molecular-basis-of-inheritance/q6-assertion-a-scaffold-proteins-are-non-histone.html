Assertion (A): Scaffold proteins are non-histone chromosomal proteins.

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 3Q141 mark
Question 6 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Assertion (A): Scaffold proteins are non-histone chromosomal proteins. Reason (R): They are rich in lysine and arginine. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) A is true, R is false. Scaffold proteins are non-histone chromosomal (NHC) proteins; it is the histones that are rich in lysine and arginine.
