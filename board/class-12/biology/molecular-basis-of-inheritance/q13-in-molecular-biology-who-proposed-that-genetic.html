In molecular biology, who proposed that genetic information flows in one direction?

CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 3Q41 mark

Question as text
In molecular biology, who proposed that genetic information flows in one direction? (A) Hargobind Khorana (B) Francis Crick (C) Watson and Crick (D) Marshall Nirenberg
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Francis Crick – proposed the central dogma: DNA → RNA→ Protein.
