Explain the mechanism of DNA replication with the help of a replication fork.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ163 marks
Question 39 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
Explain the mechanism of DNA replication with the help of a replication fork. What role does the enzyme DNA-ligase play in a DNA replication fork? [3] OR Construct and label a transcription unit from which the RNA segment given below has been transcribed. Write the complete name of the enzyme that transcribed this RNA. 5′ A T G C A T G C A T G C “RNA molecule” [3]
Answer
Answer as text
DNA replication at the fork: begins at the origin of replication (ori); helicase unwinds DNA to form a replication fork; each parental strand acts as template; DNA-dependent DNA polymerase adds dNTPs only in the 5 ′→3′ direction – continuously on the leading strand (template 3 ′→5′) and discontinuously as Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand (template 5 ′→3′). Role of DNA ligase: joins the Okazaki fragments of the lagging strand. Diagram: replication fork with labels – template strands, leading strand, lagging strand, Okazaki fragments, 5′/3′ ends. OR The given sequence contains T, so it cannot be RNA (the marking scheme treated the question as flawed). Taking it as the coding sequence, the transcription unit is: coding strand 5 ′ ATGCATGCATGC 3′; template strand 3 ′ TACGTACGTACG 5′, with the promoter at the 5′ (upstream) end and the terminator at the 3 ′ (downstream) end of the coding strand; RNA transcript 5′ AUGCAUGCAUGC 3′. Enzyme: DNA-dependent RNA polymerase.
