State the reasons for which Hershey and Chase carried out their experiments.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IIIQ255 marks
Question 44 · Class 12 Biology · Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Question as text
(a) State the reasons for which Hershey and Chase carried out their experiments. (b) Answer the following questions based on the experiments of Hershey and Chase: (i) Name the different radioactive isotopes they used, and explain how they used them. (ii) Why did they need to agitate and spin their culture? (iii) Write their observations and the conclusions they arrived at.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) To find out (prove) whether DNA or protein is the genetic material. (b)(i) 32P (labelled the DNA of bacteriophages) and 35S (labelled the protein coat). Phages grown on 32P medium had radioactive DNA; those grown on 35S medium had radioactive protein (DNA has P but no S; protein has S but no P). Each set was used to infect E. coli. (ii) Agitation (blending) removed the viral coats from the bacteria; spinning (centrifugation) separated the light viral particles (supernatant) from the heavier bacteria (pellet). (iii) Observations: bacteria infected with 32P phages were radioactive; with 35S phages, radioactivity was in the supernatant, not in bacteria. Conclusion: DNA, not protein, passes from virus to bacterium – DNA is the genetic material.
