The human chromosome with the highest and least number of genes in them are respectively: [1] (A) Chromosome 21 and Y. (B) Chromosome 1 and X. (C) Chromosome 1 and Y. (D) Chromosome X and Y.
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Chromosome 1 (most genes, 2968) and Y (fewest, 231).
