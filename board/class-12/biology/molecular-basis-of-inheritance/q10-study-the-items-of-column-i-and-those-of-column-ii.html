Question as text
Study the items of Column-I and those of Column-II: Column-I Column-II (a) RNA polymerase I (i) 18s rRNA (b) RNA polymerase II (ii) SnRNAs (c) RNA polymerase III (iii) hnRNA Choose the option that correctly matches the items of Column-I with those of Column-II: (a) (b) (c) (A) (i) (ii) (iii) (B) (iii) (ii) (i) (C) (ii) (iii) (i) (D) (i) (iii) (ii)
Answer
Answer as text
(D) (a)–(i), (b)–(iii), (c)–(ii). RNA pol I: rRNAs (28S, 18S, 5.8S); RNA pol II: hnRNA; RNA pol III: tRNA, 5S rRNA, snRNAs.
