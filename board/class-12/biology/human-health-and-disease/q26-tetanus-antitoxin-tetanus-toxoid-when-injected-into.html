Tetanus antitoxin (Tetanus toxoid) when injected into the human body it immediately provides: [1] (A) Innate immunity (B) Passive immunity (C) Auto immunity (D) Active immunity
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Passive immunity. An antitoxin contains pre-formed antibodies, so protection is immediate. (Strictly, a tetanus toxoid would induce active immunity; the question means antitoxin.)
