Question as text
‘An HIV patient normally doesn’t die of ‘AIDS’, but death is caused due to many other infections.’ Do you agree with the statement? Give explanatory reasons in support of your answer. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Yes. HIV destroys helper T-lymphocytes (and multiplies in macrophages), so their number falls progressively and the person becomes severely immunodeficient. AIDS itself is a syndrome (deficiency of the immune system), so death results from opportunistic infections that a healthy body would overcome – bacteria likeMycobacterium (TB), viruses, fungi and parasites likeToxoplasma.
