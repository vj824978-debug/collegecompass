Question as text
The principle of vaccination is based on the property of (A) Specificity (B) Diversity (C) Memory (D) Discrimination between ‘self’ and ‘non-self’ [1] OR Opioids act as (A) Depressants (B) Pain killers (C) Euphoria providers (D) Stimulants [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(C) Memory. OR (B) Pain killers. Opioids (e.g. morphine) are effective sedatives and painkillers. (Alternative: (A) Depressants is also defensible by NCERT, since heroin acts as a depressant and slows down body functions.)
