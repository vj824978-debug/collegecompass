Question as text
Select the pathogen mismatched with the symptoms of disease caused by it from the list given below: [1] (A) Entamoeba histolytica: Constipation, abdominal pain. (B) Epidermophyton: Dry scaly lesions on nail. (C) Wuchereria bancrofti: Chronic inflammation of lymphatic vessels of lower limb. (D) Haemophilus influenzae : Blockage of the intestinal passage.
Answer
Answer as text
(D) Haemophilus influenzae: Blockage of the intestinal passage. H. influenzae causes pneumonia (affects alveoli of lungs); intestinal blockage is caused by Ascaris.
