Question as text
(b) Name any two techniques used to detect the cancer of internal organs and write about any one of them.
Answer
Answer as text
Techniques (any two): radiography (X-rays), CT (computed tomography), MRI (magnetic resonance imaging). E.g. CT uses X-rays to generate a three-dimensional image of the internal organs; MRI uses strong magnetic fields and non-ionising radiation to detect pathological and physiological changes in living tissue.
