List two diseases that spread through inhaling droplets or aerosols.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 2Q82 marks
Question 41 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
List two diseases that spread through inhaling droplets or aerosols. Write one prominent symptoms for each one of them. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Pneumonia – alveoli fill with fluid; fever, chills, cough, headache; lips and fingernails may turn grey to bluish in severe cases. Common cold – nasal congestion and discharge, sore throat, hoarseness, cough. (Diphtheria/tuberculosis are also acceptable.)
