Answer the following questions: (i) State what do you understand by “MALT”?

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 1Q315 marks
Question 4 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(A) Answer the following questions: (i) State what do you understand by “MALT”? Where is it located inside our body? (ii) Explain cytokine barriers. (iii) Name the diagnostic test for AIDS. On what principle does it work? (iv) Bone marrow and thymus play an important role in human immune system. Explain how are they able to achieve this.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) MALT = mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue: lymphoid tissue lying in the lining of the major tracts – respiratory, digestive (e.g. Peyer’s patches) and urogenital tracts; it forms about 50% of the lymphoid tissue in the body. (ii) Cytokine barriers: virus-infected cells secrete proteins called interferons, which protect noninfected cells from further viral infection. (iii) ELISA (Enzyme Linked Immuno-Sorbent Assay); works on the principle of antigen–antibody interaction (detects HIV antigens or antibodies against HIV in blood). (iv) Both are primary lymphoid organs where immature lymphocytes differentiate into antigensensitive lymphocytes. Bone marrow produces all blood cells including lymphocytes (B-cells mature here); thymus is the site of maturation of T-lymphocytes.
