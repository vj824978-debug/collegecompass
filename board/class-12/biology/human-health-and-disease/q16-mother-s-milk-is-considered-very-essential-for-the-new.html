“Mother’s milk is considered very essential for the new born infant.” Justify.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 3Q233 marks
Question 16 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) “Mother’s milk is considered very essential for the new born infant.” Justify. [1] (b) What is a ‘vaccine’? Explain the principle on which it works. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Colostrum (milk of the initial days of lactation) contains abundant IgA antibodies, which give the newborn passive immunity against infections. (b) A vaccine is a preparation of antigenic proteins of a pathogen or inactivated/weakened pathogens. Principle – ‘memory’ of the immune system: the vaccine induces a primary response, producing antibodies and memory B and T cells; on actual infection these memory cells recognise the pathogen quickly and mount a rapid, massive secondary response that neutralises it.
