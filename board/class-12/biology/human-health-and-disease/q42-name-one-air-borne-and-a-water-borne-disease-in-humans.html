Name one air-borne and a water borne disease in humans.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 3Q62 marks
Question 42 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
Name one air-borne and a water borne disease in humans. List one specific symptom of each one of them. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Air-borne: e.g. pneumonia (fever, cough, problems in breathing) or common cold (nasal congestion and discharge). Water-borne: e.g. typhoid (sustained high fever of 39–40 ° C) or amoebiasis (constipation, abdominal pain, stools with mucus and blood clots).
