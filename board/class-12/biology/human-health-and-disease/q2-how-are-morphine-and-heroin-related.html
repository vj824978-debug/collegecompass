How are morphine and heroin related?

CBSE 2025Delhi Set 1Q172 marks
Question 2 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(A) How are morphine and heroin related? Mention their effect on the human body.
Answer
Answer as text
Both are opioids from the latex of poppy ( Papaver somniferum); morphine is extracted from the latex, and heroin (smack) is diacetylmorphine, obtained by acetylation of morphine. Effect: they bind to opioid receptors in the CNS and gastrointestinal tract; act as depressants and slow down body functions (morphine is a strong sedative and painkiller); heroin is highly addictive.
