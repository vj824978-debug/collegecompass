Question 37 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
A patient complains of suffering from constipation, stomach ache, stool with blood clots and excess mucous. The physician diagnosed it as amoebiasis, after stool test. [3] (a) Write the scientific name of the microbe identified in the stool sample. (b) How do you think, the patient must have contracted it? (c) Write your suggestions to the patient to avoid infection in future.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Entamoeba histolytica. (b) By consuming food or water contaminated with faecal matter containing cysts of the parasite; houseflies act as mechanical carriers. (c) Drink boiled/clean water, eat properly cooked and covered food, wash hands before eating, maintain personal and public hygiene, proper disposal of excreta, and control houseflies.
