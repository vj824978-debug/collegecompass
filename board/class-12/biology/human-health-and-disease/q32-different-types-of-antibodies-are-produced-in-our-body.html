Question as text
Different types of antibodies are produced in our body by the B-lymphocytes in response to a pathogen that enters in our blood. Name any two antibodies produced and draw a schematic representation of an antibody molecule and label its four parts. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Antibodies (any two): IgA, IgM, IgE, IgG. Diagram: Y-shaped antibody (H 2L2). Labels (any four): heavy chain, light chain, antigenbinding site, variable region, constant region, disulphide bond.
