Question as text
Differentiate between opioids and cannabinoids on the basis of their (a) specific receptor site in human body. (b) mode of action in human body. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Opioids bind to opioid receptors in the central nervous system and gastrointestinal tract; cannabinoids bind to cannabinoid receptors present principally in the brain. (b) Opioids (e.g. heroin) act as depressants and slow down body functions; cannabinoids affect the cardiovascular system of the body.
