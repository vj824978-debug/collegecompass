Describe the life cycle of HIV from the time of its entry into the human body till full blown AIDS sets in.

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q335 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) Describe the life cycle of HIV from the time of its entry into the human body till full blown AIDS sets in. [5] OR (b) (i) Write the symptoms of malaria in human and explain what causes these symptoms. [2½ ] (ii) Describe the different steps in the sexual mode of reproduction in the life cycle of a malarial parasite from the time of its initiation till where it is completed and ready to start a fresh cycle. [2½ ]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) HIV enters the body and first infects macrophages; its RNA is copied into viral DNA by reverse transcriptase. Viral DNA is incorporated into the host DNA and directs production of new viruses – macrophages act as an ‘HIV factory’. HIV also enters helper T-lymphocytes (T H), replicates and releases progeny viruses into blood, which attack more helper T-cells. Helper T- cell count falls progressively. The patient suffers bouts of fever, diarrhoea and weight loss; finally becomes so immunodeficient that infections normally overcome (bacteria such as Mycobacterium, viruses, fungi, parasites like Toxoplasma) set in – full-blown AIDS. OR (b) (i) Symptoms: chill and high fever recurring every 3–4 days (also headache, nausea, anaemia). Cause: when infected RBCs rupture, merozoites and a toxin called haemozoin are released, which causes the chill and fever. (ii) Some merozoites in RBCs develop into male and female gametocytes → female Anopheles sucks them in with blood → gametes formed and fertilisation occurs in the mosquito’s gut → zygote develops in the stomach wall into thousands of sporozoites → sporozoites migrate to the salivary glands→ injected into a new human at the next bite, starting a fresh cycle.
