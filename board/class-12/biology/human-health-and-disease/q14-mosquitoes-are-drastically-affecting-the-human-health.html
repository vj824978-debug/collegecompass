“Mosquitoes are drastically affecting the human health in almost all the developing tropical countries.

CBSE 2024Delhi Set 1Q304 marks
Question 14 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
Read the following passage and answer the questions that follow: “Mosquitoes are drastically affecting the human health in almost all the developing tropical countries. Different species of mosquitoes cause very fatal diseases so much so that many humans lose their life and if they survive, are unable to put in productive hours to sustain their life. With the result, the health index of the country goes down.” (a) Name the form in which Plasmodium gains entry into (i) human body (ii) the female Anopheles body. [1] (b) Why do the symptoms of malaria not appear in a person immediately after being bitten by an infected female Anopheles? Give one reason. Explain when and how do the symptoms of the disease would appear. [2] OR (b) Explain the events which occur within a female Anopheles mosquito after it has sucked blood from a malaria patient. [2] (c) Name the species of mosquito other than female Anopheles and the disease, for which it carries the pathogen. [1]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Sporozoites; (ii) gametocytes (taken in with blood). (b) Sporozoites first reach the liver and multiply in liver cells, then enter RBCs and multiply (incubation period). Symptoms appear when infected RBCs rupture and release haemozoin, causing chill and high fever recurring every 3–4 days. OR (b) Mosquito takes up gametocytes with the blood meal → gametes form and fertilisation occurs in the mosquito’s gut/stomach → zygote develops in the stomach wall into sporozoites → sporozoites migrate to and are stored in the salivary glands, ready to be injected into a new human host when the mosquito bites. (c) Aedes mosquito – dengue/chikungunya (Culex – filariasis/elephantiasis is also acceptable).
