(b) Why a malignant tumour considered to be more damaging than a benign tumour?

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q202 marks
Question 21 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(b) Why a malignant tumour considered to be more damaging than a benign tumour? Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
Benign tumours stay confined to their original site and do not spread. Malignant tumour cells grow rapidly, invade and damage surrounding normal tissues, and some cells slough off, travel through blood to distant sites and start new tumours (metastasis); hence malignant tumours are more damaging.
