CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 1Q151 mark
Question 6 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
Assertion (A): Streptococcus pneumoniae and Haemophilus influenzae are responsible for causing infectious disease in human beings. Reason (R): A healthy person acquires the infection by inhaling the aerosols released by an infected person. (A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A). (C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false. (D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) explains (A): these bacteria cause pneumonia, and a healthy person is infected by inhaling droplets/aerosols released by an infected person.
