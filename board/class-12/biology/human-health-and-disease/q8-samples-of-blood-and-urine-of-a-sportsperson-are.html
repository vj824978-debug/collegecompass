Question as text
Samples of blood and urine of a sportsperson are collected before any sports event for drug tests. (a) Why there is a need to conduct such tests? (b) Name the drugs the authorities usually look for. (c) Write the generic names of two plants from which these drugs are obtained.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) To detect use of banned performance-enhancing drugs (misuse of drugs to increase muscle strength/performance), ensuring fair play and protecting athletes’ health. (b) Narcotic analgesics, anabolic steroids, diuretics and certain hormones. (c) Papaver (opioids/morphine) and Cannabis (cannabinoids) [also Erythroxylum – cocaine].
