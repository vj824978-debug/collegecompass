Question as text
The decrease in the T-lymphocytes count in human blood will result in: [1] (A) Decrease in antigens (B) Decrease in antibodies (C) Increase in antibodies (D) Increase in antigens
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Decrease in antibodies. Helper T-cells are required for B-cells to produce antibodies.
