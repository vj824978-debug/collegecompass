Question as text
Study the diagram showing the entry of HIV into the human body and the processes that are followed: (a) Name the human cell ‘A’ HIV enters into. (b) Mention the genetic material ‘B’ HIV releases into the cell. (c) Identify enzyme ‘C’. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) A – helper T-lymphocyte (T H cell); HIV also enters macrophages. (b) B – viral RNA (single-stranded). (c) C – reverse transcriptase (makes viral DNA from RNA).
