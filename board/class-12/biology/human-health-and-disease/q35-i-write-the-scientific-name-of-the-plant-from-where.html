(i) Write the Scientific name of the plant from where natural cannabinoids are obtained.

CBSE 2022Term II Outside Delhi Set 1Q22 marks
Question 35 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) (i) Write the Scientific name of the plant from where natural cannabinoids are obtained. (ii) Mention the parts of the plant that are used for extracting the drug. (iii) How does the drug affect human body? [2] OR (b) Epithelial lining of our intestine is considered as secondary lymphoid organ. Justify the statement.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Cannabis sativa . (ii) Flower tops, leaves and resin (used to make marijuana, hashish, charas, ganja). (iii) Cannabinoids interact with cannabinoid receptors present principally in the brain and affect the cardiovascular system. OR (b) The intestinal lining contains mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue (MALT, e.g. Peyer’s patches), which constitutes about 50% of lymphoid tissue in the body. Mature lymphocytes migrate here, interact with antigens and proliferate into effector cells – the function of a secondary lymphoid organ.
