“Plasmodium protozoan needs both a mosquito and a human host for its continuity.” Explain.

CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q263 marks
Question 22 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) “Plasmodium protozoan needs both a mosquito and a human host for its continuity.” Explain. [3] OR (b) We all must work towards maintaining good health because “health is wealth”. Enlist any six ways of achieving good health.
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Plasmodium completes its life cycle in two hosts. In humans (intermediate host): sporozoites injected by the mosquito multiply asexually in liver cells and then RBCs; rupture of RBCs releases haemozoin (chill, fever); some stages form gametocytes in RBCs. In the femaleAnopheles (definitive host): gametocytes taken in with blood form gametes, fertilisation occurs in the gut, the zygote forms sporozoites that move to the salivary glands and are injected into a new human at the next bite. Sexual reproduction occurs only in the mosquito and asexual multiplication in humans, so both hosts are needed. OR (b) Any six: balanced diet; personal hygiene; regular exercise (and yoga); adequate rest; vaccination/immunisation; awareness about diseases and their prevention; proper disposal of waste and control of vectors; avoiding drugs and alcohol.
