Question 27 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(b) Immunotherapy these days is one of the most efficient way of treatment of cancer. The therapy involved activates the immune system and destroys the tumour. (i) Write an example of one such biological response modifier used in immunotherapy. (ii) Why do patients need such substances if immune system is already working in body? (iii) State what is ‘Contact inhibition’?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) α-interferon. (ii) Tumour cells avoid detection and destruction by the immune system; biological response modifiers activate the immune system to destroy the tumour. (iii) Contact inhibition: the property of normal cells by which contact with other cells inhibits their uncontrolled growth; cancer cells have lost this property.
