Name the causative agents of pneumonia and common cold.

CBSE 2019Outside Delhi Set IQ193 marks
Question 47 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) Name the causative agents of pneumonia and common cold. (b) How do these differ in their symptoms? (c) Mention two symptoms common to both. [3] OR (a) Write the scientific names of the causative agent and vector of malaria, and write its symptoms. (b) Name any two diseases spread by Aedes sp. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Pneumonia: Streptococcus pneumoniae/Haemophilus influenzae ; common cold: rhinoviruses. (b) Pneumonia infects the alveoli of the lungs (fluid-filled), with chills and fever; in severe cases lips and fingernails turn grey to bluish. Common cold infects the nose and respiratory passage but not lungs: nasal congestion and discharge, sore throat, hoarseness. (c) Common: cough and headache (also fever). OR (a) Causative agent: Plasmodium vivax/P. malariae /P. falciparum; vector: female Anopheles mosquito. Symptoms: chill and high fever recurring every 3–4 days. (b) Dengue and chikungunya.
