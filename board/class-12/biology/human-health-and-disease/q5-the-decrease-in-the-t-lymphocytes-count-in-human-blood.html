The decrease in the T-Lymphocytes count in human blood will finally result in (A) decrease in antigens (B) decrease in antibodies (C) increase in antibodies (D) increase in antigens
Answer
Answer as text
(B) decrease in antibodies. Helper T-cells are needed to activate B-cells to produce antibodies.
