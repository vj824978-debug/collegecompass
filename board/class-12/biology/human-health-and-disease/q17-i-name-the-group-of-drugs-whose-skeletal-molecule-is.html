(i) Name the group of drugs whose skeletal molecule is shown below: (ii) How are such drugs consumed?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 1Q253 marks
Question 17 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) (i) Name the group of drugs whose skeletal molecule is shown below: (ii) How are such drugs consumed? (iii) Name the human body organ affected by the consumption of these drugs. [3×1=3] OR (b) Draw a schematic diagram of an antibody molecule and label any four parts. Mention their chemical nature. Name the cells which produce them. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) (i) Cannabinoids. (ii) Generally taken by inhalation (smoking) and oral ingestion. (iii) Brain – cannabinoids act on cannabinoid receptors present principally in the brain. (NCERT also states that they affect the cardiovascular system/heart; that answer is equally acceptable.) OR (b) Diagram: Y-shaped antibody molecule (H 2L2) made of two heavy and two light polypeptide chains joined by disulphide bonds. Labels (any four): heavy chain, light chain, antigenbinding site, variable region, constant region, disulphide bond. Chemical nature: proteins (glycoproteins – immunoglobulins). Produced by B-lymphocytes (plasma cells).
