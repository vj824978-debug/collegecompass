Name the source plant of heroin drug.

CBSE 2018Q102 marks
Question 51 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
(a) Name the source plant of heroin drug. How is it obtained from the plant? (b) Write the effects of heroin on the human body. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Poppy plant, Papaver somniferum. Morphine is extracted from the latex of the plant and heroin is obtained by its acetylation (diacetylmorphine). (b) Heroin is a depressant: it binds to opioid receptors in the CNS and slows down body functions; it is highly addictive.
