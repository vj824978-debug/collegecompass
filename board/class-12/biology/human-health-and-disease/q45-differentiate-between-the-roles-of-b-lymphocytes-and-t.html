Differentiate between the roles of B-lymphocytes and T-lymphocytes in generating immune responses.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ82 marks
Question 45 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
Differentiate between the roles of B-lymphocytes and T-lymphocytes in generating immune responses. [2] OR Principle of vaccination is based on the property of “memory” of the immune system. Taking one suitable example, justify the statement. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
B-lymphocytes produce antibodies (humoral immune response). T-lymphocytes do not secrete antibodies but help B-cells to produce them, and also kill pathogens/infected cells directly (cellmediated immune response; graft rejection). OR A vaccine (inactivated/weakened pathogen or its antigenic proteins), e.g. polio vaccine, induces antibody production and generates memory B and T cells. On subsequent exposure to the actual pathogen, the memory cells recognise it quickly and overwhelm it with a massive production of antibodies, preventing disease.
