Mention the chemical nature of an antibody and name the type of cells they are produced by.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q183 marks
Question 44 · Class 12 Biology · Human Health and Disease
Question as text
Mention the chemical nature of an antibody and name the type of cells they are produced by. Write the difference between active and passive immune responses on the basis of antibodies. [3] OR Name the cells that act as HIV factory in humans when infected by HIV. Explain the events that occur in these infected cells. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
Antibodies are proteins (glycoproteins – immunoglobulins) produced by B-lymphocytes (plasma cells). Active immunity: the host produces its own antibodies on exposure to antigen; slow, longlasting. Passive immunity: ready-made antibodies are given directly to the body; immediate but short-lived. OR Macrophages act as the ‘HIV factory’. In them, viral RNA is converted into viral DNA by reverse transcriptase; the viral DNA is incorporated into the host cell’s DNA and directs the infected cells to produce virus particles continuously. Progeny viruses also infect helper T-lymphocytes, whose number progressively falls, leading to immunodeficiency.
