Describe the contributions of Alexander Fleming, Ernest Chain and Howard Florey in the field of microbiology.

CBSE 2020Delhi Set 1Q92 marks
Question 28 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
Describe the contributions of Alexander Fleming, Ernest Chain and Howard Florey in the field of microbiology. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Alexander Fleming, while working onStaphylococci, observed that a mould (Penicillium notatum ) growing on an unwashed culture plate prevented their growth; he named the chemical penicillin (the first antibiotic). Ernest Chain and Howard Florey later established its full potential as an effective antibiotic (purified and produced it), which was used to treat soldiers in World War II.
