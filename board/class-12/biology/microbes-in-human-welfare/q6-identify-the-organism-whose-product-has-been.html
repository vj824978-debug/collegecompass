CBSE 2025Outside Delhi Set 2Q121 mark

Question as text
Identify the organism whose product has been commercialised as blood cholesterol lowering agent. (A) Trichoderma polysporum (B) Monascus purpureus (C) Saccharomyces cerevisiae (D) Aspergillus niger
Answer
Answer as text
(B) Monascus purpureus ; it produces statins, which competitively inhibit the enzyme of cholesterol synthesis.
