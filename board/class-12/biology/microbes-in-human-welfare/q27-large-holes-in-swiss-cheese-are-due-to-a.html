Question as text
Large-holes in ‘Swiss-Cheese’ are due to [1] (A) Propionibacterium shermanii (B) Saccharomyces cerevisiae (C) Penicillium chrysogenum (D) Acetobacter aceti
Answer
Answer as text
(A) Propionibacterium shermanii ; it releases large amounts of CO 2, which forms the holes.
