Can we use slurry of human excreta instead of cowdung slurry to produce biogas in a typical biogas plant?

CBSE 2022Term II Delhi Set 1Q22 marks
Question 21 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
Can we use slurry of human excreta instead of cowdung slurry to produce biogas in a typical biogas plant? Support your answer giving reasons. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Board (marking-scheme) answer: No. Cattle dung is rich in methanogens such as Methanobacterium, which come from the rumen of cattle and carry out the anaerobic breakdown of organic matter to methane; human excreta does not carry this load of methanogens, so a typical (gobar gas) biogas plant uses cow-dung slurry. (Note: human excreta can be digested to biogas in specially designed sewage-based digesters, but that is not a typical biogas plant.)
