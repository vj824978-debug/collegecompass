Question as text
Explain how are plants benefitted by their association with “ Glomus species ”. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Glomus forms mycorrhiza with plant roots. The fungal partner absorbs phosphorus from the soil and passes it to the plant. Such plants also show resistance to root-borne pathogens, tolerance to salinity and drought, and an overall increase in growth and development.
