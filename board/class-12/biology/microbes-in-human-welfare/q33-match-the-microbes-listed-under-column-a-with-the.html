Match the microbes listed under Column-A with the products mentioned under Column-B.

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ203 marks
Question 33 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
(a) Match the microbes listed under Column-A with the products mentioned under Column-B. Column-A Column-B (H) Penicillium notatum (i) Statin (I) Trichoderma polysporum (ii) Ethanol (J) Monascus purpureus (iii) Antibiotic (K) Saccharomyces cerevisiae (iv) Cyclosporin-A (b) Why does ‘Swiss Cheese’ develop large holes? [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) H–(iii) Antibiotic; I–(iv) Cyclosporin-A; J–(i) Statin; K–(ii) Ethanol. (b) The bacterium Propionibacterium shermanii produces a large amount of CO 2, which forms the large holes.
