CBSE 2023Delhi Set 1Q202 marks
Question 18 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
(a) (i) Give an example of a genus of virus used as narrow spectrum insecticidal biocontrol agent. (ii) How does its use serve as an aid in overall integrated pest management programme? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Nucleopolyhedrovirus (a baculovirus). (ii) It is species-specific and narrow-spectrum: it kills the target insect pest but has no negative impact on plants, mammals, birds, fish or non-target insects. Beneficial insects are thus conserved, which aids an overall integrated pest management (IPM) programme, also in ecologically sensitive areas.
