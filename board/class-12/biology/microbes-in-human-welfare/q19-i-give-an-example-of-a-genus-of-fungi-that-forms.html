CBSE 2023Delhi Set 2Q182 marks
Question 19 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
(a) (i) Give an example of a genus of fungi that forms mycorrhizal association with plants. [2] (ii) How does the plant derive benefits from this association?
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Glomus. (ii) The fungus absorbs phosphorus from the soil and passes it to the plant; the plant also gains resistance to root-borne pathogens and tolerance to salinity and drought, with an overall increase in growth (any two).
