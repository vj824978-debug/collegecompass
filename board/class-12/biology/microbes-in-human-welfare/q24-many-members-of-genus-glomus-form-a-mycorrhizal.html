CBSE 2022Term II Delhi Set 3Q12 marks
Question 24 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
Many members of genus Glomus form a mycorrhizal association with plants. Elaborate how is this association beneficial to the plants. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
In mycorrhiza the fungus ( Glomus) absorbs phosphorus from the soil and passes it to the plant. Such plants also show resistance to root-borne pathogens, tolerance to salinity and drought, and an overall increase in growth and development.
