Question 11 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
The first antibiotic was discovered accidentally by A while working on B. ‘A’ and ‘B’ are [1] (A) A-Waksman; B-Streptococcus (B) A-Fleming; B- Penicillium notatum (C) A-Waksman; B-Bacillus brevis (D) A-Fleming; B- Staphylococci
Answer
Answer as text
(D) A-Fleming; B- Staphylococci. Fleming discovered penicillin while working on Staphylococci bacteria (the mould was Penicillium notatum ). (Source key: (B).)
