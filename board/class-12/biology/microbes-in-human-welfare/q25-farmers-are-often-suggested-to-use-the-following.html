Question as text
Farmers are often suggested to use the following organisms in their crop land so as to improve the soil fertility. (i) Rhizobium, (ii) Anabaena [2] Explain.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) Rhizobium forms symbiotic root nodules on leguminous plants and fixes atmospheric nitrogen into organic forms used by the plant, enriching the soil with nitrogen. (ii) Anabaena is a free-living, autotrophic cyanobacterium that fixes atmospheric nitrogen (important in paddy fields) and adds organic matter to the soil, increasing its fertility.
