Write the scientific name of methanogen bacteria.

CBSE 2020Outside Delhi Set 2Q133 marks
Question 30 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
(a) Write the scientific name of methanogen bacteria. Where are these bacteria generally found? Explain their role in biogas production. (b) Name the components of biogas. [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Methanobacterium. Found in anaerobic sludge during sewage treatment and in the rumen of cattle. They grow anaerobically on cellulosic material and produce large amounts of methane along with CO2 and H2; cattle dung (gobar) is rich in them, so it is used to produce biogas (gobar gas). (b) Methane (predominant), carbon dioxide and hydrogen (with traces of hydrogen sulphide).
