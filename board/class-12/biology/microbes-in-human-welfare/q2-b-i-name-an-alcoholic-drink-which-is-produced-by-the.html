Question as text
(B) (i) Name an alcoholic drink which is produced by the help of microbes: (1) With distillation (2) Without distillation (ii) Explain how cyanobacteria can be used as bio-fertilizer.
Answer
Answer as text
(i) (1) With distillation: whisky, brandy or rum. (2) Without distillation: wine or beer. (ii) Cyanobacteria (e.g. Anabaena, Nostoc, Oscillatoria) are autotrophic microbes that fix atmospheric nitrogen; in paddy fields they serve as important biofertilisers, and they also add organic matter to the soil and increase its fertility.
