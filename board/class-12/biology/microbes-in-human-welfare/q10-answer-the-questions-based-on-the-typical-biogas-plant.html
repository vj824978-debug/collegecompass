Question as text
Answer the questions based on the typical biogas plant diagram given below: (a) Identify ‘X’, ‘Y’ and ‘Z’. (b) Why is dung preferred for the production of biogas? [2]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) X – Sludge (spent slurry outlet); Y – Gas (biogas) outlet pipe; Z – Dung + water (slurry inlet). (b) Cattle dung is rich in methanogenic bacteria ( Methanobacterium) from the rumen and in cellulosic matter, which they break down anaerobically to produce methane-rich biogas.
