How do the following organisms act as bio-fertilisers?

CBSE 2024Outside Delhi Set 2Q273 marks
Question 15 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
How do the following organisms act as bio-fertilisers? Explain. (a) Mycorrhiza (b) Anabaena. (c) Rhizobium [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Mycorrhiza: the fungal partner (e.g. Glomus) absorbs phosphorus from the soil and passes it to the plant; plants also gain resistance to root-borne pathogens, tolerance to salinity and drought, and better overall growth. (b) Anabaena: a free-living cyanobacterium that fixes atmospheric nitrogen; it is an important biofertiliser in paddy fields and also adds organic matter to the soil, increasing its fertility. (c) Rhizobium: forms symbiotic root nodules on leguminous plants and fixes atmospheric nitrogen into organic forms used by the plant, enriching soil nitrogen.
