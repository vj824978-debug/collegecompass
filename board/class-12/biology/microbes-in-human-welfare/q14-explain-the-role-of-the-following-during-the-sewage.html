Question as text
Explain the role of the following during the sewage treatment: (a) flocs (b) anaerobic sludge digester [3]
Answer
Answer as text
(a) Flocs are masses of aerobic bacteria held together by fungal filaments into mesh-like structures, formed in the aeration tanks during secondary treatment. They consume the major part of the organic matter in the effluent and so reduce its BOD. In the settling tank they sediment as activated sludge, a small part of which is pumped back into the aeration tank as inoculum. (b) The major part of the activated sludge is pumped into anaerobic sludge digesters. Here anaerobic bacteria digest the bacteria and fungi of the sludge, producing biogas (methane, hydrogen sulphide and CO 2) that can be used as a source of energy.
