What are ‘flocs’, formed during secondary treatment of sewage?

CBSE 2019Delhi Set IQ41 mark
Question 31 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
What are ‘flocs’, formed during secondary treatment of sewage? [1] OR Write any two places where methanogens can be found.
Answer
Answer as text
Flocs are masses of bacteria associated with fungal filaments forming mesh-like structures. OR: Any two: anaerobic sludge (of sewage treatment), rumen of cattle, biogas plant, marshy areas/flooded paddy fields.
