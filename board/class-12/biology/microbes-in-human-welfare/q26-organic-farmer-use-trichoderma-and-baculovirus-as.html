Organic farmer use Trichoderma and Baculovirus as biological control agents.

Question 26 · Class 12 Biology · Microbes in Human Welfare
Question as text
Organic farmer use Trichoderma and Baculovirus as biological control agents. Explain. [2]
Answer
Answer as text
Trichoderma: free-living fungi common in the root ecosystem; they are effective biocontrol agents of several plant pathogens. Baculoviruses (genus Nucleopolyhedrovirus): pathogens of insects and other arthropods; speciesspecific, narrow-spectrum insecticides harmless to plants, mammals, birds, fish and non-target insects, so beneficial insects are conserved in integrated pest management. Both avoid toxic chemical pesticides.
