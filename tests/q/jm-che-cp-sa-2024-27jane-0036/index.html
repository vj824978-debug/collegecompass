Bond line formula of HOCH(CN)2 is:

  1. (1) HO-CH with C=N and C=N bonded to the carbon
  2. (2) HO-CH with CN and CN bonded to the carbon
  3. (3) H bonded to C, with OH, CN, and CN groups (where C-CN and C-CN are shown vertically)
  4. (4) OH bonded to CN and CN (without the CH group)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The molecular formula HOCH(CN)2 indicates a carbon atom bonded to one OH group and two CN groups. Bond line representation does not include representation of H-atoms, so we show only the carbon skeleton with the functional groups. Since the central carbon has OH and two CN groups attached, and H-atoms are not shown in bond line notation, the correct representation is option (4) showing OH with two CN groups attached to a central carbon.
