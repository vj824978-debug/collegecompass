An amplitude modulated signal is plotted below: [Graph showing V(t) with oscillations between 8V and 10V over 100 μs period, with 8 μs marked on the right]. Which one of the following best describes the above signal?

  1. (1) (1 + 9sin(2π × 10^4 t)) sin(2.5π × 10^5 t) V
  2. (2) (9 + sin(4π × 10^4 t)) sin(5π × 10^5 t) V
  3. (3) (9 + sin(2π × 10^4 t)) sin(2.5π × 10^5 t) V
  4. (4) (9 + sin(2.5π × 10^4 t)) sin(2π × 10^4 t) V

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

From the graph: Modulating signal period T_s = 100 μs Carrier signal period T_c = 8 μs Angular frequencies: ω_s = 2π/T_s = 2π/(100 × 10^(-6)) = 2π × 10^4 s^(-1) ω_c = 2π/T_c = 2π/(8 × 10^(-6)) = 2.5π × 10^5 s^(-1) From the graph: V_max = V_c + V_s = 10 V V_min = V_c - V_s = 8 V Solving: V_c = (V_max + V_min)/2 = (10 + 8)/2 = 9 V V_s = (V_max - V_min)/2 = (10 - 8)/2 = 1 V Equation of AM wave: V_AM = (V_c + V_s sin ω_s t) sin ω_c t = (9 + sin(2π × 10^4 t)) sin(2.5π × 10^5 t) (in mV) The answer is option (3).
