When an unpolarized light falls at a particular angle on a glass plate (placed in air), it is observed that the reflected beam is linearly polarized. The angle of refracted beam with respect to the normal is ____. (tan^(-1)(1.52) = 57.7°, refractive indices of air and glass are 1.00 and 1.52, respectively)

  1. (1) 39.6°
  2. (2) 32.3°
  3. (3) 42.6°
  4. (4) 36.3°

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

When reflected beam is linearly polarized, the incident angle is the Brewster's angle (angle of polarization). At Brewster's angle: tan(i) = μ_2/μ_1 = μ_g/μ_a tan(i) = 1.52/1.00 = 1.52 Given: tan^(-1)(1.52) = 57.7° Therefore: i = 57.7° At Brewster's angle, the reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular to each other. So: i + r = 90° where r is the angle of refraction with respect to the normal. r = 90° - i r = 90° - 57.7° r = 32.3°
