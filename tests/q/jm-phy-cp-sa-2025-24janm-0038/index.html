A particle is executing simple harmonic motion with time period 2s and amplitude 1 cm. If D and d are the total distance and displacement covered by the particle in 12.5 s, then D/d is :-

  1. (1) 15/4
  2. (2) 25
  3. (3) 10
  4. (4) 16/5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

A = 1 cm Diagram showing motion from -A to A: -A -------- 0 -------- A n = 12.5/2 = 6.25 cycles In one complete cycle, distance traveled D = 4A = 4 × 1 = 4 cm For 6 complete cycles: D = 4 × 6 = 24 cm For the remaining 0.25 cycle (1/4 cycle): The particle travels from center (0) to extreme position (A) = 1 cm Total distance: D = 24 + 1 = 25 cm For displacement d: After 6 complete cycles, the particle returns to its starting position. For the remaining 0.25 cycle, displacement = 1 cm (from center to extreme) Total displacement: d = 1 cm Therefore: D/d = 25/1 = 25 Ans. (2)
