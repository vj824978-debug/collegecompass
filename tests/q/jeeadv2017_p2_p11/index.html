Two coherent monochromatic point sources S₁ and S₂ of wavelength λ = 600 nm are placed symmetrically on either side of the centre of the circle as shown. The sources are separated by a distance d = 1.8 mm. This arrangement produces interference fringes visible as alternate bright and dark spots on the circumference of the circle. The angular separation between two consecutive bright spots is ΔΘ. Which of the following options is/are correct? [A circle with two point sources S₁ and S₂ on a horizontal diameter separated by distance d, with points P₁ at top and P₂ on the right of the circle. An angle ΔΘ is marked at P₁ between the vertical and the line to one of the sources.]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) A dark spot will be formed at the point P₂
  2. (B) At P₂ the order of the fringe will be maximum
  3. (C) The total number of fringes produced between P₁ and P₂ in the first quadrant is close to 3000
  4. (D) The angular separation between two consecutive bright spots decreases as we move from P₁ to P₂ along the first quadrant

Answer

Correct answer: BC

Solution

Sol. B, C Path difference at point P Δx = d cos θ ... (i) At P₁, θ = 90° ⟹ Δx = 0 ⟹ maxima At P₂, θ = 0 ⟹ Δx = d = nλ ⟹ n = (1.8 × 10⁻³)/(600 × 10⁻⁹) = 3000 → maxima For maxima at P, d cos θ = nλ or d[-sin θ] dθ = (dn)λ Angular fringe width, |dθ/dn| = λ/(d sin θ) as θ↑ dθ↑ [Figure: Circle with two points P₁ (top) and P₂ (right) on circumference, center O, vertical and horizontal diameters shown, angle θ marked between radius OP and horizontal, distance d marked horizontally between points S₁ and S₂, two dotted lines from P₁ and P₂ to point P on right showing path difference]
