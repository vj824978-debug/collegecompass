A sample of red ink (a colloidal suspension) is prepared by mixing eosin dye, egg white, HCHO and water. The component which ensures stability of the ink sample is:

  1. (1) Eosin dye
  2. (2) HCHO
  3. (3) Water
  4. (4) Egg white

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

In a colloidal suspension, stability is maintained by a protective colloid. Let's analyze each component: (1) Eosin dye - This is the dispersed phase (colored component) that gives the ink its color. It does not provide stability. (2) HCHO (Formaldehyde) - This acts as a preservative to prevent microbial growth, but does not provide colloidal stability. (3) Water - This is the dispersion medium but does not prevent aggregation of colloidal particles. (4) Egg white - Egg white is a protein (albumin) that acts as a protective colloid. Proteins adsorb on the surface of colloidal particles and form a protective layer around them. This prevents particle aggregation and flocculation, thereby ensuring the stability of the colloidal suspension. The protein coating reduces interfacial tension and prevents the dispersed particles from coming together. This is why egg white is used as a stabilizing agent in the preparation of red ink. Answer: (4) Egg white
