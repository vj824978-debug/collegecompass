The lines L1, L2, ..., L20 are distinct. For n = 1, 2, 3, ..., 10 all the lines L(2n-1) are parallel to each other and all the lines L2n pass through a given point P. The maximum number of points of intersection of pairs of lines from the set [L1, L2, ..., L20] is equal to ___.

Answer

Correct answer: 101

Solution

10 lines are concurrent (L2, L4, L6, ..., L20 pass through point P), 10 lines are parallel (L1, L3, L5, ..., L19 are parallel to each other). For maximum intersection: Even lines C10(2) (zero point of intersection) + One line from odd × One line from even lines + 1 point of intersection of concurrent lines = C10(0) + 10C1 × 10C1 + 1 = 101
