The major product of the following reaction is P. CH3-C≡C-CH3 (i) Na/liq. NH3 (ii) dil.KMnO4 at 273K → 'P'. Number of oxygen atoms present in product 'P' is ___ (nearest integer).

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Step 1: CH3-C≡C-CH3 reacts with Na/liq. NH3 (Birch reduction) to form cis-alkene: CH3-CH=CH-CH3 (cis geometry). Step 2: Dilute KMnO4 at 273K performs dihydroxylation of the cis-alkene to form a diol. Step 3: Product P is CH3-CH(OH)-CH(OH)-CH3 (2,3-butanediol). Step 4: Count oxygen atoms in P: Two OH groups = 2 oxygen atoms.
