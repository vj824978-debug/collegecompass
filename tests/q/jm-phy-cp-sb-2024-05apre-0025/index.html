A hollow sphere is rolling on a plane surface about its axis of symmetry. The ratio of rotational kinetic energy to its total kinetic energy is x/5. The value of x is __________.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

For a hollow sphere rolling on a plane, the moment of inertia is I = (2/3)mR^2. The rotational kinetic energy is KE_rot = (1/2)Iω^2 = (1/2)(2/3)mR^2·ω^2 = (1/3)mR^2·ω^2. The translational kinetic energy is KE_trans = (1/2)mv^2 = (1/2)m(Rω)^2 = (1/2)mR^2·ω^2. Total kinetic energy is KE_total = KE_rot + KE_trans = (1/3)mR^2·ω^2 + (1/2)mR^2·ω^2 = (5/6)mR^2·ω^2. The ratio is KE_rot/KE_total = [(1/3)mR^2·ω^2] / [(5/6)mR^2·ω^2] = (1/3)/(5/6) = (1/3)·(6/5) = 2/5. Therefore x = 2.
