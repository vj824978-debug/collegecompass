What is the correct order of acidity of the protons marked A-D in the given compounds?

  1. (1) H_C > H_A > H_D > H_B
  2. (2) H_D > H_C > H_B > H_A
  3. (3) H_C > H_D > H_B > H_A
  4. (4) H_C > H_D > H_A > H_B

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

H_C > H_D > H_A > H_B H_C is hydrogen of carboxylic acid - it is the most acidic due to the carboxyl group's strong electron-withdrawing nature and resonance stabilization of the conjugate base. H_D removal will lead to stable carbanion - The benzylic position adjacent to the aromatic ring can form a stabilized carbanion through resonance with the pi electrons of the benzene ring. This makes H_D the second most acidic. H_A is a terminal alkyne hydrogen - Terminal alkynes have moderate acidity (pK_a ~ 25) due to the sp hybridization of carbon, which is more electronegative than sp^2 or sp^3 carbons. H_B is a saturated sp^3 carbon hydrogen - This is the least acidic as it lacks any special stabilization features. It is a simple alkyl hydrogen with very high pK_a. Therefore, the order is: H_C > H_D > H_A > H_B
