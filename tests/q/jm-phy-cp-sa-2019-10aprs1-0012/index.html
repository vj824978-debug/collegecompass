A stationary source emits sound waves of frequency 500 Hz. Two observers moving along a line passing through the source detect sound to be of frequencies 480 Hz and 530 Hz. Their respective speeds are, in m/s⁻¹, (Given speed of sound = 300 m/s)

  1. (1) 12, 16
  2. (2) 12, 18
  3. (3) 16, 14
  4. (4) 8, 18

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using Doppler effect formula for observer moving relative to stationary source: For observer A (detecting 480 Hz - moving away from source): n_A = n(v - v_A)/v = (500/300)(300 - v_A) = 480 500(300 - v_A)/300 = 480 500(300 - v_A) = 480 × 300 300 - v_A = (480 × 300)/500 = 288 v_A = 300 - 288 = 12 m/s For observer B (detecting 530 Hz - moving towards source): n_B = n(v + v_B)/v = (500/300)(300 + v_B) = 530 500(300 + v_B)/300 = 530 500(300 + v_B) = 530 × 300 300 + v_B = (530 × 300)/500 = 318 v_B = 318 - 300 = 18 m/s Therefore, the speeds are 12 m/s and 18 m/s.
