A car is moving with speed of 150 km/h and after applying the brake it will move 27 m before it stops. If the same car is moving with a speed of one third the reported speed then it will stop after travelling ___ m distance.

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Using F_R·d = (1/2)mv^2, the stopping distance is proportional to v^2. When initial speed is V1 = 150 km/h, stopping distance d1 = 27 m. When speed is V2 = V1/3, the ratio d2/d1 = (V2/V1)^2 = (1/3)^2 = 1/9. Therefore, d2 = d1 × (1/9) = 27 × (1/9) = 3 m
