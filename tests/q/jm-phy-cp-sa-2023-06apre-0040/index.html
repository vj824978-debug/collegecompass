The temperature of an ideal gas is increased from 200 K to 800 K. If r.m.s. speed of gas at 200 K is v0, then, r.m.s. speed of the gas at 800 K will be:

  1. (1) v0/4
  2. (2) v0
  3. (3) 4v0
  4. (4) 2v0

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The r.m.s. speed of gas is given by v = sqrt(3RT/M). Since r.m.s. speed is proportional to sqrt(T), we have v'/v0 = sqrt(T'/T0) = sqrt(800/200) = sqrt(4) = 2. Therefore, v' = 2v0.
