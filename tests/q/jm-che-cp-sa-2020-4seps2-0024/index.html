The osmotic pressure of a solution of NaCl is 0.10 atm and that of a glucose solution is 0.20 atm. The osmotic pressure of a solution formed by mixing 1 L of the sodium chloride solution with 2 L of the glucose solution is x × 10^(-3) atm. x is _______. (nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 167

Solution

Osmotic pressure = π = i × C × RT For NaCl i = 2 so π_NaCl = i × C_NaCl × RT 0.1 = 2 × C_NaCl × RT C_NaCl = 0.05/RT For glucose i = 1 so π_glucose = i × C_glucose × RT 0.2 = 1 × C_glucose × RT C_glucose = 0.2/RT η_NaCl = No. of moles NaCl η_NaCl in 1 L = C_NaCl × V_Litre = 0.05/RT η_glucose = No. of moles glucose η_glucose in 2 L = C_glucose × V_Litre = 0.4/RT V_Total = 1 + 2 = 3L so final conc. NaCl = 0.05/(3RT) Final conc. glucose = 0.4/(3RT) π_Total = π_NaCl + π_glucose = [i × C_NaCl + C_glucose] × RT = (2 × 0.05/(3RT) + 0.4/(3RT)) × RT = (0.1/3RT + 0.4/3RT) × RT = 0.5/3 atm = 0.1666 atm = 166.6 × 10^(-3) atm ⟹ 167.00 × 10^(-3) atm so x = 167.00
