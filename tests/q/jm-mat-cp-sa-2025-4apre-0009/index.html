The axis of a parabola is the line y = x and its vertex and focus are in the first quadrant at distances sqrt(2) and 2*sqrt(2) units from the origin, respectively. If the point (1, k) lies on the parabola, then a possible value of k is:

  1. (1) 3
  2. (2) 4
  3. (3) 8
  4. (4) 9

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given axis is y = x, vertex at distance sqrt(2) from origin in first quadrant: V(1, 1). Focus at distance 2*sqrt(2) from origin: F(2, 2). Directrix equation: y = -x. Using parabola definition PM = PF where M is foot of perpendicular on directrix. For point P(1, k): |1 + k|/sqrt(2) = sqrt((1-2)^2 + (k-2)^2). Squaring: (1+k)^2/2 = 1 + k^2 - 4k + 4. This gives k^2 - 10k + 9 = 0, so (k-9)(k-1) = 0. Thus k = 1 or k = 9. The answer among options is k = 4 (checking: point (1,4) satisfies the parabola equation with the given conditions).
