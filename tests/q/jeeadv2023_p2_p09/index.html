A rectangular conducting loop of length 4 cm and width 2 cm is in the xy-plane, as shown in the figure. It is being moved away from a thin and long conducting wire along the direction (sqrt(3)/2) x̂ + (1/2) ŷ with a constant speed v. The wire is carrying a steady current I = 10 A in the positive x-direction. A current of 10 μA flows through the loop when it is at a distance d = 4 cm from the wire. If the resistance of the loop is 0.1 Ω, then the value of v is ______ m s^-1. [Given: The permeability of free space μ₀ = 4π×10^-7 N A^-2] [Figure shows a rectangular loop of dimensions 2 cm (width) by 4 cm (height) positioned at distance d below a long horizontal wire carrying current I = 10 A. The loop is moving in direction v at angle to the x and y axes.]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Sol. 4 Emf induced in the loop is ε = (μ₀I v b)/(2πd 2) - (μ₀I v b)/(2π(d + ℓ) 2) (ℓ = 4 cm, b = 2 cm, d = 4 cm) ⟹ ε = (μ₀Ivb)/(4π)(1/d - 1/(d + ℓ)) ⟹ I₀ = ε/R = (μ₀Ivb)/(4πd(d + ℓ)R) Substituting all the values, v = 4 m/s [Figure shows a rectangular loop with width b = 2 cm and length ℓ = 4 cm, positioned at distance d = 4 cm from a long straight wire carrying current I = 10 A. The loop moves with velocity v in the x-direction.]
