For a dimerization reaction, 2A(g) → A2(g) at 298 K, ΔU° = -20 kJ mol^(-1), ΔS° = -30 J K^(-1) mol^(-1), then the ΔG° will be ________ J.

Answer

Correct answer: -13538.00

Solution

ΔS° = ΔH° - TΔS° = (ΔU° + Δn_g RT) - TΔS° = {-20 + (-1) × (8.314/1000) × 298} - (298/1000) × (-30) kJ = -13.537572 kJ = -13537.57 Joule
