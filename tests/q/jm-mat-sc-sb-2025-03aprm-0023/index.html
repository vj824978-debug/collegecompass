Let the product of the focal distance of the point P(4, 2*sqrt(3)) on the hyperbola H: x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 be 32. Let the length of the conjugate axis of H be p and the length of its latus rectum be q. Then p^2 + q^2 is equal to

Answer

Correct answer: 120

Solution

For a hyperbola x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1, if P(x₁, y₁) is on it, then product of focal distances = |a^2 - ex₁| × |a^2 + ex₁| where e is eccentricity. For point P(4, 2√3): 16/a^2 - 12/b^2 = 1. Product of focal distances = (a^2 - 4e)(a^2 + 4e) = a^4 - 16e^2 = 32, so a^4 - 16(a^2+b^2)/a^2 × a^2 = 32, giving a^4 - 16a^2 - 16b^2 = 32. From point equation and product condition: a^2 = 4, b^2 = 12. Conjugate axis length p = 2b = 4√3, so p^2 = 48. Latus rectum q = 2b^2/a = 24/2 = 12. Therefore p^2 + q^2 = 48 + 144 = 192.
