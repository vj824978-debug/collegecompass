An electromagnetic wave of frequency 100 MHz propagates through a medium of conductivity, σ = 10 mho/m. The ratio of maximum conducting current density to maximum displacement current density is ____. [Take 1/(4πε₀) = 9×10^9 Nm²/C²]

Answer

Correct answer: 1800

Solution

jc = σE E ⟹ E₀sin(ωt - kx) jc = σE₀sin(ωt - kx) ⟹ (jc)max = σE₀ ...(1) jd ⟹ id/A = (1/A) × ε₀ × AdE/dt ⟹ ε₀ × E₀ω cos(ωt - kx) (jd)max ⟹ ε₀E₀ω ...(ii) (i)/(ii) (jc)max/(jd)max = σE₀/(ε₀ωE₀) ⟹ σ/(ε₀ω) ⟹ (10 × 4π × 9×10^9)/(2π × 100 × 10^6) ⟹ 1800
