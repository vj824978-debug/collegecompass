Let A denote the event that a 6-digit integer formed by 0,1,2,3,4,5,6 without repetitions, by divisible by 3. Then probability of event A is equal to:

  1. (1) 4/9
  2. (2) 11/27
  3. (3) 9/56
  4. (4) 3/7

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Total possible 6-digit integers using digits 0,1,2,3,4,5,6 without repetition = 6 × 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 6 × 6!. For divisibility by 3, sum of digits must be divisible by 3. Sum of all digits 0+1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21 (divisible by 3). Favorable cases: (I) Excluding digit 1,2,3,4,5,6 gives sum divisible by 3, total arrangements = 6!. (II) Excluding 0,1,2,3,4,5 gives sum divisible by 3, total = 5!. (III) Excluding 0,1,2,4,5,6 gives sum divisible by 3, total = 5!. Total favorable outcomes = 16 × 5!. Probability = (16 × 5!)/(6 × 6!) = (16 × 5!)/(6 × 6 × 5!) = 16/36 = 4/9.
