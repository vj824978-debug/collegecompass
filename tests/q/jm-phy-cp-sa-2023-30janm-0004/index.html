The magnetic moment associated with two closely wound circular coils A and B of radius r_A = 10 cm and r_B = 20 cm respectively are equal if: (where N_A, I_A and N_B, I_B are number of turn and current of A and B respectively)

  1. (1) N_A I_A = 4N_B I_B
  2. (2) 2N_A I_A = N_B I_B
  3. (3) N_A = 2N_B
  4. (4) 4N_A I_A = N_B I_B

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

M_A = M_B The magnetic moment of a circular coil is given by: M = NI(pi r^2) For coil A: M_A = I_A N_A(pi r_A^2) For coil B: M_B = I_B N_B(pi r_B^2) Given that M_A = M_B: I_A N_A(pi r_A^2) = I_B N_B(pi r_B^2) I_A N_A(pi (0.1)^2) = I_B N_B(pi (0.2)^2) I_A N_A(0.01) = I_B N_B(0.04) I_A N_A = 4 I_B N_B Therefore: N_A I_A = 4N_B I_B
