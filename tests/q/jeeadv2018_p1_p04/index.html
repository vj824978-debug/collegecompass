In the figure below, the switches S₁ and S₂ are closed simultaneously at t = 0 and a current starts to flow in the circuit. Both the batteries have the same magnitude of the electromotive force (emf) and the polarities are as indicated in the figure. Ignore mutual inductance between the inductors. The current I in the middle wire reaches its maximum magnitude I_max at time t = τ. Which of the following statements is (are) true? [Circuit diagram with two voltage sources V, resistors R and 2R, inductors L and 2L, and switches S₁ and S₂]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) I_max = V/(2R)
  2. (B) I_max = V/(4R)
  3. (C) τ = (L/R) ln 2
  4. (D) τ = (2L/R) ln 2

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

Sol. B, D I = (V/R)(1 - e^(-Rt/L)) - (V/R)(1 - e^(-Rt/2L)) For I_max dI/dt = 0 ∴ t = 2L ln 2 / R and I_max = V / 4R
