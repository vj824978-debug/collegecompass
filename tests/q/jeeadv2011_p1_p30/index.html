A ball of mass (m) 0.5 kg is attached to the end of a string having length (L) 0.5 m. The ball is rotated on a horizontal circular path about vertical axis. The maximum tension that the string can bear is 324 N. The maximum possible value of angular velocity of ball (in radian/s) is [Figure shows a ball of mass m attached to a string of length L rotating horizontally about a vertical axis]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 9
  2. (B) 18
  3. (C) 27
  4. (D) 36

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Sol. (D) 324 = 0.5 ω² (0.5) ω² = 324 × 4 ω = √1296 = 36 rad/s
