A point source is kept at the center of a spherically enclosed detector. If the volume of the detector increased by 8 times, the intensity will

  1. (1) increase by 8 times
  2. (2) increase by 64 times
  3. (3) decrease by 8 times
  4. (4) decrease by 4 times

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

V → 8V ⇒ R → 2R ⇒ A → 4A ⇒ I → I_0/4 Explanation: For a spherical detector with a point source at the center: - Volume V = (4/3)πR^3 - If V → 8V, then (4/3)πR^3 → 8(4/3)πR^3 - This gives R^3 → 8R^3, so R → 2R The surface area of the sphere is A = 4πR^2 - If R → 2R, then A → 4π(2R)^2 = 4π(4R^2) = 4(4πR^2) = 4A Intensity is defined as power per unit area: - I = P/A - The power P from the point source remains constant - Initial intensity: I_0 = P/A_0 - Final intensity: I = P/(4A_0) = I_0/4 Therefore, the intensity decreases by a factor of 4, which means it decreases by 4 times (or decreases to 1/4 of original).
