Water is being filled at the rate of 1 cm³/sec in a right circular conical vessel (vertex downwards) of height 35 cm and diameter 14 cm. When the height of the water level is 10 cm, the rate (in cm²/sec) at which the wet conical surface area of the vessel increase, is

  1. (1) 5
  2. (2) sqrt(21)/5
  3. (3) sqrt(26)/5
  4. (4) sqrt(26)/10

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: dV/dt = 1 cm³/sec, cone height H = 35 cm, diameter = 14 cm so radius R = 7 cm. For similar cones: r/h = R/H = 7/35 = 1/5, so r = h/5. Volume V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)π(h/5)²h = (1/75)πh³. Differentiating: dV/dt = (1/25)πh² dh/dt = 1, so dh/dt = 25/(πh²). Lateral surface area S = πrl where l = sqrt(h² + r²) = sqrt(h² + h²/25) = (h/5)sqrt(26). So S = π(h/5) · (h/5)sqrt(26) = (π/25)sqrt(26)h². Differentiating: dS/dt = (2π sqrt(26)/25)h · dh/dt = (2sqrt(26)/25)h · (25/πh²) = 2sqrt(26)/(πh). At h = 10: dS/dt = 2sqrt(26)/(10π) = sqrt(26)/5π.
