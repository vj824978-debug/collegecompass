A coil having N turns is wound tightly in the form of a spiral with inner and outer radii 'a' and 'b' respectively. Find the magnetic field at centre, when a current I passes through coil.

  1. (1) (mu_0*I*(a+b))/(8*(a-b))
  2. (2) (mu_0*N*I)/(2*(b-a))*log(b/a)
  3. (3) (mu_0*I)/(4*(a-b))*(1/a - 1/b)
  4. (4) (mu_0*I*(a-b))/(8*(a+b))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Consider an elementary ring of radius r and thickness dr. Number of turns in this ring: dN = (N/(b-a))*dr. Magnetic field due to this ring at centre: dB = (mu_0*I*dN)/(2*r). Therefore B = integral from a to b of (mu_0*I/(2*r)) * (N/(b-a)) dr = (mu_0*I*N)/(2*(b-a)) * integral from a to b of (1/r) dr = (mu_0*I*N)/(2*(b-a)) * log(b/a)
