The least acidic compound among the following is:

  1. (1) A) EtO-benzene-OH (ethoxy phenol)
  2. (2) B) benzene-SO3H (benzenesulfonic acid)
  3. (3) C) cyclopentane-COOH (cyclopentanecarboxylic acid)
  4. (4) D) EtO2C-C≡C-H (ethyl propiolate)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Acidity depends on the stability of conjugate bases. SO3H is the strongest acid (pKa ~0), COOH is moderately strong (pKa ~4-5), alkyne H is weakly acidic (pKa ~25), and phenolic OH is weak (pKa ~10). Among these, the alkyne proton in option D is the least acidic because sp-hybridized carbons holding the hydrogen make it very weak. Option D (ethyl propiolate with terminal alkyne) has the least acidic proton.
