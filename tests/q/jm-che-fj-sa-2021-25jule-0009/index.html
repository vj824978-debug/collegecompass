Starting with Br-CH2-CHO, treatment with EtOH excess and dry HCl gas gives product 'A' (major product). Product 'A' is then treated with t-BuO' K' to give product 'B' (major product). Identify products 'A' and 'B' respectively. [where Et = C2H5, 1-Bu = (CH3)3C-]

  1. (1) EtO-CH2-CHO and EtO-CH=CH-O-Bu with OH group
  2. (2) Br-CH2-CH(OEt)2 and BuO-CH=CH-OEt with H2C side chain
  3. (3) Br-CH2-CH(OEt)2 and H2C=CH-OEt with OEt groups
  4. (4) EtO-CH2-CH(OEt)2 and H2C=CH-OEt with OEt groups

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Step 1: Br-CH2-CHO reacts with excess EtOH and dry HCl gas. The aldehyde group undergoes acetal formation with ethanol, converting -CHO to -CH(OEt)2. The Br remains unchanged. Product A is Br-CH2-CH(OEt)2 (acetal). Step 2: Product A is treated with t-BuO' K' (potassium tert-butoxide), a strong base. This causes elimination of HBr and simultaneous elimination of an ethoxy group, forming a double bond. The product B is H2C=CH-OEt (vinyl ethyl ether), where the brominated carbon converts to sp2 and forms a C=C double bond with loss of Br and OEt groups.
