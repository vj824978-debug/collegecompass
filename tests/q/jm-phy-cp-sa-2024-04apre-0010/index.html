Given below are two statements: Statement I: The contact angle between a solid and a liquid is a property of the material of the solid and liquid as well. Statement II: The rise of a liquid in a capillary tube does not depend on the inner radius of the tube. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  2. (2) Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
  3. (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false.
  4. (4) Both Statement I and Statement II are true.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Statement I is correct as contact angle depends on cohesive and adhesive forces between the solid and liquid. Statement II is incorrect because the height of liquid rise in a capillary tube is given by h = 2T cos(theta_c)/(rho*g*r), where r is the radius of the tube. Since h is inversely proportional to r, the rise depends on the inner radius of the tube.
