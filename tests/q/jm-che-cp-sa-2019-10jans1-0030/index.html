5.1 g NH4SH is introduced in 3.0 L evacuated flask at 327°C. 30% of the solid NH4SH decomposed to NH3 and H2S as gases. The Kp of the reaction at 327°C is (R = 0.082 L atm mol^(-1) K^(-1), Molar mass of S = 32 g mol^(-1) molar mass of N = 14 g mol^(-1))

  1. (1) 0.242 × 10^(-4) atm^2
  2. (2) 1 × 10^(-4) atm^2
  3. (3) 4.9 × 10^(-3) atm^2
  4. (4) 0.242 atm^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

NH4SH(s) ⇌ NH3(g) + H2S(g) t = 0: 5.1/51 = 0.1 mole t = t: 0.1 - 0.1 × 0.3, 0.03, 0.03 n_NH3 + n_H2S = 0.06 PV = nRT PT × 3 = 0.06 × 0.082 × 600 PT = 0.984 ∴ P_NH3 = P_H2S = 0.492 ∴ Kp = (0.492)^2 = 0.242 atm^2
