A wire of length 20 m to be cut into two pieces. A piece of length l₁ is bent to make a square of area A₁ and the other piece of length l₂ is made into a circle of area A₂. If 2A₁ + 3A₂ is minimum then (πl₁) : l₂ is equal to

  1. (1) 1 : 6
  2. (2) 3 : 1
  3. (3) 6 : 1
  4. (4) 4 : 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

l₁ = 20 - x, l₂ = x 2A₁ + 3A₂ = 2((20-x)/4)² + 3π(x/(2π))² f(x) = (20-x)²/8 + 3x²/(4π) f'(x₀) = (1/8)·2(20-x)·(-1) + (3/(4π))·2x = 0 0 = -(1/4)(20-x₀) + (6x₀)/(4π) (20-x₀)/4 = (6x₀)/(4π) π(20-x₀) = 6x₀ 20π = (6+π)x₀ x₀ = 20π/(π+6) πl₁/l₂ = π((20-x₀)/x₀) = π((x₀+6)/π - 1) = 6 Therefore, (πl₁) : l₂ = 6 : 1
