A tube of length 50 cm is filled completely with an incompressible liquid of mass 250 g and closed at both ends. The tube is then rotated in horizontal plane about one of its ends with a uniform angular velocity x*sqrt(F) rad s^(-1). If F be the force exerted by the liquid at the other end then the value of x will be ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 4.00

Solution

When the tube rotates about one end with angular velocity ω = x*sqrt(F) rad/s, each element of liquid experiences centrifugal force. The force exerted by liquid at the other end is found by integrating the centrifugal forces: F = ∫(dm)ω²x = ∫₀ᴸ(m/L)ω²x dx = (m/L)ω²(L²/2) = mω²L/2. Given: m = 250g = 0.25 kg, L = 50 cm = 0.5 m. Substituting ω = x*sqrt(F): F = (0.25)(x²F)(0.5)/2 = 0.0625x²F. This gives 1 = 0.0625x², so x² = 16, thus x = 4.
