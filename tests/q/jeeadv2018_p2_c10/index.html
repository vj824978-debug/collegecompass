Answer

Correct answer: 7

Solution

The image shows 9 different prostaglandin-like structures, each containing a cyclopentanone ring with hydroxyl groups and two long hydrocarbon chains with hydroxyl substituents. The structures are differentiated by: 1. The stereochemistry at the cyclopentanone ring (wedge/dash bonds) 2. The geometry of the double bonds in the chains (E/Z configuration) 3. The position and stereochemistry of hydroxyl groups on the side chains The first structure at the top is marked as (optically inactive), while all others are marked as (optically active). The variations in stereochemistry between consecutive structures demonstrate how small changes in molecular configuration affect optical activity in these natural product analogs.
