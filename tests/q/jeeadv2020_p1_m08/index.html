Let M be a 3 × 3 invertible matrix with real entries and let I denote the 3 × 3 identity matrix. If M^(-1) = adj(adj M), then which of the following statements is/are ALWAYS TRUE?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) M = I
  2. (B) det M = 1
  3. (C) M^2 = I
  4. (D) (adj M)^2 = I

Answer

Correct answer: BCD

Solution

M⁻¹ = adj (adj M) = |M| M ⟹ |M⁻¹| = ||M| M| = 1/|M| = |M|³ |M| ⟹ |M| = 1 ..... (1) adj M/|M| = |M|M adj M = |M|² M ⟹ adj M = M as |M| = 1 ⟹ M (adj M) = M² ⟹ M² = I adj M = M as |M| = 1 (adj M)² = I (as adj M = M) M adj M = [|M|] I replace M by adj M (adj M) adj (adj M) = [adj M] I = |M|² I = |M|² I Multiplying by M M(adj M) adj (adj M) = |M|²M |M| adj(adj M) = |M|² M adj (adj M) = |M| M
