A monochromatic beam of light is incident at 60° on one face of an equilateral prism of refractive index n and emerges from the opposite face making an angle θ(n) with the normal (see the figure). [Figure shows an equilateral triangular prism with light ray entering at 60° to the normal on the left face and emerging at angle θ to the normal on the right face] For n = sqrt(3) the value of θ is 60° and dθ/dn = m. The value of m is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Snell's Law on 1st surface: √3/2 = n sin r₁ sin r₁ = √3/(2n) ...(i) ⟹ cos r₁ = √(1 - 3/(4n²)) = √(4n² - 3)/(2n) r₁ + r₂ = 60° ...(ii) Snell's Law on 2nd surface: n sin r₂ = sin θ Using equation (i) and (ii) n sin(60° - r₁) = sin θ n[√3/2 cos r₁ - 1/2 sin r₁] = sin θ d/dn[√3/4(√(4n² - 3) - 1)] = cos θ · dθ/dn for θ = 60° and n = √3 ⟹ dθ/dn = 2
