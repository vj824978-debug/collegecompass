Match list-I with List-II. List-I Polymers: A. Phenol-formaldehyde resin, B. Copolymer of 1,3-butadiene styrene, C. Polyester of glycol and phthalic acid, D. Polyester of glycol and terephthalic acid. List-II Commercial names: I. Glyptal, II. Novolac, III. Buna-S, IV. Dacron

  1. (1) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
  2. (2) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
  3. (3) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
  4. (4) A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Matching polymers with their commercial names: A. Phenol-formaldehyde resin = Novolac (II), B. Copolymer of 1,3-butadiene styrene = Buna-S (III), C. Polyester of glycol and phthalic acid = Glyptal (I), D. Polyester of glycol and terephthalic acid = Dacron (IV). Therefore A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
