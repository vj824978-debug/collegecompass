Consider the above reactions, the product A and product B respectively are: Benzene reacts with Br2 (excess) in H2O to give Major Product A. Then Product A reacts with (i) (CH3CO)2O (ii) Br2, CH3COOH (iii) HCl to give Major Product B.

  1. (1) 2,4,6-tribromaniline and 2,4,6-tribromoaniline with acetyl substitution
  2. (2) 2,4,6-tribromaniline and 2,4,6-tribromobenzamide
  3. (3) 2,4,6-tribromaniline and 4-bromoaniline
  4. (4) aniline and 4-bromoaniline

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Aniline reacts with Br2 (excess) in H2O. The NH2 group is strongly activating and ortho/para directing. With excess Br2, tribromination occurs at 2,4,6 positions giving 2,4,6-tribromaniline (Product A). When Product A is treated with (CH3CO)2O, the NH2 is acetylated to form an amide. The acetyl group is weakly deactivating and meta-directing. Subsequent bromination with Br2 and CH3COOH occurs at the meta positions. Finally, HCl treatment removes the acetyl protecting group, giving 4-bromoaniline (Product B) as the major product.
