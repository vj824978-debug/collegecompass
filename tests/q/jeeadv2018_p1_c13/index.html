The solubility of a salt of weak acid (AB) at pH 3 is Y × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹. The value of Y is ___. (Given that the value of solubility product of AB (Ksp) = 2 × 10⁻¹⁰ and the value of ionization constant of HB (Ka) = 1 × 10⁻⁸)

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4.47

Solution

Sol. 4.47 AB ⇌ A⁺ + B⁻ S (S-x) 2×10⁻¹⁰ = S(S-x) ... (1) B⁻ + H⁺ ⇌ HB (S-x) 10⁻³ x 10⁻⁸ = x/((S-x)×10⁻³) x/(S-x) = 10⁵ ... (2) Multiply equation (1) and (2). S.x = 2 × 10⁻⁵ From Eq. (1) S² - Sx = 2×10⁻¹⁰ S² - 2×10⁻⁵ = 2×10⁻¹⁰ S² = 2×10⁻⁵ + 2×10⁻¹⁰ ≅ 2×10⁻⁵ S = 4.47 × 10⁻³ y = 4.47
