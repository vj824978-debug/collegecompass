The area of the region bounded by the curve y = max{|x|, |x - 2|}, the x-axis and the lines x = -2 and x = 4 is equal to ____

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

For y = max{|x|, |x - 2|}: When x < 1, |x| >= |x - 2|, so y = |x|. When x >= 1, |x - 2| >= |x|, so y = |x - 2|. On [-2, 4]: For x in [-2, 0], y = -x. For x in [0, 1], y = x. For x in [1, 2], y = 2 - x. For x in [2, 4], y = x - 2. Area = integral from -2 to 0 of (-x)dx + integral from 0 to 1 of x dx + integral from 1 to 2 of (2-x)dx + integral from 2 to 4 of (x-2)dx = 2 + 1/2 + 1/2 + 2 = 5
