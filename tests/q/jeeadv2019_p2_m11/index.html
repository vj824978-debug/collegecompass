Five persons A, B, C, D and E are seated in a circular arrangement. If each of them is given a hat of one of the three colours red, blue and green, then the number of ways of distributing the hats such that the persons seated in adjacent seats get different coloured hats is ______

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 30.00

Solution

Maximum number of hats of same colour are 2 and minimum colour required are 3. So, the selection of hats will have to be of the type AABBC ³C₁ ways to select hat having single colour. Then distribute that single hat in ⁵C₁ ways. ⁵C₁ ways to distribute hat to adjacent person, after that alternate coloured hat will be given so 3 × 5 × 2 = 30 [Circle diagram with 5 points labeled A, B, C, D, E arranged around the circumference] [Mathematical notation showing summation formulas with k and n]
