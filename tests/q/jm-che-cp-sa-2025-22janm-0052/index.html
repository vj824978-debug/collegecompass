Which of the following statement is not true for radioactive decay ?

  1. (1) Amount of radioactive substance remained after three half lives is 1/8 th of original amount.
  2. (2) Decay constant does not depend upon temperature.
  3. (3) Decay constant increases with increase in temperature.
  4. (4) Half life is ln 2 times of 1/rate constant.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Decay constant is independent of temperature. This is a fundamental property of radioactive decay. The decay constant (lambda) is a characteristic property of a particular radioactive nuclide and depends only on the nuclear properties of that nuclide, not on external conditions like temperature, pressure, or chemical state. Therefore, statement (3) which says 'Decay constant increases with increase in temperature' is NOT true for radioactive decay. All other statements are correct: (1) After three half-lives, the amount remaining is (1/2)^3 = 1/8 of the original amount - TRUE. (2) Decay constant does not depend upon temperature - TRUE. (4) Half-life is related to decay constant by t_(1/2) = ln(2)/lambda, which means half-life is ln 2 times of 1/rate constant - TRUE.
