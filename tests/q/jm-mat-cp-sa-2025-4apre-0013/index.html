Let A be the point of intersection of the lines L1: (x-7)/1 = (y-5)/0 = (z-3)/(-1) and L2: (x-1)/3 = (y+3)/4 = (z+7)/5. Let B and C be the points on the lines L1 and L2 respectively such that AB = AC = sqrt(15). Then the square of the area of the triangle ABC is:

  1. (1) 57
  2. (2) 63
  3. (3) 60
  4. (4) 54

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Find point A by solving the intersection of L1 and L2. From L1: x=7+t, y=5, z=3-t. From L2: x=1+3s, y=-3+4s, z=-7+5s. Solving gives A=(7,5,3). Direction vectors: d1=(1,0,-1), d2=(3,4,5). Find angle θ between lines: cos(θ) = |3+0-5|/(sqrt(2)×sqrt(50)) = 2/10 = 1/5. Therefore sin(θ) = 2sqrt(6)/5. Since AB=AC=sqrt(15) and angle at A is θ, Area = (1/2)×sqrt(15)×sqrt(15)×sin(θ) = (1/2)×15×(2sqrt(6)/5) = 3sqrt(6). Therefore (Area)^2 = 9×6 = 54.
