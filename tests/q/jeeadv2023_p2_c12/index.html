50 mL of 0.2 molal urea solution (density = 1.012 g mL⁻¹ at 300 K) is mixed with 250 mL of a solution containing 0.06 g of urea. Both the solutions were prepared in the same solvent. The osmotic pressure (in Torr) of the resulting solution at 300 K is ___. [Use: Molar mass of urea = 60 g mol⁻¹; gas constant, R = 62 L Torr K⁻¹ mol⁻¹; Assume, Δ_mix H = 0, Δ_mix V = 0]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 682

Solution

Sol. 682 0.2 molal means 0.2 moles in 1000 g of solvent. Volume = M/d Mass of solution = 1012 g Volume = 1012g/(1.012 g ml⁻¹) V = 1000.00 ml 1000.00 ml → 0.2 moles 50 ml of solution = (0.2/1000) × 50 moles n_urea = 0.01 moles In 2ⁿᵈ solution: n_urea = 0.06/60 = 0.001 Final molarity (M) = (n₁ + n₂)/(V₁ + V₂) = (0.01 + 0.001)/((50 + 250)/1000) M = 11/300 π = CRT = (11/300) × 62 × 300 = 682 torr
