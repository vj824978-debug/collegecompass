Assume that the nuclear binding energy per nucleon (B/A) versus mass number (A) is as shown in the figure. Use this plot to choose the correct choice(s) given below. Figure: [A graph showing B/A (MeV) on y-axis from 0 to 8, and A (mass number) on x-axis. The curve shows B/A ≈ 2 for A < 50, rises to a peak of approximately 8.5 at A ≈ 60, remains high around 8 for A from about 60-100, then decreases to about 5 for A > 200]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) Fusion of two nuclei with mass numbers lying in the range of 1 < A < 50 will release energy
  2. (B) Fusion of two nuclei with mass numbers lying in the range of 51 < A < 100 will release energy
  3. (C) Fission of a nucleus lying in the mass range of 100 < A < 200 will release energy when broken into two equal fragments
  4. (D) Fission of a nucleus lying in the mass range of 200 < A < 260 will release energy when broken into two equal fragments

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

If (BE)_final − (BE)_initial > 0 Energy will be released.
