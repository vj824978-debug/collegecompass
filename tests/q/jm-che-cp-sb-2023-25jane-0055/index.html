Number of compounds giving (i) red colouration with ceric ammonium nitrate and also (ii) positive iodoform test from the following is ____

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

The compounds which give red colour with ceric ammonium nitrate and also give positive iodoform test are those containing both: (i) A hydroxyl group (-OH) that gives red colour with ceric ammonium nitrate (ii) A methyl ketone group (CH3-CO-) or a secondary alcohol adjacent to CH3 that gives positive iodoform test Analyzing each compound: 1. Acetophenone (C6H5-CO-CH3): Has methyl ketone but no -OH group. Does not give red colour with ceric ammonium nitrate. ✗ 2. Benzyl alcohol with OH on side chain (C6H5-CH2-CH(OH)-CH3): Has -OH group (red with ceric ammonium nitrate) and CH3 group on secondary alcohol (positive iodoform test). ✓ 3. Formaldehyde (O=CH-H): No -OH group. ✗ 4. Secondary alcohol without CH3: May give red colour but not iodoform test. ✗ 5. Ether: No -OH group, no iodoform test. ✗ 6. Benzyl alcohol (C6H5-CH2-CH2-OH): Has -OH group but no methyl ketone or secondary alcohol with CH3. ✗ 7. Cyclohexane-methanol (cyclohexyl-CH2-OH): Has -OH group but no methyl ketone group. ✗ 8. Cyclohexane-methanol variant with OH on ring (cyclohexyl-CH(OH)-CH3): Has -OH group (red with ceric ammonium nitrate) and CH3 on secondary alcohol (positive iodoform test). ✓ The compounds meeting both criteria are: - Benzyl alcohol derivative with secondary alcohol and methyl group - Cyclohexyl secondary alcohol with methyl group - One more similar compound Total: 3 compounds
