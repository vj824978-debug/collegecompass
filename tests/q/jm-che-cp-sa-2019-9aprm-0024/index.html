At a given temperature T, gases Ne, Ar, Xe and Kr are found to deviate from ideal gas behaviour. Their equation of state is given as P = RT/(V - b) at T.

  1. (1) Kr
  2. (2) Ar
  3. (3) Xe
  4. (4) Ne

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

P = RT/(V - b) P(V - b) = RT PV - Pb = RT (P + a/V^2)(V - b) = RT At high pressure. P(V - b) = RT PV - Pb = RT PV/RT - Pb/RT = 1 Z = 1 + Pb/RT Z > 1, Z ∝ b Since Z is greater than 1 and proportional to b, the gas with the largest value of b (excluded volume) will have the largest compressibility factor. Among Ne, Ar, Xe, and Kr, Xenon (Xe) has the largest atomic size and therefore the largest excluded volume parameter b, making it deviate most significantly from ideal behaviour at high pressures.
