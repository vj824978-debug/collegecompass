With respect to the compounds I – V, choose the correct statement(s). [Figure shows: I - triphenylmethane (three benzene rings connected to central CH); II - benzene with H substituent; III - methane (H-CH3); IV - cyclopentadiene with H substituent; V - acetylene (H≡C≡H)]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The acidity of compound I is due to delocalization in the conjugate base
  2. (B) The conjugate base of compound IV is aromatic
  3. (C) Compound II becomes more acidic, when it has a −NO₂ substituent
  4. (D) The acidity of compounds follows the order I > IV > V > II > III

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution

Sol. A, B, C [Figure I: Triphenylmethane with three phenyl groups attached to central carbon with hydrogen] (pKₐ = 31.5) [Figure II: Benzene ring with hydrogen at top] (pKₐ = 43) CH₄ III (pKₐ = 48) [Figure IV: Cyclopentadiene ring with two hydrogens shown above the ring] (pKₐ = 16) [Figure V: Acetylene with triple bond between two carbons, hydrogens on ends] (pKₐ = 25) Correct order and acidity = IV > V > I > II > III (Decreasing acidity)
