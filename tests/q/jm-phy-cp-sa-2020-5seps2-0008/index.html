A driver in a car, approaching a vertical wall notices that the frequency of his car horn, has changed from 440 Hz to 480 Hz, when it gets reflected from the wall. If the speed of sound in air is 345 m/s, then the speed of the car is -

  1. (1) 36 km/hr
  2. (2) 24 km/hr
  3. (3) 18 km/hr
  4. (4) 54 km/hr

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

f1 = frequency heard by wall = f_s * (v_s / (v_s - v_c)) f2 = frequency heard by driver after reflection from wall f2 = ((v_s + v_c) / v_s) * f1 = ((v_s + v_c) / (v_s - v_c)) * f_0 f2 / f_0 = (v_s - v_c) / (v_s + v_c) 480 / 440 = (v_s - v_c) / (v_s + v_c) 48 / 44 = (v_s - v_c) / (v_s + v_c) 12(v_s + v_c) = 11(v_s - v_c) 12v_s + 12v_c = 11v_s - 11v_c 23v_c = v_s v_c = v_s / 23 = 345 / 23 = 15 m/s = (15 × 18) / 5 = 54 km/hr
