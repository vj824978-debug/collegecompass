The major products of the following reaction are: Starting material is a benzene ring with OH group at top and Cl group at bottom. Reaction conditions: (1) CHCl3/aq. NaOH (2) HCHO, NaOH (conc.) (3) H3O+

  1. (1) OH group at top, CH2OH group on side, and formic acid as byproduct
  2. (2) OH group at top, Cl at bottom, CH2OH group on side, and formic acid as byproduct
  3. (3) OH group at top, Cl at bottom, COOH group on side, and methanol as byproduct
  4. (4) OH group at top, OH at bottom, COOH group on side, and methanol as byproduct

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The reaction sequence involves a Reimer-Tiemann reaction followed by Cannizzaro reaction. Step 1: CHCl3 with aq. NaOH reacts with the phenol (activated by OH group). The OH group is ortho/para directing. Due to steric factors and electronic effects, the formyl group (-CHO) is preferentially introduced at the ortho position to the OH group. The Cl atom remains on the ring. This gives 4-chloro-2-hydroxybenzaldehyde. Step 2: HCHO and concentrated NaOH under heating causes the aldehyde to undergo Cannizzaro reaction (disproportionation). One molecule of the aldehyde is oxidized to carboxylic acid while another is reduced to primary alcohol. However, with excess formaldehyde and the presence of the aldehyde from step 1, the aldehyde from step 1 gets reduced to CH2OH while formic acid (HCOOH) is formed as a byproduct from formaldehyde's disproportionation. Step 3: Acidification with H3O+ protonates any remaining bases and preserves the products. The final major products are: 4-chloro-2-hydroxybenzyl alcohol (CH2OH at ortho position to OH, with Cl at para position) and formic acid (HCOOH) as byproduct.
