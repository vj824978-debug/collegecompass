What will be the nature of flow of water form a circular tap, when its flow rate increased from 0.18L/min to 0.48L/min? The radius of the tap and viscosity of water are 0.5 cm and 10^(-3) Pa·s, respectively. (Density of water: 10^3 kg/m^3)

  1. (1) Remains steady flow
  2. (2) Unsteady to steady flow
  3. (3) Remains turbulent flow
  4. (4) Steady flow to unsteady flow

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using Reynolds number R = (ρvD)/η. In first case: v₁ = 0.0382 m/s, R₁ = 3822 < 4000 → Steady flow. In second case: v₂ = 0.1019 m/s, R₂ = 10191 > 4000 → Turbulent flow. Therefore, flow changes from steady to turbulent, indicating transition from steady flow to unsteady flow.
