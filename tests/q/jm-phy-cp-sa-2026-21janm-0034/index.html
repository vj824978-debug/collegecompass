A parallel plate capacitor has capacitance C, when there is vacuum within the parallel plates. A sheet having thickness (1/3)^rd of the separation between the plates and relative permittivity K is introduced between the plates. The new capacitance of the system is:

  1. (1) 3KC/(2K+1)
  2. (2) CK/(2+K)
  3. (3) 3CK^2/(2K+1)^2
  4. (4) 4KC/(3K-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Initial capacitance: C = A*ε₀/d. After introducing a dielectric sheet of thickness t = d/3 and permittivity K between the plates, the system acts as two capacitors in series: C₁ (vacuum gap of 2d/3) and C₂ (dielectric gap of d/3). C₁ = 3Aε₀/(2d) = (3/2)C, C₂ = 3Aε₀K/d = 3KC. Using series formula: Ceq = (C₁×C₂)/(C₁+C₂) = [(3/2)C × 3KC]/[(3/2)C + 3KC] = [9KC²/2]/[(3/2)C(2K+1)] = 3KC/(2K+1)
