A concave mirror for face viewing has focal length of 0.4 m. The distance at which you hold the mirror from your face in order to see your image upright with a magnification of 5 is:

  1. (1) 1.60 m
  2. (2) 0.16 m
  3. (3) 0.32 m
  4. (4) 0.24 m

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: Focal length f = 0.4 m = 40 cm Magnification m = 5 (upright image) For upright image with concave mirror, the object must be between pole and focus (virtual image) Using magnification formula: m = f/(f - u) 5 = -40/(-40 - u) 5 = 40/(40 + u) 5(40 + u) = 40 200 + 5u = 40 5u = -160 u = -32 cm = 0.32 m However, this gives the object distance as 32 cm from the mirror.
