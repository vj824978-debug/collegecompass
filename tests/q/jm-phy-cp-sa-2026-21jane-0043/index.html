A large drum having radius R is spinning around its axis with angular velocity omega, as shown in figure. The minimum value of omega so that a body of mass M remains stuck to the inner wall of the drum, taking the coefficient of friction between the drum surface and mass M is mu, is:

  1. (1) sqrt(mu*g/R)
  2. (2) sqrt(2*g/(mu*R))
  3. (3) sqrt(g/(2*mu*R))
  4. (4) sqrt(g/(mu*R))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For a body of mass M on the inner wall of a spinning drum: Forces acting on the body: - Normal force N (radially inward) = m*omega^2*r = m*omega^2*R - Weight mg (vertically downward) - Friction force f (vertically upward at minimum omega) At minimum omega, the friction force is at its maximum: f = mu*N = mu*m*omega^2*R For the body to remain stuck (not sliding down), the friction must balance the weight: mu*m*omega^2*R = mg Simplifying: mu*omega^2*R = g omega^2 = g/(mu*R) omega = sqrt(g/(mu*R)) Therefore, the minimum angular velocity is omega_min = sqrt(g/(mu*R))
