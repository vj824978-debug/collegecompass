The major product (A) formed in the following reaction sequence is: nitrobenzene treated with (i) Sn, HCl (ii) Ac2O, Pyridine (iii) Br2, AcOH (iv) NaOH (aq)

  1. (1) 2,6-dibromaniline (ortho and para dibrominated product)
  2. (2) 2,4,6-tribromaniline (trisubstituted product with NH2 at ortho positions and Br at ortho, para positions)
  3. (3) 4-bromoaniline (monosubstituted product)
  4. (4) 3,5-dibromoaniline (meta disubstituted product)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Starting material is nitrobenzene. Step (i): Sn/HCl reduces NO2 to NH2, forming aniline. Step (ii): Ac2O/Pyridine acetylates NH2 to form acetanilide (NHCOCH3). Step (iii): Br2/AcOH - acetanilide undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution. The acetamido group is ortho/para directing, so bromination occurs at ortho and para positions, giving 2,4,6-tribromoacetanilide as major product. Step (iv): NaOH (aq) hydrolyzes the acetamido group back to NH2, yielding 2,4,6-tribromoaniline. The answer is option 2.
