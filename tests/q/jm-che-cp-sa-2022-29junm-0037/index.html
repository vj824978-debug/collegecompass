In isolation of which one of the following metals from their ores, the use of cyanide salt is not commonly involved?

  1. (1) Zinc
  2. (2) Gold
  3. (3) Silver
  4. (4) Copper

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

In the extraction of Silver and Gold, NaCN is used to leach the metal. The equations show: 4Au(s) + 8CN(aq) + 2H2O + O2(g) → 4[Au(CN)2]^-(aq) + 4OH^-(aq) and 2[Au(CN)2]^-(aq) + Zn → 2Au(s) + [Zn(CN)4]^2-(aq). In ores containing ZnS and PbS, the depressant used is NaCN. However, Zinc is extracted from its ores (mainly ZnS) using roasting and reduction processes, not by cyanide salt leaching. Copper is also extracted without commonly using cyanide salts. But the key answer is Zinc as cyanide salt is NOT commonly involved in its isolation.
