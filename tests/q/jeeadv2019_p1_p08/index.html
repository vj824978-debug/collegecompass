A charged shell of radius R carries a total charge Q. Given φ as the flux of electric field through a closed cylindrical surface of height h, radius r and with its center same as that of the shell. Here, center of the cylinder is a point on the axis of the cylinder which is equidistant from its top and bottom surfaces. Which of the following option(s) is/are correct? [ε₀ is permittivity of free space]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) If h < 8R/5 and r = 3R/5 then φ = 0
  2. (B) If h > 2R and r > R then φ = Q/ε₀
  3. (C) If h > 2R and r = 4R/5 then φ = Q/5ε₀
  4. (D) If h > 2R and r = 3R/5 then φ = Q/5ε₀

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

The problem shows four options for geometric configurations with a sphere of radius R. Option (A) shows a cylinder with radius 3R/5 and height h > 8R/5 inscribed in a sphere. Option (B) shows a cylinder with radius r > R and height h > 2R with a sphere inside. Option (C) is not shown. Option (D) shows a cylinder with radius r = 3R/5 and height h > 2R with a sphere inside, and includes the formula φ = Q/(4πε₀(2)2π(1 - cos 37°)). [Option A: sphere circumscribing a cylinder with radius 3R/5 and height h > 8R/5] [Option B: cylinder with radius r > R and height h > 2R containing a sphere] [Option D: cylinder with radius 3R/5 and height h > 2R containing a sphere, with 37° angle marked and electric field formula] The correct solutions are options A, B, and D.
