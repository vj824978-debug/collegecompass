The least count of a screw gauge is 0.01 mm. If the pitch is increased by 75% and number of divisions on the circular scale is reduced by 50%, the new least count will be ______ × 10^(-3) mm.

Answer

Correct answer: 35

Solution

Given least count of Screw Gauge = 0.01 mm L.C. = (pitch)/(No.of circular turn) = P/N = 0.01 m New pitch = P(1 + 0.75)/(N(1 - 0.5)) = P/N × (1.75)/(0.5) = (0.01) × 3.5 = 0.035 mm = 35 × 10^(-3) mm ∴ Ans. is 35
