The roots of the quadratic equation 3x^2 - px + q = 0 are 10th and 11th terms of an arithmetic progression with common difference 3/2. If the sum of the first 11 terms of this arithmetic progression is 88, then q - 2q is equal to ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 474

Solution

S_11 = 11/2 (2a + 10d) = 88 a + 5d = 8 a = 8 - 5 × 3/2 = 1/2 Roots are T_10 = a + 9d = 1/2 + 9 × 3/2 = 14 T_11 = a + 10d = 1/2 + 10 × 3/2 = 31/2 p/3 = T_10 + T_11 = 14 + 31/2 = 59/2 p = 177/2 q/3 = T_10 × T_11 = 7 × 31 = 217 q = 651 q - 2p = 651 - 177 = 474
