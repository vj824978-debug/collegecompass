Two point charges 2q and q are placed at vertex A and centre of face CDEF of the cube as shown in figure. The electric flux passing through the cube is:

  1. (1) 3q/epsilon_0
  2. (2) q/epsilon_0
  3. (3) 3q/(2*epsilon_0)
  4. (4) 3q/(4*epsilon_0)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

phi = Q_in/epsilon_0 To find the electric flux through the cube, we need to determine how much of each charge is enclosed by the cube. Charge at vertex A (2q): Vertex A is at a corner of the cube. A point charge at a corner of a cube is shared by 8 cubes. Therefore, only 1/8 of the charge 2q is effectively inside this cube. Contribution from A = (1/8) * 2q = q/4 Charge at centre of face CDEF (q): A point charge at the centre of a face is shared by 2 cubes (inside and outside). Therefore, only 1/2 of the charge q is effectively inside this cube. Contribution from face centre = (1/2) * q = q/2 Total enclosed charge: Q_in = q/4 + q/2 = q/4 + 2q/4 = 3q/4 Electric flux: phi = Q_in/epsilon_0 = (3q/4)/epsilon_0 = 3q/(4*epsilon_0)
