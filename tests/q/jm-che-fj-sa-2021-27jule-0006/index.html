What is A in the following reaction? Phenyl-CH2Br reacts with (i) N-K compound (ii) OH-/H2O to give major product A

  1. (1) Phenyl-CH2NH2
  2. (2) Phenyl-CO-NH-CH2-Phenyl
  3. (3) Phthalimide derivative with NH
  4. (4) Phenyl-CH2OH

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction shows Gabriel's synthesis of primary amines. Phenyl-CH2Br undergoes nucleophilic substitution with potassium phthalimide (the N-K compound shown in step i). The phthalimide intermediate is then hydrolyzed with OH-/H2O in step ii to release the primary amine phenyl-CH2NH2. The solution diagram at the bottom confirms this mechanism, showing the bromide displacement by phthalimide anion followed by hydrolysis to give phenyl-CH2NH2 as the major product A.
