An atomic substance A of molar mass 12 g mol^(-1) has a cubic crystal structure with edge length of 300 pm. The no. of atoms present in one unit cell of A is (Nearest integer). Given the density of A is 3.0 g mL^(-1) and N_A = 6.02 x 10^23 mol^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Using the formula ρ = (z × M)/(N_A × a^3), where ρ = 3.0 g mL^(-1), M = 12 g mol^(-1), a = 300 pm = 3 × 10^(-8) cm, N_A = 6.02 × 10^23 mol^(-1). Substituting: 3 = (z × 12)/(6.02 × 10^23 × (3)^3 × 10^(-24)). Solving for z: z = (6.02 × 10^(-1) × 27)/4 = 4
