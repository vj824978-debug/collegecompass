Given below are two statements: One is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R. Assertion A: Amongst He, Ne, Ar and Kr; 1 g of activated charcoal adsorbs more of Kr. Reason R: The critical volume V_c(cm^3 mol^(-1)) and critical pressure P_c (atm) is highest for Krypton but the compressibility factor at critical point Z_c is lowest for Krypton. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below.

  1. (1) Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
  2. (2) A is true but R is false
  3. (3) A is false but R is true
  4. (4) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

MW order: Kr > Ar > Ne > He Z(at critical point) = 3/8 The adsorption of gases on activated charcoal depends on the molecular weight and van der Waals forces. Among He, Ne, Ar and Kr, Krypton has the highest molecular weight and therefore exhibits the strongest van der Waals interactions with the charcoal surface. Hence, 1 g of activated charcoal adsorbs more of Kr. So Assertion A is TRUE. For Reason R: - Critical volume V_c and critical pressure P_c are indeed properties that vary with the gas - However, the compressibility factor at critical point Z_c = P_c V_c / (R T_c) is a dimensionless quantity - For most real gases, Z_c is approximately 0.27 to 0.30 and does not vary significantly among noble gases - In fact, Z_c is relatively constant around 3/8 = 0.375 for ideal behavior, and real gases deviate from this - The statement that Z_c is lowest for Krypton is not accurate. All noble gases have similar Z_c values around 0.29-0.30 - Therefore, Reason R is FALSE. Answer: A is true but R is false.
