A Carnot engine operating between two reservoirs has efficiency 1/3. When the temperature of cold reservoir raised by x, its efficiency decreases to 1/6. The value of x, if the temperature of hot reservoir is 99°C, will be

  1. (1) 66 K
  2. (2) 33 K
  3. (3) 62 K
  4. (4) 16.5 K

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

η = 1 - T_C/T_H Initially T_C/T_H = 2/3 ...(1) Finally (T_C + x)/T_H = 5/6 ...(2) T_H = 99°C = 372 K From equation (1): T_C/372 = 2/3 T_C = 248 K From equation (2): (T_C + x)/372 = 5/6 T_C + x = (5/6) × 372 T_C + x = 310 K Therefore: x = 310 - 248 = 62 K
