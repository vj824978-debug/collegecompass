A transverse sinusoidal wave moves along a string in the positive x-direction at a speed of 10 cm/s. The wavelength of the wave is 0.5 m and its amplitude is 10 cm. At a particular time t, the snap–shot of the wave is shown in figure. The velocity of point P when its displacement is 5 cm is [Figure shows a sinusoidal wave with point P marked on the wave, moving in positive x-direction]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) √(3π)/50 j m/s
  2. (B) -√(3π)/50 j m/s
  3. (C) √(3π)/50 i m/s
  4. (D) -√(3π)/50 i m/s

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

y = 5 cm and V = +ve y = A sin(ωt ± φ) V = Aω cos(ωt ± φ) We get ωt ± φ = 30° ω = 2π/λ × v = 2π/5 v = Aω cos(ωt + φ) = (10/100) × (2π/5)(√3/2) = π√3/50 m/s
