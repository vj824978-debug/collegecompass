An infinite number of point charges, each carrying 1 µC charge, are placed along y-axis at y = 1 m, 2 m, 4 m, 8 m, ... The total force on a 1 C point charge, placed at the origin is x × 10^7 N. The value of x, to the nearest integer, is.....................

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

The force on the 1 C charge at origin due to charges at distances y = 1, 2, 4, 8, ... m is: F = (qQ)/(4πε₀r₁²) + (qQ)/(4πε₀r₂²) + (qQ)/(4πε₀r₃²) + (qQ)/(4πε₀r₄²) + ... = (qQ)/(4πε₀)[1/1² + 1/2² + 1/4² + 1/8² + ...] = (qQ)/(4πε₀)[1 + 1/4 + 1/16 + 1/64 + ...] This is a geometric series with first term a = 1 and common ratio r = 1/4. Sum = a/(1-r) = 1/(1-1/4) = 4/3. Therefore F = (qQ)/(4πε₀) × (4/3) = (1 × 1)/(4πε₀) × (4/3) = 10^-1 × 1 × 9 × 10^9 × (4/3) = 12 × 10^7 N. So x = 12.
