Let the tangent and normal at the point (3√3,1) on the ellipse x²/36 + y²/4 = 1 meet the y-axis at the points A and B respectively. Let the circle C be drawn taking AB as a diameter and the line x = 2√5 intersect C at the points P and Q. If the tangents at the points P and Q on the circle intersect at the point (α, β), then α² - β² is equal to

  1. (1) 61
  2. (2) 60
  3. (3) 304/5
  4. (4) 314/5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

From the ellipse equation x²/36 + y²/4 = 1, the tangent at (3√3,1) is: √3x/12 + y/4 = 1. This meets y-axis at A(0,4). The normal equation is: 3x - √3y = 8√3, which meets y-axis at B(0,-8). Circle C has diameter AB, so its equation is: x² + (y-4)(y+8) = 0, which gives x² + y² + 4y - 32 = 0. The line x = 2√5 intersects C at points P and Q. Substituting x = 2√5: 20 + y² + 4y - 32 = 0, so y² + 4y - 12 = 0, giving (y+6)(y-2) = 0. Thus P(2√5,-6) and Q(2√5,2). The tangents at P and Q on circle C are: T₁: √5x - 2y = 22 and T₂: √5x + 2y = 14. Solving these: α = 18/√5, β = -2. Therefore α² - β² = (18/√5)² - (-2)² = 324/5 - 4 = 324/5 - 20/5 = 304/5.
