The number of pairs of the solutions having the same value of the osmotic pressure from the following is (Assume 100% ionization)

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Osmotic pressure is given by π = iCRT, where i is the van't Hoff factor, C is molarity, R is gas constant, and T is temperature. For solutions to have the same osmotic pressure, their iC values must be equal. Analyzing each pair: (A) 0.500 M C2H5OH (aq) and 0.25 M KBr (aq) C2H5OH: i = 1 (non-electrolyte), iC = 1 × 0.500 = 0.500 KBr: i = 2 (100% ionization), iC = 2 × 0.25 = 0.500 Same osmotic pressure ✓ (B) 0.100 M K4[Fe(CN)6] (aq) and 0.100 M FeSO4(NH4)2SO4(aq) K4[Fe(CN)6]: i = 5 (produces 4 K+ and 1 [Fe(CN)6]^4-), iC = 5 × 0.100 = 0.500 FeSO4(NH4)2SO4: i = 5 (produces 1 Fe^2+, 3 SO4^2-, 2 NH4+), iC = 5 × 0.100 = 0.500 Same osmotic pressure ✓ (C) 0.05 M K4[Fe(CN)6] (aq) and 0.25 M NaCl (aq) K4[Fe(CN)6]: i = 5, iC = 5 × 0.05 = 0.250 NaCl: i = 2, iC = 2 × 0.25 = 0.500 Different osmotic pressure ✗ (D) 0.15 M NaCl (aq) and 0.1 M BaCl2 (aq) NaCl: i = 2, iC = 2 × 0.15 = 0.300 BaCl2: i = 3, iC = 3 × 0.1 = 0.300 Same osmotic pressure ✓ (E) 0.02 M KCl.MgCl2.6H2O (aq) and 0.05 M KCl(aq) KCl.MgCl2.6H2O: i = 5 (produces 1 K+, 1 Mg^2+, 3 Cl-), iC = 5 × 0.02 = 0.100 KCl: i = 2, iC = 2 × 0.05 = 0.100 Same osmotic pressure ✓ Pairs with same osmotic pressure: A, B, D, E = 4 pairs
