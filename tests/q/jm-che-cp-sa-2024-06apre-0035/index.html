During the detection of acidic radical present in a salt, a student gets a pale yellow precipitate soluble with difficulty in NH4OH solution when sodium carbonate extract was first acidified with dil. HNO3 and then AgNO3 solution was added. This indicates presence of:

  1. (1) Br−
  2. (2) CO3^(2−)
  3. (3) I−
  4. (4) Cl−

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

When AgNO3 is added to acidified sodium carbonate extract: Ag+ + I− → AgI (Yellow ppt), Ag+ + Cl− → AgCl (White ppt), Ag+ + Br− → AgBr (Pale yellow ppt). The pale yellow precipitate that is soluble with difficulty in NH4OH indicates AgBr (Silver bromide), which forms a pale yellow precipitate and is slightly soluble in NH4OH, unlike AgI which is yellow and insoluble in NH4OH.
