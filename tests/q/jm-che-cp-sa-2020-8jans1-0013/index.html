The correct order of the calculated spin-only magnetic moments of complexes (A) to (D) is: (A) Ni(CO)4 (B) [Ni(H2O)6]Cl2 (C) Na2[Ni(CN)4] (D) PdCl2(PPh3)2

  1. (1) (C) ≤ (D) < (B) < (A)
  2. (2) (A) ≈ (C) < (B) = (D)
  3. (3) (A) ≈ (C) ≈ (D) < (B)
  4. (4) (C) < (D) < (B) < (A)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

(a) Ni(CO)4 Ni = 3d^8 4s^2 [S F L] M = 0 (b) [Ni(H2O)6]Cl2 Ni^2+ = 3d^8 4s^0 Weak field ligand Electron configuration: ↑↓ ↑↓ | ↑ ↑ | ↑↓ ↑↓ ↑↓ No. of unpaired electron = 2 M = sqrt(n(n + 2)) = sqrt(2(2 + 2)) = sqrt(8) B.M (c) Na2[Ni(CN)4] Ni^2+ = 3d^8 (S F L) pairing take place (n = 0) M = 0 (d) PdCl2(PPh3)2 Pd^2+ = 4d^8 square plane M = 0
