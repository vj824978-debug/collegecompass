A 5.0 mmol dm^-3 aqueous solution of KCl has a conductance of 0.55 mS when measured in a cell of cell constant 1.3 cm^-1. The molar conductivity of this solution is _______ mSm^2 mol^-1. (Round off to the nearest integer).

Answer

Correct answer: 14

Solution

Using specific conductance κ = (l/a) × (1/R) × a × conductance, where κ = 1.3 × 0.55 mS cm^-1 = 1.3 × 0.55 × 100 mS m^-1. Then using molar conductivity λ_m = κ/M × 1000 mS m^2 mol^-1, where M is molarity in mol dm^-3. Calculate: (1.3 × 0.55 × 100)/(5 × 10^-3 × 1000) = 14.3 mS m^2 mol^-1. Answer = 14 (rounded off).
