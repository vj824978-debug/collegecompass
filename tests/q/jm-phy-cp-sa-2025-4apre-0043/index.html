From the combination of resistors with resistances values R₁ = R₂ = R₃ = 5 Ω and R₄ = 10 Ω, which of the following combination is the best circuit to get an equivalent resistance of 60 Ω?

  1. (1) Series combination of R₁, R₂ in upper branch and R₃, R₄ in lower branch
  2. (2) R₁ in series with parallel combination of R₂, R₃, and R₄
  3. (3) Series combination of R₁, R₂, R₃ in upper branch and R₄ in lower branch
  4. (4) All four resistors in parallel

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For circuit (1): R₁ and R₂ in series (upper) = 5+5 = 10 Ω. R₃ and R₄ in series (lower) = 5+10 = 15 Ω. Two branches in parallel: R_eq = (10 × 15)/(10 + 15) = 150/25 = 6 Ω. This gives 6 Ω, not 60 Ω. However, if the question intends multiple sets in series, 10 sets would give 60 Ω. The answer key indicates [1] is correct.
