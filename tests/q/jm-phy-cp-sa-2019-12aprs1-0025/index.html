The transfer characteristic curve of a transistor, having input and output resistance 100 ohm and 100 kΩ respectively is shown in the figure. The voltage and power gain, are respectively:

  1. (1) 5 × 10^4, 5 × 10^5
  2. (2) 5 × 10^4, 5 × 10^6
  3. (3) 5 × 10^4, 2.5 × 10^6
  4. (4) 2.5 × 10^4, 2.5 × 10^6

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

From the transfer characteristic curve: At I_B = 100 μA, I_C = 5 mA At I_B = 200 μA, I_C = 10 mA At I_B = 300 μA, I_C = 15 mA Current gain (β) is: B = I_C / I_B = (5 × 10^(-3)) / (100 × 10^(-9)) = 50 Voltage gain is: A_V = V_output / V_input = (I_output × R_0) / (I_input × R_in) = (I_C / I_B) × (R_0 / R_in) = β × (R_0 / R_in) A_V = (50) × [(100 × 10^3) / 100] A_V = (50) × [10^3] A_V = 5 × 10^4 Power gain is: Power gain = β^2 = (R_0 / R_in) = (50)^2 × 10^3 = 25 × 10^2 × 10^3 = 25 × 10^5 × 10^3 = 2.5 × 10^6
