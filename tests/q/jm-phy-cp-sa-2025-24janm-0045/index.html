An ideal gas goes from an initial state to final state. During the process, the pressure of gas increases linearly with temperature.

  1. (1) The work done by gas during the process is zero.
  2. (2) The heat added to gas is different from change in its internal energy.
  3. (3) The volume of the gas is increased.
  4. (4) The internal energy of the gas is increased.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given that P = kT where P/T = constant. This means volume is constant or isochoric process. Therefore W_D = 0 and Q = ΔU. Also temperature increases hence internal energy increases. The correct statements are: A (work done is zero), D (internal energy increases), and E (isochoric process).
