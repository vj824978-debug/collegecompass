A block of mass 1.9 kg is at rest at the edge of a table, of height 1m. A bullet of mass 0.1 kg collides with the block and sticks to it. If the velocity of the bullet is 20 m/s in the horizontal direction just before the collision then the kinetic energy just before the combined system strikes the floor, is [Take g = 10 m/s^2. Assume there is no rotational motion and loss of energy after the collision is negligible.]

  1. (1) 21 J
  2. (2) 23 J
  3. (3) 20 J
  4. (4) 19 J

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: m_bullet = 0.1 kg m_block = 1.9 kg v_bullet = 20 m/s (horizontal) height of table h = 1 m g = 10 m/s^2 Step 1: Apply conservation of momentum for the collision (horizontal direction) p_i = p_f 0.1 × 20 = 2v where v is the velocity of combined system after collision 2 = 2v v = 1 m/s (horizontal velocity after collision) Step 2: After collision, the combined system falls from height h = 1 m Using kinematic equation for vertical motion: v_y^2 = u_y^2 + 2gh v_y^2 = 0 + 2(10)(1) v_y^2 = 20 v_y = sqrt(20) m/s (vertical velocity just before hitting floor) Step 3: Total velocity just before hitting the floor The combined system has horizontal velocity v_x = 1 m/s and vertical velocity v_y = sqrt(20) m/s Total velocity magnitude: v_total^2 = v_x^2 + v_y^2 = 1^2 + 20 = 21 m^2/s^2 Step 4: Kinetic energy just before striking the floor KE_f = mgh + (1/2)mv^2 where m = m_bullet + m_block = 0.1 + 1.9 = 2 kg KE_f = 2(10)(1) + (1/2)(2)(21) KE_f = 20 + 21 = 41 J Alternatively, using energy conservation: Total KE just before collision = (1/2)(0.1)(20)^2 = 20 J After collision, KE of combined system = (1/2)(2)(1)^2 = 1 J Energy lost in collision = 20 - 1 = 19 J (inelastic) As the system falls, PE converts to KE: KE_f = KE_after_collision + PE lost KE_f = 1 + 2(10)(1) = 1 + 20 = 21 J
