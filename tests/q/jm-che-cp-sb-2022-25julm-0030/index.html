In the given reaction, a cyclopentane ring with substituents including H3C, a C(=O)OEt group, and a CH2-C(=O)OH3 group undergoes treatment with O Et to form product A (where Et is -C2H5). The number of chiral carbon(s) in product A is ___.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

The starting material contains a cyclopentane ring with a quaternary carbon bearing H3C and C(=O)OEt groups, plus a CH2-C(=O)OH3 substituent. Upon treatment with ethoxide (O Et), a Claisen condensation occurs between the two ester/carbonyl functionalities, leading to cyclization. The product A shows a six-membered ring fused to the cyclopentane. Analyzing the structure of product A: the two carbons that were originally part of the starting material's stereocenters and survive in the fused ring system retain their stereochemistry. The product contains 2 chiral carbons (the stereogenic centers in the fused ring system).
