Three objects A, B and C are kept in a straight line on a frictionless horizontal surface. These have masses m, 2m and m, respectively. The object A moves towards B with a speed 9 m/s and makes an elastic collision with it. Thereafter, B makes a completely inelastic collision with C. All motions occur on the same straight line. Find the final speed (in m/s) of the object C. [Diagram showing three objects labeled A (mass m), B (mass 2m), and C (mass m) in a line]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

[Diagram showing three objects A, B, C on a horizontal line. A has mass m and velocity 9 m/s, B has mass m and distance 2m marked, C has mass m] After 1st collision mvA = mv'A + 2mv'B −1 = (v'B − v'A)/(0 − vA) ⟹ v'B = 6 m/s After 2nd collision 2mv'B = (2m + m)vC ⟹ vC = (2/3)v'B ⟹ vC = 4 m/s
