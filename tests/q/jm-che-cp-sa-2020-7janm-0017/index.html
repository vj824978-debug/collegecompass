Match the following: (i) Riboflavin (ii) Thiamine (iii) Pyridoxine (iv) Ascorbic acid with (a) Beriberi (b) Scurvy (c) Calosis (d) Spasm

  1. (1) (i)-(d), (ii)-(b), (iii)-(a), (iv)-(c)
  2. (2) (i)-(a), (ii)-(d), (iii)-(c), (iv)-(b)
  3. (3) (i)-(c), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(d), (iv)-(b)
  4. (4) (i)-(c), (ii)-(d), (iii)-(a), (iv)-(b)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Theory based The correct matching of vitamins with their deficiency diseases is: (i) Riboflavin - (c) Calosis: Riboflavin (Vitamin B2) deficiency causes calosis, characterized by cracked lips and skin lesions. (ii) Thiamine - (a) Beriberi: Thiamine (Vitamin B1) deficiency causes beriberi, a disease affecting the nervous and cardiovascular systems. (iii) Pyridoxine - (d) Spasm: Pyridoxine (Vitamin B6) deficiency causes spasm and neurological symptoms. (iv) Ascorbic acid - (b) Scurvy: Ascorbic acid (Vitamin C) deficiency causes scurvy, characterized by bleeding gums and poor wound healing. Therefore, the correct option is (3): (i)-(c), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(d), (iv)-(b)
