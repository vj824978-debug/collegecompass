A perfectly reflecting mirror of mass M mounted on a spring constitutes a spring-mass system of angular frequency Ω such that 4πMΩ/h = 10^24 m^-2 with h as Planck's constant. N photons of wavelength λ = 8π × 10^-6 m strike the mirror simultaneously at normal incidence such that the mirror gets displaced by 1 μm. If the value of N is x × 10^12, then the value of x is _________. [Consider the spring as massless] [Figure shows a light beam striking a mirror mounted on a spring between two fixed walls]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 1.00

Solution

Sol. 1.00 If v is speed of mirror just after absorption of photons Mv - N(2h/λ) = 0 ⟹ v = 2Nh/Mλ If ℓ is the maximum compression in the spring (1/2)kℓ² = (1/2)mv² ⟹ v = √(k/m)ℓ ⟹ 2Nh/Mλ = Ω×10⁻⁶ ⟹ N = Ω×10⁻⁶ × (Mλ/2h) = 8π×10⁻⁶×10⁻⁶ × (Mλ/2h) = (4πMλ/h)×10⁻¹² = 10²⁴×10⁻¹² = 10¹² ⟹ x = 1
