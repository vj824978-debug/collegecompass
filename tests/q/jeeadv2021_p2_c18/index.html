Consider a helium (He) atom that absorbs a photon of wavelength 330 nm. The change in the velocity (in cm s^-1) of He atom after the photon absorption is ___. (Assume: Momentum is conserved when photon is absorbed. Use: Planck constant = 6.6 × 10^-34 J s, Avogadro number = 6 × 10^23 mol^-1, Molar mass of He = 4 g mol^-1)

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 30

Solution

Sol.: 30 λ = h / [m(ΔV)] 330×10⁻⁹ = (6.6×10⁻³⁴) / [(4×10⁻³ / 6×10²³) × ΔV] ΔV = (6.6 × 6 × 10²³ × 10⁻³⁴) / (4×10⁻³ × 330×10⁻⁹) = 0.30 m/s = 0.30 × 100 cm/s = 30 cm/s
