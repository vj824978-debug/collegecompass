Let alpha, beta (alpha ≠ beta) be the values of m, for which the equations x + y + z = 1; x + 2y + 4z = m and x + 4y + 10z = m^2 have infinitely many solutions. Then the value of sum from n=1 to 10 of (n^alpha + n^beta) is equal to:

  1. (1) 440
  2. (2) 3080
  3. (3) 3410
  4. (4) 560

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Delta = |1 1 1; 1 2 4; 1 4 10| = 1(20 - 16) - 1(10 - 4) + 1(4 - 2) = 4 - 6 + 2 = 0 For infinite solutions Delta_x = Delta_y = Delta_z = 0 m^2 - 3x + 2 = 0 m = 1, 2 alpha = 1, beta = 2 Therefore, sum from n=1 to 10 of (n^alpha + n^beta) = sum from n=1 to 10 of n^1 + sum from n=1 to 10 of n^2 = 10(11)/2 + 10(11)(21)/6 = 55 + 385 = 440
