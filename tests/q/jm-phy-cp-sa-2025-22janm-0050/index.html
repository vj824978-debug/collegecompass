A particle is projected at an angle of 30° from horizontal at a speed of 60 m/s. The height traversed by the particle in the first second is h0 and height traversed in the last second, before it reaches the maximum height, is h1. The ratio h0 : h1 is ______. [Take, g = 10 m/s^2]

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

Initial vertical component of velocity: uy = 60 sin(30°) = 60 × (1/2) = 30 m/s Height traversed in the first second (h0): S1 = uy × t - (1/2) × g × t^2 S1 = 30 × 1 - (1/2) × 10 × 1^2 = 30 - 5 = 25 m Time to reach maximum height: t_max = uy/g = 30/10 = 3 seconds Height traversed in the last second before reaching maximum height (during 3rd second, from t=2 to t=3): Using S_n = uy + (g/2)(2n - 1) where n is the nth second (but with upward as positive and g as negative in this context) Alternatively, distance in 3rd second: S3 = 30 + (-10/2) × (2 × 3 - 1) = 30 - 5 × 5 = 30 - 25 = 5 m Or using S = ut + (1/2)at^2: Displacement in first 3 seconds: s3 = 30(3) - (1/2)(10)(3)^2 = 90 - 45 = 45 m Displacement in first 2 seconds: s2 = 30(2) - (1/2)(10)(2)^2 = 60 - 20 = 40 m h1 = s3 - s2 = 45 - 40 = 5 m Ratio: h0/h1 = 25/5 = 5
