A cell of emf 90 V is connected across series combination of two resistors each of 100 Ω resistance. A voltmeter of resistance 400 Ω is used to measure the potential difference across each resistor. The reading of the voltmeter will be:

  1. (1) 45V
  2. (2) 40V
  3. (3) 80V
  4. (4) 90V

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: - EMF = 90 V - Two resistors in series, each R = 100 Ω - Voltmeter resistance R_V = 400 Ω When voltmeter is connected across one resistor (100 Ω), it forms a parallel combination with that resistor. Equivalent resistance of 100 Ω and 400 Ω in parallel: R_parallel = (100 × 400)/(100 + 400) = 40000/500 = 80 Ω Total circuit resistance: R_eq = 100 Ω (second resistor) + 80 Ω (parallel combination) = 180 Ω Current in circuit: I = V/R_eq = 90/180 = 1/2 A = 0.5 A Voltage across the parallel combination (which is the voltmeter reading): V_voltmeter = I × R_parallel = (1/2) × 80 = 40 V Alternatively: V_total across first resistor (without voltmeter) = (100/(100+100)) × 90 = 45 V But with voltmeter connected: Voltage drop across second resistor = I × 100 = 0.5 × 100 = 50 V Voltage across voltmeter = 90 - 50 = 40 V Reading of voltmeter = 90 - 50 = 40V
