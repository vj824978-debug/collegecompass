Total number of non bonded electrons present in NO2^- ion based on Lewis theory is _______.

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

To find the total number of non-bonded electrons in NO2^- ion: 1) First, calculate total valence electrons: - N: 5 valence electrons - O: 6 valence electrons each (2 oxygen atoms) = 12 - Negative charge: +1 electron - Total = 5 + 12 + 1 = 18 valence electrons 2) Draw the Lewis structure of NO2^-: - N is the central atom - Two O atoms are bonded to N - N-O bonds: We need to distribute 18 electrons - Structure has N in center with two O atoms - One N=O double bond and one N-O single bond (or resonance structures) - N has one lone pair - Each O has lone pairs 3) Count non-bonded (lone pair) electrons: - N atom: 1 lone pair = 2 electrons - First O atom: 3 lone pairs = 6 electrons - Second O atom: 3 lone pairs = 6 electrons - Total non-bonded electrons = 2 + 6 + 6 = 12 electrons
