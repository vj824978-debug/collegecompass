One mole of a monatomic ideal gas goes through a thermodynamic cycle, as shown in the volume versus temperature (V - T) diagram. The correct statement(s) is/are: [R is the gas constant] [V-T diagram showing a cycle with 4 points: - Point 1: (T₀/2, V₀) - Point 4: (T₀/2, V₀) [same as point 1] - Point 2: (2T₀, 2V₀) - Point 3: (3T₀/2, 2V₀) Process 1→2: curved line from (T₀/2, V₀) to (2T₀, 2V₀) Process 2→3: vertical line from (2T₀, 2V₀) to (3T₀/2, 2V₀) Process 3→4: line from (3T₀/2, 2V₀) to (T₀/2, V₀) Process 4→1: returns to starting point]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) Work done in this thermodynamic cycle (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1) is |W| = (1/2)RT₀
  2. (B) The ratio of heat transfer during processes 1 → 2 and 2 → 3 is |Q₁₊₂|/|Q₂₊₃| = 5/3
  3. (C) The above thermodynamic cycle exhibits only isochoric and adiabatic processes.
  4. (D) The ratio of heat transfer during processes 1 → 2 and 3 → 4 is |Q₁₊₂|/|Q₃₊₄| = 1/2

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

W_cycle = P₀V₀ = RT₀/2 |Q₁₋₂₋₃|/|Q₂₋₃| = |nC_p(T₂ - T₁)|/|nC_V(T₃ - T₂)| = |5|/|3| = 5/3 |Q₁₋₂|/|Q₂₋₃| = |nC_p(T₂ - T₁)|/|nC_V(T₃ - T₃)| = 2 [P-V diagram showing a rectangular cycle with: - Horizontal axis labeled V (showing V₀ and 2V₀) - Vertical axis labeled P (showing P₀ and 2P₀) - Four points forming a rectangle: Point 1: (V₀, 2P₀) at temperature T₀ Point 2: (2V₀, 2P₀) at temperature 2T₀ Point 3: (2V₀, P₀) at temperature T₀ Point 4: (V₀, P₀) at temperature T₀/2 - Segments labeled 1-2, 2-3, 3-4, 4-1]
