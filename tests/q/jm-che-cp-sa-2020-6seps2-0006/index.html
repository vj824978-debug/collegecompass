The IUPAC name of the following compound is: [Benzene ring with CHO at position 1, O2N at position 2, OH at position 4, NH2 at position 5]

  1. (1) 3-amino-4-hydroxymethyl-5-nitrobenzaldehyde
  2. (2) 2-nitro-4-hydroxymethyl-5-aminobenzaldehyde
  3. (3) 4-amino-2-formyl-5-hydroxymethylnitrobenzene
  4. (4) 5-amino-4-hydroxymethyl-2-nitrobenzaldehyde

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

To name this compound, we need to identify the principal functional group and number the benzene ring accordingly. Functional groups present: - Aldehyde (CHO) - principal group - Amino group (NH2) - Hydroxyl group (OH) - Nitro group (NO2) Step 1: The aldehyde group is the principal functional group, so the compound is named as a benzaldehyde. Step 2: Number the benzene ring starting from the carbon bearing the CHO group as position 1. From the structure: - Position 1: CHO (aldehyde) - Position 2: NO2 (nitro group) - Position 4: OH (hydroxyl group on a methylene - this is hydroxymethyl) - Position 5: NH2 (amino group) Step 3: List substituents in alphabetical order: - Amino at position 5 - Hydroxymethyl at position 4 - Nitro at position 2 Step 4: The IUPAC name is: 5-amino-4-hydroxymethyl-2-nitrobenzaldehyde
