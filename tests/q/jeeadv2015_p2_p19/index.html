Consider two different metallic strips (1 and 2) of the same material. Their lengths are the same, widths are w₁ and w₂ and thicknesses are d₁ and d₂, respectively. Two points K and M are symmetrically located on the opposite faces parallel to the x-y plane (see figure). V₁ and V₂ are the potential differences between K and M in strips 1 and 2, respectively. Then, for a given current I flowing through them in a given magnetic field strength B, the correct statement(s) is(are) [Figure showing two metallic strips with points K and M marked on opposite faces]

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) If w₁ = w₂ and d₁ = 2d₂, then V₂ = 2V₁
  2. (B) If w₁ = w₂ and d₁ = 2d₂, then V₂ = V₁
  3. (C) If w₁ = 2w₂ and d₁ = d₂, then V₂ = 2V₁
  4. (D) If w₁ = 2w₂ and d₁ = d₂, then V₂ = V₁

Answer

Correct answer: AD

Solution

I₁ = I₂ ⇒ neA₁v₁ = neA₂v₂ ⇒ d₁w₁v₁ = d₂w₂v₂ Now, potential difference developed across MK V = Bvw ⇒ V₁/V₂ = (v₁w₁)/(v₂w₂) = d₂/d₁ & hence correct choice is A & D
