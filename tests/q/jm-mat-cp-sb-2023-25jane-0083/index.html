Points P(-3, 2), Q(9, 10) and R(α, 4) lie on a circle C with PR as its diameter. The tangents to C at the points Q and R intersect at the point S. If S lies on the line 2x - ky = 1, then K is equal to

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Since PR is the diameter of circle C, the centre O is the midpoint of PR. O = ((-3 + α)/2, (2 + 4)/2) = ((α - 3)/2, 3) Since Q(9, 10) lies on the circle with diameter PR, we have PQ ⊥ QR (angle in a semicircle). Slope of PQ = (10 - 2)/(9 - (-3)) = 8/12 = 2/3 Slope of QR = (4 - 10)/(α - 9) = -6/(α - 9) Since PQ ⊥ QR: (2/3) × (-6)/(α - 9) = -1 -4/(α - 9) = -1 α - 9 = 4 α = 13 So O = (5, 3) and R = (13, 4) Slope of OQ = (10 - 3)/(9 - 5) = 7/4 Since tangent at Q is perpendicular to OQ: m_OQ = 7/4, so m_tangent at Q = -4/7 Tangent at Q: y - 10 = (-4/7)(x - 9) 7y - 70 = -4x + 36 4x + 7y = 106 ... (i) Slope of OR = (4 - 3)/(13 - 5) = 1/8 Since tangent at R is perpendicular to OR: m_OR = 1/8, so m_tangent at R = -8 Tangent at R: y - 4 = -8(x - 13) y - 4 = -8x + 104 8x + y = 108 ... (ii) Finding intersection point S of tangents (i) and (ii): From (ii): y = 108 - 8x Substituting in (i): 4x + 7(108 - 8x) = 106 4x + 756 - 56x = 106 -52x = -650 x = 25/2 y = 108 - 8(25/2) = 108 - 100 = 8 So S = (25/2, 8) S lies on 2x - ky = 1: 2(25/2) - k(8) = 1 25 - 8k = 1 8k = 24 K = 3
