Consider the following sequence of reactions: Aniline + NaNO2 at 0.5°C → A' + N,N-Dimethylaniline → B'. The product B' is

  1. (1) Phenyl group bonded to N-N with methyl groups on the other nitrogen attached to aniline derivative
  2. (2) Phenyl group bonded to N-N with H atoms on the other nitrogen attached to dimethylamino-substituted benzene
  3. (3) Phenyl group double bonded to N, N-N bonded to dimethylamino-substituted benzene
  4. (4) H2N-phenyl-phenyl-N(CH3)2 structure

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Aniline reacts with NaNO2 at 0-5°C to form benzenediazonium ion (A'). This diazonium ion then undergoes coupling with N,N-dimethylaniline in an azo coupling reaction. The electrophilic aromatic substitution occurs at the para position of N,N-dimethylaniline, forming an azo dye (azobenzene derivative) with N=N linkage between two aromatic rings, where one ring has the dimethylamino group and the other is from the original aniline.
