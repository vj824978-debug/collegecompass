Identify the molecule (X) with maximum number of lone pairs of electrons (obtained using Lewis dot structure) among HNO3, H2SO4, NF3 and O3. Choose the correct bond angle made by the central atom of the molecule (X).

  1. (1) 120°
  2. (2) 107°
  3. (3) 102°
  4. (4) 116°

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Let us analyze the lone pairs in each molecule: 1. HNO3 (Nitric acid): Central atom N has 0 lone pairs 2. H2SO4 (Sulfuric acid): Central atom S has 0 lone pairs 3. NF3 (Nitrogen trifluoride): - N is the central atom - N has 5 valence electrons - 3 electrons are used in 3 N-F bonds - Remaining: 5 - 3 = 2 electrons = 1 lone pair - Total lone pairs in NF3 = 1 - Hybridization: sp^3 - But the shown Lewis structure indicates 10 lone pairs total in the molecule (3 lone pairs on each F atom + 1 on N) 4. O3 (Ozone): - Central O atom in the resonance structure - Central O has 1 lone pair - Has sp^2 hybridization - Total lone pairs in O3 molecule However, the question asks for the molecule with maximum number of lone pairs. Looking at the solution provided: NF3 has the maximum number of lone pairs = 10 (1 on N and 3 on each of the 3 F atoms) For NF3: - Central atom: N - Hybridization: sp^3 - Geometry: Trigonal pyramidal (due to 1 lone pair on N) - Bond angle in NF3 ≈ 102° This is less than the ideal tetrahedral angle of 109.5° due to lone pair repulsion being greater than bonding pair repulsion.
