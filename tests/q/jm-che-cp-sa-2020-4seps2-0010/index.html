The major product [R] in the following sequence of reactions is: HC ≡ CH (i) LiNH2/ether → [P] (ii) H3C-CH-Br with (CH3)2CH attached (i) HgSO4/H2SO4 (ii) NaBH4 → [Q] Conc. H2SO4, Δ → [R]

  1. (1) H3C-C-CH2-CH3 with CH(CH3)2 attached
  2. (2) H3C-C≡CH-CH3 with (CH3)2CH attached
  3. (3) H3C-C≡C(CH3)2 with H3CCH2 attached
  4. (4) H3C-C=CH-CH2 with (CH3)2CH attached

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

(i) HgSO4/dil.H2SO4 (ii) NaBH4 is convert triple bond into ketone and formed ketone is reduced by NaBH4 and covert into Alcohol. Step 1: HC ≡ CH reacts with LiNH2/ether and H3C-CH-Br with (CH3)2CH attached This gives: CH3-CH=C=CH with isopropyl group attached at first carbon - [P] Step 2: [P] reacts with (i) HgSO4/H2SO4 (ii) NaBH4 The triple bond is converted to a ketone, which is then reduced by NaBH4 to form an alcohol: CH3-CH-OH with isopropyl groups attached - [Q] Step 3: [Q] reacts with Conc. H2SO4 and heat (Δ) Dehydration occurs, removing water and forming a double bond: The major product is H3C-C≡C(CH3)2 with H3CCH2 attached This corresponds to option [3]
