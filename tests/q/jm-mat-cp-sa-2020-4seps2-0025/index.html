A test consists of 6 multiple choice questions, each having 4 alternative answers of which only one is correct. The number of ways, in which a candidate answers all six questions such that exactly four of the answers are correct, is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 135

Solution

Ways = ^6C_4 . 1^4 . 3^2 = 15 × 9 = 135 Explanation: - We need to select exactly 4 questions out of 6 to answer correctly: ^6C_4 ways - For the 4 correct answers, there is only 1 way to answer each (the correct option): 1^4 - For the 2 incorrect answers, there are 3 wrong options to choose from for each: 3^2 - Total ways = ^6C_4 × 1^4 × 3^2 = 15 × 1 × 9 = 135
