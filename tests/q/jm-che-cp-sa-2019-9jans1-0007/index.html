Correct statements among a to d regarding silicones are -

  1. (1) (a), (b) and (d) only
  2. (2) (a), (b) and (c) only
  3. (3) (a), (b), (c) and (d)
  4. (4) (a) and (b) only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

These are the properties and uses of silicones. (a) They are polymers with hydrophobic character. - CORRECT. Silicones are polymers with Si-O backbone and organic groups (usually methyl) attached to silicon. The organic groups impart hydrophobic character. (b) They are biocompatible. - CORRECT. Silicones are biocompatible and are used in medical applications like implants, contact lenses, etc. (c) In general, they have high thermal stability and low dielectric strength. - INCORRECT. While silicones do have high thermal stability, they actually have HIGH dielectric strength (not low). This makes them useful as insulators. (d) Usually, they are resistant to oxidation and used as greases. - CORRECT. Silicones are resistant to oxidation due to the strong Si-O bonds and are used as lubricants and greases. Therefore, statements (a), (b), and (d) are correct. Statement (c) is incorrect because silicones have high (not low) dielectric strength.
