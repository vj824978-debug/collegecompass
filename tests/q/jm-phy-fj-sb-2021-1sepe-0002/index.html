A steel rod with y = 2.0 × 10^11 N m^-2 and α = 10^-5 °C^-1 of length 4 m and area of cross-section 10 cm^2 is heated from 0°C to 400°C without being allowed to extend. The tension produced in the rod is x × 10^5 N where the value of x is ___

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

Force = Ayα.ΔT, Force = (10 × 10^-4) × (2 × 10^11) × 10^-5 × 400, F = 8 × 10^5 N, x × 10^5 = 8 × 10^5, x = 8
