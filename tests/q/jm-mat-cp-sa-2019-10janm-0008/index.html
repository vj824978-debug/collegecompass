The mean of five observations is 5 and their variance is 9.20. If three of the given five observations are 1, 3 and 8, then a ratio of other two observations is -

  1. (1) 6 : 7
  2. (2) 10 : 3
  3. (3) 4 : 9
  4. (4) 5 : 8

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

μ = 5; variance = 9.20 μ = (1 + 3 + 8 + x₁ + x₂)/5 = 5 ⟹ x₁ + x₂ = 13 9.20 = (1/n)Σx_i² - μ² ⟹ Σx_i² = (9.20 + 25)5 1 + 9 + 64 + x₁² + x₂² = 171 x₁² + x₂² = 97 ⟹ x₁² + (13 - x₁)² = 97 (x₁ - 9)(x₁ - 4) = 0 x₁ = 9 ; x₂ = 4 x₁ = 4 ; x₂ = 9 x₁ : x₂ = 4 : 9 or 9 : 4
