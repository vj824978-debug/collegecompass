Two electric dipoles of dipole moments 1.2 × 10^(-30) C·m and 2.4 × 10^(-30) C·m are placed in two different uniform electric fields of strength 5 × 10^3 NC^(-1) and 15 × 10^4 NC^(-1) respectively. The ratio of maximum torque experienced by the electric dipoles will be 1/x. The value of x is ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

Maximum torque on a dipole: τ = μB sin(90°) = μB For dipole 1: τ₁ = μ₁B₁ = (1.2 × 10^(-30)) × (5 × 10^3) For dipole 2: τ₂ = μ₂B₂ = (2.4 × 10^(-30)) × (15 × 10^4) Ratio: τ₁/τ₂ = (1.2 × 10^(-30) × 5 × 10^3)/(2.4 × 10^(-30) × 15 × 10^4) = (1.2 × 5)/(2.4 × 15) = 6/36 = 1/6 Therefore x = 6
