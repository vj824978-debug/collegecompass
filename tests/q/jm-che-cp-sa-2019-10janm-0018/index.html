The major product of the following reaction is: Starting material is a cyclohexane ring with Ph (phenyl) group and two Br (bromine) atoms attached. Treating with KOH alc (excess) and heat (delta).

  1. (1) Ph with a propenyl chain showing double bonds: Ph-CH(CH3)-CH=CH-CH=CH2
  2. (2) Ph with a longer conjugated diene: Ph-CH(CH3)-CH=CH-CH=CH-CH3
  3. (3) Ph with a shorter chain: Ph-CH(CH3)-CH=CH-CH2-CH3
  4. (4) Ph attached to benzene ring with a side chain: Ph-CH(CH3) attached to a benzene ring

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

This is an example of dehydrohalogenation by E2 mechanism. The starting material is a dibrominated cyclohexane with a phenyl substituent. When treated with excess alcoholic KOH under heat, E2 elimination occurs. The reaction proceeds by removal of two HBr molecules (dehydrohalogenation) from the substrate. The E2 mechanism follows Zaitsev's rule, where the more stable alkene forms preferentially. The two eliminations lead to the formation of a conjugated diene system. The major product is the one with the extended conjugation, which is option (1): Ph-CH(CH3)-CH=CH-CH=CH2. This product has the maximum number of double bonds in conjugation with the phenyl ring, making it the most stable and thus the major product.
