A tank contains two immiscible liquids of densities 6ρ and 2ρ. The higher density liquid is filled up to a height L/2 from the bottom. A thin rod of density ρ and length L is fully immersed and hinged at the bottom so that it can oscillate freely, as shown in the figure. If the rod is slightly disturbed from its equilibrium, the time period of small oscillations is (2π/n)√(L/g), where g is the acceleration due to gravity. The value of n is:

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 1.73

Solution

Solution: Let A be the cross-sectional area of the rod. Mass of the rod, M = ρAL. Weight of the rod, W = ρALg, acting at a distance L/2 from the hinge. When the rod is displaced by a small angle θ, the restoring torque is provided by the buoyant forces from the two liquids. Buoyant force due to the lower liquid, F_B1 = (6ρ)A(L/2)g = 3ρALg. This force acts at the center of the immersed part in the lower liquid, which is at a distance L/4 from the hinge. Torque due to F_B1 is τ_B1 = F_B1(L/4)θ = (3ρALg)(L/4)θ = (3/4)ρAL²gθ. Buoyant force due to the upper liquid, F_B2 = (2ρ)A(L/2)g = ρALg. This force acts at the center of the immersed part in the upper liquid, which is at a distance L/2 + L/4 = 3L/4 from the hinge. Torque due to F_B2 is τ_B2 = F_B2(3L/4)θ = (ρALg)(3L/4)θ = (3/4)ρAL²gθ. Destabilizing torque due to gravity is τ_g = W(L/2)θ = (ρALg)(L/2)θ = (1/2)ρAL²gθ. Net restoring torque, τ_net = τ_B1 + τ_B2 - τ_g = (3/4 + 3/4 - 1/2)ρAL²gθ = ρAL²gθ. Moment of inertia of the rod about the hinge, I = ML²/3 = ρAL³/3. The equation of motion is Iα = -τ_net (ρAL³/3)α = -ρAL²gθ α = -(3g/L)θ This represents simple harmonic motion with ω² = 3g/L. Time period, T = 2π/ω = 2π/√3 √(L/g) Answer: √3 = 1.73
