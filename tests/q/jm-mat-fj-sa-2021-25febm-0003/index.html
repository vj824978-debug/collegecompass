The value of integral from -1 to 1 of x^2 * e^(x^2) * [x] dx, where [x] denotes the greatest integer ≤ x, is:

  1. (1) (e-1)/(3e)
  2. (2) (e+1)/(3e)
  3. (3) 1/(3e)
  4. (4) (e+1)/3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Split the integral at x=0 since [x] = -1 for -1≤x<0 and [x]=0 for 0≤x≤1. For -1≤x<0: [x]=-1, so integral becomes -1 times integral of x^2*e^(x^2)dx from -1 to 0. For 0≤x≤1: [x]=0, so integral is 0. Using integration by parts on the first part with u=x, dv=x*e^(x^2)dx, we get [-1/e * (x^3/3)] evaluated from -1 to 0, minus the integral of e^(x^2)/3 dx from -1 to 0. After evaluation: -1/e * (0 - (-1/3)) - contribution from second integral = -1/(3e) - (e-1)/(3e) = (e+1)/(3e)
