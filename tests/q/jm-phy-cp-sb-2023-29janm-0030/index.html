A body cools from 60°C to 40°C in 6 minutes. If, temperature of surroundings is 10°C. Then, after the next 6 minutes, its temperature will be ______°C.

Answer

Correct answer: 28

Solution

Using Newton's Law of Cooling: ΔT/Δt = -k(T_av - T_0) Case 1: For the first 6 minutes, the body cools from 60°C to 40°C with surrounding temperature 10°C. -20/6 = -k(50 - 10) where T_av = (60 + 40)/2 = 50°C (average temperature) -20/6 = -k(40) 10/3 = 40k k = 1/12 Case 2: For the next 6 minutes, the body cools from 40°C to T (unknown) with surrounding temperature 10°C. Let the average temperature be (40 + T)/2 (40 - T)/6 = (1/12)((40 + T)/2 - 10) (40 - T)/6 = (1/12)((40 + T - 20)/2) (40 - T)/6 = (1/12)((20 + T)/2) 80 - 2T = (20 + T)/2 160 - 4T = 20 + T 160 - 20 = 4T + T 140 = 5T T = 140/5 = 28°C
