The major product of the following reaction is –

  1. (1) Structure showing isopropyl group attached to benzene fused with 5-membered ring containing ketone with methyl substituent
  2. (2) Structure showing isopropyl group attached to benzene fused with 5-membered ring containing ketone
  3. (3) Structure showing isopropyl group attached to benzene fused with 5-membered ring containing ketone with methyl substituent in different position
  4. (4) Structure showing isopropyl group attached to benzene fused with 6-membered ring containing ketone

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Starting material: 3-isopropylphenyl group connected to a saturated 6-membered ring with Cl at one position and C=O at another position Step 1: Treatment with tBuOk (potassium tert-butoxide) - a strong base This acts as a base in E2 elimination mechanism (Stany base) The Cl is eliminated along with a nearby proton to form a C=C double bond This creates an α,β-unsaturated carbonyl system Step 2: Treatment with conc. H2SO4/Δ (concentrated sulfuric acid with heat) This provides acidic conditions at elevated temperature The conjugated diene system formed undergoes intramolecular Friedel-Crafts type cyclization The benzene ring attacks the electrophilic carbon of the α,β-unsaturated ketone This forms a new C-C bond between the aromatic ring and the side chain Mechanism Detail: - Protonation of carbonyl oxygen to activate the carbonyl - The π-electrons of the benzene ring attack the β-carbon of the α,β-unsaturated ketone - Formation of an intermediate carbocation stabilized by resonance with the aromatic system - Deprotonation leads to formation of a fused bicyclic system Product: A tricyclic compound with the isopropyl group on the benzene ring and a new 5-membered ring fused to the benzene containing a ketone The tautomerization shown at the bottom indicates the equilibrium between the ketone form and enol form, with the ketone being the major tautomer. Answer: Option [1] - the fused bicyclic system with the 5-membered ketone ring
