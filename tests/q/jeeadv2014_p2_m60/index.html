Let z_k = cos(2πk/10) + i sin(2πk/10); k = 1, 2, ..., 9. [TABLE] List - I | List - II (P) For each z_k there exists a z_j such that z_k · z_j = 1 | (1) True (Q) There exists a k ∈ {1, 2, ..., 9} such that z_1 · z = z_k has no solution z in the set of complex numbers | (2) False (R) |1 - z_1||1 - z_2|...|1 - z_9| / 10 equals | (3) 1 (S) 1 - Σ_{k=1}^9 cos(2πk/10) equals | (4) 2 [END TABLE] Codes: P | Q | R | S (A) 1 | 2 | 4 | 3 (B) 2 | 1 | 3 | 4 (C) 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 (D) 2 | 1 | 4 | 3

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) (A) P-1, Q-2, R-4, S-3
  2. (B) (B) P-2, Q-1, R-3, S-4
  3. (C) (C) P-1, Q-2, R-3, S-4
  4. (D) (D) P-2, Q-1, R-4, S-3

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

(P) z_k is 10^th root of unity ⇒ z̄_k will also be 10^th root of unity. Take z_j as z̄_k. (Q) z_1 ≠ 0 take z = z_k/z_1, we can always find z. (R) z^10 - 1 = (z - 1)(z - z_1)...(z - z_9) ⇒ (z - z_1)(z - z_2)...(z - z_9) = 1 + z + z^2 + ... + z^9 ∀ z ∈ complex number. Put z = 1 (1 - z_1)(1 - z_2)...(1 - z_9) = 10. (S) 1 + z_1 + z_2 + ... + z_9 = 0 ⇒ Re(1) + Re(z_1) + ... + Re(z_9) = 0 ⇒ Re(z_1) + Re(z_2) + ... + Re(z_9) = -1. ⇒ 1 - ∑(k=1 to 9) cos(2kπ/10) = 2.
