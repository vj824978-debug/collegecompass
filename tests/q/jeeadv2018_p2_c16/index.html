The desired product X can be prepared by reacting the major product of the reactions in LIST-I with one or more appropriate reagents in LIST-II. (Given, order of migratory aptitude: aryl > alkyl > hydrogen) [FIGURE: Structure of X showing a carboxylic acid with two phenyl groups and one methyl group on the carbon adjacent to COOH] LIST-I contains four reactions (P, Q, R, S) with starting materials and reagents: P. Starting material with two OH groups, two Ph groups, and Me group + H2SO4 Q. Starting material with NH2 group, two Ph groups, H group, and OH group on different carbons + HNO2 R. Starting material with OH group, Me group, Ph group, and another Ph group on different carbons + H2SO4 S. Starting material with Br group, two Ph groups, H group, and OH group on different carbons + AgNO3 LIST-II contains five reagents: 1. I2, NaOH 2. [Ag(NH3)2]OH 3. Fehling solution 4. HCHO, NaOH 5. NaOBr The correct option is

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) (A) P → 1; Q → 2,3; R → 1,4; S → 2,4
  2. (B) (B) P → 1,5; Q → 3,4; R → 4,5; S → 3
  3. (C) (C) P → 1,5; Q → 3,4; R → 5; S → 2,4
  4. (D) (D) P → 1,5; Q → 2,3; R → 1,5; S → 2,3

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Sol. D (P): [Structure with two phenyl groups, one hydroxyl group, and two methyl groups] → (i) H₂SO₄ → [Structure with ketone carbonyl replacing one hydroxyl] → branches into two pathways: (1) I₂/NaOH → [carboxylic acid product] + CHI₃ (2) NaOBr → [carboxylic acid product] + CHBr₃ (Q): [Structure with amino group, two phenyl groups, methyl and hydroxyl groups] → (i) HNO₂, -N₂ → [ketone product] → branches into: Tollens' Reagent → [carboxylic acid] and Fehling's solution → [carboxylic acid] (R): [Structure with hydroxyl groups, methyl and phenyl groups] → H₂SO₄ → [ketone product] → branches into: (1) NaOBr → [product] + CHBr₃ (2) NaOH, I₂ → [carboxylic acid] + CHI₃ (S): [Structure with bromine, two phenyl groups, methyl and hydroxyl] → AgNO₃, Agitation → [ketone product] → branches into: (1) Fehling's solution → [carboxylic acid] (2) Tollens' reagent → [carboxylic acid]
