When a part of a straight capillary tube is placed vertically in a liquid, the liquid raises upto certain height h. If the inner radius of the capillary tube, density of the liquid and surface tension of the liquid decrease by 1% each, then the height of the liquid in the tube will change by ____ %.

  1. (1) -1
  2. (2) +3
  3. (3) -3
  4. (4) +1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

h = (2T*cos(theta))/(rho*g*r) For fractional change: Delta(h)/h % = Delta(T)/T % - Delta(rho)/rho % - Delta(r)/r % Given: Delta(T)/T = -1%, Delta(rho)/rho = -1%, Delta(r)/r = -1% Delta(h)/h % = (-1)% - (-1)% - (-1)% Delta(h)/h % = -1 + 1 + 1 Delta(h)/h % = +1%
