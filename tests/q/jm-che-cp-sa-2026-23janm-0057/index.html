Consider the following sequence of reactions. 4-Nitrotoluene with (i)Sn, HCl/A (ii)pH neutralisation gives (A). (A) with (i)(CH3CO)2O (ii)Br2/AcOH gives (B). Assuming that the reaction proceeds to completion, then 137 mg of 4-nitrotoluene will produce ___ mg of B. (Given molar mass in g mol^(-1) H:1;C:12, N:14, O:16, Br:80)

  1. (1) 301
  2. (2) 146
  3. (3) 228
  4. (4) 208

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Step 1: Identify the structure and reactions 4-Nitrotoluene undergoes: - Reduction of NO2 to NH2 using Sn/HCl followed by pH neutralisation to give intermediate (A): 4-methylaniline (CH3-C6H4-NH2) - Acetylation using (CH3CO)2O to form N-acetyl derivative - Bromination using Br2/AcOH to give final product (B) Step 2: Determine structure of B Product B is 4-bromo-N-(4-methylphenyl)acetamide with molecular formula C9H10BrNO Step 3: Calculate molar mass of 4-nitrotoluene Molecular formula: C7H7NO2 Molar mass = 7(12) + 7(1) + 14 + 2(16) = 84 + 7 + 14 + 32 = 137 g/mol Step 4: Calculate moles of 4-nitrotoluene Moles = (137 × 10^(-3))/137 = 0.001 mol Step 5: Determine molar mass of product B Molecular formula: C9H10BrNO Molar mass = 9(12) + 10(1) + 80 + 14 + 16 = 108 + 10 + 80 + 14 + 16 = 228 g/mol Step 6: Calculate mass of product B From stoichiometry: 1 mol of 4-nitrotoluene → 1 mol of B Moles of product B = 0.001 mol Mass of B = 0.001 × 228 = 0.228 g = 228 mg
