Identify the product formed (A and E). Starting material: Me group at position 1, NO2 group at position 4 on benzene ring. Reaction sequence: Br2 → A → Sn/HCl → B → NaNO2/HCl (273-278K) → C → H3PO2/H2O → D → (i) KMnO4/KOH (ii) H3O+ → E

  1. (1) A = Br-substituted product with Me and NO2 groups; E = Me-substituted benzene with Br
  2. (2) A = Br-substituted product with Me and NMe groups; E = COOH with Br in ortho position
  3. (3) A = Dibr-substituted product with Me and NO2; E = COOH with Br in meta position
  4. (4) A = Br-substituted product with Me, NO2 and OH; E = COOH with Br and OH groups

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Starting material: 4-nitrotoluene (Me at position 1, NO2 at position 4) Step A (Br2): Bromination occurs at ortho position (position 2) to the electron-donating Me group. The NO2 is electron-withdrawing and deactivating, but the Me is ortho/para directing. A = 2-bromo-4-nitrotoluene (with Br ortho to Me, NO2 para to Me) Step B (Sn/HCl): Reduction of NO2 group to NH2 B = 2-bromo-4-aminotoluene Step C (NaNO2/HCl, 273-278K): Diazotization of primary amine to form diazonium salt C = 2-bromo-4-methylbenzenediazonium chloride (diazonium intermediate) Step D (H3PO2/H2O): Replacement of diazonium group with H (Sandmeyer-type reaction with hypophosphorous acid) D = 2-bromostoluene (o-bromotoluene with Me group) Step E (i) KMnO4/KOH (ii) H3O+): Oxidation of Me group to COOH using permanganate in basic conditions followed by acidification E = 2-bromobenzoic acid (COOH at position 1 with Br at position 2, ortho to COOH) Therefore: A = 2-bromo-4-nitrotoluene; E = 2-bromobenzoic acid (option 2)
