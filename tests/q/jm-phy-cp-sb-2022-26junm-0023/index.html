The elastic behaviour of material for linear stress and linear strain, is shown in the figure. The energy density for a linear strain of 5 × 10^-4 is ____ kJ/m³. Assume that material is elastic upto the linear strain of 5 × 10^-4.

Answer

Correct answer: 25

Solution

Using the formula U_d = (1/2) × Y × (strain)^2, where Y is Young's modulus. From the graph, stress/strain = 20/(10^-10) = 2 × 10^11 N/m². Therefore, U_d = (1/2) × (2 × 10^11) × (5 × 10^-4)^2 = 10^11 × 25 × 10^-8 = 25 × 10^3 J/m³ = 25 kJ/m³
