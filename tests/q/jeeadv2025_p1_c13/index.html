The reaction sequence given below is carried out with 16 moles of X. The yield of the major product in each step is given below the product in parentheses. The amount (in grams) of S produced is_____. [Reaction sequence diagram showing: X (benzene ring with Br and dioxolane substituents) → (i) Na, dry ether; (ii) H₃O⁺ → P (molar mass 210, 100% yield) → (i) NaOH, Δ; (ii) H₃O⁺ (50% yield) → Q → (i) NaOH, CaO; (ii) Δ (50% yield) → R → PBr₃, (C₂H₅)₂O (50% yield) → T → NaH, (C₂H₅)₂O → S (50% yield)] Use: Atomic mass (in amu): H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Br = 80

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 175

Solution

Sol. [Chemical structure X: Benzene ring with Br and a dioxolane ring containing O-CH₂-CH₂-O substituents] 16 moles i) Na / Ether ii) H₂O⁺ → [Chemical structure P: Two connected benzene rings, each with CHO group] (100%) 8 moles i) NaOH / Δ ii) H₂O ↓ [Chemical structure Q: Two connected benzene rings with CH₂OH and COOH groups] (50%) 4 moles i) NaOH / CaO ii) Δ ↓ [Chemical structure R: Two connected benzene rings with CH₂OH group] (50%) (2 moles) PBr₃ (C₂H₅)₂O ↓ [Chemical structure T: Two connected benzene rings with CH₂Br group] (50%) 1 mole NaH / (C₂H₅)₂O → [Chemical structure: Two connected benzene rings with CH₂OH group] → [Chemical structure S: Two benzene rings connected by CH₂-O-CH₂ linkage, repeated pattern] (50%) 0.5 mole mass of 'S' produced = 350 × 1/2 = 175 g
