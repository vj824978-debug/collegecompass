The correct option(s) related to the extraction of iron from its ore in the blast furnace operating in the temperature range 900 – 1500 K is(are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) Limestone is used to remove silicate impurity.
  2. (B) Pig iron obtained from blast furnace contains about 4% carbon.
  3. (C) Coke (C) converts CO₂ to CO.
  4. (D) Exhaust gases consist of NO₂ and CO.

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution

(i) CaCO₃ —Δ→ CaO + CO₂ ↑ SiO₂ + CaO —→ CaSiO₃ ↓ Gangue Flux Slag (ii) Pig iron contains 4% of carbon. (iii) C(s) + CO₂(g) —→ 2CO(g)
