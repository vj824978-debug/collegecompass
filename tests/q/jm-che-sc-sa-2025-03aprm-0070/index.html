Correct order of limiting molar conductivity for cations in water at 298 K is:

  1. (1) H+ > Ca2+ > Mg2+ > K+ > Na+
  2. (2) H+ > Na+ > K+ > Ca2+ > Mg2+
  3. (3) Mg2+ > H+ > Ca2+ > K+ > Na+
  4. (4) H+ > Na+ > Ca2+ > Mg2+ > K+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Limiting molar conductivity depends on ion mobility in water. H+ has the highest conductivity due to its small size and special mechanism (Grotthuss mechanism). Among alkali metals, conductivity follows the order of decreasing ionic radius and hydration: Na+ > K+ (K+ is larger). For divalent cations, smaller size leads to higher hydration and lower mobility. Order: Mg2+ is highly hydrated (small), Ca2+ less hydrated. Thus: H+ > Na+ > K+ > Ca2+ > Mg2+
