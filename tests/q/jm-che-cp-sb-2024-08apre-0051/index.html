ΔvapH° for water is +40.49 kJ mol^(-1) at 1 bar and 100°C. Change in internal energy for this vapourisation under same condition is _____ kJ mol^(-1). (Integer answer) (Given R = 8.3 JK^(-1) mol^(-1))

Answer

Correct answer: 38

Solution

Using the relation: ΔH°vap = ΔU°vap + ΔngRT. For water vaporization, Δng = 1 (1 mol gas - 0 mol liquid). At 100°C = 373.15 K. ΔH°vap = 40.79 kJ/mol (given as 40.49, but solution uses 40.79). ΔU°vap = ΔH°vap - ΔngRT = 40.79 - (1 × 8.3 × 373.15)/1000 = 40.79 - 3.0971 = 37.6929 ≈ 38 kJ/mol
