A particle of mass m is projected with a speed u from the ground at an angle theta = pi/3 w.r.t. horizontal (x-axis). When it has reached its maximum height, it collides completely inelastically with another particle of the same mass and velocity u i. The horizontal distance covered by the combined mass before reaching the ground is -

  1. (1) (3*sqrt(3)*u^2)/(8*g)
  2. (2) (2*sqrt(2)*u^2)/g
  3. (3) (5*u^2)/(8*g)
  4. (4) (3*sqrt(2)*u^2)/(4*g)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

mu + mu*cos(theta) = 2mV V = (u + u*cos(theta))/2 = (u + u*(1/2))/2 = (3u)/4 H_max = (u^2*sin^2(60°))/(2g) = (u^2*(3/4))/(2g) = (3u^2)/(8g) Time taken = sqrt(2*H_max/g) = sqrt(2/g * (3u^2)/(8g)) = sqrt(3)*u/(2*g) Horizontal distance travelled = (3u)/4 * (sqrt(3)*u)/(2*g) = (3*sqrt(3)*u^2)/(8*g)
