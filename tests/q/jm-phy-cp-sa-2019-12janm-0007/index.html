Determine the electric dipole moment of the system of the three charges, placed on the vertices of an equilateral triangle, as shown in the figure: An equilateral triangle with side length ℓ has charges: +q at bottom left vertex, +q at bottom right vertex, and -2q at top vertex.

  1. (1) 2q ℓ j
  2. (2) (q ℓ)(i + j)/sqrt(2)
  3. (3) sqrt(3) q ℓ (i - j)/sqrt(2)
  4. (4) -sqrt(3) q ℓ j

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

To find the electric dipole moment of the system, we need to calculate the vector sum of individual dipole moments. Let's set up coordinates with the origin at the center of the triangle: - Bottom left charge +q at position: (-ℓ/2, -ℓ/(2*sqrt(3))) - Bottom right charge +q at position: (ℓ/2, -ℓ/(2*sqrt(3))) - Top charge -2q at position: (0, ℓ/sqrt(3)) The dipole moment is given by: P = Σ q_i * r_i For the bottom left charge (+q): P_1 = q * (-ℓ/2, -ℓ/(2*sqrt(3))) For the bottom right charge (+q): P_2 = q * (ℓ/2, -ℓ/(2*sqrt(3))) For the top charge (-2q): P_3 = -2q * (0, ℓ/sqrt(3)) Total dipole moment: P_net = P_1 + P_2 + P_3 X-component: -q*ℓ/2 + q*ℓ/2 + 0 = 0 Y-component: -q*ℓ/(2*sqrt(3)) - q*ℓ/(2*sqrt(3)) - 2q*ℓ/sqrt(3) = -q*ℓ/sqrt(3) - 2q*ℓ/sqrt(3) = -3q*ℓ/sqrt(3) = -sqrt(3)*q*ℓ Alternatively, from the solution shown: P_net = -2p cos(30°) j = -2q*ℓ * (sqrt(3)/2) j = -sqrt(3)*q*ℓ j Therefore: P_net = -sqrt(3) q ℓ j
