RBr undergoes reaction with (i) Mg, dry ether (ii) H2O to produce 2-Methylbutane. The maximum number of RBr producing 2-methylbutane by above sequence of reactions is _____. (Consider the structural isomers only)

  1. (1) 4
  2. (2) 5
  3. (3) 3
  4. (4) 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction sequence shows: (i) RBr + Mg in dry ether → RMgBr (Grignard reagent) (ii) RMgBr + H2O → RH + MgBrOH The product obtained is 2-methylbutane, which has the structure: CH3-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH3 For 2-methylbutane to be formed from RH (after hydrolysis of Grignard reagent), the R group in RBr must be the same as the R group in the final alkane. Since the final product is 2-methylbutane, we need RBr where R = 2-methylbutyl group. The structural isomers of 2-methylbutyl bromide (brominated form of 2-methylbutane) are: 1. 1-Bromo-2-methylbutane: CH3-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH2-Br 2. 2-Bromo-2-methylbutane: CH3-C(Br)(CH3)-CH2-CH3 3. 3-Bromo-2-methylbutane: CH3-CH(CH3)-CHBr-CH3 4. 1-Bromo-3-methylbutane: (CH3)2CH-CH2-CH2-Br Among these, when converted to Grignard reagent and then hydrolyzed: - 1-Bromo-2-methylbutane → 2-methylbutane ✓ - 2-Bromo-2-methylbutane → 2-methylbutane ✓ - 3-Bromo-2-methylbutane → 2-methylbutane ✓ - 1-Bromo-3-methylbutane → 3-methylbutane ✗ The solution shows 4 possible structures for RBr that can produce 2-methylbutane. However, the marked answer is [1], indicating that only 1 RBr produces 2-methylbutane through this specific reaction sequence when considering all structural isomers.
