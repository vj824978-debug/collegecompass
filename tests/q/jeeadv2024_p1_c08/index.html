Consider the following volume – temperature (V – T) diagram for the expansion of 5 moles of an ideal monatomic gas. [V-T diagram showing: X at (335 K, 10 L), Y at (335 K, 20 L), Z at (415 K, 20 L). Path goes from X up to Y, then horizontally to Z.] Consider only P – V work is involved, the total change in enthalpy (in Joule) for the transformation of state in the sequence X → Y → Z is _________. [Use the given data: Molar heat capacity of the gas for the given temperature range, C_{V, m} = 12 J K^{-1} mol^{-1} and gas constant, R = 8.3 J K^{-1} mol^{-1}]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 8120

Solution

Since X → Y is an isothermal process (dT = 0). So, ΔH_{x→y} = nC_{p,m}dT = 0 Y → Z is isochoric. ΔU_{y→z} = nC_{v,m}dT = 5 × 12 × (415 − 335) ΔU_{y→z} = 4800 J ΔH_{y→z} = ΔU_{y→z} + Δ(PV) = ΔU_{y→z} + nRΔT = 4800 + 5 × 8.3 × 80 = 8120 J
