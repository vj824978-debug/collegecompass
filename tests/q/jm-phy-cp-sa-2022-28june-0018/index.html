Following statements related to radioactivity are given below: (a) Radioactivity is a random and spontaneous process and is dependent on physical and chemical conditions. (b) The number of un-decayed nuclei in the radioactive sample decays exponentially with time. (c) Slope of the graph of log_e (no. of undecayed nuclei) Vs. time represents the reciprocal of mean life time (τ). (d) Product of decay constant (λ) and half-life time (T_1/2) is not constant. Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) (a) and (b) only
  2. (2) (b) and (d) only
  3. (3) (b) and (c) only
  4. (4) (c) and (d) only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Analysis of each statement: (a) False - Radioactivity does not depend on physical and chemical conditions, only on nuclear properties. (b) True - N = N_0 e^(-λt) shows exponential decay. (c) True - From lnN = -λt + lnN_0, slope = -λ = -1/τ, so slope represents negative reciprocal of mean life time. (d) False - t_1/2 = ln2/λ, so t_1/2 × λ = ln2 = constant. Therefore, statements (b) and (c) are correct.
