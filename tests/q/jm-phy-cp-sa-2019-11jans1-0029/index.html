The magnitude of torque on a particle of mass 1 kg is 2.5 N·m about the origin. If the force acting on it is 1 N, and the distance of the particle from the origin is 5m, the angle between the force and the position vector is (in radians):

  1. (1) pi/8
  2. (2) pi/6
  3. (3) pi/4
  4. (4) pi/3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

tau = vector r × vector F The magnitude of torque is given by: tau = rF sin(theta) where theta is the angle between the position vector and the force vector. Substituting the given values: 2.5 = 5 × 1 × sin(theta) 2.5 = 5 sin(theta) sin(theta) = 2.5/5 = 0.5 theta = sin^(-1)(0.5) = 30° = pi/6 radians
