Chiral complex from the following is. Here en = ethylene diamine. (1) trans - [Co(NH3)4Cl2]^+. (2) cis - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+. (3) cis - [PtCl2(NH3)2]. (4) trans - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+

  1. (1) trans - [Co(NH3)4Cl2]^+
  2. (2) cis - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+
  3. (3) cis - [PtCl2(NH3)2]
  4. (4) trans - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Cis - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+ The structure shows: - Pt at center with square planar geometry - Two ethylene diamine (en) ligands in cis positions - Two Cl ligands in cis positions The diagram displays: - Cl ligand pointing up (wedge) - en ligands on left and bottom positions (shown with dashed lines indicating different planes) - Cl ligand on right (dashed line) - en ligand on bottom This structure has no element of symmetry (no mirror plane, no rotation axis that would make it superimposable on its mirror image). Therefore it is optically active and chiral. Analysis of other options: (1) trans - [Co(NH3)4Cl2]^+ has a center of inversion symmetry, so it is achiral (3) cis - [PtCl2(NH3)2] has a mirror plane of symmetry, so it is achiral (4) trans - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+ has a center of inversion symmetry, so it is achiral Only option (2) cis - [PtCl2(en)2]^2+ is chiral because ethylene diamine is a bidentate chelating ligand that creates asymmetry in the cis configuration.
