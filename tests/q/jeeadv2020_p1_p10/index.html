[Figure shows uniform electric field E⃗ pointing downward (in negative y direction), represented by vertical arrows. A particle enters at angle θ from horizontal with initial velocity u⃗. Target T is 5m away horizontally from entry point.] Take q/m = 10¹⁰ Ckg⁻¹. Then

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) the particle will hit T if projected at an angle 45° from the horizontal
  2. (B) the particle will hit T if projected either at an angle 30° or 60° from the horizontal
  3. (C) time taken by the particle to hit T could be √(5/6) μs as well as √(5/2) μs
  4. (D) time taken by the particle to hit T is √(5/3) μs

Answer

Correct answer: BC
