For the reaction sequence given below, the correct statement(s) is(are) [Reaction scheme: Cyclohexane → P (via HBr) → Q (via Finkelstein reaction); P → R (via Swarts reaction)] (In the options, X is any atom other than carbon and hydrogen, and it is different in P, Q and R)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) C-X bond length in P, Q and R follows the order Q > R > P.
  2. (B) C-X bond enthalpy in P, Q and R follows the order R > P > Q.
  3. (C) Relative reactivity toward S_N2 reaction in P, Q and R follows the order P > R > Q.
  4. (D) pK_a value of the conjugate acids of the leaving groups in P, Q and R follows the order R > Q > P.

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

The reaction sequence shows: 1. Benzene + HBr → (P) cyclohexyl bromide 2. (P) + NaI/Acetone Finkelstein Reaction → (Q) cyclohexyl iodide 3. (P) + HgF₂ Swarts Reaction → (R) cyclohexyl fluoride The C-X bond enthalpy in P, Q, R follows the order: R > P > Q This means: C-F > C-Br > C-I This is the correct order of C-halogen bond strengths, where fluorine forms the strongest bond with carbon due to its small size and high electronegativity, while iodine forms the weakest bond due to its large size. The Swarts reaction replaces Br with F, the Finkelstein reaction replaces Br with I, and the bond enthalpy trend confirms the known relative bond strengths of C-X bonds.
