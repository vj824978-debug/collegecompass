If a triangle ABC has vertices A(-1, 7), B(-7, 1) and C(5, -5), then its orthocentre has coordinates -

  1. (1) (3/5, -3/5)
  2. (2) (3, -3)
  3. (3) (-3/5, 3/5)
  4. (4) (-3, 3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let orthocentre is H(x0, y0) The altitude from A is perpendicular to BC. Slope of BC = (-5 - 1)/(5 - (-7)) = -6/12 = -1/2 Slope of altitude from A = 2 The altitude from B is perpendicular to AC. Slope of AC = (-5 - 7)/(5 - (-1)) = -12/6 = -2 Slope of altitude from B = 1/2 Since H lies on altitude from A (passing through A(-1, 7)) with slope 2: y - 7 = 2(x + 1) y = 2x + 9 This gives: 2x0 - y0 + 9 = 0 .....(1) Since H lies on altitude from B (passing through B(-7, 1)) with slope 1/2: y - 1 = (1/2)(x + 7) 2y - 2 = x + 7 x - 2y + 9 = 0 This gives: x0 - 2y0 + 9 = 0 .....(2) Alternatively, using the condition that AH ⊥ BC and BH ⊥ AC: mAH · mBC = -1 ((y0 - 7)/(x0 + 1)) · (-1/2) = -1 (y0 - 7)/(x0 + 1) = 2 y0 - 7 = 2(x0 + 1) 2x0 - y0 + 9 = 0 .....(1) and mBH · mAC = -1 ((y0 - 1)/(x0 + 7)) · (-2) = -1 (y0 - 1)/(x0 + 7) = 1/2 2(y0 - 1) = x0 + 7 x0 - 2y0 + 9 = 0 .....(2) Solving equation (1) and (2): From (1): y0 = 2x0 + 9 Substituting in (2): x0 - 2(2x0 + 9) + 9 = 0 x0 - 4x0 - 18 + 9 = 0 -3x0 - 9 = 0 x0 = -3 From (1): y0 = 2(-3) + 9 = -6 + 9 = 3 Therefore (x0, y0) = (-3, 3)
