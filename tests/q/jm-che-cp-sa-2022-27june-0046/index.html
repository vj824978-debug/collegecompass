Decarboxylation of all six possible forms of diaminobenzoic acid C6H3(NH2)2COOH yields three products A, B and C. Three acids give a product 'A', two acids give a product 'B' and one acid gives a product 'C'. The melting point of product 'C' is

  1. (1) 63°C
  2. (2) 90°C
  3. (3) 104°C
  4. (4) 142°C

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The six isomers of diaminobenzoic acid are decarboxylated to give three different products. Product A (1,4-diaminobenzene) is formed from three isomers. Product B (1,2-diaminobenzene) is formed from two isomers. Product C (1,3-diaminobenzene) is formed from only one isomer. The melting point of 1,3-diaminobenzene (product C) is 142°C.
