The ionization energy of sodium in kJ mol^(-1), if electromagnetic radiation of wavelength 242 nm is just sufficient to ionize sodium atom is ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 494

Solution

Given: λ = 242 nm = 242×10^(-9) m. Energy to remove 1 electron from 1 atom: E = hc/λ = (6.62×10^(-34) × 3×10^8)/(242×10^(-9)). Energy for removal of 1e^(-) from 1 mol atoms: E = (hc/λ) × N_A = (6.62×10^(-34) × 3×10^8 × 6.023×10^23)/(242×10^(-9)) = 494.28 kJ/mol ≈ 494 kJ/mol
