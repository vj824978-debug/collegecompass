Choose the correct waveform that can represent the voltage across R of the following circuit, assuming the diode is ideal one: V_i = 10 sin(ωt) volt

  1. (1) Waveform (A) showing voltage oscillating between +3V and -3V with sinusoidal shape
  2. (2) Waveform (B) showing voltage oscillating between +3V and -3V with rectified positive half and negative half cycles
  3. (3) Waveform (C) showing voltage oscillating between +3V and -3V with rectified shape
  4. (4) Waveform (D) showing voltage oscillating between +3V and -3V with rectified shape

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

In forward biased condition, the diode allows current to flow through it. The voltage across R when diode conducts is V_R = V_i - V_diode = V_i - 0 = V_i (since ideal diode). When V_i is positive and exceeds 3V, current flows and V_R follows the input. When V_i is negative or when V_i + 3V (battery) is insufficient to forward bias diode, no current flows. The potential across R due to battery is 3V. When input is positive (>3V), diode conducts and V_R = V_i - 0. When input cannot overcome battery, V_R = 3V. The waveform shows V_R reaching maximum at +3V when input is positive, and remaining at +3V (clamped by battery) when input tries to go negative. This matches waveform (B).
