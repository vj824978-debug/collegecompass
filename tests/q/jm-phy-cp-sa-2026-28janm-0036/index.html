The magnitudes of power of a biconvex lens (refractive index 1.5) and that of a plano-concave lens (refractive index = 1.7) are same. If the curvature of plano-concave lens exactly matches with the curvature of back surface of the biconvex lens, then ratio of radius of curvature of front and back surface of the biconvex lens is ____.

  1. (1) 5:2
  2. (2) 5:12
  3. (3) 12:5
  4. (4) 2:5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For biconvex lens with refractive index μ = 1.5 and radii R_1 and R_2: Power P_A = (μ - 1)(1/R_1 + 1/R_2) = 0.5(1/R_1 + 1/R_2) For plano-concave lens with refractive index μ' = 1.7 and radii R_3 (plane surface) and R_2 (curved surface, matching back surface of biconvex): Power P_B = (μ' - 1)(1/R_3 - 1/R_2) = 0.7(0 - 1/R_2) = -0.7/R_2 Given |P_A| = |P_B|: 0.5(1/R_1 + 1/R_2) = 0.7/R_2 Multiplying both sides by R_2: 0.5(R_2/R_1 + 1) = 0.7 R_2/R_1 + 1 = 1.4 R_2/R_1 = 0.4 = 2/5 Therefore: R_1/R_2 = 5/2 Ratio of radius of curvature of front and back surface of biconvex lens is R_1:R_2 = 5:2
