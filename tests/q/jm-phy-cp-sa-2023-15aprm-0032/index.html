A 12 V battery connected to a coil of resistance 6 Ω through a switch, drives a constant current in the circuit. The switch is opened in 1 ms. The emf induced across the coil is 20 V. The inductance of the coil is:

  1. (1) 10 mH
  2. (2) 8 mH
  3. (3) 5 mH
  4. (4) 12 mH

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: V = 12 V, R = 6 Ω, t = 1 ms, induced emf = 20 V. When switch opens, emf is induced: emf = L × (dI/dt). Initial steady current I = V/R = 12/6 = 2 A. When switch opens in 1 ms, dI/dt = 2/(10^-3) = 2000 A/s. From emf = L × dI/dt: 20 = L × 2000/1000, so 20 = L × 2, therefore L = 10 mH
