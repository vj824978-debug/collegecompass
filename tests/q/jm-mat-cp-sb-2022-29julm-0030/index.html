Let the mirror image of a circle c1: x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y + a = 0 in line y = x + 1 be c2: 5x^2 + 5y^2 + 10gx + 10fy + 38 = 0. If r is the radius of circle c2, then a + 6r^2 is equal to ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

Step 1: Find the image of centre c1 = (1, 3) in line x - y + 1 = 0 using reflection formula. Image coordinates: x1 = 2, y1 = 2, so centre of c2 is (2, 2). Step 2: Rewrite c2 equation in standard form: x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 4y + 38/5 = 0. Step 3: Calculate radius of c2: r = sqrt(4 + 4 - 38/5) = sqrt(2/5). Step 4: Since radius of c1 equals radius of c2 (mirror image property), we have (10 - a)^(1/2) = sqrt(2/5), giving 10 - a = 2/5, so a = 48/5. Step 5: Calculate a + 6r^2 = 48/5 + 6(2/5) = 48/5 + 12/5 = 60/5 = 12.
