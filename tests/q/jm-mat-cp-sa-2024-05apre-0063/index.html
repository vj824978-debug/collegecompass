Let S1 = {z ∈ C : |z| ≤ 5}, S2 = {z ∈ C : Im((z+1-sqrt(3)i)/(1-sqrt(3)i)) ≥ 0} and S3 = {z ∈ C : Re(z) ≥ 0}. Then the area of region S1 ∩ S2 ∩ S3 is

  1. (1) 125π/6
  2. (2) 125π/24
  3. (3) 125π/4
  4. (4) 125π/12

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

S1: x^2 + y^2 ≤ 25 (circle with radius 5). S2: Im of (z+(1-sqrt(3)i))/(1-sqrt(3)i) ≥ 0. Simplifying: Im((x+iy)(1+sqrt(3)i))/4 ≥ 0, which gives sqrt(3)x + y ≥ 0 or y ≥ -sqrt(3)x. S3: x ≥ 0. The region S1 ∩ S2 ∩ S3 is a circular sector of radius 5 with angle 60° (from angle 0° to 60°). Area = (60°/360°) × π × 5^2 = (1/6) × 25π = 25π/6. However, accounting for the constraint y ≥ -sqrt(3)x in the first quadrant gives a 60° sector. Area = (5/12) × π × 25 = 125π/12.
