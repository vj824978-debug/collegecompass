Two liquids of densities ρ₁ and ρ₂ (ρ₂ = 2ρ₁) are filled up behind a square wall of side 10 m as shown in figure. Each liquid has a height of 5m. The ratio of the forces due to these liquids exerted on upper part MN to that at the lower part NO is (Assume that the liquids are not mixing):

  1. (1) 1/2
  2. (2) 1/3
  3. (3) 2/3
  4. (4) 1/4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The hydrostatic force on a vertical surface is given by F = (pressure at center of mass) × area. For the upper part MN (liquid with density ρ₁): - Height of liquid: h = 5 m - Width of wall: a = 10 m - Pressure at center: acts at depth h/2 = 2.5 m from top - F_MN = (ρ₁gh/2) × a × h For the lower part NO (liquid with density ρ₂): - The lower part has both liquids contributing - Liquid 1 (top 5m): pressure at center = ρ₁g(5 + 2.5) = ρ₁g(7.5) but we need pressure at center of lower 5m section - At top of lower section (depth 5m): pressure = ρ₁g(5) - At bottom of lower section (depth 10m): pressure = ρ₁g(5) + ρ₂g(5) = ρ₁g(5) + 2ρ₁g(5) = 3ρ₁g(5) - Average pressure in lower section = [ρ₁g(5) + 3ρ₁g(5)]/2 = 2ρ₁g(5) - F_NO = (ρ₁g + ρ₂g/2) × a × h = (ρ₁g + ρ₁g) × a × h = 2ρ₁gh × a × h Wait, using the given solution approach: F_MN = (ρ₁gh/2) × a × h F_NO = (ρ₁gh + ρ₂gh/2) × a × h Ratio: F_MN/F_NO = (ρ₁gh/2)/(ρ₁gh + ρ₂gh/2) = (ρ₁/2)/(ρ₁ + ρ₂/2) Substituting ρ₂ = 2ρ₁: F_MN/F_NO = (ρ₁/2)/(ρ₁ + 2ρ₁/2) = (ρ₁/2)/(ρ₁ + ρ₁) = (ρ₁/2)/(2ρ₁) = 1/4
