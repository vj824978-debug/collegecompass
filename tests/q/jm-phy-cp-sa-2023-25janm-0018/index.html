Assume that the earth is a solid sphere of uniform density and a tunnel is dug along its diameter throughout the earth. It is found that when a particle is released in this tunnel, it executes a simple harmonic motion. The mass of the particle is 100 g. The time period of the motion of the particle will be (approximately) (Take g = 10 m s^(-2), radius of earth = 6400 km)

  1. (1) 1 hour 40 minutes
  2. (2) 12 hours
  3. (3) 24 hours
  4. (4) 1 hour 24 minutes

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Gravitational acceleration at a distance of r from centre of earth is given by: g' = (g/R) * r Where R is the radius of earth So, d^2(r)/dt^2 = -(g/R) * r This is the equation for simple harmonic motion with angular frequency omega = sqrt(g/R) The time period is: T = 2π/omega = 2π * sqrt(R/g) = 2π * sqrt(6400000/10) = 2π * sqrt(640000) = 2π * 800 sec = 5024 sec = 1 hour 24 minutes (approx)
