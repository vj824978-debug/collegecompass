Let the three sides of a triangle are on the lines 4x - 7y + 10 = 0, x + y = 5 and 7x + 4y = 15. Then the distance of its orthocentre from the orthocentre of the triangle formed by the lines x = 0, y = 0 and x + y = 1 is

  1. (1) sqrt(5)
  2. (2) 5
  3. (3) 20
  4. (4) sqrt(20)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

First find the vertices of the first triangle by solving pairs of equations: 4x - 7y + 10 = 0 with x + y = 5, then x + y = 5 with 7x + 4y = 15, and finally 7x + 4y = 15 with 4x - 7y + 10 = 0. Find the orthocentre of this triangle. For the second triangle formed by x = 0, y = 0, x + y = 1 (vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (0,1)), the orthocentre is at (0,0) since it is a right triangle with right angle at origin. Calculate the distance between the two orthocentres.
