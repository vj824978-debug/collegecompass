A body of mass 1 kg falls freely from a height of 100 m, on a platform mass 3 kg which is mounted on a spring having spring constant k = 1.25 × 10^6 N/m. The body sticks to the platform and the spring's maximum compression is found to be x. Given that g = 10 ms^(-2), the value of x will be close to :

  1. (1) 8 cm
  2. (2) 4 cm
  3. (3) 40 cm
  4. (4) 80 cm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

initial compression mg = kx 30 = 1.25 × 10^6 (x/100) 3 = 1250 x x = 3/1250 negligible P_i = P_f 1 × sqrt(2 × 10 × 100) = 4v 20sqrt(5) = 4v v = 5sqrt(5) (1/2)mv^2 = (1/2)kx^2 4 × 5 × 5 × 5 = 1.25 × 10^6 (x/100)^2 x = 2cm
