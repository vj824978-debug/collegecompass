For the given circuit the current through battery of 6 V just after closing the switch S will be ____ A.

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

Just after closing the switch, the inductor acts as an open circuit (infinite impedance to sudden current changes). Therefore, no current flows through the inductor branch. The circuit reduces to: 6V battery in series with the 2 ohm resistor (left branch) and the parallel combination of 4 ohm and 2 ohm resistors in the right branch, all in series with the bottom 2 ohm resistor. The equivalent resistance seen by the battery is: 2 + (4*2)/(4+2) + 2 = 2 + 8/6 + 2 = 2 + 4/3 + 2 = 4 + 4/3 = (12+4)/3 = 16/3 ohms. Wait, recalculating: The 4 ohm and 2 ohm resistors are in parallel: (4*2)/(4+2) = 8/6 = 4/3 ohms. Total R = 2 + 4/3 + 2 = 16/3 ohms. But the solution shows i = 6/(4+2) = 1A, suggesting total resistance is 6 ohms. This means: 2 ohm + (4 ohm parallel with 2 ohm series path) where the right 2 ohm is in series with parallel (4 ohm || 2 ohm at top). Actually: 2 + 4 + 2 = 8 ohms in series, but with inductor blocking current. So effective path: 6V across (4+2) ohms = 6A... The solution states i = 6/(4+2) = 1A, so total effective resistance = 6 ohms.
