Match List-I with List-II. List-I Elements: A. K, B. Ca, C. Sr, D. Ba. List-II Colour imparted to the flame: 1. Brick Red, 2. Violet, 3. Apple Green, 4. Crimson Red. Choose the correct answer from the options given below (1) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III (2) A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I (3) A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III (4) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

  1. (1) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
  2. (2) A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
  3. (3) A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
  4. (4) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The flame colors imparted by alkali and alkaline earth metals are characteristic and based on their electron transitions: A. K (Potassium) - Gives Violet (II) color in flame test B. Ca (Calcium) - Gives Brick Red (I) color in flame test C. Sr (Strontium) - Gives Crimson Red (III) color in flame test D. Ba (Barium) - Gives Apple Green (IV) color in flame test Therefore, the correct matching is: A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV This corresponds to option (3).
