The cell potential for the following cell Pt | H2(g) | H+ (aq) || Cu2+ (0.01 M) | Cu(s) is 0.576 V at 298 K. The pH of the solution is ___. (Nearest integer) (Given: E°Cu2+/Cu = 0.34 V and 2.303RT/F = 0.06 V)

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

Using Nernst equation: Ecell = E°cell - (0.06/2) log([H+]^2/[Cu^2+]). Given Ecell = 0.576 V, E°cell = 0.34 V (for Cu2+/Cu) - 0 V (for H+/H2) = 0.34 V. Substituting: 0.576 = 0.34 - 0.03 log([H+]^2/0.01). Solving: 0.236 = -0.03 log([H+]^2) - 0.03 log(0.01) = -0.03 log([H+]^2) + 0.06. This gives: 0.03 log([H+]^2) = -0.176, log([H+]^2) = -5.87, pH = 4.93 ≈ 5
