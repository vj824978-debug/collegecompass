A rod of length 2 cm makes an angle 2π/3 rad with the principal axis of a thin convex lens. The lens has a focal length of 10 cm and is placed at a distance of 40/3 cm from the object as shown in the figure. [Figure shows a rod at angle 2π/3 to the principal axis on the left side of a convex lens, with the lens positioned 40/3 cm from the rod. On the right side, the image is shown at distance 30√3/13 cm from the lens, making angle α with the principal axis.] The height of the image is 30√3/13 and the angle made by it with respect to the principal axis is α rad. The value of α is π/n rad, where n is __________

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 6.00

Solution

6.00 u₁ = -40/3 f = 10 1/V₁ + 1×3/40 + 1/10 V₁ = 40 u₂ = 43/3 1/V₂ = 1/10 - 3/43 = (43-30)/430 V₂ = 430/13 x₂ = V₁ - V₂ = 40 - 430/13 = 90/13 tan α = (30√3)/(13×x) = (30√3×13)/(13×90) = 1/√3 [Optical system diagram showing: lens with object distance 40/3 cm on left, focal length 3π/2, image distance 30√3/13 cm on right, with angle α marked] α = π/6 = π/n n = 6
