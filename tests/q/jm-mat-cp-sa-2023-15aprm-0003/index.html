If (α, β) is the orthocenter of the triangle ABC with vertices A(3, -7), B(-1, 2) and C(4, 5), then 9α - 6β + 60 is equal to

  1. (1) 25
  2. (2) 35
  3. (3) 30
  4. (4) 40

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The orthocenter is the intersection of altitudes. AD is altitude from A to BC, perpendicular to BC. Slope of BC = (5-2)/(4-(-1)) = 3/5, so slope of AD = -5/3. Equation of AD: y + 7 = (-5/3)(x - 3) gives 5x + 3y + 6 = 0...(i). BE is altitude from B to AC, perpendicular to AC. Slope of AC = (5-(-7))/(4-3) = 12, so slope of BE = -1/12. Equation of BE: y - 2 = (-1/12)(x + 1) gives x + 12y - 23 = 0...(ii). From (ii): x = 23 - 12y. Substituting in (i): 5(23 - 12y) + 3y + 6 = 0 gives 115 - 60y + 3y + 6 = 0, so 57y = 121, y = 121/57. Then x = 23 - 12(121/57) = (1311 - 1452)/57 = -141/57. Therefore 9α - 6β + 60 = 9(-141/57) - 6(121/57) + 60 = (-1269 - 726)/57 + 60 = -1995/57 + 60 = -35 + 60 = 25.
