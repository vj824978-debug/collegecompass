Let B_i be the magnitude of magnetic field at center of a circular coil of radius R carrying current I. Let B_x be the magnitude of magnetic field at an axial distance 'x' from the center. For x:R = 3:4, B_x/B_i is:

  1. (1) 25:16
  2. (2) 4:5
  3. (3) 64:125
  4. (4) 16:25

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Magnetic field at center of coil: B_i = μ₀I/(2R). Magnetic field at axial distance x: B_x = μ₀IR²/[2(R² + x²)^(3/2)]. Ratio: B_x/B_i = R³/(R² + x²)^(3/2). Given x:R = 3:4, so x = 3k, R = 4k. Then R² + x² = 16k² + 9k² = 25k². B_x/B_i = (4k)³/(25k²)^(3/2) = 64k³/(125k³) = 64/125.
