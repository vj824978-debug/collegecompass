From the magnetic behaviour of [NiCl4]^(2-) (paramagnetic) and [Ni(CO)4] (diamagnetic), choose the correct geometry and oxidation state.

  1. (1) [NiCl4]^(2-) : Ni^(II), square planar [Ni(CO)4] : Ni(0), square planar
  2. (2) [NiCl4]^(2-) : Ni^(II), tetrahedral [Ni(CO)4] : Ni(0), tetrahedral
  3. (3) [NiCl4]^(2-) : Ni^(II), tetrahedral [Ni(CO)4] : Ni^(II), square planar
  4. (4) [NiCl4]^(4)2- : Ni(0), tetrahedral [Ni(CO)4] : Ni(0), square planar

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

[NiCl4]^(2-) Ni^(2-) - [Ar] 3d^8 4s^0 → sp^3, Tetrahedral Number of unpaired electron = 2 paramagnetic [Ni(CO)4], Ni(0) → [Ar] 3d^10 4s^0 (After rearrangement) No unpaired electron sp^3, Tetrahedral, Diamagnetic
