Let ABC be the triangle with AB = 1, AC = 3 and ∠BAC = π/2. If a circle of radius r > 0 touches the sides AB, AC and also touches internally the Circumcircle of the triangle ABC, then the value of r is _________.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 0.83 (0.82 to 0.84)

Solution

Let A be (0, 0), B(1, 0) and C(0, 3) ∴ AB lies on x-axis and AC lies on y-axis ∴ equation of circle touching both x and y-axis is of the form (x - h)² + (y - h)² = h² (∴ h = k = r) It touches the circle (x - 1/2)² + (y - 3/2)² = 5/2 ∴ c₁c₂ = |r₁ - r₂| √[(h - 1/2)² + (h - 3/2)²] = h - √5/√2 ⇒ h² + 1/4 - h + h² + 9/4 - 3h = h² + 5/2 - √10h ⇒ h² + (√10 - 4)h = 0 ⇒ h = 4 - √10 ∴ r = 4 - √10 = 0.8377 [Diagram shows a circle with points A(0,0), B(1,0), C(0,3) marked, with A and B on horizontal axis, C on vertical axis, and a line from A to C and from B to C inscribed in the circle]
