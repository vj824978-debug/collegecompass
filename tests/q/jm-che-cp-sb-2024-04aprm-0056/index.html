Consider the following reaction MnO2 + KOH + O2 → A + H2O. Product 'A' in neutral or acidic medium disproportionate to give products 'B' and 'C' along with water. The sum of spin-only magnetic moment values of B and C is __________ BM. (nearest integer) (Given atomic number of Mn is 25)

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

MnO2 + KOH + O2 → K2MnO4 + H2O (A is K2MnO4). In neutral/acidic medium, K2MnO4 disproportionates: K2MnO4 → KMnO4 + MnO2. Product B is KMnO4 (Mn^+7 with [Ar]3d^0, n=0, μ=0 BM) and Product C is MnO2 (Mn^+4 with [Ar]3d^3, n=3, μ=sqrt(3(3+2))=sqrt(15)≈3.87≈4 BM). Sum of magnetic moments = 0 + 4 = 4 BM (nearest integer).
