The time period of simple harmonic motion of mass M in the given figure is pi*sqrt(aM/5k), where the value of a is ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

From the figure, there are springs in a combination. Two springs of spring constant k are in series (top configuration), and one spring of spring constant k is in parallel with this combination. For two springs in series: k_series = k/2. For this in parallel with k: k_eq = k/2 + k = 3k/2. But looking at the diagram more carefully, the effective spring constant is k_eq = 2k/3 + k = 5k/3. Using T = 2π*sqrt(M/k_eq), we get T = 2π*sqrt(3M/5k) = π*sqrt(12M/5k). Therefore, a = 12.
