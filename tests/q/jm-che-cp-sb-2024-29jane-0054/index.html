A constant current was passed through a solution of AuCl4 ion between gold electrodes. After a period of 10.0 minutes, the increase in mass of cathode was 1.314g. The total charge passed through the solution is ____ × 10^-2 F. (Given atomic mass of Au = 197)

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Moles of Au deposited = 1.314/197. Since Au^3+ + 3e^- → Au, equivalents of Au deposited equals equivalents of charge conducted. Charge in Faraday = (1.314 × 3)/197 = 0.02001 ≈ 2 × 10^-2 F. One equivalent charge is one faraday.
