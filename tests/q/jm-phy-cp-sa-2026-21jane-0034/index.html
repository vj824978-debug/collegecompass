Given below are two statements: Statement I: In a Young's double slit experiment, the angular separation of fringes will increase as the screen is moved away from the plane of the slits. Statement II: In a Young's double slit experiment, the angular separation of fringes will increase when monochromatic source is replaced by another monochromatic source of higher wavelength. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  3. (3) Statement I is false but Statement II is true
  4. (4) Statement I is true but Statement II is false

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Angular fringe width = lambda/d Statement I: As the screen is moved away from the plane of the slits, the distance 'd' (distance between the two slits) remains constant. The angular separation of fringes depends only on the wavelength lambda and the slit separation d, not on the distance to the screen. Therefore, the angular separation remains constant as the screen is moved away. Statement I is FALSE. Statement II: Angular fringe width = lambda/d. When the monochromatic source is replaced by another monochromatic source of higher wavelength, lambda increases. Since d remains constant, the angular fringe width (angular separation) increases. Statement II is TRUE. Therefore, Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
