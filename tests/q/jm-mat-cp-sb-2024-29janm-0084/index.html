All the letters of the word GTWENTY are written in all possible ways with or without meaning and these words are written as if in a dictionary. The serial number of word GTWENTY is _____.

Answer

Correct answer: 553

Solution

The word GTWENTY has 6 distinct letters: G, T, W, E, N, Y. Total arrangements = 6! = 720. To find the position of GTWENTY in dictionary order: (1) Words starting with E: 6!/2! = 360 (2) Words starting with GE: 5!/2! = 60, Words starting with GN: 5!/2! = 60 (3) Words starting with GTE: 4! = 24, GTN: 4! = 24, GTT: 4! = 24 (4) GTWENTY = 1. Total = 360 + 60 + 60 + 24 + 24 + 24 + 1 = 553
