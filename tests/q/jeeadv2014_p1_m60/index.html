For a point P in the plane, let d₁(P) and d₂(P) be the distances of the point P from the lines x - y = 0 and x + y = 0 respectively. The area of the region R consisting of all points P lying in the first quadrant of the plane and satisfying 2 ≤ d₁(P) + d₂(P) ≤ 4, is ________

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

2 ≤ d₁(p) + d₂(p) ≤ 4 For P(α, β), α > β ⟹ 2√2 ≤ 2α ≤ 4√2 √2 ≤ α ≤ 2√2 ⟹ Area of region = ((2√2)² - (√2)²) = 8 - 2 = 6 sq. units [Coordinate plane showing shaded region bounded by y = x line, with vertical lines at x = √2 and x = 2√2, point P(α, β) marked in the region]
