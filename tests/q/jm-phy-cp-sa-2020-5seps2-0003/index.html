Ten charges are placed on the circumference of a circle of radius R with constant angular separation between successive charges. Alternate charges 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 have charge (+q) each, while 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 have charge (-q) each. The potential V and the electric field E at the centre of the circle are respectively - (Take V = 0 at infinity)

  1. (1) V = 10q/(4π ε₀ R); E = 10q/(4π ε₀ R²)
  2. (2) V = 0, E = 10q/(4π ε₀ R²)
  3. (3) V = 0, E = 0
  4. (4) V = 10q/(4π ε₀ R); E = 0

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Potential of centre = V = Σ(kq/R) Vc = K(Σq)/R Σq = 5(+q) + 5(-q) = 0 Vc = K(0)/R = 0 Electric field at centre: En = Σ E Let E be electric field produced by each charge at the centre, then resultant electric field will be 2E at angles of 72° between successive charges. The charges are arranged alternately as +q, -q, +q, -q, +q, -q, +q, -q, +q, -q around the circle with equal angular separation of 360°/10 = 36° between successive charges. Each +q charge produces field 2E (pointing outward from that charge) and each -q charge produces field 2E (pointing inward towards that charge). Since the charges alternate equally around the circle with equal spacing, the electric field vectors from opposite charges cancel each other due to symmetry. Due to the symmetric arrangement of 5 positive and 5 negative charges alternating uniformly, the vector sum of all electric fields equals zero. Ec = 0, Since equal electric field vectors are acting at equal angles so their resultant is equal to zero.
