Let a variable line passing through the centre of the circle x^2 + y^2 - 16x - 4y = 0, meet the positive coordinate axes at the points A and B. Then the minimum value of OA + OB, where O is the origin, is equal to

  1. (1) 12
  2. (2) 18
  3. (3) 20
  4. (4) 24

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given x^2 + y^2 - 16x - 4y = 0, which is (x-8)^2 + (y-2)^2 = 20. The center is C(8,2). A variable line through C(8,2) meets the positive x-axis at A and y-axis at B. If line is y - 2 = m(x - 8), then X-intercept: (-2/m + 8), Y-intercept: (-8m + 2). So OA + OB = -2/m + 8 - 8m + 2 = -2/m - 8m + 10. Taking derivative: d/dm(-2/m - 8m + 10) = 2/m^2 - 8 = 0, giving m^2 = 1/4, so m = -1/2. Therefore OA + OB = -2/(-1/2) - 8(-1/2) + 10 = 4 + 4 + 10 = 18.
