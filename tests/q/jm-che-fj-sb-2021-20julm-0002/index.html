An average person needs about 10000 kJ energy per day. The amount of glucose (molar mass = 180.0 g mol^(-1)) needed to meet this energy requirement is ________ g. (Nearest integer) (Use: ΔcH (glucose) = -2700kJ mol^(-1))

Answer

Correct answer: 667

Solution

2700 kJ energy is released from 180g (1 mole) of glucose. Therefore, 1 kJ energy is released from (180/2700) g of glucose. Therefore, 10000 kJ energy is released from ((180 × 10000)/2700) g of glucose. Amount of glucose = 18000/27 = 666.66 ≈ 667g
