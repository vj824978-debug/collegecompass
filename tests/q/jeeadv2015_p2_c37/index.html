Enthalpy of dissociation (in kJ mol⁻¹) of acetic acid obtained from the Expt. 2 is

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) 1.0
  2. (B) 10.0
  3. (C) 24.5
  4. (D) 51.4

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

HCl + NaOH ⟶ NaCl + H₂O n = 100 × 1 = 100 m mole = 0.1 mole Energy evolved due to neutralization of HCl and NaOH = 0.1 × 57 = 5.7 kJ = 5700 Joule Energy used to increase temperature of solution = 200 × 4.2 × 5.7 = 4788 Joule Energy used to increase temperature of calorimeter = 5700 − 4788 = 912 Joule ms.Δt = 912 m.s×5.7 = 912 ms = 160 Joule/°C [Calorimeter constant] Energy evolved by neutralization of CH₃COOH and NaOH = 200×4.2×5.6+160×5.6 = 5600 Joule So energy used in dissociation of 0.1 mole CH₃COOH = 5700 − 5600 = 100 Joule Enthalpy of dissociation = 1 kJ/mole 1×100/1
