If the mean and the variance of the data are μ and 19 respectively, then the value of λ + μ is Class: 4-8, 8-12, 12-16, 16-20 Frequency: 3, λ, 4, 7

  1. (1) 18
  2. (2) 21
  3. (3) 20
  4. (4) 19

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

μ = (∑f_i x_i)/(∑f_i) = (18 + 10λ + 56 + 126)/(14 + λ) = (200 + 10λ)/(λ + 14) = 10 + (60)/(λ + 14) λ + 14 is multiple of 60 ⟹ λ = 1 or 6 or 16 (assuming μ is natural number) σ^2 = (∑x_i^2)/(λ + 14) - (μ)^2 σ^2 = (6^2(3) + 10^2(λ) + 14^2(4) + (18)^2(7))/(λ + 14) - μ^2 ⟹ 19λ^2 - 28λ - 516 = 0 ⟹ λ = 6 for λ = 6, μ = 10 + 3 = 13 λ + μ = 19
