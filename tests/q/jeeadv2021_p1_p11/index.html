A horizontal force F is applied at the centre of mass of a cylindrical object of mass m and radius R, perpendicular to its axis as shown in the figure. The coefficient of friction between the object and the ground is μ. The center of mass of the object has an acceleration a. The acceleration due to gravity is g. Given that the object rolls without slipping, which of the following statement(s) is/are correct? [Figure shows a circle with radius R marked, and a horizontal force F applied at the center]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) (A) For the same F, the value of a does not depend on whether the cylinder is solid or hollow
  2. (B) (B) For a solid cylinder, the maximum possible value of a is 2μg
  3. (C) (C) The magnitude of the frictional force on the object due to the ground is always μmg
  4. (D) (D) For a thin-walled hollow cylinder, a = F/(2m)

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

F - f = ma fR = Iα a = Rα (about center of mass) (pure rolling) For hollow cylinder a = F/(2m), f = F/2 For solid cylinder, a = 2F/(3m), f = F/3 Also for solid cylinder F/2 ≤ μmg Therefore a ≤ 2μg [Diagram shows a circle with force F and acceleration a pointing right, friction f pointing left, and angular acceleration α indicated at top. Center of mass marked with dot.]
