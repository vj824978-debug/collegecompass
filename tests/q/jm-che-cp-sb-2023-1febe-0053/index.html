A → B. The above reaction is of zero order. Half life of this reaction is 50 min. The time taken for the concentration of A to reduce to one-fourth of its initial value is ________ min.

Answer

Correct answer: 75

Solution

A → B (Zero Order reaction) For zero order reaction: a - x = a/4 ⇒ x = 3a/4 t_(1/2) = a/(2K) = 50 min. ⇒ a/K = 100 min. t = x/K = (3a/4)/K = 3a/(4K) = 75 min.
