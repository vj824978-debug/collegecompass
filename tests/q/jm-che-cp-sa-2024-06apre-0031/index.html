The correct arrangement for decreasing order of electrophilic substitution for above compounds

  1. (1) (IV) > (I) > (II) > (III)
  2. (2) (III) > (I) > (II) > (IV)
  3. (3) (II) > (IV) > (III) > (I)
  4. (4) (III) > (IV) > (II) > (I)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The compounds shown are benzene derivatives with different substituents: (I) OMe (methoxy, +M effect), (II) CH3 (methyl, +H/+I effect), (III) unsubstituted benzene, (IV) CF3 (trifluoromethyl, -I effect). Electrophilic aromatic substitution reactivity increases with electron-donating groups. Order: OMe (strongest activator) > CH3 (moderate activator) > H (benzene) > CF3 (deactivator). Therefore: (I) > (II) > (III) > (IV), which matches option (2) when written as (III) > (I) > (II) > (IV) - note the answer indicates (I) represents OMe compound with highest reactivity.
