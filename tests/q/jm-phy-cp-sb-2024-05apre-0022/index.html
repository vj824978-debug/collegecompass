The shortest wavelength of the spectral lines in the Lyman series of hydrogen spectrum is 915 A. The longest wavelength of spectral lines in the Balmer series will be _____ A.

Answer

Correct answer: 6588

Solution

For Lyman series (shortest wavelength): Using Rydberg formula hc/λ = -13.6(1/n1² - 1/n2²). For shortest wavelength, transition is from n=∞ to n=1: hc/λ0 = -13.6(1/1² - 1/∞²) = -13.6. Given λ0 = 915 A. For Balmer series (longest wavelength): Transition is from n=3 to n=2. hc/λ1 = -13.6(1/2² - 1/3²) = -13.6(1/4 - 1/9) = -13.6(5/36). Therefore: -13.6λ0/λ1 = -13.6 × 5/36. λ1 = λ0 × 36/5 = 915 × 36/5 = 6588 A.
