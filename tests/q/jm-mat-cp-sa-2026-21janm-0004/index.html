Let a point A lie between the parallel lines L1 and L2 such that its distances from L1 and L2 are 6 and 3 units, respectively. Then the area (in sq. units) of the equilateral triangle ABC, where the points B and C lie on the lines L1 and L2 respectively, is:

  1. (1) 15*sqrt(6)
  2. (2) 27
  3. (3) 21*sqrt(3)
  4. (4) 12*sqrt(2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Let the side of equilateral triangle ABC be a. Set up coordinates with A at origin, B on L1 at distance 6, C on L2 at distance 3. Using sin(theta) = 3/a and sin(60° + theta) = 9/a, we get: sqrt(3)*cos(theta) + (1/2)*sin(theta) = 9/a. Substituting sin(theta) = 3/a: sqrt(3)*sqrt(1 - 9/a²) + 3/(2a) = 9/a. Simplifying: sqrt(3)*sqrt(1 - 9/a² + 3/a) = 18/a. Solving: a = sqrt(84) = 2*sqrt(21). Area = (sqrt(3)/4)*a² = (sqrt(3)/4)*84 = 21*sqrt(3)
