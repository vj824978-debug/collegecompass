Consider the following reactions: NaCl + K2Cr2O7 + H2SO4 → (A) + side products (Conc.) (A) + NaOH → (B) + side products (B) + H2SO4 + H2O2 → (C) + Side products (dilute) The sum of the total number of atoms in one molecule each of (A), (B) and (C) is _________.

Answer

Correct answer: 18

Solution

NaCl + K2Cr2O7 + H2SO4 → CrO2Cl + side products (A) = CrO2Cl CrO2Cl → 5 atoms (1 Cr + 2 O + 2 Cl) CrO2Cl + NaOH → NaCrO4 + side products (B) = NaCrO4 NaCrO4 → 7 atoms (1 Na + 1 Cr + 4 O) NaCrO4 + H2SO4 + H2O2 → CrO5 + side products (C) = CrO5 CrO5 → 6 atoms (1 Cr + 5 O) Total atoms in (A), (B), and (C): CrO2Cl: 5 atoms NaCrO4: 7 atoms CrO5: 6 atoms 5 + 7 + 6 = 18 atoms
