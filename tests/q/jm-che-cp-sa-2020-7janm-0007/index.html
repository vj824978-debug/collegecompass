At 35°C, the vapour pressure of CS2 is 512 mm Hg and that of acetone is 344 mm Hg. A solution of CS2 in acetone has a total vapour pressure of 600 mm Hg. The false statement amongst the following is:

  1. (1) A mixture of 100 mL CS2 and 100 mL acetone has a volume < 200 mL
  2. (2) Raoult's law is not obeyed by this system
  3. (3) CS2 and acetone are less attracted to each other than to themselves
  4. (4) Heat must be absorbed in order to produce the solution at 35°C

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The above mixture will show positive deviation from Raoults law Let's analyze each statement: Given: - Vapour pressure of CS2 = 512 mm Hg - Vapour pressure of acetone = 344 mm Hg - Total vapour pressure of solution = 600 mm Hg According to Raoult's law: P_total = P_CS2 * x_CS2 + P_acetone * x_acetone If we assume equimolar mixture (x_CS2 = x_acetone = 0.5): P_expected = 512(0.5) + 344(0.5) = 256 + 172 = 428 mm Hg Since observed P_total (600 mm Hg) > expected P_total (428 mm Hg), this shows positive deviation from Raoult's law. Positive deviation indicates: - Intermolecular attractions between CS2 and acetone molecules are weaker than between like molecules - Statement (3) is TRUE Statement (2): Raoult's law is NOT obeyed - TRUE (positive deviation observed) Statement (4): Heat must be absorbed - TRUE (positive deviation requires heat absorption as the process is endothermic) Statement (1): A mixture of 100 mL CS2 and 100 mL acetone has volume < 200 mL This would be TRUE only for negative deviation (contraction on mixing). Since we have positive deviation, the volume would be > 200 mL (expansion on mixing). Therefore, statement (1) is FALSE.
