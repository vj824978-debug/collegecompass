KMnO4 oxidises I- in acidic and neutral/faintly alkaline solution, respectively, to

  1. (1) I2 & I2
  2. (2) I2 & IO3-
  3. (3) IO3- & I2
  4. (4) IO3- & IO3-

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

MnO4- + I- → I2 + Mn2+ (acidic medium) MnO4- + I- → IO3- + MnO2 (faintly alkaline) In acidic medium, KMnO4 oxidises I- to I2 (oxidation state of I changes from -1 to 0). In neutral/faintly alkaline medium, KMnO4 oxidises I- to IO3- (oxidation state of I changes from -1 to +5). The oxidizing power of MnO4- decreases in neutral/alkaline medium, but when it does oxidise, it goes to completion producing IO3-.
