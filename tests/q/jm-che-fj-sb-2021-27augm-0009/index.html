200 mL of 0.2 M HCl is mixed with 300 mL of 0.1 M NaOH. The molar heat of neutralization of this reaction is -57.1 kJ. The increase in temperature in °C of the system on mixing is x × 10^-2. The value of x is ___ (Nearest integer). [Given: Specific heat of water = 4.18 J g^-1 K^-1, Density of water = 1.00 g cm^-3] (Assume no volume change on mixing)

Answer

Correct answer: 82

Solution

Millimoles of HCl = 200 × 0.2 = 40. Millimoles of NaOH = 300 × 0.1 = 30. NaOH is limiting reagent. Heat released = (30/1000) × 57.1 × 1000J = 1713 J. Mass of solution = 500 × 1g = 500 g. Using ΔT = q/(mc), ΔT = 1713/(500 × 4.18) = 1713/2090 = 0.8196 °C = 81.96 × 10^-2 °C = 82 × 10^-2 °C. Therefore, x = 82.
