Which of the following pair is not isoelectronic species? (At. no. Sm, 62; Er, 68; Yb, 70; Lu, 71; Eu, 63; Tb, 65; Tm, 69)

  1. (1) Sm^2+ and Er^3+
  2. (2) Yb^2+ and Lu
  3. (3) Eu^2+ and Tb^4+
  4. (4) Tb^2+ and Tm^4+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Species having same number of electrons are isoelectronic. Check each pair: (1) Sm^2+ → 60 electrons, Er^3+ → 65 electrons (not isoelectronic). (2) Yb^2+ → 68 electrons, Lu → 71 electrons (not isoelectronic, but checking: Yb has 70, so Yb^2+ has 68; Lu has 71 electrons, not same). (3) Eu^2+ → 61 electrons, Tb^4+ → 61 electrons (isoelectronic). (4) Tb^2+ → 63 electrons, Tm^4+ → 65 electrons (not isoelectronic). The solution shows Tb^2+ → 63 electrons and Tm^4+ are not isoelectronic, and Sm^2+ → 60 electrons and Er^3+ → 65 electrons are not isoelectronic.
