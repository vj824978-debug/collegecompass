B has a smaller first ionization enthalpy than Be. Consider the following statements: (i) it is easier to remove 2p electron than 2s electron (ii) 2p electron of B is more shielded from the nucleus by the inner core of electrons than the 2s electrons of Be (iii) 2s electron has more penetration power than 2p electron (iv) atomic radius of B is more than Be (atomic number B = 5, Be = 4) The correct statements are:

  1. (1) (i), (iii) and (iv)
  2. (2) (i), (ii) and (iii)
  3. (3) (i), (ii) and (iv)
  4. (4) (ii), (iii) and (iv)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Radius of Boron is less than Radius of Be. Hence IV statement is false. Rest are True. Statement (i): It is easier to remove 2p electron than 2s electron - TRUE. The first ionization enthalpy of B is smaller than Be because the 2p electron in B is easier to remove compared to 2s electron in Be. Statement (ii): 2p electron of B is more shielded from the nucleus by the inner core of electrons than the 2s electrons of Be - FALSE. The 2s electrons of Be are more shielded because they are in the outermost shell while B has both 2s and 2p electrons, but the 2s electrons experience greater shielding. Actually, this needs reconsideration - the 2p electron of B experiences shielding from the 1s electrons, while Be's 2s electron also experiences shielding from 1s. However, the core is same (1s^2), so comparison is about the orbital type. 2p electrons are more shielded than 2s electrons in the same shell. TRUE. Statement (iii): 2s electron has more penetration power than 2p electron - TRUE. s electrons have greater penetration power compared to p electrons due to their radial distribution. Statement (iv): atomic radius of B is more than Be - FALSE. Atomic radius of B (88 pm) is less than atomic radius of Be (112 pm) because B has higher nuclear charge and same number of shells, so electrons are pulled closer to nucleus. Therefore, correct statements are (i), (iii) and (iv) - but (iv) is false. The correct statements are (i), (ii) and (iii).
