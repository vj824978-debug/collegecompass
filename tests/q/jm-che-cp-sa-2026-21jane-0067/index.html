For a closed circuit Daniell cell, which of the following plots is the accurate one at a given temperature?

  1. (1) E°_cell vs time showing a vertical line
  2. (2) E°_cell vs time showing a horizontal line that drops to a lower horizontal line
  3. (3) E°_cell vs time showing a decreasing linear trend
  4. (4) E°_cell vs time showing an increasing linear trend

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

E°_cell remain constant with time. For a closed circuit Daniell cell (galvanic cell) at a given temperature, the standard cell potential E°_cell is a thermodynamic property that depends only on the nature of the electrodes and electrolytes, and the temperature. It does NOT depend on time. Therefore, E°_cell remains constant with time at a fixed temperature. Analyzing the plots: (1) Shows E°_cell as a vertical line - not realistic representation (2) Shows E°_cell as constant (horizontal line) at a given temperature, which may show a step change if temperature changes - this is the correct representation (3) Shows E°_cell decreasing with time - incorrect, as E°_cell does not change with time at constant temperature (4) Shows E°_cell increasing with time - incorrect, as E°_cell does not change with time at constant temperature The correct answer is plot (2), which shows E°_cell remaining constant (horizontal line) with time at a given temperature.
