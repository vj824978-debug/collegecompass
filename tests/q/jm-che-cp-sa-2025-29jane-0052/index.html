For hydrogen like species, which of the following graphs provides the most appropriate representation of E vs Z plot for a constant n? [E: Energy of the stationary state, Z: atomic number, n = principal quantum number]

  1. (1) Graph showing E vs Z with a horizontal line at O, then dropping steeply downward after Z increases
  2. (2) Graph showing E vs Z with a horizontal line at O, then curving downward after Z increases
  3. (3) Graph showing E vs Z with a straight line passing through O with positive slope
  4. (4) Graph showing E vs Z with a curved line passing through O with increasing positive slope

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For hydrogen-like species, the energy of a stationary state is given by: E_n = -13.6 × Z^2 / n^2 eV Where: - Z is the atomic number - n is the principal quantum number - 13.6 eV is the Rydberg energy For a constant value of n, the energy depends on Z^2 (inversely proportional to Z^2, with a negative sign). Therefore: E ∝ -Z^2 This means: 1. E is always negative (lies below the E = 0 line) 2. As Z increases, E becomes more negative (energy decreases) 3. The relationship is non-linear - it follows a parabolic curve (proportional to Z^2) 4. The curve is symmetric about the E-axis and curves downward Among the given options: - Option (1) shows a sharp drop - incorrect (should be smooth curve) - Option (2) shows a smooth downward curving relationship - CORRECT - Option (3) shows a linear relationship - incorrect (should be quadratic) - Option (4) shows increasing curvature - incorrect direction Therefore, graph (2) is the most appropriate representation.
