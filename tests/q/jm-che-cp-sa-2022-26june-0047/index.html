Which statement is NOT correct for p-toluenesulphonyl chloride? (1) It is known as Hinsberg's reagent (2) It is used to distinguish primary and secondary amines (3) On treatment with secondary amine, it leads to a product, that is soluble in alkali (4) It doesn't react with tertiary amines

  1. (1) It is known as Hinsberg's reagent
  2. (2) It is used to distinguish primary and secondary amines
  3. (3) On treatment with secondary amine, it leads to a product, that is soluble in alkali
  4. (4) It doesn't react with tertiary amines

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

p-Toluenesulphonyl chloride (Ts-Cl) is Hinsberg's reagent. It reacts with primary and secondary amines to form sulfonamides. Primary amines form N-substituted sulfonamides which are soluble in alkali. Secondary amines form N,N-disubstituted sulfonamides which are insoluble in alkali (NOT soluble). Tertiary amines do not react with p-toluenesulphonyl chloride. Therefore, statement (3) is incorrect.
