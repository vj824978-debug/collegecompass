A container of fixed volume contains a gas at 27°C. To double the pressure of the gas, the temperature of gas should be raised to ________ °C.

Answer

Correct answer: 327

Solution

For a gas in a container of fixed volume, using Gay-Lussac's Law: P1/T1 = P2/T2 Given: T1 = 27°C = 300 K P2 = 2P1 (pressure is doubled) Substituting in the formula: P/300 = 2P/T2 Simplifying: 1/300 = 2/T2 T2 = 2 × 300 T2 = 600 K Converting to Celsius: T2 = 600 - 273 = 327°C
