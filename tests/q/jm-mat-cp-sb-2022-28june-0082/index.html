Suppose a class has 7 students. The average marks of these students in the mathematics examination is 62, and their variance is 20. A student fails in the examination if he/she gets less than 50 marks, then in worst case, the number of students can fail is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 0

Solution

Given: n = 7, mean = 62, variance = 20. The variance is defined as sum of (x_i - 62)^2 / 7 = 20, which gives sum of (x_i - 62)^2 = 140. For any student to score less than 50, we need (x_i - 62)^2 > 144 (since (50-62)^2 = 144). But the sum of all squared deviations is only 140, which is less than 144. Therefore, it's impossible for any single student to have (x_i - 62)^2 > 144. Hence, no student can score less than 50 marks, and the number of students that can fail is 0.
