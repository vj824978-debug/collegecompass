Three moles of AgCl get precipitated when one mole of an octahedral co-ordination compound with empirical formula CrCl3·3NH3·3H2O reacts with excess of silver nitrate. The number of chloride ions satisfying the secondary valency of the metal ion is ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 0

Solution

The compound CrCl3·3NH3·3H2O is octahedral. When it reacts with excess AgNO3, 3 moles of AgCl precipitate, meaning 3 chloride ions are free (ionic). Since Cr is +3 and has 6 coordination positions (secondary valency = 6), and 3 NH3 ligands occupy 3 positions, the remaining 3 positions must be occupied by chloride ions to satisfy the secondary valency. The structure is [Cr(NH3)3Cl3]·3H2O where all 3 chloride ions are coordinated. However, since 3 AgCl precipitate, this indicates 3 Cl- are ionic/free. This means the compound is [Cr(NH3)3(H2O)3]Cl3, where the 3 water molecules coordinate to Cr and 3 chloride ions are outside. But this contradicts. Re-analyzing: if 3 Cl precipitate as AgCl, then 3 Cl are ionic. For Cr3+ octahedral coordination, 6 ligands are needed. With 3 NH3 coordinated, 3 more positions need filling. If 3 Cl are ionic (outside), then 3 H2O must be coordinated: [Cr(NH3)3(H2O)3]Cl3. Secondary valency satisfied by: 3 NH3 + 3 H2O = 6. Chloride ions satisfying secondary valency = 0.
