For 1 mol of gas, the plot of pV vs. p is shown below. p is the pressure and V is the volume of the gas. What is the value of compressibility factor at point A?

  1. (1) 1 - a/(RTV)
  2. (2) 1 + b/V
  3. (3) 1 - b/V
  4. (4) 1 + a/(RTV)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

At point 'A', 'a' is considerable and 'B' is negligible. (p + a/V^2)V = RT pV + a/V = RT Z = 1 - a/(VRT) The compressibility factor Z at point A is given by Z = 1 - a/(VRT), which matches option (1).
