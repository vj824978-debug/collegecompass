5 moles of AB2 weigh 125 × 10^(-5) kg and 10 moles of A2B3 weigh 300 × 10^(-3) kg. The molar mass of A (M_A) and molar mass of B (M_B) in kg mol are:

  1. (1) M_A = 10 × 10^(-3) and M_B = 5 × 10^(-3)
  2. (2) M_A = 25 × 10^(-3) and M_B = 50 × 10^(-3)
  3. (3) M_A = 5 × 10^(-3) and M_B = 10 × 10^(-3)
  4. (4) M_A = 50 × 10^(-3) and M_B = 25 × 10^(-3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

From the given data: For AB2: 5 moles weigh 125 × 10^(-5) kg Molar mass of AB2 = (125 × 10^(-5))/5 = 25 × 10^(-5) kg/mol Since molar mass is in g/mol scale: M_A + 2M_B = 25 ... (i) For A2B3: 10 moles weigh 300 × 10^(-3) kg Molar mass of A2B3 = (300 × 10^(-3))/10 = 30 × 10^(-3) kg/mol Since molar mass is in g/mol scale: 2M_A + 2M_B = 30 ... (2) Solving equations (i) and (2): From equation (i): M_A + 2M_B = 25 From equation (2): 2M_A + 2M_B = 30 Subtracting (i) from (2): (2M_A + 2M_B) - (M_A + 2M_B) = 30 - 25 M_A = 5 g/mol = 5 × 10^(-3) kg/mol Substituting M_A = 5 in equation (i): 5 + 2M_B = 25 2M_B = 20 M_B = 10 g/mol = 10 × 10^(-3) kg/mol Therefore: M_A = 5 × 10^(-3) kg/mol and M_B = 10 × 10^(-3) kg/mol
