A radioactive element 242/92 X emits two α-particles, one electron and two positrons. The product nucleus is represented by 234/P Y. The value of P is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 87

Solution

Starting with 242/92 X Emission of 2 α-particles: - Each α-particle is 4/2 He - Mass number decreases by: 2 × 4 = 8 - Atomic number decreases by: 2 × 2 = 4 After 2α: 242-8/92-4 = 234/88 A Emission of 1 electron (β⁻ decay): - Atomic number increases by 1 After β⁻: 234/89 B Emission of 2 positrons (β⁺ decay): - Each positron emission decreases atomic number by 1 - 2 positrons decrease atomic number by 2 After 2β⁺: 234/(89-2) = 234/87 Y Therefore, the sequence is: 242/92 X → (2α) → 234/88 A → (e⁻) → 234/89 B → (2e⁺) → 234/87 Y So, P = 87
