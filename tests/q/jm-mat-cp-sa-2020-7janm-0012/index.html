Let P be a plane passing through the points (2, 1, 0), (4, 1, 1) and (5, 0, 1) and R be any point (2, 1, 6). Then the image of R in the plane P is

  1. (1) (4, 3, 2)
  2. (2) (6, 5, 2)
  3. (3) (6, 5, -2)
  4. (4) (3, 4, -2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3
