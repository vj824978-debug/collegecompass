Two vertices of a triangle are (0, 2) and (4, 3). If its orthocenter is at the origin, then its third vertex lies in which quadrant -

  1. (1) third
  2. (2) fourth
  3. (3) second
  4. (4) first

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

(BC and AD are perpendicular) Let C = (0, 2), A = (4, 3), B = (h, k), and D = (0, 0) (orthocenter) Slope of BC = (k - 2)/(h - 0) = (k - 2)/h Slope of AD = (0 - 3)/(0 - 4) = -3/(-4) = 3/4 Since BC and AD are perpendicular: (k - 2)/h × 3/4 = -1 (k - 2)/h = -4/3 3(k - 2) = -4h 3k - 6 = -4h -12h - 6 = -4h -6 = 8h h = -3/4 Slope of AC = (3 - 2)/(4 - 0) = 1/4 Slope of BD = (0 - k)/(0 - h) = -k/(-h) = k/h Since AC and BD are perpendicular: 1/4 × k/h = -1 k/h = -4 k = -4h k = -4(-3/4) = 3 B = (-3/4, 3); IInd Quadrant
