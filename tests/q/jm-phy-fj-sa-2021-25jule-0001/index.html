A ray of light entering from air into a denser medium of refractive index 4/3, as shown in figure. The light ray suffers total internal reflection at the adjacent surface as shown. The maximum value of angle θ should be equal to:

  1. (1) sin^(-1)(sqrt(5)/4)
  2. (2) sin^(-1)(sqrt(7)/3)
  3. (3) sin^(-1)(sqrt(7)/4)
  4. (4) sin^(-1)(sqrt(6)/3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

By Snell's law at first surface: sin(θ)/sin(θ') = 4/3, so sin(θ') = 3sin(θ)/4. For total internal reflection at second surface: sin(θ'') > sin(θ_c), where θ'' = 90° - θ'. This gives sin(90° - θ') > sin(θ_c), so cos(θ') > 3/4. Using 1 - sin²(θ') > 9/16 with sin(θ') = 3sin(θ)/4, we get 1 - 9sin²(θ)/16 > 9/16. Simplifying: 7/16 > 9sin²(θ)/16, so sin²(θ) < 7/9, giving sin(θ) < sqrt(7)/3. Therefore sin(θ) = sqrt(7)/3.
