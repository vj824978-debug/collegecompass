Adsorption of phenol from its aqueous solution on to fly ash obeys Freundlich isotherm. At a given temperature, from 10 mg g^-1 and 16 mg g^-1 aqueous phenol solutions, the concentrations of adsorbed phenol are measured to be 4 mg g^-1 and 10 mg g^-1, respectively. At this temperature, the concentration (in mg g^-1) of adsorbed phenol from 20 mg g^-1 aqueous solution of phenol will be _______. Use: log₁₀2 = 0.3

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 16.00

Solution

x/m = KC^(1/n) C = 10 mg g⁻¹ ⟹ x/m = 4mg g⁻¹ C = 16 mg g⁻¹ ⟹ x/m = 10mg g⁻¹ ⟹ 4 = K(10)^(1/n) (1) 10 = K(16)^(1/n) (2) ⟹ 4/10 = (10/16)^(1/n) ⟹ log4 - log 10 = (1/n)[log10 - log16] ⟹ n = (1 - 4 × .30)/(2 × .30 - 1) = 0.2/0.4 ⟹ n = 1/2 Now x/m = K(20)^x (3) Dividing (3) by (1): (x/m)/4 = (20/10)^x = 4 x/m = 16 mg g⁻¹
