If the maximum load carried by an elevator is 1400 kg (600 kg - Passengers + 800 kg - elevator), which is moving up with a uniform speed of 3 m s^(-1) and the frictional force acting on it is 2000 N, then the maximum power used by the motor is _____ kW (g = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 48

Solution

Given: Total mass = 1400 kg, velocity = 3 m/s, friction force = 2000 N, g = 10 m/s^2. Weight of system = 1400 × 10 = 14000 N. Since moving with uniform speed (constant velocity), net force = 0. Therefore, applied force by motor = Weight + Friction = 14000 + 2000 = 16000 N. Power = Force × velocity = 16000 × 3 = 48000 W = 48 kW
