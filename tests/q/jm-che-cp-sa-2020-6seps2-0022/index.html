A solution of phenol in chloroform when treated with aqueous NaOH gives compound P as a major product. The mass percentage of carbon in P is __________ (to the nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 69

Solution

Phenol (C6H5OH) when treated with CHCl3 (chloroform) and aqueous NaOH undergoes Reimer Tiemann reaction to give salicylaldehyde as the major product. The reaction mechanism: - Phenol is deprotonated by NaOH to form phenoxide ion - CHCl3 reacts with phenoxide in presence of base to form compound P - The product P is salicylaldehyde with structure: OH group and CHO group on benzene ring (ortho positions) Molecular formula of P: C7H6O2 Molecular weight of C7H6O2 = 7(12) + 6(1) + 2(16) = 84 + 6 + 32 = 122 Mass percentage of carbon in P: %C = (12 × 7 × 100)/122 = (84 × 100)/122 = 8400/122 = 68.85 ≈ 69%
