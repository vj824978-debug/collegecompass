150 g of acetic acid was contaminated with 10.2 g ascorbic acid (C6H8O6) to lower down its freezing point by (x × 10⁻¹)°C. The value of x is ___. (Nearest integer) (Given: Kf = 3.9 K kg mol⁻¹; molar mass of ascorbic acid = 176 g mol⁻¹)

Answer

Correct answer: 15

Solution

Using colligative property formula: ΔTf = Kf × m, where m is molality. Molality = (moles of solute)/(mass of solvent in kg) = (10.2/176)/(0.150) = 0.3857 mol/kg. ΔTf = 3.9 × 0.3857 = 1.506 K = 15.06 × 10⁻¹ °C. Therefore x = 15
