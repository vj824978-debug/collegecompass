A block of sqrt(3) kg is attached to a string whose other end is attached to the wall. An unknown force F is applied so that the string makes an angle of 30° with the wall. The tension T is: (Given g = 10 ms^(-2))

  1. (1) 15 N
  2. (2) 20 N
  3. (3) 10 N
  4. (4) 25 N

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Drawing the FBD of the point where F is applied Three forces act at the point: 1. Tension T along the string (at 30° from vertical/wall) 2. Force F (horizontal) 3. Weight W = sqrt(3)g (vertical downward) For equilibrium at the point: Vertical: T cos(30°) = sqrt(3)g Horizontal: T sin(30°) = F From vertical equilibrium: T cos(30°) = sqrt(3)g T × (sqrt(3)/2) = sqrt(3) × 10 T × (sqrt(3)/2) = 10sqrt(3) T = 10sqrt(3) × (2/sqrt(3)) T = 20 N
