For an isosceles prism of angle A and refractive index μ, it is found that the angle of minimum deviation δ_m = A. Which of the following options is/are correct?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) At minimum deviation, the incident angle i₁ and the refracting angle r₁ at the first refracting surface are related by r₁ = (i₁/2).
  2. (B) For this prism the refractive index μ and the angle of prism A are related as A = (1/2)cos⁻¹(μ/2).
  3. (C) For this prism, the emergent ray at the second surface will be tangential to the surface when the angle of incidence at the first surface is i₁ = sin⁻¹[sin A√(4cos²(A/2) - 1 - cos A)].
  4. (D) For the angle of incidence i₁ = A, the ray inside the prism is parallel to the base of the prism.

Answer

Correct answer: ACD

Solution

The minimum deviation produced by a prism δm = 2i - A = A ∴ i1 = i2 = A and r1 = r2 = A/2 ∴ r1 = i1/2 Now using Snell's law sin A = μsin A/2 ⟹ μ = 2 cos (A/2) For this prism when the emergent ray at the second surface is tangential to the surface i2 = π/2 ∴ r2 = θc ∴ r1 = A − θc so, sin i1 = μ sin (A − θc) so, i1 = sin⁻¹[sin A√(4cos² A/2 − 1 − cos A)] For minimum deviation through isosceles prism, the ray inside the prism is parallel to the base of the prism.
