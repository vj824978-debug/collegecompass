A particle moves along the x-axis and has its displacement x varying with time t according to the equation: x = c₀(t² - 2) + c(t - 2)². Where c₀ and c are constants of appropriate dimensions. Then which of the following statements is correct?

  1. (1) the initial velocity of the particle is 4c
  2. (2) the acceleration of the particle is 2(c + c₀)
  3. (3) the acceleration of the particle is 2c
  4. (4) the acceleration of the particle is 2c₀

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: x = c₀(t² - 2) + c(t - 2)². Find velocity: v = dx/dt = 2c₀t + 2c(t - 2) = 2c₀t + 2ct - 4c. At t = 0: v(0) = -4c. Find acceleration: a = dv/dt = 2c₀ + 2c. The acceleration is constant and equals 2(c₀ + c) = 2(c + c₀). Option 2 is correct.
