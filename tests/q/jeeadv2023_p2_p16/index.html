Considering the air flow to be streamline, the steady mass flow rate of air exiting the chimney is _______ gm s^-1.

Comprehension · Marks: +3 / 0.0

Passage:

Answer

Correct answer: 49.61

Solution

Pₐ = constant So, pₐTₐ = ρT So, ρ = (ρ₀Tₐ)/T = (1.2 × 300)/360 ρ = 1 kg/m³ Applying Bernoulli's equation between points (1) and (2) Assuming velocity of hot air inside furnace ~ 0 Pₐ + 0 + 0 = Pₐ - ρₐ g (10) + ρg(10) + (1/2)ρv² So, v = √[2(ρₐ - ρ)g(10)/ρ] = √[(2)(0.2)100] = √40 m/s Q = [π(0.1)²/4](√40)(10²) = 49.61 gm/s [Diagram shows a furnace setup with a vertical chimney of height h and diameter d at top, connected to a main chamber of height H and diameter D at bottom.
