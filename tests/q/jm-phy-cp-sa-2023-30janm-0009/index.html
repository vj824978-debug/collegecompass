Match Column-I with Column-II: Column-I contains (x-t graphs) and Column-II contains (v-t graphs). Match the graphs appropriately.

  1. (1) A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II
  2. (2) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
  3. (3) A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
  4. (4) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

To match x-t graphs with v-t graphs, we use the relationship: v = dx/dt (velocity is the derivative of position with respect to time). (A) x-t graph shows a curve that is concave upward (parabolic shape opening upward). The derivative (slope) starts small and increases with time, showing increasing velocity. This matches (I) where v-t graph shows a linear increase - constant positive acceleration. (B) x-t graph shows a curve that is concave downward (decreasing slope). The derivative starts large and decreases with time, showing decreasing velocity. This matches (IV) where v-t graph shows v decreasing from v₀ and becoming negative, with asymptotic behavior. (C) x-t graph shows a triangular shape - linear increase then linear decrease. The derivative would be constant positive, then constant negative, creating a rectangular pulse. This matches (III) where v-t graph shows a rectangular pulse with positive then negative constant values. (D) x-t graph shows a straight line through origin with constant slope. The derivative is constant, indicating constant velocity. This matches (I) where v-t graph shows constant velocity (horizontal line).
