A particle of mass m with an initial velocity ui collides perfectly elastically with a mass 3m at rest. It moves with a velocity vj after collision, then, v is given by:

  1. (1) v = sqrt(2/3)u
  2. (2) v = (1/sqrt(6))u
  3. (3) v = u/sqrt(3)
  4. (4) v = u/sqrt(2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Before collision: particle of mass m has velocity ui, mass 3m is at rest. After collision: particle m has velocity vj, mass 3m has velocity v1 with magnitude |v1|. From momentum conservation: P_i = P_f M(ui) + 3m(0) = mvj + 3m v1 mui - mvj = 3m v1 v1 = (ui - vj)/3 or |v1| = sqrt(u^2 + v^2)/3 or v1^2 = (u^2 + v^2)/9 ......(1) As collision is perfectly elastic hence k_i = k_j: (1/2)mu^2 + (1/2)3m(0)^2 = (1/2)mv^2 + (1/2)3m v1^2 u^2 = v^2 + 3v1^2 u^2 = v^2 + 3((u^2 + v^2)/9) u^2 = v^2 + (u^2 + v^2)/3 3u^2 = 3v^2 + u^2 + v^2 3u^2 = 3v^2 + u^2 + v^2 2u^2 = 4v^2 v = u/sqrt(2)
