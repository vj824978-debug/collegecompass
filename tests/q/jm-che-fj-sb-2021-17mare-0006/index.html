A 1 molal K₄[Fe(CN)₆] solution has a degree of dissociation of 0.4. Its boiling point is equal to that of another solution which contains 18.1 weight percent of a non-electrolytic solute A. The molar mass of A is _____ u. (Round off to the nearest integer) [Density of water = 1.0 g/cm³]

Answer

Correct answer: 85

Solution

K₄[Fe(CN)₆] dissociates into 4K⁺ + [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻ with degree of dissociation α = 0.4. Using i = 1 + (n-1)α where n = 5 (total ions): i = 1 + 4(0.4) = 2.6. Molality of K₄[Fe(CN)₆] solution: m = 18.1/(M×(100-18.1)) × 1000 where M is molar mass of A. Since both solutions have same boiling point, ΔTb is equal, so: i₁m₁ = i₂m₂. For K₄[Fe(CN)₆]: 2.6 × 1 = 1 × m₂, where m₂ = 2.6. For solute A: 2.6 = 18.1×1000/(M×81.9). Solving: M×81.9 = 18.1×1000/2.6 = 6962. Therefore M = 81.9 ≈ 82 u (rounding to nearest integer gives 81.9 u).
