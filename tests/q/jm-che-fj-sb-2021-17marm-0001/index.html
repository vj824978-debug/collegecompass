The oxygen dissolved in water exerts a partial pressure of 20 kPa in the vapour above water. The molar solubility of oxygen in water is ________ × 10^-5 mol dm^-3. (Round off to the nearest integer) [Given: Henry's law constant = K_H = 8.0 × 10^4 kPa for O2. Density of water with dissolved oxygen = 1.0 kg dm^-3]

Answer

Correct answer: 25

Solution

Using Henry's law: Partial Pressure of O2 = K_H × solubility. Therefore, solubility = P_O2 / K_H = 20 / (8.0 × 10^4) = 2.5 × 10^-4 = 25 × 10^-5 M. Answer: 25
