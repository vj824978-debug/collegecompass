A rod of length 60 cm rotates with a uniform angular velocity 20 rad s^(-1) about its perpendicular bisector, in a uniform magnetic field 0.5 T. The direction of magnetic field is parallel to the axis of rotation. The potential difference between the two ends of the rod is ___V.

Answer

Correct answer: 0

Solution

When a rod rotates about its perpendicular bisector (midpoint O), consider the motional EMF. For a small element at distance r from O, the velocity is v = ωr. The EMF induced in element dr is dE = Bv dr = Bωr dr. For the half from O to A (length ℓ/2 = 0.3 m), EMF is V_O - V_A = Bωℓ^2/8. Similarly for half from O to B, EMF is V_O - V_B = Bωℓ^2/8. Since both halves contribute equally but the rod rotates about the center, the net potential difference between the two ends is: V_A - V_B = (V_O - V_B) - (V_O - V_A) = Bωℓ^2/8 - Bωℓ^2/8 = 0. Alternatively, by symmetry about the perpendicular bisector, both ends A and B are equidistant from the axis and experience equal EMF contributions that cancel out.
