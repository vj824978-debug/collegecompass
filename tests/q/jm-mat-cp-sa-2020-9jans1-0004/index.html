Let a_n be the n^th term of a G.P. of positive terms. If sum from n=1 to 100 of a_{2n+1} = 200 and sum from n=1 to 100 of a_{2n} = 100, then sum from n=1 to 200 of a_n is equal to-

  1. (1) 175
  2. (2) 225
  3. (3) 300
  4. (4) 150

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let the G.P. is a, ar, ar^2 .... sum from n=1 to 100 of a_{2n+1} = 200 => a_3 + a_5 + a_7 + .... + a_{201} = 200 => ar^2(r^200 - 1)/(r^2 - 1) = 200 ...(1) sum from n=1 to 100 of a_{2n} = 100 => a_2 + a_4 + .... + a_{200} = 100 => ar(r^200 - 1)/(r^2 - 1) = 100 ...(2) dividing (1) by (2) we get, r = 2 adding both eq. (1) & (2) => a_2 + a_3 + a_4 + a_5 + ..... + a_{201} = 300 => r(a_1 + a_2 + ..... + a_{200}) = 300 => a_1 + a_2 + ..... + a_{200} = 300/r => sum from n=1 to 200 of a_n = 300/2 = 150
