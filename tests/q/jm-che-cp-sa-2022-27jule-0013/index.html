Major product 'B' of the following reaction sequence is: CH3-C=CH-CH3 with CH3 substituent, treated with Br2/CH3OH to form A, then HI to form B (major product)

  1. (1) CH3-C-CH-CH3 with OH and Br substituents and CH3
  2. (2) CH3-C-CH-CH3 with I and Br substituents and CH3
  3. (3) CH3-C-CH-CH3 with Br and OH substituents and CH3
  4. (4) CH3-C-CH-CH3 with Br and I substituents and CH3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The starting material is 2-methylbut-2-ene. When treated with Br2 in CH3OH, addition occurs across the double bond following Markovnikov's rule, and the OH from methanol also adds, giving intermediate A with both Br and OMe groups. Upon treatment with HI, the OH is replaced by I through SN1 mechanism, giving the major product B with Br and I substituents at the appropriate positions. The answer is structure (2).
