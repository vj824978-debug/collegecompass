Compound(s) that on hydrogenation produce(s) optically inactive compound(s) is (are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [Structure A: H3C-CH=CH-CH(H)(Br)-CH3 with H and Br in wedge/dash notation]
  2. (B) [Structure B: H2C=CH-CH(H)(Br)-CH2-CH3 with H and Br in wedge/dash notation]
  3. (C) [Structure C: H2C=C(CH3)-CH(H)(Br)-CH3 with H and Br in wedge/dash notation]
  4. (D) [Structure D: H3C-CH=CH-CH(Br)(H)-CH2-CH3 with Br and H in wedge/dash notation]

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

(1) H₃C-CH=CH-C(CH₃)(H)(Br) + H₂/Pt → H₃C-CH₂-CH₂-C(CH₃)(H)(Br), Optically active (2) H₂C=CH-C(H)(Br)-CH₂-CH₃ + H₂/Pt → H₃C-CH-C(H)(Br)-CH₂-CH₃, Optically inactive (3) H₂C=C(H₃C)-C(H)(Br)-CH₃ + H₂/Pt → H₃C-C(H₃C)-C(H)(Br)-CH₃, Optically active (4) H₂C=CH-C(H)(Br)-CH₂-CH₃ + H₂/Pt → H₃C-CH₂-C(H)(Br)-CH₂-CH₃, Optically inactive
