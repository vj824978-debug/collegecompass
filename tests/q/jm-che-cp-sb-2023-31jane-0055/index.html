The number of molecules which gives haloform test among the following molecules is ___.

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Haloform test is given by compounds containing CH3-CO- group (methyl ketones) or CH3-CH(OH)- group (secondary alcohols with methyl group adjacent to the hydroxyl group). Analyzing each molecule: 1. First molecule (top left): Benzene ring with CH3 and CHO groups - This is benzaldehyde derivative. Does NOT give haloform test. 2. Second molecule (top middle): CH3-CH2-CO-CH2-CO-OEt (ethyl ester with ketone groups) - Contains CH3 adjacent to CO, so gives haloform test. 3. Third molecule (top right): Benzene ring with COCH3 group (acetophenone) - Contains CH3-CO- group directly attached to benzene, gives haloform test. 4. Fourth molecule (middle left): CH3-CH2-CH(OH)-CH2-CH3 - Secondary alcohol but CH3 is NOT adjacent to OH group, does NOT give haloform test. 5. Fifth molecule (middle right): CH3-CH(OH)-CH2-CH3 - Secondary alcohol with CH3 adjacent to OH, gives haloform test. 6. Sixth molecule (top right corner): Cyclohexanone derivative with gem-dimethyl groups - Does NOT have CH3-CO- or CH3-CH(OH)- structure in the required form. Molecules giving positive haloform test: - Ethyl ester with ketone (second molecule) - Acetophenone (third molecule) - Secondary alcohol with CH3-CH(OH) (fifth molecule) Total = 3 molecules
