Match the List-I with List-II. List-I contains: (a) a five-membered ring with oxygen, (b) a bicyclic structure with a three-membered ring fused to a five-membered ring with substituents, (c) a spiro compound structure with two rings, (d) a six-membered ring with oxygen. List-II contains: (i) Spiro compound, (ii) Aromatic compound, (iii) Non-planar Heterocyclic compound, (iv) Bicyclo compound

  1. (1) (a) - (II), (b) - (I), (c) - (IV), (d) - (III)
  2. (2) (a) - (IV), (b) - (III), (c) - (I), (d) - (II)
  3. (3) (a) - (III), (b) - (IV), (c) - (I), (d) - (II)
  4. (4) (a) - (IV), (b) - (III), (c) - (II), (d) - (I)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Analyze each structure: (a) Five-membered ring with oxygen = Non-planar Heterocyclic compound (iii). (b) Bicyclic fused ring system = Bicyclo compound (iv). (c) Spiro compound with two rings sharing one atom = Spiro compound (i). (d) Six-membered ring with oxygen = Aromatic compound (ii). Therefore: (a)-(iii), (b)-(iv), (c)-(i), (d)-(ii) matches option C.
