Among CrO, Cr2O3 and CrO3, the sum of spin-only magnetic moment values of basic and amphoteric oxides is ___ × 10^-2 BM (nearest integer). (Given atomic number of Cr is 24)

Answer

Correct answer: 877

Solution

CrO is basic oxide where Cr exists as Cr^+2 with spin-only magnetic moment μ = 4.90 BM. Cr2O3 is amphoteric oxide where Cr exists as Cr^+3 with spin-only magnetic moment μ = 3.87 BM. Sum of spin-only magnetic moments = 4.90 + 3.87 = 8.77 BM. Converting to the required form: μ_only = 8.77 × 10^-2 = 877 × 10^-4, which equals 877 when expressed as ___ × 10^-2.
