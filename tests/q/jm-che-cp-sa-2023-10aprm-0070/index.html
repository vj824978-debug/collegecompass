The one that does not stabilize 2° and 3° structures of proteins is

  1. (1) S-S-linkage
  2. (2) H-bonding
  3. (3) O-O-linkage
  4. (4) van der Waals forces

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

H-bonding, van der Waals forces, and S-S linkage are responsible for the formation and stabilization of secondary and tertiary structures of proteins. O-O linkage is not a recognized chemical bond that stabilizes protein structures.
