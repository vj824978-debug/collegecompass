One mole of an ideal monatomic gas undergoes two reversible processes (A → B and B → C) as shown in the given figure: [A P-V diagram showing temperature vs volume. Point A is at V₁, T₁ (600 K). Process A→B is a curved line (adiabatic) from A to B at V₂, T₂ (60 K). Process B→C is a horizontal line from B to C at V₃, T₂ (60 K). Volume axis shows 10 m³, temperature axis shows 60 K and 600 K.] A → B is an adiabatic process. If the total heat absorbed in the entire process (A → B and B → C) is RT, in 10, the value of 2 log V₃ is ____. [Use, molar heat capacity of the gas at constant pressure, C_{p,m} = (5/2)R]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 7

Solution

Sol. (7) A → B (reversible adiabatic), γ = 5/3 q_total = E_AB + q_BC = 0 + q_BC = q_BC q_BC = RT₂ ln10 For the process A to B T₁ × V₁^(γ-1) = T₂ × V₂^(γ-1) (V₂/V₁)^(γ-1) = T₁/T₂ = 600/60 = 10 (V₂/V₁)^(5/3 - 1) = 10 (V₂/V₁)^(2/3) = 10 V₂/V₁ = (10)^(3/2) V₂ = 10 × (10)^(3/2) = (10)^(5/2) V₂ = (10)^(5/2) -RT₂ ln10 = -RT₂ ln(V₃/V₂) 10 = V₃/V₂ V₃ = 10V₂ = 10 × (10)^(5/2) = (10)^(7/2) 2ln V₃ = 2 × ln 10^(7/2) 2log V₃ = 2 × (7/2) × 1 = 7
