Under hydrolytic conditions, the compounds used for preparation of linear polymer and for chain termination, respectively, are

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) CH₃SiCl₃ and Si(CH₃)₄
  2. (B) (CH₃)₂SiCl₂ and (CH₃)₃SiCl
  3. (C) (CH₃)₂SiCl₂ and CH₃SiCl₃
  4. (D) SiCl₄ and (CH₃)₃SiCl

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

The reaction sequence shows: 1) Starting material: Dichlorodimethylsilane (CH₃)₂SiCl₂ 2) First step (hydrolysis with H₂O): Conversion to silanediol HO-Si(CH₃)₂-OH 3) Second step (condensation): Formation of linear polysiloxane with Si-O-Si backbone and pendant CH₃ groups, with terminal hydroxyl groups: H-O-[Si(CH₃)₂-O]ₙ-H 4) Third step (end-capping with Me₃SiCl and H₂O): Reaction of terminal hydroxyl groups with chlorotrimethylsilane to form trimethylsilyl end caps, producing: Me₃Si-O-[Si(CH₃)₂-O]ₙ-SiMe₃ The final product is polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS) with trimethylsilyl (TMS) terminal groups, commonly used as a silicone fluid with improved properties.
