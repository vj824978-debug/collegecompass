The left and right compartments of a thermally isolated container of length L are separated by a thermally conducting, movable piston of area A. [Figure shows a container of length L divided by a piston at L/2, with spring coils drawn on left side] The left and right compartments are filled with 3/2 and 1 moles of an ideal gas, respectively. In the left compartment the piston is attached by a spring with spring constant k and natural length 2L/5. In thermodynamic equilibrium, the piston is at a distance L/2 from the left and right edges of the container as shown in the figure. Under the above conditions, if the pressure in the right compartment is P = (kL*alpha)/A, then the value of alpha is ____

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 0.20

Solution

In equilibrium P₂A + kx = P₁A kx = (P₁ - P₂)A = (3RT/2V₀ - RT/V₀)A = RT/2V₀ A ∴ P₂V₀ = 1(RT) P₂ = RT/V₀ = 2kx/A = 2k/A [0.5L - 0.4L] = kL/5A Comparing α = 1/5 = 0.2 [Diagram shows a horizontal spring-mass system with a spring of stiffness k and mass A on the left side, connected to a piston. The chamber on the left has volume L/2 and pressure P₁A. The right chamber has volume L/2 and pressure P₂A. Total length is L with markings showing the spring compression distance kx.]
