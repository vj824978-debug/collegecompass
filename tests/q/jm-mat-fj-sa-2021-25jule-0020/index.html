Consider the statement 'The match will be played only if the weather is good and ground is not wet'. Select the negation from the following:

  1. (1) The match will not be played or weather is good and ground is not wet.
  2. (2) The match will not be played or weather is not good and ground is wet.
  3. (3) If the match will not be played, then either weather is not good or ground is wet.
  4. (4) The match will be played and weather is not good or ground is wet.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let P: weather is good, q: ground is not wet. The statement is: match will be played only if (P AND q), which means (match played) → (P AND q). The negation of (P AND q) is (~P OR ~q) which equals 'weather is not good or ground is wet'. The negation of the entire statement gives: The match will be played and weather is not good or ground is wet.
