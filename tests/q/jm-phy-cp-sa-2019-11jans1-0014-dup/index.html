A undergoes 4KOH,O2 (Green) to give 2B + 2H2O. 3B undergoes 4HCl (Purple) to give 2C + MnO2 + 2H2O. 2C undergoes H2O,KJ to give 2A + 2KOH + D. In the above sequence of reactions, A and D, respectively, are:

  1. (1) KI and KMnO4
  2. (2) KI and K2MnO4
  3. (3) KIO3 and MnO2
  4. (4) MnO2 and KIO3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

MnO2 + 4KOH + O2 (Green) → 2K2MnO4 + 2H2O 3K2MnO4 + 4HCl (Purple) → 2KMnO4 + 2H2O 2KMnO4 + H2O,KJ → 2MnO4 + 2KOH + KIO3 A → MnO2 D → KIO3 The sequence shows: - A (MnO2) is oxidized by KOH and O2 to form green K2MnO4 (compound B) - B reacts with HCl to form purple KMnO4 (compound C) and MnO2 - C (KMnO4) undergoes disproportionation in H2O with KJ to form MnO4^2-, KOH, and KIO3 (compound D) Therefore, A = MnO2 and D = KIO3
