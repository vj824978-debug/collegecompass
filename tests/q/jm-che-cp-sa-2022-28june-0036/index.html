Match List-I with List-II: List-I (Oxide): (a) Cl2O7 (b) Na2O (c) Al2O3 (d) N2O; List-II (Nature): (I) Amphoteric (II) Basic (III) Neutral (IV) Acidic. Choose the correct answer from the options given below: (1) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II (2) A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III (3) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I (4) A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV

  1. (1) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
  2. (2) A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
  3. (3) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I
  4. (4) A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(a) Cl2O7 → Acidic (non-metal oxide); (b) Na2O → Basic (metal oxide); (c) Al2O3 → Amphoteric (amphoteric oxide); (d) N2O → Neutral oxide. Oxides of metals are basic in nature whereas oxides of non-metals are acidic in nature. N2O is a neutral oxide.
