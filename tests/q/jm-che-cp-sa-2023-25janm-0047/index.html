Match List-I with List-II List-I Cations: A. Pb^(2+), Cu^(2+) B. Al^(3+), Fe^(3+) C. Co^(2+), Ni^(2+) D. Ba^(2+), Ca^(2+) List-II Group reagents: I. H2S gas in presence of dilute HCl II. (NH4)2CO3 in presence of NH4OH III. NH4OH in presence of NH4Cl IV. H2S in presence of NH4OH

  1. (1) A → i; B → iii; C → iv; D → ii
  2. (2) A → i; B → iii; C → ii; D → iv
  3. (3) A → iv; B → ii; C → iii; D → i
  4. (4) A → iii; B → i; C → iv; D → ii

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

To match cations with their appropriate group reagents, we need to analyze which reagent gives characteristic precipitates with each group of cations: A. Pb^(2+), Cu^(2+): These cations form precipitates with H2S in presence of dilute HCl (acidic medium). - PbS (black precipitate) - CuS (black precipitate) Match: A → I B. Al^(3+), Fe^(3+): These cations form hydroxide precipitates with NH4OH in presence of NH4Cl. - Al(OH)3 (white precipitate, amphoteric) - Fe(OH)3 (reddish-brown precipitate) Note: In presence of NH4Cl, the pH is controlled to prevent hydrolysis of other cations. Match: B → iii C. Co^(2+), Ni^(2+): These cations form colored hydroxide precipitates with H2S in presence of NH4OH (basic medium). - Co(OH)2 (pink/blue precipitate) - Ni(OH)2 (green precipitate) Match: C → iv D. Ba^(2+), Ca^(2+): These cations form carbonate precipitates with (NH4)2CO3 in presence of NH4OH. - BaCO3 (white precipitate) - CaCO3 (white precipitate) Match: D → ii Therefore: A → i; B → iii; C → iv; D → ii
