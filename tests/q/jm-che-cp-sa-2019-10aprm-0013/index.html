For the reaction of H2 with I2, the rate constant is 2.5 × 10^(-4) dm^3 mol^(-1) s^(-1) at 327°C and 1.0 dm^3 mol^(-1) s^(-1) at 527°C. The activation energy for the reaction, in kJ mol^(-1) is: (R = 8.314 JK^(-1) mol^(-1))

  1. (1) 59
  2. (2) 166
  3. (3) 72
  4. (4) 150

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using the Arrhenius equation in logarithmic form: log(k2/k1) = (Eq)/(2.303k) × (1/T1 - 1/T2) Given: k1 = 2.5 × 10^(-4) dm^3 mol^(-1) s^(-1) at T1 = 327°C = 600 K k2 = 1.0 dm^3 mol^(-1) s^(-1) at T2 = 527°C = 800 K R = 8.314 JK^(-1) mol^(-1) log(1.0/(2.5 × 10^(-4))) = (Eq)/(2.3 × 8.314) × (1/600 - 1/800) log(4 × 10^3) = (Eq)/(2.3 × 8.314) × (1/2400) 3.6 = (Eq)/(2.3 × 8.314) × (1/2400) 3.6 × 2.3 × 8.314 × 2400 / 1000 = Eq Eq = 164.93 ≈ 166 kJ/mol
