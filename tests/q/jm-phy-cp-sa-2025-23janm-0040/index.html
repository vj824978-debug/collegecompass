Refer to the circuit diagram given in the figure, which of the following observation are correct? A. Total resistance of circuit is 6Ω. B. Current in Ammeter is 1A C. Potential across AB is 4 Volts. D. Potential across CD is 4 Volts. E. Total resistance of circuit is 8Ω.

  1. (1) A, B and D only
  2. (2) A, C and D only
  3. (3) B, C and E only
  4. (4) A, B and C only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From the circuit diagram: - Battery voltage = 6V - Resistances: 4Ω (top), 4Ω (middle left), 4Ω (right), with a diode at D Simplifying the circuit: The 4Ω resistors on the right and middle left are in parallel: R_parallel = (4 × 4)/(4 + 4) = 16/8 = 2Ω Total resistance in circuit: R_total = 4Ω (top) + 2Ω (parallel combination) = 6Ω Therefore, observation A is correct: Total resistance = 6Ω Current through ammeter: I = V/R_total = 6V/6Ω = 1A Therefore, observation B is correct: Current = 1A Potential across AB (right 4Ω resistor): V_AB = I × R_AB = 1A × 4Ω = 4 Volts Therefore, observation C is correct: Potential across AB = 4V For observation D: Potential across CD (middle left 4Ω resistor): V_CD = I × R_CD = 1A × 4Ω = 4 Volts However, due to the diode at D, the voltage calculation needs consideration of diode behavior. From the solution shown: V_AB = 0.5 × 4 = 2 volt and V_CD = 1 × 4 = 4 volt But the current distribution shows I_A = 0.5A and I_CD = 1A, which means the ammeter reads total current = 1A. Correct observations: A, B, and D
