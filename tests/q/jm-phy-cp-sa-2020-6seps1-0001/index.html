Four point masses, each of mass m, are fixed at the corners of a square of side l. The square is rotating with angular frequency ω, about an axis passing through one of the corners of the square and parallel to its diagonal, as shown in the figure. The angular momentum of the square about this axis is

  1. (1) 2ml^2ω
  2. (2) ml^2ω
  3. (3) 3ml^2ω
  4. (4) 4ml^2ω

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Setting up the geometry: The axis passes through one corner of the square and is parallel to the diagonal. From the figure, we identify the distances of each mass from this axis. The diagonal of the square has length l*sqrt(2). The axis is along the diagonal direction through one corner. Distance calculations from the axis: - Mass at the axis (corner): distance = 0 - Two masses adjacent to the corner: Each is at perpendicular distance l/sqrt(2) from the axis - Mass at the opposite corner: distance = sqrt(2)l from the axis (along the diagonal direction), but perpendicular distance = l*sqrt(2) Wait, let me recalculate using the solution provided: From the solution: - One mass at the origin (on the axis): distance r₁ = 0 - Two adjacent masses: perpendicular distance r₂ = l/sqrt(2) each - Opposite corner mass: perpendicular distance r₃ = sqrt(2)l Moment of inertia calculation: I = m(0)^2 + m(l/sqrt(2))^2 × 2 + m(sqrt(2)l)^2 I = 0 + m(l^2/2) × 2 + m(2l^2) I = ml^2 + 2ml^2 = 3ml^2 Angular momentum: L = Iω = 3ml^2ω
