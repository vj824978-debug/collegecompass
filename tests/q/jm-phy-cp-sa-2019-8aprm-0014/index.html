A convex lens (of focal length 20 cm) and a concave mirror, having their principal axes along the same lines, are kept 80 cm apart from each other. The concave mirror is to the right of the convex lens. When an object is kept at a distance of 30 cm to the left of the convex lens, its image remains at the same position even if the concave mirror is removed. The maximum distance of the object for which this concave mirror, by itself would produce a virtual image would be -

  1. (1) 20 cm
  2. (2) 10 cm
  3. (3) 25 cm
  4. (4) 30 cm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: - Focal length of convex lens: f_lens = 20 cm - Distance between lens and mirror: 80 cm - Object distance from lens: u = 30 cm Step 1: Find image formed by lens using lens formula 1/f = 1/v - 1/u 1/20 = 1/v - 1/(-30) 1/20 = 1/v + 1/30 1/v = 1/20 - 1/30 = (3-2)/60 = 1/60 v = 60 cm Step 2: Interpret the condition The image remains at the same position even if mirror is removed. This means the image formed by the lens acts as the object for the mirror, and the mirror forms its image at the same location as the lens forms it. Step 3: Find focal length of mirror The image by lens is at 60 cm from lens (to the right) Distance of this image from mirror = 80 - 60 = 20 cm For the final image to coincide with the original image formed by lens (60 cm from lens, or 20 cm from mirror on the right side), the mirror must form an image at 20 cm on its right side. Using mirror formula: 1/f_m = 1/v_m + 1/u_m Here u_m = 20 cm (object distance from mirror) v_m = -20 cm (image distance, on the same side, virtual) 1/f_m = 1/(-20) + 1/20 = 0 This is not correct. Let me reconsider. Step 4: Correct interpretation For the image to remain at same position, the rays after reflection from mirror should retrace back and form image at 60 cm from lens. This occurs when object for mirror is at center of curvature or at a specific position. R = 2f_m = 20 cm f_m = 10 cm Step 5: Maximum distance for virtual image by mirror A concave mirror produces virtual image when object is between pole and focus. Maximum distance for virtual image = focal length = f_m = 10 cm
