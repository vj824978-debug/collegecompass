Identify products A and B. Starting material is cyclopentene with CH3 group. First step: dil. KMnO4 at 273 K gives product A. Second step: CrO3 oxidation of A gives product B.

  1. (1) A: cyclopentanol with CH3 and two OH groups; B: cyclopentanone with CH3 and one OH group with ketone
  2. (2) A: cyclopentanol with CH3 and two OH groups; B: cyclopentene with CH3 and ketone group
  3. (3) A: aldehyde with CH2CH2CH2-C(=O)-CH3; B: carboxylic acid with HOOC-CH2CH2CH2-C(=O)-CH3
  4. (4) A: cyclopentanol with CH3 and one OH group; B: cyclopentanone with CH3 and ketone group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The starting material is cyclopentene with a methyl substituent. Dilute KMnO4 at low temperature (273 K) performs hydroxylation of the alkene, adding two OH groups in a syn manner to give diol A (cyclopentyl diol with CH3). CrO3 oxidation of the secondary alcohol(s) in A produces ketone(s), giving product B (cyclopentanone with CH3 and ketone functional group). The bottom section shows the mechanism: dil. KMnO4 hydroxylates the double bond to form a diol intermediate with OH groups on adjacent carbons, then CrO3 oxidizes one of the alcohols to a ketone.
