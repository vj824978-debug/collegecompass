The unsaturated ether on acidic hydrolysis produces carbonyl compounds as shown below:- CH₃ - CH = CH - O - CH = CH₂. Upon hydrolysis with H₂O(+), this produces CH₃ - CH₂ - CH = O and O = CH - CH₃. Based on this, predict the solution / reagent that will help to distinguish "P" and "Q" obtained in the following reaction. CH₃ - CH = CH - O - C(CH₃) = CH₂ with H₂O(+) and heat gives P + Q. (1) Lucas reagent (2) 2,4-DNP reagent (3) Saturated NaHSO₃ solution (4) Fehling solution

  1. (1) Lucas reagent
  2. (2) 2,4-DNP reagent
  3. (3) Saturated NaHSO₃ solution
  4. (4) Fehling solution

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From the given example, the unsaturated ether CH₃ - CH = CH - O - CH = CH₂ undergoes acidic hydrolysis to produce two carbonyl compounds: CH₃ - CH₂ - CH = O (aldehyde) and O = CH - CH₃ (ketone). Similarly, for the compound CH₃ - CH = CH - O - C(CH₃) = CH₂, acidic hydrolysis will break the ether linkage and produce: P: CH₃ - CH₂ - CH = O (aldehyde, propanal) Q: A ketone with structure (CH₃)₂C=O (acetone or 2-propanone) P is an aldehyde and Q is a ketone. To distinguish between an aldehyde (P) and a ketone (Q): (1) Lucas reagent: Used to distinguish alcohols, not aldehydes and ketones. (2) 2,4-DNP reagent: Forms colored derivatives with both aldehydes and ketones, so cannot distinguish between them. (3) Saturated NaHSO₃ solution: Forms bisulfite addition products with both aldehydes and ketones, so cannot distinguish between them. (4) Fehling solution: This is the correct reagent. Fehling's test is specific for aldehydes. Aldehydes give a positive Fehling test (produces a brick-red precipitate of Cu₂O), while ketones give a negative Fehling test. P (aldehyde) gives positive Fehling test. Q (ketone) gives negative Fehling test. Therefore, Fehling solution can be used to distinguish P and Q.
