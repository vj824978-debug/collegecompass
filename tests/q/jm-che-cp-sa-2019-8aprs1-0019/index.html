An organic compound neither reacts with neutral ferric chloride solution nor with Fehling solution. It however, reacts with Grignard reagent and gives positive iodoform test. The compound is -

  1. (1) A benzene ring with OH group attached to one carbon, CH3 group to an adjacent carbon, and C2H5 group to another adjacent carbon, with O double bonded to a carbon
  2. (2) A benzene ring with O bonded to a carbon with CH3 group attached, and H bonded to an adjacent carbon, with O double bonded
  3. (3) A benzene ring with O double bonded to a carbon with C2H5 group and CH3 group attached, with O bonded to a carbon with CH3 group
  4. (4) A benzene ring with O double bonded to a carbon with CH3 group attached, and OH group bonded to an adjacent carbon

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

To identify the compound, we need to analyze the given reactions: 1. Does NOT react with neutral ferric chloride solution → No phenolic OH group (phenols give color with FeCl3) 2. Does NOT react with Fehling solution → No aldehyde group (aldehydes reduce Fehling's reagent) 3. Reacts with Grignard reagent → Presence of carbonyl group (C=O) or other reactive hydrogen groups 4. Positive iodoform test → Presence of CH3-CO- group (methyl ketone) or CH3-CH(OH)- group Analyzing the options: Option (1): Contains OH group and carbonyl group. The OH is secondary (on aromatic ring with CH3 and C2H5 groups), not phenolic. Has CH3-CO- group (methyl ketone). This fits all criteria. Option (2): Has an ether linkage and carbonyl. No phenolic OH, but structure doesn't clearly show CH3-CO group required for iodoform test. Option (3): Has ether groups and carbonyl with C2H5 substituent, not CH3-CO-, so may not give iodoform test as readily. Option (4): Has phenolic OH group (directly on benzene ring), which would react with neutral FeCl3, violating the first condition. Option (1) correctly shows: - Secondary alcohol (not phenolic) - does not react with FeCl3 - Methyl ketone (CH3-CO-) - reacts with Grignard and gives iodoform test - No aldehyde group - does not react with Fehling's reagent
