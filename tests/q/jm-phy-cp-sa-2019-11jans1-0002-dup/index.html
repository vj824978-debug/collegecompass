The correct option with respect to the Pauling electronegativity values of the element is:

  1. (1) Ga < Ge
  2. (2) Si < Al
  3. (3) Te > Se
  4. (4) P > S

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Theoretical question Along the period, electronegativity increases. Let us analyze each option: (1) Ga < Ge: Ga and Ge are in the same period (Period 4). Ge comes after Ga in the period, so Ge has higher electronegativity than Ga. Therefore, Ga < Ge is CORRECT. (2) Si < Al: Si and Al are in the same period (Period 3). Al comes before Si in the period, so Al has lower electronegativity than Si. Therefore, Si < Al is INCORRECT. (3) Te > Se: Te and Se are in the same group (Group 16). Down the group, electronegativity decreases. Se is above Te, so Se has higher electronegativity than Te. Therefore, Te > Se is INCORRECT. (4) P > S: P and S are in the same period (Period 3). S comes after P in the period, so S has higher electronegativity than P. Therefore, P > S is INCORRECT. The correct answer is option (1): Ga < Ge
