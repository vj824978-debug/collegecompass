For the reaction, 2A + B → products, when the concentrations of A and B both were doubled, the rate of the reaction increased from 0.3 mol L^(-1) s^(-1) to 2.4 mol L^(-1) s^(-1). When the concentration of A alone is doubled, the rate increased from 0.3 mol L^(-1) s^(-1) to 0.6 mol L^(-1) s^(-1). Which one of the following statements is correct?

  1. (1) Order of the reaction with respect to A is 2.
  2. (2) Total order of the reaction is 4.
  3. (3) Order of the reaction with respect to B is 1.
  4. (4) Order of the reaction with respect to B is 2.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

r = k [A]^x [B]^y When concentrations of both A and B are doubled: 8 ∝ (2)^x (2)^y ....(1) When concentration of A alone is doubled: 2 ∝ (2)^x ....(2) From equation (2): 2 = 2^x ∴ x = 1 Substituting x = 1 in equation (1): 8 = (2)^1 (2)^y 8 = 2(2)^y 4 = 2^y 2^2 = 2^y ∴ y = 2 ∴ order w.r.t. A is 1 ∴ order w.r.t. B is 2 ∴ total order of reaction = 1 + 2 = 3
