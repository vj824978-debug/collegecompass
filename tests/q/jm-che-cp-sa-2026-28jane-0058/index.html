Which of the following reaction is NOT correctly represented?

  1. (1) Cyclohexene with CH3 substituent + Br2, hv → Cyclohexene with CH3 and CHBr substituents (Major Product)
  2. (2) Benzene with N⊕Cl⊖ + CuBr/HBr → Benzene with Br substituent
  3. (3) Benzene with CH3 + Br2, hv → Benzene with CH2Br substituent
  4. (4) Benzene with CH3 + Br2, Fe, dark → Ortho and para Bromotoluenes

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Analysis of each reaction: (1) Cyclohexene with CH3 + Br2, hv → Major product shown as CHBr on the ring This reaction shows bromination at the allylic position (adjacent to double bond). Under UV light (hv), free radical bromination occurs. The allylic C-H is more reactive than the double bond C-H in free radical bromination. The major product should be allylic bromination (CH2Br), not CHBr as shown. This representation is INCORRECT. (2) Benzene with diazonium salt (N⊕Cl⊖) + CuBr/HBr → Benzene with Br This is the Sandmeyer reaction - correct representation. The diazonium salt is replaced by Br using CuBr/HBr. CORRECT. (3) Benzene with CH3 + Br2, hv → Benzene with CH2Br This is free radical bromination of the methyl group on benzene under UV light. The C-H bonds of CH3 are attacked, giving CH2Br as the major product. This is CORRECT. (4) Benzene with CH3 + Br2, Fe, dark → ortho and para Bromotoluenes This is electrophilic aromatic substitution. With Fe as Lewis acid catalyst and Br2, bromination occurs on the benzene ring. The methyl group is activating and ortho/para directing, giving ortho and para isomers. This is CORRECT. Reaction (1) is NOT correctly represented because the major product should show allylic bromination (CH2Br) not CHBr.
