A wire of cross sectional area A, modulus of elasticity 2 × 10^11 N/m^2 and length 2 m is stretched between two vertical rigid supports. When a mass of 2 kg is suspended at the middle it sags lower from its original position making angle θ = 1/100 radian on the points of support. The value of A is _____ × 10^-4 m^2 (consider x << L). (given: g = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

From vertical equilibrium: 2T sin(θ) = 20, so T sin(θ) = 10. Using small angle approximation sin(θ) ≈ θ, we get T × (1/100) = 10, thus T = 1000 N. Change in length: ΔL = 2√(x^2 + L^2) - 2L ≈ 2L[√(1 + x^2/(2L^2)) - 1] ≈ x^2/L. Using modulus of elasticity E = stress/strain = (T/A)/(ΔL/(2L)). So 2 × 10^11 = (1000/(A)) × (2L)/(x^2/L) = (1000 × 2L^2)/(A × x^2). With L = 1 m and x^2/L from geometry: 2 × 10^11 = (10^3)/(A) × (2)/(x^2/L). Solving gives A = 1 × 10^-4 m^2
