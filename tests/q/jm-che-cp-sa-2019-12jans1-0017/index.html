An open vessel at 27°C is heated until two fifth of the air (assumed as an ideal gas) it has escaped from the vessel assuming that the volume of the vessel remains constant, the temperature at which the vessel has been heated is -

  1. (1) 750 K
  2. (2) 500 °C
  3. (3) 750 °C
  4. (4) 500 K

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given: Initial temperature T1 = 27°C = 300 K Volume is constant (open vessel) Two fifth of air escapes, meaning 3/5 of air remains Initially: n1 moles at T1 = 300 K Finally: n2 = (3/5)n1 moles at T2 = ? Using ideal gas law at constant volume: n1/T1 = n2/T2 Substituting n2 = (3/5)n1: n1/300 = [(3/5)n1]/T2 1/300 = (3/5)/T2 T2 = 300 × (5/3) T2 = 500 K
