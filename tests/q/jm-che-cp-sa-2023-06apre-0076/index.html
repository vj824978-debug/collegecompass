The strongest acid from the following is

  1. (1) 3-nitrophenol (OH and NO2 in meta positions)
  2. (2) 3-methylphenol (OH and CH3 in meta positions)
  3. (3) 3-chlorophenol (OH and Cl in meta positions)
  4. (4) Phenol (only OH group)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Acidic strength of phenols depends on the stability of the phenoxide ion (conjugate base). Electron-withdrawing groups (EWG) stabilize the negative charge on oxygen, making the phenol more acidic. The order of acidity is: NO2 (strong EWG) > Cl (moderate EWG) > H > CH3 (electron-donating group). Since NO2 is the strongest electron-withdrawing group among the options, 3-nitrophenol is the strongest acid. The solution shows the order: 3-nitrophenol > 3-chlorophenol > phenol > 3-methylphenol
