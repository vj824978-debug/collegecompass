ABC is a triangular park with AB = AC = 100 metres. A vertical tower is situated at the mid-point of BC. If the angles of elevation of the top of the tower at A and B are cot^(-1)(3*sqrt(2)) and cosec^(-1)(2*sqrt(2)) respectively, then the height of the tower (in metres) is:

  1. (1) 100/(3*sqrt(3))
  2. (2) 25
  3. (3) 20
  4. (4) 10*sqrt(5)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

cosec(beta) = 2*sqrt(2) So sin(beta) = 1/(2*sqrt(2)) = sqrt(2)/4 cos(beta) = sqrt(1 - sin^2(beta)) = sqrt(1 - 1/8) = sqrt(7/8) = sqrt(7)/(2*sqrt(2)) tan(beta) = sin(beta)/cos(beta) = (sqrt(2)/4)/(sqrt(7)/(2*sqrt(2))) = (sqrt(2)/4) * (2*sqrt(2)/sqrt(7)) = 1/sqrt(7) cot(alpha) = 3*sqrt(2) So tan(alpha) = 1/(3*sqrt(2)) = sqrt(2)/6 Let P be the mid-point of BC, and Q be the top of the tower. Let h be the height of the tower. Let AP = x (distance from A to P along the ground) From the angle of elevation at A: tan(alpha) = h/x h/x = sqrt(2)/6 So x = h*6/sqrt(2) = 3*sqrt(2)*h ... (i) From the angle of elevation at B: tan(beta) = h/BP h/BP = 1/sqrt(7) So BP = h*sqrt(7) ... (ii) Since P is the mid-point of BC, we have BP = PC = BC/2. In triangle ABC, AB = AC = 100 (isosceles triangle) Since P is the mid-point of BC and the triangle is isosceles, AP is perpendicular to BC. So AP^2 + BP^2 = AB^2 x^2 + (BP)^2 = 100^2 Substituting from (i) and (ii): (3*sqrt(2)*h)^2 + (h*sqrt(7))^2 = 10000 18*h^2 + 7*h^2 = 10000 25*h^2 = 10000 h^2 = 400 h = 20 metres
