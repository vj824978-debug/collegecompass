Given below are the four isomeric compounds (P, Q, R, S). P is a secondary alcohol with a phenyl group and a tert-butyl group attached to the same carbon. Q is a three-carbon chain with a benzene ring at one end and an aldehyde group at the other end. R is a benzene ring with a two-carbon chain where the carbons form a ketone (alpha-dicarbonyl). S is a benzene ring with an ethyl ketone group. Identify correct statements from below. A. Q, R and S will give precipitate with 2, 4 - DNP. B. P and Q will give positive Bayer's test. C. Q and R will give sooty flame. D. R and S will give yellow precipitate with I2 / NaOH. E. Q alone will deposit silver with Tollen's reagent. Choose the correct option.

  1. (1) A, C and E only
  2. (2) A and E only
  3. (3) C and E only
  4. (4) A, B, D and E only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Let us analyze each compound: (P) Secondary alcohol with phenyl and tert-butyl groups - contains -OH group, no carbonyl (Q) Phenyl-CH2-CH2-CHO - contains aldehyde group (-CHO) (R) Phenyl-CH2-CO-CH3 - contains ketone group (-CO-) (S) Phenyl-CO-CH2-CH3 - contains ketone group (-CO-) Now checking each statement: A. Q, R and S will give precipitate with 2, 4 - DNP: - Q has aldehyde group → gives DNP precipitate ✓ - R has ketone group → gives DNP precipitate ✓ - S has ketone group → gives DNP precipitate ✓ Statement A is CORRECT B. P and Q will give positive Bayer's test: - Bayer's test is for C=C double bonds - P has secondary alcohol (no C=C) → negative - Q has only aromatic ring and aldehyde (no C=C) → negative Statement B is INCORRECT C. Q and R will give sooty flame: - Sooty flame indicates high carbon:hydrogen ratio (aromatic compounds burn with sooty flame) - Q has phenyl group → sooty flame ✓ - R has phenyl group → sooty flame ✓ Statement C is CORRECT D. R and S will give yellow precipitate with I2 / NaOH: - This is the iodoform test for methyl ketones (CH3-CO-R) - R: Phenyl-CH2-CO-CH3 has methyl ketone → gives iodoform (yellow) ✓ - S: Phenyl-CO-CH2-CH3 does NOT have methyl ketone at the correct position → negative Statement D is INCORRECT E. Q alone will deposit silver with Tollen's reagent: - Tollen's reagent oxidizes aldehydes to carboxylic acids, depositing silver - Q has -CHO group → gives positive Tollen's test ✓ - P has secondary alcohol → negative - R has ketone → negative - S has ketone → negative Statement E is CORRECT Correct statements: A, C, and E only
