A temperature difference can generate e.m.f. in some materials. Let S be the e.m.f. produced per unit temperature difference between the ends of a wire, σ the electrical conductivity and κ the thermal conductivity of the material of the wire. Taking M, L, T, I and K as dimensions of mass, length, time, current and temperature, respectively, the dimensional formula of the quantity Z = s²σ/κ is:

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [M⁰L⁰T⁰I⁰K⁰]
  2. (B) [M⁰L⁰T⁰K⁻¹]
  3. (C) [ML²T⁻³I⁻¹K⁻¹]
  4. (D) [M L² T⁻⁴ I⁻¹ K⁻¹]

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol. S = emf/T R = (1/σ) × (L/A) σ = (L·I)/(A·emf) J/sec = K·A·(ΔT/L) k = (J·L)/(A·ΔT·sec) z = (s²σ)/k = ((emf)²/T²) × (L/(A·emf)) × (A·(ΔT)·sec)/(J·L) = emf · (Isec)/(T·J) = (J/Isec) × (I·sec)/(T·J) Dimensional formula of Z = [M⁰L⁰T⁰I⁰K⁻¹] where: T → Temperature A → Area L → Length J → Joule I → Current
