Observe structures of the following compounds. The total number of structures/compounds which possess asymmetric carbon atoms is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

An asymmetric carbon (chiral center) is a carbon atom bonded to four different groups. Analyzing each structure: 1) Chloroalkene with NH2 and COOH groups - has one asymmetric carbon (the carbon bearing NH2 and COOH). 2) Compound with OH and NH2 groups on adjacent carbons - has asymmetric carbons. 3) Styrene (benzene with vinyl group) - no asymmetric carbons (sp2 carbons in aromatic ring and double bond). 4) Cyclohexane derivative with OH and isopropyl groups - has asymmetric carbons. Among the solution structures shown: compound 1 (isobutyl structure) has no asymmetric carbons. The cyclohexane with OH and isopropyl has 2 asymmetric carbons. The amino acid structure has 1 asymmetric carbon. Total compounds with at least one asymmetric carbon = 3.
