A uniform conducting wire of length is 24a, and resistance R is wound up as a current carrying coil in the shape of an equilateral triangle of side 'a' and then in the form of a square of side 'a'. The coil is connected to a voltage source Vs. The ratio of magnetic moment of the coils in case of equilateral triangle to that for square is 1:√y where y is......

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

For equilateral triangle: Length = 24a, so perimeter = 24a, side a. Number of turns Nt = 240/(3a) = 8. Area At = sqrt(3)/4 * a^2. Magnetic moment Mt = Nt * I * At = 8 * sqrt(3)/4 * a^2. For square: Length = 24a, so perimeter = 24a, side a. Number of turns Ns = 24a/(4a) = 6. Area As = a^2. Magnetic moment Ms = Ns * I * As = 6 * a^2. Ratio Mt/Ms = (8 * sqrt(3)/4 * a^2)/(6 * a^2) = (2*sqrt(3))/6 = sqrt(3)/3 = 1/sqrt(3). Therefore Mt/Ms = 1/sqrt(3) = 1/sqrt(y), so y = 3.
