Consider a large disk of radius R and two smaller disks, each of radius r = R/50, lying on its circumference, as shown in the figure. The smaller disks are initially in contact with each other, with an angular separation Δθ between their centers. They are made to roll without slipping in opposite directions, with constant angular velocities ω and 2ω while the large disk is held stationary. The time τ at which the smaller disks are again in contact is: [Use sin(Δθ) = Δθ and ignore gravity.]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) τ = 51 × (2π - 4/51)/ω
  2. (B) τ = 51 × (2π - 2/51)/3ω
  3. (C) τ = 51 × (2π - 4/51)/3ω
  4. (D) τ = 51 × (2π - 2/51)/ω

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

Solution: Let the radius of the large disk be R and the radius of the small disks be r. We are given r = R/50, which implies R = 50r. The centers of the small disks move along a circular path of radius R + r = 50r + r = 51r. When the two small disks are in contact, the distance between their centers is 2r. The angular separation Δθ between their centers is given by the arc length (which is approximately the chord length for small angles): (R + r)Δθ = 2r ⟹ Δθ = 2/(51) rad The small disks roll without slipping on the stationary large disk. For a disk of radius r rolling with angular velocity ω, the velocity of its center is v = ωr. The angular velocity of the center of the first small disk about the center of the large disk is: Ω₁ = v₁/(R + r) = ωr/(51r) = ω/51 Similarly, for the second small disk rolling with angular velocity 2ω, the angular velocity of its center is: Ω₂ = v₂/(R + r) = 2ωr/(51r) = 2ω/51 Since they move in opposite directions, their relative angular velocity is: Ω_rel = Ω₁ + Ω₂ = ω/51 + 2ω/51 = 3ω/51 The disks are initially in contact, meaning their centers are separated by an angle Δθ. They will be in contact again when their centers are once again separated by Δθ after crossing each other on the opposite side of the large disk. The total angular distance that the two centers must cover together is: θ_total = 2π - 2Δθ Substituting Δθ = 2/51: θ_total = 2π - 2(2/51) = 2π - 4/51 The time τ taken for them to meet again is: τ = θ_total/Ω_rel = (2π - 4/51)/(3ω/51) = (51/3ω)(2π - 4/51) = (51/3ω) × (2π - 4/51) Answer: τ = 51 × (2π - 4/51)/3ω
