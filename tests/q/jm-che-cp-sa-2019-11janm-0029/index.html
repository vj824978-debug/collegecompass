An organic compound is estimated through Duma's method and was found to evolve 6 moles of CO2, 4 moles of H2O and 1 mole of nitrogen gas. The formula of the compound is :

  1. (1) C6H8N
  2. (2) C6H8N2
  3. (3) C12H8N
  4. (4) C12H8N2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Organic compound ---[DUMA method]---> 6 mole CO2 (6C) + 4 mole of H2O (8H) + 1 mole of N2 (2N) From the combustion products: - From 6 moles of CO2: Number of Carbon atoms = 6 - From 4 moles of H2O: Number of Hydrogen atoms = 4 × 2 = 8 - From 1 mole of N2: Number of Nitrogen atoms = 1 × 2 = 2 Therefore, the empirical formula contains: - C: 6 atoms - H: 8 atoms - N: 2 atoms The molecular formula is C6H8N2 However, examining the options and the combustion data more carefully: If we have 6 moles CO2, this gives 6 carbons If we have 4 moles H2O, this gives 8 hydrogens If we have 1 mole N2, this gives 2 nitrogens But option (1) C6H8N has only 1 nitrogen atom, which would come from 0.5 moles of N2. Since the problem states 1 mole of N2 gas (which contains 2 nitrogen atoms), the formula should contain 2 nitrogen atoms. Re-examining: The answer given is [2], which corresponds to C6H8N2.
