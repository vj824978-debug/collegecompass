The results given in the below table were obtained during kinetic studies of the following reaction: 2A + B → C + D. Find X and Y in the given table.

  1. (1) 0.3, 0.4
  2. (2) 0.4, 0.3
  3. (3) 0.4, 0.4
  4. (4) 0.3, 0.3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From rate law: r = -(1/2) d[A]/dt = -d[B]/dt = K[A]^x[B]^y For Experiment I: 6 × 10^(-3) = K(0.1)^x(0.1)^y ...(1) For Experiment II: 2.4 × 10^(-2) = K(0.1)^x(0.2)^y ...(2) For Experiment III: 1.2 × 10^(-2) = K(0.2)^x(0.1)^y ...(3) Dividing (3) by (1): (1.2 × 10^(-2))/(6 × 10^(-3)) = [(0.2)/(0.1)]^x 2 = 2^x x = 1 Dividing (2) by (3): (2.4 × 10^(-2))/(1.2 × 10^(-2)) = [(0.2)/(0.1)]^y 2 = 2^y y = 1 So, order with respect to A = 1 Order with respect to B = 1 For Experiment IV, dividing (4) by (3): (X/0.2) × (0.2/0.1)^2 × (7.2 × 10^(-2))/(1.2 × 10^(-2)) X/(0.2) × (0.2)^2/(0.1)^2 = (7.2 × 10^(-2))/(1.2 × 10^(-2)) X = (6 × 0.2)/4 X = 0.3 M For Experiment V, dividing (5) by (4): (Y/0.2)^2 = (2.88 × 10^(-1))/(7.2 × 10^(-2)) y^2 = 4 × 0.2^2 y = 0.4 M
