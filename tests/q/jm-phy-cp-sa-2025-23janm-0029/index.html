A sub-atomic particle of mass 10^(-30) kg is moving with a velocity 2.21 × 10^6 m/s. Under the matter wave consideration, the particle will behave closely like ______. (h = 6.63 × 10^(-34) J.s)

  1. (1) Infra-red radiation
  2. (2) X-rays
  3. (3) Gamma rays
  4. (4) Visible radiation

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

λ = h/p = h/(m × v) = (6.63 × 10^(-34))/(10^(-30) × 2.21 × 10^6) = (6.63 × 10^(-34))/(2.21 × 10^(-24)) = 3 × 10^(-10) m Hence particle will behave as x-ray. The de Broglie wavelength of the particle is 3 × 10^(-10) m, which falls in the X-ray region of the electromagnetic spectrum (approximately 0.01 to 10 nm or 10^(-11) to 10^(-9) m). Therefore, the particle will behave like X-rays.
