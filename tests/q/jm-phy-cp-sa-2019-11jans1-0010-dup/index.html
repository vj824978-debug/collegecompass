Match the following items in column I with the corresponding items in column II. Column I: (i) Na₂CO₃.10H₂O (ii) Mg(HCO₃)₂ (iii) NaOH (iv) Ca₃Al₂O₆. Column II: (A) Portland cement ingredient (B) Castner-Kellner process (C) Solvay process (D) Temporary hardness

  1. (1) (1) (i)→(D); (ii)→(A); (iii)→(B); (iv)→(C)
  2. (2) (2) (i)→(B); (ii)→(C); (iii)→(A); (iv)→(D)
  3. (3) (3) (i)→(C); (ii)→(B); (iii)→(D); (iv)→(A)
  4. (4) (4) (i)→(C); (ii)→(D); (iii)→(B); (iv)→(A)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Na₂CO₃.10H₂O → Solvay process Mg(HCO₃)₂ → Temporary hardness NaOH → Castner-Kellner process Ca₃Al₂O₆ → Portland cement ingredient Therefore, the correct matching is: (i)→(C); (ii)→(D); (iii)→(B); (iv)→(A) Explanation: - Na₂CO₃.10H₂O (washing soda) is the final product of the Solvay process - Mg(HCO₃)₂ causes temporary hardness in water due to presence of bicarbonate ions - NaOH (sodium hydroxide) is produced in the Castner-Kellner process (chlor-alkali process) - Ca₃Al₂O₆ (tricalcium aluminate) is one of the important ingredients in Portland cement
