At temperature T, compound AB_2(g) dissociates as AB_2(g) ⇌ AB(g) + (1/2)B_2(g) having degree of dissociation x (small compared to unity). The correct expression for x in terms of K_p and p is

  1. (1) 3*sqrt(2*K_p/p)
  2. (2) 4*sqrt(2*K_p/p)
  3. (3) 3*sqrt(2*K_p^2/p)
  4. (4) sqrt(K_p)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

AB_2(g) ⇌ AB(g) + (1/2)B_2(g) K_eq = [(1-x)*p] / [x*p * ((x/2)*p)^(1/2)] / [x*p / (1 + x/2) * (1 + x/2) * (1 + x/2)] Since x << 1 ⇒ 1 + x/2 ≈ 1 and 1 - x ≈ 1 ⇒ K_p = (xp) * [(xp/2)^(1/2)] / p ⇒ K_p^2 = x^2 * (xp/2) ⇒ x = 3*sqrt(2*K_p^2/p)
