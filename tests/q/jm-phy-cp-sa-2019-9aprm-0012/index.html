The resistance of a galvanometer is 50 ohm and the maximum current which can be passed through it is 0.002 A. What resistance must be connected to it in order to convert it into an ammeter of range 0-0.5 A ?

  1. (1) 0.2 ohm
  2. (2) 0.002 ohm
  3. (3) 0.02 ohm
  4. (4) 0.5 ohm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

To convert a galvanometer into an ammeter, a shunt resistance S must be connected in parallel with the galvanometer. Given: G = 50 ohm (galvanometer resistance) I_g = 0.002 A (maximum current through galvanometer) I = 0.5 A (desired ammeter range) When total current I flows through the ammeter: - Current through galvanometer: I_g = 0.002 A - Current through shunt: I_s = I - I_g = 0.5 - 0.002 = 0.498 A Since galvanometer and shunt are in parallel, voltage across both is same: V_g = V_s I_g × G = I_s × S 0.002 × 50 = 0.498 × S 0.1 = 0.498 × S S = 0.1/0.498 = 0.2 ohm
