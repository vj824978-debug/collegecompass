A long cylindrical vessel is half filled with a liquid. When the vessel is rotated about its own vertical axis, the liquid rises up near the wall. If the radius of vessel is 5 cm and its rotational speed is 2 rotations per second, then the difference in the heights between the centre and the sides, in cm, will be :

  1. (1) 2.0
  2. (2) 1.2
  3. (3) 0.1
  4. (4) 0.4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

When a cylindrical vessel is rotated about its vertical axis, the liquid surface forms a paraboloid due to the centrifugal force. Consider the force balance on a liquid element at distance x from the axis: - The net pressure difference causes the liquid to rise - From force balance: dmxω^2 = dpA - Where dm = ρA dx (mass element) This gives: xρAω^2 dx = dpA Integrating: ∫[p₀ to p] dp = ρω^2 ∫[0 to x] x dx p - p₀ = ρω^2 x^2/2 Since p = p₀ + ρgh (pressure at height h): p₀ + ρgh = p₀ + ρω^2 x^2/2 Therefore: h = ω^2 x^2/(2g) At the wall, x = R (radius): hg = ω^2 R^2/(2g) Given: - R = 5 cm = 5 × 10^(-2) m - Rotational speed = 2 rotations per second - ω = 2π × 2 = 4π rad/s - g = 10 m/s^2 h = ω^2 R^2/(2g) = (2.2π)^2 × (5 × 10^(-2))^2 / (2 × 10) h = (4π)^2 × (5 × 10^(-2))^2 / (2 × 10) h = 16π^2 × 25 × 10^(-4) / (2 × 10) h = 16 × π^2 × 25 × 10^(-4) / (2 × 10) h = 16 × 9.87 × 25 × 10^(-4) / 20 h = 4000 × 10^(-4) / 2 h = 200 × 10^(-4) h = 2 cm
