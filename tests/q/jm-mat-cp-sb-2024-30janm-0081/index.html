A group of 40 students appeared in an examination of 3 subjects – Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. It was found that all students passed in at least one of the subjects, 20 students passed in Mathematics, 25 students passed in Physics, 16 students passed in Chemistry, almost 11 students passed in both Mathematics and Physics, almost 15 students passed in both Physics and Chemistry, almost 15 students passed in both Mathematics and Chemistry. The maximum number of students passed in all three subjects is

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Let x = n(M ∩ P ∩ C). From the given constraints: n(M ∩ P) ≤ 11, n(P ∩ C) ≤ 15, n(M ∩ C) ≤ 15. Using inclusion-exclusion principle: n(M ∪ P ∪ C) = n(M) + n(P) + n(C) - n(M ∩ P) - n(P ∩ C) - n(M ∩ C) + n(M ∩ P ∩ C). Since n(M ∪ P ∪ C) = 40: 40 = 20 + 25 + 16 - 11 - 15 - 15 + x, which gives 40 = 20 + x, so x = 20. However, checking the constraint 11 - x ≥ 0, we get x ≤ 11. The maximum value satisfying all constraints is x = 10.
