Let f'(x) = 192x/(2 + sin^3(πx)) for all x ∈ ℝ with f(1/2) = 0. If m ≤ integral from 1/2 to 1 of f(x)dx ≤ M, then the possible values of m and M are

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) m = 13, M = 24
  2. (B) m = 1/4, M = 1/2
  3. (C) m = -11, M = 0
  4. (D) m = 1, M = 12

Answer

Correct answer: D
