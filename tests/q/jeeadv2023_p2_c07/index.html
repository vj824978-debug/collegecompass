In the following reactions, P, Q, R, and S are the major products. [Four reaction schemes shown: 1) H3CH2C-phenyl-C(CH3)3 undergoes (i) KMnO4, KOH, Δ (ii) H3O⊕ to give P 2) MeOOC-phenyl-COCl with COCl group undergoes (i) NaOH, H2O (ii) H3O⊕ to give Q 3) O=C-phenyl-CH2-COOMe with CN group undergoes (i) H3O⊕, Δ (ii) H2CrO4 to give R 4) Br-phenyl-O-dioxolane ring undergoes (i) Mg, dry ether (ii) CO2, then H2O⊕ (iii) Ammonioacal AgNO3, H2O⊕ to give S] The correct statement(s) about P, Q, R, and S is(are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) P and Q are monomers of polymers dacron and glyptal, respectively.
  2. (B) P, Q, and R are dicarboxylic acids.
  3. (C) Compounds Q and R are the same.
  4. (D) R does not undergo aldol condensation and S does not undergo Cannizzaro reaction.

Answer

Correct answer: CD

Solution

The image shows a solution key for organic chemistry synthesis problems. Problem (P): Starting material with ethyl group and tert-butyl group on benzene ring is converted to carboxylic acid using (i) KMnO₄, KOH.A (ii) H⁺ oxidation of both alkyl groups. Problem (Q): Starting material with methyl ester and acid chloride groups on benzene ring is converted to dicarboxylic acid using (i) NaOH, H₂O (ii) H⁺ hydrolysis of both carbonyl groups. Problem for (R): Starting material with cyano group and malonic acid derivative on benzene ring undergoes (i) H⁺, Δ treatment. The malonic acid decarboxylates losing CO₂ to form ketone intermediate, which is then reduced with H₂CO₃ or similar to give isophthalic acid (R) with two carboxylic acids in meta positions. Problem for (S): Starting material is bromobenzene with dioxolane protecting group. This converts to Grignard reagent MgBr in dry ether, which then reacts with O=C=O (carbon dioxide) and H⁺/H₂O workup to form benzoic acid. The dioxolane protected compound then undergoes deprotection with ammonia AgNO₃/H⁺ to reveal hydroxyl group and form compound (S) with both carboxylic acid and hydroxyl groups on benzene ring.
