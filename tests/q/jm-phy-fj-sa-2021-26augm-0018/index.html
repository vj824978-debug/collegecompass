The initial mass of a rocket is 1000 kg. Calculate at what rate the fuel should be burnt so that the rocket is given an acceleration of 20 m/s^2. The gases come out at a relative speed of 500ms^(-1) with respect to the rocket. [Use g = 10m/s^2]

  1. (1) 500 kg s^(-1)
  2. (2) 10 kg s^(-1)
  3. (3) 6.0 × 10^2 kg s^(-1)
  4. (4) 60kg s^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using the rocket equation: v_rel × (dm/dt) - mg = F_net. Where v_rel is relative velocity of exhaust (500 m/s), dm/dt is fuel burn rate, m is mass (1000 kg), and F_net = ma = 1000 × 20 = 20000 N. Substituting: 500 × (dm/dt) - 1000 × 10 = 1000 × 20. Therefore: 500 × (dm/dt) = 20000 + 10000 = 30000. So dm/dt = 60 kg/s.
