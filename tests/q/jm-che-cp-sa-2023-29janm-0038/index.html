A obtained by Ostwald's method involving air oxidation of NH3, upon further air oxidation produces B. B on hydration forms an oxoacid of Nitrogen along with evolution of A. The oxoacid also produces A and gives positive brown ring test. Identify A and B, respectively

  1. (1) NO, NO2
  2. (2) N2O3, NO2
  3. (3) NO2, N2O4
  4. (4) NO2, N2O5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Ostwald's process is: 4NH3 + 5O2 --> 4NO + 6H2O (A) 2NO + O2 --> 2NO2 (A) (B) 4NO2 + 2H2O + O2 --> 4HNO3 ∴ A and B are NO and NO2 respectively Explanation: - A is NO (obtained from air oxidation of NH3 in Ostwald's method) - Upon further air oxidation, NO produces B which is NO2 - When NO2 is hydrated with water and O2, it forms HNO3 (an oxoacid of nitrogen) and releases NO (which is A) - HNO3 gives the characteristic positive brown ring test with Fe2+ ions
