The correct decreasing order for metallic character is

  1. (1) Na > Mg > Be > Si > P
  2. (2) P > Si > Be > Mg > Na
  3. (3) Si > P > Be > Na > Mg
  4. (4) Be > Na > Mg > Si > P

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Metallic character increases from top to bottom in a group and decreases from left to right in a period. Na is from group 1 (more metallic), Mg from group 2 (less metallic than Na), Be comes above Mg hence less metallic than Mg, Si is more metallic than P. Order: Na > Mg > Be > Si > P
