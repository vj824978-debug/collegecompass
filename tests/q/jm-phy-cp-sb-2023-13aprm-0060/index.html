A fish rising vertically upward with a uniform velocity of 8 m/s observes that a bird is diving vertically downward towards the fish with the velocity of 12 m/s. If the refractive index of water is 4/3, then the actual velocity of the diving bird to pick the fish, will be _______ m/s.

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Using refraction at water surface, the apparent velocity of bird as seen by fish is 12 m/s (observed). The separation between bird and fish w.r.t. fish is x + 4y/3. Taking time derivative: d/dt(x + 4y/3) = -12. This gives (4/3)(dy/dt) = -12 + 8 = -4, so dy/dt = -3 m/s. The actual velocity of the bird is 3 m/s.
