The final product 'C' in the following series of reactions: Nitrobenzene + Sn/HCl → A, A + NaNO2/HCl → B, B + β-Naphthol/NaOH → C

  1. (1) Phenyl azo compound with naphthalene having OH at position 2
  2. (2) 1-Phenylazo-2-naphthol
  3. (3) Phenyldiazonium compound attached to 1-naphthol
  4. (4) Phenylazo-2-naphthol with OH at position 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Step 1: Nitrobenzene + Sn/HCl reduces NO2 to NH2, giving aniline [A]. Step 2: Aniline + NaNO2/HCl gives phenyldiazonium chloride [B] (diazotization). Step 3: Phenyldiazonium chloride + β-Naphthol/NaOH undergoes azo coupling reaction. β-Naphthol couples at position 1 (more activated position due to OH), giving 1-phenylazo-2-naphthol [C], which shows phenyl N=N linkage attached to naphthalene with OH in ortho position.
