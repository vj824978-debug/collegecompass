If the angle of intersection at a point where the two circles with radii 5 cm and 12 cm intersect is 90°, then the length (in cm) of their common chord is:

  1. (1) 13/5
  2. (2) 60/13
  3. (3) 120/13
  4. (4) 13/2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Let the two circles have centers C1 and C2 with radii r1 = 5 cm and r2 = 12 cm respectively. They intersect at points A and B. The angle of intersection is 90°, which means the tangents to the two circles at point A are perpendicular. Consider triangle AC1C2: - AC1 = 5 (radius of first circle) - AC2 = 12 (radius of second circle) - Angle C1AC2 = 90° (angle of intersection) By Pythagorean theorem: C1C2^2 = AC1^2 + AC2^2 C1C2^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169 C1C2 = 13 Now, let AB be the common chord. Let D be the point where AB intersects C1C2. The line C1C2 is perpendicular to the chord AB. In triangle AC1C2: tan(angle AC1C2) = AC2/AC1 = 12/5 ⟹ sin(angle AC1C2) = 12/13 .....(i) In triangle ACD (right angle at D): sin(angle AC1D) = AD/(AC1) = AD/5 .....(ii) From (i) and (ii): AD/5 = 12/13 AD = 60/13 Since D is the midpoint of AB: AB = 2 × AD = 2 × 60/13 = 120/13 Therefore, the length of the common chord AB = 120/13 cm
