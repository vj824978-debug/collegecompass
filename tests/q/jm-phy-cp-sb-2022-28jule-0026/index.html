An electrical bulb rated 220 V, 100 W, is connected in series with another bulb rated 220 V, 60 W. If the voltage across combination is 220 V, the power consumed by the 100 W bulb will be about ____ W.

Answer

Correct answer: 14

Solution

Calculate resistance of each bulb using P = V^2/R. For 100W bulb: R_100 = V^2/P_100 = 220^2/100. For 60W bulb: R_60 = V^2/P_60 = 220^2/60. When connected in series, total resistance is R_total = R_60 + R_100. Net power developed: P_net = V^2/(R_60 + R_100) = (P_60 × P_100)/(P_60 + P_100) = (60 × 100)/160 = 37.5 W. Power consumed by 100W bulb is proportional to its resistance: P'_100 = P_net × (R_100/(R_60 + R_100)) = 37.5 × (100/160) = 37.5 × (100/160) ≈ 14 W
