When a long glass capillary tube of radius 0.015 cm is dipped in a liquid, the liquid rises to a height of 15 cm within it. If the contact angle between the liquid and glass to close to 0°, the surface tension of the liquid, in milliNewton m^(-1), is [ρ(liquid) = 900 kgm^(-3), g = 10 ms^(-2)] (Give answer in closest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 101

Solution

Capillary rise h = (2S*cos(θ))/(ρgr) S = (ρgrh)/(2cos(θ)) = ((900)(10)(15 × 10^(-5))(15 × 10^(-2)))/2 S = 1012.5 × 10^(-4) S = 101.25 × 10^(-3) = 101.25 mN/m In question closest integer is asked so closest integer = 101.00 Ans.
