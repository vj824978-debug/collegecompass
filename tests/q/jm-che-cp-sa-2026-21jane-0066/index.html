Given below are some of the statements about Mn and Mn2O7. Identify the correct statements A. Mn forms the oxide Mn2O7 in which Mn is in its highest oxidation state. B. Oxygen stabilizes the Mn in higher oxidation states by forming multiple bonds with Mn C. Mn2O7 is an ionic oxide. D. The structure of Mn2O7 consists of one bridged oxygen. Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) A, B, C and D
  2. (2) A, B and D Only
  3. (3) A, C and D Only
  4. (4) A, B and C Only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Mn2O7 : Mn in +7 oxidation state Structure of Mn2O7: O O || || O=Mn---O---Mn=O | | O O Analysis of each statement: A. Mn forms the oxide Mn2O7 in which Mn is in its highest oxidation state. CORRECT: In Mn2O7, Mn has +7 oxidation state, which is the highest oxidation state for manganese (Mn is in group 7). B. Oxygen stabilizes the Mn in higher oxidation states by forming multiple bonds with Mn CORRECT: Oxygen forms multiple bonds (double bonds) with Mn atoms in Mn2O7, which stabilizes the higher +7 oxidation state of Mn. C. Mn2O7 is an ionic oxide. INCORRECT: Mn2O7 is a covalent oxide, not ionic. It contains covalent bonds between Mn and O atoms. D. The structure of Mn2O7 consists of one bridged oxygen. CORRECT: The structure contains one bridging oxygen atom connecting the two Mn atoms (shown as ---O--- in the structure). Therefore, statements A, B, and D are correct.
