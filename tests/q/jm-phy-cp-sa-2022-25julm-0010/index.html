A condenser of 2μF capacitance is charged steadily from 0 to 5 C. Which of the following graph represents correctly the variation of potential difference (V) across its plates with respect to the charge (Q) on the condenser?

  1. (1) Graph (1): Linear relationship starting from origin, reaching 2.5×10^6 V at Q = 5 C
  2. (2) Graph (2): Curved relationship (exponential-like) starting from origin, reaching 5×10^6 V at Q = 5 C
  3. (3) Graph (3): Linear relationship starting from origin, reaching 5×10^6 V at Q = 5 C
  4. (4) Graph (4): Curved relationship (logarithmic-like) starting from origin, reaching approximately 2.5×10^6 V at Q = 5 C

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Using Q = CV, since capacitance is constant, Q is proportional to V (Q ∝ V). The relationship is linear. At Q = 5 C and C = 2×10^(-6) F: V_f = Q_f/C = 5/(2×10^(-6)) = 2.5×10^6 V. The correct graph is linear passing through origin with maximum voltage of 2.5×10^6 V at Q = 5 C, which is graph (1).
