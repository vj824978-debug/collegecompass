A clock has a continuously moving second's hand of 0.1 m length. The average acceleration of the tip of the hand (in units of ms^(-2)) is of the order of -

  1. (1) 10^(-3)
  2. (2) 10^(-4)
  3. (3) 10^(-1)
  4. (4) 10^(-2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

R = 0.1 m For a second's hand, period T = 60 seconds Angular velocity: ω = 2π/T = 2π/60 = 0.105 rad/sec For circular motion, the centripetal acceleration at the tip of the hand is: a = ω²R Substituting values: a = (0.105)² × (0.1) a = 0.01105 × 0.1 a = 0.0011 a = 1.1 × 10^(-3) ms^(-2) Therefore, the average acceleration is of the order of 10^(-3) ms^(-2).
