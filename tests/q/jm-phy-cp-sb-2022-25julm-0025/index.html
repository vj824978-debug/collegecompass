An observer is riding on a bicycle and moving towards a hill at 18 km/h. He hears a sound from a source at some distance behind him directly as well as after its reflection from the hill. If the original frequency of the sound as emitted by source is 640 Hz and velocity of the sound in air is 320 m/s, the beat frequency between the two sounds heard by observer will be ___ Hz.

Answer

Correct answer: 20

Solution

Convert velocity: 18 km/h = 5 m/s. f3 = f0 = 640 Hz (no relative motion between source and hill). f2 = f0[(320 + 5)/320] = 640(325/320) = 650 Hz (observer moving towards reflected sound source). f1 = f0[320/(320 + 5)] = 640(320/325) = 630 Hz (observer moving away from direct sound source). Beat frequency = f2 - f1 = 650 - 630 = 20 Hz.
