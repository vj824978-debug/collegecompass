Consider the following reactions, (i) NaNO2/HCl, 0-5°C (ii) β-naphthol/NaOH → Colored Solid; [P] → Br2/H2O → C7H6NBr3. The compound [P] is:

  1. (1) NH2 group attached to benzene ring with CH3 group (ortho position)
  2. (2) NH2 group attached to benzene ring with CH3 group (meta position)
  3. (3) NH2 group attached to benzene ring with CH3 group (para position)
  4. (4) NHCH3 group attached to benzene ring with CH3 group (para position)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The compound [P] undergoes two different reactions: 1) With NaNO2/HCl at 0-5°C followed by β-naphthol/NaOH → Colored Solid This is a diazonium coupling reaction. The amine group is converted to diazonium salt (N2+ Cl-) which then couples with β-naphthol to form a colored azo dye. 2) With Br2/H2O → C7H6NBr3 This is an electrophilic aromatic bromination reaction. The product is C7H6NBr3 (three bromines attached). Analyzing the second reaction: - Molecular formula C7H6NBr3 indicates: - 7 carbons (benzene ring + 1 carbon substituent) - 6 hydrogens (benzene ring hydrogens + substituent hydrogens) - 1 nitrogen (from amine group) - 3 bromines - The amine group (-NH2) is an activating group and ortho/para directing - Since 3 bromines are added, they must occupy all available positions ortho and para to the activating -NH2 group - With a methyl group (-CH3) also present, we need a configuration where: - The -NH2 and -CH3 can be positioned such that bromination gives exactly C7H6NBr3 - If -NH2 is meta to -CH3 (meta position), bromination at ortho and para positions to -NH2 gives three bromines, which is consistent with the formula Therefore, [P] is meta-methylaniline (3-methylaniline or m-toluidine): NH2 and CH3 are in meta positions on the benzene ring.
