Hydrolysis of sucrose gives:

  1. (1) alpha - D - (+) - Glucose and alpha - D - (-) - Fructose
  2. (2) alpha - D - (-) - Glucose and alpha - D - (+) - Fructose
  3. (3) alpha - D - (+) - Glucose and beta - D - (-) - Fructose
  4. (4) alpha - D - (-) - Glucose and beta - D - (-) - Fructose

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Sucrose undergoes hydrolysis in the presence of H2O and H+ catalyst to produce alpha - D - (+) - glucose and beta - D - (-) - fructose. The reaction is: Sucrose + H2O → alpha - D(+)glucose + beta - D(-)fructose
