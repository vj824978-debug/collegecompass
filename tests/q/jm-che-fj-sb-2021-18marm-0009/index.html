Complete combustion of 3 g of ethane gives x×10^22 molecules of water. The value of x is (Round off to the nearest integer). [Use: N_A = 6.023 × 10^23; Atomic masses in u: C:12.0; O:16.0; H:1.0]

Answer

Correct answer: 18

Solution

C2H6 + 7/2 O2 → 2CO2 + 3H2O. Molar mass of C2H6 = 30 g/mol. Moles of C2H6 = 3/30 = 0.1 mol. From stoichiometry, 1 mole of C2H6 produces 3 moles of H2O. Therefore, moles of H2O produced = 0.3 mol. Number of H2O molecules = 0.3 × 6.023 × 10^23 = 1.8069 × 10^23 ≈ 18.06 × 10^22. So x = 18 (Rounded off).
