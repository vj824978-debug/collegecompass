CH4 is adsorbed on 1 g charcoal at 0°C following the Freundlich adsorption isotherm. 10.0 mL of CH4 is adsorbed at 100 mm of Hg, whereas 15.0 mL is adsorbed at 200 mm of Hg. The volume of CH4 adsorbed at 300 mm of Hg is _____ × 10^(-2). The value of x is _____ (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 128

Solution

Using Freundlich adsorption isotherm: (x/m) = KP^(1/n). From given data: 10/1 = K(100)^(1/n) ... (1), 15/1 = K(200)^(1/n) ... (2), V/1 = K(300)^(1/n) ... (3). Dividing (2) by (1): 15/10 = (200/100)^(1/n), so 3/2 = 2^(1/n), taking log: log3 - log2 = (1/n)log2. Therefore 1/n = (0.477 - 0.301)/0.301 = 0.585. Dividing (3) by (1): V/10 = (3)^(1/n), so log(V/10) = (1/n)log3 = 0.585 × 0.477 = 0.279. Thus V/10 = 10^0.279, V = 10 × 10^0.279 = 10^1.279 = 10^x. Therefore x = 1.279 ≈ 128 × 10^(-2). Answer: x = 128
