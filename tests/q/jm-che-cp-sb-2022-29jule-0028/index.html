A 1.84 mg sample of polyhydric alcoholic compound 'X' of molar mass 92.0 g/mol gave 1.344 mL of H2 gas at STP. The number of alcoholic hydrogens present in compound 'X' is____.

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

Using R(OH)x → H2 reaction (PoAC on H). Moles of X = 1.84×10^(-3)/92 = 2×10^(-5) mol. Moles of H2 = 1.344/22400 = 6×10^(-5) mol. From reaction: x moles of R(OH)x gives x/2 moles of H2. Therefore: 2×10^(-5) × x = 2 × 6×10^(-5). Solving: x = 6
