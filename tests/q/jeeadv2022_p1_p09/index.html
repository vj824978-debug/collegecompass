A medium having dielectric constant K > 1 fills the space between the plates of a parallel plate capacitor. The plates have large area, and the distance between them is d. The capacitor is connected to a battery of voltage V, as shown in Figure (a). Now, both the plates are moved by a distance d/2 of from their original positions, as shown in Figure (b). [Figure (a) shows a parallel plate capacitor with dielectric material (hatched) filling the space between plates separated by distance d, connected to voltage V] [Figure (b) shows the same capacitor with both plates moved d/2 away from center, creating three regions: d/2 gap, d gap with dielectric, d/2 gap] In the process of going from the configuration depicted in Figure (a) to that in Figure (b), which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The electric field inside the dielectric material is reduced by a factor of 2K.
  2. (B) The capacitance is decreased by a factor of 1/(K+1).
  3. (C) The voltage between the capacitor plates is increased by a factor of (K+1).
  4. (D) The work done in the process DOES NOT depend on the presence of the dielectric material.

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

B Refer to figure (a) V = E₁d C₁ = kε₀A/d [Figure (a) shows a parallel plate capacitor with dielectric material E₁ of thickness d between two plates] Refer to figure (b) V = E₃(d/2) + E₂(d/2) + E₃(d/2) Also E₃ = E₂K V = E₂d + KE₂d V = (K + 1)E₂d C₂ = ε₀A/(d + d/K) = Kε₀A/((K + 1)d) [Figure (b) shows a three-layer parallel plate capacitor with E₃, E₂, and E₃ regions, with spacing d/2, d, and d/2 respectively] Now E₁/E₂ = K + 1 C₁/C₂ = (K + 1) Wₑₓₜ + Wᵦₐₜₜₑᵣᵧ = ΔU Wₑₓₜ + (C₂ - C₁)V² = (C₂ - C₁)V²/2 Wₑₓₜ = (C₁ - C₂)V²/2 = (Kε₀A/d)(1 - 1/(K+1))V²/2 = K²ε₀AV²/2d(K + 1)
