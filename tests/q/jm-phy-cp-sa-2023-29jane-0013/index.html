Identify the correct statements from the following: A. Work done by a man in lifting a bucket out of a well by means of rope tied to the bucket is negative. B. Work done by gravitational force in lifting a bucket out of a well by a rope tied to the bucket is negative. C. Work done by friction on a body sliding down an inclined plane is positive. D. Work done by an applied force on a body moving on a rough horizontal plane with uniform velocity is zero. E. Work done by the air resistance on an oscillating pendulum is negative.

  1. (1) A and C only
  2. (2) B and E only
  3. (3) B, D and E only
  4. (4) B and D only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

B. Work done by gravitation will be negative if something is lifted upward. When lifting a bucket out of a well, the gravitational force acts downward while displacement is upward. Since force and displacement are in opposite directions, work done by gravity is negative. This statement is CORRECT. E. Work done by air resistance is negative. Air resistance always opposes the motion of the oscillating pendulum. During both upswing and downswing, the air resistance force acts opposite to the direction of motion. Since force and displacement are in opposite directions, work done by air resistance is always negative. This statement is CORRECT. Let us verify other statements: A. Work done by a man in lifting a bucket is positive, not negative. The man applies force in the direction of motion (upward), so work is positive. INCORRECT. C. Work done by friction on a body sliding down an inclined plane is negative, not positive. Friction opposes motion, acting up the plane while displacement is down the plane. INCORRECT. D. Work done by applied force on a body moving with uniform velocity on rough horizontal plane: At uniform velocity, net force is zero (a = 0). The applied force equals friction force in magnitude but opposite in direction. Work done by applied force = F·d (positive), Work done by friction = -f·d (negative). The net work is zero, but work done by the applied force alone is NOT zero. INCORRECT.
