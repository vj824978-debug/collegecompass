Consider the following reactions: A + NaCl + H2SO4 → CrO2Cl2 + Side Products (Little amount), CrO2Cl2 (Vapour) + NaOH → B + NaCl + H2O, B + H+ → C + H2O. The number of terminal 'O' present in the compound 'C' is ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

First reaction: A is likely a chromium compound (Cr2O3 or similar) that reacts with NaCl and H2SO4 to form CrO2Cl2 (chromyl chloride). Second reaction: CrO2Cl2 reacts with NaOH to form B. CrO2Cl2 + 2NaOH → NaCrO2 + NaCl + H2O, so B is NaCrO2 (sodium chromite). Third reaction: B (NaCrO2) reacts with H+ to form C. NaCrO2 + H+ → CrO2+ (chromyl cation) or more likely forms HCrO2 which further oxidizes to Cr(VI) species. However, the most likely product C is CrO3 or Cr2O7^2-. Given the context, C is likely CrO3 (chromic acid equivalent) which has 3 terminal oxygen atoms, or if it forms dichromate Cr2O7^2-, it would have 7 oxygen atoms. Most probable answer is 3 (for CrO3 or chromyl-related structure).
