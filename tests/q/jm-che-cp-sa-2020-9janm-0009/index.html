Identify (A) in the following reaction sequence: (A) Gives Positive iodoform test → (i) CH3MgBr (ii) H+, H2O (iii) Conc. H2SO4/Δ → (B) → O3/Zn,H2O → Product shown

  1. (1) Structure (1): Benzene ring with CH3 group attached to double bond, with two CH3 groups and one C(=O)CH3 group
  2. (2) Structure (2): Benzene ring with double bond to adjacent carbon, with CH3 groups and C(=O)CH3 group
  3. (3) Structure (3): Benzene ring with double bond to adjacent carbon, with CH3 group and CHO group
  4. (4) Structure (4): Benzene ring fused to cyclohexene with C(=O)CH3 group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Compound (A) gives a positive iodoform test, indicating the presence of CH3CO- group (methyl ketone) or CH3CH(OH)- group (secondary alcohol with methyl adjacent to OH). Reaction sequence analysis: 1. (A) undergoes Grignard reaction with CH3MgBr followed by H+, H2O workup → adds methyl group and forms alcohol 2. Treatment with conc. H2SO4 at heat causes dehydration → forms alkene (B) 3. Ozonolysis of (B) with O3/Zn,H2O cleaves the double bond and forms carbonyl compounds The final product shown has: - Benzene ring fused to a cyclopentanone - CHO group and ketone groups - CH3 groups Working backwards from the ozonolysis product: - The aldehyde CHO and ketone C(=O)CH3 groups come from cleaving a C=C double bond - Before ozonolysis, (B) must have had a C=C double bond between these carbon atoms - This double bond came from dehydration of the alcohol formed after Grignard addition (A) must be structure (2): An aromatic ketone with structure showing benzene ring attached to a carbon chain with both ketone and hydroxyl groups capable of forming the observed products after the reaction sequence.
