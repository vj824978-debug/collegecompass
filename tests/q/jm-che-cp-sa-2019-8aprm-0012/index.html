For the solution of the gases w, x, y and z in water at 298 K, the Henry law constants (K_H) are 0.5, 2, 35 and 40 kbar, respectively. The correct plot for the given data is -

  1. (1) Plot showing z, y, x, w lines in descending order with steeper slopes
  2. (2) Plot showing z, y, x, w lines in descending order with moderate slopes
  3. (3) Plot showing z, y, x, w lines in descending order with gentler slopes
  4. (4) Plot showing z, y, x, w lines with z and y having steeper slopes than x and w

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

According to Henry's law: P_g = k_H x_g = k_H(1 - x_w) = k_H - k_H x_w = -k_H x_w + k_H This can be written in the form: y = -mx + c where: - y-intercept (c) = k_H (partial pressure when x_w = 0) - slope (m) = -k_H (negative slope) Given Henry law constants: - k_H(w) = 0.5 kbar - k_H(x) = 2 kbar - k_H(y) = 35 kbar - k_H(z) = 40 kbar Therefore: k_H(z) > k_H(y) > k_H(x) > k_H(w) Since the slope magnitude is |−k_H|, gases with higher k_H values have steeper (more negative) slopes. Hence, the order of slope steepness is: z > y > x > w At x_w = 0 (pure water solution), the y-intercepts are at the k_H values, so the initial partial pressures follow the same order: z > y > x > w As x_w increases (moving right on the x-axis), the partial pressures decrease with slopes in the order z (steepest) > y > x > w (least steep). Plot (2) correctly shows all four lines with z having the steepest descent, followed by y, then x, and finally w with the gentlest slope, all decreasing as mole fraction of water increases.
