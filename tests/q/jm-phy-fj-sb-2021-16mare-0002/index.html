In a parallel plate capacitor set up, plate area of capacitor is 2 m^2 and the plates are separated by 1m. If the space between the plates is filled with a dielectric material of thickness 0.5 m and area 2 m^2 (See fig) the capacitance of the set-up will be _______ pF. (Dielectric constant of the material = 3.2) (Round off to the Nearest Integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Using the equivalent capacitance formula for dielectric inserted between capacitor plates: C = (epsilon_0 * A) / (d - t + t/k) where A = 2 m^2, d = 1 m, t = 0.5 m, k = 3.2. Substituting: C = (8.85 × 10^-12 × 2) / (1 - 0.5 + 0.5/3.2) = (17.7 × 10^-12) / (0.5 + 0.15625) = (17.7 × 10^-12) / 0.65625 ≈ 26.96 × 10^-12 F ≈ 27 pF
