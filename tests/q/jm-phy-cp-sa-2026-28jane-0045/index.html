Match List-I with List-II. List-I: A. Coefficient of viscosity B. Surface tension C. Pressure D. Surface energy List-II: I. [ML^(-1)T^(-2)] II. [MLT^(-2)] III. [ML^0T^(-2)] IV. [ML^(-1)T^(-1)] Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
  2. (2) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
  3. (3) A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
  4. (4) A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(A) Coefficient of viscosity η = F·dr/(A·dv) = [MLT^(-2)][L]/([L^2][LT^(-1)]) = [ML^(-1)T^(-1)] = IV (B) Surface tension S = F/L = [MLT^(-2)]/[L] = [MT^(-2)] = II (C) Pressure P = F/A = [MLT^(-2)]/[L^2] = [ML^(-1)T^(-2)] = I (D) Surface energy E = S × A = [MT^(-2)][L^2] = [ML^2T^(-2)] = III Therefore: A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III The correct matching is A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
