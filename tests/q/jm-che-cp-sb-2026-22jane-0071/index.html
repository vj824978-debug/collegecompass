Consider the following electrochemical cell: Pt|O2(g)(bar)|HCl(aq)||M^(2+)(aq,1.0M)|M(s). The pH above which, oxygen gas would start to evolve at anode is ____ (nearest integer). Given: E°_(M^(2+)/M) = 0.994 V, E°_(O2/H2O) = 1.23 V, and (RT/F)(2.303) = 0.059 V at the given condition.

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

For spontaneity E_cell > 0 At limiting condition: E_Oxi(anode) = -E_Red(cathode) Oxidation at anode: H2O → 2H^+ + (1/2)O2 + 2e^- Reduction at cathode: M^(2+) + 2e^- → M, E°_Red = -0.994 V For oxygen oxidation: E = E° - (0.059/2) log([H^+]^2 × p_(O2)^(1/2)/1) At limiting condition: -E_Oxi = E_Red -E_O2/H2O = -(-0.994) -(1.23 - (0.059/2) log([H^+]^2 × p_(O2)^(1/2))) = -0.994 -1.23 + 0.0295 log([H^+]^2 × p_(O2)^(1/2)) = -0.994 0.0295 log([H^+]^2 × p_(O2)^(1/2)) = 1.23 - 0.994 = 0.236 log([H^+]^2 × p_(O2)^(1/2)) = 0.236/0.0295 = 8 Assuming p_(O2) = 1 bar: log([H^+]^2) = 8 2 log[H^+] = 8 log[H^+] = 4 [H^+] = 10^(-4) M pH = 4
