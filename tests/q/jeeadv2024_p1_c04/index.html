Among the following options, select the option in which each complex in Set-I shows geometrical isomerism and the two complexes in Set-II are ionization isomers of each other. [en = H₂NCH₂CH₂NH₂]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) Set - I: [Ni(CO)₄] and [PdCl₂(PPh₃)₂] Set - II: [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]SO₄ and [Co(NH₃)₅(SO₄)]Cl
  2. (B) Set - I: [Co(en)(NH₃)₂Cl₂] and [PdCl₂(PPh₃)₂] Set - II: [Co(NH₃)₅][Cr(CN)₆] and [Cr(NH₃)₅][Co(CN)₆]
  3. (C) Set - I: [Co(NH₃)₃(NO₂)₃] and [Co(en)₂Cl₂] Set - II: [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]SO₄ and [Co(NH₃)₅(SO₄)]Cl
  4. (D) Set - I: [Cr(NH₃)₅Cl]Cl₂ and [Co(en)(NH₃)₂Cl₂] Set - II: [Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ and [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

[Diagram showing coordination chemistry isomers of cobalt complexes] Set - I [Co(NH₃)₃(NO₂)₃] Two geometric isomers shown: Left structure (fac): Co center with three NH₃ ligands and three NO₂ ligands in facial arrangement Right structure (mer): Co center with three NH₃ ligands and three NO₂ ligands in meridional arrangement (pair of geometrical isomers) [Co(en)₂Cl₂] Two geometric isomers shown: Left structure (Trans): Co center with two ethylenediamine (en) bidentate ligands and two Cl ligands in trans positions Right structure (cis): Co center with two ethylenediamine (en) bidentate ligands and two Cl ligands in cis positions (geometrical isomers) [Co(NH₃)₅]Cl]SO₄ and [Co(NH₃)₅(SO₄)]Cl are ionization isomers.
