For the given reaction: A benzene ring with CH2CH3 group and CN group undergoes bromination with Br2 under UV light to give 'A' (major product monobrominated). What is 'A'?

  1. (1) Benzene with CH2CH3 at top and Br below it, with CN group positioned meta to Br
  2. (2) Benzene with CH2CH3 at top and Br positioned meta to CH2CH3, with CN group positioned meta to Br
  3. (3) Benzene with CH at center bonded to Br and CH3, with CN group at bottom

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The reaction is a free radical bromination (not electrophilic aromatic substitution). Under UV light, Br2 undergoes homolytic cleavage to form Br radicals. The CH2CH3 group undergoes allylic/benzylic bromination at the CH2 position (next to the benzene ring) because the benzylic C-H bond is weaker and the benzylic radical formed is stabilized by resonance with the benzene ring. The CN group as an electron-withdrawing group and the aromatic ring itself do not undergo bromination under these free radical conditions. The major product is bromination at the benzylic position of the ethyl group, giving BrCH(CH3) attached to the benzene ring with CN intact.
