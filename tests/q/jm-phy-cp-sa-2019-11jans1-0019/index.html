In the experimental set up of metre bridge shown in the figure, the null point is obtained at a distance of 40 cm from A. If a 10 Ω resistor is connected in series with R₁, the null point shifts by 10 cm. The resistance that should be connected in parallel with (R₁ + 10) Ω such that the null point shifts back to its initial position is:

  1. (1) 40 Ω
  2. (2) 30 Ω
  3. (3) 20 Ω
  4. (4) 60 Ω

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Initial condition: Null point at 40 cm from A means 60 cm from B For metre bridge: R₁/R₂ = l₁/l₂ = 40/60 = 2/3 When 10 Ω is connected in series with R₁: Null point shifts by 10 cm, so new position is at 50 cm New ratio: (R₁ + 10)/R₂ = 50/50 = 1 Therefore: R₁ + 10 = R₂ From first condition: R₁/R₂ = 2/3 So: R₁ = (2/3)R₂ From second condition: R₁ + 10 = R₂ Substituting R₁ = (2/3)R₂: (2/3)R₂ + 10 = R₂ 10 = R₂ - (2/3)R₂ 10 = (1/3)R₂ R₂ = 30 Ω Therefore: R₁ = (2/3) × 30 = 20 Ω Now, let R be the resistance connected in parallel with (R₁ + 10) = 30 Ω For null point to return to 40 cm position: Effective resistance in parallel = (30 × R)/(30 + R) This should give the ratio 2/3 again: [(30R)/(30 + R)]/R₂ = 40/60 = 2/3 [(30R)/(30 + R)]/30 = 2/3 30R/(30 + R) = 20 30R = 20(30 + R) 30R = 600 + 20R 10R = 600 R = 60 Ω
