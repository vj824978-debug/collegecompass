Match the List-I with List-II. LIST-I: A. 0n + 92U → 54Xe + 38Sr + 2(0n), B. 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O, C. 1H + 1H → 2He + 0n, D. 1H + 3H → 2H + 2H. LIST-II: I. chemical reaction, II. fusion with +ve Q value, III. fission, IV. fusion with -ve Q value

  1. (1) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
  2. (2) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
  3. (3) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
  4. (4) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

A is nuclear fission of U-235 producing Xe and Sr (List III). B is a chemical reaction between hydrogen and oxygen (List I). C is nuclear fusion of two deuterium nuclei forming He with energy release, positive Q value (List II). D is fusion reaction but with negative Q value since it requires energy input (List IV). Answer: A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
