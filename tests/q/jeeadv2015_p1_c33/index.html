The structure of D-(+)-glucose is [Fischer projection showing: CHO at top, then H-OH, HO-H, H-OH, H-OH, CH2OH at bottom] The structure of L-(-)-glucose is

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [Fischer projection showing: CHO at top, then HO-H, H-OH, HO-H, HO-H, CH2OH at bottom]
  2. (B) [Fischer projection showing: CHO at top, then H-OH, HO-H, H-OH, HO-H, CH2OH at bottom]
  3. (C) [Fischer projection showing: CHO at top, then HO-H, HO-H, H-OH, HO-H, CH2OH at bottom]
  4. (D) [Fischer projection showing: CHO at top, then HO-H, HO-H, HO-H, H-OH, CH2OH at bottom]

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

D(+)glucose structure (left side): CHO H-C-OH HO-C-H H-C-OH H-C-OH CH₂OH L(-)glucose structure (right side): CHO HO-C-H H-C-OH HO-C-H HO-C-H CH₂OH [The structures are mirror images of each other, separated by a vertical mirror line with hatching]
