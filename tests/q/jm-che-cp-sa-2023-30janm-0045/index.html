Which of the following compounds would give the following set of qualitative analysis? (i) Fehling's Test: Positive (ii) Na fusion extract upon treatment with sodium nitroprusside gives a blood red colour but not prussian blue.

  1. (1) Thiazole with CHO group (benzene ring with N and S, CHO substituent)
  2. (2) Imidazole with CHO group (benzene ring with two N atoms, CHO substituent)
  3. (3) Oxazole with CHO group (benzene ring with N and O, CHO substituent)
  4. (4) Thiazole with propyl-CHO group (benzene ring with N and S, propyl chain with CHO)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Fehling solution is not given by aromatic aldehydes. 1, 2, 3 are aromatic aldehydes. Only compound 4 has an aliphatic aldehyde (the CHO group is on a propyl chain attached to the heterocyclic ring, not directly on the aromatic ring itself). Therefore, compound 4 would give a positive Fehling's test. The sodium nitroprusside test gives blood red colour with compounds containing Fe^(2+) ions (from sulfur-containing compounds in the Na fusion extract), but not prussian blue (which would indicate Fe^(3+) ions). Compound 4, being a thiazole derivative, would show this behavior.
