A rod of mass M and length L is lying on a horizontal frictionless surface. A particle of mass 'm' travelling along the surface hits at one end of the rod with a velocity 'u' in a direction perpendicular to the rod. The collision is completely elastic. After collision, the particle comes to rest. The ratio of masses (m/M) is 1/x. The value of 'x' will be ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Using conservation of linear momentum: mu = Mv_1, so v_1 = mu/M. Using conservation of angular momentum about centre of mass of rod: (mur)/2 + (Mω)r/2 + (mv_1)r/2 = 6mu = Mrω, so ω = 6mu/M. Using definition of e (coefficient of restitution): v_1 + ωr/2 = u, which gives mu/(3mu) = u. Solving: m/M = 1/3, therefore x = 3.
