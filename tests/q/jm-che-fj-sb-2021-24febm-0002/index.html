The stepwise formation of [Cu(NH3)4]2+ is given below: Cu2+ + NH3 ⇌(k1) [Cu(NH3)]2+, [Cu(NH3)]2+ + NH3 ⇌(k2) [Cu(NH3)2]2+, [Cu(NH3)2]2+ + NH3 ⇌(k3) [Cu(NH3)3]2+, [Cu(NH3)3]2+ + NH3 ⇌(k4) [Cu(NH3)4]2+. The value of stability constants k1, k2, k3 and k4 are 10^4, 1.58 × 10^3, 5 × 10^2 and 10^2 respectively. The overall equilibrium constant for dissociation of [Cu(NH3)4]2+ is x × 10^-12. The value of x is _____ (Rounded off to the nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

Stability constant K1 = 10^4, K2 = 1.58 × 10^3, K3 = 5 × 10^2, K4 = 10^2. Overall stability constant K = K1 × K2 × K3 × K4 = 10^4 × 1.58 × 10^3 × 5 × 10^2 × 10^2 = 7.9 × 10^11. Overall equilibrium constant for dissociation K' = 1/K = 1/(7.9 × 10^11) = 0.126 × 10^-11 = 1.26 × 10^-12. Therefore x = 1 (rounded to nearest integer).
