The first of the two samples in a group has 100 items with mean 15 and standard deviation 3. If the whole group has 250 items with mean 15.6 and standard deviation sqrt(13.44), then the standard deviation of the second sample is:

  1. (1) 4
  2. (2) 6
  3. (3) 5
  4. (4) 8

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For 1st sample: n1=100, mean1=15, sigma1=3. Calculate Σx1=1500 and Σx1^2=23400. For combined sample: n=250, mean=15.6, sigma=sqrt(13.44)=3.66 (approximately). Using combined variance formula: sigma^2 = (Σx1^2 + Σy1^2)/250 - (15.6)^2 = 13.44. This gives Σy1^2=40800. For 2nd sample: n2=150, mean2=y_bar. From combined mean: 100(15) + 150(y_bar) = 250(15.6), so y_bar=16. Using sigma2^2 = Σy1^2/150 - (16)^2 = 40800/150 - 256 = 272 - 256 = 16, therefore sigma2=4.
