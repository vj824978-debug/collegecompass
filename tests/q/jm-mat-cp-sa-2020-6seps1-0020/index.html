A ray of light coming from the point (2, 2*sqrt(3)) is incident at an angle 30° on the line x = 1 at the point A. The ray gets reflected on the line x = 1 and meets x-axis at the point B. Then, the line AB passes through the point:

  1. (1) (4, -sqrt(3)/2)
  2. (2) (4, -sqrt(3))
  3. (3) (3, -1/sqrt(3))
  4. (4) (3, -sqrt(3))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For point A The incident ray makes an angle of 30° with the line x = 1 (vertical line). The normal to x = 1 is horizontal. The incident angle with the normal is 60°. From the geometry, the incident ray comes from (2, 2*sqrt(3)) to point A on line x = 1. The slope of incident ray with angle 60° from horizontal: tan(60°) = (2*sqrt(3) - k)/(2 - 1) where A = (1, k) sqrt(3) = 2*sqrt(3) - k ∴ k = sqrt(3) So point A(1, sqrt(3)) Now slope of line AB in m_AB = tan(120°) m_AB = -sqrt(3) Now equation of line AB is: y - sqrt(3) = -sqrt(3)(x - 1) sqrt(3)x + y = 2*sqrt(3) Now satisfy options: For option (4): (3, -sqrt(3)) sqrt(3)(3) + (-sqrt(3)) = 3*sqrt(3) - sqrt(3) = 2*sqrt(3) ✓ This satisfies the equation of line AB.
