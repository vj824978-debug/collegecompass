For Freundlich adsorption isotherm, a plot of log (x/m) (y-axis) and log p (x-axis) gives a straight line. The intercept and slope for the line is 0.4771 and 2, respectively. The mass of gas, adsorbed per gram of adsorbent if the initial pressure is 0.04 atm, is _______ × 10^(-4) g. (log 3 = 0.4771)

Answer

Correct answer: 48.00

Solution

The Freundlich adsorption isotherm is given by: x/m = KP^(1/n) Taking logarithm on both sides: log(x/m) = (1/n) log P + log K This is a linear equation of the form y = mx + c, where: - y-axis: log(x/m) - x-axis: log P - Slope: 1/n = 2 - Intercept: log K = 0.4771 From slope: 1/n = 2 n = 1/2 From intercept: log K = 0.4771 = log 3 K = 3 Now, at initial pressure P = 0.04 atm: x/m = KP^(1/n) x/m = 3 × (0.04)^2 x/m = 3 × 0.0016 x/m = 48 × 10^(-4) g Therefore, the mass of gas adsorbed per gram of adsorbent is 48 × 10^(-4) g.
