Consider the parabola y^2 = 4x. Let S be the focus of the parabola. A pair of tangents drawn to the parabola from the point P = (-2, 1) meet the parabola at P₁ and P₂. Let Q₁ and Q₂ be points on the lines SP₁ and SP₂ respectively such that PQ₁ is perpendicular to SP₁ and PQ₂ is perpendicular to SP₂. Then, which of the following is/are TRUE?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) SQ₁ = 2
  2. (B) Q₁Q₂ = 3√10/5
  3. (C) PQ₁ = 3
  4. (D) SQ₂ = 1

Answer

Correct answer: BCD

Solution

B, C, D Let P₁ and P₂ be (t₁², 2t₁) and (t₂², 2t₂) ⇒ P ≡ (t₁t₂, t₁ + t₂) ≡ (-2, 1) ⇒ t₁ = 2, t₂ = -1 or t₁ = -1, t₂ = 2 ⇒ P₁(4, 4) and P₂(1, -2) Slope of SP₁ = 4/3 slope of SP₂ = ∞ Equation of SP₁ y - 0 = (4/3)(x - 1) ⇒ 4x - 3y - 4 = 0 Equation of PQ₁ y - 1 = -(4/3)(x + 2) ⇒ 3x + 4y + 2 = 0 ∴ Q₁(2/5, -4/5) Equation of SP₂ x = 1 Equation of PQ₂ y = 1 ∴ Q₂(1, 1) ∴ SQ₁ = 1, Q₁Q₂ = (3√10)/5 PQ₁ = √[(-2 - 2/5)² + (1 + 4/5)²] = √[(144/25) + (81/25)] = 3 and SQ₂ = √[(1-1)² + (1-0)²] = 1 [Diagram showing a coordinate system with point P on the left, point S at (1,0) on the x-axis, and two parabolic curves P₁ and P₂ passing through points Q₁ and Q₂ respectively]
