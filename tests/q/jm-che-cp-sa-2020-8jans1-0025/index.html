NaClO3 is used, even in spacecraft, to produce O2. The daily consumption of pure O2 by a person is 492L at 1 atm, 300 K. How much amount of NaClO3, in grams, is required to produce O2 for the daily consumption of a person at 1 atm, 300 K?

Answer

Correct answer: 2130.00

Solution

Given reaction: NaClO3(s) + Fe(s) → O2(g) + NaCl(s) + FeO(s) Given data: - Volume of O2 consumed daily = 492 L - Pressure = 1 atm - Temperature = 300 K - Gas constant R = 0.082 L atm mol^(-1) K^(-1) Step 1: Calculate moles of O2 using ideal gas law PV = nRT n = PV/(RT) n(O2) = (1 × 492)/(0.082 × 300) n(O2) = 492/24.6 n(O2) = 20 moles Step 2: From the balanced equation, mole ratio of NaClO3 : O2 = 1 : 1 Therefore, moles of NaClO3 required = 20 moles Step 3: Calculate mass of NaClO3 Molar mass of NaClO3 = 23 + 35.5 + 3(16) = 106.5 g/mol Mass of NaClO3 = 20 × 106.5 = 2130 g
