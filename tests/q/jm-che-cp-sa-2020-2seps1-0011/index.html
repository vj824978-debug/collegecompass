In Carius method of estimation of halogen, 0.172g of an organic compound showed presence of 0.08g of bromine. Which of these is the correct structure of the compound:

  1. (1) Aniline with Br at para position (C6H6NBr)
  2. (2) H3C-CH2-Br (C3H7Br)
  3. (3) Aniline with Br at ortho and para positions (C6H5NBr2)
  4. (4) H3C-Br (CH3Br)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

In Carius method: mass of organic compound = 0.172 gm mass of Bromine = 0.08 gm Hence % of Bromine = (0.08/0.172) × 100 = 46.51 % For option (1): C6H6NBr Molecular weight = 6(12) + 6(1) + 14 + 80 = 72 + 6 + 14 + 80 = 172 %Br = (80/172) × 100 = 46.51% ✓ For option (2): CH3CH2Br or C3H7Br Molecular weight = 3(12) + 7(1) + 80 = 36 + 7 + 80 = 109 %Br = (80/109) × 100 = 73.33% ✗ For option (3): C6H5NBr2 Molecular weight = 6(12) + 5(1) + 14 + 2(80) = 72 + 5 + 14 + 160 = 251 %Br = (160/251) × 100 ≠ 46.51% ✗ For option (4): CH3Br Molecular weight = 12 + 3 + 80 = 95 %Br = (80/95) × 100 ≠ 46.51% ✗ Therefore, the correct structure is option (1): 4-bromoaniline (para-bromoaniline with NH2 and Br groups)
