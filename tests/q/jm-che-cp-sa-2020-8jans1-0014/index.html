An unsaturated hydrocarbon X absorbs two hydrogen molecules on catalytic hydrogenation, and also gives following reaction: X --O3--> A --[Ag(NH3)2]+]--> B(3-oxo-hexanedicarboxylic acid) X will be:

  1. (1) Cyclohex-2-enone (structure with O double bond at position 1, double bond at position 2)
  2. (2) Cyclohex-2-ene (structure with double bond at position 2)
  3. (3) Cyclohex-3-ene (structure with double bond at position 3)
  4. (4) Methylcyclopent-1-ene (structure with double bond and methyl substituent)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From the reaction sequence: 1) X absorbs 2 moles of H2, indicating X has 2 degrees of unsaturation (could be 2 double bonds, 1 triple bond, 1 ring + 1 double bond, etc.) 2) X undergoes ozonolysis followed by oxidative workup to give 3-oxo-hexanedicarboxylic acid 3) The product structure HOOC-CH2-C(=O)-CH2-CH2-COOH indicates: - A symmetrical cleavage product - A keto group in the middle - Two carboxylic acid groups 4) Working backwards from the product: - The molecule must contain a ketone (to form the C=O in product) - Ozonolysis gives symmetric fragments - This suggests X has both a ketone and an alkene 5) The structure of X must be cyclohex-2-enone: - Cyclohex-2-enone has a ketone at position 1 and a double bond between positions 2 and 3 - This accounts for 2 degrees of unsaturation - Upon hydrogenation: the C=C bond is saturated → cyclohexanone - Upon ozonolysis: the C=C bond is cleaved - The ketone carbonyl remains and helps form the product 6) Ozonolysis of cyclohex-2-enone followed by oxidation yields the required 3-oxo-hexanedicarboxylic acid product. Therefore, X is cyclohex-2-enone (option 1).
