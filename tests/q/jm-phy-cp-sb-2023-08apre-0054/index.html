The number density of free electrons in copper is nearly 8 × 10^28 m^-3. A copper wire has its area of cross section = 2 × 10^-6 m^2 and is carrying a current of 3.2 A. The drift speed of the electrons is _______ × 10^-6 ms^-1

Answer

Correct answer: 125

Solution

Using I = nAe*V_d where I is current, n is number density, A is cross-sectional area, e is electron charge (1.6 × 10^-19 C), and V_d is drift velocity. Rearranging: V_d = I/(nAe) = 3.2/(8 × 10^28 × 2 × 10^-6 × 1.6 × 10^-19) = 3.2/(16 × 1.6 × 1000) = 3.2/25600 = 125 × 10^-6 m/s
