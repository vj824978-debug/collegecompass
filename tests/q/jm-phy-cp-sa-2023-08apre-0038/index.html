For particle P revolving round the centre O with radius of circular path r and angular velocity ω, as shown in the figure, the projection of OP on the x-axis at time t is

  1. (1) x(t) = r cos(ωt - π/6)
  2. (2) x(t) = r cos(ωt + π/6)
  3. (3) x(t) = r sin(ωt + π/6)
  4. (4) x(t) = r cos(ωt)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

From the figure, at t=0, the particle P is at an angle of 30° from the x-axis. The projection of OP on the x-axis is x(t) = r cos(θ(t)) where θ(t) = ωt + initial angle. Since the initial angle is 30° = π/6, we have x(t) = r cos(ωt + π/6).
