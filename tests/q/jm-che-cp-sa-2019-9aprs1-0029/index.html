Which of the following statements is not true about sucrose? (1) On hydrolysis, it produces glucose and fructose (2) It is also named as invert sugar. The glycosidic linkage is present (3) between C1 of α-glucose and C1 of β-fructose (4) It is a non reducing sugar

  1. (1) On hydrolysis, it produces glucose and fructose
  2. (2) It is also named as invert sugar. The glycosidic linkage is present
  3. (3) between C1 of α-glucose and C1 of β-fructose
  4. (4) It is a non reducing sugar

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Sucrose + H2O → α-D-Glucose + β-D-Fructose Let us analyze each statement: (1) On hydrolysis, sucrose produces glucose and fructose - TRUE. Sucrose undergoes hydrolysis to form α-D-glucose and β-D-fructose. (2) It is also named as invert sugar. The glycosidic linkage is present - TRUE. Sucrose is called invert sugar because upon hydrolysis it forms glucose and fructose which are reducing sugars, and the optical rotation changes (inversion occurs). (3) Between C1 of α-glucose and C1 of β-fructose - FALSE/NOT TRUE. The glycosidic linkage in sucrose is between C1 of α-D-glucose and C2 of β-D-fructose, NOT C1 of β-fructose. This is the key point that makes this statement incorrect. (4) It is a non reducing sugar - TRUE. Sucrose is a non-reducing sugar because both anomeric carbons (C1 of glucose and C1 of fructose) are involved in the glycosidic linkage, making it unable to reduce Fehling's or Benedict's reagent. In β-D-Fructose → C-2 is involved In β-D-Glucose → C-1 is involved Therefore, statement (3) is NOT true about sucrose.
