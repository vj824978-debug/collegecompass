At time t = 0, a material is composed of two radioactive atoms A and B, where N_A(0) = 2N_B(0). The decay constant of both kind of radioactive atoms is λ. However, A disintegrates to B and B disintegrates to C. Which of the following figures represents the evolution of N_B(t)/N_B(0) with respect to time t?

  1. (1) Graph showing N_B(t)/N_B(0) rising to a peak at t = 1/(2λ) then decreasing
  2. (2) Graph showing N_B(t)/N_B(0) decreasing monotonically starting from 1 at t = 0, leveling off after t = 1/(2λ)
  3. (3) Graph showing N_B(t)/N_B(0) rising to a peak at t = 1/λ then decreasing
  4. (4) Graph showing N_B(t)/N_B(0) decreasing monotonically starting from 1 at t = 0, reaching near zero by t = 1/(2λ)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: N_A(0) = 2N_B(0), both have decay constant λ. A decays to B, B decays to C. The differential equation for N_B is: dN_B/dt = λN_A - λN_B = λ(2N_B(0))e^(-λt) - λN_B. Solving this gives N_B(t) = N_B(0)(1 + 2λt)e^(-λt). N_B(t) is maximum when dN_B/dt = 0, which gives t = 1/(2λ). At t = 0, N_B(t)/N_B(0) = 1. The ratio rises to a maximum at t = 1/(2λ) and then decreases as B disintegrates faster than it is produced.
