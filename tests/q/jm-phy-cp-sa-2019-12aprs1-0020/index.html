To verify Ohm's law, a student connects the voltmeter across the battery as shown in the figure. The measured voltage is plotted as a function of the current, and the following graph is obtained: [Circuit diagram with battery E, internal resistance r, external resistance R, voltmeter V, and ammeter A]. [Graph showing V (voltmeter reading) vs I (current in mA), with V-axis from 0 to 1.5 V and I-axis from 0 to 1000 mA = 1 A. The graph shows a linear relationship starting at 1.5 V at I=0 and decreasing to V_L at I=1000 mA]. If V_0 is almost zero, identify the correct statement:

  1. (1) The value of the resistance R is 1.5 Ω
  2. (2) The emf of the battery is 1.5 V and its internal resistance is 1.5 Ω
  3. (3) The emf of the battery is 1.5 V and the value of R is 1.5 Ω
  4. (4) The potential difference across the battery is 1.5 V when it sends a current of 1000 mA

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

When voltmeter reading is zero => E - ir = 0 From the circuit equation: E - (E/(R+r))r = 0 => 1 - r/(R+r) = 0 => R + r - r = 0 => R = 0: when voltmeter reading is zero => R_eq = r (for circuit) i = E/r => 1000 mA = 1.5/r (E = 1.5V from graph) => 1 = 1.5/r => r = 1.5 Ω From the graph, the y-intercept (voltmeter reading at zero current) is 1.5 V, which equals the emf E. The slope of the graph gives -r/(R+r) = -1.5/1000 / 1.5 = -1/(R+r) Since at I = 1000 mA = 1 A, V = 0, and E = 1.5 V: E = I(R + r) 1.5 = 1 × (R + r) R + r = 1.5 Ω Since r = 1.5 Ω and R + r = 1.5 Ω: R = 0 Ω Therefore, the emf of the battery is 1.5 V and its internal resistance is 1.5 Ω.
