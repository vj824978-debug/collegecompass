Which among the following statement(s) is(are) true for the extraction of aluminium from bauxite?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) Hydrated Al₂O₃ precipitates, when CO₂ is bubbled through a solution of sodium aluminate.
  2. (B) Addition of Na₃AlF₆ lowers the melting point of alumina.
  3. (C) CO₂ is evolved at the anode during electrolysis.
  4. (D) The cathode is a steel vessel with a lining of carbon.

Answer

Correct answer: ABCD

Solution

Sodium meta aluminate is precipitated in the form of Al(OH)₃ or Al₂O₃.xH₂O. i.e hydrated aluminium hydroxide by passing CO₂ gas. 2NaAlO₂ + CO₂(g) + 3H₂O(ℓ) → 2Al(OH)₃↓ + Na₂CO₃ Or Al₂O₃.xH₂O In electrolytic reduction of Al₂O₃ by Hall-Heroult process, cryolite (Na₃AlF₆) and fluorspar (CaF₂) are added in order to reduce the melting temperature of Al₂O₃. Anode is made up of graphite. So, C(s) + 2O²⁻ → CO₂(g) + 4e⁻ Cathode is a steel vessel lined with graphite.
