A point source of light is placed at the centre of curvature of a hemispherical surface. The source emits a power of 24 W. The radius of curvature of hemisphere is 10 cm and the inner surface is completely reflecting. The force on the hemisphere due to the light falling on it is _____ × 10^(-8) N.

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

dA = 2πR sin(θ)Rdθ = 2πR^2 sin(θ)dθ So, dF = 2 × (IdA)/c = (2 × 24)/(4πR^2) × (2πR^2 sin(θ)dθ)/c dF = (24/c) sin(θ)dθ This dF force will be radially outward so the component of this force in vertical direction is dF_y = dF cos(θ) F_y = ∫[0 to π/2] dF = (24/c) ∫[0 to π/2] sin(θ)cos(θ)dθ = 24/(2C) = 24/(2 × 3 × 10^8) = 4 × 10^(-8) N
