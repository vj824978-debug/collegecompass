The number of stereoisomers possible for 1,2-dimethyl cyclopropane is

  1. (1) Three
  2. (2) Two
  3. (3) One
  4. (4) Four

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

1,2-dimethyl cyclopropane has a cyclopropane ring with two methyl groups attached to adjacent carbons (positions 1 and 2). The cyclopropane ring is planar, and the two methyl groups can be oriented either on the same side (cis form) or on opposite sides (trans form) of the ring plane. The cis form is optically inactive (no chiral center makes it inactive). The trans form has two chiral centers and exists as a pair of enantiomers. Therefore, there are three stereoisomers total: one cis form and two enantiomeric trans forms.
