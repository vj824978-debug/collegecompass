Which of the following reaction is correct? (1) C2H5CONH2 + Br2 + NaOH → C2H5CH2NH2 + Na2CO3 + NaBr + H2O (2) CH3CH2CH2NH2 + HNO2, 0°C, H2O → CH3CH2OH + N2 + HCl (3) Cyclohexene + Br2 + UV light → Cyclohexene with two Br substituents (4) Toluene (methylbenzene) + HI → Cyclohexane with CH3 and I substituents

  1. (1) C2H5CONH2 + Br2 + NaOH → C2H5CH2NH2 + Na2CO3 + NaBr + H2O
  2. (2) CH3CH2CH2NH2 + HNO2, 0°C, H2O → CH3CH2OH + N2 + HCl
  3. (3) Cyclohexene + Br2 + UV light → Cyclohexene with two Br atoms (monobromination major product)
  4. (4) Toluene + HI → Cyclohexane with CH3 and I substituents

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Reaction (1): Hofmann bromamide reaction gives C2H5NH2, not C2H5CH2NH2. Reaction (2): HNO2 converts primary amines to alcohols via diazonium salts, but reaction shown is incomplete. Reaction (3): Cyclohexene + Br2 gives addition product (1,2-dibromocyclohexane), not substitution. Reaction (4): Toluene undergoes addition of HI to the aromatic ring (under appropriate conditions) giving cyclohexane derivative with CH3 and I substituents. This is the correct reaction.
