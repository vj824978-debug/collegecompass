Match gases under specified conditions listed in Column-I with their properties/laws in Column-II. Column-I: (A) hydrogen gas (P = 200 atm, T = 273K) (B) hydrogen gas (P ≈ 0, T = 273K) (C) CO₂ (P = 1 atm, T = 273K) (D) real gas with very large molar volume Column-II: (p) compressibility factor ≠ 1 (q) attractive forces are dominant (r) PV = nRT (s) P(V - nb) = nRT

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) A → p, s B → r C → p, q D → p, s
  2. (B) A → q, s B → r C → p, q D → q, s
  3. (C) A → r B → r ,s C → p, q D → p, s
  4. (D) A → p, s B → q C → p, q D → p, r

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

(A) Z = PV_m/RT at high pressure and low temperature. Equation (P + an²/V²)(V - nb) = nRT reduces to P(V - nb) = nRT. (B) For hydrogen gas value of Z = 1 at P = 0 and it increase continuously on increasing pressure. (C) CO₂ molecules have larger attractive forces, under normal conditions. (D) Z = PV_m/RT, at very large molar volume Z ≠ 1.
