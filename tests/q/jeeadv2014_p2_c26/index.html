The major product in the following reaction is [Figure: Starting material (A) shows a chain with Cl at one end and a ketone (C(=O)CH3) at the other end. Reaction conditions: 1. CH3MgBr, dry ether, 0°C 2. aqueous acid]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) (A) H3C followed by a chain and C(=O)CH3
  2. (B) (B) H2C=C(CH3)C(OH)(CH3)2
  3. (C) (C) A cyclic structure with oxygen in the ring and =CH2 attached
  4. (D) (D) A cyclic structure with oxygen in the ring and two CH3 groups attached to a carbon in the ring

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Step 1: The primary alcohol with a chlorine at the 4-position is treated with CH₃MgBr in dry ether at 0°C. The Grignard reagent attacks the OH group (after deprotonation) and the Mg coordinates to form a Grignard alkoxide intermediate with OMgX. Step 2: Upon aqueous acid workup, the Grignard reagent is quenched and the intermediate undergoes intramolecular cyclization. The nucleophilic alkoxide oxygen attacks the nearby carbon bearing the chlorine (SN2 mechanism), displacing chloride and forming a five-membered tetrahydrofuran (THF) ring. The product is a substituted THF with two methyl groups - one exocyclic methyl and one on the ring carbon adjacent to oxygen, plus another methyl substituent on the same carbon bearing the exocyclic methyl group.
