A radiation is emitted by 1000 W bulb and it generates an electric field and magnetic field at P, placed at a distance of 2 m. The efficiency of the bulb is 1.25%. The value of peak electric field at P is x × 10^1 V/m. Value of x is ______. (Rounded – off to the nearest integer) [Take ε₀ = 8.85 × 10^(-12) C² N^(-1) m^(-2), c=3×10^8 m s^(-1)]

Answer

Correct answer: 137

Solution

Using intensity formula I = (1/2)cε₀E₀² = η(P/4πr²). Given η = 1.25/100, P = 1000 W, r = 2 m. Calculate E₀ = sqrt(nP/(2πr²cε₀)) = sqrt(1.25/100 × 1000/(2 × 3.14 × 4 × 3 × 10^8 × 8.85 × 10^(-12))) = sqrt(12.5/(8 × 3.14 × 3 × 8.85 × 10^(-4))) = 13.69 V/m = 136.9 × 10^(-1) V/m. Therefore x = 137 (rounded to nearest integer).
