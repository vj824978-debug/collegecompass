A solution is 0.1 M in Cl⁻ and 0.001 M in CrO₄²⁻. Solid AgNO₃ is gradually added to it. Assuming that the addition does not change in volume and K_sp(AgCl) = 1.7×10^(-10) M² and K_sp(Ag₂CrO₄) = 1.9×10^(-10) M³. Select correct statement from the following:

  1. (1) Ag₂CrO₄ precipitates first because the amount of Ag⁺ needed is low.
  2. (2) Ag₂CrO₄ precipitates first as its K_sp is low.
  3. (3) AgCl will precipitate first as the amount of Ag⁺ needed to precipitate is low.
  4. (4) AgCl precipitates first because its K_sp is high.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For AgCl: [Ag⁺]_eq = K_sp/[Cl⁻] = 1.7×10^(-10)/0.1 = 1.7×10^(-9) M. For Ag₂CrO₄: [Ag⁺]_eq = sqrt(K_sp/[CrO₄²⁻]) = sqrt(1.9×10^(-10)/0.001) = 4.3×10^(-4) M. Since AgCl requires a lower [Ag⁺] concentration (1.7×10^(-9) M) to precipitate compared to Ag₂CrO₄ (4.3×10^(-4) M), AgCl will precipitate first.
