In the given circuit the input voltage Vin is shown in figure. The cut-in voltage of p-n junction diode (D1 or D2) is 0.6 V. Which of the following output voltage (Vo) waveform across the diode is correct?

  1. (1) Waveform showing full sinusoidal output with positive and negative peaks
  2. (2) Waveform showing only positive half of sinusoidal output
  3. (3) Waveform showing flat top at 10.6 V level
  4. (4) Waveform showing clipped output at 0.6 V level with both positive and negative portions

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The circuit is a precision diode clipper. When input voltage |Vin| ≤ 0.6V (cut-in voltage), the diodes are off and output follows the input. When input voltage exceeds ±0.6V, the diodes conduct and limit the output to ±0.6V. Therefore, the output waveform is clipped at ±0.6V, showing the input waveform clipped at the cut-in voltage levels.
