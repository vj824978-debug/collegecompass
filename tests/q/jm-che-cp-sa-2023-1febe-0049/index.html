Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R). Assertion (A): Gypsum is used for making fireproof wall boards. Reason (R): Gypsum is unstable at high temperatures. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

  1. (1) Both (A) and (R) are correct and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
  2. (2) (A) is correct but (R) is not correct
  3. (3) Both (A) and (R) are correct but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
  4. (4) (A) is not correct but (R) is correct

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Both statements are correct. However, the IInd statement (Reason) has no relation with the Ist statement (Assertion). Gypsum is indeed used for making fireproof wall boards, and it is also true that gypsum is unstable at high temperatures. However, the reason gypsum is used for fireproof wall boards is not because it is unstable at high temperatures. Rather, gypsum is used because it contains water of crystallization (CaSO4·2H2O), and when exposed to heat, this water is released, which absorbs heat energy and provides a cooling effect, thereby making it fireproof. The instability at high temperatures is a separate property but does not explain why it is used in fireproof applications. Therefore, while both (A) and (R) are correct statements, (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
