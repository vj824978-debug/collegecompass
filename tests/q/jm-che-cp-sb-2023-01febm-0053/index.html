25 mL of an aqueous solution of KCl was found to require 20 mL of 1 M AgNO3 solution when titrated using K2CrO4 as an indicator. What is the depression in freezing point of KCl solution of the given concentration? (Given: Kf = 2.0 kg mol^(-1)) Assume (1) 100% ionization and (2) Density of the aqueous solution as 1 g mol^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

From the titration: 25 × M = 20 × 1 M = 20/25 = 4/5 = 0.8 M For freezing point depression: ΔTf = (i)(Kf)(m) where i = van't Hoff factor, Kf = freezing point depression constant, m = molality For KCl with 100% ionization: i = 2 (K+ and Cl- ions) Assuming density of solution is 1 g mol^(-1), for 0.8 M solution: m = 0.8 molal ΔTf = (2)(2.0)(4/5) ΔTf = 4 × (4/5) ΔTf = 16/5 = 3.2 Nearest Integer = 3
