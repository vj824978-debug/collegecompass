The total pressure observed by mixing two liquids A and B is 350 mm Hg when their mole fractions are 0.7 and 0.3 respectively. The total pressure becomes 410 mm Hg if the mole fractions are changed to 0.2 and 0.8 respectively for A and B. The vapour pressure of pure A is ______ mm Hg. (Nearest integer). Consider the liquids and solutions behave ideally.

Answer

Correct answer: 314

Solution

Using Raoult's Law for ideal solutions, the total pressure is given by: P_total = x_A * P_A^0 + x_B * P_B^0 From the first condition: 350 = P_A^0(0.7) + P_B^0(0.3) ... (equation 1) From the second condition: 410 = P_A^0(0.2) + P_B^0(0.8) ... (equation 2) Multiplying equation 1 by 0.7: 245 = 0.49 * P_A^0 + 0.21 * P_B^0 Multiplying equation 2 by 0.35: 143.5 = 0.07 * P_A^0 + 0.28 * P_B^0 Alternatively, multiply equation 1 by 8 and equation 2 by 3: 2800 = 5.6 * P_A^0 + 2.4 * P_B^0 1230 = 0.6 * P_A^0 + 2.4 * P_B^0 Subtracting: 1570 = 5 * P_A^0 P_A^0 = 314 mm Hg Verification: From equation 1: 350 = 314(0.7) + P_B^0(0.3) 350 = 219.8 + 0.3 * P_B^0 P_B^0 = 433.33 ≈ 434 mm Hg Checking with equation 2: 410 = 314(0.2) + 434(0.8) 410 = 62.8 + 347.2 = 410 ✓ Therefore, P_A^0 = 314 mm Hg
