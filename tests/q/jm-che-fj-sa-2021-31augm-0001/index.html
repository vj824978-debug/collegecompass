Assertion (A): Metallic character decreases and non-metallic character increases on moving from left to right in a period. Reason (R): It is due to increase in ionisation enthalpy and decrease in electron gain enthalpy, when one moves from left to right in a period. Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) (A) is false but (R) is true
  2. (2) Both (A) and (R) are correct and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
  3. (3) Both (A) and (R) are correct but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
  4. (4) (A) is true but (R) is false

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Assertion (A) is correct: Metallic character decreases from left to right in a period and non-metallic character increases from left to right. Reason (R) is incorrect: The primary reason is increase in ionisation enthalpy AND increase in electron gain enthalpy (not decrease). As we move left to right, both ionisation enthalpy and electron gain enthalpy increase, making elements more non-metallic. Therefore, (A) is true but (R) is false.
