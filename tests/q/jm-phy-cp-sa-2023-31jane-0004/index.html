A body is moving with constant speed, in a circle of radius 10 m. The body completes one revolution in 4 s. At the end of 3rd second, the displacement of body (in m) from its starting point is:

  1. (1) 30
  2. (2) 15π
  3. (3) 5π
  4. (4) 10√2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

r = 10 m T = 4 sec At t = 3 s, the body has completed 3/4 of the circle. Angle covered in 3 seconds: θ = (3/4) × 2π = (3π/2) radians Starting point: t = 0 (at top of circle) Position at t = 3 s: The body has moved 3/4 of the circumference, which corresponds to an angle of 270° or 3π/2 radians from the starting point. Using the diagram, if we place the starting point at the top (t = 0): - At t = 1 s: 1/4 revolution (90°) - position at right - At t = 2 s: 1/2 revolution (180°) - position at bottom - At t = 3 s: 3/4 revolution (270°) - position at left The displacement is the straight-line distance from starting point (top) to final position (left side). Using the circle geometry, when the body is at 3/4 revolution from the starting point: The two radii form a 270° angle, or equivalently, a 90° angle from the opposite direction. Displacement d = sqrt(r^2 + r^2 - 2r^2×cos(270°)) Or more directly: d = sqrt(r^2 + r^2) = r√2 d = 10√2 m
