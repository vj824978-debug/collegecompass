The organic compound that gives following qualitative analysis is: Test (a) Dil. HCl - Insoluble (b) NaOH solution - soluble (c) Br2/water - Decolourization

  1. (1) Cyclohexanol
  2. (2) Phenol
  3. (3) Cyclohexylamine
  4. (4) Aniline

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Analysis of the qualitative tests: Test (a) Dil. HCl - Insoluble: The compound does not dissolve in dilute HCl, eliminating aniline (4) and cyclohexylamine (3) as they are amines and would form soluble salts with HCl. Test (b) NaOH solution - soluble: The compound dissolves in NaOH solution. This indicates an acidic compound. Phenol (2) is acidic due to its -OH group attached to the benzene ring and forms a soluble sodium phenoxide salt. Cyclohexanol (1) is not acidic enough to dissolve significantly in NaOH. Test (c) Br2/water - Decolourization: The bromine water is decolorized, indicating the presence of an unsaturated system or an easily oxidizable/reactive aromatic group. Phenol undergoes bromination readily due to the activation of the benzene ring by the -OH group, causing decolorization of Br2/water. The compound that satisfies all three conditions is phenol (2): - Insoluble in dilute HCl (phenol is a weak acid) - Soluble in NaOH (forms sodium phenoxide, C6H5O-Na+) - Decolorizes Br2/water (undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution)
