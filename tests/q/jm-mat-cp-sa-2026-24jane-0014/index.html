Let X = {x ∈ N : 1 ≤ x ≤ 19} and for some a, b ∈ R, Y = {ax + b : x ∈ X}. If the mean and variance of the elements of Y are 30 and 750 respectively then the sum of all possible values of b is

  1. (1) 20
  2. (2) 80
  3. (3) 100
  4. (4) 60

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Σy_i = aΣx_i + Σb = a × (1 + 2 + ..... + 19) + 19b Σy_i/19 = (a × 19 × 20)/(2 × 19) + b 30 = 10a + b .....(1) Variance of X = Σx_i^2/19 - (Σx_i/19)^2 = (19 × 20 × 39)/(19 × 6) - (10)^2 = 30 Variance of Y = a^2 (variance of X) 750 = a^2 × 30 a^2 = 25 ⟹ a = ±5 if a = +5 ⟹ b = 30 - 50 = -20 ....from (1) if a = -5 ⟹ b = 30 + 50 = 80 ....from (1) sum of values of b = 80 - 20 = 60 options (4)
