Remainder when 64^3232 is divided by 9 is equal to _________.

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

To find remainder when 64^3232 is divided by 9: 64 ≡ 1 (mod 9) Therefore, 64^3232 ≡ 1^3232 (mod 9) 64^3232 ≡ 1 (mod 9) Remainder = 1
