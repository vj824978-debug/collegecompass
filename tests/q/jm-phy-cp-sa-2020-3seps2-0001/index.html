To raise the temperature of a certain mass of gas by 50°C at a constant pressure, 160 calories of heat is required. When the same mass of gas is cooled by 100°C at constant volume, 240 calories of heat is released. How many degrees of freedom does each molecules of this gas have (assume gas to be ideal)?

  1. (1) 2
  2. (2) 5
  3. (3) 6
  4. (4) 3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3
