A stone is projected at angle 30° to the horizontal. The ratio of kinetic energy of the stone at point of projection to its kinetic energy at the highest point of flight will be

  1. (1) 4 : 3
  2. (2) 4 : 1
  3. (3) 1 : 2
  4. (4) 1 : 4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Let the initial velocity be v at angle 30° to the horizontal. At point of projection: KE_in = (1/2)mv^2 At the highest point of flight, the vertical component of velocity becomes zero and only horizontal component remains: v_horizontal = v*cos(30°) = v*(sqrt(3)/2) At the highest point: KE_final = (1/2)m(v*cos(30°))^2 = (1/2)mv^2*cos^2(30°) = (1/2)mv^2*(sqrt(3)/2)^2 = (1/2)mv^2*(3/4) Ratio: KE_in/KE_final = [(1/2)mv^2]/[(1/2)mv^2*(3/4)] = 1/(3/4) = 4/3 Therefore, KE_in : KE_final = 4 : 3
