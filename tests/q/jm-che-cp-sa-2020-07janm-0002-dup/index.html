The value of alpha for which 4*alpha * integral from -1 to 2 of e^(-alpha*|x|) dx = 5, is:

  1. (1) log_e(sqrt(2))
  2. (2) log_e(3/2)
  3. (3) log_e(4/3)
  4. (4) log_e(2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

4*alpha * integral from -1 to 2 of e^(-alpha*|x|) dx = 5 4*alpha * [integral from -1 to 0 of e^(alpha*x) dx + integral from 0 to 2 of e^(-alpha*x) dx] = 5 4*alpha * [(e^(alpha*x)/alpha) from -1 to 0 + (e^(-alpha*x)/(-alpha)) from 0 to 2] = 5 4*alpha * [(1 - e^(-alpha))/alpha - (e^(-2*alpha) - 1)/alpha] = 5 4*alpha * [(1 - e^(-alpha) - e^(-2*alpha) + 1)/alpha] = 5 Let e^(-alpha) = t 4*alpha * [(2 - t - t^2)/alpha] = 5 4*(2 - t - t^2) = 5 8 - 4*t - 4*t^2 = 5 4*t^2 + 4*t - 3 = 0 t = (-4 ± sqrt(16 + 48))/8 = (-4 ± 8)/8 t = 1/2 or t = -3/2 Since t = e^(-alpha) > 0, we have t = 1/2 e^(-alpha) = 1/2 alpha = ln(2) = log_e(2)
