A wire is bent in the shape of a right angled triangle and is placed in front of a concave mirror of focal length f, as shown in the figure. Which of the figures shown in the four options qualitatively represent(s) the shape of the image of the bent wire? (These figures are not to scale.) [FIGURE: A concave mirror with a right-angled triangle wire in front of it. The triangle has a 45° angle, with one leg horizontal at distance f/2 from the mirror vertex and extending to distance f]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [FIGURE: A triangle with angle α labeled, where α > 45°, with a vertical dashed line]
  2. (B) [FIGURE: A curved line approaching infinity (∞) with a vertical dashed line at the bottom]
  3. (C) [FIGURE: A triangle with angle α labeled, where 0 < α < 45°, with a vertical dashed line]
  4. (D) [FIGURE: A curved line from infinity (∞) approaching a vertical dashed line at the bottom]

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

tan φ = x₀/(f - x₀) h/x = x₀/(f - x₀) ⟹ x = (h/x₀)(f - x₀) [Diagram shows a curved mirror with focal point f, object at distance x₀ from mirror, height h, angles φ and θ marked, and distances 2f marked on horizontal axis] tan θ = x₀/(f + x₀) h/(x + 2f) = x₀/(f + x₀) ⟹ (h/x₀)(f + x₀) = x + 2f ⟹ (h/x₀)(f + x₀) = (h/x₀)(f - x₀) + 2f ⟹ 2hx₀ = 2fx₀ ∴ h = f
