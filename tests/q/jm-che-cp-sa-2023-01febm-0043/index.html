But-2-yne is reacted separately with one mole of Hydrogen as shown below. B is formed by reaction of CH3-C≡C-CH3 with Na in liq NH3. A is formed by reaction of CH3-C≡C-CH3 with Pd/C and H2 under heat. (A) A is more than soluble than B. (B) The boiling point & melting point of A are higher and lower than B respectively. (C) A is more polar than B because dipole moment of A is zero. (D) Br2 adds easily to B than A. Identify the incorrect statements from the option given below

  1. (1) A, C & D only
  2. (2) B, C & D only
  3. (3) B and C only
  4. (4) A and B only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

A : Cis – But-2-ene B : Trans-But-2-ene BP : A > B mp : B > A μ-order : B > A (μ of A = 0) Addition of Br2 is easy in A. Analysis of statements: (A) A is more than soluble than B - CORRECT (cis isomer is more polar and soluble in polar solvents) (B) The boiling point & melting point of A are higher and lower than B respectively - INCORRECT (BP of A > B is correct, but mp of B > A, so statement is contradictory in saying "lower than B") (C) A is more polar than B because dipole moment of A is zero - INCORRECT (A has zero dipole moment, making it LESS polar than B, not more polar. B is trans isomer with net dipole moment) (D) Br2 adds easily to B than A - INCORRECT (Addition of Br2 is easy in A because cis isomer is more strained and reactive) Incorrect statements are B, C & D only
