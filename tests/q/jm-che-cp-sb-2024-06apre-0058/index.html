Consider the two different first order reactions given below: A + B → C (Reaction 1) and P → Q (Reaction 2). The ratio of the half life of Reaction 1 : Reaction 2 is 5 : 2. If t₁ and t₂ represent the time taken to complete 2/3 rd and 4/5 th of reaction 1 and Reaction 2, respectively, then the value of the ratio t₁ : t₂ is _______ × 10⁻¹ (nearest integer). [Given: log₁₀(3) = 0.477 and log₁₀(5) = 0.699]

Answer

Correct answer: 17

Solution

For first order reactions: (t₁/₂)ᵢ / (t₁/₂)ᵢᵢ = K₂/K₁ = 5/2, so K₁t₁ = ln(1/(1-2/3)) = ln(3) and K₂t₂ = ln(1/(1-4/5)) = ln(5). Therefore (K₁/K₂) × (t₁/t₂) = ln(3)/ln(5) = 0.477/0.699. Thus t₁/t₂ = (0.477/0.699) × (5/2) = 1.7 = 17 × 10⁻¹
