Two dice are rolled. If both dice have six faces numbered 1,2,3,5,7 and 11, then the probability that the sum of the numbers on the top faces is less than or equal to 8 is:

  1. (1) 1/2
  2. (2) 17/36
  3. (3) 4/9
  4. (4) 5/12

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Total number of outcomes n(s) = 6^2 = 36. Favorable outcomes E where sum <= 8 are: (1,1), (1,2), (1,3), (1,5), (1,7), (2,1), (2,2), (2,3), (2,5), (3,1), (3,2), (3,3), (3,5), (5,1), (5,2), (5,3), (7,1). Number of favorable outcomes n(E) = 17. Required probability = n(E)/n(S) = 17/36.
