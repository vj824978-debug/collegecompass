Find out the major products from the following reactions. Starting material is a branched alkene (appears to be 2-methylbut-3-en-1-ol or similar structure). Two reaction pathways shown: Top pathway: BH3, THF followed by H2O2/OH^- gives product A. Bottom pathway: Hg(OAc)2, H2O followed by NaBH4 gives product B.

  1. (1) (1) A = branched alcohol with OH on tertiary carbon, B = branched primary alcohol
  2. (2) (2) A = branched primary alcohol with CH2OH group, B = branched tertiary alcohol with OH
  3. (3) (3) A = branched tertiary alcohol with OH, B = branched primary alcohol with CH2OH group
  4. (4) (4) A = branched primary alcohol with CH2OH group, B = branched primary alcohol with CH2OH group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The starting material is a branched alkene (2-methylbut-3-en-1-ol structure). Pathway 1 - BH3/THF followed by H2O2/OH^-: BH3 adds to alkene following anti-Markovnikov's rule (boron adds to less substituted carbon, H adds to more substituted carbon). This is oxymercuration-type hydration but with borane. After hydroboration-oxidation with H2O2/OH^-, the OH group adds anti-Markovnikov. Product A = primary alcohol with -CH2OH group (the OH adds to the terminal carbon of the double bond) Pathway 2 - Hg(OAc)2/H2O followed by NaBH4: Hg(OAc)2 forms a mercurinium ion intermediate. The mechanism follows Markovnikov's rule (OH adds to more substituted carbon, H adds to less substituted carbon). NaBH4 reduces the C-HgOAc bond to C-H. Product B = tertiary alcohol with OH on the more substituted carbon of the original double bond. Therefore: A = branched primary alcohol (CH2OH group from anti-Markovnikov addition) B = branched tertiary alcohol (OH from Markovnikov addition) The correct answer is option (2).
