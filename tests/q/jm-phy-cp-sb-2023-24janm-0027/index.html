A hollow cylindrical conductor has length of 3.14 m, while its inner and outer diameters are 4 mm and 8 mm respectively. The resistance of the conductor is n × 10^(-3) Ω. If the resistivity of the material is 2.4 × 10^(-8) Ω·m, the value of n is _____.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Given: - Length, l = 3.14 m - Inner diameter = 4 mm, so inner radius r₁ = 2 mm = 2 × 10^(-3) m - Outer diameter = 8 mm, so outer radius r₂ = 4 mm = 4 × 10^(-3) m - Resistivity, ρ = 2.4 × 10^(-8) Ω·m - Resistance, R = n × 10^(-3) Ω For a hollow cylindrical conductor, the cross-sectional area is: A = π(r₂² - r₁²) A = π[(4 × 10^(-3))² - (2 × 10^(-3))²] A = π[16 × 10^(-6) - 4 × 10^(-6)] A = π × 12 × 10^(-6) m² Using the formula R = ρl/A: R = (2.4 × 10^(-8) × 3.14)/(π × 12 × 10^(-6)) R = (2.4 × 10^(-8) × 3.14)/(π(16 - 4) × 10^(-6)) R = (2.4 × 10^(-8) × 3.14)/(π × 12 × 10^(-6)) R = (2.4/12) × 10^(-2) Ω R = (2.4/12) × 10^(-2) Ω R = 0.2 × 10^(-2) Ω R = 2 × 10^(-3) Ω Comparing with R = n × 10^(-3) Ω: n = 2
