All surfaces shown in figure are assumed to be frictionless and all the pulleys and the string are light. The acceleration of the block of mass 2 kg is:

  1. (1) g
  2. (2) g/2
  3. (3) g/4
  4. (4) g/3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For the 4 kg hanging mass: 4g - 2T = 4a. For the 2 kg block on incline: T - 2g sin(30°) = 2a. From first equation: T = (4g - 4a)/2 = 2g - 2a. Substituting in second equation: 2g - 2a - 2g sin(30°) = 2a. Since sin(30°) = 1/2: 2g - 2a - g = 2a. Therefore: g = 4a, so a = g/4.
