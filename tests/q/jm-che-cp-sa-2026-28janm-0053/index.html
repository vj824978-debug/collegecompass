At T(K), 2 moles of liquid A and 3 moles of liquid B are mixed. The vapour pressure of ideal solution formed is 320 mm Hg. At this stage, one mole of A and one mole of B are added to the solution. The vapour pressure is now measured as 328.6 mm Hg. The vapour pressure (in mm Hg) of A and B are respectively:

  1. (1) 300, 200
  2. (2) 600, 400
  3. (3) 400, 300
  4. (4) 500, 200

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

2 moles of A + 3 moles of B X_A = 2/5, X_B = 3/5 P_s = X_A P°_A + X_B P°_B 320 = P_A(2/5) + P_B(3/5) 2P°_A + 3P°_B = 1600 ...(I) Now 1 mole of A & 1 mole of B is added X'_A = 3/7, X'_B = 4/7 P'_s = 328.6 = P'_A(3/7) + P'_B(4/7) 3P°_A + 4P°_B = 2300.2 ...(II) Now eq (I) ×3 - eq (II) ×2 6P°_A + 9P°_B = 4800 6P°_A + 8P°_B = 4600.4 P°_B = 200 mm of Hg P°_A = 500 mm of Hg
