Following figure shows spectrum of an ideal black body at four different temperatures. The number of correct statement/s from the following is ___

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

A. T_1 > T_2 > T_3 > T_4 From the figure, the peak of the curve for T_1 is at the highest energy distribution, and as we move from T_1 to T_4, the peak shifts to longer wavelengths. According to Wien's displacement law, lambda_max is inversely proportional to temperature. Since the peak shifts to longer wavelengths as we go from T_1 to T_4, we have T_1 > T_2 > T_3 > T_4. Statement A is incorrect. B. It is incorrect as particles do not undergo simple harmonic motion. A black body is an ideal body that absorbs and emits all wavelengths of radiation. The spectrum is explained by the quantisation of energy levels in atoms, not by simple harmonic motion of particles. C. It is correct According to Wien's displacement law: lambda_max × T = constant As temperature increases, lambda_max decreases, meaning the peak shifts to shorter wavelengths. This is clearly visible in the figure where T_1 (highest temperature) has its peak at the shortest wavelength. D. It is incorrect According to Wien's displacement law: lambda_max × T = constant If we denote nu as frequency, then nu = c/lambda, so lambda = c/nu Therefore: (c/nu) × T = constant This gives: T/nu = constant, not T/nu ≠ constant Statement D contradicts Wien's law. E. It is correct The spectrum of a black body is explained by Planck's quantisation theory, which states that energy is emitted in discrete packets called quanta (photons). The energy of each photon is E = h*nu, where h is Planck's constant and nu is frequency. The distribution curve shown follows Planck's radiation law, which is based on energy quantisation. Therefore, statements C and E are correct. The number of correct statements is 2.
