The number of hydroxyl group(s) in Q is [Chemical structure showing a bicyclic compound with a hydroxyl group and two methyl groups attached to a fused ring system. The structure undergoes two reactions: first with H+ and heat to form P, then P reacts with aqueous dilute KMnO4 (excess) at 0°C to form Q]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

[Bicyclic fused ring system with decalin-like structure] Step 1: The starting material undergoes acid-catalyzed dehydration (H⁺/Δ) to form a carbocation intermediate. Step 2: The carbocation rearranges and loses a proton to form alkene product P, which contains a methyl substituent on the bicyclic system. Step 3: Product P undergoes dihydroxylation using aqueous dilute KMnO₄ at 0°C (cold, dilute conditions favor syn addition). Step 4: The syn dihydroxylation adds two OH groups across the double bond in P, resulting in product Q with four hydroxyl groups total on the bicyclic system, with defined stereochemistry shown by the wedge and dash bonds.
