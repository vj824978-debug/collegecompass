The major product of the following reaction is: A cyclohexene ring with OH group and O double bonded to one carbon, treated with 1. PBr3 and 2. KOH(alc.)

  1. (1) A cyclohexene ring with OH group and O double bonded to one carbon
  2. (2) A benzene ring with OH group
  3. (3) A cyclohexene ring with O double bonded to one carbon
  4. (4) A cyclohexene ring with O double bonded to one carbon (saturated ring)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Starting material: cyclohexanone with OH group at adjacent position (hydroxycyclohexanone) Step 1: Treatment with PBr3 - PBr3 converts the OH group to OPBr2 (converts hydroxyl to a better leaving group) - Forms intermediate with B2 (boron-containing species from PBr3) Step 2: Treatment with alc. KOH (alcoholic potassium hydroxide) - Eliminates the OPBr2 group - Forms a double bond between the carbon that had OH and the adjacent carbon - This creates an unsaturated ketone (alpha,beta-unsaturated carbonyl compound) - The result is a cyclohexene ring with a C=O group Major product: cyclohexene with ketone functional group at one position and a double bond in the ring The OH group is converted to a leaving group by PBr3, then eliminated by KOH to form the C=C double bond adjacent to the carbonyl group.
