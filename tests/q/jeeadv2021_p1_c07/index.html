For the reaction, X(s) ⇌ Y(s) + Z(g), the plot of ln(pz/p°) versus 10^4/T is given below (in solid line), where pz is the pressure (in bar) of the gas Z at temperature T and p° = 1 bar. [Graph showing ln(pz/p°) on y-axis versus 10^4/T (K^-1) on x-axis. A downward sloping line passes through points at approximately (10, -3) and (12, -7)] (Given: d(ln K)/d(1/T) = -ΔH°/R, where the equilibrium constant, K = pz/p°, and the gas constant, R = 8.314 J K^-1 mol^-1) Q.7 The value of standard enthalpy, ΔH° (in kJ mol^-1) for the given reaction is ____.

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

Answer

Correct answer: 166.28

Solution

X(s) ⇌ Y(s) + Z(g) Kₚ = pz/p⁰, also ΔG⁰ = -RT ln kₚ = -RT ln(pz/p⁰) Now, ΔG⁰ = ΔH⁰ - TΔS⁰ -RT ln(pz/p⁰) = ΔH⁰ - TΔS⁰ ln(pz/p⁰) = -(ΔH⁰/R)·(1/T) + ΔS⁰/R ...(1) [Graph showing ln(pz/p⁰) vs (10⁴/T)K⁻¹ with linear relationship, y-intercept around -3, point at x=10 and x=12, reaching -7] (1) ⇒ ln(pz/p⁰) = -(ΔH⁰/10⁴R) × 10⁴ + ΔS⁰/T ...(2) Slope of the line = -ΔH⁰/10⁴R = [-7-(-3)]/(12-10) = -2 ∴ ΔH⁰ = 2 × R × 10⁴ = 2 × 8.314 × 10⁻³ × 10⁴ = 1.66.28 kJ mol⁻¹ K⁻¹
