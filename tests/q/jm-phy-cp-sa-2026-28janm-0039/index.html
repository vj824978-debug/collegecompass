Two wires A and B made of different materials of length 6.0 cm and 5.4 cm, respectively and area of cross sections 3.0×10^(-5) m^2 and 4.5×10^(-5) m^2, respectively are stretched by the same magnitude under a given load. The ratio of the Young's modulus of A to that of B is x:3. The value of x is ____.

  1. (1) 1
  2. (2) 4
  3. (3) 2
  4. (4) 5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Young's modulus is defined as: Y = (F/A)/(Δl/l) = Fl/(AΔl) For two wires stretched by the same magnitude (same force F and same extension Δl): Y_A/Y_B = (l_A/l_B) × (A_B/A_A) Given: l_A = 6.0 cm l_B = 5.4 cm A_A = 3.0×10^(-5) m^2 A_B = 4.5×10^(-5) m^2 Y_A/Y_B = (6/5.4) × (4.5×10^(-5))/(3.0×10^(-5)) = (6/5.4) × (4.5/3.0) = (6/5.4) × (9/5.4) = (6/5.4) × (4.5/3.0) = (6/5.4) × (3/2) = 9/5.4 = 9/(27/5) = 9 × 5/27 = 45/27 = 5/3 Therefore, x/3 = 5/3 x = 5
