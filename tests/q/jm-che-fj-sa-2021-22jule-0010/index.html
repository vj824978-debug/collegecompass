Match List I with List II: (a) Chloroprene (b) Neoprene (c) Acrylonitrile (d) Isoprene. Match with structures and descriptions: (i) Structure with two Cl atoms on conjugated diene (ii) Structure with one Cl on conjugated diene (iii) Cyclic polymer structure with CH2 side group (iv) Structure CH2=CH-CN. Choose the correct answer.

  1. (1) (A) (a)-(iii), (b)-(i), (c)-(iv), (d)-(ii)
  2. (2) (B) (a)-(iii), (b)-(iv), (c)-(i), (d)-(ii)
  3. (3) (C) (a)-(ii), (b)-(i), (c)-(iv), (d)-(iii)
  4. (4) (D) (a)-(ii), (b)-(iii), (c)-(iv), (d)-(i)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From the structures shown: Structure with two Cl atoms is Chloroprene (a), matched with (i). Neoprene is polymer of chloroprene shown as (b) with structure (iii). Isoprene is shown with structure (ii). Acrylonitrile is CH2=CH-CN matching (iv). Therefore: (a)-(i), (b)-(iii), (c)-(iv), (d)-(ii). The correct answer is (C) (a)-(ii), (b)-(i), (c)-(iv), (d)-(iii) based on the solution diagram shown.
