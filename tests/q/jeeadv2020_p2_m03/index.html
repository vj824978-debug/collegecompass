Let O be the centre of the circle x² + y² = r², where r > √3/2. Suppose PQ is a chord of this circle and the equation of the line passing through P and Q is 2x + 4y = 5. If the centre of the circumcircle of the triangle OPQ lies on the line x + 2y = 4, then the value of r is ____

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Let R(h, k) be point of intersection of tangents at P and Q on x² - y² = r² ⇒ equation of chord of contact PQ is xh + yk = r² Which is also 2x + 4y = 5 ⇒ (h, k) ≡ (2r²/5, 4r²/5) Mid-point of OR is circum-centre of △OPQ ⇒ (r²/5, 2r²/5) lies on x + 2y = 4 ⇒ r = 2
