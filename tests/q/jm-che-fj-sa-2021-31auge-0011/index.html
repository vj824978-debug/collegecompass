The structures of A and B formed in the following reaction are: [Ph = -C6H5]. Benzene + phthalic anhydride reacted with AlCl3 (2eq) gives A, then Zn/Hg and HCl gives B

  1. (1) A = Ph-C(=O)-C(=O)-Ph with two phenyl groups, B = Ph-CH2-CH2-Ph
  2. (2) A = Ph-C(=O)-C(=O)-Ph with two phenyl groups, B = Ph-CH(OH)-CH2-Ph
  3. (3) A = Ph-CH(=O)-CH2-CH2-C(=O)OH, B = Ph-CH2-CH2-CH2-C(=O)OH
  4. (4) A = Ph-CH(=O)-CH2-CH2-C(=O)OH, B = Ph-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH2-OH

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Phthalic anhydride reacts with benzene in presence of AlCl3 (Friedel-Crafts acylation) to form A, which is a diketone with formula containing Ph-C(=O) groups. Treatment with Zn/Hg and HCl (Clemmensen reduction) reduces the carbonyl groups to CH2 groups to form B. The correct answer is (D) where A is the diketo compound with carboxylic acid moiety and B is the corresponding reduced product with primary alcohol.
