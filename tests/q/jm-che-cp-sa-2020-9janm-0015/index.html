The major product Z obtained in the following reaction scheme is: starting material has NH2 and Br groups on benzene ring. Step 1: NaNO2/HCl at 273-178 K gives X. Step 2: CuBr2 gives Y. Step 3: HNO3/H2SO4 gives Z.

  1. (1) Br and NO2 groups at positions 1,3,5 on benzene ring (1,3,5-dibromobenzene with NO2 at position 1)
  2. (2) Br and NO2 groups at positions 1,2,4 on benzene ring (1,2-dibromobenzene with NO2 at position 4)
  3. (3) Br and NO2 groups at positions 1,2,3 on benzene ring (1,2,3-tribromobenzene with NO2 groups at positions 1,3)
  4. (4) Br and NO2 groups at positions 1,2,4,6 on benzene ring (multiple bromines with two NO2 groups)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Step 1: 4-bromoaniline (starting material with NH2 and Br) reacts with NaNO2/HCl at low temperature (273-178 K) to form diazonium salt X. The diazonium ion N2+ is formed from the amino group. Step 2: The diazonium compound X reacts with CuBr2 (Sandmeyer reaction) where the diazonium group (-N2+) is replaced by Br, giving Y which is 1,4-dibromobenzene (para-dibromobenzene). Step 3: Y undergoes nitration with HNO3/H2SO4. Since both bromine atoms are deactivating groups (halogens are electron-withdrawing), they are ortho/para directors. However, the two bromines are in para positions, so nitration will occur at the ortho positions relative to each Br. This gives primarily 2,5-dibromonitrobe­nzene (1,2,4-trisubstituted product with Br at positions 1,2,4 and NO2 at position 4) or the isomer with NO2 at position 2. The major product is the 1,2,4-isomer where NO2 enters at the position ortho to one Br and meta to the other Br.
