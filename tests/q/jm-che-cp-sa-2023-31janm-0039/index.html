Consider the following reaction: Propanal Methanal with (i) dil.NaOH (ii) A (iii) NaCN (iv) H3O+ gives Product B (C5H8O3). The correct statement for product B is. It is

  1. (1) racemic mixture and gives a gas with saturated NaHCO3 solution
  2. (2) optically active alcohol and is neutral
  3. (3) optically active and adds one mole of bromine
  4. (4) racemic mixture and is neutral

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

CH3-CH2CHO + HCHO with dil.base gives CH3-CH-CHO with CH2OH (aldol condensation) Then with HCN addition gives CH3-C(OH)(CH2CN)-CHO with a cyanohydrin intermediate With heating (delta), hydrolysis and further reactions lead to: CH3-C(OH)(OH)-CH-COOH with CH3 group This gives a racemic mixture (the reaction creates a new stereocenter without asymmetric induction, so equal amounts of both enantiomers form). The product contains a carboxylic acid group (-COOH), which: - Reacts with NaHCO3 to produce CO2 gas (effervescence observed) - Makes the compound acidic, not neutral The product is a hydroxy acid with the molecular formula C5H8O3, which matches the racemic mixture that gives gas with saturated NaHCO3 solution. Therefore, the correct answer is option (1): racemic mixture and gives a gas with saturated NaHCO3 solution
