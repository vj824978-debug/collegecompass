A cube of ice floats partly in water and partly in kerosene oil. The ratio of volume of ice immersed in water to that in kerosene oil (specific gravity of Kerosene oil = 0.8, specific gravity of ice = 0.9)

  1. (1) 8 : 9
  2. (2) 5 : 4
  3. (3) 9 : 10
  4. (4) 1 : 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let v1 = volume immersed in water, v2 = volume immersed in kerosene oil. By equilibrium: v1*ρ_w*g + v2*ρ_o*g = (v1 + v2)*ρ_c*g. Substituting ρ_w = 1, ρ_o = 0.8, ρ_c = 0.9: v1 + 0.8*v2 = 0.9*v1 + 0.9*v2. Simplifying: v1 - 0.9*v1 = 0.9*v2 - 0.8*v2, which gives 0.1*v1 = 0.1*v2, therefore v1 : v2 = 1 : 1
