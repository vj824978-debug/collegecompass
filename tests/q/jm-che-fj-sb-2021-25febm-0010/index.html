Consider the following chemical reaction. HC≡CH under 1) Red hot Fe tube, 873 K and 2) CO, HCl, AlCl3 gives Product. The number of sp2 hybridized carbon atoms (s) present in the product is __________.

Answer

Correct answer: 7

Solution

Step 1: HC≡CH (acetylene) undergoes trimerization at 873 K in red hot Fe tube to form benzene (C6H6). Step 2: Benzene is then treated with CO, HCl, and AlCl3 (Gattermann-Koch reaction) to form benzaldehyde (C6H5CHO). Step 3: In benzaldehyde, the benzene ring has 6 sp2 hybridized carbons, and the aldehyde carbon (CHO) is also sp2 hybridized. Step 4: Total sp2 hybridized carbon atoms = 6 (from benzene ring) + 1 (aldehyde carbon) = 7.
