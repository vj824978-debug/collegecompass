In the following reaction; xA → yB Log₁₀[-d[A]/dt] = Log₁₀[d[B]/dt] + 0.3010 'A' and 'B' respectively can be:

  1. (1) n-Butane and Iso-butane
  2. (2) C₂H₄ and C₄H₈
  3. (3) C₂H₄ and C₆H₆
  4. (4) N₂O₄ and NO₂

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

xA → yB From stoichiometry: -1/x × dA/dt = +1/y × dB/dt -dA/dt = +x/y × dB/dt -----(1) Given: Log₁₀[-d[A]/dt] = Log₁₀[d[B]/dt] + 0.3010 ∴ log[-dA/dt] = log[+dB/dt] + log2 -dA/dt = +2 × dB/dt -----(2) Compare (1) & (2): x/y = 2/1 x = 2, y = 1 Therefore the reaction is: 2A → 1B Looking at the options: (1) n-Butane and Iso-butane - isomers, same molecular formula, cannot be in stoichiometric ratio 2:1 (2) C₂H₄ and C₄H₈ - 2C₂H₄ → C₄H₈ (polymerization reaction, stoichiometric ratio 2:1) ✓ (3) C₂H₄ and C₆H₆ - cannot form this ratio (4) N₂O₄ and NO₂ - N₂O₄ ⇌ 2NO₂ (ratio 1:2, not 2:1) The answer is option (2): C₂H₄ and C₄H₈ 2C₂H₄ → C₄H₈
