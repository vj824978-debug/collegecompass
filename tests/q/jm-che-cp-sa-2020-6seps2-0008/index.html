Match the following: Test/Method - Reagent (i) Lucas Test - (a) C6H5SO2Cl/aq. KOH (ii) Dumas method - (b) HNO3/AgNO3 (iii) Kjeldahl's method - (c) CuO/CO2 (iv) Hinsberg Test - (d) Conc. HCl and ZnCl2 (e) H2SO4

  1. (1) (i)-(d), (ii)-(c), (iii)-(e), (iv)-(a)
  2. (2) (i)-(b), (ii)-(d), (iii)-(e), (iv)-(a)
  3. (3) (i)-(d), (ii)-(c), (iii)-(b), (iv)-(e)
  4. (4) (i)-(b), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(c), (iv)-(d)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Test - Correct reagent (i) Lucas test → conc. HCl + ZnCl2 The Lucas reagent is a mixture of concentrated hydrochloric acid and zinc chloride used to classify alcohols based on their reactivity with the reagent. (ii) Dumas method → CuO/CO2 The Dumas method is used for estimation of nitrogen in organic compounds. Copper oxide is used to oxidize organic matter, and the nitrogen is converted to N2 gas which is measured. (iii) Kjeldahl's method → H2SO4 The Kjeldahl method is used for the determination of nitrogen in organic compounds. Concentrated sulfuric acid is used to digest the organic sample in the presence of a catalyst. (iv) Hinsberg Test → C6H5SO2Cl + aq. KOH The Hinsberg test uses benzenesulfonyl chloride (C6H5SO2Cl) in aqueous KOH to distinguish between primary, secondary, and tertiary amines. Therefore, the correct matching is: (i)-(d), (ii)-(c), (iii)-(e), (iv)-(a)
