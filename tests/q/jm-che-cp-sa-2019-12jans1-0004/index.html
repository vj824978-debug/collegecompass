The major product of the following reaction is – H3C-C(with CH2-CH3 above, Cl to the right, COOCH2CH3 below)-Cl with NaOEt and heat (Δ)

  1. (1) H3C-C(with CH2-CH3 above, OCH2CH3 to the right, COOCH2CH3 below)
  2. (2) CH3-C(=CHCH3)-CO2CH2-CH3 with CH3C double bond
  3. (3) H3CH2C-C(with OCH2CH3 to the right, CO2CH2CH3 below)-CH3
  4. (4) CH3CH2C=CH2 with CO2CH2CH3 below the double bond

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Elimination occur due to the presence of strong base and heating. The reaction proceeds via E2 mechanism. Starting material: H-CH with CH3 above, CH3-C-Cl with COOCH2CH3 below and C-O-C2H5 with O double bonded. Under E2/EtO- and heat conditions, the hydrogen and chlorine are eliminated. The hydrogen is taken from the β-carbon (the CH carbon), and chlorine leaves from the α-carbon bearing the ester group. This results in formation of a double bond between the carbons. The product formed is: CH3 with C double bonded to CHCH3, and CO2CH2-CH3 attached, giving CH3-C(=CHCH3)-CO2CH2-CH3
