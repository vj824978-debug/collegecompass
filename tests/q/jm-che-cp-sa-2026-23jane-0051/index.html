Given above is the concentration vs time plot for a dissociation reaction: A → nB. Based on the data of the initial phase of the reaction (initial 10 min), the value of n is ____.

  1. (1) 4
  2. (2) 3
  3. (3) 2
  4. (4) 5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For the dissociation reaction: A → nB From the graph: At t = 0: [A] = 0.05 M, [nB] = 0 M At t = 10 min: [A] = 0.04 M, [nB] = 0.01 × n M Change in concentration of A: Δ[A] = 0.05 - 0.04 = 0.01 M From stoichiometry of the reaction A → nB: For every 1 mole of A that dissociates, n moles of B are formed. Therefore: Δ[nB] = n × Δ[A] From the graph, the concentration of nB at t = 10 min is: 0.01 × n = 0.03 M (reading from the graph where the upper curve shows approximately 0.03 M) Therefore: 0.01 × n = 0.03 n = 3 However, using the stoichiometric relationship: Δ[nB] = n × Δ[A] 0.03 = n × 0.01 n = 3 But checking the graph more carefully at t = 10 min: The product concentration appears to be 0.02 M (not 0.03 M as initially stated in the solution shown) If [nB] = 0.02 at t = 10 min: 0.01 × n = 0.02 n = 2
