The shortest wavelength of hydrogen atom in Lyman series is λ. The longest wavelength in Balmer series of He⁺ is

  1. (1) 5/(9λ)
  2. (2) 5λ/9
  3. (3) 36λ/5
  4. (4) 9λ/5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

hc/λ = 13.6 ... (i) For longest wavelength in Balmer series transition will be 3 → 2 ∴ ΔE = 13.6 × 2² × (1/4 - 1/9) = 13.6 × 4 × 5/(4×9) hc/λ¹ = 13.6 × 5/9 ∴ λ¹ = 9λ/5
