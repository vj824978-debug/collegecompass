The angle of elevation of the top of a hill from a point on the horizontal plane passing through the foot of the hill is found to be 45°. After walking a distance of 80 meters towards the top, up a slope inclined at an angle of 30° to the horizontal plane, the angle of elevation of the top of the hill becomes 75°. Then the height of the hill (in meters) is

Answer

Correct answer: 80.00

Solution

Let h be the height of the hill. From the initial position at the foot of the hill, the angle of elevation to the top is 45°. This means: tan(45°) = h / (horizontal distance) 1 = h / (horizontal distance) So the horizontal distance from the foot = h After walking 80 m up a slope inclined at 30° to the horizontal: - Vertical gain = 80 * sin(30°) = 80 * (1/2) = 40 m - Horizontal distance covered = 80 * cos(30°) = 80 * (sqrt(3)/2) = 40*sqrt(3) m New horizontal distance from foot = h + 40 - 40*sqrt(3) New height above ground = 40 m Remaining vertical distance to top = h - 40 From this new position, angle of elevation = 75° tan(75°) = (h - 40) / (h + 40 - 40*sqrt(3)) We know tan(75°) = tan(45° + 30°) = (tan(45°) + tan(30°)) / (1 - tan(45°)*tan(30°)) = (1 + 1/sqrt(3)) / (1 - 1/sqrt(3)) = (sqrt(3) + 1) / (sqrt(3) - 1) = (sqrt(3) + 1)^2 / ((sqrt(3) - 1)(sqrt(3) + 1)) = (3 + 2*sqrt(3) + 1) / (3 - 1) = (4 + 2*sqrt(3)) / 2 = 2 + sqrt(3) So: (2 + sqrt(3)) = h / (h + 40 - 40*sqrt(3)) (2 + sqrt(3))(h + 40 - 40*sqrt(3)) = h 2h + 80 - 80*sqrt(3) + sqrt(3)*h + 40*sqrt(3) - 120 = h 2h + 80 - 80*sqrt(3) + sqrt(3)*h + 40*sqrt(3) - 120 = h h + sqrt(3)*h - 40 - 40*sqrt(3) = 0 h(1 + sqrt(3)) = 40 + 40*sqrt(3) h(1 + sqrt(3)) = 40(1 + sqrt(3)) h = 40 Height of hill = h + 40 = 40 + 40 = 80 m
