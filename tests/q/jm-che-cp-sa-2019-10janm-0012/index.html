Which of the graphs shown below does not represent the relationship between incident light and the electron ejected from metal surface?

  1. (1) K.E. of e^- s vs energy of Light (linear relationship with positive slope starting from origin)
  2. (2) K.E. of e^- s vs Frequency of Light (linear relationship with positive slope starting from origin)
  3. (3) K.E. of e^- s vs Intensity of Light (horizontal line independent of intensity)
  4. (4) Number of e^- s vs Frequency of Light (step function showing threshold frequency)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using Einstein's photoelectric equation: K.E. = hv - hv0 where: - K.E. is kinetic energy of ejected electron - h is Planck's constant - v is frequency of incident light - v0 is threshold frequency This can be rewritten as: y = mx - c where m is slope (h), x is frequency (v), c is work function (hv0), and y is K.E. Analyzing each graph: Graph (1): K.E. vs Energy of Light Since E = hv, this is equivalent to K.E. vs hv This gives K.E. = hv - hv0, which is a linear relationship with positive slope and positive intercept on the K.E. axis. This is CORRECT. Graph (2): K.E. vs Frequency of Light Directly from K.E. = hv - hv0, this should be a linear relationship with negative intercept (at v = 0, K.E. = -hv0) However, the graph shows the line passing through the origin with positive slope. The intercept should be NEGATIVE (at -hv0), not passing through origin. This is INCORRECT. Graph (3): K.E. vs Intensity of Light K.E. is independent of intensity according to photoelectric effect. The K.E. depends only on frequency, not on intensity. This correctly shows a horizontal line. This is CORRECT. Graph (4): Number of e^- s vs Frequency of Light No electrons are ejected below threshold frequency (v < v0). Above threshold frequency, number of electrons increases with intensity (but graph shows frequency). This shows a step function starting at threshold frequency. This is CORRECT. Therefore, option (2) is INCORRECT because it shows the line passing through origin when it should have a negative intercept of -hv0.
