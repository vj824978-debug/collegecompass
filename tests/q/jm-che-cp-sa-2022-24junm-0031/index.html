If a rocket runs on a fuel (C15H30) and liquid oxygen, the weight of oxygen required and CO2 released for every litre of fuel respectively are: (Given: density of the fuel is 0.756 g/mL)

  1. (1) 1188 g and 1296 g
  2. (2) 2376 g and 2592 g
  3. (3) 2592 g and 2376 g
  4. (4) 3429 g and 3142 g

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Balanced equation: C15H30 + (45/2)O2 → 15CO2 + 15H2O. One litre of fuel has mass 0.756 × 1000 = 756 g. Moles of C15H30 = 756/210 = 3.6 mol. Moles of O2 required = (45/2) × (756/210) = (45/2) × (756/210). Mass of O2 required = (45/2) × (756/210) × 32 = 2592 g. Mass of CO2 formed = 15 × (756/210) × 44 = 2376 g.
