Two circuits are shown in the figure (a) and (b). At a frequency of ___ rad/s the average power dissipated in one cycle will be same in both the circuits.

Answer

Correct answer: 500

Solution

For equal power dissipation in both circuits: P_a = P_b. From circuit (a): P = (220^2)/5 = 220 × (220/5) = 220 × 44 = 9680 W. For circuit (b), at resonance condition where z^2 = 25, z = 5 (Resonance condition). Since z = R = 5 Ω. At resonance: omega = 1/sqrt(LC) = 1/sqrt(0.1 × 40 × 10^(-6)) = 1/(2 × 10^(-3)) = 500 rad/s
