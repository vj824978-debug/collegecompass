Let P be a point on the ellipse x^2/9 + y^2/4 = 1. Let the line passing through P and parallel to y-axis meet the circle x^2 + y^2 = 9 at point Q such that P and Q are on the same side of the x-axis. Then, the eccentricity of the locus of the point R on PQ such that PR : RQ = 4 : 3 as P moves on the ellipse, is:

  1. (1) sqrt(139)/23
  2. (2) sqrt(13)/7
  3. (3) 11/19
  4. (4) 13/21

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

P(3cos(θ), 2sin(θ)) on ellipse, Q(3cos(θ), 3sin(θ)) on circle. R divides PQ in ratio 4:3, so R = (3cos(θ), (12cos(θ) + 9cos(θ))/7) = (3cos(θ), (12sin(θ) + 6sin(θ))/7). Let R(h,k), then cos(θ) = 7h/21, sin(θ) = 7k/18. Using sin^2(θ) + cos^2(θ) = 1: (7h/21)^2 + (7k/18)^2 = 1. This gives e^2 = 1 - 18^2/21^2 = (21^2 - 18^2)/21^2 = 117/441 = 13/49. Therefore e = sqrt(13)/7.
