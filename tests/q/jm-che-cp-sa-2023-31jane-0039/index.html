A hydrocarbon 'X' with formula C6H8 uses two moles H2 on catalytic hydrogenation of its one mole. On ozonolysis, 'X' yields two moles of methane dicarbaldehyde. The hydrocarbon 'X' is

  1. (1) hexa - 1, 3, 5-triene
  2. (2) cyclohexa - 1, 3 - diene
  3. (3) cyclohexa - 1, 4 - diene
  4. (4) 1 - methylcyclopenta - 1, 4 - diene

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given information: - Molecular formula: C6H8 - Uses 2 moles of H2 on catalytic hydrogenation - On ozonolysis yields 2 moles of methane dicarbaldehyde Step 1: Determine the degree of unsaturation For C6H8, degree of unsaturation = (2×6 + 2 - 8)/2 = (12 + 2 - 8)/2 = 6/2 = 3 This indicates 3 degrees of unsaturation (could be double bonds, triple bonds, or rings). Step 2: Analyze hydrogenation data 2 moles of H2 are used, which means 2 double bonds (or equivalent) are present. Since 3 degrees of unsaturation exist and 2 are accounted for as double bonds, 1 must be a ring. Step 3: Analyze ozonolysis product Ozonolysis yields 2 moles of methane dicarbaldehyde: OHC-CH2-CHO This is a C3 compound (3 carbons), and 2 moles means 6 carbons total, which matches C6H8. For ozonolysis to produce 2 identical molecules of OHC-CH2-CHO from a C6H8 compound, the original compound must be symmetric with a -CH2- unit in the middle connecting two identical C3H4 units. Step 4: Structure determination The compound must be cyclohexa-1,4-diene: - It has a 6-membered ring (1 degree of unsaturation) - It has 2 double bonds (2 degrees of unsaturation) - Total = 3 degrees of unsaturation ✓ - It reacts with 2 moles of H2 ✓ - When ozonized, the double bonds at positions 1 and 4 open and react with the middle -CH2-CH2- unit, yielding 2 moles of OHC-CH2-CHO ✓ Hence, the correct answer is cyclohexa - 1, 4 - diene (option 3).
