The predominant intermolecular forces present in ethyl acetate, a liquid, are:

  1. (1) hydrogen bonding and London dispersion
  2. (2) Dipole-dipole and hydrogen bonding
  3. (3) London dispersion, dipole-dipole and hydrogen bonding
  4. (4) London dispersion and dipole-dipole

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Ethyl acetate (CH3 - C(=O) - OC2H5) is a polar molecule. So dipole-dipole and London Forces will be present between them. The molecule does not have hydrogen bonded to nitrogen, oxygen, or fluorine directly (the hydrogen atoms are bonded to carbon), so hydrogen bonding is NOT a significant intermolecular force in ethyl acetate. Therefore, the predominant intermolecular forces are London dispersion and dipole-dipole interactions.
