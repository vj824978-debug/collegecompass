In a Young's double slit experiment, a combination of two glass wedges A and B, having refractive indices 1.7 and 1.5, respectively are placed in front of the slits, as shown in the figure. [Figure shows two slits with glass wedges A and B in front, separated by distance d, with distance D to screen O, and distance l marked as 1 mm] The separation between the slits is d = 2mm and the shortest distance between the slits and the screen is D = 2m. Thickness of the combination of the wedges is t = 12 μm. The value of l as shown in the figure is 1 mm. Neglect any refraction effect at the slanted interface of the wedges. Due to the combination of the wedges, the central maximum shifts (in mm) with respect to O by ______

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 1.2

Solution

tan θ = t₁/ℓ = t₂/(L - ℓ) L = d + 2ℓ = 2 + 2 = 4 mm ⟹ t₁/1 = t₂/3 ⟹ t₂ = 3t₁ ∴ t₁ + t₂ = 12μm ⟹ 4t₁ = 12 ⟹ t₁ = 3μm and t₂ = 9μm Optical length of ray from S₁ reaching at P L₁ = S₁P + t₁(n_B - 1) + t₂(n_A - 1) Optical length of ray reaching at P from S₂ L₂ = S₂P + t₂(n_B - 1) + t₁(n_A - 1) Δx = L₂ - L₁ = S₂P - S₁P + t₂(n_B - n_A) + t₁(n_A - n_B) For central maxima, Δx = 0 0 = d sin θ + (n_A - n_B)(t₁ - t₂) = d·y/D + (1.7 - 1.5)(3 - 9)×10⁻⁶ ⟹ (2×10⁻³)/2·y = 0.2×6×10⁻⁶ y = (1.2×10⁻⁶)/(10⁻³) = 1.2×10⁻³ m = 1.2 mm [Diagram shows a rectangular slab of thickness L with two layers of thicknesses t₁ and t₂, with refractive indices n_A and n_B, and an incident ray at angle θ]
