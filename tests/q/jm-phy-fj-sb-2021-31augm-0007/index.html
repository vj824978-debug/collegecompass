A particle of mass 1 kg is hanging from a spring of force constant 100 N m^(-1). The mass is pulled slightly downward and released so that it executes free simple harmonic motion with time period T. The time when the kinetic energy and potential energy of the system will become equal, is T/x. The value of x is _______.

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

For SHM, x = A sin(ωt). When KE = PE: (1/2)mv^2 = (1/2)kx^2. From energy conservation: (1/2)m ω^2(A^2 - x^2) = (1/2)kx^2. Since k = mω^2, we get A^2 - x^2 = x^2, so x^2 = A^2/2, thus x = A/√2. For A/√2 = A sin(ωt), we have sin(ωt) = 1/√2, so ωt = π/4. Therefore t = π/(4ω). Since T = 2π/ω, we have t = T/8. Thus x = 8.
