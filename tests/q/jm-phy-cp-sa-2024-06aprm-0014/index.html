A sample contains mixture of helium and oxygen gas. The ratio of root mean square speed of helium and oxygen in the sample, is:

  1. (1) 1/32
  2. (2) 2*sqrt(2)/1
  3. (3) 1/4
  4. (4) 1/(2*sqrt(2))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using the formula V_rms = sqrt(3RT/M_w), the ratio of rms speeds is V_He/V_O2 = sqrt(M_w,O2/M_w,He) = sqrt(32/4) = sqrt(8) = 2*sqrt(2). Therefore V_He/V_O2 = 2*sqrt(2)/1
