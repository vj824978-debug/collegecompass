A sample of milk splits after 60 min. at 300 K and after 40 min. at 400 K when the population of lactobacillus acidophilus in it doubles. The activation energy (in kJ/mol) for this process is closest to ____ (Given, R = 8.3 J mol^(-1) K^(-1), ln(2/3) = 0.4, e^(-3) = 4.0)

Answer

Correct answer: 3.98

Solution

Using the Arrhenius equation in the form: ln(K2/K1) = (Ea/R)[1/T1 - 1/T2] Since the population doubles, K2/K1 represents the ratio of rate constants at the two temperatures. At 300 K, population doubles in 60 min. At 400 K, population doubles in 40 min. ln(60/40) = (Ea/8.3)[1/300 - 1/400] ln(1.5) = (Ea/8.3)[1/300 - 1/400] 1/300 - 1/400 = (4 - 3)/(1200) = 1/1200 ln(60/40) = (Ea/8.3) × (1/1200) Ea = 3.98 kJ/mole
