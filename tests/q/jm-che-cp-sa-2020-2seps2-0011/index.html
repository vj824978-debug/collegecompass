Consider the reaction sequence given below: t-BuBr + OH^⊖ in H2O → t-BuOH + Br^⊖ ......(1) with rate = k[t-BuBr]. t-BuBr + OH^⊖ in C2H5OH → alkene with CH3 groups + HOH + Br^⊖ ......(2) with rate = k[t-BuBr][OH^⊖]. Which of the following statements is true: (1) Changing the concentration of base will have no effect on reaction (1). (2) Changing the concentration of base will have no effect on reaction (2). (3) Changing the base from OH^⊖ to OR^⊖ will have no effect on reaction (2). (4) Doubling the concentration of base will double the rate of both the reactions.

  1. (1) Changing the concentration of base will have no effect on reaction (1)
  2. (2) Changing the concentration of base will have no effect on reaction (2)
  3. (3) Changing the base from OH^⊖ to OR^⊖ will have no effect on reaction (2)
  4. (4) Doubling the concentration of base will double the rate of both the reactions

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Reaction 1: SN1 mechanism. Rate = k[t-BuBr]. SN1 is independent of concentration of nucleophile/base. Therefore, changing the concentration of base will have no effect on reaction (1). This makes statement (1) TRUE. Reaction 2: E2 mechanism. Rate = k[t-BuBr][OH^⊖]. This is dependent on the concentration of base. Therefore, changing the concentration of base will affect reaction (2), making statement (2) FALSE. For statement (3): In E2 reactions, the nature of the base affects reactivity. Changing from OH^⊖ to OR^⊖ (where R is a bulky alkyl group) would change the steric effects and reactivity, so statement (3) is FALSE. For statement (4): Doubling [base] would double the rate of reaction (2) but would NOT affect reaction (1) since it is zero-order in base, making statement (4) FALSE. Therefore, only statement (1) is true.
