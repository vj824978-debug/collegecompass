In order to measure the internal resistance r₁ of a cell of emf E, a meter bridge of wire resistance R₀ = 50 Ω, a resistance R₀/2, another cell of emf E/2 (internal resistance r) and a galvanometer G are used in a circuit, as shown in the figure. [Circuit diagram showing: emf E with internal resistance r₁ in series with R₀/2, connected to a meter bridge with wire length ℓ and resistance R₀, and a second cell of emf E/2 with internal resistance r in series with galvanometer G]. If the null point is found at ℓ = 72 cm, then the value of r₁ = ___ Ω.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Sol. 3 R_AB = 50 Ω So, R_AP = (50/100) × 72 = 36Ω I = ε/(r₁ + 50 + 25) ...(i) -36I - ε/2 - Ir₁ + ε = 0 ...(ii) Solving equation (i) and (ii) r₁ = 3Ω [Circuit diagram showing: EMF E₁ with internal resistance r₁ connected to resistor R_D/2, with a parallel branch containing E/2 and r, connected between points A and B through point P]
