An ideal monatomic gas of n moles is taken through a cycle WXYZW consisting of consecutive adiabatic and isobaric quasi-static processes, as shown in the schematic V-T diagram. [A V-T diagram showing a cycle with points W, X, Y, Z. The path goes from W to X (upward), X to Y (curved upward), Y to Z (curved downward), and Z back to W (downward).] The volume of the gas at W, X and Y points are, 64 cm³, 125 cm³ and 250 cm³ respectively. If the absolute temperature of the gas T_W at the point W is such that nRT_W = 1J (R is the universal gas constant), then the amount of heat absorbed (in J) by the gas along the path XY is _____

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 1.6

Solution

Given V_W = 64 cm³ V_X = 125 cm³ V_Y = 250 cm³ Process WX is adiabatic T_W(64)^(3/5) = T_X(125)^(3/5) ⟹ T_X = (16/25)T_W Process XY is isobaric ⟹ V_X/V_Y = T_X/T_Y 125/250 = T_X/T_Y T_Y = 2T_X T_Y = (32/25)T_W Heat absorbed in XY process Q_XY = nC_P∆T = n(5/2)R(T_Y - T_X) = (5/2)nR(32/25 T_W - 16/25 T_W) = (8/5)nRT_W = (8/5)J
