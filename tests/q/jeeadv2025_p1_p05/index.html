A conducting square loop of side L, mass M and resistance R is moving in the XY plane with its edges parallel to the X and Y axes. The region y ≥ 0 has a uniform magnetic field, B = B₀k. The magnetic field is zero everywhere else. At time t = 0, the loop starts to enter the magnetic field with an initial velocity v₀ j m/s, as shown in the figure. [Figure shows a square loop approaching a magnetic field region from below, with field pointing in +z direction] Considering the quantity K = B₀²L²/(RM) in appropriate units, ignoring self-inductance of the loop and gravity, which of the following statements is/are correct:

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) If v₀ = 1.5 KL, the loop will stop before it enters completely inside the region of magnetic field.
  2. (B) When the complete loop is inside the region of magnetic field, the net force acting on the loop is zero.
  3. (C) If v₀ = KL/10, the loop comes to rest at t = (1/K) ln(5/2).
  4. (D) If v₀ = 3KL, the complete loop enters inside the region of magnetic field at time t = (1/K) ln(3/2).

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

E = vBL j = E/R = vBL/R Fₘ = ILB Fₘ = B²L²v/R -a = B²L²v/(mR) -v(dv/dx) = B²L²v/(mR) ⇒ -∫[v₀ to 0] dv = (B²L²)/(mR) ∫[0 to x₀] dx v₀ = (B²L²/mR)x₀ ⇒ v₀ = Kx₀ ⇒ x₀ = (1/k)v₀ Case - (i) KL = (2/3)v₀ ⇒ x₀ = (3L/2) Incorrect Case - (iii) φ = constant ⇒ ε = 0 ⇒ force F = 0 correct KL = 10v₀ ⇒ x₀ = (L/10)v₀ = (L/10)v₀ From (i) -dv/dt = B²L²v/(mR) ⇒ ∫[v₀ to v] dv/v = -(B²L²)/(mR) ∫ dt v = v₀e^(-B²L²t/mR) t → ∞ Case - (iv) KL = v₀/3 and from (ii) x₀ = 3L, Hence during entering loop will have non-zero velocity. Since -vdv/dx = B²L²v/(mR) ⇒ -∫[v₀ to v] dv = (B²L²)/(mR) ∫[0 to L] dx ⇒ v - v₀ = -(B²L²)/(mR)L v = v₀ - KL = v₀ - v₀/3 = 2v₀/3 Since v = v₀e^(-B²L²t/mR) ⇒ 2v₀/3 = v₀e^(-B²L²t/mR) ⇒ 2/3 = e^(-kt) ⇒ t = (1/k)ln(3/2) [Graph showing V vs t curve exponentially decreasing from initial value] [Diagram showing rectangular loop entering magnetic field region marked with x symbols]
