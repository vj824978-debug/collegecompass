The ionic radii of O^(2-), F^(-), Na^(+) and Mg^(2+) are in the order

  1. (1) O^(2-) > F^(-) > Na^(+) > Mg^(2+)
  2. (2) Mg^(2+) > Na^(+) > F^(-) > O^(2-)
  3. (3) F^(-) > O^(2-) > Na^(+) > Mg^(2+)
  4. (4) O^(2-) > F^(-) > Mg^(2+) > Na^(+)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

To determine the order of ionic radii, we consider the relationship between ionic size and the z/e ratio, where z is the nuclear charge and e is the number of electrons. Given data: - O^(2-): z = 8, e = 10, z/e = 0.8 - F^(-): z = 9, e = 10, z/e = 0.9 - Na^(+): z = 11, e = 10, z/e = 1.1 - Mg^(2+): z = 12, e = 10, z/e = 1.2 All four ions are isoelectronic (have 10 electrons). As the z/e ratio increases, the size decreases. This is because as nuclear charge increases while the number of electrons remains constant, the electrons are pulled closer to the nucleus, resulting in smaller ionic radius. Order of z/e ratio: 0.8 < 0.9 < 1.1 < 1.2 Therefore: O^(2-) > F^(-) > Na^(+) > Mg^(2+) The correct ionic radii order is O^(2-) > F^(-) > Na^(+) > Mg^(2+) Therefore correct option is (1)
