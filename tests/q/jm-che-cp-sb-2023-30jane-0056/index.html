Iron oxide FeO, crystallises in a cubic lattice with a unit cell edge length of 5.0 Å. If density of the FeO in the crystal is 4.0 g cm^(-3), then the number of FeO units present per unit cell is _____. (Nearest integer) Given: Molar mass of Fe and O is 56 and 16 g mol^(-1) respectively. N_A = 6.0 × 10^23 mol^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Using the formula for density: d = (z × m)/(a^3) Where: - d = density = 4.0 g cm^(-3) - z = number of FeO units per unit cell - m = molar mass of FeO = 56 + 16 = 72 g mol^(-1) - a = edge length = 5.0 Å = 5 × 10^(-8) cm - N_A = 6.0 × 10^23 mol^(-1) Substituting: 4 = (z × 72)/(6 × 10^23 × (5 × 10^(-8))^3) 4 = (z × 72)/(6 × 10^23 × 125 × 10^(-24)) 4 = (z × 72)/(6 × 125 × 10^(-1)) 4 = (z × 72)/(750 × 10^(-1)) 4 = (z × 72)/75 z × 72 = 4 × 75 z × 72 = 300 z = 300/72 ≈ 4.17 ≈ 4 (nearest integer)
