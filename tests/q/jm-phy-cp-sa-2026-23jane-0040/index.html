A prism of angle 75° and refractive index sqrt(3) is coated with thin film of refractive index 1.5 only at the back exit surface. To have total internal reflection at the back exit surface the incident angle must be ____.

  1. (1) between 25° and 30°
  2. (2) 15°
  3. (3) > 25°
  4. (4) 26

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For TIR at back surface sqrt(3)sin(r_2) = (3/2)sin(90°) r_2 ≥ 60° r_1 ≤ 15° (since r_1 + r_2 = 75°) Using Snell's law at entry surface: sin(i) = sqrt(3)sin(r_1) When r_1 = 15°: sin(i) = sqrt(3)sin(15°) sin(i) = 1.73 × 0.25 sin(i) = 0.433 i = 25° ⟹ i < 25° Therefore, the incident angle must satisfy: i < 25°, which includes angles between 15° and 20°, exactly 15°, and angles less than 15°.
