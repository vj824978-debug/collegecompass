Let a total charge 2Q be distributed in a sphere of radius R, with the charge density given by ρ(r) = kr, where r is the distance from the centre. Two charges A and B, of -Q each, are placed on diametrically opposite points, at equal distance, a from the centre. If A and B do not experience any force, then:

  1. (1) a = 8^(-1/4) R
  2. (2) a = 2^(-1/4) R
  3. (3) a = 3R / 2^(1/4)
  4. (4) a = R / sqrt(3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Total charge = 2Q Charging density ρ = kr Radius = R d(vol.) = 4πr²dr dq = ρ(d(vol.)) Force on charge at B will be due to charge at A and due to force applied by the charge in sphere Integral dq = integral from 0 to R of kq 4πr² dr 2Q = k4π integral from 0 to R of r³ dr 2Q = k4π R⁴/4 k = 2Q / (πR⁴) ...(1) F_sphere × F_BA F_BA = F_sphere Force on charge B due to element dF = k(dq)Q / a² = kq(K4πr³)dr / a² F = kQK4π / a² integral from 0 to a of r³ dr = kQK4πa² / 4 F = kQKπa² F_BA = F_sphere ⟹ kQ² / (2a)² = kQKa² ⟹ By replace value of K from (1) Q² / 4a² = 2Q² / πR⁴ · πa² ⟹ a⁴ = R⁴ / 8 ⟹ a = R 8^(-1/4)
