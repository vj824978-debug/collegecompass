The complex of Ni^(2+) ion and dimethyl glyoxime contains _____ number of Hydrogen (H) atoms.

Answer

Correct answer: 14

Solution

From the structure shown: The complex is [Ni(dmg)_2] where dmg is dimethyl glyoxime. Dimethyl glyoxime ligand structure: CH_3-C(=N-OH)-C(=N-OH)-CH_3 Each dimethyl glyoxime ligand contains: - 2 methyl groups (CH_3): 2 × 3 = 6 hydrogen atoms - 2 oxime groups (-NOH): 2 × 1 = 2 hydrogen atoms Total per ligand = 6 + 2 = 8 hydrogen atoms In the complex [Ni(dmg)_2]: - There are 2 dimethyl glyoxime ligands - Total H atoms from ligands = 2 × 8 = 16 hydrogen atoms However, from the structure diagram provided, we can count: - Top side: O-H-------O^- contributes 1 H from O-H - Bottom side: O-------H-O contributes 1 H from H-O - 4 CH_3 groups: 4 × 3 = 12 H atoms - Total = 1 + 1 + 12 = 14 hydrogen atoms Number of H atoms = 14
