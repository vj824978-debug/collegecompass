An air bubble of diameter 6 mm rises steadily through a solution of density 1750 kg/m³ at the rate of 0.35 cm/s. The co-efficient of viscosity of the solution (neglect density of air) is _______ Pa (given, g = 10 ms⁻²)

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Using Buoyancy = 6πηrv_T, where (4/3)πr³ρg = 6πηrv_T. Solving for η: η = (4r²ρg)/(6v_T) = (2/9) × (3×10⁻³)² × (1750×10)/0.35 = 2×10⁻⁶ × (175×10000)/0.35 = 10 Pa
