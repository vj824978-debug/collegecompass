The correct match between Item-I and Item-II:

  1. (1) (a) - (III), (b) - (IV), (c) - (I), (d) - (II)
  2. (2) (a) - (IV), (b) - (III), (c) - (II), (d) - (I)
  3. (3) (a) - (IV), (b) - (III), (c) - (I), (d) - (II)
  4. (4) (a) - (III), (b) - (IV), (c) - (II), (d) - (I)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(a) Natural rubber is formed from isoprene (2-methyl-1,3-butadiene). The monomer structure is nCH2=C(CH3)-CH=CH2 which polymerizes to form poly cis-isoprene (Natural rubber). This matches with (IV) Isoprene. (b) Neoprene is formed from chloroprene (2-chloro-1,3-butadiene). The monomer structure is nCH2=C(Cl)-CH=CH2 which polymerizes to form -[CH2-C(Cl)=CH-CH2]n. This matches with (III) Chloroprene. (c) Buna-N is formed from 1,3-butadiene and acrylonitrile. The copolymerization of nCH2=CH-CH=CH2 and nCH2=CH(CN) gives [-CH2-CH=CH-CH2-CH2-CH(CN)]n. This matches with (II) 1,3-butadiene + acrylonitrile. (d) Buna-S is formed from 1,3-butadiene and styrene. The copolymerization of CH2=CH-CH=CH2 and CH2=CH(C6H5) gives [-CH2-CH=CH-CH2-CH2-CH(C6H5)]n. This matches with (I) 1,3-butadiene + styrene. Therefore: (a) - (IV), (b) - (III), (c) - (II), (d) - (I)
