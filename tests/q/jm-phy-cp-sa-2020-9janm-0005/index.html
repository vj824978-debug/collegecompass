A charged particle of mass 'm' and charge 'q' moving under the influence of uniform electric field E i and a uniform magnetic field B k follows a trajectory from point P to Q as shown in figure. The velocities at P and Q are respectively, v i and -2v j. Then which of the following statements (A, B, C, D) are the correct?

  1. (1) B, C, D
  2. (2) A, B, C
  3. (3) A, B, C, D
  4. (4) A, C, D

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Work done by magnetic force = zero Thus ΔKE = Work done by electric field (1/2)m(2v)^2 - (1/2)mv^2 = qE × 2a (1/2)m(4v^2) - (1/2)mv^2 = qE × 2a (1/2)m(4v^2 - v^2) = qE × 2a (1/2)m(3v^2) = qE × 2a ⟹ E = (3mv^2)/(4qa) Statement (A) is CORRECT Rate of work done by electric field at p = qEv = (3mv^3)/(4a) Statement (B) is CORRECT Rate of work done by magnetic field at any point is zero since force ⊥ velocity. At Q, E is perpendicular to velocity thus rate of work done by it is zero. Statement (C) is CORRECT For angular momentum, at P: velocity is in +i direction, position is at distance 'a' from origin in -j direction vector L_P = vector r × m vector v = (-a j) × m(v i) = -mav k |vector L_P| = mav At Q: velocity is in -2v j direction, position is at distance 2a from origin in +i direction vector L_Q = vector r × m vector v = (2a i) × m(-2v j) = -4mav k |vector L_Q| = 4mav Difference = |vector L_Q| - |vector L_P| = 4mav - mav = 3mav ≠ 2mav Statement (D) is INCORRECT
