Two cars A and B each of mass 10^3 kg are moving on parallel tracks separated by a distance of 10 m, in same direction with speeds 72 km/h and 36 km/h. The magnitude of angular momentum of car A with respect to car B is ____ J.s.

  1. (1) 3.6 × 10^5
  2. (2) 10^5
  3. (3) 3 × 10^5
  4. (4) 2 × 10^5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

L = m.v_rel.r_perp where: m = 1000 kg v_A = 72 km/h = 72 × (5/18) = 20 m/s v_B = 36 km/h = 36 × (5/18) = 10 m/s v_rel = v_A - v_B = 20 - 10 = 10 m/s r_perp = 10 m (perpendicular distance between tracks) L = 1000 × 10 × 10 = 10^5 kg m^2/s
