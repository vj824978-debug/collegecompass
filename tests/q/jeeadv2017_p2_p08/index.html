A uniform magnetic field B exists in the region between x = 0 and x = 3R/2 (region 2 in the figure) pointing normally into the plane of the paper. A particle with charge +Q and momentum p directed along x-axis enters region 2 from region 1 at point P₁ (y = -R). Which of the following option(s) is/are correct? [Figure shows three regions: Region 1 (x < 0), Region 2 (0 ≤ x ≤ 3R/2) with magnetic field B pointing into page (marked with ×), Region 3 (x > 3R/2). A particle with charge +Q enters at point P₁ at coordinates (0, -R) with momentum directed along +x axis. The width of Region 2 is 3R/2.]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [A] For B > (2p)/(3QR), the particle will re-enter region 1
  2. (B) [B] For B = (8p)/(13QR), the particle will enter region 3 through the point P₂ on x-axis
  3. (C) [C] When the particle re-enters region 1 through the longest possible path in region 2, the magnitude of the change in its linear momentum between point P₁ and the farthest point from y-axis is p/√2
  4. (D) [D] For a fixed B, particles of same charge Q and same velocity v, the distance between the point P₁ and the point of re-entry into region 1 is inversely proportional to the mass of the particle

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

Sol. A, B R' = P/(qB) (R' - R)² + R² = R'² ⟹ R' = (13/8)R [FIGURE: Circle with center point. Two vertical dotted lines shown - one at distance R from center (right side) and one at distance R' from center (right side). Horizontal dotted line shown at center level. Labels indicate R' - R as horizontal distance between the two vertical lines, and R' as the radius or distance marker.]
