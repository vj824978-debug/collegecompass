A moving coil galvanometer, having a resistance G, produces full scale deflection when a current I_g flows through it. This galvanometer can be converted into (i) an ammeter of range 0 to I_0(I_0 > I_g) by connecting a shunt resistance R_A to it and (ii) into a voltmeter of range 0 to V (V = GI_0) by connecting a series resistance R_V to it. Then,

  1. (1) R_A R_V = G^2 and R_A/R_V = I_g/(I_0 - I_g)
  2. (2) R_A R_V = G^2(I_g/(I_0 - I_g)) and R_A/R_V = (I_0 - I_g)^2/I_g^2
  3. (3) R_A R_V = G^2((I_0 - I_g)/I_g) and R_A/R_V = (I_g/(I_0 - I_g))^2
  4. (4) R_A R_V = G^2 and R_A/R_V = (I_g/(I_0 - I_g))^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Galvanometer is converted into ammeter of range 0 to I_0. From the circuit diagram for ammeter: I_0 = I_g + I_S, where I_S = shunt current I_S G = (I_0 - I_g) R_A I_g G = (I_0 - I_g) R_A R_A = I_g G/(I_0 - I_g) ... (1) Galvanometer is converted into voltmeter of range 0 to V From the circuit diagram for voltmeter: V = I_g(G + R_V) GI_0 = I_g(G + R_V) GI_0 = I_g G + I_g R_V R_V = G(I_0 - I_g)/I_g ... (2) So from (1) & (2) R_A R_V = G^2 R_A/R_V = (I_g/(I_0 - I_g))^2
