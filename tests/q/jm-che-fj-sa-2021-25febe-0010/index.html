Carbylamine test is used to detect the presence of primary amino group in an organic compound. Which of the following compound is formed when this test is performed with aniline?

  1. (1) Benzene with NC group
  2. (2) Benzene with NHCH3 group
  3. (3) Benzene with CONH2 group
  4. (4) Benzene with CN group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The carbylamine test (isocyanide test) detects primary amines. When aniline (C6H5NH2) is warmed with CHCl3 and alcoholic KOH, it forms phenyl isocyanide (C6H5NC). The primary amine reacts with CHCl3 and base to form an isocyanide (R-NC) as the product. The reaction shows: R-NH2 + CHCl3 + alc.KOH → R-NC + KCl + H2O. For aniline, this gives phenylisocyanide (benzene-NC). The answer is (A).
