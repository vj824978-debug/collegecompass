For the reaction A → B the following graph was obtained. The time required (in seconds) for the concentration of A to reduce to 2.5 g L^(-1) (if the initial concentration of A was 50 g L^(-1)) is......... (Nearest integer) Given: log 2 = 0.3010

Answer

Correct answer: 67

Solution

From the graph, this appears to be a first-order reaction. For first-order kinetics: ln([A]₀/[A]ₜ) = kt or log([A]₀/[A]ₜ) = kt/2.303. Here [A]₀ = 50 g L^(-1) and [A]ₜ = 2.5 g L^(-1), so [A]₀/[A]ₜ = 50/2.5 = 20. log(20) = log(2 × 10) = log 2 + 1 = 0.3010 + 1 = 1.3010. From the graph, the rate constant k can be determined from the slope. Reading from the graph at t = 5s, [A] ≈ 25 g L^(-1), so half-life t₁/₂ ≈ 5s. For first-order: t₁/₂ = 0.693/k, so k = 0.693/5 ≈ 0.1386 s^(-1). Using t = (2.303/k)log([A]₀/[A]ₜ) = (2.303/0.1386) × 1.3010 ≈ 21.6 seconds ≈ 22 seconds.
