Two solid cylinders P and Q of same mass and same radius start rolling down a fixed inclined plane form the same height at the same time. Cylinder P has most of its mass concentrated near its surface, while Q has most of its mass concentrated near the axis. Which statement(s) is (are) correct?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) Both cylinders P and Q reach the ground at the same time
  2. (B) Cylinder P has larger linear acceleration than cylinder Q.
  3. (C) Both cylinder Q reaches the ground with same translational kinetic energy.
  4. (D) Cylinder Q reaches the ground with larger angular speed.

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

aₚ = g sin θ / (Iₚ + mR²) aQ = g sin θ / (IQ + mR²) aₚ < aQ ⟹ V = u + at ⟹ t ∝ 1/a tₚ > tQ V² = u² + 2as ⟹ v ∝ a ⟹ Vₚ < VQ Translational K.E. = ½mV² ⟹ TR KEₚ < TR KEQ V = ωR ⟹ ω ∝ V ⟹ ωₚ < ωQ
