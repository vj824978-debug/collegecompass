Prolonged heating is avoided during the preparation of ferrous ammonium sulphate to

  1. (1) Prevent hydrolysis
  2. (2) Prevent reduction
  3. (3) Prevent breaking
  4. (4) Prevent oxidation

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Prolonged heating may oxidise ferrous ions to ferric ions. Therefore, prolonged heating is avoided during the preparation of ferrous ammonium sulphate to prevent oxidation of Fe2+ to Fe3+.
