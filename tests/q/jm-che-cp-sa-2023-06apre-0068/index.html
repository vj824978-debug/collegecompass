During the reaction of permanganate with thiosulfate, the change in oxidation of manganese occurs by value of 3. Identify which of the below medium will favour the reaction.

  1. (1) Both aqueous acidic and neutral
  2. (2) Aqueous neutral
  3. (3) Both aqueous acidic and faintly alkaline
  4. (4) Aqueous acidic

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

In the reaction MnO4^- + S2O3^2- → MnO2 + SO4^2-, manganese changes oxidation state by 3 (from +7 to +4). This ionic mechanism is favoured in neutral aqueous medium because the formation of MnO2 precipitate is stable in neutral conditions, whereas in acidic conditions Mn^2+ is preferred and in alkaline conditions the reaction is less favourable.
