A beam of light consisting of wavelengths 650 nm and 550 nm illuminates the Young's double slits with separation of 2 mm such that the interference fringes are formed on a screen, placed at a distance of 1.2 m from the slits. The least distance of a point from the central maximum, where the bright fringes due to both the wavelengths coincide, is ____ ×10^(-5) m.

Answer

Correct answer: 429

Solution

For Young's double slit interference, the position of bright fringes is given by: y = n(lambda*D)/d For coinciding bright fringes of both wavelengths: y1 = y2 n1*lambda1*D/d = n2*lambda2*D/d n1*lambda1 = n2*lambda2 n1/n2 = lambda2/lambda1 = 550/650 = 11/13 The least distance occurs when n1 = 11 and n2 = 13 (smallest integers satisfying the ratio). Using lambda1 = 650 nm = 650×10^(-9) m: y = 11 × (lambda1*D)/d = 11 × (650×10^(-9) × 1.2)/(2×10^(-3)) y = 11 × (650×10^(-9) × 1.2)/(2×10^(-3)) y = (11 × 650 × 1.2 × 10^(-9))/(2×10^(-3)) y = (8580 × 10^(-9))/(2×10^(-3)) y = 4290 × 10^(-6) y = 429 × 10^(-5) m
