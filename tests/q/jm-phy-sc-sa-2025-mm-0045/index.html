Two infinite identical charged sheets and a charged spherical body of charge density 'p' are arranged as shown in figure. Then the correct relation between the electrical fields at A, B, C and D points is:

  1. (1) E_A > E_B; E_C = E_D
  2. (2) E_C ≠ E_D; E_A > E_B
  3. (3) |E_A| = |E_B|; E_C > E_D
  4. (4) E_A = E_B; E_C = E_D

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Point A and B are both inside the sphere and on axis. The two infinite sheets have equal charge density σ and are symmetrically placed. Between two parallel sheets with same charge density, the field is zero. Inside the sphere, the field due to the sphere at points A and B on the axis are equal in magnitude due to symmetry. Since both sheets produce zero field between them, E_A = E_B. Point C is 2 cm to the left of the left sheet and D is 2 cm to the right of the right sheet. Due to symmetry of the two identical sheets and equal distances, E_C = E_D.
