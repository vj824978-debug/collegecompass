The structure of a peptide is given below: [peptide structure showing a tripeptide with tyrosine (HO-benzene ring with H2N and carboxyl group), aspartic acid (with CO2H side chain), and lysine (with NH2 group on side chain) residues connected by peptide bonds] If the absolute values of the net charge of the peptide at pH = 2, pH = 6, and pH = 11 are |z₁|, |z₂|, and |z₃|, respectively, then what is |z₁| + |z₂| + |z₃|?

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

Sol. 5 At pH = 2 (highly acidic), the structure of the peptide will be as shown below. [Structural diagram showing a tripeptide with a hydroxylated tyrosine residue on the left, a central amino acid with a carboxyl group side chain, and a lysine residue on the right. The N-terminus is protonated (N⁺H₃), the carboxyl group is protonated (COOH), and the lysine amino group is protonated (N⁺H₃).] So, |Z₁| = 2 At pH = 6 it will exist as neutral or zwitter-ion structure as shown below. [Structural diagram of the same tripeptide showing the N-terminus protonated (N⁺H₃), the side chain carboxyl group deprotonated (COO⁻), the C-terminus deprotonated (O⁻), and the lysine amino group protonated (N⁺H₃).] So, according to this condition, |Z₂| = 0 At pH = 11 (highly basic), the given peptide will exist as: [Structural diagram showing the phenolic oxygen deprotonated (O⁻), the N-terminus deprotonated (H₂N), the side chain carboxyl group deprotonated (COO⁻), the C-terminus deprotonated (O⁻), and the lysine amino group deprotonated (NH₂).] So, |Z₃| = |−3| = 3 |Z₁| + |Z₂| + |Z₃| = |2 + 0 + 3| = 5
