Two flasks I and II shown below are connected by a valve of negligible volume. Flask I contains 2.8 N2 at 300 K in 1 L. Flask II contains 0.2 O N2 at 60 K in 2 L. When the valve is opened, the final pressure of the system in bar is x × 10^-5. The value of x is ________ (Integer answer). [Assume-ideal gas; 1 bar = 10^5 Pa; Molar mass of N2 = 28.0 g mol^-1; R = 8.31 J mol^-1 K^-1]

Answer

Correct answer: 84

Solution

Using ideal gas law, calculate moles in each flask: n1 = (2.8 × 10^5 × 1)/(8.31 × 300) ≈ 0.112 mol for flask I; n2 = (0.2 × 10^5 × 2)/(8.31 × 60) ≈ 0.008 mol for flask II. Total moles = 3/28 mol. Using energy conservation, heat lost by N2 in flask I = heat gained by N2 in flask II: n1·Cm(300 - T) = n2·Cm(T - 60), which gives T = 284 K. Using ideal gas law for combined system: P(V1 + V2) = (n1 + n2)RT, P × 3 = (3/28) × 8.31 × 284 × 10^-5, P ≈ 84.287 × 10^-5 bar ≈ 84 × 10^-5 bar. Answer: x = 84
