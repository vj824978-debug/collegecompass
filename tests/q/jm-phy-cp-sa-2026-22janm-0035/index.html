Net gravitational force at the centre of a square is found to be F1 when four particles having mass M, 2M, 3M and 4M are placed at the four corners of the square as shown in figure and it is F2 when the positions of 3M and 4M are interchanged. The ratio F1/F2 is alpha/sqrt(5). The value of alpha is ____.

  1. (1) 2
  2. (2) 3
  3. (3) 1
  4. (4) 2*sqrt(5)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Initial configuration Let side of square = 2L, so distance from each corner to centre = sqrt(2)*L Forces due to each mass at centre: - Due to 4M at top-left: G*4m*m0/(sqrt(2)*L)^2 = G4mm0/(2L^2), directed towards top-left - Due to 3M at top-right: G*3m*m0/(sqrt(2)*L)^2 = G3mm0/(2L^2), directed towards top-right - Due to M at bottom-left: G*m*m0/(sqrt(2)*L)^2 = Gmm0/(2L^2), directed towards bottom-left - Due to 2M at bottom-right: G*2m*m0/(sqrt(2)*L)^2 = G2mm0/(2L^2), directed towards bottom-right Component analysis from diagram: G4mm0/(2L^2) and G3mm0/(2L^2) have components Gmm0/(2L^2) and G2mm0/(2L^2) have components Net force: F1 = 2*sqrt(2)*Gmm0/r^2 New configuration (3M and 4M interchanged) - Due to 3M at top-left: G3mm0/(2L^2) - Due to 4M at top-right: G4mm0/(2L^2) - Due to M at bottom-left: Gmm0/(2L^2) - Due to 2M at bottom-right: G2mm0/(2L^2) Net force: F' = sqrt(10)*Gmm0/r^2 Ratio: F1/F2 = 2*sqrt(2)/sqrt(10) * 1 = 2*sqrt(2) * (1/sqrt(10)) = 2/sqrt(5) Given: F1/F2 = alpha/sqrt(5) Therefore: alpha/sqrt(5) = 2/sqrt(5) ∴ alpha = 2
