ABC is a plane lamina of the shape of an equilateral triangle. D, E are mid points of AB, AC and G is the centroid of the lamina. Moment of inertia of the lamina about an axis passing through G and perpendicular to the plane ABC is I_0. If part ADE is removed, the moment of inertia of the remaining part about the same axis is NI_0/16, where N is an integer. Value of N is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 11

Solution

Let side of triangle be a and mass is m MOI of plate ABC about centroid: I_0 = (m/3) * ((a/(2*sqrt(3)))^2) * 3 = ma^2/12 Triangle ADE is also an equilateral triangle of side a/2. Let moment of inertia of triangular pliant ADE about its centroid (G') is I_1 and mass is m_1. m_1 = (m/(sqrt(3)a^2)) * sqrt(3)/4 * (a/2)^2 = m/4 I_1 = (m_1/12) * (a/2)^2 = (m/12) * (a^2/4) = ma^2/192 Distance GG' = a/sqrt(3) - 2*sqrt(3) = a/(2*sqrt(3)) so MOI of part ADE about centroid G is: I_2 = I_1 + m_1 * (a/(2*sqrt(3)))^2 = ma^2/192 + m/4 * a^2/12 = 5ma^2/192 now MOI of remaining part: = ma^2/12 - 5ma^2/192 = 11ma^2/(12*16) = 111*I_0/16 => N = 11
