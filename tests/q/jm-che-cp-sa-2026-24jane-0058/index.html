One mole of Cl2(g) was passed into 2 L of cold 2 M KOH solution. After the reaction, the concentrations of Cl⁻, ClO⁻ and OH⁻ are respectively (assume volume remains constant)

  1. (1) 0.75M, 0.75M, 1M
  2. (2) 0.5M, 0.5M, 0.5M
  3. (3) 0.5M, 0.5M, 1M
  4. (4) 1M, 1M, 1M

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Cl2 + 2KOH → KCl + KClO + H2O t = 0: 1 mole Cl2, 4 mole KOH t_f: 0 mole Cl2, 2 mole KCl, 1 mole KClO, 1 mole KOH remaining From the reaction stoichiometry: - 1 mole Cl2 reacts with 2 mole KOH - Produces 1 mole KCl and 1 mole KClO Initial KOH: 2 M × 2 L = 4 mole Cl2 added: 1 mole After reaction: - KCl produced: 1 mole - KClO produced: 1 mole - KOH remaining: 4 - 2(1) = 2 mole Concentrations in 2 L solution: [Cl⁻] = 1 mole / 2 L = 0.5 M [ClO⁻] = 1 mole / 2 L = 0.5 M [OH⁻] = 2 mole / 2 L = 1 M
