A normal with slope 1/sqrt(6) is drawn from the point (0, -α) to the parabola x^2 = -4ay, where a > 0. Let L be the line passing through (0, -α) and parallel to the directrix of the parabola. Suppose that L intersects the parabola at two points A and B. Let r denote the length of the latus rectum and s denote the square of the length of the line segment AB. If r : s = 1 : 16, then the value of 24α is ______

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

Rotating the axis by an angle of 90° in clock-wise direction then slope of normal becomes −√6 and point (0, −α) becomes (α, 0) and parabola being y² = 4ax ⇒ Equation of normal y = mx − 2am − am³ passes through (α, 0) ⇒ 0 = −√6α + 2√6a + 6√6a ⇒ α = 8a = at² ⇒ t = 2√2 ⇒ AB = 4at = 8√2a Now AB² = 64 × 2 × a² r/s = 4a/128a² = 1/16 ⇒ a = 1/2 ⇒ 24a = 12 [Diagram shows a parabola y² = 4ax with a normal line of slope m = −√6 passing through point A on the parabola and point (α, 0) on the x-axis, with point B also marked on the parabola]
