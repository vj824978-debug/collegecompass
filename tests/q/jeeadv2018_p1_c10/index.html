For the electrochemical cell, Mg(s) | Mg²⁺(aq, 1 M) || Cu²⁺(aq, 1 M) | Cu(s) the standard emf of the cell is 2.70 V at 300 K. When the concentration of Mg²⁺ is changed to x M, the cell potential changes to 2.67 V at 300 K. The value of x is _____. (given, F/R = 11500 K V⁻¹, where F is the Faraday constant and R is the gas constant, ln(10) = 2.30)

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Sol. 10 Case I: E_cell = E°_cell - (RT/2F)ln(1/1) = 2.7 - 0 = 2.7 V Case II: E_cell = E°_cell - (RT/2F)ln(x/1) 2.67 = 2.7 - (R × 300)/(2F)lnx -0.03 = -(R × 300)/(2F)lnx lnx = (0.03 × 2 × F)/(300 × R) = 2.3 x = 10
