Two coaxial discs, having moments of inertia I₁ and (11/2)I₁, are rotating with respective angular velocities ω₁ and (ω₁/2), about their common axis. They are brought in contact with each other and thereafter they rotate with a common angular velocity. If Ef and Ei are the final and initial total energies, then (Ef - Ei) is:

  1. (1) I₁ω₁²/6
  2. (2) (3/8)I₁ω₁²
  3. (3) I₁ω₁²/12
  4. (4) I₁ω₁²/24

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

conservation of angular momentum Li = Lf Initial angular momentum: Li = I₁ω₁ + (11/2)I₁(ω₁/2) = I₁ω₁ + (11/4)I₁ω₁ = (15/4)I₁ω₁ Final angular momentum: Lf = (I₁ + (11/2)I₁)ω = (13/2)I₁ω From Li = Lf: (15/4)I₁ω₁ = (13/2)I₁ω ω = (15/4)ω₁ × (2/13) = (15/26)ω₁ Initial kinetic energy: Ei = (1/2)I₁ω₁² + (1/2)(11/2)I₁(ω₁/2)² Ei = (1/2)I₁ω₁² + (11/8)I₁ω₁² Ei = (4/8)I₁ω₁² + (11/8)I₁ω₁² = (15/8)I₁ω₁² Final kinetic energy: Ef = (1/2)(13/2)I₁ω² = (1/2)(13/2)I₁((15/26)ω₁)² Ef = (13/4)I₁ × (225/676)ω₁² Ef = (13 × 225)/(4 × 676)I₁ω₁² = (2925/2704)I₁ω₁² = (225/208)I₁ω₁² Alternatively: Ef = (1/2)(13/2)I₁ × (225/676)ω₁² = (13 × 225)/(4 × 676)I₁ω₁² = (2925/2704)I₁ω₁² Ef - Ei = (2925/2704)I₁ω₁² - (15/8)I₁ω₁² Ef - Ei = (2925/2704 - 5070/2704)I₁ω₁² = (-2145/2704)I₁ω₁² = -I₁ω₁²/24 Therefore (Ef - Ei) = -I₁ω₁²/24 The energy loss is I₁ω₁²/24, and since the question asks for (Ef - Ei), the answer is -I₁ω₁²/24, but the magnitude given in option (4) is I₁ω₁²/24
