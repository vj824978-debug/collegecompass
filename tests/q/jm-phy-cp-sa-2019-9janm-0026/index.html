A particle having the same charge as of electron moves in a circular path of radius 0.5 cm under the influence of a magnetic field of 0.5 T. If an electric field of 100 V/m makes it to move in a straight path, then the mass of the particle is (Given charge of electron = 1.6 × 10^(-19) C)

  1. (1) 1.6 × 10^(-19) kg
  2. (2) 1.6 × 10^(-27) kg
  3. (3) 9.1 × 10^(-31) kg
  4. (4) 2.0 × 10^(-24) kg

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

When a charged particle moves in a circular path under magnetic field alone: The magnetic force provides centripetal force. qvB = mv^2/R R = mV/(qB) For the particle to move in a straight path, the electric force and magnetic force must balance: qVB = qE V = E/B Substituting this velocity back into the radius formula: R = mE/(qB^2) Rearranging for mass: m = qRB^2/E Given: q = 1.6 × 10^(-19) C R = 0.5 cm = 0.5 × 10^(-2) m = 5 × 10^(-3) m B = 0.5 T E = 100 V/m m = (1.6 × 10^(-19) × 0.5 × 10^(-2) × (0.5)^2)/100 m = (1.6 × 10^(-19) × 0.5 × 10^(-2) × 0.25)/100 m = (1.6 × 10^(-19) × 0.5 × 10^(-2) × 0.25)/100 m = (2 × 10^(-24) × 0.5 × 10^(-2) × 0.25)/0.5 × 10^(-2) m = 2 × 10^(-24) kg
