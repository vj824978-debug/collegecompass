Circle(s) touching x-axis at a distance 3 from the origin and having an intercept of length 2√7 on y-axis is (are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y + 9 = 0
  2. (B) x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 7y + 9 = 0
  3. (C) x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 8y + 9 = 0
  4. (D) x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 7y + 9 = 0

Answer

Correct answer: AC

Solution

Sol. (A), (C) Equation of circle can be written as (x - 3)² + y² + λ(y) = 0 ⟹ x² + y² - 6x + λy + 9 = 0. Now, (radius)² = 7 + 9 = 16 ⟹ 9 + λ²/4 - 9 = 16 ⟹ λ² = 64 ⟹ λ = ±8. ∴ Equation is x² + y² - 6x ± 8y + 9 = 0.
