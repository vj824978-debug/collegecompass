A person observes two moving trains, 'A' reaching the station and 'B' leaving the station with equal speed of 30 m/s. If both trains emit sounds with frequency 300 Hz, (Speed of sound: 330 m/s) approximate difference of frequencies heard by the person will be:

  1. (1) 33 Hz
  2. (2) 10 Hz
  3. (3) 55 Hz
  4. (4) 80 Hz

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

By Doppler effect : f' = f0[(v - v0)/(v - vs)] For train A (approaching the station): f_A' = 300[(330)/(330 - 30)] Hz = 300[(330)/(300)] Hz = 330 Hz For train B (leaving the station): f_B' = 300[(330)/(330 + 30)] Hz = 300[(330)/(360)] Hz = (5/6) × 330 Hz = 275 Hz Difference of frequencies heard by the person: f_A' - f_B' = 330 - 275 = 55 Hz
