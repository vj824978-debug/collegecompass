Aniline reacts with mixed acid (conc. HNO₃ and conc. H₂SO₄) at 288 K to give P (51%), Q (47%) and R(2%). The major product(s) of the following reaction sequence is (are) R —(1) Ac₂O, pyridine —(2) Br₂, CH₃CO₂H —(3) H₂O⁺ —(4) NaNO₂, HCl/273–278K —(5) EtOH, Δ→ S —(1) Sn/HCl —(2) Br₂, H₂O(excess)→ major product(s) —(3) NaNO₂, HCl/273–278K —(4) H₃PO₂

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [Benzene ring with four Br atoms: one at top, one at bottom-left, one at bottom-right, and one at right side]
  2. (B) [Benzene ring with five Br atoms: one at top, one at top-right, one at right, one at bottom-right, and one at bottom-left]
  3. (C) [Benzene ring with three Br atoms: one at top, one at bottom-left, and one at bottom-right in a 1,3,5-substitution pattern]
  4. (D) [Benzene ring with four Br atoms: one at top, one at left, one at bottom, and one at right in a roughly symmetric pattern, with an additional Br attached to the top Br forming a diphenyl-like structure]

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Sol. D [First reaction box: Benzene ring with NH₂ group] + conc.HNO₃/conc.H₂SO₄ → [Benzene with NH₂ and NO₂ ortho] (51%) (P) + [Benzene with NH₂ and NO₂ meta] (47%) (Q) + [Benzene with NH₂ ortho and NO₂ para] (2%) (R) [Second reaction sequence] [Benzene with NH₂ and NO₂] (R) + Ac₂O → [Benzene with NHAc and NO₂] + Br₂/CH₃COOH → [Benzene with NHAc, NO₂, and Br] + H₂O⁺ → [Benzene with NH₂, NO₂, and Br] + NaNO₂ + HCl, 0-5°C → [Benzene with N₂Cl⁺, NO₂, and Br diazonium salt] + EtOH/Δ → [Benzene with NO₂ and Br] (S) + Sn/HCl → [Benzene with NH₂ and Br] + Br₂/H₂O → [Benzene with NH₂ and two Br groups] + NaNO₂ + HCl, 0-5°C → [Benzene with N₂Cl⁺, and three Br groups] + H₃PO₂ → [Benzene with four Br groups]
