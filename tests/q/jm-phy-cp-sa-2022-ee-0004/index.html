Two identical charged particles each having a mass 10 g and charge 2.0 × 10^(-7) C are placed on a horizontal table with a separation of L between them such that they stay in limited equilibrium. If the coefficient of friction between each particle and the table is 0.25, find the value of L. [Use g = 10 ms^(-2)]

  1. (1) 12 cm
  2. (2) 10 cm
  3. (3) 8 cm
  4. (4) 5 cm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For limited equilibrium, the electrostatic repulsive force equals the maximum static friction force. Electrostatic force: F_e = kQ^2/L^2. Friction force: f = μmg = 0.25 × 0.01 × 10 = 0.025 N. Setting F_e = f: kQ^2/L^2 = μmg. L^2 = kQ^2/(μmg). L^2 = (9×10^9 × (2.0×10^(-7))^2)/(0.25 × 0.01 × 10) = (9×10^9 × 4×10^(-14))/(0.025) = (36×10^(-5))/0.025 = 0.0144. L = 0.12 m = 12 cm.
