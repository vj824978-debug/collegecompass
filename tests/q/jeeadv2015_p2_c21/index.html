In dilute aqueous H₂SO₄, the complex diaquodioxalatοferrate(II) is oxidized by MnO₄⁻. For this reaction, the ratio of the rate of change of [H⁺] to the rate of change of [MnO₄⁻] is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

[Fe(C₂O₄)(H₂O)]²⁻ + MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ ⟶ Mn²⁺ + Fe³⁺ + 4CO₂ + 6H₂O So the ratio of rate of change of [H⁺] to that of rate of change of [MnO₄⁻] is 8.
