Concentration of H₂SO₄ and Na₂SO₄ in a solution is 1 M and 1.8×10⁻² M, respectively. Molar solubility of PbSO₄ in the same solution is X×10⁻Y M (expressed in scientific notation). The value of Y is [Given: Solubility product of PbSO₄(Ksp) = 1.6×10⁻⁸. For H₂SO₄, Ka is very large and Ka₂ = 1.2×10⁻²]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

H₂SO₄ ⟶ H⁺ + HSO₄⁻ Na₂SO₄ ⟶ 2Na⁺ + SO₄²⁻ 0 0.036 0.018 HSO₄⁻ ⇌ H⁺ + SO₄²⁻ 1 1 0.018 (Q > Kc so the reaction will go in backward direction) (1+a) (1-a) (0.018-a) ≈ 1 ≈ 1 Now, 0.012 = 1×(0.018-a)/1 a = 0.006 [SO₄²⁻] = 0.018 - 0.006 = 1.2×10⁻² M PbSO₄(s) ⇌ Pb²⁺(aq) + SO₄²⁻(aq) s (0.012+s) ≈ 0.012 s = 1.6×10⁻⁸/0.012 = 1.33×10⁻⁶ ∴ y = 6 Pᵒ - P
