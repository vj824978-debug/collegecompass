A → D is an endothermic reaction occurring in three steps (elementary). (i) A → B ΔH_i = +ve (ii) B → C ΔH_ii = -ve (iii) C → D ΔH_iii = -ve Which of the following graphs between potential energy (y-axis) vs reaction coordinate (x-axis) correctly represents the reaction profile of A → D?

  1. (1) Graph (1) showing potential energy curve with three peaks
  2. (2) Graph (2) showing potential energy curve with three peaks, final state higher
  3. (3) Graph (3) showing potential energy curve with three peaks, final state highest
  4. (4) Graph (4) showing potential energy curve with three peaks, final state lowest

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: A → D; ΔH = (+)ve = E_D - E_A ∴ E_D > E_A Mechanism analysis: A → B; ΔH = (+)ve ⟹ E_B > E_A (energy increases) B → C; ΔH = (-)ve ⟹ E_C < E_B (energy decreases) C → D; ΔH = (-)ve ⟹ E_D < E_C (energy decreases) Energy level ordering: From step (i): E_B > E_A From step (ii): E_C < E_B From step (iii): E_D < E_C Combining these constraints: E_A < E_D < E_C < E_B Since the overall reaction is endothermic (ΔH = +ve), the final product D must have higher energy than reactant A. E_D > E_A The reaction profile must show: - Start at A (lowest energy) - Rise to B (highest energy - first activation energy) - Drop to C (intermediate energy) - Drop further to D (higher than A but lower than C) Graph (3) correctly shows this sequence where: - A is at the lowest point - B is at the highest point (first peak) - C is intermediate (second peak lower than B) - D is the final point, higher than A but lower than C, making the overall reaction endothermic
