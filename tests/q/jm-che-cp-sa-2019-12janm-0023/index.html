The increasing order of reactivity of the following compounds towards reaction with alkyl halides directly is-

  1. (1) B < A < C < D
  2. (2) A < C < D < B
  3. (3) A < B < C < D
  4. (4) B < A < D < C

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Nucleophilicity order B < A < C < D To determine the reactivity towards reaction with alkyl halides, we need to consider the nucleophilicity of the compounds: (A) Benzamide (C6H5-CO-NH2): The NH2 group is attached to a carbonyl carbon. The nitrogen's lone pair is delocalized into the carbonyl, reducing its nucleophilicity. (B) Phthalimide (cyclic imide): The nitrogen is part of a cyclic imide with two carbonyl groups. Both carbonyls withdraw electron density from nitrogen, significantly reducing nucleophilicity. (C) 2-Aminobenzonitrile (o-C6H4(NH2)(CN)): The NH2 group is on a benzene ring with a cyano group nearby. The cyano group is electron-withdrawing through the benzene ring, which reduces the nucleophilicity of NH2 but not as severely as in (A) or (B). (D) Aniline (C6H5-NH2): The NH2 group is directly attached to benzene. The lone pair on nitrogen is partially delocalized into the aromatic ring, but aniline is still a stronger nucleophile than compounds A, B, and C due to the inductive effects of the electron-withdrawing groups in A, B, and C. Nucleophilicity: B (least nucleophilic - most hindered) < A (carbonyl effect) < C (cyano withdrawing effect) < D (aniline - most nucleophilic) Therefore, the increasing order of reactivity is: B < A < C < D
