Given below are two statements: Statement (I): For ClF3, all three possible structures may be drawn as follows. [Three structures shown with F and Cl atoms] Statement (II): Structure III is most stable, as the orbitals having the lone pairs are axial, where the l-p-bp repulsion is minimum. In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

  1. (1) Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
  4. (4) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For ClF3, Statement I is correct - all three possible structures can be drawn as shown. However, Statement II is incorrect. Structure III (where both lone pairs are axial) would actually have maximum l-p-lp repulsion, not minimum. Structure I, where the lone pairs are in equatorial positions, is most stable because it minimizes l-p-bp repulsion. In VSEPR theory, equatorial lone pair positions experience less repulsion than axial positions.
