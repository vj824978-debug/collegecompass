If G be the gravitational constant and u be the energy density then which of the following quantity have the dimension as that the sqrt(uG)

  1. (1) Pressure gradient per unit mass
  2. (2) Force per unit mass
  3. (3) Gravitational potential
  4. (4) Energy per unit mass

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

[uG] = (M^1 L^2 T^-1)(M^-1 L^3 T^-2) = [M^0 L^2 T^-1]. [sqrt(uG)] = [L^1 T^-1]. Force per unit mass has dimension [MLT^-2]/M = [LT^-2]... Wait, let me recalculate. Energy density u = [ML^-1T^-2]. G = [M^-1L^3T^-2]. [uG] = [ML^-1T^-2][M^-1L^3T^-2] = [L^2T^-4]. [sqrt(uG)] = [LT^-2]. This matches Force per unit mass dimension.
