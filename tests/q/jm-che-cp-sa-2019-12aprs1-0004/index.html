The major product of the following reaction is: Starting material is a cyclohexadiene ring fused to a benzene ring with HO groups at two positions, subjected to (1) CrO3 (2) SOCl2/delta (3) delta

  1. (1) A cyclopentanone fused to benzene with HO at one position
  2. (2) A cyclopentanone fused to benzene with Cl at one position
  3. (3) A cyclopentanone fused to benzene with Cl at different position
  4. (4) A cyclopentanone fused to benzene with HO at different position

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Starting material: 1,2-dihydroxycyclohexadiene fused to benzene ring Step 1: CrO3 oxidation - The two hydroxyl groups (-OH) are oxidized. Since they are on adjacent carbons of the saturated ring portion, CrO3 oxidizes these primary/secondary alcohols to carbonyl groups. The primary alcohol on the 5-membered ring saturated portion gets oxidized to a carboxylic acid or ketone depending on structure. The secondary alcohol on the 6-membered aromatic-adjacent portion gets oxidized to a ketone. Intermediate: A keto-carboxylic acid or diketo compound Step 2: SOCl2 treatment - SOCl2 (thionyl chloride) converts carboxylic acids to acid chlorides, but in presence of heat (delta/reflux), it can promote further reactions. However, the primary product after SOCl2 treatment would convert -COOH to -COCl or promote ring closure. Step 3: Heat (delta) - Under heating, the system undergoes cyclization. The carbonyl group adjacent to the aromatic ring and the remaining OH group or activated species participate in intramolecular cyclization forming a 5-membered cyclopentanone fused to the benzene ring, with one hydroxyl group remaining. Final product: 6,7-dihydro-5H-benzo[c]cyclohepten-5-one with OH group at position 3 of benzene ring (option 1) The answer is [1] because the oxidation of diols followed by SOCl2 and heating leads to formation of the cyclopentanone with retention of one OH group on the benzene ring.
