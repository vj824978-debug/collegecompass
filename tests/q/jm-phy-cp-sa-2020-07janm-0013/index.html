An elevator in a building can carry a maximum of 10 person, with the average mass of each person being 68 kg. The mass of the elevator itself is 920 kg and it moves with a constant speed of 3 m/s. The frictional force opposing the motion is 6000 N. If the elevator is moving up with its full capacity, the power delivered by the motor to the elevator (g = 10 m/s^2) must be at least :

  1. (1) 56300 W
  2. (2) 48000 W
  3. (3) 66000 W
  4. (4) 62360 W

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: - Maximum capacity: 10 persons - Average mass per person: 68 kg - Mass of elevator: 920 kg - Constant speed: v = 3 m/s - Frictional force: f = 6000 N - g = 10 m/s^2 Total mass when elevator is full: M_total = 10 × 68 + 920 = 680 + 920 = 1600 kg Weight of system: W = M_total × g = 1600 × 10 = 16000 N Since the elevator moves with constant speed (a = 0), the net force is zero. Therefore, tension T in the cable equals: T = W + f = 16000 + 6000 = 22000 N Power delivered by motor: Power = T × v = 22000 × 3 = 66000 W
