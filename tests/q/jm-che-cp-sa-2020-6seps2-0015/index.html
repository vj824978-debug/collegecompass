The average molar mass of chlorine is 35.5 g mol^(-1). The ratio of ^35Cl to ^37Cl in naturally occurring chlorine is close to:

  1. (1) 4 : 1
  2. (2) 1 : 1
  3. (3) 2 : 1
  4. (4) 3 : 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Let mole ratio of ^35Cl : ^37Cl = x : 1 Using the formula for average molar mass: Av. molar mass = (n1*M1 + n2*M2)/(n1 + n2) Where: n1 = x (moles of ^35Cl) M1 = 35 (molar mass of ^35Cl) n2 = 1 (moles of ^37Cl) M2 = 37 (molar mass of ^37Cl) Substituting into the formula: 35.5 = (x × 35 + 1 × 37)/(x + 1) Multiplying both sides by (x + 1): 35.5(x + 1) = 35x + 37 35.5x + 35.5 = 35x + 37 35.5x - 35x = 37 - 35.5 0.5x = 1.5 x = 3 Therefore, the ratio of ^35Cl : ^37Cl = 3 : 1
