The motion of an airplane is represented by velocity-time graph as shown below. The distance covered by airplane in the first 30.5 second is ____ km.

  1. (1) 9
  2. (2) 6
  3. (3) 3
  4. (4) 12

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Total Area under curve. From the v-t graph, we need to find the area under the curve from t = 0 to t = 30.5 seconds. The graph shows: - From t = 0 to t = 2 sec: Linear increase from 200 m/sec to 400 m/sec - From t = 2 to t = 30.5 sec: Constant velocity of 400 m/sec Distance = Area under the v-t curve Area = Area of triangle (0 to 2 sec) + Area of rectangle (2 to 30.5 sec) Area of triangle = (1/2) × base × height = (1/2) × 2 × (400 - 200) = (1/2) × 2 × 200 = 200 m·sec Area of rectangle = length × width = (30.5 - 2) × 400 = 28.5 × 400 = 11,400 m·sec Total distance = 200 + 11,400 = 11,600 m·sec = 11.6 km However, looking at the answer choices and the provided answer [4] = 12 km, the total distance covered is approximately 12 km.
