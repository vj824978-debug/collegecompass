The Crystal Field Stabilization Energy (CFSE) and magnetic moment (spin-only) of an octahedral aqua complex of a metal ion (M^n+) are -0.8 λ_o and 3.87 B_M, respectively. Identify (M^n+).

  1. (1) Cr^3+
  2. (2) Mn^4+
  3. (3) V^3+
  4. (4) Co^3+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using spin-only magnetic moment formula μ = sqrt(n(n+2)) B_M where n = number of unpaired electrons. From μ = 3.87 B_M, we get n = 3 unpaired electrons. Checking electron configurations: Cr^3+ is 4s^0 3d^3 (n=3), Mn^4+ is 4s^0 3d^3 (n=3), V^3+ is 4s^0 3d^2 (n=2), Co^3+ is 4s^0 3d^6 (n=3). For CFSE in octahedral complex = (-0.4n_t2g + 0.6n_eg)λ_o. For Cr^3+, Mn^4+, and Co^3+, CFSE = (-0.4×3 + 0.6×0)λ_o = -1.2λ_o. For Co^3+ in aqua complex (weak field ligand, H2O): 4s^0 3d^6 splits as t_2g^6 eg^0, giving CFSE = (-0.4×5 + 0.6×2)λ_o = -0.8λ_o. Hence M^n+ is Co^3+.
