Select the correct statements. (A) Crystalline solids have long range order. (B) Crystalline solids are isotropic. (C) Amorphous solids are sometimes called pseudo solids. (D) Amorphous solids soften over a range of temperatures. (E) Amorphous solids have a definite heat of fusion. Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) (A), (B), (D) only
  2. (2) (A), (B), (E) only
  3. (3) (C), (A), (C), (D) only
  4. (4) (C), (D) only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Characteristics of crystalline solids: 1. Crystalline solids have long range order - TRUE (A is correct). 2. Crystalline solids are anisotropic in nature - FALSE (B is incorrect as they are anisotropic, not isotropic). Characteristics of amorphous solids: 1. Amorphous solids have short range order - TRUE. 2. Amorphous solids are sometimes called pseudo solids - TRUE (C is correct). 3. Amorphous solids soften over a range of temperatures - TRUE (D is correct). 4. Amorphous solids do not have a definite heat of fusion - FALSE (E is incorrect as they lack a definite melting point and heat of fusion). Correct statements are A, C, and D.
