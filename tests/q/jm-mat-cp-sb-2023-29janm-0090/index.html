If the co-efficient of x^9 in (alpha*x^3 - 1/(beta*x)) and the co-efficient of x^(-9) in (alpha*x - 1/(beta*x^3)) are equal, then (alpha*beta)^2 is equal to ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 01

Solution

T_{r1+1} = ^{11}C_r (alpha*x^3)^{11-r} (beta*x)^{-r} = ^{11}C_r alpha^{11-r} beta^{-r} x^{33-4r} 33 - 4r_1 = 9 => r_1 = 6 T_{r2+1} = ^{11}C_{r2} (alpha*x)^{11-r2} (beta*x^3)^{-r2} = (-1)^{r2} ^{11}C_{r2} alpha^{11-r2} beta^{-r2} x^{11-4r2} 11 - 4r_2 = 9 => r_2 = 5 Equating the coefficients: ^{11}C_6 alpha^5 beta^{-6} = ^{11}C_5 alpha^6 beta^{-5} => alpha*beta = 1
