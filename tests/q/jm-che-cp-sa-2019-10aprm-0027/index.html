Which one of the following graphs between molar conductivity (Λ_m) versus sqrt(C) is correct

  1. (1) Graph showing NaCl and KCl lines decreasing with sqrt(C), NaCl above KCl
  2. (2) Graph showing KCl and NaCl lines decreasing with sqrt(C), KCl above NaCl
  3. (3) Graph showing KCl and NaCl lines decreasing with sqrt(C), KCl above NaCl
  4. (4) Graph showing NaCl and KCl lines decreasing with sqrt(C), NaCl above KCl

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

At infinite dilution molar conductivity of KCl is greater than the molar conductivity of NaCl. This is because at infinite dilution, the molar conductivity depends on the ionic conductivity of the individual ions. KCl dissociates into K+ and Cl- ions, while NaCl dissociates into Na+ and Cl- ions. Since K+ has higher ionic conductivity than Na+, KCl has higher molar conductivity at infinite dilution compared to NaCl. As concentration increases (moving right on the sqrt(C) axis), both salts show a decrease in molar conductivity due to increased ion-ion interactions. The y-intercept (at sqrt(C) = 0, representing infinite dilution) for KCl is higher than that for NaCl. Therefore, the correct graph should show KCl line starting at a higher value on the Λ_m axis and both lines decreasing as sqrt(C) increases, with KCl remaining above NaCl throughout. This matches option (4).
