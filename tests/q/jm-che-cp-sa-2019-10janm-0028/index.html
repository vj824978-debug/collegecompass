Liquids A and B form an ideal solution in the entire composition range. At 350 K, the vapor pressures of pure A and pure B are 7 × 10^3 Pa and 12 × 10^3 Pa, respectively. The composition of the vapor in equilibrium with a solution containing 40 mole percent of A at this temperature is:

  1. (1) x_A = 0.76; x_B = 0.24
  2. (2) x_A = 0.28; x_B = 0.72
  3. (3) x_A = 0.4; x_B = 0.6
  4. (4) x_A = 0.37; x_B = 0.63

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For an ideal solution, the composition of vapor in equilibrium is given by Raoult's Law: y_A = P_A / P_T = (P_A^0 × X_A) / (P_A^0 × X_A + P_B^0 × X_B) Given: P_A^0 = 7 × 10^3 Pa P_B^0 = 12 × 10^3 Pa X_A = 0.40 (40 mole percent of A in solution) X_B = 1 - 0.40 = 0.60 Substituting values: y_A = (7 × 10^3 × 0.4) / (7 × 10^3 × 0.4 + 12 × 10^3 × 0.6) y_A = (2800) / (2800 + 7200) y_A = 2800 / 10000 y_A = 0.28 Therefore: y_B = 1 - 0.28 = 0.72 So x_A = 0.28 and x_B = 0.72
