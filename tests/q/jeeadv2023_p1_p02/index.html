A plane polarized blue light ray is incident on a prism such that there is no reflection from the surface of the prism. The angle of deviation of the emergent ray is δ= 60° (see Figure-1). The angle of minimum deviation for red light from the same prism is δ_min = 30° (see Figure-2). The refractive index of the prism material for blue light is √3. Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct? [Figure-1 shows a triangular prism with plane polarized blue light incident on one surface, with deviation angle δ = 60° marked at the exit] [Figure-2 shows a triangular prism with plane polarized red light incident on one surface, with deviation angle δ = 30° marked at the exit]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The blue light is polarized in the plane of incidence.
  2. (B) The angle of the prism is 45°.
  3. (C) The refractive index of the material of the prism for red light is √2.
  4. (D) The angle of refraction for blue light in air at the exit plane of the prism is 60°

Answer

Correct answer: ACD

Solution

For Figure-1 (Blue light) μ = tan ip ip = tan⁻¹√3 = 60° δ = i + e - A ⟹ 60° = 60° + e - A ⟹ e = A ... (i) At incident surface, sin60° = √3 sin r₁ ⟹ r₁ = 30° ∵ r₁ + r₂ = A ⟹ r₂ = A - 30° At emergent surface, √3 sin(A - 30°) = sin A (3/2) sin A - (√3/2) cos A = sin A ⟹ tan A = √3 A = 60° ⟹ e = 60° For Figure 2 (red line) For minimum deviation sin((A + δm)/2) / sin(A/2) = μR ⟹ sin 45° / sin 30° = μR Or, μR = √2
