An electromagnetic wave is transporting energy in the negative z direction. At a certain point and certain time the direction of electric field of the wave is along positive y direction. What will be the direction of the magnetic field of the wave at that point and instant?

  1. (1) Negative direction of x
  2. (2) Negative direction of y
  3. (3) Positive direction of z
  4. (4) Positive direction of x

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For an electromagnetic wave, the relationship between electric field vector E, magnetic field vector B, and the direction of propagation is given by: E × B = C (where C is the direction of propagation/Poynting vector) Given: - Wave is transporting energy in the negative z direction - Electric field E is along positive y direction - Therefore, the direction of propagation (Poynting vector) is in the negative z direction Using the cross product relation: E × B = Direction of propagation Let E be along +y direction and propagation along -z direction. We need to find B such that: E × B points in -z direction If E is along +y and we need E × B to point in -z direction: (+y) × B = -z Using right-hand rule: if E is along +y and we need the cross product to point in -z, then B must be along +x direction. Verification: (+y) × (+x) = -z ✓ Therefore, B should be in the positive x direction.
