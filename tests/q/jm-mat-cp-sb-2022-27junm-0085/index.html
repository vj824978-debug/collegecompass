If the coefficient of x^10 in the binomial expansion of (sqrt(x)/5^(1/4) + 5^(1/3)/x^3)^60 is 5^(k/l), where l, k ∈ N and d 1 is co-prime to 5, then k is equal to ___.

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

General term T_{r+1} = 60C_r (x^2)^(60-r) (x^(-1/3))^r (5^(-1/4))^(60-r) (5^(1/3))^r. For coefficient of x^10: 10 = 2(60-r) - r/3, which gives r = 24. Coefficient of x^10 = 60C_24 * 5^(12/9) = 5^k/l where k = 3 + exponent of 5 in 60C_24. Using Legendre's formula for exponent of 5 in 60C_24: k = 3 + (12 + 2 - 4 - 0 - 7 - 1) = 3 + 2 = 5
