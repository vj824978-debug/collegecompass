The substituents R₁ and R₂ for nine peptides are listed in the table given below. How many of these peptides are positively charged at pH = 7.0? [Peptide structure showing: H₃N⁺-CH(H)-CO-NH-CH-CO-NH-CH-CO-NH-CH(H)-COO⁻ with R₁ and R₂ as side chains] Table of peptides with R₁ and R₂ substituents: Peptide I: R₁=H, R₂=H Peptide II: R₁=H, R₂=CH₃ Peptide III: R₁=CH₂COOH, R₂=H Peptide IV: R₁=CH₂CONH₂, R₂=(CH₂)₃NH₂ Peptide V: R₁=CH₂CONH₂⁻, R₂=CH₂CONH₂ Peptide VI: R₁=(CH₂)₄NH₂, R₂=(CH₂)₃NH₂ Peptide VII: R₁=CH₂COOH, R₂=CH₂CONH₂ Peptide VIII: R₁=CH₂OH, R₂=(CH₂)₃NH₂ Peptide IX: R₁=(CH₂)₄NH₂⁻, R₂=CH₃

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Peptides with isoelectric point (pI) > 7, would exist as cation in neutral solution (pH = 7).
