A compound with structure containing a benzene ring with CN group, CH(OCH3) group undergoes the following reaction: (i) C6H5MgBr (1.0 equivalent) in dry Ether (ii) H3O+ to give Major Product X. The structure of X is:

  1. (1) Structure (A): Benzene ring with C(=O)C6H5 ketone and C6H5 substituent
  2. (2) Structure (B): Benzene ring with C(=O)C6H5 ketone and CH(OCH3) substituent
  3. (3) Structure (C): Benzene ring with CH2NH2 and C6H5 substituent
  4. (4) Structure (D): Benzene ring with CH2NH2 and CH(OCH3) substituent

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The starting material has a cyano group (CN) and a methoxymethine group (CH(OCH3)). When treated with phenylmagnesium bromide (PhMgBr), the Grignard reagent attacks the electrophilic carbon of the nitrile group. The CN group is converted to an imine intermediate (C=NMgBr), which upon aqueous workup with H3O+ hydrolyzes to form a ketone (C=O). The phenyl group from the Grignard reagent becomes attached to the carbonyl carbon. The acetal/methoxymethine group (CH(OCH3)) remains intact as it is relatively unreactive toward Grignard reagents under these conditions. The final product after dehydration is the aromatic ketone with phenyl substituent and the methoxymethine group preserved.
