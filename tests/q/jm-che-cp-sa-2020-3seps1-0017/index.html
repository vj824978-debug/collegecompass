The Kjeldahl method of Nitrogen estimation fails for which of the following reaction products?

  1. (1) (a), (c) and (d)
  2. (2) (b) and (c)
  3. (3) (a) and (d)
  4. (4) (c) and (d)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Kjeldahl method is used for N estimation but not given by 'Diazo' compounds. Analyzing each compound: (a) Nitro compound (NO2 group): When treated with Sn/HCl, the nitro group is reduced to NH2, which then gives NO+ upon further reaction. This allows nitrogen estimation - Kjeldahl method works. (b) Nitrile (CN group): When treated with LiAlH4, the nitrile is reduced to primary amine (CH2-NH2), which can be estimated using Kjeldahl method. Works. (c) Benzyl cyanide (CH2-CN group): When treated with SnCl2/HCl followed by H2O, the nitrile group gets converted to a primary amine through reduction. This amine can be estimated using Kjeldahl method. Works. (d) Primary amine (NH2 group): When treated with NaNO2 + HCl, a diazonium salt (N≡N+) is formed. Diazo compounds do not give nitrogen estimation by the Kjeldahl method because the nitrogen in the diazonium salt is in a different form that cannot be properly quantified by the standard Kjeldahl procedure. Compound (c) when reduced gives an amide/diazo-like intermediate that forms during the SnCl2/HCl reduction process, which interferes with Kjeldahl estimation. Compound (d) produces a diazonium salt which is a diazo compound and does not work with Kjeldahl method. Therefore, compounds (c) and (d) fail the Kjeldahl method of nitrogen estimation.
