Consider the hyperbola H: x^2 - y^2 = 1 and a circle S with center N(x₂, 0). Suppose that H and S touch each other at a point P(x₁, y₁) with x₁ > 1 and y₁ > 0. The common tangent to H and S at P intersects the x-axis at point M. If (l, m) is the centroid of the triangle ΔP MN, then the correct expression(s) is(are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) dl/dx₁ = 1 - 1/(3x₁²) for x₁ > 1
  2. (B) dm/dx₁ = x₁/(3(sqrt(x₁² - 1))) for x₁ > 1
  3. (C) dl/dx₁ = 1 + 1/(3x₁²) for x₁ > 1
  4. (D) dm/dy₁ = 1/3 for y₁ > 0

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

Tangent at P, xx₁ - yy₁ = 1 intersects x axis at M(1/x₁, 0) Slope of normal = -y₁/x₁ = (y₁ - 0)/(x₁ - x₂) ⟹ x₂ = 2x₁ ⟹ N ≡ (2x₁, 0) For centroid ℓ = (3x₁ + 1/x₁)/3, m = y₁/3 dℓ/dx₁ = 1 - 1/(3x₁²) dm/dy₁ = 1/3, dx₁/dm = 1/(3 dy₁/dx₁) = x₁/(3√(x₁² - 1))
