Choose the correct representation of conductometric titration of benzoic acid vs sodium hydroxide.

  1. (1) Graph showing conductance vs V_NaOH with initial decrease then increase
  2. (2) Graph showing conductance vs V_NaOH with V-shaped curve (decrease then increase)
  3. (3) Graph showing conductance vs V_NaOH with flat region then sharp increase
  4. (4) Graph showing conductance vs V_NaOH with triangular shape (increase then decrease)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For conductometric titration of benzoic acid (weak acid) with sodium hydroxide (strong base): Initial stage: Before equivalence point - Benzoic acid (weak acid) is being neutralized by NaOH - The reaction is: C6H5COOH + NaOH → C6H5COONa + H2O - H+ ions (high mobility) are replaced by Na+ ions (lower mobility) - The conductivity decreases initially At equivalence point: - All benzoic acid is converted to sodium benzoate - Minimum conductance is reached After equivalence point: - Excess NaOH is added - OH- ions (very high mobility) are present in solution - The conductivity increases sharply due to highly mobile OH- ions Therefore, the curve shows: 1. Initial decrease in conductance (weak acid neutralization) 2. Minimum at equivalence point 3. Sharp increase after equivalence point (due to excess OH-) This matches graph (1) which shows a minimum followed by an increase.
