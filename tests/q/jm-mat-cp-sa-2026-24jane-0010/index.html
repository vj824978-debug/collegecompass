The largest value of n, for which 40^n divides 60!, is

  1. (1) 13
  2. (2) 11
  3. (3) 12
  4. (4) 14

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

40^n = 2^(3n) × 5^n E_2(60!) = floor(60/2) + floor(60/2^2) + floor(60/2^3) + floor(60/2^4) + floor(60/2^5) = 30 + 15 + 7 + 3 + 1 = 56 E_5(60!) = floor(60/5) + floor(60/5^2) = 12 + 2 = 14 40^n = (2^3)^n × 5^n = (2^3 × 5)^n 60! = 2^56 × 5^14 × ... = 2^14 × (2^3 × 5)^14 Therefore, maximum value of n is 14.
