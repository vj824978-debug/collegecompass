A particle is traveling 4 times as fast as an electron. Assuming the ratio of de-Broglie wavelength of a particle to that of electron is 2:1, the mass of the particle is:

  1. (1) (A) 1/16 times the mass of e-
  2. (2) (B) 8 times the mass of e-
  3. (3) (C) 1/8 times the mass of e-
  4. (4) (D) 16 times the mass of e-

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Using de-Broglie wavelength formula: λ = h/(mv). Given: v_p = 4v_e and λ_p/λ_e = 2/1. From λ = h/(mv), we have λ_p/λ_e = (m_e * v_e)/(m_p * v_p) = 2/1. Substituting v_p = 4v_e: (m_e * v_e)/(m_p * 4v_e) = 2/1, which gives m_e/(4m_p) = 2/1. Therefore m_p = m_e/8 or m_p = (1/8) times the mass of electron.
