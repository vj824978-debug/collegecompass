*12 As shown schematically in the figure, two vessels contain water solutions (at temperature T) of potassium permanganate (KMnO₄) of different concentrations n₁ and n₂ (n₁>n₂) molecules per unit volume with Δn=(n₁-n₂)<n₁. When they are connected by a tube of small length l and cross-sectional area S, KMnO₄ starts to diffuse from the left to the right vessel through the tube. Consider the collection of molecules to behave as dilute ideal gases and the difference in their partial pressure in the two vessels causing the diffusion. The speed v of the molecules is limited by the viscous force -βv on each molecule, where β is a constant. Neglecting all terms of the order (Δn)², which of the following is/are correct? (kB is the Boltzmann constant) [Two connected vessels with water solutions of different concentrations n₁ and n₂ connected by a tube of length l and cross-sectional area S]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) the force causing the molecules to move across the tube is Δnk_BTS
  2. (B) force balance implies n_Jβvl = Δnk_BT
  3. (C) total number of molecules going across the tube per sec is (Δn/l)(k_BT/β)S
  4. (D) rate of molecules getting transferred through the tube does not change with time

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution

PV = NKT P₁ = n₁KᵦT P₂ = n₂KᵦT n₁, n₂ → no. of molecules per unit volume Force acting on the molecules F = ΔPS =(P₁ - P₂)S F = (n₁ - n₂)KᵦTS = ΔnkᵦTS...(i) So Option (A) is correct Total no. of molecules in the tube = (n₁S𝓁) Force acting on each molecules F = -βv So total force acting on (n₁s𝓁) molecules = -(βv)(n₁s𝓁)...(ii) From (i) and (ii) ΔnKᵦTS = (βv)(n₁S𝓁) So, ΔnKᵦT = (βvn₁𝓁)...(iii) So, Option (B) is correct. No. of molecules going across the tube per second = (svn₁) = (sn₁)(ΔnKᵦT/βn₁𝓁) = (SΔnKᵦT/β𝓁) So, Option (C) is correct. With respect to time Δn changes hence rate of molecules getting transferred through the tube changes with time. So, Option (D) is incorrect. [Two cylindrical containers connected by a tube.
