Let the ellipse, E₁: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1, a > b and E₂: x²/A² + y²/B² = 1, A < B have same eccentricity 1/sqrt(3). Let the product of their lengths of latus rectum be 32/sqrt(3), and the distance between the foci of E₁ be 4. If E₁ and E₂ meet at A,B,C and D, then the area of the quadrilateral ABCD equals:

  1. (1) 6*sqrt(6)
  2. (2) 18*sqrt(6)/5
  3. (3) 12*sqrt(6)/5
  4. (4) 24*sqrt(6)/5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

2ae = 4 2a(1/sqrt(3)) = 4 => a = 2*sqrt(3) => 1 - b²/12 = 1/3 => b² = 8 Now 2b²/a · 2A²/B = 32/sqrt(3) => 2(8/(2*sqrt(3))) · 2A²/B = 32/sqrt(3) => A² = 2B 1 - A²/B² = 1/3 => 1 - 2B/B² = 1/3 => B = 3 => A² = 6 Ellipse E₁: x²/12 + y²/8 = 1 ...(1) Ellipse E₂: x²/6 + y²/9 = 1 ...(2) On solving (1) & (2) we get (x, y) ≡ (sqrt(6)/sqrt(5), 6/sqrt(5)), (-sqrt(6)/sqrt(5), 6/sqrt(5)), (sqrt(6)/sqrt(5), -6/sqrt(5)), (-sqrt(6)/sqrt(5), -6/sqrt(5)) The four points are vertices of rectangle and its area = 24*sqrt(6)/5
