If the sum of the heights of transmitting and receiving antennas in the line of sight of communication is fixed at 160 m, then the maximum range of LOS communication is ____ km. (Take radius of Earth = 6400 km)

Answer

Correct answer: 64

Solution

Given: h_t + h_r = 160 m, R = 6400 km. The maximum range is dM = sqrt(2Rh_t) + sqrt(2Rh_r). Substituting h_r = 160 - h_t: dM = sqrt(2Rh_t) + sqrt(2R(160 - h_t)). To find maximum, differentiate with respect to h_t and set equal to zero. This gives h_t = h_r = 80 m. Therefore, dM = sqrt(2 × 6400000 × 80) + sqrt(2 × 6400000 × 80) = 2 × sqrt(2 × 6400000 × 80) = 2 × 4.8 × 10^3 = 64 km
