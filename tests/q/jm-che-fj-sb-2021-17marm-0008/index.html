The mole fraction of a solute in a 100 molal aqueous solution is __________ × 10^-2. (Round off to the nearest integer). [Given: Atomic masses: H: 1.0u, O: 16.0u]

Answer

Correct answer: 64

Solution

Given: Molality = 100 mol/kg. For 100 molal solution, 100 = n_solute × 1000 / (n_solvent × molw(solvent)). Setting n_solvent × molw(solvent) = 1000, we get n_solute = 100. Then n_solute × 1000 / ((1 - n_solute) × 18) = 100, solving: n_solute / (1 - n_solute) = 18/10. Therefore n_solute = 18/28 = 0.6428 = 64.28 × 10^-2. Rounded to nearest integer: 64 × 10^-2.
