The ion that has sp^3d^2 hybridization for the central atom is -

  1. (1) [ICl4]^-
  2. (2) [ICl2]^-
  3. (3) [BrF2]^-
  4. (4) [IF6]^-

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

To determine sp^3d^2 hybridization, we need to find the ion where the central atom has 6 electron pairs (4 bonding + 2 lone pairs or 2 bonding + 4 lone pairs, etc.) in an octahedral electron geometry. Analyzing each option: (1) [ICl4]^-: - Central atom: I - Valence electrons in I: 7 - Additional electron from negative charge: 1 - Total electrons: 8 - 4 Cl atoms bonded to I: 4 bonding pairs - Remaining electrons: 8 - 4 = 4 electrons = 2 lone pairs - Total electron pairs: 4 + 2 = 6 - Hybridization: sp^3d^2 - Geometry: Square planar (as shown in the diagram) (2) [ICl2]^-: - Central atom: I - Total electrons: 7 + 1 = 8 - 2 Cl atoms bonded: 2 bonding pairs - Remaining: 8 - 2 = 6 electrons = 3 lone pairs - Total electron pairs: 5 - Hybridization: sp^3d (3) [BrF2]^-: - Central atom: Br - Valence electrons: 7 + 1 = 8 - 2 F atoms bonded: 2 bonding pairs - Remaining: 8 - 2 = 6 electrons = 3 lone pairs - Total electron pairs: 5 - Hybridization: sp^3d (4) [IF6]^-: - Central atom: I - Valence electrons: 7 + 1 = 8 - 6 F atoms bonded: 6 bonding pairs - Remaining: 8 - 6 = 2 electrons = 1 lone pair - Total electron pairs: 7 - Hybridization: sp^3d^3 Therefore, [ICl4]^- has sp^3d^2 hybridization with square planar geometry.
