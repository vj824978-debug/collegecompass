A string of area of cross-section 4 mm^2 and length 0.5 m is connected with a rigid body of mass 2 kg. The body is rotated in a vertical circular path of radius 0.5 m. The body acquires a speed of 5 m/s at the bottom of the circular path. Strain produced in the string when the body is at the bottom of the circle is ___ × 10^(-5). (use young's modulus 10^11 N/m^2 and g = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 30

Solution

Given: A = 4 × 10^(-6) m^2, l = 0.5 m, m = 2 kg, v_b = 5 m/s, r = 0.5 m, Y = 10^11 N/m^2, g = 10 m/s^2. At the bottom of the circular path, tension in the string: T_b = mg + m(v_b^2/l) = 20 + 2 × (25/0.5) = 20 + 100 = 120 N. Strain = (Δl/l) = (T_b/A) × (1/Y) = (120/(4×10^(-6))) × (1/10^11) = 30 × 10^(-5)
