The intensity of the light from a bulb incident on a surface is 0.22 W/m². The amplitude of the magnetic field in this light-wave is ______ × 10^(-9) T. (Given: Permittivity of vacuum ε₀ = 8.85 × 10^(-12) C²N^(-1)m^(-2), speed of light in vacuum c = 3 × 10^8 ms^(-1))

Answer

Correct answer: 43.00

Solution

Using the intensity formula for electromagnetic waves: I = (1/2)ε₀E₀²c = (1/2)ε₀(cB₀)²c = (1/2)ε₀c³B₀². Substituting values: 0.22 = (1/2)(8.85 × 10^(-12))(3 × 10^8)³B₀². Solving: 0.22 = (1/2)(8.85 × 10^(-12))(27 × 10^24)B₀². Therefore B₀ = 43 × 10^(-9) T.
