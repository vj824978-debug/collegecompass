If 80 g of copper sulphate CuSO₄.5H₂O is dissolved in deionised water to make 5 L of solution. The concentration of the copper sulphate solution is x × 10^(-3) mol L^(-1). The value of x is ______. [Atomic masses Cu: 63.54 u, S: 32 u, O: 16 u, H: 1 u]

Answer

Correct answer: 64

Solution

Mass of CuSO₄.5H₂O = 80 g. Volume of solution = 5 L. Molar mass of CuSO₄.5H₂O = 249.54 g/mol. Moles of CuSO₄.5H₂O = 80/249.54 = 0.32 mol. Concentration = moles/volume of solution = 0.32/5 = 0.064 M = 64 × 10^(-3) mol/L. Therefore, x = 64.
