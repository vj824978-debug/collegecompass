If A(1, -1, 2), B(5, 7, -6), C(3, 4, -10) and D(-1, -4, -2) are the vertices of a quadrilateral ABCD, then its area is:

  1. (1) 12√29
  2. (2) 24√29
  3. (3) 24√7
  4. (4) 48√7

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Using the formula for area of quadrilateral: Area = (1/2)|AC × BD|. Calculate vectors AC = (2i + 5j - 12k) and BD = (6i + 11j - 4k). Compute cross product AC × BD = (112i - 64j - 8k). Find magnitude: |AC × BD| = √(196 + 64 + 1) = √261 = √(9×29) = 3√29. Therefore Area = (1/2) × 24√29 = 12√29. However the solution shown simplifies as: (1/2)|112i - 64j - 8k| = 4|14i - 8j - k| = 4√(196 + 64 + 1) = 4√261 = 4×3√29 = 12√29.
