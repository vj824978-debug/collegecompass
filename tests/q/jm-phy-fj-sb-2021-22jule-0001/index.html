The position of the centre of mass of a uniform semi-circular wire of radius 'R' placed in x-y plane with its centre at the origin and the line joining its ends as x-axis is given by (0, 2R/pi). Then, the value of |x| is _______.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

For a uniform semi-circular wire of radius R with center at origin and diameter along x-axis, the center of mass lies on the y-axis due to symmetry, so x-coordinate is 0. The y-coordinate of center of mass for a semi-circular arc is found by integration: y_com = (2R/pi). Therefore |x| = 0.
