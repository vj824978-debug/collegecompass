50 mL of 0.5 M oxalic acid is needed to neutralize 25 mL of sodium hydroxide solution. The amount of NaOH in 50 mL of the given sodium hydroxide solution is -

  1. (1) 20 g
  2. (2) 4 g
  3. (3) 80 g
  4. (4) 10 g

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

number of equivalent of oxalic acid = number of equivalent of NaOH [n_f × M × V](Oxalic acid) = [n_f × M × V](NaOH) 2 × 0.5 × 50 × 10^(-3) = 1 × M × 25 × 10^(-3) M_NaOH = 2 M n_NaOH in 50 ml = 50 × 10^(-3) × 2 = 0.1 moles ∴ mass of NaOH = 0.1 × 40 = 4 gm Ans. is not given in options So, bonus
