Let N be the sum of the numbers appeared when two fair dice are rolled and let the probability that N-2, sqrt(3N), N+2 are in geometric progression be k/48. Then the value of k is

  1. (1) 16
  2. (2) 8
  3. (3) 4
  4. (4) 2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

n-2, sqrt(3n), n+2 → G.P. For three terms in G.P., the middle term squared equals the product of the first and third terms. (sqrt(3n))^2 = (n-2)(n+2) 3n = n^2 - 4 ⇒ n^2 - 3n - 4 = 0 ⇒ n = 4, -1 (rejected) When two fair dice are rolled, the sum N can range from 2 to 12. We need P(N = 4). Number of ways to get sum = 4: (1,3), (2,2), (3,1) → 3 ways Total number of outcomes when rolling two dice = 36 P(S = 4) = 3/36 = 1/12 = 4/48 ∴ k = 4 Option (3) is correct.
