Given below are two statements: Statement - I: Compound (X), shown below, dissolves in NaHCO3 solution and has two chiral carbon atoms. The structure shows a benzene ring with Br substituent, connected to a carbonyl group (C=O) and a cyclopentane ring with a COOH group. Statement - II: Compound (Y), shown below, has two carbons with sp^3 hybridization, one carbon with sp^2 and one carbon with sp hybridization. The structure shows C=C=O. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  2. (2) Statement I is false but Statement II is true
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  4. (4) Both Statement I and Statement II are false

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For Compound (X): The structure contains a benzene ring with Br substituent attached to a carbonyl group (C=O) and a cyclopentane ring with a carboxylic acid group (COOH). The carboxylic acid group (-COOH) is acidic and can donate a proton to NaHCO3, allowing the compound to dissolve in NaHCO3 solution. Therefore, Statement I is TRUE. The cyclopentane ring has two chiral centers (marked with asterisks in the solution): one bearing the carbonyl group and one bearing the COOH group. Both carbons are bonded to four different groups, making them chiral centers. For Compound (Y): The structure is C=C=O (allene or ketene structure). Analyzing the hybridization: The central carbon (middle C in C=C=O) is sp hybridized (forms two double bonds). The first carbon (left C) is sp^2 hybridized (forms one double bond with central carbon). The oxygen is sp^2 hybridized (forms one double bond with central carbon). However, re-examining: In an allene structure R2C=C=O, the central carbon is sp hybridized (linear geometry with two double bonds). The terminal carbons are sp^2 hybridized. But the statement says two carbons with sp^3, one with sp^2, and one with sp. This requires substituents. If the allene has alkyl groups, those carbons could be sp^3. With proper substitution pattern, this arrangement is possible. Therefore, Statement II is TRUE. Both statements are true.
