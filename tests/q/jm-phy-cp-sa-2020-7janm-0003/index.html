The time period of revolution of electron in its ground state orbit in a hydrogen atom is 1.6 × 10^(-16) s. The frequency of revolution of the electron in its first excited state (in s^(-1)) is -

  1. (1) 1.6 × 10^14
  2. (2) 5.6 × 10^12
  3. (3) 6.2 × 10^15
  4. (4) 7.8 × 10^14

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From Bohr's model, we have the relationship for frequency: f ∝ z^2/n^3 where n is the principal quantum number and z is the atomic number. For hydrogen atom, z = 1 For ground state (n = 1): T1 = 1.6 × 10^(-16) s f1 = 1/T1 = 1/(1.6 × 10^(-16)) = 6.25 × 10^14 s^(-1) For first excited state (n = 2): Using the relationship f ∝ 1/n^3: f2/f1 = (n1/n2)^3 = (1/2)^3 = 1/8 Therefore: f2 = (1/8) × f1 = (1/8) × (1/(1.6 × 10^(-16))) f2 = (1/8) × (1/(1.6 × 10^(-16))) f2 = (1/(8 × 1.6 × 10^(-16))) f2 = 1/(1.28 × 10^(-15)) f2 = 7.8 × 10^14 s^(-1)
