The mean of the numbers a, b, 8, 5, 10 is 6 and their variance is 6.8. If M is the mean deviation of the numbers about the mean, then 25 M is equal to:

  1. (1) 60
  2. (2) 55
  3. (3) 50
  4. (4) 45

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From mean: (a + b + 8 + 5 + 10)/5 = 6, so a + b = 7. From variance: (a^2 + b^2 + 64 + 25 + 100)/5 - 36 = 6.8, so a^2 + b^2 = 25. Solving a + b = 7 and a^2 + b^2 = 25 gives (a,b) = (3,4) or (4,3). Mean deviation M = (1/5)(|3-6| + |4-6| + |8-6| + |5-6| + |10-6|) = (1/5)(3 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 4) = 12/5. Therefore 25M = 60.
