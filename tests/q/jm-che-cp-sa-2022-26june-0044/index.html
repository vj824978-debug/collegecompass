Halogenation of which one of the following will yield m-substituted product with respect to methyl group as a major product?

  1. (1) Benzene ring with CH3 group and CHO group in meta positions
  2. (2) Benzene ring with CH3 group and NO2 group in ortho positions
  3. (3) Benzene ring with CH3 group and OH group in ortho positions
  4. (4) Benzene ring with CH3 group and NO2 group in meta positions

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For halogenation to give m-substituted product with respect to methyl group, we need a deactivating group (meta-directing) already present on the benzene ring. Option (1) has CHO (meta-directing, deactivating) - halogenation occurs meta to both CH3 and CHO. Option (2) has NO2 ortho to CH3 (deactivating, meta-directing), but CHO is meta-directing. Option (3) has OH (ortho/para-directing, activating) ortho to CH3 - halogenation would be ortho/para to both groups. Option (4) has NO2 meta to CH3. The aldehyde group (CHO) in option (1) is a strong deactivating, meta-directing group. Halogenation will occur at the meta position to both the methyl group and the aldehyde group, giving m-substituted product as major product.
