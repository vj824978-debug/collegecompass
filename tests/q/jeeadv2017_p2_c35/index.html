The product S is [Four structural formulas of organic compounds labeled A, B, C, and D, showing variations of indene-based structures with different substituents including tert-butyl groups, methoxy groups, methyl groups, and sulfonic acid groups]

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) [A] Structure showing an indene ring system with tert-butyl group at position 5, methoxy group at position 4, and methyl groups on the five-membered ring
  2. (B) [B] Structure showing an indene ring system with tert-butyl group at position 5, methoxy carbonyl group at position 4, and methyl groups on the five-membered ring
  3. (C) [C] Structure showing a naphthalene-like fused ring system with tert-butyl group at position 5, methoxy group at position 4, and methyl groups
  4. (D) [D] Structure showing an indene ring system with tert-butyl group at position 5, sulfonic acid and methoxy groups at positions 4 and 3, and a ketone on the five-membered ring with methyl group

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

The solution shows a reaction sequence starting with compound P, which contains a tert-butyl group and a tetralin structure with an ester group (CO₂Et). Step 1: P undergoes a Grignard reaction with excess CH₃MgBr in ether followed by aqueous workup (C₂H₅OH, O, H₂O) to form compound Q. The ester is reduced to a tertiary alcohol with two methyl groups (-C(OH)(CH₃)₂), giving a structure with a hydroxyl group on the tetralin ring. Step 2: Q is treated with H₂SO₄ at 0°C to cause dehydration. The hydroxyl group is eliminated, forming a carbocation intermediate. Step 3: This leads to compound R through an intramolecular Friedel-Craft alkylation and dehydration process. The carbocation is attacked by the aromatic ring, creating a new five-membered ring with a double bond that subsequently undergoes dehydration. Step 4: R then undergoes Friedel-Craft alkylation using CH₃COCl (acetyl chloride) with anhydrous AlCl₃ catalyst in CH₂Cl₂, followed by aqueous workup (H₂O). This introduces an acetyl group (C(=O)CH₃) onto the aromatic ring, forming the final product A. The sequence demonstrates the interplay of Grignard reactions, acid-catalyzed dehydration, intramolecular Friedel-Craft alkylation, and standard Friedel-Craft acylation.
