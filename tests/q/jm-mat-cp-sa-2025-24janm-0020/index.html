If the system of equations 2x - y + z = 4, 5x + lambda*y + 3z = 12, 100x - 47y + mu*z = 212, has infinitely many solutions, then u - 2*lambda is equal to

  1. (1) 56
  2. (2) 59
  3. (3) 55
  4. (4) 57

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution
