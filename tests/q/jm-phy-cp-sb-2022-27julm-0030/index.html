A ball of mass m is thrown vertically upward. Another ball of mass 2m is thrown at an angle θ with the vertical. Both the balls stay in air for the same period of time. The ratio of the heights attained by the two balls respectively is 1/x. The value of x is ___.

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

For ball 1 (mass m, thrown vertically upward with initial velocity u1): Time in air t1 = 2u1/g, Maximum height H1 = u1^2/(2g) For ball 2 (mass 2m, thrown at angle θ with vertical with initial velocity u2): The vertical component of velocity is u2*sin(θ). Time in air t2 = 2u2*sin(θ)/g, Maximum height H2 = (u2*sin(θ))^2/(2g) Since both stay in air for same time: t1 = t2 2u1/g = 2u2*sin(θ)/g Therefore: u1 = u2*sin(θ) Ratio of heights: H1/H2 = [u1^2/(2g)] / [(u2*sin(θ))^2/(2g)] = u1^2 / (u2*sin(θ))^2 = (u2*sin(θ))^2 / (u2*sin(θ))^2 = 1 Given H1/H2 = 1/x, therefore x = 1
