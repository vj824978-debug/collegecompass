A vertical line passing through the point (h, 0) intersects the ellipse x/4 + y/3 = 1 at the points P and Q. Let the tangents to the ellipse at P and Q meet at the point R. If Δ(h) = area of the triangle PQR, Δ₁ = max Δ(h) and Δ₂ = min Δ(h), then (8/sqrt(5))Δ₁ - 8Δ₂ = ________

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 9

Solution

Sol. (9) x²/4 + y²/3 = 1 y = (√3/2)√(4-h²) at x = h Let R(x₁, 0) PQ is chord of contact, so xx₁/4 = 1 ⟹ x = 4/x₁ which is equation of PQ, x = h so 4/x₁ = h ⟹ x₁ = 4/h Δ(h) = area of ΔPQR = (1/2) × PQ × RT = (1/2) × (2√3/2)√(4-h²) × (x₁-h) = (√3/2h)(4-h²)^(3/2) Δ'(h) = (-√3(4+2h²))/(2h²) √(4-h²) which is always decreasing so Δ₁ = maximum of Δ(h) = (45√5)/8 at h = 1/2 Δ₂ = minimum of Δ(h) = 9/2 at h = 1 so (8/√5)Δ₁ - 8Δ₂ = (8/√5) × (45√5)/8 - 8 · (9/2) = 45 - 36 = 9.
