Suppose two planets (spherical in shape) of radii R and 2R, but mass M and 9M respectively have a centre to centre separation 8R as shown in the figure. A satellite of mass 'm' is projected from the surface of the planet of mass 'M' directly towards the centre of the second planet. The minimum speed 'u' required for the satellite to reach the surface of the second planet is u = a*sqrt(GM/(7R)) then the value of 'a' is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Find the point where gravitational field is zero: GM/x^2 = G(9M)/(8R-x)^2. Solving: (8R-x)/x = 3, so x = 2R. Calculate potential at point A (surface of first planet): V_A = -GM/R - G(9M)/(7R) = -16GM/(7R). Calculate potential at point on second planet surface (6R from first planet): V_x = -GM/(2R) - G(9M)/(6R) - 12GM/(6R) = -2GM/R. Find potential difference: ΔV = -2GM/R - (-16GM/(7R)) = 2GM/(7R). Using conservation of mechanical energy: (1/2)mv^2 = mΔV, so (1/2)mv^2 = 2GMm/(7R). Therefore v = sqrt(4GM/(7R)), which gives a = 4.
