A 100 m long wire having cross-sectional area 6.25 × 10^(-4) m^2 and Young's modulus is 10^10 Nm^(-2) is subjected to a load of 250 N, then the elongation in the wire will be:

  1. (1) 6.25 × 10^(-5) m
  2. (2) 6.25 × 10^(-3) m
  3. (3) 4 × 10^(-3) m
  4. (4) 4 × 10^(-4) m

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Using the formula for elongation: Δℓ = Fℓ/(AY) Given: F = 250 N ℓ = 100 m A = 6.25 × 10^(-4) m^2 Y = 10^10 Nm^(-2) Substituting values: Δℓ = (250 × 100)/(6.25 × 10^(-4) × 10^10) Δℓ = 25000/(6.25 × 10^6) Δℓ = 25000/(6250000) Δℓ = 25/6250 Δℓ = 1/250 Δℓ = 0.004 m Δℓ = 4 × 10^(-3) m
