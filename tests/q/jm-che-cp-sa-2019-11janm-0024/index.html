The major product of the following reaction is: A compound with a Cl atom and a C=O group (ketone) in a benzene ring undergoes reaction with (i) HBr (ii) alc. KOH

  1. (1) A compound with Cl and C=O (ketone) groups in benzene ring
  2. (2) A compound with OH and C=O (ketone) groups in benzene ring
  3. (3) Phenol (benzene ring with OH group)
  4. (4) A compound with Cl and C=O (ketone) groups in benzene ring at different positions

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction sequence involves: 1. Starting material: 4-chlorocyclohex-3-enone (conjugated diene-ketone system with Cl substituent) 2. Step (i) - Addition of HBr: - HBr adds to the conjugated system following Markovnikov's rule - The Br adds to the more substituted position of the double bond - The H adds to the less substituted position - Product: 4-chloro-1-bromo-cyclohexanone (Br added to the ring) 3. Step (ii) - Alcoholic KOH (elimination): - This is a base-catalyzed elimination reaction - The first elimination occurs: removal of H and Br from adjacent positions - This regenerates the double bond in conjugation with the ketone - The conjugated system is restored to give a stable product - Subsequent elimination can occur leading to aromatic character - Further elimination removes H and forms the aromatic ring - The final product undergoes tautomerism 4. Final product mechanism: - The conjugated enone system is formed - With continued base treatment and the stability of the aromatic system, the product approaches phenolic character - However, the major kinetic product before complete aromatization is the chlorinated ketone (option 1) - The chlorine remains on the ring and the ketone functionality is retained in the major product Therefore, the major product is the compound with both Cl and C=O groups in the benzene ring (option 1).
