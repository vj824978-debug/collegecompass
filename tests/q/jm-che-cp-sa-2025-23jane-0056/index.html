Identify A, B and C in the given below reaction sequence

  1. (1) (1) PbCl2, PbSO4, PbCrO4 (3) PbS, PbSO4, Pb(CH3COO)2
  2. (2) (2) PbS, PbSO4, PbCrO4 (4) PbCl2, Pb(SO4)2, PbCrO4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

A = PbS Reaction sequence analysis: Step 1: PbS + HNO3 → Pb(NO3)2 PbS reacts with dilute nitric acid to form lead nitrate. A is PbS (lead sulfide). Step 2: Pb(NO3)2 + H2SO4 → PbSO4 + B Lead nitrate reacts with sulfuric acid to form lead sulfate precipitate and B remains in solution as Pb(NO3)2. B is PbSO4 (lead sulfate). Step 3: B (PbSO4) + (1) Ammonium acetate, (2) Acetic acid, (3) K2CrO4 → C When PbSO4 is treated with: - Ammonium acetate (converts to Pb(CH3COO)2) - Acetic acid (buffer) - K2CrO4 (chromate source) The lead acetate formed reacts with K2CrO4 to give PbCrO4 (yellow precipitate). Therefore: A = PbS B = PbSO4 C = PbCrO4 (yellow ppt) This matches option [2]: PbS, PbSO4, PbCrO4
