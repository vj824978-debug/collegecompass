The major products A and B for the following reactions are, respectively: Starting material is a cyclohexanone with a propyl iodide side chain. KCN/DMSO converts this to [A], then H2/Pd converts [A] to [B].

  1. (1) (1) HO and CN groups on cyclohexane with CH2NH2 and H side chain
  2. (2) (2) Cyclohexanone with CN side chain converts to cyclohexanone with CH2NH2 side chain
  3. (3) (3) Cyclohexanone with CN side chain (OH group on ring) converts to cyclohexanone (OH group on ring) with CH2NH2 side chain
  4. (4) (4) HO and CN groups on cyclohexane with CH2NH2 and I side chain

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Starting material: Cyclohexanone with propyl iodide side chain (C=O and -CH2CH2CH2I) Step 1: KCN/DMSO reaction - KCN/DMSO performs a nucleophilic substitution (SN2) on the alkyl iodide - The iodide (-I) is replaced by cyanide (-CN) - Product [A]: Cyclohexanone with -CH2CH2CH2CN side chain Step 2: H2/Pd reduction - H2/Pd (catalyst) performs reduction of the nitrile group (-CN) to primary amine (-CH2NH2) - The nitrile triple bond is reduced to a primary amine - The ketone (C=O) on the cyclohexanone ring is also reduced to a secondary alcohol (-OH) under these hydrogenation conditions - Product [B]: Cyclohexanol (with OH group on ring) with -CH2CH2CH2CH2NH2 side chain The correct answer shows the cyclohexanone ring with OH group (reduced ketone) and the side chain with CH2NH2 group (reduced nitrile), which corresponds to option (3).
