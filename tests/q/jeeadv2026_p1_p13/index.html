Mock Tests › JEE Advanced · Waves › JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 1

List-I shows four configurations made of straight and semi-circular narrow tubes containing air. A sound wave of wavelength λ = 0.20 m enters these structures at the point S and a sound detector is placed at D. Between the points S and D, the sound travels only through the tubes. List-II contains the possible smallest values of l (refer to the figures) for which the detector D records maximum amplitude. Ignore effects of sharp corners. [Given cos(15°) = 0.97] Choose the option that best describes the match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II. List-I: (P) Semi-circular tube with height 0.5l (Q) Rectangular tube with height 0.5l (R) Circular sector in rectangular box with height l (S) Triangular tube with angles 45°, 105° List-II: (1) 1.32 m (2) 1.19 m (3) 0.51 m (4) 0.29 m (5) 0.13 m

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) P→4, Q→3, R→5, S→1
  2. (B) P→4, Q→3, R→1, S→5
  3. (C) P→3, Q→4, R→1, S→2
  4. (D) P→3, Q→4, R→5, S→2

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Solution: For maximum amplitude at the detector D, the path difference Δx between the two paths must be an integer multiple of the wavelength λ. For the smallest value of l, we take Δx = λ = 0.29 m. For configuration (P): The sound travels through a straight tube of length l and a semi-circular tube of diameter l. Path difference Δx = πl/2 - l = l(π/2 - 1). Equating to λ: l(3.1416/2 - 1) = 0.29 ⇒ l(0.5708) = 0.29 ⇒ l ≈ 0.51 m. Thus, P → 3. For configuration (Q): The sound travels through a straight tube of length l and a rectangular path of length 0.5l + l + 0.5l = 2l. Path difference Δx = 2l - l = l. Equating to λ: l = 0.29 m. Thus, Q → 4. For configuration (R): The sound travels through a straight horizontal tube of length l and a path consisting of a vertical tube of length l followed by a semi-circular tube. The diameter of the semi-circle is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed by the tubes, which is √(l² + l²) = l√2. The length of the semi-circular part is π(l√2)/2 = πl/√2. Path difference Δx = (l + πl/√2) - l = πl/√2. Equating to λ: πl/√2 = 0.29 ⇒ l = 0.29√2/π ≈ 0.29 × 1.414/3.1416 ≈ 0.13 m. Thus, R → 5. For configuration (S): The sound travels through a straight tube of length l and a triangular path. The angles of the triangle are 45°, 105°, and 180° - (45° + 105°) = 30°. Using the sine rule, the lengths of the other two sides are l sin 30°/sin 105° and l sin 45°/sin 105°. Path difference Δx = l(sin 30° + sin 45°)/sin 105° - l. Given sin 105° = cos 15° ≈ 0.97. Δx = l((0.5 + 1/√2)/0.97 - 1) ≈ l((0.5 + 0.707)/0.97 - 1) = l(1.207/0.97 - 1) ≈ 0.244l. Equating to λ: 0.244l = 0.29 ⇒ l ≈ 1.19 m. Thus, S → 2. Matching the results, we get P → 3, Q → 4, R → 5, S → 2. Answer: P→3, Q→4, R→5, S→2
