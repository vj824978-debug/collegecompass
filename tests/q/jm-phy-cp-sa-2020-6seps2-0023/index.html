The output characteristics of a transistor is shown in the figure. When V_CE is 10 V and I_C = 4.0 mA, then value of beta_ac is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 150

Solution

To find beta_ac (AC current gain), we use the formula: beta_ac = delta(I_C) / delta(I_B) From the output characteristics graph at V_CE = 10 V and I_C = 4.0 mA: We need to find the change in collector current for a change in base current. From the graph, looking at the curve that passes through the point (V_CE = 10 V, I_C = 4.0 mA): - This corresponds to I_B = 30 microA To find delta(I_C) and delta(I_B), we examine two nearby curves: - For I_B = 20 microA: I_C = 3.0 mA (at V_CE = 10 V) - For I_B = 30 microA: I_C = 4.5 mA (at V_CE = 10 V) Therefore: delta(I_B) = 30 - 20 = 10 microA delta(I_C) = 4.5 - 3.0 = 1.5 mA beta_ac = delta(I_C) / delta(I_B) = 1.5 mA / 10 microA = 1.5 × 10^(-3) A / (10 × 10^(-6) A) = 1500 × 10^(-6) / (10 × 10^(-6)) = 150
