It has been found that for a chemical reaction with rise in temperature by 9 K the rate constant gets doubled. Assuming a reaction to be occurring at 300 K, the value of activation energy is found to be _______ kJ mol^(-1). [nearest integer] (Given ln10 = 2.3, R = 8.3 J K^(-1) mol^(-1), log 2 = 0.30)

Answer

Correct answer: 59

Solution

Using Arrhenius equation: log(K2/K1) = Ea/(2.3R) × [(T2 - T1)/(T2 × T1)]. Given: K2 = 2K1 (rate constant doubles), T1 = 300 K, T2 = 309 K (increase by 9 K). log 2 = Ea/(2.3 × 8.3) × [9/(309 × 300)]. 0.30 = Ea/19.09 × [9/92700]. 0.30 = Ea × 9/(19.09 × 92700). Ea = (0.30 × 19.09 × 92700)/9 = 58988.1 J/mol ≈ 59 kJ/mol
