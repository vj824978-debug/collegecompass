Let PQR be a triangle with R(-1, 4, 2). Suppose M (2, 1, 2) is the mid-point of PQ. The distance of the centroid of triangle PQR from the point of intersection of the lines (x-2)/0 = (y)/2 = (z+3)/(-1) and (x-1)/1 = (y+3)/(-3) = (z+1)/1 is-

  1. (1) sqrt(69)
  2. (2) 69
  3. (3) sqrt(99)
  4. (4) 9

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For point of intersection: From line 1: x = 2, y = 2λ, z = -λ - 3. From line 2: x = μ + 1, y = -3μ - 3, z = μ - 1. Equating: 2 = μ + 1 ⟹ μ = 1, and 2λ = -3μ - 3 ⟹ λ = -3. Point of intersection is (2, -6, 0). Since M is midpoint of PQ: x₁ + x₂ = 4, y₁ + y₂ = 2, z₁ + z₂ = 4. Centroid of triangle PQR = ((x₁ + x₂ - 1)/3, (y₁ + y₂ + 4)/3, (z₁ + z₂ + 2)/3) = ((4-1)/3, (2+4)/3, (4+2)/3) = (1, 2, 2). Distance between (1, 2, 2) and (2, -6, 0) = sqrt((1)² + (8)² + (2)²) = sqrt(1 + 64 + 4) = sqrt(69).
