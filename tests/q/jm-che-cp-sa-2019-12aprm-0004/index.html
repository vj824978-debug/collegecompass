The IUPAC name for the following compound is: [Structure shows a branched hydrocarbon with: a triple bond between C1-C2, a double bond between C6-C7, with methyl groups at positions 3 and 5, a propyl group at position 4, and an ethyl group at position 3]

  1. (1) 3,5-dimethyl-4-propylhept-1-en-6-yne
  2. (2) 3-methyl-4-(3-methylprop-1-enyl)-1-heptyne
  3. (3) 3-methyl-4-(1-methylprop-2-enyl)-1-heptene
  4. (4) 3,5-dimethyl-4-propylhept-6-en-1-yne

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

To name this compound, we need to identify the longest carbon chain and number it to give the lowest numbers to the functional groups (triple and double bonds). Identifying the structure: - The main chain has 7 carbons (hept) - There is a triple bond between C1 and C2 (yne at position 1) - There is a double bond between C6 and C7 (en at position 6) - At C3: there is a methyl group - At C4: there is a propyl group - At C5: there is a methyl group Numbering from left to right (from the triple bond end): C1≡C2-C3(CH3)-C4(propyl)-C5(CH3)-C6=C7-H Functional groups in order of priority in nomenclature: 1. Triple bond at position 1 → 1-yne 2. Double bond at position 6 → 6-en 3. Substituents: 3,5-dimethyl and 4-propyl Complete name: 3,5-dimethyl-4-propylhept-1-en-6-yne Note: When both double and triple bonds are present, the main chain is numbered to give the triple bond the lowest number. The name is written with both unsaturations listed in order.
