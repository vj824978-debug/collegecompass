As shown in figure, a cuboid lies in a region with electric field E = 2x^2*i - 4y*j + 6k N/C. The magnitude of charge within the cuboid is n*e0*C. The value of n is _____ (if dimension of cuboid is 1 × 2 × 3 m^3).

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

Flux through planes parallel to y-z = 2(1)^2 × Area = 2(1)^2 × 2 × 3 = 12 Nm^2/C Flux through planes parallel to x-z = -4(2) × Area = -4(2) × 1 × 3 = -24 Nm^2/C Flux through planes parallel to x-y = 0 ⇒ φ_total = 12 - 24 = -12 ⇒ -12 = q_enc/e0 ⇒ |q_enc| = 12*e0 ⇒ n = 12
