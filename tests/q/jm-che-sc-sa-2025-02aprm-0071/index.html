0.1 mol of the following given antiviral compound (P) will weigh _____ ×10^(-1) g. The compound P contains a purine base (iodinated) attached to a sugar (with fluorine and hydroxyl groups). Given: molar mass in g mol^(-1) H:1, C:12, N:14, O:16, F:19, I:127

Answer

Correct answer: 372

Solution

Count atoms in compound P from the structure: The molecule contains an iodinated purine (5-iodouraciL-like base) with a modified sugar. Counting atoms: C atoms in base + sugar, H atoms, N atoms in base, O atoms, F atoms, I atoms. Calculate total molar mass = (C×12) + (H×1) + (N×14) + (O×16) + (F×19) + (I×127). From structure: approximately C9H11FIN2O5 or similar. Molar mass ≈ 354 g/mol. Mass of 0.1 mol = 0.1 × 354 = 35.4 g = 354 × 10^(-1) g. Answer = 354
