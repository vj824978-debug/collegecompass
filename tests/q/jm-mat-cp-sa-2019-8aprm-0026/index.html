If the eccentricity of the standard hyperbola passing through the point (4, 6) is 2, then the equation of the tangent to the hyperbola at (4, 6) is -

  1. (1) 2x - y - 2 = 0
  2. (2) 3x - 2y = 0
  3. (3) 2x - 3y + 10 = 0
  4. (4) x - 2y + 8 = 0

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Let equation of hyperbola x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 → (4, 6) 16/a^2 - 36/b^2 = 1 ..... (1) a^2 = 1 + b^2/a^2 4 = 1 + b^2/a^2 b^2 = 3a^2 ....(ii) from (i) & (ii) a^2 = 4 b^2 = 12 x^2/4 - y^2/12 = 1 tangent at (4, 6) 4x/4 - 6y/12 = 1 x - y/2 = 1 2x - y - 2 = 0
