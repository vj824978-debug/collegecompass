A wheel of radius R and mass M is placed at the bottom of a fixed step of height R as shown in the figure. A constant force is continuously applied on the surface of the wheel so that it just climbs the step without slipping. Consider the torque τ about an axis normal to the plane of the paper passing through the point Q. Which of the following options is/are correct? [Figure: A wheel of radius R at the bottom of a step of height R. Point P is at the center of the wheel, point S at the top of the wheel, point Q at the edge where the wheel meets the step, and point X at the bottom left of the wheel where it contacts the lower surface.]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) If the force is applied at point P tangentially then τ decreases continuously as the wheel climbs
  2. (B) If the force is applied normal to the circumference at point X then τ is constant
  3. (C) If the force is applied normal to the circumference at point P then τ is zero
  4. (D) If the force is applied tangentially at point S then τ ≠ 0 but the wheel never climbs the step

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

• As the force acts as shown and considering its direction to be constant, then τ_net = F(2R cos θ) - mgR cos θ = R cos θ(2F-mg). Which definitely infers that as θ increases, τ_net decreases. • As soon as force is applied as shown, it leads to frictional force between bottom point and horizontal surface, which will produce non-zero torque about point Q. [First diagram shows a circle of radius R positioned on a horizontal surface with point P at top, point Q at contact point on right, point R at bottom left on surface. A force F is shown at angle θ from horizontal, applied at or near point P. The angle θ is marked between the dashed line from center to Q and the force direction.] [Second diagram shows a circle of radius R on a horizontal surface with point P on left, point Q at center, point R at bottom. A horizontal force F is applied at point P. A friction force f is shown acting on the surface at the bottom contact point.]
