When 35 mL of 0.15 M lead nitrate solution is mixed with 20mL of 0.12 M chromic sulphate solution, how many mmoles of lead sulphate precipitate out. (Round off to the nearest integer).

Answer

Correct answer: 525

Solution

The reaction is: 3Pb(NO3)2 + Cr2(SO4)3 → 3PbSO4 + 2Cr(NO3)3. Initial moles: Pb(NO3)2 = 35×0.15 = 5.25 mmole, Cr2(SO4)3 = 20×0.12 = 2.4 mmole. From stoichiometry, 3 moles of Pb(NO3)2 react with 1 mole of Cr2(SO4)3. For 2.4 mmole Cr2(SO4)3, moles of Pb(NO3)2 needed = 3×2.4 = 7.2 mmole. Since available Pb(NO3)2 is 5.25 mmole (less than 7.2 mmole), Pb(NO3)2 is the limiting reagent. Moles of PbSO4 formed = (2/3)×5.25 = 3.5 mmole. Total moles of PbSO4 precipitated = 5.25×10^(-3) = 525×10^(-5) mol = 525 mmol. Ans: 525.
