For the first order reaction 2N₂O₅(g) → 4NO₂(g) + O₂(g)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) the concentration of the reactant decreases exponentially with time
  2. (B) the half-life of the reaction decreases with increasing temperature
  3. (C) the half-life of the reaction depends on the initial concentration of the reactant
  4. (D) the reaction proceeds to 99.6% completion in eight half-life duration

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

For first order reaction [A] = [A]₀e^(-kt) Hence concentration of [NO₂] decreases exponentially. Also, t₁/₂ = 0.693/K. Which is independent of concentration and t₁/₂ decreases with the increase of temperature. t₉₉.₆ = (2.303/K)log(100/0.4) t₉₉.₆ = (2.303/K)(2.4) = 8 × (0.693/K) = 8 t₁/₂
