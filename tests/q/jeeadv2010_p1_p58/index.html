To Verify Ohm's law, a student is provided with a test resistor R_T, a high resistance R_1, a small resistance R_2, two identical galvanometers G_1 and G_2, and a variable voltage source V. The correct circuit to carry out the experiment is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [Circuit diagram A: G_1 with R_2 in series at top; R_T and G_2 in series in middle branch with R_1 in parallel to G_2; voltage source V at bottom]
  2. (B) [Circuit diagram B: G_1 with R_1 in series at top; R_T and G_2 in series in middle branch with R_2 in parallel to G_2; voltage source V at bottom]
  3. (C) [Circuit diagram C: G_1 with R_1 in series at top; R_T and G_2 in series in middle branch with R_2 in parallel to G_2; voltage source V at bottom]
  4. (D) [Circuit diagram D: G_1 with R_2 in series at top; R_T and G_2 in series in middle branch with R_1 in parallel to G_2; voltage source V at bottom]

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

G₁ is acting as voltmeter and G₂ is acting as ammeter.
