[P] on treatment with Br2/FeBr3 in CCl4 produced a single isomer C8H7O2Br while heating [P] with sodalime gave toluene. The compound [P] is

  1. (1) para-Cresol (4-methylphenol) with COOH group
  2. (2) Ortho-cresol carboxylic acid (2-methylbenzoic acid with COOH and CH3 ortho)
  3. (3) para-Cresol acetic acid (4-methylphenol with CH2COOH)
  4. (4) meta-Cresol carboxylic acid (3-methylbenzoic acid with COOH and CH3 meta)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given information: 1. [P] treatment with Br2/FeBr3 in CCl4 produces a single isomer C8H7O2Br 2. Heating [P] with sodalime gives toluene 3. Molecular formula of product after bromination: C8H7O2Br Analysis: Step 1: Determine the structure of [P] - Sodalime heating causes decarboxylation of carboxylic acids - If [P] decarboxylates to toluene (C7H8), then [P] must contain a methyl group and a carboxylic acid group - Since toluene is produced, [P] has formula C8H8O2 (adding CO2 to toluene) - Therefore [P] = methylphenol + COOH = cresol carboxylic acid Step 2: Determine the isomer of [P] - Bromination with Br2/FeBr3 produces only a SINGLE isomer C8H7O2Br - This indicates high regioselectivity in electrophilic aromatic substitution - The COOH group is deactivating and meta-directing - The CH3 group is activating and ortho/para-directing Step 3: Analyze each possibility - For para-substitution: CH3 at para and COOH at ortho positions (1,4-disubstituted) Both groups are on the benzene ring in para relationship The ortho/para-directing CH3 would favor bromination at the ortho position to CH3 (which is meta to COOH) Or at para to CH3 (which is meta to COOH) This gives a single product due to symmetry - The structure is: COOH at position 1, CH3 at position 4 (para position), and bromination occurs at position 2 (ortho to COOH, meta to CH3) or position 6 (ortho to COOH, meta to CH3) Due to the strong deactivating effect of COOH, bromination is directed away from positions ortho to it Actually, bromination occurs ortho to the activating CH3 group With COOH and CH3 in para positions, there are two equivalent ortho positions to CH3 (and meta to COOH) This gives a single product due to symmetry Step 4: Identify compound [P] [P] is 4-methylbenzoic acid (p-toluic acid) = para-cresol carboxylic acid Structure: CH3 at position 4 and COOH at position 1 on benzene ring The answer shown in the solution confirms [P] is 4-methylbenzoic acid (option 1 structure shown).
