There are 3 sections in a question paper and each section contains 5 questions. A candidate has to answer a total of 5 questions, choosing at least one question from each section. Then the number of ways, in which the candidate can choose the questions, is :

  1. (1) 1500
  2. (2) 2255
  3. (3) 3000
  4. (4) 2250

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let the three sections be A, B, and C, each with 5 questions. The candidate must choose a total of 5 questions with at least one from each section. Possible distributions (a, b, c) where a + b + c = 5 and a, b, c ≥ 1: (1, 1, 3): Choose 1 from A, 1 from B, 3 from C (1, 2, 2): Choose 1 from A, 2 from B, 2 from C (1, 3, 1): Choose 1 from A, 3 from B, 1 from C (2, 1, 2): Choose 2 from A, 1 from B, 2 from C (2, 2, 1): Choose 2 from A, 2 from B, 1 from C (3, 1, 1): Choose 3 from A, 1 from B, 1 from C Total number of selection: = (C(5,1) × C(5,1) × C(5,3)) × 3 + (C(5,1) × C(5,2) × C(5,2)) × 3 Where we have 3 ways to arrange (1,1,3) as (1,1,3), (1,3,1), (3,1,1) And 3 ways to arrange (1,2,2) as (1,2,2), (2,1,2), (2,2,1) = (5 × 5 × 10) × 3 + (5 × 10 × 10) × 3 = 250 × 3 + 500 × 3 = 750 + 1500 = 2250
