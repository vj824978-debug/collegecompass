A thin strip 10 cm long is on a U shaped wire of negligible resistance and it is connected to a spring of spring constant 0.5 Nm^(-1) (see figure). The assembly is kept in a uniform magnetic field of 0.1 T. If the strip is pulled from its equilibrium position and released, the number of oscillations it performs before its amplitude decreases by a factor of e is N. If the mass of the strip is 50 grams, its resistance 10 ohm and air drag negligible, N will be close to -

  1. (1) 50000
  2. (2) 1000
  3. (3) 10000
  4. (4) 5000

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The equation of motion for the strip in the magnetic field with spring restoring force and electromagnetic damping is: -KX - (l^2 B^2 v)/R = ma The amplitude decays as: A = A_0 e^(-bt/2m) where b is the damping coefficient. The damping coefficient is determined by the electromagnetic damping: t = 2mR/(B^2 l^2) = (2(50×10^(-3))(10))/((0.1)^2(0.1)^2) = (2(50×10^(-3))(10))/(0.01×0.01) = (1)/(0.0001) = 10^4 seconds The period of oscillation is: t = 2π√(m/K) = 2π√(0.05/0.5) = 2π√(0.1) = 2π×0.316... ≈ 2 seconds Frequency f = 1/T = 0.5 Hz The number of oscillations before amplitude decreases by a factor of e: N = (time constant)/(period) = 10^4/(2) = 5000 Alternatively: N = (t/2)/(T) = (10^4/2)/(2) = 5000/2 ≈ but calculation gives N = 5000
