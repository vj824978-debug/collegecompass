How many of the transformations given below would result in aromatic amines? (1) C6H5CONH2 + Br2 + NaOH (2) A compound with O double bonded to a benzene ring with NK group, reacted with chlorobenzene (3) Nitrobenzene (NO2) + H2 over Pd/C (4) N-H-CO-CH3 on benzene ring + dil H2SO4 + heat

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Gabriel phthalimide synthesis cannot be used to prepare Aniline. (1) C6H5CONH2 + Br2/NaOH → C6H5NH2 (Aniline) Hoffmann Bromamide synthesis - YES, gives aromatic amine (2) The imide with chlorobenzene - Gabriel phthalimide synthesis cannot be used to prepare Aniline - NO, does not give aromatic amine (3) NO2 group on benzene + H2/Pd/C → NH2 group on benzene (Aniline) Reduction of nitro compound - YES, gives aromatic amine (4) NHCOCH3 on benzene ring + dil H2SO4 + heat → NH2 on benzene ring (Aniline) Hydrolysis of acetamide - YES, gives aromatic amine Therefore, transformations 1, 3, and 4 will give aniline (aromatic amine). Total = 3
