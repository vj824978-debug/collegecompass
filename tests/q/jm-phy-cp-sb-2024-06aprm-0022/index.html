Three infinitely long charged thin sheets are placed as shown in figure. The magnitude of electric field at the point P is xσ/ε₀. The value of x is _____ (all quantities are measured in SI units).

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Three infinitely long charged sheets are at positions X = -a (charge density -σ), X = a (charge density -2σ), and X = 3a (charge density σ). Point P is located at X = 3a. Electric field due to infinite sheet with charge density σ is E = σ/(2ε₀). At point P: Field from sheet at X = -a (charge -σ): E₁ = σ/(2ε₀) pointing right. Field from sheet at X = a (charge -2σ): E₂ = 2σ/(2ε₀) = σ/ε₀ pointing right. Field from sheet at X = 3a (charge σ): E₃ = σ/(2ε₀) pointing left. Net field E_P = σ/(2ε₀) + σ/ε₀ - σ/(2ε₀) = σ/ε₀ + σ/(2ε₀) - σ/(2ε₀) = 2σ/(2ε₀) = σ/ε₀. Therefore x = 2.
