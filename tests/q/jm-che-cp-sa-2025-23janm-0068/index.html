Which of the following happens when NH4OH is added gradually to the solution containing 1M A^(2+) and 1M B^(3+) ions? Given: Ksp[A(OH)2] = 9 × 10^(-10) and Ksp[B(OH)3] = 27 × 10^(-18) at 298 K.

  1. (1) B(OH)3 will precipitate before A(OH)2
  2. (2) A(OH)2 and B(OH)3 will precipitate together
  3. (3) A(OH)2 will precipitate before B(OH)3
  4. (4) Both A(OH)2 and B(OH)3 do not show precipitation with NH4OH

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Condition for precipitation Qip > Ksp For [A(OH)2]: [A^(2+)][OH^(-)]^2 > 9 × 10^(-10) [A^(2+)] = 1 M ⟹ [OH^(-)] > 3 × 10^(-5) M For [B(OH)3]: [B^(3+)][OH^(-)]^3 > 27 × 10^(-18) [B^(3+)] = 1 M ⟹ [OH^(-)] > 3 × 10^(-6) M So, B(OH)3 will precipitate before A(OH)2
