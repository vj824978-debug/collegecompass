Sea water contains 29.25% NaCl and 19% MgCl2 by weight of solution. The normal boiling point of the sea water is ___ °C (Nearest integer). Assume 100% ionization for both NaCl and MgCl2. Given: Kb(H2O = 0.52 K kg mol^(-1)). Molar mass of NaCl and MgCl2 is 58.5 and 95 g mol^(-1) respectively.

Answer

Correct answer: 116

Solution

Calculate molality of NaCl and MgCl2. For 100g solution: NaCl = 29.25g, MgCl2 = 19g, water = 51.75g. Molality of NaCl = (29.25/58.5)/(51.75/1000) = 0.5 × 1000/51.75 = 9.66 mol/kg. Molality of MgCl2 = (19/95)/(51.75/1000) = 0.2 × 1000/51.75 = 3.86 mol/kg. Using colligative property: ΔTb = i × kb × m. For NaCl (i=2): ΔTb1 = 2 × 0.52 × 9.66. For MgCl2 (i=3): ΔTb2 = 3 × 0.52 × 3.86. Total ΔTb = 16.077°C ≈ 16°C. Boiling point = 100 + 16 = 116°C
