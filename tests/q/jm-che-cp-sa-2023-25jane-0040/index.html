Match List-I with List-II. List-I contains: A. Propanamine and N-Methylethanamine, B. Hexan-2-one and Hexan-3-one, C. Ethanamide and Hydroxyethanamine, D. o-nitrophenol and p-nitrophenol. List-II contains: I. Metamers, II. Positional isomers, III. Functional isomers, IV. Tautomers

  1. (1) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
  2. (2) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
  3. (3) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
  4. (4) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

(A) Propanamine (CH3CH2CH2NH2) and N-Methylethanamine (CH3CH2NHCH3) are functional isomers as one is a primary amine while the other is a secondary amine. However, checking the solution: Propanamine CH3CH2CH2NH2 and N-Methylethanamine CH3CH2NHCH3 — these have the same molecular formula C3H9N but different functional groups, making them functional isomers — III (B) Hexan-2-one and Hexan-3-one are both ketones with the same molecular formula C6H12O but the carbonyl group is at different positions (position 2 vs position 3), making them positional isomers.
