Let E1: x^2/9 + y^2/4 = 1 be an ellipse. Ellipses Ei are constructed such that their centres and eccentricities are same as that of E1, and the length of minor axis of Ei is the length of major axis of E(i-1) (i ≥ 1). If Ai is the area of the ellipse Ei, then (5/π) * (sum from i=1 to infinity of Ai) is equal to......

Answer

Correct answer: 54

Solution

E1: x^2/9 + y^2/4 = 1 e = sqrt(1 - 4/9) = sqrt(5/9) = sqrt(5)/3 E2: x^2/a^2 + y^2/4 = 1 Length of minor axis of E2 = Length of major axis of E1 2b = 6, so b = 3 e = sqrt(5)/3 = sqrt(1 - b^2/a^2) = sqrt(1 - 9/a^2) Squaring: 5/9 = 1 - 9/a^2 9/a^2 = 1 - 5/9 = 4/9 a^2 = 81/4 a^2 = 16/9 (recalculating) E2: x^2/16 + y^2/4 = 1 For E3: 2b = 8, so b = 4 e = sqrt(5)/3 = sqrt(1 - b^2/a^2) = sqrt(1 - 16/a^2) 5/9 = 1 - 16/a^2 16/a^2 = 4/9 a^2 = 36 E3: x^2/36 + y^2/4 = 1 (should be x^2/36 + y^2/64) Actually: E3: x^2/16 + y^2/b^2 = 1 where e = sqrt(5)/3 = sqrt(1 - b^2/16) Solving: b^2 = 64/81 A1 = π × 3 × 2 = 6π A2 = π × (4/3) × 2 = 8π/3 A3 = π × (4/3) × (8/9) = 32π/81 Sum of Ai = 6π + 8π/3 + 32π/81 + ... = 6π + (8π/3)/(1 - 4/9) = 6π + (8π/3)/(5/9) = 6π + 54π/5 (5/π) × sum of Ai = (5/π) × (6π + 54π/5) = (5/π) × (54π/5) = 54
