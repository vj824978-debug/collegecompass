The mean and variance of a set of 15 numbers are 12 and 14 respectively. The mean and variance of another set of 15 numbers are 14 and σ^2 respectively. If the variance of all the 30 numbers in the two sets is 13, then σ^2 is equal to-

  1. (1) 10
  2. (2) 11
  3. (3) 9
  4. (4) 12

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For first set: Σx_i = 15 × 12 and Σx_i^2/15 - 12^2 = 14 For second set: Σy_i = 15 × 14 and Σy_i^2/15 - 14^2 = σ^2 Using combined variance formula: 13 = [(14 + 144) × 15 + (σ^2 + 196) × 15]/30 - 13^2 13 = [158 × 15 + (σ^2 + 196) × 15]/30 - 169 169 + 13 = [2370 + 15σ^2 + 2940]/30 182 = [5310 + 15σ^2]/30 5460 = 5310 + 15σ^2 150 = 15σ^2 σ^2 = 10
