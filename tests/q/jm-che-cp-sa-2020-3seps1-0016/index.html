The mechanism of S_N^1 reaction is given as: R - X → R⊕X⊖ → R⊕ || X⊖ → R - Y + X⊖ where the second step shows Ion pair formation and the third step shows Solvent with Separated ion pair. A student writes general characteristics based on the given mechanism as (a) The reaction is favoured by weak nucleophiles (b) R⊕ would be easily formed if the substituents are bulky (c) The reaction is accompanied by racemization (d) The reaction is favoured by non-polar solvents. Which observations are correct?

  1. (1) (a) and (d)
  2. (2) (a) and (c)
  3. (3) (a) and (b)
  4. (4) (a), (b) and (c)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

S_N^1 favours (a) The reaction is favoured by weak nucleophiles - CORRECT. S_N^1 is a unimolecular nucleophilic substitution where the rate-determining step is the formation of the carbocation intermediate. Weak nucleophiles favour this mechanism because the nucleophile is not involved in the rate-determining step. Strong nucleophiles favour S_N^2 mechanism. (b) R⊕ would be easily formed if the substituents are bulky - INCORRECT. Bulky substituents hinder the formation of carbocation intermediates due to steric hindrance. However, bulky substituents favour S_N^1 over S_N^2 because they make the S_N^2 bimolecular attack difficult. But the ease of R⊕ formation depends on carbocation stability (3° > 2° > 1°), not on bulkiness. (c) The reaction is accompanied by racemization - CORRECT. In S_N^1 mechanism, the carbocation intermediate is planar and can be attacked by the nucleophile from either side (front or back). This leads to a mixture of both stereoisomers, resulting in racemization (50% inversion + 50% retention = racemization). (d) The reaction is favoured by non-polar solvents - INCORRECT. S_N^1 is favoured by polar protic solvents because they can stabilize the carbocation intermediate through solvation. Non-polar solvents favour S_N^2 mechanism. Therefore, statements (a) and (c) are correct.
