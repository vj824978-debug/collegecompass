Molar conductivity of a weak acid HQ of concentration 0.18 M was found to be 1/30 of the molar conductivity of another weak acid HZ with concentration 0.02 of M. If lambda_Q^0 happened to be equal with lambda_Z^0, then the difference of the pK_a values of the two weak acids (pK_a(HQ) - pK_a(HZ)) is ____ (Nearest integer). [Given: degree of dissociation (alpha) << 1 for both weak acids, lambda^infinity: limiting molar conductivity of ions]

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

K_a(HQ) = C_1 alpha_1^2 alpha_1 = lambda_m(HQ) / lambda_m^infinity(HQ) K_a(HZ) = C_2 alpha_2^2 alpha_2 = lambda_m(HZ) / lambda_m^infinity(HZ) K_a(HQ) / K_a(HZ) = (C_1 / C_2) * (alpha_1 / alpha_2)^2 = 0.18 / 0.02 * [lambda_m(HQ) / lambda_m(HZ)]^2 Given: lambda_m(HQ) = (1/30) * lambda_m(HZ) K_a(HQ) / K_a(HZ) = 9 * (1/30)^2 = 9 * (1/900) = 1/100 pK_a(HQ) - pK_a(HZ) = 2
