The velocity of a small ball of mass 0.3g and density 8g/cc when dropped in a container filled with glycerine becomes constant after some time. If the density of glycerine is 1.3 g/cc, then the value of viscous force acting on the ball will be x × 10^(-4) N, the value of x is _____. [use g = 10m/s^2]

Answer

Correct answer: 25.00

Solution

At terminal velocity: F_V + F_B = mg, where F_V is viscous force and F_B is buoyant force. F_V = mg - F_B = ρ_ball × V_g × g - ρ_liquid × V_g × g = (ρ_ball - ρ_liquid) × V_g × g. With V_g = m/ρ_ball = (0.3 × 10^(-3))/(8 × 10^3), F_V = (8 - 1.3) × 10^3 × (0.3 × 10^(-3))/(8 × 10^3) × 10 = (6.7 × 0.3 × 10)/8 × 10^(-2) = (67 × 3)/8 × 10^(-4) = 25.125 × 10^(-4) N. Therefore x = 25.00 (rounded to 25.125)
