The correct functional group X and the reagent/reaction conditions Y in the following scheme are X−(CH₂)₄−X —(i) Y—→ condensation polymer [Chemical structure showing: (i) with O double bonded to C, connected to (CH₂)₄, connected to another O double bonded to C, with HO below first C and OH below second C, labeled as heat]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) X = COOCH₃, Y = H₂/Ni/heat
  2. (B) X = CONH₂, Y = H₂/Ni/heat
  3. (C) X = COOH, Y = P₂O₅/heat
  4. (D) X = CHO, Y = H₂/Ni

Answer

Correct answer: ABCD

Solution

Condensation polymers are formed by condensation of a diols or diamine with dicarboxylic acids. Hence, X may be -C(=O)-OR or -C(=O)-NH₂ or -C≡N [Chemical equation showing ester formation] H₃CO-C(=O)-(CH₂)₄-C(=O)-OCH₃ --H₂/Ni--> OH-H₂C(CH₂)₄-CH₂-OH --> HO-C(=O)-(CH₂)₄-C(=O)-OH --> Poly-ester [Chemical equation showing polyamide formation] H₂N-C(=O)-(CH₂)₄-C(=O)-NH₂ --H₂/Ni--> H₂N-CH₂(CH₂)₄-CH₂-NH₂ --> HO-C(=O)-(CH₂)₄-C(=O)-OH --> Polyamide [Arrow with conditions] Br₂/OH⁻, Δ Hoffmann Bromamide Reaction [Chemical equation] H₂N-(CH₂)₄-NH₂ --> HO-C(=O)-(CH₂)₄-C(=O)-OH --> Polyamide [Chemical equation] N≡C-(CH₂)₄-C≡N --H₂/Ni--> H₂N-CH₂-(CH₂)₄-CH₂-NH₂ --> HO-C(=O)-(CH₂)₄-C(=O)-OH --> Polyamides
