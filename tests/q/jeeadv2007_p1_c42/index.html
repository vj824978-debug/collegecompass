Match the complexes in Column-I with their properties listed in Column-II. Column-I (A) [Co(NH₃)₄(H₂O)₂]Cl₂ (B) [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] (C) [Co(H₂O)₂Cl]Cl (D) [Ni(H₂O)₆]Cl₂ Column-II (p) geometrical isomers (q) paramagnetic (r) diamagnetic (s) metal ion with +2 oxidation state

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) A → p, r, s B → p, q, s C → q, s D → q, s
  2. (B) A → p, q, s B → p, r, s C → r, s D → r, s
  3. (C) A → p, s B → p, s C → q, r D → q, r
  4. (D) A → p, q, s B → p, r, s C → q, s D → q, s

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

(A) [Structure showing Co²⁺ complex with trans arrangement of two OH₂ and two NH₃ ligands, plus two more NH₃ ligands] (trans) Co²⁺ = 3d⁷ (Paramagnetic) [Structure showing Co²⁺ complex with cis arrangement of H₂O and OH₂ ligands, plus NH₃ ligands] (cis) (B) [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] is square planar. [Structure showing Pt²⁺ with cis arrangement of two NH₃ and two Cl ligands] (cis) Pt²⁺ = 5d⁸ (diamagnetic) [Structure showing Pt²⁺ with trans arrangement of two NH₃ and two Cl ligands] (trans) (C) [Structure showing Co²⁺ complex with four OH₂ ligands, one Cl ligand, and one OH₂ ligand] [Shows octahedral coordination] Co²⁺ = 3d⁷ (paramagnetic) (D) [Structure showing Ni²⁺ complex with six OH₂ ligands in octahedral arrangement] 2+ Ni²⁺ = 3d⁸ (weak field ligand, paramagnetic)
