Two concentric circular coils with radii 1 cm and 1000 cm and number of turns 10 and 200 respectively are placed coaxially with centers coinciding. The mutual inductance of this arrangement will be _____ × 10^-8 H. (Take, π^2 = 10)

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

For concentric coaxial coils, mutual inductance M = (μ₀ × N₁ × N₂ × π × r₁²) / (2 × r₂) where r₁ is radius of smaller coil (1 cm = 0.01 m), r₂ is radius of larger coil (1000 cm = 10 m), N₁ = 10, N₂ = 200. M = (200 × μ₀ × π × (0.01)²) / (2 × 10) = (200 × μ₀ × π × 10^-4) / 20 = (200 × μ₀ × π × 10^-4) / 2 = 10 × μ₀ × π × 10^-4 × 10 = 4 × 10^-8 H (using μ₀ π = 4π × 10^-7 and π^2 = 10)
