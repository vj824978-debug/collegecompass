An inorganic compound 'X' on treatment with concentrated H2SO4 produces brown fumes and gives dark brown ring with FeSO4 in presence of concentrated H2SO4. Also compound 'X' gives precipitate 'Y', when its solution in dilute HCl is treated with H2S gas. The precipitate 'Y' on treatment with concentrated HNO3 followed by excess of NH4OH further gives deep blue coloured solution. Compound 'X' is:

  1. (1) Cu(NO3)2
  2. (2) Pb(NO3)2
  3. (3) Cu(NO3)2
  4. (4) Pb(NO3)2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

X reacts with conc. H2SO4 to produce brown fumes (NO2), indicating presence of nitrate. Brown ring test with FeSO4 confirms NO3- ions. When X solution in dilute HCl is treated with H2S, precipitate Y forms. This precipitate when treated with conc. HNO3 dissolves, and with excess NH4OH gives deep blue coloured solution [Cu(NH3)4]2+. This indicates Cu2+ ions. Brown fumes from nitrate sample and cation analysis of Cu2+ ions with H2S precipitation forming CuS, which dissolves in HNO3 and produces blue complex solution in NH4OH. Therefore salt is Cu(NO3)2.
