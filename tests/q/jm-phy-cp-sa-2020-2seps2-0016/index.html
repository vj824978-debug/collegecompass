A small point mass carrying some positive charge on it, is released from the edge of a table. There is a uniform electric field in this region in the horizontal direction. Which of the following options then correctly describe the trajectory of the mass? (Curves are drawn schematically and are not to scale).

  1. (1) Curve (1): Starting high on y-axis, curving downward and to the right
  2. (2) Curve (2): Starting at origin, curving upward and to the right
  3. (3) Curve (3): Starting at origin, curving upward and to the right
  4. (4) Curve (4): Straight line starting at origin going upward and to the right

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

At the initial position (edge of table), u = 0 (initial velocity is zero). Forces acting on the particle: 1. Gravitational force: mg (downward, in -y direction) 2. Electric force: qE (horizontal, in +x direction) Net force: F_net = sqrt((qE)^2 + (mg)^2) Since the particle starts from rest (u = 0) and experiences a constant net force, the acceleration a will be constant in magnitude and direction. Using Newton's second law: a = F_net/m = constant Since initial velocity is zero and acceleration is constant, the particle will travel in a straight line along the direction of the net force. The direction of motion is determined by the vector sum of qE (horizontal) and mg (downward), which gives a straight line path at an angle to both axes. This straight line path passes through the origin (starting point) and goes in the direction of the net acceleration, which is downward and to the right at some angle. Therefore, the trajectory is a straight line, which corresponds to option (4).
