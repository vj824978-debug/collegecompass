Let a and b be positive real numbers such that a > 1 and b < a. Let P be a point in the first quadrant that lies on the hyperbola x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1. Suppose the tangent to the hyperbola at P passes through the point (1, 0), and suppose the normal to the hyperbola at P cuts off equal intercepts on the coordinate axes. Let Δ denote the area of the triangle formed by the tangent at P, the normal at P and the x-axis. If e denotes the eccentricity of the hyperbola, then which of the following statements is/are TRUE?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) 1 < e < sqrt(2)
  2. (B) sqrt(2) < e < 2
  3. (C) Δ = a^4
  4. (D) Δ = b^4

Answer

Correct answer: AD

Solution

As in first quadrant if normal at P is making equal intercepts on axes, then slope of the normal = −1 ⟹ slope of tangent = 1 ⟹ equation of tangent at P: (y−0)/(x−1) = 1 and equation of tangent at (x₁, y₁): xx₁/a² − yy₁/b² = 1 ⟹ x₁ = a² and y₁ = b² ⟹ P(x₁, y₁) ≡ P(a², b²) ⟹ equation of normal at P: x + y = a² + b² ⟹ B = (a² + b², 0) ⟹ Δ = (1/2) × b² × (a² + b² − 1) = (1/2) × b² × 2b² = b⁴ ⟹ Δ = b⁴ (∵ (a², b²) lies on x²/a² − y²/b² = 1 ⟹ a² − b² = 1) Also e = √(1 + b²/a²) = √((a² + b²)/a²) ⟹ e = √((2a² − 1)/a²) ⟹ e = √(2 − 1/a²) ⟹ 1 < e < √2 ⟹ e ∈ (1, √2) [Diagram shows a hyperbola in the first quadrant with point P(x₁, y₁) marked, normal line making equal intercepts at points A(1, 0) and B on the axes]
