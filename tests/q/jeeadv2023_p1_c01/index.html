The correct statement(s) related to processes involved in the extraction of metals is (are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) Roasting of Malachite produces Cuprite.
  2. (B) Calcination of Calamine produces Zincite.
  3. (C) Copper pyrites is heated with silica in a reverberatory furnace to remove iron.
  4. (D) Impure silver is treated with aqueous KCN in the presence of oxygen followed by reduction with zinc metal.

Answer

Correct answer: BCD

Solution

Sol. CuCO₃·Cu(OH)₂ --Δ--> 2CuO + H₂O + CO₂ (Malachite) Cuprite – Cu₂O ZnCO₃ --Δ--> ZnO + CO₂ (Calamine) (Zincite) 2CuFeS₂ + (11/2)O₂ ----> Cu₂O + 2FeO + 4SO₂ (Copper pyrite) FeO + SiO₂ ----> FeSiO₃ ↓ (Slag) 4Ag + 8KCN + 2H₂O + O₂ ----> 4K[Ag(CN)₂] + 4KOH (In pure) 2K[Ag(CN)₂] + Zn ----> K₂[Zn(CN)₄] + 2Ag ↓
