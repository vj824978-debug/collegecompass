When both jaws of vernier callipers touch each other, zero mark of the vernier scale is right to zero mark of main scale, 4th mark on vernier scale coincides with certain mark on the main scale. While measuring the length of a cylinder, observer observes 15 divisions on main scale and 5th division of vernier scale coincides with a min scale division. Measured length of cylinder is ____ mm. (Least count of Vernier calliper = 0.1 mm)

  1. (1) 15.4
  2. (2) 15.1
  3. (3) 15.5
  4. (4) 15.9

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Reading = MSR + (VSR × LC) − (zero Error) Given: MSR (Main Scale Reading) = 15 mm VSR (Vernier Scale Reading) = 5th division LC (Least Count) = 0.1 mm Zero Error = 4th mark on vernier scale coincides with main scale mark = 4 × 0.1 mm = 0.4 mm Since the zero mark of vernier scale is to the right of zero mark of main scale, the zero error is positive (instrumental error). Reading = 15 mm + (5 × 0.1 mm) − (4 × 0.1 mm) Reading = 15 mm + 0.5 mm − 0.4 mm Reading = 15.1 mm ∴ ℓ = 15.1 mm
