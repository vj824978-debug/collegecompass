A diatomic molecule has a dipole moment of 1.2 D. If the bond distance is 1 Å, then fractional charge on each atom is ______ × 10^-10 esu. (Given 1 D = 10^-18 esu cm)

Answer

Correct answer: 1.2

Solution

Dipole moment μ = q × d, where q is charge and d is distance. Given: μ = 1.2 D = 1.2 × 10^-18 esu cm, d = 1 Å = 10^-8 cm. Therefore, 1.2 × 10^-18 = q × 10^-8, so q = 1.2 × 10^-10 esu = 1.2 × 10^-9 × 10^-1 esu.
