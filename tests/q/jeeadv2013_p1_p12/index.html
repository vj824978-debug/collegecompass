A particle of mass M and positive charge Q, moving with a constant velocity u_i = 4i ms^{-1}, enters a region of uniform static magnetic field normal to the x-y plane. The region of the magnetic field extends from x = 0 to x = L for all values of y. After passing through this region, the particle emerges on the other side after 10 milliseconds with a velocity u_f = 2(sqrt(3)i + j) ms^{-1}. The correct statement(s) is (are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) The direction of the magnetic field is -z direction.
  2. (B) The direction of the magnetic field is +z direction.
  3. (C) The magnitude of the magnetic field is 50πM/(3Q) units.
  4. (D) The magnitude of the magnetic field is 100πM/(3Q) units.

Answer

Correct answer: AC

Solution

So magnetic field is along −ve, z-direction. Time taken in the magnetic field = 10×10⁻³ = πM/(6QB) B = (πM)/(60×10⁻³Q) = (1000πM)/(60Q) = (50πM)/(3Q) [FIGURE: Coordinate system showing y-axis pointing up and x-axis pointing right.
