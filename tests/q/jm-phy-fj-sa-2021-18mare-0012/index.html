If the angular velocity of earth's spin is increased such that the bodies at the equator start floating, the duration of the day would be approximately: [Take g = 10 m/s^2, the radius of earth, R = 6400 × 10^3 m, Take pi = 3.14]

  1. (1) 1200 minutes
  2. (2) does not change
  3. (3) 60 minutes
  4. (4) 84 minutes

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

At the equator, for bodies to float, the centripetal acceleration equals g. Using g_eff = g - omega^2*R = 0, we get omega = sqrt(g/R). The period T = 2*pi/omega = 2*pi*sqrt(R/g) = 2 × 3.14 × sqrt(6400×10^3/10) = 2 × 3.14 × 80 × 10 = 83.733 min ≈ 84 min.
