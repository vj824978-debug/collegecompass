Let each of the two ellipses E₁: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1, (a > b) and E₂: x²/A² + y²/B² = 1, (A < B) have eccentricity 4/5. Let the lengths of the latus rectum of E₁ and E₂ be l₁ and l₂, respectively, such that 2l₁² = 9l₂. If the distance between the foci of E₁ is 8, then the distance between the foci of E₂ is

  1. (1) 96/5
  2. (2) 32/5
  3. (3) 16/5
  4. (4) 8/5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For E₁: 2ae = 8 ⟹ a = 5 b² = a²(1 - e²) b² = a² × (1 - 9/25) = a² × 9/25 b² = 9 E₁: x²/25 + y²/9 = 1 Latus rectum: l₁ = 2b²/a = (2 × 9)/5 = 18/5 For E₂: e = 4/5 A² = B²(1 - e²) ⟹ A² = 9/25 B² ⟹ A = (3/5)B 2l₁² = 9l₂ ⟹ 2(18/5)² = 9l₂ ⟹ l₂ = (4 × 18)/25 l₂ = 2A²/B = 72/25 ⟹ A² = (36/25)B 9/25 B² = 36B/25 ⟹ B = 4 Distance between foci of E₂: 2Be = 2 × 4 × (4/5) = 32/5
