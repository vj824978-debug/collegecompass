Let a line l pass through the origin and be perpendicular to the lines l₁: r = (i-11j-7k) + λ(i+2j+3k), λ ∈ R and l₂: r = (-i+k) + μ(2i+2j+k), μ ∈ R. If P is the point of intersection of l and l₁, and Q (α, β, γ) is the foot of perpendicular from P on l₂, then 9(α+β+γ) is equal to _____.

Answer

Correct answer: 05.00

Solution

Direction of line l is perpendicular to both l₁ and l₂. Using cross product of direction vectors (1,2,3) and (2,2,1): direction of l is (-4i+5j-2k). Line l: r = μ(-4i+5j-2k). For point P on both l and l₁: solving 1+λ=-4μ, -11+2λ=5μ, -7+3λ=-2μ gives λ=3, μ=-1, so P=(4,-5,2). For Q on l₂ and PQ perpendicular to l: Q lies on l₂ with parameter value giving foot of perpendicular from P. Using perpendicularity condition 2(-5+2μ)+2(2μ+5)+1(μ-1)=0 gives μ=1/9. Thus Q=(-1+2/9, 2/9, 1+1/9)=(-7/9, 2/9, 10/9). Therefore α+β+γ=5/9, and 9(α+β+γ)=5.
