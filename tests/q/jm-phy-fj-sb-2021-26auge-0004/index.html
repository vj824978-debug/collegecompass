The acceleration due to gravity is found upto an accuracy of 4% on a planet. The energy supplied to a simple pendulum of known mass 'm' to undertake oscillations of time period T is being estimated. If time period is measured to an accuracy of 3%, the accuracy to which E is known as ________%.

Answer

Correct answer: 14

Solution

Let E ∝ m^a g^b T^c. So [E] = [m]^a [g]^b [T]^c. This gives ML^2T^-2 = M^a(LT^-2)^b(T)^c, so a=1, b=2 and c=2. Thus E = km g^2 T^2. Taking differential: ΔE/E = Δm/m + 2(Δg/g) + 2(ΔT/T). Substituting Δm/m = 0 (assumed), Δg/g = 4% = 0.04, ΔT/T = 3% = 0.03: ΔE/E = 0 + 2(0.04) + 2(0.03) = 0.08 + 0.06 = 0.14 = 14%.
