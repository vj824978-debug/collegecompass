The major products A and B formed in the following reaction sequence are: Aniline reacted with acetic anhydride to form product A, which is then treated with Br2/CH3COOH at room temperature to form product B.

  1. (1) A = N-phenylacetamide, B = 4-bromo-N-phenylacetamide
  2. (2) A = N-phenylacetamide, B = 2-bromo-N-phenylacetamide
  3. (3) A = N-phenylacetamide with amino and methoxy groups, B = dibrominated product with amino and methoxy groups
  4. (4) A = N-phenylacetamide with amino and methoxy groups, B = monobrominated product with amino and methoxy groups

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Aniline (NH2-C6H5) reacts with acetic anhydride to form N-phenylacetamide (A = HN-C(=O)-CH3 attached to benzene). The N-phenylacetamide undergoes electrophilic aromatic bromination with Br2/CH3COOH. The acetamido group (-NH-C(=O)-CH3) is an ortho/para directing group and weakly deactivating. Bromination occurs at the para position (and ortho position) relative to the acetamido group, giving 4-bromo-N-phenylacetamide as the major product (B). Therefore A = N-phenylacetamide and B = 4-bromo-N-phenylacetamide.
