Find out the correct statement from the options given below for the above 2 reactions. Reaction (I): 4-methoxybenzyl chloride + Nu → 4-methoxybenzyl Nu. Reaction (II): 4-nitrobenzyl chloride + Nu → 4-nitrobenzyl Nu. Where Nu = Nucleophile

  1. (1) Reaction (I) is of 2nd order and reaction (II) is of 1st order
  2. (2) Reactions (I) and (II) both are of 2nd order
  3. (3) Reactions (I) is of 1st order and reaction (II) is of 2nd order
  4. (4) Reaction (I) and (II) both are of 1st order

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Reaction (I): The methoxy group (-OMe) is electron-donating and stabilizes the carbocation intermediate formed during SN1 mechanism, making it a 1st order reaction (SN1 pathway). Reaction (II): The nitro group (-NO2) is electron-withdrawing and does not stabilize the carbocation intermediate. The reaction proceeds via SN2 mechanism, making it a 2nd order reaction (SN2 pathway). Therefore, reaction (I) is 1st order and reaction (II) is 2nd order.
