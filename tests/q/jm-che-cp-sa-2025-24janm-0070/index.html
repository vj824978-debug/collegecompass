The product (A) formed in the following reaction sequence is: CH3-C≡CH (i) Hg^(2+), H2SO4 (ii) HCN (iii) H2/Ni → (A) Product

  1. (1) CH3-C(NH2)(CH2OH) with CH3 branch
  2. (2) CH3-C(OH)(CH2-NH2) with CH3 branch
  3. (3) CH3-CH2-CH(NH2)-CH2-OH
  4. (4) CH3-CH2-CH(OH)-CH2-NH2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Step 1: Hydration of alkyne CH3-C≡CH with Hg^(2+) and H2SO4 follows Markovnikov's rule. The OH adds to the carbon with more hydrogen atoms (terminal carbon), and H adds to the internal carbon. This gives: CH3-C(=O)-CH3 (acetone is formed as the keto tautomer of the enol) Step 2: Addition of HCN to the ketone CH3-C(=O)-CH3 gives a cyanohydrin: CH3-C(OH)(CN)-CH3 Step 3: Reduction with H2/Ni reduces the CN group to CH2-NH2. The OH group remains intact. The reduction of nitrile (CN) to primary amine (CH2-NH2) is achieved by catalytic hydrogenation. Final Product (A): CH3-C(OH)(CH2-NH2)-CH3 This matches option (2): CH3-C(OH)(CH2-NH2) with CH3 branch, which is 1-amino-2-propanol or the structure shown in option (2).
