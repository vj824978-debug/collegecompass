Resistance of the wire is measured as 2 Ω and 3 Ω at 10°C and 30°C respectively. Temperature coefficient of resistance of the material of the wire is:

  1. (1) 0.033°C^(-1)
  2. (2) -0.033°C^(-1)
  3. (3) 0.011°C^(-1)
  4. (4) 0.055°C^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Using the formula R_T = R_0(1 + alpha × ΔT). At 10°C: R_10 = 2 = R_0(1 + alpha × 10). At 30°C: R_30 = 3 = R_0(1 + alpha × 30). Dividing the equations: 3/2 = (1 + 30alpha)/(1 + 10alpha). Solving: 3(1 + 10alpha) = 2(1 + 30alpha) → 3 + 30alpha = 2 + 60alpha → alpha = 0.033°C^(-1)
