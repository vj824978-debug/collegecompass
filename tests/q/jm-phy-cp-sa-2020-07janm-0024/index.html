M grams of steam at 100° C is mixed with 200 g of ice at its melting point in a thermally insulated container. If it produces liquid water at 40° C [heat of vaporization of water is 540 cal/g and heat of fusion of ice is 80 cal/g], the value of M is ............... .

Answer

Correct answer: 40

Solution

M gm steam at 100° C + M gm ice at 0° C Steam at 100° C: Heat released = M × 540 cal Converts to M gm water at 100° C Ice at 0° C: Heat required to melt = 200 × 80 = 16000 cal Converts to 200 gm water at 0° C Water from steam at 100° C: Using MSAT = M × 1 × 60 = 60 M cal This cools M gm water from 100° C to 40° C Water from ice at 0° C: Heat required = 200 × 1 × 40 = 8000 cal This heats 200 gm water from 0° C to 40° C Total heat released from steam = 600 M cal Total heat required for ice and cooling = 24000 M cal Energy balance: 600 M = 24000 M Actually, heat released by steam condensing and cooling: M × 540 (condensation) + M × 1 × 60 (cooling from 100° C to 40° C) = 600 M cal Heat required by ice: 200 × 80 (melting) + 200 × 1 × 40 (warming from 0° C to 40° C) = 16000 + 8000 = 24000 cal Setting heat released = heat required: 600 M = 24000 M = 40 gm
