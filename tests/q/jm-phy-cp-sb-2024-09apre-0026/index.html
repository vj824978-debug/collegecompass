To determine the resistance (R) of a wire, a circuit is designed. The V-I characteristic curve for this circuit is plotted for the voltmeter and the ammeter readings as shown in figure. The value of R is _______ Ω.

Answer

Correct answer: 2500

Solution

The circuit has a 10 kΩ resistor in series with the unknown resistance R. The equivalent resistance is Req = (10^4 * R)/(10^4 + R). From the V-I characteristic curve, at V = 8V, I = 4mA. Using E = 4V and the circuit equation: I = E/Req, we get 2 × 10^(-3) = 4(10^4 + R)/(10^4 * R). Solving: 20R = 40000 + 4R, which gives 16R = 40000, therefore R = 2500Ω.
