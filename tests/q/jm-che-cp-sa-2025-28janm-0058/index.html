A molecule ("P") on treatment with acid undergoes rearrangement and gives ("Q") ("Q") on ozonolysis followed by reflux under alkaline condition gives ("R"). The structure of ("R") is given below: O with a cyclopentene ring and CH3 and CH3 groups attached. The structure of ("P") is

  1. (1) Cyclohexene ring with CH3 and CH3 groups
  2. (2) Cyclopentene ring with OH group and two methyl groups
  3. (3) Cyclopropane fused structure with Me and OH groups
  4. (4) Cyclopentene ring with two methyl groups

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From the structure of R given (cyclopentene with ketone and two methyl groups), we work backwards: 1) R is formed by ozonolysis of Q followed by alkaline reflux treatment 2) The ozonolysis followed by alkaline treatment suggests Q contains a C=C double bond that gets cleaved 3) Working backwards from R structure: the cyclopentene ketone with Me groups suggests Q had a double bond that when cleaved and treated with alkaline medium gives the final product R 4) Q is formed from acid-catalyzed rearrangement of P 5) The rearrangement likely involves: - P (an alkene/alcohol) undergoes acid-catalyzed rearrangement - Produces Q (rearranged structure) - Q undergoes ozonolysis giving a diketone intermediate - Alkaline treatment of the diketone leads to ring contraction/closure to give R 6) From the synthetic pathway shown in solution: P is a cyclopentene or similar alkene with methyl substituents 7) The note indicates that P could be either an alcohol or alkene since both undergo rearrangement in acidic conditions 8) Both options (2) and (4) satisfy the conditions as alkene also undergoes rearrangement in presence of acid (even though option 2 has alcohol which explicitly undergoes rearrangement)
