The correct sequential addition of reagents in the preparation of 3-nitrobenzoic acid from benzene

  1. (1) Br2 / AlBr3, HNO3 / H2SO4, Mg / ether, CO2, H2O+
  2. (2) Br2 / AlBr3, HNO3 / H2SO4, NaCH2H2O+
  3. (3) HNO3 / H2SO4, Br2 / AlBr3, Mg / ether, CO2, H2O+
  4. (4) Br2 / AlBr3, NaCH2H2O+, HNO3 / H2SO4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The synthesis of 3-nitrobenzoic acid from benzene requires: (1) Nitration with HNO3/H2SO4 to form nitrobenzene, (2) Bromination with Br2/AlBr3 to form 1-bromo-3-nitrobenzene (ortho and para directors conflict, meta position favored by NO2), (3) Conversion of Br to COOH via Grignard reagent: Mg/ether to form Grignard, then CO2, and H2O+ workup to form carboxylic acid. The correct order is HNO3/H2SO4 first (nitration), then Br2/AlBr3 (bromination), then Mg/ether and CO2/H2O+ (Grignard conversion). This matches option (C).
