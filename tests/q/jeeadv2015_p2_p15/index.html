A parallel plate capacitor having plates of area S and plate separation d, has capacitance C₁ in air. When two dielectrics of different relative permittivities (ε₁ = 2 and ε₂ = 4) are introduced between the two plates as shown in the figure, the capacitance becomes C₂. The ratio C₂/C₁ is [Figure shows a parallel plate capacitor with two dielectric materials inserted between the plates: the lower portion has dielectric with ε₁ = 2 occupying area S/2 and thickness d, while the upper portion has dielectric with ε₂ = 4 occupying area S/2 and thickness d/2]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) 6/5
  2. (B) 5/3
  3. (C) 7/5
  4. (D) 7/3

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

C₁₀ = (v/2)/(d/2) = 4ε₀S/d C₂₀ = 2ε₀S/d, C₃₀ = ε₀S/d 1/C'₁₀ = 1/C₁₀ + 1/C₁₀ = d/(2ε₀S)[1 + 1/2] ⟹ C'₁₀ = 4ε₀S/3d C₂ = C₃₀ + C'₁₀ = 7ε₀S/3d C₂/C₁ = 7/3 [Circuit diagram showing a hexagonal configuration with C₃₀ in the middle and parallel/series capacitor arrangements]
