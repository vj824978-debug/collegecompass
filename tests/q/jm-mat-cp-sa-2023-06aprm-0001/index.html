The straight lines l1 and l2 pass through the origin and trisect the line segment of the line L: 9x + 5y = 45 between the axes. If m1 and m2 are the slopes of the lines l1 and l2, then the point of intersection of the line y = (m1 + m2)x with L lies on

  1. (1) y - 2x = 5
  2. (2) 6x + y = 10
  3. (3) y - x = 5
  4. (4) 6x - y = 15

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Line L: 9x + 5y = 45 can be written as x/5 + y/9 = 1. So A(5,0) and B(0,9). Lines l1 and l2 through origin trisect AB at points C and D. C = (10/3, 3) and D = (5/3, 6). Slopes: m1 = 9/10, m2 = 18/5. Line y = (m1 + m2)x becomes y = (9/10 + 36/10)x = (9/2)x. Substituting in L: 9x + 5(9x/2) = 45, which gives 7y = 45, so y = 45/7 and x = 10/7. Check: 45/7 - 10/7 = 35/7 = 5. This lies on y - x = 5.
