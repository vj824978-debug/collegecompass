Melamine polymer is formed by the condensation of:

  1. (1) H2N-N-NH2 (triazine with NH2 groups) + HCHO
  2. (2) H2N-benzene-NH2 (with N in ring) + HCHO
  3. (3) H2N-benzene-NH2 (with NH2 at 1,3,5 positions) + HCHO
  4. (4) pyridine with NH2 groups + HCHO

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Melamine polymer is formed by condensation polymerization of melamine (2,4,6-triamino-1,3,5-triazine) and formaldehyde (HCHO). The amino groups (-NH2) of melamine react with formaldehyde to form methylol intermediates (N-CH2OH), which then undergo further condensation to form methylene bridges (-N-CH2-N-) connecting melamine units into a three-dimensional polymer network. Option (1) correctly shows melamine (the triazine ring with three NH2 groups) reacting with formaldehyde.
