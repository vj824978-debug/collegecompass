For the resistance network shown in the figure, choose the correct option(s) [Circuit diagram showing: A network with nodes P, Q, S, T connected by resistors. P-S has a 2Ω resistor with current I₂ marked. P has a 2Ω resistor on left side. From P going down: 4Ω resistor. P-Q has 1Ω resistor. Q-T has 4Ω resistor. S-T has 1Ω resistor on right side and 2Ω resistor on top right. S has 2Ω resistor on right side. Q-T has 4Ω resistor below. A 12V voltage source is connected at the bottom with current I₁ marked entering from bottom left.]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) The current through PQ is zero.
  2. (B) I₁ = 3A
  3. (C) The potential at S is less than that at Q.
  4. (D) I₂ = 2A

Answer

Correct answer: ABCD

Solution

Nodes P and Q are equipotential and nodes S and T are equipotential from wheatstone bridge, no current passes through PQ and ST. [Circuit diagram showing: 6Ω and 12Ω resistors in series on upper branch with current I₂, parallel combination with lower branch containing current I₁, and 12V source] I₁ = 12/4 = 3A I₂ = I₁(12/(6+12)) = 2A
