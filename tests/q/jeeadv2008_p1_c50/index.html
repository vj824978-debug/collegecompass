The major product of the following reaction is [Figure shows a benzene ring with substituents: Me and Br on one carbon (top left), F on ortho position (top), and NO2 on para position (bottom). Arrow indicates reaction conditions: Pd5.Na, dimethylformamide]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [Structure A shows: benzene ring with NO2 at para position, F ortho to NO2, and a CHMeMePh group (with SPh and Me, where SPh is wedged) at ortho position to F]
  2. (B) [Structure B shows: benzene ring with NO2 at para position, F ortho to NO2, and a CHMeSPh group (with Me and SPh, both in plane) at ortho position to F]
  3. (C) [Structure C shows: benzene ring with NO2 at para position, SPh ortho to NO2, and a CHMeBr group (with Br wedged, Me in plane) at ortho position to SPh]
  4. (D) [Structure D shows: benzene ring with NO2 at para position, SPh at ortho position to NO2, and a CHMeSPh group (with SPh wedged, Me in plane) at ortho position to first SPh]

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

[Chemical reaction scheme showing transformation of a brominated aromatic compound to a sulfide-substituted product] Sol. (A) Me-Br and Me-SPh substituents on ortho-fluorinated benzene ring with NO₂ in para position. Reaction conditions: PhS⁻Na⁺ in dimethyl formamide The Br substituent is replaced by SPh group. It is easier to do nucleophilic substitution on alkyl halides than on aryl halides.
