The wavelength of photon 'A' is 400 nm. The frequency of photon 'B' is 10^16 s^(-1). The wave number of photon 'C' is 10^4 cm^(-1). The correct order of energy of these photons is:

  1. (1) C > B > A
  2. (2) B > A > C
  3. (3) A > B > C
  4. (4) A > C > B

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(1) Wavelength of A = 400 nm. (2) Wavelength of B(lambda) = C/nu = (3 × 10^8)/(10^16) = 3 × 10^(-8) = 30 × 10^(-9) = 30 nm. (3) Wavelength of C(lambda) = 1/nu = 1/(10^4) = 10^(-4) cm = 10^(-6) m = 1000 nm Here lambda_C > lambda_A > lambda_B Energy (E) ∝ 1/lambda So E_B > E_A > E_C Ans. (2) is correct.
