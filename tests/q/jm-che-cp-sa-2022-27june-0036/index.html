The gas produced by treating an aqueous solution of ammonium chloride with sodium nitrite is

  1. (1) NH3
  2. (2) N2
  3. (3) N2O
  4. (4) Cl2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

When ammonium chloride (NH4Cl) solution is treated with sodium nitrite (NaNO2), a reaction occurs between the ammonium ion (NH4+) and nitrite ion (NO2-). The reaction is: NH4Cl(aq) + NaNO2(aq) → N2(g) + 2H2O(l) + NaCl(aq). This is a characteristic reaction where ammonium salt reacts with sodium nitrite to produce nitrogen gas (N2), water, and sodium chloride. N2 is the main gaseous product.
