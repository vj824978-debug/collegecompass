A steel wire of length 3.2 m (Y_s = 2.0 × 10^11 Nm^-2) and a copper wire of length 4.4 m (Y_c = 1.1 × 10^11 Nm^-2), both of radius 1.4 mm are connected end to end. When stretched by a load, the net elongation is found to be 1.4 mm. The load applied, in Newton, will be: (Given π = 22/7)

  1. (1) 360
  2. (2) 180
  3. (3) 1080
  4. (4) 154

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For two wires in series with same load W: Δℓ_s + Δℓ_c = 1.4 mm. Using strain formula: (Wℓ_s)/(Y_s A) + (Wℓ_c)/(Y_c A) = 1.4 × 10^-3. With A = π(1.4 × 10^-3)^2, substituting values: W = 1.4 × 10^-3 / [3.2/(2 × (π × 1.4 × 10^-3)^2) + 4.4/(1.1 × (π × 1.4 × 10^-3)^2)] × 10^11. Solving gives W = 154 N.
