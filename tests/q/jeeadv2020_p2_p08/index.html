A student skates up a ramp that makes an angle 30° with the horizontal. He/she starts (as shown in the figure) at the bottom of the ramp with speed v₀ and wants to turn around over a semicircular path xyz of radius R during which he/she reaches a maximum height h (at point y) from the ground as shown in the figure. Assume that the energy loss is negligible and the force required for this turn at the highest point is provided by his/her weight only. Then (g is the acceleration due to gravity) [Figure shows a ramp at 30° angle with a semicircular path of radius R at the top. Point x is at bottom of semicircle on the ramp, point y is at the top of the semicircle at height h, and point z is at the other end of the semicircle on the ramp. The semicircle is labeled with radius R and height h measured from the ground.]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) v₀² - 2gh = (1/2)gR
  2. (B) v₀² - 2gh = (√3/2)gR
  3. (C) the centripetal force required at points x and z is zero
  4. (D) the centripetal force required is maximum at points x and z

Answer

Correct answer: AD

Solution

Let v be the speed at y Energy conservation ⇒ (1/2)mv₀² = mgh + (1/2)mv² ...(1) FBD at y ⇒ mg sin 30° = mv²/R ...(2) From (1) and (2) ⇒ v₀² - 2gh = gR/2, (A) option is correct. (D) is true as on the circular path he/she will have maximum speed at x and z.
