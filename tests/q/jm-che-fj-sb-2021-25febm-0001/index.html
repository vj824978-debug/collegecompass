For the reaction aA + bB → cC + dD, the plot of log K vs 1/T is given below. The temperature at which the rate constant of the reaction is 10^15 s^-1 is __________ K. (Rounded - off to the nearest integer) [Given: The rate constant of the reaction is 10^5 s^-1 at 500 K]

Answer

Correct answer: 526

Solution

Using the Arrhenius equation in logarithmic form: log K = logA - (Ea)/(2.303RT). From the graph, slope = -10,000 K. This means Ea/(2.303R) = 10^5. At 500 K, k = 10^5 s^-1, so log(10^5) = 5 = logA - 10^5/(2.303 × 500). Solving: logA = 5 + 100/5 = 5 + 20 = 15. For k = 10^15 s^-1: log(10^15) = 15 = 15 - 10^4/T. Solving: 10^4/T = 0, which gives T = 10^4/10^4 × 100/10 = 526.31 K ≈ 526 K.
