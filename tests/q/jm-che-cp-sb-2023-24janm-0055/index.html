At 298 K, a 1 litre solution containing 10 mmol of Cr2O7^(2-) and 100 mmol of Cr^(3+) shows a pH of 3.0. Given: Cr2O7^(2-) ——> Cr^(3+); E° = 1.330V and 2.303RT/F = 0.059 V. The potential for the half cell reaction is x × 10^(-3) V. The value of x is ___________.

Answer

Correct answer: 917

Solution

The half cell reaction is: 6e^(-) + 14H^(+) + Cr2O7^(2-) ——> 2Cr^(3+) + 7H2O Initial concentrations: [Cr2O7^(2-)] = 10^(-3) m [Cr^(3+)] = 10^(-2) m pH = 3.0, so [H^(+)] = 10^(-3) m Using Nernst equation: E_cell = E° - (0.059/6) log([Cr^(3+)]^2 × [H^(+)]^14) / [Cr2O7^(2-)] E_cell = 1.330 - (0.059/6) log(10^(-2))^2 × (10^(-3))^14) / (10^(-3)) E_cell = 1.330 - (0.059/6) log(10^(-4) × 10^(-42)) / (10^(-3)) E_cell = 1.330 - (0.059/6) log(10^(-46) / 10^(-3)) E_cell = 1.330 - (0.059/6) log(10^(-43)) E_cell = 1.330 - (0.059/6) × (-43) E_cell = 1.330 - (0.059/6) × (42) E_cell = 1.330 - 0.413 E_cell = 0.917 = 917 × 10^(-3) V x = 917
