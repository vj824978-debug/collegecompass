Two satellites A and B of masses 200 kg and 400 kg are revolving round the earth at height of 600 km and 1600 km respectively. If T_A and T_B are the time periods of A and B respectively then the value of T_B - T_A is? [Given: radius of earth = 6400 km, mass of earth = 6 × 10^24 kg]

  1. (1) 3.33 × 10^5 s
  2. (2) 1.33 × 10^5 s
  3. (3) 4.24 × 10^5 s
  4. (4) 4.24 × 10^3 s

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using Kepler's third law: T = 2π × sqrt(r^3/(GM)). For satellite A: r_A = 6400 + 600 = 7000 km = 7 × 10^6 m. For satellite B: r_B = 6400 + 1600 = 8000 km = 8 × 10^6 m. T_B - T_A = (2π/sqrt(GM)) × (r_B^(3/2) - r_A^(3/2)) = (2 × 3.14/sqrt(6.67 × 10^(-11) × 6 × 10^24)) × ((8 × 10^6)^(3/2) - (7 × 10^6)^(3/2)) = 1.3 × 10^5 s
