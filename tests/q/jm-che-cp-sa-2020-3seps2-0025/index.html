If 250 cm³ of an aqueous solution containing 0.73 g of a protein A is isotonic with one litre of another aqueous solution containing 1.66 g of a protein B, at 298 K, the ratio of the molecular masses of A and B is ................... × 10^(-2) (to the nearest integer).

Answer

Correct answer: 177

Solution

Let molar mass of protein A = x g/mol Let molar mass of protein B = y g/mol For osmotic pressure of protein A: π_A = (0.73/x)/0.25 × RT = (0.73/(x × 0.25)) RT For osmotic pressure of protein B: π_B = (1.65/y)/1 × RT = (1.65/y) RT Since the solutions are isotonic, π_A = π_B (0.73/(x × 0.25)) RT = (1.65/y) RT Cancelling RT from both sides: 0.73/(x × 0.25) = 1.65/y Rearranging: x/y = 0.73/(0.25 × 1.65) = 0.73/0.4125 = 1.769 ≈ 1.77 Therefore, the ratio of molecular masses (x/y) = 1.77 × 10^(-2) = 177 × 10^(-2) To the nearest integer: 177
