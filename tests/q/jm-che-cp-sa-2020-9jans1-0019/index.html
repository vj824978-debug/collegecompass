In the following reaction A is: A undergoes reaction with (i) Br2, hv (ii) KOH (alc.) (iii) O3 (iv) (CH3)2S (v) NaOH(aq) + Δ to give a product with structure shown as cyclopentanone with an aldehyde group

  1. (1) Methylcyclopentane
  2. (2) Cyclohexane
  3. (3) Cyclopropylmethane
  4. (4) Cyclopentane with methyl group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Starting material A undergoes the following transformations: 1) Br2, hv: Radical bromination of A to give brominated product 2) KOH (alc.): Elimination reaction (E2) removes HBr to form an alkene (elimination of HBr from the brominated compound) 3) O3: Ozonolysis of the alkene breaks the double bond 4) (CH3)2S: Reductive workup of the ozonide with dimethyl sulfide gives the carbonyl compound(s) 5) NaOH(aq) + Δ: The dialdehyde undergoes aldol condensation followed by dehydration and intramolecular aldol to form the cyclopentanone with aldehyde side chain shown Working backwards from the final product (cyclopentanone carboxaldehyde): - This contains 6 carbons in the main structure - The ozonolysis must have produced a dialdehyde OHC-(CH2)4-CHO - Before ozonolysis, this was a double-bonded compound - The double bond must have been formed by elimination of HBr from a brominated precursor - Radical bromination of A (a methylcyclopentane) would give the brominated intermediate - Therefore, A is methylcyclopentane (option 1)
