Consider a configuration of n identical units, each consisting of three layers. The first layer is a column of air of height h = 1/3 cm, and the second and third layers are of equal thickness d = (sqrt(3) - 1)/2 cm, and refractive indices μ₁ = sqrt(3/2) and μ₂ = sqrt(3), respectively. A light source O is placed on the top of the first unit, as shown in the figure. A ray of light from O is incident on the second layer of the first unit at an angle of θ=60° to the normal. For a specific value of n, the ray of light emerges from the bottom of the configuration at a distance l = 8/sqrt(3) cm, as shown in the figure. The value of n is_________. [Figure shows a stacked configuration of n identical units. Each unit has three layers: air (height h), layer with refractive index μ₁, and layer with refractive index μ₂. Light source O is at the top with incident angle θ. The ray emerges at horizontal distance l from the bottom.]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

1 sin 60° = (√3/2)^r r = 45° (√3/2) sin 45° = √3 sin r₂ r₂ = 30° (1/√3 + (√3-1)/2 + (√3-1)/(2√3)) × n = 8/√3 n = 4 [Diagram showing three parallel horizontal lines with angle measurements. Top line shows angles 60° and 45° with horizontal distances 1/3 and 1/√3. Middle line shows 45° angle with distance 1/√3. Bottom line shows 30° angle with distance (√3-1)/(2√3). Vertical spacing between lines marked as d.]
