The statement that is not true about ozone is:

  1. (1) in the stratosphere, it forms a protective shield against UV radiation.
  2. (2) it is a toxic gas and its reaction with NO gives NO2.
  3. (3) in the atmosphere, it is depleted by CFCs.
  4. (4) in the stratosphere, CFCs release chlorine free radicals (Cl) which reacts with O3 to give chlorine dioxide radicals.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

In the stratosphere, CFCs release chlorine free radical (Cl) CF2Cl2(g) --UV--> Cl(g) + CF2Cl(g) Which react with O3 to give chlorine oxide (ClO) radical not chlorine dioxide (ClO2) radical. Cl(g) + O3(g) → ClO(g) + O2(g) Therefore, statement (4) is not true because it incorrectly states that Cl reacts with O3 to give chlorine dioxide (ClO2) radical, when in fact it gives chlorine oxide (ClO) radical.
