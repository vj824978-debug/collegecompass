The area of the region enclosed by the parabolas y = 4x - x^2 and 3y = (x - 4)^2 is equal to

  1. (1) 14/3
  2. (2) 32/9
  3. (3) 4
  4. (4) 6

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Find intersection points by solving 4x - x^2 = (x - 4)^2/3. Intersection points are (1, 3) and (4, 0). Set up the integral: Area = ∫[1 to 4] [(4x - x^2) - (x - 4)^2/3] dx. Evaluate: Area = [4x^2/2 - x^3/3 - (x - 4)^3/9] from 1 to 4. At x=4: 32 - 64/3 - 0 = 32/3. At x=1: 2 - 1/3 - (-27/9) = 2 - 1/3 + 3 = 14/3. Area = |32/3 - 14/3| = |18/3| = 6. However, rechecking the calculation gives Area = 4 (verify by absolute value of difference).
