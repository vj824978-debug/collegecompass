The oxidation states of nitrogen in NO, NO2, N2O and NO3- are in the order of:

  1. (1) NO2- > NO3 > NO > N2O
  2. (2) NO3- > NO2 > NO > N2O
  3. (3) NO > NO2 > N2O > NO3-
  4. (4) N2O > NO2 > NO > NO3-

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

From the table: NO has oxidation state +2, NO2 has +4, N2O has +1, NO3- has +5. Therefore, the order of oxidation states from lowest to highest is: N2O (+1) < NO (+2) < NO2 (+4) < NO3- (+5). This corresponds to the order N2O > NO > NO2 > NO3- when arranged in ascending order, or NO3- > NO2 > NO > N2O in descending order.
