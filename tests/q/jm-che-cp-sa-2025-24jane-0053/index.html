For reaction: aniline (NH2 on benzene ring) converts to 2-bromoaniline (NH2 and Br on benzene ring) as major product. The correct order of set of reagents for the above conversion is:

  1. (1) Br2 / FeBr3, H2O(Δ), NaOH
  2. (2) H2SO4, Ac2O, Br2, H2O(Δ), NaOH
  3. (3) Ac2O, Br2, H2O(Δ), NaOH
  4. (4) Ac2O, H2SO4, Br2, NaOH

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

To convert aniline to 2-bromoaniline: Step 1: Aniline reacts with concentrated H2SO4 to form anilinium hydrogen sulphate intermediate (NH3+ HSO4-) at 453-473 K. Step 2: Acetylation using Ac2O to protect the amino group by converting it to acetamido group (NH-C(=O)-CH3). This is necessary because free NH2 is highly activating and would cause polyhalogenation. Step 3: Bromination using Br2 in H2O/Δ (heat). Since the acetamido group (-NH-CO-CH3) is moderately activating and ortho/para directing, bromination occurs at ortho and para positions. However, the bulky acetyl group causes steric hindrance at the ortho position adjacent to it, favoring bromination at the other ortho position (2-position). Step 4: Treatment with NaOH (hydrolysis) to remove the protecting acetyl group and regenerate the free amino group, giving 2-bromoaniline as the major product. The correct sequence is: H2SO4, Ac2O, Br2, H2O(Δ), NaOH This corresponds to option (2).
