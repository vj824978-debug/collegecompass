Let ABC be the triangle such that the equations of lines AB and AC be 3y - x = 2 and x + y = 2, respectively, and the points B and C lie on the x-axis. If P is the orthocentre of the triangle ABC, then the area of the triangle PBC is equal to

  1. (1) 4
  2. (2) 6
  3. (3) 8
  4. (4) 10

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Equation of line AB is 3y - x = 2 and AC is x + y = 2. For point B on x-axis (y = 0): 3(0) - x = 2, so x = -2, thus B(-2, 0). For point C on x-axis (y = 0): x + 0 = 2, so x = 2, thus C(2, 0). Slope of BC is 0 (horizontal line). Altitude from A to BC is vertical: x = x_A. Slope of AB is 1/3, so altitude from C has slope -3: y = -3x + 6. Slope of AC is -1, so altitude from B has slope 1: y = x + 2. Solving y = x + 2 and y = -3x + 6: x + 2 = -3x + 6, so 4x = 4, x = 1, y = 3. Orthocentre P(1, 3). Area of triangle PBC with base BC = 4 and height = 3: Area = (1/2) × 4 × 3 = 6
