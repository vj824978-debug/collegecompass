A3B2 is a sparingly soluble salt of molar mass M (g mol^-1) and solubility x g L^-1. The solubility product satisfies Ksp = a(x/M)^5. The value of a is ________. (Integer answer)

Answer

Correct answer: 108

Solution

For A3B2: A3B2(s) ⇌ 3A^2+(aq) + 2B^3-(aq). Solubility = x g L^-1. Molar solubility = x/M mol L^-1. At equilibrium: [A^2+] = 3x/M and [B^3-] = 2x/M. Ksp = [A^2+]^3[B^3-]^2 = (3x/M)^3(2x/M)^2 = 27(x/M)^3 × 4(x/M)^2 = 108(x/M)^5. Therefore, a = 108.
