If the r.m.s speed of chlorine molecule is 490 m/s at 27°C, the r.m.s speed of argon molecules at the same temperature will be (Atomic mass of argon = 39.9 u, molecular mass of chlorine = 70.9 u)

  1. (1) 551.7 m/s
  2. (2) 651.7 m/s
  3. (3) 451.7 m/s
  4. (4) 751.7 m/s

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using the relation for r.m.s speed: V_rms = sqrt(3RT/M). At same temperature, V_Cl2/V_Ar = sqrt(M_Ar/M_Cl2). Therefore, V_Ar = sqrt(M_Cl2/M_Ar) × V_Cl2 = sqrt(70.9/39.9) × 490 = sqrt(1.778) × 490 ≈ 1.334 × 490 ≈ 651.7 m/s
