The solubility product of a sparingly soluble salt A₂X₃ is 1.1 × 10^(-23). If specific conductance of the solution is 3 × 10^(-5) S m^(-1), the limiting molar conductivity of the solution is x × 10^(-3) S m² mol^(-1). The value of x is

Answer

Correct answer: 3.00

Solution

For sparingly soluble salt A₂X₃: A₂X₃ ⇌ 2A + 3X. From Ksp = (2S)²(3S)³ = 1.1 × 10^(-23), we get S ≈ 10^(-5). For sparingly soluble salts, Λm = Λm^0 (at infinite dilution). Using Λm = k/(S × 10^(-3)) where k is specific conductance: Λm = (3 × 10^(-5))/(10^(-5)) × 10^(-3) = 3 × 10^(-3) S m² mol^(-1). Therefore x = 3.00
