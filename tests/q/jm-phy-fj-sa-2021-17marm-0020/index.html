If an electron is moving in the n th orbit of the hydrogen atom, then its velocity (v_n) for the n th orbit is given as:

  1. (1) v_n α 1/n
  2. (2) v_n α n^2
  3. (3) v_n α n
  4. (4) v_n α 1/n^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For hydrogen atom, the electron velocity in the nth orbit is given by Bohr's model. The velocity is inversely proportional to the principal quantum number n. From Bohr's theory: v_n = (Z × e^2)/(2 × epsilon_0 × h × n), where the constant terms simplify to show v_n α 1/n. Therefore, velocity is inversely proportional to n.
