A body of mass 4 kg is placed on a plane at a point P having coordinate (3, 4) m. Under the action of force F = (2i + 3j) N, it moves to a new point Q having coordinates (6, 10)m in 4 sec. The average power and instantaneous power at the end of 4 sec are in the ratio of:

  1. (1) 13 : 6
  2. (2) 6 : 13
  3. (3) 1 : 2
  4. (4) 4 : 3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Average power <P> = (F · displacement) / time = ((2i + 3j) · (3i + 6j)) / 4 = (6 + 18) / 4 = 6 W. Acceleration a = F/m = (2i + 3j)/4 = (1/2)i + (3/4)j. Velocity at t=4 sec: v = at = ((1/2)i + (3/4)j) × 4 = (2i + 3j) m/s. Instantaneous power at t=4 sec: P_ins = F · v = (2i + 3j) · (2i + 3j) = 4 + 9 = 13 W. Ratio = <P> / P_ins = 6 / 13.
