The wavelength of light, while it is passing through water is 540 nm. The refractive index of water is 4/3. The wavelength of the same light when it is passing through a transparent medium having refractive index of 3/2 is ___ nm.

  1. (1) 380
  2. (2) 840
  3. (3) 480
  4. (4) 540

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: - Wavelength in water: lambda_water = 540 nm - Refractive index of water: mu_water = 4/3 - Refractive index of medium B: mu_B = 3/2 - Need to find: Wavelength in medium B (lambda_B) The relationship between refractive index and wavelength is: mu_1/mu_2 = lambda_2/lambda_1 For water and medium B: mu_water/mu_B = lambda_B/lambda_water (4/3)/(3/2) = lambda_B/540 lambda_B = (4/3) × (2/3) × 540 lambda_B = (4 × 2)/(3 × 3) × 540 lambda_B = (8/9) × 540 lambda_B = 480 nm
