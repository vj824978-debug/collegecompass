Two short magnetic dipoles m1 and m2 each having magnetic moment of 1 Am^2 are placed at point O and P respectively. The distance between OP is 1 meter. The torque experienced by the magnetic dipole m2 due to the presence of m1 is ________ × 10^-7 Nm.

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

The torque on m2 due to m1 is given by: τ = B·M·sin(90°) = B·M, where B is the magnetic field due to m1 at the location of m2, and M is the magnetic moment of m2. The magnetic field due to dipole m1 at distance r along the axis is B = (μ₀/4π)·(2m1/r³). Therefore, τ = (μ₀/4π)·(2m1/r³)·m2 = 10^-7·(2×1×1)/1³ = 2×10^-7 Nm. The answer is 2 (in units of 10^-7 Nm).
