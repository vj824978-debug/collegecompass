In a non-right-angled triangle ∆PQR, let p, q, r denote the lengths of the sides opposite to the angles at P, Q, R respectively. The median from R meets the side PQ at S, the perpendicular from P meets the side QR at E, and RS and PE intersect at O. If p = sqrt(3), q = 1, and the radius of the circumcircle of ∆PQR equals 1, then which of the following options is/are correct?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) length of OE = 1/6
  2. (B) Radius of incircle of ∆PQR = (sqrt(3)/2)(2 - sqrt(3))
  3. (C) Length of RS = sqrt(7)/2
  4. (D) Area of ∆SOE = sqrt(3)/12

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution
