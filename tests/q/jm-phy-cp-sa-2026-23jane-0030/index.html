A paratrooper jumps from an aeroplane and opens a parachute after 2 s of free fall and starts deaccelerating with 3 m/s^2. At 10 m height from ground, while descending with the help of parachute, the speed of paratrooper is 5 m/s. The initial height of the aeroplane is ____ m. (g = 10 m/s^2)

  1. (1) 62.5
  2. (2) 92.5
  3. (3) 20
  4. (4) 82.5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The motion can be divided into three phases: Phase A to B (Free fall for 2 s): Initial velocity u = 0 Acceleration a = g = 10 m/s^2 Time t = 2 s Distance fallen: x_1 = (1/2) × 10 × 2^2 = 20 m Velocity at B: v_B = 0 + 10 × 2 = 20 m/s Phase B to C (Deceleration with parachute): Initial velocity at B: u = 20 m/s Final velocity at C: v = 5 m/s Deceleration: a = -3 m/s^2 Using v^2 = u^2 + 2as: 5^2 = 20^2 - 2(3)x_2 25 = 400 - 6x_2 6x_2 = 375 x_2 = 375/6 = 62.5 m Phase C to D (Constant velocity): Velocity = 5 m/s Distance from C to D: x_3 = 10 m Total height: H = x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 20 + 62.5 + 10 = 92.5 m
