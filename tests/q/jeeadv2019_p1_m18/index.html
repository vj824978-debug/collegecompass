Three lines are given by r = λi, λ ∈ ℝ, r = μ(i + j), μ ∈ ℝ and r = v(i + j + k), v ∈ ℝ. Let the lines cut the plane x + y + z = 1 at the points A, B and C respectively. If the area of the triangle ABC is Δ then the value of (6Δ)² equals

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 0.75

Solution

O is origin point C will be foot of perpendicular from O to plane so C(1/3, 1/3, 1/3) so, AB⃗ = -1/2 i + 1/2 j AC⃗ = -2/3 i + 1/3 j + 1/3 k Δ = 1/2|AB⃗ × AC⃗| = 1/2|î + ĵ + k̂|/|6 6 6| = √3/12 (6Δ)² = 3/4 = 0.75 [Right side shows a 3D geometric figure with a triangular pyramid, with points O at origin, A(1,0,0), B(1/2, 1/2, 0), and Y on the vertical axis, with point C inside the structure]
