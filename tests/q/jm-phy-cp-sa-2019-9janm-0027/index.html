A rod of length 50 cm is pivoted at one end. It is raised such that if makes an angle of 30° from the horizontal as shown and released from rest. Its angular speed when it passes through the horizontal (in rad s^(-1)) will be (g = 10 ms^(-2))

  1. (1) sqrt(30/2)
  2. (2) sqrt(20)/3
  3. (3) sqrt(30)/2
  4. (4) sqrt(30)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using conservation of energy, the gravitational potential energy lost by the rod equals the rotational kinetic energy gained. The rod is pivoted at one end and rotates about this pivot point. Initial position: rod makes 30° with horizontal Final position: rod is horizontal The center of mass of the rod is at distance L/2 from the pivot, where L = 50 cm = 0.5 m Change in height of center of mass: Δh = (L/2) sin(30°) = (L/2) × (1/2) = L/4 Initial potential energy (taking horizontal as reference): PE_i = mg(L/4) Final potential energy: PE_f = 0 Loss in PE = mg(L/4) Moment of inertia of rod about one end: I = (1/3)mL^2 By conservation of energy: mg(L/4) = (1/2)Iω^2 mg(L/4) = (1/2) × (1/3)mL^2 × ω^2 g(L/4) = (1/6)L^2 × ω^2 ω^2 = (6g)/(4L) = (3g)/(2L) Substituting g = 10 m/s^2 and L = 0.5 m: ω^2 = (3 × 10)/(2 × 0.5) = 30/1 = 30 ω = sqrt(30) rad/s
