A rectangular metallic loop is moving out of a uniform magnetic field region to a field free region with a constant speed. When the loop is partially inside the magnet field, the plot of magnitude of induced emf (ε) with time (t) is given by

  1. (1) Curved graph showing ε increasing with time (concave up curve)
  2. (2) Linear graph showing ε increasing linearly with time
  3. (3) Curved graph showing ε decreasing with time (concave down curve)
  4. (4) Horizontal line showing ε constant with time

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

When a rectangular metallic loop moves out of a uniform magnetic field region with constant speed: The induced emf is given by: ε = Blv Where: - B = magnetic field strength (constant) - l = length of the side of the loop cutting the magnetic field lines (constant) - v = velocity of the loop (constant) Since B, l, and v are all constants, the induced emf ε = Blv remains constant throughout the motion. The diagram shows: - A rectangular loop partially inside a uniform magnetic field region (indicated by × symbols showing field into the page) - The loop moving to the right with constant velocity V - The magnetic field B is constant As long as the loop is partially inside the field (i.e., one side of the loop is cutting through the magnetic field boundary), only one side contributes to the motional emf, and this contribution remains constant because all parameters (B, l, and v) are constant. Therefore, the emf versus time graph is a horizontal line, indicating constant emf. The answer is graph (4).
