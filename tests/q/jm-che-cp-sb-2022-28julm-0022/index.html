An element M crystallises in a body centred cubic unit cell with a cell edge of 300 pm. The density of the element is 6.0 g cm^(-3). The number of atoms present in 180 g of the element is ______×10^(-3). (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 22

Solution

Given: a = 300 pm = 3×10^(-8) cm, density ρ = 6 g/cm³, mass = 180 g. For body centred cubic, volume of unit cell = a³ = (3×10^(-8))³ = 27×10^(-24) cm³. Volume occupied per atom in BCC = 27×10^(-24)/2. Using density formula: Volume of 180 g = mass/density = 180/6 = 30 cm³. Number of atoms = (30 × 2)/(27×10^(-24)) = 60/27 × 10^24 = 22.2 × 10^23 = 22 × 10^(-3) × 10^26 (expressing as ×10^(-3)).
