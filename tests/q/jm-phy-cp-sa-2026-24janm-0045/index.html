Three masses 200 kg, 300 kg and 400 kg are placed at the vertices of an equilateral triangle with sides 20 m. They are rearranged on the vertices of a bigger triangle of side 25 m and with the same centre. The work done in this process ____ J. (Gravitational constant G = 6.7 × 10^(-11) Nm^2/kg^2)

  1. (1) 9.86 × 10^(-6)
  2. (2) 2.85 × 10^(-7)
  3. (3) 1.74 × 10^(-7)
  4. (4) 4.77 × 10^(-7)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Work done by external agent: W_ext = ΔU U_i = -(Gm₁m₂)/r_i - (Gm₂m₃)/r_i - (Gm₃m₁)/r_i ; r_i = 20 m U_f = -(Gm₁m₂)/r_f - (Gm₂m₃)/r_f - (Gm₃m₁)/r_f ; r_f = 25 m U_i = (-6.67 × 10^(-11))/20 [200 × 300 + 300 × 400 + 200 × 400] = (-6.67 × 10^(-11))/20 × 26 × 10^4 = -86.71 × 10^(-8) J U_f = (-6.67 × 10^(-11))/0.25 [200 × 300 + 300 × 400 + 200 × 400] = (-6.67 × 10^(-11))/0.25 × 26 × 10^3 = -693.68 × 10^(-9) = -69.36 × 10^(-8) J ΔU = U_f - U_i = 1.74 × 10^(-7) J
