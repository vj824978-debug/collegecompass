Compound I is heated with Conc. HI to give a hydroxy compound A which is further heated with Zn dust to give compound B. Identify A and B.

  1. (1) A = isopropanol (CH3CH(OH)CH3), B = propene (CH3CH=CH2)
  2. (2) A = phenol (C6H5OH), B = 1,4-benzoquinone (C6H4O2)
  3. (3) A = cresol (methylphenol), B = toluene (methylbenzene)
  4. (4) A = phenol (C6H5OH), B = benzene (C6H6)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Compound I is phenetole (C6H5-O-CH2CH3, ethyl phenyl ether). When heated with concentrated HI, the C-O bond in the ether cleaves via SN2 mechanism, producing phenol (A) and ethyl iodide. The phenol is further heated with Zn dust and HI, which reduces the phenol to benzene (B) through replacement of the hydroxyl group with hydrogen via a free radical mechanism or through formation of phenyl iodide intermediate followed by reduction.
