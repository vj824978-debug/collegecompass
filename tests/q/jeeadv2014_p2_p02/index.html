A glass capillary tube is of the shape of truncated cone with an apex angle α so that its two ends have cross sections of different radii. When dipped in water vertically, water rises in it to a height h, where the radius of its cross section is b. If the surface tension of water is S, its density is ρ, and its contact angle with glass is θ, the value of h will be (g is the acceleration due to gravity) [Figure shows a truncated cone tube dipped vertically in water, with water risen to height h inside the tube]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) (A) (2S/bρg)cos(θ - α)
  2. (B) (B) (2S/bρg)cos(θ + α)
  3. (C) (C) (2S/bρg)cos(θ - α/2)
  4. (D) (D) (2S/bρg)cos(θ + α/2)

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

If R be the meniscus radius R cos(θ + α/2) = b Excess pressure on concave side of meniscus = 2S/R hρg = 2S/R - 2S/b cos(θ + α/2) ⟹ h = (2S/bρg) cos(θ + α/2) [Diagram shows a meniscus in a V-shaped container with angle α/2 at the apex, meniscus radius R, contact angle θ, width b at the top, and height h marked vertically]
