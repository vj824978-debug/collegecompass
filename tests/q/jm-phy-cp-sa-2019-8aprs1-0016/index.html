Water from a pipe is coming at a rate of 100 liters per minute. If the radius of the pipe is 5 cm, the Reynolds number for the flow is of the order of - (density of water = 1000 kg/m^3, coefficient of viscosity of water = 1 mPa·s)

  1. (1) 10^4
  2. (2) 10^3
  3. (3) 10^2
  4. (4) 10^6

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

R_c = (d*v*ρ)/η Given: - Flow rate = 100 liters per minute = 100/60 liters per second = (10^(-1))/60 m^3/s - Radius r = 5 cm = 0.05 m, so diameter d = 0.1 m = 10^(-1) m - Density ρ = 1000 kg/m^3 - Viscosity η = 1 mPa·s = 10^(-3) Pa·s Velocity v = Flow rate / Area = [(10^(-1))/60] / [π(0.05)^2] = [(10^(-1))/60] / [π × 25 × 10^(-4)] v = [(10^(-1))/60] / [π × 25 × 10^(-4)] = (10^(-1))/(60 × π × 25 × 10^(-4)) = (10^(-1) × 10^4)/(60 × 25π) = 1000/(1500π) ≈ (10^3)/(1.5π) m/s R_c = [10^(-1) × (10^3)/(1.5π) × 10^3] / 10^(-3) = [(10^(-1)) × (10^(-1))/60 × 1/(π × 25 × 10^(-4)) × 10^3] / 10^(-3) = (10^(-1)) × (10^(-1))/60 × 1/(π × 25 × 10^(-4)) × (10^3) × 10^3 ≈ 21.2 × 10^3 ≈ 2 × 10^4 Order = 10^4
