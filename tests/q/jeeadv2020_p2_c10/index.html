Choose the correct statement(s) among the following

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) SnCl₂· 2H₂O is a reducing agent.
  2. (B) SnO₂ reacts with KOH to form K₂[Sn(OH)₆].
  3. (C) A solution of PbCl₂ in HCl contains Pb⁴⁺ and Cl⁻ ions.
  4. (D) The reaction of Pb₃O₄ with hot dilute nitric acid to give PbO₂ is a redox reaction.

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

(A) Sn²⁺ is less stable than Sn⁴⁺, therefore SnCl₂·2H₂O is a Lewis acid. (B) SnO₂ is amphoteric, so it will dissolve in KOH solution forming K₂SnO₃ or K₂[Sn(OH)₆]. SnO₂ + 2KOH + 2H₂O ⟶ K₂[Sn(OH)₆] (C) PbCl₂ will form a complex with HCl PbCl₂(s) + 2HCl ⟶ H₂[PbCl₄] So, we may assume that now this complex will dissociate as: H₂[PbCl₄] ⟶ 2H⁺ + [PbCl₄]²⁻ So, considering this possibility, option (C) should be incorrect. (D) Pb₃O₄ reacts with conc.HNO₃ to form PbO₂ but the reaction is not a redox reaction. Pb₃O₄·2PbO + 4HNO₃ ⟶ PbO₂ + 2Pb(NO₃)₂ + 6H₂O (+4) (+2) (+4) (+2) or Pb₃O₄ So, option (D) is incorrect.
