Let the position vectors of the vertices A, B and C of a tetrahedron ABCD be i + 2j + k, i + 3j - 2k and 2i + j - k respectively. The altitude from the vertex D to the opposite face ABC meets the median line segment through A of the triangle ABC at the point E. If the length of AD is sqrt(110)/3 and the volume of the tetrahedron is sqrt(805)/(6*sqrt(2)), then the position vector of E is

  1. (1) (1/2)(i + 4j + 7k)
  2. (2) (1/12)(7i + 4j + 3k)
  3. (3) (1/6)(12i + 12j + k)
  4. (4) (1/6)(7i + 12j + k)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given position vectors: A = i + 2j + k B = i + 3j - 2k C = 2i + j - k Vector AB = B - A = 5i + 3j + k Vector AC = C - A = i - j - 2k Area of triangle ABC = (1/2)|AB × AC| = (1/2)|5i + 3j + k| = (1/2)*sqrt(35) Volume of tetrahedron = (1/3) × Base area × h = sqrt(805)/(6*sqrt(2)) From volume formula: (1/3) × (1/2)*sqrt(35) × h = sqrt(805)/(6*sqrt(2)) (1/6)*sqrt(35) × h = sqrt(805)/(6*sqrt(2)) h = sqrt(23/2) For point E on the altitude from D meeting the median through A: The median through A of triangle ABC passes through A and the midpoint F of BC. F = (B + C)/2 = (3/2, 2, -3/2) Using the constraint that E lies on both: 1. The altitude from D (perpendicular to plane ABC) 2. The median line through A From AD^2 - DE^2 = AE^2: AE^2 = (110/9) - (23/18) = 13/18 AE = sqrt(13/18) Unit vector along normal to plane ABC: n = (AB × AC)/|AB × AC| = (i - 5k)/sqrt(26) Vector AE = |AE| × (i - 5k)/sqrt(26) = sqrt(13/18) × (i - 5k)/sqrt(26) = (1/6)(i - 5k) Position vector of E = A + AE = (i + 2j + k) + (1/6)(i - 5k) = (1/6)(7i + 12j + k)
