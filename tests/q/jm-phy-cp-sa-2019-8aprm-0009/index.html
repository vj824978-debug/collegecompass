In a simple pendulum experiment for determination of acceleration due to gravity (g), time taken for 20 oscillations is measured by using a watch of 1 second least count. The mean value of time taken comes out to be 30 s. The length of pendulum is measured by using a meter scale of least count 1 mm and the value obtained is 55.0 cm. The percentage error in the determination of g is close to -

  1. (1) 0.7 %
  2. (2) 6.8 %
  3. (3) 0.2 %
  4. (4) 3.5 %

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

T = 2*pi*sqrt(l/g) g = 4*pi^2*l/T^2 |delta g/g| = |delta l/l| + 2|delta T/T| For time measurement: Time for 20 oscillations = 30 s Least count of watch = 1 s delta T (for 20 oscillations) = 1 s Time for 1 oscillation T = 30/20 = 1.5 s delta T (for 1 oscillation) = 1/20 = 0.05 s |delta T/T| = 0.05/1.5 = 1/30 For length measurement: Length l = 55.0 cm = 55 cm Least count = 1 mm = 0.1 cm delta l = 0.1 cm |delta l/l| = 0.1/55 Percentage error in g: % |delta g/g| = (0.1/55) × 100 + 2 × (1/30) × 100 = 0.1/55 × 100 + 2/30 × 100 = (0.1 × 100)/55 + (2 × 100)/30 = 10/55 + 200/30 = 0.1818... + 6.666... = 6.8 %
