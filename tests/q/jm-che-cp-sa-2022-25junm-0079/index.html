Let x = 2t, y = t/3 be a conic. Let S be the focus and B be the point on the axis of the conic such that SA ⊥ BA, where A is any point on the conic. If k is the ordinate of the centroid of triangle SAB, then lim(t→1) k is equal to

  1. (1) 17/18
  2. (2) 19/18
  3. (3) 11/18
  4. (4) 13/18

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From parametric equations x = 2t, y = t/3, eliminating t gives x^2 = 12y (parabola). Focus S is at (0, 3). Let B = (0, β) on y-axis. Point A on conic: A = (2t, t/3). For SA ⊥ BA, use dot product condition: SA · BA = -1. Vector SA = (1/3, β - 1/3), vector BA = (2 - 3, -2) = (-1, -2). This gives (1/3)(-1) + (β - 1/3)(-2) = -1, which yields β = -17/9. Centroid of triangle SAB: k = (y_S + y_A + y_B)/3 = (3 + t/3 - 17/9)/3. As t→1: k = (3 + 1/3 - 17/9)/3 = (-17/9 + 10/3)/3 = 13/18.
