Given below are two statements: Statement I: On passing HCl(g) through a saturated solution of BaCl2, at room temperature white turbidity appears. Statement II: When HCl gas is passed through a saturated solution of NaCl, sodium chloride is precipitated due to common ion effect. In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
  3. (3) Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
  4. (4) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

BaCl2, NaCl are soluble but on adding HCl(g) to BaCl2, NaCl solutions, Sodium or Barium chlorides may precipitate out, as a consequence of the law of mass action. Statement I is correct because HCl dissolves to form H+ and Cl- ions, but this does not cause BaCl2 precipitation as both are soluble. However, the white turbidity appears due to other factors. Statement II is incorrect because HCl gas dissolves to form H+ and Cl- ions. The additional Cl- ions (from HCl) shift the equilibrium of NaCl dissolution backwards (common ion effect), causing NaCl to precipitate, not due to simple common ion effect mechanism as stated.
