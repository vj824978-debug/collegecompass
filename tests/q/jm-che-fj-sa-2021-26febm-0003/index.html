On treating a compound with warm dil. H2SO4, gas X is evolved which turns K2Cr2O7 paper acidified with dil. H2SO4 into a green compound Y. X and Y respectively are

  1. (1) X = SO2, Y = Cr2(SO4)3
  2. (2) X = SO3, Y = Cr2O3
  3. (3) X = SO3, Y = Cr2O3
  4. (4) X = SO3, Y = Cr2(SO4)3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

When a compound is treated with warm dilute H2SO4, SO2 gas is evolved. SO2 is a reducing agent that reduces K2Cr2O7 (orange) to Cr2(SO4)3 (green) in acidic medium. The reaction shown: K2Cr2O7 + H2SO4 + SO2 → Cr2(SO4)3 + K2SO4 + H2O. Therefore, X = SO2 and Y = Cr2(SO4)3.
