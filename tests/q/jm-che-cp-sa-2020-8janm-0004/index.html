The third ionization enthalpy is minimum for:

  1. (1) Ni
  2. (2) Co
  3. (3) Mn
  4. (4) Fe

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

26Fe = [Ar] 3d^6 4s^2 Among Ni, Co, Mn, Fe Fe having minimum third ionization energy Explanation: For third ionization enthalpy, we need to remove the third electron. Ni: [Ar] 3d^8 4s^2 → After removing 2 electrons: [Ar] 3d^8 (stable d^8 configuration) Co: [Ar] 3d^7 4s^2 → After removing 2 electrons: [Ar] 3d^7 Mn: [Ar] 3d^5 4s^2 → After removing 2 electrons: [Ar] 3d^5 (stable d^5 configuration - half-filled) Fe: [Ar] 3d^6 4s^2 → After removing 2 electrons: [Ar] 3d^6 The third ionization enthalpy is minimum for Fe because after the first two electrons are removed (from 4s orbital), the third electron must be removed from the 3d orbital. Fe^2+ has a 3d^6 configuration which is less stable compared to Mn^2+ (3d^5 - half-filled, more stable). Therefore, Fe requires the least energy to remove the third electron, making its third ionization enthalpy minimum.
