A solution containing 10g of an electrolyte AB₂ in 100g of water boils at 100.52°C. The degree of ionization of the electrolyte (α) is ___ × 10⁻¹. (nearest integer). [Given: Molar mass of AB₂ = 200g mol⁻¹, Kb (molal boiling point elevation const. of water) = 0.52 K kg mol⁻¹, boiling point of water = 100°C; AB₂ ionises as AB₂ → A²⁺ + 2B⁻]

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

AB₂ → A²⁺ + 2B⁻. For ionization: i = 1 + (3-1)α = 1 + 2α. Using ΔTb = kb × i × m. Mass of AB₂ = 10g, molar mass = 200g/mol, so moles = 10/200 = 0.05 mol. Molality m = 0.05/0.1 = 0.5 mol/kg. ΔTb = 100.52 - 100 = 0.52°C. Therefore: 0.52 = 0.52 × (1 + 2α) × 0.5 = 0.52 × (1 + 2α) × (10/20). Simplifying: 1 = (1 + 2α) × (10/20), so 2 = 1 + 2α, giving α = 0.5 = 5 × 10⁻¹.
