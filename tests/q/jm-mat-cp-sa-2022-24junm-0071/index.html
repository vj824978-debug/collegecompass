If the tangent at the point (x1, y1) on the curve y = x^3 + 3x^2 + 5 passes through the origin, then (x1, y1) does NOT lie on the curve:

  1. (1) x^2 + y^2/81 = 2
  2. (2) y^2/9 - x^2 = 8
  3. (3) y = 4x^2 + 5
  4. (4) x/3 - y^2 = 2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given curve: y = x^3 + 3x^2 + 5. At point (x1, y1), slope of tangent = dy/dx = 3x1^2 + 6x1. Since tangent passes through origin (0,0), the slope from origin to (x1, y1) is y1/x1. Therefore: y1/x1 = 3x1^2 + 6x1. Since (x1, y1) is on the curve: y1 = x1^3 + 3x1^2 + 5. Substituting: (x1^3 + 3x1^2 + 5)/x1 = 3x1^2 + 6x1. This gives: x1^3 + 3x1^2 + 5 = 3x1^3 + 6x1^2, so 2x1^3 + 3x1^2 - 5 = 0. Factoring: (x1 - 1)(2x1^2 + 5x1 + 5) = 0. So x1 = 1, and y1 = 1 + 3 + 5 = 9. Point is (1, 9). Check all options: (1) 1 + 81/81 = 2 ✓, (2) 81/9 - 1 = 8 ✓, (3) 9 = 4 + 5 ✓, (4) 1/3 - 81 ≠ 2 ✗
