The major product in the following reaction is: A cyclopentane ring with H3C and CH=CH2 groups attached at the top, and OH group attached at the bottom, treated with H3O+ and Heat

  1. (1) A cyclohexane ring with two CH3 groups attached
  2. (2) A cyclopentane ring with OH group and three CH3 groups attached
  3. (3) A cyclohexane ring with CH3 groups attached
  4. (4) A cyclopentene ring with two CH3 groups attached

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The starting material is a cyclopentanol with an exocyclic double bond (H3C-C=CH2) attached to the same carbon bearing the OH group. Step 1: Under acidic conditions (H3O+) with heat, the OH group is protonated and acts as a leaving group. Step 2: The exocyclic C=C double bond (vinyl group) attacks to form a carbocation, creating a 6-membered ring intermediate through ring expansion. Step 3: The carbocation intermediate formed is a resonance-stabilized allylic cation. Step 4: Ring expansion occurs as the double bond electrons migrate to stabilize the positive charge, converting the cyclopentane ring to a cyclohexane ring. Step 5: Deprotonation (-H+) of the carbocation intermediate yields the major product: a cyclohexene derivative with two CH3 groups (one from the original methyl group and one from the vinyl carbon). The major product is a cyclohexadiene or cyclohexene with methyl substituents, which corresponds to option (3).
