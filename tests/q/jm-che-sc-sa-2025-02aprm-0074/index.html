Consider the following electrochemical cell at standard condition: Au(s)|QH2,Q|NH4X(0.01 M)||Ag+(1 M)|Ag(s) E_cell = +0.4 V. The couple OH2/O represents quinhydrone electrode, the half cell reaction is given: Q + 2e- + 2H+ → QH2, E°_Q/QH2 = +0.7 V. Given: E°_Ag+/Ag = +0.8V and (2.303RT)/F = 0.06V. The pKa value of the ammonium halide salt (NH4X) used here is _______ (nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

At standard conditions, E_cell = E°_cathode - E°_anode. The cathode is Ag+/Ag (E° = +0.8V) and anode is QH2/Q (E° = +0.7V). E°_cell = 0.8 - 0.7 = +0.1V. However, given E_cell = +0.4V at non-standard conditions. Using Nernst equation: E_cell = E°_cell - (0.06/n)log(Q). With NH4+ present (0.01 M) and Ag+ (1 M), the potential difference accounts for pH effect via the quinhydrone electrode. The NH4X affects the pH through hydrolysis. For the half-reaction Q + 2e- + 2H+ → QH2, pH changes affect potential. E_Q/QH2 = 0.7 - (0.06/2)log([QH2]/[Q][H+]^2) = 0.7 - 0.03log(1/[H+]^2) = 0.7 + 0.06pH. At equilibrium with E_cell = +0.4V: 0.4 = 0.8 - (0.7 + 0.06pH), solving gives pH ≈ 1.67. For NH4X hydrolysis: Ka = Kw/Kb, and pKa ≈ 9 (nearest integer)
