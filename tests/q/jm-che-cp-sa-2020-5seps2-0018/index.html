Consider the complex ions, trans-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ (A) and cis-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ (B). The correct statement regarding them is:

  1. (1) both (A) and (B) can be optically active
  2. (2) both (A) and (B) cannot be optically active
  3. (3) (A) can be optically active, but (B) cannot be optically active
  4. (4) (A) cannot be optically active, but (B) can be optically active

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

(A) trans-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ Structure shows: - Co center with two ethylenediamine (en) ligands and two Cl ligands - In trans form, the two Cl ligands are opposite to each other - The two en ligands are also opposite to each other - This arrangement possesses a plane of symmetry ⇒ (A) is trans form and shows plane of symmetry which is optically inactive (not optically active) (B) cis-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ Structure shows: - Co center with two ethylenediamine (en) ligands and two Cl ligands - In cis form, the two Cl ligands are adjacent to each other - The arrangement does not possess a plane of symmetry - The complex lacks a plane of symmetry and inversion center ⇒ (B) is cis form and does not show plane of symmetry, hence it is optically active. Conclusion: (A) cannot be optically active, but (B) can be optically active.
