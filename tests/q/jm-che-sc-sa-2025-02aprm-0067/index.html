The vessels A and B are connected via stopcock. The vessel A is filled with a gas at a certain pressure. The entire assembly is immersed in water and is allowed to come to thermal equilibrium with water. After opening the stopcock the gas from vessel A expands into vessel B and no change in temperature is observed in the thermometer. Which of the following statement is true. 1) dq ≠ 0, 2) dU ≠ 0, 3) dw ≠ 0, 4) The pressure in the vessel B before opening the stopcock is zero.

  1. (1) dq ≠ 0
  2. (2) dU ≠ 0
  3. (3) dw ≠ 0
  4. (4) The pressure in the vessel B before opening the stopcock is zero.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

This is a free expansion process. When the stopcock is opened, gas expands from A into B. Since temperature remains constant (isothermal process for ideal gas), dU = 0 (internal energy depends only on temperature). The system is immersed in water at constant temperature, so this is an isothermal expansion. During free expansion into vacuum (or near-vacuum in B), no work is done by the gas against external pressure, so dw = 0. By first law: dQ = dU + dw = 0 + 0 = 0. However, the system exchanges heat with surroundings to maintain constant temperature during the process. For an isothermal free expansion where dU = 0 and dw = 0, we have dq = 0. Since vessel B is evacuated (or at negligible pressure) before opening, option 4 is true.
