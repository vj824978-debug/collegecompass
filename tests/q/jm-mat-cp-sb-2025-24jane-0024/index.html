Let H1: x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 and H2: x^2/A^2 + y^2/B^2 = 1 be two hyperbolas having length of latus rectums 15√2 and 12√5 respectively. Let their eccentricities be e1 = sqrt(5/2) and e2 respectively. If the product of the lengths of their transverse axes is 100√10, then 25e2^2 is equal to ____

Answer

Correct answer: 55

Solution

For H1: x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 Length of latus rectum = 2b^2/a = 15√2 For eccentricity e1 = sqrt(5/2): e1^2 = 5/2 = 1 + b^2/a^2 b^2/a^2 = 5/2 - 1 = 3/2 From 2b^2/a = 15√2: b^2 = (15√2 · a)/2 Substituting in b^2/a^2 = 3/2: (15√2 · a)/(2a^2) = 3/2 15√2/(2a) = 3/2 a = 15√2/(2 · 3/2) = 15√2/3 = 5√2 Then b^2 = (3/2) · a^2 = (3/2) · 50 = 75 b = 5√3 Verification: 2b^2/a = 2(75)/(5√2) = 150/(5√2) = 30/√2 = 15√2 ✓ Transverse axis of H1 = 2a = 10√2 For H2: x^2/A^2 + y^2/B^2 = 1 (This appears to be a typo; should be x^2/A^2 - y^2/B^2 = 1 for a hyperbola) Length of latus rectum = 2B^2/A = 12√5 Product of transverse axes: 2a · 2A = 100√10 10√2 · 2A = 100√10 2A = 100√10/(10√2) = 10√10/√2 = 10√5 A = 5√5 From 2B^2/A = 12√5: 2B^2 = 12√5 · A = 12√5 · 5√5 = 60 · 5 = 300 B^2 = 150 For H2 (hyperbola): e2^2 = 1 + B^2/A^2 = 1 + 150/(125) = 1 + 6/5 = 11/5 Therefore: 25e2^2 = 25 · (11/5) = 5 · 11 = 55
