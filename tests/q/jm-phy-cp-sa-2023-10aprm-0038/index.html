The de Broglie wavelength of a molecule in a gas at room temperature (300 K) is λ₁. If the temperature of the gas is increased to 600 K, then the de Broglie wavelength of the same gas molecule becomes

  1. (1) (1/2)λ₁
  2. (2) sqrt(2)λ₁
  3. (3) (1/sqrt(2))λ₁
  4. (4) 2λ₁

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

de Broglie wavelength λ = h/p = h/(mv). Since v ∝ sqrt(T), we have v ∝ sqrt(T). Therefore λ ∝ 1/v ∝ 1/sqrt(T). Taking ratio: λ₁/λ₂ = sqrt(T₂/T₁) = sqrt(600/300) = sqrt(2). Thus λ₂ = λ₁/sqrt(2) = (1/sqrt(2))λ₁
