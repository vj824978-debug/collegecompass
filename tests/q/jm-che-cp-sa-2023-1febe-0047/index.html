The effect of addition of helium gas to the following reaction in equilibrium state, is PCl5(g) ⇌ PCl3(g) + Cl2(g)

  1. (1) the equilibrium will go backward due to suppression of dissociation of PCl5
  2. (2) addition of helium will not affect the equilibrium
  3. (3) the equilibrium will shift in the forward direction and more of Cl2 and PCl3 gases will be produced
  4. (4) helium will deactivate PCl5 and reaction will stop

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

If we consider addition of He gas at constant pressure, the reaction will shift in forward direction [As rigid container is not given] When helium gas is added at constant pressure to the system: - The addition of an inert gas (He) at constant pressure increases the total volume of the system - According to Le Chatelier's principle, when volume increases, the equilibrium shifts towards the side with more number of moles of gas - In this reaction: PCl5(g) ⇌ PCl3(g) + Cl2(g) Left side: 1 mole of gas Right side: 2 moles of gas - Since the right side has more moles, equilibrium shifts in the forward direction - This results in increased production of PCl3 and Cl2 gases Note: If the container were rigid (constant volume), addition of He would not affect the equilibrium as partial pressures of the reactants and products remain unchanged.
