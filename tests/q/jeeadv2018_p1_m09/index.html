Let X be the set consisting of the first 2018 terms of the arithmetic progression 1, 6, 11, . . . . , and Y be the set consisting of the first 2018 terms of arithmetic progression 9, 16, 23, . . . . Then, the number of elements in the set X ∪ Y is _____.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 3748

Solution

n(X ∪ Y) = n(X) + n(Y) - n(X ∩ Y) 1, 6, 11, …, 2018 term Tₙ = 1 + (n - 1)5 = 5n - 4 Uₖ = 9 + (K - 1)7 = 7K + 2 for common terms 5n - 4 = 7K + 2 ⟹ n = (7K + 6)/5 ≤ 2018, K ≤ 1440 K = 2, 7, …, r 2 + (r - 1)5 ≤ 1440 ∴ rₘₐₓ = 288 ∴ number of common term = 288 = n(X ∩ Y) n(X ∪ Y) = 2018 + 2018 - 288 = 3748.
