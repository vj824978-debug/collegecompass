Which of the following compounds is not aromatic?

  1. (1) Pyrrole (5-membered ring with N-H)
  2. (2) Pyridinium cation (6-membered ring with N+)
  3. (3) Cyclopentadienyl cation (5-membered ring with positive charge)
  4. (4) Furan (5-membered ring with O)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

(1) Pyrrole: The nitrogen atom has a lone pair that participates in the aromatic system. The ring has 6π electrons (4 from two double bonds + 2 from nitrogen lone pair), making it aromatic with 6π e- aromatic (2) Pyridinium cation: The nitrogen is sp2 hybridized and part of the aromatic ring. The ring has 6π electrons from the double bonds and the lone pair is not in the π system. This gives 6π e- aromatic (3) Cyclopentadienyl cation: This has 5 carbon atoms in the ring. A positive charge means one less electron. The ring has only 4π electrons (4 π e-), which does not satisfy Hückel's rule (4n+2). This makes it antiaromatic with 4π e- (4) Furan: The oxygen atom has a lone pair that participates in the aromatic system. The ring has 6π electrons (4 from two double bonds + 2 from oxygen lone pair), making it aromatic with 6π e- aromatic Therefore, compound (3) is not aromatic; it is antiaromatic.
