The volume of an ideal gas increases 8 times and temperature becomes (1/4)^th of initial temperature during a reversible change. If there is no exchange of heat in this process (ΔQ = 0) then identify the gas from the following options (Assuming the gases given in the options are ideal gases):

  1. (1) CO2
  2. (2) O2
  3. (3) NH3
  4. (4) He

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

PV^gamma = constant TV^(gamma-1) = constant Initial state: T, V Final state: T/4, 8V Applying TV^(gamma-1) = constant: T × V^(gamma-1) = (T/4) × (8V)^(gamma-1) T × V^(gamma-1) = (T/4) × 8^(gamma-1) × V^(gamma-1) 1 = (1/4) × 8^(gamma-1) 4 = 8^(gamma-1) 2^2 = (2^3)^(gamma-1) 2^2 = 2^(3(gamma-1)) 2 = 3(gamma-1) 2 = 3gamma - 3 5 = 3gamma gamma = 5/3 For a monoatomic ideal gas, gamma = Cp/Cv = (5/3)R / (3/2)R = 5/3 Monoatomic gases are: He, Ar, Ne, etc. Among the given options, only He is monoatomic. CO2 is polyatomic (linear) O2 is diatomic NH3 is polyatomic He is monoatomic Answer is He.
