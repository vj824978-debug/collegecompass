A(g) → 2B(g) + C(g) is a first order reaction. The initial pressure of the system was found to be 800 mm Hg which increased to 1600 mm Hg after 10 min. The total pressure of the system after 30 min will be _______ mm Hg. (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 2200

Solution

For reaction A(g) → 2B(g) + C(g), initial pressure = 800 mm Hg. At t=10 min, total pressure = 1600 mm Hg. Let x = pressure of A decomposed at t=10 min. From stoichiometry: (800-x) + 2x + x = 1600, so 800 + 2x = 1600, giving x = 400. For first order reaction: K = (2.303/10) × log(800/400) = (2.303 × log 2)/10. At t=30 min, using K = (2.303/30) × log(800/(800-y)), equate the two K values to get (800/(800-y)) = 8, so 800-y = 100, thus y = 700. Total pressure after 30 min = (800-y) + 2y + y = 800 + 2y = 800 + 1400 = 2200 mm Hg.
