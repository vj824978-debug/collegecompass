A body of mass 2 kg is driven by an engine delivering a constant power 1 J/s. The body starts from rest and moves in a straight line. After 9 seconds, the body has moved a distance (in m) ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 18.00

Solution

P = constant u = 0 at x = 0 P = mav m(dv/dt)v = P Integrating both sides: integral from 0 to v of v dv = (P/m) integral from 0 to t of dt v^2/2 = Pt/m => v = sqrt(2Pt/m) dx/dt = sqrt(2P/M) t^(1/2) Integrating: integral from 0 to x of dx = sqrt(2P/M) integral from 0 to t of t^(1/2) dt x = sqrt(2P/M) * t^(3/2)/(3/2) = sqrt(2P/M) * (2/3) * t^(3/2) Substituting values: P = 1 J/s, M = 2 kg, t = 9 s x = sqrt(2×1/2) × (2/3) × 9^(3/2) = sqrt(1) × (2/3) × 27 = (2/3) × 27 = 18 m
