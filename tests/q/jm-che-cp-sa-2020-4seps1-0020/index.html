For the equilibrium A ⇌ B, the variation of the rate of the forward (a) and reverse (b) reaction with time is given by the graphs shown. Which graph correctly represents the equilibrium?

  1. (1) Graph (1)
  2. (2) Graph (2)
  3. (3) Graph (3)
  4. (4) Graph (4)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

At equilibrium, the rate of forward reaction equals the rate of reverse reaction, i.e., r_a = r_b. Let us analyze each graph: Graph (1): The forward reaction rate (a) decreases with time and the reverse reaction rate (b) increases with time, but they do not meet at equilibrium. This is incorrect because at equilibrium both rates should be equal. Graph (2): Both forward (a) and reverse (b) reaction rates increase initially and then meet at equilibrium. This is incorrect because the forward reaction rate should decrease as reactants are consumed, not increase. Graph (3): The forward reaction rate (a) starts high and decreases with time. The reverse reaction rate (b) starts low and increases with time. At equilibrium, both rates become equal (r_a = r_b) and remain constant. This correctly represents the equilibrium condition. Graph (4): The forward reaction rate (a) and reverse reaction rate (b) intersect, but the forward rate continues to increase after equilibrium. This is incorrect because once equilibrium is reached, the rates should remain constant. Therefore, graph (3) correctly represents the equilibrium where r_a = r_b.
