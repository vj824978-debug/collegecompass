An organic compound 'A' (C9H10O) when treated with conc. HI undergoes cleavage to yield compounds 'B' and 'C'. 'B' gives yellow precipitate with AgNO3 where as 'C' tautomerizes to 'D'. 'D' gives positive idoform test. 'A' could be :

  1. (1) Phenyl-O-CH=CH-CH3
  2. (2) Phenyl-CH2-O-CH=CH2
  3. (3) Phenyl-O-CH2-CH=CH2
  4. (4) H3C-Phenyl-O-CH=CH2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Compound A has molecular formula C9H10O. When treated with conc. HI, it undergoes cleavage to yield compounds B and C. From the solution diagram: - A is phenyl-CH2-O-CH=CH2 (C9H10O) - Upon HI treatment, protonation occurs at the ether oxygen - Cleavage of ether by SN2 mechanism gives: - B: Phenyl-CH2-I (benzyl iodide) - gives yellow precipitate AgI with AgNO3 (characteristic of primary alkyl halides) - C: HO-CH=CH2 (vinyl alcohol) Vinyl alcohol (C) tautomerizes to acetaldehyde (D): HO-CH=CH2 ⇌ CH3-CHO Acetaldehyde gives positive iodoform test (CH3-CHO + I2 + NaOH → CHI3↓ yellow precipitate + CH3COONa) This mechanism confirms option (2) is correct: - B gives yellow AgI precipitate (benzyl iodide is a primary alkyl halide) - C tautomerizes to acetaldehyde - D (acetaldehyde) gives positive iodoform test
