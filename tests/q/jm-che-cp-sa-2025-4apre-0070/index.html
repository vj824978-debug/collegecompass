Consider the given data: (a) HCl(g) + 10 H2O(l) → HCl.10 H2O, ΔH = -69.01 kJ mol^(-1) (b) HCl(g) + 40 H2O(l) → HCl.40 H2O, ΔH = -72.79 kJ mol^(-1) Choose the correct statement: (1) Dissolution of gas in water is an endothermic process. (2) The heat of solution depends on the amount of solvent. (3) The heat of formation of HCl solution is represented by both (a) and (b). (4) The heat of dilution for the HCl (HCl.10 H2O to HCl.40 H2O) is 3.78 kJ mol^(-1).

  1. (1) Dissolution of gas in water is an endothermic process.
  2. (2) The heat of solution depends on the amount of solvent.
  3. (3) The heat of formation of HCl solution is represented by both (a) and (b).
  4. (4) The heat of dilution for the HCl (HCl.10 H2O to HCl.40 H2O) is 3.78 kJ mol^(-1).

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

HCl(g) + ∞H2O(l) → HCl(aq). Heat released in this process is heat of solution. From reaction (b) - (a), we get heat of dilution for HCl (HCl.10 H2O to HCl.40 H2O) as -72.79 - (-69.01) = -3.78 kJ mol^(-1), which equals 3.78 kJ mol^(-1) in magnitude.
