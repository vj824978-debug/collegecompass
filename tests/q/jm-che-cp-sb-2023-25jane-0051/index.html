A first order reaction has the rate constant, k = 4.6 × 10^(-3) s^(-1). The number of correct statement/s from the following is/are___________. Given: log 3 = 0.48. A. Reaction completes in 1000 s. B. The reaction has a half-life of 500 s. C. The time required for 10% completion is 25 times the time required for 90% completion. D. The degree of dissociation is equal to (1 - e^(-kt)). E. The rate and the rate constant have the same unit.

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

A ⟶ Products (1-α) k = 4.6 × 10^(-3) s^(-1) For first order reaction: kt = ln(1/(1-α)) α = 1 - e^(-kt) Reaction completes at infinite time Half-life = 0.693/k = 0.693/(4.6 × 10^(-3)) = 150.65 s For 10% completion: t₁₀% = (2.303/k) × log(100/90) = (2.303 × 0.04)/k For 90% completion: t₉₀% = (2.303/k) × log(100/10) = (2.303 × 1)/k t₁₀% = 0.04 × t₉₀% Units of rate and rate constant are different Statement A: False - Reaction completes at infinite time Statement B: False - Half-life = 150.65 s, not 500 s Statement C: False - t₁₀% = 0.04 × t₉₀%, not 25 times Statement D: True - Degree of dissociation α = 1 - e^(-kt) is correct for first order reaction Statement E: False - For first order reaction, k has units s^(-1) while rate has units M/s Number of correct statements = 1
