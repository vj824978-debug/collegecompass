Two identical glass rods S₁ and S₂ (refractive index = 1.5) have one convex end of radius of curvature 10 cm. They are placed with the curved surfaces at a distance d as shown in the figure, with their axes (shown by the dashed line) aligned. When a point source of light P is placed inside rod S₁ on its axis at a distance of 50 cm from the curved face, the light rays emanating from it are found to be parallel to the axis inside S₂. The distance d is [Figure shows two glass rods S₁ and S₂ with curved surfaces facing each other, separated by distance d, with point P marked 50 cm from the curved surface of S₁]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) 60 cm
  2. (B) 70 cm
  3. (C) 80 cm
  4. (D) 90 cm

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

For 1st refraction 1/v - 1.5/(−50) = (1−1.5)/(−10) ⇒ v = 50 cm For IInd refraction 1.5/∞ − 1/(−x) = (1.5−1)/(+10) ⇒ x = 20 cm ⇒ d = 70 cm [Diagram shows two refracting surfaces S₁ and S₂ separated by distance d, with light ray entering from left at point P, separated by 50 cm from first surface, and emerging from second surface]
