Two identical concave mirrors each of focal length f are facing each other as shown in the schematic diagram. The focal length f is much larger than the size of the mirrors. A glass slab of thickness t and refractive index n₀ is kept equidistant from the mirrors and perpendicular to their common principal axis. A monochromatic point light source S is embedded at the center of the slab on the principal axis, as shown in the schematic diagram. For the image to be formed on S itself, which of the following distances between the two mirrors is/are correct: [Schematic shows two concave mirrors facing each other with focal length f marked, separated by distance with a glass slab of thickness t and refractive index n₀ in the center, containing point source S on the axis]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) 4f + (1 - 1/n₀)t
  2. (B) 2f + (1 - 1/n₀)t
  3. (C) 4f + (n₀ - 1)t
  4. (D) 2f + (n₀ - 1)t

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

Shift in position of object due to refraction at surface of sheet = (t/2)(1 - 1/n₀) To make image at object itself O' must be at common focal point or common centre of curvature ⟹ distance between mirrors d₁ = f + f + 2 OO' = 2f + 2 · (t/2)(1 - 1/n₀) = 2f + t(1 - 1/n₀) d₂ = 2f + 2f + 2 OO' = 4f + t(1 - 1/n₀) [Right side shows a diagram of a sheet of thickness t/2 with a mirror at the bottom and point O marked]
