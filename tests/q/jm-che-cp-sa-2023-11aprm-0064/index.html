L-isomer of tetrose X(C4H6O4) gives positive Schiff's test and has two chiral carbons. On acetylation 'X' yields triacetate. 'X' also undergoes following reactions. 'A' + HNO3 → 'X' + NaBH4 → 'B' (Chiral compound). Identify the structure of X from the given options (1), (2), (3), (4).

  1. (1) Structure with CHO at top, H-OH, H-OH, O with CH2OH at bottom
  2. (2) Structure with CHO at top, H-OH, HO-H, CH2OH at bottom
  3. (3) Structure with H-OH, HO-OH, O with CH2OH at bottom
  4. (4) Structure with CHO at top, HO-H, HO-H, CH2OH at bottom

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

X is a tetrose (C4H6O4) with positive Schiff's test indicating an aldehyde group. It has two chiral carbons. Oxidation with HNO3 gives a dicarboxylic acid. Reduction with NaBH4 converts the aldehyde to primary alcohol yielding a chiral compound. The structure must be an L-isomer of erythrose or threose. Based on the Fischer projections and the requirement of two chiral centers with specific stereochemistry, option (2) represents L-erythrose with CHO group, and the correct stereochemistry at both chiral centers.
