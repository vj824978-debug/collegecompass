Two point charges q1(sqrt(10) μC) and q2(-25 μC) are placed on the x-axis at x = 1 m and x = 4 m respectively. The electric field (in V/m) at a point y = 3 m on y-axis is, [take 1/(4π ε0) = 9 × 10^9 Nm^2C^-2]

  1. (1) (-81i + 81j) × 10^2
  2. (2) (81i - 81j) × 10^2
  3. (3) (63i - 27j) × 10^2
  4. (4) (-63i + 27j) × 10^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Electric field due to sqrt(10) μC charge at point (0, 3): Distance from q1 at (1, 0) to point (0, 3): r1 = sqrt(1^2 + 3^2) = sqrt(10) m Magnitude: |E1| = (k × sqrt(10) × 10^(-6))/10 = (9 × 10^9 × sqrt(10) × 10^(-6))/10 = 9 × 10^3/sqrt(10) Direction: The vector from (1, 0) to (0, 3) is (-1, 3) Unit vector: (-1/sqrt(10), 3/sqrt(10)) E1 vector = E1 sin(θ) i + E1 cos(θ) j E1 vector = (9 × 10^3/sqrt(10)) × [(-1/sqrt(10)) i + (3/sqrt(10)) j] E1 vector = 9 × 10^3 (-i + 3j)/10 = 9 × 10^2(-i + 3j) Electric field due to -25 μC charge at point (0, 3): Distance from q2 at (4, 0) to point (0, 3): r2 = sqrt(4^2 + 3^2) = sqrt(25) = 5 m Magnitude: |E2| = (k × 25 × 10^(-6))/25 = 9 × 10^3 Direction: The vector from (4, 0) to (0, 3) is (-4, 3) Unit vector: (-4/5, 3/5) For negative charge, field points away from point toward charge: E2 vector = E2 sin(θ) i - E2 cos(θ) j E2 vector = 9 × 10^3 × [(4/5) i - (3/5) j] E2 vector = 18 × 10^2 [4i - 3j] Total electric field: E vector = E1 vector + E2 vector E vector = 9 × 10^2(-i + 3j) + 18 × 10^2(4i - 3j) E vector = 10^2[9(-i + 3j) + 18(4i - 3j)] E vector = 10^2[-9i + 27j + 72i - 54j] E vector = 10^2[63i - 27j] E vector = (63i - 27j) × 10^2 V/m Option (3)
