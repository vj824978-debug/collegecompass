Let θ = π/5 and A = [cos θ, sin θ; -sin θ, cos θ]. If B = A + A^4, then det(B):

  1. (1) is one
  2. (2) lies in (1, 2)
  3. (3) is zero
  4. (4) lies in (2, 3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

A = [cos θ, sin θ; -sin θ, cos θ] A^2 = [cos θ, sin θ; -sin θ, cos θ] × [cos θ, sin θ; -sin θ, cos θ] A^2 = [cos 2θ, sin 2θ; -sin 2θ, cos 2θ] B = A + A^4 = [cos θ, sin θ; -sin θ, cos θ] + [cos 4θ, sin 4θ; -sin 4θ, cos 4θ] = [(cos θ + cos 4θ), (sin θ + sin 4θ); -(sin θ + sin 4θ), (cos θ + cos 4θ)] |B| = (cos θ + cos 4θ)^2 + (sin θ + sin 4θ)^2 Expanding: |B| = cos^2 θ + 2cos θ cos 4θ + cos^2 4θ + sin^2 θ + 2sin θ sin 4θ + sin^2 4θ |B| = 1 + 1 + 2(cos θ cos 4θ + sin θ sin 4θ) |B| = 2 + 2cos 3θ When θ = π/5: |B| = 2 + 2cos(3π/5) |B| = 2 + 2cos(3π/5) = 2(1 - sin 18°) Since sin 18° = (√5 - 1)/4: |B| = 2(1 - (√5 - 1)/4) = 2((4 - √5 + 1)/4) = 2((5 - √5)/4) = (5 - √5)/2 Numerically: √5 ≈ 2.236, so |B| ≈ (5 - 2.236)/2 ≈ 2.764/2 ≈ 1.382 Therefore det(B) lies in (1, 2).
