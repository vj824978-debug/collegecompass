Find the magnetic field at the point P in figure. The curved portion is a semicircle connected to two long straight wires.

  1. (1) mu_0*i/(2r) * (1 + 2/pi)
  2. (2) mu_0*i/(2r) * (1 + 1/pi)
  3. (3) mu_0*i/(2r) * (1/2 + 1/(2*pi))
  4. (4) mu_0*i/(2r) * (1/2 + 1/pi)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

B_P = mu_0*i/(4*pi*r) + 1/2 * (mu_0*i/(2*r)) The magnetic field at point P consists of two contributions: 1) From the semicircular portion: The magnetic field at the center of a semicircle of radius r carrying current i is B_semicircle = mu_0*i/(4*pi*r) (half of the field from a full circle) 2) From the long straight wire (lower vertical portion): The magnetic field at distance r from a long straight wire is B_wire = mu_0*i/(2*pi*r). However, this is for the full length. Since we only have a semi-infinite wire below point P, we get half the field: B_wire = (1/2) * mu_0*i/(2*pi*r) = mu_0*i/(4*pi*r) Total magnetic field: B_P = mu_0*i/(4*pi*r) + mu_0*i/(4*pi*r) B_P = 2 * mu_0*i/(4*pi*r) B_P = mu_0*i/(2*pi*r) B_P = mu_0*i/(2*r) * (1/pi) Alternatively expressed as: B_P = mu_0*i/(2*r) * (1/2 + 1/(2*pi))
