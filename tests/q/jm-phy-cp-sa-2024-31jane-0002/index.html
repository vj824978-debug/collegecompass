A uniform magnetic field of 2 × 10^-3 T acts along positive Y-direction. A rectangular loop of sides 20 cm and 10 cm with current of 5 A is in Y-Z plane. The current is in anticlockwise sense with reference to negative X axis. Magnitude and direction of the torque is

  1. (1) 2 × 10^-4 N-m along positive Z-direction
  2. (2) 2 × 10^-4 N-m along negative Z-direction
  3. (3) 2 × 10^-4 N-m along positive Y-direction
  4. (4) 2 × 10^-4 N-m along positive X-direction

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

m = iA where A = 0.2 × 0.1 = 0.02 m^2. τ = iAB = 5 × 0.02 × 2 × 10^-3 = 2 × 10^-4 N-m. M = -i (magnetic moment points in negative X direction due to anticlockwise current). B = j (positive Y direction). M × B = (-i) × j = -k (negative Z direction).
