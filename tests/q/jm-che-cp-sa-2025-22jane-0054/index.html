Consider the given figure and choose the correct option: [Figure shows an energy diagram with reactant at lower level, activated complex at peak with energy E1 marked, and product at a level E2 above reactant]

  1. (1) Activation energy of backward reaction is E1 and product is more stable than reactant.
  2. (2) Activation energy of forward reaction is E1 + E2 and product is more stable than reactant.
  3. (3) Activation energy of forward reaction is E1 + E2 and product is less stable than reactant.
  4. (4) Activation energy of both forward and backward reaction is E1 + E2 and reactant is more stable than product.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

From the energy diagram: 1. The activation energy of forward reaction (reactant to activated complex) = E1 + E2 This is the energy difference between the activated complex peak and the reactant level. 2. Energy of product > Energy of reactant From the diagram, the product level is higher than the reactant level by E2. This means the product is less stable than the reactant (higher energy = less stable). 3. Stability: Reactant > Product Since the product has higher energy, it is less stable. Therefore, the correct statement is: Activation energy of forward reaction = E1 + E2 and product is less stable than reactant.
