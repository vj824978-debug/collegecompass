A radioactive material decays by simultaneous emissions of two particles with half lives of 1400 years and 700 years respectively. What will be the time after which one third of the material remains? (Take ln 3 = 1.1)

  1. (1) 340 years
  2. (2) 1110 years
  3. (3) 700 years
  4. (4) 740 years

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The equivalent half life for radioactive material decaying by simultaneous emissions is given by: 1/T = 1/t1 + 1/t2 = 1/700 + 1/1400 = 3/1400, so T = 1400/3 years. Using N = N0 * e^(-λt), for N = N0/3: 1/3 = e^(-λt), so λt = ln(3). Therefore t = ln(3)/λ = ln(3)/[ln(2)/T] = T * ln(3)/ln(2) = (1400/3) * (1.1/0.693) ≈ 740.74 years ≈ 740 years.
