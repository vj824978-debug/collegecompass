Which of the following compounds produces an optically inactive compound on hydrogenation?

  1. (1) Structure with H and CH3 on a carbon with a double bond to another carbon with two methyl groups
  2. (2) Structure with H and CH3 on a carbon with a conjugated diene system
  3. (3) Structure with H and CH3 on a carbon in a chain with a double bond between two carbons
  4. (4) Structure with H and CH3 on a carbon in a chain with a double bond

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

When a compound undergoes hydrogenation, H2 is added across double bonds. The product becomes optically inactive if it loses its chiral center or if the hydrogenation creates a meso compound (which has internal plane of symmetry despite having chiral centers). Compound (1) has a double bond with H and CH3 groups on one end of the molecule. Upon hydrogenation with H2/Ni, the double bond is reduced. The resulting product has the structure shown in the solution diagram where the stereochemistry is such that the molecule becomes optically inactive (meso compound) - it develops an internal plane of symmetry making it achiral despite the presence of stereocenters. Compounds (2), (3), and (4) contain double bonds that, when hydrogenated, would either: - Retain the original chiral center without creating a plane of symmetry (remaining optically active), or - Not create a meso compound Therefore, compound (1) is the answer as its hydrogenation produces an optically inactive (meso) compound.
