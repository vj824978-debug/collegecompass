A ray of laser of a wavelength 630 nm is incident at an angle of 30° at the diamond-air interface. It is going from diamond to air. The refractive index diamond is 2.42 and that of air is 1. Choose the correct option.

  1. (1) angle of refraction is 53.4°
  2. (2) angle of refraction is 30°
  3. (3) angle of refraction is 24.41°
  4. (4) refraction is not possible

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using Snell's law: μ₁ sin(30°) = μ₂ sin(θ). Here 2.42 × sin(30°) = 1 × sin(θ), so 2.42 × (1/2) = sin(θ), giving sin(θ) = 1.21 > 1. Since sin(θ) cannot exceed 1, refraction is not possible. This is a case of total internal reflection.
