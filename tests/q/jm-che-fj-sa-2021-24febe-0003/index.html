Most suitable salt which can be used for efficient clotting of blood will be:

  1. (1) Mg(HCO3)2
  2. (2) FeSO4
  3. (3) NaHCO3
  4. (4) FeCl3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Blood is a negatively charged colloid. According to Hardy-Schulze Rule, for efficient coagulation of a negatively charged colloid, a cation with high positive charge and low size (high charge density) is needed. FeCl3 provides Fe3+ ions which have high positive charge (+3) and small ionic radius, making it most effective for blood clotting. The ferric ion can neutralize the negative charge on blood colloidal particles, causing rapid coagulation.
