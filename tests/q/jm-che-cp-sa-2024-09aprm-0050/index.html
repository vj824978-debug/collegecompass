0.05M CuSO4 when treated with 0.01M K2Cr2O7 gives green colour solution of Cu2Cr2O7. The [SPM: Semi Permeable Membrane] setup has K2Cr2O7 on Side X and CuSO4 on Side Y separated by SPM. Due to osmosis, which of the following observations are correct? (1) Green colour formation observed on side Y. (2) Green colour formation observed on side X. (3) Molarity of K2Cr2O7 solution is lowered. (4) Molarity of CuSO4 solution is lowered.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Only solvent molecules (water) can pass through the semi-permeable membrane (SPM), not solute molecules. Water moves from the side with lower solute concentration (higher water potential) to the side with higher solute concentration (lower water potential). Side Y has CuSO4 (0.05M) which is more concentrated than Side X with K2Cr2O7 (0.01M). Therefore, water flows from X to Y, causing volume increase on Y and volume decrease on X. This results in: (1) False - Green colour forms on side X when K2Cr2O7 and Cu2+ meet; (2) False - Green colour forms on X, not Y; (3) False - Molarity of K2Cr2O7 increases on X due to water loss; (4) True - Molarity of CuSO4 decreases on Y due to water gain (dilution).
