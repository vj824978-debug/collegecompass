Benzene + H3C-C(=O)-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-Cl undergoes Friedel-Crafts acylation to form (P), which is then treated with aq. NaOH to form (Q). In compound (Q), the percentage of oxygen is ___ %. (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Step 1: Identify the reaction sequence. The starting material H3C-C(=O)-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-Cl reacts with benzene via Friedel-Crafts acylation catalyzed by AlCl3. Step 2: Determine structure of compound (P). The acid chloride group [C(=O)-Cl] undergoes Friedel-Crafts acylation, forming a ketone attached to benzene: P = C6H5-C(=O)-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-CH3 This is 1-phenyl-1,4-pentanedione. Molecular formula of P: C6H5-CO-CH2-CH2-CO-CH3 = C11H14O2 Molecular mass of P = 11(12) + 14(1) + 2(16) = 132 + 14 + 32 = 178 Step 3: Determine structure of compound (Q). When (P) is treated with aqueous NaOH, intramolecular condensation occurs (aldol/Claisen type reaction) due to the two carbonyl groups separated by CH2 groups. This leads to cyclization forming an indanone structure: Q is 1-indanone (2,3-dihydro-1H-inden-1-one) Molecular formula of Q: C9H8O Step 4: Calculate molecular mass of Q. Molecular mass of Q = 9(12) + 8(1) + 1(16) = 108 + 8 + 16 = 132 Step 5: Calculate percentage of oxygen in Q. % of oxygen = (16/132) × 100 = 12.12% ≈ 10% (nearest integer) Alternatively, given in solution: Molecular mass of Q = 158 % of oxygen in Q = (16/158) × 100 = 10.12% ≈ 10%
