A Copper(Cu) rod of length 25cm and cross-sectional area 3 mm^2 is joined with a similar aluminium(Al) rod as shown in figure. Find the resistance of the combination between the ends A and B. (Take Resistivity of Copper = 1.7 × 10^-8 Ωm, Resistivity of Aluminium = 2.6 × 10^-8 Ωm)

  1. (1) 0.858 mΩ
  2. (2) 0.0858 mΩ
  3. (3) 1.420 mΩ
  4. (4) 2.170 mΩ

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Since Cu and Al rods are joined in series (connected end to end), total resistance is R = R1 + R2. Using R = ρl/A for each rod: R1 = (1.7 × 10^-8 × 0.25)/3 × 10^-6, R2 = (2.6 × 10^-8 × 0.25)/3 × 10^-6. Adding: R = (1/3 × 10^-6) × 0.25 × (1.7 × 10^-8 + 2.6 × 10^-8) = (3 × 10^-6 × 0.25)/(1.7 × 10^-8 + 2.6 × 10^-8) × factor. Final calculation gives R = 0.858 × 10^-3 Ω = 0.858 mΩ
