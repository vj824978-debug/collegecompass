A solute A dimerizes in water. The boiling point of a 2 molal solution of A is 100.52°C. The percentage association of A is _____ (Round off to the nearest integer). [Use: Kb for water = 0.52 K kg mol^(-1), Boiling point of water = 100°C]

Answer

Correct answer: 100

Solution

Given: m = 2 molal, ΔTb = 100.52 - 100 = 0.52°C, Kb = 0.52 K kg mol^(-1). Using ΔTb = i × Kb × m, we get 0.52 = i × 0.52 × 2, so i = 0.5. For dimerization where n = 2, the degree of association α is calculated using α = (1-i)/(1-1/n) = (1-0.5)/(1-0.5) = 0.5/0.5 = 1. Therefore, percentage association = 1 × 100 = 100%
