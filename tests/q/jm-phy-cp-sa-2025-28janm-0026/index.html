Two capacitors C1 and C2 are connected in parallel to a battery. Charge-time graph is shown below for the two capacitors. The energy stored with them are U1 and U2, respectively. Which of the given statements is true?

  1. (1) C1 > C2, U1 > U2
  2. (2) C2 > C1, U2 < U1
  3. (3) C1 > C2, U1 < U2
  4. (4) C2 > C1, U2 > U1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For capacitors connected in parallel to a battery: Potential difference V → same for both capacitors Energy stored: U = (1/2)CV^2 From the charge-time graph: - C1 reaches charge q1 at time t - C2 reaches charge q2 at time t - From the graph: q1 < q2 Since both capacitors have the same voltage V: Q = CV Since q1 < q2 and V is same for both: C1·V < C2·V ∴ C1 < C2 For energy stored: U = (1/2)CV^2 Since C2 > C1 and V is the same: U2 = (1/2)C2·V^2 > (1/2)C1·V^2 = U1 ∴ U2 > U1 Therefore: C2 > C1 and U2 > U1
