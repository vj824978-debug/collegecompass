A liquid at 30°C is poured very slowly into a Calorimeter that is at temperature of 110°C. The boiling temperature of the liquid is 80°C. It is found that the first 5 gm of the liquid completely evaporates. After pouring another 80 gm of the liquid the equilibrium temperature is found to be 50°C. The ratio of the Latent heat of the liquid to its specific heat will be ___________°C. [Neglect the heat exchange with surrounding]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 270.00

Solution

Sol. 270.00 5(s)(50) + 5L = C(30) ...(i) 80(s)(20) = C(30) ...(ii) ∴ from (i) and (ii) L/S = 270°C ∴ 270.00
