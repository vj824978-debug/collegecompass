Match the LIST-I with LIST-II LIST-I: Thermodynamic Process A. Work done in reversible, isothermal expansion of 2 mol of ideal gas from 2 dm^3 to 20 dm^3 at 300 K. B. Work done in irreversible isothermal expansion of 1 mol ideal gas from 1 m^3 to 3 m^3 at 300 K against a constant pressure of 3 kPa. C. Change in internal energy for adiabatic expansion of a 1 mol ideal gas with change of temperature = 320 K and C_V = (3/2)R. D. Change in enthalpy at constant pressure of 1 mole ideal gas with change of temperature = 337 K and C_p = (5/2)R. LIST-II: Magnitude in kJ I. 4 II. 11.5 III. 6 IV. 7 Choose the correct answer from the option given below:

  1. (1) (1) A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
  2. (2) (2) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
  3. (3) (3) A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
  4. (4) (4) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Option (A): W = -nRT ln(V_2/V_1) = (-2 × 8.314 × 300 / 1000) × ln(20/2) kJ = -11.5 kJ Option (B): W = -P_ext[V_2 - V_1] = -3[3 - 1] = -6 kJ Option (C): ΔU = nC_VΔT = 1 × (3/2) × (8.314 × 320 / 1000) × 100 kJ = 3.99 ≈ 4 kJ (but magnitude is 6 based on recalculation) Actually: ΔU = 1 × (3/2) × 8.314 × 320 / 1000 = 3.99 kJ ≈ 4 kJ But the answer key shows this matches with III which is 6 kJ. Let me recalculate: ΔU = nC_VΔT = 1 × (3/2) × 8.314 × 320 / 1000 = 3.99 kJ However reviewing the solution provided shows ΔU calculation for different ΔT scenario. Option (D): ΔH = nC_pΔT = 1 × (5/2) × (8.314 × 337 / 1000) kJ = 7 kJ Matching: A matches with II (11.5 kJ) B matches with III (6 kJ) C matches with I (4 kJ) D matches with IV (7 kJ) Therefore: A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
