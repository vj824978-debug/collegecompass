Given below are two statements: Statement I: The temperature of a gas is -73°C. When the gas is heated to 527°C, the root mean square speed of the molecules is doubled. Statement II: The product of pressure and volume of an ideal gas will be equal to translational kinetic energy of the molecules. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Both statement I and statement II are true
  2. (2) Statement I is false but statement II is true
  3. (3) Both statement I and statement II are false
  4. (4) Statement I is true but statement II is false

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

T_i = 200K T_f = 800K V_rms ∝ sqrt(T) V_i/V_f = sqrt(T_i)/sqrt(T_f) = sqrt(200)/sqrt(800) = sqrt(1/4) = 1/2 V_f = 2V_i Statement I is TRUE: When temperature increases from 200K to 800K, the root mean square speed doubles. For Statement II: Translational K.E. = (3/2)PV The product of pressure and volume (PV) is NOT equal to translational kinetic energy. The translational kinetic energy equals (3/2)PV, not PV itself. Statement II is FALSE. Therefore, Statement I is true but statement II is false.
