A compass needle of oscillation magnetometer oscillates 20 times per minute at a place P of dip 30°. The number of oscillations per minute become 10 at another place Q of 60° dip. The ratio of the total magnetic field at the two places (B_Q : B_P) is-

  1. (1) sqrt(3) : 4
  2. (2) 4 : sqrt(3)
  3. (3) sqrt(3) : 2
  4. (4) 2 : sqrt(3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Using T ∝ 1/sqrt(B cos δ), we have T₁/T₂ = sqrt(B₂ cos δ₂)/(B₁ cos δ₁). Given: T₁ = 3s (20 osc/min), T₂ = 6s (10 osc/min), δ₁ = 30°, δ₂ = 60°. Therefore: (3s)/(6s) = sqrt(B₂ × cos 60°)/(B₁ × cos 30°). Simplifying: 1/2 = sqrt(B₂/B₁) × sqrt((1/2)/(sqrt(3)/2)) = sqrt(B₂/B₁) × (1/sqrt(3)). Solving: B₂/B₁ = (1/4) × sqrt(3) = sqrt(3)/4, so B_Q : B_P = sqrt(3) : 4
