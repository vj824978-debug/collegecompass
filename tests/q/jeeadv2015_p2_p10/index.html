Two spheres P and Q of equal radii have densities ρ₁ and ρ₂, respectively. The spheres are connected by a massless string and placed in liquids L₁ and L₂ of densities σ₁ and σ₂ and viscosities η₁ and η₂, respectively. They float in equilibrium with the sphere P in L₁ and sphere Q in L₂ and the string being taut (see figure). [Figure shows sphere P in liquid L₁ above, connected by a string to sphere Q in liquid L₂ below] If sphere P alone in L₂ has terminal velocity V_P, and Q alone in L₁ has terminal velocity V_Q, then

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) (A) |V_P|/|V_Q| = η₁/η₂
  2. (B) (B) |V_P|/|V_Q| = η₂/η₁
  3. (C) (C) V_P · V_Q > 0
  4. (D) (D) V_P · V_Q < 0

Answer

Correct answer: AD

Solution

The solution demonstrates the relationship between velocities of two particles P and Q under given density and viscosity conditions. Using the equilibrium condition that the sum of stresses equals the sum of densities, the terminal velocities are derived. The ratio of absolute velocities is shown to depend on the viscosity ratio η₁/η₂, and the dot product is negative, indicating the velocities are in opposite directions.
