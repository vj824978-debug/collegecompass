Benzyl isocyanide can be obtained by: (A) Benzene with CH2Br followed by AgCN (B) Benzene with CH2NH2 followed by CHCl3 and Aq.KOH (C) Benzene with CH2-NHCH3 followed by CHCl3 and Aq.KOH (D) Benzene with CH2OTs followed by KCN

  1. (1) A and D
  2. (2) Only B
  3. (3) A and B
  4. (4) B and C

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

(A) Benzene-CH2Br + AgCN → Benzene-CH2NC (benzyl isocyanide). AgCN acts as a cyanide source and promotes the formation of isocyanide through nucleophilic substitution. This pathway is correct. (B) Benzene-CH2NH2 + CHCl3 + Aq.KOH → Benzene-CH2N=C (benzyl isocyanide). This is the Hofmann isocyanide synthesis where primary amine reacts with chloroform in basic conditions to form isocyanide. This pathway is correct. (C) Benzene-CH2-NHCH3 + CHCl3 + Aq.KOH → This involves a secondary amine (N-methylamine). The Hofmann isocyanide synthesis requires a primary amine (-NH2). Secondary amines do not readily form isocyanides under these conditions. This pathway is incorrect. (D) Benzene-CH2OTs + KCN → This gives benzyl cyanide (Benzene-CH2-CN), not benzyl isocyanide. KCN forms the nitrile group (-CN), not the isocyanide group (-NC). This pathway is incorrect. Therefore, only pathways A and B produce benzyl isocyanide.
