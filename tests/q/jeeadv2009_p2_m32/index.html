The centres of two circles C₁ and C₂ each of unit radius are at a distance of 6 units from each other. Let P be the mid point of the line segment joining the centres of C₁ and C₂ and C be a circle touching circles C₁ and C₂ externally. If a common tangent to C₁ and C passing through P is also a common tangent to C₂ and C, then the radius of the circle C is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

Sol. 8 cos α = 2√2/3 sin α = 1/3 tan α = 2√2/R ⟹ R = 2√2/tan α = 8 units. [Diagram showing three circles labeled A, B, and C with a triangle formed by their centers. Circle A (radius 2√2) and circle B (radius 2√2) are externally tangent to each other and to circle C (radius R). Angle α is marked at point P between the line connecting centers and a tangent line.] Alternate: (R + 1)² = (R - 1)² + (4√2)² ⟹ R = 8.
