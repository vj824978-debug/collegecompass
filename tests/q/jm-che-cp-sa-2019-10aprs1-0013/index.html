Consider the following statements (a) The pH of a mixture containing 400 mL of 0.1 M H2SO4 and 400 mL of 0.1 M NaOH will be approximately 1.3 (b) Ionic product of water is temperature dependent. (c) A monbasic acid with Ka = 10^(-5) has pH = 5. The degree of dissociation of this acid is 50%. (d) The Le Chatelier's principle is not applicable to common-ion effect. The correct statements are:

  1. (1) (a) and (b)
  2. (2) (a), (b) and (c)
  3. (3) (a), (b) and (d)
  4. (4) (b) and (c)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(a) H2SO4 + 2NaOH → Na2SO4 + 2H2O Initially 40 m mole 40 m mole Finally 20 m mole 0 20 m mole [H+] = (20×10^(-3)×10^3 × 2)/800 [H+] = 1/20 -log[H+] = -log(1/20) pH = log 20 pH = 1.3 approximately So statement (a) is CORRECT. (b) kw depends on temperature kw ↑ with temperature ↑ So statement (b) is CORRECT. (c) pH = 5 ∴ [H+] = α × a = 10^(-5) Ka = (c×alpha^2)/(1-α) ka = ([H+]^2)/(1-α) 10^(-5) = (10^(-5) × alpha)/(1-α) ⇒ I = 2α ∴ α = 0.5 Degree of dissociation is 50%. So statement (c) is CORRECT. (d) Le Chatelier's principle is applicable to common-ion effect because common-ion effect is itself dependent on le chatelier's principle. So statement (d) is INCORRECT. Therefore, statements (a), (b) and (c) are correct.
