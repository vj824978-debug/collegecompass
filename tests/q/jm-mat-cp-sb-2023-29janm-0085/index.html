Let the co-ordinates of one vertex of triangle ABC be A(0, 2, alpha) and the other two vertices lie on the line (x + alpha)/5 = (y - 1)/2 = (z + 4)/3. For alpha in Z, if the area of triangle ABC is 21 sq. units and the line segment BC has length 2*sqrt(21) units, then alpha^2 is equal to ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 09

Solution

Let coordinate of D = (5k - alpha, 2k + 1, 3k - 4) ∴ D.R. of AD = <5k - alpha, 2k - 1, 3k - 4 - alpha> ∴ 5(5k - alpha) + 2(2k - 1) + 3(3k - 4 - alpha) = 0 ∴ 19k - 4alpha - 7 = 0 ...(i) and (1/2) × 2*sqrt(21) × AD = 21 ∴ AD = sqrt(21) ∴ (5k - alpha)^2 + (2k - 1)^2 + (3k - 4 - alpha)^2 = 21 ∴ 19k^2 - 8k*alpha + alpha^2 - 14k + 4*alpha = 2 ...(ii) from eq. (i) and (ii): alpha = 3 ∴ alpha^2 = 9
