Let circle C be the image of x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 4 = 0 in the line 2x - 3y + 5 = 0 and A be the point on C such that OA is parallel to x-axis and A lies on the right hand side of the centre O of C. If B(alpha, beta), with beta < 4, lies on C such that the length of the arc AB is (1/6)^th of the perimeter of C, then beta - sqrt(3)alpha is equal to

  1. (1) 3
  2. (2) 3 + sqrt(3)
  3. (3) 4 - sqrt(3)
  4. (4) 4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Centre (1, -2), r = 3 Reflection of (1, -2) about 2x - 3y + 5 = 0 (x - 1)/2 = (y + 2)/(-3) = -2(2 + 6 + 5)/13 = -2 x = -3, y = 4 Equation of circle 'C' C: (x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 9 A.T.Q B(alpha, beta) AB = 3*sqrt(3) l(arc AB) = (1/6) × 2πr r*theta = (1/6) × 2πr theta = π/3 (alpha + 6)^2 + (beta - 4)^2 = 27 (alpha + 3)^2 + (beta - 4)^2 = 9 (alpha + 6)^2 - (alpha + 3)^2 = 18 6alpha - 9 alpha = -3/2, beta = 4 - 3*sqrt(3)/2 beta - sqrt(3)*alpha = (4 - 3*sqrt(3)/2) + sqrt(3) × 3/2 = 4 - 3*sqrt(3)/2 + 3*sqrt(3)/2 = 4
