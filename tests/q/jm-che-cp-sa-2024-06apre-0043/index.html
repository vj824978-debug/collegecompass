Identify the product (A) in the following reaction: Aniline is treated with (i) NaNO2 + HCl (ii) CuCl2 (iii) NaOH, 623K, 300atm (iv) H+

  1. (1) 4-Aminophenol
  2. (2) Phenol
  3. (3) 4-Chloroaniline
  4. (4) 4-Chlorophenol

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Aniline reacts with NaNO2 + HCl to form benzenediazonium chloride. Treatment with CuCl2 produces chlorobenzene via Sandmeyer reaction. The chlorobenzene is then treated with NaOH at 623K and 300 atm (high temperature and pressure nucleophilic aromatic substitution) to replace Cl with OH, producing phenol as product (A).
