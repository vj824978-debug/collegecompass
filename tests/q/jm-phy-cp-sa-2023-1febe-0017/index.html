As shown in the figure, a long straight conductor with semicircular arc of radius pi/10 m is carrying current I = 3 A. The magnitude of the magnetic field at the center O of the arc is: (The permeability of the vacuum = 4π × 10^(-7) NA^(-2))

  1. (1) 6μT
  2. (2) 1μT
  3. (3) 4μT
  4. (4) 3μT

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Magnetic field at B will be only due to the semicircular arc. So, B = (μ₀i)/(4r) = (4π×10^(-7))×3 / (4×(π/10)) = 3 × 10^(-6) T = 3μT
