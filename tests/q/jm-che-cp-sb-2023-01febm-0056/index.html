The density of 3 solution of NaCl is 1.0 g mL^(-1). Molality of the solution is ____ × 10^(-2) m. (Nearest integer). Given: Molar mass of Na and Cl is 23 and 35.5 g mol^(-1) respectively.

Answer

Correct answer: 364

Solution

m = 1000M / (1000ρ - M.mw) where M = molarity = 3, ρ = density = 1.0 g mL^(-1), mw = molar weight of NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 g mol^(-1) m = (1000 × 3) / (1000 - 3 × 58.5) m = 3000 / (1000 - 175.5) m = 3000 / 824.5 m = 3.638 m = 363.8 × 10^(-2) Nearest integer = 364
