The reaction of NO with N2O4 at 250 K gives:

  1. (1) N2O5
  2. (2) NO2
  3. (3) N2O
  4. (4) N2O3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

2NO + N2O4 --250K--> 2N2O3 (blue solid) At 250 K, NO reacts with dinitrogen tetroxide (N2O4) to produce dinitrogen trioxide (N2O3), which is a blue solid. This can be understood by analyzing the oxidation states: - In NO: N is +2 - In N2O4: N is +4 - In N2O3: N is +3 (average oxidation state) The reaction shows that nitrogen in NO (oxidation state +2) is oxidized to +3, while nitrogen in N2O4 (oxidation state +4) is reduced to +3 in the product N2O3. Therefore, the product is N2O3.
