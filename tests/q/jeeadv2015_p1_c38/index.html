The % yield of ammonia as a function of time in the reaction N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g), ΔH < 0 at (P, T₁) is given below: [Graph showing % yield vs time curve that increases and plateaus at T₁] If this reaction is conducted at (P, T₂), with T₂ > T₁, the % yield of ammonia as a function of time is represented by

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [Graph A shows two curves labeled T₁ and T₂, where T₂ reaches equilibrium faster and higher than T₁, both plateauing but T₂ at a higher yield]
  2. (B) [Graph B shows two curves labeled T₁ (dashed) and T₂ (solid), where T₁ reaches higher equilibrium yield than T₂, both reaching plateaus]
  3. (C) [Graph C shows two curves labeled T₁ (dashed) and T₂ (solid), where T₁ reaches higher equilibrium yield than T₂, with T₂ lower and plateauing later]
  4. (D) [Graph D shows two curves labeled T₂ (solid) and T₁ (dashed), where T₂ reaches higher equilibrium yield than T₁, plateauing at a higher level]

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g); ΔH < 0 Increasing the temperature lowers equilibrium yield of ammonia. However, at higher temperature the initial rate of forward reaction would be greater than at lower temperature that is why the percentage yield of NH₃ too would be more initially.
