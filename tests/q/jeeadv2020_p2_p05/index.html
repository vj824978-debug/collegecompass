A point charge q of mass m is suspended vertically by a string of length l. A point dipole of dipole moment p⃗ is now brought towards q from infinity so that the charge moves away. The final equilibrium position of the system including the direction of the dipole, the angles and distances is shown in the figure below. [Figure shows a string of length l suspended at angle α from vertical, with charge q at distance h horizontally from the vertical line through suspension point, dipole p⃗ positioned at distance 2l sin(α/2) horizontally from q] If the work done in bringing the dipole to this position is N × (mgh), where g is the acceleration due to gravity, then the value of N is _____. (Note that for three coplanar forces keeping a point mass in equilibrium, F/sin θ is the same for all forces, where F is any one of the forces and θ is the angle between the other two forces)

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

[Diagram showing a charged particle suspended by a spring at angle α from vertical, with forces labeled: tension, mg (weight), 2kqp (electric force), and angle measurements] mg/sin(90° + α/2) = 2kqp/r³sin(180° - α) mg sin α/cos(α/2) = 2kqp/r³ U_elec - PE = kqp/r² = mgr sin(α/2) = mgh U = U_grav + U_elec = 2mgh
