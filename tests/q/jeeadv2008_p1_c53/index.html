A gas described by van der Waal's equation

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) behaves similar to an ideal gas in the limit of large molar volumes
  2. (B) behaves similar to an ideal gas in the limit of large pressures
  3. (C) is characterized by van der Waal's coefficients that are dependent on the identify of the gas but are independent of the temperature
  4. (D) has the pressure that is lower than the pressure exerted by the same gas behaving ideally

Answer

Correct answer: ACD

Solution

Sol. (A, C, D) (P + n²a/V²)(V - nb) = nRT At low pressure, when the sample occupies a large volume, the molecules are so far apart for most of the time that the intermolecular forces play no significant role, and the gas behaves virtually perfectly. a and b are characteristic of a gas and are independent of temperature. The term (P + n²a/V²) represents the pressure exerted by an ideal gas while P represents the pressure exerted by a real gas.
