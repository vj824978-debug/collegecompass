A mixture of one mole each of H2, He and O2 each are enclosed in a cylinder of volume V at temperature T. If the partial pressure of H2 is 2 atm, the total pressure of the gases in the cylinder is -

  1. (1) 14 atm
  2. (2) 22 atm
  3. (3) 38 atm
  4. (4) 6 atm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

According to Dalton's law of partial pressure p_i = x_i × P_T where: p_i = partial pressure of the i^th component x_i = mole fraction of the i^th component P_T = total pressure of mixture Given: n_H2 = 1 mole n_He = 1 mole n_O2 = 1 mole Partial pressure of H2 = 2 atm Using Dalton's law: 2 atm = (n_H2)/(n_H2 + n_He + n_O2) × P_T 2 atm = (1)/(1 + 1 + 1) × P_T 2 atm = (1)/(3) × P_T P_T = 2 atm × 3 = 6 atm
