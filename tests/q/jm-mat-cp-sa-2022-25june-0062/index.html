Let a, b ∈ R be such that the equation ax^2 - 2bx + 15 = 0 has a repeated root a. If α and β are the roots of the equation x^2 - 2bx + 21 = 0, then α^2 + β^2 is equal to

  1. (1) 37
  2. (2) 58
  3. (3) 68
  4. (4) 92

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For ax^2 - 2bx + 15 = 0 with repeated root a: b^2 = 15a and a = 15/b. Since α is a root of x^2 - 2bx + 21 = 0: α^2 - 2bα + 21 = 0. From 225/b^2 = 9, we get b^2 = 25. For x^2 - 2bx + 21 = 0, sum of roots α + β = 2b and product αβ = 21. Therefore α^2 + β^2 = (α + β)^2 - 2αβ = (2b)^2 - 2(21) = 4(25) - 42 = 100 - 42 = 58
