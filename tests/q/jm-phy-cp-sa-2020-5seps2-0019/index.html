Two Zener diodes (A and B) having breakdown voltages of 6V and 4V respectively, are connected as shown in the circuit below. The output voltage V0 variation with input voltage linearly increasing with time, is given by - (V_input = 0V at t = 0) (figures are qualitative)

  1. (1) Graph showing V0 rising linearly to 6V then plateauing at 6V, with a dashed line at 4V
  2. (2) Graph showing V0 rising linearly then plateauing at 6V
  3. (3) Graph showing V0 rising linearly then plateauing at 4V
  4. (4) Graph showing V0 rising linearly to 6V then plateauing at 6V, with a dashed line at 4V

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Till input voltage reaches 4V No zener is in breakdown region, So V0 = V_in. Then now when V_in changes between 4V to 6V one zener with 4V will breakdown are P.D. across. This zener will become constant and remaining potential will drop across. Resistance in series with 4V zener. Now current in circuit increases abruptly and source must have an internal resistance due to which. Some potential will get drop across the source also so correct graph between V0 and t will be. We have to Assume some resistance in series with source.
