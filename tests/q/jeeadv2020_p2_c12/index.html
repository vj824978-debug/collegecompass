Consider the following transformations of a compound P. R (Optically active) --[(i) NaNH2, (ii) C6H5COCH3, (iii) H3O+ / Δ]--> P (C9H12) --[Pt / H2]--> [structure: cyclohexane ring with propyl side chain CH3] P (C9H12) --[(i) X (reagent), (ii) KMnO4 / H2SO4 / Δ]--> Q (C6H12O6) (Optically active acid) Choose the correct option(s).

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) P is [structure: cyclohexane ring with terminal alkyne side chain]
  2. (B) X is Pd-C/quinoline/H2
  3. (C) P is [structure: cyclohexane ring with propargyl side chain (terminal alkyne on propyl)]
  4. (D) R is [structure: cyclohexane ring with phenyl-substituted alkyne side chain]

Answer

Correct answer: BC

Solution

On the basis of the given conditions, correct structure of (P) should be: [cyclohexane ring]-CH₂-C≡C-H as explained below: [Reaction scheme showing:] (P) (C₈H₁₂) with NaH converts the terminal alkyne to cyclohexyl-CH₂-C≡C⁻Na⁺ This reacts with CH₃I and the anion attacks a benzene ring with phenyl group, followed by H₃O⁺ workup to give intermediate Then treatment with H⁺ causes elimination to give (R) (optically active): cyclohexyl-CH₂-C≡C-CH(OH)-Ph-Me After reduction with H₂, Pd-C quinoline (X), the compound becomes cyclohexyl-CH₂-CH=CH₂ Oxidative cleavage with KMnO₄, H⁺ and Δ gives (Q) (C₆H₈O₆) (optically active): cyclohexane with substituents including COOH groups [The structure shows terminal alkyne chemistry and asymmetric synthesis]
