The fundamental frequency of vibration of a string between two rigid support is 50 Hz. The mass of the string is 18 g and its linear mass density is 20 g/m. The speed of the transverse waves so produced in the string is ______ ms^(-1).

Answer

Correct answer: 90

Solution

Given: fundamental frequency f = 50 Hz, mass m = 18 g, linear mass density μ = 20 g/m. The length of string l = m/μ = 18/20 = 0.9 m. For fundamental frequency: f = v/(2l), so v = 2fl = 2 × 50 × 0.9 = 90 m/s.
