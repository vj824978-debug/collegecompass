Let ABCD be a quadrilateral with area 18, with side AB parallel to the side CD and AB = 2CD. Let AD be perpendicular to AB and CD. If a circle is drawn inside the quadrilateral ABCD touching all the sides, then its radius is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 3
  2. (B) 2
  3. (C) 3/2
  4. (D) 1

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol. (B) 18 = ½(3α)(2r) ⇒ αr = 6 Line y = -2r/α(x - 2α) is tangent to (x - r)² + (y - r)² = r² 2α = 3r and αr = 6 r = 2. Alternate ½(x + 2x) × 2r = 18 xr = 6 ...(1) tan θ = (x - r)/r tan(90 - θ) = (2x - r)/r (x - r)/r = r/(2x - r) x(2x - 3r) = 0 x = 3r/2 ...(2) From (1) and (2) r = 2.
