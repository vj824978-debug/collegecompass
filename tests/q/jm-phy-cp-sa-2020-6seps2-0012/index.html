A student measuring the diameter of a pencil of circular cross-section with the help of a vernier scale records the following four readings 5.50 mm, 5.55 mm, 5.45 mm; 5.65 mm. The average of these four readings is 5.5375 mm and the standard deviation of the data is 0.07395 mm. The average diameter of the pencil should therefore be recorded as:

  1. (1) (5.5375 ± 0.0739) mm
  2. (2) (5.538 ± 0.074) mm
  3. (3) (5.54 ± 0.07) mm
  4. (4) (5.5375 ± 0.0740) mm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Use significant figures. Answer must be upto three significant figures. Given: - Average diameter = 5.5375 mm - Standard deviation = 0.07395 mm The answer must be expressed with upto three significant figures. For the average value: 5.5375 mm rounded to three significant figures = 5.54 mm For the standard deviation: 0.07395 mm should be rounded to match the significant figures of the mean. Since we are expressing the mean to three significant figures (5.54), the uncertainty should also be limited accordingly. 0.07395 mm rounded to two significant figures (or one decimal place in this context) = 0.07 mm Therefore, the result should be recorded as (5.54 ± 0.07) mm Ans. (3)
