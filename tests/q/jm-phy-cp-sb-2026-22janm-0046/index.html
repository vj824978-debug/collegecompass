Two loudspeakers (L1 and L2) are placed with a separation of 10 m, as shown in figure. Both speakers are fed with an audio input signal of same frequency with constant volume. A voice recorder, initially at point A, at equidistance to both loud speakers, is moved by 25 m along the line AB while monitoring the audio signal. The measured signal was found to undergo 10 cycles of minima and maxima during the movement. The frequency of the input signal is ____ Hz. (Speed of sound in air is 324 m/s and sqrt(5) = 2.23)

Answer

Correct answer: 600

Solution

From the geometry of the setup: L1 is 10 m away horizontally from the line AB. Point A is at equidistance from both speakers, located 40 m from L1 horizontally. B is 25 m above A. Distance from L1 to point B: L1B = sqrt(20^2 + 40^2) = sqrt(400 + 1600) = sqrt(2000) = 20*sqrt(5) = 44.6 m Distance from L2 to point B: L2B = sqrt(40^2 + 30^2) = sqrt(1600 + 900) = sqrt(2500) = 50 m Path difference at point B: Δx = L2B - L1B = 50 - 44.6 = 5.4 m Path difference at point A (equidistant from both speakers): Δx = 0 m Total change in path difference as the recorder moves from A to B: Δx = 5.4 m The signal undergoes 10 cycles of minima and maxima during this movement. 10 cycles means the path difference changes by: Δx = 10 * λ 5.4 = 10 * λ λ = 0.54 m Using the wave equation: v = f * λ 324 = f * 0.54 f = 324/0.54 = 600 Hz
