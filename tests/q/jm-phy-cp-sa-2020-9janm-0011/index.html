The aperture diameter of a telescope is 5 m. The separation between the moon and the earth is 4 × 10^5 km. With light of wavelength of 5500 Å, the minimum separation between objects on the surface of moon, so that they are just resolved, is close to-

  1. (1) 20 m
  2. (2) 200 m
  3. (3) 60 m
  4. (4) 600 m

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

θ = 1.22 λ/a d_min = θD = 1.22 λ D / a λ = 5500 × 10^(-10) m D = 4 × 10^8 m a = 5 m d_min = (1.22 × 5500 × 10^(-10) × 4 × 10^8) / 5 = 53.6 m Correct answer = 60 m
