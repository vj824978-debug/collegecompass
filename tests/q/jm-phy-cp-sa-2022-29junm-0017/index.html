A parallel plate capacitor filled with a medium of dielectric constant 10, is connected across a battery and is charged. The dielectric slab is replaced by another slab of dielectric constant 15. Then the energy of capacitor will -

  1. (1) increased by 50%
  2. (2) decrease by 15%
  3. (3) increase by 25%
  4. (4) increase by 33%

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

U = (1/2)(kC0)V^2. Since the capacitor remains connected to the battery, voltage V remains constant. When dielectric constant changes from k=10 to k=15, the energy becomes U' = (1/2)(15*C0)V^2. Therefore U'/U = 15/10 = 1.5, which means energy increases by 50%.
