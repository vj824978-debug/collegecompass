A small spherical droplet of density d is floating exactly half immersed in a liquid of density ρ and surface tension T. The radius of the droplet is (take note that the surface tension applies an upward force on the droplet)

  1. (1) r = sqrt(T/((d-ρ)g))
  2. (2) r = sqrt(3T/((2d-ρ)g))
  3. (3) r = sqrt(T/((d+ρ)g))
  4. (4) r = sqrt(2T/(3(d+ρ)g))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

For a spherical droplet floating exactly half immersed in a liquid: Forces acting on the droplet: 1. Weight of droplet: W = (4/3)πR³dg (downward) 2. Buoyant force: F_b = (2/3)πR³ρg (upward, from displaced liquid) 3. Surface tension force: F_s = 2πR·T (upward, acts along the contact line) At equilibrium (half immersed): T·2πR + (2/3)πR³ρg = (4/3)πR³dg Simplifying: T·2πR = (4/3)πR³dg - (2/3)πR³ρg T·2πR = (2/3)πR³(2d - ρ)g Dividing both sides by πR: 2T = (2/3)R²(2d - ρ)g Solving for R²: R² = 3T/(g(2d - ρ)) Therefore: R = sqrt(3T/(g(2d - ρ)))
