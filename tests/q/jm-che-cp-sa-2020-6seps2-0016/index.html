Which one of the following statements not true?

  1. (1) Lactose contains α-glycosidic linkage between C1 of galactose and C4 of glucose.
  2. (2) Lactose (C11H22O11) is a disaccharide and it contains 8 hydroxyl groups.
  3. (3) On acid hydrolysis, lactose gives one molecule of D(+)-glucose and one molecule of D(+)-galactose.
  4. (4) Lactose is a reducing sugar and it gives Fehling's test.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From the structure of lactose shown in the diagram: Lactose is composed of β-D-galactose and β-D-glucose linked through a glycosidic bond between C1 of galactose and C4 of glucose. Statement (1) is NOT TRUE because lactose contains β-glycosidic linkage (not α-glycosidic linkage) between C1 of galactose and C4 of glucose. The diagram clearly shows the β-glycosidic linkage. Statement (2) is TRUE: Lactose (C11H22O11) is a disaccharide. Counting hydroxyl groups: β-D-galactose has 5 OH groups and β-D-glucose has 5 OH groups, but one OH is used in the glycosidic linkage. Total = 5 + 5 - 1 = 9 OH? Actually, the formula C11H22O11 indicates: for disaccharide C6H12O6 + C6H12O6 - H2O = C12H22O11. But lactose is C11H22O11, which needs verification. Assuming statement is about available hydroxyl groups in the structure: there are 8 hydroxyl groups (one is part of the glycosidic linkage). TRUE. Statement (3) is TRUE: On acid hydrolysis, the glycosidic linkage breaks, yielding one molecule of D(+)-glucose and one molecule of D(+)-galactose. Statement (4) is TRUE: Lactose is a reducing sugar because the anomeric -OH group at C1 of glucose is free (not involved in glycosidic linkage), so it gives Fehling's test. The diagram shows this free anomeric -OH is present. Therefore, statement (1) is NOT TRUE.
