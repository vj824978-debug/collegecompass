Two identical discs of same radius R are rotating about their axes in opposite directions with the same constant angular speed ω. The disc are in the same horizontal plane. At time t = 0, the points P and Q are facing each other as shown in the figure. The relative speed between the two points P and Q is v_r as function of times best represented by [Figure shows two identical discs rotating in opposite directions with angular speed ω. Points P and Q are on the edges of their respective discs, initially facing each other horizontally. Below are four graphs (A, B, C, D) showing v_r vs t, each with a period T marked.]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [Graph A shows v_r starting at 0, rising to a peak, returning to 0, with this pattern repeating twice before time T, with a dashed vertical line at T]
  2. (B) [Graph B shows v_r starting at 0, rising to a peak, returning to 0, with this pattern repeating twice before time T, appearing similar to A with a dashed vertical line at T]
  3. (C) [Graph C shows v_r with more frequent oscillations (approximately 4 peaks) before time T, with a dashed vertical line at T]
  4. (D) [Graph D shows v_r with more frequent oscillations (approximately 4 peaks) before time T, with a dashed vertical line at T]

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

[Two circular diagrams showing velocity vector decomposition with angle θ] Left diagram: Vector v at angle θ below horizontal, decomposed into v sinθ (horizontal component) and v cosθ (vertical component) Right diagram: Vector v at angle θ, decomposed into v sinθ (horizontal component) and v cosθ (vertical component) Vr = |2v sin θ| = |2v sin ωt| [Graph showing Vr on vertical axis and t on horizontal axis, displaying two semi-circular humps, with period T marked at T/2 and T]
