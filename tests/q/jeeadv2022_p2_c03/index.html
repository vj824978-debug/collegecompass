Consider the strong electrolytes Z_m X_n, U_m Y_p, and V_n X_n. Limiting molar conductivity (Λ⁰) of U_m Y_p and V_n X_n are 250 and 440 S cm² mol⁻¹, respectively. The value of (m + n + p) is_________. Given: [Table with Ion row showing: Z^n+, U^p+, V^n+, X^m-, Y^m-] [Table with Λ⁰ (S cm² mol⁻¹) row showing: 50.0, 25.0, 100.0, 80.0, 100.0] Λ⁰ is the limiting molar conductivity of ions. The plot of molar conductivity (Λ) of Z_m X_n vs c^{1/2} is given below. [Graph showing Λ of Z_m X_n (S cm² mol⁻¹) on y-axis ranging from 336 to 339, and c^{1/2} (mol L⁻¹)^{1/2} on x-axis ranging from 0 to 0.04. A downward sloping line is shown with y-intercept at approximately 339 and passing through points around (0.01, 339), (0.02, 337), (0.03, 336.5), and continuing to approximately (0.04, 336).]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 7

Solution

Let λₘ⁰ the molar conductance by ZₘXₙ From the graph 339 = λₘ⁰ - b√c 339 = λₘ⁰ - b × 0.01 ...(1) 336 = λₘ⁰ - b × 0.04 ...(2) On solving, we get, λₘ⁰ = 340 Now, 25m + 100p = 250 ...(3) 100m + 80n = 440 ...(4) 50m + 80n = 340 ...(5) On solving (3), (4) and (5) We get, m = 2, n = 3, p = 2
