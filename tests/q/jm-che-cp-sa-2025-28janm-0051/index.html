The incorrect decreasing order of atomic radii is:

  1. (1) Mg > Al > C > O
  2. (2) Al > B > N > F
  3. (3) Be > Mg > Al > Si
  4. (4) Si > P > Cl > F

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Correct order of atomic radii: Be > Mg > Al > Si To determine which option shows an INCORRECT decreasing order, we need to recall that atomic radius decreases across a period (left to right) and increases down a group. Let's check each option: (1) Mg > Al > C > O Mg and Al are in the same period (period 3), Mg is to the left so Mg > Al ✓ C and O are in the same period (period 2), C is to the left so C > O ✓ Mg is in period 3, C is in period 2. Period 3 elements are larger than period 2, so Mg > C ✓ Therefore: Mg > Al > C > O is CORRECT (2) Al > B > N > F Al is in period 3, B, N, F are in period 2 Al > B ✓ (different periods) B > N > F ✓ (same period, left to right) Therefore: Al > B > N > F is CORRECT (3) Be > Mg > Al > Si Be is in period 2, Mg in period 3, Al in period 3, Si in period 3 Be is in group 2, Mg is below Be in group 2, so Be > Mg ✓ But Mg, Al, Si are all in period 3 In period 3: Mg > Al > Si (left to right) ✓ However, we need to verify: Be is very small (period 2) but actually atomic radius of Be ≈ 112 pm, Mg ≈ 160 pm So Be should NOT be greater than Mg. This order is INCORRECT. (4) Si > P > Cl > F All in the same period or comparing across periods correctly Si > P > Cl > F ✓ (same period, left to right) Therefore: Si > P > Cl > F is CORRECT The answer is option (3) because the correct order should be Mg > Be > Al > Si or Be > Mg > Al > Si depending on exact values, but Be is smaller than Mg, making Be > Mg > Al > Si incorrect.
