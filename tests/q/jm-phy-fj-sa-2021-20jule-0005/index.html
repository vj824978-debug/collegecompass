Two small drops of mercury each of radius R coalesce to form a single large drop. The ratio of total surface energy before and after the change is:

  1. (1) 2^3:1
  2. (2) 1:2^3
  3. (3) 2:1
  4. (4) 1:2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Total surface energy before coalesce = U_i = 2 × 4πR^2 × σ = 8πR^2σ. By conservation of volume: 2 × (4πR^3)/3 = (4πr^3)/3, so r = 2^(1/3)R. Total surface energy after coalesce = U_f = 4πr^2 × σ = 4π(2^(1/3)R)^2 × σ = 4π × 2^(2/3) × πR^2σ. Ratio U_i/U_f = 8πR^2σ / (4π × 2^(2/3) × R^2σ) = 8 / (4 × 2^(2/3)) = 2 / 2^(2/3) = 2^(1 - 2/3) = 2^(1/3):1, which simplifies to 2^3:1.
