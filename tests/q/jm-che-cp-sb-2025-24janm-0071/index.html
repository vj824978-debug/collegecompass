37.8 g N2O5 was taken in a 1 L reaction vessel and allowed to undergo the following reaction at 500 K: 2N2O5(s) → 2N2O4(g) + O2(g). The total pressure at equilibrium was found to be 18.65 bar. Then, Kp = _____ × 10^(-2) [nearest integer]. Assume N2O5 to behave ideally under these conditions. Given: R = 0.082 bar L mol^(-1) K^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: 962

Solution

Initial pressure of N2O5: P = (n/V) × R × T = (37.8/108) × 0.082 × 500 = 14.35 bar For the reaction: 2N2O5 ⇌ 2N2O4 + O2 At t = 0: 14.35 0 0 At t = eq: 14.35 - 2P 2P P P_Total at equilibrium = 14.35 + P = 18.65 P = 4.3 bar Equilibrium pressures: P_N2O5 = 14.35 - 2(4.3) = 5.75 bar P_N2O4 = 2(4.3) = 8.6 bar P_O2 = 4.3 bar Kp = (P_N2O4)^2 × P_O2 / (P_N2O5)^2 Kp = (8.6)^2 × (4.3) / (5.75)^2 Kp = 73.96 × 4.3 / 33.0625 Kp = 317.828 / 33.0625 Kp = 9.619 = 9.62 × 10^(-2) In the form ___ × 10^(-2): Kp = 961.9 ≈ 962 × 10^(-2) Therefore x = 962
