Cyclohexylamine when treated with nitrous acid yields (P). On treating (P) with PCC results in (Q). When (Q) is heated with dil. NaOH we get (R). The final product (R) is

  1. (1) A ketone with cyclohexyl substituent
  2. (2) Two cyclohexanone units connected by a CH2 group
  3. (3) A cyclohexanone with a cyclohexyl group attached
  4. (4) A hydroxyl compound with cyclohexyl group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Starting material: Cyclohexylamine (C6H11-NH2) Step 1: Treatment with HNO2 (nitrous acid) - Cyclohexylamine undergoes diazotization followed by loss of N2 - This gives cyclohexanol (P): C6H11-OH Step 2: Treatment with PCC (Pyridinium Chlorochromate) - PCC oxidizes primary and secondary alcohols to carbonyl compounds - Cyclohexanol (P) is oxidized to cyclohexanone (Q): C6H10=O Step 3: Heating with dilute NaOH - Cyclohexanone (Q) undergoes aldol condensation under basic conditions - Two molecules of cyclohexanone condense with loss of water - This forms a C-C bond between the alpha-carbon of one molecule and the carbonyl carbon of another - The product (R) is a compound with cyclohexanone unit attached to another cyclohexyl group through a double bond (enone product after dehydration) - Looking at option (3): It shows a cyclohexanone with a cyclohexyl group attached, which matches the condensation product Therefore, the final product (R) is option (3).
