Two identical capacitors A and B, charged to the same potential 5V are connected in two different circuits as shown below at time t = 0. If the charge on capacitors A and B at time t = CR is Q_A and Q_B respectively, then (Here e is the base of natural logarithm)

  1. (1) Q_A = VC, Q_B = VC/e
  2. (2) Q_A = CV/2, Q_B = CV/e
  3. (3) Q_A = VC, Q_B = CV
  4. (4) Q_A = VC/e, Q_B = CV/2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For Circuit 1 (with diode): The diode prevents the capacitor from discharging. Once charged to 5V, the capacitor A cannot discharge through the resistor. Therefore, the charge remains constant. At t = CR: Q_A = CV (initial charge, no discharge) For Circuit 2 (with capacitor only): The capacitor B discharges through the resistor R following the RC discharge equation. For discharging: Q(t) = Q_0 * e^(-t/RC) At t = CR: Q_B = Q_0 * e^(-CR/CR) = Q_0 * e^(-1) = (CV)/e Therefore: Q_A = VC Q_B = VC/e
