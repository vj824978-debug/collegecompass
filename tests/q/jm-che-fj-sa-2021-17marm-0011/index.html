Product 'A' in the above chemical reaction is: A cyclohexene with a methyl substituent reacts with HBr in CCl4 to form major product A

  1. (1) A cyclohexane ring with a methyl group and bromine attached to the double bond carbon
  2. (2) A cyclohexane ring with two methyl groups and bromine attached
  3. (3) A cyclohexane ring with a methyl group and bromine in geminal positions
  4. (4) A cyclohexane ring with two methyl groups and bromine in different positions

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The reaction follows Markovnikov's rule with carbocation rearrangement. The alkene reacts with HBr in CCl4. Initial protonation of the alkene forms a carbocation. A methyl shift (CH3 shifting) occurs to form a more stable tertiary carbocation. Bromide then attacks from the opposite side. The major product shows the bromine adding to the position that results from carbocation rearrangement, with the final structure having the bromide and methyls arranged to give the most stable product via 1,2-methyl shift mechanism.
