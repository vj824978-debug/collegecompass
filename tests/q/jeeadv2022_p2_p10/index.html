In Circuit-1 and Circuit-2 shown in the figures, R₁ = 1 Ω, R₂ = 2 Ω and R₃ = 3 Ω. P₁ and P₂ are the power dissipations in Circuit-1 and Circuit-2 when the switches S₁ and S₂ are in open conditions, respectively. and Q₂ are the power dissipations in Circuit-1 and Circuit-2 when the switches S₁ and S₂ are in closed conditions, respectively. [Circuit-1 shows R₁, R₂, R₃ in series at top, with S₁ switch in parallel with R₁/2 resistor below, points A and B at bottom] [Circuit-2 shows R₁, R₂, R₃ in series vertically with S₂ switch in parallel with 2R₃ resistor, points A and B at bottom] Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) When a voltage source of 6 V is connected across A and B in both circuits, P₁ < P₂.
  2. (B) When a constant current source of 2 Amp is connected across A and B in both circuits, P₁>P₂.
  3. (C) When a voltage source of 6 V is connected across A and B in Circuit-1, Q₁>P₁.
  4. (D) When a constant current source of 2 Amp is connected across A and B in both circuits, Q₂<Q₁.

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution

A, B, C [First set of circuits showing three configurations with resistors] (1) Circuit with 1Ω, 2Ω, 3Ω resistors in series and parallel arrangement with 0.5Ω resistor. Req = 16/11 Ω (2) Circuit with 1Ω, 2Ω, 3Ω resistors in parallel arrangement. Req = 16/11 Ω If 6v voltage source connected to both circuits: P ∝ 1/R, voltage constant R₁ > R₂, P₁ < P₂ If 2 Amp current source connected: P ∝ R P₁ > P₂ [Second set of circuits showing modified configurations] (1) Circuit with 2Ω, 3Ω resistors in series and parallel with 0.5Ω. Req = 5/11 Ω (2) Circuit with 1Ω, 2Ω, 3Ω, 6Ω resistors in parallel. Req = 1/2 Ω P₁ = 6²/(16/11) = 36 × 11/16 Q₁ = 6²/(5/11) = 36 × 11/5 > P₁ P = i²R, R₂ > R₁ ∴ Q₂ > Q₁
