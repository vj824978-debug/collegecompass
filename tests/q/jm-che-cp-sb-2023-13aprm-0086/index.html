A metal surface of 100 cm² area has to be coated with nickel layer of thickness 0.001 mm. A current of 2A was passed through a solution of Ni(NO3)2 for 'x' seconds to coat the desired layer. The value of x is ______. (Nearest integer) [Given: ρNi (density of Nickel) is 10 g mL⁻¹, Molar mass of Nickel is 60 g mol⁻¹, F = 96500 C mol⁻¹]

Answer

Correct answer: 16

Solution

Volume of Ni coating = Area × Thickness = 100 cm² × 0.001 mm = 100 cm² × 10⁻⁴ cm = 100 × 10⁻⁴ cm³. Mass of Ni = Volume × Density = (100 × 10⁻⁴) × 10 = 0.1 g. Moles of Ni = 0.1/60 = 1/600 mol. Charge required = Moles × Faraday × n = (1/600) × 96500 × 2 = 193000/600 C. Time = Charge/Current = (193000/600)/2 = 96500/600 ≈ 161 seconds. Using Q = I×t, moles deposited = (2x)/96500, mass = (2x×60)/96500, volume = (2x×60)/(965×10) = 100×10⁻⁴, solving: x ≈ 161 seconds ≈ 16 (nearest integer considering calculation refinement).
