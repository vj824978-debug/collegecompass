Let PQ be a chord of the parabola y^2 = 12x and the midpoint of PQ be at (4, 1). Then, which of the following point lies on the line passing through the points P and Q?

  1. (1) (3, -3)
  2. (2) (3/2, -16)
  3. (3) (2, -9)
  4. (4) (1/2, -20)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Since P and Q lie on y^2 = 12x, let P = (y1^2/12, y1) and Q = (y2^2/12, y2). The midpoint is ((y1^2 + y2^2)/24, (y1 + y2)/2) = (4, 1). So y1 + y2 = 2. The equation of chord PQ is given by T = S1. Using the property that the slope of chord with midpoint (4,1) on y^2 = 12x: The chord equation is y - 1 = m(x - 4) where m = 6/(y1 + y2) = 6/2 = 3. So y - 1 = 3(x - 4), which gives 6x - y = 23. Checking option 4: 6(1/2) - (-20) = 3 + 20 = 23. ✓
