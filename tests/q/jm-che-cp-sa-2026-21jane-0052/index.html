Match List-I with List-II.

  1. (1) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
  2. (2) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
  3. (3) A-I, B-IV, C-III, D-II
  4. (4) A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(A) 2-Methylpropene and but-1-ene 2-Methylpropene: (CH3)2C=CH2 but-1-ene: CH2=CH-CH2-CH3 These have the same molecular formula C4H8 but different carbon chain arrangements. (III) Chain isomers (B) Cis-but-2-ene and trans-but-2-ene Both have the same molecular formula C4H8 and same structural arrangement, but differ in spatial orientation around the double bond. (I) Stereoisomers (C) 2-Butanol and diethyl ether 2-Butanol: CH3-CH(OH)-CH2-CH3 (alcohol, functional group -OH) Diethyl ether: CH3-CH2-O-CH2-CH3 (ether, functional group -O-) Both have molecular formula C4H10O but different functional groups. (II) Functional group isomers (D) But-1-ene and but-2-ene But-1-ene: CH2=CH-CH2-CH3 (double bond at position 1) but-2-ene: CH3-CH=CH-CH3 (double bond at position 2) Both have same molecular formula C4H8 and same functional group, but double bond at different positions. (IV) Position isomers Therefore: A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
