Consider the following sequence of reactions. Starting material: O-CH2-CH3 with NH2 attached to benzene ring. Reaction (i): NaNO2, HCl at 0-5°C gives (A) with N≡Cl on benzene ring and O-CH2-CH3. Reaction (ii): (i) OH and NaOH, (ii) HCl dil. gives (B) with molecular formula C14H14N2O2. Further reaction of (B): (i) NaOH, (ii) H3CCH2Br gives (C) with molecular formula C16H18N2O2. Total number of sp3 hybridised carbon atoms in the major product C formed is _______.

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

Step 1: Identify the starting material structure. Starting material has: benzene ring with NH2 group and O-CH2-CH3 (ethoxy) group in para positions. Step 2: Formation of (A) - Diazotization. When NH2 reacts with NaNO2 and HCl at 0-5°C, it forms diazonium salt (N≡Cl). Structure (A): Benzene ring with N≡Cl and O-CH2-CH3 groups. Step 3: Formation of (B) - Coupling reaction. The diazonium salt (A) undergoes coupling with phenol in alkaline medium (NaOH). This is an azo coupling reaction forming an azo dye. Structure (B): Two benzene rings connected by N=N bond. One ring has OH group, the other has O-CH2-CH3 group. Molecular formula C14H14N2O2 confirms this structure: 2 benzene rings (C12H8) + N=N + O-CH2-CH3 + OH = C14H14N2O2. Step 4: Formation of (C) - Williamson ether synthesis. Both OH groups in (B) react: (i) NaOH converts OH to O(-) (ii) H3CCH2Br (ethyl bromide) performs Williamson ether synthesis Both OH groups are converted to O-CH2-CH3 groups. Structure (C): Two benzene rings connected by N=N bond, with both rings bearing O-CH2-CH3 groups. Molecular formula: C16H18N2O2 (two additional ethoxy groups added: +C2H4O each) Step 5: Count sp3 hybridised carbons in (C). In structure (C): - Benzene ring carbons: all sp2 hybridized (8 sp2 carbons from 2 rings) - N=N: sp2 hybridized (nitrogen) - O-CH2-CH3 groups (two of them): * First O-CH2-CH3: CH2 is sp3 (1 carbon), CH3 is sp3 (1 carbon) = 2 sp3 carbons * Second O-CH2-CH3: CH2 is sp3 (1 carbon), CH3 is sp3 (1 carbon) = 2 sp3 carbons Total sp3 hybridised carbons = 2 + 2 = 4
