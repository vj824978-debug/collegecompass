A copper complex crystallizing in a CCP lattice with a cell edge of 0.4518 nm has been revealed by employing X-ray diffraction studies. The density of a copper complex is found to be 7.26gcm^(-3). The molar mass of copper complex is ____ g mol^(-1). (Nearest integer) [Given: N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^(-1)]

Answer

Correct answer: 106

Solution

Using the formula ρ = (Z × A)/(N_A × a^3), where Z = 4 for CCP lattice, a = 0.4518 nm = 0.4518×10^(-7) cm, and ρ = 7.62 gcm^(-3). Rearranging: A = (ρ × N_A × a^3)/Z = (7.62 × 6.022×10^23 × (0.4518)^3 × 10^(-21))/4 = 105.79 g/mol ≈ 106 g/mol
