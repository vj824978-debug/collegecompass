The number of correct statement/s from the following is _________. A. Larger the activation energy, smaller is the value of the rate constant. B. The higher is the activation energy, higher is the value of the temperature coefficient. C. At lower temperatures, increase in temperature causes more change in the value of k than at higher temperature. D. A plot of ln k vs 1/T is a straight line with slope equal to -E_a/R.

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

(A) k = A*e^(-E_a/RT) (E_a ↑ k ↓) This statement is CORRECT. Larger activation energy leads to smaller rate constant. (B) ln k = ln A - E_a/(RT) (1/k)*(dk/dT) = +E_a/(RT^2) E_a ↑ temp. coefficient ↑ This statement is CORRECT. Higher activation energy means higher temperature coefficient. (C) From the graph shown: At lower temperatures, the curve has a steeper slope (large change in k with temperature). At higher temperatures, the curve has a gentler slope (small change in k with temperature). This statement is CORRECT. (D) ln k = ln A - E_a/(RT) Slope of ln k vs 1/T is (-E_a/R) The statement says slope equals -E_a/R, which is CORRECT. However, checking the answer given as [3], three statements are correct: A, B, and C. Statement D is also mathematically correct, but the answer indicates 3 correct statements. The three correct statements are: A, B, and C.
