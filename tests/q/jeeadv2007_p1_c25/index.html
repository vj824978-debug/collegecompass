When 20 g of naphthoic acid (C₁₁H₁₀O₂) is dissolved in 50 g of benzene (Kf = 1.72 K kg mol⁻¹), a freezing point depression of 2 K is observed. The van't Hoff factor (i) is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 0.5
  2. (B) 1
  3. (C) 2
  4. (D) 3

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

ΔTf = Kf × molality × i 2 = 1.72 × (20/172) × (1000/50) × i i = 0.5 Hence (A) is correct.
