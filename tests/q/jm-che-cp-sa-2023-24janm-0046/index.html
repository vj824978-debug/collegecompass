Compound (X) undergoes following sequence of reactions to give the Lactone (Y). (i) HCHO, KOH (ii) KCN(alc) (iii) H2O. Compound (X) is

  1. (1) H-C(CH3)2-CHO
  2. (2) H2C=CH-CH2-CH2-CHO with OH group on second carbon
  3. (3) H2C=CH-CH2-CHO with CH3 group
  4. (4) HOH2C-C(CH3)2-CHO

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The compound X undergoes the following reaction sequence: Step 1: Aldol Condensation with HCHO, KOH H-C(CH3)2-CHO + HCHO → CH3-C(CH3)-CHO with O^- forming (oxyanion intermediate) Step 2: Cannizzaro-type reaction with HCHO gives addition product CH3-C(CH3)-CHO + HCHO → CH3-C(CH3)-CH2OH-CHO (after protonation) Step 3: Cyanide Addition (KCN in alcoholic medium) The aldehyde group reacts with KCN to form cyanohydrin: CH3-C(CH3)-CH2OH-CHO + KCN → CH3-C(CH3)-CH2-CH(OH)CN Step 4: Hydrolysis (H2O) The cyanohydrin and carboxylic acid formation occurs: The nitrile (CN) is hydrolyzed to carboxylic acid CH3-C(CH3)-CH2-CH(OH)CN + H2O → CH3-C(CH3)-CH2-CH(OH)COOH (after H+) Step 5: Lactonization Under acidic conditions (H+), intramolecular esterification occurs between the OH group and COOH group to form the lactone ring: CH3-C(CH3)-CH2-CH(OH)COOH → Lactone (Y) with a 5-membered ring containing oxygen, and the structure matches the given lactone Y with OH and CH3 groups on the ring. Therefore, Compound (X) is H-C(CH3)2-CHO (option 1)
