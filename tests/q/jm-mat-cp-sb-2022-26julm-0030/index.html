Let Q and R be two points on the line (x+1)/2 = (y+2)/3 = (z-1)/2 at a distance sqrt(26) from the point P(4, 2, 7). Then the square of the area of the triangle PQR is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 153

Solution

Parametric form of line: T(2t-1, 3t-2, 2t+1). Since PT perpendicular to QR, find t using dot product = 0: 2(2t-5) + 3(3t-4) + 2(2t-6) = 0 gives 17t = 34, so t = 2 and T(3, 4, 5). PT = sqrt(1+4+4) = 3. Distance PQ = sqrt(26), so QT = sqrt(26-9) = sqrt(17). Area of triangle PQR = (1/2) × base × height = (1/2) × 2sqrt(17) × 3 = 3sqrt(17). Square of area = 9 × 17 = 153.
