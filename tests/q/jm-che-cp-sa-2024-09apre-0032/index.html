The correct stability order of the following resonance structures of CH3-CH=CH-CHO is: Structure I: CH3-CH=CH-C(=O+)H with :O:- below Structure II: CH3-CH=CH-C(=O:-)H with :O:+ above Structure III: CH3-CH=CH-C(=O:)H

  1. (1) II > III > I
  2. (2) III > II > I
  3. (3) I > II > III
  4. (4) II > I > III

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Structure III (non-polar R.S. with more covalent bonds) is most stable. Structure II (having negative charge on more electronegative oxygen atom) is more stable than Structure I (having negative charge on less electronegative carbon atom). Therefore, stability order: III > II > I
