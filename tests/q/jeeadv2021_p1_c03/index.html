For the given close packed structure of a salt made of cation X and anion Y shown below (ions of only one face are shown for clarity), the packing fraction is approximately (packing fraction = packing efficiency / 100) [Figure shows a cubic unit cell with one cation X in the center and four anions Y positioned at the corners and faces of the cube]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 0.74
  2. (B) 0.63
  3. (C) 0.52
  4. (D) 0.48

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol. B Packing fraction (f) = (3 × (4/3)πr₁³ + 1 × (4/3)πr³) / a³ = (1 × (4/3)π × 3[(r₁/r)³ + 1]) / (a/r)³ Now 2r₁ = a ∴ a/r = 2 Also, r₁/r = 0.414 So, f = (1 × (4/3) × 3.14 × [3 × (0.414)³ + 1]) / (2)³ = 0.634 ≈ 0.63
