Two cars are approaching each other at an equal speed of 7.2 km/hr. When they see each other, both blow horns of 676 Hz. The beat frequency heard by each driver will be..............Hz. [Velocity of sound in air is 340 m/s]

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

Convert speed: 7.2 km/hr = 7.2 × (5/18) = 2 m/s. Using Doppler effect formula for approaching sources: f'_B = f_A × [(v + v_B)/(v - v_A)] where v = 340 m/s, v_A = v_B = 2 m/s. f'_B = 676 × [(340 + 2)/(340 - 2)] = 676 × (342/338) = 676 × (171/169) = 684 Hz. Similarly, f'_A = 684 Hz. Beat frequency = |f'_B - f_B| = |684 - 676| = 8 Hz
