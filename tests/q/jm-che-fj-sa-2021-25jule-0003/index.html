The ionic radii of F⁻ and O²⁻ respectively are 1.33 Å and 1.4 Å, while the covalent radius of N is 0.74 Å. The correct statement for the ionic radius of N³⁻ from the following is:

  1. (1) It is smaller than O²⁻ and F⁻, but bigger than N
  2. (2) It is bigger than F⁻ and N, but smaller than O²⁻
  3. (3) It is smaller than F⁻ and N
  4. (4) It is bigger than O²⁻ and F⁻

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Anions have larger radii than atoms. Also, higher the negative charge (e/p ratio), higher the ionic radii. So, r(N³⁻) > r(O²⁻) > r(F⁻). Since N³⁻ has more negative charge than O²⁻ and F⁻, it will have the largest radius among the three anions.
