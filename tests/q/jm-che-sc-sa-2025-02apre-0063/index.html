When a concentrated solution of sulphanilic acid and 1-naphthylamine is treated with nitrous acid (273 K) and acidified with acetic acid, the mass (g) of 0.1 mole of product formed as (Given molar mass in g mol^(-1) H:1, C:12, N:14, O:16, S:32)

  1. (1) 33
  2. (2) 330
  3. (3) 66
  4. (4) 343

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

This is a diazotization reaction. Sulphanilic acid undergoes diazotization with nitrous acid at 273 K to form benzenediazonium chloride. This then couples with 1-naphthylamine to form an azo dye product. The product is likely a naphthyl azo dye derivative. Calculating the molar mass of the diazo coupling product and then finding mass of 0.1 mole gives 330 g/mol × 0.1 = 33 g.
