The half-life of 65Zn is 245 days. After x days, 75% of original activity remained. The value of x in days is _____. (Nearest integer) (Given: log3 = 0.4771 and log2 = 0.3010)

Answer

Correct answer: 102

Solution

t_(1/2) = ln2/K K = ln2/245 t = (1/K)ln(a_0/a_t) t_(25%) = (1/K)ln(4/3) t_(25%) = (1/(ln2/245))ln(4/3) t_(25%) = (245/ln2)ln(4/3) t_(25%) = 245 × [ln(4/3)/ln2] t_(25%) = 245 × [(2log2 - log3)/log2] t_(25%) = 245 × [(2 × 0.3010 - 0.4771)/0.3010] t_(25%) = 245 × [(0.6020 - 0.4771)/0.3010] t_(25%) = 245 × [0.1249/0.3010] t_(25%) = 245 × 0.4149 = 101.66 day Rounded to nearest integer: 102 days
