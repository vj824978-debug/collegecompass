The volume (in mL) of 0.1 M AgNO₃ required for complete precipitation of chloride ions present in 30 mL of 0.01 M solution of [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂, as silver chloride is close to

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

Number of ionisable Cl⁻ in [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂ is 2 ∴ Millimoles of Cl⁻ = 30 × 0.01 × 2 = 0.6 ∴ Millimoles of Ag⁺ required = 0.6 ∴ 0.6 = 0.1 V V = 6 ml
