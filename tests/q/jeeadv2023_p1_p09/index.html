An optical arrangement consists of two concave mirrors M₁ and M₂, and a convex lens L with a common principal axis, as shown in the figure. The focal length of L is 10 cm. The radii of curvature of M₁ and M₂ are 20 cm and 24 cm, respectively. The distance between L and M₂ is 20 cm. A point object S is placed at the mid-point between L and M₂ on the axis. When the distance between L and M₁ is n/7 cm, one of the images coincides with S. The value of n is ______ [Figure shows: M₁ (concave mirror on left) --- L (convex lens in middle) --- S (point on axis) --- M₂ (concave mirror on right). Distance between L and M₂ is marked as 20 cm.]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 220

Solution

Sol. 220 or 80 or 150 and you can explore other possibilities also Case I: If M₁ is placed at distance (20 + 80/7) cm from lens, the rays retrace its path and image will be formed at S. [Diagram showing optical system with M₁ at 20cm, lens L, M₂ at 60cm, with S marked 10cm from lens] ∴ n = 220 From mirror: 1/V + 1/(-10) = 1/(-12) ⇒ V = 60cm Refraction from lens: 1/V - 1/(-80) = 1/10 V = 80/7 cm Case II: Consider reflection from M₂ then refraction from lens. Image is at 80/7 cm left of lens if M₁ is placed at position of this image by lens, rays reflect back and final image is formed at S ∴ n = 80. [Diagram showing optical system with M₁ at 80/7 cm, lens L, M₂ at 60cm, with S marked 10cm from lens] Case III: First consider refraction from lens then reflection from M₁ if image due to this reflection is formed at 80/7 cm left of the lens, then image after refraction with lens and reflection with M₂ will be formed at S. Then distance between L and M₁ = 10 + 80/7 = 150/7 = n/7 ⇒ n = 150 [Diagram showing optical system with M₁ at 10cm, lens L at center, M₂ on right, with measurements: 10cm, 80/7 cm, 10cm, 10cm, 60cm marked along axis, and S marked]
