A ball is drawn at random from box I and transferred to box II. If the probability of drawing a red ball from box I, after this transfer, is 1/3, then the correct option(s) with the possible values of n₁ and n₂ is(are)

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) n₁ = 4, n₂ = 6
  2. (B) n₁ = 2, n₂ = 3
  3. (C) n₁ = 10, n₂ = 20
  4. (D) n₁ = 3, n₂ = 6

Answer

Correct answer: CD

Solution

P(Red after Transfer) = P(Red Transfer) · P(Red Transfer in II Case) + P(Black Transfer) · P(Red Transfer in II Case) P(R) = n₁/(n₁+n₂) · (n₁-1)/(n₁+n₂-1) + n₂/(n₁+n₂) · n₁/(n₁+n₂-1) = 1/3 Of the given options, option C and D satisfy above condition.
