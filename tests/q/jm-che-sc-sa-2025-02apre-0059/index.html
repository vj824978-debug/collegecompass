Which among the following molecules (a) involved in sp3d hybridization (b) has different bond lengths and (c) has lone pair of electrons on the central atom? 1. PF5 2. XeF4 3. SF4 4. XeF2

  1. (1) PF5
  2. (2) XeF4
  3. (3) SF4
  4. (4) XeF2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Analyzing each molecule: (1) PF5 - sp3d hybridization, but all bonds are identical (no different bond lengths), no lone pairs. (2) XeF4 - sp3d2 hybridization, not sp3d. (3) SF4 - sp3d hybridization with 1 lone pair on central S atom. Has different bond lengths (axial bonds differ from equatorial bonds due to lone pair distortion). Satisfies all three conditions. (4) XeF2 - sp3d hybridization with 3 lone pairs, but all Xe-F bonds are identical. Answer: SF4 satisfies all conditions (a), (b), and (c).
