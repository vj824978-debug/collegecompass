The correct statement(s) related to oxoacids of phosphorous is(are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) Upon heating, H₃PO₃ undergoes disproportionation reaction to produce H₃PO₄ and PH₃.
  2. (B) While H₃PO₃ can act as reducing agent, H₃PO₄ cannot.
  3. (C) H₃PO₃ is a monobasic acid.
  4. (D) The H atom of P–H bond in H₃PO₃ is not ionizable in water.

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

H₃PO₃ →(Δ) H₃PO₄ + PH₃ [Two structural formulas shown: Left: H₃PO₃ with structure H-O-P-O-H with H below and O double bonded above P Right: H₃PO₄ with structure H-O-P-O-H with OH below and O double bonded above P] P-H bond is responsible for it is reducing character. H₃PO₄ does not have. H₃PO₃ is a dibasic acid in oxyacids of phosphorus. O-H bond is ionisable where as P-H bond is non ionizable.
