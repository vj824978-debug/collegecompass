In basic medium CrO4^(2-) oxidises S2O3^(2-) to form SO4^(2-) and itself changes into Cr(OH)4^(-). The volume of 0.154 M CrO4^(2-) required to react with 40mL of 0.25 M S2O3^(2-) is _____ mL. (Rounded-off to the nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 173

Solution

Balanced equation: CrO4^(2-) + S2O3^(2-) → Cr(OH)4^(-) + SO4^(2-). O.N.C = 4, n factor of S2O3^(2-) = 8, n factor of CrO4^(2-) = 3. Using m.e of CrO4^(2-) = m.e of S2O3^(2-): V × (0.154 × 3) = 40 × (0.25 × 8). V = 80/0.462 ≈ 173 mL
