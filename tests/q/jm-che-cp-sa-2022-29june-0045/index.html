Two isomers (A) and (B) with Molar mass 184 g/mol and elemental composition C, 52.2%; H, 4.9% and Br 42.9% gave benzoic acid and p-bromobenzoic acid, respectively on oxidation with KMnO4. Isomer 'A' is optically active and gives a pale yellow precipitate when warmed with alcoholic AgNO3. Isomers 'A' and 'B' are, respectively

  1. (1) H3C-CHBr-C6H5 and the structure with CH2Br and CH3 on benzene ring
  2. (2) the structure with CH2Br and CH3 on benzene ring and the structure with CH2CH3 and Br on benzene ring
  3. (3) H3C-CHBr-C6H5 and the structure with CH2CH3 and Br on benzene ring
  4. (4) the structure with CH2CH3 and Br on benzene ring and H3C-CHBr-C6H5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Empirical formula calculation: C: 52.2/12 = 4.35, H: 4.9/1 = 4.9, Br: 42.9/80 = 0.5. Simplest ratio is C8H9Br (molar mass 184). Isomer A is optically active (chiral center) and gives pale yellow AgBr precipitate with AgNO3 (contains CH2Br), so A is H3C-CHBr-C6H5. Oxidation gives benzoic acid. Isomer B oxidizes to p-bromobenzoic acid, indicating para-substituted bromobenzene with ethyl group: p-Br-C6H4-CH2CH3.
