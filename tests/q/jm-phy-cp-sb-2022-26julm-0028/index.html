The graph between 1/u and 1/v for a thin convex lens in order to determine its focal length is plotted as shown in the figure. The refractive index of lens is 1.5 and its both surfaces have same radius of curvature R. The value of R will be ___ cm. (where u = object distance, v = image distance)

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Using lens maker's formula: 1/f = (μ - 1)(1/R - 1/(-R)) = (μ - 1)(2/R). With μ = 1.5: 1/f = (1.5 - 1)(2/R) = 0.5 × (2/R) = 1/R. From the graph, the y-intercept (1/v when 1/u = 0) is 0.10 cm^(-1), which equals 1/f. So 1/f = 0.10, giving f = 10 cm. Since 1/f = 1/R, we get R = 10 cm.
