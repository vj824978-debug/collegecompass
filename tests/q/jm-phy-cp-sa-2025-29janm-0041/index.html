A body of mass 'm' connected to a massless and unstretchable string goes in verticle circle of radius 'R' under gravity g. The other end of the string is fixed at the center of circle. If velocity at top of circular path is n*sqrt(gR), where n ≥ 1, then ratio of kinetic energy of the body at bottom to that at top of the circle is:

  1. (1) n/(n+4)
  2. (2) (n+4)/n
  3. (3) n^2/(n^2+4)
  4. (4) (n^2+4)/n^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

V_Top = sqrt(n^2*gR) Using energy conservation from top to bottom of the circular path: (1/2)*m*V_Bottom^2 = (1/2)*m*V_Top^2 + m*g*(2R) V_Bottom^2 = V_Top^2 + 4gR V_Bottom^2 = n^2*gR + 4gR V_Bottom = sqrt(n^2*gR + 4gR) Ratio of kinetic energies = KE_Bottom/KE_Top = [(1/2)*m*V_Bottom^2]/[(1/2)*m*V_Top^2] = V_Bottom^2/V_Top^2 = (n^2*gR + 4gR)/(n^2*gR) = (n^2 + 4)/n^2
