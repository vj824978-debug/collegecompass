Reaction A(g) → 2B(g) + C(g) is a first order reaction. It was started with pure A. A table shows: at t = 10 min, pressure = 160 mm Hg; at t = ∞, pressure = 240 mm Hg. Which of the following option is incorrect?

  1. (1) Partial pressure of A after 10 minute is 40 mm Hg
  2. (2) Initial pressure of A is 80 mm Hg
  3. (3) The reaction never goes to completion
  4. (4) Rate constant of the reaction is 1.693 min^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From stoichiometry A → 2B + C. At t=0: a₀. At time t: (a₀-x), 2x, x. Total pressure = a₀ + 2x = 160 at t=10 min. At t=∞: a₀ + 2a₀ = 3a₀ = 240, so a₀ = 80 mm Hg. Thus x = 40, and P_A = 40 mm Hg at t=10 min. For first order: k = (2.303/t) × log(a₀/(a₀-x)) = (2.303/10) × log(80/40) = 0.2303 × log(2) = 0.2303 × 0.301 = 0.0693 min^(-1), not 1.693 min^(-1). Option (4) is incorrect.
