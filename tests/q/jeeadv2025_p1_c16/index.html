Match the compounds in List-I with the appropriate observations in List-II and choose the correct option. [Table with List-I containing compounds P, Q, R, S and List-II containing 5 observations about chemical reactions] (P) Structure showing: benzene ring with OH group at para position, with NH2 group attached to a carbon bearing a carbonyl and NH-CO-OMe chain (Q) Structure showing: benzene ring with NH group attached to a carbon bearing two carbonyl groups (one forming CO-NH with an isobutyl chain and OMe ester) (R) Structure showing: benzene ring with NH3+Cl- group (S) Structure showing: trimethylbenzene with NHNH2 group List-II: (1) Reaction with phenyl diazonium salt gives yellow dye. (2) Reaction with ninhydrin gives purple color and it also reacts with FeCl3 to give violet color. (3) Reaction with glucose will give corresponding hydrazone. (4) Lassiagne extract of the compound treated with dilute HCl followed by addition of aqueous FeCl3 gives blood red color. (5) After complete hydrolysis, it will give ninhydrin test and it DOES NOT give positive phthalein dye test.

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) P → 1; Q → 5; R → 4; S → 2
  2. (B) P → 2; Q → 5; R → 1; S → 3
  3. (C) P → 5; Q → 2; R → 1; S → 4
  4. (D) P → 2; Q → 1; R → 5; S → 3

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

P - 2 [Chemical reaction diagram showing amino acid with phenolic group reacting with Ninhydrin to form purple colour product] Also give violet colour with FeCl₃ Due to presence of phenolic group Q - 5 [Peptide structure with cyclohexene group] + H₂O, -CH₃COOH → [Cyclohexene-CH(NH₂)-COOH] + H₂N-CH(CH(CH₃)₂)-COOH + MeOH (Gives Ninhydrin test) Does not give phthalate test due to absence of phenolic group) R - 1 [Benzene ring]-N≡N⁺ + [Benzene ring]-NH₂ → [Benzene ring]-N=N-[Benzene ring]-NH₂ Yellow dye S - 3 C₆H₅O₆ + [Cyclohexene with CH(NH-NH₂) group] → [Structure with HC=NNH- and C=NNH- bonds attached to methylated benzene rings, with HO-H, H-OH, H-OH, CH₂OH groups below] Hydrazone
