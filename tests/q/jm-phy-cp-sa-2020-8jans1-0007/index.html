Consider two charged metallic spheres S₁ and S₂ of radii R₁ and R₂, respectively. The electric fields E₁ (on S₁) and E₂ (on S₂) on their surfaces are such that E₁/E₂ = R₁/R₂. Then the ratio V₁(on S₁)/V₂(on S₂) of the electrostatic potentials on each sphere is:

  1. (1) (R₂/R₁)
  2. (2) (R₁/R₂)^3
  3. (3) (R₁/R₂)^2
  4. (4) R₁/R₂

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: E₁/E₂ = R₁/R₂ For a charged metallic sphere, the electric field on the surface is: E = kQ/R² And the potential on the surface is: V = kQ/R For sphere S₁: E₁ = kQ₁/R₁² and V₁ = kQ₁/R₁ For sphere S₂: E₂ = kQ₂/R₂² and V₂ = kQ₂/R₂ From the given condition: E₁/E₂ = R₁/R₂ (kQ₁/R₁²)/(kQ₂/R₂²) = R₁/R₂ (Q₁/R₁²) × (R₂²/Q₂) = R₁/R₂ Q₁/Q₂ = (R₁²/R₂²) × (R₁/R₂) = R₁³/R₂³ Now, the ratio of potentials: V₁/V₂ = (kQ₁/R₁)/(kQ₂/R₂) V₁/V₂ = (Q₁/R₁) × (R₂/Q₂) V₁/V₂ = (Q₁/Q₂) × (R₂/R₁) V₁/V₂ = (R₁³/R₂³) × (R₂/R₁) V₁/V₂ = (R₁/R₂)²
