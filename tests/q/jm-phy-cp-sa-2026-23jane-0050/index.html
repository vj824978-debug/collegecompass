The velocity of sound in air is doubled when the temperature is raised from 0°C to α°C. The value of α is

Answer

Correct answer: 819

Solution

V = sqrt(γRT/M) V₁/V₂ = sqrt(T₁/T₂) V₀/(2V₀) = sqrt(273/T₂) 1/4 = 273/T₂ T₂ = 4 × 273 = α + 273 α = 3 × 273 α = 819°C
