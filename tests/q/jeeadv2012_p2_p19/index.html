Two spherical planets P and Q have the same uniform density ρ, masses M_p and M_Q, an surface areas A and 4A, respectively. A spherical planet R also has uniform density ρ and its mass is (M_p + M_Q). The escape velocities from the planets P, Q and R are V_p, V_Q and V_R respectively. Then

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) V_Q > V_R > V_p
  2. (B) V_R > V_Q > V_p
  3. (C) V_R/V_p = 3
  4. (D) V_p/V_Q = 1/2

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

Ves = √(2GM/R) = √(2Gp·(4/3)πR³/R) = √(4Gp/3·R) Ves ∝ R Surface area of P = A = 4πRₚ² Surface area of Q = 4A = 4π·Rq² ⟹ Rq = 2Rₚ mass R is MR = Mₚ + Mq ρ·(4/3)πR³R = ρ·(4/3)πR³ₚ + ρ·(4/3)πR³q ⟹ R³R = R³ₚ + R³q = 9R³ₚ RR = 9^(1/3)Rₚ ⟹ RR > Rq > Rₚ Therefore VR > Vq > Vₚ VR/Vₚ = 9^(1/3) and Vₚ/Vq = 1/2
