A monatomic gas having γ = 5/3 is stored in a thermally insulated container and the gas is suddenly compressed to (1/8)^th of its initial volume. The ratio of final pressure and initial pressure is (γ is the ratio of specific heats of the gas at constant pressure and at constant volume)

  1. (1) 40
  2. (2) 28
  3. (3) 32
  4. (4) 16

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For adiabatic compression of ideal gas: P₁V₁^γ = P₂V₂^γ. Given V₂ = V₁/8 and γ = 5/3. Therefore, P₂/P₁ = (V₁/V₂)^γ = (8)^(5/3) = (2³)^(5/3) = 2^5 = 32
