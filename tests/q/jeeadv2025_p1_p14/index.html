List-I shows four configurations, each consisting of a pair of ideal electric dipoles. Each dipole has a dipole moment of magnitude p, oriented as marked by arrows in the figures. In all the configurations the dipoles are fixed such that they are at a distance 2r apart along the x direction. The midpoint of the line joining the two dipoles is X. The possible resultant electric fields E at X are given in List-II. Choose the option that describes the correct match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II. List-I: (P) [Two dipoles: left dipole pointing up (ĵ direction), right dipole pointing up (ĵ direction), separated by distance 2r along x-axis, X at midpoint] (Q) [Two dipoles: left dipole pointing up (ĵ direction), right dipole pointing down (-ĵ direction), separated by distance 2r along x-axis, X at midpoint] (R) [Two dipoles: left dipole pointing up (ĵ direction), right dipole pointing right (î direction), separated by distance 2r along x-axis, X at midpoint] (S) [Two dipoles: left dipole pointing right (î direction), right dipole pointing right (î direction), separated by distance 2r along x-axis, X at midpoint] List-II: (1) E = 0 (2) E = -p/(2πε₀r³) ĵ (3) E = -p/(4πε₀r³) (î - ĵ) (4) E = p/(4πε₀r³) (2î - ĵ) (5) E = p/(πε₀r³) î

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) P→3, Q→1, R→2, S→4
  2. (B) P→4, Q→5, R→3, S→1
  3. (C) P→2, Q→1, R→4, S→5
  4. (D) P→2, Q→1, R→3, S→5

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

[Four diagrams showing electric dipole configurations with charges +p and -p separated by distance r, with electric field calculations at different points] Diagram 1: Two charges +p and -p separated by distance r on horizontal axis. Point of interest is on vertical axis equidistant from both charges. E = -P/(2πε₀r³) j Diagram 2: Two charges +p and -p separated by distance r on horizontal axis. Point of interest is on vertical axis equidistant from both charges. E = 0 Diagram 3: Single charge +p on horizontal axis with point of interest on horizontal axis at distance r from charge. E = p/(2πε₀r³) i - p/(4πε₀r³) j Diagram 4: Single charge +p on horizontal axis with point of interest on horizontal axis at distance r from charge. E = p/(4πε₀r³) i
