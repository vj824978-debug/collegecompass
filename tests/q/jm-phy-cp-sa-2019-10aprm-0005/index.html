A coil of self inductance 10 mH and resistance 0.1 Ω is connected through a switch to a battery of internal resistance 0.9 Ω. After the switch is closed, the time taken for the current to attain 80% of the saturation value is: [take ln 5 = 1.6]

  1. (1) 0.324 s
  2. (2) 0.002 s
  3. (3) 0.103 s
  4. (4) 0.016 s

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given: L = 10 mH = 10 × 10^(-3) H R (coil resistance) = 0.1 Ω r (internal resistance) = 0.9 Ω Total resistance R_total = R + r = 0.1 + 0.9 = 1.0 Ω The current in an RL circuit as a function of time is given by: i = i_0[1 - e^(-Rt/L)] where i_0 is the saturation (steady-state) current. For 80% of saturation current: 0.8i_0 = i_0[1 - e^(-Rt/L)] 0.8 = 1 - e^(-Rt/L) e^(-Rt/L) = 0.2 = 1/5 Taking natural logarithm on both sides: -Rt/L = ln(1/5) = -ln(5) Rt/L = ln(5) t = (L/R) × ln(5) Substituting values: t = (10 × 10^(-3))/(1.0) × 1.6 t = 0.01 × 1.6 t = 0.016 s
