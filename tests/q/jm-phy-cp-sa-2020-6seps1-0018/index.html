Shown in the figure is a hollow icecream cone (it is open at the top). If its mass is M, radius of its top, R and height, H, then its moment of Inertia about its axis is

  1. (1) M(R^2 + H^2)/4
  2. (2) MR^2/3
  3. (3) MR^2/2
  4. (4) MH^2/3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Consider a thin ring element at height h from the apex of the cone. From the geometry of the cone, at height h, the radius r is given by: r = h tan(theta), where tan(theta) = R/H So r = hR/H Area of element dA = 2πr dℓ = 2πr(dh/cos(theta)) Mass of element dm = (M/(πR*sqrt(H^2 + R^2))) × 2πr(dh/cos(theta)) dm = (2Mh tan(theta) dh)/(R*sqrt(H^2 + R^2)*cos(theta)) where r = h tan(theta) Moment of inertia dI = (dm)r^2 = (h^2 tan^2(theta)/cos(theta)) × (2m/(R)) × (h tan(theta)/sqrt(R^2 + H^2)) dh dI = (2M tan^3(theta))/(cos(theta)R*sqrt(R^2 + H^2)) × h^3 dh Integrating from h = 0 to h = H: I = ∫[0 to H] (2M tan^3(theta))/(cos(theta)R*sqrt(R^2 + H^2)) × h^3 dh = (2M tan^3(theta))/(cos(theta)R*sqrt(R^2 + H^2)) × [h^4/4] from 0 to H = (2M tan^3(theta) × H^4)/(4cos(theta)R*sqrt(R^2 + H^2)) Substituting tan(theta) = R/H and cos(theta) = H/sqrt(H^2 + R^2): I = (MR^2H^4)/(2H^3*sqrt(H^2 + R^2)) × (sqrt(H^2 + R^2))/(H*sqrt(H^2 + R^2)) = MR^2/2
