Let 3, 6, 9, 12, ... upto 78 terms and 5, 9, 13, 17, ... upto 59 terms be two series. Then, the sum of terms common to both the series is equal to ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 2223.00

Solution

Series 1 (S1): 3, 6, 9, 12, ... (78 terms) with first term a=3, d=3. T78 = 3 + 77×3 = 234. Series 2 (S2): 5, 9, 13, 17, ... (59 terms) with first term a=5, d=4. T59 = 5 + 58×4 = 237. Common terms form sequence: 9, 21, 33, ... (with common difference 12). The nth common term ≤ 234, so 9 + (n-1)×12 ≤ 234, giving n < 237/12, thus n = 19. Sum of 19 common terms = (19/2)[2(9) + 18×12] = (19/2)[18 + 216] = 19 × 117 = 2223.
