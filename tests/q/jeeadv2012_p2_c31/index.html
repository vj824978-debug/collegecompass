25 mL of household bleach solution was mixed with 30 mL of 0.50 M KI and 10 mL of 4N acetic acid. In the titration of the liberated iodine, 48 mL of 0.25 N Na₂S₂O₃ was used to reach the end point. The molarity of the household bleach solution is

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) 0.48 M
  2. (B) 0.96 M
  3. (C) 0.24 M
  4. (D) 0.024 M

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

milli mole of Hypo = 0.25 × 48 = 2 × milli mole of Cl₂ milli mole of Cl₂ = (0.25 × 48)/2 = 6 milli mole = milli mole of Cl₂ = milli mole of CaOCl₂ So, molarity = 6/25 M = 0.24 M
