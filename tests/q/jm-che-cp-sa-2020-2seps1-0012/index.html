The major aromatic product C in the following reaction sequence will be: Starting material is a benzene ring fused with a pyran ring. Step 1: HBr(excess), heat gives (A). Step 2: (i) KOH (Alc.), (ii) H+ gives (B). Step 3: O3, Zn/H3O+ gives (C).

  1. (1) A benzene ring with OH group and CO2H group attached
  2. (2) A benzene ring with OH group and CHO group attached
  3. (3) A benzene ring with Br and CHO groups attached
  4. (4) A benzene ring with Br and CO2H groups attached

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Starting material is 4H-chromen-4-one (a benzofused pyran). Step 1: HBr (excess) at heat: The HBr attacks the double bonds in the pyran ring. The excess HBr and heat conditions lead to ring opening and formation of an intermediate. The product (A) is 2-bromo-1-(2-bromophenyl)ethane or similar dibromide intermediate. Step 2: (i) KOH (alcoholic), (ii) H+: KOH in alcoholic medium causes elimination of HBr, forming double bonds. The E2 elimination forms a conjugated diene system. Upon acidic workup (ii) H+, this forms a cyclic structure through electrophilic aromatic substitution, regenerating the benzene ring with an unsaturated side chain. The product (B) contains a hydroxyl group and an alkene chain. Step 3: O3, Zn/H3O+: Ozonolysis followed by reductive workup (Zn/H3O+) cleaves the carbon-carbon double bond. The alkene chain is oxidatively cleaved to form aldehyde groups. The C-C double bond in the side chain is cleaved by ozonolysis. With Zn/H3O+ workup (reductive conditions), aldehydes are formed instead of carboxylic acids. Product (C) contains: - The benzene ring with OH group - A CHO (aldehyde) group on the side chain Therefore, the major aromatic product C is hydroxybenzaldehyde (option 2).
