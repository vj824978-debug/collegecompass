A uniform magnetic field B exists in a direction perpendicular to the plane of a square loop made of a metal wire. The wire has a diameter of 4 mm and a total length of 30 cm. The magnetic field changes with time at a steady rate dB/dt = 0.032 Ts^(-1). The induced current in the loop is close to (Resistivity of the metal wire is 1.23 × 10^(-8) Ωm)

  1. (1) 0.34 A
  2. (2) 0.53 A
  3. (3) 0.61 A
  4. (4) 0.43 A

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: - Wire diameter d = 4 mm = 4 × 10^(-3) m, so radius r = 2 × 10^(-3) m - Total length of wire L = 30 cm = 0.30 m - Rate of change of magnetic field dB/dt = 0.032 Ts^(-1) - Resistivity ρ = 1.23 × 10^(-8) Ωm - Square loop side a = 7.5 cm = 0.075 m Step 1: Calculate the induced EMF using Faraday's law q_i = d(Ba^2)/dt = a^2 × dB/dt q_i = (0.075)^2 × 0.032 q_i = 0.005625 × 0.032 q_i = 1.8 × 10^(-4) Wb/s = 1.8 × 10^(-4) V Step 2: Calculate the resistance of the wire R = ρL/(πr^2) R = (1.23 × 10^(-8) × 0.30)/(π × (2 × 10^(-3))^2) R = (3.69 × 10^(-9))/(π × 4 × 10^(-6)) R = (3.69 × 10^(-9))/(1.2566 × 10^(-5)) R = 2.935 × 10^(-4) Ω Step 3: Calculate the induced current i = q_i/R = a^2(dB/dt)/(ρL/(πr^2)) i = (a^2 × dB/dt × πr^2)/(ρL) i = ((0.075)^2 × 0.032 × π × (2 × 10^(-3))^2)/(1.23 × 10^(-8) × 0.30) i = (0.005625 × 0.032 × π × 4 × 10^(-6))/(3.69 × 10^(-9)) i = (5.625 × 10^(-4) × 1.2566 × 10^(-5))/(3.69 × 10^(-9)) i = (7.07 × 10^(-9))/(3.69 × 10^(-9)) i ≈ 0.61 A
