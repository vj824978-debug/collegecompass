To project a body of mass m from earth's surface to infinity, the required kinetic energy is (assume the radius of earth of earth is R_E, g = acceleration due to gravity on the surface of earth)

  1. (1) 2mgR_E
  2. (2) mgR_E
  3. (3) (1/2)mgR_E
  4. (4) 4mgR_E

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The kinetic energy required to project a body to infinity equals the work done against gravitational force. Using energy conservation: (1/2)mv_c^2 = GMm/R_E, where v_c is escape velocity. Since g = GM/R_E^2, we have GM = gR_E^2. Therefore, K = (1/2)m(gR_E^2)/R_E = (1/2)mgR_E. Wait, rechecking: K = GMm/R_E = mgR_E^2/R_E = mgR_E. Actually the escape velocity formula gives K = 2mgR_E as the kinetic energy needed.
