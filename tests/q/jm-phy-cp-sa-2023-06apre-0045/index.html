A particle starts with an initial velocity of 10.0 m/s along x-direction and accelerates uniformly at the rate of 2.0 m/s^2. The time taken by the particle to reach the velocity of 60.0 m/s is __________.

  1. (1) 25 s
  2. (2) 3 s
  3. (3) 6 s
  4. (4) 30 s

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Using the kinematic equation v = u + at, where v = 60 m/s, u = 10 m/s, a = 2.0 m/s^2. So 60 = 10 + 2t, which gives 2t = 50, therefore t = 25 s.
