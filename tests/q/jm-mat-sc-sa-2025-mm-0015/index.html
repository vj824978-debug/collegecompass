Let f:ℝ→ℝ be a continuous function satisfying f(0)=1 and f(2x)-f(x)=x for all x∈ℝ. If lim(n→∞) {f(x)-f(x/2^n)}=G(x), then sum from r=1 to 10 of G(r^2) is equal to

  1. (1) 215
  2. (2) 385
  3. (3) 420
  4. (4) 540

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

From f(2x)-f(x)=x, we get f(x)-f(x/2)=x/2, f(x/2)-f(x/4)=x/4, etc. Telescoping: f(x)-f(x/2^n)=x/2+x/4+...+x/2^n. As n→∞, G(x)=lim(f(x)-f(x/2^n))=x(1/2+1/4+1/8+...)=x. Therefore G(r^2)=r^2. Sum from r=1 to 10: 1+4+9+16+25+36+49+64+81+100=385
