Diborane (B2H6) reacts independently with O2 and H2O to produce, respectively -

  1. (1) H3BO3 and B2O3
  2. (2) B2O3 and H3BO3
  3. (3) B2O3 and [BH4]^-
  4. (4) HBO2 and H3BO3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

B2H6 + O2 → B2O3 + H2O (Boric anhydride) B2H6 + H2O → H3BO3 + H2 (Boric acid) When diborane reacts with oxygen, it undergoes complete oxidation to form boric anhydride (B2O3) and water. When diborane reacts with water, it undergoes hydrolysis to form boric acid (H3BO3) and hydrogen gas.
