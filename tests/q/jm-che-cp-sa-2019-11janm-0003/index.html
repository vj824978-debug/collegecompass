The major product of the following reaction is: Starting material is a benzene ring fused to a six-membered ring containing ethyl ester and nitrile groups. Reagents: (i) Ni/H2 (ii) DIBAL-H

  1. (1) Benzene fused to six-membered ring with CHO group and H on the ring
  2. (2) Benzene fused to six-membered ring with NH group (isoindoline structure)
  3. (3) Benzene fused to six-membered ring with C=N double bond (tetrahydroisoquinoline structure)
  4. (4) Benzene fused to six-membered ring with OH group and CH2-CH2-NH2 chain

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Step 1: The starting material is an ethyl ester with a nitrile (CN) group attached to a benzene-fused ring system. Step 2: Treatment with Ni/H2 (Nickel/Hydrogen) reduces the nitrile group (-C≡N) to a primary amine (-CH2-CH2-NH2). Step 3: The ethyl ester (-CO2Et) is reduced by DIBAL-H (Diisobutylaluminum hydride) to an aldehyde (-CHO) group. Step 4: After these reduction steps, we have a benzene-fused six-membered ring with an aldehyde group (-CHO) and an ethylamine side chain (-CH2-CH2-NH2). Step 5: The primary amine group undergoes intramolecular cyclization by attacking the aldehyde carbon, forming an imine (C=N) bond. Step 6: This intramolecular attack results in the formation of a new six-membered ring, creating a tetrahydroisoquinoline structure with a C=N double bond. Step 7: The major product is the imine-containing bicyclic compound (option 3), where the benzene ring is fused to a six-membered ring containing a C=N double bond.
