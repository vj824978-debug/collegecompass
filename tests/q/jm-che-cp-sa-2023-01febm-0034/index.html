The correct representation in six membered pyranose form for the following sugar [X] is CHO HO-H HO-H H-OH H-OH H2COH Sugar [X]

  1. (1) H2COH with H and H on top, OH and OH on sides, and H and OH on bottom in pyranose ring form
  2. (2) H2COH with HO and H on top, H and OH on sides, and OH and H on bottom in pyranose ring form
  3. (3) CH2OH with H and O on top, OH and OH on sides, and H and H on bottom in pyranose ring form
  4. (4) CH2OH with H and H on top, HO and OH on sides, and OH and H on bottom in pyranose ring form

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The given sugar [X] is an aldopentose with the following structure: CHO (aldehyde group at C1) HO-H (C2: OH on left) HO-H (C3: OH on left) H-OH (C4: OH on right) H-OH (C5: OH on right) H2COH (primary alcohol at C6) To convert this to pyranose form, the aldehyde group at C1 attacks the OH group at C5, forming a six-membered ring with one oxygen. In the pyranose ring: - C1 becomes part of the ring (hemiacetal carbon) - C5 oxygen becomes the ring oxygen - The CH2OH group attached to C5 becomes CH2OH substituent Analyzing the stereochemistry: - At C2: OH is on left in Fischer (equatorial in pyranose) - At C3: OH is on left in Fischer (axial in pyranose) - At C4: OH is on right in Fischer (equatorial in pyranose) - The condition states: C2 and C3OH are cis (both on same face after cyclization) - C3 and C4 are anti to each other Option (3) correctly shows: - CH2OH group at position 1 (from C5) - O in the ring - OH and OH groups with correct stereochemistry (cis at C2 and C3OH) - H and H at appropriate positions - Correct anti relationship between C3 and C4
