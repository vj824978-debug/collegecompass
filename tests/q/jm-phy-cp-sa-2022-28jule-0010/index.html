A uniform electric field E = (8m/e) V/m is created between two parallel plates of length 1 m as shown in figure, (where m = mass of electron and e = charge of electron). An electron enters the field symmetrically between the plates with a speed of 2 m/s. The angle of deviation (θ) of the path of the electron as it comes out of the field will be

  1. (1) tan^(-1)(4)
  2. (2) tan^(-1)(2)
  3. (3) tan^(-1)(1/3)
  4. (4) tan^(-1)(3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: E = (8m/e) V/m, length l = 1 m, initial speed v_x = 2 m/s, acceleration a_y = -8 m/s^2. Time in field: t = l/v_x = 1/2 s. Final vertical velocity: v_y = a_y × t = -8 × (1/2) = -4 m/s. Angle of deviation: tan(θ) = |v_y|/v_x = 4/2 = 2. Therefore θ = tan^(-1)(2).
