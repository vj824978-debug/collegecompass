The major compound Y is

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) [Benzene ring connected to a three-carbon chain with double bond between first and second carbons, and methyl group at the end: Ph-CH=CH-CH3]
  2. (B) [Benzene ring connected to a three-carbon chain with double bond between second and third carbons, and methyl group at the end: Ph-CH2-CH=CH-CH3]
  3. (C) [Benzene ring connected to a carbon with double-bonded CH2, and an ethyl group: Ph-C(=CH2)-CH2-CH3]
  4. (D) [Benzene ring connected to a carbon with two methyl groups attached: Ph-CH(CH3)2]

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

C4H6 ——> double bond equivalent = 8+1-6/2 = 6 [Reaction scheme showing:] Starting material: phenylacetylene (C6H5-C≡CH) Path 1 (top right): - Pd/BaSO4, H2 leads to styrene (C6H5-CH=CH2) - (1) B2H6, (2) H2O2, NaOH·H2O leads to compound X: C6H5-CH2-CH2-OH (phenethyl alcohol) Path 2 (left): - HgSO4, H2SO4, H2O leads to acetophenone (C6H5-CO-CH3) - (i) EtMgBr, (ii) H2O leads to secondary alcohol: C6H5-CH(OH)-CH2-CH3 - H+/heat leads to compound Y: C6H5-C(CH3)=CH-CH3 (2-methyl-1-phenylpropene, via elimination and rearrangement)
