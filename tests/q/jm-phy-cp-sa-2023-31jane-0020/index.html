Heat energy of 735 J is given to a diatomic gas allowing the gas to expand at constant pressure. Each gas molecule rotates around an internal axis but do not oscillate. The increase in the internal energy of the gas will be:

  1. (1) 572 J
  2. (2) 525 J
  3. (3) 441 J
  4. (4) 735 J

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

ΔQ = nC_p ΔT = 735 J For a diatomic gas with rotation but no oscillation: C_p = (5/2)R (diatomic gas has 3 translational + 2 rotational degrees of freedom) Since ΔQ = nC_p ΔT: (5nRΔT)/2 = 735J The change in internal energy is: ΔU = nC_v ΔT = (3/2)(nRΔT) For a diatomic gas: C_v = (5/2)R ΔU = nC_v ΔT = (3/2)(nRΔT) = (3/2) × (2/5) × 735 = (3/2) × (2/5) × 735 = (3 × 2 × 735)/(2 × 5) = (3/5) × 735 = 441 J
