Given below are two statements: Statement I: (CH3)3C+ is more stable than CH3+ as nine hyperconjugation interactions are possible in (CH3)3C+. Statement II: CH3+ is less stable than (CH3)3C+ as only three hyperconjugation interactions are possible in CH3+. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below.

  1. (1) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  4. (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

S-1: (CH3)3C+ > CH3+; due to hyperconjugation interaction. The structure shows CH3-C+-CH3 with CH3 below the central carbon. In (CH3)3C+, there are nine hyperconjugation interactions possible (three from each of the three methyl groups, where each C-H bond can participate in hyperconjugation). This stabilizes the tertiary carbocation more than the primary methyl cation CH3+ which can have only limited hyperconjugation interactions. Therefore Statement I is true. S-2: False. Statement II claims that CH3+ has only three hyperconjugation interactions, but actually CH3+ has even fewer significant hyperconjugation interactions due to its primary nature. The comparison statement is correct but the reasoning about exactly three interactions in CH3+ is not accurate. Statement I correctly identifies that (CH3)3C+ is more stable due to nine hyperconjugation interactions, making Statement I true and Statement II false.
