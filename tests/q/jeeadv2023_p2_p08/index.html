A thin circular coil of mass 5 gm and radius 4/3 cm is initially in a horizontal xy–plane. The coin is tossed vertically up (+z direction) by applying an impulse of sqrt(π/2)×10^-2 N·s at a distance 2/3 cm from its center. The coin spins about its diameter and moves along the +z direction. By the time the coin reaches back to its initial position, it completes n rotations. The value of n is ________. [Given: The acceleration due to gravity g = 10 m s^-2] [Figure shows a circular disk of radius 4/3 cm with an impulse applied at distance 2/3 cm from center, with coordinate axes showing x, y, z directions and gravity g pointing downward]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 30

Solution

Sol. 30 J = MVcm ... (1) J · R/2 = MR²/4 · ω ... (2) From (1) Vcm = J/M Time when coin reaches back to its initial position is T = 2Vcm/g = 2J/gM Angle rotated in time T is θ = ωt = 2J/MR · 2J/Mg [From (2)] ⇒ θ = 60π ∴ n = θ/2π = 60π/2π = 30 [Figure shows a coin/disk with radius marked as 4/3 cm and 2/3 cm, with angle of 90° marked. An impulse is shown applied to the disk. Coordinate system shows x, y, z axes with g pointing downward.]
