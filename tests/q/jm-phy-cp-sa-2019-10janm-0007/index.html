A parallel plate capacitor is of area 6 cm^2 and a separation 3 mm. The gap is filled with three dielectric materials of equal thickness (see figure) with dielectric constants K_1 = 10, K_2 = 12 and K_3 = 14. The dielectric constant of a material which when fully inserted in above capacitor, gives same capacitance would be -

  1. (1) 12
  2. (2) 36
  3. (3) 14
  4. (4) 4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The three dielectric materials are arranged in series between the parallel plates. For capacitors in series with dielectrics: C_eq = ε_0(A/d)(1/(d_1/(K_1*d) + d_2/(K_2*d) + d_3/(K_3*d))) Since all three materials have equal thickness d/3: C_eq = ε_0(A/d)(1/((d/3)/(K_1*d) + (d/3)/(K_2*d) + (d/3)/(K_3*d))) C_eq = ε_0(A/d)(1/(1/(3*K_1) + 1/(3*K_2) + 1/(3*K_3))) C_eq = ε_0(A/d) * 3/(1/K_1 + 1/K_2 + 1/K_3) Alternatively, for dielectrics in series of equal thickness: C_eq = ε_0(A/d)(K_1*K_2*K_3)/(K_2*K_3 + K_1*K_3 + K_1*K_2) * 3 Or using the direct formula: C_eq = ε_0(A/d)[(1/3)K_1 + (1/3)K_2 + (1/3)K_3] Wait, for series arrangement of equal thickness dielectrics: 1/C_eq = (1/3)[1/K_1 + 1/K_2 + 1/K_3] * (d/(ε_0*A)) So: C_eq = ε_0*A/d * 3/(1/K_1 + 1/K_2 + 1/K_3) If replaced by single dielectric K: C = K*ε_0*A/d Equating: K*ε_0*A/d = ε_0*A/d * 3/(1/K_1 + 1/K_2 + 1/K_3) K = 3/(1/10 + 1/12 + 1/14) K = 3/(1/10 + 1/12 + 1/14) = 3/[(42 + 35 + 30)/(420)] = 3 * 420/107 = 1260/107 ≈ 11.78 ≈ 12 Therefore K = 12
