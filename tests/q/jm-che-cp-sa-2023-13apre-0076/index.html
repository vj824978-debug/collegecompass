Given below are two statements: Statement I: Tropolone is an aromatic compound and has 8π electrons. Statement II: π electrons of >C=O group in tropolone is involved in aromaticity. In the light of the above statements choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  2. (2) Statement I is false but Statement II is true
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  4. (4) Both Statement I and Statement II are true

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Tropolone is a 7-membered cyclic compound with alternating double bonds and a ketone group (>C=O). It has 8π electrons total: 6π electrons from the aromatic system (following Hückel's rule: 4n+2 where n=1 gives 6 electrons) plus 2π electrons from the C=O group. However, the π electrons of the >C=O group do NOT participate in the aromaticity of the ring system. The aromaticity comes from the 6π electrons of the conjugated system. Therefore, Statement I is true (8π electrons present) but Statement II is false (the C=O π electrons are not involved in aromaticity).
