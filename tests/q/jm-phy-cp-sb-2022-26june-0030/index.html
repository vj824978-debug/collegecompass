The stopping potential for photoelectrons emitted from a surface illuminated by light of wavelength 6630 A is 0.42 V. If the threshold frequency is x × 10^15/s, where x is ____ (nearest integer). (Given, speed of light = 3 × 10^8 m/s, Planck's constant = 6.63 × 10^-34 Js)

Answer

Correct answer: 35

Solution

Using photoelectric equation: (hc/λ) - φ = eV₀, where φ = hf_th. Rearranging: hf_th = (hc/λ) - eV₀. Therefore: f_th = (hc)/(hλ) - eV₀/h = c/λ - eV₀/h. Substituting values: f_th = (3×10^8)/(6630×10^-10) - (1.6×10^-19 × 0.4)/(6.63×10^-34). Calculating: f_th ≈ 4.52×10^14 - 9.64×10^13 ≈ 3.56×10^14 Hz ≈ 35.6 × 10^12 Hz = 35 × 10^15 s^-1 (nearest integer).
