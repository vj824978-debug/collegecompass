Zener breakdown occurs in a P-n junction having P and n both:

  1. (1) Lightly doped and have wide depletion layer.
  2. (2) Heavily doped and have wide depletion layer.
  3. (3) Lightly doped and have narrow depletion layer.
  4. (4) Heavily doped and have narrow depletion layer.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Zener breakdown occurs in p-n junction having p and n both: Heavily doped and have narrow depletion layer. When both P and N regions are heavily doped, the depletion layer becomes very narrow, creating a strong electric field. This strong electric field can directly ionize atoms in the depletion region, causing Zener breakdown at relatively low reverse voltages.
