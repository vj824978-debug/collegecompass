Four gases, A, B, C and D have critical temperatures 5.3, 33.2, 126.0 and 154.3K respectively. For their adsorption on a fixed amount of charcoal, the correct order is:

  1. (1) C > D > B > A
  2. (2) C > B > D > A
  3. (3) D > C > B > A
  4. (4) D > C > A > B

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The extent of adsorption of different gases on the surface of a fixed amount of charcoal is directly proportional to their critical temperatures. Given critical temperatures: A = 5.3K, B = 33.2K, C = 126.0K, D = 154.3K. Arranging in decreasing order: D (154.3) > C (126.0) > B (33.2) > A (5.3). Therefore, the order of adsorption is D > C > B > A.
