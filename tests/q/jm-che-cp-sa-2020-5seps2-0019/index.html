Adsorption of a gas follows Freundlich adsorption isotherm. If x is the mass of the gas adsorbed on mass m of the adsorbent, the correct of x/m versus p is -

  1. (1) Graph (1): Linear plots with 200 K, 250 K, 270 K curves increasing with p
  2. (2) Graph (2): Linear plots with 270 K, 250 K, 200 K curves (reversed order) increasing with p
  3. (3) Graph (3): Curved plots with 200 K, 250 K, 270 K curves showing concave pattern
  4. (4) Graph (4): Curved plots with 270 K, 250 K, 200 K curves (reversed order) showing concave pattern

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The Freundlich adsorption isotherm is given by: x/m = K.P^(1/n), where x is mass of gas adsorbed, m is mass of adsorbent, P is pressure, K and n are constants. Taking logarithm: log(x/m) = log(K) + (1/n)log(P). This is a linear relationship between log(x/m) and log(P). However, when plotting x/m versus P (linear scale), the relationship is non-linear and follows a curve that is concave downward. The equation x/m = K.P^(1/n) shows that at constant pressure, as temperature increases, the adsorption decreases (K decreases with temperature). Therefore, at higher temperatures, the curves are lower. The correct graph should show: (1) Curved (non-linear) plots following Freundlich equation (2) Temperature order from bottom to top: 200 K, 250 K, 270 K (higher temperature = lower adsorption). Graph (3) matches both criteria: it shows curved plots with the correct temperature ordering where 200 K has maximum adsorption, followed by 250 K and 270 K.
