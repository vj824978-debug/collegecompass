An organic compound 'X' showing the following solubility profile is – X is tested with: Water → insoluble, 5% HCl → insoluble, 10% NaOH → soluble, 10% NaHCO3 → insoluble

  1. (1) Benzamide
  2. (2) Oleic acid
  3. (3) m-Cresol
  4. (4) o-Toluidine

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

To identify compound X, we analyze its solubility profile: 1. Insoluble in water: Rules out highly polar compounds 2. Insoluble in 5% HCl: Rules out basic compounds (amines) like o-Toluidine which would form soluble salt with HCl 3. Soluble in 10% NaOH: Indicates presence of acidic group (phenolic -OH or carboxylic -COOH) 4. Insoluble in 10% NaHCO3: Rules out carboxylic acids (which dissolve in NaHCO3 as it's strong enough base). This indicates a phenol (weak acid) rather than carboxylic acid. Analyzing each option: (1) Benzamide: Has -CONH2 group, would be insoluble in NaOH (2) Oleic acid: Carboxylic acid, would be soluble in NaHCO3 ✗ (3) m-Cresol: Phenol with methyl group, insoluble in water ✓, insoluble in HCl ✓, soluble in NaOH (phenolic -OH reacts with strong base) ✓, insoluble in NaHCO3 (phenol is too weak acid for NaHCO3) ✓ (4) o-Toluidine: Primary amine, would be soluble in 5% HCl ✗ The phenolic group (-OH) is responsible for solubility in NaOH while remaining insoluble in NaHCO3. Answer: m-Cresol [3]
