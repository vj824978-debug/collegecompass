The correct order of the rate of reaction of the following reactants with nucleophile by S_N1 mechanism is: (Given: Structure I and II are rigid) [Structure I: Br attached to bicyclic system] [Structure II: Br attached to different bicyclic system] [Structure III: Br attached to neopentyl-like carbon] [Structure IV: Br attached to carbon with three Ph groups]

  1. (1) IV < III < II < I
  2. (2) III < I < II < IV
  3. (3) II < I < III < IV
  4. (4) I < II < III < IV

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Rate of S_N1 ∝ Stability of C^+ formed (I) and (II) are unstable due to Bredt's rule, (I) has more +1 effect. (II) < (I) < (III) < (IV) Explanation: - Structure II: Bicyclic system with Br at bridgehead - violates Bredt's rule, very unstable C^+ formed - Structure I: Bicyclic system with Br at bridgehead - also violates Bredt's rule but has more +1 effect from adjacent carbons compared to II, slightly more stable than II - Structure III: Neopentyl system - Br is on primary carbon but can form secondary/tertiary C^+ through rearrangement, more stable than I - Structure IV: Br attached to carbon bearing three Ph groups - forms very stable tertiary C^+ with three phenyl groups providing strong electron donation and resonance stabilization, most stable C^+ Therefore, the order is: II < I < III < IV
