Density of water at 4°C and 20°C are 1000 kg/m^3 and respectively. The increase in internal energy of 4 kg water when it is heated from 4°C to 20°C is ____ J. (Specific heat capacity of water = 4.2 J/kg and 1 atmospheric pressure = 10^5 Pa)

  1. (1) 315826.2
  2. (2) 234699.2
  3. (3) 258700.8
  4. (4) 268799.2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Q = mSΔT = 4 × 4200 × 16 J = 268800 J W = PΔV ΔV = (m/ρ_f - m/ρ_i) = 4[1/998 - 1/1000] P = 10^5 Pa W = 10^5 × 4 × [1/998 - 1/1000] = (8 × 10^5)/(10^3 × 998) ≈ 0.8 J ΔU = Q - W = 268799.2 J
