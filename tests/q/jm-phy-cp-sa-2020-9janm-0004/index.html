Two particals of equal mass m have respective initial velocities ui and u[(i+j)/2]. They collide completely inelastically. The energy lost in the process is-

  1. (1) (3/4)mu^2
  2. (2) (1/8)mu^2
  3. (3) (1/3)mu^2
  4. (4) (sqrt(2)/3)mu^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using conservation of momentum: mu i + m(u(i+j)/2) = 2m v̄ v̄ = (3u/4)i + (u/4)j |v̄|^2 = 10u^2/16 ΔKE = KE_f - KE_i = (1/2)(2m) × (10u^2/16) - (1/2)mu^2 - (1/2)m(u^2/2) = m × (10u^2/16) - (1/2)mu^2 - (1/4)mu^2 = (10mu^2/16) - (8mu^2/16) - (4mu^2/16) = -mu^2/8
