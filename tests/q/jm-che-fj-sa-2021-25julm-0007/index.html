Consider the above reaction, the major product 'P' is: A vinyl ketone (CH2=CH-CO-CH2-CH3) is treated with (i) C2H5MgBr in dry ether (ii) H2O, HCl to give major product P

  1. (1) OH group bonded to quaternary carbon with Cl substituent and ethyl groups
  2. (2) OH group bonded to quaternary carbon with long alkyl chain and ethyl groups
  3. (3) OH group bonded to quaternary carbon with Cl substituent and methyl/ethyl groups
  4. (4) OH group bonded to quaternary carbon with vinyl group and ethyl groups

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The Grignard reagent C2H5MgBr acts as a nucleophile and attacks the electrophilic carbonyl carbon of the alpha,beta-unsaturated ketone. The attack occurs at the carbonyl carbon (1,2-addition) rather than conjugate addition due to steric factors and reaction conditions. This forms an alkoxide intermediate with O-MgBr. The second Grignard equivalent attacks the beta carbon creating a carbanion. Upon aqueous acidic workup (H2O, HCl), the alkoxide is protonated to OH and the carbanion is protonated to form a secondary carbon. The product is a tertiary alcohol with OH group on the quaternary carbon bearing the original carbonyl carbon position, with ethyl groups from the Grignard reagent. The mechanism shown indicates formation of a tertiary alcohol as the major product after acidic workup.
