A uniform metallic wire has a resistance of 18 Ω and is bent into an equilateral triangle. Then, the resistance between any two vertices of the triangle is -

  1. (1) 12 Ω
  2. (2) 2 Ω
  3. (3) 4 Ω
  4. (4) 8 Ω

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

A uniform wire resistance is 18Ω. When it is bended into equilateral triangle then length of each side is same and its resistance is 18/3 = 6Ω. The triangle with three sides each of resistance 6Ω is shown: - Side AB has resistance 6Ω - Side BC has resistance 6Ω - Side CA has resistance 6Ω To find resistance between vertices A and B: The wire can be viewed as two parallel paths: 1. Direct path from A to B with resistance 6Ω 2. Path from A to C to B with resistance 6Ω + 6Ω = 12Ω These two paths are in parallel, so equivalent resistance between A and B is: R_eq(AB) = (12 × 6)/(12 + 6) = 72/18 = 4Ω
