Match List-I with List-II according to shape. List-I: A. XeO3 B. XeF2 C. XeO2F2 D. XeOF4 List-II: I. BrF5 II. NH3 III. [I3]− IV. SF4

  1. (1) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
  2. (2) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
  3. (3) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
  4. (4) A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

To match the compounds according to shape, we need to determine the geometry of each compound using VSEPR theory. List-I compounds: A. XeO3: Xe has 8 valence electrons, 3 bonding pairs with O atoms, 1 lone pair. Total 4 electron pairs. Geometry: Pyramidal (similar to NH3) B. XeF2: Xe has 8 valence electrons, 2 bonding pairs with F atoms, 3 lone pairs. Total 5 electron pairs. Geometry: Linear C. XeO2F2: Xe has 8 valence electrons, 2 bonding pairs with O atoms, 2 bonding pairs with F atoms, 1 lone pair. Total 5 electron pairs. Geometry: See saw D. XeOF4: Xe has 8 valence electrons, 1 bonding pair with O atom, 4 bonding pairs with F atoms, 1 lone pair. Total 6 electron pairs. Geometry: Square pyramidal List-II compounds: I. BrF5: Br has 7 valence electrons, 5 bonding pairs with F atoms, 1 lone pair. Total 6 electron pairs. Geometry: Square pyramidal II. NH3: N has 5 valence electrons, 3 bonding pairs with H atoms, 1 lone pair. Total 4 electron pairs. Geometry: Pyramidal III. [I3]−: I has 7 valence electrons + 1 from negative charge = 8 total, with 2 bonding pairs and 3 lone pairs. Total 5 electron pairs. Geometry: Linear IV. SF4: S has 6 valence electrons, 4 bonding pairs with F atoms, 1 lone pair. Total 5 electron pairs. Geometry: See saw Matching: A. XeO3 (Pyramidal) matches II. NH3 (Pyramidal) B. XeF2 (Linear) matches III. [I3]− (Linear) C. XeO2F2 (See saw) matches IV. SF4 (See saw) D. XeOF4 (Square pyramidal) matches I. BrF5 (Square pyramidal) Therefore: A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
