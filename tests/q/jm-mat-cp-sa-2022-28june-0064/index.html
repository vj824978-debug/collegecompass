The term independent of x in the expansion of (1 - x^2 + 3x^3)(5x^3/2 - 1/(5x^2))^11, x ≠ 0 is:

  1. (1) 7/40
  2. (2) 33/200
  3. (3) 39/200
  4. (4) 11/50

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

General term of (5x^3/2 - 1/(5x^2))^11 is T_(r+1) = 11Cr(5/2)^(11-r)x^(33-3r)(-1/5)^r x^(-2r) = 11Cr(5/2)^(11-r)(-1/5)^r x^(33-5r). For term independent of x in the expansion of (1 - x^2 + 3x^3) multiplied by this, we need: 33-5r = 0 (from 1), 33-5r = 2 (from -x^2), 33-5r = -3 (from 3x^3). Only 33-5r = 0 gives r = 33/5 (not integer). Check r = 7: 33-5(7) = -2, so we need x^2 term which gives 33/200.
