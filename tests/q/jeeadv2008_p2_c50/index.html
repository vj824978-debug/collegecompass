Electrolysis of dilute aqueous NaCl solution was carried out by passing 10 milli ampere current. The time required to liberate 0.01 mol of H₂ gas at the cathode is (1 Faraday = 96500 C mol⁻¹)

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 9.65 × 10³ sec
  2. (B) 19.3 × 10⁴ sec
  3. (C) 28.95 × 10³ sec
  4. (D) 38.6 × 10³ sec

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Q = i × t Q = 10 × 10⁻³ × t 2H₂O + 2e⁻ ⟶ H₂ + 2OH⁻ To liberate 0.01 mole of H₂, 0.02 Faraday charge is required Q = 0.02 × 96500 C ∴ 0.02 × 96500 = 10⁻² × t t = 19.30 × 10⁴ sec
