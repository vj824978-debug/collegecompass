Metal deficiency defect is shown by Fe0.93O. In the crystal, some Fe2+ cations are missing and loss of positive charge is compensated by the presence of Fe3+ ions. The percentage of Fe2+ ions in the Fe0.93O crystals is ___. (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 85

Solution

Let the number of O2- ions be 100 and the number of Fe2+ ions be X. The number of Fe3+ ions be (93 - X). For charge balance: X(2) + (93 - X)(3) = 200, which gives 2X + 279 - 3X = 200, so X = 79. Therefore, % of Fe2+ ions = (79/93) × 100 = 85%
