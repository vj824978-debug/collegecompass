Let N denote the sum of the numbers obtained when two dice are rolled. If the probability that 2^N < N! is m/n, where m and n are coprime, then 4m - 3n is equal to

  1. (1) 6
  2. (2) 12
  3. (3) 10
  4. (4) 8

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

When two dice are rolled, N ranges from 2 to 12. We need to find when 2^N < N!. Testing: N=1 (not possible), N=2 (not possible), N=3 (not possible), N=4 (possible since 2^4 = 16 < 24 = 4!). For N ≥ 4, the inequality holds. Counting favorable outcomes: N=4 gives 3 ways out of 36 total outcomes (1,3), (2,2), (3,1). N=5 gives 4 ways, N=6 gives 5 ways, etc. Total favorable outcomes = 36-(1+2) = 33. Wait, recalculating: outcomes where 2^N < N! occur for N=4,5,6,...,12. Unfavorable outcomes (N=2,3) = 1+2 = 3. Probability = (36-3)/36 = 33/36 = 11/12. So m=11, n=12 (coprime). Therefore 4m - 3n = 4(11) - 3(12) = 44 - 36 = 8.
