The correct order of acidic strength of the major products formed in the given reactions, is : A. PhNH2 -->(1)NaNO2+HCl(<-5°C) (2)CuCN (3)H3O+/D--> [A] B. CH3CH2CHO -->[Ag(NH3)2]+,OH- Δ--> [B] C. CH4 + O2 -->(i)MoO3 (ii)Na2Cr2O7/H+--> [C] D. PhCH2MgBr + CO2 -->[Dry ether] H3O+--> [D]

  1. (1) C > B > A > D
  2. (2) A > D > C > B
  3. (3) A > D > B > C
  4. (4) C > A > D > B

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Correct order of acidic strength of major product formed in the given reaction is (A) PhNH2 -->(1)NaNO2+HCl (2)CuCN (3)H3O+--> benzoic acid (COOH) (B) CH3CH2CHO -->[Ag(NH3)2]+,OH- Δ--> CH3CH2COOH (propionic acid) (C) CH4 + O2 -->(i)MoO3 (ii)Na2Cr2O7/H+--> HCOOH (formic acid) (D) PhCH2MgBr + CO2 -->[Dry ether] H3O+--> PhCH2COOH (phenylacetic acid) Ans. (4) C > A > D > B Acidic strength order: HCOOH > C6H5COOH > PhCH2COOH > CH3CH2COOH Formic acid is strongest because hydrogen is directly attached to carboxyl group. Benzoic acid is stronger than phenylacetic acid due to direct conjugation with benzene ring. Phenylacetic acid is stronger than propionic acid due to benzene ring stabilization.
