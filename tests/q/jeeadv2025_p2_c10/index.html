The solubility of barium iodate in an aqueous solution prepared by mixing 200 mL of 0.010 M barium nitrate with 100 mL of 0.10 M sodium iodate is X × 10^-6 mol dm^-3. The value of X is_____. Use: Solubility product constant (K_sp) of barium iodate = 1.58 × 10^-9.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 3.95

Solution

Ba(NO₃)₂ + 2NaIO₃ ⟶ Ba(IO₃)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃ 200 × 0.010 = 2 milimol 100 × 0.10 = 10 milimol - 6 milimol, 2 milimol, 4 milimol ∴ [IO₃⁻] = 6/(200 + 100) = 1/50 M [Ba²⁺] = Ksp/[IO₃⁻]² = (1.58 × 10⁻⁹)/((1/50) × (1/50)) [Ba²⁺] = 1.58 × 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ ∴ Solubility = [Ba²⁺] = 3.95 × 10⁻⁶ M
