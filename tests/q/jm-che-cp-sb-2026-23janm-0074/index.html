Consider all the structural isomers with molecular formula C5H11Br are separately treated with KOH (aq) to give respective substitution products, without any rearrangement. The number of products which can exhibit optical isomerism from these is ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 6

Solution

C5H11Br All structural isomers of C5H11Br: 1. Linear chain with Br at terminal position: Br-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH3 → aq. KOH → HO-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH3 (no chiral center, no optical isomerism) 2. Br on secondary carbon (2-position): CH3-CHBr-CH2-CH2-CH3 → aq. KOH → CH3-CHOH-CH2-CH2-CH3 (has one chiral center, shows optical isomerism) ✓ 3. Br on tertiary carbon (3-position): CH3-CH2-CHBr-CH2-CH3 → aq. KOH → CH3-CH2-CHOH-CH2-CH3 (has one chiral center, shows optical isomerism) ✓ 4. Branched with Br on secondary carbon: (CH3)2CH-CHBr-CH3 → aq. KOH → (CH3)2CH-CHOH-CH3 (has one chiral center, shows optical isomerism) ✓ 5. Branched with Br on tertiary carbon: (CH3)3C-CH2Br → aq. KOH → (CH3)3C-CH2OH (no chiral center, no optical isomerism) 6. Geminal disubstituted isomer: CH3-CHBr-CHBr-CH2-CH3 would be C5H10Br2 (not matching formula) 7. Another secondary position isomer: CH3-CH2-CHBr-CH2-CH3 → aq. KOH → CH3-CH2-CHOH-CH2-CH3 (one chiral center) ✓ 8. With methyl branches: CH3-CHBr-(CH3)CH-CH3 → aq. KOH → CH3-CHOH-(CH3)CH-CH3 (one chiral center, shows optical isomerism) ✓ 9. Another branched isomer: (CH3)2CH-CH2-CH2Br → aq. KOH → (CH3)2CH-CH2-CH2OH (no chiral center) 10. Branched secondary position: (CH3)2CH-CH2-CHBr-CH3 would exceed 5 carbons As per the language given and considering the condition we are going with answer 3 and considering both active isomers we will be giving 6 too. Products showing optical isomerism: 3 (or 6 if counting enantiomers separately)
