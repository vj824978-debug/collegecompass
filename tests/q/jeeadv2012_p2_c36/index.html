The given graph / data I, II, III and IV represent general trends observed for different physisorption and chemisorption processes under mild conditions of temperature and pressure. Which of the following choice (s) about I, II, III and IV is (are) correct [Four graphs shown: (i) Amount of gas adsorbed vs T at P constant - shows decreasing curve (ii) Amount of gas adsorbed vs T at P constant - shows increasing curve (iii) Amount of gas adsorbed vs P - shows two curves at 200 K and 250 K, both increasing (iv) Potential Energy vs Distance of molecule from surface - shows energy well with Eads marked, ΔHads = 150 kJ mol⁻¹]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) I is physisorption and II is chemisorption
  2. (B) I is physisorption and III is chemisorption
  3. (C) IV is chemisorption and II is chemisorption
  4. (D) IV is chemisorption and III is chemisorption

Answer

Correct answer: AC

Solution

In physisorption on increasing temperature at constant pressure, adsorption decreases while in chemical adsorption on increasing temperature due to requirement of activation energy adsorption will increase at same pressure. So, I is physisorption while II is chemisorption. III is physical adsorption as on increasing temperature, extent of adsorption is decreasing. IV is representing enthalpy change (which is high) during chemical adsorption (due to bond formation) So, is valid for chemical adsorption. So, answer is (A) and (C)
