A line l passing through the origin is perpendicular to the lines l₁: (3+t)î+(-1+2t)ĵ+(4+2t)k̂, -∞<t<∞, l₂: (3+2s)î+(3+2s)ĵ+(2+s)k̂, -∞<s<∞ Then, the coordinate(s) of the point(s) on l₂ at a distance of sqrt(17) from the point of intersection of l and l₁ is (are)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) (7/3, 7/3, 5/3)
  2. (B) (-1, -1, 0)
  3. (C) (1, 1, 1)
  4. (D) (7/9, 7/9, 8/9)

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

Sol. (B, D) [Figure showing two skew lines ℓ₁ and ℓ₂ with point M at origin and perpendicular distance marked] The common perpendicular is along |i j k | |1 2 2 | = -2i + 3j - 2k |2 2 1 | Let M = (2λ, -3λ, 2λ) So, (2λ - 3)/1 = (-3λ + 1)/2 = (2λ - 4)/2 ⟹ λ = 1 So, M ≡ (2, -3, 2) Let the required point be P Given that PM = √17 ⟹ (3 + 2s - 2)² + (3 + 2s + 3)² + (2 + s - 2)² = 17 ⟹ 9s² + 28s + 20 = 0 ⟹ s = -2, -10/9 So, P ≡ (-1, -1, 0) or P ≡ (7/9, 7/9, 8/9)
