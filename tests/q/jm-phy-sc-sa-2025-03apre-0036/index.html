Using a battery, a 100 pF capacitor is charged to 60 V and then the battery is removed. After that, a second uncharged capacitor is connected to the first capacitor in parallel. If the final voltage across the second capacitor is 20 V, its capacitance is: (in pF)

  1. (1) 600
  2. (2) 200
  3. (3) 100
  4. (4) 400

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Initial charge on first capacitor: Q1 = C1 × V1 = 100 pF × 60 V = 6000 pC. When second uncharged capacitor is connected in parallel, charge redistributes. Final voltage across both capacitors = 20 V. Using charge conservation: Q1 = Q1_final + Q2_final, where Q1_final = C1 × 20 = 100 × 20 = 2000 pC and Q2_final = C2 × 20. Therefore: 6000 = 2000 + C2 × 20, which gives C2 × 20 = 4000, so C2 = 200 pF.
