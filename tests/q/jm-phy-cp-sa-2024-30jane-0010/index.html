In a nuclear fission reaction of an isotope of mass M, three similar daughter nuclei of same mass are formed. The speed of a daughter nuclei in terms of mass defect ΔM will be:

  1. (1) c*sqrt(3ΔM/M)
  2. (2) c*sqrt(2cΔM/M)
  3. (3) ΔMc^2/3
  4. (4) c*sqrt(2ΔM/M)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From conservation of energy: (M - 3m)c^2 = 3(1/2*m*v^2). Energy released equals kinetic energy of three daughter nuclei. With Δm = M - 3m and m = (M - Δm)/3, substituting into energy equation gives v = sqrt(2Δmc^2/(M - Δm)) = c*sqrt(2ΔM/M)
