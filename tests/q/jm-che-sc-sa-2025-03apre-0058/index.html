10 mL of 2M NaOH solution is added to 20 mL of 1M HCl solution kept in a beaker. Now, 10 mL of this mixture is poured into a volumetric flask of 100 mL containing 2 moles of HCl and made the volume upto the mark with distilled water. The solution in this flask is:

  1. (1) 20 M HCl solution
  2. (2) 10 M HCl solution
  3. (3) 0.2 M NaCl solution
  4. (4) Neutral solution

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Initial: 20 mL of 1M HCl = 20 mmol HCl; 10 mL of 2M NaOH = 20 mmol NaOH. These completely neutralize each other forming NaCl. The mixture is neutral (20 mmol NaCl in 30 mL). When 10 mL of this mixture is taken, it contains (20 mmol NaCl × 10/30) = 6.67 mmol NaCl. This is added to 100 mL flask containing 2 moles (2000 mmol) HCl. Final concentration of HCl ≈ 2000/100 = 20 M, but since excess HCl dominates, solution is acidic. The answer is option 1: 20 M HCl solution (approximately, as the small amount of NaCl is negligible).
