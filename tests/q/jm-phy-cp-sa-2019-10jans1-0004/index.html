Consider the nuclear fission Ne^20 → 2He^4 + C^12. Given that the binding energy/nucleon of Ne^20, He^4 and C^12 are, respectively, 8.03 MeV, 7.07 MeV and 7.86 MeV, identify the correct statement

  1. (1) 8.3 MeV energy will be released
  2. (2) energy of 11.9 MeV has to be supplied
  3. (3) energy of 12.4 MeV will be supplied
  4. (4) energy of 3.6 MeV will be released

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

E_supplied = E_1 = 20 × BE_N of Ne^20 E_released = 12 × BE_N of C^12 + 2 × 4 × BE_N of He^4 Supplied energy = 20 × BE_N of Ne^20 - 12 × BE_N of C^12 - 2 × 4 × BE_N of He^4 = 20 × 8.03 - 12 × 7.86 - 8 × 7.07 = 160.6 - 94.32 - 56.56 = 11.9 MeV Since the calculated value is positive, energy of 11.9 MeV has to be supplied to the system for the fission to occur.
