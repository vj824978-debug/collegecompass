The major products A and B in the following reactions are: Starting with a compound with tert-butyl group and CN group, treated with Peroxide and Heat to form [A], then [A] reacted with a diene (shown as wavy bonds) to form B

  1. (1) A = tert-butyl group with CN and radical, and B = long chain with tert-butyl and terminal CN
  2. (2) A = tert-butyl group with CN and radical, and B = long chain with two methyl branches and CN group
  3. (3) A = tert-butyl group with CN and radical, and B = long chain with tert-butyl and CN at end
  4. (4) A = tert-butyl group with CN and radical, and B = compact structure with tert-butyl and CN

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The reaction involves homolytic cleavage of a nitrile compound under peroxide and heat conditions: 1) Initial compound: tert-butyl-CH(CN) is treated with peroxide and heat 2) Under peroxide/heat conditions, homolytic cleavage occurs to generate a carbon-centered radical adjacent to the CN group: - The C-H bond breaks homolytically - This produces a radical intermediate [A] with the structure: tert-butyl-C•(CN) 3) Product A is the tert-butyl radical with CN substituent (carbon-centered radical) 4) This radical [A] then undergoes radical addition to the conjugated diene: - The carbon radical adds to the pi-bond of the diene - This is a radical addition reaction following Markovnikov's rule - The diene chain gets incorporated with the CN group remaining 5) Product B is formed by addition of the radical A to the diene, resulting in a longer chain structure with the tert-butyl group, extended carbon chain from diene addition, and CN group at the terminal position 6) The final structure of B shows a linear chain with tert-butyl branch and CN at one end, matching option (3)
