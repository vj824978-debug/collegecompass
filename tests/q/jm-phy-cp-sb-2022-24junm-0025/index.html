Sodium light of wavelengths 650 nm and 655 nm is used to study diffraction at a single slit of aperture 0.5 mm. The distance between the slit and the screen is 2.0 m. The separation between the positions of the first maxima of diffraction pattern obtained in the two cases is ____ × 10^-5 m.

Answer

Correct answer: 3.00

Solution

Position of 1st maxima in single slit diffraction is y = (3 λ D)/(2 a). For λ₁ = 650 nm and λ₂ = 655 nm, separation between first maxima = (3/2) × (655 nm - 650 nm) × (D/a) = (3/2) × (5 nm) × (2 m)/(0.5 mm) = (3/2) × 5 × 10^-9 × (2)/(0.5 × 10^-3) = 3 × 10^-5 m
