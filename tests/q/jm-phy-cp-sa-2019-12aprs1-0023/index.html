A magnetic compass needle oscillates 30 times per minute at a place where the dip is 45°, and 40 times per minute where the dip is 30°. If B_1 and B_2 are respectively the total magnetic field due to the earth at the two places, then the ratio B_1/B_2 is best given by:

  1. (1) 1.8
  2. (2) 2.2
  3. (3) 0.7
  4. (4) 3.6

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

τ = m × B ⇒ τ = -mB sin θ ⇒ τ = -mBθ (small angular displacement) ⇒ Iα = -m(B cos δ)θ ⇒ α = -(mB cos δ / I)θ ⇒ ω^2 = mB cos δ / I T = 2π√(I / (mB cos δ)) T_1/T_2 = √(B_2 cos δ_2 / (B_1 cos δ_1)) (60/30)/(60/40) = √(B_2 cos δ_2 / (B_1 cos δ_1)) (4/3)^2 = (B_2 cos δ_2) / (B_1 cos δ_1) ⇒ 16 cos δ_1 / (9 cos δ_2) = B_2/B_1 ⇒ B_1/B_2 = (9 cos δ_2) / (16 cos δ_1) ⇒ B_1/B_2 = (9 × √3√2) / (16 × 2×1) = (9 × √6) / 32 = (9 × 2.44) / 32 = 22/32 = 0.7
