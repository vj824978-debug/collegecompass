A particle is moving in a circle of radius 50 cm in such a way that at any instant the normal and tangential components of its acceleration are equal. If its speed at t = 0 is 4 m/s, the time taken to complete the first revolution will be (1/alpha)[1 - e^(-2*pi)] s, where alpha = ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

Given: radius r = 50 cm = 0.5 m, v0 = 4 m/s, normal acceleration = tangential acceleration. Normal acceleration = v^2/r, tangential acceleration = dv/dt. Setting equal: v^2/r = dv/dt. Rearranging: dt = (r*dv)/v^2. For one complete revolution, s = 2*pi*r. From v^2/r = dv/dt and integrating with s = integral(v*dt): ln(v/v0) = s/r = 2*pi. Thus v = v0*e^(2*pi). Also: t/r = 1/v0 - 1/(v0*e^(2*pi)). Simplifying: t = (r/v0)[1 - e^(-2*pi)] = (1/alpha)[1 - e^(-2*pi)]. With r = 0.5 m and v0 = 4 m/s: alpha = v0/r = 4/0.5 = 8.
