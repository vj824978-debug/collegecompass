Which one of the following alkenes when treated with HCl yields majorly an anti Markovnikov product?

  1. (1) CH3O-CH=CH2
  2. (2) H2N-CH=CH2
  3. (3) F3C-CH=CH2
  4. (4) Cl-CH=CH2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

F3C-CH=CH2 yields majorly an anti Markovnikov product when treated with HCl. Explanation: The CF3 group is the most electron withdrawing group among all the given substituents. It withdraws electrons through the -H effect (inductive effect). In the presence of an electron-withdrawing group like CF3 on one carbon of the double bond, the electron density on that carbon decreases significantly. This makes the other carbon of the double bond more electron-rich and more susceptible to attack by the nucleophile (Cl-). According to Markovnikov's rule, H+ adds to the carbon with more hydrogen atoms. However, the strong electron-withdrawing CF3 group reverses the normal regioselectivity, leading to anti-Markovnikov addition. The electron-withdrawing CF3 group stabilizes the negative charge that would develop on the adjacent carbon during the reaction mechanism, favoring the anti-Markovnikov product. Among the options: (1) CH3O- is electron donating (activating group) (2) H2N- is electron donating (activating group) (3) F3C- is strongly electron withdrawing (deactivating group) - CORRECT (4) Cl- is weakly electron withdrawing
