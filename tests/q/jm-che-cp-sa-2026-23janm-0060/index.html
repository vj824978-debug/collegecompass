But-2-yne and hydrogen (one mole each) are separately treated with (i) Pd/C and (ii) Na/liq. NH3 to give the products X and Y respectively. Identify the incorrect statements.

  1. (1) X and Y are stereoisomers.
  2. (2) Dipole moment of X is zero
  3. (3) Boiling point of X is higher than Y.
  4. (4) X and Y react with O3/Zn + H2O to give different products.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

But-2-yne: CH3-C≡C-CH3 Treatment with Pd/C (Lindlar's catalyst): Pd/C is a selective catalyst that converts alkynes to cis-alkenes. But-2-yne + H2 with Pd/C → Cis-But-2-ene (X) Structure: H3C and CH3 groups are on the same side (cis configuration) Treatment with Na/liq. NH3: Na/liq. NH3 converts alkynes to trans-alkenes. But-2-yne + H2 with Na/liq. NH3 → Trans-But-2-ene (Y) Structure: H3C and CH3 groups are on opposite sides (trans configuration) Analyzing each statement: A. X and Y are stereoisomers. Cis-but-2-ene and trans-but-2-ene are indeed stereoisomers (geometric isomers). This statement is CORRECT. B. Dipole moment of X is zero. Cis-but-2-ene has both methyl groups on the same side. The molecule is not symmetric, so it has a net dipole moment ≠ 0. This statement is INCORRECT. C. Boiling point of X is higher than Y. Cis-but-2-ene (X): bp ≈ 3.7°C Trans-but-2-ene (Y): bp ≈ 0.9°C Cis isomer has higher boiling point due to greater polarity and dipole-dipole interactions. This statement is CORRECT. D. X and Y react with O3/Zn + H2O to give different products. Both cis and trans but-2-ene have the same molecular structure (C4H8). Ozonolysis breaks the C=C double bond: Both X and Y → 2 CH3-CHO (acetaldehyde) Since the double bond is between identical carbons with identical substituents, both give the same product. This statement is INCORRECT. Incorrect statements are B and D. However, looking at options: the question asks to identify incorrect statements. Option (1) states "B and C only" - this would mean A and D are correct, but D is incorrect. Let me reconsider: The correct answer provided is [2], which is "B and D only". This makes sense: B is incorrect (dipole moment ≠ 0) and D is incorrect (both give same ozonolysis product).
