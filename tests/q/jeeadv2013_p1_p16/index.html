A bob of mass m, suspended by a string of length l₁ is given a minimum velocity required to complete a full circle in the vertical plane. At the highest point, it collides elastically with another bob of mass m suspended by a string of length l₂, which is initially at rest. Both the strings are mass-less and inextensible. If the second bob, after collision acquires the minimum speed required to complete a full circle in the vertical plane, the ratio l₁/l₂ is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

The initial speed of 1ˢᵗ bob (suspended by a string of length l₁)is √(5gl₁). The speed of this bob at highest point will be √(gl₁). When this bob collides with the other bob there speeds will be interchanged. √(gl₁) = √(5gl₂) ⟹ l₁/l₂ = 5
