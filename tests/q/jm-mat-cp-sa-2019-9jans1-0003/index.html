The maximum volume (in cu.m) of the right circular cone having slant height 3 m is :

  1. (1) 6π
  2. (2) (4/3)π
  3. (3) 2sqrt(3)π
  4. (4) 3sqrt(3)π

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For a right circular cone with slant height l = 3 m, radius r, and height h: Given: h^2 + r^2 = 9 (slant height constraint) Volume formula: V = (1/3)πr^2h Substituting r^2 = 9 - h^2: V = (1/3)π(9 - h^2)h To find maximum volume: dV/dh = (1/3)π[9 - 3h^2] For maximum: dV/dh = 0 9 - 3h^2 = 0 3h^2 = 9 h^2 = 3 h = sqrt(3) Verify maximum (second derivative test): d^2V/dh^2 = (1/3)π(-6h) = (1/3)π(-6sqrt(3)) < 0 ✓ (confirms maximum) When h = sqrt(3): r^2 = 9 - h^2 = 9 - 3 = 6 r = sqrt(6) Maximum volume: V_max = (1/3)π × 6 × sqrt(3) = 2sqrt(3)π cu.m
