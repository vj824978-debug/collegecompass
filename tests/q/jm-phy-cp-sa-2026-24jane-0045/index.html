Distance between an object and three times magnified real image is 40 cm. The focal length of the mirror used is ___ cm.

  1. (1) -15/2
  2. (2) -10
  3. (3) -20
  4. (4) -15

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

m = -3 = v/u v = -3u |v| - |u| = 40 |-3u| - |u| = 40 3|u| - |u| = 40 2|u| = 40 u = 20 cm v = 60 cm Using mirror formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f 1/(-60) + 1/(-20) = 1/f -1/60 - 1/20 = 1/f -1/60 - 3/60 = 1/f -4/60 = 1/f f = -15 cm
