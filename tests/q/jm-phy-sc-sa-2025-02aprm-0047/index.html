A vessel with square cross section and height of 6 m is vertically partitioned. A small window of 100 cm^2 with hinged door is fitted at a depth of 3 m in the partitioned wall. One part of the vessel is filled completely with water and the other side is filled with liquid having density 1.5×10^3 kg/m^3. What force one needs to apply on the hinged door so that it does not get opened? (Acceleration due to gravity = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 150

Solution

The window is at depth 3 m. Water side: pressure at 3 m depth = ρ_water × g × h = 1000 × 10 × 3 = 30,000 Pa. Liquid side: pressure at 3 m depth = 1.5×10^3 × 10 × 3 = 45,000 Pa. Net pressure difference = 45,000 - 30,000 = 15,000 Pa (liquid side pushes harder). Force on door = pressure difference × area = 15,000 × (100 × 10^-4) = 15,000 × 0.01 = 150 N. The applied force must be at least 150 N to prevent opening.
