Metal M crystallizes into a fcc lattice with the edge length of 4.0 × 10^(-8) cm. The atomic mass of the metal is _____ g/mol. (Nearest integer) (Use: N_A = 6.02 × 10^23 mol^(-1), density of metal, M = 9.03 g cm^(-3))

Answer

Correct answer: 87

Solution

For fcc lattice, number of atoms per unit cell Z = 4. Using density formula: ρ = ZM/(N_A × a^3), we can rearrange to find M = (ρ × N_A × a^3)/Z. Substituting values: M = (9.03 × 6.02 × 10^23 × (4.0 × 10^(-8))^3)/4 = (9.03 × 6.02 × 10^23 × 64 × 10^(-24))/4 = (9.03 × 6.02 × 64 × 10^(-1))/4 = 86.9 ≈ 87 g/mol
