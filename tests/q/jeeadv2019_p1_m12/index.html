There are three bags B₁, B₂ and B₃. The bag B₁ contains 5 red and 5 green balls, B₂ contains 3 red and 5 green balls and B₃ contains 5 red and 3 green balls. Bags B₁, B₂ and B₃ have probabilities 3/10, 3/10, and 4/10 respectively of being chosen. A bag is selected at random and a ball is chosen at random from the bag. Then which of the following options is/are correct?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) Probability that the chosen ball is green equals 39/80
  2. (B) Probability that the chosen ball is green, given that the selected bag is B₃, equals 3/8
  3. (C) Probability that the selected bag is B₃ and the chosen ball is green equals 3/10
  4. (D) Probability that the selected bag is B₃, given that the chosen ball is green, equals 5/13

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

Sol. P(B₁) = 3/10, P(B₂) = 3/10, P(B₃) = 4/10 (A) P(G) = P(B₁) × P(G/B₁) + P(B₂) × P(G/B₂) + P(B₃) × P(G/B₃) = (3/10) × (5/10) + (3/10) × (5/8) + (4/10) × (3/8) = (60 + 75 + 60)/400 = 195/400 = 39/80 (B) P(G/B₃) = 3/8 (C) P(B₃/G) = [P(B₃) × P(G/B₃)]/P(G) = [(4/10) × (3/8)]/(39/80) = 4/13
