In the given structure, number of sp and sp^2 hybridized carbon atoms present respectively are:

  1. (1) 3 and 6
  2. (2) 3 and 5
  3. (3) 4 and 6
  4. (4) 4 and 5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

To determine the number of sp and sp^2 hybridized carbon atoms, we need to analyze the structure: The structure shows: - A carbonyl group (C=O) at the left: sp^2 hybridized carbon - Alternating single and double bonds in the middle section - A triple bond (C≡C) in the middle: both carbons are sp hybridized - Another double bond section - Another triple bond (C≡N) at the end: the carbon is sp hybridized Counting sp hybridized carbons (involved in triple bonds): - One carbon in the C≡C triple bond - One carbon in the C≡N triple bond Total sp hybridized = 3 (including the carbon bonded to N in the nitrile group and accounting for the structure) Counting sp^2 hybridized carbons (involved in double bonds or resonance): - Carbonyl carbon (C=O): 1 sp^2 - Carbons in C=C double bonds: multiple sp^2 carbons Total sp^2 hybridized = 5 Therefore, the number of sp and sp^2 hybridized carbon atoms are 3 and 5 respectively.
