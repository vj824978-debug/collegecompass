The mean and standard deviation of the marks of 10 students were found to be 50 and 12 respectively. Later, it was observed that two marks 20 and 25 were wrongly read as 45 and 50 respectively. Then the correct variance is

Answer

Correct answer: 269

Solution

Given: n=10, mean=50, SD=12. So variance=144. Sum of marks (wrong)=500. Sum of squares (wrong)=26440. Correct sum: 500-45-50+20+25=450, so correct mean=45. Correct sum of squares: 26440-45^2-50^2+20^2+25^2=26440-2025-2500+400+625=22940. Correct variance = 22940/10 - (45)^2 = 2294 - 2025 = 269
