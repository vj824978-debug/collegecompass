The number of given statement/s which is/are correct is____

  1. (1) The stronger the temperature dependence of the rate constant, the higher is the activation energy.
  2. (2) If a reaction has zero activation energy, its rate is independent of temperature.
  3. (3) The stronger the temperature dependence of the rate constant, the smaller is the activation energy.
  4. (4) If there is no correlation between the temperature and the rate constant then it means that the reaction has negative activation energy.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Rate constant is given by Arrhenius equation: k = Ae^(-Ea/RT). From this equation, we can analyze each statement: (A) Stronger temperature dependence of k means higher Ea - CORRECT. (B) If Ea = 0, then k = A (independent of T) - CORRECT. (C) Stronger temperature dependence means higher Ea, not smaller - INCORRECT. (D) No correlation between T and k doesn't mean negative Ea; it means Ea ≈ 0 - INCORRECT. Therefore, statements (A) and (B) are correct, giving us 2 correct statements.
