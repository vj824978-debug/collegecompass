Which one of the following statements is correct for electrolysis of brine solution? (1) Cl2 is formed at cathode (2) H2 is formed at anode (3) O2 is formed at cathode (4) OH- is formed at cathode

  1. (1) Cl2 is formed at cathode
  2. (2) H2 is formed at anode
  3. (3) O2 is formed at cathode
  4. (4) OH- is formed at cathode

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

During electrolysis of Brine 2NaCl → Na+ + Cl- 2H2O → 2H+ + 2OH- Cathode: 2H+ + 2e- → H2 Anode: 2Cl- → Cl2 + 2e. At cathode H2 is liberated At anode Cl2 is formed. Since H2 is formed at cathode through reduction of H+ ions, and OH- ions remain in solution near the cathode region, OH- is formed/produced at cathode (in the sense that it accumulates in the cathode region as the H+ ions are consumed).
