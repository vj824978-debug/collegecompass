A conducting circular loop is placed in a uniform magnetic field of 0.4 T with its plane perpendicular to the field. Somehow, the radius of the loop starts expanding at a constant rate of 1 mm/s. The magnitude of induced emf in the loop at an instant when the radius of the loop is 2 cm will be ______ μV.

Answer

Correct answer: 50

Solution

Using Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction: emf = -dΦ/dt where Φ = πR^2 × B. Taking derivative: emf = 2πR × (dR/dt) × B. Substituting R = 2 cm = 2 × 10^(-2) m, dR/dt = 1 mm/s = 10^(-3) m/s, B = 0.4 T: emf = 2 × (22/7) × 2 × 10^(-2) × 10^(-3) × 0.4 = 5 × 10^(-5) V = 50.28 μV ≈ 50 μV
