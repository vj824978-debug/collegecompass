A copper wire is wound on a wooden frame, whose shape is that of an equilateral triangle. If the linear dimension of each side of the frame is increased by a factor of 3, keeping the number of turns of the coil per unit length of the frame the same, then the self inductance of the coil:

  1. (1) decreases by a factor of 9*sqrt(3)
  2. (2) increases by a factor of 27
  3. (3) decreases by a factor of 9
  4. (4) increases by a factor of 3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: Self inductance formula L = (p*N^2*A)/ℓ Where: - N = number of turns - A = cross-sectional area of the loop - ℓ = length of the frame Given conditions: - N/ℓ = constant (number of turns per unit length is the same) - Therefore N ∝ ℓ For an equilateral triangle: - A = (sqrt(3)/4)*a^2, where a is the side length When all dimensions increase by a factor of 3: - ℓ becomes 3ℓ - a becomes 3a - A becomes (sqrt(3)/4)*(3a)^2 = 9*(sqrt(3)/4)*a^2 = 9A - N becomes 3N (since N ∝ ℓ and ℓ → 3ℓ) Therefore: L_new = (p*(3N)^2*(9A))/(3ℓ) L_new = (p*9*N^2*9*A)/(3ℓ) L_new = (81*p*N^2*A)/(3ℓ) L_new = 27*(p*N^2*A)/ℓ L_new = 27*L Hence L becomes 27 times (increases by a factor of 27)
