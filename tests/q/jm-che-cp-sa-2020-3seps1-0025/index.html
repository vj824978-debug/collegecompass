An element with molar mass 2.7 × 10^(-2) kg mol^(-1) forms cubic unit cell with edge length 405 pm. If its density is 2.7 × 10^3 kg m^(-3), the radius of the element is approximately ________ × 10^(-12) m (to the nearest integer).

Answer

Correct answer: 143

Solution

Given: Molar mass M = 2.7 × 10^(-2) kg mol^(-1) Edge length a = 405 pm = 405 × 10^(-12) m Density d = 2.7 × 10^3 kg m^(-3) NA = 6 × 10^23 Using density formula: d = (Z × M)/(NA × a^3) 2.7 × 10^3 = Z × (2.7 × 10^(-2))/(6 × 10^23 × (405 × 10^(-12))^3) 2.7 × 10^3 = Z × (2.7 × 10^(-2))/(6 × 10^23 × 66.43 × 10^(-30)) 3.98 = Z z ≈ 4 structure is fcc For fcc structure: a/sqrt(2) = 2r r = a/(2sqrt(2)) = (sqrt(2)a)/4 = (1.414 × 405 × 10^(-12))/4 r = 143.16 × 10^(-12) m r ≈ 143 × 10^(-12) m
