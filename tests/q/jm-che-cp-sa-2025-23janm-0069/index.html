Match the LIST-I with LIST-II LIST-I (Classification of molecules based on octet rule): A. Molecules obeying octet rule B. Molecules with incomplete octet C. Molecules with incomplete octet with odd electron D. Molecules with expanded octet LIST-II (Example): I. NO, NO2 II. BCl3, AlCl3 III. H2SO4, PCl5 IV. CCl4, CO2 Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
  2. (2) A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV
  3. (3) A-IV, B-I, C-III, D-II
  4. (4) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

(A) A → IV CO2 and CCl4 obey the octet rule. In CO2, carbon has 4 bonding pairs (2 double bonds), and in CCl4, carbon has 4 bonding pairs (4 single bonds). Both achieve complete octets. (B) B → II BCl3 and AlCl3 have incomplete octets. Boron in BCl3 has only 6 electrons around it (3 bonding pairs), and aluminum in AlCl3 has only 6 electrons around it (3 bonding pairs). Neither achieves an octet. (C) C → I NO and NO2 have odd electrons. NO has 11 valence electrons (unpaired), and NO2 has 17 valence electrons (unpaired). Both are radicals with incomplete octets and odd electron count. (D) D → III H2SO4 and PCl5 have expanded octets. Sulfur in H2SO4 has more than 8 electrons around it (forms bonds with 4 oxygen atoms), and phosphorus in PCl5 has 10 electrons around it (5 bonding pairs). Both exceed the octet. Therefore, the correct matching is A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III, which corresponds to option (1).
