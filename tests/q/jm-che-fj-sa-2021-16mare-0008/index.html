Identify the reagent(s) 'A' and condition(s) for the reaction: Benzene to 1,4-dichlorobenzene

  1. (1) A = HCl; Anhydrous AlCl3
  2. (2) A = Cl2; UV light
  3. (3) A = Cl2; dark, Anhydrous AlCl3
  4. (4) A = HCl, ZnCl2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The reaction converts benzene to 1,4-dichlorobenzene (para-dichlorobenzene). In presence of UV light, Cl2 is converted into free radicals (Cl•) which attack the allylic carbon for substitution via free radical mechanism. The reaction proceeds as: Cl2 under UV light gives 2Cl• radicals, which then attack the benzene ring. Since the product is 1,4-dichlorobenzene with chlorine atoms in para positions on the benzene ring, the reagent is Cl2 under UV light conditions. The correct answer is option (B).
