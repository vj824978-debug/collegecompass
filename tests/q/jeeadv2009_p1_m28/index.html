Tangents drawn from the point P (1, 8) to the circle x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 4y - 11 = 0 touch the circle at the points A and B. The equation of the circumcircle of the triangle PAB is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 6y + 19 = 0
  2. (B) x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 10y + 19 = 0
  3. (C) x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 6y - 29 = 0
  4. (D) x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 4y + 19 = 0

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol. (B) The centre of the circle is C (3, 2). Since CA and CB are perpendicular to PA and PB, CP is the diameter of the circumcircle of triangle PAB. Its equation is (x − 3)(x − 1) + (y − 2)(y − 8) = 0 or x² + y² − 4x − 10y + 19 = 0.
