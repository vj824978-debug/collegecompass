The molar solubility of Cd(OH)2 is 1.84 × 10^(-5) M in water. The expected solubility of Cd(OH)2 in a buffer solution of pH = 12 is:

  1. (1) 2.49 × 10^(-10) M
  2. (2) 1.84 × 10^(-9) M
  3. (3) 6.23 × 10^(-11) M
  4. (4) (2.49/1.84) × 10^(-9) M

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

[OH^(-)] = 10^(-2) for Buffer solution Ksp = [Cd^(2+)][OH^(-)]^2 [Cd^(2+)] = Ksp/[OH^(-)]^2 = solubility in buffer solution ... (1) while Ksp = 4S^3 for Cd(OH)2 => Ksp = 4 × (1.84 × 10^(-5))^3 ... (2) So solubility in Buffer solution is [Cd^(2+)] = Ksp/[OH^(-)]^2 = (4 × (1.84 × 10^(-5))^3)/(10^(-2))^2 = 24.9 × 10^(-11) [Cd^(2+)] = 24.9 × 10^(-11) Solubility = 2.49 × 10^(-10)
