Molarity (M) of an aqueous solution containing x g of anhydrous CuSO4 in 500 mL solution at 32°C is 2 × 10^-1 M. Its molality will be _______ × 10^-m. (nearest integer). [Given density of the solution = 1.25 g/mL.]

Answer

Correct answer: 164

Solution

Mass of solution = Volume × density = 500 × 1.25 = 625 g. From molarity, n_solute = 0.1 mol. Mass of solute (CuSO4) = 0.1 × 0.5 × 159.5 = 15.95 g (where 159.5 is molar mass and 0.5 L is volume). Mass of solvent = 625 - 15.95 = 609.05 g. Molality m = (n_solute × 1000)/(mass of solvent in g) = (0.1 × 1000)/609.05 = 0.164 = 164 × 10^-3.
