Match the LIST-I with LIST-II LIST-I: Name reaction A. Swarts reaction B. Sandmeyer's reaction C. Wurtz Fittig reaction D. Finkelstein reaction LIST-II: Product obtainable I. Ethyl benzene II. Ethyl iodide III. Cyanobenzene IV. Ethyl fluoride Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
  2. (2) A-IV, B-I, C-III, D-II
  3. (3) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
  4. (4) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Analysis of each reaction: A. Swarts reaction: Et-I + KF/DMF → Et-F This reaction converts alkyl iodides to alkyl fluorides. Product: Ethyl fluoride (IV) Match: A-IV B. Sandmeyer's reaction: PhN2(+)Cl(-) + CuCN/KCN → PhCN + N2 This reaction converts diazonium salts to nitriles. Product: Cyanobenzene (III) Match: B-III C. Wurtz Fittig reaction: Ph-Cl + EtCl + Na/ether → Ph-Et + Ph-Ph + Et-Et This reaction couples aryl halides with alkyl halides in the presence of sodium. Product: Ethyl benzene (I) Match: C-I D. Finkelstein reaction: Et-Cl + NaI/acetone → Et-I + NaCl This reaction converts alkyl chlorides to alkyl iodides. Product: Ethyl iodide (II) Match: D-II Final Matching: A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
