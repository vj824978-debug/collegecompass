A capacitor of 10 µF capacitance whose plates are separated by 10 mm through air and each plate has area 4 cm² is now filled equally with two dielectric media of K₁ = 2, K₂ = 3 respectively as shown in figure. If new force between the plates is 8 N. The supply voltage is ____ V.

Answer

Correct answer: 80

Solution

The capacitor is divided into two parts with dielectric constants K₁ = 2 and K₂ = 3, each occupying half the area A/2. The capacitors are in parallel: C₁ = K₁ε₀(A/2)/d and C₂ = K₂ε₀(A/2)/d. Equivalent capacitance: Ceq = C₁ + C₂ = ε₀A(K₁ + K₂)/(2d) = ε₀A(2+3)/(2d) = 5ε₀A/(2d). With original capacitance 10 µF: Ceq = (5/2) × 10 = 25 µF. Using force between plates F = CV²/(2d) = 8 N, we get V² = 8 × 2 × 10⁻² / (25 × 10⁻⁶) = 6400, so V = 80 V.
