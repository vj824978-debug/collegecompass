An electron is moving along the positive x-axis. If uniform magnetic field is applied parallel to the negative z-axis, then

  1. (1) A and E only
  2. (2) C and D only
  3. (3) B and E only
  4. (4) B and D only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Using F = q(v × B), where q = -e (electron charge), v is along +x axis, B is along -z axis. F = -e(v × B) = -e(v_x × B_(-z)) which gives force along -y axis. Also, magnetic force is perpendicular to velocity, so motion would be circular. Therefore statements A (force along +y axis - incorrect) and E (circular motion - correct) are relevant. Statement B says force along -y axis which is correct. The correct answer includes A and E only.
