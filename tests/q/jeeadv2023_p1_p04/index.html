A bar of mass M = 1.00 kg and length L = 0.20 m is lying on a horizontal frictionless surface. One end of the bar is pivoted at a point about which it is free to rotate. A small mass m = 0.10 kg is moving on the same horizontal surface with 5.00 m·s⁻¹ speed on a path perpendicular to the bar. It hits the bar at a distance L/2 from the pivoted end and returns back on the same path with speed v. After this elastic collision, the bar rotates with an angular velocity ω. Which of the following statement is correct?

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) ω = 6.98 rad·s⁻¹ and v = 4.30 m·s⁻¹
  2. (B) ω = 3.75 rad·s⁻¹ and v = 4.30 m·s⁻¹
  3. (C) ω = 3.75 rad·s⁻¹ and v = 10.0 m·s⁻¹
  4. (D) ω = 6.80 rad·s⁻¹ and v = 4.10 m·s⁻¹

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

Sol. A: Li = Lf m × 5 × L/2 = ML²/3 × ω - mv × L/2 5 = 4ω/3 - v v₂ - v₁ = e(u₁ - u₂) L/2 ω - (-v) = l(5 - 0) ω/10 + v = 5 Solving (1) & (2) ω = 6.98 rad/sec v = 4.3 m/s [Diagram shows a vertical hinge with a horizontal rod/beam of mass m extending to the right]
