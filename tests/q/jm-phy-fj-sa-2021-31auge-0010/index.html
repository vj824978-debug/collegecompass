The magnetic field vector of an electromagnetic wave is given by B = B_0 * (i + j)/sqrt(2) * cos(kz - ωt), where i,j represents unit vector along x and y axis respectively. At t = 0 s, two electric charges q1 of 4π coulomb and q2 of 2π coulomb located at (0, 0, 3π/k) and (0, 0, 3π/k) respectively, have the same velocity of 0.5 c (where c is the velocity of light). The ratio of the force acting on charge q1 to q2 is:

  1. (1) 1 : sqrt(2)
  2. (2) sqrt(2) : 1
  3. (3) 2 : 1
  4. (4) 2*sqrt(2) : 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Force on q1: F_q1 = q(V × B) = 4π(0.5c) × B_0/sqrt(2) (cos(kz - ωt)i + cos(kz - ωt)j) = 4π(0.5c)(B_0/sqrt(2))cos(3π)k. Force on q2: F_q2 = q(V × B) = 2π(0.5c) × B_0/sqrt(2) (cos(kz - ωt)i + cos(kz - ωt)j) = 2π(0.5c)(B_0/sqrt(2))cos(3π)k. Ratio F_q1/F_q2 = 4π(0.5c) / 2π(0.5c) = 2:1
