Let x₁, x₂, x₃, ..., x₂₀ be in geometric progression with x₁ = 3 and the common ratio r = 1/2. A new data construction replacing each xᵢ by (xᵢ - i)². If x̄ is the mean of new data, then the greatest integer less than or equal to x̄ is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 142

Solution

Given: x₁, x₂, x₃, ..., x₂₀ in G.P. with x₁ = 3, r = 1/2. So xᵢ = 3·(1/2)^(i-1). New data replaces xᵢ with (xᵢ - i)². The mean x̄ = Σ(xᵢ - i)²/20 = Σ(xᵢ² - 2xᵢi + i²)/20. Computing the three sums: Σxᵢ² = 12(1 - 1/2⁴⁰), Σ(2xᵢi) = 6(4 - 11/2¹⁸), Σi² = 2870. After calculation: x̄ = (1/20)[12(1 - 1/2⁴⁰) - 6(4 - 11/2¹⁸) + 70×41] = 2858/20 - (12/240 - 66/2¹⁸)·(1/20) = 142 + small correction. Thus ⌊x̄⌋ = 142.
