The energy released in the fusion of 2 kg of hydrogen deep in the sun is E_H and the energy released in the fission of 2 kg of U-235 is E_U. The ratio E_H/E_U is approximately:

  1. (1) 9.13
  2. (2) 15.04
  3. (3) 7.62
  4. (4) 25.6

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

In each fusion reaction, 4 hydrogen nuclei are used. Energy released per nucleus of H = 26.7/4 MeV. Energy released by 2 kg hydrogen (E_H) = (2000/1) × N_A × (26.7/4) MeV. Energy released by 2 kg Uranium (E_U) = (2000/235) × N_A × 200 MeV. Therefore, E_H/E_U = 235 × (26.7)/(4 × 200) = 235 × 26.7/800 = 7.84, approximately close to 7.62
