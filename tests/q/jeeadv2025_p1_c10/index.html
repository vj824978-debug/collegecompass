Molar volume (V_m) of a van der Waals gas can be calculated by expressing the van der Waals equation as a cubic equation with V_m as the variable. The ratio (in mol dm^-3) of the coefficient of V_m^2 to the coefficient of V_m for a gas having van der Waals constant a = 6.0 dm^6 atm mol^-2 and b = 0.060 dm^3 mol^-1 at 300 K and 300 atm is______. Use: Universal gas constant (R) = 0.082 dm^3 atm mol^-1 K^-1.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: –7.1

Solution

(P + a/V_m²)(V_m − b) = RT, (For 1 mole) PV_m − Pb + a/V_m − ab/V_m² − RT = 0 PV_m³ − V_m²Pb + aV_m − ab − RTV_m² = 0 PV_m³ − V_m²(Pb + RT) + V_m a − ab = 0 Ratio = −(Pb + RT)/a = (300 × 0.06 + 0.082 × 300)/6 = −7.1
