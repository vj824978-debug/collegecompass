List-I shows four planar structures made of uniform solid rods each of mass m and length L. In the List-II the possible moment of inertia of these structures about an axis OOʹ which lies in the plane of the structures, are given. Choose the option that describes the correct match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II. List-I: (P) A structure with point O at the origin, point A on the vertical axis, point B on the horizontal axis to the right, point C at the intersection, with OA making 45° angle and OB making 90° angle, and OʹC marked above C. (Q) A structure showing a triangle with vertex at the top (marked C), with O marked on the axis through C, and Oʹ marked above. The angle at O is 60° on both sides. (R) A square structure rotated 45° (diamond orientation) with vertices at C (top), B (right), A (bottom), D (left), with O at the center and Oʹ marked above O. All internal angles marked as 90°. (S) An isosceles triangle with apex C at the top, base AB at the bottom, with O on the vertical axis through the base, and Oʹ marked above C. The angles at C are marked as 30° each. List-II: (1) 5/4 m² (2) 7/12 m² (3) 7/6 m² (4) 5/3 m² (5) 1/3 m² (A) P → 5, Q → 1, R → 4, S → 2 (B) P → 1, Q → 3, R → 4, S → 2 (C) P → 5, Q → 1, R → 2, S → 1 (D) P → 5, Q → 4, R → 2, S → 1

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) (A) P → 5, Q → 1, R → 4, S → 2
  2. (B) (B) P → 1, Q → 3, R → 4, S → 2
  3. (C) (C) P → 5, Q → 1, R → 2, S → 1
  4. (D) (D) P → 5, Q → 4, R → 2, S → 1

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

Solution: The moment of inertia of a uniform rod of mass m and length l about an axis passing through one end and making an angle θ with the rod is given by I = (ml²/3)sin²θ. For structure (P): The axis OO' makes an angle of 45° with both rods CA and CB. I_P = I_CA + I_CB = (ml²/3)sin²(45°) + (ml²/3)sin²(45°) = 2 × (ml²/3) × (1/2) = ml²/3. This matches with (5). For structure (Q): The axis OO' makes an angle of 60° with rods CA and CB. Rod AB is parallel to the axis at a distance d = l sin(60°) = (√3/2)l. I_Q = I_CA + I_CB + I_AB = 2 × ((ml²/3)sin²(60°)) + md² I_Q = 2 × (ml²/3) × (3/4) + m((√3/2)l)² = (ml²/2) + (3ml²/4) = 5ml²/4. This matches with (1). For structure (R): The axis OO' is the diagonal of the square, making an angle of 45° with all four rods. I_R = 4 × ((ml²/3)sin²(45°)) = 4 × (ml²/3) × (1/2) = 2ml²/3. This matches with (4). For structure (S): The axis OO' makes an angle of 30° with both rods CA and CB. I_S = 2 × ((ml²/3)sin²(30°)) = 2 × (ml²/3) × (1/4) = ml²/6. This matches with (2). Therefore, the correct matching is P→5, Q→1, R→4, S→2. Answer: P→5, Q→1, R→4, S→2
