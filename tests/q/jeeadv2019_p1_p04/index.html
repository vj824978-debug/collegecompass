In a radioactive sample, K-40 nuclei either decay into stable Ca-40 nuclei with decay constant 4.5 × 10^-10 per year or into stable Ar-40 nuclei with decay constant 0.5 × 10^-10 per year. Given that in this sample all the stable Ca-40 and Ar-40 nuclei are produced by the K-40 nuclei only. In time t × 10^9 years, if the ratio of the sum of stable Ca-40 and Ar-40 nuclei to the radioactive K-40 nuclei is 99, the value of t will be [Given: ln10 = 2.3]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 1.15
  2. (B) 4.6
  3. (C) 9.2
  4. (D) 2.3

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

So equivalent decay constant = λ₁ + λ₂ = 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ per year N/N₀ = e^(-λₜ) and given that (N₀ - N)/N = 99 So t = 9.2 × 10⁹ year [Decay scheme diagram showing: ⁴⁰₁₉K decaying via λ₁ to ⁴⁰₂₀Ca and via λ₂ to ⁴⁰₁₈Ar]
