The conductivity of a weak acid HA of concentration 0.001 mol L^(-1) is 2.0 × 10^(-5) S cm^(-1). If λ_m^∞(HA) = 190S cm^2 mol^(-1), the ionization constant (K_a) of HA is equal to _______ × 10^(-4) (Round off to the nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

Step 1: Calculate molar conductivity at infinite dilution λ_m = (K × 1000)/M = (2 × 10^(-5) × 10^3)/(10^(-3)) = 20 S·cm^2·mol^(-1). Step 2: Calculate degree of ionization α = λ_m/λ_m^∞ = 20/190 = 2/19. Step 3: Calculate K_a using the formula K_a = Ca^2/(1-a) = (10^(-3) × (2/19)^2)/(1 - 2/19) = (10^(-3) × 4/361)/(17/19) = 0.01238 × 10^(-3) = 12.38 × 10^(-4). Step 4: Round off to nearest integer = 12 × 10^(-4).
