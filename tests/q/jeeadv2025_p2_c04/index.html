The correct reaction/reaction sequence that would produce a dicarboxylic acid as the major product is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [Chemical structure: HO-CH2-CH2-Cl] with conditions (i) NaCN (ii) HO⁻, H2O (iii) H3O⁺
  2. (B) [Chemical structure: CHO-CHOH-CHOH-CHOH-CH2OH (glucose)] with condition Br2, H2O
  3. (C) [Chemical structure: cyclohexane with Br substituent] with conditions (i) KOH, EtOH (ii) KMnO4, H2SO4, Δ
  4. (D) [Chemical structure: (CH3)2CH-CO-CH(CH3)-CH2OH] with condition H2CrO4

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

Analyzing each reaction: (A) OH-CH₂-CH₂-Cl + NaCN, OH⁻, H₂O, H₃O⁺ → OH-CH₂-CH₂-COOH The Cl is replaced by CN⁻ (nucleophilic substitution), then the nitrile is hydrolyzed to carboxylic acid. This is correct. (B) CHO with (CHOH)₄ and CH₂OH + Br₂, H₂O → COOH with (CHOH)₄ and CH₂OH This represents glucose oxidation where the aldehyde group is oxidized to carboxylic acid by Br₂/H₂O. This is correct. (C) [Cyclohexane ring]-Br + KOH, EtOH; KMnO₄, H₂SO₄, Δ → [Cyclohexane ring]-COOH and -COOH (two carboxylic acids) The KOH/EtOH would cause elimination (E2) to form a double bond, then KMnO₄ oxidation would cleave the double bond to form two carboxylic acids. However, the product shown indicates both positions are carboxylic acids, which is incorrect for this mechanism. (D) Isobutyl ketone with -CH₂OH group + H₂CrO₄ → Same structure with -COOH The primary alcohol is oxidized to carboxylic acid by chromic acid. This is correct. The answer is C because the reaction mechanism and product are inconsistent with what would actually occur.
