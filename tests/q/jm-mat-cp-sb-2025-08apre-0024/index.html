Let the area of the bounded region {(x, y) : 0 ≤ 9x ≤ y², y ≥ 3x - 6} be A. Then 6A is equal to

Answer

Correct answer: 15

Solution

The region is bounded by the parabola x = y²/9, the line y = 3x - 6, and their intersection points. Finding intersection: y²/9 = (y+6)/3 gives y = -3 and y = 0. Area A = A₁ + A₂ where A₁ is the triangular part (base 3, height 1) = 3/2, and A₂ is the integral from -3 to 0 of (y²/9) dy = [y³/27] from -3 to 0 = 0 - (-27/27) = 1. Total A = 3/2 + 1 = 5/2. Therefore 6A = 6 × 5/2 = 15.
