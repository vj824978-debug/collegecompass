Electric potential at a point 'P' due to a point charge of 5 × 10^-9 C is 50 V. The distance of 'P' from the point charge is: (Assume, 1/(4πε₀) = 9 × 10^9 Nm^2C^-2)

  1. (1) 9 cm
  2. (2) 3 cm
  3. (3) 0.9 cm
  4. (4) 90 cm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using electric potential formula: V = (1/(4πε₀)) × (Q/r). Substituting values: 50 = 9 × 10^9 × (5 × 10^-9)/r. Solving: 50r = 9 × 10^9 × 5 × 10^-9 = 45. Therefore r = 0.9 m = 90 cm.
