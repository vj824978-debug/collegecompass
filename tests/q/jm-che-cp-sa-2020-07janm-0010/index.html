Identify the correct labels of A, B and C in the following graph from the option given below: The graph shows a distribution curve of number of molecules vs speed, with three vertical lines labeled A, B, and C at different positions on the speed axis.

  1. (1) A - V_rms; B - V_mp; C - V_av
  2. (2) A - V_mp; B - V_av; C - V_rms
  3. (3) A - V_mp; B - V_rms; C - V_av
  4. (4) A - V_av; B - V_rms; C - V_mp

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

From kinetic theory of gases, we know the relationship between three characteristic speeds: V_rms > V_av > V_mp Where: - V_rms = Root mean square speed - V_av = Average speed - V_mp = Most probable speed Looking at the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution curve: - The peak of the curve occurs at V_mp (most probable speed) - this is the leftmost vertical line - V_av (average speed) lies to the right of V_mp - this is the middle vertical line - V_rms (root mean square speed) lies furthest to the right - this is the rightmost vertical line From the graph, analyzing the positions from left to right: - A is at the leftmost position = V_mp - B is at the middle position = V_av - C is at the rightmost position = V_rms Therefore: A - V_mp; B - V_av; C - V_rms This corresponds to option (2).
