A steel wire of length 2 m and Young's modulus 2.0×10^11 Nm^-2 is stretched by a force. If Poisson ratio and transverse strain for the wire are 0.2 and 10^-3 respectively. Then the elastic potential energy density of the wire is ___ ×10^5 (in SI Units)

Answer

Correct answer: 25

Solution

Given: L = 2 m, Y = 2.0×10^11 Nm^-2, Poisson's ratio σ = 0.2, transverse strain = 10^-3. The longitudinal strain can be found using: transverse strain = -σ × longitudinal strain. Therefore, longitudinal strain = 10^-3/0.2 = 5×10^-3. Elastic potential energy density = (1/2) × Y × (longitudinal strain)^2 = (1/2) × 2.0×10^11 × (5×10^-3)^2 = (1/2) × 2.0×10^11 × 25×10^-6 = 2.5×10^5 Jm^-3
