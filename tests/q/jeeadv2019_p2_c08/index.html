Choose the correct option(s) for the following reaction sequence [Starting material: 4-methoxybenzene with an alkyne substituent bearing an aldehyde group (CHO)] i) H₂², dil.H₂SO₄ ii) AgNO₃, NH₄OH iii) Zn-Hg, conc. HCl → Q i) SOCl₂, pyridine ii) AlCl₃ → R Zn-Hg, conc. HCl → S Consider Q, R and S as major products

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) Q: [4-methoxyphenyl with 3-carbon chain with CO₂H group] R: [6-methoxy-3,4-dihydronaphthalen-1(2H)-one structure] S: [Not shown]
  2. (B) R: [6-methoxy-3,4-dihydronaphthalen-1(2H)-one structure] S: [6-methoxytetralin structure (fully saturated)]
  3. (C) Q: [4-methoxyphenyl with 3-carbon chain with CO₂H and OH group] S: [6-methoxytetralin structure (fully saturated)]
  4. (D) R: [6-methoxy-3,4-dihydronaphthalen-1(2H)-one structure] S: [6-methoxytetralin structure (fully saturated)]

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

The reaction sequence shows: 1. Starting material: phenylacetylene with aldehyde group (C≡C-CH₂-C(=O)H) 2. First step (H₂SO₄, dil. H₂SO₄): Hydration of triple bond gives intermediate with (CH₂)₂C(=O)H structure attached to benzene ring with OMe substituent 3. Second step (i) AgNO₃ + NH₄OH, (ii) Zn-Hg, HCl: Tollens oxidation followed by Clemmensen reduction converts aldehyde to CH₂ group, yielding compound Q with structure H₂C-(CH₂)₂-C(=O)-OH attached to methoxybenzene 4. Third step (i) SOCl₂/Py, (ii) AlCl₃: Friedel-Crafts acylation with acid chloride forms new C-C bond to benzene ring, creating bicyclic system (R) with ketone group in newly formed ring 5. Final step (Zn-Hg, HCl): Clemmensen reduction of ketone to CH₂ gives compound S with saturated fused ring system (tetralin derivative) Compounds identified: - A and B represent the two possible products or intermediates in this synthesis
