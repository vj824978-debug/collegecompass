A crystal is made up of metal ions 'M₁' ana 'M₂' and oxide ions. Oxide ions form a ccp lattice structure. The cation 'M₁' occupies 50% of octahedral voids and the cation 'M₂' occupies 12.5% of tetrahedral voids of oxide lattice. The oxidation numbers of 'M₁' and 'M₂' are, respectively:

  1. (1) +2, +4
  2. (2) +3, +1
  3. (3) +1, +3
  4. (4) +4, +2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

O^2- ions form ccp. O₄ (−8 charge) M₁ = 50% of O.V. ⇒ (50/100) × 4 = 2:(M₁)₂ M₂ = 12.5% of T.V. ⇒ (12.5/100) × 8 = 1:(M₂)₁ So formula is: (M₁)₂(M₂)₁O₄ This must be neutral. Both metals must have +8 charge in total. From given options: O.N. of M₁ = +2 M₂ = +4
