The instantaneous voltages at three terminals marked X, Y and Z are given by V_X = V_0 sin ωt V_Y = V_0 sin(ωt + 2π/3) and V_Z = V_0 sin(ωt + 4π/3). An ideal voltmeter is configured to read rms value of the potential difference between its terminals. It is connected between points X and Y and then between Y and Z. The reading(s) of the voltmeter will be

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) V_XY^rms = V_0 sqrt(3/2)
  2. (B) V_YZ^rms = V_0 sqrt(1/2)
  3. (C) V_XY^rms = V_0
  4. (D) independent of the choice of the two terminals

Answer

Correct answer: AD

Solution

V_XYO = V_Y2O = V_ZXO = √3 V₀ V_XX^rms = V_YZ^rms = √(3/2) V₀ [Figure shows a phasor diagram with V₀ on vertical axis, and two other phasors labeled V₀ extending from the origin at equal angles on either side]
