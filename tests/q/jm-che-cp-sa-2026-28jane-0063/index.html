A student performed analysis of aliphatic organic compound 'X' which on analysis gave C = 61.01%, H = 15.25%, N = 23.74%. This compound, on treatment with HNO₂/H₂O produced another compound 'Y' which did not contain any nitrogen atom. However, the compound 'Y' upon controlled oxidation produced another compound 'Z' that responded to iodoform test. The structure of 'X' is:

  1. (1) CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂
  2. (2) Ph - CH - NH₂ with CH₃ below the CH
  3. (3) CH₃ with CH₃ below bonded to CH - NH₂
  4. (4) CH₃ - CH₂CH - CH₃ with NH₂ below the CH

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Step 1: Analyze the elemental composition of compound X. C = 61.01%, H = 15.25%, N = 23.74% Assuming 100 g of compound: C: 61.01/12 = 5.08 mol H: 15.25/1 = 15.25 mol N: 23.74/14 = 1.70 mol Dividing by smallest (1.70): C: 5.08/1.70 = 3 H: 15.25/1.70 = 9 N: 1.70/1.70 = 1 Empirical formula: C₃H₉N Empirical formula mass = 3(12) + 9(1) + 1(14) = 59 Since molecular mass ≈ 59, molecular formula is C₃H₉N Step 2: Structure determination of X With molecular formula C₃H₉N containing one amine group, possible structures are: (1) CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂ (n-propylamine) - primary amine (3) (CH₃)₂CHNH₂ (isopropylamine) - primary amine (4) CH₃CH₂CH(NH₂)CH₃ - would be C₄H₁₁N (not matching) Step 3: Reaction with HNO₂/H₂O When X reacts with HNO₂/H₂O, nitrogen is removed and Y is formed without nitrogen. Primary amines react with HNO₂ to form diazonium ions which decompose to alcohols: R-NH₂ + HNO₂ → R-N≡N⁺ → R-OH + N₂ For (3): (CH₃)₂CHNH₂ → (CH₃)₂CHOH (Y) Step 4: Oxidation of Y to Z Y is (CH₃)₂CHOH (isopropanol/secondary alcohol) Controlled oxidation of secondary alcohol gives ketone: (CH₃)₂CHOH → (CH₃)₂C=O (Z) (acetone) Step 5: Iodoform test Acetone (CH₃)₂C=O contains the CH₃CO- group (methyl ketone) Methyl ketones respond positively to iodoform test: CH₃CO- + I₂ + OH⁻ → CHI₃ (yellow precipitate) + other products Step 6: Verification of option (1) For CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂: After HNO₂ treatment: CH₃CH₂CH₂OH (primary alcohol) After oxidation: CH₃CH₂CHO (aldehyde, not ketone) Aldehydes do not give positive iodoform test Therefore, the structure of X is (CH₃)₂CHNH₂ (option 3).
