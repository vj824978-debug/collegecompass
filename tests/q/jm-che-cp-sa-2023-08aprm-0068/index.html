The reaction (1/2)H2(g) + AgCl(s) ⇌ H+(aq) + Cl-(aq) + Ag(s) occurs in which of the given galvanic cell?

  1. (1) Pt|H2(g)|HCl(sol°)|AgCl(s)|Ag
  2. (2) Ag|AgCl(s)|KCl(sol°)|AgNO3|Ag
  3. (3) Pt|H2(g)|HCl(sol°)|AgNO3(sol°)|Ag
  4. (4) Pt|H2(g)|KCl(sol°)|AgCl(s)|Ag

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

At anode: (1/2)H2(g) → H+(aq) + e-. At cathode: AgCl(s) + e- → Ag(s) + Cl-(aq). Overall reaction: (1/2)H2(g) + AgCl(s) → H+(aq) + Cl-(aq) + Ag(s). The cell representation must have H2(g) electrode in HCl solution at anode and AgCl(s) electrode at cathode. Option (1) Pt|H2(g)|HCl(sol°)|AgCl(s)|Ag satisfies this arrangement.
