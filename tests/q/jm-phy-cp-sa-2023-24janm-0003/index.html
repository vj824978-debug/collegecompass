Consider the following radioactive decay process: 218/84 A --alpha--> A1 --beta−--> A2 --gamma--> A3 --alpha--> A4 --beta+--> A5 --gamma--> A6. The mass number and the atomic number of A6 are given by:

  1. (1) 210 and 80
  2. (2) 210 and 82
  3. (3) 210 and 84
  4. (4) 211 and 80

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Starting with 218/84 A Alpha decay: reduces mass number by 4, reduces atomic number by 2 218/84 A --alpha--> 214/82 A1 Beta− decay: mass number unchanged, increases atomic number by 1 214/82 A1 --beta−--> 214/83 A2 Gamma decay: mass number unchanged, atomic number unchanged 214/83 A2 --gamma--> 214/83 A3 Alpha decay: reduces mass number by 4, reduces atomic number by 2 214/83 A3 --alpha--> 210/81 A4 Beta+ decay: reduces mass number by 1 (due to positron emission converting proton to neutron), reduces atomic number by 1 210/81 A4 --beta+--> 210/80 A5 Gamma decay: mass number unchanged, atomic number unchanged 210/80 A5 --gamma--> 210/80 A6 Therefore: Mass number of A6 = 210 Atomic number of A6 = 80
