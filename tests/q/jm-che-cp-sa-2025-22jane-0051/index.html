Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their dipole moment: HBr, H2S, NF3 and CHCl3

  1. (1) NF3 < HBr < H2S < CHCl3
  2. (2) HBr < H2S < NF3 < CHCl3
  3. (3) H2S < HBr < NF3 < CHCl3
  4. (4) CHCl3 < NF3 < HBr < H2S

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Increasing order of Dipole moment NF3 < HBr < H2S < CHCl3 μ = 0.24 D 0.79 D 0.95 D 1.04 D It is NCERT Data Based Explanation: The dipole moment of a molecule depends on the electronegativity difference between atoms and the molecular geometry. 1. NF3 (0.24 D): Although N and F have a large electronegativity difference, the geometry is trigonal pyramidal. The lone pair on nitrogen causes the bond dipoles to partially cancel, resulting in a very small net dipole moment. 2. HBr (0.79 D): This is a diatomic molecule with electronegativity difference between H and Br. The dipole moment is moderate. 3. H2S (0.95 D): This molecule has a bent geometry with the sulfur atom having two lone pairs. The bond dipoles do not completely cancel due to the bent shape, resulting in a higher dipole moment than HBr. 4. CHCl3 (1.04 D): In chloroform, the three C-Cl bonds are polar and their dipoles do not completely cancel due to the presence of the hydrogen atom (which is less electronegative than chlorine), resulting in the highest dipole moment among these compounds.
