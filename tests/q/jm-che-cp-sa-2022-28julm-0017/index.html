Terylene polymer is obtained by condensation of:

  1. (1) Ethane-1, 2-diol and Benzene-1, 3 dicarboxylic acid
  2. (2) Propane-1, 2-diol and Benzene-1, 4 dicarboxylic acid
  3. (3) Ethane-1, 2-diol and Benzene-1, 4 dicarboxylic acid
  4. (4) Ethane-1, 2-diol and Benzene-1, 2 dicarboxylic acid

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Terylene (polyethylene terephthalate) is a polyester formed by the condensation reaction between ethane-1,2-diol (ethylene glycol) and benzene-1,4-dicarboxylic acid (terephthalic acid). The two hydroxyl groups of the diol react with the two carboxylic acid groups to form ester linkages, creating the polymer chain. The para-substitution (1,4-positions) on the benzene ring is essential for terylene formation, not the meta (1,3) or ortho (1,2) isomers.
