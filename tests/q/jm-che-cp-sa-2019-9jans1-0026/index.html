The highest value of the calculated spin only magnetic moment (in BM) among all the transition metal complexes is -

  1. (1) 6.93
  2. (2) 5.92
  3. (3) 4.90
  4. (4) 3.87

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Metal complex can contain maximum 5 unpaired e^- in d-subshell of metal μ = sqrt(n(n + 2)) BM where n = no. of unpaired e^- For maximum unpaired electrons: n = 5 μ = sqrt(5(5 + 2)) BM μ = sqrt(5 × 7) BM μ = sqrt(35) = 5.92 BM Example: Mn^(+2) complex
