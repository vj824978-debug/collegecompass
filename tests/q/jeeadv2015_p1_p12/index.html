Two independent harmonic oscillators of equal mass are oscillating about the origin with angular frequencies ω₁ and ω₂ and have total energies E₁ and E₂, respectively. The variations of their momenta p with positions x are shown in the figures. If a/b = n² and a/R = n, then the correct equation(s) is(are) [Figure: Left shows an elliptical phase space diagram for oscillator 1 with Energy = E₁, with semi-major axis b (vertical, p-axis) and semi-minor axis a (horizontal, x-axis). Right shows a circular phase space diagram for oscillator 2 with Energy = E₂, with radius R (horizontal, x-axis)]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) E₁ω₁ = E₂ω₂
  2. (B) ω₂/ω₁ = n²
  3. (C) ω₁ω₂ = n²
  4. (D) E₁/ω₁ = E₂/ω₂

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

For first oscillator E₁ = ½mω₁²a², and p = mv = mω₁a = b ⟹ a/b = 1/(mω₁) ...(i) For second oscillator E₂ = ½mω₂²R², and mω₂ = 1 ...(ii) (a/b) = ω₂/ω₁ = n² E₁/(ω₁²a²) = E₂/(ω₂²R²) ⟹ E₁/ω₁ = E₂/ω₂
