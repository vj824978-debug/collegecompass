A body of mass 2kg moves under a force of (2i + 3j + 5k)N. It starts from rest and was at the origin initially. After 4 s, its new coordinates are (a, b, 20). The value of b is ___________. (Round off to the Nearest Integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

Force F = (2i + 3j + 5k)N, mass m = 2kg. Acceleration a = F/m = (1i + 1.5j + 2.5k)m/s^2. For y-direction: a_y = 1.5 m/s^2. Using equation of motion s_y = (1/2)a_y*t^2 = (1/2) * 1.5 * (4)^2 = (1/2) * 1.5 * 16 = 12, therefore b = 12
