The total number of distinct naturally occurring amino acids obtained by complete acidic hydrolysis of the peptide shown below is [A peptide structure showing a chain of amino acids connected by peptide bonds. The structure contains 5 amino acid residues with the following side chains: a pyroglutamic acid (cyclic structure on left), phenylalanine (with benzene ring), an amino acid with tert-butyl group, another amino acid with tert-butyl group, and phenylalanine (with benzene ring), and a proline-like residue (cyclic on right)]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

This peptide on complete hydrolysis produced 4 distinct amino acids which are given below: (1) H₂N—CH₂—C(=O)—OH Glycine (natural) (2) HO—C(=O)—CH(NH₂)—CH₂—[cyclopropyl ring] (3) HO—C(=O)—C(NH₂)(C(CH₃)₂)₂ [tert-butyl substituted amino acid] (4) HO—C(=O)—CH(NH—CH₂—C(=O)—OH)—CH₂—[cyclopropyl ring] [amino acid with amide-linked carboxylic acid chain] Only glycine is naturally occurring amino acid.
