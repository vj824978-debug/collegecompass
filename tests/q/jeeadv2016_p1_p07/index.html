Two loudspeakers M and N are located 20 m apart and emit sound at frequencies 118 Hz and 121 Hz, respectively. A car is initially at a point P, 1800 m away from the midpoint Q of the line MN and moves towards Q constantly at 60 km/hr along the perpendicular bisector of MN. It crosses Q and eventually reaches a point R, 1800 m away from Q. Let v(t) represent the beat frequency measured by a person sitting in the car at time t. Let v_P, v_Q and v_R be the beat frequencies measured at locations P, Q and R, respectively. The speed of sound in air is 330 m s^-1. Which of the following statement(s) is/are true regarding the sound heard by the person in the car? [A v-t plot showing beat frequency starting at peak at P, passing through v_Q at point Q, and decreasing towards R]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) v_P + v_R = 2v_Q
  2. (B) The rate of change in beat frequency is maximum when the car passes through Q
  3. (C) The plot below represents schematically the variation of beat frequency with time [A v-t plot showing beat frequency starting high at P, decreasing smoothly through Q, and continuing to decrease towards R]
  4. (D) The plot below represents schematically the variation of beat frequency with time [A v-t plot showing beat frequency starting high at P, decreasing to v_Q at Q, and decreasing further towards R]

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution

νₚ = (121 - 118)(v + vD cos α)/v = 3(v + vD cos α)/v Hz νQ = 121 - 118 = 3 Hz νR = (121 - 118)(v - vD cos α)/v = 3(v - vD cos α)/v ∴ νₚ + νR = 2νQ Now, v (between P and Q) = 3((v + vD cos θ)/v) dv/dθ = 3vD sin θ/v Now, sin θ = 1 at Q (maximum) ∴ rate of change in beat frequency is maximum at Q [Diagram shows: Triangle PQR with P at top right, M at left (118 Hz), N at right (121 Hz), Q at center. Angles α and θ marked, with vD velocity vector shown at P pointing toward Q. Right angle marked at Q.]
