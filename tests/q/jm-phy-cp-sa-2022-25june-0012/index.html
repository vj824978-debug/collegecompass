A sinusoidal voltage V(t) = 210 sin 3000 t volt is applied to a series LCR circuit in which L = 10 mH, C = 25 μF and R = 100 Ω. The phase difference (Φ) between the applied voltage and resultant current will be:

  1. (1) tan^(-1)(0.17)
  2. (2) tan^(-1)(9.46)
  3. (3) tan^(-1)(0.30)
  4. (4) tan^(-1)(13.33)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: V(t) = 210 sin 3000t, L = 10 mH, C = 25 μF, R = 100 Ω, ω = 3000 rad/s. Calculate XL = ωL = 3000 × 10 × 10^(-3) = 30 Ω. Calculate XC = 1/(ωC) = 1/(3000 × 25 × 10^(-6)) = 40/3 Ω. Calculate net reactance: XL - XC = 30 - 40/3 = 50/3 Ω. Phase difference: tan(Φ) = (XL - XC)/R = (50/3)/100 = 50/300 = 1/6 ≈ 0.1667. Therefore Φ = tan^(-1)(0.17).
