The formal changes on the atoms marked as (1) to (4) in the Lewis representation of HNO3 molecule respectively are

  1. (1) +1,0,0,-1
  2. (2) 0,-1,0,+1
  3. (3) 0,+1,0,-1
  4. (4) 0,0,-1,+1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Consider the structure of HNO3: H-O-N(=O)-O: (1)(2)(3)(4) To find formal charge, use the formula: Formal charge = valence e's - non bonding e's - (bonding electrons/2) For atom (1) - Oxygen bonded to H: - Valence electrons = 6 - Non-bonding electrons = 4 (2 lone pairs) - Bonding electrons = 2 (single bond with H) - Formal charge = 6 - 4 - (2/2) = 6 - 4 - 1 = +1 Wait, reconsidering the structure shown in the solution: H-O(1)-N(2)=O(3) with O(4) as another oxygen on N (lone pairs shown) For atom (1) - Oxygen: - Valence electrons = 6 - Non-bonding electrons = 4 - Bonding electrons = 2 (single bond) - Formal charge = 6 - 4 - 1 = +1 For atom (2) - Nitrogen: - Valence electrons = 5 - Non-bonding electrons = 0 - Bonding electrons = 8 (one single bond with O(1), one double bond with O(3), one single bond with O(4)) - Formal charge = 5 - 0 - (8/2) = 5 - 0 - 4 = +1 For atom (3) - Oxygen (double bonded): - Valence electrons = 6 - Non-bonding electrons = 4 - Bonding electrons = 4 (double bond) - Formal charge = 6 - 4 - (4/2) = 6 - 4 - 2 = 0 For atom (4) - Oxygen (with lone pairs): - Valence electrons = 6 - Non-bonding electrons = 6 - Bonding electrons = 2 (single bond) - Formal charge = 6 - 6 - (2/2) = 6 - 6 - 1 = -1 Therefore: (1): 0, (2): +1, (3): 0, (4): -1 Which corresponds to option (3): 0,+1,0,-1
