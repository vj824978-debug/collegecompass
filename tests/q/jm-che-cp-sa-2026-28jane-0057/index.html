Consider the following statements about manganate and permanganate ions. Identify the correct statements: A. The geometry of both manganate and permanganate ions is tetrahedral. B. The oxidation states of Mn in manganate and permanganate are +7 and +6, respectively. C. Oxidation of Mn (II) salt by peroxodisulphate gives manganate ion as the final product. D. Manganate ion is paramagnetic and permanganate ions is diamagnetic. E. Acidified permanganate ion reduces oxalate, nitrite and iodide ions.

  1. (1) A, C and D Only
  2. (2) A, B and C Only
  3. (3) A, D and E Only
  4. (4) A and D Only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Statement says manganate, so this is INCORRECT) (D) MnO4^(-) → Diamagnetic MnO4^(-2) → Paramagnetic - CORRECT (Statement reverses this, making it INCORRECT) (E) It is oxidising agent - CORRECT (Acidified permanganate reduces oxalate, nitrite and iodide ions because it acts as an oxidising agent) Correct statements are A, C and D.
