Three isomers A, B and C (mol. formula C8H11N) give the following results: A and C undergo azotization to give P + Q, which upon (i) hydrolysis or (ii) oxidation (KMnO4 + H+) give R (product of A) and S (product of C) respectively. R has lower boiling point than S. B reacts with C6H5SO2Cl to give an alkali-insoluble product. A, B and C, respectively are:

  1. (1) (1) ortho-NH2 with CH2CH3 substituent; meta-CH2NHCH3; para-NH2 with CH2CH3 substituent
  2. (2) (2) ortho-NH2 with CH2CH3 substituent; CH2CH3 with meta-CH3; para-NH2 with CH2CH3 substituent
  3. (3) (3) para-CH2CH3 with NH2; meta-NHCH2CH3; ortho-NH2 with CH2CH3 substituent
  4. (4) (4) ortho-CH2CH3 with NH2; meta-CH2NHCH3; para-NH2 with CH2CH3 substituent

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From the given information: A and C undergo diazotization (NaNO2/HCl) to form diazonium salts P and Q respectively. For compound A: - Diazonium salt P undergoes hydrolysis to give R (lower boiling point) - Diazonium salt P undergoes oxidation with KMnO4 + H+ to give R - R is a phenolic compound formed from A (which has NH2 group) - R has lower boiling point, suggesting it is formed by substitution at ortho position (intramolecular H-bonding reduces boiling point) - Therefore A is o-NH2-C6H4-CH2CH3 (o-ethylaniline) - P is o-N2+-C6H4-CH2CH3 - R is o-HO-C6H4-COOH (salicylic acid with intramolecular H-bonding, lower b.p.) For compound C: - Diazonium salt Q undergoes similar reactions - S has higher boiling point than R, indicating no intramolecular H-bonding - Therefore C is p-NH2-C6H4-CH2CH3 (p-ethylaniline) - Q is p-N2+-C6H4-CH2CH3 - S is p-HO-C6H4-COOH (4-hydroxybenzaldehyde derivative) For compound B: - B reacts with C6H5SO2Cl (benzenesulfonyl chloride) to form alkali-insoluble product - This indicates B has a secondary amine group (not a primary amine, which would form alkali-soluble sulfonamide) - B is m-CH2NHCH3 (meta-N-methylbenzylamine) - The N-methyl group prevents alkali solubility of the sulfonamide product Therefore, A, B, and C respectively are: o-NH2-C6H4-CH2CH3, m-CH2NHCH3, p-NH2-C6H4-CH2CH3, which corresponds to option (1).
