Mass per unit area of a circular disc of radius a depends on the distance r from its centre as σ(r) = A + Br. The moment of inertia of the disc about the axis perpendicular to the plane and passing through its centre is-

  1. (1) 2πa^4(A/4 + aB/5)
  2. (2) πa^4(A/4 + aB/5)
  3. (3) 2πa^4(aA/4 + B/5)
  4. (4) 2πa^4(A/4 + B/5)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Consider a circular disc of radius a with mass per unit area σ(r) = A + Br. Consider a thin ring element at distance r from the centre with thickness dr. Area of ring element: dl = 2πr dr Mass of ring element: dm = σ(r) × dl = (A + Br) × 2πr dr Moment of inertia of ring element about the axis perpendicular to the plane and passing through the centre: dI = r^2 dm = r^2 × (A + Br) × 2πr dr = 2πr^3(A + Br) dr Total moment of inertia: I = ∫dI = ∫₀^a 2πr^3(A + Br) dr I = ∫₀^a 2πr^3 A dr + ∫₀^a 2πr^3 Br dr I = 2πA ∫₀^a r^3 dr + 2πB ∫₀^a r^4 dr I = 2πA [r^4/4]₀^a + 2πB [r^5/5]₀^a I = 2πA(a^4/4) + 2πB(a^5/5) I = 2πa^4(A/4) + 2πa^5(B/5) I = 2πa^4(A/4 + aB/5)
