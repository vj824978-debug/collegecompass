Match List-I with List-II.

  1. (1) A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
  2. (2) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
  3. (3) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
  4. (4) A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

(A) Hydrogen reacts with carbon monoxide in presence of cobalt catalyst to give methanol 2H(g) + CO(g) --cobalt catalyst--> CH3OH(l) So A matches with IV (Methanol production) (B) Syn gas is produced from coal and the process is called coal gasification. C(s) + H2O(g) --1270 K--> CO(g) + H2(g) (syn gas) So B matches with III (Coal gasification) (C) Reaction of steam with hydrocarbons or coke at high temperature in presence of nickel catalyst gives a mixture of CO and H2, called water gas CH4(g) + H2O(g) --1270 K, Ni--> CO(g) + 3H2(g) (water gas) So C matches with II (Water gas production) (D) Electrolysis of brine solution produces H2 gas at cathode and Cl2 gas at anode NaCl(aq) --> Na+(aq) + Cl-(aq) Cathode: 2H2O(l) + 2e- --> H2(g) + 2OH-(aq) Anode: 2Cl-(aq) --> Cl2(g) + 2e- So D matches with I ((H2 + Cl2) production) Therefore: A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
