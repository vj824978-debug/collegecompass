20 mL of 0.1 M H2SO4 solution is added to 30 mL of 0.2 M NH4OH solution. The pH of the resultant mixture is: [pKb of NH4OH = 4.7].

  1. (1) 5.2
  2. (2) 9.4
  3. (3) 9.0
  4. (4) 5.0

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

H2SO4 + 2NH4OH → (NH4)2SO4 + 2H2O At t = 0: H2SO4: 2 millimole NH4OH: 6 millimole At t = t: H2SO4: 0 NH4OH: 2 millimole Salt: 2 millimole Using the formula: p^OH = pKb + log([Salt]/[Base]) p^OH = 4.7 + log(4/50)/(2/50) p^OH = 4.7 + log(4/2) p^OH = 4.7 + 0.3 = 5 p^H = 14 - 5 = 9 Therefore, pH = 9.0
