28.0 L of CO2 is produced on complete combustion of 16.8 L gaseous mixture of ethene and methane at 25°C and 1 atm. Heat evolved during the combustion process is __________ kJ. Given: ΔHc(CH4) = -900 kJ mol^(-1), ΔHc(C2H4) = -1400 kJ mol^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: 925

Solution

CH4(g) + 2O2(g) → CO2(g) + 2H2O(l) (16.8-x) volume produces (16.8-x) volume of CO2 C2H4(g) + 3O2(g) → 2CO2(g) + 2H2O(l) x volume produces 2x volume of CO2 Total CO2 produced = 28.0 L (16.8-x) + 2x = 28 16.8 + x = 28 x = 11.2 L Volume of CH4 = 16.8 - 11.2 = 5.6 L Volume of C2H4 = 11.2 L At 25°C and 1 atm, using ideal gas law: No. of moles of CH4 = 5.6/22.4 = 0.25 mol No. of moles of C2H4 = 11.2/22.4 = 0.50 mol Heat evolved from CH4 = 0.25 × 900 = 225 kJ Heat evolved from C2H4 = 0.50 × 1400 = 700 kJ Total heat evolved = 225 + 700 = 925 kJ
