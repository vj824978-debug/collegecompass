0.5 g of an organic compound on combustion gave 1.46 g of CO2 and 0.9 g of H2O. The percentage of carbon in the compound is _____ (nearest integer). [Given: Molar mass (in g mol^-1) C:12, H:1, O:16]

Answer

Correct answer: 80

Solution

Step 1: Calculate moles of CO2 and H2O produced. Moles of CO2 = 1.46/44 = 0.0332 mol. Moles of H2O = 0.9/18 = 0.05 mol. Step 2: Calculate moles of C and H in the compound. Moles of C = 0.0332 mol (from CO2). Moles of H = 2 × 0.05 = 0.1 mol (from H2O). Step 3: Calculate mass of C and H. Mass of C = 0.0332 × 12 = 0.3984 g. Mass of H = 0.1 × 1 = 0.1 g. Step 4: Find mass of O by difference. Mass of O = 0.5 - 0.3984 - 0.1 = 0.0016 g. Step 5: Calculate percentage of carbon. Percentage of C = (0.3984/0.5) × 100 = 79.68% ≈ 80% (nearest integer).
