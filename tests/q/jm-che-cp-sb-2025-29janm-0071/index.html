Given below are some nitrogen containing compounds. NH2 with NO2 (para position), CH2-NH2, N-COCH3 with H (on benzene), NH2 (on benzene) Each of them is treated with HCl separately. 1.0 g of the most basic compound will consume _____ mg of HCl. (Given molar mass in g mol^(-1) C:12, H:1, O:16, Cl:35.5)

Answer

Correct answer: 341

Solution

Benzyl Amine is most basic due to localised lone pair. CH2-NH2 (Benzyl Amine) + HCl → CH2-NH3+Cl- (Benzyl Ammonium Chloride) Molar mass of benzyl amine = C6H5-CH2-NH2 = 12(7) + 1(9) + 14 = 84 + 9 + 14 = 107 g/mol Mole of benzyl Amine = 1.0/107 = 0.00934 mole 1 Mole of Benzyl amine consumed 1 mole of HCl So, Mole of HCl consumed = 0.00934 mole Mass of HCl consumed = 0.00934 × molar mass of HCl = 0.00934 × 36.5 = 0.341 gm = 341 mg
