A uniform rod of length 'ℓ' is pivoted at one of its ends on a vertical shaft of negligible radius. When the shaft rotates at angular speed ω the rod makes an angle θ with it (see figure). To find equate the rate of change of angular momentum (direction going in the paper) mℓ^2/12 ω^2 sin θ cos θ about the centre of mass (CM) to the torque provided by the horizontal and vertical forces F_H and F_v about the CM. The value of θ is then such that:

  1. (1) cos θ = g/(2ℓω^2)
  2. (2) cos θ = g/(ℓω^2)
  3. (3) cos θ = 2g/(3ℓω^2)
  4. (4) cos θ = 3g/(2ℓω^2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From the force diagram: F_v = mg F_H = mω^2 (ℓ/2) sin θ Torque about CM due to forces: mg(ℓ/2) sin θ - mω^2 (ℓ/2) sin θ (ℓ/2) cos θ = mℓ^2/12 ω^2 sin θ cos θ Simplifying: mg(ℓ/2) sin θ - mω^2 (ℓ^2/4) sin θ cos θ = mℓ^2/12 ω^2 sin θ cos θ Dividing by m sin θ (assuming sin θ ≠ 0): g(ℓ/2) - ω^2 (ℓ^2/4) cos θ = ℓ^2/12 ω^2 cos θ g(ℓ/2) = ω^2 (ℓ^2/4) cos θ + ℓ^2/12 ω^2 cos θ g(ℓ/2) = ω^2 ℓ^2 cos θ [(1/4) + (1/12)] g(ℓ/2) = ω^2 ℓ^2 cos θ [(3 + 1)/12] g(ℓ/2) = ω^2 ℓ^2 cos θ (4/12) g(ℓ/2) = ω^2 ℓ^2 cos θ (1/3) cos θ = 3g/(2ℓω^2)
