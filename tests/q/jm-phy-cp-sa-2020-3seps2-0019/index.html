Concentric metallic hollow spheres radii R and 4R hold charges Q1 and Q2 respectively. Given that surface charge densities of the concentric spheres are equal, the potential difference V(R) - V(4R) is:

  1. (1) 3Q1/(16π ε_0 R)
  2. (2) 3Q1/(4π ε_0 R)
  3. (3) Q2/(4π ε_0 R)
  4. (4) 3Q2/(4π ε_0 R)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: Two concentric metallic hollow spheres with radii R and 4R carrying charges Q1 and Q2 respectively. Condition: Surface charge densities are equal. Surface charge density σ = Q/(4πr^2) For sphere of radius R: σ = Q1/(4πR^2) For sphere of radius 4R: σ = Q2/(4π(4R)^2) = Q2/(64πR^2) Since surface charge densities are equal: Q1/(4πR^2) = Q2/(64πR^2) Q1 = Q2/16 Or Q2 = 16Q1 Electric field at distance r (where R < r < 4R) due to inner sphere: E = KQ1/r^2 Potential difference: V(R) - V(4R) = ∫[from R to 4R] E dr = ∫[from R to 4R] (KQ1/r^2) dr = KQ1 ∫[from R to 4R] (1/r^2) dr = KQ1 [-1/r] [from R to 4R] = KQ1 [-1/(4R) + 1/R] = KQ1 [1/R - 1/(4R)] = KQ1 [(4 - 1)/(4R)] = KQ1 [3/(4R)] = 3KQ1/(4R) Since K = 1/(4π ε_0): V(R) - V(4R) = 3Q1/(4π ε_0 · 4R) = 3Q1/(16π ε_0 R)
