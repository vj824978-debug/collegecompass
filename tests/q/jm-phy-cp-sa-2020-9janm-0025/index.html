Both the diodes used in the circuit shown are assumed to be ideal and have negligible resistance when these are forward biased. Built in potential in each diode is 0.7 V. For the input voltages shown in the figure, the voltage (in Volts) at point A is _________

Answer

Correct answer: 12.00

Solution

From the circuit diagram, we have: - Input voltage Vin = 12.7 V - Output voltage on right side = 4 V - Two ideal diodes with forward bias potential drop = 0.7 V each - Point A is between the two diodes Analysis: When current flows through the circuit from left to right: - The left diode (forward biased) has a voltage drop of 0.7 V - At point A (between the diodes), the voltage can be found using Kirchhoff's voltage law Starting from the 12.7 V source: Vin - V(left diode) = V_A 12.7 - 0.7 = V_A V_A = 12 V Verification: From point A to the right side (4 V): V_A - V(right diode) = 4 12 - 0.7 = 11.3 V (this represents the voltage across the resistor and output) However, considering the circuit topology and that point A is the node between the two diodes: V_A = 12.7 - 0.7 = 12.0 V
