The height of liquid column raised in a capillary tube of certain radius when dipped in liquid A vertically is, 5 cm. If the tube is dipped in a similar manner in another liquid B of surface tension and density double the values of liquid A, the height of liquid column raised in liquid B would be _____ m.

  1. (1) 0.20
  2. (2) 0.05
  3. (3) 0.5
  4. (4) 0.10

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

height of capillary rise = (2s cos(theta))/(rho*g*R) When in A: 5 cm = (2*s_A*cos(theta))/(rho_A*g*R) When in B: h = (2*s_B*cos(theta))/(rho_B*g*R) Given: s_B = 2*s_A and rho_B = 2*rho_A h = (2 × 2*s_A × cos(theta))/(2*rho_A*g*R) = 5 cm Therefore h = (4*s_A*cos(theta))/(2*rho_A*g*R) = (2*s_A*cos(theta))/(rho_A*g*R) = 5 cm So h = 5 cm = 0.05 m
