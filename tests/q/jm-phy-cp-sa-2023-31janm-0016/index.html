If 1000 droplets of water of surface tension 0.07 N/m, having same radius 1 mm each, combine to form a single drop. In the process the released surface energy is- (Take π = 22/7)

  1. (1) 7.92 × 10^(-5) J
  2. (2) 7.92 × 10^(-4) J
  3. (3) 9.68 × 10^(-4) J
  4. (4) 8.8 × 10^(-5) J

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Radius of bigger drop = 10r = R (r = radius of smaller droplet) ΔE = 1000 × 4πr^2 × T - 4πR^2 × T = 4πT [1000 × r^2 - 100r^2] = 3600 πr^2 T = 3600 × (22/7) × 1 × 10^(-6) × (7/100) = 22 × 36 × 10^(-6) = 792 × 10^(-6) J = 7.92 × 10^(-4) J
