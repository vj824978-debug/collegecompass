Six infinitely large and thin non-conducting sheets are fixed in configurations I and II. As shown in the figure, the sheets carry uniform surface charge densities which are indicated in terms of σ₀. The separation between any two consecutive sheets is 1 μm. The various regions between the sheets are denoted as 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5. If σ₀ = 9 μC/m², then which of the following statements is/are correct: (Take permittivity of free space ε₀ = 9×10^-12 F/m) [Configuration I shows 6 sheets with surface charge densities: +σ₀, -σ₀, +σ₀, -σ₀, +σ₀, -σ₀ from left to right, with regions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 between them] [Configuration II shows 6 sheets with surface charge densities: +σ₀/2, -σ₀, +σ₀, -σ₀, +σ₀, -σ₀/2 from left to right, with regions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 between them]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) In region 4 of the configuration I, the magnitude of the electric field is zero.
  2. (B) In region 3 of the configuration II, the magnitude of the electric field is σ₀/ε₀.
  3. (C) Potential difference between the first and the last sheets of the configuration I is 5 V.
  4. (D) Potential difference between the first and the last sheets of the configuration II is zero.

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

In region -4 of configuration-I E₄ = σ₀/2ε₀ - σ₀/2ε₀ + σ₀/2ε₀ - σ₀/2ε₀ - σ₀/2ε₀ + σ₀/2ε₀ = 0 [Two configuration diagrams shown side by side: Configuration I shows 6 regions labeled 1-5 with alternating +σ₀ and -σ₀ charges, with spacing σ/ε₀ marked Configuration II shows 5 regions with varying charges including +σ/2, -σ₀, +σ₀, -σ₀, +σ₀, -σ/2, with energies E'₁ through E'₅ marked] In region -3 of configuration-II E'₃ = (σ₀/2)/2ε₀ + (σ₀/2)/2ε₀ - σ₀/2ε₀ = 0 In configuration-I Vₐ = (2σ₀/2ε₀) × d - (2σ₀/2ε₀) × d - (2σ₀/2ε₀) × d = Vₓ Vₐ - Vₓ = (6σ₀/2ε₀) × d = (6 × 9 × 10⁻⁶)/(2 × 9 × 10⁻¹²) × 10⁻⁶ = 3 volt In configuration-II E'₁ = (σ₀/4ε₀) × 2 = σ₀/2ε₀ E'₂ = -(2σ₀/2ε₀) + 2·(σ₀/2)/2ε₀ = -σ₀/2ε₀ E'₃ = (σ₀/4ε₀) × 2 = σ₀/2ε₀ E'₄ = -(2σ₀/2ε₀) + (2σ₀/2)/2ε₀ = -σ₀/2ε₀ E'₅ = σ₀/2ε₀ V'ₐ - (σ₀/2ε₀)d + (σ₀/2ε₀)d - (σ₀/2ε₀)d + (σ₀/2ε₀)d - (σ₀/2ε₀)d = V'ₓ ⟹ V'ₐ - (σ₀/2ε₀)d = V'ₓ V'ₐ - V'ₓ = (σ₀/2ε₀)d = (9 × 10⁻⁶)/(2 × 9 × 10⁻¹²) × 10⁻⁶ = 1/2 Volts
