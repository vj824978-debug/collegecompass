Let the first term a and the common ratio r of a geometric progression be positive integers. If the sum of squares of its first three terms is 33033, then the sum of these three terms is equal to

  1. (1) 241
  2. (2) 231
  3. (3) 210
  4. (4) 220

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The first three terms of GP are a, ar, ar^2. Sum of squares: a^2 + a^2*r^2 + a^2*r^4 = 33033. Factor: a^2(1 + r^2 + r^4) = 33033 = 3 × 7 × 11^2 × 13. So a^2 = 121, giving a = 11. Then 1 + r^2 + r^4 = 273, so r^4 + r^2 - 272 = 0. Solving: r^2 = 16, so r = 4. The three terms are 11, 44, 176. Sum = 11 + 44 + 176 = 231.
