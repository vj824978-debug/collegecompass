Consider the following sequence of reactions to produce major product (A). Starting material has CH3 group at one position and NO2 group at another position on benzene ring. i) Br2, Fe ii) Sn, HCl iii) NaNO2, HCl, 273 K iv) H3PO2, H2O. Molar mass of product (A) is _____ g mol^(-1). (Given molar mass in g mol^(-1) of C: 12, H: 1, O: 16, Br: 80, N: 14, P: 31)

Answer

Correct answer: 171

Solution

Step 1: Identify the starting material structure. It is a methylnitrobenzene with CH3 and NO2 groups on the benzene ring at meta positions. Step 2: Reaction i) Br2, Fe Electrophilic aromatic bromination occurs. Br substituent adds to the benzene ring at an ortho or para position (directing effects of existing groups). Product: Bromomethylnitrobenzene (C7H6BrNO2) Step 3: Reaction ii) Sn, HCl The NO2 group is reduced to NH2 group by tin and hydrochloric acid. Product: Bromoaminomethylbenzene or 4-bromo-2-methylaniline (C7H8BrN) Step 4: Reaction iii) NaNO2, HCl, 273 K Diazotization of the primary amine (NH2) occurs, forming diazonium salt (N2^+ Cl^-) Product: 4-bromo-2-methylbenzenediazonium chloride (C7H7BrN2Cl) Step 5: Reaction iv) H3PO2, H2O Reduction of diazonium salt to hydrogen using hypophosphorous acid. The N2+ group is replaced by H. Final Product (A): 4-bromomethylbenzene or 1-bromo-3-methylbenzene (C7H7Br) Step 6: Calculate molar mass of C7H7Br Molar mass = 7(12) + 7(1) + 80 Molar mass = 84 + 7 + 80 Molar mass = 171 g mol^(-1)
