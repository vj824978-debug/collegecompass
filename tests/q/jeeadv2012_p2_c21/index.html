NiCl₂{P(C₆H₅)₂(C₂H₅)}₂ exhibits temperature dependent magnetic behaviour (paramagnetic/diamagnetic). the coordination geometries of Ni²⁺ in the paramagnetic and diamagnetic states are respectively

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) tetrahedral and tetrahedral
  2. (B) square planar and square planar
  3. (C) tetrahedral and square planar
  4. (D) square planar and tetrahedral

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

[NiCl₂ (PEt₃Ph)] contains Ni²⁺ with electronic configuration Ni²⁺ = [Ar] 3d⁸4s⁰ [orbital diagram showing 3d orbitals with 8 electrons paired across five boxes, 4s empty, 4p empty] In high spin state, it is paramagnetic, sp³ hybridised, tetrahedral. In low spin state, it is diamagnetic, dsp², square planar.
