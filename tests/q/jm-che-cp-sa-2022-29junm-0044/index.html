In the given conversion the compound A is: Starting material is bromobenzene with OH group. Treatment with (CH3)3CLi gives [A], then treatment with (i) CO2 and (ii) H3O+ gives a product with COOH and OH groups.

  1. (1) Benzene with Li and OH groups (ortho positions)
  2. (2) Benzene with Li and OLi groups (ortho positions)
  3. (3) Benzene with Br and OC(CH3)3 groups (ortho positions)
  4. (4) Benzene with C(CH3)3 and OH groups (ortho positions)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Starting with o-bromophenol, treatment with (CH3)3CLi (tert-butyllithium) causes: (1) Lithium-halogen exchange with Br to form o-lithiophenol intermediate, (2) The OH group gets deprotonated by the strong base to form OLi. Therefore compound A has both Li and OLi substituents in ortho positions on the benzene ring. Subsequent treatment with CO2 and H3O+ converts the Li to COOH group.
