A deviation of 2' is produced in the yellow ray when prism of crown and flint glass are achromatically combined. Taking dispersive powers of crown and flint glass as 0.02 and 0.03 respectively and refractive index for yellow light for these glasses are 1.5 and 1.6 respectively. The refracting angles for crown glass will be ________. (in degree). Round off to the Nearest Integer

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

For deviation without dispersion: delta = delta_C + delta_F = (mu_C - 1)A_C - (mu_F - 1)A_F = 2'. Condition for no dispersion: (mu_YC - mu_RC)A_C = (mu_YF - mu_RF)A_F. Using omega_C = 0.02 = (mu_C - mu_C)A_C/(mu_C - 1)A_C and omega_F = 0.03, we get 0.03(mu_F - 1)A_F = 0.02(mu_C - 1)A_C, which gives 3(1.6 - 1)A_F = 2(1.5 - 1)A_C = 1.8A_F = A_C = 12'. Substituting in deviation equation: 0.5A_C - 0.6(A_C/1.8) - 2' = A_C/2 - A_C/3 - 2' = A_C/6 - 2' = A_C = 12'
