Match List I with List II. List I contains oxides: A. N2O4, B. NO2, C. N2O5, D. N2O. List II contains types of bonds: I. 1 N=O bond, II. 1 N-O-N bond, III. 1 N-N bond, IV. 1 N=N/N=N bond. Match the oxides with their corresponding bond types.

  1. (1) A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
  2. (2) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I
  3. (3) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
  4. (4) A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

N2O4 has N-N bond (structure shows two NO2 units connected by N-N bond) = III. NO2 has N=O bond = I. N2O5 has N-O-N bond (structure shows N-O-N linkage) = II. N2O has N=N→O structure (N=N/N=N bond) = IV. Therefore A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV matches option (1).
