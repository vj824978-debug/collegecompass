A block moving horizontally on a smooth surface with a speed of 40 m/s splits into two parts with masses in the ratio 1:2. If the smaller part moves at 60 m/s in the same direction, then the fractional change in kinetic energy is:-

  1. (1) (A) 2/3
  2. (2) (B) 1/3
  3. (3) (C) 1/4
  4. (4) (D) 1/8

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using conservation of linear momentum: 40 × 3m = 60 × m + v × 2m, which gives v = 30 m/s. Initial kinetic energy: KEi = (1/2) × 3m × (40)^2. Final kinetic energy: KEf = (1/2) × m × (60)^2 + (1/2) × 2m × (30)^2. Taking the ratio: KEf/KEi = 54/48. Fractional change in kinetic energy = 1 - KEf/KEi = 1 - 54/48 = -1/8, so the magnitude of fractional change is 1/8.
