In the reaction, 2Al(s) + 6HCl(aq) → 2Al^(3+)(aq) + 6Cl^(-)(aq) + 3H_2(g)

  1. (1) 11.2 L H_2(g) at STP is produced for every mole of HCl consumed.
  2. (2) 67.2 L H_2(g) at STP is produced for every mole of Al that reacts.
  3. (3) 12 L HCl(aq) is consumed for every 6 L H_2(g) produced.
  4. (4) 33.6 L H_2(g) is produced regardless of temperature and pressure for every mole of Al that reacts.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

2Al(s) + 6HCl(aq) → 2Al^(3+)(aq) + 6Cl^(-)(aq) + 3H_2(g) Mole of H_2 produced = 2 × mole of HCl used = (2/3) × mole of Al used Analyzing each option: (1) For every mole of HCl consumed: From the equation: 6 mol HCl produces 3 mol H_2 For 1 mol HCl: H_2 produced = (3/6) = 0.5 mol At STP, 0.5 mol H_2 = 0.5 × 22.4 = 11.2 L This is CORRECT. (2) For every mole of Al that reacts: From the equation: 2 mol Al produces 3 mol H_2 For 1 mol Al: H_2 produced = (3/2) = 1.5 mol At STP, 1.5 mol H_2 = 1.5 × 22.4 = 33.6 L Not 67.2 L. This is INCORRECT. (3) 12 L HCl(aq) is consumed for every 6 L H_2(g) produced: This compares volumes of aqueous HCl and gaseous H_2, which is invalid. Molar ratios cannot be directly compared as volume ratios when states differ. This is INCORRECT. (4) 33.6 L H_2(g) is produced regardless of temperature and pressure: Gas volume depends on temperature and pressure (ideal gas law: PV = nRT). This statement contradicts the ideal gas behavior. This is INCORRECT.
