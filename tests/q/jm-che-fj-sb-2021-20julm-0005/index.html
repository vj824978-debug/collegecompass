At 20°C, the vapour pressure of benzene is 70 torr and that of methyl benzene is 20 torr. The mole fraction of benzene in the vapour phase at 20°C above an equimolar mixture of benzene and methyl benzene is ___×10^(-2). (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 78

Solution

Given: P_B^0 = 70 torr (benzene), P_T^0 = 20 torr (toluene/methyl benzene), equimolar mixture so x_B = 0.5 and x_T = 0.5. Total vapour pressure P_T = 70(0.5) + 20(0.5) = 45 torr. Mole fraction of benzene in vapour phase = (P_B^0 × x_B)/P_T = (70 × 0.5)/45 = 35/45 = 0.777 = 77.7 × 10^(-1) torr = 78 × 10^(-2) (nearest integer)
