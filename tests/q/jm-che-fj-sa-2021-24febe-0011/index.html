Which one of the following carbonyl compound cannot be prepared by addition of water on an alkyne in the presence of HgSO4 and H2SO4?

  1. (1) CH3-CH2-C(=O)-H
  2. (2) CH3-C(=O)-CH2CH3
  3. (3) Cyclohexane ring with C(=O)-CH3 attached
  4. (4) CH3-C(=O)-H

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The hydration of alkynes with HgSO4 and H2SO4 follows Markovnikov's rule. For unsymmetrical alkynes, the OH group adds to the carbon with more alkyl groups (more positive charge stabilizing), and H adds to the carbon with fewer alkyl groups. This produces ketones or aldehydes. Option (A) CH3-CH2-CHO (propanal) cannot be formed because it would require OH to add to the terminal carbon of an alkyne, which is not favored by Markovnikov's rule. The reaction would instead form CH3-C(=O)-CH3 (acetone). Option (D) CH3-CHO (acetaldehyde) also cannot be formed from a simple alkyne hydration. The answer is (A) or (D), but based on the explanation given, the answer is (A) because CH3-CH2-CHO cannot be formed from alkyne hydration.
