A bi-convex lens is formed with two thin plano-convex lenses as shown in the figure. Refractive index n of the first lens is 1.5 and that of the second lens is 1.2. Both the curved surface are of the same radius of curvature R = 14 cm. For this bi-convex lens, for an object distance of 40 cm, the image distance will be [Figure shows two plano-convex lenses placed back-to-back with their curved surfaces facing outward, labeled with n = 1.5 on left and n = 1.2 on right, R = 14 cm for curvature]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) -280.0 cm
  2. (B) 40.0 cm
  3. (C) 21.5 cm
  4. (D) 13.3 cm

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

P_r = (1.5-1)(1/14 - 0) + (1.2-1)(0 - 1/-14) = 0.5/14 + 0.2/14 = 1/20 f = +20 cm 1/v - 1/-40 = 1/20 1/v = 1/20 - 1/40 = 1/40 ∴ v = 40 cm
