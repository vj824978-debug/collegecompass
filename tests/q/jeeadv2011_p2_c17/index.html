In 1 L saturated solution of AgCl [Ksp(AgCl) = 1.6 × 10^-10], 0.1 mol of CuCl [Ksp(CuCl) = 1.0 × 10^-4] is added. The resultant concentration of Ag^+ in the solution is 1.6 × 10^-x. The value of "x" is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 7

(i) Similarly Ksp of CuCl = [Cu⁺][Cl⁻] 1.6 × 10⁻⁶ = y(x + y) ... (ii) On solving (i) and (ii) [Ag⁺] = 1.6 × 10⁻⁷ ∴ x = 7
