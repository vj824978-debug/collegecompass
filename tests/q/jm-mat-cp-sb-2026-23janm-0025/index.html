The number of 4-letter words, with or without meaning, which can be formed using the letters PQRQRSTUVP, is ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 1422

Solution

P → 3, Q → 2, R → 2, S, T, U, V (each appears once) Case I: 3 alike, 1 different ^1C_1 × ^6C_1 × (4!)/3! = 1 × 6 × 4 = 24 Case II: 2 alike, 2 alike ^3C_2 × (4!)/(2!2!) = 3 × 6 = 18 Case III: 2 alike, 2 different ^3C_1 × ^6C_2 × (4!)/2! = 3 × 15 × 12 = 540 Case IV: All 4 different ^7C_4 × 4! = 35 × 24 = 840 Total words = 24 + 18 + 540 + 840 = 1422
