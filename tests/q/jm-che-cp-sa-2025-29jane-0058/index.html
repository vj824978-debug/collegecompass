Given below are two statements: Statement (I): On nitration of m-xylene with HNO3, H2SO4 followed by oxidation, 4-nitrobenzene-1,3-dicarboxylic acid is obtained as the major product. Statement (II): CH3 group is o/p-directing while -NO2 group is m-directing group. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  2. (2) Statement I is false but Statement II is true
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  4. (4) Statement I is true but Statement II is false

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Statement-I: m-xylene has two CH3 groups at meta positions. When m-xylene undergoes nitration with HNO3/H2SO4, both CH3 groups are o/p-directing groups. The incoming NO2 group will be directed to the ortho and para positions with respect to both CH3 groups. The positions that are ortho/para to both CH3 groups are positions 1, 3, and 5 (considering one CH3 at position 1 and another at position 3). The nitration predominantly occurs at position 4 (which is para to one CH3 and meta to the other, but also ortho to the other). The major product is 4-nitro-1,3-dimethylbenzene. Upon oxidation, the CH3 groups are converted to COOH groups, yielding 4-nitrobenzene-1,3-dicarboxylic acid. Therefore, Statement I is TRUE. Statement-II: CH3 is an alkyl group, which is electron-donating through inductive effects and activates the benzene ring. It directs incoming electrophiles to ortho and para positions (o/p-directing). The NO2 group is a strong electron-withdrawing group (deactivating) and directs incoming electrophiles to meta positions (m-directing). Therefore, Statement II is TRUE. Both statements are correct.
