A solid sphere of mass M and radius R is divided into two unequal parts. The first part has a mass of 7M/8 and is converted into a uniform disc of radius 2R. The second part is converted into a uniform solid sphere. Let I₁ be the moment of inertia of the disc about its axis and I₂ be the moment of inertia of the new sphere about its axis. The ratio I₁/I₂ is given by:

  1. (1) 65
  2. (2) 140
  3. (3) 185
  4. (4) 285

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The first part has mass 7M/8 and is converted into a uniform disc of radius 2R. For a uniform disc about its axis: I₁ = (1/2) × m × r² I₁ = (1/2) × (7M/8) × (2R)² I₁ = (7M/8) × (4R²)/2 I₁ = (7M/8) × 2R² I₁ = (7/4)MR² The second part has mass M - 7M/8 = M/8 and is converted into a uniform solid sphere. Let the radius of this new sphere be R₁. For a uniform solid sphere about its axis: I₂ = (2/5) × m × r² I₂ = (2/5) × (M/8) × R₁² I₂ = (MR₁²)/80 Since the original sphere had radius R and the disc has radius 2R, we can find R₁ using volume conservation: Volume of original sphere: (4/3)πR³ Volume of disc: π(2R)² × h = 4πR² × h (where h is thickness) From the original sphere: Mass 7M/8 → disc of radius 2R Mass M/8 → sphere of radius R₁ Using density conservation and volume: (M/8)/(4/3)πR₁³ = M/(4/3)πR³ (1/8)/R₁³ = 1/R³ R₁³ = R³/8 R₁ = R/2 So: I₂ = (MR₁²)/80 = (M(R/2)²)/80 = (MR²)/(4 × 80) = (MR²)/320 Wait, let me recalculate I₂: I₂ = (2/5) × (M/8) × (R/2)² I₂ = (2/5) × (M/8) × (R²/4) I₂ = (2MR²)/(5 × 8 × 4) I₂ = (2MR²)/160 I₂ = (MR²)/80 Therefore: I₁/I₂ = [(7/4)MR²] / [(MR²)/80] I₁/I₂ = (7/4) × 80 I₁/I₂ = 7 × 20 I₁/I₂ = 140
