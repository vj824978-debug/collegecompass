For the decomposition of azomethane, CH3N2CH3(g) → CH3CH3(g) + N2(g), a first order reaction, the variation in partial pressure with time at 600 K is given as ln(p/p0) vs time graph with Slope = -3.465 × 10^3. The half life of the reaction is _____ × 10^-5 s. [Nearest integer]

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

For first order reaction: ln A = ln A0 - kt, so Slope = -k. Therefore -k = -3.465 × 10^3, giving k = 3.465 × 10^3 s^(-1). Wait, checking units: k = 0.693/t(1/2). So 3.465 × 10^4 = 0.693/t(1/2). Therefore t(1/2) = 0.693/(3.465 × 10^4) = 2 × 10^(-5) s. Expressing as ___ × 10^(-5) s gives 2.
