Let C₁ be the circle of radius 1 with center at the origin. Let C₂ be the circle of radius r with center at the point A = (4, 1), where 1 < r < 3. Two distinct common tangents PQ and ST of C₁ and C₂ are drawn. The tangent PQ touches C₁ at P and C₂ at Q. The tangent ST touches C₁ at S and C₂ at T. Mid points of the line segments PQ and ST are joined to form a line which meets the x-axis at a point B. If AB = sqrt(5), then the value of r² is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Sol. 2 Line joining M₁M₂ will be radical axis of two circles [Figure shows two circles: C₁ centered at origin with radius 1, and C₂ centered at A(4,1) with radius r. Points P, Q on C₁; point B on radical axis M₁M₂; distance AB = √5 marked] C₁: x² + y² - 1 = 0 C₂: (x - 4)² + (y - 1)² = r² Expanding C₂: x² + y² - 8x - 2y + (17 - r²) = 0 Line M₁M₂: (x² + y² - 1) - (x² + y² - 8x - 2y + (17 - r²)) = 0 8x + 2y + (r² - 18) = 0 Point B = ((18 - r²)/8, 0) AB = √5 √[((18 - r²)/8 - 4)² + (0 - 1)²] = √5 √[((18 - r² - 32)/8)² + 1] = √5 √[((-14 - r²)/8)² + 1] = √5 ((r² + 14)/8)² + 1 = 5 ((r² + 14)/8)² = 4 (r² + 14)/8 = 2 ⟹ r² + 14 = 16 r² = 2
