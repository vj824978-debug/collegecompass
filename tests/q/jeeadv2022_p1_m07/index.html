The number of 4-digit integers in the closed interval [2022, 4482] formed by using the digits 0, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 is __________.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 569

Solution

Number of numbers whose digits are 2 0 — — — — 6 × 6 − 7 = 209 2 — — — — 5 × 6 × 6 = 180 3 — — — 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 4 — — — 4 × 6 × 6 = 144 Total number are (36 − 7) + (5 × 6 × 6) + (6 × 6 × 6) + 4 × 6 × 6 = 569
