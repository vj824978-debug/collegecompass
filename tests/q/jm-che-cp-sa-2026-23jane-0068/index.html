The work functions of two metals (M_A and M_B) are in the 1:2 ratio. When these metals are exposed to photons of energy 6 eV, the kinetic energy of liberated electrons of M_A : M_B is in the ratio of 2.642 : 1. The work functions (in eV) of M_A and M_B are respectively.

  1. (1) 3.1, 6.2
  2. (2) 2.3, 4.6
  3. (3) 1.4, 2.8
  4. (4) 1.5, 3.0

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

KE_max = E - phi (KE_max)_1 = 6 - phi_1 ...(1) (KE_max)_2 = 6 - phi_2 ...(2) By equation (1) divide equation (2) (KE_max)_1 / (KE_max)_2 = 2.642 / 1 = (6 - phi_1) / (6 - phi_2) 2.642 / 1 = (6 - phi_1) / (6 - 2*phi_1) Given that phi_1 : phi_2 = 1 : 2, so phi_2 = 2*phi_1 2.642(6 - 2*phi_1) = 6 - phi_1 15.852 - 5.284*phi_1 = 6 - phi_1 15.852 - 6 = 5.284*phi_1 - phi_1 9.852 = 4.284*phi_1 phi_1 = 2.3 eV phi_2 = 2 × 2.3 = 4.6 eV
