In the given TLC, the distance of spot A & B are 5 cm & 7 cm from the bottom of TLC plate, respectively. Rf value of B is x × 10^(-1) times more than A. The value of x is ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 15

Solution

From the TLC diagram: Base line is at 1 cm from bottom. Solvent front is at 9 cm from bottom (1 cm from top of 10 cm plate). Distance moved by solvent from base line = 9 - 1 = 8 cm. For spot A at 5 cm from bottom: Distance moved by A from base line = 5 - 1 = 4 cm. (Rf)A = 4/8 = 1/2. For spot B at 7 cm from bottom: Distance moved by B from base line = 7 - 1 = 6 cm. (Rf)B = 6/8 = 3/4. Ratio: (Rf)B / (Rf)A = (6/8) / (4/8) = 6/4 = 1.5. Given: (Rf)B = x × 10^(-1) × (Rf)A. Therefore: 1.5 = x × 10^(-1). x = 1.5 × 10 = 15.
