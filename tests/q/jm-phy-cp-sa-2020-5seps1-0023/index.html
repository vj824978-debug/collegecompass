A beam of electrons of energy E scatters from a target having atomic spacing of 1Å. The first maximum intensity occurs at θ = 60°. Then E (in eV) is _____ (Planck constant h = 6.64 × 10^(-34) Js, 1eV = 1.6 × 10^(-19) J, electron mass m = 9.1 × 10^(-31) kg)

Answer

Correct answer: 50.00

Solution

For electron diffraction from crystal planes, the condition for constructive interference is: 2d sin(θ) = λ where λ is the de Broglie wavelength of electrons given by: λ = h/sqrt(2mE) Given: d = 1Å = 1 × 10^(-10) m θ = 60° h = 6.64 × 10^(-34) Js m = 9.1 × 10^(-31) kg 1 eV = 1.6 × 10^(-19) J For the first maximum (n = 1): 2d sin(θ) = h/sqrt(2mE) 2 × 10^(-10) × sin(60°) = 6.64 × 10^(-34)/sqrt(2mE) 2 × 10^(-10) × (sqrt(3)/2) = 6.64 × 10^(-34)/sqrt(2mE) sqrt(3) × 10^(-10) = 6.64 × 10^(-34)/sqrt(2mE) Squaring both sides: 3 × 10^(-20) = (6.64 × 10^(-34))^2/(2mE) E = (6.64 × 10^(-34))^2/(2m × 3 × 10^(-20)) E = (6.64)^2 × 10^(-68)/(2 × 9.1 × 10^(-31) × 3 × 10^(-20)) E = 44.0896 × 10^(-68)/(54.6 × 10^(-51)) E = 44.0896 × 10^(-68)/(54.6 × 10^(-51)) = 0.8072 × 10^(-17) J Converting to eV: E = (0.8072 × 10^(-17))/(1.6 × 10^(-19)) = 50.45 eV ≈ 50.00 eV Alternatively: E = (1/2) × (6.64^2 × 10^(-48))/(9.1 × 10^(-31) × 3 × 1.6 × 10^(-19)) = 50.47 eV ≈ 50.00 eV
