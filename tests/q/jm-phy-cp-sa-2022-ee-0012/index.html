An object of mass 5 kg is thrown vertically upwards from the ground. The air resistance produces a constant retarding force of 10 N throughout the motion. The ratio of time of ascent to the time of descent will be equal to [Use g = 10 ms^(-2)].

  1. (1) 1:1
  2. (2) sqrt(2):sqrt(3)
  3. (3) sqrt(3):sqrt(2)
  4. (4) 2:3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Let time taken to ascent is t₁ and that to descent is t₂. Height will be same so H = (1/2)×12t₁² = (1/2)×8t₂². During ascent: net downward force = mg + air resistance = 50 + 10 = 60 N, so a₁ = 60/5 = 12 m/s². During descent: net downward force = mg - air resistance = 50 - 10 = 40 N, so a₂ = 40/5 = 8 m/s². From H equation: 12t₁² = 8t₂², so t₁/t₂ = sqrt(8/12) = sqrt(2/3) = sqrt(2):sqrt(3).
