Suppose that intensity of a laser is (315/pi) W/m^2. The rms electric field, in units of V/m associated with this source is close to the nearest integer is (epsilon_0 = 8.86 × 10^(-12) C^2Nm^(-2); c = 3 × 10^8 ms^(-1))

Answer

Correct answer: 194

Solution

I = epsilon_0 E_rms^2 C E_rms^2 = (1/(epsilon_0 C)) × I = (315/pi) × (1/(epsilon_0 C)) = (4 × 315)/(4π epsilon_0) × (1/(3 × 10^8)) = (4 × 315 × 9 × 10^9)/(3 × 10^8) E_rms^2 = 4 × 315 × 30 E_rms = 2√(315 × 30) = 194.42 Rounded to nearest integer: 194
