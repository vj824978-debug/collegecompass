For A + B ⇌ 2AB, E_a for forward and backward reaction are 180 and 200 kJ mol^(-1) respectively. If catalyst lowers E_a for both reaction by 100 kJ mol^(-1). Which of the following statement is correct?

  1. (1) The enthalpy change for the reaction is +20 kJ mol^(-1).
  2. (2) Catalyst can cause non-spontaneous reactions to occur.
  3. (3) The enthalpy change for the catalysed reaction is different from that of uncatalysed reaction.
  4. (4) Catalyst does not alter the Gibbs energy change of a reaction.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For the reaction A + B ⇌ 2AB: ΔH = E_a(forward) - E_a(backward) = 180 - 200 = -20 kJ mol^(-1). A catalyst lowers activation energy equally for forward and backward reactions, so ΔH remains -20 kJ mol^(-1) (not +20). Catalyst lowers E_a but does not change ΔH or ΔG of the reaction itself, only makes the reaction proceed faster. Statement 4 is correct: catalyst does not alter the Gibbs energy change of a reaction.
