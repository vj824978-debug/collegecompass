Rods x and y of equal dimensions but of different materials are joined as shown in figure. Temperatures of end points A and F are maintained at 100°C and 40°C respectively. Given the thermal conductivity of rod x is three times of that of rod y, the temperature at junction points B and E are (close to):

  1. (1) 89°C and 73°C respectively
  2. (2) 80°C and 60°C respectively
  3. (3) 80°C and 70°C respectively
  4. (4) 60°C and 45°C respectively

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Let R = ℓ/(3KA) The thermal circuit can be represented as: - Rod x has thermal resistance R - Rod y has thermal resistance 3R (since K_x = 3K_y) The equivalent circuit shows: T_A = 100°C connected through R to point B From B, the path splits: - Upper path through C: resistance 3R - Lower path through D: resistance 3R These parallel paths (each 3R) give equivalent resistance of 3R/2 Then from E through R to T_F = 40°C Total equivalent resistance = R + 3R/2 + R = 11R/2 Total heat flow H = (T_A - T_F)/(11R/2) = (100 - 40)/(11R/2) = 120/(11R) From equation (1): H = (100 - T_B)/R From equation (2): H = (100 - T_B)/R From equation (3): H = (T_E - 40)/(3R) Using equations (1) and (2): 120/(11R) = (100 - T_B)/R 120/11 = 100 - T_B T_B = 100 - 120/11 = (1100 - 120)/11 = 980/11 ≈ 89°C Using equations (1) and (3): 120/(11R) = (T_E - 40)/(3R) 360/(11R) = (T_E - 40)/R 360/11 = T_E - 40 T_E = 40 + 360/11 = (440 + 360)/11 = 800/11 ≈ 73°C
