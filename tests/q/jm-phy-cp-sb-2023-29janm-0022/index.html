A 0.4 kg mass takes 8 s to reach ground when dropped from a certain height 'P' above surface of earth. The loss of potential energy in the last second of fall is ____ J. (Take g = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 300

Solution

8 = sqrt(2h/g) => h = 320 m Distance covered in last second = (1/2) g × 8^2 - (1/2) g × 7^2 h' = 75 m => Loss of potential energy = mgh' = 0.4 × 10 × 75 J = 300 J
