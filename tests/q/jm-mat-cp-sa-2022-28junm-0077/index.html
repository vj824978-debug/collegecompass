Let the plane P: r.a = d contain the line of intersection of two planes r.(i+3j-k) = 6 and r.(-6i+5j-k) = 7. If the plane P passes through the point (2, 3, 1/2), then the value of |13a|^2/d^2 is equal to

  1. (1) 90
  2. (2) 93
  3. (3) 95
  4. (4) 97

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

First find the family of planes passing through the line of intersection of P1: x + 3y - z = 6 and P2: 6x + 5y - z = 7. This is given by x(1-6λ) + y(3+5λ) + z(-1-λ) - (6+7λ) = 0. Since plane P passes through (2, 3, 1/2), substitute: 2(1-6λ) + 3(3+5λ) + (1/2)(-1-λ) - (6+7λ) = 0. Simplifying: 2 - 12λ + 9 + 15λ - 1/2 - λ/2 - 6 - 7λ = 0 gives 9/2 - 9λ/2 = 0, so λ = 1. The required plane is -5x + 8y - 2z = 13 or r.(-5i + 8j - 2k) = 13. Thus a = (-5i + 8j - 2k)/13 and d = 1. Therefore |13a|^2/d^2 = 13^2/13^2 × (25+64+4) = 93.
