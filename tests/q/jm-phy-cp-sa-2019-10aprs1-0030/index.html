The displacement of a damped harmonic oscillator is given by x(t) = e^(-0.1t) cos (10π t + φ). Here t is in seconds. The time taken for its amplitude of vibration to drop to half of its initial value is close to:

  1. (1) 4 s
  2. (2) 13 s
  3. (3) 7 s
  4. (4) 27 s

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The amplitude of the damped harmonic oscillator is A(t) = A_0 e^(-0.1t) We need to find the time when the amplitude drops to half of its initial value: A(t) = A_0/2 A_0 e^(-0.1t) = A_0/2 e^(-0.1t) = 1/2 Taking natural logarithm on both sides: -0.1t = ln(1/2) -0.1t = -ln(2) t = ln(2)/0.1 t = 10 ln(2) t = 10 × 0.693 t ≈ 6.93 s t ≈ 7 sec
