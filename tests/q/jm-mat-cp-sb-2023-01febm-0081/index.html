Let a1 = 8, a2, a3, ...an be an A.P. If the sum of its first four terms is 50 and the sum of its last four terms is 170, then the product of its middle two terms is ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 754

Solution

Given, a1 = 8, a2, a3,...an are in A.P. For an A.P. with first term a1 = 8 and common difference d: Sum of first four terms: a1 + a2 + a3 + a4 = 50 8 + (8+d) + (8+2d) + (8+3d) = 50 32 + 6d = 50 6d = 18 d = 3 Sum of last four terms: an-3 + an-2 + an-1 + an = 170 Using an = a1 + (n-1)d = 8 + (n-1)3 Sum of last four terms = 2(an-3 + an)/2 × 2 = 2(2an - 9) = 170 2(2an - 9) = 170 4an - 18 = 170 4an = 188 an = 47 Finding n: 8 + (n-1)3 = 47 (n-1)3 = 39 n-1 = 13 n = 14 The middle two terms are a7 and a8 (since n = 14, middle positions are 7 and 8) a7 = 8 + 6(3) = 8 + 18 = 26 a8 = 8 + 7(3) = 8 + 21 = 29 Product of middle two terms = a7 × a8 = 26 × 29 = 754
