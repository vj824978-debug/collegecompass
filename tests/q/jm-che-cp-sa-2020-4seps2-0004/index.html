In the following reaction sequence, [C] is: Starting material is a benzene ring with NH2 group at top and CH3 group at bottom. (i) NaNO2 + HCl, 0-5°C leads to [A], then (ii) Cu2Cl2 + HCl. [A] goes through Cl2/hv to form [B]. [B] undergoes Na/dry ether to form [C] (Major Product).

  1. (1) Cl-phenyl-CH2-CH2-phenyl-Cl (where phenyl rings are para-substituted with Cl and CH2Cl respectively)
  2. (2) Cl-phenyl-CH2-phenyl-CH2-Cl (where phenyl rings are para-substituted)
  3. (3) Cl-phenyl-CH2-CH2-phenyl-Cl (where first phenyl has Cl, second has Cl at para position)
  4. (4) CH3-phenyl-phenyl-CH3 (where both phenyl rings are para-substituted with CH3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Step 1: Starting material is 4-methylaniline (p-methylaniline) with NH2 and CH3 groups in para positions on benzene ring. Step 2: Treatment with NaNO2 + HCl at 0-5°C converts the amino group (-NH2) to a diazonium group (-N2+). This gives [A] = 4-methylbenzenediazonium chloride. Step 3: Treatment of [A] with Cu2Cl2 + HCl (Sandmeyer reaction) replaces the diazonium group with Cl, giving [B] = 4-chlorotoluene (p-chlorotoluene) with Cl on benzene ring and CH3 as methyl substituent. Step 4: [B] (4-chlorotoluene) undergoes free radical chlorination with Cl2 under hv (light). The CH3 group undergoes benzylic chlorination to give CH2Cl group. This produces the benzyl chloride intermediate. Step 5: Treatment with Na/dry ether performs a Wurtz-type coupling reaction between two molecules of the benzyl chloride. Two benzyl chloride molecules couple via the CH2Cl groups to form a C-C bond between the benzylic carbons. Step 6: The product [C] is Cl-phenyl-CH2-CH2-phenyl-Cl, which is 1,2-bis(4-chlorophenyl)ethane or 4,4'-dichlorobibenzyl, where two 4-chlorophenyl groups are connected by an ethylene bridge (-CH2-CH2-).
