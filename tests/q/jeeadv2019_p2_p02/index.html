A block of mass 2M is attached to a massless spring with spring-constant k. This block is connected to two other blocks of masses M and 2M using two massless pulleys and strings. The accelerations of the blocks are a₁, a₂ and a₃ as shown in the figure. [Figure shows: a spring attached to a wall on left, connected to a block labeled 2M with acceleration a₁ pointing right. Via a pulley system, this connects to a block M below with acceleration a₂ pointing down, and a block 2M at bottom with acceleration a₃ pointing down.] The system is released from rest with the spring in its unstretched state. The maximum extension of the spring is x₀. Which of the following option(s) is/are correct? [g is the acceleration due to gravity, neglect friction]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) a₂ - a₁ = a₁ - a₃
  2. (B) At an extension of x₀/4 of the spring, the magnitude of acceleration of the block connected to the spring is 3g/10
  3. (C) x₀ = 4Mg/k
  4. (D) When spring achieves an extension of x₀/2 for the first time, the speed of the block connected to the spring is 3g√(M/5k)

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

Sol. A [FIGURE: Pulley system with spring attached to fixed support, two masses M and 2M connected via pulley B, with accelerations a₁ and a₂ marked] In the frame of pulley B, the hanging masses have accelerations: M → (a₂ - a₁), 2M → (a₃ - a₁) : downward. ∴ (a₃ - a₁) = -(a₃ - a₁) [constant] Assuming that the extension of the spring is x We consider the FBD of A: 2M. d²x/dt² = 2T - kx where a₁ = d²x/dt² ...(i) and the FBD of the rest of the system in the frame of pulley B: [FIGURE: Free body diagrams showing forces T and weights for blocks M and 2M] Upward acceleration of block M w.r.t. the pulley B = Downward acceleration of block 2M w.r.t the pulley T - M(g - a₁)/M = 2M(g - a₁) - T/2M ⟹ T = 4M/3(g - a₁) ...(ii) substituting in equation (i), we get 2M. a₁ = 8M/3(g - a₁) - kx or 14M/3 a₁ = 8Mg/3 - kx ...(iii) This is the equation of SHM Maximum extension = 2 × Amplitude i.e. x₀ = 2 × 8Mg/3k At x₀/4, acceleration is easily found from equation (iii): 14M/3 a₁ = 8Mg/3 - 4Mg/3 a₁ = 2g/7 At x₀/2, speed of the block (2M) = ω × amplitude = √(3k/14M) × 8Mg/3k ∴ option (A) is correct
