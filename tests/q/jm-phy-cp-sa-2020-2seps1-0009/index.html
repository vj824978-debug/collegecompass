Shown in the figure is rigid and uniform one meter long rod AB held in horizontal position by two strings tied to its ends and attached to the ceiling. The rod is of mass 'm' and has another weight of mass 2m hung at a distance of 75 cm from A. The tension in the string at A is:

  1. (1) 2 mg
  2. (2) 0.5 mg
  3. (3) 0.75 mg
  4. (4) 1 mg

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given: - Rod AB is 1 meter (100 cm) long - Rod has mass m - Weight of mass 2m is hung at 75 cm from A - Rod is held horizontally by two strings at A and B For equilibrium, taking torque about point B: τ_B = 0 (torque about point B is zero) (T_A) × 100 - (mg) × 50 - (2mg) × 25 = 0 The rod's weight mg acts at its center (50 cm from A) The hanging weight 2mg acts at 75 cm from A, which is 25 cm from B 100 T_A = mg × 50 + 2mg × 25 100 T_A = 50mg + 50mg 100 T_A = 100mg T_A = 1 mg Therefore, the tension in the string at A is 1 mg
