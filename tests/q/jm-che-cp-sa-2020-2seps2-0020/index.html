The molecular geometry of SF6 is octahedral. What is the geometry of SF4 (including lone pair(s) of electrons, if any)?

  1. (1) Trigonal bipyramidal
  2. (2) Square planar
  3. (3) Tetrahedral
  4. (4) Pyramidal

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For SF4: Step 1: Determine valence electrons of sulfur (S) Sulfur is in group 16, so it has 6 valence electrons. Step 2: Count bonding electrons SF4 has 4 S-F bonds, which means 4 bonding pairs (8 electrons used in bonding) Step 3: Calculate lone pairs on sulfur Total valence electrons = 6 Electrons used in 4 bonds = 4 electrons (one from S in each bond) Remaining electrons on S = 6 - 4 = 2 electrons = 1 lone pair Step 4: Determine electron geometry and molecular geometry Total electron groups around S = 4 bonding pairs + 1 lone pair = 5 electron groups With 5 electron groups, the electron geometry is trigonal bipyramidal. With 4 bonding pairs and 1 lone pair in a trigonal bipyramidal arrangement, the lone pair occupies one of the equatorial positions, resulting in a trigonal bipyramidal molecular geometry (including the lone pair of electrons). Therefore, the shape (including lone pair of electrons) is Trigonal bipyramidal.
