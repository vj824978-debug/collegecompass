The first and second ionisation enthalpies of a metal are 496 and 4560 kJ mol^(-1), respectively. How many moles of HCl and H2SO4, respectively, will be needed to react completely with 1 mole of the metal hydroxide?

  1. (1) 1 and 1
  2. (2) 1 and 2
  3. (3) 2 and 0.5
  4. (4) 1 and 0.5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Metal: First ionisation enthalpy = 496 kJ/mole Second ionisation enthalpy = 4560 kJ/mole According to the given information, ionisation enthalpies Metal belong to 1st group i.e. Monovalent cation MOH + HCl → MCl + H2O 1 mole 1 mole For reaction with HCl: 1 mole of HCl is needed to react with 1 mole of MOH 2MOH + H2SO4 → M2SO4 + H2O 1 mole 1/2 mole For reaction with H2SO4: 1/2 mole of H2SO4 is needed to react with 1 mole of MOH Therefore, 1 mole of HCl and 0.5 mole of H2SO4 will be needed.
