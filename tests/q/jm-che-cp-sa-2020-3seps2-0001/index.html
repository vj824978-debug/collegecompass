The incorrect statement(s) among (a) - (d) regarding acid rain is (are):

  1. (1) (a) It can corrode water pipes
  2. (2) (b) It can damage structures made up of stone
  3. (3) (c) It cannot cause respiratory ailments in animals
  4. (4) (d) It is not harmful for trees

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(1) Acid rain corrodes water pipes resulting in the leaching of heavy metals such as iron, lead and copper into the drinking water. So statement (a) is CORRECT. (2) Acid rain damages buildings and other structures made of stone or metal. So statement (b) is CORRECT. (3) It causes respiratory ailments in human beings and animals. So statement (c) is INCORRECT - acid rain CAN cause respiratory ailments. (4) It is harmful for agriculture, trees and plants as it washes down the nutrients needed for its growth. So statement (d) is INCORRECT - acid rain IS harmful for trees. Therefore, the incorrect statements are (c) and (d).
