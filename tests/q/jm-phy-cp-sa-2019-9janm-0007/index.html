A series AC circuit containing an inductor (20 mH), a capacitor (120 μF) and a resistor (60 Ω) is driven by an AC source of 24 V/50 Hz. The energy dissipated in the circuit in 60 s is:

  1. (1) 5.65 × 10^2 J
  2. (2) 2.26 × 10^3 J
  3. (3) 5.17 × 10^2 J
  4. (4) 3.39 × 10^3 J

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: L = 20 mH = 20 × 10^(-3) H C = 120 μF = 120 × 10^(-6) F R = 60 Ω V = 24 V (RMS) f = 50 Hz t = 60 s Calculating reactances: X_L = 2πfL = 2π × 50 × 20 × 10^(-3) = 100π × 20 × 10^(-3) = 2π = 6.28 Ω X_C = 1/(2πfC) = 1/(100π × 120 × 10^(-6)) = 10^6/(12000π) = 10^3/(12π) X_C = 250/(3π) Ω Converting to decimal: X_C ≈ 26.53 Ω Net reactance: |X_L - X_C| = |6.28 - 26.53| = 20.25 Ω Impedance: Z = sqrt(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2) = sqrt(60^2 + 20.25^2) = sqrt(3600 + 410.06) = sqrt(4010.06) ≈ 63.33 Ω RMS current: I_rms = V/Z = 24/63.33 ≈ 0.379 A Energy dissipated (only in resistor): Energy = I_rms^2 × R × t = (0.379)^2 × 60 × 60 = 0.1437 × 3600 = 5.17 × 10^2 J
