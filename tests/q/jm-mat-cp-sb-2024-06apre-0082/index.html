The length of the latus rectum and directrices of a hyperbola with eccentricity e are 9 and x = ±4/√3, respectively. Let the line y - √3 x + √3 = 0 touch this hyperbola at (x₀, y₀). If m is the product of the focal distances of the point (x₀, y₀), then 4e² + m is equal to ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 61

Solution

Given: 2b²/a = 9 and a/c = ±4/√3. From tangency condition of line y - √3 x + √3 = 0 with hyperbola, we get 6 = 6a² - 9a, solving gives a = 2, b² = 9. Hyperbola equation: x²/4 - y²/9 = 1. Point of contact: (x₀, y₀) = (4, 3√3). Eccentricity: e = √(1 + 9/4) = √13/2. Product of focal distances: m = (x₀e + a)(x₀e - a) = x₀²e² - a² = 20e² - 4. Therefore: 4e² + m = 4(13/4) + 20(13/4) - 4 = 13 + 65 - 4 = 61.
