How many compounds among the following compounds show inductive, mesomeric as well as hyperconjugation effects?

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

To show all three effects (inductive, mesomeric, and hyperconjugation), a compound needs: 1) An electron-donating or withdrawing group (inductive effect), 2) A group with lone pair or pi electrons for resonance (mesomeric effect), 3) An alpha hydrogen on a carbon adjacent to an unsaturated system (hyperconjugation). Analyzing the compounds: 1) Anisole (OCH3 on benzene) - shows inductive and mesomeric but limited hyperconjugation. 2) Unsaturated ketone - shows all three effects. 3) Chlorobenzene with cyclohexyl - shows inductive but limited mesomeric. 4) Nitrobenzene with ethyl - shows all three effects. 5) Nitrobenzene with ethyl substituent - shows all three. 6) Dimethylbenzene (m-xylene) - limited mesomeric. 7) Cyclohexene with COCH3 and CH3 - shows all three effects. The compounds showing all three effects total to 4.
