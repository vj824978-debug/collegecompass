The correct statement(s) for the following addition reactions is(are) (i) [Structure: alkene with H3C and H groups on one carbon, H and CH3 groups on the other carbon] + Br2/CHCl3 → M and N (ii) [Structure: alkene with H3C and CH3 groups on one carbon, H and H groups on the other carbon] + Br2/CHCl3 → O and P

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) (M and O) and (N and P) are two pairs of diastereomers
  2. (B) Bromination proceeds through trans-addition in both the reactions
  3. (C) O and P are identical molecules
  4. (D) (M and O) and (N and P) are two pairs of enantiomers

Answer

Correct answer: AB

Solution

[Two chemical reaction schemes showing bromination of alkenes] First reaction: 2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene with one methyl and one hydrogen on each carbon, treated with Br₂ in CHCl₃, produces two diastereomeric products labeled (M) and (N). In structure (M): top carbon has H and CH₃ pointing up, Br pointing down on both carbons, bottom carbon has H and CH₃ pointing down. In structure (N): top carbon has Br and H pointing to sides, bottom carbon has Br and H pointing to sides, with CH₃ groups pointing up and down respectively. Second reaction: 2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene with two hydrogens on each carbon, treated with Br₂ in CHCl₃, produces two diastereomeric products labeled (O) and (P). In structure (O): shown with vertical hatching on left side separating the two stereoisomers. In structure (P): similar structure with vertical hatching. Note states: (M and O) and (N and P) have no mirror image relationship. Hence these two pairs are diastereomers. Bromination proceeds through trans-addition in both the reactions.
