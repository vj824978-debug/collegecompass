Let the maximum and minimum values of |sqrt(8x - x^2 - 12 - 4)| + (x - 7)^2, x ∈ R be M and m respectively. Then M^2 - m^2 is equal to ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 1600

Solution

From y = sqrt(8x - x^2 - 12), we get y^2 = -(x - 4)^2 + 16 - 12, which gives (x - 4)^2 + y^2 = 4. This is a circle with center C(4, 0) and radius 2. The function represents the distance from point (x, y) on the semicircle to point P(7, 4) plus the square of distance from x to 7. The minimum value m = 9 occurs at the closest point on the circle to P. The maximum value M = 41 occurs at the farthest point. Therefore, M^2 - m^2 = 41^2 - 9^2 = 1681 - 81 = 1600.
