Which of the following postulate of Bohr's model of hydrogen atom is not in agreement with quantum mechanical model of an atom?

  1. (1) The electron in a H atom's stationary state moves in a circle around the nucleus.
  2. (2) An atom in a stationary state does not emit electromagnetic radiation as long as it stays in the same state.
  3. (3) An atom can take only certain distinct energies E1, E2, E3, etc. These allowed states of constant energy are called the stationary states of atom.
  4. (4) When an electron makes a transition from a higher energy stationary state to a lower energy stationary state, then it emits a photon of light.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

According to quantum mechanics, electrons do not move in definite circular orbits around the nucleus. Instead, they exist in orbitals (regions of probability) described by wave functions. Option 1 describes the classical circular orbit concept of Bohr's model, which contradicts the quantum mechanical model where electrons are described by probability distributions, not fixed circular paths. All other postulates (2, 3, and 4) are consistent with quantum mechanics.
