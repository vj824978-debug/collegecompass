The correct combination of names for isomeric alcohols with molecular formula C₄H₁₀O is/are

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) tert-butanol and 2-methylpropan-2-ol
  2. (B) tert-butanol and 1, 1-dimethylethan-1-ol
  3. (C) n-butanol and butan-1-ol
  4. (D) isobutyl alcohol and 2-methylpropan-1-ol

Answer

Correct answer: ACD

Solution

The image shows four structural isomers of C₄H₁₀O: 1. H₃C-CH₂-CH₂-CH₂-OH labeled as n-butanol or butan-1-ol (primary alcohol) 2. H₃C-CH₂-CH(OH)-CH₃ labeled as sec-butyl alcohol or butan-2-ol (secondary alcohol) 3. H₃C-CH(CH₃)-CH₂-OH labeled as 2-methylpropan-1-ol or isobutyl alcohol (primary alcohol) 4. H₃C-C(OH)(CH₃)₂ labeled as tert-butanol or 2-methylpropan-2-ol (tertiary alcohol) These four compounds all have the molecular formula C₄H₁₀O but differ in the position and type of hydroxyl group attachment, making them constitutional isomers.
