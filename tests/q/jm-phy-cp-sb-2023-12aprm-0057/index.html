To maintain a speed of 80 km/h by a bus of mass 500 kg on a plane rough road for 4 km distance, the work done by the engine of the bus will be __ kJ. [The coefficient of friction between tyre of bus and road is 0.04]

Answer

Correct answer: 784

Solution

Given: v = 80 km/h, m = 500 kg, S = 4 km, μ = 0.04. Friction force F = μmg = 0.04 × 500 × 10 = 200 N. Converting speed: v = 80 × (5/18) = 22.22 m/s. Power required: P = F × v = 200 × 22.22 watts. Time taken: t = 4000/22.22 = 180 s. Work done: W = P × t = (20 × 80 × 5 × 9.8)/18 × (4 × 10^3)/(80 × 5/18) = 784 kJ
