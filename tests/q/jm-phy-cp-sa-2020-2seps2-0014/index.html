An inductance coil has a reactance of 100 Ω. When an AC signal of frequency 1000 Hz is applied to the coil, the applied voltage leads the current by 45°. The self-inductance of the coil is :

  1. (1) 1.1 × 10^(-2) H
  2. (2) 1.1 × 10^(-1) H
  3. (3) 5.5 × 10^(-3) H
  4. (4) 6.7 × 10^(-7) H

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Reactance of inductance coil = sqrt(R^2 + x_L^2) = 100 ...(i) f = 1000 Hz of applied AC signal Voltage leads current by 45° From phasor diagram: tan(45°) = ix_L / iR = ωL / R i.e. R = X_L = ωL Putting in eqn(i): sqrt(X_L^2 + X_L^2) = 100 sqrt(2X_L) = 100 ⟹ X_L = 50√2 i.e. ωL = 50√2 L = (50√2) / ω = (50√2) / (2πf) = (25√2) / (π × 1000) = 1.125 × 10^(-2) H Rounded to 1.1 × 10^(-2) H
