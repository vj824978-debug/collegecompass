A student has been given a compound "x" of molecular formula -C_6H_7 N . 'x' is sparingly soluble in water. However, on addition of dilute mineral acid , 'x' becomes soluble in water. 'x' when treated with CHCl_3 and KOH (alc.) 'y' is produced. 'y' has a specific unpleasant smell. On treatment with benzenesulphonyl chloride, 'x' gives a compound 'z' which is soluble in alkali. The number of different "H" atoms present in 'z' is:-

  1. (1) 5
  2. (2) 8
  3. (3) 4
  4. (4) 7

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The compound 'x' with molecular formula C_6H_7N that is sparingly soluble in water but becomes soluble upon addition of dilute mineral acid indicates it contains a basic nitrogen (primary amine, -NH_2). The structure of 'x' is aniline (C_6H_5-NH_2). When 'x' (aniline) is treated with CHCl_3 and KOH (alcoholic), it undergoes the Carbylamine reaction (Isocyanide reaction) to produce 'y', which is phenyl isocyanide (C_6H_5-NC) - this has the characteristic unpleasant smell typical of isocyanides. When 'x' (aniline) is treated with benzenesulphonyl chloride (C_6H_5-SO_2-Cl), it produces 'z': C_6H_5-NH_2 + C_6H_5-SO_2-Cl → C_6H_5-NH-SO_2-C_6H_5 (N-phenylbenzenesulphonamide) This compound 'z' is soluble in alkali because the N-H group is acidic and can be deprotonated to form a salt. Counting the different H atoms in 'z' (C_6H_5-NH-SO_2-C_6H_5): - The NH hydrogen: 1 unique H atom (acidic) - The first phenyl group: 5 H atoms that are equivalent (aromatic) - The second phenyl group: 5 H atoms that are equivalent (aromatic) However, due to symmetry and magnetic equivalence in NMR: - The NH group: 1 type of H - The aromatic hydrogens on both phenyl rings are in the same chemical environment: 1 type - Total distinct H environments: 2 types But counting by position and considering the structure C_6H_5-NH-SO_2-C_6H_5: - NH hydrogen: 1 - Ortho hydrogens on both rings: equivalent - Meta hydrogens on both rings: equivalent - Para hydrogens on both rings: equivalent - Total: 4 different types of H atoms (1 for NH, 1 for ortho, 1 for meta, 1 for para) The answer is 4.
