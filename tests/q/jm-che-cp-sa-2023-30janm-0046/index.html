In the extraction of copper, its sulphide ore is heated in a reverberatory furnace after mixing with silica to

  1. (1) Decrease the temperature needed for roasting of Cu2S
  2. (2) Remove calcium as CaSiO3
  3. (3) Separate CuO as CuSiO3
  4. (4) Remove FeO as FeSiO3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

FeO + SiO2 → FeSiO3 Basic Acidic (Slag) When silica (acidic oxide) is mixed with the sulphide ore during roasting in a reverberatory furnace, it reacts with the basic oxides formed during the process. Iron oxide (FeO), which is a basic oxide, combines with silica (SiO2), an acidic oxide, to form iron silicate (FeSiO3). This iron silicate forms a slag that can be easily separated from the copper-containing matte. This process helps remove iron impurities as a fusible slag, which is the primary reason for adding silica to the ore.
