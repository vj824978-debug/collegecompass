The correct match of the group reagents in List-I for precipitating the metal ion given in List-II from solutions, is List-I: (P) Passing H₂S in the presence of NH₄OH (Q) (NH₄)₂CO₃ in the presence of NH₄OH (R) NH₄OH in the presence of NH₄Cl (S) Passing H₂S in the presence of dilute HCl List-II: (1) Cu²⁺ (2) Al³⁺ (3) Mn²⁺ (4) Ba²⁺ (5) Mg²⁺

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) P → 3; Q → 4; R → 2; S → 1
  2. (B) P → 4; Q → 2; R → 3; S → 1
  3. (C) P → 3; Q → 4; R → 1; S → 5
  4. (D) P → 5; Q → 3; R → 2; S → 4

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

P - 3 H₂S in the presence of NH₄OH precipitates Mn²⁺ i.e. group IV cation Q - 4 (NH₄)₂CO₃ in the presence of NH₄OH precipitate Ba²⁺ i.e. group V cation R - 2 NH₄OH in the presence of NH₄Cl precipitate Al³⁺ i.e. group III cation S - 1 H₂S in presence of HCl will ppt. Cu²⁺, i.e. group II cation.
