The area of the region A = {(x,y) : 4x^2 + y^2 <= 8 and y^2 <= 4x} is :

  1. (1) pi/2 + 2
  2. (2) pi + 2/3
  3. (3) pi + 4
  4. (4) pi/2 + 1/3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

A = 2 integral from 0 to 1 of 2*sqrt(x) dx + 2 integral from 1 to sqrt(2) of sqrt(8 - 4x^2) dx = 8/3(x^(3/2)) evaluated from 0 to 1 + 4 integral from 1 to sqrt(2) of sqrt(2 - x^2) dx = 8/3 + 4 * [1/2(x*sqrt(2 - x^2) + 2*sin^(-1)(x/sqrt(2)))] evaluated from 1 to sqrt(2) = 8/3 + 2[2 * pi/2 - 1 - 2 * pi/4] = 8/3 + 2*pi - 2 - pi = pi + 2/3 sq. units
