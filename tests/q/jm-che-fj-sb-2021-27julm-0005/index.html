1.46 g of a biopolymer dissolved in a 100 mL water at 300 K exerted an osmotic pressure of 2.42×10^-3 bar. The molar mass of the biopolymer is ________ × 10^4 g mol^-1. (Round off to the Nearest integer) [Use: R = 0.083 L bar mol^-1 K^-1]

Answer

Correct answer: 15

Solution

Using osmotic pressure formula: π = CRT where C is concentration in mol/L. Rearranging: π = (n/V)RT = (m/(M×V))RT. Therefore: M = (m×R×T)/(π×V). Substituting values: M = (1.46×1000×0.083×300)/(2.42×10^-3×100) = (1.46×1000×0.083×300)/(2.42×10^-3×100) = 14.96×10^3 gm = 15×10^3 gm, which equals 1.5×10^4 g mol^-1
