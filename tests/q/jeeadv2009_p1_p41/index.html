Look at the drawing given in the figure which has been drawn with ink of uniform line-thickness. The mass of ink used to draw each of the two inner circles, and each of the two line segments is m. The mass of the ink used to draw the outer circle is 6m. The coordinates of the centres of the different parts are: outer circle (0, 0) left inner circle (-a, a), right inner circle (a, a), vertical line (0, 0) and horizontal line (0, -a). The y-coordinate of the centre of mass of the ink in this drawing is [Figure shows a circle with two smaller circles inside it at the top, a vertical line segment below, and a horizontal line segment at the bottom]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) a/10
  2. (B) a/8
  3. (C) a/12
  4. (D) a/3

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

Y_cm = (6m × 0 + m × a + m × a + m × 0 + m × (-a)) / (10 × m) Y_cm = (0 + ma + ma + 0 - ma) / (10m) Y_cm = ma / (10m) Y_cm = a/10 [Diagram shows a coordinate system with 5 point masses arranged in a symmetric pattern: one mass m at top center (0, 6m), two masses m on left and right at (-a, a) and (a, a), one mass m at bottom center (0, -a), and labels indicating the configuration with total mass 10m]
