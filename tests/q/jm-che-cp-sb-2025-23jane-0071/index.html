0.01 mole of an organic compound (X) containing 10% hydrogen, on complete combustion produced 0.9 g H2O. Molar mass of (X) is _____ g mol^(-1).

Answer

Correct answer: 100

Solution

Organic compound combustion → H2O 0.9 gm ∴ mole of H2O = 0.9/18 = 0.05 mole ∴ mole of H in H2O = 0.05 × 2 = 0.1 mole = mole of H in 0.01 mole Organic compound ∴ wt of H atom in 0.01 mole compound = 0.1 × 1 = 0.1 gm ∴ wt of H atom in one mole compound = 0.1/0.01 = 10 gm ∴ wt. % of H = (wt. of H in one mole compound)/(Molar mass of compound) × 1 10 = (10/M) × 100 M = 100
