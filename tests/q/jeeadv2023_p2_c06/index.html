Atoms of metals x, y, and z form face-centred cubic (fcc) unit cell of edge length L_x, body-centred cubic (bcc) unit cell of edge length L_y, and simple cubic unit cell of edge length L_z, respectively. If r_z = (sqrt(3)/2)r_y ; r_y = (8/sqrt(3))r_x ; M_z = (2/3)M_y and M_Z = 3M_x. then the correct statement(s) is/are [Given: M_x, M_y, and M_z are molar masses of metals x, y, and z, respectively. r_x, r_y, and r_z are atomic radii of metals x, y, and z, respectively.]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) Packing efficiency of unit cell of x > Packing efficiency of unit cell of y > Packing efficiency of unit cell of z
  2. (B) L_y > L_z
  3. (C) L_z > L_y
  4. (D) Density of x > Density of y

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

For metal 'x' Fcc: Edge length, a₁ = L_x For metal 'y' Bcc: Edge length, a₂ = L_y For metal 'z' Bcc: Edge length, a₃ = L_z r_x = (√3/2)r_y, r_y = 8/√3 r_z, M_x = (3/2)M_y and M_z = 3M_x For option (A) (i) For FCC (Z = 4) metal 'x', 4r_x = √2L_x P.E = (Z × (4/3)π(r_x)³)/(a₁³) = (4 × (4/3)π(r_x)³)/(L_x)³ = (4 × (4/3)π(r_x)³)/((4/√2)r_x)³ = 0.24π (ii) For BCC (Z = 2) metal 'y', 4r_y = √3L_y P.E = (Z × (4/3)π(r_y)³)/(a₂³) = (2 × (4/3)π(r_y)³)/(L_y)³ = (2 × (4/3)π(r_y)³)/((4/√3)r_y)³ = 0.22π (iii) For SC (Z = 1) metal 'z', 2r_z = L_z P.E = (Z × (4/3)π(r_z)³)/(a₃³) = (1 × (4/3)π(r_z)³)/(L_z)³ = (1 × (4/3)π(r_z)³)/((2r_z)³) = π/6 = 0.17π (P.E)_FCC > (P.E)_BCC > (P.E)_SC For option (B) 4r_y = √3L_y, 2r_z = L_z L_y = 4r_y/√3 L_y/L_z = (4r_y)/(√3 × 2r_z) = 2r_y/(√3r_z) = (√3/2)r_y × 2/(√3 × (1/2)r_y) = 4/3 So, L_y > L_z For option (C) 4r_x = √2L_x, 4r_y = √3L_y L_x = 4r_x/√2, L_y = 4r_y/√3 L_x/L_y = (√3r_x)/(8/√3r_y) = √3/(8√2) = 3/(8√2) So, L_x < L_y incorrect For option (D) d_x = (4 × M_x)/((4r_x/√2) × N_A) d_y = (2 × M_y)/((4r_y/√3) × N_A) r_y = (8/√3)r_z, M_y = (1/2)M_x d_x/d_y = 512/(2√2) = 256/√2 So d_x > d_y (correct)
