A cylindrical capillary tube of 0.2 mm radius is made by joining two capillaries T₁ and T₂ of different materials having water contact angles of 0° and 60°, respectively. The capillary tube is dipped vertically in water in two different configurations, case I and II as shown in figure. Which of the following option(s) is (are) correct? [Figure shows two configurations: Case I: T₂ (contact angle 60°) on top, T₁ (contact angle 0°) on bottom, tube dipped in water container Case II: T₁ (contact angle 0°) on top, T₂ (contact angle 60°) on bottom, tube dipped in water container] [Surface tension of water = 0.075 N/m, density of water = 1000 kg/m³, take g = 10 m/s²]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) For case I, if the joint is kept at 8 cm above the water surface, the height of water column in the tube will be 7.5 cm. (Neglect the weight of the water in the meniscus)
  2. (B) For case I, if the capillary joint is 5 cm above the water surface, the height of water column raised in the tube will be more than 8.75 cm. (Neglect the weight of the water in the meniscus)
  3. (C) For case II, if the capillary joint is 5 cm above the water surface, the height of water column raised in the tube will be 3.75 cm. (Neglect the weight of the water in the meniscus)
  4. (D) The correction in the height of water column raised in the tube, due to weight of water contained in the meniscus, will be different for both cases.

Answer

Correct answer: ACD

Solution

When T₁ is in contact with water then h = 2T cos θ₁/rpg = 7.5 cm < 8m. But in option (B) height is insufficient. When T₂ is in contact with water then h = 2T cos θ₂/rpg = 3.75 cm < 5 cm. Volume of water in the meniscus depends upon the angle of contact.
