Galena (an ore) is partially oxidized by passing air through it at high temperature. After some time, the passage of air is stopped, but the heating is continued in a closed furnace such that the contents undergo self-reduction. The weight (in kg) of Pb produced per kg of O₂ consumed is ____. (Atomic weights in g mol⁻¹: O = 16, S = 32, Pb = 207)

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 6.47

Solution

Sol. 6.47 kg 2PbS + 3O₂ ⟶ 2PbO + 2SO₂ 2PbO + PbS ⟶ 3Pb + SO₂ _________________________________________________ 3PbS + 3O₂ ⟶ 3Pb + 3SO₂ 32 kg 207 kg 1 kg ⟶ (207/32) = 6.47 kg
