Let P be the plane passing through the intersection of the planes r·(i + 3j - k) = 5 and r·(2i - j + k) = 3, and the point (2, 1, -2). Let the position vectors of the points X and Y be i - 2j + 4k and 5i - j + 2k respectively. Then the points

  1. (1) X and X + Y are on the same side of P
  2. (2) Y and Y - X are on the opposite sides of P
  3. (3) X and Y are on the opposite sides of P
  4. (4) X + Y and X - Y are on the same side of P

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The equation of required plane is π: (x + 3y - z - 5) + λ(2x - y + z - 3) = 0. Since (2, 1, -2) lies on it: 2 + λ(-2) = 0, so λ = 1. Hence π: 3x + 2y - 8 = 0. For X: π_x = -9, π_y = 5, π_xy = -22 (negative). For Y: π_x = 15, π_y = -5, π_xy = 6 (positive). Since π_xy values have opposite signs, X and Y are on opposite sides of plane P.
