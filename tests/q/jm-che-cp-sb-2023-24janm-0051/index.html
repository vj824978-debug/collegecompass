The d-electronic configuration of [CoCl4]^(2-) in tetrahedral crystal field is e^m t2^n. Sum of "m" and "number of unpaired electrons" is ______

Answer

Correct answer: 7

Solution

[CoCl4]^(2-) Co^(2+) = 4s^0 3d^7 In tetrahedral crystal field, the d-orbital splitting is inverted compared to octahedral: The energy order is: t2 < e For d^7 configuration in tetrahedral field: e^4 t2g^3 Electron configuration in tetrahedral field: e orbital: ↑↓ ↑↓ (4 electrons) t2 orbital: ↑ ↑ ↑ (3 electrons) Configuration = e^4 t2^3 Therefore: m = 4 (number of electrons in e orbitals) Number of unpaired electrons = 3 (all three electrons in t2 orbitals are unpaired) Sum of m and number of unpaired electrons = 4 + 3 = 7
