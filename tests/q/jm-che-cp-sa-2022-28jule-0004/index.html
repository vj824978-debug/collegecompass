The products obtained during treatment of hard water using Clark's method are:

  1. (1) CaCO3 and MgCO3
  2. (2) Ca(OH)2 and Mg(OH)2
  3. (3) CaCO3 and Mg(OH)2
  4. (4) Ca(OH)2 and MgCO3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Clark's method involves adding Ca(OH)2 to hard water containing Mg(HCO3)2 and Ca(HCO3)2. The reaction is: Mg(HCO3)2 + 2Ca(OH)2 → 2CaCO3↓ + Mg(OH)2↓ + 2H2O. This produces a precipitate of CaCO3 and Mg(OH)2, which can be filtered out to soften the water.
