A body of mass 14 kg initially at rest explodes and breaks into three fragments of masses in the ratio 2:2:3. The two pieces of equal masses fly off perpendicular to each other with a speed of 18 m/s each. The velocity of the heavier fragment is ____ m/s.

  1. (1) 10sqrt(2)
  2. (2) 12sqrt(2)
  3. (3) 12
  4. (4) 24sqrt(2)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given: Total mass M = 14 kg, mass ratio = 2:2:3 Masses: M1 = 2k, M2 = 2k, M3 = 3k where 2k + 2k + 3k = 14 kg 7k = 14, so k = 2 Therefore: M1 = 4 kg, M2 = 4 kg, M3 = 6 kg The two equal mass fragments (1 and 2) fly off perpendicular to each other with speed v1 = v2 = 18 m/s Setup coordinate system: Fragment 1 moves along negative x-axis: vector V1 = -18i m/s Fragment 2 moves along negative y-axis: vector V2 = -18j m/s Fragment 3 (heavier) moves with velocity: vector V3 = V3x*i + V3y*j Apply Conservation of Momentum (initially at rest, so initial momentum = 0): M1*vector V1 + M2*vector V2 + M3*vector V3 = 0 4(-18i) + 4(-18j) + 6*vector V3 = 0 -72i - 72j + 6*vector V3 = 0 6*vector V3 = 72i + 72j vector V3 = 12i + 12j Magnitude of vector V3: |vector V3| = sqrt((12)^2 + (12)^2) = sqrt(144 + 144) = sqrt(288) = sqrt(144*2) = 12sqrt(2) m/s
