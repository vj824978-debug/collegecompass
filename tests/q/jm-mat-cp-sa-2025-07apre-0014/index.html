If the sum of the second, fourth and sixth terms of a G.P. of positive terms is 21 and the sum of its eighth, tenth and twelfth terms is 15309, then the sum of its first nine terms is:

  1. (1) 755
  2. (2) 750
  3. (3) 760
  4. (4) 757

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let the G.P. have first term a and common ratio r. From the conditions: ar + ar^3 + ar^5 = 21 and ar^7 + ar^9 + ar^11 = 15309. Dividing equation (ii) by equation (i): r^6 = 729, so r = 3. Substituting r = 3 into equation (i): a(3 + 27 + 243) = 21, so a(273) = 21, thus a = 1/13. The sum of first nine terms: S9 = a(r^9 - 1)/(r - 1) = (1/13)(3^9 - 1)/(3 - 1) = (1/13)(19683 - 1)/2 = (1/26)(19682) = 757
