An organic compound A (C6H6O) gives dark green colouration with ferric chloride. On treatment with HCl and KOH, followed by acidification gives compound B. Compound B can also be obtained from compound C on reaction with pyridinium chlorochromate (PCC). Identify A, B and C.

  1. (1) A = phenol with OH group, B = phenol with OH and CHO groups, C = phenol with CH2OH group
  2. (2) A = phenol with CH2OH and OH groups, B = phenol with OH and CHO groups, C = phenol with OH groups
  3. (3) A = phenol with CHO group, B = phenol with OH and CHO groups, C = phenol with OH groups
  4. (4) A = phenol with OH group, B = phenol with OH and CHO groups, C = phenol with CH2OH group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Compound A is phenol since phenol gives dark green colour with FeCl3. A has molecular formula C6H6O which is phenol (C6H5OH). On treatment with HCl and KOH (Reimer-Tiemann reaction), A is converted to B which has both OH and CHO groups (ortho to OH). Compound C is obtained from B by PCC oxidation of CH2OH to CHO group. Working backwards: B has OH and CHO groups. C must have CH2OH group instead of CHO. When C (with CH2OH) is oxidized by PCC, the primary alcohol CH2OH is oxidized to aldehyde CHO, giving compound B. Therefore: A = phenol (single OH), B = salicylaldehyde derivative (OH and CHO), C = phenol with CH2OH group.
