The density of a solid metal sphere is determined by measuring its mass and its diameter. The maximum error in the density of the sphere is (x/100) %. If the relative errors in measuring the mass and the diameter are 6.0% and 1.5% respectively, the value of x is _________.

Answer

Correct answer: 1050.00

Solution

ρ = M/V = M/((4/3)π(D/2)^3) ρ = (6/π) M D^(-3) Taking log: ln(ρ) = ln(6/π) + ln(M) - 3 ln(D) Differentiates: dρ/ρ = 0 + dM/M - 3 d(D)/D For maximum error: 100 × dρ/ρ = (dM/M) × 100 + 3(dD/D) × 100 = 6 + 3 × 1.5 = 6 + 4.5 = 10.5% = 1050/100 % so x = 1050.00
