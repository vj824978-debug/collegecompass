The figure represents a voltage regulator circuit using a Zener diode. The breakdown voltage of the Zener diode is 6 V and the load resistance is, R_L = 4kΩ. The series resistance of the circuit is R_i = 1 kΩ. If the battery voltage V_B varies from 8 V to 16 V, what are the minimum and maximum values of the current through Zener diode?

  1. (1) 0.5 mA; 8.5 mA
  2. (2) 1.5 mA; 8.5 mA
  3. (3) 1 mA; 8.5 mA
  4. (4) 0.5 mA; 6 mA

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: Zener breakdown voltage V_Z = 6 V Load resistance R_L = 4 kΩ = 4000 Ω Series resistance R_i = 1 kΩ = 1000 Ω Battery voltage V_B varies from 8 V to 16 V In a Zener regulator circuit, the output voltage across the load is maintained at V_Z = 6 V. Current through load: I_L = V_Z / R_L = 6 / 4000 = 1.5 mA Total current from battery: I_total = (V_B - V_Z) / R_i Current through Zener diode: I_Z = I_total - I_L When Battery = 8 V: I_total = (8 - 6) / 1000 = 2 / 1000 = 2 mA I_Z = 2 - 1.5 = 0.5 mA (minimum) When Battery = 16 V: I_total = (16 - 6) / 1000 = 10 / 1000 = 10 mA I_Z = 10 - 1.5 = 8.5 mA (maximum) Therefore, minimum and maximum values of Zener current are 0.5 mA and 8.5 mA respectively.
