Consider a badminton racket with length scales as shown in the figure. If the mass of the linear and circular portions of the badminton racket are same (M) and the mass of the threads are negligible, the moment of inertia of the racket about an axis perpendicular to the handle and in the plane of the ring at r/2 distance from the end A of the handle will be _____ Mr^2.

Answer

Correct answer: 52

Solution

The moment of inertia consists of three parts: 1) Linear portion (handle) about the axis: I_handle = M(5r/2)^2 = M·25r^2/4, 2) Circular portion (disc) about its center: I_disc = Mr^2/2, 3) Circular portion about the parallel axis at distance 13r/2: I_disc_parallel = M·r^2/2 + M(13r/2)^2 = Mr^2/2 + 169Mr^2/4. Total: I = M·25r^2/4 + M·36r^2/12 + Mr^2/4 + Mr^2/2 + 169Mr^2/4 = M(25r^2/4 + 3r^2 + Mr^2/4 + Mr^2/2 + 169Mr^2/4) = 52Mr^2
