A plano convex lens of refractive index μ₁ and focal length f₁ is kept in contact with another plano concave lens of refractive index μ₂ and focal length f₂. If the radius of curvature of their spherical faces is R each and f₁ = 2f₂, then μ₁ and μ₂ are related as -

  1. (1) 3μ₂ - 2μ₁ = 1
  2. (2) μ₁ + μ₂ = 3
  3. (3) 2μ₁ - μ₂ = 1
  4. (4) 2μ₂ - μ₁ = 1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: f₁ = 2f₂ For plano convex lens: 1/f₁ = (μ₁ - 1)(1/R) For plano concave lens: 1/f₂ = (μ₂ - 1)(-1/R) From these: f₂ = R/(μ₂ - 1) f₁ = R/(μ₁ - 1) Using f₁ = 2f₂: R/(μ₁ - 1) = 2R/(μ₂ - 1) Simplifying: 1/(μ₁ - 1) = 2/(μ₂ - 1) μ₂ - 1 = 2(μ₁ - 1) μ₂ - 1 = 2μ₁ - 2 μ₂ = 2μ₁ - 1 Rearranging: 2μ₁ - μ₂ = 1
