As shown in the figure, an insulated container is fitted with a thermally conducting but immovable partition (P₁) and a freely movable but thermally insulated piston (P₂). The partition P₁ with thermal conductivity K, cross sectional area A and width x divides the container into two sections, S₁ and S₂, each containing one mole of a monatomic gas. The piston P₂ moves freely such that the gas in S₂ is always at the atmospheric pressure. Initially, the difference between the temperatures of S₁ and S₂ is ΔT₀. The time it takes for the temperature difference to become ΔT₀/2 is nxR/KA, where R is the universal gas constant. The value of n is: [Given: ln 2 ≈ 0.7]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 0.66

Solution

Solution: Let T₁ and T₂ be the temperatures of the gases in sections S₁ and S₂ respectively. Assume T₁ > T₂, so heat flows from S₁ to S₂. The rate of heat transfer through the partition P₁ is given by Fourier's law of heat conduction: dQ/dt = KA/x(T₁ - T₂) For section S₁, the volume is constant (since P₁ is immovable). Thus, the process is isochoric. The heat lost by S₁ is: dQ = -n₁Cᵥ dT₁ Since the gas is monatomic and n₁ = 1 mole, Cᵥ = 3/2 R. ⟹ dT₁ = -2dQ/3R For section S₂, the pressure is constant (since P₂ is freely movable and exposed to atmospheric pressure). Thus, the process is isobaric. The heat gained by S₂ is: dQ = n₂Cₚ dT₂ Since the gas is monatomic and n₂ = 1 mole, Cₚ = 5/2 R. ⟹ dT₂ = 2dQ/5R The change in the temperature difference d(ΔT) = d(T₁ - T₂) is: d(T₁ - T₂) = dT₁ - dT₂ = -2dQ/3R - 2dQ/5R = -16dQ/15R Substituting the rate of heat transfer dQ/dt into the equation: d(T₁ - T₂)/dt = -16/15R · (KA/x(T₁ - T₂)) Let ΔT = T₁ - T₂. The differential equation becomes: d(ΔT)/ΔT = -16KA/(15Rx) dt Integrating both sides from t = 0 to t, where the temperature difference goes from ΔT₀ to ΔT₀/2: ∫[ΔT₀ to ΔT₀/2] d(ΔT)/ΔT = -16KA/(15Rx) ∫₀ᵗ dt ln(1/2) = -16KA/(15Rx) t -ln 2 = -16KA/(15Rx) t t = 15Rx ln 2/(16KA) Given that ln 2 ≈ 0.7: t = (15 × 0.7 × R)/(16 × KA) = (10.5 × R)/(16 × KA) = (21/32) × xR/KA = 0.65625 × xR/KA Comparing this with the given expression t = n × xR/KA, we get: n = 0.65625 Answer: 0.66
