On heating, lead (II) nitrate gives a brown gas (A). The gas (A) on cooling changes to a colorless solid/liquid (B). (B) on heating with NO changes to a blue solid (C). The oxidation number of nitrogen in solid (C) is-

  1. (1) +2
  2. (2) +3
  3. (3) +4
  4. (4) +5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Pb(NO3)2 --[heat]--> PbO + 2NO2 + (1/2)O2(g) Brown gas (A) is NO2 NO2 --[Cooling]--> N2O4 Colorless solid/liquid (B) is N2O4 N2O4 + NO --[heat]--> N2O3 Blue solid (C) is N2O3 O.S. of nitrogen in N2O3 is +3 N2O3: 2x + 3(-2) = 0 2x - 6 = 0 x = +3
