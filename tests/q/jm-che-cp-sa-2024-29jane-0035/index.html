Given below are two statements: Statement I: Fluorine has most negative electron gain enthalpy in its group. Statement II: Oxygen has least negative electron gain enthalpy in its group. In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate from the options given below.

  1. (1) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  4. (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Electron gain enthalpy (ΔegH) trends: For group 17 (halogens): Cl > F > Br > I. Fluorine does NOT have the most negative electron gain enthalpy in its group; Chlorine does (Statement I is false). For group 16 (chalcogens): S > Se > Te > Po > O. Oxygen has the least negative (or least favorable) electron gain enthalpy in its group (Statement II is true). Therefore, Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
