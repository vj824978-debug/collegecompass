A group 15 element, which is a metal and forms a hydride with strongest reducing power among group 15 hydrides. The element is:

  1. (1) Bi
  2. (2) Sb
  3. (3) As
  4. (4) P

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Bismuth (Bi) is a group 15 element. Its hydride is BiH3, which is the strongest reducing agent among group 15 hydrides. The reducing power of hydrides increases down the group due to decreasing bond strength of the E-H bond (where E is the group 15 element). As we go down from N to Bi, the hydride becomes less stable and more reducing in character. BiH3 is extremely unstable and has the strongest reducing power among NH3, PH3, AsH3, SbH3, and BiH3.
