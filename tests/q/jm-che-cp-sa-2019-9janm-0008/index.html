The entropy change associated with the conversion of 1 kg of ice at 273 K to water vapours at 383 K is : (Specific heat of water liquid and water vapour are 4.2 kJ K^(-1) kg^(-1) and 2.0 kJ K^(-1) kg^(-1) heat of liquid fusion and vapourisation of water are 334 kJ kg^(-1) and 2491 kJ kg^(-1), respectively). (log 273 = 2.436, log 373 = 2.572, log 383 = 2.583)

  1. (1) 2.64 kJ kg^(-1) K^(-1)
  2. (2) 9.26 kJ kg^(-1) K^(-1)
  3. (3) 7.90 kJ kg^(-1) K^(-1)
  4. (4) 8.49 kJ kg^(-1) K^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

H2O(s) → H2O(l) ΔS1 = ΔH_fusion / T = 334 / 273 = 1.22 273K 273K H2O(l) → H2O(l) ΔS2 = 4.2 × ln(373/273) = 1.31 273K 373K H2O(l) → H2O(g) ΔS3 = ΔH_vap / T = 2491 / 373 = 6.67 373K 373K H2O(g) → H2O(g) ΔS4 = 2.0 ln(383/373) = 0.0529 373K 383K ΔS_total = 9.26 kJ kg^(-1) K^(-1)
