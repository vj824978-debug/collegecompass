In the above reaction, 3.9 g of benzene on nitration gives 4.92 g of nitrobenzene. The percentage yield of nitrobenzene in the above reaction is ___%. (Round off to the nearest integer). (Given atomic mass: C:12.0u, H:1.0u, O:16.0u, N:14.0u)

Answer

Correct answer: 80

Solution

Molar mass of benzene (C6H6) = 78 g/mol. Molar mass of nitrobenzene (C6H5NO2) = 123 g/mol. Moles of benzene = 3.9/78 mol. From stoichiometry, 3.9/78 mol of benzene produces 3.9/78 mol of nitrobenzene in 100% conversion. Mass of nitrobenzene in 100% conversion = (3.9/78) × 123 g. Actual mass of nitrobenzene produced = 4.92 g. Percentage yield = (4.92/((3.9/78) × 123)) × 100 = (4.92 × 100 × 78)/(123 × 3.9) = (4.92 × 78 × 100)/(123 × 3.9) = 80.0% ≈ 80%
