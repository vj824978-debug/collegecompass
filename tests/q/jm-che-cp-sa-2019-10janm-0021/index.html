The correct structure of product 'P' in the following reactions is: Asn - Ser + (CH3CO)2O → P (excess) with NaEt3 as catalyst

  1. (1) Structure with acetyl group on nitrogen of asparagine side chain, OCOCH3 on serine side chain hydroxyl, and carboxylic acid groups
  2. (2) Structure with acetyl group on nitrogen of asparagine side chain, no acetyl on serine side chain, and carboxylic acid and primary amine groups
  3. (3) Structure with acetyl groups on both asparagine nitrogen and serine hydroxyl, and carboxylic acid groups with one additional acetyl
  4. (4) Structure with carbamate protecting group (NHCOCH2O) on asparagine nitrogen, OCOCH3 on serine side chain, and carboxylic acid

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The reaction involves acetylation of the dipeptide Asn-Ser using acetic anhydride (CH3CO)2O with Et3N (triethylamine) as base catalyst. Starting material: Asn-Ser dipeptide - Asparagine has: primary amine at N-terminus, carboxylic acid at C-terminus, and amide side chain (-CH2-CO-NH2) - Serine has: hydroxyl group on side chain (-CH2-OH) Acetylation mechanism: 1. The excess acetic anhydride will acetylate all nucleophilic positions (NH2 and OH groups) 2. The N-terminal primary amine (NH2) gets acetylated: NH2 → NH-CO-CH3 3. The asparagine side chain primary amide (NH2) gets acetylated: -CO-NH2 → -CO-NH-CO-CH3 4. The serine hydroxyl group gets acetylated: -OH → -O-CO-CH3 (giving -OCOCH3) 5. The C-terminal carboxylic acid remains as -COOH (carboxylic acids are not acetylated under these mild conditions) The product structure contains: - Acetyl group on the N-terminus - Acetylated asparagine side chain amide - Acetylated serine hydroxyl (OCOCH3) - Free carboxylic acid at C-terminus This matches option (2), which shows the N-acetyl group, the acetylated serine side chain with OCOCH3, the amide side chain on asparagine without additional acetylation on the backbone amide, and the free carboxylic acid terminus.
