In a semiconductor, the number density of intrinsic charge carriers at 27°C is 1.5 × 10^8/m^3. If the semiconductor is doped with impurity atom, the hole density increases to 4.5 × 10^22/m^3. The electron density in the doped semiconductor is _______ × 10^4/m^3.

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

Using the relation N_e^2 = N_h × N_e for intrinsic semiconductors. Initially, at intrinsic state: (1.5 × 10^8)^2 = (4.5 × 10^22) × N_e. Solving: N_e = (1.5 × 1.5 × 10^16)/(4.5 × 10^22) = (2.25 × 10^16)/(4.5 × 10^22) = 0.5 × 10^(-6) = 5 × 10^4/m^3
