Given below are two statements: Statement I: The number of species among BF4-, SiF4, XeF4 and SF4, that have unequal E-F bond lengths is two. Here, E is the central atom. Statement II: Among O2, O2+, F2 and O2+, O2- has the highest bond order. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

  1. (1) Both Statement I and Statement II are false
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  3. (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  4. (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

In BF4-, SiF4 and XeF4, all bond lengths are identical. Molecules B.O. O2+ 2.5 O2 2 O2- 1.5 F2 1 BF4-: All B-F bonds are equivalent (tetrahedral geometry with 4 identical bonds) SiF4: All Si-F bonds are equivalent (tetrahedral geometry with 4 identical bonds) XeF4: All Xe-F bonds are equivalent (square planar geometry with 4 identical bonds) SF4: Has unequal S-F bond lengths (see-saw geometry) So only SF4 has unequal E-F bond lengths. This means only ONE species has unequal E-F bond lengths, not two. Therefore, Statement I is FALSE. For Statement II: Looking at bond orders: - O2 has B.O. = 2 - O2+ has B.O. = 2.5 - O2- has B.O. = 1.5 - F2 has B.O. = 1 The highest bond order is 2.5 (O2+), not O2- which has B.O. = 1.5. Therefore, Statement II is FALSE. Both statements are false.
