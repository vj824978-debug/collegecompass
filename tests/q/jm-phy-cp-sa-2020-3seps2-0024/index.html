When an object is kept at a distance of 30 cm from a concave mirror, the image is formed at a distance of 10 cm from the mirror. If the object is moved with a speed of 9 cms^(-1), the speed (in cms^(-1)) with which image moves at that instant is .....................

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

|dv/dt| = |v^2/u^2| |du/dt| = (10/30)^2 × 9 = 1 m/s
