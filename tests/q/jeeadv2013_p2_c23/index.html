The major product(s) of the following reaction is (are) [Structural diagram showing: A benzene ring with OH group at top and SO3H group at bottom, with arrow labeled "aqueous Br2 (3.0 equivalents)" pointing to "?"] Followed by four product options P, Q, R, and S: P: Benzene ring with OH at top, SO3H at bottom, and three Br atoms at ortho and para positions (2 ortho, 1 para to OH) Q: Benzene ring with OH at top, and four Br atoms at ortho, meta, and para positions (2 ortho, 1 meta, 1 para to OH) R: Benzene ring with OH at top, and four Br atoms at positions 2,4,5,6 (all except position 1 with OH and position 3) S: Benzene ring with OH at top, SO3H at bottom, and three Br atoms at ortho and para positions

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) (A) P
  2. (B) (B) Q
  3. (C) (C) R
  4. (D) (D) S

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

[Reaction scheme showing bromination of 4-hydroxybenzene sulfonic acid] Starting material: 4-hydroxybenzene sulfonic acid (para-substituted benzene with OH and SO₃H groups) Reaction conditions: Br₂ (3 equivalents) Product Q: 2,4,6-tribromobenzene-1,4-diol (benzene ring with OH at position 1, Br atoms at positions 2, 4, and 6, and SO₃H group at position 4) Mechanism: The hydroxyl group (-OH) is an ortho/para directing group and activating group. The sulfonic acid group (-SO₃H) is a meta directing group but deactivating. In this case, the OH group dominates due to its stronger activation effect. Three equivalents of Br₂ lead to bromination at all available ortho and para positions relative to the OH group (positions 2, 4, and 6). The SO₃H group is replaced or involved in the substitution pattern shown in the product structure.
