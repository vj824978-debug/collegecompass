Among the following, the conformation that corresponds to the most stable conformation of meso-butane-2,3-diol is [Four Newman projection diagrams labeled A, B, C, and D showing different conformations of a butane molecule with OH groups and methyl groups in various positions]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [Newman projection showing: top - OH and Me groups; bottom - OH and Me groups in specific arrangement with H atoms]
  2. (B) [Newman projection showing: top - OH and OH groups; bottom - H and Me groups with Me in specific position]
  3. (C) [Newman projection showing: top - OH and OH groups; bottom - H and Me groups in specific arrangement]
  4. (D) [Newman projection showing: top - OH and OH groups; bottom - Me and H groups with Me in specific position]

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

In option (B), given configuration represents meso – butane – 2, 3-diol and due to intramolecular hydrogen bonding, the gauche form is more stable. Option (C) and (D) does not represent meso – isomer.
