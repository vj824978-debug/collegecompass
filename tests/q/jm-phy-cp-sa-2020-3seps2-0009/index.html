A metallic sphere cools from 50°C to 40°C in 300 s. If atmospheric temperature around is 20°C, then sphere's temperature after the next 5 minutes will be close to:

  1. (1) 33°C
  2. (2) 31°C
  3. (3) 35°C
  4. (4) 28°C

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

(50 - 40)/300 = beta((50 + 40)/2 - 20) (40 - T)/300 = beta((40 + T)/2 - 20) Therefore, T = 100/3 T = 33.33°C ≈ 33°C
