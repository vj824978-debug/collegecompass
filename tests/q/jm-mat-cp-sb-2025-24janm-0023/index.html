The number of 3-digit numbers, that are divisible by 2 and 3, but not divisible by 4 and 9, is

Answer

Correct answer: 125

Solution

No. of 3 digits = 999 - 99 = 900 No. of 3 digit numbers divisible by 2 & 3 i.e. by 6 900/6 = 150 No. of 3 digit numbers divisible by 4 & 9 i.e. by 36 900/36 = 25 ∴ No. of 3 digit numbers divisible by 2 & 3 but not by 4 & 9 150 - 25 = 125
