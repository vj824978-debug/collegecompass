Potassium chlorate is prepared by electrolysis of KCl in basic solution as shown by the following equation: 6 OH^- + Cl^- → ClO3^- + 3H2O + 6e^-. A current of xA has to be passed for 10 h to produce 10.0g of potassium chlorate. The value of x is _____ (Nearest Integer). (Molar mass of KClO3 = 122.6 g mol^-1, F = 96500 C)

Answer

Correct answer: 1

Solution

From the equation, 6 moles of electrons produce 1 mole of KClO3. Moles of KClO3 = 10/122.6 = 0.08456 mol. Since 1 mole of KClO3 is produced by 6 faraday (F), 0.08456 moles of KClO3 is produced by (6 × 0.08456) F = 0.50736 F. Using Q = I × t and Q = n × F, we have I × t = 6 × 0.08156 × F. I = (6 × 0.08156 × 96500)/(10 × 60 × 60) A = 1.311 A ≈ 1 A
