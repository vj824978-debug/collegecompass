Consider the bcc unit cells of the solids 1 and 2 with the position of atoms as shown below. The radius of atom B is twice that of atom A. The unit cell edge length is 50% more in solid 2 than in 1. What is the approximate packing efficiency in solid 2?

  1. (1) 75%
  2. (2) 90%
  3. (3) 45%
  4. (4) 65%

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given information: - r_B = 2r_A - a_2 = 1.5a_1 - Solid 1 contains atom A (at corners and body center of bcc) - Solid 2 contains atom A at corners and atom B at body center For Solid 1: 4r_A = sqrt(3)a_1 a_1 = (4r_A)/sqrt(3) For Solid 2: a_2 = 1.5a_1 = 1.5 × (4r_A)/sqrt(3) = (6r_A)/sqrt(3) = 2sqrt(3)r_A Packing efficiency PE_2 = [Volume of atoms in unit cell]/[Volume of unit cell] In Solid 2: - 8 corner atoms of type A contribute: 8 × (1/8) × (4/3)π(r_A)^3 = (4/3)πr_A^3 - 1 body-centered atom of type B contributes: 1 × (4/3)π(2r_A)^3 = (4/3)π(8r_A^3) = (32/3)πr_A^3 Total volume of atoms = (4/3)πr_A^3 + (32/3)πr_A^3 = (36/3)πr_A^3 = 12πr_A^3 Volume of unit cell = a_2^3 = (2sqrt(3)r_A)^3 = 8 × 3sqrt(3) × r_A^3 = 24sqrt(3)r_A^3 PE_2 = (12πr_A^3)/(24sqrt(3)r_A^3) = (12π)/(24sqrt(3)) = π/(2sqrt(3)) = (π × sqrt(3))/(2 × 3) = (π sqrt(3))/6 ≈ (3.14 × 1.73)/6 ≈ 0.90 = 90%
