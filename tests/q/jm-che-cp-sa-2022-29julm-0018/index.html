Consider the following reaction sequence: Starting material is 4-aminobenzonitrile (NH2 group at top, CN group at bottom). Step 1: (i) AlH (i-Bu)2 (ii) H2O gives intermediate 'A'. Step 2: A reacts with CH3CHO in dilute NaOH with heat to give major product B. What is the structure of product B?

  1. (1) OHC-benzene-N=CH-CH3
  2. (2) H2N-benzene-CH=CH-CHO
  3. (3) H2N-benzene-CH2-N=CH-CH3
  4. (4) H2N-benzene-C(=O)-N=CH-CHO

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The nitrile (CN) group is reduced by AlH(i-Bu)2 to give primary amine (CH2NH2) in intermediate A. The benzene ring already has NH2 group. When A reacts with CH3CHO in dilute NaOH with heat, a cross aldol condensation occurs between the aldehyde and the primary amine to form an imine, which then undergoes aldol condensation with the aromatic amine. The major product B is formed via cross aldol condensation between the two amine groups and the aldehyde, resulting in H2N-benzene-CH=CH-CHO structure shown in option 2.
