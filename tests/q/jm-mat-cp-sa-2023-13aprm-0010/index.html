Let PQ be a focal chord of the parabola y^2 = 36x of length 100, making an acute angle with the positive x-axis. Let the ordinate of P be positive and M be the point on the line segment PQ such that PM : MQ = 3 : 1. Then which of the following points does NOT lie on the line passing through M and perpendicular to the line PQ?

  1. (1) (-6, 45)
  2. (2) (6, 29)
  3. (3) (3, 33)
  4. (4) (-3, 43)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For parabola y^2 = 36x, we have 4a = 36, so a = 9. Using focal chord length formula: a(t + 1/t)^2 = 100, we get t + 1/t = ±10/3. Solving: t = 3 or t = 1/3. Since P has positive ordinate and PQ makes acute angle, t = 3. This gives P(81, 54) and Q(1, -6). Point M divides PQ in ratio 3:1, so M(21, 9). The line perpendicular to PQ through M has slope -80/60 = -4/3, giving equation 4x + 3y = 111. Checking option (4): 4(-3) + 3(43) = -12 + 129 = 117 ≠ 111, so (-3, 43) does NOT lie on the line.
