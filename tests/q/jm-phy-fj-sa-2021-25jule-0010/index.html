Consider a planet in some solar system which has a mass double the mass of earth and density equal to the average density of earth. If the weight of an object on earth is W, the weight of the same object on the planet will be:

  1. (1) 2 W
  2. (2) W
  3. (3) sqrt(2) W
  4. (4) 2^(1/3) W

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given: Mass of planet M_p = 2M_e, Density of planet ρ_p = ρ_e. Using g = (4/3)πRρG, we have g ∝ Rρ. Since ρ_p = ρ_e, we get g_p/g_e = R_p/R_e. From M = (4/3)πR^3ρ, we have 2M_e = (4/3)πR_p^3ρ_e and M_e = (4/3)πR_e^3ρ_e. Dividing: 2 = (R_p/R_e)^3, so R_p/R_e = 2^(1/3). Therefore g_p/g_e = 2^(1/3), and W' = 2^(1/3)W.
