In the given electrochemical cell, Ag(s)|AgCl(s)|FeCl2(aq), FeCl3(aq)|Pt(s) at 298 K, the cell potential (E_cell) will increase when:

  1. (1) Concentration of Fe^2+ is increased.
  2. (2) Concentration of Fe^3+ is decreased
  3. (3) Concentration of Fe^2+ is decreased
  4. (4) Concentration of Fe^3+ is increased

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Cl^-_aq + Ag_(s) + Fe^3+_aq → Fe^2+_aq + AgCl_(s) E_cell = E^0_cell - (0.059/1) log([Fe^2+]/([Cl^-][Fe^3+])) From the Nernst equation, E_cell increases when the logarithmic term becomes more negative (i.e., when the argument of the logarithm decreases). The argument [Fe^2+]/([Cl^-][Fe^3+]) decreases when: - [Fe^2+] decreases (option C is correct) - [Fe^3+] increases (option D is correct) - [Cl^-] increases (option E is correct) Therefore, E_cell increases for options C, D, and E.
