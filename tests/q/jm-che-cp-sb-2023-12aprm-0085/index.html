One mole of an ideal gas at 350 K is in a 2.0 L vessel of thermally conducting walls, which are in contact with the surroundings. It undergoes isothermal reversible expansion from 2.0 L to 3.0 L against a constant pressure of 4 atm. The change in entropy of the surroundings (ΔS) is _____ J K^(-1) (Nearest integer). Given: R = 8.314 J K^(-1) mol^(-1).

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

For isothermal process Q = -W. The work for isothermal reversible process is W = -nR ln(V2/V1). For the surroundings: ΔS_surroundings = -nR ln(V2/V1) = -1 × 8.314 × 2.303 × log(3/2) = -1 × 8.314 × 2.303 × log(1.5) = -3.37 JK^(-1) ≈ -3 JK^(-1). The negative sign indicates entropy decrease of surroundings due to heat absorption by the system during isothermal expansion.
