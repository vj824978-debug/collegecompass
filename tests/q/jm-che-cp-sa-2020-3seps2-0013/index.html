The incorrect statement is :

  1. (1) Manganate and permanganate ions are paramagnetic
  2. (2) Manganate ion is green in colour and permanganate ion is purple in colour
  3. (3) In manganate and permanganate ions, the π-bonding, takes place by overtap of p-orbitals of oxygen and d-orbitals of manganese
  4. (4) Manganate and permanganate ions are tetrahedral

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Option 1) Manganate => MnO4^(2-), Permanganate => MnO4^(-) For Manganate (MnO4^(2-)): Mn hybridisation => d^3s Mn configuration: [1|1|1|1|1|_|1|_|] 3d^5 4s^2 After excitation: Mn => [1|1|1|1|1|.|1|_|] with 4p π and π bonds with O atoms from 2p and 2p orbitals 2 × 2p_π - 3d_so For Permanganate (MnO4^(-)): Mn hybridisation => d^3s 2 × 2p_π - 3d_c 1 × 2p_π - 4p_π (2) MnO4^(2-) => Green MnO4^(-) => purple/violet (3) Manganate contains 1 unpaired electron hence it is paramagnetic. Permanganate contains no unpaired electrons hence it is diamagnetic. (4) Both have d^2 hybridisation hence both have tetrahedral geometry. Therefore, statement (1) is INCORRECT because permanganate ion is diamagnetic (no unpaired electrons), not paramagnetic.
