Inside a uniform spherical shell: (a) the gravitational field is zero (b) the gravitational potential is zero (c) the gravitational field is same everywhere (d) the gravitation potential is same everywhere (e) all the above. Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) (e) only
  2. (2) (a), (c) and (d) only
  3. (3) (b), (c) and (d) only
  4. (4) (a), (b) and (c) only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Inside a uniform spherical shell, by the shell theorem: (a) The gravitational field is zero everywhere inside - TRUE. (b) The gravitational potential is zero everywhere - FALSE, it is constant but not zero. (c) The gravitational field is the same everywhere - TRUE (it is zero everywhere). (d) The gravitational potential is the same everywhere - TRUE (it is constant). Therefore, statements (a), (c), and (d) are correct, making option (B) the answer. The analogy with electrostatics is provided: inside a uniform spherical shell, electric field is zero everywhere and electric potential is constant but not zero.
