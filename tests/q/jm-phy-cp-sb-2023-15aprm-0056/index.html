In the given figure the total charge stored in the combination of capacitors is 100 µC. The value of 'x' is _____________.

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

For capacitors in parallel, the total capacitance is the sum of individual capacitances: C_total = C1 + C2 + C3 = 2 + x + 3 = (5 + x) µF. Using Q = CV, where Q is total charge, V is voltage: 100 = (5 + x) × 10. Solving: 100 = 10(5 + x), so 10 = 5 + x, therefore x = 5 µF.
