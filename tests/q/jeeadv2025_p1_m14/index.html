Consider the following frequency distribution: [Table with Value row: 4, 5, 8, 9, 6, 12, 11 and Frequency row: 5, f₁, f₂, 2, 1, 1, 3] Suppose that the sum of the frequencies is 19 and the median of this frequency distribution is 6. For the given frequency distribution, let α denote the mean deviation about the mean, β denote the mean deviation about the median, and σ² denote the variance. Match each entry in List-I to the correct entries in List-II. List-I: (P) 7f₁ + 9f₂ is equal to (Q) 19α is equal to (R) 19β is equal to (S) 19σ² is equal to List-II: (1) 146 (2) 47 (3) 48 (4) 145 (5) 55

Matrix match · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) (P) → (5) (Q) → (3) (R) → (2) (S) → (4)
  2. (B) (P) → (5) (Q) → (2) (R) → (3) (S) → (1)
  3. (C) (P) → (5) (Q) → (3) (R) → (2) (S) → (1)
  4. (D) (P) → (3) (Q) → (2) (R) → (5) (S) → (4)

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

f₁ + f₂ = 7 median is the 10ᵗʰ term i.e. 6 for this, f₁ must be 4 ⟹ f₂ = 3 7f₁ + 9f₂ = 28 + 27 = 55 now, x̄ = Σfᵢxᵢ/Σfᵢ = 7 α = Σfᵢ(xᵢ - x̄)/Σfᵢ = 48/19 β = Σfᵢ(xᵢ - M)/Σfᵢ = 47/19 σ² = Σfᵢxᵢ²/Σfᵢ - (x̄)² = 146/19
