Two cylindrical rods A and B made of different materials, are joined in a straight line. The ratios of lengths, radii and thermal conductivities of these rods are: L_A/L_B = 1/2, r_A/r_B = 2 and K_A/K_B = 1/2. The free ends of rods A and B are maintained at 400 K, 200 K respectively. The temperature of rods interface is T, when equilibrium is established.

Answer

Correct answer: 360

Solution

At thermal equilibrium, heat flow rate through rod A equals heat flow rate through rod B. Using Fourier's law of heat conduction: (K_A * pi * r_A^2 * (400 - T))/L_A = (K_B * pi * r_B^2 * (T - 200))/L_B. Substituting the given ratios: K_A/K_B = 1/2, (r_A/r_B)^2 = 4, L_A/L_B = 1/2. This gives: (1/2) * 4 * (400 - T) * (L_B/L_A) = (T - 200). Simplifying: (1/2) * 4 * (400 - T) * 2 = (T - 200). Therefore: 4(400 - T) = (T - 200). 1600 - 4T = T - 200. 1800 = 5T. T = 360 K
