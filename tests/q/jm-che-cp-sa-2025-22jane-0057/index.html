Match the Compounds (List-I) with the appropriate Catalyst/Reagents (List-II) for their reduction into corresponding amines. (A) R-C(=O)-NH2 with (I) NaOH (aqueous), (B) Nitrobenzene with (II) H2/Ni, (C) R-C≡N with (III) LiAlH4, H2O, (D) A compound with structure showing a benzene ring fused to a six-membered ring with two carbonyl groups (at positions 1 and 4) and N-R at position 3 with (IV) Sn, HCl

  1. (1) (A)-(III), (B)-(II), (C)-(IV), (D)-(I)
  2. (2) (A)-(II), (B)-(IV), (C)-(III), (D)-(I)
  3. (3) (A)-(II), (B)-(I), (C)-(III), (D)-(IV)
  4. (4) (A)-(III), (B)-(IV), (C)-(II), (D)-(I)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

(A) R-C(=O)-NH2 (primary amide) is reduced by LiAlH4, H2O to give R-CH2-NH2. So (A)-(III) (B) Nitrobenzene (aromatic nitro compound) is reduced by Sn, HCl to give aniline (C6H5-NH2). So (B)-(IV) (C) R-C≡N (nitrile) is reduced by H2/Ni (or H2/Pt) to give R-CH2-NH2. So (C)-(II) (D) The phthalimide derivative (isoquinoline-1,3-dione with N-R substituent) undergoes reduction with aqueous NaOH to cleave the N-R bond and produce primary amine R-NH2 along with the disodium salt of phthalic acid. This is the Gabriel synthesis. So (D)-(I) Therefore, the correct matching is: (A)-(III), (B)-(IV), (C)-(II), (D)-(I)
