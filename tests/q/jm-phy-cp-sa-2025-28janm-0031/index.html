Consider a long thin conducting wire carrying a uniform current I. A particle having mass 'M' and charge 'q' is released at a distance 'a' from the wire with a speed v₀ along the direction of current in the wire. The particle gets attracted to the wire due to magnetic force. The particle turns round when it is at a distance x from the wire. The value of x is [μ₀ is vacuum permeability]

  1. (1) a[1 - (mv₀)/(2μ₀qI a)]
  2. (2) (2v₀)/2
  3. (3) a[1 - (mv₀)/(qμ₀I)]
  4. (4) ae^(-(4πmv₀)/(μ₀qI))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

A → B V = -v_x i + v_y j B = (μ₀I)/(2πr)(-k) F = q(V × B) = (μ₀qI)/(2πr)(-v_y j - v_x i) a_x = -(μ₀qI v_y)/(2πm r) a_y = -(μ₀qI v_x)/(2πm r) v_x(dv_x)/dr = -(μ₀qI v_y)/(2πm r) (v_y dv_y)/v_y = -(μ₀qI dr)/(2πm r) ∫[v₀ to v_x] (v_x dv_x)/(sqrt(v₀² - v_x²)) = -(μ₀qI)/(2πm) ∫[a to r] dr/r Let z² = v₀² - v_x² 2z dz = -2v_x dv_x z dz = -v_x dv_x (v_x dv_x)/(sqrt(v₀² - v_x²)) = -(z dz)/z = -dz then integral becomes -∫ dz = -(μ₀qI)/(2πm) ln(x_l/a) v₀ = (μ₀qI)/(2πm) ln(x₁/a) x₁ = a e^((2πmv₀)/(μ₀qI)) For B → C V = -v_x i - v_y j B = (μ₀I)/(2πr)(-k) F = q(V × B) = (μ₀qI)/(2πr)(-v_y j + v_x i) a_x = (μ₀qI v_y)/(2πm r) ∫[v₀ to 0] (v_x dv_x)/(sqrt(v₀² - v_x²)) = -(μ₀qI)/(2πm) ∫[x₁ to x] dr/r -(μ₀qI)/(2πm) ln(x/x₁) = ∫ dz = -v₀ v₀ = (μ₀qI)/(2πm) ln(x/x₁) x = x₁ e^((2πmv₀)/(μ₀qI)) From equation (1) and (2) X = a e^((4πmv₀)/(μ₀qI))
