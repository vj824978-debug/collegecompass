The rate of a reaction decreased by 3.555 times when the temperature was changed from 40°C to 30°C. The activation energy (in kJ mol^(-1)) of the reaction is ______. Take: R = 8.314 J mol^(-1) K^(-1) ln 3.555 = 1.268

Answer

Correct answer: 100.00

Solution

ln(K_T2/K_T1) = (E_a/R)[1/T1 - 1/T2] T1 = 303 K; T2 = 313 K K_T2/K_T1 = 3.555 ln(3.555) = (E_a/8.314)[1/303 - 1/313] 1.268 = (E_a/8.314)[1/303 - 1/313] Calculating 1/303 - 1/313: 1/303 - 1/313 = (313 - 303)/(303 × 313) = 10/94839 = 0.0001054 1.268 = (E_a/8.314) × 0.0001054 E_a = (1.268 × 8.314)/0.0001054 E_a = 10.542/0.0001054 E_a = 99980.715 J/mol E_a = 99.98 kJ/mol ≈ 100.00 kJ/mol
