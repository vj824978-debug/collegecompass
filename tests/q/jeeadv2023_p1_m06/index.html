Let X: {(x,y) ∈ Z×Z: x/8 + y/20 < 1 and y² < 5x}. Three distinct points P, Q and R are randomly chosen from X. Then the probability that P, Q and R form a triangle whose area is a positive integer, is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 71/220
  2. (B) 73/220
  3. (C) 79/220
  4. (D) 83/220

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol.
