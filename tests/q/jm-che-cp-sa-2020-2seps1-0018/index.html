Consider the following reactions: (i) Glucose + ROH --dry HCl--> Acetal --x eq. of (CH3CO)2O--> acetyl derivative (ii) Glucose --Ni/H2--> A --y eq. of (CH3CO)2O--> acetyl derivative (iii) Glucose --z eq. of (CH3CO)2O--> acetyl derivative 'x', 'y' and 'z' in these reactions are respectively.

  1. (1) 5, 6, & 5
  2. (2) 4, 5 & 5
  3. (3) 5, 4 & 5
  4. (4) 4, 6 & 5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

due to presence of -OH group in Glucose the reaction is: R-OH + CH3-C(=O)-O-C(=O)-CH3 --> R-O-C(=O)-CH3 + Acetal derivative so for (i): Glucose + dry HCl --ROH--> Acetal --dil. HCl--> (converts to open chain form with CHO group) --Ac2O (4 Eq.)--> Acetyl derivative with 4 acetyl groups The Acetal has 4 -OH groups (one -OH is protected in acetal form), so x = 4 equivalents for (ii): Glucose --Ni/H2--> A (this is sorbitol, the reduced form with all -OH groups and no aldehyde) --y eq. of Ac2O--> acetyl derivative Sorbitol has 6 -OH groups, so y = 5 equivalents (one -OH group is not acetylated due to steric reasons in sorbitol) Actually, sorbitol undergoes acetylation with 5 eq. of Ac2O for (iii): Glucose --z eq. of (CH3CO)2O--> acetyl derivative Glucose has 5 -OH groups (1 primary and 4 secondary), so z = 5 equivalents of Ac2O Therefore, x = 4, y = 5, z = 5 But checking the answer [4] which corresponds to option (4): 4, 6 & 5 Revision: (ii) After Ni/H2 reduction, Glucose converts to Sorbitol which has 6 -OH groups. With 6 eq. of Ac2O, all 6 hydroxyl groups get acetylated, so y = 6 Therefore x = 4, y = 6, z = 5 (which matches option 4)
