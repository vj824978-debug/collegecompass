Two vertical poles are 150 m apart and the height of one is three times that of other. If from the middle point of the line joining their feet, an observer finds the angles of elevation of their tops to be complementary, then the height of the shorter pole (in meters) is:

  1. (1) 25
  2. (2) 20*sqrt(3)
  3. (3) 30
  4. (4) 25*sqrt(3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let the height of shorter pole be h, then height of taller pole is 3h. The poles are 150 m apart. From the middle point (75 m from each pole), let the angles of elevation be theta and (90 - theta) respectively. Using tan(theta) = h/75 and tan(90 - theta) = 3h/75 = cot(theta). Since tan(theta) * cot(theta) = 1, we have (h/75) * (75/3h) = 1. This gives 3h^2/(75*75) = 1, so 3h^2 = 5625, h^2 = 1875, h = 25*sqrt(3) meters.
