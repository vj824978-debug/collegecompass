The figure shows a square loop L of side 5 cm which is connected to a network of resistances. The whole setup is moving towards right with a constant speed of 1 cm s^(-1). At some instant, a part of L is in a uniform magnetic field of 1 T, perpendicular to the plane of the loop. If the resistance of L is 1.7 Ω, the current in the loop at that instant will be close to:

  1. (1) 115 μA
  2. (2) 150 μA
  3. (3) 170 μA
  4. (4) 60 μA

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

V = 1 cm/s = 10^(-2) m/s R_loop = 1.7 Ω A = 5 cm = 5 × 10^(-2) m equivalent circuit: R_total = R_loop + R_whetstone R_eq = (4)(2)/(4+2) = 8/6 = 4/3 = 1.3 Ω R_total = 1.7 + 1.3 ⇒ R_total = 3Ω Induced emf = VBℓ ⇒ current = I = (VBℓ)/R_total = (10^(-2))(1)(5×10^(-2))/3 = (5/3)×10^(-4) = 1.67×10^(-4) A = 167×10^(-6) A ≈ 170 μA
