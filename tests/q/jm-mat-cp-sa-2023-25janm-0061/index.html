The mean and variance of the marks obtained by the students in a test are 10 and 4 respectively. Later, the marks of one of the students is increased from 8 to 12. If the new mean of the marks is 10.2, then their new variance is equal to:

  1. (1) 4.08
  2. (2) 3.92
  3. (3) 3.96
  4. (4) 4.04

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Given: x̄ = 10 & σ^2 = 4, No. of students = N (let) ∴ Σx_i/N = 10 & Σx_i^2/N - (10)^2 = 4 Now if one of x_i is changed from 8 to 12 we have New mean = (Σx_i + 4)/N = 10 + 4/N = 10.2 ⟹ N = 20 and σ^2_new = (Σx_i^2 - (8)^2 + (12)^2)/20 - (10.2)^2 = Σx_i^2/20 + (144 - 64)/20 - (10.2)^2 = Σx_i^2/20 + 80/20 - (10.2)^2 = 104 + 4 - (10.2)^2 = 108 - 104.04 = 3.96
