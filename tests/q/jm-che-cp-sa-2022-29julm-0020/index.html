A compound 'X' is acidic and it is soluble in NaOH solution, but insoluble in NaHCO3 solution. Compound 'X' also gives violet colour with neutral FeCl3 solution. The compound 'X' is:

  1. (1) Cyclohexanol (OH group on cyclohexane ring)
  2. (2) Phenol (OH group on benzene ring)
  3. (3) Benzyl alcohol (CH2OH group on benzene ring)
  4. (4) Cyclohexylmethanol (CH2OH group on cyclohexane ring)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Compound X must be phenol because: (1) It is acidic and soluble in NaOH solution - phenol is weakly acidic and dissolves in NaOH as sodium phenoxide. (2) It is insoluble in NaHCO3 solution - phenol is too weak an acid to react with NaHCO3, unlike carboxylic acids. (3) It gives violet colour with FeCl3 - this is the characteristic test for phenol, forming [Fe(C6H5O)6]^3- complex with violet colour. Other options: (1) Cyclohexanol is not acidic, (3) and (4) are alcohols with no acidic properties and don't give colour with FeCl3.
