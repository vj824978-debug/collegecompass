A ship is fitted with three engines E₁, E₂ and E₃. The engines function independently of each other with respective probabilities 1/2, 1/4 and 1/7. For the ship to be operational at least two of its engines must function. Let X denote the event that the ship is operational and let X₁, X₂ and X₃ denote respectively the events that the engines E₁, E₂ and E₃ are functioning. Which of the following is/are true?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) P[X₁ᶜ | X] = 3/16
  2. (B) P[Exactly two engines of the ship are functioning | X] = 7/8
  3. (C) P[X | X₂] = 5/16
  4. (D) P[X | X₁] = 7/16

Answer

Correct answer: BD

Solution

P(X₁) = 1/2, P(X₂) = 1/4, P(X₃) = 1/4 P(X) = P(X₁ ∩ X₂ ∩ X₃ᶜ) + P(X₁ ∩ X₂ᶜ ∩ X₃) + P(X₁ᶜ ∩ X₂ ∩ X₃) + P(X₁ ∩ X₂ ∩ X₃) = 1/4 (A) P(X₁ᶜ / X) = P(X ∩ X₁ᶜ)/P(X) = (32/1)/(1/4) = 1/8 (B) P [exactly two engines of the ship are functioning | X] = (7/32)/(1/4) = 7/8 (C) P(X/X₂) = (5/32)/(1/4) = 5/8 (D) P(X/X₁) = (7/32)/(1/2) = 7/16
