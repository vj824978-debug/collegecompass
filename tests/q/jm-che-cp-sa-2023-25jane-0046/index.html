'A' in the given reaction is

  1. (1) A five-membered ring with two oxygens and a ketone group, with a hydroxyl group and tert-butyl substituent
  2. (2) A five-membered ring with two oxygens, a hydroxyl group on one carbon and another carbon with OH and C(=O)O-tert-butyl
  3. (3) A five-membered ring with oxygen, a hydroxyl group, and a carboxylic acid group with tert-butyl substituents
  4. (4) A five-membered ring with oxygens, hydroxyl group, and a tert-butyl ester group C(=O)O-tert-butyl

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction involves a Prins-type cyclization between an enol ether (aldehyde with tert-butyl substitution) and a carboxylic acid under acidic conditions (H+). Step 1: The aldehyde is protonated to form O=C(+)H species. Step 2: The tert-butyl enol ether acts as a nucleophile, attacking the electrophilic carbon of the protonated aldehyde. Step 3: The hydroxyl group from the carboxylic acid and the carboxy group participate in forming a cyclic oxonium intermediate. Step 4: Water is eliminated (- H2O) to form a stable five-membered lactone ring. Step 5: The final product 'A' is a five-membered cyclic structure containing two oxygens (one in the ether linkage and one as a carbonyl in the lactone), a ketone group at one position, with hydroxyl and tert-butyl substituents. The major product is the lactone (option 1) formed through intramolecular cyclization and dehydration.
