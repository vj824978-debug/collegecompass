For the given reaction: CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂. If 90 g CaCO₃ is added to 300 mL of HCl which contains 38.55% HCl by mass and has density 1.13 g mL⁻¹, then which of the following option is correct? Given molar mass of H, Cl, Ca and O are 1, 35.5, 40 and 16 g mol⁻¹ respectively.

  1. (1) 64.97 g of HCl remains unreacted
  2. (2) 32.85 g of CaCO₃ remains unreacted
  3. (3) 97.30 g of HCl reacted
  4. (4) 60.32 g of HCl reamains unreacted

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Density of HCl solution (d) = 1.13 g/ml V = 300 ml Wt. of HCl solution = 339 g Wt. of HCl = 339 × (38.55/100) = 130.68 g (LR) CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂ 90 130.68 100 36.5 = 0.90 mole = 3.58 mole Moles of HCl remained = 1.78 mole. Mass of HCl remained = 64.97 g.
