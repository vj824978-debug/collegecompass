The energy required to ionize a hydrogen like ion in its ground state is 9 Rydbergs. What is the wavelength of the radiation emitted when the electron in this ion jumps from the second excited state to the ground state?

  1. (1) 11.4 nm
  2. (2) 24.2 nm
  3. (3) 8.6 nm
  4. (4) 35.8 nm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

E_n = 13.6 z^2/n^2 eV For ground state: n = 1 E_1 = -13.6 z^2 = -13.6 × 9 Z = 3 Energy of photon emitted = 13.6z^2(1/1^2 - 1/3^2) = 13.6 × 3^2 × 8/9 = 108.8 eV ∴ λ = 1240/108.8 nm = 11.39 nm ≈ 11.4 nm
