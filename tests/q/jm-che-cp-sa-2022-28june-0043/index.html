The correct IUPAC name of the following compound is: A structure showing a 7-carbon chain with O2N group at position 6, a methyl group at position 4, and two carbonyl groups (oxo) at positions 3 and 2, with an aldehyde group (CHO) at position 1.

  1. (1) 4-methyl-2-nitro-5-oxohept-3-enal
  2. (2) 4-methyl-5-oxo-2-nitrohept-3-enal
  3. (3) 4-methyl-6-nitro-3-oxohept-4-enal
  4. (4) 6-formyl-4-methyl-2-nitrohex-3-enal

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

To name this compound: 1) Identify the longest carbon chain containing the principal functional group (aldehyde CHO at C1). The chain has 7 carbons (hept-). 2) Number from the end with the aldehyde (position 1). 3) Identify substituents: nitro group (NO2) at C6, methyl group (CH3) at C4, and oxo group (C=O) at C3. 4) The principal functional group is the aldehyde (enal). 5) Arrange substituents alphabetically: methyl comes before nitro and oxo. 6) IUPAC name: 4-methyl-6-nitro-3-oxohept-4-enal. This matches option (3).
