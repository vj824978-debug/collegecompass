Using very little soap while washing clothes, does not serve the purpose of cleaning of clothes, because:

  1. (1) soap particles remain floating in water as ions.
  2. (2) the hydrophobic part of soap is not able to take away grease.
  3. (3) the micelles are not formed due to concentration of soap, below its CMC value.
  4. (4) colloidal structure of soap in water is completely distributed.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

When soap concentration is below the Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC), micelles cannot form. Micelles are necessary for effective cleaning as they trap dirt and grease particles. Without micelle formation, soap molecules remain as individual ions in water and cannot effectively remove grease from clothes. Therefore, very little soap (below CMC) does not serve the cleaning purpose.
