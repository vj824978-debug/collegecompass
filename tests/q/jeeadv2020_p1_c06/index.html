The Fischer projection of D-erythrose is shown below: [Fischer projection showing CHO at top, H-OH, H-OH, and CH2OH at bottom] D-Erythrose and its isomers are listed as P, Q, R and S in Column-I. Choose the correct relationship of P, Q, R and S with D-erythrose from Column-II. Column-I: P. [Fischer projection with OHC, HO H, H OH, OH] Q. [Fischer projection with OHC, HO H, H with OH and OH] R. [Fischer projection with OHC, H OH, H OH, OH] S. [Fischer projection with OHC, HO H, H with OH and H] Column-II: 1. Diastereomer 2. Identical 3. Enantiomer

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) P → 2, Q → 3, R → 2, S → 2
  2. (B) P → 3, Q → 1, R → 1, S → 2
  3. (C) P → 2, Q → 1, R → 1, S → 3
  4. (D) P → 2, Q → 3, R → 3, S → 1

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

Sol. C Given structure: (2R, 3R) P ⇒ (2R,3R), so, identical. Hence P → 2 Q ⇒ (2S,3R), so, Diastereomers. Hence Q → 1 R ⇒ (2R,3S), so, Diastereomers. Hence R → 1 S ⇒ (2S,3S), so, Enantiomers. Hence S → 3
