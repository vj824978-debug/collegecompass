Let [.] denote the greatest integer function and f(x) = lim(n→∞) (1/n²) * sum(k=1 to n) [k/3^x]. Then 12 * sum(j=1 to 12) f(j) is equal to ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

We need to find f(x) = lim(n→∞) (1/n²) * sum(k=1 to n) [k²/3^x] Using the property of greatest integer function: sum(k=1 to n) (k²/3^x - 1) < sum(k=1 to n) [k²/3^x] <= sum(k=1 to n) k²/3^x We know that sum(k=1 to n) k² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 So: n(n+1)(2n+1)/(6·3^x) - n < sum(k=1 to n) [k²/3^x] <= n(n+1)(2n+1)/(6·3^x) Dividing by n³: lim(n→∞) [n(n+1)(2n+1)/(6n³·3^x) - n/n³] < lim(n→∞) (1/n³) * sum(k=1 to n) [k²/3^x] <= lim(n→∞) n(n+1)(2n+1)/(6n³·3^x) Evaluating the limits: lim(n→∞) n(n+1)(2n+1)/(6n³·3^x) = lim(n→∞) (2n³ + 3n² + n)/(6n³·3^x) = 2/(6·3^x) = 1/(3·3^x) = 1/3^(x+1) Therefore: 1/3^(x+1) < lim(n→∞) (1/n³) * sum(k=1 to n) [k²/3^x] <= 1/3^(x+1) This gives us: f(x) = 1/3^(x+1) Now calculating 12 * sum(j=1 to 12) f(j): 12 * sum(j=1 to 12) f(j) = 12 * sum(j=1 to ∞) 1/3^(j+1) = 12 * [1/9 + 1/27 + 1/81 + ...] This is a geometric series with first term a = 1/9 and common ratio r = 1/3: Sum = (1/9)/(1 - 1/3) = (1/9)/(2/3) = 1/6 12 * sum(j=1 to ∞) 1/3^(j+1) = 12 * [(1/9)/(1 - 1/3)] = 12 * (1/9)/(2/3) = 12 * 1/6 = 2
