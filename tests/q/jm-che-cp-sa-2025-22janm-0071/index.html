Some CO2 gas was kept in a sealed container at a pressure of 1 atm and at 273 K. This entire amount of CO2 gas was later passed through an aqueous solution of Ca(OH)2. The excess unreacted Ca(OH)2 was later neutralized with 0.1 M of 40 mL HCl. If the volume of the sealed container of CO2 was x, then x is ____ cm³ (nearest integer). [Given: The entire amount of CO2(g) reacted with exactly half the initial amount of Ca(OH)2 present in the aqueous solution.]

Answer

Correct answer: 45

Solution

Let moles of CO2 = n moles of Ca(OH)2 total initially = 2n excess Ca(OH)2 = n gm equivalent of Ca(OH)2 = gm equivalent of HCl n × 2 = 0.1 × (40/1000) × 1 n = 2 × 10^(-3) Volume of CO2 = 2 × 10^(-3) × 22400 = 44.8 cm³ Rounding to nearest integer: x = 45 cm³
