Let a be a positive real number such that ∫₀ᵃ e^(-[x])dx = 10e - 9, where [x] is the greatest integer less than or equal to x. Then a is equal to:

  1. (1) 10 + log_e(2)
  2. (2) 10 - log_e(1 + e)
  3. (3) 10 + log_e(1 + e)
  4. (4) 10 + log_e(3)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Split the integral ∫₀ᵃ e^(-[x])dx into parts from 0 to [a] and [a] to a. For integer parts, [x] is constant. ∫₀¹ e⁰dx + ∫₁² e^(-1)dx + ... + ∫₍[a]-1₎^[a] e^(-([a]-1))dx + ∫₍[a]₎ᵃ e^(-[a])dx. This equals [a](e-1) + e^(-[a]) - ([a]+1). Setting this equal to 10e - 9 and solving: For [a] = 10 and e^(a) = 2, we get a = 10 + log_e(2).
