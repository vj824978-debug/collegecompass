A thin and uniform rod of mass M and length L is held vertical on a floor with large friction. The rod is released from rest so that it falls by rotating about its contact-point with the floor without slipping. Which of the following statement(s) is/are correct, when the rod makes an angle 60° with vertical ? [g is the acceleration due to gravity]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The radial acceleration of the rod's center of mass will be 3g/4
  2. (B) The angular speed of the rod will be sqrt(3g/2L)
  3. (C) The angular acceleration of the rod will be 2g/L
  4. (D) The normal reaction force from the floor on the rod will be Mg/16

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

To find ω, we apply COE: (1/2)(1/3 ML²)ω² = Mg(L/2)(1 - cos 60°) ω = √(3g/2L) a_CM, radial = ω²(L/2) = 3g/4 To find α, we take torque about A: Mg(L/2)sin 60° = (1/3)ML²α so, α = (3g/2L)sin 60° a_CM, tang = α(L/2) = (3g/4)sin 60° ∴ Mg - N = (a_CM,radial cos 60° + a_CM,tang sin 60°) = M((3g/8) + (9g/16)) ∴ N = Mg/16 ∴ Correct options are A, B, D [Diagram shows a rod AB of length L pivoted at A, inclined at 60° to the vertical, with angular velocity ω and angular acceleration α marked, and forces mg and N labeled]
