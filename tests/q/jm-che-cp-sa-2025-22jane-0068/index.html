Toluene (excess) is treated with (i) CrO2Cl2, CS2 (ii) H2O+ (iii) NaHSO3. After filtration, residue (A) is obtained. Residue (A) + HCl (dil.) gives compound (B). Structure of residue (A) and compound (B), formed respectively is:

  1. (1) [A] benzaldehyde with H and O, [B] benzaldehyde with COOH and OH
  2. (2) [A] benzaldehyde with CH(OCrOHCl2)2, [B] benzaldehyde with COOH and H
  3. (3) [A] benzaldehyde with CHO, [B] benzaldehyde with COONa and H
  4. (4) [A] benzaldehyde with H, OH, and SO3Na, [B] benzaldehyde with CHO

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Starting with toluene (CH3 attached to benzene ring): Step 1: Treatment with CrO2Cl2 in CS2 oxidizes the methyl group to aldehyde, forming benzaldehyde (CHO attached to benzene ring). Step 2: Treatment with H2O+ provides aqueous acidic conditions. Step 3: Treatment with NaHSO3 (sodium bisulfite) forms a bisulfite addition product with the aldehyde group. The bisulfite ion adds to the carbonyl carbon of the aldehyde: Benzaldehyde + NaHSO3 → Benzene-CH(OH)SO3Na This bisulfite adduct is the residue (A) that precipitates out and is filtered. Residue (A) structure: Benzene ring with -CH(OH)SO3Na substituent Step 4: When residue (A) is treated with dilute HCl, the bisulfite adduct is cleaved. The HCl protonates and removes the SO3Na group, regenerating the aldehyde: Benzene-CH(OH)SO3Na + HCl → Benzaldehyde (CHO) + HSO3- + Na+ + Cl- Compound (B) structure: Benzaldehyde (CHO attached to benzene ring) Therefore: Residue (A) = Benzaldehyde with H, OH, and SO3Na groups on the same carbon Compound (B) = Benzaldehyde (CHO)
