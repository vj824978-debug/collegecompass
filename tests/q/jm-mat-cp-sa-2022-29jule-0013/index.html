Let A(α, -2), B(α, 6) and C(α/4, -2) be vertices of a triangle ABC. If (5α/4, -2) is the circumcentre of triangle ABC, then which of the following is NOT correct about triangle ABC:

  1. (1) area is 24
  2. (2) perimeter is 25
  3. (3) circumradius is 5
  4. (4) inradius is 2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Given vertices A(α, -2), B(α, 6), C(α/4, -2) and circumcentre at (5α/4, -2). Since AC is perpendicular to AB, triangle ABC is right-angled at A. The circumcentre is the midpoint of BC (hypotenuse), so (5α/4, -2) = ((α + α/4)/2, (6 - 2)/2). This gives 5α/8 = 5 and α/4 = 2, so α = 8. Vertices are A(8, -2), B(8, 6), C(2, -2). AC = 6, AB = 8, BC = 10. Area = (1/2)(6)(8) = 24. Perimeter = 6 + 8 + 10 = 24. Circumradius = BC/2 = 5. Inradius = Area/s = 24/12 = 2. Statement (2) perimeter is 25 is NOT correct.
