R formed in the following sequence of reactions is: Starting material is 4-chlorobenzaldehyde (4-Cl-C6H4-CHO). Step 1: NaCN, HOAc gives P. Step 2: EtOH, H+ gives Q (marked as 'Q' in reaction). Step 3: (i) 2MeMgBr (ii) H3O+ gives R (major product).

  1. (1) (1) 4-chlorophenyl compound with C(OH)(CH3)2 and two additional CH3 groups on the same carbon (geminal diol derivative)
  2. (2) (2) 4-chlorophenyl compound with C(OH) attached to two CH3 groups (secondary alcohol with two methyls)
  3. (3) (3) 4-cyanophenyl compound with C(OH)(CH3) and multiple CH3 groups
  4. (4) (4) 4-cyanophenyl compound with C(OH) attached to CH3 and CH3 groups

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Starting material: 4-Cl-C6H4-CHO (4-chlorobenzaldehyde) Step 1 - NaCN/HOAc: Cyanohydrin formation The aldehyde undergoes nucleophilic addition with CN- to form cyanohydrin P: 4-Cl-C6H4-CHO + NaCN → 4-Cl-C6H4-CH(OH)CN (P) Step 2 - EtOH/H+: Esterification and nitrile reduction The compound is treated with EtOH and H+ which converts the cyanohydrin to ethyl ester Q: 4-Cl-C6H4-CH(OH)CN → 4-Cl-C6H4-CH(OEt)-COOC2H5 (approximately, this is intermediate Q) Alternatively, the nitrile is reduced to give Q as an ethyl ester carboxylic acid derivative: Q: 4-Cl-C6H4-CH(OEt)COOC2H5 Step 3 - (i) 2MeMgBr (ii) H3O+: Grignard addition The ester carbonyl undergoes nucleophilic addition by Grignard reagent MeMgBr. Two equivalents of MeMgBr add to the ester to form a tertiary alcohol: Each MeMgBr adds to the C=O, and upon aqueous workup (H3O+), the intermediate Grignard complex is hydrolyzed. With 2 equivalents of MeMgBr reacting with the ester group: Ester COOR' + 2 CH3MgBr → C(OH)(CH3)2 + R'OMgBr The resulting R (major product) has the structure: 4-Cl-C6H4-CH(OH)-C(OH)(CH3)2 This corresponds to option (1): The 4-chlorophenyl group attached to a carbon bearing OH, which is itself attached to a quaternary carbon with OH and two CH3 groups. The product is a tertiary alcohol with a geminal diol-like arrangement where the secondary OH (on the benzylic carbon) and the quaternary carbon bearing two methyls and OH are present.
