Two sources of sound S1 and S2 produce sound waves of same frequency 660 Hz. A listener is moving from source S1 towards S2 with a constant speed u m/s and he hears 10 beats/s. The velocity of sound is 330 m/s. Then, u equals:

  1. (1) 10.0 m/s
  2. (2) 5.5 m/s
  3. (3) 15.0 m/s
  4. (4) 2.5 m/s

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

As observer goes away from source S1 so apparent frequency v1 = ((v - v0)/v) * v where v = speed of sound, v0 = speed of observer v1 = ((330 - u)/330) * 660 v1 = 2 × 330 - 2u ... (1) As observer goes towards source S2 so apparent frequency v2 = ((v + v0)/v) * v v2 = ((330 + u)/330) * 660 v2 = 2 × 330 + 2u ... (2) According to question v2 - v1 = 10 4u = 10 u = 2.5 m/s
