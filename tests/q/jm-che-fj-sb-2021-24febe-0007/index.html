The magnitude of the change in oxidising power of the MnO4−/Mn2+ couple is x × 10^−4 V, if the H+ concentration is decreased from 1M to 10^−4 M at 25°C (Assume concentration of MnO4− and Mn2+ do not change on change in H+ concentration). The value of x is ___________. (Rounded off to the nearest integer) [Given: 2.303RT/F = 0.059]

Answer

Correct answer: 3776

Solution

For the MnO4−/Mn2+ couple: MnO4− + 8H+ + 5e− → Mn2+ + 4H2O. Using the Nernst equation: E1 = E° − (0.059/5)log([Mn2+]/[MnO4−][H+]^8). At [H+] = 1M: E1 = E° − (0.059/5)log([Mn2+]/[MnO4−]). At [H+] = 10^−4 M: E2 = E° − (0.059/5)log([Mn2+]/[MnO4−]) − (0.059/5)log(1/(10^−4)^8) = E° − (0.059/5)log([Mn2+]/[MnO4−]) − (0.059/5)log(10^32). The difference in E1 and E2 is: ΔE = (0.059/5) × 32 × log(10) = (0.059/5) × 32 × 1 = 0.3776 V = 3776 × 10^−4 V. Therefore, x = 3776, rounded to nearest integer is 3776.
