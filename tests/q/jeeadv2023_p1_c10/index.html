The plot of log kf versus 1/T for a reversible reaction A(g) ⇌ P(g) is shown [A linear graph with log kf on y-axis and 1/T (K^-1) on x-axis. The line passes through approximately (0.002, 9) with a negative slope extending to lower values] Pre-exponential factors for the forward and backward reactions are 10^15 s^-1 and 10^11 s^-1, respectively. If the value of log K for the reaction at 500 K is 6, the value of |log kc| at 250 K is ___. [K = equilibrium constant of the reaction kf = rate constant of forward reaction kb = rate constant of backward reaction]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

log k_f = 9 at 500 K, ∴ k_f = 10^9 log k_b = -(E_a)_b/(2.303R) × 1/T + log A_b K_eq = 10^6 = 10^9/k_b k_b = 10^3 3 = -(E_a)_b/(2.303R) × 0.02 + 11 (E_a)_b/(2.303R) = 8/0.002 = 4000 log k_b = -4000 × 1/250 + 11 = -16 + 11 = -5 |log k_b| = 5
