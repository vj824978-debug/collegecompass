80 mole percent of MgCl2 is dissociated in aqueous solution. The vapour pressure of 1.0 molal aqueous solution of MgCl2 at 38°C is _______ mm Hg. (Nearest integer). Given: Vapour pressure of water at 38°C is 50 mm Hg

Answer

Correct answer: 48

Solution

MgCl2 dissociates as: MgCl2 → Mg^(2+) + 2Cl^(-). Initial moles = 1, dissociated = 0.8. Final moles: MgCl2 = 0.2, Mg^(2+) = 0.8, Cl^(-) = 1.6. Total moles of solute = 2.6. Using Raoult's law: (p° - p)/p° = n_solute/(n_solute + n_solvent) = 2.6/(2.6 + 1000/18) = 2.6/57.6. Therefore: (p° - p)/p° = 2.6/1000/18 + 2.6. Solving: p = 50 × (1 - 2.6/(1000/18 + 2.6)) = 47.66 ≈ 48 mm Hg
