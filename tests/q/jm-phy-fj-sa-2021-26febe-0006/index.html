A tuning fork A of unknown frequency produces 5 beats/s with a fork of known frequency 340 Hz. When fork A is filed, the beat frequency decreases to 2 beats/s. What is the frequency of fork A?

  1. (1) 345 Hz
  2. (2) 338 Hz
  3. (3) 342 Hz
  4. (4) 335 Hz

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

When fork A produces 5 beats/s with 340 Hz fork, the frequency of A is either 340 + 5 = 345 Hz or 340 - 5 = 335 Hz. When fork A is filed (frequency increases), the beat frequency decreases to 2 beats/s. If initial frequency of A was 345 Hz, filing would increase it further, increasing beats. If initial frequency was 335 Hz, filing increases it toward 340 Hz, decreasing beats to 2 beats/s. So initial frequency of A is 335 Hz. After filing: |f_A - 340| = 2, which gives f_A = 342 Hz or 338 Hz. Since filing increases frequency from 335 Hz, the final frequency is 338 Hz (which is closer to the expected increase). Therefore, initial frequency of fork A is 335 Hz.
