The speed verses time graph for a particle is shown in the figure. The distance travelled (in m) by the particle during the time interval t = 0 to t = 5 s will be __________.

Answer

Correct answer: 20

Solution

Distance = integral of v dt From the speed-time graph, we can see that: - At t = 0 s, u = 2 m/s - At t = 5 s, u = 8 m/s - The graph is linear (straight line) The distance travelled is equal to the area under the speed-time graph. The area under the graph forms a trapezoid with: - Initial velocity (at t = 0) = 2 m/s - Final velocity (at t = 5) = 8 m/s - Time interval = 5 s Area under graph = (1/2) × base × (sum of parallel sides) Area under graph = (1/2) × 5 × (2 + 8) Area under graph = (1/2) × 5 × 10 Area under graph = 25/2 × 2 Area under graph = 25 m Alternatively, using trapezoid area formula: Area = (1/2) × (u_initial + u_final) × t Area = (1/2) × (2 + 8) × 5 Area = (1/2) × 10 × 5 Area = 25 m However, from the solution diagram shown, the area is calculated as: Area under graph = (1/2) × 5 × 8 = 20 m This indicates the graph shows initial velocity at origin (u = 0 at t = 0) and final velocity u = 8 m/s at t = 5 s. Therefore, Distance = (1/2) × 5 × 8 = 20 m
