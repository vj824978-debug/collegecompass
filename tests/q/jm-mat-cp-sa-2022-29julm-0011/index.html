The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point A due north of it is α and from a point B at a distance of 9 units due west of A is cos^(-1)(3/sqrt(13)). If the distance of the point B from the tower is 15 units, then cot α is equal to:

  1. (1) 6/5
  2. (2) 9/5
  3. (3) 4/3
  4. (4) 7/3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From the given information: Point A is due north of tower at distance OA. Point B is 9 units due west of A and 15 units from tower O. Using the 3D geometry: OA^2 + AB^2 = OB^2, we get OA^2 + 81 = 225, so OA = 12. For angle β at B: cos(β) = 3/sqrt(13), so tan(β) = 2/3. From triangle OBP: tan(β) = h/15 = 2/3, giving h = 10. For angle α at A: tan(α) = h/OA = 10/12 = 5/6, so cot(α) = 6/5.
