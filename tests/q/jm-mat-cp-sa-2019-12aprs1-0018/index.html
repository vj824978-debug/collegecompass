If the normal to the ellipse 3x^2 + 4y^2 = 12 at a point P on it is parallel to the line, 2x + y = 4 and the tangent to the ellipse at P passes through Q(4,4) then PQ is equal to:

  1. (1) sqrt(61)/2
  2. (2) sqrt(221)/2
  3. (3) sqrt(157)/2
  4. (4) 5*sqrt(5)/2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Ellipse: x^2/4 + y^2/9 = 1 Normal at P: (2cos(theta), sqrt(3)*sin(theta)) is 2x*sec(theta) - sqrt(3)*y*cosec(theta) = 4 = 3 = 1 Normal at P: 2x*sec(theta) - sqrt(3)*y*cosec(theta) = 1 Slope of normal = (-2*sec(theta))/(-sqrt(3)*cosec(theta)) = (2*sin(theta))/(sqrt(3)*cos(theta)) Normal parallel to 2x + y = 4 then (2/sqrt(3))*tan(theta) = -2; tan(theta) = -sqrt(3) theta = 2*pi/3 Point P(-1, 3/2), Q(4, 4) PQ = sqrt((4+1)^2 + (4 - 3/2)^2) = sqrt(125/4) = 5*sqrt(5)/2
