A quasi-static cycle of a monatomic ideal gas contains an isothermal process (ab), followed by an isochoric process (bc) and an adiabatic process (ca) as shown in the figure. The volumes of the gas are V₁ and V₂ at a and b, respectively. If the cycle has heat input Q_in and output Q_out, then the efficiency of the cycle is defined as η = (Q_in - Q_out)/Q_in. The correct statement(s) is/are: [Given: ln 2 ≈ 0.7]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) If V₂/V₁ = 8, the heat released in the process bc is smaller than the heat absorbed in the process ab.
  2. (B) For a given value of V₂/V₁, η does not depend on the temperature of the isothermal process.
  3. (C) If V₂/V₁ = 8, then the temperature of the gas at a is 4 times the temperature of the gas at c.
  4. (D) If V₂/V₁ = 8, then the pressure of the gas at a is 4 times the pressure of the gas at b.

Answer

Correct answer: A, B, C

Solution

Solution: For the monoatomic ideal gas, γ = 5/3. Process ab is isothermal, so Tₐ = Tᵦ and Vₐ = V₁, Vᵦ = V₂. Process bc is isochoric, so Vᵦ = Vc = V₂. Process ca is adiabatic, so TcVc^(γ-1) = TₐVₐ^(γ-1). TcV₂^(2/3) = TₐV₁^(2/3) ⟹ Tc = Tₐ(V₁/V₂)^(2/3). If V₂/V₁ = 8, Tc = Tₐ(1/8)^(2/3) = Tₐ/4 ⟹ Tₐ = 4Tc. Thus, statement (C) is correct. Heat absorbed in isothermal process ab is Qₐᵦ = nRTₐ ln(V₂/V₁). For V₂/V₁ = 8, Qₐᵦ = nRTₐ ln(8) = 3nRTₐ ln 2 ≈ 3 × 0.7nRTₐ = 2.1nRTₐ. Heat released in isochoric process bc is Qᵦc = nCᵥ(Tᵦ - Tc) = n(3/2 R)(Tₐ - Tₐ/4) = 9/8 nRTₐ = 1.125nRTₐ. Since 1.125nRTₐ < 2.1nRTₐ, the heat released in bc is smaller than the heat absorbed in ab. Thus, statement (A) is correct. --- The efficiency of the cycle is η = 1 - Qₒᵤₜ/Qᵢₙ = 1 - Qᵦc/Qₐᵦ. η = 1 - [n(3/2 R)Tc(1-(V₁/V₂)^(2/3))]/[nRTₐ ln(V₂/V₁)] = 1 - [3(1-(V₁/V₂)^(2/3))]/[2 ln(V₂/V₁)]. This expression depends only on the volume ratio V₂/V₁ and is independent of the temperature Tₐ. Thus, statement (B) is correct. For the isothermal process ab, PₐVₐ = PᵦVᵦ ⟹ PₐV₁ = PᵦV₂ ⟹ Pₐ = 8Pᵦ. Thus, statement (D) is incorrect. Answer: If V₂/V₁ = 8, the heat released in the process bc is smaller than the heat absorbed in the process ab.; For a given value of V₂/V₁, η does not depend on the temperature of the isothermal process.; If V₂/V₁ = 8, then the temperature of the gas at a is 4 times the temperature of the gas at c.
