Identify major product 'X' formed in the following reaction: Benzene + CO, HCl with Anhydrous AlCl3/CuCl

  1. (1) Benzoyl chloride (C6H5COCl)
  2. (2) Benzophenone (C6H5-CO-C6H5)
  3. (3) Benzaldehyde (C6H5CHO)
  4. (4) Benzyl chloride (C6H5CH2Cl)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

This is Gattermann-Koch reaction. Benzene reacts with CO and HCl in the presence of anhydrous AlCl3/CuCl catalyst to form benzaldehyde as the major product. However, looking at the answer key marked as [3], the major product is actually benzaldehyde (C6H5CHO), which corresponds to option (3). The mechanism involves formation of formyl cation [HCO]+ which undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution on benzene ring.
