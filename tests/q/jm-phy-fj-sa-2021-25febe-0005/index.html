For extrinsic semiconductors, when doping level is increased:

  1. (1) Fermi-level of p and n-type semiconductors will not be affected.
  2. (2) Fermi-level of p-type semiconductor will go upward and Fermi-level of n-type semiconductors will go downward.
  3. (3) Fermi-level of p-type semiconductors will go downward and Fermi-level of n-type semiconductor will go upward.
  4. (4) Fermi-level of both p-type and n-type semiconductors will go upward for T > T_F K and downward for T < T_F K, where T_F is Fermi temperature.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

When doping level is increased in extrinsic semiconductors: For p-type semiconductors, increased doping moves the Fermi level upward (closer to valence band). For n-type semiconductors, increased doping moves the Fermi level downward (closer to conduction band). This is because higher doping concentration shifts the Fermi level toward the majority carrier band.
