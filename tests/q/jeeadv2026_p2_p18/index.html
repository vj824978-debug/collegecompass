Passage: A uniform circular disk of radius 0.2 m and mass 1 kg is pivoted at its top point C such that it can rotate freely around C in the XY plane, as shown in the figure. Initially, when the disk is at rest, a particle of mass 20 g, travelling along negative x direction in the XY plane with speed 100 m s⁻¹, hits the circumference of the disk at a point P. After collision the particle moves along negative y direction at a speed of 90 m s⁻¹. [Given: the acceleration due to gravity (g) = −10ĵ m s⁻²] Question: Amount of energy loss (in J) in the collision is:

Comprehension · Marks: +3 / 0.0

Passage:

Answer

Correct answer: 17.47

Solution

Solution: Let the center of the uniform circular disk be the origin O(0,0). The disk is pivoted at its top point C(0, R). Radius disk R = 0.2 m, Mass M = 1 kg. Mass of particle m = 20 g = 0.02 kg. Initial velocity of particle v⃗ᵢ = −100î ms⁻¹. Final velocity of particle v⃗f = −90ĵ ms⁻¹. The particle hits the disk at point P. From the figure, the line OP makes an angle of 45° with the negative y-axis. Coordinates of P with respect to O are (R sin 45°, −R cos 45°). Position vector of P with respect to the pivot C is: r⃗_{P/C} = (R sin 45° − 0)î + (−R cos 45° − R)ĵ = R/√2 î − R(1 + 1/√2)ĵ Angular momentum is conserved about the pivot C during the collision. Initial angular momentum of the system about C is only due to the particle: L⃗ᵢ = r⃗_{P/C} × (mû⃗ᵢ) = [R/√2 î − R(1 + 1/√2)ĵ] × (−100mî) L⃗ᵢ = −100mR(1 + 1/√2)(ĵ × î) = −100mR(1 + 1/√2)k̂ Substituting m = 0.02 kg and R = 0.2 m: L⃗ᵢ = −100(0.02)(0.2)(1 + 1/√2)k̂ = −0.4(1 + 1/√2)k̂ = −(0.4 + 0.2√2)k̂ kg m²s⁻¹ Final angular momentum of the particle about C: L⃗_{f,p} = r⃗_{P/C} × (mû⃗f) = [R/√2 î − R(1 + 1/√2)ĵ] × (−90mĵ) L⃗_{f,p} = −90mR/√2(î × ĵ) = 90mR/√2 k̂ L⃗_{f,p} = 90(0.02)(0.2)/√2 k̂ = 0.36/√2 k̂ = −0.18√2 k̂ kg m²s⁻¹ Let ω be the angular velocity of the disk after collision. Its moment of inertia about C is: I_C = I_{cm} + MR² = 1/2 MR² + MR² = 3/2 MR² = 3/2(1)(0.2)² = 0.06 kg m² Final angular momentum of the disk: L⃗_{f,d} = I_C ω = 0.06ω k̂ By conservation of angular momentum about C: L⃗ᵢ = L⃗_{f,p} + L⃗_{f,d} −(0.4 + 0.2√2) = −0.18√2 + 0.06ω 0.06ω = −0.4 − 0.02√2 = ω = (40 + 2√2)/6 = (20 + √2)/3 rad s⁻¹ Initial kinetic energy of the system: Eᵢ = 1/2 mûᵢ² = 1/2(0.02)(100)² = 100 J Final kinetic energy of the system: Ef = 1/2 mûf² + 1/2 I_C ω² = 1/2(0.02)(90)² + 1/2(0.06)((20 + √2)/3)² Ef = 81 + 0.03((400 + 2 + 40√2)/9) = 81 + (402 + 40√2)/300 = 81 + (201 + 20√2)/150 Amount of energy loss: ΔE = Eᵢ − Ef = 100 − (81 + (201 + 20√2)/150) = 19 − (201 + 20√2)/150 ΔE = (2850 − 201 − 20√2)/150 = (2649 − 20√2)/150 Using √2 ≈ 1.414, ΔE ≈ (2649 − 28.28)/150 = 2620.72/150 ≈ 17.47 J
