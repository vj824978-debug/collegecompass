Two open organ pipes of length 60 cm and 90 cm resonate at 6th and 5th harmonics respectively. The difference of frequencies for the given modes is _____ Hz. (Velocity of sound in air = 333 m/s)

Answer

Correct answer: 740

Solution

For open organ pipe, f = nv/(2L). For first pipe (L1 = 0.6 m, n1 = 6): f1 = (6 × 333)/(2 × 0.6) = 1998/1.2 = 1665 Hz. For second pipe (L2 = 0.9 m, n2 = 5): f2 = (5 × 333)/(2 × 0.9) = 1665/1.8 = 925 Hz. Difference = f1 - f2 = 1665 - 925 = 740 Hz.
