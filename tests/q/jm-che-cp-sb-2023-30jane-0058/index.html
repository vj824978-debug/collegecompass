Number of compounds from the following which will not dissolve in cold NaHCO3 and NaOH solutions but will dissolve in hot NaOH solution is ________

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

To solve this problem, we need to identify which compounds will not dissolve in cold NaHCO3 and NaOH solutions but will dissolve in hot NaOH solution. Analysis of each compound: 1. 4-methylbenzoic acid (p-CH3-C6H4-COOH): Carboxylic acid - dissolves in cold NaOH and NaHCO3. NOT the answer. 2. Methyl benzoate (C6H5-COOCH3): Ester - does not dissolve in cold NaHCO3 or NaOH at room temperature. May partially dissolve in hot NaOH due to hydrolysis. Possible candidate. 3. Salicylaldehyde with carboxylic acid group (o-CHO-C6H4-COOH): Has COOH group - dissolves in cold NaHCO3 and NaOH. NOT the answer. 4. Compound with both OH and aldehyde: The aldehyde and phenolic OH allow some solubility. NOT the answer. 5. 3-hydroxy-3-methylphenol (m-OH-C6H4-CH(OH)CH3): Phenolic compound - phenol dissolves in cold NaOH but not in NaHCO3. This is a candidate. 6. Anisole derivative (aromatic ether-ester): Does not dissolve in cold NaHCO3 or NaOH. Dissolves in hot NaOH. Candidate. 7. Another anisole/ether-ester: Does not dissolve in cold solutions but dissolves in hot NaOH. Candidate. The compounds that do not dissolve in cold NaHCO3 and NaOH but dissolve in hot NaOH are: - Esters (undergo hydrolysis in hot NaOH) - Hindered phenols - Aromatic ethers with electron-withdrawing groups According to the solution, the three compounds are: 1. The methylbenzoate (ester) 2. A hindered phenolic compound 3. An aromatic ether/ester compound These three compounds satisfy the condition of not dissolving in cold solutions but dissolving in hot NaOH solution.
