Let A₁, B₁, C₁ be three points in the xy-plane. Suppose that the lines A₁C₁ and B₁C₁ are tangents to the curve y² = 8x at A₁ and B₁, respectively. If O = (0, 0) and C₁ = (-4, 0), then which of the following statements is(are) TRUE?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The length of the line segment OA₁ is 4√3
  2. (B) The length of the line segment A₁B₁ is 16
  3. (C) The orthocenter of the triangle A₁B₁C₁ is (0, 0)
  4. (D) The orthocenter of the triangle A₁B₁C₁ is (1, 0)

Answer

Correct answer: AC

Solution

Let OA₁ be y = mx + c ⇒ 0 = -4m + c or c = 4m Also, 2 = mc ∴ m = ± 1/√2, c = ± 2√2 1/t = m ⇒ A₁(4, 4√2) and B₁(4, -4√2) Orthocentre is (0, 0) A₁B₁ = 8√2 OA₁ = 4√3 [Right side shows coordinate plane with y-axis vertical and x-axis horizontal.
