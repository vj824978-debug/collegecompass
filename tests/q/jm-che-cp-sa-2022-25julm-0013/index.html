A compound 'A' on reaction with 'X' and 'Y' produces the same major product but different by product 'a' and 'b'. Oxidation of 'a' gives a substance produced by ants. Compound A is: H3C=C(CH3)-CH2-C(CH3)2-CH3. 'X' and 'Y' respectively are

  1. (1) KMnO4/H+ and dil. KMnO4, 273 K
  2. (2) KMnO4(dilute), 273 K and KMnO4/H+
  3. (3) KMnO4/H+ and O3, H2O/Zn
  4. (4) O3, H2O/Zn and KMnO4/H+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Compound A undergoes oxidation with X and Y to produce the same major product but different minor products a and b. Product a oxidizes to formic acid (produced by ants). X is O3, H2O/Zn (ozonolysis) which cleaves double bond producing aldehyde (a). Y is KMnO4/H+ which oxidizes the double bond to carboxylic acid (b). The aldehyde (a) from ozonolysis can be further oxidized to formic acid, matching the clue that a's oxidation gives ant venom compound.
