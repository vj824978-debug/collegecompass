AB₂ is 10% dissociated in water to A^+ and B^-. The boiling point of a 10.0 molal aqueous solution of AB₂ is ___°C. (Round off to the nearest Integer). [Given: Molar elevation constant of water K_b = 0.5 K kg mol^-1, boiling point of pure water = 100°C]

Answer

Correct answer: 106

Solution

Given: m = 10 molal, K_b = 0.5 K kg mol^-1. Using ΔT_b = i K_b m, where i = 1 + (n-1)α and α = (i-1)/(n-1). For AB₂, n = 3 (dissociates into 3 particles), α = 0.1. Therefore, 0.1 = (i-1)/(3-1), which gives i = 1.2. ΔT_b = 1.2 × 0.5 × 10 = 6°C. Boiling point of solution = 100 + 6 = 106°C
