If the sum and product of the first three terms in an A.P. are 33 and 1155, respectively, then a value of its 11th term is:

  1. (1) -25
  2. (2) -35
  3. (3) -36
  4. (4) 25

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Let the three numbers in A.P. be a - d, a, a + d given that: a - d + a + d = 33 ⟹ a = 11 and (a - d)(a)(a + d) = 1155 ⟹ a(a^2 - d^2) = 1155 ⟹ 11(121 - d^2) = 1155 ⟹ 121 - d^2 = 105 ⟹ d^2 = 16 ⟹ d = ±4 If d = 4 then first term a - d = 7 If d = -4 then first term a - d = 15 T_11 = 7 + 10(4) = 47 or T_11 = 15 + 10(-4) = -25
