An object of mass 'm' is being moved with a constant velocity under the action of an applied force of 2N along a frictionless surface with following surface profile. The correct applied force vs distance graph will be:

  1. (1) Graph (A) showing rectangular force profile with +2N for distance D, then -2N
  2. (2) Graph (B) showing +2N spike at start, then -2N for distance D
  3. (3) Graph (C) showing force that decreases linearly from +2N to -2N over distance D
  4. (4) Graph (D) showing triangular force profile with peak at +2N over distance D

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Since the object is moving with constant velocity, net force must be zero (Newton's first law). As the object moves up the incline, it needs +2N applied force to maintain constant velocity against gravity component. As it moves down the incline, it needs -2N applied force to prevent acceleration. The surface profile shows an upward slope for distance D, then a downward slope for distance D. During upward motion: F_applied = +2N. During downward motion: F_applied = -2N. This matches option (A) which shows a rectangular profile with +2N for the upward portion D, and -2N for the downward portion D.
