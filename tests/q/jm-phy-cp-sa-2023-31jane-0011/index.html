A stone of mass 1 kg is tied to end of a massless string of length 1m. If the breaking tension of the string is 400 N, then maximum linear velocity, the stone can have without breaking the string, while rotating in horizontal plane, is:

  1. (1) 40 ms^(-1)
  2. (2) 10 ms^(-1)
  3. (3) 20 ms^(-1)
  4. (4) 400 ms^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For a stone rotating in a horizontal plane, the tension in the string provides the centripetal force. From the diagram and force analysis: Tcos(theta) = mg Tsin(theta) = mv^2/(l*sin(theta)) From the first equation: cos(theta) = mg/T .... (1) From the second equation: sin(2*theta) = mv^2/(T*l^2) .... (2) From equations (1) and (2): l^2 = (mg/T)^2 + mv^2/(T*l^2) Substituting values: m = 1 kg g = 10 m/s^2 T = 400 N (maximum breaking tension) l = 1 m 1 = (1×10/400)^2 + v^2/400 1 = (10/400)^2 + v^2/400 1 = (1/40)^2 + v^2/400 1 = 1/1600 + v^2/400 v^2 = 400(1 - 1/1600) v^2 = 400(1599/1600) v^2 = 399.75 ≈ 400 v^2 = 399.78 v = 20 m/s
