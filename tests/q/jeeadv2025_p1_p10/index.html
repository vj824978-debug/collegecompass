Two identical plates P and Q, radiating as perfect black bodies, are kept in vacuum at constant absolute temperatures T_P and T_Q, respectively, with T_Q < T_P, as shown in Fig. 1. The radiated power transferred per unit area from P to Q is W_0. Subsequently, two more plates, identical to P and Q, are introduced between P and Q, as shown in Fig. 2. Assume that heat transfer takes place only between adjacent plates. If the power transferred per unit area in the direction from P to Q (Fig. 2) in the steady state is W_S, then the ratio W_0/W_S is ____ [Fig. 1 shows two parallel plates P and Q with temperatures T_P and T_Q, with arrow labeled W_0 pointing from P to Q] [Fig. 2 shows four parallel plates with P and Q at the ends and two intermediate plates, with arrow labeled W_S pointing from P to Q]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Stefan's Law: W₀ = σ(T_P⁴ - T_Q⁴) W₀/σ = (T_P⁴ - T_Q⁴) ...(A) Rate of flow of heat will be same. W_S/σ = (T_P⁴ - T₁⁴) ...(i) W_S/σ = (T₁⁴ - T₂⁴) ...(ii) W_S/σ = (T₂⁴ - T_Q⁴) ...(iii) Adding (i), (ii) and (iii) T_P⁴ - T_Q⁴ = 3W_S/σ ...(B) Equ. (A) and (B) W₀ = 3W_S ⟹ W₀/W_S = 3
