Consider a uniform rod of mass M = 4m and length ℓ pivoted about its centre. A mass m moving with velocity v making angle θ = π/4 to the rod's long axis collides with one end of the rod and sticks to it. The angular speed of the rod-mass system just after the collision is:

  1. (1) 3v/(7√2 ℓ)
  2. (2) 3√2 v/(7ℓ)
  3. (3) 3v/(7ℓ)
  4. (4) 4v/(7ℓ)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Conservation of angular momentum mv(ℓ/(2√2)) = (mℓ^2/4 + 4mℓ^2/12)ω ⟹ ω = 6v/(7√2 ℓ) ω = 3√2 v/(7ℓ)
