Consider a thin spherical shell of radius R with centre at the origin, carrying uniform positive surface charge density. The variation of the magnitude of the electric field |E(r)| and the electric potential V(r) with the distance r from the centre, is best represented by which graph? [Four graphs labeled A, B, C, D showing different representations of |E(r)| and V(r) versus r, with vertical line at r=R]

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) Graph A: |E(r)| shows zero until R, then peaks, then decreases. V(r) shows continuous decrease after R.
  2. (B) Graph B: |E(r)| shows constant value until R, then decreases. V(r) shows continuous decrease after R.
  3. (C) Graph C: |E(r)| shows constant value until R, then peaks at R, then decreases. V(r) shows continuous decrease after R.
  4. (D) Graph D: |E(r)| shows zero until R, then drops discontinuously, then decreases. V(r) shows continuous decrease after R.

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Sol. (D)
