For a reaction consider the plot of ln k versus 1/T given in the figure. If the rate constant of this reaction at 400 K is 10^(-5) s^(-1), then the rate constant at 500 K is –

  1. (1) 10^(-4) s^(-1)
  2. (2) 4 × 10^(-4) s^(-1)
  3. (3) 10^(-6) s^(-1)
  4. (4) 2 × 10^(-4) s^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

ln k = ln A - E_a/(RT) From the plot, slope = -E_a/R = -4606 Therefore, E_a/R = 4606 Using the Arrhenius equation in the form: ln(k_2/k_1) = (E_a/R)(1/T_1 - 1/T_2) Given: k_1 = 10^(-5) s^(-1) at T_1 = 400 K Find: k_2 at T_2 = 500 K ln(k_2/10^(-5)) = 4606(1/400 - 1/500) ln(k_2/10^(-5)) = 4606(0.0025 - 0.002) ln(k_2/10^(-5)) = 4606(0.0005) ln(k_2/10^(-5)) = 2.303 k_2/10^(-5) = e^(2.303) = 10 k_2 = 10 × 10^(-5) = 10^(-4) s^(-1)
