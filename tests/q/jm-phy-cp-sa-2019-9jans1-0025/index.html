Three charges +Q, q, +Q are placed respectively, at distance, 0, d/2 and d from the origin, on the x-axis. If the net force experienced by +Q placed at x = 0, is zero, then value of q is:

  1. (1) +Q/2
  2. (2) -Q/4
  3. (3) +Q/4
  4. (4) -Q/2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The charge +Q is placed at x = 0. The charge q is placed at x = d/2 (distance d/2 from origin). The charge +Q is placed at x = d (distance d from origin). For the net force on +Q at x = 0 to be zero, the forces from q and the other +Q must balance. Force due to charge q at distance d/2: F1 = k(Q)(q)/(d/2)^2 = kQq/(d^2/4) = 4kQq/d^2 Force due to charge +Q at distance d: F2 = k(Q)(Q)/d^2 = kQ^2/d^2 For equilibrium, these forces must be equal and opposite: |F1| = |F2| |4kQq/d^2| = |kQ^2/d^2| |4q| = |Q| Since the charge at x = d is positive (+Q), it repels the charge at x = 0 (which is also +Q), pushing it in the negative x-direction. For the net force to be zero, the charge q at x = d/2 must attract the charge at x = 0, pushing it in the positive x-direction. This is only possible if q is negative. Therefore: 4|q| = Q q = -Q/4 Option (2) is correct.
