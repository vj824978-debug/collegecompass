If an unbiased die, marked with -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3 on its faces, is thrown five times, then the probability that product of the outcomes is positive is:

  1. (1) 881/2592
  2. (2) 440/2592
  3. (3) 27/288
  4. (4) 521/2592

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

For the product of outcomes to be positive, we need an even number of negative outcomes (0, 2, or 4 negative outcomes from two possible negative values: -2 and -1). Total outcomes = 6^5 = 7776 Favorable cases: 1) Zero negative outcomes (all 5 throws are from {0, 1, 2, 3}): C(5,0) × 4^5 = 1 × 1024 = 1024 But we need to exclude cases where product is 0 (i.e., at least one 0). Cases with no 0: C(5,0) × 3^5 = 243 2) Exactly 2 negative outcomes (from {-2, -1}) and 3 from {0, 1, 2, 3}: C(5,2) × 2^2 × 4^3 = 10 × 4 × 64 = 2560 Cases with no 0: C(5,2) × 2^2 × 3^3 = 10 × 4 × 27 = 1080 3) Exactly 4 negative outcomes (from {-2, -1}) and 1 from {0, 1, 2, 3}: C(5,4) × 2^4 × 4 = 5 × 16 × 4 = 320 Cases with no 0: C(5,4) × 2^4 × 3 = 5 × 16 × 3 = 240 Required probability = (243 + 1080 + 240)/(6^5) = 1563/7776 Simplifying: 1563/7776 = 521/2592 Alternatively using the given approach: C(5,0) × 3^5 = 243 C(5,2) × 2^2 × 3^3 = 1080 C(5,4) × 2^4 × 3 = 240 Probability = (243 + 1080 + 240)/(6 × 6 × 6 × 6 × 6) = 1563/7776 = 521/2592
