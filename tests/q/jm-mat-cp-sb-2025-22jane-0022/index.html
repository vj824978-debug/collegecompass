Let A(6, 8), B(10 cos(alpha), -10 sin(alpha)) and C(-10 sin(alpha), 10 cos(alpha)) be the vertices of a triangle. If L(a, 9) and G(h, k) be its orthocenter and centroid respectively, then (5a - 3h + 6k + 100 sin(2*alpha)) is equal to ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 145

Solution

All the three points A, B, C lie on the circle x^2 + y^2 = 100 so circumcentre is (0, 0) From centroid property: (a + 0)/3 = h => a = 3h and (9 + 0)/3 = k => k = 3 also centroid: (6 + 10*cos(alpha) - 10*sin(alpha))/3 = h => 10(cos(alpha) - sin(alpha)) = 3h - 6 ......(i) and (8 + (-10*sin(alpha)) + 10*cos(alpha))/3 = k => 10(cos(alpha) - sin(alpha)) = 3k - 8 = 9 - 8 = 1 ......(ii) On squaring 100(1 - sin(2*alpha)) = 1 => 100*sin(2*alpha) = 99 from equ. (i) and (ii) we get h = 7/3 Now 5a - 3h + 6k + 100*sin(2*alpha) = 15h - 3h + 6k + 100*sin(2*alpha) = 15 × (7/3) - 3 × (7/3) + 6 × 3 + 99 = 35 - 7 + 18 + 99 = 145
