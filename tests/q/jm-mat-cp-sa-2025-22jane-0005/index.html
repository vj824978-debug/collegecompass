Suppose that the number of terms in an A.P is 2k, k∈N. If the sum of all odd terms of the A.P. is 40, the sum of all even terms is 55 and the last terms of the A.P. exceeds the first term by 27, then k is equal to

  1. (1) 5
  2. (2) 8
  3. (3) 6
  4. (4) 4

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

a₁, a₂, a₃,...........a₂ₖ → A.P Sum of odd terms: ∑(r=1 to k) a₂ᵣ₋₁ = 40 Sum of even terms: ∑(r=1 to k) a₂ᵣ = 55 a₂ₖ - a₁ = 27 ⟹ d = 27/(2k - 1) Sum of odd terms: k/2[2a₁ + (k - 1)2d] = 40 Sum of even terms: k/2[2a₂ + (k - 1)2d] = 55 a₁ = 40/k - (k - 1)d = 55/k - kd d = 15/k ⟹ 27/(2k - 1) = 15/k ⟹ 9k = 10k - 5 ∴ k = 5
