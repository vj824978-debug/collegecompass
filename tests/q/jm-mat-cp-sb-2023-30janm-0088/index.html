The mean and variance of 7 observations are 8 and 16 respectively. If one observation 14 is omitted and a and b are respectively mean and variance of remaining 6 observation, then a + 3b - 5 is equal to ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 37

Solution

Given: For 7 observations, mean = 8 and variance = 16 Sum of observations: sum x_i = 7 × 8 = 56 Variance formula: sum x_i^2 / n - (sum x_i / n)^2 = 16 sum x_i^2 / 7 - (56/7)^2 = 16 sum x_i^2 / 7 - 64 = 16 sum x_i^2 / 7 = 80 sum x_i^2 = 560 When observation 14 is omitted: New sum: sum x_i = 56 - 14 = 42 New mean: a = 42 / 6 = 7 New sum of squares: sum x_i^2 = 560 - 14^2 = 560 - 196 = 364 New variance: b = sum x_i^2 / 6 - (sum x_i / 6)^2 b = 364/6 - (42/6)^2 b = 364/6 - 49 b = 364/6 - 294/6 b = 70/6 = 35/3 3b = 35 Therefore: a + 3b - 5 = 7 + 35 - 5 = 37
