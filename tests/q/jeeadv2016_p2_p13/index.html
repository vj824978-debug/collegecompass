In the circuit shown below, the key is pressed at time t = 0. Which of the following statement(s) is (are) true? [Circuit diagram with: 40µF capacitor in series with 25kΩ resistor connected to a voltmeter (V), ammeter (A) with 50kΩ resistor in parallel, 20µF capacitor, 5V source, and a key switch]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The voltmeter displays −5 V as soon as the key is pressed, and displays +5 V after a long time
  2. (B) The voltmeter will display 0 V at time t = ln 2 seconds
  3. (C) The current in the ammeter becomes 1/e of the initial value after 1 second
  4. (D) The current in the ammeter becomes zero after a long time

Answer

Correct answer: ABCD

Solution

at t = 0, voltage across each capacitor is zero, so reading of voltmeter is −5 Volt. at t = ∞, capacitors are fully charged. So for ideal voltmeter, reading is 5 Volt. at transient state, I₁ = (5/50)e^(-t) mA, I₂ = (5/25)e^(-t) and I = I₁ + I₂ where τ = 1 sec So I becomes 1/e times of the initial current after 1 sec. [Circuit diagram showing: 5V source connected to parallel branches - top branch has 40 μF capacitor and 25 kΩ resistor in series with I₂; bottom branch has 50 kΩ resistor with I₁ and 20 μF capacitor in series, with voltmeter V across the capacitor] The reading of voltmeter at any instant = ΔV₄₀μF − ΔV₅₀kΩ = 5(1 − e^(-t)) − 5e^(-t) So at t = ln2 sec, reading of voltmeter is zero.
