Which of the following is the correct structure of L-Glucose?

  1. (1) CHO at top with HO-OH, HO-, HO- groups going down, then CH2OH at bottom
  2. (2) CHO at top with OH-HO, OH-, HO- groups going down, then CH2OH at bottom
  3. (3) CHO at top with HO-, HO-, OH-, OH- groups going down, then CH2OH at bottom
  4. (4) CHO at top with HO-, HO-, HO-, HO- groups going down, then CH2OH at bottom

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

L-Glucose is the mirror image (enantiomer) of D-Glucose. The structure of L-Glucose has the CHO group at C1 (top), and the configuration at each chiral center is opposite to that of D-Glucose. The correct structure shows: C1 (CHO), C2 (HO on left, OH on right), C3 (HO on left), C4 (HO on left), and C5 (CH2OH). This matches option (1).
