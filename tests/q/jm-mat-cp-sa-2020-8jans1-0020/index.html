The mean and variance of 20 observations are found to be 10 and 4, respectively. On rechecking, it was found that an observation 9 was incorrect and the correct observation was 11. Then the correct variance is:

  1. (1) 3.99
  2. (2) 3.98
  3. (3) 4.01
  4. (4) 4.02

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Given: n = 20, mean = 10, variance = 4 Incorrect observation = 9, Correct observation = 11 From mean = (sum of x_i)/20 = 10 sum(x_i) = 200 From variance = (sum(x_i^2))/20 - (mean)^2 = 4 (sum(x_i^2))/20 - 100 = 4 sum(x_i^2) = 2080 Actual mean = (200 - 9 + 11)/20 = 202/20 = 101/10 = 10.1 Actual variance = (2080 - 81 + 121)/20 - (10.1)^2 = (2080 - 81 + 121)/20 - (10.1)^2 = 2120/20 - 102.01 = 106 - 102.01 = 3.99
