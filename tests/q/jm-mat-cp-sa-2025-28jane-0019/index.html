If A and B are the points of intersection of the circle x^2 + y^2 - 8x = 0 and the hyperbola x^2/9 - y^2/4 = 1 and a point P moves on the line 2x - 3y + 4 = 0, then the centroid of triangle PAB lies on the line:

  1. (1) 4x - 9y = 12
  2. (2) x + 9y = 36
  3. (3) 9x - 9y = 32
  4. (4) 6x - 9y = 20

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Find intersection points A and B of circle and hyperbola by solving x^2 + y^2 - 8x = 0 and x^2/9 - y^2/4 = 1. From circle: y^2 = 8x - x^2. Substitute in hyperbola equation to get 4x^2 - 9(8x - x^2) = 36, which gives 13x^2 - 72x - 36 = 0. Solving: x = 6 (x = -6/13 rejected as y becomes imaginary). At x = 6: y^2 = 12, so y = ±sqrt(12). Thus A(6, sqrt(12)), B(6, -sqrt(12)). Point P on line 2x - 3y + 4 = 0 has form P(α, (2α+4)/3). Centroid is ((6+6+α)/3, (sqrt(12)-sqrt(12)+(2α+4)/3)/3) = ((12+α)/3, (2α+4)/9). For centroid to lie on a line, eliminate the parameter α. From h = (12+α)/3, we get α = 3h - 12. From k = (2α+4)/9, we get 2α + 4 = 9k, so α = (9k-4)/2. Equating: 3h - 12 = (9k-4)/2, which gives 6h - 9k = 20, or 6x - 9y = 20.
