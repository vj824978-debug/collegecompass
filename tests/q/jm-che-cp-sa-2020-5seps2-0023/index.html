For a reaction: X + Y ⇌ 2Z, 1.0 mol of X, 1.5 mol of Y and 0.5 mol of Z were taken in a 1 L vessel and allowed to react. At equilibrium, the concentration of Z was 1.0 mol L^(-1). The equilibrium constant of the reaction is x/15. The value of x is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 16

Solution

X + Y ⇌ 2Z At t = 0: [X] = 1 mol/L [Y] = 1.5 mol/L [Z] = 0.5 mol/L At equilibrium: [Z] = 1.0 mol/L Change in [Z] = 1.0 - 0.5 = 0.5 mol/L From stoichiometry of the reaction X + Y ⇌ 2Z: If 2 mol of Z is produced, 1 mol of X is consumed and 1 mol of Y is consumed Since 0.5 mol/L of Z is produced: X consumed = 0.5/2 = 0.25 mol/L Y consumed = 0.5/2 = 0.25 mol/L At equilibrium: [X] = 1 - 0.25 = 0.75 mol/L [Y] = 1.5 - 0.25 = 1.25 mol/L [Z] = 1.0 mol/L K_eq = [Z]^2 / ([X][Y]) K_eq = (1.0)^2 / (0.75 × 1.25) K_eq = 1 / (3/4 × 5/4) K_eq = 1 / (15/16) K_eq = 16/15 Given that K_eq = x/15: x/15 = 16/15 Therefore, x = 16
