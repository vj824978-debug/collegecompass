The radius of gyration of a uniform rod of length l, about an axis passing through a point l/4 away from the centre of the rod, and perpendicular to it, is -

  1. (1) sqrt(3/8) * l
  2. (2) (1/8) * l
  3. (3) sqrt(7/48) * l
  4. (4) (1/4) * l

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Radius of gyration The axis passes through a point l/4 away from the centre of the rod, perpendicular to it. Using parallel axis theorem: I = I_cm + M * d^2 where I_cm = M*l^2/12 (moment of inertia about centre) and d = l/4 (distance from centre) I = M*l^2/12 + M*(l/4)^2 I = M*l^2/12 + M*l^2/16 I = M*l^2*(1/12 + 1/16) I = M*l^2*(4 + 3)/(48) I = (7/48)*M*l^2 Radius of gyration k is given by: I = M*k^2 Therefore: M*k^2 = (7/48)*M*l^2 k^2 = (7/48)*l^2 k = l*sqrt(7/48)
