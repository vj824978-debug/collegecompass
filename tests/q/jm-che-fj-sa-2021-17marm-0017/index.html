A ketone compound with molecular formula C6H10O3 containing two carbonyl groups is treated with ethylene glycol in acidic medium. The more reactive carbonyl (ketone) is selectively masked first to form product A. What is the structure of product A?

  1. (1) A structure with one ketone protected as ethylene glycol acetal and one free carboxylic acid group with OC2H5 ester
  2. (2) A structure with a dioxolane ring (five-membered cyclic acetal) and a free carboxylic acid group with OC2H5 ester
  3. (3) A structure with a dioxolane ring protecting the ketone and a carboxylic acid group with OC2H5 ester
  4. (4) A structure with a dioxolane ring and an OC2H5 group attached to a hydroxyl carbon

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The starting material is a keto-ester with a ketone (C=O) and an ester (CO2C2H5) group. In acidic medium with ethylene glycol, the more reactive ketone carbonyl is selectively protected first by forming a 1,3-dioxolane ring (five-membered cyclic acetal). The less reactive carboxylic ester group remains unprotected. The product A is therefore the structure with a five-membered dioxolane ring formed from the ketone and ethylene glycol, with the ester group (CO2C2H5) remaining intact. This corresponds to option (C).
