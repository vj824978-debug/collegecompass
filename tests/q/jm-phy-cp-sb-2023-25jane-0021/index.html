Two long parallel wires carrying currents 8 A and 15 A in opposite directions are placed at a distance of 7 cm from each other. A point P is at equidistant from both the wires such that the lines joining the point P to the wires are perpendicular to each other. The magnitude of magnetic field at P is _____ × 10^(-6) T. (Given: sqrt(2) = 1.4)

Answer

Correct answer: 68

Solution

From question From the geometry, the two wires are 7 cm apart. Point P is equidistant from both wires, so distance from P to each wire is 7/sqrt(2) cm. The lines joining P to the wires are perpendicular to each other. Magnetic field due to wire carrying 15 A: B_15A = (μ₀ × 15)/(2π(7/sqrt(2) cm)) Magnetic field due to wire carrying 8 A: B_8A = (μ₀ × 8)/(2π(7/sqrt(2) cm)) B_15A & B_8A are perpendicular to each other Hence B_P = B_15A + B_8A (vector sum) = sqrt((B_15A)^2 + (B_8A)^2) = (μ₀/(2π(7/sqrt(2) cm))) × sqrt(15^2 + 8^2) = (μ₀ × 17)/(2π × (7/sqrt(2)) × 10^(-2)) = (4π × 17 × 10^(-7))/(2π × (7/sqrt(2)) × 10^(-2)) = 68 × 10^(-6)
