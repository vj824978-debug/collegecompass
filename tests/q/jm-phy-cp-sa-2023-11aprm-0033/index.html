Three vessels of equal volume contain gases at the same temperature and pressure. The first vessel contains neon (monatomic), the second contains chlorine (diatomic) and third contains uranium hexafluoride (polyatomic). Arrange these on the basis of their root mean square speed (v_rms) and choose the correct answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) v_rms (mono) > v_rms (dia) > v_rms (poly)
  2. (2) v_rms (mono) = v_rms (dia) = v_rms (poly)
  3. (3) v_rms (mono) < v_rms (dia) < v_rms (poly)
  4. (4) v_rms (dia) < v_rms (poly) < v_rms (mono)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

v_rms is same for all, if mass and temperature is same. v_rms is proportional to 1/sqrt(M). Since v_poly < v_dia < v_mono, we have v_rms (mono) > v_rms (dia) > v_rms (poly). The root mean square speed depends on molar mass inversely. Neon has smallest molar mass (20 g/mol), chlorine has intermediate molar mass (71 g/mol), and uranium hexafluoride has largest molar mass (352 g/mol). Therefore v_rms (mono) > v_rms (dia) > v_rms (poly).
