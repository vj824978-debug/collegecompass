The required height of a TV tower which can cover the population of 6.03 lakh is h. If the average population density is 100 per square km and the radius of earth is 6400 km, then the value of h will be _______ m.

Answer

Correct answer: 150

Solution

Given: Population = 6.03 lakh = 6.03 × 10^5, Population density = 100 per sq km, Earth's radius R = 6400 km. Area covered A = Population/Density = (6.03 × 10^5)/100 = 6.03 × 10^3 sq km. For a TV tower of height h on Earth's surface, the coverage area is given by A = πr² where r = sqrt(2hR) (derived from geometry of circle on sphere). So πr² = 6.03 × 10³. This gives π(sqrt(2hR))² = 6.03 × 10³, hence π(2hR) = 6.03 × 10³. Solving: h = (6.03 × 10³)/(2π × R) = (6.03 × 10³)/(2π × 6400) = 0.015 × 10 × 10³ m = 150 m.
