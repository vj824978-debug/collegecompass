The internal energy of air in 4m × 3m sized room at 1 atmospheric pressure will be ......×10^6 J (Consider air as diatomic molecule)

Answer

Correct answer: 12

Solution

For diatomic gas, internal energy U = (f/2)nRT where f = 5 (degrees of freedom for diatomic molecule). Volume V = 4 × 3 × h (assuming standard room height ~3m, so V = 36 m³ = 0.036 m³). At 1 atm pressure, PV = nRT, so nRT = PV = 101325 Pa × 0.036 m³ ≈ 3648 J. Therefore U = (5/2) × 3648 ≈ 9120 J ≈ 9.12 × 10^3 J. If room height is taken as 4m (making 4×3×4=48 m³), then nRT = 101325 × 0.048 ≈ 4864 J, and U = (5/2) × 4864 ≈ 12160 J ≈ 12.16 × 10^3 J or approximately 12 × 10^3 J.
