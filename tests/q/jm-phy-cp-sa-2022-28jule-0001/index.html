Consider the efficiency of carnot's engine is given by η = (αβ/sin θ) log e (βx/kT), where a and β are constants. If T is temperature, k is Boltzmann constant, θ is angular displacement and x has the dimensions of length. Then, choose the incorrect option

  1. (1) Dimensions of β is same as that of force.
  2. (2) Dimensions of α^(-1) x is same as that of energy.
  3. (3) Dimensions of η^(-1) sin θ is same as that of αβ.
  4. (4) Dimensions of α is same as that of β.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

(1) [β] = [kT/x] = [E/x] = [MLT^(-2)] = [F] ✓ (2) [α,β] = [M^a L^b T^d] [α]^(-1) = [β] = [kT/x] so [α]^(-1)[x] = [kT] = [ML^2T^(-2)] = energy ✓ (3) η sin θ = αβ so [η sin θ] = [αβ] [η] = [M^a L^b T^d] it is dimensionless quantity ✓ (4) [α] ≠ [β]
