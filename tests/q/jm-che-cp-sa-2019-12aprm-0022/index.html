The ratio of number of atoms present in a simple cubic, body centered cubic and face centered cubic structure are, respectively:

  1. (1) 8 : 1 : 6
  2. (2) 4 : 2 : 1
  3. (3) 1 : 2 : 4
  4. (4) 4 : 2 : 3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Z_SC : Z_BCC : Z_FCC For Simple Cubic (SC): - Corner atoms: 8 × (1/8) = 1 atom per unit cell - Z_SC = 1 For Body Centered Cubic (BCC): - Corner atoms: 8 × (1/8) = 1 atom - Body center atoms: 1 × 1 = 1 atom - Z_BCC = 1 + 1 = 2 For Face Centered Cubic (FCC): - Corner atoms: 8 × (1/8) = 1 atom - Face center atoms: 6 × (1/2) = 3 atoms - Z_FCC = 1 + 3 = 4 Therefore, Z_SC : Z_BCC : Z_FCC = 1 : 2 : 4
