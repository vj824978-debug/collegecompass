Let a₁, a₂, a₃, ... be a G.P of increasing positive numbers. If the product of fourth and sixth terms is 9 and the sum of fifth and seventh terms is 24, then a₁a₉ + a₂a₄a₆ + a₅ + a₇ is equal to

Answer

Correct answer: 60

Solution

Let r be the common ratio of the G.P a₁r³ × a₁r⁵ = 9 a₁²r⁸ = 9 ⟹ a₁r⁴ = 3 And a₁(r⁴ + r⁶) = 24 ⟹ 3(1 + r²) = 24 ∴ r² = 7 and a₁ = 3/49 Now a₁a₉ + a₂a₄a₆ + a₅ + a₇ = a₁²r⁸ + a₁³r¹² + 24 = 24 + (9/7⁴) × 7⁴ + (27/7⁶) · 7⁶ = 60
