In the given reaction, cyclohexanol is treated with (i) K2Cr2O7/H+, then (ii) C6H5MgBr, (iii) H2O, and finally (ii) H+, heat to give major product 'X'. The number of sp2 hybridised carbon(s) in compound 'X' is ___.

Answer

Correct answer: 8

Solution

Step 1: Cyclohexanol is oxidized by K2Cr2O7/H+ to cyclohexanone. Step 2: Cyclohexanone reacts with C6H5MgBr (phenylmagnesium bromide) to form a Grignard adduct. Step 3: After H2O workup, this gives 1-phenyl-1-cyclohexanol (secondary alcohol with OH and phenyl group on cyclohexane ring). Step 4: Heating with H+ causes dehydration and rearrangement, forming 1-phenyl-1-cyclohexene. Step 5: Further heating under acidic conditions leads to rearrangement and formation of a stable aromatic system. The major product is a phenylcyclohexadiene or similar conjugated structure where the cyclohexene ring conjugates with the benzene ring. Step 6: Counting sp2 carbons: 6 carbons in benzene ring (all sp2) + 2 carbons in the conjugated double bonds of cyclohexene = 8 sp2 hybridised carbons total.
