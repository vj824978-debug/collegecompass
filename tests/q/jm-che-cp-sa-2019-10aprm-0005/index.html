Compound A(C9H10O) shows positive iodoform test. Oxidation of A with KMnO4/KOH given acid B(C8H6O4). Anhydride of B is used for the preparation of phenolphthalein. Compound A is:

  1. (1) 4-methylacetophenone (structure showing benzene ring with CH3 and COCH3 groups)
  2. (2) 2-methylacetophenone (structure showing benzene ring with CH3 and COCH3 groups in ortho positions)
  3. (3) 2-methylbenzyl formate (structure showing benzene ring with CH3, CH2 and CHO groups)
  4. (4) 2-methylphenylacetone (structure showing benzene ring with CH2, CH3 and COCH3 groups)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given information: - Compound A has molecular formula C9H10O - A shows positive iodoform test (indicates presence of CH3CO- group) - Oxidation of A with KMnO4/KOH gives acid B with formula C8H6O4 - Anhydride of B is used for preparation of phenolphthalein Analysis: 1) Positive iodoform test indicates presence of CH3CO- (acetyl) group or CH3CH(OH)- group 2) The anhydride of B forms phenolphthalein, which is derived from phthalic anhydride 3) This means B must be a dicarboxylic acid with benzene ring = phthalic acid (C8H6O4) 4) Phthalic acid has two COOH groups on adjacent carbons of benzene ring 5) When A is oxidized to give phthalic acid (C8H6O4): - A must have molecular formula C9H10O - The oxidation converts CH3 group and CH3CO group to COOH groups - Structure must be: benzene ring with CH2CH3 and COCH3 groups (or CH3 and CH2COCH3) 6) For structure (4): 2-methylphenylacetone - Contains benzene ring with CH3 and CH2COCH3 groups - The CH3COCH2- contains the acetyl group (positive iodoform test) - Upon oxidation: CH2COCH3 → COOH and CH3 → COOH - This gives phthalic acid (1,2-benzenedicarboxylic acid) 7) Verification: - Molecular formula: C6H4 + CH3 + CH2COCH3 = C9H10O ✓ - Iodoform test: CH3CO group present ✓ - Oxidation product: Phthalic acid (C8H6O4) ✓ - Anhydride forms phenolphthalein ✓
