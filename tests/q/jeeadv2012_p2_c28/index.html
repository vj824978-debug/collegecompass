For a dilute solution containing 2.5 g of a non-volatile non-electrolyte solute in 100 g of water, the elevation in boiling point at 1 atm pressure is 2°C. Assuming concentration of solute is much lower than the concentration of solvent, the vapour pressure (mm of Hg) of the solution is (take K_b = 0.76 K kg mol⁻¹)

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 724
  2. (B) 740
  3. (C) 736
  4. (D) 718

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

ΔT_b = K_b × m 2 = 0.76 × m ∴ m = 2/0.76 P⁰ - P/P = m × MM × 10⁻³ ∴ (760 - P)/P = (2/0.76) × 18 × 10⁻³ 760 - P = (36/760)P ∴ 760 = (796/760)P ∴ P = 760(796/760)torr = 725.6 torr ≈ 724 torr
