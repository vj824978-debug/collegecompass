What will be the effect on the root mean square velocity of oxygen molecules if the temperature is doubled and oxygen molecule dissociates into atomic oxygen?

  1. (1) The velocity of atomic oxygen remains same
  2. (2) The velocity of atomic oxygen doubles
  3. (3) The velocity of atomic oxygen becomes half
  4. (4) The velocity of atomic oxygen becomes four times

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using v_rms = sqrt(3RT/M_0). When temperature is doubled (T becomes 2T) and O2 dissociates into atomic oxygen (molar mass becomes M_0/2): v_rms_new = sqrt(3RT × 2T / (M_0/2)) = sqrt(3RT × 2T × 2 / M_0) = sqrt(4 × 3RT/M_0) = 2 × v_rms. Therefore velocity of atomic oxygen is doubled.
