Let g(x) = integral from 0 to x of f'(t)/(1+t^2) dt which of the following is true?

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) g'(x) is positive on (-∞, 0) and negative on (0, ∞)
  2. (B) g'(x) is negative on (-∞, 0) and positive on (0, ∞)
  3. (C) g'(x) changes sign on both (-∞, 0) and (0, ∞)
  4. (D) g'(x) does not change sign on (-∞, ∞)

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol. (B) g'(x) = f'(e^x)e^x / (1 + e^2x) Hence positive for (0, ∞) and negative for (-∞, 0). IIT-JEE2008-PAPER-2-8
