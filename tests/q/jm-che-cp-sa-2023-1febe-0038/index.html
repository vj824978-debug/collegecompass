The structures of major products A, B and C in the following reaction are sequence. Starting material: a chain with O=C(H) group. First step: NaHSO4, dil HCl, NaCN, H2O leads to [A]. Second step: LiAlH4 leads to [B]. Alternative path from [A]: HCl/H2O with heat (triangle) leads to [C].

  1. (1) A = HO-C(CN)(H) with chain, B = HO-C(CH2NH2)(H) with chain, C = HO-C(CO2H)(H) with chain
  2. (2) A = HO-C(CN)(H) with chain, B = HO-C(CHO)(H) with chain, C = HO-C(CO2H)(H) with chain
  3. (3) A = OSO3Na-C(H) with chain, B = HO-C(H) with chain, C = Cl-C(H) with ethyl chain
  4. (4) A = HO-C(SO3H)(H) with chain, B = HO-C(CH2OH)(H) with chain, C = HO-C(SO2Cl)(H) with chain

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The starting material is an aldehyde with the structure: a hydrocarbon chain with an aldehyde group C(=O)H. Step 1: Treatment with NaHSO4, dil HCl, NaCN, H2O This is a Strecker synthesis. The aldehyde undergoes nucleophilic addition with CN- ion under acidic conditions to form a cyanohydrin intermediate. The product [A] is: HO-C(CN)(H) where the OH and CN are on the same carbon (geminal positions), with the hydrocarbon chain attached. Step 2: From [A] with LiAlH4 LiAlH4 is a strong reducing agent. It reduces the nitrile group (C≡N) to a primary amine (CH2NH2). The alcohol group remains intact. The product [B] is: HO-C(CH2NH2)(H) with the hydrocarbon chain attached. Alternative reaction from [A]: HCl/H2O with heat (reflux) Under acidic aqueous conditions with heating, the cyanohydrin [A] undergoes hydrolysis. The CN group is converted to a carboxylic acid group (CO2H). The product [C] is: HO-C(CO2H)(H) with the hydrocarbon chain attached. Therefore, the correct answer is option (1).
