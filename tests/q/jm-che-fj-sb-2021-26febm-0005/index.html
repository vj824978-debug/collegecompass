A homogeneous ideal gaseous reaction AB(g) ⇌ A(g) + 2B(g) is carried out in a 25 litre flask at 27°C. The initial amount of AB₅ was 1 mole and the equilibrium pressure was 1.9 atm. The value of Kₚ is x × 10^(-2). The value of x is __________. (Integer answer). [R = 0.08206 dm³ atm K^(-1) mol^(-1)]

Answer

Correct answer: 73

Solution

ICE table setup: Initial AB = 1 mole, at equilibrium AB = 1-x mole, A = x mole, B = 2x mole. Total moles at equilibrium = 1 + 2x. Using PV = nRT: 1.9 × 25 = (1+2x) × 0.08206 × 300. Solving: x = 0.465. Partial pressures at equilibrium: P(AB) = 0.535/1.93 × 1.9 atm, P(A) = 0.465/1.93 × 1.9 atm, P(B) = 2 × 0.465/1.93 × 1.9 atm. Using Kₚ = P(A) × P(B)²/P(AB) = [(0.465 × 1.9/1.93) × (2 × 0.465 × 1.9/1.93)²] / [0.535 × 1.9/1.93] = 0.728. Kₚ = 0.73 = 73 × 10^(-2). Therefore x = 73.
