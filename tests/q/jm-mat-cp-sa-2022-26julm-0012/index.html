A point P moves so that the sum of squares of its distances from the points (1, 2) and (-2, 1) is 14. Let f(x, y) = 0 be the locus of P, which intersects the x-axis at the points A, B and the y-axis at the points C, D. Then the area of the quadrilateral ACBD is equal to

  1. (1) 9/2
  2. (2) 3*sqrt(17)/2
  3. (3) 3*sqrt(17)/4
  4. (4) 9

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Let point P be (h, k). From the given condition: (h-1)^2 + (k-2)^2 + (h+2)^2 + (k-1)^2 = 14. Expanding and simplifying: 2h^2 + 2k^2 + 2h - 6k - 4 = 0. Dividing by 2: h^2 + k^2 + h - 3k - 2 = 0. So locus of P is x^2 + y^2 + x - 3y - 2 = 0. For x-axis intersection (y=0): x^2 + x - 2 = 0, giving x = -2, 1. So A and B are at x = -2 and x = 1. For y-axis intersection (x=0): y^2 - 3y - 2 = 0, giving y = (3 ± sqrt(17))/2. So C and D are at y = (3 + sqrt(17))/2 and (3 - sqrt(17))/2. Area of quadrilateral = (1/2)|x1| + |x2| × (|y1| + |y2|) = (1/2) × 3 × sqrt(17) = 3*sqrt(17)/2
