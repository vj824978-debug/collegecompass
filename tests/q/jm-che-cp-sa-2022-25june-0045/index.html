Amongst the following, the major product of the given chemical reaction is: 2,3-dihydropyran + Br2 in CH3OH → Major Product

  1. (1) Structure with Br and OCH3 groups in axial/equatorial positions (1,2-trans arrangement with Br axial, OCH3 equatorial)
  2. (2) Structure with Br and OCH3 groups (Br equatorial, OCH3 axial)
  3. (3) Structure with OCH3 axial and Br equatorial
  4. (4) Structure with OCH3 equatorial and Br axial (1,2-trans arrangement opposite to option 1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction involves electrophilic addition of Br2 to 2,3-dihydropyran followed by nucleophilic attack by CH3OH. The bromine adds first to form a bromonium ion intermediate. Methanol attacks from the back side, and due to the preference for anti-addition and the stability of the resulting carbocation intermediate, the major product is formed with Br and OCH3 in trans relationship. The stereochemistry shown in option (1) with Br in axial position and OCH3 in equatorial position represents the major product due to steric and electronic factors favoring this stereoisomer.
