A water tank has the shape of a right circular cone with axis vertical and vertex downwards. Its semi-vertical angle is tan^(-1)(3/4). Water is poured in it at a constant rate of 6 cubic meter per hour. The rate (in square meter per hour), at which the wet curved surface area of the tank is increasing, when the depth of water in the tank is 4 meters, is

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

Given: semi-vertical angle theta where tan(theta) = 3/4, dV/dt = 6 cu m/hr, h = 4 m. From tan(theta) = 3/4 = r/h, when h = 4, r = 3. Volume V = (1/3)pi*r^2*h = (1/3)pi*(4h/3)^2*h = (4pi/27)*h^3. dV/dt = (4pi/9)*h^2*dh/dt, so 6 = (4pi/9)*9*dh/dt, giving dh/dt = 1/(2pi). Curved surface area A = pi*r*sqrt(r^2 + h^2) = pi*r*sqrt(r^2 + (3r/4)^2) = (5pi/3)*r^2. dA/dt = (10pi/3)*r*dr/dt = (10pi/3)*3*(1/2pi) = 5 sq m/hr.
