The variation of refractive index of a crown glass thin prism with wavelength of the incident light is shown. Which of the following graphs is the correct one, if D_m is the angle of minimum deviation?

  1. (1) Graph (1): D_m decreases as wavelength increases from 400 to 700 nm
  2. (2) Graph (2): D_m increases as wavelength increases from 400 to 700 nm
  3. (3) Graph (3): D_m increases as wavelength increases from 400 to 700 nm (curved upward)
  4. (4) Graph (4): D_m has a minimum value at some intermediate wavelength between 400 and 700 nm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

D_m => (mu - 1) A as lambda increases, mu decreases and hence deviation (D_m) also decreases From the given graph of refractive index vs wavelength: - At lambda = 400 nm, n_2 = 1.531 (maximum) - At lambda = 700 nm, n = 1.513 (minimum) Since the refractive index decreases with increasing wavelength, and D_m is proportional to (mu - 1), the angle of minimum deviation D_m must also decrease with increasing wavelength. Therefore, the graph should show D_m decreasing as wavelength increases from 400 nm to 700 nm. Graph (1) correctly represents this relationship where D_m decreases monotonically as wavelength increases.
