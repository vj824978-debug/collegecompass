Given below are two statements: Statement I: For transmitting a signal, size of antenna (ℓ) should be comparable to wavelength of signal (at least ℓ = lambda/4 in dimension) Statement II: In amplitude modulation, amplitude of carrier wave remains constant (unchanged). In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

  1. (1) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
  2. (2) Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
  3. (3) Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
  4. (4) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

• In amplitude modulation frequency of carrier wave remains unchanged. • Minimum size of antenna should be (1/4)th of wavelength Analysis of Statement I: The statement correctly identifies that the antenna size should be comparable to the wavelength of the signal, with the minimum size being ℓ = lambda/4. This is a fundamental principle of antenna design for efficient radiation and reception of electromagnetic waves. This statement is CORRECT. Analysis of Statement II: The statement claims that in amplitude modulation, the amplitude of the carrier wave remains constant (unchanged). This is INCORRECT. In amplitude modulation (AM), by definition, the amplitude of the carrier wave is modulated (varied) in accordance with the modulating signal. The frequency remains constant, but the amplitude changes. Therefore, Statement II is INCORRECT. Conclusion: Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect. Answer: [4]
