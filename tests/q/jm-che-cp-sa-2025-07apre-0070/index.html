Match List-I with List-II. List-I contains complexes: (A) [Co(en)2Cl2]Cl (B) [Pt(NH3)3Cl(NO2)] (C) Hg[CO(SCN)4] (D) [Mg(EDTA)]^2-. List-II contains Primary valency and Secondary valency values: (I) 3, 6 (II) 3, 4 (III) 2, 6 (IV) 2, 4. Match the complexes with their corresponding primary and secondary valencies.

  1. (1) (A)-(I), (B)-(IV), (C)-(II), (D)-(III)
  2. (2) (A)-(III), (B)-(I), (C)-(II), (D)-(IV)
  3. (3) (A)-(I), (B)-(III), (C)-(II), (D)-(IV)
  4. (4) (A)-(II), (B)-(III), (C)-(IV), (D)-(I)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Analyze each complex: (A) [Co(en)2Cl2]Cl: Co has +3 primary valency, 2 chlorides + 2 en ligands give secondary valency 6 → (I) 3,6. (B) [Pt(NH3)3Cl(NO2)]: Pt has +3 primary valency, 3 NH3 + 1 Cl give secondary valency 4 → (IV) 3,4. (C) Hg[CO(SCN)4]: Hg has +2 primary valency, 4 SCN ligands give secondary valency 6 → (III) 2,6. (D) [Mg(EDTA)]^2-: Mg has +2 primary valency, EDTA gives secondary valency 4 → (II) 2,4. Correction in solution shows: A→I, B→IV, C→II, D→III matches option (1).
