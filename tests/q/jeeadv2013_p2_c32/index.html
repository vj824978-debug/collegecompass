In the following reaction sequences V and W are, respectively Q --H2/Ni--> V A [Benzene ring] + V --AlCl3(anhydrous)--> --1. Zn-Hg/HCl--> W 2. H3PO4

Comprehension · Marks: +4 / -1.0

Passage:

  1. (A) V: [Five-membered ring with O at top and two C=O groups]; W: [Tetralin structure with ketone group at position 1]
  2. (B) V: [Benzene ring with two CH2OH groups]; W: [Naphthalene structure]
  3. (C) V: [Five-membered ring with O at top and two C=O groups]; W: [Tetralin structure without ketone]
  4. (D) V: [Benzene ring with alternating CH2OH groups]; W: [Tetralin structure with CH2OH group]

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

Sol. (A) Q --[H₂/Ni, Δ]--> [5-membered ring with two carbonyl groups and one oxygen atom (glutaric anhydride)] [benzene ring] + [glutaric anhydride] --[AlCl₃]--> [benzene ring attached to 4-carbon chain with carboxylic acid group and ketone] --[Zn-Hg, HCl]--> [benzene ring attached to 4-carbon chain with hydroxyl group and ketone] --[H₃PO₄]--> [fused benzene and 6-membered ring system with one ketone (1-tetralone)]
