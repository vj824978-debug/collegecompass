The de Broglie wavelength of an electron having kinetic energy E is λ. If the kinetic energy of electron becomes E/4, then its de-Broglie wavelength will be:

  1. (1) sqrt(2)λ
  2. (2) λ/sqrt(2)
  3. (3) λ/2
  4. (4) 2λ

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

de-Broglie wavelength λ = h/sqrt(2mKE). When KE becomes E/4, the new wavelength λ' = h/sqrt(2m(E/4)) = h/sqrt(mE/2) = h*sqrt(2)/(sqrt(mE)) = sqrt(2) * h/sqrt(mE). Since λ = h/sqrt(2mE), we have h/sqrt(mE) = λ*sqrt(2). Therefore λ' = sqrt(2) * λ*sqrt(2) = 2λ
