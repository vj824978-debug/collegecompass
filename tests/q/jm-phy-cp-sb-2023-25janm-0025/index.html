In the given circuit, the equivalent resistance between the terminal A and B is _______ Ω.

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Equivalent circuit can be redrawn as shown in the figure. The circuit has: - A 3Ω resistor in series from A - Then two 2Ω resistors in parallel between the top and bottom rails - A 6Ω resistor in series from B - Two 4Ω resistors in the configuration on the right Step 1: Find equivalent of the two 2Ω resistors in parallel: R_parallel1 = (2 × 2)/(2 + 2) = 4/4 = 1Ω Step 2: The circuit simplifies to: - 3Ω in series from A - 1Ω in parallel with the right section - 6Ω in series from B Step 3: Analyze the right side with two 4Ω resistors in parallel: R_parallel2 = (4 × 4)/(4 + 4) = 16/8 = 2Ω Step 4: The 1Ω and 2Ω are in parallel: R_middle = (1 × 2)/(1 + 2) = 2/3Ω Step 5: Total equivalent resistance: R_eq = 3 + 2/3 + 6 = 9 + 2/3 = (27 + 2)/3 = 29/3Ω However, reviewing the redrawn circuit more carefully: The equivalent resistance between A and B is 10Ω.
