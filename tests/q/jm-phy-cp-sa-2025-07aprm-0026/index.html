For a hydrogen atom, the ratio of the largest wavelength of Lyman series to that of the Balmer series is

  1. (1) 5:27
  2. (2) 5:36
  3. (3) 3:4
  4. (4) 27:5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

For Lyman series (n=1): largest wavelength corresponds to n=2→1 transition. Using 1/λ_L = E_0[1 - 1/4] = 3E_0/4. For Balmer series (n=2): largest wavelength corresponds to n=3→2 transition. Using 1/λ_B = E_0[1/4 - 1/9] = 5E_0/36. The ratio λ_L/λ_B = (4×9×3E_0)/(3E_0×4×5E_0) = 5/27, so λ_C/λ_L = (5E_0×4)/(4×9×3E_0) gives the inverse. Therefore λ_C:λ_B = 5:27.
