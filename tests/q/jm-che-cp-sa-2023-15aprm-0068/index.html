During water-gas shift reaction (1) Carbon monoxide is oxidized to carbon dioxide (2) Water is evaporated in presence of catalyst (3) Carbon is oxidized to carbon monoxide (4) Carbon dioxide is reduced to carbon monoxide

  1. (1) Carbon monoxide is oxidized to carbon dioxide
  2. (2) Water is evaporated in presence of catalyst
  3. (3) Carbon is oxidized to carbon monoxide
  4. (4) Carbon dioxide is reduced to carbon monoxide

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

In the water-gas shift reaction: C + H2O → CO + H2 (syngas formation), followed by CO + H2O → CO2 + H2 (water-gas shift reaction). The primary reaction is the oxidation of carbon monoxide to carbon dioxide by water, which is the characteristic feature of the water-gas shift reaction. Water is not evaporated (option 2 is incorrect), carbon is not oxidized to CO in this step (option 3 describes syngas formation), and CO2 is not reduced in the shift reaction (option 4 is incorrect). The water-gas shift reaction specifically oxidizes CO to CO2.
