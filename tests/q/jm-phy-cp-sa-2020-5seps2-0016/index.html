A galvanometer is used in laboratory for detecting the null point in electrical experiments. If, on passing a current of 6 mA it produces a deflection of 2°, its figure of merit is close to -

  1. (1) 3 × 10^(-3) A/div.
  2. (2) 333° A/div.
  3. (3) 6 × 10^(-3) A/div.
  4. (4) 666° A/div.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Figure of Merit = C = i/theta Where i is the current in Amperes and theta is the deflection in degrees. Given: i = 6 mA = 6 × 10^(-3) A theta = 2° C = (6 × 10^(-3))/2 = 3 × 10^(-3) A/degree Since 1 division typically corresponds to 1 degree in a galvanometer scale, the figure of merit is 3 × 10^(-3) A/div.
