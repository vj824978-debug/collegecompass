During Kinetic study of reaction 2A + B → C + D, the following results were obtained. Based on the data, find the overall order of the reaction. Data: Experiment I: A[M]=0.1, B[M]=0.1, Initial rate of formation of D=6.0×10^(-3). Experiment II: A[M]=0.3, B[M]=0.2, Initial rate of formation of D=7.2×10^(-2). Experiment III: A[M]=0.3, B[M]=0.4, Initial rate of formation of D=2.88×10^(-1). Experiment IV: A[M]=0.4, B[M]=0.1, Initial rate of formation of D=2.40×10^(-2).

Answer

Correct answer: 3

Solution

Using rate law: r = K[A]^x[B]^y. Dividing experiment IV by I: 2.4×10^(-2)/(6.0×10^(-3)) = ([0.4]/[0.1])^x × ([0.1]/[0.1])^y. This gives 4 = 4^x, so x = 1. Dividing experiment III by II: 2.88×10^(-1)/(7.2×10^(-2)) = ([0.3]/[0.3])^x × ([0.4]/[0.2])^y. This gives 4 = 2^y, so y = 2. Overall order = x + y = 1 + 2 = 3.
