A pole is broken at a point but does not separate. The top of the pole bends so that it touches the ground at a point 18 m away from the base. The upper part is shorter than the lower part. If the two parts subtend equal angles at a point on the ground 18 m away from the base of the pole, then the height of the pole (in meters) is:

  1. (1) 8*sqrt(10)
  2. (2) 12/15
  3. (3) 12/10
  4. (4) 6*sqrt(10)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Let the pole be broken at height h1 from the ground, with lower part = h1 and upper part = h2, where h2 < h1. Total height = h1 + h2. When bent, the upper part touches ground 18 m away. The bent part has length h2 and reaches from point (0, h1) to (18, 0). So h2 = sqrt(18^2 + h1^2). The two parts subtend equal angles at point (18, 0). Using the angle condition and constraint h2^2 = 324 + h1^2, along with h1 + h2 being the total height, solving yields total height = 12*sqrt(10) meters.
