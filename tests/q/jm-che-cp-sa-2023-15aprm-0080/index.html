Decreasing order of reactivity towards electrophilic substitution for the following compounds is: (a) toluene (CH3), (b) benzene, (c) fluorobenzene (CF3), (d) anisole (OCH3), (e) N,N-dimethylaniline (NMe2)

  1. (1) d > a > e > c > b
  2. (2) e > d > a > b > c
  3. (3) a > d > e > b > c
  4. (4) c > b > a > d > e

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Reactivity of aromatic compounds towards electrophilic substitution depends on electron density on benzene ring. Higher the electron density on benzene ring, more is the reactivity towards electrophilic substitution. Electron donating groups (like NMe2, OCH3, CH3) increase reactivity while electron withdrawing groups (like CF3) decrease reactivity. Order of electron donating ability: NMe2 > OCH3 > CH3 > H > CF3. Hence the correct decreasing order is: NMe2 > OCH3 > CH3 > benzene > CF3, which corresponds to e > d > a > b > c
