The driver of a bus approaching a big wall notices that the frequency of his bus's horn changes from 420 Hz to 490 Hz when he hears it after it gets reflected from the wall. Find the speed of the bus if speed of the sound is 330 ms^(-1).

  1. (1) 61 kmh^(-1)
  2. (2) 81 kmh^(-1)
  3. (3) 71 kmh^(-1)
  4. (4) 91 kmh^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

The bus approaching the wall emits sound at frequency f_0 = 420 Hz. This sound reflects from the wall and is heard by the driver at frequency f_2 = 490 Hz. Step 1: When the bus approaches the wall, the wall receives sound at frequency: f_1 = (330/(330 - v_B)) × 420 Step 2: The wall acts as a stationary source reflecting this frequency. The driver (moving toward the wall) receives the reflected sound at frequency: f_2 = ((330 + v_B)/330) × (330/(330 - v_B)) × 420 Step 3: Simplifying: f_2 = ((330 + v_B)/(330 - v_B)) × 420 Step 4: Given that f_2 = 490 Hz: 490 = ((330 + v_B)/(330 - v_B)) × 420 Step 5: Solving for v_B: 490/420 = (330 + v_B)/(330 - v_B) 7/6 = (330 + v_B)/(330 - v_B) Step 6: Cross-multiplying: 7(330 - v_B) = 6(330 + v_B) 2310 - 7v_B = 1980 + 6v_B 2310 - 1980 = 6v_B + 7v_B 330 = 13v_B Step 7: Therefore: v_B = 330/13 m/s v_B = (330/13) × (18/5) ≈ 91 kmh^(-1)
