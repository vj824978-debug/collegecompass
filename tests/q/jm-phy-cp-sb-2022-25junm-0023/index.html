The velocity of upper layer of water in a river is 36 kmh^(-1). Shearing stress between horizontal layers of water is 10^(-3) Nm^(-2). Depth of the river is ____ m. (Co-efficient of viscosity of water is 10^(-2) Pa.s)

Answer

Correct answer: 100

Solution

Using Newton's law of viscosity: F = -η A (du/dx). The shearing stress τ = F/A = -η (du/dx). Given: τ = 10^(-3) Nm^(-2), η = 10^(-2) Pa.s, du = 36 kmh^(-1) = 10 ms^(-1). Therefore: 10^(-3) = 10^(-2) × (10/h), which gives h = (10^(-1))/(10^(-3)) = 100 m
