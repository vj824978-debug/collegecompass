A small uncharged conducting sphere is placed in contact with an identical sphere but having 4 × 10^(-8) C charge and then removed to a distance such that the force of repulsion between them is 9 × 10^(-3) N. The distance between them is (Take 1/(4πε₀) as 9 × 10^9 in SI units)

  1. (1) 2 cm
  2. (2) 3 cm
  3. (3) 4 cm
  4. (4) 1 cm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Q = 4 × 10^(-8) C When the uncharged sphere is placed in contact with the charged sphere, the charge distributes equally between them. Each sphere gets Q/2 = 2 × 10^(-8) C After separation, both spheres have charge Q/2 each. The force of repulsion is given by Coulomb's law: F = k(Q/2)(Q/2)/r^2 F = k(Q^2)/4r^2 Substituting values: 9 × 10^(-3) = (9 × 10^9 × (4 × 10^(-8))^2)/(4 × r^2) 9 × 10^(-3) = (9 × 10^9 × 16 × 10^(-16))/(4 × r^2) 9 × 10^(-3) = (9 × 10^9 × 16 × 10^(-16))/(4r^2) r^2 = (9 × 10^9 × 16 × 10^(-16))/(4 × 9 × 10^(-3)) r^2 = (144 × 10^(-7))/(36 × 10^(-3)) r^2 = (144 × 10^(-7))/(36 × 10^(-3)) = 4 × 10^(-4) r = 2 × 10^(-2) m = 2 cm
