An observer can see through a small hole on the side of a jar (radius 15 cm) at a point at height of 15 cm from the bottom (see figure). The hole is at a height of 45 cm. When the jar is filled with a liquid up to a height of 30 cm the same observer can see the edge at the bottom of the jar. If the refractive index of the liquid is N/100, where N is an integer, the value of N is _______.

Answer

Correct answer: 158

Solution

Initially, the observer at the hole (height 15 cm, radius 15 cm from axis) can see through to the jar. When liquid is filled to height 30 cm, the observer can now see the edge at the bottom of the jar through refraction at the liquid surface. From the geometry: - The hole is at height 15 cm from bottom and 15 cm from the axis (at radius 15 cm) - The observer's line of sight must reach the edge at the bottom (radius 15 cm from axis, height 0 cm) - The liquid surface is at height 30 cm The ray travels from the hole through the liquid surface to the bottom edge. For the initial condition (no liquid): tan r = 15/30 = 1/2 sin r = 1/sqrt(5) For the refracted ray in liquid reaching the bottom edge: The refracted ray must travel from point on surface (height 30 cm) to bottom edge (height 0 cm, radius 15 cm). The perpendicular distance from hole to jar axis gives the incident angle. Applying Snell's law at the liquid surface: 1 × sin(45°) = μ × sin r (1/sqrt(2)) = μ × (1/sqrt(5)) μ = sqrt(5)/sqrt(2) = sqrt(5/2) = 1.581 Since refractive index = N/100: N/100 = sqrt(5)/sqrt(2) N = 100 × sqrt(5)/sqrt(2) N = 100 × sqrt(5/2) N = 100 × 1.581 N = 158.1 So integer value of N = 158
