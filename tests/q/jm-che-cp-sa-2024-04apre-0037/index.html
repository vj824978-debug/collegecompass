CH3 - CH2 - CH2 - Br + NaOH with C2H5OH gives Product 'A'. Product A undergoes: (1) H+ in H2O to give Product 'B', and (2) Diborane followed by H2O/H2O2/OH- to give Product 'C'. Identify Product B and Product C.

  1. (1) B = C = 2-Propanol
  2. (2) B = 2-Propanol, C = 1-Propanol
  3. (3) B = 1-Propanol, C = 2-Propanol
  4. (4) B = C = 1-Propanol

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Starting material is CH3-CH2-CH2-Br (1-bromopropane). With NaOH in C2H5OH (ethanol), elimination occurs to form Product A: CH3-CH=CH2 (propene). When Product A reacts with H+ in H2O (hydration), it follows Markovnikov's rule to give Product B: CH3-CHOH-CH3 (2-propanol). When Product A reacts with diborane (B2H6) followed by H2O2/OH- (hydroboration-oxidation), it follows anti-Markovnikov's rule to give Product C: CH3-CH2-CH2-OH (1-propanol).
