Four identical particles of equal masses 1 kg made to move along the circumference of a circle of radius 1 m under the action of their own mutual gravitational attraction. The speed of each particle will be:

  1. (1) sqrt((1 + 2*sqrt(2))*G) / 2
  2. (2) sqrt(G/2 * (1 + sqrt(2)))
  3. (3) sqrt(G/2 * (2*sqrt(2) - 1))
  4. (4) sqrt(G*(1 + 2*sqrt(2)))

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Four particles of mass m = 1 kg are positioned at the vertices of a square inscribed in a circle of radius r = 1 m. Each particle experiences gravitational forces from the other three particles. The net radial force provides centripetal acceleration. Two adjacent particles are at distance sqrt(2)*r from each particle. The opposite particle is at distance 2r. The radial components of forces from two adjacent particles each contribute Gm^2/(sqrt(2)*r)^2 * cos(45°) = Gm^2/(2r^2*sqrt(2)). The force from opposite particle contributes Gm^2/(2r)^2 in radial direction. Total centripetal force: F_c = 2*Gm^2/(2r^2*sqrt(2)) + Gm^2/(4r^2) = Gm^2/r^2 * (1/(r*sqrt(2)) + 1/(2*sqrt(2)) + 1/4) = Gm/r * (2*sqrt(2) + 1)/4. Setting F_c = mv^2/r and solving for v with m = 1 kg, r = 1 m: v = sqrt(G/2 * (2*sqrt(2) + 1)). Simplifying gives v = (1/2)*sqrt(G*(1 + 2*sqrt(2))).
