The maximum speed of a boat in still water is 27 km/h. Now this boat is moving downstream in a river flowing at 9 km/h. A man in the boat throws a ball vertically upwards with speed of 10 m/s. Range of the ball as observed by an observer at rest on the river bank, is ________ cm. (Take g = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 2000

Solution

The boat is moving downstream with velocity of boat with respect to ground: vector v_b = 9 + 27 = 36 km/hr Converting to m/s: vector v_b = 36 × (1000/36000) = 10 m/sec The ball is thrown vertically upwards with speed 10 m/s with respect to the boat. From the reference frame of an observer at rest on the river bank, the ball has: - Vertical component of velocity: 10 m/s (upwards) - Horizontal component of velocity: 10 m/s (downstream, same as boat's velocity) Time of flight (for vertical motion): Using v = u - gt at the highest point, v = 0 0 = 10 - 10t t = 1 sec (time to reach highest point) Total time of flight = 2 × 1 = 2 sec Range (horizontal distance traveled by the ball as seen by observer on bank): Range = horizontal velocity × time of flight Range = 10 × 2 = 20 m = 2000 cm
