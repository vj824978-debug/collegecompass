For the following compounds, the correct statement(s) with respect to nucleophilic substitution reactions is(are) [Compound I: benzene ring with CH2Br substituent] [Compound II: cyclohexane ring with CH2Br substituent] [Compound III: (CH3)3C-Br structure] [Compound IV: benzene ring with CHBr(CH3) substituent]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) [A] I and III follow SN1 mechanism
  2. (B) [B] I and II follow SN2 mechanism
  3. (C) [C] Compound IV undergoes inversion of configuration
  4. (D) [D] The order of reactivity for I, III and IV is: IV > I > III

Answer

Correct answer: ABCD

Solution

Benzylic and 3° halides both follow SN1 mechanism. Benzylic and 1° halides both follow SN2 mechanism. Benzylic 2° halides can undergo inversion of configuration. The order of reactivity would be IV > I > III if both SN1 and SN2 are considered suitably for substrates.
