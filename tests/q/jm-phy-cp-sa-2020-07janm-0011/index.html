The figure gives experimentally measured B vs. H variation in a ferromagnetic material. The retentivity, co-ercivity and saturation, respectively, of the material are -

  1. (1) 1.0 T, 50A/m and 1.5 T
  2. (2) 1.5 T, 50A/m and 1.0 T
  3. (3) 1.5 T, 50A/m and 1.0 T
  4. (4) 150A/m, 1.0 T and 1.5 T

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

From the B vs. H hysteresis curve shown in the figure: 1. Retentivity (Remanence): This is the value of B when H = 0 (the magnetic field is removed). From the graph, when H = 0, B = 1.0 T. 2. Co-ercivity (Coercive field): This is the magnitude of the applied magnetic field H required to reduce the magnetic induction B to zero. From the graph, when B = 0, H = 50 A/m (in magnitude). 3. Saturation magnetization: This is the maximum value of B that the material can achieve. From the graph, the maximum value of B is 1.5 T (at high values of H like around 150 A/m). Therefore: - Retentivity = 1.0 T - Co-ercivity = 50 A/m - Saturation = 1.5 T
