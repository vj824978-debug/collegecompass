Solute A associates in water. When 0.7 g of solute A is dissolved in 42.0 g of water, if depresses the freezing point by 0.2°C. The percentage association of solute A in water is: [Given: Molar mass of A = 93 g mol^(-1). Molal depression constant of water is 1.86 K kg mol^(-1).]

  1. (1) 50%
  2. (2) 60%
  3. (3) 70%
  4. (4) 80%

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Using ΔT_f = i * k_f * m, where m = (0.7/93) × (1000/42). From 0.2 = i × 1.86 × (0.7×1000)/(93×42), we get i = 0.6. Using α = (i-1)/(n-1) where n = 2 (for association), α = (0.6-1)/(1-1) = -0.4/(-0.5) = 0.8 = 80%
