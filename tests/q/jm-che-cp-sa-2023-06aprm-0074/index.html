Compound P with molecular formula C14H13ON is treated with HCl and heat. After filtration, the residue Q gives effervescence with NaHCO3 while the filtrate when treated with NaOH gives an oily liquid R that reacts with Hinsberg's reagent to give a solid soluble in NaOH. Compound P is neutral. Identify P from the given options.

  1. (1) Structure (1): Benzophenone with N-methylamine side chain
  2. (2) Structure (2): Benzamide with N-methyl-N-phenyl substitution
  3. (3) Structure (3): Biphenyl carboxamide with methylamine
  4. (4) Structure (4): para-Methylbenzamide with aniline

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

When P is heated with HCl, the amide bond is hydrolyzed. Residue Q is a carboxylic acid (gives effervescence with NaHCO3). Filtrate R is a primary amine (reacts with Hinsberg's reagent to give solid soluble in NaOH, indicating primary amine). Structure (4) is p-methylbenzamide with aniline, which upon hydrolysis gives p-toluic acid (Q) and aniline (R, primary amine).
