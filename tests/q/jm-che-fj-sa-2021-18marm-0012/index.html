A compound with molecular formula C8H10O2 containing a methyl group and a methoxy group on a benzene ring is oxidized with alkaline KMnO4 followed by acidification with H+. Identify the product X.

  1. (1) X = 4-methoxybenzoic aldehyde (CHO group and OCH3 group on benzene ring)
  2. (2) X = 4-methoxybenzoic acid (COOH group and OCH3 group on benzene ring)
  3. (3) X = methylphenol (CH3 group and OH group on benzene ring)
  4. (4) X = 4-methoxybenzyl alcohol (CH2OH group and OCH3 group on benzene ring)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The starting material is 4-methyl-4-methoxybenzene (para-CH3 and OCH3 on benzene ring). Under alkaline KMnO4, the benzylic methyl group (CH3) is oxidized to carboxylic acid (COOH). The methoxy group (OCH3) remains unchanged as it is not oxidized under these conditions. After acidification with H+, the product is 4-methoxybenzoic acid with COOH and OCH3 groups on the benzene ring.
