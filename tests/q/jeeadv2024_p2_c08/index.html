To form a complete monolayer of acetic acid on 1g of charcoal, 100 mL of 0.5 M acetic acid was used. Some of the acetic acid remained unadsorbed. To neutralize the unadsorbed acetic acid, 40 mL of 1 M NaOH solution was required. If each molecule of acetic acid occupies P×10^-29 m² surface area on charcoal, the value of P is _____. [Use given data: Surface area of charcoal = 1.5×10² m²g⁻¹; Avogadro's number (N_A) = 6.0×10²³ mol⁻¹]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 2500

Solution

Number of moles of NaOH = 40 × 1 × 10⁻³ = 4 × 10⁻² Number of moles of Acetic acid = 100 × 0.5 × 10⁻³ = 5 × 10⁻² ∴ Number of moles of acetic acid adsorbed on charcoal = 5 × 10⁻² - 4 × 10⁻² = 1 × 10⁻² ∴ Number of molecules of acetic acid adsorbed = 1 × 10⁻² × Nₐ = 6 × 10²¹ Total surface area adsorbed by one molecule of acetic acid = (1.5 × 10²)/(6 × 10²¹) = 2500 × 10⁻²³ ∴ P = 2500
