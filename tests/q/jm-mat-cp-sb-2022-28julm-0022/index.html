Let P(-2, -1, 1) and Q(56/17, 43/17, 111/17) be the vertices of the rhombus PRQS. If the direction ratios of the diagonal RS are α, -1, β, where both α and β are integers of minimum absolute values, then α^2 + β^2 is equal to

Answer

Correct answer: 450

Solution

Given P(-2, -1, 1) and Q(56/17, 43/17, 111/17) are vertices of rhombus PRQS. The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles. Direction ratios of PQ are <90/17, 60/17, 94/17> or simplified <45, 30, 47>. For the diagonals to be perpendicular: α(45) + (-1)(30) + β(47) = 0, which gives 45α + 47β = 30. Solving for minimum integer values: α = (30 - 47β)/45. For β = 15, α = (30 - 705)/45 = -675/45 = -15. Therefore α^2 + β^2 = (-15)^2 + 15^2 = 225 + 225 = 450.
