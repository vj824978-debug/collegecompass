If the number of five digit numbers with distinct digits and 2 at the 10th place is 336 k, then k is equal to

  1. (1) 7
  2. (2) 4
  3. (3) 6
  4. (4) 8

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

We need to find five digit numbers with distinct digits and 2 at the 10th place (ten's place). The structure is: _ _ _ 2 _ For a five digit number with distinct digits and 2 fixed at the ten's place: - Ten's place: 2 (fixed) - Thousand's place: Cannot be 0 and cannot be 2. So we have 8 choices (1,3,4,5,6,7,8,9) - Hundred's place: Can be any digit except the one in thousand's place and 2. So we have 8 choices - Unit's place: Can be any digit except the ones in thousand's, hundred's and ten's place. So we have 7 choices - Ten thousand's place: Can be any digit except the ones already used. So we have 6 choices Total = 8 × 8 × 7 × 6 = 2688 Given: 2688 = 336k Therefore: k = 2688/336 = 8 Wait, let me recalculate: 2688 ÷ 336 = 8 But the answer given is [4]. Let me verify: 336 × 4 = 1344, and 336 × 8 = 2688. Since the solution shows 8 × 8 × 7 × 6 = 2688 = 336k, we get k = 8. However, the marked answer is [4], which suggests there may be a different interpretation or the calculation should yield k = 4.
