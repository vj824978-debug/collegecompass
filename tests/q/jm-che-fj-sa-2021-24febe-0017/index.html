According to Bohr's atomic theory: (A) Kinetic energy of electron is proportional to Z^2/n^2. (B) The product of velocity (v) of electron and principal quantum number (n) is proportional to Z^2. (C) Frequency of revolution of electron in an orbit is proportional to Z^2/n^3. (D) Coulombic force of attraction on the electron is proportional to Z^2/n^4. Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

  1. (1) (A) and (D) only
  2. (2) A only
  3. (3) (A), (C) and (D) only
  4. (4) (C) only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

According to Bohr's theory: K.E = 13.6 Z^2/n^2 (confirms A is correct). Frequency of revolution is proportional to Z^2/n^3 (confirms C is correct). Coulombic force F = kq1q2/r^2 = Kze^2/r^2, and with r proportional to n^2/Z, we get F proportional to Z^2/n^4 (confirms D is correct). For option B: v = 2.18 × 10^6 Z/n, so v is proportional to Z/n, not Z^2. Therefore options A, C, and D are correct.
