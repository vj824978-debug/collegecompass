A convex lens of refractive index 1.5 and focal length 18 cm in air is immersed in water. The change in focal length of the lens will be ________ cm. (Given refractive index of water = 4/3)

Answer

Correct answer: 54

Solution

From lens makers formula: 1/f = (μ_lens/μ_medium - 1)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] When in air: 1/18 = (1.5/1 - 1)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] 1/18 = (0.5)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] [1/R_1 - 1/R_2] = 1/9 ......(1) μ_lens = 1.5, μ_air = 1 When in water: 1/f' = (1.5/(4/3) - 1)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] 1/f' = (1.5 × 3/4 - 1)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] 1/f' = (9/8 - 1)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] 1/f' = (1/8)[1/R_1 - 1/R_2] 1/f' = (1/8) × (1/9) [using equation (1)] 1/f' = 1/72 f' = 72 cm Change in focal length = f' - f = 72 - 18 = 54 cm
