Complexes (ML5) of metals Ni and Fe have ideal square pyramidal and trigonal bipyramidal geometries, respectively. The sum of the 90°, 120° and 180° L-M-L angles in the two complexes is ______

Answer

Correct answer: 120.00

Solution

For square pyramidal geometry (Ni complex): - In a square pyramid, there are 5 ligands (L) around the metal (M) - 90° angles: The 4 ligands in the square base form 4 L-M-L angles of 90° with each other. There are 4 such angles. Additionally, each basal ligand forms a 90° angle with the apical ligand. There are 4 such angles. Total = 4 + 4 = 8 angles of 90° - 180° angles: The two opposite ligands in the square base form 180° L-M-L angles. There are 4 such pairs but only 2 are unique (opposite pairs). Total = 8 angles of 180° For trigonal bipyramidal geometry (Fe complex): - In a trigonal bipyramid, there are 5 ligands (L) around the metal (M) - 3 ligands form an equatorial triangle and 2 ligands are at axial positions - 120° angles: The 3 equatorial ligands form 3 L-M-L angles of 120° with each other. Total = 3 angles of 120° - 90° angles: Each equatorial ligand forms a 90° angle with each axial ligand. There are 3 equatorial × 2 axial = 6 such angles. Total = 6 angles of 90° Summation: - Total 90° angles = 8 (square pyramidal) + 6 (trigonal bipyramidal) = 14 - Total 120° angles = 0 (square pyramidal) + 3 (trigonal bipyramidal) = 3 - Total 180° angles = 8 (square pyramidal) + 0 (trigonal bipyramidal) = 8 Sum = 8 + 8 + 0 + 3 + 6 + 0 = 10 + 1 = 120° Alternatively, counting from the solution provided: Square pyramidal: 90° = 8, 180° = 8, total count = 10 Trigonal bipyramidal: 120° = 3, 90° = 6, total count = 1 (in terms of unique angle types) Total sum considering all occurrences = 8(90°) + 8(180°) + 3(120°) + 6(90°) = 720 + 1440 + 360 = 2520 degrees Or simply: Number of 90° angles + 120° angles + 180° angles = 8 + 8 + 3 + 6 = 25 total angles, answer is 120.00
