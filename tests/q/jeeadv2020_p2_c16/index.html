Consider the reaction sequence from P to Q shown below. The overall yield of the major product Q from P is 75%. What is the amount in grams of Q obtained from 9.3 mL of P? (Use density of P = 1.00 g mL⁻¹; Molar mass of C = 12.0, H = 1.0, O = 16.0 and N = 14.0 g mol⁻¹) [Chemical reaction scheme showing: Starting material P is aniline (benzene ring with NH2 group). Step (i): NaNO2 + HCl / 0-5°C. Step (ii): A naphthalene structure with OH group + NaOH. Step (iii): CH3CO2H/H2O. Product Q is formed.] What is the amount in grams of Q obtained from 9.3 mL of P?

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 18.60

Solution

Sol. 18.60 [Reaction scheme showing: - Starting material P: aniline (C6H5-NH2, Molar mass = 93) - Step 1: (i) NaNO2, HCl at 0°-5°C (diazotisation) produces diazonium salt (C6H5-N=N-Cl) - Step 2: (ii) Coupling with naphthol-1-ol in basic conditions produces azo dye (C6H5-N=N-C10H6OH) - Step 3: (iii) Treatment with CH3COOH(aq) (protonation) produces final product Q - Final product Q: C6H5-N=N-C10H6OH with Molar mass = 248] Moles of P = 9.3/93 = 0.1 = moles of Q. So, mass of (Q) = 0.1 × 248 × 0.75 = 18.60 gram
