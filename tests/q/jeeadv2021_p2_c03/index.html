For the following reaction 2X + Y →^k P the rate of reaction is d[P]/dt = k[X]. Two moles of X are mixed with one mole of Y to make 1.0 L of solution. At 50 s, 0.5 mole of Y is left in the reaction mixture. The correct statement(s) about the reaction is(are) (Use: ln 2 = 0.693)

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The rate constant, k, of the reaction is 13.86×10^-4 s^-1
  2. (B) Half-life of X is 50s
  3. (C) At 50 s, -d[X]/dt = 13.86×10^-3 mol L^-1 s^-1
  4. (D) At 100 s, -d[Y]/dt = 3.46×10^-3 mol L^-1 s^-1

Answer

Correct answer: BCD

Solution

2X + y → P t = 0: 2, 1, 0 t = 50: 2 - 2×0.5, 1 - 0.5, 0 1 mole, 0.5 rate = -1/2 dx/dt = -dy/dt = dP/dt = K[X] -1/2 dx/dt = K[X] dx/dt = 2K[X] = K'[X] Half life is t = 50 sec 2K = 0.653 L/50 K = 0.6932/100 = 6.332×10⁻³ t = 50 sec dx/dt = 2K[X] dx/dt = 2 × 6.332×10⁻³ × 1 = 13.864×10⁻³ mole/L/Sec dy/dt = K[X] = 6.332×10⁻³ (1/2) = 3.46×10⁻³ mole/L/Sec⁻¹
