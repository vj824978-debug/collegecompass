Major product of the following reaction is: Cl-C(=O)-CCl + H2N-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-NH2 with (1) Et3N and (1) Free radical polymerisation

  1. (1) Polymer with Cl and N-H groups in backbone with side chain C(=O)-NH2
  2. (2) Polymer with Cl and N-H groups in backbone with side chain C(=O)-NH2 attached to different carbon
  3. (3) Polymer with Cl, C=O and N-H groups with side chain NH2
  4. (4) Polymer with N-H linker and side chain NH2, with Cl and C=O in backbone

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction involves: 1) Initial condensation reaction: - The amine (H2N-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-NH2) acts as nucleophile - Et3N (triethylamine) is used as base to deprotonate the amine - The NH2 group attacks the acid chloride (acyl chloride) - One Cl is eliminated as chloride - This gives: Cl-C(=O)-NH-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-NH2 - This is a better nucleophile due to the N-H group 2) Free radical polymerization: - The C=C double bond (from the acrylic acid derivative) undergoes free radical addition polymerization - The monomers link through the C=C bonds - The N-H and other functional groups remain as part of the backbone or side chains 3) The resulting polymer has: - Cl atoms in the backbone - N-H amide linkages in the backbone - Primary amide side chains (-C(=O)-NH2) Therefore, option (1) is the major product.
