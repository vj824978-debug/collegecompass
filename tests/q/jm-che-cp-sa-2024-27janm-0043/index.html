Which of the following has highly acidic hydrogen?

  1. (1) H3C-C(=O)-CH(CH3)-C(=O)
  2. (2) H3C-C(=O)-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-CH3
  3. (3) H3C-C(=O)-C-C-C-CH3 with double bonded O on first C
  4. (4) H3C-C(=O)-CH2-C(=O)-CH2-CH3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

After removal of H+ ion, negative charge is formed. More stable the negative charge, more acidic the hydrogen. In compound (4), the methylene group (-CH2-) between two carbonyl groups is an active methylene group. When deprotonated by base, the resulting carbanion is stabilized by cross-conjugation with both adjacent carbonyl groups (C=O). This resonance stabilization of the negative charge makes the hydrogen most acidic. Therefore, compound (4) is most acidic.
