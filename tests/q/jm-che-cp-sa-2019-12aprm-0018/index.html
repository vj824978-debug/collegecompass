NO2 required for a reaction is produced by the decomposition of N2O5 in CCl4 as per the equation, 2N2O5(g) → 4NO2(g) + O2(g). The initial concentration of N2O5 is 3.00 mol L^(-1) and it is 2.75 mol L^(-1) after 30 minutes. The rate of formation of NO2 is:

  1. (1) 2.083 × 10^(-3) mol L^(-1) min^(-1)
  2. (2) 8.333 × 10^(-3) mol L^(-1) min^(-1)
  3. (3) 4.167 × 10^(-3) mol L^(-1) min^(-1)
  4. (4) 1.667 × 10^(-2) mol L^(-1) min^(-1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

From the balanced equation: 2N2O5(g) → 4NO2(g) + O2(g) The relationship between rates of reaction is: (1/2) × d[N2O5]/dt = (1/4) × d[NO2]/dt Therefore: d[NO2]/dt = -2 × d[N2O5]/dt Change in concentration of N2O5: Δ[N2O5] = 2.75 - 3.00 = -0.25 mol L^(-1) Rate of decomposition of N2O5: d[N2O5]/dt = -0.25/30 mol L^(-1) min^(-1) Rate of formation of NO2: d[NO2]/dt = -2 × (-0.25/30) d[NO2]/dt = 2 × 0.25/30 d[NO2]/dt = 0.5/30 d[NO2]/dt = (2 × 0.25)/30 = 1.667 × 10^(-2) mol L^(-1) min^(-1)
