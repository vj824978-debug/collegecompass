The K_sp for the following dissociation is 1.6 × 10^(-5): PbCl_2(s) ⇌ Pb^(2+)_(aq) + 2Cl^(-)_(aq). Which of the following choices is correct for a mixture of 300 mL 0.134 M Pb(NO_3)_2 and 100 mL 0.4 M NaCl?

  1. (1) Not enough data provided
  2. (2) Q > K_sp
  3. (3) Q < K_sp
  4. (4) Q = K_sp

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Q = [Pb^(2+)][Cl^(-)]^2 Calculate initial concentrations after mixing: [Pb^(2+)] = (300 × 0.134)/400 = (3 × 0.134)/4 [Cl^(-)] = (100 × 0.4)/400 = 0.1 M Q = ((3 × 0.134)/4) × (0.1)^2 Q = (0.402/4) × (0.01) Q = (3 × 0.134)/4 × (0.1)^2 Q = 1.005 × 10^(-3) Comparing with K_sp = 1.6 × 10^(-5): Q = 1.005 × 10^(-3) > K_sp = 1.6 × 10^(-5) Therefore Q > K_sp, which means precipitation will occur.
