Considering a group of positive charges, which of the following statements is correct?

  1. (1) Net potential of the system cannot be zero at a point but net electric field can be zero at that point.
  2. (2) Both the net potential and the net field can be zero at a point.
  3. (3) Net potential of the system at a point can be zero but net electric field can't be zero at that point.
  4. (4) Both the net potential and the net field cannot be zero at a point.

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

V = (Σ K*Q_i)/r_i Here, Q_i & r_i are positive ∴ V > 0 Since all charges are positive and the potential due to each charge is positive (V = KQ/r with Q > 0 and r > 0), the net potential at any point must always be positive and cannot be zero. However, the electric field is a vector quantity. The net electric field can be zero at a point due to vector addition even when all charges are positive. The electric fields from different charges can point in different directions and cancel out at a particular point. Therefore, statement (1) is correct: Net potential of the system cannot be zero at a point but net electric field can be zero at that point.
