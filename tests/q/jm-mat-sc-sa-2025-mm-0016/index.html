The probability of forming a 12 persons committee from 4 engineers, 2 doctors and 10 professors containing at least 3 engineers and at least 1 doctor, is

  1. (1) 129/182
  2. (2) 17/26
  3. (3) 19/26
  4. (4) 103/182

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Total ways to select 12 from 16 people = C(16,12). For favorable outcomes with at least 3 engineers and at least 1 doctor: Consider cases - (3 engineers, 1 doctor, 8 professors) + (4 engineers, 1 doctor, 7 professors) + (4 engineers, 2 doctors, 6 professors) + (3 engineers, 2 doctors, 7 professors). Calculate: C(4,3)×C(2,1)×C(10,8) + C(4,4)×C(2,1)×C(10,7) + C(4,4)×C(2,2)×C(10,6) + C(4,3)×C(2,2)×C(10,7) = 180 + 240 + 210 + 840 = 1470. Total = C(16,12) = 1820. Probability = 1470/1820 = 147/182 (simplified). Check against options.
