A particle of mass m moves in a circular orbit in a central potential field U(r) = (1/2)kr^2. If Bohr's quantization conditions are applied, radii of possible orbits and energy levels vary with quantum number n as:

  1. (1) r_n ∝ sqrt(n), E_n ∝ n
  2. (2) r_n ∝ sqrt(n), E_n ∝ 1/n
  3. (3) r_n ∝ n, E_n ∝ n
  4. (4) r_n ∝ n^2, E_n ∝ 1/n^2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

U = (1/2)kr^2 Centripetal force F = -dU/dr = -(1/2)k(2r) = -kr F = kr = mv^2/r mv^2 = kr^2 Bohr's quantization condition: mVr = nh/(2π) => V = nh/(2π(mr)) Substituting in mv^2 = kr^2: m[nh/(2π(mr))]^2 = kr^2 m(n^2h^2)/(4π^2m^2r^2) = kr^2 (n^2h^2)/(4π^2mr^2) = kr^2 r^4 ∝ n^2 => r ∝ sqrt(n) Energy ∝ r^2 E ∝ r^2 E ∝ n
