Consider the Assertion and Reason given below. Assertion (A): Ethane polymerized in the presence of Ziegler Natta catalyst at high temperature and pressure is used to make buckets and dustbins. Reason (R): High density polymers are closely packed and are chemically inert.

  1. (1) Both (A) and (R) are correct and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
  2. (2) Both (A) and (R) are correct but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
  3. (3) (A) is correct but (R) is wrong
  4. (4) (A) and (R) both are wrong

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

Assertion (A) is correct: Ethane undergoes polymerization in the presence of Ziegler Natta catalyst (also known as Ziegler-Natta catalyst) at high temperature and pressure to form polyethylene. High-density polyethylene (HDPE) produced this way is indeed used to manufacture buckets, dustbins, and other rigid containers due to its strength and durability. Reason (R) is correct: High density polymers like HDPE are tightly packed with strong intermolecular forces, giving them high density. They are also chemically inert due to the strong C-C and C-H bonds, which make them resistant to corrosion and chemical attack. Connection between (A) and (R): The reason correctly explains why ethane polymerized via Ziegler Natta catalyst is suitable for making buckets and dustbins. The process at high temperature and pressure produces high-density polyethylene, which possesses the properties mentioned in (R) - close packing and chemical inertness. These properties make it ideal for storing substances in buckets and dustbins as it won't react with the contents and is mechanically strong. Therefore, both (A) and (R) are correct, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
