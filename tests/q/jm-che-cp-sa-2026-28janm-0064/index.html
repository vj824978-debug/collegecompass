In the given pentapeptide, find out an essential amino acid (Y) and the sequence present in the pentapeptide: H Q H H Q H H Q H H Q H H with structure shown where H3N-C-C-N-C-C-N-C-C-N-C-C-N-C-COOH with side chains HO and CH3, OH, COOH H, CH3

  1. (1) (Y): Threonine, (Sequence): Ser - Thr - Asp - Gly - Ala
  2. (2) (Y): Serine, (Sequence): Thr - Ser - Asp - Ala - Gly
  3. (3) (Y): Threonine, (Sequence): Thr - Ser - Asp - Gly - Ala
  4. (4) (Y): Serine, (Sequence): Ser - Asp - Thr - Ala - Gly

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

To identify the amino acids in the pentapeptide, we need to analyze the structure from N-terminus to C-terminus. Analyzing the side chains shown in the pentapeptide structure: 1. First residue: HO and CH3 side chain = Threonine (Thr) 2. Second residue: CH3 side chain = Alanine (Ala) - No, looking at the pattern From the hydrolysis products shown in the solution: - Threonine: NH2-CH-COOH with CH(HO)CH3 side chain - Serine: NH2-CH-COOH with CH2-OH side chain - Aspartic acid: NH2-CH-COOH with CH2-COOH side chain - Glycine: NH2-CH2-COOH - Alanine: NH2-CH-COOH with CH3 side chain Reading the pentapeptide structure from left (N-terminus) to right (C-terminus): 1. HO and CH3 = Threonine 2. CH3 = Alanine - but checking again the second position shows CH3 only = Alanine... wait, re-examining: position 2 shows only CH3 3. OH group = Serine 4. COOH side chain = Aspartic acid 5. CH3 = Alanine - but the C-terminus shows different arrangement Correct sequence reading from N to C terminus: Thr - Ser - Asp - Gly - Ala The essential amino acid among these is Threonine (Thr), as it is one of the essential amino acids that cannot be synthesized by the human body. Answer: (Y) = Threonine, Sequence = Thr - Ser - Asp - Gly - Ala
