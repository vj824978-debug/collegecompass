A submarine (A) travelling at 18 km/hr is being chased along the line of its velocity by another submarine (B) travelling at 27 km/hr. B sends a sonar signal of 500 Hz to detect A and receives a reflected sound of frequency v. The value of v is close to: (Speed of sound in water = 1500 ms^(-1))

  1. (1) 507 Hz
  2. (2) 504 Hz
  3. (3) 499 Hz
  4. (4) 502 Hz

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given: - Submarine A velocity: V_A = 18 km/hr = 5 m/s - Submarine B velocity: V_B = 27 km/hr = 7.5 m/s - Initial frequency: f_0 = 500 Hz - Speed of sound in water: v = 1500 m/s This is a two-step Doppler effect problem: Step 1: Frequency received by A from B (B is source, A is observer) f = [V - V_A]/[V - V_B] × f_0 = [1500 - 5]/[1500 - 7.5] × f_0 Step 2: Frequency received by B from A (A acts as source reflecting the sound, B is observer) f' = [V + V_B]/[V + V_A] × f = [1500 + 7.5]/[1500 + 5] × [1500 - 5]/[1500 - 7.5] × f_0 f' = [(1500 + 7.5)/(1500 - 7.5)] × [(1500 - 5)/(1500 + 5)] × (500) f' = [1507.5/1492.5] × [1495/1505] × (500) f' = (1507.5 × 1495)/(1492.5 × 1505) × 500 f' = 502 Hz
