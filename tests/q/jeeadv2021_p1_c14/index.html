An ideal gas undergoes a reversible isothermal expansion from state I to state II followed by a reversible adiabatic expansion from state II to state III. The correct plot(s) representing the changes from state I to state III is(are) (p: pressure, V: volume, T: temperature, H: enthalpy, S: entropy) [Four plots shown: (A) p vs V plot showing curve from point I to II to III, decreasing pressure and increasing volume (B) p vs T plot showing curve from point I down to II then down to III (C) H vs S plot showing point I, horizontal arrow to point II, then vertical arrow up to point III (D) T vs S plot showing point I, horizontal arrow to point II, then downward arrow to point III]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) p vs V plot with I→II→III showing decreasing pressure with increasing volume
  2. (B) p vs T plot with I→II→III showing both pressure and temperature decreasing
  3. (C) H vs S plot with I→II showing constant enthalpy, then II→III showing increasing entropy at constant enthalpy
  4. (D) T vs S plot with I→II showing constant temperature and increasing entropy, then II→III showing decreasing temperature at constant entropy

Answer

Correct answer: ABD

Solution

So, the correct option is/are: (A) [P-V diagram showing three connected states: (I) at high pressure curving down as volume increases (reversible isothermal expansion), (II) at medium pressure, and (III) at lower pressure (reversible adiabatic expansion)] (B) [P-T diagram showing: (I) at high temperature and pressure (reversible isothermal expansion), (II) at same pressure but lower temperature, and (III) at lower pressure and temperature (reversible adiabatic expansion)] (D) [H-S diagram showing: (I) and (II) at same enthalpy H with arrow between them pointing right (reversible isothermal expansion), and (III) below at lower enthalpy (reversible adiabatic expansion)] Reversible isothermal process is isenthalpic while reversible adiabatic process is isentropic.
