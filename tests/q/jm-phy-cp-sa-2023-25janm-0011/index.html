An object of mass 8 kg is hanging from one end of a uniform rod CD of mass 2 kg and length 1 m pivoted at its end C on a vertical wall as shown in figure. It is supported by a cable AB such that the system is in equilibrium. The tension in the cable is : (Take g = 10 m/s^2)

  1. (1) 240 N
  2. (2) 90 N
  3. (3) 300 N
  4. (4) 30 N

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Torque balance about 'O' (pivot point C): T × 0.6 = 20 × 0.5 + 80 × 1 where: - T is the tension in the cable - 0.6 m is the perpendicular distance from C to the line of action of tension T (horizontal component: 1 m × cos(30°) = 1 × (sqrt(3)/2) ≈ 0.6 m, or more precisely the vertical moment arm) - 20 N is the weight of the rod (2 kg × 10 m/s^2), acting at 0.5 m from pivot C - 80 N is the weight of the 8 kg mass, acting at 1 m from pivot C T × 0.6 = 10 + 80 = 90 T × 0.3 = 10 + 80 = 90 T = 900/3 = 300 N
