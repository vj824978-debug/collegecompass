Let the six numbers a₁, a₂, a₃, a₄, a₅, a₆ be in A.P. and a₁ + a₃ = 10. If the mean of these six numbers is 19/2 and their variance is σ², then 8σ² is equal to:

  1. (1) 200
  2. (2) 105
  3. (3) 220
  4. (4) 210

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Given: a₁, a₂, a₃, a₄, a₅, a₆ are in A.P. and a₁ + a₃ = 10 Let the common difference be d then, 2a₁ + 2d = 10 ∴ a₁ + d = 5 ...(i) and (a₁ + a₂ + ... + a₆)/6 = 19/2 ⇒ 2a₁ + 5d = 19 ...(ii) By (i) and (ii) d = 3 and a₁ = 2 ∴ a₂ = 5, a₃ = 8, a₄ = 11, a₅ = 14, a₆ = 17 ∴ Variance = 233/2 - 361/4 = 105/4 = σ² ∴ 8σ = 210
