Let alpha_0 and beta_0 be the distinct roots of 2x^2 + (cos(theta))x - 1 = 0, theta in (0, 2pi). If m and M are the minimum and the maximum values of alpha_0^4 + beta_0^4, then 16(M + m) equals.

  1. (1) 24
  2. (2) 25
  3. (3) 27
  4. (4) 17

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

(alpha^2 + beta^2)^2 - 2alpha^2*beta^2 [(alpha + beta)^2 - 2alpha*beta]^2 - 2(alpha*beta)^2 [(cos(theta)/4 + 1]^2 - 2. 1/4 (cos^2(theta)/4 + 1)^2 - 1/2 M = 25/16 - 1/2 = 17/16 m = 1/2, 16(M + m) = 25
