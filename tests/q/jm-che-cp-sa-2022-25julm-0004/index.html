20 mL of 0.1 M NH4OH is mixed with 40 mL of 0.05 M HCl. The pH of the mixture is nearest to (Given: Kb(NH4OH) = 1 × 10^(-5), log2 = 0.30, log3 = 0.48, log5 = 0.69, log7 = 0.84, log11 = 1.04)

  1. (1) 3.2
  2. (2) 4.2
  3. (3) 5.2
  4. (4) 6.2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

NH4OH + HCl → NH4Cl + H2O. Moles of NH4OH = 20 × 0.1/1000 = 2 mmol. Moles of HCl = 40 × 0.05/1000 = 2 mmol. All HCl reacts, forming 2 mmol NH4Cl in final 60 mL solution. [NH4Cl] = 2/60 = 1/30 molar. Using pH = (1/2)[pKa - pkb - logC] where pKa = 14 - 5 = 9, pkb = 5, logC = log(1/30) = -(log3 + log10) = -(0.48 + 1) = -1.48. pH = (1/2)[14 - 5 - (-1.48)] = (1/2)[9 + 1.48] = (1/2)[10.48] ≈ 5.24, nearest to 5.2
