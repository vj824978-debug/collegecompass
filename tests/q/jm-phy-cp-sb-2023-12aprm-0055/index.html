A compass needle oscillates 20 times per minute at a place where the dip is 30° and 30 times per minute where the dip is 60°. The ratio of total magnetic field due to the earth at two places respectively is 4/sqrt(x). The value of x is

Answer

Correct answer: 243

Solution

Using T = 2*pi*sqrt(1/(MB_H)) where B_H is horizontal component of earth's magnetic field. B_H = B*cos(theta) where theta is dip angle. For place 1: dip = 30°, frequency = 20 oscillations/min. For place 2: dip = 60°, frequency = 30 oscillations/min. T1/T2 = 20/30 = 2/3. From T formula: sqrt(B2*cos(60°))/(B1*cos(30°)) = 2/3. sqrt(B2*(1/2))/(B1*(sqrt(3)/2)) = 2/3. (B1/B2)*sqrt(3) = 4/9. B1/B2 = 4/(9*sqrt(3)) = 4/sqrt(243). Therefore x = 243.
