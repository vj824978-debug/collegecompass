Consider the following reactions: 'A' (C7H14) undergoes Ozonolysis to give 'B' + 'C'. 'B' with (I2 + NaOH) gives Yellow ppt and with Ag2O gives Silver mirror. 'C' with (I2 + NaOH) gives No yellow ppt and with LiAlH4 gives 'D', which with Anhydrous ZnCl2 & Conc.HCl gives white turbidity within 5 minutes. Identify 'A' from the given options (1), (2), (3), (4).

  1. (1) CH3-CH=CH-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH3
  2. (2) CH3-C(=CH2)-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH3
  3. (3) CH3-CH=CH-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH3
  4. (4) CH3-CH(CH3)-CH=CH-CH2-CH3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Analysis of the reactions: 1) 'B' gives yellow ppt with (I2 + NaOH) → 'B' is an aldehyde (CHO group) 2) 'B' gives silver mirror with Ag2O → Confirms 'B' is an aldehyde 3) 'C' gives no yellow ppt with (I2 + NaOH) → 'C' is not an aldehyde, must be a ketone 4) 'C' with LiAlH4 gives 'D' → 'D' is an alcohol (from reduction of ketone) 5) 'D' with anhydrous ZnCl2 & Conc.HCl gives white turbidity within 5 minutes → 'D' is a secondary alcohol (forms alkene/turbidity via dehydration) From ozonolysis of 'A' (C7H14): - 'B' is an aldehyde - 'C' is a ketone that gives secondary alcohol when reduced For option (3): CH3-CH=CH-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH3 Ozonolysis breaks the double bond: - Gives CH3-CHO (aldehyde - 'B') ✓ Yellow ppt with I2/NaOH, silver mirror ✓ - Gives CH3-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH3 with C=O (ketone - 'C') ✓ No yellow ppt ✓ - Reduction gives CH3-CH(OH)-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH3 (secondary alcohol - 'D') ✓ White turbidity ✓ All observations match option (3).
