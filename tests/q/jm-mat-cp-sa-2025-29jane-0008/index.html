Let the line x + y = 1 meet the axes of x and y at A and B, respectively. A right angled triangle AMN is inscribed in the triangle OAB, where O is the origin and the points M and N lie on the lines OB and AB, respectively. If the area of the triangle AMN is 4/9 of the area of the triangle OAB and AN : NB = λ : 1, then the sum of all possible value(s) of λ is:

  1. (1) 1/2
  2. (2) 13/6
  3. (3) 5/2
  4. (4) 2

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Area of triangle OAB = 1/2 Area of triangle AMN = (4/9) × (1/2) = 2/9 Equation of AB is x + y = 1 OA = 1, AM = sec(45° - θ) AN = sec(45° - θ) cos θ MN = sec(45° - θ) sin θ Ar(triangle AMN) = (1/2) sec²(45° - θ) sin θ cos θ = 2/9 => tan θ = 2, 1/2 tan θ = 2 is rejected AN/NB = λ/1 = cot θ = 2 From the condition that the area ratio is 4/9 and analyzing the geometric constraints: When tan θ = 1/2, we get cot θ = 2, so λ = 2 Alternatively, considering different configurations and the ratio constraint AN : NB = λ : 1: The possible values of λ satisfy the area and geometric conditions. Sum of all possible values of λ = 2
