Which of the following graphs between pressure 'P' versus volume 'V' represent the maximum work done?

  1. (1) Graph (1): Curved decrease from P=2.0 bar at V=0 to P=1.0 bar at V=22.4, then to lower pressure at V=44.8
  2. (2) Graph (2): Rectangular path with constant P=2.0 bar from V=0 to V=22.4, then vertical drop to P=0 at V=22.4
  3. (3) Graph (3): Curved decrease from P=2.0 bar at V=0 to P=1.0 bar at V=22.4, extending to V=44.8
  4. (4) Graph (4): Curved decrease from P=2.0 bar at V=0 to P=1.0 bar at V=22.4, then constant P=1.0 bar to V=44.8

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Work done in a thermodynamic process equals the area under the P-V curve. For maximum work, we need the largest enclosed area. Graph (4) has a combination of a curved path (from 2.0 to 1.0 bar over 0-22.4 L) followed by a constant pressure path (at 1.0 bar from 22.4-44.8 L), which encloses the maximum area compared to other options. Option (2) has zero work done as volume remains constant initially. Options (1) and (3) have smaller areas than option (4).
