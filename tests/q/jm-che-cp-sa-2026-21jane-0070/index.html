On heating a mixture of common salt and K2Cr2O7 in equal amount along with concentrated H2SO4 in a test tube, a gas is evolved. Formula of the gas evolved and oxidation state of the central metal atom in the gas respectively are:

  1. (1) CrO2Cl2 and +5
  2. (2) CrO2Cl2 and +6
  3. (3) Cr2O2Cl2 and +6
  4. (4) Cr2O2Cl2 and +3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

4NaCl + K2Cr2O7 + 6H2SO4 → 2KHSO4 + 2CrO2Cl2 + 4NaHSO4 + 3H2O The gas evolved is chromyl chloride (CrO2Cl2). In chromyl chloride, Cr is in +6 oxidation state. Verification of oxidation state: In CrO2Cl2: Let oxidation state of Cr = x x + 2(-2) + 2(-1) = 0 x - 4 - 2 = 0 x = +6 Therefore, the gas evolved is CrO2Cl2 and the central metal atom Cr is in +6 oxidation state.
