A group of 9 students s₁, s₂, ..., s₉ is to be divided to form three teams X, Y and Z of sizes 2, 3 and 4 respectively. Suppose that s₁ cannot be selected for the team X, and s₂ cannot be selected for team Y. Then the number of ways to form such teams, is _____.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 665

Solution

Case-1: S₂ is in team X and S₁ is in team Y, ⁷C₁ × ⁶C₂ × ⁴C₄ = 105 Case-2: S₂ is in team X but S₁ is not in team Y, ⁷C₂ × ⁵C₂ × ³C₃ = 140 Case-3: S₂ is not in team X but S₁ is in team Y, ⁷C₂ × ⁵C₂ × ³C₃ = 210 Case-4: S₂ is not in team X and S₁ is not in team Y, ⁷C₂ × ⁵C₃ × ²C₂ = 210 ∴ Total ways to form such teams = 105 + 140 + 210 + 210 = 665
