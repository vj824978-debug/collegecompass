Ice at -5°C is heated to become vapor with temperature of 110°C at atmospheric pressure. The entropy change associated with this process can be obtained from:

  1. (1) ∫[268K to 383K] Cp dT + ΔH_melting/273 + ΔH_boiling/373
  2. (2) ∫[268K to 273K] Cp,m dT/T + ΔH_m,fusion/Tf + ΔH_m,vaporisation/Tb + ∫[273K to 373K] Cp,m dT/T + ∫[373K to 383K] Cp,m dT/T
  3. (3) ∫[268K to 383K] Cp dT + q_rev/T
  4. (4) ∫[268K to 273K] Cp,m dT/T + ΔH_m,fusion/Tf + ΔH_m,vaporisation/Tb + ∫[273K to 373K] Cp,m dT + ∫[373K to 383K] Cp,m dT

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The process involves five steps: Ice → Ice ⇌ Water → Water ⇌ Water vapor → Water vapor 268 K 273 K 273 K 373 K 373 K 383 K (1) (2) (3) (4) (5) ΔS_overall = ΔS₁ + ΔS₂ + ΔS₃ + ΔS₄ + ΔS₅ Step 1: Heating ice from 268 K to 273 K ΔS₁ = ∫[268K to 273K] Cp,m dT/T Step 2: Melting at Tf = 273 K ΔS₂ = ΔH_m,fusion/273 Step 3: Heating water from 273 K to 373 K ΔS₃ = ∫[273K to 373K] Cp,m dT/T Step 4: Vaporization at Tb = 373 K ΔS₄ = ΔH_m,vaporisation/373 Step 5: Heating water vapor from 373 K to 383 K ΔS₅ = ∫[373K to 383K] Cp,m dT/T For entropy change, since phase transitions occur at constant temperature and heating occurs in a reversible manner, we use dS = dq_rev/T. For heating: dS = Cp dT/T, and for phase transitions: ΔS = ΔH/T. Therefore: ΔS_overall = ∫[268K to 273K] Cp,m dT/T + ΔH_m,fusion/Tf + ∫[273K to 373K] Cp,m dT/T + ΔH_m,vaporisation/Tb + ∫[373K to 383K] Cp,m dT/T Answer: (2)
