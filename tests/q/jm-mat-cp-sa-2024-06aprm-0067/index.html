Let C be the circle of minimum area touching the parabola y = 6 - x^2 and the lines y = sqrt(3)|x|. Then which one of the following points lies on the circle C?

  1. (1) (2, 4)
  2. (2) (1, 2)
  3. (3) (2, 2)
  4. (4) (1, 1)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The circle has center at (0, 6-r) and radius r. For the circle to touch the line sqrt(3)x - y = 0, the distance from center to line equals r: |0 - (6-r)|/2 = r, which gives |r - 6| = 2r, so r = 2. The circle equation is x^2 + (y-4)^2 = 4. Checking point (2, 4): 4 + 0 = 4. ✓
