The hydrogen spectrum consists of several spectral lines in Lyman series (L1, L2, L3, ...., Ll has lowest energy among Lyman series). Similarly it consists of several spectral lines in Balmer series (B1, B2, B3, ......, Bl has lowest energy among Balmer lines). The energy of L1 is x times the energy of B1. The value of x is ____ × 10^(-1) (Nearest integer)

Answer

Correct answer: 54

Solution

ΔE(L1) = 13.6 × Z^2 × (1/1^2 - 1/2^2) = 13.6Z^2 × 3/4 ΔE(B1) = 13.6 × Z^2 × (1/2^2 - 1/3^2) = 13.6 × Z^2 × 5/(4 × 9) ΔE(L1)/ΔE(B1) = (3/5) × 9 = 27/5 = x x = (27/5 × 10) × 10^(-1) = 54 × 10^(-1)
