Let a circle C in complex plane pass through the points z1 = 3 + 4i, z2 = 4 + 3i and z3 = 5i. If z(≠z1) is a point on C such that the line through z and z1 is perpendicular to the line through z2 and z3, then arg(z) is equal to:

  1. (1) tan^(-1)(2/sqrt(5)) - π
  2. (2) tan^(-1)(24/7) - π
  3. (3) tan^(-1)(3) - π
  4. (4) tan^(-1)(3/4) - π

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Circle C passes through z1 = 3 + 4i, z2 = 4 + 3i, z3 = 5i. Equation: x^2 + y^2 = 25. The slope of line through z2 and z3 is (3-5)/(4-0) = -1/2. Line through z and z1 perpendicular to this has slope 2. Equation: y - 4 = 2(x - 3), so y = 2x - 2. Intersection with circle: x^2 + (2x-2)^2 = 25, giving x = -7/5, y = -24/5. Thus z = (-7/5, -24/5). Since z is in third quadrant, arg(z) = -π + tan^(-1)(24/7).
