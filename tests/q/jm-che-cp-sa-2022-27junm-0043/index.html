Which of the following is structure of a separating funnel?

  1. (1) Diagram showing a pear-shaped flask with a stopcock at the bottom and a ground glass stopper at the top
  2. (2) Diagram showing a conical funnel shape with a stem
  3. (3) Diagram showing a cylindrical container with a mesh/filter inside and a stem
  4. (4) Diagram showing a bent tube with a stopcock

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

A separating funnel (also called a separation funnel) is used to separate two immiscible liquids. Its characteristic structure includes: a pear-shaped or round-bottomed flask body, a ground glass stopper at the top to prevent evaporation and allow gas exchange, and a stopcock (tap) at the bottom to control the flow of liquid when separating the layers. Option (1) clearly shows all these features and is the correct representation of a separating funnel.
