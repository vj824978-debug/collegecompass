The major product of the following reaction is – H3C-C(=O)-O-C6H4-(CH2)3-NH2 (i) NaNO2/H+ (ii) CrO3/H+ (iii) H2SO4 (conc., Δ)

  1. (1) HO-C6H4-CH2-CH2-C(=O)O (5-membered ring with C=O)
  2. (2) CH3-C(=O)-O-C6H4-CH2-CH2-C(=O)O (5-membered ring with C=O)
  3. (3) HO-C6H4-CH2-CH2-C(=O)O (5-membered indanone structure)
  4. (4) CH3-C(=O)-O-C6H4-CH2-CH2-C(=O)O (5-membered ring structure)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The reaction sequence involves: (i) NaNO2/H+ - The primary amine (-NH2) is converted to a diazonium salt (ArN2+) (ii) CrO3/H+ - The propyl chain (-CH2-CH2-CH2-) is oxidized. The terminal carbon of the propyl side chain is oxidized to a carboxylic acid group (-COOH), and the diazonium salt is reduced to a hydroxyl group (-OH), replacing the N2+ with OH (iii) H2SO4 (conc., Δ) - Under acidic heating conditions, intramolecular Friedel-Crafts acylation occurs. The acetate ester group (CH3CO2-) acts as an acylating agent. However, the carboxylic acid formed from oxidation undergoes dehydration and cyclization. The phenolic OH group and the nearby carboxylic acid group undergo intramolecular condensation with ring closure, forming a 5-membered lactone ring (indanone-type product). The net result is removal of the acetate group and formation of a hydroxylated indanone structure with a ketone in the newly formed 5-membered ring. The major product is the compound with the hydroxyl group on the benzene ring and a 5-membered ring with ketone functionality (indanone derivative).
