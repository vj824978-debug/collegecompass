The work function (φ) of some metals is listed below. The number of metals which will show photoelectric effect when light of 300 nm wavelength falls on the metal is [TABLE: Metal | Li | Na | K | Mg | Cu | Ag | Fe | Pt | W φ (eV) | 2.4 | 2.3 | 2.2 | 3.7 | 4.8 | 4.3 | 4.7 | 6.3 | 4.75]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 4

Solution

The energy associated with incident photon = hc/λ ⇒ E = (6.6×10⁻³⁴ × 3×10⁸)/(300×10⁻⁹) J E in eV = (6.6×10⁻³⁴ × 3×10⁸)/(300×10⁻⁹ × 1.6×10⁻¹⁹) = 4.16 eV So, number of metals showing photo-electric effects will be (4), i.e., Li, Na, K, Mg
