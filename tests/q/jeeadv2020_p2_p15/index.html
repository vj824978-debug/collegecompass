Two capacitors with capacitance values C₁ = 2000 ± 10 pF and C₂ = 3000 ± 15 pF are connected in series. The voltage applied across this combination is V = 5.00 ± 0.02 V. The percentage error in the calculation of the energy stored in this combination of capacitors is _______.

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 1.30 (1.30 to 1.30)

Solution

Sol. 1.30 (1.30 to 1.30) 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ ⟹ 1/C² ΔC = 1/C₁² ΔC₁ + 1/C₂² ΔC₂ ⟹ ΔC/C = C(ΔC₁/C₁² + ΔC₂/C₂²) = 1200(10/(4×10⁸) + 15/(9×10⁸)) = 5×10⁻³ Energy stored U = (1/2)CV² ⟹ ΔU/U = ΔC/C + 2ΔV/V Percentage error in energy stored in the combination of capacitors. (ΔU/U)×100 = (5×10⁻³ + 2(0.02/5))×100 = 1.30%
