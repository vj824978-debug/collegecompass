A particle of mass 100 g is projected at time t = 0 with a speed 20 m/s at an angle 45° to the horizontal as given in the figure. The magnitude of the angular momentum of the particle about the starting point at time t = 2 s is found to be sqrt(K) kg⋅m²/s. The value of K is ________. (Take g = 10 m/s²)

Answer

Correct answer: 800

Solution

Horizontal displacement x = v⋅cos(θ)⋅t = 10√2⋅t So torque of weight about point of projection is τ = mgx⋅(-k̂) dL/dt = mgx(-k̂) Integrating from 0 to 2 s: ∫dL = 0.1 × 10 × 10√2∫tdt(-k̂) 0 to 2 0 to 2 L = -20√2 k̂ |L| = 20√2 = √800 kg⋅m²/s
