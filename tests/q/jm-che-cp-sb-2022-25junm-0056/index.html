The standard free energy change (ΔG°) for 50% dissociation of N2O4 into NO2 at 27°C and 1 atm pressure is -x J mol^(-1). The value of x is ________. (Nearest Integer). [Given: R = 8.31 J K^(-1) mol^(-1), log 1.33 = 0.1239 ln 10 = 2.3]

Answer

Correct answer: 710

Solution

For the reaction N2O4 ⇌ 2NO2, with 50% dissociation: Initial: 1 mole N2O4, 0 mole NO2. At equilibrium: (1-0.5) = 0.5 mole N2O4, 2×0.5 = 1 mole NO2. Given: P_N2O4 = 0.33 atm, P_NO2 = 0.66 atm. Calculate Kp = (P_NO2)^2 / P_N2O4 = (0.66)^2 / 0.33 = 1.33. Using ΔG° = -RT ln Kp = -8.31 × 300 × 2.3 × log(1.33) = -8.31 × 300 × 2.3 × 0.1239 ≈ -710 J mol^(-1). Therefore x = 710.
