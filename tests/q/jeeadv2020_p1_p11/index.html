In addition, it is observed that a transverse voltage ΔV develops between its inner and outer surfaces due to purely kinetic effects of moving electrons (ignore any role of the magnetic field due to the current). Then (figure is schematic and not drawn to scale) [Figure shows a semicircular metallic strip with inner radius R₁ and outer radius R₂, thickness t, with current I flowing through it and voltage V₀ applied across the ends, and transverse voltage ΔV marked between inner and outer surfaces]

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0
