A bacterial infection in an internal wound grows as N'(t) = N_0 exp(t), where the time t is in hours. A dose of antibiotic, taken orally, needs 1 hour to reach the wound. Once it reaches there, the bacterial population goes down as dN/dt = -5N^2. What will be the plot of N_0/N vs. t after 1 hour?

  1. (1) Linear increasing plot
  2. (2) U-shaped curve (decreasing then increasing)
  3. (3) Linear decreasing plot
  4. (4) Horizontal line then increasing

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Initially N > N_0 and N' is increasing through first-order kinetics. So N_0/N in initial time decrease. But after 1 hour the value of N decrease with a faster rate. So N_0/N will increase. Therefore, the plot shows a U-shaped curve: N_0/N decreases initially (reaches a minimum around t = 1 hour when antibiotic starts acting), then increases as the bacterial population N decreases rapidly due to the antibiotic effect dN/dt = -5N^2.
