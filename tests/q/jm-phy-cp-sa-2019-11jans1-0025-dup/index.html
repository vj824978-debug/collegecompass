The major product obtained in the following conversion is: A benzene ring with an acetyl ester group (CH3-C(=O)-O-) at one position and a propenoyl group (CH3-CH=CH-C(=O)-) at the adjacent position, treated with Br2(1 eqv.) in MeOH

  1. (1) A benzene ring with acetyl ester group at one position, a methoxy group and bromine substituent on the propenyl side chain
  2. (2) A benzene ring with acetyl ester group and bromine at ortho/meta positions on the ring, with propenoyl side chain intact
  3. (3) A benzene ring with bromine substituent on the ring, acetyl ester group preserved, propenoyl side chain intact
  4. (4) A benzene ring with bromine substituent on the propenyl side chain, methoxy group, and acetyl ester group

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The starting material is an aromatic compound with two substituents: an acetoxy group (CH3-C(=O)-O-) and an α,β-unsaturated ketone (propenoyl group, CH3-CH=CH-C(=O)-) attached to the benzene ring. When treated with Br2(1 equivalent) in MeOH: 1. The reaction involves electrophilic addition to the C=C double bond of the propenoyl group (allylic system). 2. Br2 adds across the C=C double bond of the unsaturated ketone side chain. 3. Simultaneously, MeOH acts as a nucleophile, adding to the carbocation intermediate formed during bromination. 4. The bromination follows Markovnikov's rule: Br adds to the carbon closer to the electron-withdrawing carbonyl group. 5. MeOH adds to the other carbon of the double bond, forming a -O-Me group. 6. The acetoxy protecting group remains intact throughout. The mechanism proceeds as: - Br+ from Br2 attacks the C=C double bond - Forms a carbocation intermediate - MeOH attacks the secondary carbocation - Results in: Br on one carbon and -OMe on the other carbon of the former double bond The major product is a compound with: - Benzene ring (aromatic core) - Acetyl ester group (CH3-C(=O)-O-) on the ring - Propenyl side chain now converted to: -CH(Br)-CH(OMe)-C(=O)- or similar arrangement - The product shown in option (1) matches this outcome
