The number of words, with or without meaning, that can be formed using all the letters of word ASSASSINATION so that vowels occur together, is ____.

Answer

Correct answer: 50400

Solution

From the word ASSASSINATION, we identify the letters: A - 3 S - 4 N - 2 T - 1 I - 2 O - 1 Total letters = 13 Vowels: A, A, A, I, I, O (6 vowels total: 3 A's, 2 I's, 1 O) Consonants: S, S, S, S, N, N, T (7 consonants total: 4 S's, 2 N's, 1 T) Since vowels must occur together, we treat all vowels as a single unit. So we have: - 1 vowel block (containing 6 vowels) - 7 consonants Total units to arrange = 8 units Number of arrangements of these 8 units = 8!/(4! × 2!) (dividing by 4! for 4 identical S's and 2! for 2 identical N's) Number of arrangements of vowels within the block = 6!/(3! × 2!) (dividing by 3! for 3 identical A's and 2! for 2 identical I's) Total number of arrangements = (8!/(4! × 2!)) × (6!/(3! × 2!)) = (40320/(24 × 2)) × (720/(6 × 2)) = (40320/48) × (720/12) = 840 × 60 = 50400
