The width of fringe is 2 mm on the screen in a double slit experiment for the light of wavelength of 400 nm. The width of the fringe for the light of wavelength 600 nm will be:

  1. (1) 4 mm
  2. (2) 2 mm
  3. (3) 1.33 mm
  4. (4) 3 mm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

In double slit experiment, fringe width β = λD/d. For same experimental setup (D and d constant), β is proportional to λ. Therefore β'/β = λ'/λ. Given β = 2 mm at λ = 400 nm. For λ' = 600 nm: β' = β × (λ'/λ) = 2 × (600/400) = 2 × 1.5 = 3 mm
