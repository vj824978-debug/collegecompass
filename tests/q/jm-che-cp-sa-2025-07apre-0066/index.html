Choose the incorrect trend in the atomic radii (r) of the elements. (1) r_Br < r_K (2) r_Rb < r_Cs (3) r_Mg < r_Al (4) r_Al < r_Cs

  1. (1) r_Br < r_K
  2. (2) r_Rb < r_Cs
  3. (3) r_Mg < r_Al
  4. (4) r_Al < r_Cs

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Atomic radius increases down a group and decreases across a period. (1) r_Br < r_K: Br is in period 4, K is in period 4 but further left, so r_K > r_Br ✓ (2) r_Rb < r_Cs: Both in group 1, Cs is below Rb, so r_Cs > r_Rb ✓ (3) r_Mg < r_Al: Both in period 3, Mg is to the left of Al. Across a period radius decreases left to right, so r_Mg > r_Al ✗ This is incorrect. (4) r_Al < r_Cs: Al is period 3, Cs is period 6, so r_Cs > r_Al ✓
