Four persons independently solve a certain problem correctly with probabilities 1/2, 3/4, 1/4, 1/8. Then the probability that the problem is solved correctly by at least one of them is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) 235/256
  2. (B) 21/256
  3. (C) 3/256
  4. (D) 253/256

Answer

Correct answer: A

Solution

P(at least one of them solves correctly) = 1 − P(none of them solves correctly) = 1 − (1/2 × 1/4 × 3/4 × 7/8) = 235/256
