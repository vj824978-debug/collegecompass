A train is moving along a straight line with a constant acceleration 'a'. A boy standing in the train throws a ball forward with a speed of 10 m/s, at an angle of 60° to the horizontal. The boy has to move forward by 1.15 m inside the train to catch the ball back at the initial height. The acceleration of the train, in m/s², is

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

0 = 10(√3/2)t - (1/2)10t² ...(i) t = √3 sec ⇒ 1.15 = 5√3 - (3/2)a ...(ii)
