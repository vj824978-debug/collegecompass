A series LCR circuit is connected to a 45 sin(ωt) Volt source. The resonant angular frequency of the circuit is 10^5 rad s^-1 and current amplitude at resonance is I₀. When the angular frequency of the source is ω = 8 × 10^4 rad s^-1, the current amplitude in the circuit is 0.05 I₀. If L = 50 mH, match each entry in List-I with an appropriate value from List-II and choose the correct option. [TABLE] List-I | List-II (P) I₀ in mA | (1) 44.4 (Q) The quality factor of the circuit | (2) 18 (R) The bandwidth of the circuit in rad s^-1 | (3) 400 (S) The peak power dissipated at resonance in Watt. | (4) 2250 | (5) 500 [/TABLE]

Matrix match · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) P → 2, Q →3, R →5, S →1
  2. (B) P → 3, Q →1, R →4, S →2
  3. (C) P → 4, Q →5, R →3, S →1
  4. (D) P → 4, Q →2, R →1, S →5

Answer

Correct answer: B

Solution

Sol. B v = 45 sin (ωt) ωᵣ = 10⁵ rad/s ωᵣ = 1/√LC 10⁵ = 1/√(50×10⁻³ × C) C = 2×10⁻⁹ F Xₗ = ωL = 4000Ω Xc = 1/(ωC) = 6250 Ω X = Xc - Xₗ 0.25 I₀ = 45/Z R = 0.05 Z R = 0.05 × √(2250² + R²) R = 112.6Ω I₀ = 45/R = 400 mA Q = Xₗ/R = 44.4 Bandwidth = R/L = 2250 rad/s P = V²/R = 18 w
