Ten moles of an ideal monoatomic gas, initially in state a at atmospheric pressure and temperature T_k = 27°C, is enclosed in a metal cylinder of volume V₀ fitted with a frictionless piston. The gas is suddenly compressed to state b with volume V₀/3. Now, keeping the piston stationary, the cylinder is submerged in a water bath of temperature 11°C until the gas reaches the temperature of the water bath, which is denoted as state c. Finally, while still in the water bath, the piston is brought slowly to its initial position, which is denoted as state f. If R is universal gas constant, then the correct option(s) is/are: [Given: 9^(1/3) = 2.08] (A) The schematic P-V diagram of the processes described above is: [diagram shown with states a, b, c, f plotted] (B) The change in internal energy in going from state a to b is 4860R. (C) The net change in the internal energy in the whole process is −240R. (D) The pressure and temperature of the state b are 2.08 times the atmospheric pressure and 624 K, respectively.

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The schematic P-V diagram of the processes described above is as shown
  2. (B) The change in internal energy in going from state a to b is 4860R
  3. (C) The net change in the internal energy in the whole process is −240R
  4. (D) The pressure and temperature of the state b are 2.08 times the atmospheric pressure and 624 K, respectively

Answer

Correct answer: A, B, C

Solution

Solution: For state a: n = 10, Tₐ = 27°C = 300 K, Pₐ = 1 atm, Vₐ = V₀. For a monoatomic gas, Cᵥ = (3/2)R and γ = 5/3. Process a → b is a sudden compression, which is an adiabatic process. Vᵦ = V₀/3 Using TₐVₐ^(γ-1) = TᵦVᵦ^(γ-1): Tᵦ = Tₐ(Vₐ/Vᵦ)^(γ-1) = 300 × (3)^(5/3-1) = 300 × 3^(2/3) = 300 × (9)^(1/3) Given 9^(1/3) = 2.08, we get Tᵦ = 300 × 2.08 = 624 K. Using PₐVₐ^γ = PᵦVᵦ^γ: Pᵦ = Pₐ(Vₐ/Vᵦ)^γ = 1 × (3)^(5/3) = 3 × 3^(2/3) = 3 × 2.08 = 6.24 atm. Thus, the pressure at state b is 6.24 times the atmospheric pressure. Option (D) is incorrect. The change in internal energy for process a → b is: ΔUₐᵦ = nCᵥ(Tᵦ - Tₐ) = 10 × (3/2)R × (624 - 300) = 15R × 324 = 4860R. Option (B) is correct. Process b → c is isochoric (stationary piston), so Vᶜ = V₀/3. The gas cools to the water bath temperature, Tᶜ = 11°C = 284 K. Process c → f is isothermal (slow expansion in water bath), so Tf = Tᶜ = 284 K and Vf = V₀. The net change in internal energy for the entire process a → f depends only on the initial and final temperatures: ΔUₙₑₜ = nCᵥ(Tf - Tₐ) = 10 × (3/2)R × (284 - 300) = 15R × (-16) = -240R. Option (C) is correct. For the P-V diagram: Process a → b is an adiabatic compression (curve upwards and to the left). Process b → c is an isochoric cooling (vertical line downwards). Process c → f is an isothermal expansion (curve downwards and to the right). Comparing pressures at volume V₀: Pₐ = nR(300)/V₀ and Pf = nR(284)/V₀ Since Pₐ > Pf, point a lies above point f. The given schematic diagram correctly represents all these features. Option (A) is correct. Answer: The schematic P-V diagram of the processes described above is: [P-V diagram showing] - Process a → b: adiabatic compression (curve upward-left from a to b) - Process b → c: isochoric cooling (vertical line downward from b to c) - Process c → f: isothermal expansion (curve downward-right from c to f) - Point b at highest pressure - Point c and f at lower pressures with c above f - Point a and f at same volume V₀ with a above f 4860R.; The change in internal energy in going from state a to b is 4860R.; Net change in the internal energy in the whole process is -240R.
