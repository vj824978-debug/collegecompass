Which of the following compounds will form the precipitate with aq. AgNO3 solution most readily?

  1. (1) N(C2H5)(CH2CH2Br) attached to benzene ring with OCH3 substituent
  2. (2) N-Br compound in cyclohexane ring
  3. (3) N(C2H5)(CH2Br) attached to benzene ring with OCH3 substituent
  4. (4) C2H5-O-CH2Br

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

R - x + aq.AgNO3 --R.D.S--> R+ + Agx (PPT) So rate of P.P.T formation of Agx depends on stability of carbocation (R+) In given question formed carbocation will be: (a) Ethoxy group attached to secondary carbocation on cyclohexane (b) Secondary carbocation on cyclohexane ring (c) Benzyl carbocation with N-Ph substituent (d) Benzyl carbocation with N attached to benzene ring with OCH3 group Most stable carbocation is (b) so N-Br compound in cyclohexane ring given fastest P.P.T of AgBr with aq AgNO3 Explanation: The rate of silver halide precipitate formation depends on the stability of the carbocation formed after loss of the halide (Br in this case). Compound (1): Forms a carbocation stabilized by the aromatic ring and the electron-donating OCH3 group, but the positive charge develops on the nitrogen attached to benzene. Compound (2): Forms a secondary carbocation on the cyclohexane ring. This is a simple secondary carbocation with no special stabilization. Compound (3): Similar to compound (1) but the aromatic ring and OCH3 provide additional stabilization through resonance. Compound (4): Forms a primary carbocation (benzylic), less stable than secondary carbocation. Among the carbocations formed, the secondary carbocation from compound (2) is sufficiently stable and most readily formed, making it the fastest to precipitate with AgNO3 solution.
