In the area enclosed by the parabolas P₁: 2y = 5x^2 and P₂: x^2 - y + 6 = 0 is equal to the area enclosed by P₁ and y = ax, a > 0, then a^3 is equal to ______.

Answer

Correct answer: 600

Solution

First, find the intersection points of P₁ and P₂: 2y = 5x^2 and x^2 - y + 6 = 0 From P₁: y = (5/2)x^2 Substituting into P₂: x^2 - (5/2)x^2 + 6 = 0 x^2 + 6 = (5/2)x^2 ⟹ x = ±2 Area between P₁ and P₂ [Say A₁]: A₁ = ∫_{-2}^{2} [(x^2 + 6) - (5/2)x^2] dx = 2∫_{0}^{2} [(6 - (3/2)x^2)] dx = 2[6x - (x^3/2)]_{0}^{2} = 2[12 - 4] = 16 For P₁ and y = ax: (5/2)x^2 = ax ⟹ x = 0, (2a/5) Area between P₁ and y = ax [Say A₂]: A₂ = ∫_{0}^{2a/5} [ax - (5/2)x^2] dx = [ax^2/2 - (5/6)x^3]_{0}^{2a/5} = a(2a/5)^2/2 - (5/6)(2a/5)^3 = (2a^3/25) - (2a^3/75) = 2a^3/75 Setting A₁ = A₂: 2a^3/75 = 16 a^3 = 600
