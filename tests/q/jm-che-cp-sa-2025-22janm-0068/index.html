The products formed in the following reaction sequence are: nitrobenzene (i) Br2, AcOH (ii) Sn, HCl (iii) NaNO2, HCl, 273 K (iv) C2H5OH gives A + B

  1. (1) 3-bromophenol, 3-bromobenzene
  2. (2) 3-ethoxybromobenzene, CH3-COOH
  3. (3) 3-bromobenzene, CH3-CHO
  4. (4) 3-hydroxybromobenzene, CH3-CHO

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Starting with nitrobenzene: Step (i) Br2, AcOH: Bromination occurs at the meta position (since NO2 is a deactivating group and meta-directing), giving m-bromonitrobenzene (3-bromonitrobenzene). Step (ii) Sn, HCl: Reduction of the nitro group (-NO2) to amino group (-NH2), giving m-bromoaniline (3-bromoaniline with NH2 and Br groups). Step (iii) NaNO2, HCl, 273 K: Diazotization of the aniline group. The -NH2 group is converted to a diazonium salt (-N2Cl+), giving m-bromobenzenediazonium chloride. Step (iv) C2H5OH: The diazonium salt undergoes a Sandmeyer-type reduction reaction with ethanol. The diazonium group (-N2+) is replaced by hydrogen (-H), yielding m-bromobenzene (3-bromobenzene). Simultaneously, the ethanol reduces the diazonium group to form nitrogen gas (N2) and also generates an aldehyde from the ethanol oxidation, producing CH3CHO (acetaldehyde). Therefore, A = 3-bromobenzene and B = CH3-CHO The answer is [3]
