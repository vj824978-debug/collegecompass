A point charge of +12 μC is at a distance 6 cm vertically above the centre of a squared of side 12cm as shown in figure. The magnitude of the electric flux through the square will be .......... ×10^5 Nm^2/C.

Answer

Correct answer: 226

Solution

Using Gauss's law, the flux through the 6 sides of a cube is φ_t = q_0/ε_0 = 12×10^(-6)/ε_0. The flux through one square is φ = φ_t/6 = (12×10^(-6))/(6ε_0) = (2×10^(-6))/ε_0. Substituting ε_0 = 8.85×10^(-12), we get φ = (2×10^(-6))/(8.85×10^(-12)) = 0.22598×10^6 = 225.98×10^3 Nm^2/C = 226 Nm^2/C. Therefore φ = 226 Nm^2/C or approximately 2.26×10^5 Nm^2/C.
