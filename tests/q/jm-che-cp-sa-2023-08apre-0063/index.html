A compound 'X' when treated with phthalic anhydride in presence of concentrated H2SO4 yields 'Y'. 'Y' is used as an acid/base indicator. 'X' and 'Y' are respectively

  1. (1) Anisole, methyl orange
  2. (2) Salicylaldehyde, Phenolphthalein
  3. (3) Toluine, Phenolphthalein
  4. (4) Carbolic acid, Phenolphthalein

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Carbolic acid (phenol) reacts with phthalic anhydride in the presence of concentrated H2SO4 (Friedel-Crafts acylation) to form phenolphthalein. Two phenol molecules react with one phthalic anhydride molecule. Phenolphthalein is a well-known acid-base indicator that is colorless in acidic conditions and pink/red in basic conditions.
