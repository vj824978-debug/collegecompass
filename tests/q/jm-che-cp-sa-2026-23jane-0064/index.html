Identify the INCORRECT statements from the following: A. Notation 12^24 Mg represents 24 protons and 12 neutrons. B. Wavelength of a radiation of frequency 4.5×10^15 s^(-1) is 6.7×10^(-8) m. C. One radiation has wavelength = lambda_1 (900 nm) and energy = E_1. Other radiation has wavelength = lambda_2 (300 nm) and energy = E_2, E_1 : E_2 = 3 : 1. D. Number of photons of light of wavelength 2000 pm that provides 1 J of energy is 1.006×10^16.

  1. (1) A and D only
  2. (2) A and C only
  3. (3) A and B only
  4. (4) B and C only

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

(A) 12^24 Mg represents 12 protons and 12 neutrons. (B) lambda = C/v = (3×10^8)/(4.5×10^15) = 6.67×10^(-8) m. (C) E_1/E_2 = (hC/lambda_1)/(hC/lambda_2) = lambda_2/lambda_1 E_1/E_2 = 300/900 = 1/3 (false) (D) No. of photons = Energy/(hC/lambda) = 10^16 (True)
