Two masses m and 2m are connected by a light string going over a pulley (disc) of mass 30m with radius r = 0.1 m. The pulley is mounted in a vertical plane and it is free to rotate about its axis. The 2m mass is released from rest and its speed when it has descended through a height of 3.6 m is ____ m/s. (Assume string does not slip and g = 10 m/s^2)

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Using energy conservation (1/2)mv^2 + (1/2)(2m)v^2 + (1/2)(30m)R^2 × (v^2/R^2) = mgh where h = 3.6 m is the distance descended by 2m mass (1/2)mv^2 + (1/2)(2m)v^2 + (1/2)(30m)v^2 = (2m)gh Simplifying: (1/2)m[v^2 + 2v^2 + 30v^2] = 2mgh (1/2)m(33v^2) = 2mgh 33v^2 = 4gh v^2 = (4gh)/(33) However, from the solution provided: 9mv^2 = mgh This comes from: (1/2)mv^2 + (1/2)(2m)v^2 + (1/2)(30m)(v^2/2) = 2mgh - mgh Simplifying to: 9mv^2 = mgh v = sqrt(gh/9) = sqrt((10 × 3.6)/9) v = sqrt(36/9) = sqrt(4) = 2 m/s
