Identify A and B in the chemical reaction. Starting material has OCH3 and NO2 groups on a benzene ring. Treatment with HCl gives [A] (major), then treatment with NaI in dry acetone gives [B] (major).

  1. (1) A = 4-chloro-1-methoxy-2-nitrobenzene, B = 4-iodo-1-methoxy-2-nitrobenzene
  2. (2) A = 2-chloro-4-iodo-1-methoxy-5-nitrobenzene, B = 1-chloro-4-iodo-2-methoxy-5-nitrobenzene
  3. (3) A = 4-chloro-1-methoxy-2-nitrobenzene, B = 1-chloro-4-iodo-2-methoxy-5-nitrobenzene
  4. (4) A = 2-chloro-1-methoxy-4-nitrobenzene, B = 2-chloro-4-iodo-1-methoxybenzene

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The starting material has OCH3 (ortho/para director) and NO2 (meta director) groups. HCl treatment causes electrophilic aromatic substitution with chlorination, giving [A] as the major product with Cl at the position ortho to OCH3 and meta to NO2. NaI in acetone causes Finkelstein reaction where Cl is displaced by I (SN2 mechanism favored in polar aprotic solvent). The OCH3 and NO2 groups remain intact. Product [B] is the iodo-substituted compound with same substitution pattern as [A] but with I instead of Cl. The answer is Option (A): A = 4-chloro-1-methoxy-2-nitrobenzene, B = 4-iodo-1-methoxy-2-nitrobenzene.
