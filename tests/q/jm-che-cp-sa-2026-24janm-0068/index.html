Consider the following two reactions A and B? (A) Phenol + Na → Main Product + gas (x) (B) Benzoic acid + NaHCO3 → Main Product + gas (y) Numerical value of [molar mass of x + molar mass of y] is ____

  1. (1) 4
  2. (2) 88
  3. (3) 46
  4. (4) 160

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

x = H2 (gas), y = CO2 (gas) Reaction A: Phenol reacts with sodium metal to produce sodium phenoxide and hydrogen gas. C6H5OH + Na → C6H5ONa + (1/2)H2 The gas evolved is H2 (hydrogen gas) Molar mass of H2 = 2 Reaction B: Benzoic acid reacts with sodium bicarbonate to produce sodium benzoate, water, and carbon dioxide gas. C6H5COOH + NaHCO3 → C6H5COONa + H2O + CO2 The gas evolved is CO2 (carbon dioxide gas) Molar mass of CO2 = 12 + 2(16) = 12 + 32 = 44 Sum of molar mass = 2 + 44 = 46
