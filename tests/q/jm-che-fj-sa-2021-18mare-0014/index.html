Consider the given reaction, percentage yield of: (A) B > C> A (B) C> B> A (C) A> C> B (D) C> A> B. During nitration of aniline, meta-nitroaniline is also formed as product due to formation of -NH3+ group. The percentage of p,m and o product is 51%, 47% and 2% respectively.

  1. (1) B > C > A
  2. (2) C > B > A
  3. (3) A > C > B
  4. (4) C > A > B

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

In the nitration of aniline using HNO3 and H2SO4 at 288 K, the -NH2 group is a strong ortho/para director. However, under acidic conditions, aniline forms anilinium ion (-NH3+), which is a meta-directing group due to its positive charge. This results in formation of three isomers: ortho-nitroaniline (2%), meta-nitroaniline (47%), and para-nitroaniline (51%). Therefore, the percentage yield order is: para (51%) > meta (47%) > ortho (2%), which corresponds to C > B > A.
