In the given figure, two wheels P and Q are connected by a belt B. The radius of P is three times that of Q. In case of same rotational kinetic energy, the ratio of rotational inertias (I_P / I_Q) will be x : 1. The value of x will be ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 9

Solution

Given: radius of P is 3 times that of Q, so r_P = 3*r_Q. For belt connection, linear velocities are equal: v_P = v_Q. Since v = omega*r, we have omega_P*r_P = omega_Q*r_Q, which gives omega_P*(3R) = omega_Q*R, so omega_P = omega_Q/3. For same rotational kinetic energy: (1/2)*I_P*omega_P^2 = (1/2)*I_Q*omega_Q^2. Substituting omega_P = omega_Q/3: I_P*(omega_Q/3)^2 = I_Q*omega_Q^2, which gives I_P*omega_Q^2/9 = I_Q*omega_Q^2. Therefore I_P/I_Q = 9/1, so x = 9.
