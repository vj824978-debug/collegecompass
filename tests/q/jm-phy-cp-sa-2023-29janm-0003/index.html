Surface tension of a soap bubble is 2.0 × 10^(-2) Nm^(-1). Work done to increase the radius of soap bubble from 3.5 cm to 7 cm will be: Take pi = 22/7

  1. (1) 5.76 × 10^(-4) J
  2. (2) 0.72 × 10^(-4) J
  3. (3) 9.24 × 10^(-4) J
  4. (4) 18.48 × 10^(-4) J

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

T = 2 × 10^(-2) N/m^2 W = T(ΔA) = 2 × 10^(-2) [2 × 4π((7/100)^2) - (3.5/100)^2] = 18.48 × 10^(-4) J Note: For a soap bubble, the surface area change is ΔA = 2 × 4π(r_final^2 - r_initial^2) because a soap bubble has two surfaces (inner and outer). With r_final = 7 cm = 0.07 m and r_initial = 3.5 cm = 0.035 m: ΔA = 2 × 4π[(0.07)^2 - (0.035)^2] = 8π[0.0049 - 0.001225] = 8 × (22/7) × 0.003675 = 8 × 22 × 0.003675 / 7 = 0.924 m^2 W = 2 × 10^(-2) × 0.924 = 0.01848 = 18.48 × 10^(-4) J
