A metal complex with a formula MCl4.3NH3 is involved in sp^3d^2 hybridisation. It upon reaction with excess of AgNO3 solution gives 'x' moles of AgCl. Consider 'x' is equal to the number of lone pairs of electron present in central atom of BrF5. Then the number of geometrical isomers exhibited by the complex is ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Step 1: BrF5 has Br with 5 F atoms bonded and 1 lone pair, so x = 1 lone pair. Step 2: The complex MCl4.3NH3 with sp^3d^2 hybridisation releases 1 mole of AgCl with excess AgNO3, meaning 1 Cl is ionic (outside coordination sphere). Step 3: The complex ion is [M(NH3)3Cl]Cl with octahedral geometry. Step 4: With 3 NH3 and 1 Cl as ligands in octahedral geometry, geometrical isomers are possible: Meridional (Mer) and Facial (Fac) configurations. Step 5: Total number of geometrical isomers = 2 (Mer and Fac).
