If e is the electronic charge, c is the speed of light in free space and h is Planck's constant, the quantity (1/(4πε₀)) * (|e|^2)/(hc) has dimensions of:

  1. (1) ML T^0
  2. (2) ML T^-1
  3. (3) M^0 L^0 T^0
  4. (4) L C^-1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

The expression (1/(4πε₀)) * (|e|^2)/(hc) can be rewritten as (|e|^2)/(4πε₀ * r^2) * (r^2)/(hc) = (Fr * r)/(E * λ). Since the dimension of 'Fr' (force times distance = energy) and 'E' (energy) are the same, and dimension of 'r' (length) and 'λ' (wavelength) are the same, the overall expression is dimensionless. Therefore, dimensions are M^0 L^0 T^0.
