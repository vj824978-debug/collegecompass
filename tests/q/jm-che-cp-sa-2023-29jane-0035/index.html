According to MO theory the bond orders for O2^(2-), CO and NO^(+) respectively, are

  1. (1) 1, 2 and 3
  2. (2) 1, 3 and 2
  3. (3) 2, 3 and 3
  4. (4) 1, 3 and 3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Species: B.O. O2^(2-): 1 CO: 3 NO^(+): 3 For O2^(2-) (oxygen dianion): Electron configuration: (sigma 1s)^2 (sigma* 1s)^2 (sigma 2s)^2 (sigma* 2s)^2 (pi 2p)^4 (pi* 2p)^2 Total bonding electrons = 6 Total antibonding electrons = 4 Bond order = (6-4)/2 = 1 For CO (carbon monoxide): Electron configuration: (sigma 1s)^2 (sigma* 1s)^2 (sigma 2s)^2 (sigma* 2s)^2 (pi 2p)^4 (sigma 2p)^2 Total bonding electrons = 8 Total antibonding electrons = 2 Bond order = (8-2)/2 = 3 For NO^(+) (nitrosyl cation): Electron configuration: (sigma 1s)^2 (sigma* 1s)^2 (sigma 2s)^2 (sigma* 2s)^2 (pi 2p)^4 (sigma 2p)^2 Total bonding electrons = 8 Total antibonding electrons = 2 Bond order = (8-2)/2 = 3
