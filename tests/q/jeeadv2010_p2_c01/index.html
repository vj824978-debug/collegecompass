Mock Tests › JEE Advanced · Hydrocarbons › JEE Advanced 2010 Paper 2

The compounds P, Q and S [FIGURE: Three benzene ring structures shown - P has COOH and CH3 groups, Q has OCH3 and CH3 groups, S has a phenyl ester group with C=O] were separately subjected to nitration using HNO3/H2SO4 mixture. The major product formed in each case respectively, is

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) [FIGURE: Three nitrated products - P product has COOH, HO, and NO2; Q product has OCH3, CH3, and NO2; S product has C=O-O-phenyl with NO2]
  2. (B) [FIGURE: Three nitrated products - P product has COOH, HO, and NO2 in different positions; Q product has OCH3, CH3, and NO2 in different positions; S product has C=O-O-phenyl with NO2 in different position]
  3. (C) [FIGURE: Three nitrated products - P product has COOH, HO, and NO2; Q product has OCH3, CH3, and NO2 in ortho position; S product has C=O-O-phenyl with no NO2]
  4. (D) [FIGURE: Three nitrated products - P product has COOH, HO, and NO2; Q product has OCH3, CH3, and NO2; S product has C=O-O-phenyl with NO2 on meta position of phenyl ring]

Answer

Correct answer: C

Solution

Sol. (C) [Reaction 1: Para-hydroxybenzene with -COOH group (P) undergoes nitration with HNO₃/H₂SO₄ to form para-nitro compound with -OH and -COOH groups. Note: (- OH gp is more activating)] [Reaction 2: Para-methoxy-methylbenzene (Q) undergoes nitration with HNO₃/H₂SO₄ to form ortho-nitro product with -OCH₃ and -CH₃ groups. Note: (- OCH₃ gp is more activating)] [Reaction 3: Phenyl benzoate (benzene ring bonded to C(=O)O-phenyl) undergoes nitration with HNO₃/H₂SO₄ to form product with -NO₂ group on the phenoxy ring] These reactions demonstrate electrophilic aromatic substitution with nitration as the electrophile. The directing effects of substituents (electron-donating groups like -OH and -OCH₃ are ortho/para directing and activating) determine the position of nitration on the benzene ring.
