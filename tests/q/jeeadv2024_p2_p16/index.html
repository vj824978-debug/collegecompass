If the collision occurs at time t₀ = 0, the value of v_cm/(a₀) will be

Comprehension · Marks: +3 / 0.0

Passage:

Answer

Correct answer: 0.75

Solution

If collision occurs at t = 0 Then, v₁(t) = aω = 2U₀ v₂(t) = -aω = -2U₀ [Diagram showing three masses before collision: - Mass 3m moving left at U₀ - Mass 2m (center) connected by spring - Mass m moving right at 2U₀] Before collision [Diagram showing three masses after collision: - Mass 3m moving left at 2U₀ - Mass 2m (center) with arrow pointing right labeled U₀ - Mass m moving right at 2U₀] After collision Vₒₘ = (mU₀ + 2mU₀)/(m + m) = 3U₀/2 Vₒₘ = (3/4)aω ⟹ Vₒₘ/(aω) = 3/4 = 0.75
