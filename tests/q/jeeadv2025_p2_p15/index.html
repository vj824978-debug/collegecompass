A projectile of mass 200 g is launched in a viscous medium at an angle 60° with the horizontal, with an initial velocity of 270 m/s. It experiences a viscous drag force F = -cv̄ where the drag coefficient c = 0.1 kg/s and v̄ is the instantaneous velocity of the projectile. The projectile hits a vertical wall after 2 s. Taking e = 2.7, the horizontal distance of the wall from the point of projection (in m) is _____

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 170

Solution

Sol. m(d²x/dt²) = -c(dx/dt) d/dt(dx/dt) = d/dt(c/m·x) ⟹ dx/dt = v₀/2 - (c/m)x At x = 0, vₓ = v₀/2 ⟹ dx/dt = v₀/2 - (c/m)x 2∫₀ˣ dx/(v₀ - x) = ∫₀² dt 2ln|v₀ - x|₀ˣ = -2 ⟹ ln|(v₀ - x)/v₀| = -1 v₀ - x = v₀/e x = v₀ - v₀/e = 270 - 100 = 170 m [Diagram shows a coordinate system with a curved trajectory.
