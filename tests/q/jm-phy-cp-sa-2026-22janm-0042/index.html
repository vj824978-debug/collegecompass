Electric field in a region is given by E = Ax i + By j, where A = 10 V/m^2 and B = 5 V/m^2. If the electric potential at a point (10,20) is 500 V, then the electric potential at origin is ____ V.

  1. (1) 1000
  2. (2) 500
  3. (3) 2000
  4. (4) 0

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

E = 10x i + 5y j V_at(10,20) = 500 V ΔV = - ∫ E · dr 500 - V_0 = - ∫_(0,0)^(10,20) (10x i + 5y j) · (dx i + dy j) 500 - V_0 = - [5x^2 + (5y^2)/2]_(0,0)^(10,20) V_0 - 500 = (500 + 5 × 400/2) - (0 - 0) V_0 - 500 = 500 + 1000 V_0 = 2000 V
