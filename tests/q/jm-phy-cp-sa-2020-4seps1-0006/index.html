A air bubble of radius 1 cm in water has an upward acceleration 9.8 cm s^(-2). The density of water is 1 gm cm^(-3) and water offers negligible drag force on the bubble. The mass of the bubble is (g = 980 cm/s^2)

  1. (1) 3.15 gm
  2. (2) 1.52 gm
  3. (3) 4.51 gm
  4. (4) 4.15 gm

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Volume V = (4/3)π r^3 = (4/3)π × (1)^3 = 4.19 cm^3 a = 9.8 cm/s^2 Applying Newton's second law: B - mg = ma Where B is buoyant force, m is mass of bubble, g is acceleration due to gravity, a is upward acceleration. From the diagram: B (upward) - mg (downward) = ma (upward) Rearranging: m = B/(g + a) Buoyant force B = ρ_water × V × g = (V ρ_water) g Therefore: m = (V ρ_water g)/(g + a) = (V ρ_water)/(1 + a/g) m = (4.19 × 1)/(1 + 9.8/980) m = 4.19/(1 + 0.01) m = 4.19/1.01 m = 4.15 gm
