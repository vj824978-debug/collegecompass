Among the following compounds, which one is found in RNA ?

  1. (1) CH3 substituted pyrimidine with two oxygen atoms (5-methyl-2,4-pyrimidinedione)
  2. (2) N-methylated pyrimidine with two oxygen atoms (1,3-dimethyl-2,4-pyrimidinedione)
  3. (3) Pyrimidine with two oxygen atoms and NH groups (2,4-pyrimidinedione with NH)
  4. (4) Pyrimidine with NH2 group and oxygen atom (4-amino-2-pyrimidinone)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Uracil base is present in RNA Uracil (u) exists in two forms: 1. Keto form: A pyrimidine ring with two carbonyl groups (C=O) at positions 2 and 4, with NH groups at positions 1 and 3 2. Enol form: The same pyrimidine ring where the keto groups (C=O) are converted to enol groups (C-OH) with corresponding lone pairs on nitrogen The keto form is the predominant and chemically stable form of uracil found in RNA. This structure corresponds to option (3), which shows a pyrimidine ring with two carbonyl oxygen atoms (at positions 2 and 4) and two NH groups (at positions 1 and 3). The equilibrium between keto and enol forms is shown, but the keto form is the standard representation of uracil in RNA.
