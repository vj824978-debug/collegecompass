The correct order of the spin-only magnetic moment of metal ions in the following low spin complexes, [V(CN)6]^4-, [Fe(CN)6]^3-, [Ru(NH3)6]^3+, and [Cr(NH3)6]^2+ is -

  1. (1) V^2+ > Cr^2+ > Ru^3+ > Fe^2+
  2. (2) V^2+ > Ru^3+ > Cr^2+ > Fe^2+
  3. (3) Cr^2+ > Ru^3+ > Fe^2+ > V^2+
  4. (4) Cr^2+ > V^2+ > Ru^3+ > Fe^2+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

All are low spin complex and no. of unpaired e^- will decide the magnetic moment. For V^2+ in [V(CN)6]^4-: Electronic configuration of V is [Ar]3d^3 4s^2 V^2+ has [Ar]3d^3 In low spin complex with strong field CN^-, all electrons are paired as much as possible in d orbitals. For d^3: t2g^3 eg^0 → 3 unpaired electrons Magnetic moment μ = sqrt(n(n+2)) = sqrt(3(5)) = sqrt(15) For Fe^2+ in [Fe(CN)6]^3-: Electronic configuration of Fe is [Ar]3d^6 4s^2 Fe^3+ has [Ar]3d^5 In low spin complex: t2g^5 eg^0 → 1 unpaired electron Magnetic moment μ = sqrt(1(3)) = sqrt(3) For Ru^3+ in [Ru(NH3)6]^3+: Electronic configuration of Ru is [Kr]4d^7 5s^1 Ru^3+ has [Kr]4d^5 In low spin complex with NH3: t2g^5 eg^0 → 1 unpaired electron Magnetic moment μ = sqrt(1(3)) = sqrt(3) For Cr^2+ in [Cr(NH3)6]^2+: Electronic configuration of Cr is [Ar]3d^5 4s^1 Cr^2+ has [Ar]3d^4 In low spin complex: t2g^4 eg^0 → 4 unpaired electrons Magnetic moment μ = sqrt(4(6)) = sqrt(24) Comparing: sqrt(15) > sqrt(24) gives V^2+ > Cr^2+ > sqrt(3) = sqrt(3) Therefore: V^2+ > Cr^2+ > Ru^3+ ≈ Fe^2+ The correct order is V^2+ > Cr^2+ > Ru^3+ > Fe^2+
