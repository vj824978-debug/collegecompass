A bag contains 6 balls. Two balls are drawn from it at random and both are found to be black. The probability that the bag contains at least 5 black balls is

  1. (1) 3/7
  2. (2) 5/6
  3. (3) 2/7
  4. (4) 5/7

Answer

Correct answer: Option 4

Solution

Let E_i → Bag have at least i black balls E → 2 balls are drawn & both black P((E_5 or E_6)/E) = (P(E/E_5) + P(E/E_6)) / (sum from i=1 to 6 of P(E/E_i)) = (C(5,2) + C(6,2)) / (C(6,2)) (C(2,2) + C(3,2) + C(4,2) + C(5,2) + C(6,2)) / C(6,2) = (10 + 15) / (1 + 3 + 6 + 10 + 15) (1 + 3 + 6 + 10 + 15) = 25 / 35 = 5/7
