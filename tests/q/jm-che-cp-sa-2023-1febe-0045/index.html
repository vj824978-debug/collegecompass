In the reaction, starting with a compound containing OH and COOCH3 groups on a benzene ring, it undergoes conversion to an intermediate with OH and COOH groups, which then converts to a final product with OCOCH3 and COOH groups. Identify reagents 'X' and 'Y' respectively.

  1. (1) (CH3CO)2O/H+ and (CH3CO)2O/H+
  2. (2) CH3OH/H+, Δ and CH3OH/H+, Δ
  3. (3) (CH3CO)2O/H+ and CH3OH/H+, Δ
  4. (4) CH3OH/H+, Δ and (CH3CO)2O/H+

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

Analyzing the reaction sequence: Starting material: Benzene ring with OH and COOCH3 groups Step 1 (Reagent Y): Conversion to intermediate with OH and COOH - The COOCH3 (methyl ester) group is converted to COOH (carboxylic acid) - This is hydrolysis of the ester under acidic conditions with heat - Reagent Y = CH3OH/H+, Δ (Fischer esterification reversal/saponification) Step 2 (Reagent X): Conversion of intermediate to final product - The intermediate has OH and COOH groups - Final product has OCOCH3 (acetyl ester of phenol) and COOH groups - The phenolic OH is acetylated to form OCOCH3 - This requires acetic anhydride in acidic medium - Reagent X = (CH3CO)2O/H+ (acetylation of phenol) Therefore: - X = (CH3CO)2O/H+ - Y = CH3OH/H+, Δ This corresponds to option (3).
