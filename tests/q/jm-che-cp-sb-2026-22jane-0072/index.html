If the enthalpy of sublimation of Li is 155 kJ mol^(-1), enthalpy of dissociation of F2 is 150 kJ mol^(-1), ionization enthalpy of Li is 520 kJ mol^(-1), electron gain enthalpy of F is -313 kJ mol^(-1), standard enthalpy of formation of LiF is -594 kJ mol^(-1). The magnitude of lattice enthalpy of LiF is ____ kJmol^(-1) (Nearest integer).

Answer

Correct answer: 1031

Solution

Using Born-Landé cycle for formation of LiF: Li(s) + (1/2) F2(g) → LiF(s), ΔHf = -594 kJ/mol Breaking down the process: Li(s) → Li(g), ΔH = 155 kJ/mol (sublimation enthalpy) Li(g) → Li^+(g) + e^(-), ΔH = 520 kJ/mol (ionization enthalpy) (1/2) F2(g) → F(g), ΔH = 150/2 = 75 kJ/mol (dissociation enthalpy) F(g) + e^(-) → F^-(g), ΔH = -313 kJ/mol (electron gain enthalpy) Li^+(g) + F^-(g) → LiF(s), ΔH = -L.E. (lattice enthalpy, negative) Applying Hess's Law: ΔHf = ΔHsub + ΔHion + (1/2)ΔHdiss + ΔHeg + (-L.E.) -594 = 155 + 520 + 75 + (-313) + (-L.E.) -594 = 155 + 520 + 75 - 313 - L.E. -594 = 437 - L.E. L.E. = 437 + 594 L.E. = 1031 kJ/mol The magnitude of lattice enthalpy of LiF is 1031 kJ/mol
