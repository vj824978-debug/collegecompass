Let L be the straight line joining the points P(1, 2, −1) and Q(2, 3, 1). Let S be the foot of the perpendicular drawn from the point R(4, −1, 5) to the line L. Another line passing through R intersects L at a point T such that the point S divides the line segment PT internally in the ratio |PS| : |ST| = 1 : 2, where |PS| and |ST| are the lengths of the line segments PS and ST, respectively. Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -2.0

  1. (A) The orthocentre of the triangle PRT is (23/5, −4, −31/5)
  2. (B) The orthocentre of the triangle PRT is (4, 3, 5)
  3. (C) The area of the triangle PRT is 6√5
  4. (D) The area of the triangle PRT is 18√5

Answer

Correct answer: A, D

Solution

Solution: The direction ratios of line L passing through P(1, 2, −1) and Q(2, 3, 1) are (2 − 1, 3 − 2, 1 − (−1)) = (1, 1, 2). The equation of line L is (x − 1)/1 = (y − 2)/1 = (z + 1)/2 = λ. Any point on L can be written as S(λ + 1, λ + 2, 2λ − 1). Since S is the foot of the perpendicular from R(4, −1, 5) to L, the direction ratios of RS are (λ − 3, λ + 3, 2λ − 6). As RS ⊥ L, the dot product of their direction ratios is zero: 1(λ − 3) + 1(λ + 3) + 2(2λ − 6) = 0 6λ − 12 = 0 ⟹ λ = 2 Thus, the coordinates of S are (3, 4, 3). The length of PS is √((3 − 1)² + (4 − 2)² + (3 − (−1))²) = √(4 + 4 + 16) = 2√6. The length of the altitude RS is √((3 − 4)² + (4 − (−1))² + (3 − 5)²) = √(1 + 25 + 4) = √30. Since S divides PT internally in the ratio 1 : 2, we have: S = (T + 2P)/3 ⟹ T = 3S − 2P T = 3(3, 4, 3) − 2(1, 2, −1) = (7, 8, 11) The length of the base PT is 3|PS| = 6√6. The area of △PRT is (1/2) × |PT| × |RS| = (1/2) × 6√6 × √30 = 18√5. The orthocentre H lies on the altitude RS. The line RS passes through S(3, 4, 3) and has direction ratios proportional to R − S = (1, −5, 2). Let the coordinates of H be (3 + t, 4 − 5t, 3 + 2t). Since H is the orthocentre, PH ⊥ RT. The direction ratios of RT are (7 − 4, 8 − (−1), 11 − 5) = (3, 9, 6), which is proportional to (1, 3, 2). The direction ratios of PH are (3 + t − 1, 4 − 5t − 2, 3 + 2t − (−1)) = (t + 2, 2 − 5t, 2t + 4). Taking the dot product of PH and RT: 1(t + 2) + 3(2 − 5t) + 2(2t + 4) = 0 t + 2 + 6 − 15t + 4t + 8 = 0 16 − 10t = 0 ⟹ t = 8/5 Substituting t = 8/5 into the coordinates of H, we get: H = (3 + 8/5, 4 − 5(8/5), 3 + 2(8/5)) = (3 + 8/5, 4 − 8, 3 + 16/5) = (23/5, −4, 31/5) Answer: The orthocentre of the triangle PRT is (23/5, −4, 31/5). The area of the triangle PRT is 18√5.
