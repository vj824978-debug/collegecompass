One mole of an ideal gas undergoes two different cyclic processes I and II, as shown in the P-V diagrams below. In cycle I, processes a, b, c and d are isobaric, isothermal, isobaric and isochoric, respectively. In cycle II, processes a', b', c' and d' are isothermal, isochoric, isobaric and isochoric, respectively. The total work done during cycle I is W_I and that during cycle II is W_II. The ratio W_I / W_II is ________. [Two P-V diagrams side by side: Diagram (I): Shows a quadrilateral cycle with pressure axis from P₀ to 4P₀ and volume axis from V₀ to 4V₀. Process a is horizontal at 4P₀ from V₀ to 2V₀. Process b curves down from (2V₀, 4P₀) to (4V₀, 2P₀). Process c is horizontal at 2P₀ from 4V₀ to V₀. Process d is vertical from (V₀, 2P₀) to (V₀, 4P₀). Diagram (II): Shows a quadrilateral cycle with pressure axis from P₀ to 4P₀ and volume axis from V₀ to 2V₀. Process a' curves down from (V₀, 4P₀) to (2V₀, 2P₀). Process b' is vertical at 2V₀ from 2P₀ to P₀. Process c' is horizontal at P₀ from 2V₀ to V₀. Process d' is vertical from (V₀, P₀) to (V₀, 4P₀).]

Numerical (NAT) · Marks: +4 / 0.0

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

Sol. 2 Work done in cycle I is W_I = 4P₀V₀ + 8P₀V₀ ln2 - 6P₀V₀ W_II = 4P₀V₀ ln2 - P₀V₀ W_I / W_II = 2
