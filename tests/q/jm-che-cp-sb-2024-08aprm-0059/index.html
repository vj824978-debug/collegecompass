Major product B of the following reaction has ___ π-bond. Starting material: benzene with CH2CH3 group. Step 1: KMnO4, KOH, Δ gives (A). Step 2: HNO3/H2SO4 gives (B).

Answer

Correct answer: 5

Solution

The ethyl group (CH2CH3) on benzene is oxidized by KMnO4/KOH under heat to form a carboxylic acid group (-COOH), giving intermediate (A): benzene with COOK group. HNO3/H2SO4 performs nitration on the benzene ring, and further oxidation converts COOK to COOH, giving product (B): a nitrobenzoic acid with structure showing benzene ring (3 π-bonds) + carboxylic acid group (1 π-bond in C=O) + nitro group (2 π-bonds in N=O resonance structures, counted as 1 π-bond in the major form). Total π-bonds = 3 (benzene) + 1 (C=O in COOH) + 1 (N=O in NO2) = 5 π-bonds.
