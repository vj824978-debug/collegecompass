Let α be the area of the larger region bounded by the curve y^2 = 8x and the line y = x and x = 2, which lies in the first quadrant. Then the value of 3α is equal to ________.

Answer

Correct answer: 22

Solution

The parabola y^2 = 8x and line y = x intersect where y^2 = 8y, so y(y - 8) = 0, giving y = 0 or y = 8. Thus they intersect at (0, 0) and (8, 8). We need to find the area of the larger region bounded by y^2 = 8x, y = x, and x = 2. From y^2 = 8x, we get x = y^2/8. For the region from x = 0 to x = 2 (between the parabola and line y = x): A_1 = ∫[0 to 2] (2√(2√x) - x) dx = 2√2 × (2/3)x^(3/2) - x^2/2 |[0 to 2] = 2√2 × (2/3) × 2^(3/2) - 2 = (4√2/3) × 2√2 - 2 = 16/3 - 2 = 10/3 For the region from x = 2 to x = 8 (between the parabola and line y = x): A_2 = ∫[2 to 8] (2√(2√x) - x) dx = (4√2/3)x^(3/2) - x^2/2 |[2 to 8] = (4√2/3)(16√2 - 2√2) - 30 = (4√2/3)(14√2) - 30 = 112/3 - 30 = 22/3 Since A_2 > A_1, the larger region has area α = 22/3. Therefore, 3α = 22.
