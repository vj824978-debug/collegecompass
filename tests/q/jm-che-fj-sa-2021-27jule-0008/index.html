The correct sequence of correct reagent for the following transformation is: nitrobenzene to 3-chlorophenol

  1. (1) (i) Cl2, FeCl3 (ii) NaNO2, HCl 0°C (iii) Fe, HCl (iv) H2O/H+
  2. (2) (i) Cl2, FeCl3 (ii) Fe, HCl (iii) NaNO2, HCl 0°C (iv) H2O/H+
  3. (3) (i) Fe, HCl (ii) Cl2, HCl (iii) NaNO2, HCl 0°C (iv) H2O/H+
  4. (4) (i) Fe, HCl (ii) NaNO2, HCl 0°C (iii) H2O/H+ (iv) Cl2, FeCl3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The transformation requires: (1) Reduction of NO2 to NH2 using Fe, HCl (2) Chlorination of benzene ring using Cl2, FeCl3 (3) Diazotization of aniline using NaNO2, HCl at 0°C (4) Hydrolysis of diazonium salt using H2O/H+ to form phenol. The correct sequence is: (i) Fe, HCl to reduce NO2 to NH2 (ii) Cl2, HCl for chlorination (iii) NaNO2, HCl 0°C for diazotization (iv) H2O/H+ for hydrolysis. Answer: (C)
