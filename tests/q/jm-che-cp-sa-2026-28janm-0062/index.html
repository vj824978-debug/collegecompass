Given below are two statements: Statement I: Griss-Ilosvay test is used for the detection of nitrite ion, which involves the use of sulphanilic acid and α-naphthylamine reagent. Statement II: In the above test, sulphanilic acid is diazotized by the acidified nitrite ion, which on further coupling with α-naphthylamine forms an azo-dye. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

  1. (1) Statement I is false but Statement II is true
  2. (2) Both Statement I and Statement II are true
  3. (3) Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  4. (4) Both Statement I and Statement II are false

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

The Griss-Ilosvay test is a qualitative test for the detection of nitrite ion (NO2-). Mechanism: 1. First, nitrite ion reacts with acetic acid to form HNO2 (nitrous acid) NO2^- + CH3COOH → HNO2 + CH3COO^- 2. The HNO2 then diazotizes sulphanilic acid: The amino group (-NH2) of sulphanilic acid is converted to a diazonium group (-N≡N+) by the acidified nitrite ion (HNO2) Sulphanilic acid + HNO2 → Diazotized sulphanilic acid (diazonium salt) + 2H2O 3. This diazotized sulphanilic acid then couples with α-naphthylamine (1-naphthylamine) to form a red azo-dye: Diazotized sulphanilic acid + α-naphthylamine → Red azo-dye (N=N colored compound) + CH3COOH Statement I: Correct. The Griss-Ilosvay test uses sulphanilic acid and α-naphthylamine reagent for nitrite ion detection. Statement II: Correct. The sulphanilic acid is indeed diazotized by the acidified nitrite ion, and this diazotized product couples with α-naphthylamine to form a red azo-dye. Both statements are true.
