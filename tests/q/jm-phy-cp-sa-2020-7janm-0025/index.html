A particle (m = 1 kg) slides down a frictionless track (AOC) starting from rest at a point A (height 2 m). After reaching C, the particle continues to move freely in air as a projectile. When it reaching its highest point P (height 1 m), the kinetic energy of the particle (in J) is: (Figure drawn is schematic and not to scale; take g = 10 m s^(-2))

Answer

Correct answer: 10

Solution

Using conservation of energy: At point A: - Height = 2 m - Velocity = 0 (starts from rest) - Total mechanical energy = mgh = 1 × 10 × 2 = 20 J At highest point P: - Height = 1 m - The particle is moving as a projectile - At the highest point, the vertical component of velocity is zero - Only horizontal component of velocity remains Using conservation of energy from A to P: Total energy at A = Total energy at P mgh_A = mgh_P + (1/2)mv_P^2 1 × 10 × 2 = 1 × 10 × 1 + (1/2) × 1 × v_P^2 20 = 10 + (1/2)v_P^2 10 = (1/2)v_P^2 Kinetic energy at P = (1/2)mv_P^2 = 10 J Alternatively: K.E. = ΔP.E. = mgh = 1 × 10 × 1 = 10 Joule
