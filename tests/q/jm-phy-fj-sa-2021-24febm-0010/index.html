A cube of side 'a' has point charges +Q located at each of its vertices except at the origin where the charge is -Q. The electric field at the centre of cube is:

  1. (1) 2Q / (3*sqrt(3)*pi*epsilon_0*a^2) * (x + y + z)
  2. (2) Q / (3*sqrt(3)*pi*epsilon_0*a^2) * (x + y + z)
  3. (3) -2Q / (3*sqrt(3)*pi*epsilon_0*a^2) * (x + y + z)
  4. (4) -Q / (3*sqrt(3)*pi*epsilon_0*a^2) * (x + y + z)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

If charge (-Q) at origin is replaced by (+Q), then electric field at the centre of the cube is zero. Thus, electric field at the centre of the cube is as if only (-2Q) charge is present at the origin. E = 1/(4*pi*epsilon_0) * (-2Q)/((sqrt(3)*a)^2) * 1/sqrt(3) * (x + y + z) = -2Q / (3*sqrt(3)*pi*epsilon_0*a^2) * (x + y + z)
