A light source, which emits two wavelengths λ₁ = 400 nm and λ₂ = 600 nm, is used in a Young's double slit experiment. If recorded fringe widths for λ₁ and λ₂ are β₁ and β₂ and the number of fringes for them within a distance y on one side of the central maximum are m₁ and m₂, respectively, then

Multiple correct (MSQ) · Marks: +4 / -1.0

  1. (A) β₂ > β₁
  2. (B) m₁ > m₂
  3. (C) From the central maximum, 3rd maximum of λ₂ overlaps with 5th minimum of λ₁
  4. (D) The angular separation of fringes for λ₁ is greater than λ₂

Answer

Correct answer: ABC

Solution

β = Dλ/d ∴ λ₂ > λ₁ ⇒ β₂ > β₁ Also m₁β₁ = m₂β₂ ⇒ m₁ > m₂ Also 3(D/d)(600 nm) = (2×5-1)(D/2d)400nm Angular width θ = λ/d
