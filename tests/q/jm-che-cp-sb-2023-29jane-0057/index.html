For conversion of compounds A → B, the rate constant of the reaction was found to be 4.6 × 10^(-5) L mol^(-1) s^(-1). The order of the reaction is _________.

Answer

Correct answer: 2

Solution

As unit is L mol^(-1) s^(-1), order of the reaction is 2. Explanation: The units of rate constant k depend on the order of the reaction. For a reaction of order n, the units of k are: (concentration)^(1-n) × (time)^(-1) Given: k = 4.6 × 10^(-5) L mol^(-1) s^(-1) The units are L mol^(-1) s^(-1), which can be written as (mol/L)^(-1) s^(-1) or (concentration)^(-1) × (time)^(-1) Comparing with the general formula: (concentration)^(1-n) = (concentration)^(-1) Therefore: 1 - n = -1 n = 2 Hence, the order of the reaction is 2 (second-order reaction).
