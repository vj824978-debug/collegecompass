Aman has been asked to synthesise the molecule with a cyclopentene ring and a side chain C-CH3(x). He thought of preparing the molecule using an aldol condensation reaction. He found a few cyclic alkenes in his laboratory. He thought of performing ozonolysis reaction on alkene to produce a dicarbonyl compound followed by aldol reaction to prepare "x". Predict the suitable alkene that can lead to the formation of "x".

  1. (1) Cyclohexene with CH3 substituent at position 1
  2. (2) Cyclohexene with CH3 substituent at position 2
  3. (3) Cyclohexene with CH2 substituent (double bond) at position 1
  4. (4) Cyclohexene with CH3 substituent at position 3

Answer

Correct answer: Option 1

Solution

The target molecule is a cyclopentene ring with a -C-CH3(x) substituent. Strategy: Ozonolysis of an alkene → dicarbonyl compound → aldol reaction → target molecule For the target molecule to form via aldol condensation, we need a dicarbonyl intermediate that can undergo aldol reaction. Working backwards: - The target has a cyclopentene ring with an aldol-condensation-derived side chain - Aldol condensation requires a ketone/aldehyde with an alpha-H - Ozonolysis of the alkene should produce this dicarbonyl For option (1): Cyclohexene with CH3 at position 1 (1-methylcyclohexene) Ozonolysis would cleave the double bond to give: - A 5-membered ring ketone (cyclopentanone) + formaldehyde or appropriate carbonyl - This cyclopentanone can then undergo aldol condensation with formaldehyde to form the cyclopentene ring with the -CH=C(CH3) side chain This is the correct precursor because: 1. Ozonolysis of 1-methylcyclohexene breaks the double bond 2. The resulting dicarbonyl compounds can undergo aldol reaction 3. The aldol product forms the desired cyclopentene-CH=C-CH3 structure Options (2), (3), and (4) would produce different regioisomers or incorrect dicarbonyl intermediates that cannot lead to the target structure.
