Quantitative analysis of an organic compound (X) shows following % composition. C: 14.5%; Cl: 64.46%; H: 1.8% (Empirical formula mass of the compound (X) is _____ × 10^(-1) (Given molar mass in g mol^(-1) of C: 12, H: 1, O: 16, Cl: 35.5)

Answer

Correct answer: 1655

Solution

Step 1: Find the percentage of oxygen C: 14.5%, Cl: 64.46%, H: 1.8% O = 100 - (14.5 + 64.46 + 1.8) = 100 - 80.76 = 19.24% Step 2: Calculate molar ratio by dividing by atomic mass C: 14.5/12 = 1.208 Cl: 64.46/35.5 = 1.816 H: 1.8/1 = 1.8 O: 19.24/16 = 1.202 Step 3: Find the simplest whole number ratio by dividing by the smallest value (1.202) C: 1.208/1.202 ≈ 1.005 ≈ 1 Cl: 1.816/1.202 ≈ 1.510 ≈ 1.5 ≈ 3/2 H: 1.8/1.202 ≈ 1.497 ≈ 1.5 ≈ 3/2 O: 1.202/1.202 = 1 Multiplying by 2 to get whole numbers: C: 2, Cl: 3, H: 3, O: 2 Step 4: Write empirical formula Empirical formula = C₂H₃Cl₃O₂ Step 5: Calculate empirical formula mass Mass = 2(12) + 3(35.5) + 3(1) + 2(16) Mass = 24 + 106.5 + 3 + 32 Mass = 165.5 g/mol Step 6: Express in the required form Mass = 165.5 = 1655 × 10^(-1)
