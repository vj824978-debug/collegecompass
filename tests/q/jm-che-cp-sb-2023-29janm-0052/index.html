Solid Lead nitrate is dissolved in 1 litre of water. The solution was found to boil at 100.15°C. When 0.2 mol of NaCl is added to the resulting solution, it was observed that the solution froze at -0.8°C. The solubility product of PbCl2 formed is _____ × 10^(-6) at 298 K. (Nearest integer) Given: K_b = 0.5 K kg mol^(-1) and K_f = 1.8 K kg mol^(-1). Assume molality to be equal to molarity in all cases.

Answer

Correct answer: 13

Solution

0.15 = 3 × 0.5 × M M_Pb(NO3)2 = 0.1 molar Pb(NO3)2 + 2NaCl ⟶ PbCl2 + 2NaNO3 ΔT_f = iK_f · m 0.8 = (0.4 + 3s) 1.8 s = 0.0148 ∴ solubility product = 4s^3 = 4 × (0.0148)^3 ≈ 13 × 10^(-6)
