Two charges q1 and q2 are separated by a distance of 30 cm. A third charge q3 initially at 'C' as shown in the figure, is moved along the circular path of radius 40 cm from C to D. If the difference in potential energy due to movement of q3 from C to D is given by q3*K/(4*pi*epsilon_0), the value of K is

  1. (1) 8q1
  2. (2) 8q2
  3. (3) 6q2
  4. (4) 6q1

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

u(C) = (k*q1/40 + k*q2/50)*q3, u(D) = (k*q1/40 + k*q2/10)*q3. Delta_u = u(D) - u(C) = k*q2*(1/10 - 1/50)*q3 = k*q2*4*q3/50 = 4*q2*q3/(4*pi*epsilon_0*50). Comparing with q3*K/(4*pi*epsilon_0), we get K = 8*q2
