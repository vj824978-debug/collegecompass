At steady state the charge on the capacitor, as shown in the circuit below, is _____ μC.

Answer

Correct answer: 16

Solution

At steady state, no current flows through the capacitor. The capacitor acts as an open circuit. The circuit consists of a 5V source connected to resistances 10Ω and 15Ω in series with a capacitor of 8μF. At steady state, the voltage across the capacitor equals the voltage across the 15Ω resistor.
