The number of 4 letter words (with or without meaning) that can be formed from the eleven letters of the word 'EXAMINATION' is

Answer

Correct answer: 2454.00

Solution

Sol. 2A, 2I, 2N, E, X, M, T, O Total words = (all 4 different letters) + (2 alike & 2 different letters) + (2alike & 2 other alike letters) ^8P_4 + ^3C_1.^C_2.(4!/2!) + ^3C_2.(4!/(2!x2!)) = (8!)/(4!) + 3 × (7.6)/(2) × (4.3)/(2) + 3 × 6 = (8 × 7 × 6 × 5) + 3 × (7.6)/2 × (4.3)/2 + 3 × 6 = 8 × 7 × 6 × 5 + 63 − 12 + 18 = 1680 + 765 + 18 = 2454
