For the reaction, H2(g) + I2(g) ⇌ 2HI(g) Attainment of equilibrium is predicted correctly by:

  1. (1) Graph (1) showing I2 highest, then H2, then HI lowest on y-axis (Molar conc.), all relatively constant over time
  2. (2) Graph (2) showing H2 highest, then I2, then HI lowest on y-axis (Molar conc.), all relatively constant over time
  3. (3) Graph (3) showing H2 highest, then I2, then HI lowest on y-axis (Molar conc.), all decreasing over time
  4. (4) Graph (4) showing H2, I2, and HI all starting high and decreasing over time on y-axis (Molar conc.)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

H2 + I2 ⇌ 2HI Concentration of H2 and I2 decreases until equilibrium condition and concentration of HI increases till equilibrium condition and after equilibrium concentration of all the reactant and products remain constant. Correct option (2) Explanation: At the start of the reaction, H2 and I2 are reactants and their concentrations are high. As the reaction proceeds towards equilibrium, H2 and I2 are consumed (concentrations decrease) and HI is produced (concentration increases). Once equilibrium is reached, all three species maintain constant concentrations. Graph (2) correctly shows this behavior: H2 and I2 starting with high concentrations that decrease and then plateau, while HI starts low, increases, and then plateaus at equilibrium.
