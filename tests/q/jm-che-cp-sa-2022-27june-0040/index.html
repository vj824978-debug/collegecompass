Arrange the following coordination compounds in the increasing order of magnetic moments. (Atomic numbers: Mn = 25; Fe = 26) (i) [FeF6]^3- (ii) [Fe(CN)6]^3- (iii) [MnCl6]^3- (high spin) (iv) [Mn(CN)6]^3-

  1. (1) i < ii < iv < iii
  2. (2) ii < iv < iii < i
  3. (3) i < iii < iv < ii
  4. (4) ii < iv < i < iii

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Determine electron configuration and unpaired electrons for each complex: A [FeF6]^3- (d^5, high spin) - 5 unpaired electrons, μ = 5.91 B.M. B [Fe(CN)6]^3- (d^5, low spin) - 1 unpaired electron, μ = 1.73 B.M. C [MnCl6]^3- (d^4, high spin) - 4 unpaired electrons, μ = 4.89 B.M. D [Mn(CN)6]^3- (d^4, low spin) - 2 unpaired electrons, μ = 2.82 B.M. Magnetic moment increases with number of unpaired electrons. Order: ii(1.73) < iv(2.82) < iii(4.89) < i(5.91)
