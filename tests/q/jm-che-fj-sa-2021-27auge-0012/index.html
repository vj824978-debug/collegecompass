Which one of the following is the major product of the given reaction? Starting material: benzene ring with NC group, a ketone group (C=O), and two CH3 groups on the tetrahydronaphthalene system. Reagents: (i) 2CH3MgBr (ii) H3O+ (iii) H2SO4, heat

  1. (1) NC group on benzene, with CH3 groups on tetrahydronaphthalene system, no ketone
  2. (2) Acetyl group (CH3CO) on benzene ring, with CH3 groups on tetrahydronaphthalene system
  3. (3) NC group on benzene with double bond in tetrahydronaphthalene ring, CH3 groups present
  4. (4) Acetyl group (CH3CO) on benzene ring with CH3 groups on tetrahydronaphthalene system

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Step 1: Grignard reagent (CH3MgBr) attacks the ketone carbonyl carbon. Step 2: Two equivalents of CH3MgBr add to form a tertiary alcohol intermediate with OMgBr group. Step 3: Aqueous workup (H3O+) converts OMgBr to OH, giving secondary alcohol. Step 4: Treatment with H2SO4 and heat causes dehydration of the alcohol, forming a C=C double bond in the tetrahydronaphthalene ring. Step 5: The NC (nitrile) group remains intact throughout. The major product is the alkene formed after dehydration with the nitrile group still attached.
