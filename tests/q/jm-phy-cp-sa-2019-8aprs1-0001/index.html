A thin circular plate of mass M and radius R has its density varying as ρ(r) = ρ₀r with ρ₀ as constant and r is the distance from its center. The moment of inertia of the circular plate about an axis perpendicular to the plate and passing through its edge is I = aMR². The value of the coefficient a is -

  1. (1) 3/2
  2. (2) 1/2
  3. (3) 8/5
  4. (4) 3/5

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

M = ∫₀^R (ρ₀r)(2πrdr) = 2πρ₀ ∫₀^R r² dr = 2πρ₀ (R³/3) Moment of inertia about center: I_center = ∫₀^R 2πρ₀r³ dr = 2πρ₀(R⁵/5) Using parallel axis theorem for axis at the edge: I = I_center + MR² I = 2πρ₀(R⁵/5) + MR² Substituting M = 2πρ₀(R³/3): I = 2πρ₀(R⁵/5) + [2πρ₀(R³/3)]R² I = 2πρ₀(R⁵/5) + 2πρ₀(R⁵/3) I = 2πρ₀R⁵(1/5 + 1/3) I = 2πρ₀R⁵(3 + 5)/(15) I = 2πρ₀R⁵(8/15) Since M = 2πρ₀(R³/3), we have: 2πρ₀ = 3M/R³ I = (3M/R³) × R⁵ × (8/15) I = 3M × R² × (8/15) I = (8MR²/5) Therefore, a = 8/5
