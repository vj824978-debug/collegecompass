Figure shows a part of an electric circuit. The potentials at points a, b and c are 30 V, 12 V and 2 V respectively. The current through the 20 Ω resistor will be,

  1. (1) 1.0 A
  2. (2) 0.4 A
  3. (3) 0.6 A
  4. (4) 0.2 A

Answer

Correct answer: Option 2

Solution

Using Kirchhoff's current law at the junction. Let potential at junction be x V. Current from a through 10 Ω: (30-x)/10. Current through 20 Ω to b: (x-12)/20. Current through 30 Ω to c: (x-2)/30. By KCL: (30-x)/10 = (x-12)/20 + (x-2)/30. Solving: 30-x + 6 - x/2 + 2 - x/3 = 0. This gives x = 20 V. Current through 20 Ω = (20-12)/20 = 8/20 = 0.4 A
