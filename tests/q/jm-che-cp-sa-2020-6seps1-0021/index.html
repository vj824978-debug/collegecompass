Potassium chlorate is prepared by the electrolysis of KCl in basic solution. 6OH^- + Cl^- → ClO3^- + 3H2O + 6e^- If only 60% of the current is utilized in the reaction, the time (rounded to the nearest hour) required to produced 10 g of KClO3 using a current of 2 A is ________. (Given: F = 96,500 C mol^(-1); molar mass of KClO3 = 122 g mol^(-1))

Answer

Correct answer: 11.00

Solution

Given: - Reaction: 6OH^- + Cl^- → ClO3^- + 3H2O + 6e^- - Mass of KClO3 to be produced = 10 g - Current (I) = 2 A - Faraday constant (F) = 96,500 C mol^(-1) - Molar mass of KClO3 = 122 g mol^(-1) - Only 60% of current is utilized Step 1: Calculate moles of KClO3 produced Moles of KClO3 = 10/122 = 0.0820 mol Step 2: From the equation, 6 electrons produce 1 mole of ClO3^- Electrons required = 6 × 0.0820 = 0.492 mol of electrons Step 3: Calculate charge required Charge required = 0.492 × 96,500 = 47,478 C Step 4: Since only 60% of current is utilized, actual charge needed Actual charge = 47,478/0.60 = 79,130 C Step 5: Calculate time using Q = I × t t = Q/I = 79,130/2 = 39,565 seconds Step 6: Convert to hours t = 39,565/3600 = 10.99 hours ≈ 11 hours (rounded to nearest hour)
