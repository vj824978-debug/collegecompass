Which among the following compounds will undergo fastest SN2 reaction.

  1. (1) Cyclobutane with CH3 and Br substituents (geminal)
  2. (2) Cyclobutane with CH3 and Br substituents (on adjacent carbons)
  3. (3) Cyclobutane with Br substituent on CH2 side chain
  4. (4) Cyclobutane with CH3 and Br substituents (on adjacent carbons, different arrangement)

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

For SN2 reaction, the rate depends on steric hindrance around the carbon bearing the leaving group (Br). The reaction proceeds via backside attack, so less steric hindrance leads to faster reaction. Option (3) has Br on a primary carbon (CH2 group attached to cyclobutane), which has minimal steric hindrance compared to secondary carbons in other options. The order of reactivity for SN2 is: 1° > 2° > 3°. Since option (3) represents a primary bromide, it undergoes the fastest SN2 reaction.
