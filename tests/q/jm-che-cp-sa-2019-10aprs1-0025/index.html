During the change of O2 to O2-, the incoming electron goes to the orbital:

  1. (1) sigma*2pz
  2. (2) pi2py
  3. (3) pi*2px
  4. (4) pi 2px

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

A/c to MOT For O2 and O2- we follow this: O2 → sigma/S^2, sigma/1S^2, sigma*2S^2, sigma2pz^2, pi2px^2 = pi2py^2 pi*2px^1 = pi*2py^1 sigma*2pz O2- → sigma/S^2, sigma*/S^2 sigma2S sigma*2S^2 sigma2S^2 pi2px^2 pi2py^2 = pi2pz^2 pi*2px^2 = pi*2py^1 sigma*2pz
