The energy of an electron in first Bohr orbit of H atom is -13.6 eV. The magnitude of energy value of electron in the first excited state of Be^3+ is _____ eV (nearest integer value)

Answer

Correct answer: 54

Solution

For hydrogen-like atoms, energy in nth orbit is E_n = -13.6 × Z^2 / n^2 eV. For Be^3+ (Z=4), in first excited state (n=2): E_2 = -13.6 × (4)^2 / (2)^2 = -13.6 × 16 / 4 = -54.4 eV. Magnitude |E_2| = 54.4 eV ≈ 54 eV (nearest integer)
