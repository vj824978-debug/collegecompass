When 1 g of compound (X) is subjected to Kjeldahl's method for estimation of nitrogen, 15 mL,1MH2SO4 was neutralised by ammonia evolved. The percentage of nitrogen in compound (X) is :

  1. (1) 21
  2. (2) 0.42
  3. (3) 42
  4. (4) 0.21

Answer

Correct answer: Option 3

Solution

eq. of H2SO4 = eq. of Ammonia => (15 × 1 × 2)/1000 = moles of ammonia × 1 => Moles of ammonia = moles of 'N' => Weight of nitrogen = (15 × 1 × 2)/1000 × 14 = 0.42 % weight of 'N' = (0.42/1) × 100 = 42%
