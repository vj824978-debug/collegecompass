Oxidation states of the metal in the minerals haematite and magnetite, respectively, are

Single correct MCQ · Marks: +3 / -1.0

  1. (A) (A) II, III in haematite and III in magnetite
  2. (B) (B) II, III in haematite and II in magnetite
  3. (C) (C) II in haematite and II, III in magnetite
  4. (D) (D) III in haematite and II, III in magnetite

Answer

Correct answer: D

Solution

Sol. (D) Haematite: Fe₂O₃: 2x + 3 × (-2) = 0 x = 3 Magnetite: Fe₃O₄ [an equimolar mixture of FeO and Fe₂O₃] FeO: x - 2 = 0 → x = 2 Fe₂O₃: x = 3
